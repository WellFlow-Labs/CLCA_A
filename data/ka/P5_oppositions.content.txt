## OPPOSITION 1

**Term A:** სიმართლე (simart'le)  
**Term B:** ტყუილი (ṭq'uili)  
**Transliteration:** simart'le vs. ṭq'uili  
**English translation:** truth vs. lie  
**Description of contrast:** Core lexical opposition between truth and falsehood in speech acts. Both terms function as nouns denoting the content of statements and appear in parallel constructions (სიმართლე თქვა "tell the truth" vs. ტყუილი თქვა "tell a lie").  
**Contrast type:** lexical  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## NOTES ON OPPOSITION PATTERNS

### Asymmetries Observed

1. **Truth vs. Falsehood**: Multiple lexical oppositions exist (სიმართლე/ტყუილი, ჭეშმარიტი/ყალბი, ნამდვილი/ყალბი) with different semantic profiles, suggesting the domain is not organized around a single binary opposition.

2. **Morphological Negation**: Two negative prefixes identified (უ- and არა-) with different distribution patterns. უ- creates stable antonyms with distinct meanings; არა- appears more compositional.

3. **Revelation vs. Concealment**: Elaborate constructional oppositions for revealing/hiding truth, with both transitive and intransitive variants, suggesting cultural salience of truth disclosure.

4. **Implicit Oppositions**: Many constructions with modifiers like ნამდვილი "true/real" presuppose implicit contrasts (true price vs. apparent price, true reason vs. stated reason) without lexicalized opposing terms.

5. **Context-Dependent Oppositions**: Some lexical items (მართალი, ჭეშმარიტი, ნამდვილი) show semantic shifts creating internal oppositions based on context, register, or noun class modified.

### Cross-Cutting Patterns

1. **Multiple Strategies**: Opposition expressed through lexical pairs, morphological negation, syntactic negation, and constructional contrasts, with no single dominant strategy.

2. **Register Variation**: Some oppositions show register sensitivity (ჭეშმარიტი in everyday vs. religious contexts), while others are register-neutral.

3. **Domain Specificity**: Legal domain shows distinct opposition patterns (კანონიერი/უკანონო), suggesting domain-specific organization.

4. **Aspectual Oppositions**: Truth-seeking constructions show aspectual oppositions (completive vs. processual) not based on lexical contrast but on verbal aspect.

### Gaps and Uncertainties

1. **Causative Opposition**: No clear opposition identified in causative domain due to lack of systematic causative constructions in the data.

2. **Scalar Oppositions**: Limited evidence for scalar or gradable oppositions (more true/less true), suggesting truth domain may be primarily categorical rather than scalar in Georgian.

3. **Asymmetric Lexicalization**: Many positive terms (true, genuine, reliable) have lexicalized opposites, but some implicit contrasts lack lexicalized opposing terms, creating asymmetric opposition structures.
