| सत्य (*satya*) | सत्यत्व | satyatva | *satya* + *-tva* (abstract nominal suffix); "the state of being true" | truthfulness, the property of being true | Parallel to *satyatā* but formed with the *-tva* suffix; used in philosophical prose to name the abstract property of truth.
