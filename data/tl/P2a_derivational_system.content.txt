# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2a Derivational System
# Language: Tagalog (tl)

## DERIVATIONAL ANALYSIS

### BASE: **totoo** (true, real)

#### Derived Form 1
- **Base Form:** totoo
- **Derived Form:** katotohanan
- **Transliteration:** katotohanan
- **Literal Breakdown:** ka- + totoo + -han (circumfix forming abstract noun)
- **Translation:** truth, reality, the truth
- **Usage:** Forms abstract noun denoting the concept or state of truth. Used in philosophical, legal, and everyday contexts to refer to factual reality or true state of affairs.
- **Status:** [CORE]

#### Derived Form 2
- **Base Form:** totoo
- **Derived Form:** totóhanán
- **Transliteration:** totóhanán
- **Literal Breakdown:** totoo + -han + -an (suffix combination marking manner/reciprocal)
- **Translation:** seriously, in earnest, for real
- **Usage:** Adverbial form indicating seriousness or genuine intent. Distinguishes serious statements from jokes or pretense.
- **Status:** [CORE]

#### Derived Form 3
- **Base Form:** totoo
- **Derived Form:** makatotohanan
- **Transliteration:** makatotohanan
- **Literal Breakdown:** maka- + totohanan (prefix marking ability/characteristic + truth-related form)
- **Translation:** realistic, plausible, true-to-life
- **Usage:** Adjectival form describing representations or accounts that correspond to reality or are believable. Used to evaluate verisimilitude.
- **Status:** [CORE]

#### Derived Form 4
- **Base Form:** totoo
- **Derived Form:** di-totoo
- **Transliteration:** di-totoo
- **Literal Breakdown:** di- (negative prefix) + totoo
- **Translation:** untrue, false, not real
- **Usage:** Negated form indicating lack of truth or reality. Direct negation used in everyday speech.
- **Status:** [CORE]

#### Derived Form 5
- **Base Form:** totoo
- **Derived Form:** nagtotoo
- **Transliteration:** nagtotoo
- **Literal Breakdown:** nag- (actor voice prefix) + totoo (with progressive reduplication)
- **Translation:** is coming true, is becoming real
- **Usage:** Verbal form describing the process of predictions, dreams, or expectations being realized or manifesting in reality.
- **Status:** [CORE]

#### Derived Form 6
- **Base Form:** totoo
- **Derived Form:** tumutuoo
- **Transliteration:** tumutuoo
- **Literal Breakdown:** tum- (actor voice intransitive prefix) + totoo (with progressive reduplication)
- **Translation:** is coming true, is materializing
- **Usage:** Alternative verbal form describing the process of something becoming real or true. Used for hopes, fears, or predictions manifesting.
- **Status:** [CORE]

#### Derived Form 7
- **Base Form:** totoo
- **Derived Form:** magkatotoo
- **Transliteration:** magkatotoo
- **Literal Breakdown:** magka- (accidental/resultative prefix) + totoo
- **Translation:** to come true, to be realized
- **Usage:** Verbal form describing the realization or fulfillment of something hoped for or predicted. Common in expressions about wishes or prophecies being fulfilled.
- **Status:** [CORE]

#### Derived Form 8
- **Base Form:** totoo
- **Derived Form:** pagtotoo
- **Transliteration:** pagtotoo
- **Literal Breakdown:** pag- (nominalizing prefix) + totoo
- **Translation:** the act of becoming true, realization
- **Usage:** Nominal form referring to the process or event of something becoming true or real. Used in formal or narrative contexts.
- **Status:** [CORE]

#### Derived Form 9
- **Base Form:** totoo
- **Derived Form:** totoong-totoo
- **Transliteration:** totoong-totoo
- **Literal Breakdown:** totoo + -ng (linker) + totoo (reduplication)
- **Translation:** very true, absolutely real, completely genuine
- **Usage:** Emphatic reduplication stressing high degree of truth or reality. Used to emphasize authenticity or remove doubt about veracity.
- **Status:** [CORE]

## SUMMARY OF DERIVATIONAL PATTERNS

### Highly Productive Patterns:

1. **ka- ... -an circumfix** (abstract noun formation): Attested with totoo → katotohanan, tunay → katunayan, tapat → katapatan, sinungaling → kasinungalingan, tama → katamaan, mali → (pagkakamali uses different pattern)

2. **mag- prefix** (actor voice verbal): Widely attested across roots: magsinungaling, magpeke, maghuwad, magkunwari, magtapat, magpalabas, etc.

3. **pag- ... reduplication** (nominalization): Common pattern for action nouns: pagpapatunay, pagdadaya, paglilinlang, paglilihim, etc.

4. **Reduplication for emphasis**: Productive across many roots: totoong-totoo, tunay na tunay, tapat na tapat, sinungaling na sinungaling, etc.

5. **Progressive aspect with reduplication**: Standard pattern: nagsisinungaling, nandadaya, nanlilinlang, nagkukunwari, etc.

### Limited Productivity:

1. **Loanwords** (peke, sinsero, hustó): Show restricted derivational morphology, primarily limited to basic verbal and emphatic forms

2. **Already derived forms** (makatotohanan, di-totoo, kathang-isip): Generally do not undergo further derivation

3. **Modal/functional items** (dapat): Limited derivational potential due to grammatical function

### Gaps and Asymmetries:

- Not all roots show equal derivational productivity
- Some semantic fields (correctness: tama, wasto, hustó) show functional overlap limiting individual productivity
- Loanwords integrate variably into native derivational patterns
- Some roots are already morphologically complex, limiting further derivation
