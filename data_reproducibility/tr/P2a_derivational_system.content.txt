### BASE: **doğru** (straight, true, correct)

#### Derived Forms

1. **doğruluk**
   - Transliteration: doğruluk
   - Literal breakdown: doğru + -luk (abstract nominalizer)
   - Translation: truth, correctness, honesty, integrity
   - Usage: Abstract noun denoting the quality of being true or honest. Used in philosophical, moral, and everyday contexts to refer to truthfulness as a property or virtue.
   - [CORE]

2. **doğrulamak**
   - Transliteration: doğrulamak
   - Literal breakdown: doğru + -la (denominative verbalizer) + -mak (infinitive)
   - Translation: to verify, to confirm, to validate
   - Usage: Transitive verb meaning to establish or confirm the truth of something. Common in scientific, legal, and administrative discourse.
   - [CORE]

3. **doğrulama**
   - Transliteration: doğrulama
   - Literal breakdown: doğrula + -ma (action/result nominalizer)
   - Translation: verification, confirmation, validation
   - Usage: Nominal form referring to the act or process of verification. Technical term in computing, law, and quality control.
   - [CORE]

4. **doğrudan**
   - Transliteration: doğrudan
   - Literal breakdown: doğru + -dan (ablative case, lexicalized as adverb)
   - Translation: directly, straightforwardly
   - Usage: Adverb indicating direct manner without intermediaries. Used for both spatial directness and communicative directness.
   - [CORE]

5. **doğrultmak**
   - Transliteration: doğrultmak
   - Literal breakdown: doğru + -lt (causative) + -mak
   - Translation: to straighten, to direct, to aim
   - Usage: Causative verb meaning to make straight or to direct something toward a goal. Used for physical straightening and metaphorical directing of efforts.
   - [CORE]

6. **doğrultu**
   - Transliteration: doğrultu
   - Literal breakdown: doğru + -ltu (direction/manner nominalizer)
   - Translation: direction, line, course
   - Usage: Noun denoting direction or trajectory. Used in navigation, geometry, and metaphorically for policy or action direction.
   - [CORE]

7. **doğruca**
   - Transliteration: doğruca
   - Literal breakdown: doğru + -ca (manner suffix)
   - Translation: directly, straight away
   - Usage: Adverbial form emphasizing direct manner of action. Slightly more emphatic than doğrudan.
   - [CORE]

8. **doğrucu**
   - Transliteration: doğrucu
   - Literal breakdown: doğru + -cu (agentive/adherent suffix)
   - Translation: truthful person, honest person
   - Usage: Agentive form describing someone characterized by truthfulness. Less common, more literary or formal.
   - [MARGINAL]

9. **doğrulukçu**
   - Transliteration: doğrulukçu
   - Literal breakdown: doğruluk + -çu (adherent suffix)
   - Translation: advocate of truth, principled person
   - Usage: Describes someone who strongly adheres to principles of truth and honesty. Used in character descriptions, sometimes with connotation of rigidity.
   - [MARGINAL]

## SUMMARY NOTES

### Productivity Patterns Observed

1. **-lık/-lik/-luk/-lük (abstract nominalizer)**: Highly productive across nearly all adjectival bases in the truth domain. Creates abstract nouns denoting qualities or states. Attested with: doğru, yanlış, gerçek, sahte, güvenilir, dürüst, haklı, haksız, kesin, özgün, tutarlı, geçerli, yalancı, inandırıcı, sağlam, doğal, yapay, and many others.

2. **-sız/-siz/-suz/-süz (privative)**: Productive for creating negative counterparts. Attested with: yanlış, hak, şüphe, kuşku, hata, isabet, geçer, tutar, adalet.

3. **-leş/-laş (inchoative "become")**: Productive for creating verbs of becoming. Attested with: gerçek, kesin, sağlam, doğal, yapay, meşru.

4. **-leştir/-laştır (causative of inchoative)**: Productive for creating causative verbs meaning "to make X." Attested with: gerçek, kesin, sağlam, doğal, meşru.

5. **-çi/-ci/-çı/-cı (agentive/adherent)**: Moderately productive for creating agent nouns or adherents. Attested with: gerçek, şüphe, taklit, yalan, hakikat.

6. **-la/-le (denominative verbalizer)**: Productive for creating verbs from nouns. Attested with: doğru, yanlış, yalan.

7. **Ablative case -dan/-den/-tan/-ten lexicalized as adverbs**: Pattern observed with: doğru → doğrudan, gerçek → gerçekten.

8. **Instrumental case -la/-le lexicalized as adverbs**: Pattern observed with: kesinlik → kesinlikle, yanlışlık → yanlışlıkla.

### Gaps and Asymmetries

1. Some bases show extensive derivational families (hak, gerçek, doğru) while others show minimal derivation (asıl, özgün).

2. Not all adjectives form agentive -çi derivatives; this appears restricted to certain semantic classes.

3. The inchoative -leş pattern is productive primarily with quality adjectives, less so with relational or inherent properties.

4. Arabic-origin terms (hak, hakikat, adalet, meşru) often preserve Arabic-derived abstract forms alongside Turkish derivational patterns.

5. Some bases participate in fixed phrases and collocations that function as units but are not strictly derivational (e.g., hak vermek, doğru söylemek).

### Notes on Borrowed Morphology

Turkish incorporates both native Turkic derivational morphology and patterns from Arabic and Persian:

- Arabic nisba pattern preserved in: hakiki (from *ḥaqīqī*)
- Arabic abstract patterns: hakikat, hakkaniyet, meşruiyet, adalet
- Persian agentive -kâr: sahtekâr
- These coexist with native Turkish derivational suffixes applied to the same roots
