## BASE FORM: **òtítọ́** (truth, truthfulness)

### Construction 1: òtítọ́ ni
**Example:** Òtítọ́ ni
**Transliteration:** Òtítọ́ ni
**Translation:** It is truth, It is true, Truly
**Usage:** Fixed expression used to emphatically affirm the truth of a statement or to introduce a truthful assertion. Very common in both formal and informal discourse.
**[CORE]**

### Construction 2: sọ òtítọ́
**Example:** Sọ òtítọ́
**Transliteration:** Sọ òtítọ́
**Translation:** Tell the truth, Speak the truth
**Usage:** Imperative construction commanding someone to speak truthfully. Very common in everyday discourse when demanding honesty.
**[CORE]**

### Construction 3: ní òtítọ́
**Example:** Ní òtítọ́
**Transliteration:** Ní òtítọ́
**Translation:** In truth, Truthfully, Honestly
**Usage:** Adverbial phrase used to emphasize that what follows is truthful or to introduce a sincere statement. Common in both speech and writing.
**[CORE]**

### Construction 4: pẹ̀lú òtítọ́
**Example:** Pẹ̀lú òtítọ́
**Transliteration:** Pẹ̀lú òtítọ́
**Translation:** With truth, Truthfully
**Usage:** Prepositional phrase indicating that an action is performed truthfully or honestly. Common in formal speech.
**[CORE]**

### Construction 5: jẹ́ òtítọ́
**Example:** Ó jẹ́ òtítọ́
**Transliteration:** Ó jẹ́ òtítọ́
**Translation:** It is truth, It is true
**Usage:** Copular construction affirming that something is true. Common in verification and confirmation contexts.
**[CORE]**

### Construction 6: òtítọ́ kọ́
**Example:** Òtítọ́ kọ́
**Transliteration:** Òtítọ́ kọ́
**Translation:** Not truth, It's not true
**Usage:** Negative construction denying that something is true. Common in disagreement and correction.
**[CORE]**

### Construction 7: òtítọ́ rẹ̀
**Example:** Òtítọ́ rẹ̀
**Transliteration:** Òtítọ́ rẹ̀
**Translation:** Your truth, Your truthfulness
**Usage:** Possessive construction referring to someone's personal truthfulness or honesty. Common in character assessment.
**[CORE]**

### Construction 8: òtítọ́ ọ̀rọ̀
**Example:** Òtítọ́ ọ̀rọ̀
**Transliteration:** Òtítọ́ ọ̀rọ̀
**Translation:** Truth of the matter, Truthfulness of the statement
**Usage:** Compound construction referring to the truthfulness of a specific statement or matter. Common in evaluative discourse.
**[CORE]**

### Construction 9: fún ni ní òtítọ́
**Example:** Fún mi ní òtítọ́
**Transliteration:** Fún mi ní òtítọ́
**Translation:** Give me the truth, Tell me the truth
**Usage:** Construction requesting truthful information from someone. Common in interrogative contexts.

### Construction 10: gbé òtítọ́ kalẹ̀
