# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2a Derivational System
# Language: Indonesian (id)

---

## Preliminary Note on Method

Derivations are documented only where the derived form is independently attested in use. Paradigm gaps are preserved. Where a morphological slot appears to exist but the specific form is not attested or is marginal, this is noted explicitly. Forms already documented in P1b are cross-referenced rather than repeated in full, but are included in the inventory for completeness.

## 1. Base Form: **benar**

---

**Derived Form 1**
**Base Form:** benar
**Derived Form:** kebenaran
**Transliteration:** N/A
**Literal Breakdown:** *ke-* (circumfix nominalizer, open) + *benar* + *-an* (circumfix nominalizer, close)
**Natural English Translation:** truth, correctness, rightness
**Usage:** Abstract noun referring to the property of being true or correct, or to a specific true fact. Used across philosophical, religious, legal, and everyday registers; *mencari kebenaran* (to seek the truth) is a stable collocation.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2**
**Base Form:** benar
**Derived Form:** membenarkan
**Transliteration:** N/A
**Literal Breakdown:** *mem-* (active transitive prefix, nasal assimilation of *meN-*) + *benar* + *-kan* (causative/applicative suffix)
**Natural English Translation:** to confirm, to justify, to validate; also to permit
**Usage:** Active transitive verb used in journalistic, institutional, and interpersonal contexts to mean either "to confirm that something is true" or "to justify/excuse an action." In administrative and regulatory language, *tidak dibenarkan* (not permitted) represents a deontic extension of the base meaning.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 3**
**Base Form:** benar
**Derived Form:** dibenarkan
**Transliteration:** N/A
**Literal Breakdown:** *di-* (passive prefix) + *benar* + *-kan*
**Natural English Translation:** to be confirmed; to be permitted, to be justified
**Usage:** Passive counterpart of *membenarkan*. The formula *tidak dibenarkan* is standard in regulations and official documents to mean "not permitted" or "not justified."
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 4**
**Base Form:** benar
**Derived Form:** pembenaran
**Transliteration:** N/A
**Literal Breakdown:** *pem-* (process/result nominalizer, nasal assimilation of *peN-*) + *benar* + *-an*
**Natural English Translation:** justification, confirmation, validation, rationalization
**Usage:** Noun referring to the act or result of justifying or confirming. Used in moral, psychological, legal, and theological discourse; *pembenaran diri* (self-justification) and *pembenaran oleh iman* (justification by faith, in Protestant theological usage) are established collocations.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 5**
**Base Form:** benar
**Derived Form:** sebenarnya
**Transliteration:** N/A
**Literal Breakdown:** *se-* (equative/specifying prefix) + *benar* + *-nya* (specifying/nominalizing enclitic)
**Natural English Translation:** actually, in truth, in fact, to tell the truth
**Usage:** Discourse adverb used to introduce a correction, clarification, or revelation of what is actually the case, frequently contrasting with a prior assumption or appearance. Extremely common in both spoken and written Indonesian.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 6**
**Base Form:** benar
**Derived Form:** sebenar-benarnya
**Transliteration:** N/A
**Literal Breakdown:** *se-* + *benar* + full reduplication of *benar* + *-nya*
**Natural English Translation:** the absolute truth, truly and completely, in all honesty
**Usage:** Emphatic form of *sebenarnya*, used in formal declarations, oaths, and solemn statements to assert that what follows is the complete and unqualified truth. Common in legal testimony and formal written declarations.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 7**
**Base Form:** benar
**Derived Form:** benar-benar
**Transliteration:** N/A
**Literal Breakdown:** *benar* + full reduplication
**Natural English Translation:** truly, really, genuinely, absolutely
**Usage:** Intensifying adverb used to emphasize the degree or genuineness of a quality or action. Functions as a general intensifier across contexts, including those where no truth claim is being evaluated (*benar-benar cantik* — truly beautiful). Very common in everyday speech and writing.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 8**
**Base Form:** benar
**Derived Form:** kebenaran hakiki
**Transliteration:** N/A
**Literal Breakdown:** *kebenaran* (truth) + *hakiki* (fundamental, essential — from Arabic *ḥaqīqī*, adjectival form of *ḥaqīqa*)
**Natural English Translation:** fundamental truth, ultimate truth, essential truth
**Usage:** Collocation used in philosophical, theological, and literary discourse to refer to truth at its deepest or most essential level, as opposed to surface or partial truth.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 9**
**Base Form:** benar
**Derived Form:** kebenaran mutlak
**Transliteration:** N/A
**Literal Breakdown:** *kebenaran* (truth) + *mutlak* (absolute — from Arabic *muṭlaq*)
**Natural English Translation:** absolute truth
**Usage:** Used in philosophical and religious discourse to refer to truth that is unconditional and not relative to perspective or context. Contrasts with *kebenaran relatif*.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 10**
**Base Form:** benar
**Derived Form:** kebenaran relatif
**Transliteration:** N/A
**Literal Breakdown:** *kebenaran* (truth) + *relatif* (relative — from Dutch *relatief*)
**Natural English Translation:** relative truth
**Usage:** Used in philosophical and academic discourse to refer to truth conditioned by perspective, context, or framework. Appears in epistemology, social science, and popular intellectual writing.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 11**
**Base Form:** benar
**Derived Form:** kebenaran ilmiah
**Transliteration:** N/A
**Literal Breakdown:** *kebenaran* (truth) + *ilmiah* (scientific — adjectival form from Arabic *ʿilm*, knowledge)
**Natural English Translation:** scientific truth
**Usage:** Used in academic and scientific discourse to refer to truth established through scientific method and empirical evidence. Appears in research methodology discussions and science education.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 12**
**Base Form:** benar
**Derived Form:** menegakkan kebenaran
**Transliteration:** N/A
**Literal Breakdown:** *menegakkan* (to uphold, to enforce — *meN-* + *tegak*, upright + *-kan*) + *kebenaran* (truth)
**Natural English Translation:** to uphold the truth, to stand up for what is right
**Usage:** Collocation used in legal, moral, and political discourse to describe the act of defending or enforcing truth and justice. Common in formal speeches, journalism, and advocacy writing.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Note on *benar* productivity:** The *ke-...-an* nominalization (*kebenaran*) and the *meN-...-kan* verb (*membenarkan*) with its passive (*dibenarkan*) and process nominalization (*pembenaran*) are all well-attested and frequent. The *se-...-nya* adverb (*sebenarnya*) and its emphatic reduplicated form are stable lexical items. Full reduplication (*benar-benar*) functions as a general intensifier. No agentive noun (*pembenar* — one who confirms/justifies) is listed here; while morphologically predictable, its independent lexicalization is not sufficiently established to include without qualification.

---

**Derived Form 5**
**Base Form:** salah
**Derived Form:** bersalah

**Natural English Translation:** guilty, at fault, in the wrong
**Usage:** Stative verb/predicate adjective used in legal and moral contexts to describe a state of guilt or culpability. *Terbukti bersalah* (proven guilty) is a standard legal formula in Indonesian court verdicts.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 6**
**Base Form:** salah
**Derived Form:** tidak bersalah
**Transliteration:** N/A
**Literal Breakdown:** *tidak* (negation) + *bersalah* (guilty)

**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 7**
**Base Form:** salah
**Derived Form:** salah paham
**Transliteration:** N/A
**Literal Breakdown:** *salah* (wrong, mistaken) + *paham* (understanding, comprehension)
**Natural English Translation:** misunderstanding, misconception
**Usage:** Compound noun/predicate used in everyday and formal contexts to describe a failure of mutual understanding. *Terjadi salah paham* (A misunderstanding occurred) is a common collocation.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 8**
**Base Form:** salah
**Derived Form:** salah kaprah
**Transliteration:** N/A
**Literal Breakdown:** *salah* (wrong) + *kaprah* (widespread, common — referring to common practice)
**Natural English Translation:** widespread misconception, commonly accepted error
**Usage:** Fixed expression used to describe an error or misconception that has become so widespread it is accepted as correct. Common in language commentary, journalism, and educational writing.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 9**
**Base Form:** salah
**Derived Form:** salah satu
**Transliteration:** N/A
**Literal Breakdown:** *salah* (one of, some — in this construction) + *satu* (one)
**Natural English Translation:** one of (the), one among
**Usage:** Fixed expression meaning "one of" — used to single out one item from a set. Note: in this construction *salah* does not carry its primary meaning of "wrong/mistaken" but functions as a partitive quantifier. This is a semantic extension documented here for completeness.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Note on *salah satu*:** The use of *salah* in *salah satu* (one of) represents a grammaticalized extension where the original meaning of "wrong/mistaken" is not operative. This is included because it is a high-frequency form derived from the base, but it does not contribute to the truth-evaluation domain.

**Usage:** Used in legal, administrative, and moral contexts to describe the improper or unauthorized use of something, particularly authority, power, or resources. *Menyalahgunakan wewenang* (to abuse authority) is a standard legal and journalistic collocation.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 11**
**Base Form:** salah

**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]
