# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P5 Oppositions
# Language: Chinese (zh)

Below are the attested contrastive pairs and oppositional structures supported by the CONTEXT. Each is grounded in explicit P1–P4 statements (direct antonym labels, negation patterns, or documented constructional contrasts).

---

## OPPOSITION 1

1. **Term A:** 真 (zhēn)
2. **Term B:** 假 (jiǎ)
3. **Transliteration:** zhēn / jiǎ
4. **English:** true, real, genuine / false, fake, counterfeit
5. **Description of contrast:** P1 explicitly labels these as direct antonyms ("Contrasts directly with 假" / "Direct antonym of 真"). They form the primary positive/negative pole of the genuineness axis, also fused in the binomial 真假/真伪.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## OPPOSITION 2

1. **Term A:** 正确 (zhèngquè)
2. **Term B:** 错误 (cuòwù)
3. **Transliteration:** zhèngquè / cuòwù
4. **English:** correct, right / wrong, mistake, error
5. **Description of contrast:** P1 labels these as direct antonyms ("Antonym of 错误" / "Direct antonym of 正确"). Both are bisyllabic and enter verb–object and attributive collocations (P2b Group 2).
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## OPPOSITION 3

1. **Term A:** 对 (duì)
2. **Term B:** 错 (cuò)
3. **Transliteration:** duì / cuò
4. **English:** right, correct / wrong, mistaken
5. **Description of contrast:** P1 labels these as direct antonyms ("Antonym of 错" / "Antonym of 对"). Both are monosyllabic and operate in colloquial complement/tag frames (说得对, 说错了).
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## OPPOSITION 4

1. **Term A:** 真诚 (zhēnchéng)
2. **Term B:** 虚伪 (xūwěi)
3. **Transliteration:** zhēnchéng / xūwěi
4. **English:** sincere, genuine / hypocritical, false, insincere
5. **Description of contrast:** P1 states 真诚 "Contrasts with 虚伪" and 虚伪 "Contrasts with 真诚." Both apply to persons/conduct, with opposite moral valuation (P3 Group 6).
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## OPPOSITION 5

1. **Term A:** 诚实 (chéngshí)
2. **Term B:** 虚伪 (xūwěi) / 撒谎
3. **Transliteration:** chéngshí / xūwěi
4. **English:** honest, truthful / hypocritical; to lie
5. **Description of contrast:** P1 states 诚实 is "Antonym of 撒谎/虚伪." The opposition is moral (honesty vs. deceit/hypocrisy). Note 撒谎 itself is not in the documented vocabulary.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## OPPOSITION 6

1. **Term A:** 名副其实 (míng fù qí shí)
2. **Term B:** 名不副实 (míng bù fù shí)
3. **Transliteration:** míng fù qí shí / míng bù fù shí
4. **English:** worthy of the name / not living up to its name
5. **Description of contrast:** P1 explicitly states 名不副实 is the "Negation of 名副其实" and the "Negative form is 名不副实." The contrast is produced internally by inserting 不 (not) into the idiom.
6. **Contrast type:** morphological (intra-idiomatic negation)
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## OPPOSITION 7

1. **Term A:** 名副其实 / 名实相符 (míng fù qí shí / míng shí xiāng fú)
2. **Term B:** 名存实亡 (míng cún shí wáng)
3. **Transliteration:** míng fù qí shí / míng cún shí wáng
4. **English:** name and reality correspond / exists in name only, defunct in substance
5. **Description of contrast:** P1b documents 名存实亡 ("exists in name only") against the correspondence idioms 名副其实/名实相符 ("name and reality match"). The contrast is between name–reality correspondence and name–reality divergence.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical (idiom-level)
7. **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

## OPPOSITION 8

1. **Term A:** 本质 (běnzhì)
2. **Term B:** 现象 / 表象 (xiànxiàng / biǎoxiàng)
3. **Transliteration:** běnzhì / xiànxiàng
4. **English:** essence, intrinsic nature / appearance, phenomenon
5. **Description of contrast:** P1 states 本质 "Contrasts with 现象/表象," and P2b documents the aphoristic frame 透过现象看本质 ("see the essence through the appearance"). The contrast is essence vs. surface appearance.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## OPPOSITION 9

1. **Term A:** Positive truth/evaluative term (e.g., 真实, 正确, 可靠)
2. **Term B:** 不 + same term (不真实, 不正确, 不可靠)
3. **Transliteration:** bù zhēnshí / bù zhèngquè / bù kěkào
4. **English:** real → unreal; correct → incorrect; reliable → unreliable
5. **Description of contrast:** P4a/P4b document negation via 不 as a productive negative-attribution strategy ("不可靠 → unreliable"); P1b also notes 可靠 is "Productive with 不 (不可靠)." Falsity can be expressed by negating the positive term rather than using a dedicated antonym.
6. **Contrast type:** morphological
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## OPPOSITION 10

1. **Term A:** 是真的 (shì zhēn de)
2. **Term B:** 是假的 (shì jiǎ de)
3. **Transliteration:** shì zhēn de / shì jiǎ de
4. **English:** "(it) is true/real" / "(it) is fake"
5. **Description of contrast:** P2b/P4 document the 是…的 truth-assertion frame and explicitly call 是假的 the "Mirror of 是真的." The contrast is a constructional polarity within the same predicate frame.
6. **Contrast type:** constructional
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## OPPOSITION 11

1. **Term A:** 没错 (méi cuò)
2. **Term B:** 说错了 / 做错了 (shuō cuò le / zuò cuò le)
3. **Transliteration:** méi cuò / shuō cuò le
4. **English:** "that's right / no mistake" / "said it wrong / did it wrong"
5. **Description of contrast:** P2b documents 没错 as a "fixed negated affirmation" (没 + 错 = affirmation), contrasted with the resultative 错 marking an action as erroneous (V + 错 + 了). The same morpheme 错 yields affirmation under negation vs. error in the resultative frame.
6. **Contrast type:** constructional
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## OPPOSITION 12

1. **Term A:** Monosyllabic correctness terms 对 / 错 (complement & tag frames)
2. **Term B:** Bisyllabic correctness terms 正确 / 错误 (verb–object & attributive frames)
3. **Transliteration:** duì / cuò vs. zhèngquè / cuòwù
4. **English:** right/wrong (colloquial) vs. correct/erroneous (formal/written)
5. **Description of contrast:** P2b and P3 (Group 1 vs. Group 2) explicitly state these "do not share construction types" — no *说得正确 paralleling 说得对, no 犯错 in the same fixed sense as 犯错误. A register/constructional split, not a conceptual one.
6. **Contrast type:** constructional
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## OPPOSITION 13

1. **Term A:** 真伪 / 真假 — authentication via discrimination verbs (辨别真伪, 分辨真假)
2. **Term B:** 正宗 / 地道 — authenticity-to-type via attributive food/culture frames (正宗川菜, 地道的北京话)
3. **Transliteration:** zhēnwěi / zhēnjiǎ vs. zhèngzōng / dìdao
4. **English:** genuine-vs-fake authentication / authentic-to-an-original-type
5. **Description of contrast:** P2b and P3 (Group 4 vs. Group 5) explicitly note "no overlap in construction type" — discrimination-verb frames vs. attributive frames. The contrast is constructional despite both concerning authenticity.
6. **Contrast type:** constructional
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## OPPOSITION 14

1. **Term A:** 真 / 真实 — genuineness (correspondence to reality)
2. **Term B:** 逼真 — lifelike resemblance to reality
3. **Transliteration:** zhēn / zhēnshí vs. bīzhēn
4. **English:** genuine, authentic / lifelike, true to life
5. **Description of contrast:** P1 explicitly states 逼真 "Emphasizes resemblance rather than genuineness," and P3 (Group 9) reiterates this. The contrast is between being genuine vs. merely resembling the real (a representation contrast).
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## OPPOSITION 15

1. **Term A:** 真品 / 真迹 (zhēnpǐn / zhēnjì)
2. **Term B:** 赝品 / 假货 (yànpǐn / jiǎhuò)
3. **Transliteration:** zhēnpǐn / zhēnjì vs. yànpǐn / jiǎhuò
4. **English:** genuine article / authentic original vs. forgery / fake goods
5. **Description of contrast:** P1b states 真品 is "contrasted with 赝品/假货" and 真迹 is "a genuine original vs. forgery." The contrast is genuine artifact vs. counterfeit. Note 赝品/假货 are mentioned only as contrast points, not as documented vocabulary entries.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

## OPPOSITION 16

1. **Term A:** 弄假成真 (nòng jiǎ chéng zhēn)
2. **Term B:** (internal 假 ↔ 真 transformation)
3. **Transliteration:** nòng jiǎ chéng zhēn
4. **English:** "what began as pretense becomes real"
5. **Description of contrast:** P1b documents this idiom (and 假戏真做) as encoding an internal 假→真 polarity shift. The opposition 假/真 operates inside the idiom as a fake-to-real transformation rather than as two separate lexemes.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical (intra-idiomatic 真/假 opposition)
7. **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

## OPPOSITION 17

1. **Term A:** 华而不实 (huá ér bù shí)
2. **Term B:** (实 "substance" negated against 华 "flashiness")
3. **Transliteration:** huá ér bù shí
4. **English:** "flashy but without substance"
5. **Description of contrast:** P1b documents this idiom as opposing 华 (flashy surface) to 实 (substance), with 实 explicitly negated by 不. The contrast is surface/show vs. real substance, internal to the idiom.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical (intra-idiomatic 华/实 opposition)
7. **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

## OPPOSITION 18

1. **Term A:** 真心 / 真情 (zhēnxīn / zhēnqíng) — genuine feeling
2. **Term B:** 假装 (jiǎzhuāng) — feigned/pretended conduct
3. **Transliteration:** zhēnxīn / zhēnqíng vs. jiǎzhuāng
4. **English:** sincere/true feeling vs. to pretend, feign
5. **Description of contrast:** P1b/P2b document 真心/真情 (true feeling) and 假装 (feigned action, 假装没看见). Both are built on the 真/假 axis applied to feeling vs. pretense, though the data presents them at the construction level (假装 + clause).
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

## NOTES ON SCOPE AND PRESERVED ASYMMETRIES

- **Asymmetric oppositions retained:** Opposition 5 (诚实 vs. 撒谎/虚伪) and Opposition 15 (真品 vs. 赝品/假货) involve contrast terms named only as reference points in the source, not as documented P1 vocabulary; these are marked [MARGINAL] / [CORE] accordingly and not forced into balance.
- **One term, multiple contrasts:** 虚伪 contrasts with both 真诚 (Opp. 4) and 诚实 (Opp. 5); 真/假 underlie multiple oppositions (1, 10, 16, 18). These overlaps are retained per task guidance.
- **Constructional contrasts (Opp. 10–13):** These are oppositions in *construction type* or *polarity within a frame*, explicitly documented in P2b/P3/P4, not lexical antonymy.
- **Not asserted as oppositions:** Group 7 (accuracy/certainty) terms shifting away from truth-correspondence (per P1b/P2a flags) represent a semantic *shift*, not an attested opposition, and are therefore excluded.
- **Accuracy terms (准确/确凿 etc.):** No direct antonym is documented in CONTEXT for these; **(no clear opposition identified)** for the accuracy/certainty subdomain beyond general 不- negation (covered under Opposition 9).
