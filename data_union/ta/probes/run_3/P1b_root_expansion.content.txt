# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P1b ROOT EXPANSION
# Language: Tamil (ta)

---

## Step 1 — Identify Central Roots

---

**Root 1**

**Form:** உண்மை / *uṇmai*
**P1 participation:** 8 items (items 1, 4, 10, 17, 18, 19, 24, 29)
**Core meaning contribution:** The primary lexical anchor for propositional truth, reality, and genuineness in the domain. Participates in adjectival, adverbial, abstract nominal, verbal, and phrasal derivation. Functions as both an epistemic term (truth of statements) and an ontological term (reality of states of affairs).

---

**Root 2**

**Form:** நம்பு / *nampu*
**P1 participation:** 4 items (items 7, 8, 27, and implicitly 28 via the trust-evaluation cluster)
**Core meaning contribution:** The verbal root encoding trust, belief, and credence. Generates a productive family of nominals, adjectivals, and verbal derivatives covering the relational dimension of truth-evaluation — whether a person, source, or claim is worthy of being believed or relied upon.

---

**Root 3**

**Form:** நேர் / *nēr*
**P1 participation:** 3 items (items 3, 9, 28)
**Core meaning contribution:** A root encoding straightness, directness, and alignment. In the truth-evaluation domain it contributes the character-oriented dimension — honesty and integrity as properties of persons and conduct rather than of propositions. Also participates in spatial and geometric senses that extend beyond the truth domain.

## Step 2 — Root Expansion

---

### Root 1: உண்மை / *uṇmai*

*Note: This root is highly productive. The 25 most common compounds, collocations, derived expressions, and fixed phrases are listed below; additional compositional forms exist in formal and literary registers.*

---

**1.**
**Form:** உண்மை
**Transliteration:** uṇmai
**Morpheme breakdown:** *uḷ* (to be/exist) + *-mai* (nominalizing suffix)
**Natural English translation:** truth, reality, fact
**Context of use:** The base nominal form, used across all registers to assert that a statement or state of affairs corresponds to reality. Appears in moral exhortation (*uṇmai pēcu* — speak the truth), philosophical discourse, and legal testimony.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**2.**
**Form:** உண்மையான
**Transliteration:** uṇmaiyāṉa
**Morpheme breakdown:** *uṇmai* + *-āṉa* (adjectival copular suffix)
**Natural English translation:** true, genuine, real, authentic
**Context of use:** Attributive adjective modifying nouns across all registers. Used to distinguish genuine instances from false or imitation ones: *uṇmaiyāṉa kaṭal* (genuine love), *uṇmaiyāṉa tகவல்* (accurate information).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**3.**
**Form:** உண்மையாக
**Transliteration:** uṇmaiyāka
**Morpheme breakdown:** *uṇmai* + *-āka* (adverbial/predicative suffix)
**Natural English translation:** truly, honestly, genuinely, in truth
**Context of use:** Adverbial form used to qualify verbs or predicates, asserting that an action or state is genuine or sincere. Common in spoken Tamil: *uṇmaiyāka colluvēṉ* (I will speak truthfully). Also used predicatively: *itu uṇmaiyāka irukku* (this is genuinely the case).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**4.**
**Form:** உண்மையிலேயே
**Transliteration:** uṇmaiyilēyē
**Morpheme breakdown:** *uṇmai* + locative *-il* + emphatic *-ē* + *-yē*
**Natural English translation:** truly, really, genuinely, in actual fact
**Context of use:** Emphatic adverbial used in spoken Tamil to reinforce the sincerity or factual accuracy of a claim. Functions as an intensifier: *avaḷ uṇmaiyilēyē nalla āḷ* (she is genuinely a good person).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**5.**
**Form:** உண்மையில்
**Transliteration:** uṇmaiyil
**Morpheme breakdown:** *uṇmai* + locative *-il*
**Natural English translation:** in truth, in fact, actually
**Context of use:** Locative adverbial used as a sentence-initial or mid-sentence frame to introduce a correction or clarification of what is actually the case. Common in both spoken and written Tamil: *uṇmaiyil avaṉ varāttāṉ* (in fact, he did not come).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**6.**
**Form:** உண்மைத்தன்மை
**Transliteration:** uṇmai-t-taṉmai
**Morpheme breakdown:** *uṇmai* + *taṉmai* (nature, quality)
**Natural English translation:** truthfulness, authenticity, genuineness
**Context of use:** Abstract nominal used in philosophical, literary-critical, and formal written Tamil to denote the property of being true or authentic. Appears in evaluations of literary works, testimonies, or representations.
**Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**7.**
**Form:** உண்மைத் தன்மையுள்ள
**Transliteration:** uṇmai-t-taṉmaiyuḷḷa
**Morpheme breakdown:** *uṇmai* + *taṉmai* + *uḷḷa* (possessing, having)
**Natural English translation:** authentic, genuinely possessing (a quality), truthful (attributive)
**Context of use:** Attributive participial phrase in formal and literary written Tamil, used to characterize persons, works, or representations as genuinely possessing a stated quality. More compositionally transparent than *uṇmaiyāṉa*.
**Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**8.**
**Form:** உண்மைப்படுத்துதல்
**Transliteration:** uṇmaippaṭuttutal
**Morpheme breakdown:** *uṇmai* + causative *-paṭuttu-* + verbal noun *-tal*
**Natural English translation:** authentication, verification, substantiation
**Context of use:** Formal and administrative verbal noun denoting the act of establishing the truth or genuineness of a document, claim, or identity. Used in official Tamil in document-processing and legal contexts.
**Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**9.**
**Form:** உண்மைப்படுத்து
**Transliteration:** uṇmaippaṭuttu
**Morpheme breakdown:** *uṇmai* + causative *-paṭuttu* (finite/imperative stem)
**Natural English translation:** authenticate, verify, substantiate, confirm
**Context of use:** The finite verb form of the above, used in administrative and legal instructions: *ātāratai uṇmaippaṭuttu* (substantiate/verify the evidence). Appears in official written Tamil.
**Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**10.**
**Form:** உண்மைக்கு மாறான
**Transliteration:** uṇmaikkum māṟāṉa
**Morpheme breakdown:** *uṇmai* + dative *-kku* + *māṟu* (contrary, opposite) + *-āṉa*
**Natural English translation:** contrary to truth, false, at variance with the facts
**Context of use:** A conventionalized formal and legal expression used to characterize statements or representations as contrary to fact. More explicit and register-marked than simply using *poy*.
**Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**11.**
**Form:** உண்மையான அர்த்தத்தில்
**Transliteration:** uṇmaiyāṉa arttattil
**Morpheme breakdown:** *uṇmaiyāṉa* + *arttam* (meaning, sense) + locative *-til*
**Natural English translation:** in the true sense, properly speaking, in the genuine meaning
**Context of use:** A semi-fixed phrase used in formal and philosophical written Tamil to specify that a term is being used in its proper or strict sense, distinguishing genuine from nominal instantiation of a category.
**Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**12.**
**Form:** உண்மை நிலை
**Transliteration:** uṇmai nilai
**Morpheme breakdown:** *uṇmai* + *nilai* (state, condition, level)
**Natural English translation:** true state (of affairs), actual condition, reality
**Context of use:** Used in journalistic and administrative Tamil to refer to the actual or real state of a situation, often contrasted with appearances or official claims: *uṇmai nilai enna?* (What is the actual state of affairs?).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**13.**
**Form:** உண்மை வெளிப்படுதல்
**Transliteration:** uṇmai veḷippaṭutal
**Morpheme breakdown:** *uṇmai* + *veḷi* (outside) + *-paṭu-* (become) + verbal noun *-tal*
**Natural English translation:** the truth coming to light, revelation of the truth
**Context of use:** A common collocation in journalistic and narrative Tamil describing the emergence or disclosure of a previously concealed truth. Used in news reporting and storytelling: *uṇmai veḷippaṭṭatu* (the truth came to light).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**14.**
**Form:** உண்மை மறைத்தல்
**Transliteration:** uṇmai maṟaittal
**Morpheme breakdown:** *uṇmai* + *maṟai* (to conceal, hide) + verbal noun *-tal*
**Natural English translation:** concealment of the truth, suppression of the truth
**Context of use:** Used in legal, journalistic, and moral discourse to characterize the act of hiding or suppressing the truth. Appears in accusations of cover-up and in legal proceedings.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**15.**
**Form:** உண்மை தெரிய வருதல்
**Transliteration:** uṇmai teriya varutal

---

**16.**
**Form:** உண்மை பேசு
**Transliteration:** uṇmai pēcu
**Morpheme breakdown:** *uṇmai* + *pēcu* (speak, imperative/infinitive)
**Natural English translation:** speak the truth, tell the truth
**Context of use:** A very common imperative collocation used in moral exhortation, parenting, legal contexts, and everyday speech. Appears in proverbs and moral instruction: *uṇmai pēcu, nīti vāḻu* (speak truth, live justly).

---

**17.**
**Form:** உண்மை சொல்
**Transliteration:** uṇmai col
**Morpheme breakdown:** *uṇmai* + *col* (say, tell; imperative/infinitive)
**Natural English translation:** tell the truth, say the truth
**Context of use:** Equivalent to *uṇmai pēcu* in most contexts; used in direct address and moral instruction. Extremely common in spoken Tamil across all age groups and registers.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**18.**
**Form:** உண்மையை அறிதல்
**Transliteration:** uṇmaiyai aṟital
**Morpheme breakdown:** *uṇmai* + accusative *-ai* + *aṟi* (to know) + verbal noun *-tal*
**Natural English translation:** knowing the truth, coming to know the truth
**Context of use:** Used in philosophical, religious, and narrative contexts to describe the epistemic act of attaining knowledge of truth or reality. Appears in Vedantic and Tamil Shaiva philosophical discourse as well as in everyday narrative.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**19.**

### Root 2: நம்பு / *nampu*

*Note: This root is moderately productive. All well-attested common forms are listed below.*

---

**1.**
**Form:** நம்பு
**Transliteration:** nampu
**Morpheme breakdown:** root verb *nampu* (to trust, believe, rely on)
**Natural English translation:** trust, believe, rely on
**Context of use:** The base verb, used across all registers to express trust in a person or belief in a proposition. *Nampukiṟēṉ* (I trust/believe); *avaṉai nampāte* (don't trust him). Extremely common in spoken and written Tamil.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**2.**
**Form:** நம்பிக்கை
**Transliteration:** nampikkai
**Morpheme breakdown:** *nampu* + *-ikkai* (abstract nominal suffix)
**Natural English translation:** trust, belief, faith, confidence, credence
**Context of use:** The primary abstract nominal from this root, used in moral, religious, and everyday contexts. *Nampikkai vaikkiṟēṉ* (I place trust); *nampikkai iḻattal* (loss of trust). Appears in religious discourse as faith and in legal discourse as credence.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**3.**
**Form:** நம்பகம்
**Transliteration:** nampakam
**Morpheme breakdown:** *nampu* + *-akam* (nominal suffix, formal register)
**Natural English translation:** trustworthiness, reliability, credibility
**Context of use:** A formal nominal used in legal, journalistic, and administrative Tamil to denote the property of being trustworthy or credible. Less common in spoken registers; appears in formal evaluative contexts.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**4.**
**Form:** நம்பகமான
**Transliteration:** nampakamāṉa
**Morpheme breakdown:** *nampakam* + *-āṉa* (adjectival suffix)
**Natural English translation:** reliable, trustworthy, credible, dependable
**Context of use:** Adjectival form used to evaluate persons, sources, institutions, or information as worthy of trust. Common in journalistic and legal evaluative contexts: *nampakamāṉa māṟṟu* (reliable source).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**5.**
**Form:** நம்பகத்தன்மை
**Transliteration:** nampakat-taṉmai
**Morpheme breakdown:** *nampakam* + *taṉmai* (nature, quality)
**Natural English translation:** reliability, trustworthiness, credibility (abstract)
**Context of use:** Abstract nominal used in formal written Tamil — legal, academic, and journalistic — to denote the property of being reliable or credible. Appears in discussions of evidence, testimony, and institutional conduct.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**6.**
**Form:** நம்பிக்கையான
**Transliteration:** nampikkaiyāṉa
**Morpheme breakdown:** *nampikkai* + *-āṉa* (adjectival suffix)
**Natural English translation:** trustworthy, reliable, faithful
**Context of use:** Adjectival form of *nampikkai*, used to characterize persons or things as worthy of trust. Common in everyday and moral evaluative speech: *nampikkaiyāṉa āḷ* (a trustworthy person).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**7.**
**Form:** நம்பிக்கையற்ற
**Transliteration:** nampikkaiyaṟṟa
**Morpheme breakdown:** *nampikkai* + *aṟṟa* (privative participial suffix)
**Natural English translation:** untrustworthy, faithless, unreliable
**Context of use:** Privative adjectival form used to characterize persons or things as lacking trustworthiness. Used in moral condemnation and evaluative discourse: *nampikkaiyaṟṟa naṭatai* (untrustworthy conduct).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**8.**
**Form:** நம்பிக்கை இழப்பு
**Transliteration:** nampikkai iḻappu
**Morpheme breakdown:** *nampikkai* + *iḻappu* (loss)
**Natural English translation:** loss of trust, loss of faith, loss of confidence
**Context of use:** A common collocation in political, institutional, and interpersonal discourse describing the erosion or loss of trust. Appears in journalistic and political commentary: *makkaḷiṉ nampikkai iḻappu* (loss of public trust).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**9.**
**Form:** நம்பிக்கை வாக்கு

**Morpheme breakdown:** *nampikkai* + *vākku* (vote, word)
**Natural English translation:** vote of confidence
**Context of use:** A fixed expression in parliamentary and political Tamil denoting a formal vote of confidence in a government or leader. Used in political journalism and legislative discourse.

**10.**

**11.**

**14.**

**15.**

**Natural English translation:** betrayal of trust, breach of faith

### Root 3: நேர் / *nēr*

*Note: This root is moderately productive across multiple semantic domains. All well-attested forms relevant to the truth-evaluation domain and its extensions are listed below.*

---

**1.**
**Form:** நேர்மை
**Transliteration:** nērmai
**Morpheme breakdown:** *nēr* (straight, direct) + *-mai* (nominalizing suffix)
**Natural English translation:** honesty, integrity, uprightness, straightforwardness
**Context of use:** The primary abstract nominal from this root in the truth-evaluation domain. Used to evaluate persons and conduct as transparent and non-deceptive in moral, professional, and civic contexts.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**2.**
**Form:** நேர்மையான
**Transliteration:** nērmayāṉa
**Morpheme breakdown:** *nērmai* + *-āṉa* (adjectival suffix)
**Natural English translation:** honest, upright, straightforward, principled
**Context of use:** Adjectival form used to characterize persons or conduct as honest and principled. Extremely common in moral evaluation across all registers: *nērmayāṉa āḷ* (an honest person), *nērmayāṉa viṭai* (a straightforward answer).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**3.**
**Form:** நேர்மையாக
**Transliteration:** nērmayāka

## Step 3 — Peripheral Extensions

The following expressions involve the identified roots in contexts where the contribution of the root appears to shift away from its core truth-evaluation function. These are flagged without interpretation or categorization.

---

**From உண்மை / *uṇmai*:**

- **உண்மை அன்பு** (*uṇmai aṉpu*, "true love") — the root modifies an emotional state rather than a proposition or epistemic claim.
- **உண்மை நண்பன்** (*uṇmai naṇpaṉ*, "true friend") — the root evaluates a relational role rather than a statement or representation.
- **உண்மை வழி** (*uṇmai vaḻi*, "the path of truth") — the root functions in a normative-conduct frame rather than a propositional one.
- **உண்மையான அர்த்தத்தில்** (*uṇmaiyāṉa arttattil*, "in the true sense") — the root operates as a degree or precision marker in definitional contexts, specifying the strict or proper application of a concept.
- **உண்மை நிகழ்வு** (*uṇmai nikaḻvu*, "real event") — the root marks ontological status (actual occurrence) rather than propositional truth.
- **உண்மை நிலை** (*uṇmai nilai*, "true state") — the root marks the actual condition of affairs, functioning closer to a reality/actuality marker than a truth-of-statement marker.

---

**From நம்பு / *nampu*:**

- **நம்பிக்கை வாக்கு** (*nampikkai vākku*, "vote of confidence") — the root participates in a fixed political-procedural expression where the trust relation is institutionalized and formal rather than interpersonal or epistemic.
- **நம்பிக்கை ஊட்டுதல்** (*nampikkai ūṭṭutal*, "instilling confidence") — the root appears in a causative-motivational frame, where the relevant state is psychological confidence rather than epistemic credence.
- **நம்பிக்கை கொள்ளுதல்** (*nampikkai koḷḷutal*, "holding faith") — in devotional and religious contexts, the root participates in expressions of religious faith that extend beyond the evaluation of empirical claims.

---

**From நேர் / *nēr*:**

- *Note: The root நேர் / nēr has a well-documented spatial and geometric sense (straight, direct, perpendicular) that is entirely distinct from its truth-evaluation uses. The following expressions from everyday Tamil involve this root in non-truth-domain contexts:*
- **நேர் கோடு** (*nēr kōṭu*, "straight line") — geometric/spatial use.
- **நேரடி** (*nēraṭi*, "direct, immediate") — the root contributes a sense of directness or immediacy in spatial, communicative, and temporal senses: *nēraṭi oḷiparappu* (live broadcast, literally "direct transmission").
- **நேர்மாறு** (*nērmāṟu*, "opposite, contrary, inverse") — the root participates in a logical/spatial opposition expression.
- **நேர்மையான போட்டி** (*nērmayāṉa pōṭṭi*, "fair competition") — the root's honesty-contribution extends into a fairness-in-procedure frame that is not strictly about truth or deception.
- **நேர்மையான வியாபாரம்** (*nērmayāṉa viyāpāram*, "honest business") — similarly, the root operates in a commercial-ethics frame where the primary concern is procedural fairness rather than propositional truth.
