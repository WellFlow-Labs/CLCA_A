# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2a Derivational System
# Language: Quechua (qu)

---

**Preliminary note on scope and method:** This step documents attested derivational morphology for the P1 base lexical items. The primary data source is the P1 and P1b output, cross-referenced against the attestation claims made there. Forms already introduced in P1b are carried forward here with their epistemic statuses preserved; no new forms are generated beyond what is supported by the prior steps. Where P1b explicitly noted a form as uncertain or its attestation as limited, that status is preserved. Paradigm completion is not performed. Gaps are preserved as gaps.

The organization follows the P1 item list. For each P1 base item, derivations are listed as attested. Where a P1 item is itself a derived form (e.g., *cheqaq*, *cheqaqta*), its own further derivations are listed under it if attested; its status as a derivative of a prior item is noted but not re-documented in full.

## P1 Item 1: cheqa

---

**1.1**
- **Base Form:** cheqa
- **Derived Form:** cheqaq
- **Transliteration:** cheqaq
- **Literal Breakdown:** cheqa + *-q* (nominalizing/adjectivizing suffix)
- **Natural English translation:** true, truthful, genuine, honest
- **Usage description:** Functions as adjectival and nominal form derived from *cheqa*, used to describe persons as honest or statements as true. Widely used in moral evaluation and in assertions about the accuracy of claims across Cusco and Ayacucho varieties.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**1.2**
- **Base Form:** cheqa
- **Derived Form:** cheqa simi
- **Transliteration:** cheqa simi
- **Literal Breakdown:** cheqa ("true") + simi ("mouth, word, language")
- **Natural English translation:** true word, honest speech, truthful statement
- **Usage description:** Used in moral and legal contexts to describe speech that is honest and accurate. *Simi* is a common collocate with *cheqa* in discourse about honest communication.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**1.3**
- **Base Form:** cheqa
- **Derived Form:** cheqa rimay
- **Transliteration:** cheqa rimay
- **Literal Breakdown:** cheqa ("true") + rimay ("to speak, speech")
- **Natural English translation:** to speak the truth, truthful speech
- **Usage description:** Used in moral evaluation of speech as honest and accurate; appears in ethical instruction, proverbs, and legal contexts where the truthfulness of testimony is at issue.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**1.4**
- **Base Form:** cheqa
- **Derived Form:** cheqa yuyay
- **Transliteration:** cheqa yuyay
- **Literal Breakdown:** cheqa ("true") + yuyay ("thought, mind, memory, opinion")
- **Natural English translation:** correct understanding, true opinion, sound judgment
- **Usage description:** Used in philosophical and ethical discourse to describe correct or sound thinking, as opposed to mistaken or confused thought; appears in educational and religious contexts.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**1.5**
- **Base Form:** cheqa

- **Transliteration:** cheqa sonqo
- **Literal Breakdown:** cheqa ("true, genuine") + sonqo ("heart, inner self, mind")
- **Natural English translation:** sincere, genuine of heart, honest at heart
- **Usage description:** Used in moral characterization of persons as genuinely sincere or honest in their inner disposition, not merely in outward conduct; appears in ethical, religious, and literary discourse.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

- **Natural English translation:** liar, one who lies, deceiver
- **Usage description:** Agentive form derived from the verbal stem *llullakuy*, used to characterize a person as one who engages in the act of lying; appears in moral and legal discourse, closely related to *llullaq* but derived from the verbal base.

**7.2**
- **Base Form:** llullakuy
- **Derived Form:** llullakusqa
- **Transliteration:** llullakusqa
- **Literal Breakdown:** llulla + *-kuy* (verbalizing) + *-sqa* (past participle)
- **Natural English translation:** lied, deceived, that which was falsely stated
- **Usage description:** Participial form used to describe a statement or act as having been a lie or deception; appears in legal and testimonial contexts when characterizing prior false statements.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**7.3**
- **Base Form:** llullakuy
- **Derived Form:** llullakuy ama
- **Transliteration:** llullakuy ama
- **Literal Breakdown:** llullakuy ("to lie") + ama (prohibitive particle)
- **Natural English translation:** do not lie, don't deceive
- **Usage description:** A standard moral injunction in Quechua ethical discourse; the fuller construction *ama llullakuychu* (with prohibitive suffix) is one of the three traditional Andean moral precepts, widely cited in Andean cultural and educational contexts.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Summary Note on Derivational Patterns

The following observations are directly supported by the data above and are reported without interpretation beyond what the data warrant:

**Differential productivity:** The root *cheqa* shows the highest number of attested derivations in this dataset, including morphological derivatives (with *-q*, *-ta*, *-chay*, *-siy*, *-niyuq*, *-manta*, *-pi*, *-puni*, *-chus*) and nominal collocations (with *simi*, *rimay*, *yuyay*, *sonqo*, *kawsay*, *ñan*, *riqsiy*, *hina*, *hatun*, *Dios*). The root *llulla* shows moderate productivity, with morphological derivatives (with *-kuy*, *-q*, *-chiy*, *-sqa*) and collocations (with *simi*, *rimay*, *willakuy*, *qelqa*, *sonqo*, *yuyay*, *kawsay*, *hina*). The root *chiqan* shows fewer attested forms, with one morphological derivative (*chiqanchay*) and a smaller set of collocations.

**Gaps:** Several P1 items show no derivations within scope (*allin*, *mana allin*, *recto/riqtu*, *pura*, *kikin*, *paylla*, *hina*, *cheqniy*). This is reported as a gap, not as evidence of unproductivity in other domains.

**Evidential and modal clitics:** The attachment of evidential clitics (*-mi/-m*, *-chus*) and the emphatic suffix *-puni* to *cheqaq* and its adverbial form is attested. These are not documented for *llulla* or *chiqan* forms in available sources; this asymmetry is preserved without explanation.
