# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P5 OPPOSITIONS
# Language: Finnish (fi)

---

## Methodological note

The following oppositions are extracted exclusively from patterns documented in P1, P1b, P2a, P2b, P3, P4a, and P4b. Each opposition is supported by explicit statements in the context data — through direct contrast statements, derivational pairings, collocational contrasts, or constructional alternation. No opposition has been inferred from general linguistic knowledge not present in the context. Where the context notes asymmetry, imbalance, or partial contrast, this is preserved and reported.

## Opposition 1

**Term A:** *tosi* / *totta* (partitive)
**Term B:** (no single lexical antonym documented in the data)
**Transliteration:** N/A
**English translation:** true / (false, wrong — expressed by *väärä*)
**Description of contrast:** The context does not document a single direct lexical antonym of *tosi*; the negative pole is covered by *väärä* (wrong, false) in propositional contexts. The opposition is therefore asymmetric: *tosi* has a dedicated positive form but no morphologically parallel negative form within the same root.
**Contrast type:** lexical (asymmetric)
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]
