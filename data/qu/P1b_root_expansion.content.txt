# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P1b ROOT EXPANSION
# Language: Quechua (qu)

## Step 1 — Identify Central Roots

### Root 1: **chiq-** (chiqan, chiqap, chiqamanta)
**Participates in:** 3 P1 items directly (#1, #2, #26)
**Core meaning contribution:** Truth, genuineness, reality, authenticity. This root marks correspondence to reality and absence of deception or falsity.

### Root 2: **llull-** (llulla, llullakuy)
**Participates in:** 2 P1 items directly (#3, #4)
**Core meaning contribution:** Falsehood, deception, lying. This root marks non-correspondence to reality and presence of deception.

### Root 3: **allin** (allin, mana allin)
**Participates in:** 2 P1 items directly (#5, #6)
**Core meaning contribution:** Goodness, correctness, propriety. This root marks positive evaluation across moral, practical, and epistemic domains.

## Step 2 — Expand Each Root

### ROOT 1: **chiq-**

#### 1. chiqan kay
**Form:** chiqan kay
**Literal breakdown:** chiqan (true) + kay (to be/being)
**Translation:** to be true, truth, reality
**Context:** Used in philosophical and everyday discourse to refer to the state of being true or to truth as an abstract concept. Common in statements affirming the reality or truth of something.
**[CORE]**

#### 2. chiqan simi
**Form:** chiqan simi
**Literal breakdown:** chiqan (true) + simi (word/mouth)
**Translation:** true word, truthful speech, honest statement
**Context:** Used to characterize speech that is truthful and honest. Common in contexts evaluating the reliability of what someone says or in praising honest communication.
**[CORE]**

#### 3. chiqan runa
**Form:** chiqan runa
**Literal breakdown:** chiqan (true) + runa (person)
**Translation:** truthful person, honest person, genuine person
**Context:** Used to describe someone who habitually tells the truth and is authentic in their dealings. Common in character evaluation and moral discourse.
**[CORE]**

#### 4. chiqan yachay
**Form:** chiqan yachay
**Literal breakdown:** chiqan (true) + yachay (knowledge)
**Translation:** true knowledge, genuine understanding
**Context:** Used in educational and philosophical contexts to distinguish genuine knowledge from mere opinion or false belief. Common in discussions of learning and wisdom.
**[CORE]**

#### 5. chiqan ñan
**Form:** chiqan ñan
**Literal breakdown:** chiqan (true) + ñan (path/way)
**Translation:** true path, right way, correct course
**Context:** Used both literally for correct routes and metaphorically for proper conduct or correct method. Common in moral instruction and practical guidance.
**[CORE]**

#### 6. chiqan sunqu
**Form:** chiqan sunqu
**Literal breakdown:** chiqan (true) + sunqu (heart)
**Translation:** true heart, sincere heart, genuine feeling
**Context:** Used to describe sincerity of emotion or intention. Common in contexts evaluating whether someone's feelings or motivations are genuine.
**[CORE]**

#### 7. chiqan willakuy

**Literal breakdown:** chiqan (true) + willakuy (to tell/narrate)

## Step 3 — Peripheral Extensions

### ROOT: **chiq-**

**Peripheral extensions noted:**

1. **chiqan ñan** — While "true path" clearly relates to correctness, its use for physical routes (not just metaphorical moral paths) extends the root into spatial/navigational domains.

2. **chiqan rikuy** — The extension to visual perception ("to see truly") moves from propositional truth to perceptual accuracy, suggesting the root may function in sensory domains.

3. **chiqan kawsay** — The application to "authentic living" or "true life" extends from truth-telling into existential and lifestyle evaluation, suggesting broader application to authenticity of being.

4. **chiqan qillqa** — Use for document authentication suggests extension into legal/administrative verification procedures beyond simple truth-evaluation.

### ROOT: **llull-**

**Peripheral extensions noted:**

1. **llulla musyay** — Extension to "false perception" or "illusion" moves from intentional deception to perceptual error, suggesting the root may apply to unintentional falsity.

2. **llulla ñan** — Like its counterpart with chiq-, this extends to physical wrong routes, not just metaphorical wrong paths.

3. **llullachakuy** — The reflexive form for "self-deception" suggests extension into psychological states where intentionality is ambiguous.

### ROOT: **allin**

**Peripheral extensions noted:**

1. **allin rikuy** — Extension to both "good eyesight" (physical capacity) and "to look good" (aesthetic evaluation) suggests the root functions across functional, aesthetic, and moral domains.

2. **allin pacha** — Application to temporal/seasonal favorability extends from moral goodness to situational appropriateness.

3. **allin puñuy**, **allin mikhuy** — Extensions to sleep quality and food quality suggest the root applies broadly to functional adequacy and physical well-being, not just moral or epistemic correctness.

4. **allin hamuy** — Conventionalization as a greeting ("welcome") suggests pragmatic extension into social formulae.

5. **allin musyay** — Application to dream interpretation suggests extension into divinatory or prophetic domains.

6. **allin kawsay** — This term functions as a central philosophical concept in Andean thought, extending well beyond simple moral evaluation to encompass holistic well-being, social harmony, and proper relationship with the cosmos.
