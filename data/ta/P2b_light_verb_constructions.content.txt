# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2b COMPOSITIONAL CONSTRUCTIONS
# Language: Tamil (ta)

---

## METHODOLOGY STATEMENT

This analysis documents **attested multi-word constructions** involving truth-domain lexical items from P1. All constructions listed are naturally occurring in Tamil usage. Constructions are organized by base lexical item and functional/semantic type. Where expected construction types are not attested or are extremely rare, this is explicitly noted.

---

## CONSTRUCTIONS BY BASE FORM

### BASE: உண்மை (uṇmai) "truth"

#### CONSTRUCTION TYPE: Verbal Collocations — Speaking/Telling Truth

##### C1.1
**Base form:** உண்மை (uṇmai)
**Construction:** உண்மை பேசு
**Transliteration:** uṇmai pēcu
**Literal breakdown:** uṇmai (truth) + pēcu (speak)
**English translation:** speak the truth, tell the truth
**Usage:** Extremely common collocation in everyday speech and moral instruction. Used to encourage or describe truthful speech, especially in parent-child discourse and moral teaching contexts.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

##### C1.2
**Base form:** உண்மை (uṇmai)
**Construction:** உண்மை சொல்லு
**Transliteration:** uṇmai collu
**Literal breakdown:** uṇmai (truth) + collu (say, tell)
**English translation:** tell the truth, speak truthfully
**Usage:** Synonymous with உண்மை பேசு, extremely common in everyday speech. Often used in direct requests or commands to tell the truth. Slightly more common in colloquial registers than பேசு.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

##### C1.3
**Base form:** உண்மை (uṇmai)
**Construction:** உண்மை கூறு
**Transliteration:** uṇmai kūṟu
**Literal breakdown:** uṇmai (truth) + kūṟu (say, utter)
**English translation:** utter the truth, speak truthfully
**Usage:** More formal or literary variant of உண்மை சொல்லு. Used in elevated discourse and written contexts. Less common in everyday speech.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

##### C1.4
**Base form:** உண்மை (uṇmai)
**Construction:** உண்மையைச் சொல்லு
**Transliteration:** uṇmaiyaic collu
**Literal breakdown:** uṇmai (truth, accusative with -ai) + c (euphonic) + collu (tell)
**English translation:** tell the truth (with explicit object marking)
**Usage:** Variant of உண்மை சொல்லு with explicit accusative case marking on the object. Used for emphasis or in formal contexts. The case marker makes the object relationship more explicit.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### CONSTRUCTION TYPE: Verbal Collocations — Revealing/Emerging Truth

##### C1.5
**Base form:** உண்மை (uṇmai)
**Construction:** உண்மை வெளிப்படு
**Transliteration:** uṇmai veḷippaṭu
**Literal breakdown:** uṇmai (truth) + veḷippaṭu (come out, be revealed)
**English translation:** the truth comes out, truth is revealed
**Usage:** Used when hidden truth becomes known or when deception is uncovered. Common in narrative contexts and discussions of investigation or discovery. Implies that truth was previously hidden.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

##### C1.6
**Base form:** உண்மை (uṇmai)
**Construction:** உண்மை தெரிய வரு
**Transliteration:** uṇmai teriya varu
**Literal breakdown:** uṇmai (truth) + teriya (to be known) + varu (come)
**English translation:** the truth comes to be known, truth emerges
**Usage:** Similar to உண்மை வெளிப்படு but emphasizes the gradual process of truth becoming known. Common when truth is discovered through investigation or over time.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

##### C1.7
**Base form:** உண்மை (uṇmai)
**Construction:** உண்மை வெளிச்சத்துக்கு வரு
**Transliteration:** uṇmai veḷiccattukku varu
**Literal breakdown:** uṇmai (truth) + veḷiccam (light) + -ukku (dative) + varu (come)
**English translation:** truth comes to light
**Usage:** Metaphorical expression using light imagery for truth revelation. Common in journalistic and narrative contexts when hidden facts are exposed. More literary than simple வெளிப்படு.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

##### C1.8
**Base form:** உண்மை (uṉmai)
**Construction:** உண்மை தெரிஞ்சுது
**Transliteration:** uṇmai teriñcutu
**Literal breakdown:** uṇmai (truth) + teriñcu (came to be known, past) + -tu (neuter past suffix)
**English translation:** the truth became known, the truth came out
**Usage:** Colloquial past tense construction indicating that truth has been discovered. Very common in everyday narrative speech when reporting that truth has emerged.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### CONSTRUCTION TYPE: Verbal Collocations — Concealing Truth

**Base form:** உண்மை (uṇmai)
**Construction:** உண்மையை மறை
**Transliteration:** uṇmaiyai maṟai
**Literal breakdown:** uṇmai (truth, accusative) + maṟai (hide, conceal)
**English translation:** hide the truth, conceal the truth
**Usage:** Used to describe deliberate concealment of truth. Common in moral condemnation and discussions of deception. Implies intentional withholding of truth.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

##### C1.10

**Transliteration:** uṇmaiyai mūṭi maṟai
**Literal breakdown:** uṇmai (truth, accusative) + mūṭi (covering, having covered) + maṟai (hide)
**English translation:** cover up and hide the truth
**Usage:** Emphatic expression for deliberate concealment of truth. The addition of மூடி (covering) intensifies the sense of deliberate cover-up. Often used in contexts of corruption, scandal, or systematic deception.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

##### C1.11
**Base form:** உண்மை (uṇmai)
**Construction:** உண்மையை மறைக்க முயற்சி செய்
**Transliteration:** uṇmaiyai maṟaikka muyaṟci cey
**Literal breakdown:** uṇmai (truth, accusative) + maṟaikka (to hide, infinitive) + muyaṟci (attempt, effort) + cey (do)
**English translation:** try to hide the truth, attempt to conceal the truth
**Usage:** Describes attempts to conceal truth, whether successful or not. Common in investigative and journalistic contexts when describing cover-up efforts.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### CONSTRUCTION TYPE: Predicative Constructions — Truth Evaluation

##### C1.24
**Base form:** உண்மை (uṇmai)
**Construction:** இது உண்மை
**Transliteration:** itu uṇmai
**Literal breakdown:** itu (this) + uṇmai (truth)
**English translation:** this is true, this is the truth
**Usage:** Simple predicative construction asserting that something is true. Extremely common in everyday speech for affirming truth of statements or situations.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

##### C1.25
**Base form:** உண்மை (uṇmai)
**Construction:** இது உண்மைதான்
**Transliteration:** itu uṇmaitāṉ
**Literal breakdown:** itu (this) + uṇmai (truth) + tāṉ (emphatic particle)
**English translation:** this is indeed true, this really is the truth
**Usage:** Emphatic variant of இது உண்மை. The particle தான் adds emphasis and certainty. Very common in spoken Tamil when strongly affirming truth.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

##### C1.26
**Base form:** உண்மை (uṇmai)
**Construction:** இது உண்மையா
**Transliteration:** itu uṇmaiyā
**Literal breakdown:** itu (this) + uṇmai (truth) + -ā (question particle)
**English translation:** is this true?, is this the truth?
**Usage:** Interrogative construction questioning whether something is true. Extremely common in everyday speech when seeking confirmation or expressing doubt.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

##### C1.27
**Base form:** உண்மை (uṇmai)
**Construction:** இது உண்மையில்லை
**Transliteration:** itu uṇmaiillai
**Literal breakdown:** itu (this) + uṇmai (truth) + illai (is not)
**English translation:** this is not true, this is not the truth
**Usage:** Negative predicative construction denying truth. Common in refutations and denials. Direct negation of இது உண்மை.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

##### C1.29

**English translation:** to tell the truth, if I'm being honest
**Usage:** Variant of உண்மையில் சொல்லப்போனால் with explicit accusative marking. Functions identically as discourse marker for introducing frank statements.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

##### C1.30
**Base form:** உண்மை (uṇmai)

**Transliteration:** uṇmaiyākac colla vēṇṭum eṉṟāl
**Literal breakdown:** uṇmai (truth) + -āka (adverbial) + c + colla (to say) + vēṇṭum (must) + eṉṟāl (if saying)
**English translation:** if I must speak truthfully, to be honest
**Usage:** More formal variant of the "to tell the truth" construction. Used in formal speech and writing when introducing candid statements.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]
