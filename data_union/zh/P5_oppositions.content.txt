# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P5 OPPOSITIONS
# Language: Chinese (zh)

---

## Preliminary Note on Method

Oppositions below are extracted exclusively from patterns documented in P1, P1b, P2a, P2b, P3, P4a, and P4b. Each opposition is supported by explicit textual evidence from the CONTEXT. Where the CONTEXT notes an opposition explicitly, this is indicated. Where an opposition is reconstructed from co-occurrence patterns, collocational data, or constructional contrasts documented across steps, this is also noted. No opposition is inferred from general linguistic knowledge not grounded in the CONTEXT data.

Asymmetries between terms are preserved and noted where the data support them. No attempt is made to force symmetry.

## Opposition 1

**Term A:** 真 (zhēn)
**Term B:** 假 (jiǎ)
**Transliteration:** zhēn / jiǎ
**English Translation:** genuine, real / fake, false, counterfeit
**Description of Contrast:** The most basic lexical opposition in the domain, explicitly documented in P1 as a direct contrast (P1 #1 and #2: "Directly contrasts with 真"). The pair names the positive and negative poles of the authenticity/truth axis and generates the binary question 真假 (P1b Root 3a #4) and the construction 是真是假 (P2b Construction 2), which frames the question of authenticity as a binary between these two poles. They co-occur in the compound 真假 (P1b Root 1, item 6) and in paired constructions such as 说真话 vs. 说假话.
**Contrast Type:** lexical
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 2

**Term A:** 真 (zhēn)
**Term B:** 伪 (wěi)
**Transliteration:** zhēn / wěi
**English Translation:** genuine, true / false, spurious, counterfeit
**Description of Contrast:** These two roots co-occur in the binomial 真伪 (authenticity vs. falsity) and in the fixed phrase 真伪难辨. P1b notes that 伪 functions as a marker of deliberate falsification, making this contrast more specifically associated with intentional fraud than the 真/假 opposition. 伪 is more restricted to formal and legal registers and appears specifically in 伪造 (to forge) and 真伪 (genuine or fake).
**Contrast Type:** lexical
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 3

**Term A:** 真实 (zhēnshí)
**Term B:** 虚假 (xūjiǎ)
**Transliteration:** zhēnshí / xūjiǎ
**English Translation:** truthful, authentic, real / false, fictitious, fraudulent
**Description of Contrast:** Documented in P1 and P3 Group 1 as near-antonyms in journalistic and legal usage, and as the formal antonyms in the domain of statements and representations (P3 Group 3 and Group 5). 真实 evaluates a representation as faithfully reflecting reality; 虚假 evaluates a claim or representation as without factual basis, typically with a stronger negative connotation implying deliberate deception. The CONTEXT notes that 虚假 is stronger and more formal than 假 alone.
**Contrast Type:** lexical
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

## Opposition 4

**Term A:** 真实 (zhēnshí)
**Term B:** 失实 (shīshí)
**Transliteration:** zhēnshí / shīshí
**English Translation:** truthful, accurate / inaccurate, contrary to fact
**Description of Contrast:** Documented in P3 Group 1 as near-antonyms in journalistic and legal usage. 真实 affirms correspondence between a representation and reality; 失实 evaluates a report or account as failing to correspond to the facts, without necessarily implying deliberate intent. P3 notes that 失实 does not necessarily imply deliberate deception, distinguishing it from 虚假.
**Contrast Type:** lexical
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

## Opposition 5

**Term A:** 真实 (zhēnshí)
**Term B:** 失真 (shīzhēn)
**Transliteration:** zhēnshí / shīzhēn
**English Translation:** truthful, accurate / distorted, having lost fidelity
**Description of Contrast:** Both evaluate fidelity to reality or an original, but 失真 specifically marks departure from an original state or source (as in audio distortion or historical distortion), while 真实 asserts positive correspondence. P3 notes that 失真 emphasizes departure from an original rather than from an external fact, distinguishing it from 失实.
**Contrast Type:** lexical
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 6

**Term A:** 正确 (zhèngquè)
**Term B:** 错误 (cuòwù)
**Transliteration:** zhèngquè / cuòwù
**English Translation:** correct, right / wrong, erroneous, mistaken
**Description of Contrast:** Explicitly documented in P1 (#5: "Contrasts with 错误") and in P3 Group 4 as the primary antonym pair for correctness relative to a standard. P1 explicitly states that 错误 "contrasts with 正确 as its standard antonym." The pair is productive in contrastive constructions: 纠正错误，走上正确的道路 (P2b Construction 24). Both appear in the same evaluative contexts (educational, technical, moral) and are treated as a contrasting pair throughout the CONTEXT.
**Contrast Type:** lexical
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 7

**Term A:** 正确 (zhèngquè)
**Term B:** 不正确 (bùzhèngquè)
**Transliteration:** zhèngquè / bùzhèngquè
**English Translation:** correct / incorrect
**Description of Contrast:** Documented in P2a (#5) as the standard morphological negation of 正确 formed with the general negator 不. Functions as an adjective evaluating a statement, method, or action as failing to meet the relevant standard of correctness. P2a Section 6 notes it is "extremely common across all registers."

**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 15

**Term A:** 真实性 (zhēnshíxìng)
**Term B:** 虚假性 (xūjiǎxìng)
**Transliteration:** zhēnshíxìng / xūjiǎxìng
**English Translation:** truthfulness, factual accuracy (as a property) / falseness, fraudulent nature (as a property)
**Description of Contrast:** Both are -性 nominalizations documented in P2a Section 1, used in legal and analytical discourse to refer to the abstract properties of being truthful and being false/fraudulent respectively. They stand in the same oppositional relationship as their bases 真实 and 虚假 but at the level of abstract property nouns. P2a notes that 虚假性 is less common than 真实性 but is attested in legal and academic writing. The CONTEXT notes that 真实性 is more frequent and broadly distributed than 虚假性, which is more restricted to legal and regulatory discourse.
**Contrast Type:** morphological
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 16

**Term A:** 正确性 (zhèngquèxìng)
**Term B:** 错误性 (cuòwùxìng)
**Transliteration:** zhèngquèxìng / cuòwùxìng
**English Translation:** correctness, the property of being right / erroneousness, the quality of being mistaken or wrong
**Description of Contrast:** These are the 性-nominalized forms of 正确 and 错误 (P2a items 4 and 5). The CONTEXT explicitly notes an asymmetry: 正确性 is highly productive and common across registers, while 错误性 is considerably less common because 错误 itself already functions as a noun in many contexts, reducing the need for the 性 form.
**Contrast Type:** morphological
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE] for 正确性; [MARGINAL] for 错误性

## Opposition 17

**Term A:** 合法 (héfǎ)
**Term B:** 违法 (wéifǎ)
**Transliteration:** héfǎ / wéifǎ
**English Translation:** lawful, legal, legitimate / illegal, unlawful, in violation of the law
**Description of Contrast:** The CONTEXT documents these as the positive and negative poles of legal evaluation (P1 items 22 and 23; P3 Group 8). 合法 evaluates conduct or status as conforming to legal requirements; 违法 evaluates it as contravening legal norms. Both are standard terms in Chinese legal, administrative, and journalistic registers.
**Contrast Type:** lexical
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 18

**Term A:** 合法性 (héfǎxìng)
**Term B:** 违法性 (wéifǎxìng)
**Transliteration:** héfǎxìng / wéifǎxìng
**English Translation:** legality, legitimacy, lawfulness / illegality, unlawfulness, the quality of being in violation of the law
**Description of Contrast:** These are the 性-nominalized forms of 合法 and 违法 (P2a items 9 and 11). Both are documented as [CORE] in legal and analytical discourse, and they stand in the same oppositional relationship as their bases. The CONTEXT notes that 合法性 also functions as a translation of "legitimacy" in the Weberian political-philosophical sense, an extension not shared by 违法性.
**Contrast Type:** morphological
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 19

**Term A:** 合法化 (héfǎhuà)
**Term B:** (no attested parallel form)
**Transliteration:** héfǎhuà / —
**English Translation:** to legalize, to legitimize / (no parallel)
**Description of Contrast:** The CONTEXT documents 合法化 as a productive 化-derivation (P2a item 10) but does not attest a corresponding *违法化 form. The inchoative/process opposition for this pair is therefore asymmetric: the process of becoming lawful is lexicalized, but the process of becoming unlawful is not expressed by a parallel 化-form in the attested data.
**Contrast Type:** morphological

## Opposition 20

**Term A:** 名副其实 (míng fù qí shí)
**Term B:** 名不副实 (míng bù fù shí)
**Transliteration:** míng fù qí shí / míng bù fù shí
**English Translation:** living up to one's name, worthy of the title / not living up to one's name, in name only
**Description of Contrast:** Documented in P1 (#14 and #15) as a direct pair: 名不副实 is explicitly described as "the negative counterpart of 名副其实." P1 explicitly states that "its antonym 名不副实 is equally common." The morphological negator 不 inserted into the fixed expression produces the antonym. Both are attested as productive in evaluative discourse. 名副其实 asserts that a label accurately corresponds to actual qualities; 名不副实 criticizes a mismatch between label and reality.
**Contrast Type:** morphological
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 22

**Term A:** 揭露真相 (jiēlù zhēnxiàng)

**Transliteration:** jiēlù zhēnxiàng / yǎngài zhēnxiàng
**English Translation:** to expose the truth / to cover up the truth

The two verbs represent opposite orientations toward the same truth-noun: revealing versus concealing. 揭露 means to expose or bring to light; 掩盖 means to conceal or cover up. Both are described as common in journalistic, legal, and political discourse.

**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 28

**Term A:** 这是真的 (zhè shì zhēn de) — predicative
**Term B:** 这不是真的 (zhè bù shì zhēn de) — negated predicative
**Transliteration:** zhè shì zhēn de / zhè bù shì zhēn de
**English Translation:** "This is true / genuine." / "This is not true / not genuine."
**Description of Contrast:** Documented in P2b (Constructions 1 and 7) and in P4a Construction 1 and 2 as the affirmative and negated forms of the same copular construction. The negated form is produced by inserting 不 before 是. The CONTEXT notes that the two forms have distinct pragmatic uses: the affirmative asserts truth or genuineness, while the negated form is used for denial, correction, or reassurance.
**Contrast Type:** constructional
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 31

**Term A:** 真相 (zhēnxiàng)
**Term B:** 假象 (jiǎxiàng)
**Transliteration:** zhēnxiàng / jiǎxiàng
**English Translation:** the true state of affairs / false appearance, illusion, misleading phenomenon

The CONTEXT documents these as contrasting compounds sharing the second morpheme 象 (appearance/phenomenon) but with opposite first morphemes 真 (true) and 假 (false) (P1b Root 1 item 2; P1b Root 3 item 4; P3 Group 3 and Group 5).

## Opposition 32

## Opposition 38

**Term A:** 伪善 (wěishàn)
**Term B:** 真诚 (zhēnchéng)
**Transliteration:** wěishàn / zhēnchéng
**English Translation:** hypocrisy, false virtue / sincerity, genuine feeling
**Description of Contrast:** Documented in P3 Group 5, where 真诚 is listed as a positive evaluation of genuine feeling and 伪善 as a negative evaluation of false virtue. Both evaluate the correspondence between outward moral presentation and inner character, but from opposite poles.
**
