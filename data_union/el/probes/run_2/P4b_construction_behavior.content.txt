# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P4b Construction Usage & Constraints
# Language: Greek (el)

---

## Construction Usage Table

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness Rating | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. ATTRIBUTION** | Ο ισχυρισμός είναι αληθής. | *O ishirismós íne alithís.* | "The claim is true." | [CORE] | Predicative copular attribution is the standard pattern across all registers; αληθής is more formal/legal, αληθινός more colloquial. Prenominal attributive use of αληθής is less frequent than predicative use. |
| **1. ATTRIBUTION** | Η κατάθεση κρίθηκε ψευδής. | *I katáthesi krítike psevdís.* | "The testimony was judged false." | [CORE] | Typical in legal and journalistic registers; κρίθηκε implies an institutional or evaluative judgment rather than simple description. |
| **2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION** | Δεν είναι αληθινό αυτό που λες. | *Den íne alithinó aftó pu les.* | "What you are saying is not true." | [CORE] | Preverbal δεν is the standard negation strategy; this construction is fully idiomatic across all registers including everyday speech. |
| **2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION** | Ο ισχυρισμός δεν είναι τεκμηριωμένος. | *O ishirismós den íne tekmirio̱ménos.* | "The claim is not substantiated." | [CORE] | Typical in journalistic and legal contexts; τεκμηριωμένος is register-restricted (formal/written) but the negation pattern itself is unrestricted. |
| **3. ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION** | αληθινή ιστορία | *alithiní istoría* | "true story" | [CORE] | Prenominal attributive position with full agreement is freely available; αληθινός is the preferred adjective in this position over αληθής in everyday and narrative registers. |
| **3. ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION** | ψευδής κατάθεση | *psevdís katáthesi* | "false testimony" | [CORE] | Typical in legal and formal written registers; ψευδής in prenominal position is more restricted to formal/legal contexts than its predicative use. |
| **4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE** | Ο ισχυρισμός αποδείχθηκε αληθής. | *O ishirismós apodíhthike alithís.* | "The claim proved to be true." | [CORE] | Standard construction in legal, journalistic, and argumentative discourse; αποδείχθηκε encodes both the evaluative process and its result. |
| **4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE** | Αποδείχθηκε αναξιόπιστος. | *Apodíhthike anaksiópistos.* | "He proved to be unreliable." | [CORE] | Fully idiomatic; subject may be a person or a proposition. The bare γίνεται alternative is not attested as idiomatic in this domain. |
| **5. CAUSATIVE** | Νόθευσε τα εκλογικά αποτελέσματα. | *Nóthefse ta eklogika apotelesmata.* | "He falsified the election results." | [CORE] | Lexical causative; νοθεύω is idiomatic in electoral, food-safety, and documentary contexts. No productive morphological causative exists for this domain. |
| **5. CAUSATIVE** | Ακύρωσε τη σύμβαση. | *Akíro̱se ti símvasi.* | "He annulled the contract." | [CORE] | Lexical causative restricted to legal and administrative contexts; each verb in this domain (νοθεύω, πλαστογραφώ, ακυρώνω) is semantically restricted to its own sub-domain rather than being interchangeable. |
| **6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION** | αλήθεια | *alíthia* | "truth" | [CORE] | Fully lexicalized; used across all registers from everyday speech to philosophy and law. Suffix choice (-εια, -ία, -τητα) is lexically determined and not rule-governed. |
| **6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION** | αξιοπιστία | *aksiopistía* | "credibility, reliability" | [CORE] | Typical in legal, journalistic, and institutional registers; -τητα and -ία nominalizations in this domain tend toward formal written use. |
| **7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION** | ψεύτης / ψεύτρα | *pséftis* / *pséftra* | "liar" | [CORE] | Primary everyday agent noun for this domain; gender distinction (ψεύτης masc., ψεύτρα fem.) is fully productive. No common agentive noun for "truth-teller" is attested in everyday use. |
| **7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION** | πλαστογράφος | *plastográfos* | "forger" | [CORE] | Register-restricted to legal and formal contexts; agentive nominalization in this domain is lexically restricted rather than productively derived. |
| **7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION** | ψευδοεπιστήμονας | *psevdoepistímonas* | "pseudoscientist" | [CORE] | ψευδο- compound pattern for pseudo-agents is productive; new formations are coined as needed across domains. |
| **8. EVIDENTIAL / EPISTEMIC-STATUS MARKING** | Αποδεδειγμένως ψευδής ισχυρισμός. | *Apodedeignménos psevdís ishirismós.* | "A demonstrably false claim." | [CORE] | Typical in legal and journalistic registers; adverbial and participial evidential markers form a productive paradigm in formal written Greek. |
| **8. EVIDENTIAL / EPISTEMIC-STATUS MARKING** | Εικαζόμενος δράστης. | *Ikazómenos drástis.* | "The alleged perpetrator." | [CORE] | Strongly associated with legal and journalistic registers; εικαζόμενος functions as a conventionalized evidential modifier in reporting contexts, parallel to English "alleged." |
| **9. DISCOURSE-FRAMING CONSTRUCTION** | Η αλήθεια είναι ότι δεν υπάρχουν αποδείξεις. | *I alíthia íne óti den ipárhun apodíksis.* | "The truth is that there is no evidence." | [CORE] | Sentence-initial or parenthetical; functions as a speaker-oriented frankness or correction marker rather than a propositional predicate. Common across spoken and written registers. |
| **9. DISCOURSE-FRAMING CONSTRUCTION** | Για να είμαι ακριβής, δεν το είπε ποτέ. | *Ya na íme akrivís, den to ípe poté.* | "To be precise, he never said it." | [CORE] | Precision-marking frame; syntactically parenthetical and speaker-oriented. More typical of careful written or formal spoken registers than casual conversation. |
| **10. PSEUDO- COMPOUND** | ψευδοεπιστήμη | *psevdoepistími* | "pseudoscience" | [CORE] | Fully productive combining form; freely attaches to nouns and adjectives across domains. New formations are coined as needed with no apparent morphological restriction. |
| **10. PSEUDO- COMPOUND** | ψευδοδημοκρατία | *psevdodimokratía* | "pseudo-democracy, sham democracy" | [CORE] | Productive in political and evaluative discourse; the pattern is open-ended and not register-restricted, appearing in both formal analysis and polemical writing. |

## Alternate Constructions

| Construction Type | Alternate Construction | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **1. ATTRIBUTION** | [NP] + αποδεικνύεται + [adjective] | Overlaps with inchoative (Construction 4); encodes ongoing or resultant evaluation rather than simple state attribution. |
| **2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION** | Privative-prefix forms: αναξιόπιστος, αδιαμφισβήτητος | Lexicalized negatives; not the productive negation strategy but available as fixed lexical items. |
| **3. ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION** | [head noun] + [genitive NP] (e.g., τεκμήριο αθωότητας) | Nominal modifier strategy using a following genitive NP; used where no suitable adjective exists or in legal fixed phrases. |
| **5. CAUSATIVE** | κάνω + [NP] + να + [subjunctive verb] | General periphrastic causative; attested as a structural resource but noted as non-idiomatic in the truth domain specifically. |
| **7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION** | Periphrastic: αυτός που λέει την αλήθεια | Used where no lexicalized agent noun exists (e.g., "truth-teller"); not a morphological construction. |
| **9. DISCOURSE-FRAMING** | αλήθεια να λέμε; στην ουσία; ουσιαστικά | Additional fixed framing expressions noted in P4a; function as discourse-level operators with similar speaker-orientation properties. |
