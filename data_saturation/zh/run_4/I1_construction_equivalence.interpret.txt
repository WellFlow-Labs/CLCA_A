## 2. Substitution Patterns (Narrative)

### 2.1 CAUSATION (factual): Substitution by general-purpose operators

No truth-domain-specific causative construction is attested in the data. The general-purpose causative constructions 使 + NP + [truth-domain predicate] and 让 + NP + [truth-domain predicate] can express causative meanings within the truth domain (e.g., a situation causing a claim to become accurate or inaccurate), but these constructions are not specific to the truth domain and are not listed among the attested truth-domain constructions in P4a. The CAUSATION (factual) operator is therefore **substituted by** these general-purpose periphrastic constructions. This is a morphosyntactic gap: the language has no dedicated truth-domain causative morpheme or frame, and the general-purpose operators serve as the only available mechanism.

### 2.2 CAUSATION (character): No substitution attested

No construction expressing the meaning "X causes someone to become a truthful or deceptive person" (i.e., causation affecting a person's truth-related character or disposition) is attested anywhere in P4a or P4b. The general-purpose causative constructions noted under CAUSATION (factual) could in principle be extended to this meaning, but no such usage is attested in the data. This is treated as a structural gap rather than a substitution.

### 2.3 BECOME: Lexically restricted, not fully substituted

The inchoative -化 construction (Construction 4) is morphosyntactically available and productive in Chinese generally, but within the truth domain it is attested only with 合法 (合法化) and 客观 (客观化). Other truth-domain adjectives (真实, 正确, 准确, 可靠, 虚假) do not take -化 in common usage according to the P4b notes. This means the BECOME operator is partially available: the morphosyntactic mechanism exists, but its application within the truth domain is lexically restricted. There is no evidence in the data that REVELATION (Construction 12) substitutes for BECOME in Chinese; the two constructions serve distinct functions (REVELATION expresses the dynamic of exposing hidden truth; BECOME expresses a change of state). No substitution relationship between them is supported by the data.

### 2.4 VERIFICATION and DISCONFIRMATION: Shared construction with differentiated result predicates

Construction 11 (Verification Result Construction) covers both VERIFICATION and DISCONFIRMATION outcomes. The same syntactic frame — 经 + [investigation verb] + result predicate — is used for both: 经查属实 expresses a positive verification outcome, while 经查失实 and 经鉴定系伪造 express disconfirmation outcomes. The two operators are not expressed by separate constructions; they share the same frame and are differentiated only by the choice of result predicate (属实 vs. 失实 / 系伪造). Additionally, Construction 10 (Correspondence Frame) provides a second syntactic mechanism specifically for DISCONFIRMATION (与实际情况不相符), which has no parallel positive-verification counterpart of the same type in the data (与事实相符 is attested but is less specifically a "result of investigation" frame than Construction 11). DISCONFIRMATION therefore has access to two constructions (10 and 11), while VERIFICATION in the strict sense of a confirmed positive outcome is primarily expressed through Construction 11 alone.

### 2.5 Positive/negative asymmetry in ATTRIBUTION

The data shows no structural asymmetry in the ATTRIBUTION construction itself: both positive attribution (Construction 1: Subject + 是 + [truth-domain adj] + 的) and negative attribution (Construction 2: Subject + 不 + [truth-domain adj]) are fully attested and rated [CORE]. The negation is analytic throughout: the negator 不 (or the more formal 非) is a separate morpheme that precedes the adjective without altering its base form. There is no morphological negation (no negative prefix fused to the adjective stem) in this domain.

### 2.6 Agentive nominalization: Deception-only asymmetry

The agentive -者 construction (Construction 7) is attested only with verbs of deliberate deception or fabrication (伪造者, 假冒者). No parallel agentive form for truth-telling roles (e.g., *真实者, *如实者) is attested in common usage. The P4b notes explicitly flag this asymmetry. This is an asymmetric lexical gap: the morphosyntactic mechanism (-者 suffixation) is available, but it is not applied to truth-telling verb bases in the attested data.

### 2.7 REVELATION: No substitution for BECOME

As noted in 2.3 above, the data does not support the claim that REVELATION substitutes for BECOME in Chinese. REVELATION (Construction 12) is a frame involving a dynamic agent who exposes or conceals truth (揭露真相, 掩盖真相); BECOME (Construction 4) is an inchoative construction expressing a change of state (合法化, 客观化). These are functionally and syntactically distinct, and no overlap or substitution relationship between them is attested in the data.

### 2.8 DISCOVERY as a potential substitute for CAUSATION

The P4a and P4b data do not identify a DISCOVERY construction as a separate category. The Verification Result Construction (Construction 11) involves a process of investigation (经查, 经鉴定) that could be described as discovery-adjacent, but it is classified in the data as a verification/result frame, not a causation frame. No evidence in the data supports treating DISCOVERY as a substitute for CAUSATION in Chinese.

## 3. Missingness Summary

### 3.1 Structural Gaps

- **CAUSATION (character):** No construction expressing "X causes someone to be/become a truthful or deceptive person" is attested. The general-purpose causative constructions (使/让 + NP + predicate) are available in principle but are not attested in this function in the data, and no truth-domain-specific causative construction exists. This is a structural gap.

### 3.2 Lexical Gaps

- **BECOME (-化) within the truth domain:** The -化 inchoative morpheme is morphosyntactically productive in Chinese but is lexically restricted within the truth domain. It is attested with 合法 and 客观 only; it does not attach to 真实, 正确, 准确, 可靠, or 虚假 in common usage. This is a lexical gap (the morphosyntactic slot exists; the lexical items do not fill it).
- **Agentive nominalization for truth-telling roles:** The -者 agentive suffix is morphosyntactically available but is not attested with truth-telling verb bases. No common-usage agentive noun for "one who tells the truth" or "one who reports accurately" is attested. This is a lexical gap within an otherwise productive morphosyntactic pattern.

### 3.3 Conceptual Gaps

- No conceptual gaps are identifiable from the data alone. All gaps identified above are either structural or lexical. The data does not provide evidence that any truth-domain concept is blocked at the conceptual level in Chinese; the gaps are gaps in attested expression, not in conceptual availability.

### 3.4 Asymmetric Gaps (only negative or only positive forms missing)

- **Agentive nominalization (-者):** Deception/fabrication agentives are attested (伪造者, 假冒者); truth-telling agentives are not attested. The gap is on the positive (truth-telling) side only.
- **DISCONFIRMATION vs. VERIFICATION:** DISCONFIRMATION has access to two constructions (Construction 10 and Construction 11); VERIFICATION in the strict confirmed-positive sense is expressed primarily through Construction 11. Construction 10 (Correspondence Frame) is more strongly associated with disconfirmation in the attested examples (与实际情况不相符 is the primary example; 与事实相符 is attested as an alternate but is less central in the data). This is a mild asymmetry favoring disconfirmation expression, not a full gap.
- **CAUSATION (factual) vs. CAUSATION (character):** CAUSATION (factual) has a circumlocutory substitute (使/让 + NP + predicate); CAUSATION (character) has no attested substitute at all. The gap is more complete on the character-causation side.
