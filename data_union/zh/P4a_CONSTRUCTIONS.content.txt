# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P4a CONSTRUCTIONS
# Language: Chinese (zh)
# Layer: P4a_CONSTRUCTIONS

---

## 1. ATTRIBUTION

### Pattern
Subject + (是) + truth/authenticity adjective/stative verb + (的)
(Copular predication: X is [true/false/genuine/accurate])

OR

[Subject] + [truth-predicate adjective] (copula-less predicate)

### Examples
- 这是真的。/ Zhè shì zhēn de. / "This is true / genuine."
- 这份报告是虚假的。/ Zhè fèn bàogào shì xūjiǎ de. / "This report is false / fraudulent."
- 这条消息是真实的。/ Zhè tiáo xiāoxi shì zhēnshí de. / "This piece of news is truthful/accurate."
- 这份报告是真实的。/ Zhè fèn bàogào shì zhēnshí de. / "This report is truthful / accurate."
- 他的陈述属实。/ Tā de chénshù shǔshí. / "His statement is confirmed as accurate."

### Notes
The particle 的 nominalizes or substantivizes the adjective in predicative position. This is the default copular attribution pattern for the entire truth-domain vocabulary; it applies equally to 真, 假, 正确, 错误, 可靠, 客观, etc. Degree adverbs (很, 完全, 根本) insert before the adjective.

Adjectival truth-predicates (真实, 正确, 准确, 可靠, 虚假, 失实) can appear either in the full copular frame 是…的 or as bare predicates without 是; the bare form is more formal and assertive. 属实 is a fixed legal-register predicate meaning "verified as true" and does not take 是…的.

The copula 是 is optional when the predicate adjective directly follows the subject. For stative verbs that already carry negative polarity (失实, 错误), the negative attribution is typically expressed by their positive antonyms (真实, 正确) rather than by negating the negative form. The adjective alone (without 是…的) can also serve as predicate in more formal registers: 报道失实 "the report is inaccurate."

## 2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION

### Pattern
Subject + 不是 + truth/authenticity adjective + 的 **or** Subject + 不 + adjective (direct negation)

OR

Subject + 并非 + truth-domain adjective/stative verb (formal)

OR

Subject + [negative compound] (lexicalized)

### Examples
- 这不是真的。/ Zhè bú shì zhēn de. / "This is not true / not genuine."
- 他的判断不准确。/ Tā de pànduàn bù zhǔnquè. / "His judgment is not accurate."
- 这份文件不是真的，是假的。/ Zhè fèn wénjiàn bù shì zhēn de, shì jiǎ de. / "This document is not genuine; it is fake."
- 这篇报道严重失实。/ Zhè piān bàodào yánzhòng shīshí. / "This report is seriously inaccurate."
- 该陈述并非属实。/ Gāi chénshù bìngfēi shǔshí. / "The statement is not confirmed as factual."

### Notes
Two competing negative patterns exist: the full copular negation 不是…的 (more emphatic, contrastive) and direct pre-adjectival 不 (more neutral). Both are productive across all truth-domain adjectives. 没有 is not used for property negation in this domain.

Negation is expressed by 不是…真的 / 是假的 for genuineness judgments, and by 不 + adjective (不正确, 不准确, 不可靠, 不真实) for property negations. Several negative forms are lexicalized (不正确, 不可靠, 不相符); others remain syntactic. The compound 失实 and 失真 lexicalize the negative outcome directly without 不.

并非 is a formal/written variant that directly negates the predicate without the 是…的 frame.

## 3. ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION

### Pattern
Truth/authenticity adjective/compound + 的 + Noun
(Attributive modifier: a [true/accurate/reliable/genuine] N)

OR

[4-character expression] + 的 + [noun]

### Examples
- 可靠的证据 / kěkào de zhèngjù / "reliable evidence"
- 客观的评价 / kèguān de píngjià / "an objective evaluation"
- 真实的情况 / zhēnshí de qíngkuàng / "the actual/true situation"
- 虚假的广告 / xūjiǎ de guǎnggào / "false / fraudulent advertising"
- 名副其实的专家 / míng fù qí shí de zhuānjiā / "a genuine expert; an expert who truly deserves the name"
- 货真价实的好东西 / huò zhēn jià shí de hǎo dōngxi / "something that is genuinely what it claims to be"

### Notes
The structural particle 的 links modifier to head noun. This pattern is highly productive across the full vocabulary: 真实的报道, 虚假的信息, 正确的方法, 准确的描述, 道地的北京话. Monosyllabic roots (真, 假) may appear without 的 in tight compounds (真相, 假话, 真品, 假货) but require 的 as free attributive modifiers.

Four-character expressions (成语) take 的 when used attributively. The modifier precedes the noun in all cases; there is no postpositive modification in Chinese.

## 4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE

### Pattern
(a) 变得 + adjective ("become [more] X")
(b) 弄假成真 / 假戏真做 (lexicalized inchoatives: fiction/pretense becomes real)
(c) Verb/process + 成 + truth-domain noun (resultative complement)
(d) Truth-domain adjective + 化 (suffix, "to become X / to make X")

### Examples
- 情况变得更加复杂，真相变得难以查明。/ Qíngkuàng biàn de gèngjiā fùzá, zhēnxiàng biàn de nányǐ chámíng. / "The situation became more complex; the truth became harder to establish."
- 报道变得失实了。/ Bàodào biàn de shīshí le. / "The reporting has become inaccurate."
- 弄假成真 / nòng jiǎ chéng zhēn / "What began as pretense became real."
- 合法化 / héfǎhuà / "to legalize / to become lawful" (lit. lawful + -ize)

### Notes
A general inchoative construction (变得 + adjective) applies to gradable truth-domain adjectives (准确, 可靠, 客观) but is semantically odd with categorical terms like 真/假, which do not readily admit of becoming. The lexicalized four-character expressions 弄假成真 and 假戏真做 encode the specific inchoative meaning of a false state transitioning to a genuine one; no productive morphological inchoative exists for this domain.

General inchoative meaning is expressed by 变得 + adjective + 了; this is a syntactic construction rather than a morphological one. The fixed expression 弄假成真 lexicalizes a specific inchoative trajectory (false → genuine) and is the only conventionalized inchoative form in this domain.

The resultative complement 成 ("become, turn into") appears in verb-complement constructions such as 弄假成真. The suffix 化 attached to stative bases forms verbs meaning "to become X" or "to make X," as in 合法化 (legalize), 道德化 (moralize), 真实化 (make realistic). The 化 pattern is productive but not uniformly available across all truth-domain bases (e.g., *真化, *假化 are not attested).

## 5. CAUSATIVE

### Pattern
(a) 使/让 + NP + adjective/verb ("cause NP to be/become X")
(b) Verb + 真/假/实 as resultative complement

### Examples
- 这种做法使报道失真。/ Zhè zhǒng zuòfǎ shǐ bàodào shīzhēn. / "This practice causes the reporting to become distorted."
- 以假乱真 / yǐ jiǎ luàn zhēn / "using fakes to confuse [what is] genuine" (causative: fakes cause confusion of the genuine)
- 这使报道失真。/ Zhè shǐ bàodào shīzhēn. / "This caused the report to become distorted."
- 掩盖真相 / yǎngài zhēnxiàng / "to cover up the truth" [causally preventing truth from being known]

### Notes
A productive periphrastic causative (使/让 + NP + result) applies to truth-domain predicates, particularly in critical or analytical registers (使数据失真, 让判断更客观). A dedicated morphological causative does not exist. The fixed expression 以假乱真 encodes a causative meaning lexically. The resultative complement pattern (verb + 真/实) is attested in 逼真 (approach + genuine → lifelike) and 属实 (belong-to + real → confirmed as true) but is not a productive open construction in this domain.

Chinese has no dedicated causative morphology. Causative meaning is expressed periphrastically via 使/让 + NP + predicate, or via result-complement verb constructions. In the truth domain, causative framing most commonly appears with distortion/concealment verbs (使失真, 使失实) rather than with truth-positive predicates.

Causative meanings are also expressed through verb-object constructions such as 证实 (to cause something to be confirmed), 核实 (to cause something to be verified), though these are not structurally distinct from ordinary transitive constructions.

## 6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION

### Pattern
Adjective/verb base + 性 (property nominalization: "the quality/property of being X")
Adjective/verb base + 上 (locative-adverbial nominalization: "at the level of X; in terms of X")
Adjective/verb base + 度 (degree nominalization: "the degree/extent of X")
Adjective/verb base + 感 (experiential/aesthetic nominalization: "sense/feeling of X")
Adjective/verb base + 主义 (ideological nominalization)

### Examples
- 新闻报道的真实性 / xīnwén bàodào de zhēnshíxìng / "the truthfulness / authenticity of news reporting"
- 本质上是一样的 / běnzhìshàng shì yīyàng de / "essentially the same" (lit. "at the level of essence, it is the same")
- 准确性 / zhǔnquèxìng / "accuracy, the property of being precise"
- 可靠性 / kěkàoxìng / "reliability (as a property)" — e.g., 数据的可靠性 "the reliability of the data"
- 真实感 / zhēnshígǎn / "sense of realism"
- 实用主义 / shíyòngzhǔyì / "pragmatism"
- 写实主义 / xiěshízhǔyì / "realism"

### Notes
The suffix 性 is the most productive abstract nominalizer in this domain, attested with: 真实性, 正确性, 准确性, 可靠性, 客观性, 主观性, 实质性, 真诚性, 诚实性, 虚假性, 合法性, 违法性, 欺骗性, 实用性. It selectively attaches to evaluative adjectives susceptible to degree or measurement; it does not attach to nouns like 真相 or 事实. It is not productive on verbal items (欺骗, 伪造) or on monosyllabic roots (真, 假) in standard usage. Application to monosyllabic bases is restricted and often yields technical rather than general abstract nouns (真性, 假性 function as medical classifiers rather than free abstract nouns).

The locative suffix 上 applied to 本质 and 实质 produces highly frequent adverbial phrases (本质上, 实质上) that function as discourse markers. These 上-forms function syntactically as adverbials rather than as noun-phrase heads (see also §10).

The suffix 度 is attested in the technical term 失真度 (degree of distortion).

The suffix 感 nominalizes experiential/aesthetic properties: 真实感 "sense of realism."

The suffix 主义 forms ideological nominalizations in a narrower set: 实用主义 (pragmatism), 写实主义 (realism).

## 7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION

### Pattern
(a) Verb/adjective base + 者 (agentive suffix: "one who X-es / one who is X")
(b) Verb + 假/伪 + 者 (agentive: "one who falsifies / one who fakes")

### Examples
- 伪善者 / wěishànzhě / "a hypocrite; one who practices false virtue"
- 造假者 / zàojiǎzhě / "a fabricator; one who manufactures fakes or falsifies data"
- 欺骗者 / qīpiànzhě / "deceiver; one who deceives"
- 伪装者 / wěizhuāngzhě / "one who disguises; impostor"
- 伪造者 / wěizàozhě / "forger, counterfeiter" (one who fabricates)
- 假冒者 / jiǎmàozhě / "one who passes off fakes; impersonator"
- 作假者 / zuòjiǎzhě / "falsifier"

### Notes
The suffix 者 is the productive agentive nominalizer in Chinese. In this domain it attaches to negative-pole bases: 伪善者 (hypocrite), 造假者 (fabricator), 作假者 (falsifier), 欺骗者 (deceiver), 伪造者 (forger), 假冒者, 伪装者. It does not attach to adjectival items (真实, 正确, 可靠).

A positive-pole agentive ("truth-teller") is not lexicalized via 者-suffixation; the nearest equivalents are periphrastic (说真话的人, 如实陈述的人) or use the compound 诚实的人. The asymmetry — agentives for falsifiers but not for truth-tellers — is supported by the data.

In everyday speech, 骗子 (piànzi, "swindler/cheat") is more common than 欺骗者 for the agent of deception; -者 forms are formal and written. The colloquial agentive for the deception pole uses the suffix 子 rather than 者, preferred in informal registers over 欺骗者.

## 8. PROHIBITIVE / NORMATIVE CONSTRUCTION

### Pattern
严禁 / 不得 + truth-domain verb phrase
("It is strictly forbidden / not permitted to [falsify / fabricate / deceive]")

### Examples
- 严禁弄虚作假。/ Yánjìn nòng xū zuò jiǎ. / "It is strictly forbidden to engage in fraud and falsification."
- 不得造假。/ Bùdé zào jiǎ. / "Fabrication is not permitted."
- 严禁弄虚作假 / yánjìn nòng xū zuò jiǎ / "it is strictly forbidden to engage in fraud and falsification"
- 不得欺骗消费者 / bùdé qīpiàn xiāofèizhě / "it is prohibited to deceive consumers"

### Notes
This construction type is specific to the deliberate-falsification cluster: 弄虚作假, 造假, 作假, 伪造, 欺骗. It does not appear with truth-evaluation adjectives (正确, 真实) or with authenticity nouns (真相, 事实). Its restriction to the falsification sub-domain reflects its origin in regulatory, administrative, and anti-corruption discourse.

This construction is characteristic of Chinese legal, regulatory, and institutional discourse. 严禁 (strictly prohibit) and 不得 (must not / it is not permitted to) are the standard formal prohibition markers; they combine productively with truth-domain violation verbs (弄虚作假, 欺骗, 伪造, 作假). The construction has no direct positive counterpart in the same register; positive obligations to be truthful are expressed differently (如实陈述, 诚实守信).

## 9. VERB + TRUTH-NOUN COLLOCATION (INVESTIGATIVE / EPISTEMIC)

### Pattern
Epistemic/investigative verb + 真相 / 事实 / 真伪 / 真理 / 本质
(Conventionalized verb-object collocations for revealing, concealing, establishing, or evaluating truth)

### Examples
- 揭露真相 / jiēlù zhēnxiàng / "to expose the truth / reveal the true state of affairs"
- 歪曲事实 / wāiqū shìshí / "to distort the facts / misrepresent the truth"
- 追求真理 / zhuīqiú zhēnlǐ / "to pursue truth"

### Notes
This is the most productive construction type in the domain. The verbs cluster into two semantic sets: (a) revealing/establishing — 揭露, 揭示, 查明, 还原, 尊重, 追求, 坚持, 辨别, 鉴别, 核实, 以…为依据; (b) concealing/distorting — 掩盖, 歪曲, 颠倒, 混淆. Each set collocates with 真相, 事实, 是非, 真理, or 真伪 as object. The contrastive pairing of one verb from each set (揭露真相 / 掩盖真相; 尊重事实 / 歪曲事实) is a stable discourse pattern across legal, journalistic, and political registers.

The verb set differs systematically by noun: 真相 collocates with investigative verbs; 真理 with commitment verbs; 真伪 with authentication verbs.

## 10. DISCOURSE MARKER (FRAME-SETTING ADVERBIAL)

### Pattern
Truth/reality noun + 上 → adverbial phrase used to frame or correct a claim
("In fact / actually / essentially / in substance")

### Examples
- 事实上，情况并非如此。/ Shìshíshàng, qíngkuàng bìng fēi rúcǐ. / "In fact, the situation is not like that."
- 本质上，这两个问题是一样的。/ Běnzhìshàng, zhè liǎng gè wèntí shì yīyàng de. / "Essentially, these two questions are the same."

### Notes
The locative suffix 上 applied to 事实, 本质, and 实质 produces a set of sentence-initial or parenthetical discourse markers that introduce a correction, clarification, or claim about underlying reality. These are among the most frequent discourse-framing devices in Chinese across all registers. The pattern is distinct from the abstract nominalization in §6 in that the 上-forms function syntactically as adverbials rather than as noun-phrase heads.

## 11. ADVERBIAL TRUTH-FRAMING CONSTRUCTION

### Pattern
如实 + [verb of reporting/stating/recording]
OR
[verb] + 实 (bound adverbial in fixed forms: 据实, 如实)
OR
Truth-domain adverb / fixed adverbial phrase + Verb phrase: manner of speaking or acting "as the facts are"

### Examples
- 如实陈述 / rúshí chénshù / "to state truthfully; to give a truthful account"
- 据实汇报 / jùshí huìbào / "to report based on the facts"
- 实事求是地说 / shíshì qiúshì de shuō / "to say it as it actually is; to speak empirically and honestly"

### Notes
如实 and 据实 are fixed adverbial expressions meaning "in accordance with the facts/reality." They precede verbs of communication, reporting, and recording (陈述, 反映, 汇报, 记录) and are especially common in legal and administrative registers. No equivalent adverbial form exists for the negative (one does not say *如假陈述); the negative is expressed by 虚假陈述 or 不如实陈述.

Chinese has a productive class of truth-domain adverbials that modify speech and reporting verbs: 如实 (as reality is), 实话实说 (say it straight), 坦白说 (frankly speaking). These are not derivationally related to the attribution or nominalization patterns above; they form a distinct constructional type in which the truth-domain element specifies the manner of a communicative act. The particle 地 links longer adverbial phrases to the verb (实事求是地说); shorter adverbs (如实) attach directly without 地.

## 12. CORRESPONDENCE PREDICATE

### Pattern
与 + [standard NP] + 相符 / 不相符
OR
与 + [standard NP] + 一致 / 不一致

### Examples
- 与事实相符 / yǔ shìshí xiāngfú / "consistent with the facts"
- 前后相符 / qiánhòu xiāngfú / "internally consistent; consistent from start to finish"

### Notes
相符 is a relational predicate requiring two terms: the item being evaluated and the standard it is compared against, introduced by the coverb 与. This construction is the standard way to express correspondence or non-correspondence between a representation and reality in legal and administrative registers. 一致 (consistent) is a near-synonym used in broader contexts.

## 13. IMPOSSIBILITY-OF-VERIFICATION FRAME

### Pattern
无法 / 无从 + [verification verb] + [truth-domain noun]

### Examples
- 无法核实 / wúfǎ héshí / "unable to verify; impossible to confirm"
- 无从判断真伪 / wúcóng pànduàn zhēnwěi / "no way to judge authenticity; unable to determine whether genuine or fake"

### Notes
无法 and 无从 are epistemic modals expressing impossibility of action. This construction is a productive frame in journalistic and investigative discourse for marking the limits of verification. It contrasts with the positive frame 经过核实 ("after verification") and 经专家鉴定 ("as authenticated by experts"), which assert that verification has been completed.

## 14. VERIFICATION / CONFIRMATION CONSTRUCTION

### Pattern
经 + verification verb + (result): "upon/through [process], [outcome confirmed]"

### Examples
- 经查属实 / jīng chá shǔshí / "verified upon investigation; confirmed as factual after inquiry"
- 经调查证实 / jīng diàochá zhèngshí / "confirmed by investigation"

### Notes
This construction is specific to Chinese administrative, legal, and journalistic registers. 经 ("through, upon") introduces the verification process; the result (属实, 证实, 核实) follows directly. The pattern is highly conventionalized: 经核实, 经查实, 经调查证实 are fixed formulas for asserting that a claim has been formally checked. The negative counterpart uses 无法: 无法核实 "cannot be verified."
