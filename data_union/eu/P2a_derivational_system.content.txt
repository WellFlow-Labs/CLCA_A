# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2a Derivational System
# Language: Basque (eu)

---

## Preliminary Note on Method

The following documents attested derivational morphology for each P1 base form. Forms already introduced in P1 or P1b are cross-referenced rather than re-described in full. Derivations are listed per base form; where a derived form itself appears as a base in P1 or P1b, this is noted. The inventory does not assume paradigm completeness. Gaps are preserved and noted where they are informative.

## 1. Base Form: **egia**

---

**Derived Form 1.1**
- **Base Form:** egia
- **Derived Form:** egiazkoa
- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Literal Breakdown:** egia (truth) + -zko (relational/adjectival suffix) + -a (definite article)
- **Natural English Translation:** true, truthful, genuine, real
- **Usage:** Adjectival form applied to statements, persons, or things evaluated as truthful or genuine. Documented in P1 item 2 and P1b 1.4.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 1.2**
- **Base Form:** egia
- **Derived Form:** egiazko
- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Literal Breakdown:** egia (truth) + -zko (relational/adjectival suffix) — indefinite attributive form without article
- **Natural English Translation:** true, genuine (attributive)
- **Usage:** The indefinite attributive form used before nouns, as in *egiazko lekukotasuna* (true testimony). Standard pre-nominal form in Basque, where the definite article is dropped in attributive position. Documented in P1b.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 1.3**
- **Base Form:** egia
- **Derived Form:** egiazkotasuna
- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Literal Breakdown:** egia (truth) + -zko (relational suffix) + -tasun (abstract nominal suffix) + -a (definite article) — "the quality of being true/genuine"
- **Natural English Translation:** truthfulness, veracity, genuineness, authenticity
- **Usage:** Abstract noun used in philosophical and formal written discourse to denote the property of being true or genuine. Documented in P1 item 20 and P1b 1.5.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Note on egia derivational productivity:** The root *egia* generates derivations primarily through the intermediate verbalizing sequence *-zta-*, which then serves as a further base for nominal (*-tze*, *-pen*) and adjectival (*-garri*, *-gaitz*, *-ezin*) suffixation. Direct suffixation on *egia* itself is limited to *-zko* (yielding *egiazko/a*), the *-zko* + *-tasun* chain (yielding *egiazkotasun/a*), the direct *-tasun* chain (yielding *egiatasuna*), the instrumental *-z* (yielding *egiaz*), and the adverbial *-zki* (yielding *egiazki*). No agentive (*-tzaile*, *-dari*) forms on the bare root *egia* have been identified. The *-zale* devotee suffix yields *egiazale/a* in marginal use.

## 5. Base Form: **gezur**

---

**Derived Form 5.1**

- **Usage:** Transitive verb used to describe the act of demonstrating that something is false or of contradicting a claim. Used in argumentative, journalistic, and legal contexts. Mentioned in P1 item 4 usage note.

- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Note on gezur derivational productivity:** The root *gezur* generates a moderately productive paradigm. The agentive/characterizing suffix *-ti* (yielding *gezurti/a*) and the pejorative practice suffix *-keria* (yielding *gezurkeria*) are both attested and in common use. The verbalizing sequence *-ta-tu* (yielding *gezurtatu*) parallels the pattern seen in *egiaztatu*. No *-tasun* abstract nominal directly on *gezur* has been identified as broadly attested; *gezurtasuna* is marginal. The verbal function is primarily covered by the collocation *gezur esan*.
