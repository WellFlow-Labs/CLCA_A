# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P5 Oppositions
# Language: Georgian (ka)

---

**Methodological note:** Each opposition below is extracted directly from explicit contrasts, co-occurrence patterns, and negation relationships documented in P1, P1b, P2a, P2b, P3, and P4. No opposition is inferred beyond what the data supports. Where the data supports asymmetry, asymmetry is preserved.

---

## Opposition 1

**Term A:** სიმართლე / simartle
**Term B:** სიყალბე / siq̇albe
**Transliteration:** simartle / siq̇albe
**English translation:** "truth, correctness" / "falsehood, falsity, fraud"
**Description of contrast:** P1 item #11 explicitly states that siq̇albe "contrasts directly with simartle." The two nominals occupy opposite poles of the truth/falsity dimension and appear in parallel constructional slots (e.g., simartle gamarjvebs vs. siq̇albe gamoašk'aravda).
**Contrast type:** lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 2

**Term A:** სიმართლე / simartle
**Term B:** სიცრუე / sicrue
**Transliteration:** simartle / sicrue
**English translation:** "truth" / "lie, deliberate untruth"
**Description of contrast:** P1 item #2 lists mtkuaroba and t'yupiloba as contrasts for č̣ešmariteba; P1 item #12 describes sicrue as referring specifically to a deliberate lie, contrasting with truth-asserting expressions. The paired formula sicrue da mot'q̇ueba (P2b) and the construction sicrues ambobs ("is telling a lie") stand in direct opposition to simartles vambob ("I am telling the truth") documented in P2b.
**Contrast type:** lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 4

**Term A:** ნამდვილი / namdvili
**Term B:** ყალბი / q̇albi
**Transliteration:** namdvili / q̇albi
**English translation:** "real, genuine, authentic" / "false, fake, counterfeit, forged"
**Description of contrast:** P3 Group 5 explicitly identifies q̇albi and p'ik't'iuri as the opposites of namdvili and avt'enturi. The canonical example namdvili oqro ("real gold") stands in direct contrast to q̇albi oqro ("fake gold"), both documented in P1b and P2b. P1 item #5 describes namdvili as asserting genuine character "as opposed to a copy, imitation, or pretense," which is precisely the domain of q̇albi.
**Contrast type:** lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 5

**Description of contrast:** Both phrases are explicitly documented (namdvili oqro in P2b as the canonical example of namdvili; q̇albi oqro in P1b item 14 of the q̇alb- expansion) and represent the same head noun modified by opposing authenticity predicates.
**Contrast type:** constructional
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 6

P1 item #14 explicitly gives sarcmuno mc̣me as the standard legal phrase for a credible witness; P1b q̇alb- item 10 gives q̇albi mc̣me as its legal opposite.

**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 7

**Term A:** სარწმუნო მოწმე / sarcmuno mc̣me
**Term B:** მტყუარი მოწმე / mt'q̇uari mc̣me
**Transliteration:** sarcmuno mc̣me / mt'q̇uari mc̣me
**English translation:** "credible witness" / "lying witness, perjurer"
**Description of contrast:** Both phrases are documented (sarcmuno mc̣me in P2b; mt'q̇uari mc̣me in P2b). P3 Group 3 notes that mt'q̇uari mc̣me "emphasizes the personal character of the witness as a liar" while q̇albi mc̣me emphasizes "the falsity of the testimony as a product," making this a distinct opposition from Opposition 6 above.
**Contrast type:** constructional
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 8

**Term A:** სიმართლეს ვამბობ / simartles vambob
**Term B:** სიცრუეს ამბობს / sicrues ambobs
**Transliteration:** simartles vambob / sicrues ambobs
**English translation:** "I am telling the truth" / "he/she is telling a lie"
**Description of contrast:** Both constructions are documented in P2b and share the same verb ambob-/ambobs with the same case frame (dative nominal + verb), differing only in the nominal (simartle vs. sicrue) and person of the verb. They represent the positive and negative poles of the truth-assertion construction.
**Contrast type:** constructional
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 9

**Term A:** დადასტურებული / dadasturebuli
**Term B:** დაუდასტურებელი / daudasturebeli
**Transliteration:** dadasturebuli / daudasturebeli
**English translation:** "confirmed, verified, attested" / "unconfirmed, unverified, unattested"
**Description of contrast:** Both forms are documented in P2a as derivations of dadastureba. daudasturebeli is formed by insertion of the privative **u-** between the preverb **da-** and the participial stem, producing the negated resultative participle. P2a notes that daudasturebeli "appears in journalistic contexts when reporting on unverified information," directly contrasting with dadasturebuli.
**Contrast type:** morphological
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 17

**Term A:** სიმართლე გამარჯვებს / simartle gamarjvebs
**Term B:** სიმართლე მწარეა / simartle mc̣area
**Transliteration:** simartle gamarjvebs / simartle mc̣area
**English translation:** "truth prevails, truth wins out" / "truth is bitter, the truth hurts"
**Description of contrast:** Both expressions are documented in P1b (mart- root expansion, items 27 and 28) and share the same subject (simartle) with predicates that represent contrasting evaluative stances toward truth — one asserting its ultimate triumph, the other acknowledging its painful character. These are not logical opposites but represent a documented constructional contrast between two proverbial framings of the same subject.
**Contrast type:** constructional

## Opposition 18

**Term A:** ნამდვილი / namdvili
**Term B:** მოჩვენებითი / močvenebiti
**Transliteration:** namdvili / močvenebiti
**English translation:** "real, genuine, actual" / "apparent, seeming, ostensible, illusory"
**Description of contrast:** P3 Group 9 distinguishes močvenebiti from namdvili by noting that močvenebiti characterizes "something that merely appears to be what it is claimed to be, without actually being so," while namdvili asserts genuine character. P3 also distinguishes močvenebiti from q̇albi by noting that močvenebiti "does not necessarily imply deliberate human deception or legal violation," making this a distinct opposition from Opposition 4.
**Contrast type:** lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 19

**Term A:** ყალბი / q̇albi
**Term B:** მოჩვენებითი / močvenebiti
**Transliteration:** q̇albi / močvenebiti
**English translation:** "false, fake, counterfeit, forged" / "apparent, seeming, ostensible, illusory"
**Description of contrast:** P3 Group 9 explicitly distinguishes these two: q̇albi implies deliberate human deception or legal violation (forgery, fraud), while močvenebiti characterizes a surface appearance that does not correspond to underlying reality without necessarily implying deliberate deception. They are not synonyms and are not interchangeable in their documented collocations.
**Contrast type:** lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 20

**Term A:** არსი / arsi ("essence, substance")
**Term B:** ფორმა / p'orma ("form")
**Transliteration:** arsi / p'orma
**English translation:** "essence, substance, core" / "form, surface presentation"
**Description of contrast:** The paired formula arsi da p'orma ("substance and form") is documented in P2b as a fixed collocation. P3 Group 10 describes arsi-based expressions as used "to distinguish what something fundamentally is from how it is formally presented or labeled," with p'orma representing the surface or formal dimension that arsi is contrasted against.
**Contrast type:** lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 21

**Term A:** სიმართლე / simartle (colloquial register)
**Term B:** ჭეშმარიტება / č̣ešmariteba (formal/philosophical register)
**Transliteration:** simartle / č̣ešmariteba
**English translation:** "truth" (everyday) / "truth, truthfulness" (philosophical/theological)
**Description of contrast:** P1 item #4 describes simartle as "often preferred in colloquial speech," while P1 item #2 describes č̣ešmariteba as used in "philosophical, religious, and ordinary discourse" with the philosophical and religious uses being the more distinctive. P3 Group 7 documents this register distinction explicitly, noting that simartle appears in everyday constructions (simartle tqvi, simartle gitxra) while č̣ešmariteba appears in philosophical and theological constructions (č̣ešmaritebis dzieba, č̣ešmariteba ertia, č̣ešmariteba maradiulia). Both translate as "truth" but their collocational distributions are distinct.
**Contrast type:** constructional
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 22

**Term A:** ყალბი / q̇albi
**Term B:** ფიქტიური / p'ik't'iuri
**Transliteration:** q̇albi / p'ik't'iuri
**English translation:** "false, fake, counterfeit, forged" / "fictitious, fictional, fabricated, sham"
**Description of contrast:** P3 Group 4 explicitly distinguishes these two: q̇albi is the native root applying broadly to physical forgery (documents, currency, signatures) and to persons and statements, while p'ik't'iuri is a borrowing with more restricted use in commercial and administrative law (transactions, companies, marriages, employment, addresses). P3 states they "are not interchangeable: p'ik't'iuri garigeba is the standard phrase for a sham transaction, while q̇albi dok'ument'i is the standard phrase for a forged document."
**Contrast type:** lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 23

**Term A:** სარწმუნო / sarcmuno
**Term B:** სანდო / sando
**Transliteration:** sarcmuno / sando
**English translation:** "credible, reliable, trustworthy" (formal/legal) / "trustworthy, reliable, dependable" (colloquial)
**Description of contrast:** P1 item #15 describes sando as "somewhat more colloquial than sarcmuno" and as tending "to emphasize practical reliability over epistemic credibility." P3 Group 6 notes that "sarcmuno and sando in source-evaluation contexts are more colloquial and journalistic than their appearances in legal proceedings." The two are near-synonyms differentiated by register and by the dimension of reliability they foreground (epistemic credibility for sarcmuno, practical dependability for sando).
**Contrast type:** lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 24

**Term A:** ნამდვილი / namdvili
**Term B:** ავთენტური / avt'enturi
**Transliteration:** namdvili / avt'enturi
**English translation:** "real, genuine, authentic, actual" (everyday) / "authentic, genuine" (formal/legal/archival)
**Description of contrast:** P1 item #24 states that avt'enturi "overlaps with namdvili but is more formal and domain-specific." P3 Group 5 documents that namdvili "is the everyday and colloquial form, appearing freely in spoken Georgian across all domains" while avt'enturi "is a formal borrowing with restricted use in legal, archival, and cultural contexts." The adverb namdvilad functions as a high-frequency spoken intensifier in ways that avt'enturad does not.
**Contrast type:** lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 25

**Term A:** მტკიცება / mt'k'iceba ("act of asserting/proving")
**Term B:** მტკიცებულება / mt'k'icebuleba ("evidence, proof as a thing")
**Transliteration:** mt'k'iceba / mt'k'icebuleba
**English translation:** "assertion, act of proving, claim" / "proof, evidence, demonstration"
**Description of contrast:** P1 item #10 explicitly states that mt'k'icebuleba is "closely related to but distinct from mt'k'iceba (the act of asserting/proving)." P2a documents the derivational relationship and notes that mt'k'iceba refers to the act while mt'k'icebuleba refers to "that which has been established/proven." P3 Group 2 preserves this distinction: "mt'k'icebuleba refers to the material of proof (evidence as a thing), while mt'k'iceba refers to the act of asserting or proving."
**Contrast type:** lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 26

**Term A:** si-...-e/le nominalization pattern (e.g., სიმართლე / simartle)
**Term B:** -eba/-oba nominalization pattern (e.g., ჭეშმარიტება / č̣ešmariteba)
**Transliteration:** simartle / č̣ešmariteba (as representative instances)
**English translation:** "truth" (colloquial nominal) / "truth, truthfulness" (formal nominal)
**Description of contrast:** P4a Construction 6 documents both patterns and explicitly notes that "si-...-e/le nominals tend toward colloquial and everyday use, while -eba nominals tend toward formal, philosophical, or legal registers." P4b preserves this contrast in the construction usage table, rating both [CORE] but noting the register differentiation.
**Contrast type:** constructional
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 27

**Term A:** მტყუარი / mt'q̇uari (root + -ari agentive)
**Term B:** გამაყალბებელი / gamaq̇albebeli (ga- + ma-...-ebeli agentive)
**Transliteration:** mt'q̇uari / gamaq̇albebeli
**English translation:** "liar, untruthful person" / "forger, falsifier, counterfeiter"
**Description of contrast:** P4a Construction 7 documents both agentive nominalization patterns and notes their distinct domains: mt'q̇uari is derived directly from the lying root and applies to persons who make false verbal statements, while gamaq̇albebeli is derived from the verbal stem via the participial agentive pattern and applies to persons who commit physical forgery or document falsification. P4b notes that gamaq̇albebeli is "more formal and domain-specific than mt'q̇uari."
**Contrast type:** constructional
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 28

**Term A:** გაყალბება / gaq̇albeba ("act of falsifying")
**Term B:** გაყალბებული / gaq̇albebuli ("that which has been falsified")
**Transliteration:** gaq̇albeba / gaq̇albebuli
**English translation:** "falsification, forgery, counterfeiting" (verbal noun) / "falsified, forged, counterfeited" (resultative participial adjective)
**Description of contrast:** Both forms are documented in P2a as derivations from the q̇alb- root. gaq̇albeba is the verbal noun encoding the act; gaq̇albebuli is the resultative participial adjective encoding the resulting state of the object. P4a Construction 9 documents the resultative participial pattern and notes it "encodes the completed state resulting from an action," contrasting with the verbal noun which encodes the action itself.
**Contrast type:** morphological
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 29

**Term B:** ყალბი გრძნობები / q̇albi grdznobebi

## Opposition 30

**Term A:** სიყალბეში დაიჭირეს / siq̇albeshi daič̣ires
**Term B:** სიცრუეში დაიჭირეს / sicrueši daič̣ires
**Transliteration:** siq̇albeshi daič̣ires / sicrueši daič̣ires
**English translation:** "was caught in a lie/fraud" (with siq̇albe) / "was caught in a lie" (with sicrue)
**Description of contrast:** Both constructions are documented in P2b and share the same locative case frame and verb (daič̣ires, "was caught"), differing only in the nominal. P2b notes that the siq̇albe construction applies to fraudulent or dishonest acts broadly (including document fraud), while the sicrue construction "applies specifically to verbal deception rather than document fraud." The two constructions are parallel in form but distinct in the domain of deception they cover.
**Contrast type:** constructional
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]
