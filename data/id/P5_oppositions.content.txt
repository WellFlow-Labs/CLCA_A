## OPPOSITION 1

**Term A:** benar  
**Term B:** salah  
**Transliteration:** benar / salah  
**English translation:** true, correct / wrong, mistaken  
**Description of contrast:** Primary lexical opposition for factual correctness evaluation. *Benar* marks correspondence to fact or proper standard; *salah* marks deviation from fact or error. This is the most fundamental and productive opposition in the truth domain, appearing across all registers and contexts.  
**Contrast type:** lexical  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]
