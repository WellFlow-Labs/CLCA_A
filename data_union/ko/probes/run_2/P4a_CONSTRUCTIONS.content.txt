# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P4a CONSTRUCTIONS
# Language: Korean (ko)

---

**Transliteration standard:** Revised Romanization of Korean (국립국어원 표준 로마자 표기법)

---

## 1. ATTRIBUTION

### Pattern
Noun + 이다 / Noun + (이)다 — predicative copula construction asserting that the subject belongs to or instantiates a truth-domain category.

### Examples
- 그것은 사실이다 / geugeoseun sasirida / "That is a fact."
- 이것은 거짓말이다 / igeoseun geojitmarida / "This is a lie."

### Notes
The copula 이다 attaches directly to the nominal base. Negation is formed with 이/가 아니다 (e.g., 사실이 아니다, "it is not a fact"), which is the standard construction for denying factual status. No verb inflection for tense or agreement beyond the copula is required.

---

## 2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION

### Pattern
Noun + 이/가 아니다 — copular negation asserting that the subject does not instantiate a truth-domain category.

### Examples
- 그 주장은 사실이 아니다 / geu jujangeun sasiri anida / "That claim is not a fact."
- 이건 진짜가 아니다 / igeon jinjjaga anida / "This is not genuine."

### Notes
이/가 아니다 is the standard negation of 이다 and is the primary construction for denying factual or authenticity status in Korean. It is more neutral in register than 거짓이다 ("it is a falsehood") and is preferred in formal corrections and public statements.

---

## 3. ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION

### Pattern
Adjective stem + -(으)ㄴ + Noun — attributive modification of a noun by a truth-domain adjective.

### Examples
- 정확한 정보 / jeonghwakan jeongbo / "accurate information"
- 올바른 판단 / olbareun pandan / "correct judgment"

### Notes
Korean truth-domain adjectives (형용사) form their attributive (adnominal) form with the suffix -(으)ㄴ attached to the stem. For 하다-adjectives (e.g., 정확하다, 정직하다), the stem is the Sino-Korean base plus 한 (e.g., 정확한, 정직한). Native adjectives (e.g., 올바르다, 옳다, 틀리다) follow standard irregular or regular conjugation patterns (올바른, 옳은, 틀린).

---

## 4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE

### Pattern
Noun + (으)로 밝혀지다 / 드러나다 / 판명되다 — inchoative construction expressing that a truth-domain status comes to be established or revealed.

### Examples
- 그 주장이 허위로 밝혀졌다 / geu jujangi heowiro balkyeojyeossda / "That claim was revealed to be false."
- 가짜로 판명되었다 / gajjaro panmyeongdoeeossda / "It was determined to be fake."

### Notes
Korean does not have a single inchoative morpheme equivalent to "become + adjective." In the truth domain, the inchoative meaning is characteristically expressed through the passive/spontaneous verbs 밝혀지다 ("to be revealed/come to light"), 드러나다 ("to be exposed"), and 판명되다 ("to be determined"), combined with the nominal + (으)로 ("as") construction. The pattern Noun + (이)라는 것이 밝혀지다 is an alternative with an embedded clause.

---

## 5. CAUSATIVE

### Pattern
Noun + 하다 (transitive) — the 하다 verb with a truth-domain nominal base functions as the primary causative-like construction, expressing the deliberate performance of a truth-domain act.

### Examples
- 거짓말하다 / geojitmal-hada / "to lie" (lit. "to do lying")
- 위조하다 / wijo-hada / "to forge / to counterfeit" (lit. "to do forgery")

### Notes
Korean does not have a productive morphological causative suffix that applies systematically to truth-domain adjectives or nouns. The closest functional equivalent is the 하다 verb construction, which converts a nominal base into a transitive verb expressing deliberate action. For stative adjectives (e.g., 정확하다, 진실하다), no causative form is identified in the data; causation is expressed periphrastically (e.g., 정확하게 만들다, "to make accurate"). This pattern is therefore limited to nominal bases that denote acts rather than states.

## 6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION

### Pattern
(a) Noun/Adjective stem + 성 (性) — nominalizing suffix yielding an abstract quality noun.
(b) Adjective stem + -(으)ㅁ — verbal/adjectival nominalizing suffix.

### Examples
**Pattern (a):**
- 진실성 / jinsilseong / "truthfulness / veracity"
- 신뢰성 / sinloeseong / "reliability / trustworthiness"

**Pattern (b):**
- 올바름 / olbareum / "rightness / correctness"
- 옳음 / oreum / "rightness / correctness" (more formal/philosophical)

### Notes
The suffix 성 (性) is the dominant abstract nominalizer in the truth domain and attaches productively to Sino-Korean bases (진실성, 정확성, 타당성, 공정성, 신빙성, 진정성, 정직성). The native Korean nominalizer -(으)ㅁ applies to native adjective stems (올바름, 옳음) and yields forms that appear primarily in philosophical and formal written registers. The two patterns are not interchangeable: 성 attaches to nominal/Sino-Korean bases; -(으)ㅁ attaches to verbal/adjectival stems.

---

## 7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION

### Pattern
Noun + 쟁이 — colloquial agentive suffix denoting a person habitually characterized by the named quality or act.

### Examples
- 거짓말쟁이 / geojitmaljaengi / "liar / habitual liar"
- (no second productive example identified in the truth domain)

### Notes
The suffix 쟁이 is the only productive agentive nominalizer identified in the truth domain in the P1–P3 data, and it attaches only to 거짓말 in common usage. No parallel agentive form for "truth-teller" (e.g., *진실쟁이) is attested as a common expression. The suffix 범 (犯, criminal/offender) yields 위조범 ("forger"), but this is a legal category label rather than a general agentive pattern. The asymmetry — a common agentive for liars but not for truth-tellers — is preserved as found.

## 8. EXISTENTIAL PREDICATION OF ABSTRACT QUALITY

### Pattern
Abstract quality noun + 이/가 있다 / 없다 — existential construction asserting the presence or absence of a truth-domain quality.

### Examples
- 진정성이 있다 / jinjeongseong-i itda / "to have sincerity / to be authentic"
- 신빙성이 없다 / sinbyeongseong-i eopda / "to lack credibility / to be implausible"

### Notes
This construction is the primary predication strategy for abstract quality nouns formed with 성 (性) in the truth domain. Because these nominalizations (진정성, 신뢰성, 신빙성, 타당성) do not take 하다 as a predicative suffix in their nominalized form, the existential predicates 있다/없다 serve as the standard affirmative and negative predicators. This pattern is highly productive across the truth domain and has no direct structural parallel in English.

---

## 9. SCALAR PREDICATION OF ABSTRACT QUALITY

### Pattern
Abstract quality noun + 이/가 높다 / 낮다 — scalar predication expressing degree of a truth-domain quality.

### Examples
- 신뢰성이 높다 / sinloeseong-i nopda / "credibility is high"
- 정확도가 낮다 / jeonghwakdoga natda / "accuracy is low"

### Notes
Truth-domain quality nouns, particularly those formed with 성 (性) and 도 (度), combine productively with the scalar adjectives 높다 ("high") and 낮다 ("low") to express degree of a quality. The 도 (度) suffix (정확도, 신뢰도) is specifically associated with quantifiable degree and appears more frequently in this scalar construction than 성 (性) forms. This construction is characteristic of journalistic, legal, and scientific evaluation contexts.

## 10. ADVERBIAL MANNER MODIFICATION

### Pattern
(a) Adjective stem + 히 — adverbializing suffix (applies to select Sino-Korean adjective bases).
(b) Adjective stem + 하게 — adverbializing suffix via 하다 form (applies to 하다-adjectives).
(c) Noun + (으)로 — instrumental/manner particle yielding adverbial expression.

### Examples
**Pattern (a):**
- 정확히 말하다 / jeonghwaki malhada / "to speak exactly / to state precisely"

**Pattern (b):**
- 정직하게 말하다 / jeongjik-hage malhada / "to speak honestly"

**Pattern (c):**
- 진정으로 사과하다 / jinjeong-euro sagwa-hada / "to apologize sincerely"
- 진심으로 감사하다 / jinsimeuro gamsahada / "to thank sincerely / from the heart"

### Notes
Three distinct adverbial formation strategies are attested in the truth domain. The 히 suffix applies to a restricted set of Sino-Korean bases (정확히 is the primary truth-domain example). The 하게 suffix applies productively to all 하다-adjectives. The (으)로 construction applies to nominal bases (진정으로, 진심으로, 허위로, 가짜로) and is particularly common with nouns that do not form 하다-adjectives. All three patterns are productive and non-interchangeable.

## 11. DISCOURSE-FRAMING CONSTRUCTION

### Pattern
Noun/Adverb + (을/를) 말하자면 / 말하다면 — conditional framing construction introducing a frank, precise, or corrective statement.

### Examples
- 정확히 말하자면 / jeonghwaki malhajamyeon / "to be precise / strictly speaking"
- 사실을 말하자면 / sasireul malhajamyeon / "to tell the truth / the fact of the matter is"

### Notes
This construction functions as a pragmatic marker signaling that what follows is the speaker's more precise or frank assessment, often correcting or refining a prior statement. It is not a morphological derivation but a syntactic pattern that is highly conventionalized in the truth domain. The construction combines a truth-domain adverb or noun with the conditional form of 말하다 ("to speak/say") and is common across spoken and written registers.

---

## 12. POLAR OPPOSITION FRAMING

### Pattern
Noun₁ + 과/와 + Noun₂ — coordinated nominal pair expressing the binary opposition of truth-domain poles.

### Examples
- 진실과 거짓 / jinsil-gwa geojit / "truth and falsehood"
- 옳고 그름 / olko geureum / "right and wrong"

### Notes
Coordinated polar pairs are a structurally distinct and highly conventionalized pattern in the Korean truth domain. They function as compound noun phrases or as framing constructions for moral and epistemic evaluation. The pair 진짜인지 가짜인지 ("whether real or fake") extends this pattern into an alternative-question construction used to express authenticity doubt. These pairs are not productive in the sense of generating new combinations freely; the attested pairs are fixed collocations.
