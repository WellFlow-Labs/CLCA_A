## Summary Notes

**Constructions rated [CORE]:** 1, 2, 3, 4 (合法化 only), 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12

**Constructions rated [MARGINAL]:** 4 (客观化), 5 (causative — no truth-domain-specific form attested)

**Constructions rated [UNCERTAIN]:** None identified in the attested data.

**Cross-construction observations (data-supported only):**
- The -性 and -度 suffixes (Construction 6) are not interchangeable; their distribution is determined by whether the property is treated as categorical (-性) or scalar and measurable (-度).
- The agentive -者 construction (Construction 7) shows an asymmetry: deception-role agentives are attested; truth-telling-role agentives are not attested in common usage.
- The Verification Result Construction (Construction 11) and the Correspondence Frame (Construction 10) are both register-restricted to formal institutional discourse but are syntactically distinct and non-interchangeable.
