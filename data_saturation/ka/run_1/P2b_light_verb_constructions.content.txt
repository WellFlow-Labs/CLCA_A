# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2b Compositional Constructions
# Language: Georgian (ka)

---

**Methodological note:** This step documents attested multi-word constructions involving P1 base forms. "Multi-word construction" is understood here as a syntactically-driven or periphrastic unit — including verb phrases, predicative constructions, postpositional phrases, and idiomatic collocations — that is not reducible to a single derived form and is not already fully documented as a derivational product in P2a. Genitive nominal compounds already documented in P1b are cross-referenced rather than re-described in full. Constructions are listed per P1 base form. Absence is recorded explicitly. Epistemic status reflects confidence in attestation and naturalness.

---

## 1. Base Form: ჭეშმარიტი / č̣ešmariti

---

**Construction 1**

1. **Base Form:** ჭეშმარიტი / č̣ešmariti
2. **Example:** ეს ჭეშმარიტი ფაქტია
3. **Transliteration:** es č̣ešmariti paqtia
4. **English translation:** This is a true fact; this is a genuine fact
5. **Usage notes:** Predicative construction using the copula -a, asserting that a given fact or state of affairs is genuinely true. Used in formal and journalistic discourse to emphasize the factual status of a claim.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 2**

1. **Base Form:** ჭეშმარიტი / č̣ešmariti
2. **Example:** ჭეშმარიტი მნიშვნელობით
3. **Transliteration:** č̣ešmariti mnišvnelobit
4. **English translation:** in the true sense; in the genuine meaning of the word
5. **Usage notes:** Postpositional phrase using the instrumental -it, used in philosophical and analytical discourse to assert that a term or concept is being used in its proper or genuine sense rather than loosely or metaphorically.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 3**

1. **Base Form:** ჭეშმარიტი / č̣ešmariti
2. **Example:** ჭეშმარიტი სიტყვით რომ ვთქვათ
3. **Transliteration:** č̣ešmariti sitq̇vit rom vtqvat
4. **English translation:** to tell the truth; to speak truly; if we are to speak truthfully
5. **Usage notes:** A periphrastic construction used in formal and literary discourse to introduce a candid or frank statement. Functions as a framing device signaling that what follows is an honest assessment.
6. **Epistemic status:** [UNCERTAIN]

## 2. Base Form: ჭეშმარიტება / č̣ešmariteba

---

**Construction 1**

1. **Base Form:** ჭეშმარიტება / č̣ešmariteba
2. **Example:** ჭეშმარიტება ითქვა
3. **Transliteration:** č̣ešmariteba itqva
4. **English translation:** the truth was spoken; truth was told
5. **Usage notes:** Passive construction using the passive aorist of tkva ("to say"). Already documented in P1b (Root 3, item 10); cross-referenced here as a predicative construction. Used in formal, literary, and religious contexts.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 2**

1. **Base Form:** ჭეშმარიტება / č̣ešmariteba
2. **Example:** ჭეშმარიტება გამარჯვდება
3. **Transliteration:** č̣ešmariteba gamarjvdeba
4. **English translation:** truth will prevail; truth will triumph
5. **Usage notes:** Predicative construction using the future form of gamarjveba ("to triumph, to prevail"). Used in moral, political, and literary discourse as an assertion of the ultimate victory of truth. Common in rhetorical and literary registers.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 3**

1. **Base Form:** ჭეშმარიტება / č̣ešmariteba
2. **Example:** ჭეშმარიტება მწარეა
3. **Transliteration:** č̣ešmariteba mc̣area
4. **English translation:** the truth is bitter; truth is hard to hear
5. **Usage notes:** A predicative construction expressing the proverbial idea that truth is often unpleasant. Used in everyday and literary discourse when acknowledging a difficult or unwelcome fact. Functions as a near-proverbial expression.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 4**

1. **Base Form:** ჭეშმარიტება / č̣ešmariteba
2. **Example:** ჭეშმარიტება ნახევარი სიცრუეა
3. **Transliteration:** č̣ešmariteba naxevari sicrueaˈ
4. **English translation:** a half-truth is a lie; a partial truth is falsehood
5. **Usage notes:** A proverbial or near-proverbial construction asserting that incomplete truth functions as deception. Used in moral and rhetorical discourse. Attested in journalistic and literary writing.
6. **Epistemic status:** [UNCERTAIN]

---

**Construction 5**

1. **Base Form:** ჭეშმარიტება / č̣ešmariteba
2. **Example:** ჭეშმარიტება ეძებება
3. **Transliteration:** č̣ešmariteba edzebeba

## 3. Base Form: სიმართლე / simartle

---

**Construction 1**

1. **Base Form:** სიმართლე / simartle
2. **Example:** სიმართლე თქვა
3. **Transliteration:** simarṭle tqva
4. **English translation:** told the truth; said the truth
5. **Usage notes:** The most frequent verb-object construction with simarṭle, using the aorist of tkva ("to say/tell"). Already noted in P1 as highly frequent in everyday speech. Used across all registers and age groups.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 2**

1. **Base Form:** სიმართლე / simartle
2. **Example:** სიმართლე გამოვიდა
3. **Transliteration:** simarṭle gamovida
4. **English translation:** the truth came out; the truth emerged
5. **Usage notes:** Intransitive construction using the preverb ga- + movida ("came out"), expressing the emergence or revelation of truth. Used in everyday, journalistic, and literary discourse when concealed truth becomes known.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 3**

1. **Base Form:** სიმართლე / simartle
2. **Example:** სიმართლეს ვამბობ
3. **Transliteration:** simarṭles vambob
4. **English translation:** I am telling the truth; I speak the truth
5. **Usage notes:** First-person present construction using ambobs ("says, tells"), with simarṭle in the dative case as the object. Used in everyday speech to assert one's own truthfulness, often in response to doubt or challenge.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 4**

1. **Base Form:** სიმართლე / simartle
2. **Example:** სიმართლე მოგახსენო
3. **Transliteration:** simarṭle mogaxseno
4. **English translation:** let me tell you the truth; to tell you the truth (polite)
5. **Usage notes:** A polite or formal periphrastic construction using the optative of axseneba ("to inform, to tell"), used to introduce a frank or candid statement. More formal and deferential in register than simarṭles vambob.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 5**

1. **Base Form:** სიმართლე / simartle
2. **Example:** სიმართლე გითხრა
3. **Transliteration:** simarṭle gitxra
4. **English translation:** let me tell you the truth; to tell you the truth (informal)
5. **Usage notes:** Informal periphrastic construction using the optative/subjunctive of tkva ("to tell"), used to introduce a candid statement. Equivalent in function to simarṭle mogaxseno but in a less formal register. Very common in everyday speech.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 6**

1. **Base Form:** სიმართლე / simartle
2. **Example:** სიმართლე ისაა, რომ
3. **Transliteration:** simarṭle isaa, rom
4. **English translation:** the truth is that; the fact is that
5. **Usage notes:** A framing construction using the copula and the subordinating conjunction rom, used to introduce a frank or factual statement. Functions as a discourse-level truth-asserting frame. Common in spoken and written Georgian across registers.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 7**

1. **Base Form:** სიმართლე / simartle
2. **Example:** სიმართლეს თუ ვიტყვი
3. **Transliteration:** simarṭles tu vitq̇vi
4. **English translation:** if I am to tell the truth; to be honest; honestly speaking
5. **Usage notes:** A conditional periphrastic construction used to introduce a candid or frank statement, often one that the speaker acknowledges may be unwelcome. Functions as a hedging or framing device in everyday and formal speech.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 8**

1. **Base Form:** სიმართლე / simartle
2. **Example:** სიმართლე ნახევარია
3. **Transliteration:** simarṭle naxevaria
4. **English translation:** it is only a half-truth; it is partially true
5. **Usage notes:** Predicative construction asserting that a claim is only partially true. Used in analytical and journalistic discourse to qualify or challenge the completeness of a statement.
6. **Epistemic status:** [UNCERTAIN]

---

**Construction 9**

1. **Base Form:** სიმართლე / simartle
2. **Example:** სიმართლე ეთქვა
3. **Transliteration:** simarṭle etqva
4. **English translation:** the truth was told to him/her; he/she was told the truth
5. **Usage notes:** Passive/indirect construction using the indirect version of tkva, indicating that truth was communicated to a specific recipient. Used in narrative and legal discourse.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]
