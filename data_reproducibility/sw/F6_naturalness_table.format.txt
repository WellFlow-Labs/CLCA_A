| 1.1 | Habari hiyo imekuwa kweli | habari hiyo i-me-kuwa kweli | [CORE] | Common construction for situations revealed to be true over time. Uses stative verb kuwa (to be/become) with perfect aspect marker -me-. Frequent in narrative contexts describing verification of claims or predictions coming true. |
| 1.2 | Jibu lake limekuwa sahihi | jibu lake li-me-kuwa sahihi | [CORE] | Used when something initially uncertain is confirmed as correct. Common in educational and verification contexts. Less frequent than kuwa kweli but well-attested. |

| 1.5 | Ukweli umekuwa dhahiri | ukweli u-me-kuwa dhahiri | [CORE] | Common construction for truth becoming manifest or obvious. Frequent in revelatory contexts and arguments. |
| 1.6a | Taarifa imekuwa kamili | taarifa i-me-kuwa kamili | [CORE] | Pattern (a) kuwa + adjective. Used when information or processes reach completion. |

| 3.1 | Ninahakikisha ukweli wa habari | ni-na-hakikisha ukweli wa habari | [CORE] | Causative derivation from hakika (certain). Extremely common in instructions, procedures, and verification contexts. Can take clausal complement: hakikisha kwamba... (ensure that...). |
| 3.2 | Wathibitisha ukweli wa taarifa | wa-thibitisha ukweli wa taarifa | [CORE] | Causative derivation meaning "to cause to be established/firm." Very common in formal, administrative, and legal contexts for verification and confirmation. |

| 3.4 | Anadhihirisha ukweli | a-na-dhihirisha ukweli | [CORE] | Causative derivation from dhahiri (evident). Common in contexts of demonstration, revelation, and making things clear. Frequent in formal and explanatory discourse. |
| 3.5 | Ananyosha njia | a-na-nyosha njia | [CORE] | Causative/extended forms from -nyofu (straight). Used for both physical straightening and moral/procedural correction. Nyosha more common than nyofua. |
