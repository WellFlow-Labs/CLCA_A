# Language: Yoruba (yo)

## Construction Usage & Constraints Table

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness Rating | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. ATTRIBUTION** — Stative predicate | *ó tọ́* | ó tọ́ | "It is correct / it is right" | [CORE] | Highly frequent in everyday evaluative speech and proverbs; *tọ́* as direct stative predicate requires no copula and is unrestricted across registers. |
| **1. ATTRIBUTION** — Copular nominal | *ọ̀rọ̀ rẹ jẹ́ òtítọ́* | ọ̀rọ̀ rẹ jẹ́ òtítọ́ | "Your statement is the truth" | [CORE] | Standard pattern when the predicate is a full nominal; *jẹ́* + nominal is the expected form in formal, testimonial, and religious discourse. |
| **2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION** — Negated stative | *kò tọ́* | kò tọ́ | "It is not correct / it is wrong" | [CORE] | Fully parallel to the affirmative; *kò* + stative is the standard negation pattern with no register restriction. |
| **2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION** — Negated copular nominal | *kò jẹ́ gidi* | kò jẹ́ gidi | "It is not genuine / it is not authentic" | [CORE] | Mirrors the affirmative copular pattern exactly; common in evaluative and disputational contexts. |
| **3. ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION** — Post-nominal modifier | *ìdáhùn tọ́* | ìdáhùn tọ́ | "Correct answer" | [CORE] | Post-nominal modifier position is the standard and only attested position for adjectival modifiers in Yoruba; productive across the full truth-domain vocabulary. |
| **3. ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION** — Post-nominal modifier | *ìwà rere* | ìwà rere | "Good character / virtue" | [CORE] | Highly lexicalized collocation; *ìwà rere* functions as a near-fixed expression in moral and proverbial discourse. |
| **4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE** — Motion-directional | *padà sí ìgbàgbọ́* | padà sí ìgbàgbọ́ | "Return to faith / come back to belief" | [CORE] | Motion-based inchoative is the more frequent pattern in the truth domain; common in religious and moral exhortation. |
| **4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE** — Motion-directional | *jáde kúrò nínú àìmọ̀* | jáde kúrò nínú àìmọ̀ | "Come out of ignorance / leave ignorance behind" | [CORE] | Attested in moral and didactic registers; the *jáde kúrò nínú* pattern encodes departure from a negative state and is productive with abstract nouns. |
| **5. CAUSATIVE** — Permissive-causative *jẹ́ kí* | *jẹ́ kí òtítọ́ jẹ́ olùdarí rẹ* | jẹ́ kí òtítọ́ jẹ́ olùdarí rẹ | "Let truth be your guide / cause truth to lead you" | [CORE] | Common in hortatory, religious, and proverbial speech; *jẹ́ kí* is the standard periphrastic causative-permissive and is not restricted to the truth domain. |
| **5. CAUSATIVE** — Instrumental serial verb *fi* | *fi òtítọ́ sọ̀rọ̀* | fi òtítọ́ sọ̀rọ̀ | "Speak with truth / speak truthfully" | [CORE] | The *fi* instrumental construction encodes manner rather than strict causation; productive in ethical and instructional contexts, though the P4a notes distinguish it from canonical causation. |
| **6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION** — *ì-* prefix | *ìmọ̀* | ìmọ̀ | "Knowledge" | [CORE] | The *ì-* prefix is the most productive nominalizer in the truth domain; the resulting nouns are fully integrated into the lexicon and unrestricted in distribution. |
| **6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION** — *à-* prefix | *àtúntọ́* | àtúntọ́ | "Correction / rectification" | [CORE] | Attested and productive; prefix choice is lexically conditioned, not semantically contrastive with *ì-* or *o-/ọ-* forms. |
| **7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION** — *a-* prefix (habitual/moral) | *aṣírọ́* | aṣírọ́ | "Liar / one who tells lies" | [CORE] | The *a-* prefix is the standard form for morally evaluative agent nouns in this domain; *aṣírọ́*, *aṣìṣe*, *aṣètànjẹ́* are all attested. |
| **7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION** — *a-* prefix (habitual/moral) | *adájọ́* | adájọ́ | "Judge / one who adjudicates" | [CORE] | Attested as a lexicalized form; functions as a role noun despite the *a-* prefix, suggesting the boundary between habitual-agent and role-agent readings is not always sharp. |
| **7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION** — *olù-* prefix (role/function) | *olùdánwò* | olùdánwò | "Examiner" | [CORE] | *olù-* is the standard prefix for functional role agents; common in institutional and formal registers. No examples of *olù-* with morally negative valence are attested in P4a. |
| **7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION** — *ẹ-/al-* prefix (possessive agent) | *ẹlẹ́rìí* | ẹlẹ́rìí | "Witness" | [CORE] | Attested as a lexicalized form; *ẹlẹ́rìí* is common in legal and testimonial discourse. The *ẹ-/al-* prefix class is smaller than *a-* or *olù-* in the attested data. |
| **8. FOCUS / EMPHATIC IDENTIFICATION** — Predicate focus *ni* | *òtítọ́ ni* | òtítọ́ ni | "It is the truth / that is true" | [CORE] | Highly productive in truth-domain assertions and proverbs; *ni* following the focused predicate is the standard identificational focus construction. |
| **8. FOCUS / EMPHATIC IDENTIFICATION** — Subject focus *ló* | *ìmọ̀ ló ń gbani là* | ìmọ̀ ló ń gbani là | "It is knowledge that saves a person" | [CORE] | Common in proverbs and emphatic assertions; *ló* marks the focused NP as contrastive or uniquely identified. This construction is syntactic, not derivational. |
| **9. PROPOSITIONAL COMPLEMENT** — Epistemic verb + *pé* | *mọ̀ pé ó jẹ́ òtítọ́* | mọ̀ pé ó jẹ́ òtítọ́ | "Know that it is the truth" | [CORE] | *pé* is the standard complementizer after epistemic and evidential verbs; unrestricted in distribution and the primary means of embedding propositional content. |
| **9. PROPOSITIONAL COMPLEMENT** — Evidential verb + *pé* | *jẹ́rìí pé ó jẹ́ òtítọ́* | jẹ́rìí pé ó jẹ́ òtítọ́ | "Testify that it is true / bear witness that it is the truth" | [CORE] | Common in legal, religious, and testimonial registers; the *jẹ́rìí pé* pattern is a natural collocation in contexts of formal attestation. |
| **10. EXISTENTIAL ASSERTION** — Affirmative *wà* | *ìdánilójú wà* | ìdánilójú wà | "There is assurance / confirmation exists" | [CORE] | *wà* as existential predicate is standard Yoruba; attested in legal and evaluative discourse for asserting the presence of abstract entities such as evidence or certainty. |
| **10. EXISTENTIAL ASSERTION** — Negative *kò sí* | *ẹ̀rí kò sí* | ẹ̀rí kò sí | "There is no evidence / proof is absent" | [CORE] | *kò sí* is the standard negative existential; common in legal and disputational contexts. Distinct from the attributive negation pattern (*kò tọ́*) in making an existential rather than predicative claim. |

## Summary Notes

1. All ten construction types identified in P4a are rated [CORE]. No construction in the attested data is rated [MARGINAL] or [UNCERTAIN] based on the evidence provided.
2. The distinction between the *a-* and *olù-* agentive prefixes (moral valence vs. functional role) is supported by the attested examples but the boundary is not absolute, as *adájọ́* illustrates.
3. The *di* + nominal inchoative alternate (Construction 4) is the only form where frequency relative to the primary pattern is explicitly flagged in P4a; no example sentence was provided for it in P4a and none has been generated here per protocol rules.
4. No constructions have been added beyond those identified in P4a.
