# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P6 CHANGE, CAUSATION, AND STABILITY
# Language: Finnish (fi)

## METHODOLOGY STATEMENT

This analysis documents **attested dynamic expressions** involving truth-domain vocabulary from the CONTEXT materials (P1-P5). All patterns are based on naturally occurring Finnish usage. Gaps are explicitly noted where common expressions do not exist. Each truth-domain term is treated separately, as they show distinct behavioral patterns.

---

## 1. BECOMING TRUE / ACQUIRING TRUTH STATUS

### 1.1 X becomes true (general)

**Pattern:** X + INCHOATIVE VERB
**Common expressions:**
- väite osoittautuu todeksi (claim proves/turns out true)
- asia käy todeksi (matter becomes true, comes to pass)
- ennustus toteutuu (prediction comes true, is realized)
- pelko todellistuu (fear is realized, becomes real)

**Verbs used:**
- *osoittautua* + translative (*todeksi*): "prove to be, turn out to be"
- *käydä* + translative (*todeksi*): "become, come to pass" (archaic/formal)
- *toteutua*: "come true, be realized" (for predictions, plans)
- *todellistua*: "be realized, become real" (for fears, possibilities)

**Case marking:** Translative case (*todeksi* "into true") marks result state with *osoittautua* and *käydä*.

**Register notes:** *Käydä todeksi* is archaic/formal; *osoittautua todeksi* is standard across registers; *toteutua/todellistua* are common for specific semantic domains (predictions/fears).

**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### 1.2 X becomes accurate/correct

**Pattern:** X + INCHOATIVE VERB
**Common expressions:**
- väite osoittautuu oikeaksi (claim proves right/correct)
- arvio osoittautuu oikeaksi (estimate turns out correct)
- tieto osoittautuu paikkansapitäväksi (information proves accurate)

**Verbs used:**
- *osoittautua* + translative (*oikeaksi*, *paikkansapitäväksi*): "prove to be"

**Case marking:** Translative case marks result state.

**Register notes:** Standard across registers. *Oikeaksi* more common than *paikkansapitäväksi* in everyday usage.

**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### 1.3 X becomes real/actual

**Pattern:** X + INCHOATIVE VERB
**Common expressions:**
- unelma toteutuu (dream comes true, is realized)
- suunnitelma toteutuu (plan is realized)
- uhka todellistuu (threat becomes real)
- mahdollisuus todellistuu (possibility becomes real)

**Verbs used:**
- *toteutua*: "come true, be realized" (for plans, dreams, predictions)
- *todellistua*: "become real, be realized" (for threats, possibilities, fears)

**Semantic distinction:** *Toteutua* typically for positive/neutral outcomes (plans, dreams); *todellistua* for any outcome including negative (threats, fears). Some overlap exists.

**Register notes:** Both common across registers. *Toteutua* more frequent in planning/achievement contexts; *todellistua* in analytical/philosophical contexts.

**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### 1.4 X becomes verified/confirmed

**Pattern:** X + INCHOATIVE VERB
**Common expressions:**
- tieto todentuu (information is confirmed/verified)
- epäilyt todentuivat (suspicions were confirmed)
- väitteet todentuivat (claims were verified)

**Verbs used:**
- *todentua*: "be verified, be confirmed, prove true"

**Usage notes:** Less common than *osoittautua* but standard in formal/investigative contexts. Emphasizes verification process.

**Register notes:** Formal/written registers, investigative and journalistic contexts.

**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### 1.5 Truth is revealed/comes to light

**Pattern:** TRUTH-NOUN + REVELATION VERB
**Common expressions:**
- totuus paljastuu (truth is revealed)
- totuus tulee ilmi (truth comes to light)
- totuus tulee esiin (truth comes forth)
- totuus selviää (truth becomes clear)
- valhe paljastuu (lie is exposed)

**Verbs used:**
- *paljastua*: "be revealed, be exposed" (dramatic)
- *tulla ilmi*: "come to light" (neutral/formal)
- *tulla esiin*: "come forth, emerge"
- *selvitä*: "become clear, be clarified"

**Semantic distinctions:** *Paljastua* implies dramatic revelation or exposure; *tulla ilmi* is neutral; *selvitä* emphasizes clarification process.

**Register notes:** *Paljastua* common in narrative and journalistic contexts; *tulla ilmi* in formal/legal contexts; *selvitä* across registers.

**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## 2. BECOMING FALSE / LOSING TRUTH STATUS

### 2.1 X becomes false/wrong

**Pattern:** X + INCHOATIVE VERB
**Common expressions:**
- väite osoittautuu vääräksi (claim proves false/wrong)
- teoria osoittautuu vääräksi (theory turns out false)
- tieto osoittautuu virheelliseksi (information proves erroneous)

**Verbs used:**
- *osoittautua* + translative (*vääräksi*, *virheelliseksi*): "prove to be, turn out to be"

**Case marking:** Translative case marks result state.

**Register notes:** Standard across registers, common in scientific and analytical contexts.

**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### 2.2 X becomes distorted

**Pattern:** X + INCHOATIVE VERB
**Common expressions:**
- totuus vääristyy (truth becomes distorted)
- tieto vääristyy (information becomes distorted)
- kuva vääristyy (picture/image becomes distorted)

**Verbs used:**
- *vääristyä*: "become distorted, be distorted"

**Usage notes:** Can apply to both intentional manipulation and unintentional distortion. Used for both abstract (information) and concrete (physical images) distortion.

**Register notes:** Common in critical discourse, media analysis, technical contexts.

**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### 2.3 X is exposed as false

**Pattern:** X + EXPOSURE VERB
**Common expressions:**
- valhe paljastuu (lie is exposed)
- valhe tulee ilmi (lie comes to light)
- väärennös paljastuu (forgery is exposed)

**Verbs used:**
- *paljastua*: "be exposed, be revealed"
- *tulla ilmi*: "come to light"

**Usage notes:** Same verbs as truth-revelation but with false/deceptive subjects. *Paljastua* more dramatic than *tulla ilmi*.

**Register notes:** *Paljastua* common in investigative and narrative contexts; *tulla ilmi* in formal/legal contexts.

**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## 3. CAUSATIVE: MAKING SOMETHING TRUE/VERIFIED

### 3.1 Y proves X (to be) true

**Pattern:** Y + todistaa + X + TRANSLATIVE
**Common expressions:**
- todistaa väite todeksi (prove claim true) [RARE]
- todistaa väite oikeaksi (prove claim right/correct)
- todistaa syyttömyytensä (prove one's innocence)

**Verbs used:**
- *todistaa*: "prove, demonstrate"

**Case marking:** Translative case (*todeksi*, *oikeaksi*) marks result state. Direct object in accusative/partitive.

**Usage notes:** *Todistaa todeksi* is rare; *todistaa oikeaksi* "prove right/vindicate" is much more common. Often the result state is implicit rather than explicitly marked.

**Register notes:** Common in legal, academic, and argumentative contexts.

**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### 3.2 Y verifies/authenticates X

**Pattern:** Y + todentaa + X
**Common expressions:**
- todentaa henkilöllisyys (verify identity)
- todentaa tiedot (verify information)
- todentaa väitteet (authenticate claims)

**Verbs used:**
- *todentaa*: "verify, authenticate, confirm"

**Usage notes:** More specific than *todistaa*; emphasizes verification/authentication process. Common in administrative and investigative contexts.

**Register notes:** Formal/administrative registers, official contexts.

**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### 3.3 Y realizes/actualizes X (makes real)

**Pattern:** Y + CAUSATIVE VERB + X
**Common expressions:**
- toteuttaa suunnitelma (implement/realize plan)
- toteuttaa unelma (realize dream)
- todellistaa unelma (actualize dream)
- todellistaa mahdollisuus (realize possibility)

**Verbs used:**
- *toteuttaa*: "realize, implement, carry out"
- *todellistaa*: "realize, actualize, make real"

**Semantic distinction:** *Toteuttaa* more common for concrete plans/projects; *todellistaa* more abstract/philosophical. Significant overlap in usage.

**Register notes:** *Toteuttaa* common in planning/administrative contexts; *todellistaa* in philosophical/literary contexts.

**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### 3.4 Y states/observes X (as fact)

**Pattern:** Y + todeta + X
**Common expressions:**
- todeta tosiasiat (state the facts)
- todeta tilanne (observe/note the situation)
- lääkäri toteaa kuoleman (doctor pronounces death)

**Verbs used:**
- *todeta*: "state, note, observe, ascertain"

**Usage notes:** Less causative than *todistaa*; emphasizes declarative act of stating what is observed. Does not necessarily involve proof.

**Register notes:** Formal/professional contexts, medical and official statements.

**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## 4. CAUSATIVE: MAKING SOMETHING FALSE/WRONG

### 4.1 Y proves X (to be) false

**Pattern:** Y + todistaa + X + vääräksi
**Common expressions:**
- todistaa väite vääräksi (prove claim false/wrong)
- todistaa teoria vääräksi (disprove theory)
- todistaa epäilyt vääriksi (prove suspicions wrong)

**Verbs used:**
- *todistaa* + translative (*vääräksi*): "prove wrong, disprove"

**Case marking:** Translative case (*vääräksi*) marks result state.

**Usage notes:** Very common construction in scientific and argumentative contexts. Standard way to express disproof.

**Register notes:** Common across registers, especially academic and argumentative.

**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### 4.2 Y distorts X

**Pattern:** Y + vääristää + X
**Common expressions:**
- vääristää totuutta (distort the truth)
- vääristää tosiasioita (distort facts)
- vääristää sanoja (distort words, misquote)
- vääristää kuvaa (distort the picture/image)

**Verbs used:**
- *vääristää*: "distort, falsify, misrepresent"

**Usage notes:** Can indicate both intentional manipulation and unintentional distortion. Common in critical discourse.

**Register notes:** Common in media criticism, analytical discourse, political commentary.

**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### 4.3 Y forges/counterfeits X

**Pattern:** Y + väärentää + X
**Common expressions:**
- väärentää allekirjoitus (forge signature)
- väärentää asiakirja (forge document)
- väärentää raha (counterfeit money)

**Verbs used:**
- *väärentää*: "forge, counterfeit, falsify"

**Usage notes:** Specifically for creating fraudulent copies or falsifying documents/objects. Legal/criminal terminology.

**Register notes:** Legal, criminal, and authentication contexts.

**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### 4.4 Y falsifies/fabricates X

**Pattern:** Y + VERB + X
**Common expressions:**
- väärentää todisteet (falsify evidence)
- vääristää tulokset (distort/falsify results)
- sepittää tarina (fabricate story)

**Verbs used:**
- *väärentää*: "falsify" (documents, evidence)
- *vääristää*: "distort, falsify" (information, results)
- *sepittää*: "fabricate, make up" (stories, lies) [not from core vocabulary but common in this domain]

**Usage notes:** *Väärentää* for concrete falsification; *vääristää* for abstract distortion; *sepittää* for complete fabrication.

**Register notes:** Legal and investigative contexts for *väärentää*; critical discourse for *vääristää*.

**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## 5. CAUSATIVE: REVEALING/CONCEALING TRUTH

### 5.1 Y reveals/exposes truth

**Pattern:** Y + REVELATION VERB + TRUTH-NOUN
**Common expressions:**
- paljastaa totuus (reveal/expose the truth)
- paljastaa valhe (expose the lie)
- selvittää totuus (clarify/find out the truth)
- tuoda totuus ilmi (bring truth to light)

**Verbs used:**
- *paljastaa*: "reveal, expose" (dramatic)
- *selvittää*: "clarify, find out, investigate"
- *tuoda ilmi*: "bring to light" (formal)

**Usage notes:** *Paljastaa* implies dramatic revelation; *selvittää* emphasizes investigative process; *tuoda ilmi* is formal/neutral.

**Register notes:** *Paljastaa* common in journalistic/narrative contexts; *selvittää* in investigative contexts; *tuoda ilmi* in formal/legal contexts.

**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### 5.2 Y conceals/hides truth

**Pattern:** Y + CONCEALMENT VERB + TRUTH-NOUN
**Common expressions:**
- salata totuus (conceal/hide the truth)
- piilottaa totuus (hide the truth)

**Verbs used:**
- *salata*: "conceal, keep secret"
- *piilottaa*: "hide"
- *vaieta*: "keep silent about" (+ elative case)

**Usage notes:** *Salata* most common for deliberate concealment; *piilottaa* more concrete; *vaieta* emphasizes silence.

**Register notes:** Common across registers in moral and investigative discourse.

**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## 7. MAINTAINING TRUTH STATUS / STABILITY

### 7.1 X remains true/valid

**Pattern:** X + CONTINUATIVE VERB + TRUTH-STATE
**Common expressions:**
- väite pitää edelleen paikkansa (claim still holds true)
- totuus pysyy muuttumattomana (truth remains unchanged)
- tieto on edelleen paikkaansapitävä (information is still accurate)

**Verbs/constructions used:**
- *pitää paikkansa* + temporal adverb (*edelleen* "still"): "hold true"
- *pysyä* + essive (*muuttumattomana* "unchanged"): "remain"
- *olla* + temporal adverb: "be (still)"

**Usage notes:** *Pitää paikkansa* most common for propositional content; *pysyä* for states; temporal adverbs mark continuity.

**Register notes:** Common across registers when emphasizing continued validity.

**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### 7.2 X continues to be accurate/reliable

**Pattern:** X + CONTINUATIVE CONSTRUCTION
**Common expressions:**
- lähde on edelleen luotettava (source is still reliable)
- tieto pysyy oikeana (information remains correct)
- väite on yhä paikkaansapitävä (claim is still accurate)

**Verbs/constructions used:**
- *olla* + temporal adverb (*edelleen* "still", *yhä* "still"): "be (still)"
- *pysyä* + essive case: "remain"

**Usage notes:** Temporal adverbs (*edelleen*, *yhä*, *vieläkin*) mark continuity. Essive case with *pysyä* marks maintained state.

**Register notes:** Common across registers.

**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### 7.3 Truth persists/endures

**Pattern:** TRUTH-NOUN + PERSISTENCE VERB
**Common expressions:**
- totuus kestää (truth endures)
- totuus säilyy (truth is preserved/maintained)
- tosiasiat eivät muutu (facts don't change)

**Verbs used:**
- *kestää*: "endure, last"
- *säilyä*: "be preserved, be maintained"
- *muuttua* (negated): "change" (negated: "not change")

**Usage notes:** Philosophical/proverbial expressions about permanence of truth. Less common in everyday discourse.

**Register notes:** Formal, philosophical, or proverbial contexts.

**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## 8. LOSS OF TRUTH STATUS / DEGRADATION

### 8.1 X ceases to be true/valid

**Pattern:** X + CESSATION CONSTRUCTION
**Common expressions:**
- väite ei enää pidä paikkaansa (claim no longer holds true)
- tieto ei ole enää paikkaansapitävä (information is no longer accurate)
- teoria menettää pätevyytensä (theory loses its validity)

**Verbs/constructions used:**
- *ei enää* + verb: "no longer" (negation + temporal adverb)
- *menettää*: "lose" (+ abstract noun object)

**Usage notes:** *Ei enää* "no longer" is standard way to mark cessation. *Menettää* "lose" used with abstract quality nouns.

**Register notes:** Common across registers.

**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### 8.2 X becomes outdated/invalid

**Pattern:** X + DEGRADATION VERB
**Common expressions:**
- tieto vanhenee (information becomes outdated)
- väite menettää merkityksensä (claim loses its significance)

**Verbs used:**
- *vanhentua*: "become outdated, become obsolete"
- *menettää*: "lose" (+ object)

**Usage notes:** *Vanhentua* specifically for temporal obsolescence; *menettää* for loss of abstract qualities.

**Register notes:** Common in technical and analytical contexts.

**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## 10. ASPECTUAL DISTINCTIONS

### 10.1 Perfective: X has become true

**Pattern:** X + on + PAST PARTICIPLE
**Common expressions:**
- väite on osoittautunut todeksi (claim has proven true)
- ennustus on toteutunut (prediction has come true)
- totuus on paljastunut (truth has been revealed)

**Construction:** Perfect tense using *olla* + past participle (*-nut/-nyt*)

**Usage notes:** Marks completed change of state. Very common for expressing that truth-status has been established.

**Register notes:** Common across all registers.

**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### 10.2 Progressive: X is becoming true

**Pattern:** X + olla + -massa (inessive of 3rd infinitive)
**Common expressions:**
- totuus on paljastumassa (truth is being revealed)
- väite on osoittautumassa todeksi (claim is proving true)

**Construction:** Progressive aspect using *olla* + 3rd infinitive inessive (*-massa*)

**Usage notes:** Marks ongoing process. Less common than perfective but standard for emphasizing process.

**Register notes:** Common in narrative and journalistic contexts.

**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Usage notes:** Standard modal + infinitive construction. Common for expressing possibility of truth-status change.

**Register notes:** Common across registers.

**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### 11.2 X must/should become verified

**Pattern:** X + MODAL VERB + VERIFICATION VERB
**Common expressions:**
- tieto pitää todentaa (information must be verified)

**Modal verbs:**

- *tulisi*: "should" (conditional of *tulla*)

**Usage notes:** Deontic modality expressing necessity or obligation. Common in formal and instructional contexts.

**Register notes:** Formal/administrative registers.

**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## 12. NEGATED CHANGE

- ennustus ei toteudu (prediction does not come true)
- pelko ei todellistu (fear is not realized)

**Construction:** Standard negation with *ei* + verb stem

**Usage notes:** Standard negation pattern. Common for expressing failed predictions or unrealized possibilities.

**Register notes:** Common across registers.

**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### 12.2 X cannot be proven/verified

**Pattern:** X + ei + MODAL + PASSIVE
**Common expressions:**
- väitettä ei voida todistaa (claim cannot be proven)
- tietoa ei voi todentaa (information cannot be verified)
- väite ei ole todistettavissa (claim is not provable)

**Constructions:**
- *ei voida* + passive infinitive: "cannot be"

**Usage notes:** Expresses impossibility of verification. Common in scientific and analytical discourse.

**Register notes:** Formal/academic registers.

**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

**Usage notes:** Standard conditional construction. Common in logical and argumentative discourse.

**Register notes:** Common across registers, especially in analytical contexts.

**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### 13.2 X would become true (counterfactual)

**Pattern:** X + CONDITIONAL VERB FORM
**Common expressions:**
- väite osoittautuisi todeksi (claim would prove true)
- tieto todentuisi (information would be verified)

**Construction:** Conditional mood (*-isi* suffix)

**Usage notes:** Expresses hypothetical or counterfactual situations. Common in speculative discourse.

**Register notes:** Common across registers.

**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## 14. GAPS AND ABSENCES

### 14.1 No common expressions for:

**Making something "more true":**
- No standard expression for increasing degree of truth
- Finnish treats truth as binary rather than gradable
- Periphrastic constructions possible but rare: *tehdä todemmaksi* (make more true) [MARGINAL/UNNATURAL]

**Becoming "partially true":**
- No standard inchoative for partial truth
- Descriptive phrases required: *osoittautua osittain todeksi* (prove partially true) [DESCRIPTIVE]

**Maintaining falsity:**
- No dedicated verb for "remaining false"
- Uses negated truth-maintenance: *ei tule todeksi* (does not become true)
- Or descriptive: *pysyy vääränä* (remains wrong) [LESS COMMON]

**Gradual truth-acquisition:**
- No dedicated aspectual marker for gradual becoming-true
- Uses adverbs with standard inchoatives: *vähitellen todentua* (gradually be verified)

## 15. SUMMARY OBSERVATIONS

### Productive patterns:

1. **Inchoative formations** highly productive with translative case marking result state
2. **Causative verbs** (*todistaa*, *todentaa*, *toteuttaa*, *todellistaa*) show clear semantic distinctions
3. **Revelation verbs** (*paljastua*, *tulla ilmi*, *selvitä*) mark different aspects of truth-emergence
4. **Distortion/falsification** has dedicated causative verbs (*vääristää*, *väärentää*)

### Asymmetries:

1. **Truth-realization** has multiple specialized verbs (*toteutua*, *todellistua*, *todentua*) with semantic distinctions
2. **Falsification** uses fewer verbs but with clear concrete/abstract distinction (*väärentää* vs. *vääristää*)
3. **Correction** uses general verbs (*korjata*, *oikaista*) rather than truth-specific forms
4. **Stability** expressed through general continuative constructions rather than truth-specific verbs

### Register stratification:

1. **Formal/legal:** *todentaa*, *tuoda ilmi*, *osoittautua*
2. **Narrative/journalistic:** *paljastua*, *toteutua*, *todellistua*
3. **Everyday:** *selvitä*, *korjata*, *pitää paikkansa*
4. **Archaic/literary:** *käydä todeksi*, *näyttää toteen*

### Case marking patterns:

1. **Translative** (*-ksi*) marks result state with inchoatives and causatives
2. **Partitive** for objects of causative verbs
3. **Essive** (*-na*) for maintained states with *pysyä*
4. **Inessive** (*-ssa*) for progressive aspect (3rd infinitive)
