# G6 — Cross-Language Opposition and Asymmetry Comparison

## Part 1: Per-Language Oppositional Structures (Summary)

### Georgian (ka)

The F5 data documents 34 opposition entries. The primary contrast types are:

**Lexical oppositions:** *simarṭle/siq̇albe* (truth/falseness), *simarṭle/sicrue* (truth/lie), *namdvili/q̇albi* (genuine/fake), *sc̣ori/mcdari* (correct/erroneous), *q̇albi/mcdari* (deliberate fake vs. honest error), *q̇albi/piqtiuri* (forged physical item vs. sham transaction), *q̇albi/moč̣venebiti* (fake with deceptive intent vs. merely apparent), *namdvili/moč̣venebiti* (genuine vs. ostensible), *namdvili/piqtiuri* (genuine vs. fictitious), *namdvili/avtentiuri* (everyday genuineness vs. formal/archival authenticity), *sarc̣muno/damajerebeliˈ* (inherent trustworthiness vs. persuasive effect on hearer), *sarc̣muno/sando* (epistemic credibility vs. practical reliability), *legitimuri/kanonieri* (normative legitimacy vs. positive-law legality), *gulc̣rpeli/tvaltmaqcoba* (sincere vs. hypocritical), *šinaˈarsi/porma* (content/substance vs. form), *arsi/porma* (essence vs. surface), *mt'k'iceba/mt'k'icebuleba* (act of proving vs. product of proof), *sarwmunoeba* (secular credibility vs. theological faith — internal bifurcation within a single form).

**Morphological oppositions:** *sc̣ori/arasc̣ori* (*ara-* prefix negation), *sc̣ori/usc̣oro* (*u-...-o* privative), *zusti/uzusto* (*u-...-o* privative), *sarc̣muno/arasc̣muno* (*ara-* prefix negation).

**Constructional oppositions:** *martalia, magram* (concessive formula specific to *martali*); *simartle gamovida* (intransitive truth-revelation) vs. *simartle damalva* (transitive concealment).

**Notable asymmetries:** The *moč̣venebiti* (ostensible/apparent) term is explicitly distinguished from both *q̇albi* (fake, implying deliberate deception) and *namdvili* (genuine) — a three-way distinction not paralleled in other languages. The *sarwmunoeba* bifurcation (credibility vs. faith) is a single-form semantic split unique in the dataset. The *q̇albi/piqtiuri* distinction (forged physical item vs. sham transaction) encodes a legal sub-distinction within the fake/counterfeit domain.

### Yoruba (yo)

The F5 data documents 31 opposition entries. The primary contrast types are:

**Lexical oppositions:** *òtítọ́/irọ́* (truth/lie — the most consistently paired terms in the dataset), *ìwà rere/ìwà burúkú* (good character/bad character), *ẹlẹ́rìí ojú/ẹlẹ́rìí irọ́* (eyewitness/false witness), *fidagarri/fidagaitz* (trustworthy/hard to trust), *olòtítọ́/aṣírọ́* (truthful person/liar), *aláìrọ́/aṣírọ́* (one who does not lie/one who tells lies — both derived from *irọ́*), *ìgbàgbọ́/àìgbàgbọ́* (belief/unbelief), *olùgbàgbọ́/aláìgbàgbọ́* (believer/unbeliever), *ìgbẹ́kẹ̀lé/àìgbẹ́kẹ̀lé* (trust/distrust), *ìmọ̀/àìmọ̀* (knowledge/ignorance), *olùmọ̀/aláìmọ̀* (knowledgeable person/ignorant person).

**Morphological oppositions:** *ìmọ̀/àìmọ̀* (*àì-* negation prefix), *ìgbàgbọ́/àìgbàgbọ́* (*àì-* negation prefix), *ìgbẹ́kẹ̀lé/àìgbẹ́kẹ̀lé* (*àì-* negation prefix), *olùgbàgbọ́/aláìgbàgbọ́* (*olù-* agentive vs. *al-* + *àì-* negative agentive), *aláìrọ́/aṣírọ́* (negative agentive vs. positive agentive on the same base *irọ́*).

**Constructional oppositions:** *sọ òtítọ́/sọ irọ́* (same verb, contrasting objects — the most structurally parallel verbal pair in the dataset), *ó tọ́/kò tọ́* (affirmative/negative predicative), *ó tọ́ pé/kò tọ́ pé* (propositional-complement versions), *òtítọ́ kò ní kú/irọ́ kò ní dúró* (parallel proverbial constructions with contrasting predicates), *ó ní ìwà rere/kò ní ìwà rere* (possessive affirmation/negation), *gbàgbọ́ pé/kò gbàgbọ́* (belief/disbelief), *jẹ́rìí fún/jẹ́rìí sí* (testify for/testify against), *ìsọ òtítọ́/ìsọ irọ́* (nominalized truth-telling/lying), *ó ní ìmọ̀ nípa rẹ̀/kò ní ìmọ̀ nípa rẹ̀* (has knowledge/lacks knowledge), *ó tọ́ láti gbẹ́kẹ̀lé rẹ̀/kò tọ́ láti gbẹ́kẹ̀lé rẹ̀* (it is right to trust/it is not right to trust).

**Notable asymmetries:** The *sọ òtítọ́/sọ irọ́* pair is the most structurally parallel verbal opposition in the entire dataset — same verb, contrasting objects, identical syntax. The *àì-* prefix generates a productive set of negated nominals (*àìmọ̀*, *àìgbàgbọ́*, *àìgbẹ́kẹ̀lé*) that are more systematically documented than equivalent privative forms in most other languages. The *aláìrọ́/aṣírọ́* pair is notable: both are derived from *irọ́* (lie), but one negates the action (*aláìrọ́*, one who does not lie) and the other asserts it (*aṣírọ́*, one who tells lies) — a morphological opposition within the same base.

### Finnish (fi)

The F5 data documents 38 opposition entries. The primary contrast types are:

**Lexical oppositions:** *tosi/väärä* (true/wrong — asymmetric: *epätosi* is the formal propositional negation, *väärä* is the general-purpose antonym), *tosi/taru* (true/legend — fixed binary *tosi vai tarua*), *totuus/valhe* (truth/lie), *totuudenmukainen/totuudenvastainen* (conforming to truth/contrary to truth), *aito/väärennös* (genuine/forgery), *aito/jäljitelmä* (genuine/imitation), *alkuperäinen/väärennös* (original/forgery), *alkuperäinen/kopio* (original/copy), *kopio/jäljitelmä* (neutral copy/imitation with lesser status), *kopio/väärennös* (neutral copy/fraudulent forgery), *luotettava/epäluotettava* (reliable/unreliable), *uskottava/epäuskottava* (credible/incredible), *oikea/väärä* (right/wrong), *oikeaoppinen/vääräoppinen* (orthodox/heterodox), *väärentää/vääristää* (to forge a fraudulent object/to distort facts), *vääristymä/vääristely* (unintentional distortion/deliberate distortion), *väärentää/todentaa* (to falsify/to verify — opposing directions), *tarkistaa/tarkastaa* (everyday checking/formal inspection), *aito/epäaito* (genuine/inauthentic), *oikeellisuus/totuus* (rule-conformity/factual correspondence — explicitly distinguished, not antonyms).

**Morphological oppositions:** *tosi/epätosi* (*epä-* prefix), *luotettava/epäluotettava* (*epä-* prefix), *aito/epäaito* (*epä-* prefix), *aitous/epäaitous* (*epä-* prefix), *totuudenmukainen/totuudenvastainen* (*mukainen* conforming vs. *vastainen* contrary — compound adjective with contrasting postpositional elements on the same genitive base), *oikeaoppinen/vääräoppinen* (parallel compound formation with *oikea* vs. *väärä*), *oikein/väärin* (adverbial pair), *olla oikeassa/olla väärässä* (inessive fixed predication pair).

**Constructional oppositions:** *olla totta/ei ole totta* (affirmative/negative copular), *pitää paikkansa/ei pidä paikkaansa* (holds/does not hold — the most colloquial propositional truth/falsity pair), *tehdä oikein/tehdä väärin* (to do right/to do wrong), *Notaari todistaa kopion oikeaksi/Tuomioistuin julisti sopimuksen pätemättömäksi* (certification vs. invalidation using translative case).

**Notable asymmetries:** The four-way distinction *alkuperäinen/kopio/jäljitelmä/väärennös* (original/neutral copy/imitation/forgery) is the most elaborated in the dataset. The *väärentää/vääristää* distinction (producing a fraudulent object vs. distorting facts) is explicitly documented as morphologically encoded within the same root. The *tarkistaa/tarkastaa* register distinction (everyday checking vs. formal inspection) is documented more explicitly than in any other language. The *totuudenmukainen/totuudenvastainen* compound adjective pair using contrasting postpositional elements (*mukainen* vs. *vastainen*) on the same genitive base is a productive pattern unique to Finnish in the dataset.

### Basque (eu)

The F5 data documents 39 opposition entries. The primary contrast types are:

**Lexical oppositions:** *egia/gezurra* (truth/lie), *egiazkoa/gezurrezkoa* (true/false — adjectival pair), *egiazko/faltsuko/faltsu* (true/false — with *faltsu* as a third term), *zuzena/okerra* (correct/wrong — spatial metaphor), *fidagarria/fidaezin* (trustworthy/untrustworthy), *fidagarria/fidagaitza* (trustworthy/hard to trust — scalar), *sinesgarria/sineskaitza* (believable/hard to believe), *baliozkoa/baliogabea* (valid/invalid), *baliozkotu/baliogabetu* (to validate/to invalidate), *jatorrizkoa/kopiakoa* (original/copy), *autentikoa/faltsua* (authentic/fake), *egiazko/benetako* (factual correspondence vs. sincerity/non-artificiality — explicitly distinguished), *fidagarri/sinesgarri* (trustworthy source vs. believable content — distinguished).

**Morphological oppositions:** *egiaztagarria/egiaztaezina* (*-garri* potential vs. *-ezin* impossibility — on the same verbal stem), *egiaztagarria/egiaztagaitza* (*-garri* potential vs. *-gaitz* difficult — on the same verbal stem), *frogagarria/frogaezina* (*-garri* vs. *-ezin*), *baliozkoa/baliogabea* (*-zko* adjectival vs. *-gabe* privative on the same root *balio*), *egiazko/gezurrezko* (parallel *-zko* relational adjectives on contrasting bases), *ziurtasun/ziurtasun eza* (certainty vs. lack of certainty), *ziur/ziurrenik* (certain vs. probably/most likely — semantic shift within the same derivational family).

**Constructional oppositions:** *egia da/gezurra da* (it is true/it is a lie — parallel copular constructions), *egia esan/gezurra esan* (to tell the truth/to tell a lie — same verb, contrasting objects), *egia ala gezurra?* (true or false? — fixed binary interrogative), *zuzen egon/oker egon* (to be right/to be wrong), *oker nago/zuzena da* (I am wrong/it is correct — asymmetric: *egon* for contingent states vs. *da* for inherent properties), *egiaztagarria/egiaztaezina/egiaztatu gabe* (three-way: can be verified/cannot be verified/not yet verified — documented as non-interchangeable), *balioa galdu/balioa eman* (to lose value/to give value), *sinesgarritasuna irabazi/sinesgarritasuna galdu* (to gain credibility/to lose credibility).

**Notable asymmetries:** The three-way *-garri/-gaitz/-ezin* distinction (capable/difficult/impossible to verify) is unique in the dataset. The *-gabe* (absence) vs. *-ezin* (impossibility) distinction is explicitly documented as non-interchangeable. The *ziurrenik* semantic shift from "certainly" to "probably/most likely" is a single-language observation. The *oker nago/zuzena da* asymmetry (contingent state *egon* vs. inherent property *da*) is explicitly documented.

### Tamil (ta)

The F5 data documents 32 opposition entries. The primary contrast types are:

**Lexical oppositions:** *uṇmai/poy* (truth/lie), *uṇmaiyāna/poyyāna* (true/false — parallel *-āna* adjectival forms), *acal/nakal/pōli* (original/neutral copy/fraudulent fake — three-way), *niyāyam/aniyāyam* (justice/injustice), *niyāyamāna/anyāyamāna* (just/unjust — parallel *-āna* forms), *nampakamāna/nampakamāṟṟa* (reliable/unreliable), *nampattakka/nampattakāta* (worth trusting/not worth trusting — morphological minimal pair), *nampattakka/nampamuṭiyāta* (worth trusting/impossible to believe), *nērmaiyāna/kaḷḷamāna* (honest/deceitful), *nērmaiyāna/vañcakamāna* (honest/treacherous), *nērmai/nērmayiṉmai* (honesty/absence of honesty), *uṇmai/uṇmaiyaṟṟa* (truth/lacking truth), *ātāramāna/ātāramaṟṟa* (evidenced/unfounded).

**Morphological oppositions:** *uṇmaiyāna/poyyāna* (parallel *-āna* derivations on contrasting bases), *nampattakka/nampattakāta* (same base, contrasting participial suffixes *takka* vs. *takāta* — the most explicit morphological minimal pair in the dataset for this function), *nampakamāna/nampakamāṟṟa* (*-āna* vs. *-aṟṟa* privative), *nērmaiyāna/nērmaiyaṟṟa* (*-āna* vs. *-aṟṟa* privative), *nērmai/nērmayiṉmai* (*iṉmai* absence nominal), *nampikkai/nampikkaiinmai* (*iṉmai* absence nominal), *niyāyam/aniyāyam* (Sanskrit privative *a-* prefix).

**Constructional oppositions:** *uṇmai veḷippaṭṭatu* (the truth came to light — intransitive) vs. *uṇmaiyai maṟai* (to conceal the truth — transitive), *uṇmai tāṉ vellum* (truth alone will prevail — proverbial), *atu uṇmaitāṉ* (that is indeed true) vs. *atu uṇmai illai* (that is not true), *nampikkai iḻantuViṭṭatu* (trust was lost) vs. *nampikkai vaḷarntatu* (trust grew).

**Notable asymmetries:** The *acal/nakal/pōli* three-way distinction (original/neutral copy/fraudulent fake) is explicitly documented with *nakal* as a neutral intermediate. The *nampattakka/nampattakāta* pair is the most explicit morphological minimal pair for the trustworthy/untrustworthy contrast in the dataset — same base, contrasting participial suffixes. The *-aṟṟa* privative and *illāta* (lacking) are documented as near-synonymous but with *illāta* slightly more common in spoken registers.

### Indonesian (id)

The F5 data documents 40 opposition entries. The primary contrast types are:

**Lexical oppositions:** *benar/salah* (correct/wrong — primary pair), *benar/bohong* (true/lie), *benar/dusta* (true/falsehood — register-differentiated near-synonyms for the negative pole), *asli/palsu* (genuine/fake), *asli/tiruan* (genuine/imitation), *palsu/tiruan* (fake/imitation — within the non-genuine category), *sah/tidak sah* (valid/invalid), *sah/belum sah* (valid/not yet valid — temporal distinction), *anak sah/anak tidak sah* (legitimate child/illegitimate child), *sah secara hukum/sah secara agama* (legally valid/religiously valid), *sah secara prosedural/dipertanyakan secara moral* (procedurally valid/morally questionable), *terbukti bersalah/tidak terbukti bersalah* (proven guilty/not proven guilty), *bersalah/tidak bersalah* (guilty/not guilty), *jujur/tidak jujur* (honest/dishonest), *jujur/bohong* (honest/lying), *adil/tidak adil* (fair/unfair), *akurat/tidak akurat* (accurate/inaccurate), *kebenaran mutlak/kebenaran relatif* (absolute truth/relative truth), *kebenaran formal/kebenaran materiil* (procedural truth vs. substantive truth), *bukti langsung/bukti tidak langsung* (direct evidence/indirect evidence), *bohong/dusta* (everyday lie/formal/literary lie — register distinction within the negative pole), *pembohong/pendusta* (everyday liar/formal/literary liar), *sah/absah* (valid/valid — register-differentiated near-synonyms), *keliru/salah* (innocent error/wrong — intent distinction), *keliru/bohong* (innocent error/deliberate lie), *salah informasi/manipulasi* (misinformation without intent/deliberate manipulation), *menyesatkan/sengaja menyesatkan* (misleading/deliberately misleading — intent distinction).

**Morphological oppositions:** *benar/tidak benar* (syntactic negation with *tidak*), *sah/tidak sah* (syntactic negation), *bersalah/tidak bersalah* (syntactic negation), *jujur/tidak jujur* (syntactic negation), *keabsahan* built on *absah* rather than *sah* (morphological asymmetry within a near-synonym pair).

**Constructional oppositions:** *benar atau tidak* (true or not — suspension-of-judgment frame), *benar atau salah* (right or wrong — binary frame), *asli atau palsu* (genuine or fake — fixed binary), *sah secara hukum tetapi tidak secara moral* (legal but not legitimate — productive contrastive construction), *tidak dibenarkan untuk* (it is not permitted to — deontic extension of the causative).

**Notable asymmetries:** The *bohong/dusta* and *pembohong/pendusta* register-differentiated near-synonym pairs within the negative pole are unique in the dataset — two terms for the same concept differentiated only by register. The *sah/absah* near-synonym pair with the morphological asymmetry (*keabsahan* built on *absah* rather than *sah*) is a documented irregularity. The *kebenaran formal/kebenaran materiil* (procedural vs. substantive truth) legal distinction is documented only in Indonesian. The *salah* + verb/noun misaction compounding pattern generates a large open class of misaction compounds with no parallel in other languages.

### Turkish (tr)

The F5 data documents 42 opposition entries. The primary contrast types are:

**Lexical oppositions:** *doğru/yanlış* (correct/wrong — primary pair), *doğru/eğri* (straight/crooked — spatial antonym pair), *doğru/yalan* (true/lie), *doğruluk/yanlışlık* (truthfulness/incorrectness — nominalized pair), *gerçek/sahte* (real/fake — primary genuineness pair), *gerçek/yalan* (real/lie), *gerçek/gerçek dışı* (real/unreal), *gerçek/kurgu* (reality/fiction), *gerçek/sanal* (real/virtual), *gerçeğe uygun/gerçeğe aykırı* (conforming to reality/contrary to reality — postpositional pair), *gerçeğe uygun beyan/gerçeğe aykırı beyan* (truthful declaration/false declaration — legal pair), *gerçekçi/hayalci* (realistic/idealistic), *gerçek kişi/tüzel kişi* (natural person/legal entity), *haklı/haksız* (justified/unjustified — primary justification pair), *haklılık/haksızlık* (justifiedness/injustice — nominalized pair), *haklı çıkmak/yalancı çıkmak* (to be proven right/to be proven a liar), *haklı çıkmak/haksız çıkmak* (to be vindicated/to be proven wrong), *hakkını vermek/hakkını yemek* (to give what is owed/to deprive of what is owed), *geçerli/geçersiz* (valid/invalid — primary validity pair), *meşru/gayri meşru* (legitimate/illegitimate), *yasal/meşru* (legal/legitimate — explicitly distinguished), *teyit etmek/yalanlamak* (to confirm/to deny — functional opposites in journalistic discourse), *doğrulama/yalanlama* (confirmation/denial — nominalized pair), *doğrulamak/yanlışlamak* (to verify/to falsify — unique morphological opposition).

**Morphological oppositions:** *doğrulamak/yanlışlamak* (parallel *-la-* factitive verbalizations on contrasting bases — the most explicit morphological opposition in the dataset), *doğrulanabilirlik/yanlışlanabilirlik* (parallel *-la-n-abil-lik* stacking on contrasting bases), *geçerli/geçersiz* (*-li* adjectival vs. *-siz* privative on the same stem), *haklı/haksız* (*-lı* possessive vs. *-sız* privative on *hak*), *meşru/gayri meşru* (Arabic-pattern negation rather than Turkish *-sız*).

**Constructional oppositions:** *doğru mu yanlış mı?* (true or false? — fixed binary), *yasal ama meşru değil* (legal but not legitimate — productive contrastive construction), *doğruya doğru, eğriye eğri demek* (to call a spade a spade — proverbial), *haklı çıkmak/haksız çıkmak* (resultative pair with *çıkmak*).

**Notable asymmetries:** The *doğrulamak/yanlışlamak* morphological opposition with parallel *doğrulanabilirlik/yanlışlanabilirlik* is unique in the dataset. The *yasal/meşru* distinction (legal vs. legitimate) is the most explicitly documented legality/legitimacy contrast in the dataset. The *meşru/gayri meşru* pair uses Arabic-pattern negation rather than Turkish *-sız*, creating a morphological asymmetry within the legitimacy domain. The *hakkını vermek/hakkını yemek* idiomatic pair encodes the fulfillment/deprivation of entitlement through contrasting verbs in the same nominal frame.

### Korean (ko)

The F5 data documents 38 opposition entries. The primary contrast types are:

**Lexical oppositions:** *진짜/가짜* (genuine/fake — primary colloquial pair), *진실/거짓* (truth/falsehood), *진실/거짓말* (truth/lie — everyday speech), *맞다/틀리다* (to be right/to be wrong — everyday factual pair), *옳다/그르다* (to be right/to be wrong — moral/philosophical pair), *옳다/틀리다* (functional pair in everyday speech — *틀리다* has largely displaced *그르다* in factual contexts), *사실이다/사실이 아니다* (it is a fact/it is not a fact), *사실이 아니다/거짓이다* (neutral denial/marked assertion of falsehood — pragmatic distinction), *사실/허위* (fact/falsehood — formal register pair), *근거가 있다/근거가 없다* (to be grounded/to be groundless), *신빙성이 있다/신빙성이 없다* (to be credible/to lack credibility), *진정성이 있다/진정성이 없다* (to be authentic/to lack authenticity), *타당성이 있다/타당성이 없다* (to be valid/to lack validity), *진실을 밝히다/진실을 숨기다* (to reveal the truth/to conceal the truth), *진실을 밝히다/진실을 외면하다* (to reveal the truth/to turn away from the truth), *진실을 말하다/거짓말을 하다* (to tell the truth/to tell a lie), *신뢰를 쌓다/신뢰를 잃다* (to build trust/to lose trust), *신뢰를 쌓다/신뢰를 저버리다* (to build trust/to betray trust), *신뢰를 잃다/신뢰를 회복하다* (to lose trust/to restore trust), *정확도가 높다/정확도가 낮다* (accuracy is high/accuracy is low), *유효하다/무효가 되다* (to be valid/to become void), *합법화하다/무효화하다* (to legalize/to invalidate — opposing *-화하다* causatives).

**Morphological oppositions:** *정확/부정확* (*부-* privative prefix), *정직/부정직* (*부-* privative prefix), *공정/불공정* (*불-* privative prefix), *신뢰/불신* (*불-* privative prefix), *유효/무효* (Sino-Korean morphological pair), *합법/불법* (Sino-Korean morphological pair), *진실하다/진실되다* (*-하다* active/performed vs. *-되다* stative/resultant — unique morphological distinction), *위조하다/위조되다* (*-하다* agentive vs. *-되다* passive/resultant), *모순되다* (only *-되다* form attested; *\*모순하다* is not attested — asymmetric).

**Constructional oppositions:** *사실이다/사실이 아니다* (copular affirmation/negation), *있다/없다* existential frame for *-성* quality nominals (primary predication strategy for this class — unique to Korean), *높다/낮다* scalar frame for degree-of-quality assessment, *정확하다/정확하지 않다* (syntactic negation with *-지 않다*), *옳고 그름* (fixed coordinate pair), *진짜인지 가짜인지 모르다* (not knowing whether genuine or fake — fixed embedded interrogative).

**Notable asymmetries:** The *-하다/-되다* stative/agentive distinction is unique in the dataset. The *있다/없다* existential frame as the primary predication strategy for *-성* quality nominals (where *-하다* is blocked) is unique in the dataset. The three-way inchoative verb distinction (*드러나다/밝혀지다/판명되다*) for truth-revelation is unique in the dataset. The *신뢰를 쌓다/잃다/저버리다/회복하다* four-verb lifecycle of trust is the most elaborated trust-dynamics cluster in the dataset.
