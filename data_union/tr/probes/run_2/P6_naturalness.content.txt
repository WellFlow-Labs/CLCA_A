# CLCA Protocol v3.3 — P6 Change, Causation, and Stability
# Language: Turkish (tr)

---

> **Scope note:** This step documents how Turkish encodes dynamic processes — becoming, ceasing to be, causing to be, and resisting change — involving the truth-related vocabulary established in P1–P5. All vocabulary is drawn from the CONTEXT. Where a given term lacks a common dynamic expression, this is stated explicitly. Patterns are treated separately per term or term-cluster where behavior differs.

---

## 1. BECOMING A TRUTH-RELATED STATE

### 1a. Morphological inchoative: *[base] + -leş- + -mek*

This pattern is available only where the *-leş-* inchoative has been conventionalized on the base. The following items from the CONTEXT have attested *-leş-* forms:

---

**meşrulaşmak** — to become legitimate, to gain legitimacy

- **Hareket zamanla meşrulaştı.**
  *hareket zamanla meşrulaştı*
  "The movement became legitimate over time."
  [CORE] — Documented in P2a Item 19 and P4a Construction 4. Temporal adverbials (*zamanla*, over time; *giderek*, gradually) are typical co-occurring elements. The subject is typically an institution, movement, or practice rather than a person.

---

**gerçekleşmek** — to come true, to materialize, to be realized

- **Hayalleri gerçekleşti.**
  *hayalleri gerçekleşti*
  "Their dreams came true / were realized."
  [CORE] — Documented in P1b Root 3 item 3, P2a Item 2, and P4a Construction 4. The subject is typically a plan, hope, event, or prediction. Note: *gerçekleşmek* has shifted toward the meaning "to occur, to take place" in addition to "to become real"; both readings are available.

- **Toplantı gerçekleşti.**
  *toplantı gerçekleşti*
  "The meeting took place / was realized."
  [CORE] — The "occur" reading, where the inchoative sense of "becoming real" is still present but the emphasis is on actualization of a planned event.

---

**özgürleşmek** — to become free, to be liberated

- **Toplumun özgürleşmesi zaman aldı.**
  *toplumun özgürleşmesi zaman aldı*
  "The liberation/emancipation of society took time."
  [CORE] — Documented in P2a Item 22. The subject is typically a collective (society, group) or an individual in a social or political context. The nominalized form *özgürleşme* is used here as subject.

---

> **Items without attested *-leş-* inchoative:** The following truth-domain adjectives from P1 do not have a conventionalized *-leş-* inchoative form in the CONTEXT data: *doğru*, *yanlış*, *sahte*, *sahici*, *özgün*, *orijinal*, *güvenilir*, *güvenilmez*, *inanılır*, *haklı*, *haksız*, *geçerli*, *geçersiz*, *dürüst*. For these items, the periphrastic strategy (*olmak*) is used; see 1b below.

---

### 1b. Periphrastic inchoative: *[truth-domain adjective] + olmak*

This is the default strategy where morphological *-leş-* is not conventionalized. *Olmak* (to be/become) serves as both a stative copula and an inchoative verb depending on context; the inchoative reading is supported by temporal adverbials, process contexts, or the *-e/-a* dative of direction.

---

**geçerli olmak** — to become valid, to come into force

- **Yeni yasa 1 Ocak'tan itibaren geçerli oldu.**
  *yeni yasa 1 Ocak'tan itibaren geçerli oldu*
  "The new law became valid / came into force from January 1st."
  [CORE] — Documented in P2a Item 20 notes. The temporal frame (*1 Ocak'tan itibaren*, from January 1st) triggers the inchoative reading of *olmak*. Typical subjects are laws, documents, rules, and agreements.

---

**haklı olmak** — to be right / to become right (in a given situation)

- **Sonunda haklı oldu.**
  *sonunda haklı oldu*
  "In the end, he/she turned out to be right."
  [CORE] — The temporal adverbial *sonunda* (in the end, finally) supports the inchoative/resultative reading. Closely related to *haklı çıkmak* (see 1c below); *haklı olmak* is slightly more stative, while *haklı çıkmak* emphasizes the process of being vindicated.

---

**güvenilir olmak** — to become reliable / to be reliable

- **Kaynak zamanla güvenilir hale geldi.**
  *kaynak zamanla güvenilir hale geldi*
  "The source became reliable over time."
  [CORE] — The construction *güvenilir hale gelmek* (to come to a state of being reliable) is the more natural inchoative expression for *güvenilir*; bare *güvenilir olmak* is more stative. See 1c below for the *hale gelmek* pattern.

---

**doğru olmak** — to be correct / to turn out to be correct

- **Tahmin doğru oldu.**
  *tahmin doğru oldu*
  "The prediction turned out to be correct."
  [CORE] — The past tense of *olmak* (*oldu*) with a temporal or resultative context produces the inchoative reading. The subject is typically a claim, prediction, or statement.

---

**gerçek olmak** — to be real / to become real

- **Bu gerçek olamaz.**
  *bu gerçek olamaz*
  "This cannot be real."
  [CORE] — Documented in P1b Root 3 item 23. The potential negative *olamaz* (cannot be) is a common construction for expressing disbelief about ontological status. The inchoative reading (*to become real*) is less common than the stative reading (*to be real*) for *gerçek olmak*.

---

### 1c. Resultative construction: *[truth-domain adjective] + hale gelmek*

*Hale gelmek* (literally "to come to a state/condition") is a productive periphrastic construction for expressing the transition into a state. It is more explicitly inchoative than bare *olmak* and is used when the process of transition is foregrounded.

---

- **Belge geçerli hale geldi.**
  *belge geçerli hale geldi*
  "The document became valid / came into a valid state."
  [CORE] — *Hale gelmek* is the most explicitly inchoative construction available for truth-domain adjectives that lack a morphological *-leş-* form. The noun *hal* (state, condition, from Arabic *ḥāl*) + dative + *gelmek* (to come) produces a resultative meaning.

- **İddia kanıtlanmış hale geldi.**
  *iddia kanıtlanmış hale geldi*
  "The claim came to be in a proven state / became established."
  [CORE] — The past participle *kanıtlanmış* (proven) can fill the pre-*hale* slot; this construction emphasizes the transition into the resultant state.

- **Kaynak güvenilir hale geldi.**
  *kaynak güvenilir hale geldi*
  "The source became reliable."
  [CORE] — More natural than bare *güvenilir olmak* for the inchoative reading; documented in 1b above.

---

### 1d. Vindication construction: *haklı çıkmak*

This is a fixed collocation documented in P2a Item 5 and P2b Item 5 that specifically encodes the process of being proven right through events or evidence. It is distinct from the stative *haklı olmak* in foregrounding the process of vindication.

- **Sonunda haklı çıktı.**
  *sonunda haklı çıktı*
  "In the end, he/she was proven right / turned out to be correct."
  [CORE] — *Çıkmak* (to come out, to emerge) encodes the resultative emergence of a state; the construction implies that events or evidence have confirmed the person's position.

- **Tarih onu haklı çıkardı.**
  *tarih onu haklı çıkardı*
  "History proved him/her right."
  [CORE] — The causative reading of *çıkarmak* (causative of *çıkmak*) is available here; see Section 3 (causation) for this pattern.

---

## 2. BECOMING A NON-TRUTH / OPPOSED STATE

### 2a. Morphological: privative and negative forms

Several truth-domain items have lexicalized privative or negative forms that name the opposed state; the transition into these states is expressed by the same inchoative strategies as in Section 1.

---

**geçerliliğini yitirmek** — to lose validity, to expire, to become invalid

- **Pasaport geçerliliğini yitirdi.**
  *pasaport geçerliliğini yitirdi*
  "The passport lost its validity / expired."
  [CORE] — Documented in P2a Item 20 and P2b Item 20. *Yitirmek* (to lose) is the standard verb for expressing the loss of a property; the construction uses the possessed accusative *geçerliliğini* (its validity). This is the primary construction for the transition from *geçerli* to *geçersiz*.

- **Karar geçerliliğini yitirdi.**
  *karar geçerliliğini yitirdi*
  "The decision lost its validity."
  [CORE] — Same construction; typical subjects are documents, decisions, rules, and agreements.

---

**güvenilirliğini yitirmek / kaybetmek** — to lose credibility, to become unreliable

- **Kaynak güvenilirliğini yitirdi.**
  *kaynak güvenilirliğini yitirdi*
  "The source lost its credibility."
  [CORE] — Parallel to *geçerliliğini yitirmek*; *yitirmek* and *kaybetmek* (to lose) are both used in this construction. The possessed accusative *güvenilirliğini* (its reliability/credibility) is the direct object.

---

**meşruiyetini yitirmek / kaybetmek** — to lose legitimacy, to become illegitimate

- **Hükümet meşruiyetini yitirdi.**
  *hükümet meşruiyetini yitirdi*
  "The government lost its legitimacy."
  [CORE] — The same *[property noun + possessive accusative] + yitirmek* construction applies to *meşruiyet* (legitimacy). Typical subjects are governments, institutions, and authorities.

---

**doğruluktan sapmak** — to deviate from truth/correctness

- **Haber zamanla doğruluktan saptı.**
  *haber zamanla doğruluktan saptı*
  "The report gradually deviated from accuracy."
  [CORE] — *Sapmak* (to deviate, to stray) with the ablative of the truth-domain noun (*doğruluktan*, from truth/correctness) expresses the transition away from a truth-related state. The ablative marks the point of departure.

---

**doğru yoldan sapmak** — to deviate from the right path

- **Doğru yoldan saptı.**
  *doğru yoldan saptı*
  "He/she deviated from the right path / went astray."
  [CORE] — Documented in P1b Root 1 item 17 and P2b Item 1. The ablative *doğru yoldan* (from the right path) marks the state being departed from; *sapmak* encodes the transition away from it.

---

**sahte çıkmak** — to turn out to be fake

- **Tablo sahte çıktı.**
  *tablo sahte çıktı*
  "The painting turned out to be a fake."
  [CORE] — Documented in P2b Item 7. *Çıkmak* (to come out, to emerge) encodes the revelation of a state that was previously unknown; the construction implies that something was discovered to be fake rather than that it became fake through a process.

---

**asılsız çıkmak** — to turn out to be unfounded

- **İddia asılsız çıktı.**
  *iddia asılsız çıktı*
  "The claim turned out to be unfounded."
  [CORE] — Parallel to *sahte çıkmak*; *çıkmak* encodes the revelation of the state. Typical subjects are claims, accusations, and rumors.

---

**haksız çıkmak** — to be proven wrong, to turn out to be unjustified

- **Sonunda haksız çıktı.**
  *sonunda haksız çıktı*
  "In the end, he/she was proven wrong / turned out to be unjustified."
  [CORE] — Documented in P5 Opposition 30 as the negative counterpart to *haklı çıkmak*. The same *çıkmak* construction encodes the revelation of the opposed state.

---

### 2b. Constructional: *geçersiz kılınmak* (passive of causative)

- **Sözleşme mahkeme tarafından geçersiz kılındı.**
  *sözleşme mahkeme tarafından geçersiz kılındı*
  "The contract was invalidated by the court."
  [CORE] — The passive of *geçersiz kılmak* (to invalidate; documented in P2a Item 21 and P2b Item 20) expresses the transition into the invalid state from the perspective of the entity undergoing the change. See also Section 3 for the active causative.

---

### 2c. Constructional: *gerçeği çarpıtmak* / *gerçeği gizlemek*

These constructions do not encode a change of state in the truth-domain item itself but rather a change in the relationship between a representation and reality.

- **Gerçeği çarpıttı.**
  *gerçeği çarpıttı*
  "He/she distorted the truth."
  [CORE] — Documented in P1b Root 3 item 29 and P2b Item 2. *Çarpıtmak* (to distort, to twist) encodes the act of misrepresenting what is real; the truth itself is not changed but its representation is.

- **Gerçeği gizledi.**
  *gerçeği gizledi*
  "He/she concealed the truth."
  [CORE] — Documented in P1b Root 3 item 30 and P2b Item 2. *Gizlemek* (to hide, to conceal) encodes the suppression of truth rather than a change in its ontological status.

---

## 3. CAUSING A TRUTH-RELATED STATE

### 3a. Morphological causative: *[base] + -leş- + -tir- + -mek*

---

**meşrulaştırmak** — to legitimize, to make legitimate

- **Şiddeti meşrulaştırmak mümkün değil.**
  *şiddeti meşrulaştırmak mümkün değil*
  "It is not possible to legitimize violence."
  [CORE] — Documented in P2a Item 19 and P4a Construction 5. The agent causes the transition of the object into a legitimate state. Typical objects are actions, authorities, and claims.

- **Seçimler iktidarı meşrulaştırdı.**
  *seçimler iktidarı meşrulaştırdı*
  "The elections legitimized the government."
  [CORE] — The agent (*seçimler*, elections) causes the object (*iktidar*, government/power) to acquire legitimacy.

---

**gerçekleştirmek** — to realize, to carry out, to bring about

- **Projeyi gerçekleştirdiler.**
  *projeyi gerçekleştirdiler*
  "They realized/carried out the project."
  [CORE] — Documented in P1b Root 3 item 4, P2a Item 2, and P4a Construction 5. The agent causes the object to transition from planned/potential to actual. Typical objects are projects, plans, and goals.

---

**özgürleştirmek** — to liberate, to free, to emancipate

- **Eğitim insanları özgürleştirir.**
  *eğitim insanları özgürleştirir*
  "Education liberates people."
  [CORE] — Documented in P2a Item 22. The agent causes the object to transition into a free state.

---

### 3b. Factitive verbalization: *[truth-domain noun] + -la- + -mak*

These verbs encode the act of performing the epistemic action associated with the base noun; they are causative in the sense that an agent brings about a truth-related state in a proposition or claim.

---

**doğrulamak** — to verify, to confirm, to validate

- **Yetkili makam iddiayı doğruladı.**
  *yetkili makam iddiayı doğruladı*
  "The competent authority confirmed the claim."
  [CORE] — Documented throughout P1, P1b, P2a, and P2b. The agent (authority, journalist, institution) causes the claim to acquire the status of being confirmed. Typical agents are official bodies, journalists, and scientific institutions.

- **Sistem kimliği doğruladı.**
  *sistem kimliği doğruladı*
  "The system verified the identity."
  [CORE] — Technical/administrative use; the agent is a system or procedure.

---

**kanıtlamak** — to prove, to demonstrate

- **Avukat müvekkilinin suçsuzluğunu kanıtladı.**
  *avukat müvekkilinin suçsuzluğunu kanıtladı*
  "The lawyer proved his/her client's innocence."
  [CORE] — Documented in P2a Item 14 and P2b Item 14. The agent causes the claim to acquire the status of being proven. Typical agents are lawyers, scientists, and investigators.

---

**ispatlamak** — to prove (formally)

- **Savcı suçu ispat etti.**
  *savcı suçu ispat etti*
  "The prosecutor proved the crime."
  [CORE] — Documented in P2a Item 15 and P2b Item 15. More formal and legal than *kanıtlamak*; the agent causes a fact to be formally established in a legal proceeding.

---

**yanlışlamak** — to falsify, to refute

- **Deney hipotezi yanlışladı.**
  *deney hipotezi yanlışladı*
  "The experiment falsified the hypothesis."
  [CORE] — Documented in P2a Item 6 and P2b Item 13. The agent (experiment, evidence, argument) causes the claim to acquire the status of being shown false. Restricted to academic and philosophical registers.

---

### 3c. Periphrastic causative: *[truth-domain adjective] + kılmak*

---

**geçersiz kılmak** — to invalidate, to render void

- **Mahkeme kararı geçersiz kıldı.**
  *mahkeme kararı geçersiz kıldı*
  "The court invalidated the decision."
  [CORE] — Documented in P2a Item 21 and P2b Item 20. The agent (court, authority) causes the object to transition into the invalid state. Formal register only.

- **Yeni yasa eski düzenlemeyi geçersiz kıldı.**
  *yeni yasa eski düzenlemeyi geçersiz kıldı*
  "The new law invalidated the old regulation."
  [CORE] — The agent is a legal instrument rather than a person.

---

**haklı kılmak** — to justify, to render justified

- **Bu durum müdahaleyi haklı kılar.**
  *bu durum müdahaleyi haklı kılar*
  "This situation justifies the intervention."
  [CORE] — Documented in P2a Item 5 and P4a Construction 5. The agent (a situation, a fact) causes the object (an action, a claim) to acquire the status of being justified. Formal register.

---

**doğru kılmak** — to make correct, to render true

- **Koşullar tahmini doğru kıldı.**
  *koşullar tahmini doğru kıldı*
  "The conditions made the prediction correct."
  [CORE] — The agent (conditions, events) causes the object (a prediction, a claim) to acquire the status of being correct. Less frequent than *doğrulamak* but attested in formal and literary registers.

---

### 3d. Causative of *çıkmak*: *çıkarmak* in vindication constructions

- **Tarih onu haklı çıkardı.**
  *tarih onu haklı çıkardı*
  "History proved him/her right."
  [CORE] — Documented in P5 Opposition 30 notes. *Çıkarmak* (causative of *çıkmak*) encodes the act of causing someone to emerge as vindicated. The agent (*tarih*, history) causes the person to acquire the status of having been right.

- **Olaylar onu haksız çıkardı.**
  *olaylar onu haksız çıkardı*
  "Events proved him/her wrong."
  [CORE] — The negative counterpart; the agent causes the person to emerge as having been unjustified.

---

### 3e. Causing deception: *yanıltmak* and *aldatmak*

These verbs encode the act of causing someone to hold a false belief; they are causative in the epistemic sense.

- **Yanıltıcı reklam tüketiciyi yanılttı.**
  *yanıltıcı reklam tüketiciyi yanılttı*
  "The misleading advertisement misled the consumer."
  [CORE] — *Yanıltmak* (causative of *yanılmak*, to err) encodes causing someone to fall into error. Documented in P1 item 28.

- **Aldatıcı davranış güveni sarstı.**
  *aldatıcı davranış güveni sarstı*
  "The deceptive conduct shook trust."
  [CORE] — *Aldatıcı* (deceptive) is the adjectival form; the base verb *aldatmak* (to deceive) encodes intentional causation of false belief. Documented in P1 item 29.

---

### 3f. Causing belief: *inandırmak*

- **Avukat jüriyi inandırdı.**
  *avukat jüriyi inandırdı*
  "The lawyer convinced the jury."
  [CORE] — Documented in P2a Item 18. *İnandırmak* (causative of *inanmak*, to believe) encodes the act of causing someone to believe something. The agent causes the object (person or group) to transition into a state of belief.

- **Onu inandırmak zor.**
  *onu inandırmak zor*
  "It is difficult to convince him/her."
  [CORE] — Infinitive used as subject nominal; the construction expresses the difficulty of causing the belief-state.

---

## 4. STABILITY AND RESISTANCE TO CHANGE

### 4a. Maintaining a truth-related state: *[property noun] + korumak*

- **Kaynak güvenilirliğini korudu.**
  *kaynak güvenilirliğini korudu*
  "The source maintained its credibility."
  [CORE] — *Korumak* (to protect, to maintain) with the possessed accusative of the property noun expresses the preservation of a truth-related state against potential change. Parallel to the *yitirmek* construction in Section 2a.

- **Karar geçerliliğini koruyor.**
  *karar geçerliliğini koruyor*
  "The decision is maintaining its validity."
  [CORE] — Present progressive; the construction expresses ongoing stability of the valid state.

- **Meşruiyetini korudu.**
  *meşruiyetini korudu*
  "It maintained its legitimacy."
  [CORE] — Same construction applied to *meşruiyet* (legitimacy).

---

### 4b. Remaining in a truth-related state: *[truth-domain adjective] + kalmak*

- **İddia kanıtlanmamış kaldı.**
  *iddia kanıtlanmamış kaldı*
  "The claim remained unproven."
  [CORE] — *Kalmak* (to remain, to stay) with a past participle or adjective expresses the persistence of a state without change. The negated participle *kanıtlanmamış* (unproven) fills the pre-*kalmak* slot.

- **Belge geçerli kaldı.**
  *belge geçerli kaldı*
  "The document remained valid."
  [CORE] — *Kalmak* with a truth-domain adjective expresses the continuation of the valid state.

- **Kaynak güvenilir kaldı.**
  *kaynak güvenilir kaldı*
  "The source remained reliable."
  [CORE] — Same construction applied to *güvenilir*.

---

### 4c. Resistance to falsification: *yanlışlanamamak*

- **Hipotez yanlışlanamadı.**
  *hipotez yanlışlanamadı*
  "The hypothesis could not be falsified."
  [CORE] — The negative potential passive *yanlışlanamadı* (could not be falsified) expresses resistance to the epistemic action of falsification. Documented in P2a Item 6 as part of the *yanlışlanabilir* / *yanlışlanabilirlik* family.

- **İddia ispat edilemez.**
  *iddia ispat edilemez*
  "The claim cannot be proven."
  [CORE] — Documented in P2b Item 15. The negative potential *edilemez* (cannot be done) expresses the impossibility of causing the proven state.

---

### 4d. Persistence of truth: *doğruluğunu korumak*

- **Bu ilke doğruluğunu koruyor.**
  *bu ilke doğruluğunu koruyor*
  "This principle maintains its truth/correctness."
  [CORE] — *Doğruluğunu korumak* (to maintain one's truth/correctness) expresses the ongoing validity of a claim or principle. The possessed accusative *doğruluğunu* (its truth/correctness) is the direct object of *korumak*.
