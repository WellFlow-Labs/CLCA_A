## Summary Notes

1. All ten construction types identified in P4a receive a [CORE] rating with the single exception of the morphological causative (Construction 5, *satyāpayati*), which is rated [MARGINAL] on the basis of its restricted productivity across the truth-domain vocabulary.

2. No construction in this inventory is rated [UNCERTAIN]; all patterns are supported by attested examples as reported in P4a.

3. The concessive construction (10) is the only pattern with an explicit lexical restriction: *satyam* is the sole truth-domain term attested in this role with consistent frequency.

4. The agentive *-tṛ* nominalization (7) is register-restricted to epistemological technical prose and is not freely productive across the vocabulary domain, distinguishing it from the *-vādin* compound pattern, which is productive across legal, ethical, and philosophical registers.

5. Alternate constructions listed are drawn exclusively from examples attested within P4a; no additional alternates have been generated.
