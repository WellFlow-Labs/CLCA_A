# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P1b ROOT EXPANSION
# Language: Turkish (tr)

---

## Step 1 — Identify Central Roots

---

**Root 1**

**Form:** doğru / doğr-
**Transliteration:** doğru / doğr-
**P1 participation:** 3 items — *doğru* (item 1), *doğruluk* (item 12), *doğrulamak* (item 13); also implicitly *yanlış* as antonym defined against it
**Core meaning contribution:** Straightness, directness, correctness — the primary native Turkic root encoding correspondence between a claim or action and a standard (factual, moral, or normative). The root supplies the primary native Turkic stem for evaluating statements, directions, and conduct as accurate or true. Highly productive in derivation and compounding.

---

**Root 2**

**Form:** hak / hak-
**Transliteration:** hak / hak-
**P1 participation:** 5 items — *hak* (item 4), *haklı* (item 5), *hakikat* (item 3), *hakiki* (item 19/24), *hakkaniyetli* and *haksız* as derivatives; also *sahih* (item 20, via Arabic *ṣaḥīḥ*, cognate domain though not morphologically related)
**Note:** *hakikat* and *hakiki* are Arabic-origin forms built on the same Arabic root *ḥ-q-q* as *hak*; they function as a morphological family in Turkish even though the Arabic internal structure is not natively transparent to Turkish speakers. Turkish speakers and lexicographers treat *hak*, *hakikat*, and *hakiki* as a related cluster.
**Core meaning contribution:** Right, justice, entitlement, truth — from Arabic *ḥaqq*. Contributes both epistemic senses (what is true, what is real) and normative senses (what is rightful, what is owed). Highly productive across legal, moral, philosophical, and everyday registers.

---

**Root 3**

**Form:** gerçek / gerçek-
**Transliteration:** gerçek / gerçek-
**P1 participation:** 2–3 items — *gerçek* (item 2), *gerçeklik* (item 11), *gerçekçi* (item 11); also the derivational family noted under item 2 (*gerçekleşmek*)
**Core meaning contribution:** Reality, genuineness, factual existence — the primary root encoding ontological correspondence (what is real as opposed to fake, illusory, or fictional). Productive in compounds and derivations across everyday, philosophical, legal, and technical registers.

## Step 2 — Expand Each Root

---

### ROOT 1: doğru / doğr-

---

**1.**
**Form:** doğru
**Transliteration:** doğru
**Morpheme breakdown:** *doğr-* (root: straight, correct) + *-u* (adjectival/nominal ending)
**Natural English translation:** straight, correct, true, right, direct
**Context of use:** Used as adjective, adverb, and noun across all registers. *Bu doğru mu?* ("Is this correct?"); *doğru yol* ("the right path/road").
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**2.**
**Form:** doğruluk
**Transliteration:** doğruluk
**Morpheme breakdown:** *doğru* (correct/straight) + *-luk* (abstract noun suffix)
**Natural English translation:** truthfulness, correctness, accuracy, integrity
**Context of use:** Used in philosophical, moral, and everyday contexts to name the property of being true or correct; also used to describe personal integrity in the sense of consistency between word and deed. Used in both epistemic contexts (accuracy of a measurement or statement) and moral contexts (honesty of a person's character). *Doğruluğa bağlılık* (commitment to truth/integrity); *Doğruluk her zaman ödüllendirilmez.* ("Truthfulness is not always rewarded.")
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**3.**
**Form:** doğrulamak
**Transliteration:** doğrulamak
**Morpheme breakdown:** *doğru* (correct) + *-la-* (factitive/verbalizer) + *-mak* (infinitive)
**Natural English translation:** to verify, to confirm, to validate, to authenticate
**Context of use:** Used in scientific, journalistic, administrative, and legal registers for the act of checking and confirming that something is accurate or true. *Bilgiyi doğrulamak* (to verify information) is a standard collocation. *Haberi doğrulamak gerekiyor.* ("The news needs to be verified.")
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**4.**
**Form:** doğrulama
**Transliteration:** doğrulama
**Morpheme breakdown:** *doğru* + *-la-* + *-ma* (verbal noun suffix)
**Natural English translation:** verification, confirmation, validation, authentication
**Context of use:** The nominalized form of *doğrulamak*; used in technical, administrative, and journalistic contexts. *Kimlik doğrulama* (identity verification/authentication) is a standard term in digital security and administrative procedure. *Doğrulama süreci* (verification process).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**5.**
**Form:** doğrulanmak
**Transliteration:** doğrulanmak
**Morpheme breakdown:** *doğru* + *-la-* + *-n-* (passive) + *-mak*
**Natural English translation:** to be verified, to be confirmed, to be validated
**Context of use:** Passive form; used in scientific and journalistic registers when a claim, identity, or fact is confirmed by an external process or authority. *İddia doğrulandı* (the claim was confirmed/verified). *İddia henüz doğrulanmadı.* ("The claim has not yet been confirmed.")
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**6.**
**Form:** doğrulatmak
**Transliteration:** doğrulatmak
**Morpheme breakdown:** *doğru* + *-la-* + *-t-* (causative) + *-mak*
**Natural English translation:** to have (something) verified, to cause to be confirmed
**Context of use:** Causative form; used when a person causes or arranges for something to be verified by another party. *Belgeyi doğrulatmak* (to have the document verified).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**7.**
**Form:** doğrultmak
**Transliteration:** doğrultmak
**Morpheme breakdown:** *doğru* + *-lt-* (causative/directional) + *-mak*
**Natural English translation:** to straighten, to correct, to align, to aim (a weapon)
**Context of use:** Used in physical and figurative senses: physically straightening something, correcting a course of action, or aiming a weapon or instrument. *Silahı doğrultmak* (to aim the weapon); *yanlışı doğrultmak* (to correct the error). *Silahı hedefe doğrulttular.* ("They aimed the weapon at the target.")
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**8.**
**Form:** doğrulmak
**Transliteration:** doğrulmak
**Morpheme breakdown:** *doğru* + *-l-* (intransitive/reflexive) + *-mak*
**Natural English translation:** to straighten up, to sit up straight, to rise upright
**Context of use:** Used in physical contexts to describe a person or object becoming upright or straight. *Yataktan doğruldu.* ("He/she sat up straight in bed.")
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**9.**
**Form:** doğrultu
**Transliteration:** doğrultu
**Morpheme breakdown:** *doğru* + *-ltu* (nominal suffix, directional; deverbal/denominal noun suffix, variant of *-ltı*)
**Natural English translation:** direction, alignment, line (of direction)
**Context of use:** Used in technical, navigational, and everyday contexts to indicate a direction or line of orientation. *Kuzey doğrultusu* (northward direction); *doğru doğrultuda ilerlemek* (to advance in the right direction). *Kuzey doğrultusunda ilerlediler.* ("They advanced in the direction of north.")
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**10.**
**Form:** doğrudan
**Transliteration:** doğrudan
**Morpheme breakdown:** *doğru* + *-dan* (ablative/adverbial suffix, here lexicalized as adverb) — "from the straight/direct"
**Natural English translation:** directly, straightforwardly, without intermediary
**Context of use:** Used as an adverb in everyday, administrative, and journalistic registers to indicate directness or the absence of mediation. *Doğrudan iletişim* (direct communication); *doğrudan seçim* (direct election). *Doğrudan konuya gelelim.* ("Let's get straight to the point.")
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**11.**
**Form:** doğrudan doğruya
**Transliteration:** doğrudan doğruya
**Morpheme breakdown:** *doğrudan* + *doğruya* (dative of *doğru*) — reduplicated directional/adverbial construction
**Natural English translation:** directly, straight, point-blank, without any intermediary
**Context of use:** A reinforced adverbial phrase used in everyday and formal speech to emphasize complete directness or immediacy. More emphatic than *doğrudan* alone. *Doğrudan doğruya söylemek* (to say it straight out). *Doğrudan doğruya ona söyledim.* ("I told him/her directly, straight out.")
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**12.**
**Form:** doğru dürüst
**Transliteration:** doğru dürüst
**Morpheme breakdown:** *doğru* (correct/straight) + *dürüst* (upright, honest — from Persian *durust*, used here as a rhyming/reinforcing compound)
**Natural English translation:** properly, decently, adequately; (of a person) honest and upright
**Context of use:** A fixed collocation used in everyday speech, often with a slightly negative framing to indicate that something is not being done properly. *Doğru dürüst çalışmıyor* (it's not working properly); *doğru dürüst bir insan* (a decent, honest person). *Doğru dürüst bir iş yapamıyor.* ("He/she can't do a proper job.")
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**13.**
**Form:** dürüst
**Transliteration:** dürüst
**Morpheme breakdown:** from Persian *dorost* / *durust* — correct, sound, honest (historically related to *doğru* through semantic convergence, now an independent lexeme; closely associated with *doğru* in the semantic field)
**Natural English translation:** honest, upright, straightforward, decent
**Context of use:** Used to characterize persons and conduct as honest and morally upright. Frequently collocates with *doğru* in the binomial *doğru dürüst*. *Dürüst bir insan* (an honest person).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**14.**
**Form:** dürüstlük
**Transliteration:** dürüstlük
**Morpheme breakdown:** *dürüst* + *-lük* (abstract noun suffix)
**Natural English translation:** honesty, integrity, uprightness
**Context of use:** Used in moral, professional, and everyday evaluative contexts to name the quality of being honest and straightforward. *Dürüstlük ilkesi* (the principle of honesty/integrity). *Dürüstlük en önemli erdemlerden biridir.* ("Honesty is one of the most important virtues.")
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

**Context of use:** Used in technical and administrative contexts to describe a person, process, or mechanism that verifies or confirms. *Doğrulayıcı kanıt* (confirmatory evidence); *Doğrulayıcı kod* ("verification code") is common in digital/technical usage.

> **Note:** The *doğru-* root is highly productive. These are noted in Step 3.

### ROOT 2: hak / hak- (and its Arabic-origin derivatives hakikat, hakiki)

---

**1.**
**Form:** hak
**Transliteration:** hak
**Morpheme breakdown:** from Arabic *ḥaqq* — right, truth, due
**Natural English translation:** right, entitlement, truth, justice
**Context of use:** Used as a standalone noun meaning "right" or "entitlement" (*insan hakları*, human rights) and in philosophical/religious contexts meaning "truth" or "the Real." Foundational to a large derivational family. *Hak aramak* (to seek one's rights); *
