| totoo | Totoo ba iyon? | Totoo ba iyon? | Is that true? / Is that real? / Really? | The construction *totoo ba* + demonstrative/noun phrase is the standard interrogative formula for challenging or requesting confirmation of a claim. The particle *ba* marks the utterance as a yes/no question; *iyon* (distal demonstrative) is the most common variant, though *iyan* (proximal-to-addressee) also occurs. The construction as a whole functions as a fixed epistemic inquiry formula across all registers; it functions both as a genuine information-seeking question and as an expression of surprise or disbelief. Cross-referenced from P1b. | [CORE] |
| totoo | Sa totoo lang, hindi ko alam. | Sa totoo lang, hindi ko alam. | To tell the truth, I don't know. / Honestly, I don't know. | *Sa totoo lang* functions as a conventionalized discourse-initial marker of candor, introducing a statement the speaker presents as frank or unguarded. Documented in P1b and cross-referenced here as the standard topic-fronted truth-framing construction in formal and journalistic registers, as well as in spoken Tagalog. | [CORE] |
