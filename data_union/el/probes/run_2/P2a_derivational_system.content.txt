# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2a Derivational System
# Language: Greek (el)

---

## Preliminary Note on Scope

This step documents attested derivational morphology for the 30 P1 base forms. Forms already fully documented in P1b (e.g., ψεύτης, ψεύδομαι, αληθεύω, πιστεύω, etc.) are cross-referenced rather than re-documented in full, to avoid redundancy. The focus here is on derivations not already captured in P1 or P1b, plus systematic documentation of the derivational profile of each P1 item.

Where a P1 item is itself a derivation of another P1 item (e.g., αλήθεια → αληθινός), the relationship is noted but not re-expanded unless additional derived forms exist.

## P1 Items — Derivational Profiles

### 1. αληθής (alithís)

---

**Derived Form 1**

1. **Base Form:** αληθής
2. **Derived Form:** αλήθεια
3. **Transliteration:** alíthia
4. **Literal Breakdown:** αληθ- + nominal suffix -εια; "the state of being unconcealed"
5. **Natural English translation:** truth, truthfulness
6. **Usage:** The primary abstract noun derived from αληθής. Already documented as P1 item 2 and expanded in P1b. Cross-reference: P1 #2, P1b Root 2 #1.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2**

1. **Base Form:** αληθής
2. **Derived Form:** αληθώς
3. **Transliteration:** alithós
4. **Literal Breakdown:** αληθής + adverbial suffix -ώς; "in a true manner"
5. **Natural English translation:** truly, in truth, verily
6. **Usage:** The adverbial form of αληθής. Used in formal, liturgical, and philosophical registers. Most commonly encountered in the liturgical exchange *Αληθώς ανέστη* ("He is truly risen"), the response to *Χριστός ανέστη* at Easter. Less common in everyday speech, where αληθινά is preferred.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 3**

1. **Base Form:** αληθής
2. **Derived Form:** αληθινός
3. **Transliteration:** alithinós
4. **Literal Breakdown:** αλήθεια + adjectival suffix -ινός; "of or belonging to truth"
5. **Natural English translation:** true, genuine, real
6. **Usage:** Already documented as P1 item 3 and expanded in P1b. Cross-reference: P1 #3, P1b Root 2 #12–16.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 4**

1. **Base Form:** αληθής
2. **Derived Form:** αληθεύω
3. **Transliteration:** alithévo
4. **Literal Breakdown:** αληθ- + verbal suffix -εύω; "to be truthful, to speak truth"
5. **Natural English translation:** to tell the truth, to be accurate
6. **Usage:** Already documented in P1b Root 2 #2. Cross-reference: P1b Root 2 #2.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

*Note: The derivational family of αληθής is among the most productive in this domain. The four forms above (αλήθεια, αληθώς, αληθινός, αληθεύω) represent the well-attested core. Additional philosophical compounds (αληθοφάνεια, αληθοφανής) are documented in P1b Root 2 #3–4.*

### 4. γνήσιος (gnísios)

---

**Derived Form 1**

1. **Base Form:** γνήσιος
2. **Derived Form:** γνησιότητα
3. **Transliteration:** gnisiótita
4. **Literal Breakdown:** γνήσιος + abstract nominal suffix -τητα; "the quality of being genuine"
5. **Natural English translation:** genuineness, authenticity, legitimacy
6. **Usage:** The primary abstract noun for the quality of being genuine or authentic. Used in legal, commercial, and cultural contexts: *έλεγχος γνησιότητας* ("authenticity check"), *πιστοποίηση γνησιότητας* ("authentication of genuineness"). Common in administrative and journalistic registers.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2**

1. **Base Form:** γνήσιος
2. **Derived Form:** γνησίως
3. **Transliteration:** gnisío̱s
4. **Literal Breakdown:** γνήσιος + adverbial suffix -ως; "in a genuine manner"
5. **Natural English translation:** genuinely, legitimately, authentically
6. **Usage:** Formal adverbial form of γνήσιος. Used in legal and official documents to indicate that something is done or produced in a genuine or legitimate manner. Less common in everyday speech.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 3**

1. **Base Form:** γνήσιος
2. **Derived Form:** γνησιοποιώ
3. **Transliteration:** gnisiopoió
4. **Literal Breakdown:** γνήσιος + ποιώ (I make) + verbal suffix; "to make genuine"
5. **Natural English translation:** to authenticate, to certify as genuine
6. **Usage:** Used in administrative and legal contexts to refer to the act of officially certifying a document or signature as genuine. *Γνησιοποίηση υπογραφής* ("certification of signature") is a standard administrative procedure in Greece.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 4**

1. **Base Form:** γνήσιος
2. **Derived Form:** γνησιοποίηση
3. **Transliteration:** gnisiopoíisi
4. **Literal Breakdown:** γνησιοποιώ + nominal suffix -ση; "the act of making genuine"
5. **Natural English translation:** authentication, certification of genuineness
6. **Usage:** The nominal form corresponding to γνησιοποιώ. Used in administrative and legal contexts. *Γνησιοποίηση υπογραφής* is the standard term for the official certification of a signature at a public authority (KEP, notary, etc.).
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### 6. ακριβής (akrivís)

---

**Derived Form 1**

1. **Base Form:** ακριβής
2. **Derived Form:** ακρίβεια
3. **Transliteration:** akrívia
4. **Literal Breakdown:** ακριβ- + nominal suffix -εια; "the state of being exact"
5. **Natural English translation:** accuracy, precision, exactness; also: high cost, expensiveness
6. **Usage:** The primary abstract noun from ακριβής. In the epistemic domain, *ακρίβεια* means accuracy or precision: *η ακρίβεια μιας μέτρησης* ("the accuracy of a measurement"). In everyday usage, the same form also means "high prices, cost of living" — a semantic split that is well-established and context-dependent.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2**

1. **Base Form:** ακριβής
2. **Derived Form:** ακριβώς
3. **Transliteration:** akrivós
4. **Literal Breakdown:** ακριβής + adverbial suffix -ώς; "in an exact manner"
5. **Natural English translation:** exactly, precisely, accurately
6. **Usage:** A very common adverb used to indicate exactness or to confirm a statement. *Ακριβώς!* functions as an affirmative response meaning "Exactly!" or "Precisely!" in everyday conversation. Also used in technical and formal registers for precise specification.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 3**

1. **Base Form:** ακριβής
2. **Derived Form:** ακριβολογία
3. **Transliteration:** akrivología
4. **Literal Breakdown:** ακριβ- + λόγος (word, speech) + -ία; "precise speech"
5. **Natural English translation:** precision of language, exactness of expression
6. **Usage:** Used in linguistic, rhetorical, and editorial contexts to refer to the quality of using language with great precision. *Η ακριβολογία είναι αρετή του επιστήμονα* ("Precision of language is a virtue of the scientist"). More common in formal and academic registers.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 4**

1. **Base Form:** ακριβής
2. **Derived Form:** ακριβολόγος
3. **Transliteration:** akrivológos
4. **Literal Breakdown:** ακριβ- + λόγος + agent suffix; "one who speaks precisely"
5. **Natural English translation:** precise in speech, exact in expression
6. **Usage:** Applied to a person who uses language with great care and precision. Used in formal and literary registers as a term of praise for careful, exact expression.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]
