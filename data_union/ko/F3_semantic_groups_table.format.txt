| Factual correspondence markers | These terms are used primarily to evaluate whether a specific claim, report, or statement matches what actually happened or is the case. They appear in constructions that affirm, deny, or investigate correspondence between a proposition and a state of affairs. They are the items most commonly used in the construction 사실이다 / 사실이 아니다 and in collocations with 확인하다 (to verify), 왜곡하다 (to distort), and 직시하다 (to face squarely). They are contrasted with each other (사실 vs. 허위사실) and with opinion or interpretation. | 사실 (sasil); 사실이다 (sasil-ida); 사실이 아니다 (sasil-i anida); 사실과 다르다 (sasil-gwa dareuda); 사실무근 (sasil-mugeun); 사실로 드러나다 (sasil-ro deureonada); 사실로 밝혀지다 (sasillo balkyeojida); 사실을 확인하다 (sasil-eul hwagin-hada); 사실을 직시하다 (sasil-eul jiksihada); 사실을 왜곡하다 (sasil-eul waegokada); 사실 여부를 따지다 (sasil yeobu-reul ttajida); 사실관계를 파악하다 (sasil gwangyereul paakada); 엄연한 사실 (eomyeonhan sasil); 실제로 일어난 일 (siljero ileonan il); 허위사실 (heowi-sasil); 허위 (heowi); 허위로 밝혀지다 (heowiro balkyeojida); 허위사실 유포 (heowi sasil yupo); 사실확인 (sasil hwagin); 사실상 (sasilsang) | 허위사실 belongs here because it specifically concerns false claims presented as factual, but 허위 more broadly belongs to Group 8; 사실상 also belongs to Group 11 when used to draw the contrast between de facto and formal status; 실제 (silje) overlaps here when used in the construction 실제와 다르다 (to differ from reality), but 실제 also belongs to Group 11; 거짓 (geojit) and 거짓말 (geojitmal) overlap with this group but are treated separately in Group 6 | 사실 is the central item in this group. 사실 also functions as a sentence-level adverb ("in fact / actually"), which is a pragmatic extension rather than a separate grouping. 허위 appears almost exclusively in formal, legal, and journalistic registers; 사실 appears across all registers. |
| Truth disclosure and investigation markers | These terms appear primarily in contexts where the central activity is uncovering, hiding, or formally establishing what is actually the case — particularly in journalistic, legal, and political discourse. They cluster around collocations with 밝히다 (to reveal), 규명하다 (to investigate and clarify), 숨기다 (to conceal), 드러나다 (to come to light), and 외면하다 (to turn away from). The emphasis is on the process of bringing truth into the open or preventing it from being known. The focus is on the process of establishing or suppressing truth rather than on evaluating a specific proposition. | 진실 (jinsil); 진실을 밝히다 (jinsil-eul balkhida); 진실을 숨기다 (jinsil-eul sumgida); 진실을 왜곡하다 (jinsil-eul waegokada); 진실을 외면하다 (jinsil-eul oemyeonhada); 진실이 밝혀지다 (jinsil-i balkhyeojida); 진실이 드러나다 (jinsil-i deureonada); 진실과 거짓을 가리다 (jinsil-gwa geojit-eul garida); 진실규명 (jinsil gyumyeong); 진실화해위원회 (jinsil-hwahae-wiwonhoe); 진실의 순간 (jinsil-ui sungan); 진상 (jinsang); 진상 규명 (jinsang gyumyeong); 진상 조사 (jinsang josa); 거짓이 드러나다 (geojit-i deureonada); 진위를 둘러싼 논란 (jinwi-reul dulleossan nollan); 실체를 밝히다 (silche-reul balkida); 실체를 드러내다 (silche-reul deureonada); 실태 (siltae); 실태조사 (siltae josa) | 사실 (Group 1) overlaps here in some collocations (사실을 은폐하다, 사실을 왜곡하다); 실체 and 실태 overlap here but extend into other contexts (실체 into philosophical discourse, 실태 into policy and administrative surveys) | 진실 and 진상 are closely related in this group but differ in typical scale and register: 진실 appears in both everyday and institutional contexts, while 진상 appears almost exclusively in formal investigative contexts. The verb 밝히다 (to illuminate/reveal) is the characteristic verb across this group, appearing with 진실, 진상, and 실체. 진실화해위원회 is a fixed institutional name rather than a productive evaluative term; it participates in this group by reference but does not itself function as an evaluative expression. |
| Inner-state authenticity markers | These terms are used to evaluate whether a person's outward expressions — apologies, political commitments, artistic works, declarations of feeling — reflect genuine inner conviction or are performed, strategic, or hollow. They appear in constructions like 진정성 있다/없다 (to have/lack authenticity) and 진정성 있는 사과 (a sincere apology). The evaluative question is not whether a proposition is true but whether a person's expression is genuine. The primary contrast is between genuine inner feeling and outward performance. | 진정성 (jinjeonseong); 진정성 있다 (jinjeonseong itda); 진정성 없다 (jinjeonseong eopda); 진정성 있는 사과 (jinjeonseong inneun saga); 진정성을 의심받다 (jinjeonseong-eul uisim-batda); 진정성을 보여주다 (jinjeonseong-eul boyeojuda); 진정성 논란에 휩싸이다 (jinjeonseong nollan-e hwipsaida); 진정성이 느껴지다 (jinjeonseong-i neukkkyeojida); 진정성에 의문을 제기하다 (jinjeonseong-e uimun-eul jegi-hada); 진정 (jinjeong); 진정한 (jinjeongan); 진정으로 (jinjeongeuro); 진정으로 사과하다 (jinjeong-euro sagwa-hada); 진정한 의미에서 (jinjeonghan uimieseo); 진심 (jinsim); 진심으로 (jinsimeuro); 진실하다 (jinsil-hada); 진실되다 (jinsil-doeda); 진실성 (jinsilseong); 진지하다 (jinji hada); 진지하게 (jinji hage); 위선 (wiseon); 위선자 (wiseonja); 위선적 (wiseonjeok); 참되다 (cham-doeda) | 진심 also participates in Group 11 in the sense that 진심 contrasts genuine feeling with performed or hollow expression | 진정성 is the most prominent term in contemporary public discourse for this evaluative function. 진심 and 진심으로 are more interpersonal and emotional in register, while 진정성 is more evaluative and public-facing. 위선 (hypocrisy) belongs here because its defining contrast is between professed virtue and actual inner character — the same inner-outer gap that 진정성 addresses, but from the negative side. 진실하다 and 진실되다 overlap with Group 6 (honesty in speech) but are placed here because their primary evaluative target in the data is a person's character or inner state rather than the truth-value of a specific statement. 참되다 appears primarily in literary and religious registers evaluating genuine character. |
| Genuineness-versus-counterfeit markers | These terms are used to evaluate whether a physical item, document, artwork, or identity is what it purports to be, as opposed to being a copy, forgery, or imitation. They appear in contexts of authentication, fraud investigation, and consumer protection. The contrast they mark is between 진짜 (genuine) and 가짜 (fake), or between 진품 (authentic item) and 위조품 (forged item). The evaluative question is one of identity and provenance rather than propositional truth. | 진짜 (jinjja); 가짜 (gajja); 진짜배기 (jinjja-baegi); 진짜인지 가짜인지 모르다 (jinjja-inji gajja-inji moreuda); 진짜와 가짜를 구별하다 (jinjja wa gajjareul gubyeolhada); 가짜로 판명되다 (gajja-ro panmyeong-doeda); 가짜를 진짜로 속이다 (gajja-reul jinjja-ro sogida); 가짜 뉴스 (gajja nyuseu); 가품 (gapum); 진품 (jinpum); 진본 (jinbon); 진본 여부를 감정하다 (jinbon yeobu-reul gamjeong-hada); 진품과 모조품을 구별하다 (jinpum-gwa mojopumeul gubyeol-hada); 위조 (wijo); 위조하다 (wijo-hada); 위조품 (wijopum); 위조문서 (wijo-munseo); 위조지폐 (wijo-jipye); 위조 화폐 (wijo hwapye); 위조작 (wijak); 위조 여부를 감정하다 (wijo yeobureul gamjeong-hada); 위조로 판명되다 (wijo-ro panmyeong-doeda); 위조죄 (wijojoe); 위조범 (wijobom); 모조 (mojo); 모조품 (mojopum); 진위 (jinwi); 진위를 가리다 (jinwi-reul garida); 진위 여부 (jinwi yeobu); 진위 논란 (jinwi nollan) | 진위 can also frame propositional questions and overlaps with Group 1; 위조 overlaps with Group 8 (formal falsity designators) given that forged documents are a legal matter | 위조 (forgery) and 모조 (imitation) are distinguished in usage: 위조 carries a stronger implication of criminal intent and appears almost exclusively in legal and criminal contexts, while 모조 can describe authorized replicas or imitations without necessarily implying fraud. 진짜/가짜 are the most colloquial pair; 진품/가품 are more formal and domain-specific to commerce and art. 진짜 also functions as an intensifying adverb (진짜 좋다, "really good") that has no connection to this group; that use is not represented here. 위조 is placed primarily here because its core contrast is genuineness-versus-counterfeit rather than true-versus-false statement. |
| Legal validity and invalidity markers | These terms appear primarily in legal, administrative, and contractual contexts where the question is whether a document, contract, action, or declaration has legal force or is operative. They are used in collocations with 계약 (contract), 선거 (election), 기간 (period), and verbs such as 선언하다 (to declare), 되다 (to become), and 처리되다 (to be processed). The central contrast is between 유효 (valid, in force) and 무효 (invalid, void). | 유효 (yuhyo); 유효하다 (yuhyo hada); 유효성 (yuhyoseong); 유효 기간 (yuhyo gigan); 유효 기간이 지나다 (yuhyo gigani jinaeda); 여전히 유효하다 (yeojeonhi yuhyohada); 계약이 유효하다 (gyeyagi yuhyohada); 무효 (muhyo); 무효화하다 (muhyohwa hada); 무효를 선언하다 (muhyoreul seoneonhada); 계약을 무효로 하다 (gyeyageul muhyoro hada); 선거가 무효가 되다 (seongeoga muhyoga doeda); 무효 처리되다 (muhyo cheoridoeda); 무효 선언 (muhyo seoneon) | 합법 / 불법 (legal / illegal) are related but distinct: they evaluate conformity to law rather than operative force; 타당 (valid/reasonable) overlaps with this group in academic and policy contexts but is not primarily a legal-force term | A contract can be 합법적 (lawful) but still 무효 (void) on procedural grounds, and vice versa. |
| Honesty-in-conduct markers | These terms are used to evaluate persons or their conduct with respect to whether they tell the truth and refrain from deception. They appear in moral, educational, and interpersonal contexts and participate in constructions like 정직하게 말하다 (to speak honestly) and 정직이 최선의 방책이다 (honesty is the best policy). They also evaluate speech acts as involving deliberate deception — the intentional production of false statements to mislead. The moral charge is prominent; these terms evaluate conduct, not just factual status. | 정직 (jeongjik); 정직하다 (jeongjik-hada); 정직한 (jeongjikan); 정직하게 (jeongjik-hage); 정직성 (jeongjikseong); 정직하게 말하다 (jeongjik-age malhada); 정직하게 살다 (jeongjik-hage salda); 정직히 (jeongjiki); 정직이 최선의 방책이다 (jeongjik-i choesonui bangchaek-ida); 정직하고 성실하다 (jeongjik-ago seongsil-hada); 정직함을 잃다 (jeongjikameul ilta); 부정직 (bujeongjik); 부정직하다 (bujeongjik-hada); 거짓말 (geojitmal); 거짓말하다 (geojitmal-hada); 거짓말쟁이 (geojitmal-jaengi); 거짓말이 들통나다 (geojitmal-i deultongnaDA); 거짓말이 들키다 (geojitmari deulkida); 거짓말을 밥 먹듯 하다 (geojitmal-eul bap meokdeut hada); 새빨간 거짓말 (saeppalgan geojitmal); 거짓말로 일관하다 (geojitmallo ilgwan-hada); 거짓말인 줄 알면서도 (geojitmalin jul almyeonseodo); 거짓말도 방편이다 (geojitmal-do bangpyeon-ida); 거짓 없이 말하다 (geojit eopsi malhada); 거짓 없이 (geojit eopsi); 거짓 (geojit); 진실을 말하다 (jinsil-eul malhada); 참말 (chammal); 올바르다 (olbareuda); 올바른 (olbareun); 올바르게 (olbareuge) | 진실을 말하다 overlaps with Group 2 (truth as something to be revealed or concealed); 올바르다 overlaps with Group 10 (correctness of reasoning and understanding: 올바른 판단, 올바르게 이해하다) and with moral-civic conduct evaluation | 거짓말 is placed here rather than in Group 8 because its register is everyday speech and its evaluative target is a person's act of deception, not a formal legal designation. The proverbial expression 거짓말도 방편이다 is notable as the only item in the data that frames deception as potentially justified. 진실을 말하다 is placed here because the collocation emphasizes the speaker's honest conduct. 거짓 as a modifier in noun phrases (거짓 정보, 거짓 희망, 거짓 평화) extends into domains not covered by any group above. |
| Lawfulness and illegality markers | These terms are used when the question is whether an action, practice, or state of affairs conforms to law. They appear in collocations with 취득하다 (to acquire), 절차 (procedure), 경계 (boundary), and in constructions such as 합법과 불법의 경계 (the boundary between legal and illegal). The central contrast is between 합법 (lawful) and 불법 (unlawful). | 합법 (hapbeop); 합법적 (hapbeopjeok); 합법성 (hapbeopseong); 합법화하다 (hapbeophwa hada); 합법화 (hapbeophwa); 합법적으로 취득하다 (hapbeopjeogеuro chwideukada); 합법과 불법의 경계 (hapbeop gwa bulbeop ui gyeonggye); 합법을 가장하다 (hapbeobeul gajanghada); 합법성 여부를 따지다 (hapbeopseong yeobureul ttajida); 불법 (bulbeop); 실정법 (siljeongbeop) | (none stated) | 합법 / 불법 evaluate conformity to existing law, while 정당 (Group 8) evaluates whether something is morally or procedurally justified — these can come apart (something can be 합법적 but 부당, or 불법 but 정당하다 in some ethical arguments). 실정법 is included here because it refers to law as it actually exists, which is the standard against which 합법 / 불법 are measured. |
| Formal falsity designators and justification-legitimacy markers | These terms appear primarily in legal, administrative, and journalistic registers to designate statements, documents, or representations as false, or to evaluate whether an action, authority, or claim is rightful, justified, or appropriate. The falsity terms are used in official contexts — criminal law, administrative proceedings, regulatory enforcement — where the falsity of a claim has legal consequences. The justification terms evaluate not merely whether something is legal but whether it has proper grounds.
