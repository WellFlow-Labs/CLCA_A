# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P5 Oppositions
# Language: Quechua (qu)

---

**Preliminary note on method:** Oppositions below are extracted solely from patterns explicitly documented in P1–P4b. Each opposition is supported by direct attestation or explicit contrast statements in the prior steps. No oppositions are inferred beyond what the data report. Where the data describe an asymmetry between two terms, this is noted rather than smoothed over.

---

## Opposition 1

1. **Term A:** cheqa
2. **Term B:** llulla
3. **Transliteration:** cheqa / llulla
4. **English translation:** true, real, correct, genuine / false, lying, deceptive, fake
5. **Description of contrast:** These are described in P1 as the primary antonym pair in the truth-evaluation domain; *llulla* "contrasts directly with *cheqa*" (P1, item 7). The opposition is attested across adjectival, nominal, and verbal uses, and is explicitly described as the foundational positive/negative polarity of the domain.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## Opposition 2

1. **Term A:** cheqam / cheqami
2. **Term B:** llullam
3. **Transliteration:** cheqa-m / cheqa-mi / llulla-m
4. **English translation:** it is true (direct assertion) / it is a lie (direct assertion)
5. **Description of contrast:** Both forms attach the direct evidential clitic *-m/-mi* to the opposing evaluative roots, yielding paired assertive expressions. The construction *cheqam, manan llullachu* and its mirror *llullam, manan cheqachu* (P2b, Constructions 6 and 18) are explicitly described as a "rhetorical pattern of double assertion — affirming one pole while denying the other."
6. **Contrast type:** lexical (with parallel morphological structure)
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## Opposition 3

1. **Term A:** cheqam / cheqami
2. **Term B:** manan cheqachu
3. **Transliteration:** cheqa-m / mana-n cheqa-chu
4. **English translation:** it is true / it is not true
5. **Description of contrast:** The positive assertive form *cheqam* (cheqa + direct evidential *-m*) is contrasted with its standard negation *manan cheqachu* (mana + cheqa + negative suffix *-chu*). P2b (Construction 5) describes *manan cheqachu* as "the standard negative predication of *cheqa*" and "extremely common in discourse as a direct denial."
6. **Contrast type:** morphological
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## Opposition 4

1. **Term A:** llullam
2. **Term B:** manan llullachu
3. **Transliteration:** llulla-m / mana-n llulla-chu
4. **English translation:** it is a lie / it is not a lie
5. **Description of contrast:** The positive assertive form *llullam* is contrasted with its standard negation *manan llullachu* (P2b, Construction 17), described as "the standard negative predication of *llulla*." This is the morphological negation of the negative evaluative root, functioning as a positive characterization by negating the negative term.
6. **Contrast type:** morphological
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 5

2. **Term B:** llulla runa

4. **English translation:** an upright person, a person of integrity / a dishonest person, a liar
5. **Description of contrast:** P1b documents both collocations with the same head noun *runa* (person) and explicitly describes *llulla runa* as "the direct antonym of *cheqa runa*" (P1b, item 9 under llulla). Both appear in moral evaluation of persons.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 10

## Opposition 18

1. **Term A:** yachanim / yachaninim

4. **English translation:** I know (it), I know for certain / I do not know, I have no knowledge of it
5. **Description of contrast:** P2b documents both as first-person testimonial formulas (Constructions 28 and 29), with *manan yachanichu* described as "a highly conventionalized testimonial formula." The opposition is between asserting and denying personal knowledge in testimonial contexts.

7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 21

1. **Term A:** cheqachiy
2. **Term B:** llullachiy
3. **Transliteration:** cheqachiy / llullachiy
4. **English translation:** to verify, to confirm, to prove, to correct / to deceive someone, to cause to lie, to mislead
5. **Description of contrast:** P2a documents both as causative derivatives of the opposing roots *cheqa* and *llulla* (Derived Forms 1.3 and 7.3). *Cheqachiy* causes a state of truth or correctness; *llullachiy* causes a state of deception or error. Both appear in legal and moral discourse.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical (with parallel morphological structure)
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 25

1. **Term A:** cheqanta rimanayki tiyan
2. **Term B:** llullayta saqiy
3. **Transliteration:** cheqanta rima-nayki tiyan / llullay-ta saqiy
4. **English translation:** you must tell the truth (deontic obligation) / to stop lying, to abandon falsehood

6. **Contrast type:** constructional
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 26

1. **Term A:** cheqachu llullachu
2. **Term B:** (the two poles within the construction)
3. **Transliteration:** cheqa-chu llulla-chu
4. **English translation:** is it true or is it false? is it real or is it a lie?
5. **Description of contrast:** P2b documents this as a disjunctive question construction (Construction 51) using the question suffix *-chu* on both poles of the truth/falsehood opposition. The construction itself encodes the opposition as an either/or question, making the contrast structurally explicit within a single utterance.
6. **Contrast type:** constructional
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 27

1. **Term A:** cheqachu pantasqachu
2. **Term B:** (the two poles within the construction)
3. **Transliteration:** cheqa-chu pantasqa-chu
4. **English translation:** is it correct or is it mistaken? is it right or is it wrong?
5. **Description of contrast:** P2b documents this as a disjunctive question construction (Construction 53) parallel to Construction 51, using *-chu* on both *cheqa* (correct) and *pantasqa* (mistaken). The construction encodes the accuracy/error opposition as an either/or question.
6. **Contrast type:** constructional
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 28

1. **Term A:** cheqa
2. **Term B:** pantasqa
3. **Transliteration:** cheqa / pantasqa
4. **English translation:** correct, right, accurate / mistaken, wrong, erroneous, in error
5. **Description of contrast:** P3 documents *pantasqa* as a negative reliability evaluation alongside *llulla* derivatives, while noting that "error does not necessarily imply intent to deceive" — distinguishing this opposition from the cheqa/llulla opposition. The contrast is between accuracy and error, without the moral condemnation of deliberate deception that attaches to *llulla*.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## Opposition 29

1. **Term A:** iñiy
2. **Term B:** yachay
3. **Transliteration:** iñiy / yachay
4. **English translation:** to believe, to trust, to have faith in / to know, to learn; knowledge
5. **Description of contrast:** P1 documents both items and notes that *iñiy* "contrasts with *yachay* in that *iñiy* does not entail certainty or direct knowledge" (P1, item 13). P3 (Group 8) further describes the contrast as "a meaningful distinction within" the epistemic state expressions, drawn in testimonial and philosophical discourse.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## Opposition 30

1. **Term A:** yachay
2. **Term B:** riqsiy
3. **Transliteration:** yachay / riqsiy
4. **English translation:** to know (propositional knowledge) / to know by acquaintance, to recognize, to identify
5. **Description of contrast:** P1 documents both items and distinguishes them: *riqsiy* "refers to knowledge by acquaintance or recognition, as opposed to propositional knowledge (*yachay*)" (P1, item 11). P3 (Group 8) describes this as "a meaningful distinction within" the epistemic state expressions that "is attested in the sources as a meaningful distinction."
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 31

1. **Term A:** cheqam (assertive, direct evidential)
2. **Term B:** llullam (assertive, direct evidential)
3. **Note on asymmetry:** P2a explicitly documents that "no evidential-cliticized form *llullam* is attested as a fixed expression, in contrast to *cheqam*" (P2a, Summary observation 2). This asymmetry is reported in the data and is noted here rather than resolved.

*(no clear opposition identified at the level of fixed evidential expressions: cheqam is attested as a fixed expression; a parallel llullam is not attested as a fixed expression in the sources consulted)*

## Opposition 32

1. **Term A:** cheqa (with emphatic *-puni*: cheqapuni)
2. **Term B:** llulla (no attested emphatic *-puni* form)
3. **Note on asymmetry:** P2a explicitly documents that "the emphatic *-puni* and contrastive *-taq* are attested on *cheqa* but not on *llulla*" (P2a, Summary observation 2). This morphological asymmetry between the two roots is reported in the data.

*(no clear opposition identified at the level of emphatic forms: cheqapuni is attested; a parallel llullapuni is not attested in the sources consulted)*

1. **Term A:** cheqan (abstract nominal "the truth")
2. **Term B:** (no parallel abstract nominal for llulla)
3. **Note on asymmetry:** P4a explicitly states that "no parallel abstract nominal is attested for *llulla* in the sources consulted; the concept of 'falsehood' as an abstract noun is expressed through the bare adjective or through verbal nominalizations" (P4a, section 6).

*(no clear opposition identified at the level of abstract nominals: cheqan is attested as a primary abstract nominal; a parallel nominal for "falsehood" is not attested in the sources consulted)*
