## SUMMARY OBSERVATIONS

**Total oppositions identified:** 30 (including uncertain cases)

**Distribution by contrast type:**
- Lexical: 5 clear oppositions (1-5)
- Morphological: 7 clear oppositions (6, 13, 17, 20, 27, 30) + 1 uncertain (29)
- Constructional: 15 clear oppositions (7-12, 14-16, 21-23, 28) + 3 uncertain (18, 24, 25, 26)

**Distribution by epistemic status:**
- [CORE]: 23 oppositions
- [MARGINAL]: 3 oppositions (9, 21, 22, 27)
- [UNCERTAIN]: 4 oppositions (18, 24, 25, 26, 29)

**Key patterns observed:**

1. **Lexical oppositions** are relatively few but fundamental (kweli/uongo, halisi/bandia, haki/batili). These form the core binary contrasts in the truth domain.

2. **Morphological oppositions** are highly productive through:
   - Copular negation (ni/si)
   - Possessive negation (nina/sina)
   - Associative construction with contrasting nouns (-a kweli / -a uongo)
   - Prepositional contrast (kwa/bila)

3. **Constructional oppositions** dominate, showing that opposition is often created through:
   - Identical frames with contrasting lexical items (sema ukweli / sema uongo)
   - Scalar modification (mkubwa/mdogo)
   - Spatial metaphors (ndani/nje)
   - Fixed contrastive coordinations (X au Y)

4. **Asymmetries noted:**
   - Some expected lexical opposites (chafu for safi, dhalimu for adili, mwaminifu for mwongo) exist in Swahili but are not attested in this truth-domain dataset
   - Certain oppositions are expressed through negation rather than dedicated antonyms (si kamili rather than a lexical term for "incomplete")
   - The causative/stative opposition (-ish-/-ik-) is not productive across all roots

5. **Overlapping oppositions:** Several terms participate in multiple oppositional structures (e.g., ukweli opposes uongo lexically, appears in spatial opposition ndani/nje, and in constructional oppositions with various verbs).
