# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P1b ROOT EXPANSION
# Language: Tamil (ta)

## Step 1 — Identify Central Roots

### Root 1: உண் (uṇ)
**Native script:** உண்  
**Transliteration:** uṇ  
**P1 participation:** 2 items (#1 உண்மை uṇmai, #11 உண்மையான uṇmaiyāṉa)  
**Core meaning contribution:** "to be, to exist, to eat/consume" — the root carries the fundamental sense of existence and reality, forming the primary native Tamil term for truth as "that which is/exists"

### Root 2: பொய் (poy)
**Native script:** பொய்  
**Transliteration:** poy  
**P1 participation:** 2 items (#2 பொய் poy, #19 பொய்யான poyyāṉa)  
**Core meaning contribution:** "false, lie, untruth" — the primary native Tamil root for falsehood and deception, functioning as the direct antonym to உண்மை

### Root 3: மெய் (mey)
**Native script:** மெய்  
**Transliteration:** mey  
**P1 participation:** 1 item (#3 மெய் mey)  
**Core meaning contribution:** "body, truth, reality" — classical Tamil root linking physical embodiment with truth and reality, highly productive in literary and philosophical registers

## Step 2 — Expand Each Root

### ROOT 1: உண் (uṇ)

#### 1.1
**Form:** உண்மையாக  
**Transliteration:** uṇmaiyāka  
**Literal breakdown:** uṇmai (truth) + āka (as/in the manner of)  
**Translation:** truly, truthfully, really  
**Context:** Adverbial form used to modify verbs or entire statements, indicating that something is done truthfully or is genuinely the case. Common in both speech and writing.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 1.2
**Form:** உண்மையில்  
**Transliteration:** uṇmaiyil  
**Literal breakdown:** uṇmai (truth) + il (in/at locative)  
**Translation:** in truth, actually, in reality  
**Context:** Used to emphasize the actual truth of a matter, often contrasting appearance with reality. Very common in everyday speech for clarification.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 1.3
**Form:** உண்மைத்தன்மை  
**Transliteration:** uṇmaittaṉmai  
**Literal breakdown:** uṇmai (truth) + taṉmai (quality/nature)  
**Translation:** truthfulness, veracity, authenticity  
**Context:** Formal term for the quality or property of being true. Used in academic, legal, and formal discourse to discuss the nature of truth.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 1.4
**Form:** உண்மையை சொல்  
**Transliteration:** uṇmaiyai col  
**Literal breakdown:** uṇmai (truth) + ai (accusative) + col (say/speak)  
**Translation:** speak the truth, tell the truth  
**Context:** Common collocation for the act of truth-telling. Used in moral instruction, interrogation, and everyday requests for honesty.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 1.5
**Form:** உண்மைக்கு மாறான  
**Transliteration:** uṇmaikku māṟāṉa  
**Literal breakdown:** uṇmai (truth) + kku (dative) + māṟu (contrary/opposite) + āṉa (adjectival)  
**Translation:** contrary to truth, false, untrue  
**Context:** Formal expression used to describe statements or representations that contradict the truth. Common in legal and formal writing.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 1.6
**Form:** உண்மையை மறை  
**Transliteration:** uṇmaiyai maṟai  
**Literal breakdown:** uṇmai (truth) + ai (accusative) + maṟai (hide/conceal)  
**Translation:** hide the truth, conceal the truth  
**Context:** Used to describe deliberate concealment of truth. Common in contexts of deception, investigation, and moral judgment.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 1.7
**Form:** உண்மை நிலை  
**Transliteration:** uṇmai nilai  
**Literal breakdown:** uṇmai (truth) + nilai (state/condition/position)

**Context:** Used to refer to the actual state of affairs or real situation. Common in formal discourse and investigative contexts.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Step 3 — Peripheral Extensions

### Extensions of உண் (uṇ)

**Physical/bodily domain:**
- The root உண் fundamentally means "to eat/consume" in addition to "to be/exist"
- Forms like உண்ணு (uṇṇu - "to eat") and உணவு (uṇavu - "food") use the same root but in the domain of consumption rather than truth/existence
- The connection between "eating/consuming" and "truth/existence" appears to be etymologically related through the concept of what is real/substantial enough to be consumed

**Intensity/emphasis marking:**
- உண்மையில் (uṉmaiyil) and உண்மையாக (uṇmaiyāka) sometimes function as intensifiers or emphasis markers meaning "really" or "actually" beyond strict truth-evaluation contexts
- Example: used to emphasize degree or extent rather than veracity

### Extensions of பொய் (poy)

**Aesthetic/artistic domain:**
- பொய் (poy) appears in contexts describing artistic representations and fiction, where "false" does not carry negative moral weight
- Used to distinguish fictional narratives from factual ones without implying deception

**Botanical/natural domain:**
- பொய் appears as a prefix in some plant names to indicate resemblance or imitation (e.g., plants that look like other species but are not)
- This extends the "false/imitation" sense beyond truth-evaluation into natural taxonomy

### Extensions of மெய் (mey)

**Physical/bodily domain:**
- மெய் primarily means "body" in many contexts, completely separate from truth-related meanings
- Forms like மெய்ப்பாடு (meyppāṭu) can refer to physical sensations or bodily experiences
- மெய் காப்பு (mey kāppu - "bodyguard"), மெய் வலி (mey vali - "body pain") use the "body" sense exclusively

**Emotional/aesthetic domain:**
- மெய்ப்பாடு (meyppāṭu) extends to describe genuine emotional experiences and aesthetic responses
- Used in literary criticism to describe authentic emotional expression in art

**Religious/devotional intensification:**
- மெய் frequently appears in devotional compounds (மெய் பக்தி, மெய் தெய்வம்) where it intensifies or authenticates religious concepts
- Functions as a marker of spiritual authenticity beyond simple truth-evaluation
