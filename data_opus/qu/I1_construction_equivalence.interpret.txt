## 2. Substitution Patterns (Narrative)

**Become replaced by causation (transitive only).** No dedicated intransitive "become X" construction is documented (#4). The data state that change-of-state is covered transitively via the causative *-cha-* (#5). Thus the inchoative function is not expressed by its own operator; only the transitive "make X" reading is available. This is a substitution that does not fully cover the intransitive meaning.

**Revelation has no documented construction.** Nothing in P4a/P4b describes a "come to light / be revealed" pattern. There is no evidence that REVELATION substitutes for BECOME, nor that any operator stands in for it.

**Discovery / Verification vs. Causation.** There is no DISCOVERY construction attested, and no indication that discovery substitutes for causation. Verification (#9) is its own enclitic-based device (*-chu*) and does not replace causation.

**Negation is analytic.** Negation uses the preposed free word *mana* plus the quality word (optionally plus agentive *-q*). It is not a bound morphological negator on these roots. Polarity behavior is asymmetric: *mana* + ADJ yields negative polarity (*mana allin* "bad"), while *mana* + AGENTIVE yields positive polarity (*mana llullaq* "honest").

**Verification and Disconfirmation do not share a construction.** Only an affirmative truth-confirmation device exists (*-chu* on the truth root: *chiqaqchu*). No dedicated disconfirmation construction is documented; the two functions therefore do not share one form because only one of them is attested.

**Asymmetry: truth-teller expressed negatively.** Agentive nominalization (#7) shows an explicit asymmetry: a liar has a dedicated positive agentive (*llullakuq*), but a truth-teller is expressed only negatively as *mana llullaq* ("one who does not lie"), with no dedicated positive agentive.

**Light/copular carrier.** In ATTR, the carrier of assertion is the evidential enclitic *-mi*, not a verb; the copula *kay* surfaces overtly chiefly in NOMINALIZATION ([X + kay]), where it functions as a copular noun ("being") carrying the abstraction.

## 3. Missingness Summary

**Structural Gaps (missing constructions):**
- BECOME / Inchoative — no intransitive "become X" construction (only transitive causative coverage).
- REVELATION — no documented construction.
- DISCONFIRMATION — no dedicated construction (only affirmative confirmation via *-chu*).

**Lexical Gaps (missing words):**
- No dedicated positive agentive for "truth-teller" (the slot is filled analytically by *mana llullaq*). This is an asymmetric lexical/derivational gap.

**Conceptual Gaps (blocked operations):**
- None can be asserted from the data as conceptually blocked; the absences above are described in P4a/P4b as undocumented or covered by other operators, not as blocked operations.

**Asymmetric Gaps (only negative or only positive forms missing):**
- Agentive: positive "true-teller" missing as a dedicated form; expressed via negation (*mana llullaq*). Negative "liar" fully lexicalized (*llullakuq*, intensive *llullasapa*).
- Polarity inversion in negation: *mana* + ADJ → negative; *mana* + AGENTIVE → positive.
- Truth-confirmation present (*chiqaqchu*); disconfirmation counterpart absent.
