# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2b Compositional Constructions
# Language: Finnish (fi)

---

## Methodological note

The following documents attested multi-word constructions — periphrastic, idiomatic, collocational, and syntactically-driven — involving P1 base items. Derivational forms already documented in P2a are excluded. Where a construction was already noted in P1b or P2a as a collocation or fixed phrase, it is cross-referenced and documented fully here. Absence of constructions for a given item or pattern is recorded explicitly. Register variation is noted where relevant.

---

## P1 Item 1: *tosi*

---

**Construction 1.**
**Base Form:** tosi
**Example:** olla totta
**Transliteration:** N/A
**English translation:** to be true; to be the case
**Usage notes:** The standard predicate construction for asserting truth in Finnish. The partitive *totta* (rather than nominative *tosi*) is required in this construction. *Se ei voi olla totta* (it cannot be true); *onko se totta?* (is it true?). Fundamental to everyday epistemic discourse across all registers.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 2.**
**Base Form:** tosi
**Example:** puhua totta
**Transliteration:** N/A
**English translation:** to tell the truth; to speak truthfully
**Usage notes:** Very common collocation for the act of telling the truth. *Hän puhuu totta* (he/she is telling the truth). The partitive *totta* is required. Used across all registers; contrasts with *puhua valhetta* (to tell a lie).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 3.**
**Base Form:** tosi
**Example:** sanoa totta
**Transliteration:** N/A
**English translation:** to say something true; to tell the truth
**Usage notes:** Collocation similar to *puhua totta* but focused on a specific utterance rather than a general disposition or ongoing act. *Hän sanoi totta* (what he/she said was true; he/she told the truth). Used across registers.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 4.**
**Base Form:** tosi
**Example:** totta puhuen
**Transliteration:** N/A
**English translation:** to tell the truth; truthfully speaking; frankly
**Usage notes:** Fixed adverbial phrase using the adverbial participle of *puhua*. Used to introduce a candid or frank statement, often with a slight concessive nuance. *Totta puhuen, en tiedä* (to tell the truth, I don't know). Common in both spoken and written Finnish.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 5.**
**Base Form:** tosi
**Example:** totta kai
**Transliteration:** N/A
**English translation:** of course; certainly; naturally
**Usage notes:** Very common colloquial affirmative expression. Used to confirm something as obvious or expected. *Totta kai tulen* (of course I'll come). The particle *kai* adds a sense of self-evidence. Used across registers, slightly more colloquial than *luonnollisesti*.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 6.**
**Base Form:** tosi
**Example:** toden totta
**Transliteration:** N/A
**English translation:** truly; really; in all truth; indeed
**Usage notes:** Fixed adverbial phrase using genitive *toden* and partitive *totta* of the same base. Used for emphatic affirmation. *Se on toden totta niin* (it is truly so; it really is the case). Registers as slightly formal or literary compared to *tosiaan* or *oikeasti*.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 7.**
**Base Form:** tosi
**Example:** tosi asia
**Transliteration:** N/A
**English translation:** a true matter; a fact; the real thing
**Usage notes:** Collocation used to assert that something is a genuine fact or a real concern. *Se on tosi asia* (that is a real fact; that is genuinely the case). Functions as a mild emphatic assertion. Used in everyday speech.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 8.**
**Base Form:** tosi
**Example:** tosiasia on, että
**Transliteration:** N/A
**English translation:** the fact is that; the truth is that; it is a fact that
**Usage notes:** Very common discourse marker used to introduce a factual claim presented as incontrovertible. *Tosiasia on, että tilanne on vakava* (the fact is that the situation is serious). Used across registers from everyday speech to formal writing as a rhetorical framing device.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 9.**
**Base Form:** tosi
**Example:** perustuu tositapahtumiin
**Transliteration:** N/A
**English translation:** based on true events; based on actual events
**Usage notes:** Fixed phrase used in film, literary, and journalistic contexts to label a narrative as factually grounded. Functions as a genre marker. *Elokuva perustuu tositapahtumiin* (the film is based on true events). Calque of the English formula.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 10.**
**Base Form:** tosi
**Example:** tosi hyvä / tosi paljon / tosi vaikea
**Transliteration:** N/A
**English translation:** really good / really much / really difficult
**Usage notes:** *Tosi* functions as a colloquial intensifying adverb before adjectives and adverbs, equivalent to *todella* (really) or *hyvin* (very) in more formal registers. Extremely common in spoken Finnish, particularly among younger speakers. Not used in formal written registers.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 11.**
**Base Form:** tosi
**Example:** pitää paikkansa
**Transliteration:** N/A
**English translation:** to be true; to hold; to be correct
**Usage notes:** Very common idiomatic expression for asserting that a claim is true or accurate. *Väite pitää paikkansa* (the claim is true/holds). Literally "holds its place." Used across all registers as a near-synonym of *olla totta*, often preferred in evaluative contexts where a claim is being assessed.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 12.**
**Base Form:** tosi
**Example:** ei pidä paikkaansa
**Transliteration:** N/A
**English translation:** is not true; does not hold; is incorrect
**Usage notes:** Negative form of *pitää paikkansa*. *Väite ei pidä paikkaansa* (the claim is not true/does not hold). Used across registers to deny the truth of a claim without using the word *väärä* directly.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## P1 Item 2: *totuus*

---

**Construction 1.**
**Base Form:** totuus
**Example:** kertoa totuus
**Transliteration:** N/A
**English translation:** to tell the truth
**Usage notes:** Standard collocation for the act of disclosing the truth. *Kerro totuus* (tell the truth). Used in everyday, legal, and moral contexts. The object *totuus* takes the accusative/nominative form in the imperative and the genitive *totuuden* in other constructions.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 2.**
**Base Form:** totuus
**Example:** koko totuus ja vain totuus
**Transliteration:** N/A
**English translation:** the whole truth and nothing but the truth
**Usage notes:** Fixed phrase used in legal oaths and in everyday demands for complete disclosure. *Hän vannoi kertovansa koko totuuden ja vain totuuden* (he/she swore to tell the whole truth and nothing but the truth). A calque of the English legal formula, now established in Finnish legal and popular usage.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 3.**
**Base Form:** totuus
**Example:** totuuden hetki
**Transliteration:** N/A
**English translation:** moment of truth
**Usage notes:** Fixed phrase used in everyday and journalistic language to describe a decisive moment when the true state of affairs becomes apparent or a critical test occurs. *Tämä on totuuden hetki* (this is the moment of truth). Calque of the Spanish/English expression, now fully established in Finnish.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 4.**
**Base Form:** totuus
**Example:** totuus on, että
**Transliteration:** N/A
**English translation:** the truth is that
**Usage notes:** Discourse marker used to introduce a frank or candid statement, often one that contradicts a previous assumption. *Totuus on, että emme tiedä* (the truth is that we don't know). Used across registers; slightly more rhetorical than *tosiasia on, että*.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 5.**
**Base Form:** totuus
**Example:** etsiä totuutta
**Transliteration:** N/A
**English translation:** to seek the truth; to search for truth
**Usage notes:** Collocation used in philosophical, journalistic, and investigative contexts. *Toimittajan tehtävä on etsiä totuutta* (the journalist's task is to seek the truth). The partitive *totuutta* is used, indicating an ongoing or incomplete process.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 6.**
**Base Form:** totuus
**Example:** totuus tulee ilmi
**Transliteration:** N/A
**English translation:** the truth comes out; the truth comes to light
**Usage notes:** Common collocation describing the revelation of a previously hidden truth. *Ennemmin tai myöhemmin totuus tulee ilmi* (sooner or later the truth will come out). Used across registers in narrative and evaluative contexts.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 7.**
**Base Form:** totuus
**Example:** totuus on karvas
**Transliteration:** N/A
**English translation:** the truth is bitter; truth hurts
**Usage notes:** Fixed proverbial expression used to acknowledge that truthful information may be unwelcome or painful. *Totuus on karvas, mutta se on parempi kuin valhe* (the truth is bitter, but it is better than a lie). Used in moral and everyday discourse.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 8.**
**Base Form:** totuus
**Example:** puhua totuuden nimissä
**Transliteration:** N/A
**English translation:** to speak in the name of truth; to invoke truth
**Usage notes:** Collocation used in rhetorical and philosophical contexts when a speaker claims to act on behalf of truth. *Hän puhui totuuden nimissä* (he/she spoke in the name of truth). Somewhat elevated in register.
**Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**Construction 9.**
**Base Form:** totuus
**Example:** totuus ja sovinto
**Transliteration:** N/A
**English translation:** truth and reconciliation
**Usage notes:** Fixed phrase used in political and historical contexts, particularly in reference to truth and reconciliation processes. *Totuus ja sovinto -prosessi* (truth and reconciliation process). Used in political science, journalism, and policy discourse.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 10.**
**Base Form:** totuus
**Example:** vannoa totuuden nimeen
**Transliteration:** N/A
**English translation:** to swear by the truth; to swear in the name of truth
**Usage notes:** Collocation used in legal and solemn contexts when a person swears to tell the truth. Less fixed than *koko totuus ja vain totuus* but attested in legal and religious registers.
**Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

## P1 Item 3: *aito*

---

**Construction 1.**
**Base Form:** aito
**Example:** aito ja alkuperäinen
**Transliteration:** N/A
**English translation:** genuine and original; the real thing
**Usage notes:** Fixed binomial phrase used to assert that something is both authentic and original, often in commercial or evaluative contexts. *Aito ja alkuperäinen tuote* (a genuine and original product). The phrase functions as a reinforced assertion of authenticity.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 2.**
**Base Form:** aito
**Example:** todistaa aidoksi
**Transliteration:** N/A
**English translation:** to certify as genuine; to authenticate
**Usage notes:** Periphrastic construction using *todistaa* + translative of *aito*. Used in legal, commercial, and art authentication contexts. *Asiantuntija todisti maalauksen aidoksi* (the expert certified the painting as genuine).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 3.**
**Base Form:** aito
**Example:** osoittautua aidoksi
**Transliteration:** N/A
**English translation:** to turn out to be genuine; to prove authentic
**Usage notes:** Periphrastic construction using *osoittautua* (to turn out to be) + translative *aidoksi*. Used when the authenticity of something is established after investigation or testing. *Asiakirja osoittautui aidoksi* (the document turned out to be genuine).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 4.**
**Base Form:** aito
**Example:** aito suomalainen
**Transliteration:** N/A
**English translation:** a genuine Finn; a real Finn; a true Finn
**Usage notes:** Collocation used to assert that a person is genuinely Finnish in character, origin, or identity. Can be used straightforwardly or ironically. *Hän on aito suomalainen* (he/she is a genuine/real Finn). Common in everyday and journalistic discourse.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 5.**
**Base Form:** aito
**Example:** aito tunne / aito ilo / aito suru
**Transliteration:** N/A
**English translation:** a genuine feeling / genuine joy / genuine grief
**Usage notes:** Collocation pattern applying *aito* to emotional states to assert that they are not performed or simulated. *Hänen ilonsa oli aitoa* (his/her joy was genuine). Used in psychological, literary, and everyday evaluative contexts.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 6.**
**Base Form:** aito
**Example:** ei ole aito
**Transliteration:** N/A
**English translation:** is not genuine; is not authentic; is fake
**Usage notes:** Simple predicative negation used to assert inauthenticity. *Tämä allekirjoitus ei ole aito* (this signature is not genuine). Used across registers as a direct evaluative statement.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## P1 Item 5: *oikea*

---

**Construction 1.**
**Base Form:** oikea
**Example:** oikea vastaus
**Transliteration:** N/A
**English translation:** the correct answer; the right answer
**Usage notes:** Standard collocation in educational, quiz, and evaluative contexts. *Anna oikea vastaus* (give the correct answer). One of the most frequent uses of *oikea* in everyday language.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 2.**
**Base Form:** oikea
**Example:** oikea ihminen oikeaan paikkaan
**Transliteration:** N/A
**English translation:** the right person in the right place
**Usage notes:** Fixed phrase used in organizational and management contexts to describe optimal placement of personnel. *Oikea ihminen oikeaan paikkaan on johtamisen perusta* (the right person in the right place is the foundation of management). Common in HR and administrative discourse.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 3.**
**Base Form:** oikea
**Example:** oikeaan aikaan oikeassa paikassa
**Transliteration:** N/A
**English translation:** at the right time in the right place
**Usage notes:** Fixed phrase used to describe fortunate or well-timed positioning. *Hän oli oikeaan aikaan oikeassa paikassa* (he/she was in the right place at the right time). Common in everyday and journalistic discourse.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 4.**
**Base Form:** oikea
**Example:** oikeassa oleminen
**Transliteration:** N/A
**English translation:** being right; being correct
**Usage notes:** Nominal construction using the inessive of *oikea* with *olla* (to be). *Olla oikeassa* (to be right/correct) is the standard predicate construction for asserting that a person holds a correct view. *Hän on oikeassa* (he/she is right). Contrasts with *olla väärässä* (to be wrong).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 5.**
**Base Form:** oikea
**Example:** olla oikeassa
**Transliteration:** N/A
**English translation:** to be right; to be correct
**Usage notes:** Standard predicate construction for asserting that a person's view or claim is correct. *Sinä olet oikeassa* (you are right). Used across all registers. The inessive case *oikeassa* is required in this construction.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 6.**
**Base Form:** oikea
**Example:** osoittautua oikeaksi
**Transliteration:** N/A
**English translation:** to turn out to be correct; to prove right
**Usage notes:** Periphrastic construction using *osoittautua* + translative *oikeaksi*. *Ennuste osoittautui oikeaksi* (the forecast turned out to be correct). Used in evaluative and retrospective contexts.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 7.**
**Base Form:** oikea
**Example:** todistaa oikeaksi
**Transliteration:** N/A
**English translation:** to certify as correct; to authenticate; to verify
**Usage notes:** Administrative and legal collocation used when an official certifies that a copy or statement is correct. *Notaari todistaa kopion oikeaksi* (the notary certifies the copy as correct). Standard in notarial and administrative practice.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 8.**
**Base Form:** oikea
**Example:** oikea käsi
**Transliteration:** N/A
**English translation:** right hand (literal); right-hand person (figurative)
**Usage notes:** *Oikea käsi* is used both literally (the right hand) and figuratively to mean a trusted assistant or deputy. *Hän on johtajan oikea käsi* (he/she is the director's right-hand person). Common in everyday and organizational discourse.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 9.**
**Base Form:** oikea
**Example:** oikea elämä
**Transliteration:** N/A
**English translation:** real life; genuine life
**Usage notes:** Collocation used to contrast actual lived experience with fiction, theory, or idealized scenarios. *Oikeassa elämässä asiat ovat monimutkaisempia* (in real life things are more complicated). Synonymous with *tosielämä* in many contexts.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 10.**
**Base Form:** oikea
**Example:** se oikea
**Transliteration:** N/A
**English translation:** the one; the right one; the real one; Mr./Ms. Right
**Usage notes:** Fixed phrase used colloquially to refer to the ideal partner or the definitive version of something. *Hän on se oikea* (he/she is the one; he/she is Mr./Ms. Right). Very common in everyday speech.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]
