# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P1b ROOT EXPANSION
# Language: Indonesian (id)

## Step 1 — Identify Central Roots

### Root 1: **benar**
**Form:** benar  
**Transliteration:** N/A (Latin script)  
**P1 participation:** 3 items (benar, kebenaran, sebenarnya)  
**Core meaning contribution:** Correctness, truth, accuracy; the fundamental root for truth-evaluation in Indonesian. Contributes the core sense of correspondence to fact or proper standard.

### Root 2: **bohong**
**Form:** bohong  
**Transliteration:** N/A (Latin script)  
**P1 participation:** 2 items (bohong, kebohongan)  
**Core meaning contribution:** Falsehood, deception, lying; the primary root for deliberate untruth. Contributes the sense of intentional misrepresentation.

### Root 3: **sungguh**
**Form:** sungguh  
**Transliteration:** N/A (Latin script)  
**P1 participation:** 2 items (sungguh, sesungguhnya)  
**Core meaning contribution:** Genuineness, sincerity, reality; emphasizes earnestness and actual truth. Contributes intensification and affirmation of truthfulness.

## Step 3 — Peripheral Extensions

### Extensions of ROOT 1 (benar):

1. **benar-benar** — While core meaning involves truth/correctness, this reduplicated form frequently functions as a pure intensifier meaning "really" or "completely" in contexts unrelated to truth-evaluation (e.g., "benar-benar lelah" = "really tired," where no truth-claim is being evaluated).

2. **membenarkan** — In addition to "confirm/verify," this verb can mean "to permit" or "to allow" in regulatory contexts, extending beyond truth-evaluation into deontic modality ("tidak dibenarkan masuk" = "not permitted to enter").

3. **pembenaran** — While primarily "justification," can extend to mean "permission" or "authorization" in administrative contexts, shifting from epistemic to deontic domain.

4. **kebenaran** — Can refer to "justice" or "righteousness" in some religious and moral contexts, extending from factual truth to moral/ethical truth.

### Extensions of ROOT 2 (bohong):

1. **membohongi diri sendiri** — Extends from interpersonal deception to psychological self-deception, involving internal mental states rather than communicative acts.

2. **bohong kecil** — The concept of "white lie" introduces pragmatic and ethical dimensions beyond simple truth/falsity evaluation.

### Extensions of ROOT 3 (sungguh):

1. **bersungguh-sungguh** — Primarily functions to evaluate effort, commitment, or seriousness of action rather than truth-value of statements. Shifts from epistemic to evaluative domain of earnestness.

2. **kesungguhan** — Refers to quality of seriousness or earnestness as character trait or manner of action, extending beyond truth-evaluation to evaluation of commitment and effort.

3. **sungguhpun** — Functions as concessive conjunction ("although") with no direct connection to truth-evaluation, representing grammaticalization into discourse marker.

4. **dengan sungguh-sungguh** — Functions primarily as manner adverbial evaluating how actions are performed (seriously, earnestly) rather than truth-value of propositions.
