# CLCA Protocol v3.3 — P6 Change, Causation, and Stability
# Language: Georgian (ka)

---

**Methodological note:** This step documents how Georgian encodes dynamic meanings — change of state, causation, and stability — involving the truth-related vocabulary established in P1–P5. All forms and constructions are drawn exclusively from the CONTEXT. Where a given term or root yields no attested dynamic expression, this is stated explicitly. Patterns are treated separately per term or root where behavior differs.

---

## 1. BECOMING TRUE / COMING TO BE CORRECT

### Pattern
**No single inchoative construction attested for "become true" or "become correct"**

P4a explicitly states: "A true inchoative ('become true') is not a natural category in Georgian truth-domain expression." No construction meaning "X becomes true" (with martali, simartle, č̣ešmariteba, or zusti as the target state) is documented in the CONTEXT.

The nearest attested functional equivalents use the resultative verb **gamodga** ("turned out to be") or the evidential copula **q̇opila** ("it turns out it was"), both of which express retrospective discovery of a state rather than a transition into one.

### Attested examples

- **ეს ამბავი მართალი გამოდგა** → es ambavi martali gamodga → "This story turned out to be correct / proved to be true"
- **ეს ნამდვილი გამოდგა** → es namdvili gamodga → "This turned out to be genuine"
- **ეს ნამდვილი ყოფილა** → es namdvili q̇opila → "This was genuine all along (evidential)"

### Notes
These constructions express the discovery that a state obtains, not a process of coming to obtain. The distinction is preserved from P4a. No form meaning "X became true" in the sense of a transition from false to true is attested in the CONTEXT.

## 2. BECOMING FALSE / COMING TO BE FAKE OR ERRONEOUS

### Pattern
**No single inchoative construction attested for "become false" or "become fake"**

Parallel to Section 1, no construction meaning "X becomes false" or "X becomes counterfeit" is documented in the CONTEXT. The same resultative and evidential constructions serve the negative pole.

### Attested examples

- **ეს ამბავი ყალბი გამოდგა** → es ambavi q̇albi gamodga → "This story turned out to be false / proved to be fake"
- **ეს ყალბი ყოფილა** → es q̇albi q̇opila → "This was fake all along (evidential, often with surprise)"
- **მცდარი გამოდგა** → mcdari gamodga → "It turned out to be erroneous / proved to be wrong"

### Notes
The evidential construction with q̇opila is particularly prominent in the falsity direction, reflecting the paradigmatic context of discovering deception or inauthenticity noted in P4a and P4b. The surprise or disillusionment connotation of q̇opila is more salient when the discovered state is negative (fake, hypocritical, fraudulent) than when it is positive.

## 3. CAUSING SOMETHING TO BE FALSE: FALSIFICATION

### Pattern
**გაყალბება / gaq̇albeba — verbal noun of the completed act of making something false**
**გაყალბება + NOUN (genitive) — standard legal collocation**

This is the most morphologically direct causative expression in the truth domain, documented in P2a (Derived Form 6.3) and P4a (Construction 5). It is root-specific to q̇alb- and does not generalize across other truth-domain adjectives.

### Attested examples

- **დოკუმენტების გაყალბება** → dokumentebis gaq̇albeba → "falsification of documents / making documents false"
- **არჩევნების გაყალბება** → arčevnebis gaq̇albeba → "electoral fraud / falsification of elections"

### Notes
gaq̇albeba (preverb ga- + q̇alb- + -eba) expresses the completed act of making something counterfeit or false. P4a notes that this is "the closest Georgian comes to a morphological causative in this domain, but it is root-specific to q̇alb- and does not generalize across truth-domain adjectives." No parallel form meaning "make-true" or "make-genuine" built on namdvil- or marṭ- is attested in the CONTEXT.

## 4. CAUSING SOMEONE TO HOLD A FALSE BELIEF: MISLEADING

### Pattern
**შეცდომაში შეყვანა / šecodomashi šeq̇vana — periphrastic causative of leading into error**

Documented in P2a (Derived Form 11.3), P2b (Construction 11.4), and P4a (Construction 5) as the standard periphrastic expression for causing someone to err or be deceived.

### Attested examples

- **შეცდომაში შეიყვანა** → šecodomashi šeiq̇vana → "He/she misled (someone) / led (someone) into error"

### Notes
The construction is locative: šecodomashi (into mistake/error) + the directional verb šeq̇vana (leading into, bringing into). P4a describes this as "the standard way to express 'to mislead' in Georgian." It is a periphrastic construction with no single-word equivalent. The causation is directed at a person's epistemic state (causing them to err) rather than at an object (making an object false), distinguishing it from gaq̇albeba.

## 5. CAUSING TRUTH TO BE ESTABLISHED: VERIFICATION AND CONFIRMATION

### Pattern
**ნამდვილობის დადასტურება / namdvilobis dadastureba — confirmation of authenticity**
**ნამდვილობის შემოწმება / namdvilobis šemoc̣meba — checking / verifying authenticity**
**სიმართლის დადგენა / simartlis dadgena — establishing the truth**

These periphrastic constructions, documented in P2b (Constructions 8.1, 8.2, 3.10 area) and P1b (item 31), express the institutional or investigative act of bringing a truth-state into confirmed existence.

### Attested examples

- **ნამდვილობის დადასტურება** → namdvilobis dadastureba → "confirmation of authenticity / authentication"
- **ნამდვილობის შემოწმება** → namdvilobis šemoc̣meba → "checking authenticity / authenticity verification"
- **სიმართლის დადგენა** → simartlis dadgena → "establishing the truth / determining the facts"
- **ნამდვილი ვინაობის დადგენა** → namdvili vinaobis dadgena → "establishing true identity"

### Notes
The verb dadgena (establishing, determining) recurs across these constructions and is the primary verb for the causative act of bringing a truth-state into institutional recognition. šemoc̣meba (checking, verification) expresses the process preceding confirmation. These constructions are documented in P2b and P3 Group 3 as standard legal and administrative language.

## 6. TRUTH PREVAILING: DYNAMIC ASSERTION OF TRUTH'S STABILITY

### Pattern
**სიმართლე გამარჯვებს / simartle gamarjvebs — "truth prevails"**
**ჭეშმარიტება გამარჯვებს / č̣ešmariteba gamarjvebs — "truth prevails" (more formal)**

Documented in P2b (Constructions 3.6 and 2.1) and P3 Group 6. These are proverbial and rhetorical expressions asserting the ultimate dynamic triumph of truth over falsehood.

### Attested examples

- **სიმართლე გამარჯვებს** → simartle gamarjvebs → "truth prevails / truth will prevail"
- **ჭეშმარიტება გამარჯვებს** → č̣ešmariteba gamarjvebs → "truth prevails / truth will prevail" (more formal/literary)

### Notes
The verb gamarjvebs (prevails, wins, is victorious) is in the present tense but carries a habitual or gnomic force in these proverbial expressions. P5 Opposition 26 pairs these with the proverbial expression about lies not lasting (sicrue pexebze dgas), establishing a dynamic contrast between truth's stability and falsehood's fragility.

## 7. FALSEHOOD BEING EXPOSED: DYNAMIC CHANGE FROM CONCEALED TO REVEALED

### Pattern
**სიყალბე გამოვლინდა / siq̇albe gamovlinda — "the falsity was revealed"**
**სიცრუე გამოვლინდა / sicrue gamovlinda — "the lie was exposed"**
**სიმართლე გამოვლინდა / simartle gamovlinda — "the truth came to light" (positive counterpart)**

Documented in P2b (Constructions 5.1, 9.4) and P1b (item 33: simartlis gamovlena).

### Attested examples

- **სიყალბე გამოვლინდა** → siq̇albe gamovlinda → "the falsity was revealed / the forgery was uncovered"
- **სიცრუე გამოვლინდა** → sicrue gamovlinda → "the lie was exposed / the falsehood came to light"
- **სიმართლის გამოვლენა** → simartlis gamovlena → "revealing the truth / uncovering the truth"

### Notes
The verb gamovlinda (was revealed, came to light — past passive) and the verbal noun gamovlena (revealing, uncovering) are shared across both the falsity-exposure and truth-revelation constructions, making this a structurally unified dynamic pattern. The direction of change is from concealed to revealed, applicable to both truth and falsehood. P1b documents simartlis gamovlena as used "in journalistic, legal, and moral contexts to describe the act of bringing hidden facts to light."

## 8. CREDIBILITY BEING LOST OR CALLED INTO QUESTION: DYNAMIC CHANGE IN RELIABILITY STATUS

### Pattern
**სანდოობა დაიკარგა / sandooba daikarga — "credibility was lost"**
**სანდოობა კითხვის ნიშნის ქვეშ დგება / sandooba kitxvis nišnis qvesh dgeба — "credibility is called into question"**

Documented in P2b (Constructions 13.2 and 13.3).

### Attested examples

- **სანდოობა დაიკარგა** → sandooba daikarga → "credibility was lost / trustworthiness was forfeited"
- **სანდოობა კითხვის ნიშნის ქვეშ დგება** → sandooba kitxvis nišnis qvesh dgeба → "credibility is called into question / trustworthiness is under a question mark"

### Notes
daikarga (was lost, was forfeited — past passive of dakargva) expresses a completed change of state: the property of reliability has been lost. The construction with kitxvis nišnis qvesh dgeба (stands under a question mark) expresses an ongoing dynamic state of being questioned rather than a completed loss. These are the only attested dynamic expressions for the reliability dimension in the CONTEXT; no construction for "gaining credibility" is documented.

## 9. LYING AS A DYNAMIC ACT AND ITS CONSEQUENCES

### Pattern
**სიცრუეში გაიჭირა / sicrueshi gaič̣ira — "was caught in a lie"**
**სიცრუე სიცრუეს ბადებს / sicrue sicrues badebs — "one lie breeds another"**
**სიცრუე ფეხებზე დგას / sicrue pexebze dgas — "a lie has short legs"**

Documented in P2b (Constructions 9.1, 9.5, 9.3).

### Attested examples

- **სიცრუეში გაიჭირა** → sicrueshi gaič̣ira → "he/she was caught in a lie / got caught lying"
- **სიცრუე სიცრუეს ბადებს** → sicrue sicrues badebs → "one lie breeds another / lies beget lies"
- **სიცრუე ფეხებზე დგას** → sicrue pexebze dgas → "a lie has short legs / lies don't last long"

### Notes
These three constructions encode different dynamic aspects of lying: the moment of exposure (gaič̣ira — was caught), the generative causation of further lying (badebs — breeds, generates), and the inherent instability of falsehood (pexebze dgas — stands on legs, i.e., is unstable). The verb badebs (breeds, generates) in sicrue sicrues badebs is the only attested causative construction in which a truth-domain noun (sicrue) is itself the causal agent rather than a person.

## 10. MAKING A MISTAKE AND ITS CORRECTION

### Pattern
**შეცდომა დაუშვა / šecdoma daušva — "made a mistake"**
**შეცდომა გამოასწორა / šecdoma gamoasq̇ora — "corrected the mistake"**
**შეცდომა გაიმეორა / šecdoma gaimeora — "repeated the mistake"**

Documented in P2b (Constructions 11.1, 11.2, 11.7).

### Attested examples

- **შეცდომა დაუშვა** → šecdoma daušva → "he/she made a mistake / committed an error"
- **შეცდომა გამოასწორა** → šecdoma gamoasq̇ora → "he/she corrected the mistake / fixed the error"
- **შეცდომა გაიმეორა** → šecdoma gaimeora → "he/she repeated the mistake / made the same error again"

### Notes
These three constructions form a dynamic sequence: the initial commission of an error (daušva), its correction (gamoasq̇ora), and its recurrence (gaimeora). The verb daušva (allowed, committed — from dauSveba, "to allow/permit to occur") is the conventional verb for making a mistake in Georgian; P2b describes it as "the standard periphrastic construction for 'to make a mistake.'" gamoasq̇ora (corrected, fixed) expresses the causative reversal of the error state. No single-word verb meaning "to err" in the sense of a completed act is documented in the CONTEXT; the periphrastic šecdoma daušva is the standard form.

## 11. JUSTIFICATION AND ACQUITTAL: DYNAMIC CHANGE IN LEGAL STATUS

### Pattern
**გამართლება / gamartleba — acquittal, vindication, justification**
**გამართლება სასამართლოში / gamartleba sasamartloši — acquittal in court**
**გამართლება ბრალდებისგან / gamartleba braldebisgan — exoneration from charges**

Documented in P1b Root 1 (items 17–19).

### Attested examples

- **გამართლება სასამართლოში** → gamartleba sasamartloši → "acquittal in court"
- **გამართლება ბრალდებისგან** → gamartleba braldebisgan → "acquittal from charges / exoneration"
- **გამართლება ძნელია** → gamartleba ʒnelia → "justification is difficult / it is hard to justify"

### Notes
gamartleba (preverb ga- + marṭl- + -eba) encodes a dynamic change of legal status: a person moves from being accused or charged to being formally cleared. This is the most institutionally prominent dynamic expression derived from the marṭ- root. P5 Opposition 30 pairs gamartleba with q̇albi braldeba (false accusation) as opposing legal acts. The construction gamartleba ʒnelia expresses a constraint on the dynamic: the change of state is possible but difficult.

## 12. TRUTH BEING HIDDEN OR SUPPRESSED

### Pattern
**ჭეშმარიტება დაიმალა / č̣ešmariteba daimala — "the truth was hidden"**
**სიმართლის ძიება / simartlis ʒieba — "the search for truth" (implying truth is not yet found)**

Documented in P2b (Construction 2.7) and P1b (item 32).

### Attested examples

- **ჭეშმარიტება დაიმალა** → č̣ešmariteba daimala → "the truth was hidden / the truth was concealed"
- **სიმართლის ძიება** → simartlis ʒieba → "seeking the truth / the search for truth"

### Notes
daimala (was hidden, was concealed — past passive of damalva) expresses a completed causative act directed against truth: an agent has caused truth to be in a concealed state. The agent is not specified in the attested construction. simartlis ʒieba (seeking the truth) implies a dynamic state in which truth is not yet accessible, complementing the exposure constructions in Section 7. P2b describes simartlis ʒieba as used "in philosophical, journalistic, and moral discourse to describe the pursuit of truth as a value or activity."
