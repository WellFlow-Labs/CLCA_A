POSSESSIVE EPISTEMIC CONSTRUCTION** (*-na-* + truth nominal) | *Nina uhakika.* | Nina uhakika. | "I am sure. / I have certainty." | [CORE] | *Nina uhakika* is the standard positive counterpart; both forms are formulaic. No parallel *nina kweli* construction is attested for propositional truth; the possessive epistemic pattern is restricted to certainty/doubt nominals. |

## SUMMARY NOTES

**Asymmetries flagged:**
- The "tell the truth" directive uses *sema ukweli* (light verb *kusema*), not a *piga* construction; *piga* is restricted to *uongo* in this domain.
- No agentive nominal for "truth-teller" is directly derived from *kweli* or *ukweli*; periphrastic expression only.
- The possessive epistemic construction (*nina/sina uhakika*) does not extend to propositional truth (*kweli*); the two sub-domains use distinct constructions.
- The inchoative periphrastic (*kuwa na uhakika*) is rated [MARGINAL] relative to the lexicalized *-ika* forms and the formulaic *nina/sina uhakika*.

**No constructions have been added beyond those identified in P4a.**
