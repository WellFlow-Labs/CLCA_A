# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2b Compositional Constructions
# Language: Finnish (fi)

---

## Preliminary Note on Scope and Method

This step documents attested multi-word constructions involving the 30 P1 base items. The focus is on periphrastic, idiomatic, and syntactically-driven constructions that are not reducible to the derivational morphology documented in P2a. Finnish is a morphologically rich language in which much semantic work done by multi-word constructions in other languages is accomplished through derivation and case inflection; this means that the inventory of genuinely multi-word constructions is smaller than it might be in a more analytic language. Absence is recorded explicitly where no common construction is identified.

Constructions are organized by P1 base item. Where a construction involves multiple P1 items, it is listed under the most semantically central one with a cross-reference.

## Item 1: tosi

---

**Construction 1**

1. **Base Form:** tosi
2. **Example:** Se on tosi.
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** It is true. / That is true.
5. **Usage notes:** The basic predicative construction asserting the truth of a proposition or state of affairs. Used in both spoken and written Finnish across all registers. The subject *se* (it/that) typically refers to a previously mentioned proposition or situation.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 2**

1. **Base Form:** tosi
2. **Example:** Onko se tosi?
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** Is it true? / Is that true?
5. **Usage notes:** The interrogative form of the predicative truth-assertion construction. Used in everyday discourse to request confirmation of a claim or piece of information. High frequency in spoken Finnish.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 3**

1. **Base Form:** tosi
2. **Example:** tosi vai tarua
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** true or legend; fact or fiction; real or mythical
5. **Usage notes:** A fixed binary construction contrasting factual truth with legend or myth (*taru* — legend, myth). Used as a framing device in journalism, popular media, and everyday discourse to introduce a question about whether something is real or merely legendary (*tosi vai tarua: toimiiko se?* — "fact or fiction: does it work?"). Functions as a formulaic phrase.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 4**

1. **Base Form:** tosi
2. **Example:** totta kai
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** of course; naturally; certainly
5. **Usage notes:** A fixed affirmative expression using the partitive form *totta* (of truth/true) combined with *kai* (surely, I suppose). Functions as a discourse particle asserting that something is obviously or naturally the case. Very high frequency in spoken Finnish. Note: *totta* is the partitive of *tosi/totuus*.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 5**

1. **Base Form:** tosi
2. **Example:** totta puhuen
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** to tell the truth; truthfully speaking; frankly
5. **Usage notes:** A participial construction using *totta* (partitive of truth) + *puhuen* (speaking, present participle of *puhua*). Functions as a sentence-initial or parenthetical adverbial signalling that the speaker is about to make a frank or honest statement. Common in spoken and written Finnish.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 6**

1. **Base Form:** tosi
2. **Example:** ei totta sanaakaan
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** not a word of truth; not a true word
5. **Usage notes:** A negated construction asserting the complete absence of truth in a statement (*siinä ei ole totta sanaakaan* — "there is not a word of truth in that"). Uses the partitive *totta* + *sanaakaan* (not even a word, partitive of *sana* with the *-kaan* additive particle). Idiomatic in force.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 7**

1. **Base Form:** tosi
2. **Example:** perillä totuudesta
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** aware of the truth; in possession of the truth; knowing the truth
5. **Usage notes:** A postpositional construction using *perillä* (informed about, aware of, literally "at the destination") + the adessive/elative of *totuus*. Used to assert that someone knows or has access to the truth of a matter (*hän on perillä totuudesta* — "he is aware of the truth"). Common in journalistic and everyday discourse.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 8**

1. **Base Form:** tosi
2. **Example:** totuus on toisenlainen
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** the truth is different; the reality is otherwise
5. **Usage notes:** A predicative construction asserting that the actual truth diverges from what has been claimed or assumed (*mutta totuus on toisenlainen* — "but the truth is different"). Functions as a corrective or contrastive framing device in journalistic and argumentative discourse.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 9**

1. **Base Form:** tosi
2. **Example:** totuus tulee ilmi
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** the truth comes out; the truth comes to light
5. **Usage notes:** A construction using *tulla ilmi* (to come to light, to be revealed) with *totuus* as subject. *Tulla ilmi* is a fixed verb-particle construction meaning "to be revealed" or "to come to light." Very common in journalistic, legal, and narrative discourse.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 10**

1. **Base Form:** tosi
2. **Example:** kertoa totuus
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** to tell the truth
5. **Usage notes:** The standard verb-object construction for the act of truth-telling (*kerro totuus* — "tell the truth"; *hän kertoi totuuden* — "he told the truth"). Uses *kertoa* (to tell, to narrate) with *totuus* in the accusative/genitive. High frequency across all registers.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 11**

1. **Base Form:** tosi
2. **Example:** totuus on karvas
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** the truth is bitter; truth is hard to swallow
5. **Usage notes:** A predicative construction using *karvas* (bitter) as a predicate of *totuus*. Functions as a semi-proverbial expression acknowledging that true information is often unwelcome or painful. Attested in journalistic and everyday discourse.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 12**

1. **Base Form:** tosi
2. **Example:** totuus on jossain siellä
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** the truth is out there
5. **Usage notes:** A construction that has entered Finnish usage partly through the influence of the television series *The X-Files* (*Salaiset kansiot*). Used both seriously and ironically in journalistic and popular discourse to suggest that the truth exists but is hidden or difficult to access. Attested in Finnish media.
6. **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**Construction 13**

1. **Base Form:** tosi
2. **Example:** puhua totta
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** to speak the truth; to tell the truth; to be truthful
5. **Usage notes:** A verb-object construction using *puhua* (to speak) + *totta* (partitive of *tosi/totuus*). Distinct from *kertoa totuus* in that it emphasizes the manner of speaking (truthfully) rather than the disclosure of a specific truth. High frequency in everyday and formal discourse.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 14**

1. **Base Form:** tosi
2. **Example:** sanoa suoraan
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** to say directly; to speak plainly; to be frank
5. **Usage notes:** A verb-adverb construction using *sanoa* (to say) + *suoraan* (directly, straight). While not derived from *tosi*, this construction functions as a near-synonym of *puhua totta* in contexts of frank truth-telling and frequently co-occurs with truth-evaluation vocabulary. Included here as an attested periphrastic alternative. High frequency in spoken Finnish.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Item 2: totuus

*Note: Several constructions involving totuus are documented under Item 1 above (tosi), as the two items share a root and many constructions use the nominal form totuus. Cross-reference: Item 1, Constructions 7–11, 13. Additional constructions specific to totuus as a nominal follow.*

---

**Construction 1**

1. **Base Form:** totuus
2. **Example:** totuuden hetki
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** the moment of truth
5. **Usage notes:** A genitive-noun construction (*totuuden* — genitive of *totuus* + *hetki* — moment) functioning as a fixed phrase for a decisive or revealing moment. Calqued from Spanish/English "moment of truth" (*el momento de la verdad*). Attested in journalistic and everyday Finnish.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 2**

1. **Base Form:** totuus
2. **Example:** totuuden etsiminen
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** the search for truth; seeking the truth
5. **Usage notes:** A genitive-noun construction (*totuuden* + *etsiminen* — the act of searching, action nominal of *etsiä*). Used in philosophical, journalistic, and everyday discourse to describe the epistemic activity of seeking truth. Common in academic and popular contexts.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 3**

1. **Base Form:** totuus
2. **Example:** totuuden nimissä
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** in the name of truth; for the sake of truth
5. **Usage notes:** A postpositional construction using *nimissä* (in the name of, in the names of) with the genitive *totuuden*. Used in rhetorical and argumentative contexts to invoke truth as a justification for a statement or action (*totuuden nimissä on sanottava* — "in the name of truth it must be said"). Attested in formal and journalistic discourse.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 4**

1. **Base Form:** totuus
2. **Example:** totuuden mukainen
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** in accordance with the truth; truthful; accurate
5. **Usage notes:** A postpositional construction using *mukainen* (in accordance with) with the genitive *totuuden*. Functions as an adjectival phrase evaluating whether a statement or representation corresponds to the truth. Note: the lexicalized compound *totuudenmukainen* (documented in P2a) is the more common form; the syntactic construction is also attested.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 5**

1. **Base Form:** totuus
2. **Example:** totuus on se, että
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** the truth is that; the fact is that
5. **Usage notes:** A cleft-like construction using *totuus on se, että* as a framing device to introduce a frank or corrective statement. Functions as a discourse marker signalling that what follows is the speaker's honest assessment of a situation. Common in spoken and journalistic Finnish.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 6**

1. **Base Form:** totuus
2. **Example:** koko totuus
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** the whole truth; the full truth
5. **Usage notes:** A quantifier-noun construction using *koko* (whole, entire) + *totuus*. Used in legal, journalistic, and everyday contexts to assert that a complete and unedited account is being given (*koko totuus ja vain totuus* — "the whole truth and nothing but the truth"). High frequency in legal and journalistic discourse.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 7**

1. **Base Form:** totuus
2. **Example:** koko totuus ja vain totuus
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** the whole truth and nothing but the truth
5. **Usage notes:** The Finnish rendering of the courtroom oath formula. Used in legal contexts and also cited in journalistic and popular discourse. The construction is a calque of the English/Latin legal formula and is attested in Finnish legal and media contexts.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 8**

1. **Base Form:** totuus
2. **Example:** totuus on usein epämukava
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** the truth is often uncomfortable; truth is often inconvenient
5. **Usage notes:** A predicative construction functioning as a semi-proverbial observation about the nature of truth. Attested in journalistic and discursive contexts as a framing device for difficult disclosures. Represents a productive pattern rather than a single fixed phrase.
6. **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

## Item 3: aito

---

**Construction 1**

1. **Base Form:** aito
2. **Example:** aito ja alkuperäinen
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** genuine and original; the real thing; authentic and original
5. **Usage notes:** A coordinated adjectival construction pairing *aito* (genuine) with *alkuperäinen* (original). Functions as a fixed or semi-fixed phrase asserting both authenticity and originality, often used in commercial, legal, and everyday contexts (*aito ja alkuperäinen tuote* — "a genuine and original product"). The pairing is formulaic.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 2**

1. **Base Form:** aito
2. **Example:** aito suomalainen
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** a true/authentic Finn; a genuine Finnish person
5. **Usage notes:** A noun phrase using *aito* as a modifier of an ethnic or national identity noun. Used in identity discourse, advertising, and cultural commentary to assert authentic national or cultural identity. Can be used straightforwardly or ironically.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 3**

1. **Base Form:** aito
2. **Example:** aito nahka
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** genuine leather; real leather
5. **Usage notes:** A noun phrase used in commercial and consumer contexts to assert that a material is not synthetic or imitation. Represents a productive pattern (*aito* + material noun) used across commercial labelling and advertising.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 4**

1. **Base Form:** aito
2. **Example:** Se on aito.
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** It is genuine. / It is authentic. / It is the real thing.
5. **Usage notes:** The basic predicative construction asserting the authenticity of an object, person, or emotion. Used in art authentication, commercial, and everyday contexts. High frequency across registers.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 5**

1. **Base Form:** aito
2. **Example:** aito tunne
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** a genuine feeling; an authentic emotion
5. **Usage notes:** A noun phrase used in psychological, literary, and everyday discourse to assert that an emotion or feeling is not performed or simulated. Represents a productive pattern (*aito* + emotion noun) common in existentialist and humanistic discourse.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Item 4: oikea

---

**Construction 1**

1. **Base Form:** oikea
2. **Example:** oikea vastaus
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** the correct answer; the right answer
5. **Usage notes:** A noun phrase using *oikea* as a modifier of *vastaus* (answer). The standard construction for evaluating correctness of answers in educational, quiz, and everyday contexts. High frequency.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 2**

1. **Base Form:** oikea
2. **Example:** oikea henkilö
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** the right person; the correct person
5. **Usage notes:** A noun phrase asserting that a person is the one properly identified or suited for a role or situation (*oikea henkilö oikeaan paikkaan* — "the right person in the right place"). Used in administrative, legal, and everyday contexts.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 3**

1. **Base Form:** oikea
2. **Example:** oikea aika
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** the right time; the proper time
5. **Usage notes:** A noun phrase asserting temporal appropriateness (*oikeaan aikaan* — "at the right time"). Part of a productive pattern (*oikea* + time/place/person noun) expressing normative or identificatory rightness. High frequency in everyday discourse.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 4**

1. **Base Form:** oikea
2. **Example:** oikeaan aikaan, oikeassa paikassa
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** at the right time, in the right place
5. **Usage notes:** A coordinated locative construction functioning as a semi-fixed phrase describing fortunate or appropriate positioning in time and space. Used in everyday and journalistic discourse. Represents a formulaic extension of the *oikea* + noun pattern.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 5**

1. **Base Form:** oikea
2. **Example:** olla oikeassa
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** to be right; to be correct
5. **Usage notes:** The standard periphrastic construction for asserting that a person's belief or claim is correct, using *olla* (to be) + *oikeassa* (inessive of *oikea*). Contrasts with *olla väärässä* (to be wrong). Very high frequency in spoken and written Finnish across all registers.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 6**

1. **Base Form:** oikea
2. **Example:** olla väärässä
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** to be wrong; to be mistaken
5. **Usage notes:** The standard periphrastic construction for asserting that a person's belief or claim is incorrect, using *olla* (to be) + *väärässä* (inessive of *väärä*). The direct antonym of *olla oikeassa*. Very high frequency across all registers. Cross-reference: Item 13 (väärä).
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 7**

1. **Base Form:** oikea
2. **Example:** osoittautua oikeaksi
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** to turn out to be correct; to prove to be right
5. **Usage notes:** A construction using *osoittautua* (to turn out to be, to prove to be) + translative *oikeaksi*. Used to assert that something that was claimed or predicted has been confirmed as correct (*ennuste osoittautui oikeaksi* — "the prediction turned out to be correct"). Common in journalistic and analytical discourse.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 8**

1. **Base Form:** oikea
2. **Example:** oikea elämä
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** real life; actual life
5. **Usage notes:** A noun phrase contrasting lived reality with fiction, simulation, or idealization (*oikeassa elämässä* — "in real life"). Functionally overlaps with *todellinen elämä* but is more colloquial. High frequency in everyday and journalistic discourse.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 9**

1. **Base Form:** oikea
2. **Example:** oikea sana
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** the right word; the correct word
5. **Usage notes:** A noun phrase used in linguistic, editorial, and everyday contexts to assert that a particular word is the appropriate or correct one for a given context (*etsiä oikeaa sanaa* — "to search for the right word"). Common in language-evaluation discourse.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 10**

1. **Base Form:** oikea
2. **Example:** oikeuden edessä
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** before the court; in court; before justice
5. **Usage notes:** A postpositional construction using *edessä* (before, in front of) with the genitive *oikeuden*. Standard expression for appearing in legal proceedings (*vastata oikeuden edessä* — "to answer before the court"). High frequency in legal and journalistic discourse.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 11**

1. **Base Form:** oikea
2. **Example:** viedä oikeuteen
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** to take to court; to sue; to bring legal proceedings against
5. **Usage notes:** A verb-phrase construction using *viedä* (to take, to bring) + *oikeuteen* (illative of *oikeus*). The standard expression for initiating legal proceedings against someone (*viedä asia oikeuteen* — "to take the matter to court"). High frequency in legal and journalistic discourse.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 12**

1. **Base Form:** oikea
2. **Example:** oikeus on sokea
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** justice is blind
5. **Usage notes:** A predicative construction functioning as a proverbial expression asserting the impartiality of justice (*oikeus on sokea, se ei katso henkilöä* — "justice is blind, it does not look at the person"). Attested in Finnish legal and popular discourse as a calque of the classical topos.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 3**

1. **Base Form:** oikeellisuus
2. **Example:** poliittinen oikeellisuus
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** political correctness
5. **Usage notes:** A noun phrase using *poliittinen* (political) as a modifier of *oikeellisuus*. The standard Finnish rendering of "political correctness." Attested in journalistic, political, and everyday discourse. Can be used descriptively or critically.

3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** real life; actual life

**Construction 3**

2. **Example:** todellinen ongelma
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** a real problem; an actual problem
5. **Usage notes:** A noun phrase asserting that a problem genuinely exists as opposed to being imagined or exaggerated. Represents a productive pattern (*todellinen* + problem/issue noun) common in political and analytical discourse.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 4**

1. **Base Form:** todellinen
2. **Example:** todellisuudessa
3. **Transliteration:** N/A

Used to assert that something previously doubted or uncertain has been confirmed as real (*uhka osoittautui todelliseksi* — "the threat turned out to be real"). Common in journalistic and analytical discourse.

## Item 7: todellisuus

Used in psychological and political discourse to characterize a person or position as disconnected from actual conditions (*hän on irti todellisuudesta* — "he is out of touch with reality"). Common in journalistic and political commentary.

**Construction 3**

3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** to correspond to reality; to match reality

**Construction 4**

5. **Usage notes:** An idiomatic construction using *iskeä vasten kasvoja* (to strike in the face) with *todellisuus* as subject. Used in journalistic and everyday discourse to describe a sudden and unwelcome confrontation with actual conditions. Attested in Finnish media.

**Construction 5**

3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** reality surpasses all imagination; truth is stranger than fiction
5. **Usage notes:** A construction functioning as a Finnish equivalent of the proverbial "truth is stranger than fiction." Uses *ylittää* (to surpass, to exceed) + *kaiken kuvittelun* (all imagination). Attested in journalistic and popular discourse as a framing device.

## Item 8: luotettava

---

**Construction 1**

1. **Base Form:** luotettava
2. **Example:** luotettava lähde
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
