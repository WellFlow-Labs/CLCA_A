# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2a Derivational System
# Language: Tamil (ta)

---

**Transliteration standard:** ISO 15919 (applied consistently throughout)

---

*Preliminary note on scope: This step documents derivational morphology — the formation of new lexical items from P1 base forms through affixation, compounding with grammatical morphemes, or other word-formation processes. Forms already fully documented in P1 and P1b are cross-referenced rather than re-described at length. The inventory below reflects only forms for which there is positive evidence of use; paradigmatic gaps are preserved and noted.*

## Base Form 1: உண்மை / *uṇmai*

**Morphological note:** *uṇmai* is itself a derived nominal (from *uḷ* + *-mai*). It functions as the base for a productive derivational series in modern Tamil. The root is highly productive; the forms below represent the attested derivational layer above *uṇmai* as a base.

---

**Derived Form 1.1**

1. **Base Form:** உண்மை / *uṇmai*
2. **Derived Form:** உண்மையான
3. **Transliteration:** uṇmaiyāṉa
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *uṇmai* + *-āṉa* (adjectival copular suffix, relativized form of *āku* "to become/be")
5. **Natural English translation:** true, genuine, real, authentic
6. **Usage:** Attributive adjective modifying nouns across all registers; the standard adjectival form of *uṇmai*. Documented in P1 (item 4) and P1b (Root 1, item 2).
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 1.2**

1. **Base Form:** உண்மை / *uṇmai*
2. **Derived Form:** உண்மையாக
3. **Transliteration:** uṇmaiyāka
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *uṇmai* + *-āka* (adverbial/predicative suffix, converbal form of *āku*)
5. **Natural English translation:** truly, honestly, genuinely; predicatively: is true/genuine
6. **Usage:** Adverbial form qualifying verbs or predicates; also used predicatively to assert that something is genuinely the case. Documented in P1 (item 1, usage note) and P1b (Root 1, item 3).
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 1.3**

1. **Base Form:** உண்மை / *uṇmai*
2. **Derived Form:** உண்மையில்
3. **Transliteration:** uṇmaiyil
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *uṇmai* + locative suffix *-il*
5. **Natural English translation:** in truth, in fact, actually
6. **Usage:** Locative adverbial functioning as a sentence-frame marker, typically introducing a correction or clarification of what is actually the case. Documented in P1b (Root 1, item 5).
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 1.4**

1. **Base Form:** உண்மை / *uṇmai*
2. **Derived Form:** உண்மையிலேயே
3. **Transliteration:** uṇmaiyilēyē
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *uṇmai* + locative *-il* + emphatic clitic *-ē* + reinforcing *-yē*
5. **Natural English translation:** truly, really, genuinely, in actual fact
6. **Usage:** Emphatic adverbial used in spoken Tamil to intensify an assertion of sincerity or factual accuracy. Documented in P1 (item 17) and P1b (Root 1, item 4).
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 1.5**

1. **Base Form:** உண்மை / *uṇmai*
2. **Derived Form:** உண்மைத்தன்மை
3. **Transliteration:** uṇmai-t-taṉmai
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *uṇmai* + linking *-t-* + *taṉmai* (nature, quality; itself *taṉ* "self/own" + *-mai*)
5. **Natural English translation:** truthfulness, authenticity, genuineness (as an abstract property)
6. **Usage:** Abstract nominal used in philosophical, literary-critical, and formal written Tamil to denote the property of being true or authentic. Documented in P1 (item 10) and P1b (Root 1, item 6).
7. **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**Derived Form 1.6**

1. **Base Form:** உண்மை / *uṇmai*
2. **Derived Form:** உண்மைப்படுத்துதல்
3. **Transliteration:** uṇmaippaṭuttutal
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *uṇmai* + *-p-* (linking consonant) + *paṭuttu* (causative verb stem from *paṭu* "to undergo/become") + verbal noun suffix *-tal*
5. **Natural English translation:** authentication, verification, substantiation (as a process)
6. **Usage:** Formal and administrative verbal noun denoting the act of establishing the truth or genuineness of a document, claim, or identity. Documented in P1 (item 24) and P1b (Root 1, item 8).
7. **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**Derived Form 1.7**

1. **Base Form:** உண்மை / *uṇmai*
2. **Derived Form:** உண்மைப்படுத்து
3. **Transliteration:** uṇmaippaṭuttu
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *uṇmai* + *-p-* + *paṭuttu* (causative verb stem; finite/imperative form)
5. **Natural English translation:** authenticate, verify, substantiate, confirm
6. **Usage:** The finite verb stem corresponding to 1.6 above, used in administrative and legal instructions and directives. Documented in P1b (Root 1, item 9).
7. **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**Derivational note on உண்மை:** The suffix *-āṉa* (1.1) and *-āka* (1.2) are productive across Tamil nominals generally; their application to *uṇmai* is fully regular. The locative forms (1.3, 1.4) represent grammatical case-marking rather than lexical derivation strictly speaking, but are included here because they yield conventionalized adverbial expressions with discourse-level functions not fully predictable from the morphology alone. The *-taṉmai* compound (1.5) and the *-paṭuttu* causative series (1.6, 1.7) represent more lexically specific derivational events.

## Base Form 2: பொய் / *poy*

**Morphological note:** *poy* is a root nominal of debated etymology. It participates in adjectival and adverbial derivation via the same suffixes as *uṇmai*, and in nominal compounding.

---

**Derived Form 2.1**

1. **Base Form:** பொய் / *poy*
2. **Derived Form:** பொய்யான
3. **Transliteration:** poyyāṉa
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *poy* + gemination *-y-* + *-āṉa* (adjectival suffix)
5. **Natural English translation:** false, untrue, deceptive, fake
6. **Usage:** Adjectival form of *poy*, used attributively to characterize statements, persons, or representations as false. Functions as the direct antonym of *uṇmaiyāṉa* in parallel evaluative constructions. Common across spoken and written registers.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2.2**

1. **Base Form:** பொய் / *poy*
2. **Derived Form:** பொய்யாக
3. **Transliteration:** poyyāka
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *poy* + *-y-* + *-āka* (adverbial/predicative suffix)
5. **Natural English translation:** falsely, deceptively; predicatively: is false
6. **Usage:** Adverbial and predicative form of *poy*, used to qualify conduct or assertions as false or deceptive. Common in spoken and written Tamil: *poyyāka colluvāṉ* (he will speak falsely).
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2.3**

1. **Base Form:** பொய் / *poy*
2. **Derived Form:** பொய்மை
3. **Transliteration:** poymai
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *poy* + *-mai* (nominalizing suffix)
5. **Natural English translation:** falsehood, falseness, untruth (as an abstract quality)
6. **Usage:** Abstract nominal denoting the quality or state of being false or deceptive. Used in literary and formal written Tamil, particularly in moral and philosophical discourse contrasting *uṇmai* (truth) with *poymai* (falsehood). Less frequent in everyday speech than *poy* alone.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2.4**

1. **Base Form:** பொய் / *poy*
2. **Derived Form:** பொய்யர்
3. **Transliteration:** poyyar
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *poy* + *-y-* + *-ar* (agentive/person suffix, honorific or plural)
5. **Natural English translation:** liars, deceivers (plural/honorific agentive)
6. **Usage:** Agentive nominal denoting persons who lie or deceive. Used in literary and formal Tamil in moral condemnation. The singular *poyyaṉ* (male liar) also occurs in classical and colloquial registers.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2.5**

1. **Base Form:** பொய் / *poy*
2. **Derived Form:** பொய்யன்
3. **Transliteration:** poyyaṉ
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *poy* + *-y-* + *-aṉ* (masculine singular agentive suffix)
5. **Natural English translation:** liar (masculine singular)
6. **Usage:** Masculine singular agentive nominal used in colloquial and literary Tamil to characterize a male person as a liar. Used in direct accusation and narrative characterization. The feminine counterpart *poyyaḷ* also occurs.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2.6**

1. **Base Form:** பொய் / *poy*
2. **Derived Form:** பொய்யாளன்
3. **Transliteration:** poyyāḷaṉ
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *poy* + *-y-* + *-āḷaṉ* (agentive suffix with *āḷ* "person" + masculine *-aṉ*)
5. **Natural English translation:** liar, deceiver (masculine, somewhat more formal than *poyyaṉ*)
6. **Usage:** An agentive nominal with slightly more formal or emphatic character than *poyyaṉ*, used in moral condemnation and narrative. The form *poyyāḷar* (honorific/plural) also occurs in formal written Tamil.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2.7**

1. **Base Form:** பொய் / *poy*
2. **Derived Form:** பொய்சாட்சி
3. **Transliteration:** poy-cāṭci
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *poy* (false) + *cāṭci* (witness, testimony; from Sanskrit *sākṣin*)
5. **Natural English translation:** false witness, perjury, false testimony
6. **Usage:** A compound nominal used in legal and moral discourse to characterize testimony as false or to denote the act of perjury. Documented in P1 (item 2, usage note). Common in legal Tamil and in moral condemnation.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derivational note on பொய்:** The *-mai* nominalization (2.3) and the agentive forms (2.4–2.6) are well-attested. The compound *poy-cāṭci* (2.7) represents nominal compounding rather than affixal derivation. No verbal derivation from *poy* as a base (e.g., a causative or inchoative) has been identified as a common independent lexical item; *poy colluvāṉ* (he will speak falsely) uses *poy* as a nominal object rather than a verbal base.

## Base Form 3: நேர்மை / *nērmai*

**Morphological note:** *nērmai* is itself derived from *nēr* + *-mai*. The forms below treat *nērmai* as the base, consistent with its status as the primary lexical item in P1. Derivations from the root *nēr* directly are noted where relevant.

---

**Derived Form 3.1**

1. **Base Form:** நேர்மை / *nērmai*
2. **Derived Form:** நேர்மையான
3. **Transliteration:** nērmayāṉa
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *nērmai* + *-āṉa* (adjectival suffix; note vowel sandhi: *-ai* + *-āṉa* → *-ayāṉa*)
5. **Natural English translation:** honest, upright, straightforward, principled
6. **Usage:** The standard adjectival form of *nērmai*, used to characterize persons or conduct as honest and principled across all registers. Documented in P1b (Root 3, item 2).
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 3.2**

1. **Base Form:** நேர்மை / *nērmai*
2. **Derived Form:** நேர்மையாக
3. **Transliteration:** nērmayāka
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *nērmai* + *-āka* (adverbial suffix; same sandhi as 3.1)
5. **Natural English translation:** honestly, uprightly, straightforwardly
6. **Usage:** Adverbial form qualifying conduct or speech as honest. Common in everyday and formal speech. Documented in P1b (Root 3, item 3).
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 3.3**

1. **Base Form:** நேர்மை / *nērmai*
2. **Derived Form:** நேர்மையற்ற
3. **Transliteration:** nērmaiyaṟṟa
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *nērmai* + *aṟṟa* (privative participial suffix, from *aṟu* "to be severed/lacking")
5. **Natural English translation:** dishonest, lacking integrity, unprincipled
6. **Usage:** Privative adjectival form used in moral condemnation of persons or conduct. Documented in P1 (item 28) and P1b (Root 3, item 4).
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 3.4**

1. **Base Form:** நேர்மை / *nērmai*
2. **Derived Form:** நேர்மையின்மை
3. **Transliteration:** nērmayiṉmai
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *nērmai* + *iṉmai* (abstract nominal of absence/non-existence, from *il* "not exist" + *-mai*)
5. **Natural English translation:** dishonesty, lack of integrity, absence of honesty
6. **Usage:** Abstract nominal used in formal and written Tamil to denote the property of lacking honesty. Appears in moral, legal, and political discourse. Documented in P1b (Root 3, item 13).
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 3.5**

1. **Base Form:** நேர்மை / *nērmai*
2. **Derived Form:** நேர்மையாளர்
3. **Transliteration:** nērmayāḷar
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *nērmai* + *-āḷar* (agentive/person suffix with honorific plural *-ar*)
5. **Natural English translation:** honest person/persons, person(s) of integrity
6. **Usage:** Agentive nominal used in formal and written Tamil to refer to persons of integrity, often in political or moral discourse praising or calling for honest conduct. Documented in P1b (Root 3, item 6).
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 3.6**

1. **Base Form:** நேர்மை / *nērmai* (via root *நேர்* / *nēr*)
2. **Derived Form:** நேர்தன்மை
3. **Transliteration:** nēr-taṉmai
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *nēr* (straight, direct) + *taṉmai* (nature, quality)
5. **Natural English translation:** straightforwardness, directness, integrity
6. **Usage:** Formed directly from the root *nēr* rather than from *nērmai*; overlaps in meaning with *nērmai* but tends to appear in more explicitly philosophical or literary contexts. Documented in P1 (item 9) and P1b (Root 3, item 5).
7. **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**Derivational note on நேர்மை:** The *-āṉa*, *-āka*, *aṟṟa*, and *iṉmai* forms follow regular productive patterns. The agentive *-āḷar* form (3.5) is attested but less frequent than the adjectival forms. No verbal derivation from *nērmai* as a base has been identified as a common independent lexical item.

## Base Form 5: சரி / *cari*

**Morphological note:** *cari* functions as both a nominal (correctness) and a response particle. Its derivational productivity is moderate.

---

**Derived Form 5.1**

1. **Base Form:** சரி / *cari*
2. **Derived Form:** சரியான
3. **Transliteration:** cariyāṉa
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *cari* + *-y-* (glide) + *-āṉa* (adjectival suffix)
5. **Natural English translation:** correct, right, proper, accurate
6. **Usage:** The standard adjectival form of *cari*, used to evaluate statements, actions, or objects as correct or appropriate. Extremely common across all registers in spoken and written Tamil.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 5.2**

1. **Base Form:** சரி / *cari*
2. **Derived Form:** சரியாக
3. **Transliteration:** cariyāka
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *cari* + *-y-* + *-āka* (adverbial suffix)
5. **Natural English translation:** correctly, properly, accurately, rightly
6. **Usage:** Adverbial form qualifying actions or states as correct or proper. Very common in spoken Tamil: *cariyāka pēcu* (speak correctly/properly), *cariyāka puriyavillai* (I did not understand correctly).
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 5.3**

1. **Base Form:** சரி / *cari*
2. **Derived Form:** சரிபார்த்தல்
3. **Transliteration:** caripārtttal
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *cari* (correct) + *pār* (to see, look; here: to check) + verbal noun suffix *-ttal*
5. **Natural English translation:** verification, checking, confirmation
6. **Usage:** Verbal noun denoting the act of checking or verifying correctness. Documented in P1 (item 23). The finite form *caripārkkiṟēṉ* (I am checking/verifying) is widely used in spoken Tamil.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 5.4**

1. **Base Form:** சரி / *cari*
2. **Derived Form:** சரிசெய்தல்
3. **Transliteration:** cari ceyttal
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *cari* + *cey* (to do, make) + verbal noun *-ttal*
5. **Natural English translation:** correcting, fixing, rectifying, setting right
6. **Usage:** Verbal noun denoting the act of correcting or rectifying something that is wrong or broken. Common in everyday, technical, and administrative Tamil: *tavaṟai cari ceyttal* (correcting the mistake), *iyantiratai cari ceyttal* (fixing the machine).
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 5.5**

1. **Base Form:** சரி / *cari*
2. **Derived Form:** சரியில்லாத
3. **Transliteration:** cariyillāta
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *cari* + *y* + *illāta* (lacking, without; negative participial of *il*)
5. **Natural English translation:** incorrect, not right, improper, not working properly
6. **Usage:** Used in everyday speech to characterize something as not correct, not functioning properly, or not appropriate. Common in both epistemic (incorrect information) and practical (malfunctioning object) contexts: *avaṉ cariyillāta āḷ* (he is not a proper/right person).
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derivational note on சரி:** The *-āṉa* and *-āka* forms (5.1, 5.2) are fully regular. The compound verbal nouns *caripārtttal* (5.3) and *cari ceyttal* (5.4) represent productive compounding with light verbs (*pār*, *cey*) rather than affixal derivation strictly speaking, but are included here as they represent the primary lexicalized verbal expressions built on this base. No *-mai* nominalization (*\*carimai*) has been identified as a common form.

## Base Form 6: தவறு / *tavaṟu*

**Morphological note:** *tavaṟu* functions as both a nominal (mistake, error) and a verbal root (*tavar-*, to err, deviate). Both nominal and verbal derivations are attested.

---

**Derived Form 6.1**

1. **Base Form:** தவறு / *tavaṟu*
2. **Derived Form:** தவறான
3. **Transliteration:** tavaṟāṉa
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *tavaṟu* + *-āṉa* (adjectival suffix)
5. **Natural English translation:** wrong, erroneous, incorrect, mistaken
6. **Usage:** The standard adjectival form of *tavaṟu*, used to characterize statements, actions, or judgments as incorrect or morally wrong. Documented in P1 (item 6, usage note). Common across all registers.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 6.2**

1. **Base Form:** தவறு / *tavaṟu*
2. **Derived Form:** தவறாக
3. **Transliteration:** tavaṟāka
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *tavaṟu* + *-āka* (adverbial suffix)
5. **Natural English translation:** wrongly, erroneously, incorrectly, by mistake
6. **Usage:** Adverbial form qualifying actions or states as erroneous or mistaken. Common in spoken and written Tamil: *tavaṟāka purintukkoṇṭēṉ* (I understood it wrongly/mistakenly).
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 6.3**

1. **Base Form:** தவறு / *tavaṟu*
2. **Derived Form:** தவறில்லாத
3. **Transliteration:** tavaṟillāta
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *tavaṟu* + *illāta* (lacking, without)
5. **Natural English translation:** faultless, without error, correct, flawless
6. **Usage:** Used to characterize work, conduct, or statements as free from error or fault. Common in evaluative contexts in both spoken and written Tamil: *tavaṟillāta pāṭam* (a flawless lesson/performance).
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 6.4**

1. **Base Form:** தவறு / *tavaṟu*
2. **Derived Form:** தவறுதல்
3. **Transliteration:** tavaṟutal
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *tavaṟu* (verbal stem) + verbal noun suffix *-tal*
5. **Natural English translation:** erring, making a mistake, going wrong, missing (a target or obligation)
6. **Usage:** Verbal noun from the verbal use of the root, denoting the act of erring or failing. Used in moral and practical contexts: *கடமையில் தவறுதல்* (*kaṭamaiyil tavaṟutal*, failing in one's duty). Also used for missing an appointment or target.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 6.5**

1. **Base Form:** தவறு / *tavaṟu*
2. **Derived Form:** தவறவிடுதல்
3. **Transliteration:** tavaṟaviṭutal
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *tavaṟa* (infinitive of *tavaṟu*) + *viṭu* (to let go, allow; here: to miss/let slip) + verbal noun *-tal*
5. **Natural English translation:** missing (an opportunity), letting slip, failing to catch
6. **Usage:** Used to describe the act of missing an opportunity, event, or object. Common in everyday spoken Tamil: *vaāyppaittavaṟaviṭāte* (don't miss the opportunity). This form extends the root into a practical rather than strictly epistemic or moral domain.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derivational note on தவறு:** The adjectival and adverbial forms (6.1, 6.2) are fully regular. The verbal noun *tavaṟutal* (6.4) reflects the root's dual nominal/verbal status. The compound *tavaṟaviṭutal* (6.5) involves a light verb construction. No *-mai* nominalization (*\*tavaṟumai*) has been identified as a common form.

## Base Form 8: நம்பகத்தன்மை / *nampakat-taṉmai*

**Morphological note:** *nampakat-taṉmai* is a compound abstract nominal. It does not appear to generate further derivations of its own. The base *nampakam* participates in the following derivations.

---

**Derived Form 8.1**

1. **Base Form:** நம்பகத்தன்மை / *nampakat-taṉmai* (via base *நம்பகம்* / *nampakam*)
2. **Derived Form:** நம்பகமான
3. **Transliteration:** nampakamāṉa
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *nampakam* + *-āṉa* (adjectival suffix)
5. **Natural English translation:** reliable, trustworthy, credible, dependable
6. **Usage:** Adjectival form of *nampakam*, used to evaluate persons, sources, or information as worthy of trust. Documented in P1 (item 7) and P1b (Root 2, item 4).
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 8.2**

1. **Base Form:** நம்பகத்தன்மை / *nampakat-taṉmai* (via base *நம்பகம்* / *nampakam*)
2. **Derived Form:** நம்பகமற்ற
3. **Transliteration:** nampakam aṟṟa
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *nampakam* + *aṟṟa* (privative participial suffix)
5. **Natural English translation:** unreliable, untrustworthy, not credible
6. **Usage:** Privative adjectival form used to characterize persons, sources, or information as lacking credibility or reliability. Used in legal, journalistic, and everyday evaluative contexts as the antonym of *nampakamāṉa*.
