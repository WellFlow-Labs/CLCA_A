## 4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Stem + -დება (ongoing inchoative) | ის მართლდება | is martldeba | "He is being vindicated / He is proven right" | [CORE] | Attested for the mart- root; marks an ongoing process of becoming justified or vindicated. Not attested for namdvili, q̇albi, or č̣ešmariti (per P4a). |
| Preverb გა- + stem + -დება (completive inchoative) | გამართლდება | gamartldeba | "will be vindicated / becomes justified" | [CORE] | Completive aspect marked by preverb გა-; expresses a bounded transition to a resultant state. Restricted to the mart- root within this domain per P4a. |
| Idiomatic inchoative event (non-morphological) | სიმართლე გამოვიდა | simartle gamovida | "The truth came out" | [CORE] | Standard expression for truth transitioning from concealed to revealed; P4a notes this is not morphologically inchoative but functions as the closest natural equivalent for "truth becomes known." |

**Attested alternates:** The simartle gamovida construction is noted as functionally equivalent but structurally distinct from the -deba inchoative. No productive inchoative forms attested for namdvili, q̇albi, or č̣ešmariti.

## 6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| სი-...-ე circumfix (Pattern a) | სიმართლე | simartle | "truth" | [CORE] | Most semantically central pattern; produces abstract nouns naming a quality as a domain or substance (per P4a). Attested forms include simartle, siq̇albe, sizuste, sicrue. |
| Adjective + -ობა suffix (Pattern b) | ნამდვილობა | namdviloba | "authenticity, genuineness" | [CORE] | Names a property as possessed by a specific entity rather than as an abstract domain; not interchangeable with si-...-e forms per P4a. Attested: namdviloba, sandooba, legitimuroba, gulwrp̕eloba. |
| Adjective/participle + -ება suffix (Pattern c) | ჭეშმარიტება | č̣ešmariteba | (abstract noun of truth/verity) | [CORE] | Can function as either abstract noun or verbal noun depending on context; č̣ešmariteba is purely nominal while gaq̇albeba covers both "act of falsifying" and "falsification as category" (per P4a). |

**Attested alternates:** The three patterns are documented as non-interchangeable in P4a; no additional alternates attested within this domain.

| Compound agentive (religious/ideological) | მართლმადიდებელი | martlmadidebeli | "Orthodox Christian; right-glorifier" | [MARGINAL] | Restricted to a specific institutional and religious context; not a general-purpose agentive in the truth domain. | Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| მართალია, მაგრამ... ## 10. PRIVATIVE / NEGATED PROPERTY FORMATION

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| უ- prefix + stem (privative adjective) | უტყუარი | uẗq̇uari | "infallible, beyond doubt" (lit. "without lie") | [CORE] | Productive across the truth domain per P4a; tends to produce lexicalized adjectives with specific meanings rather than compositional negations. Attested: uẗq̇uari, usamart̕lo. |
| უ- prefix + stem (privative adjective) | უსამართლო | usamart̕lo | "unjust, unfair" (lit. "without justice") | [CORE] | Same morphological pattern; the resulting meaning is lexicalized and not fully predictable from the components (per P4a's note on u- vs. ara-). |

**Attested alternates:** P4a documents ara- as an alternative negating prefix (e.g., arasando, "not trustworthy"), described as producing more compositional negations. The two prefixes are noted as not freely interchangeable: u- yields lexicalized forms, ara- yields compositional negations. No further alternates attested within P4a data.
