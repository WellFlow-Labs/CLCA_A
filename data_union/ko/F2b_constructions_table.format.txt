| 진실 (jinsil) | 진실을 말하다 | jinsil-eul malhada | to tell the truth, to speak the truth | The most basic and frequent verbal construction with 진실. Used across all registers in moral, legal, and everyday contexts. Appears in both affirmative and negative forms (진실을 말하지 않다, to not tell the truth). Cross-referenced from P1b. | [CORE] |
| 진실 (jinsil) | 진실을 밝히다 | jinsil-eul balkhida | to reveal the truth, to bring the truth to light | A highly conventionalized collocation used in investigative, journalistic, and legal contexts to describe the act of uncovering or disclosing what is actually the case. 밝히다 (to illuminate, to clarify) is the preferred verb in this collocation; contrasts with 진실을 말하다 in that it implies prior concealment. Cross-referenced from P1b. | [CORE] |
| 진실 (jinsil) | 진실을 숨기다 | jinsil-eul sumgida | to hide the truth, to conceal the truth | Used to describe deliberate concealment of what is actually the case. Common in journalistic, legal, narrative, and interpersonal contexts. Contrasts with 진실을 밝히다 as the negative counterpart; frequently appears in opposition to it. | [CORE] |
| 진실 (jinsil) | 진실을 알다 | jinsil-eul alda | to know the truth | Verb-object collocation describing the epistemic state of knowing what is actually the case. Common across all registers; frequently appears in narrative and investigative contexts. Appears in constructions such as 진실을 알고 싶다 (to want to know the truth) and 진실을 알게 되다 (to come to know the truth). | [CORE] |
| 진실 (jinsil) | 진실을 외면하다 | jinsil-eul oemyeonhada | to turn away from the truth, to ignore the truth, to avert one's eyes from the truth | Used to characterize willful avoidance of an uncomfortable or inconvenient truth. 외면하다 (to turn one's face away) carries a moral dimension of culpable avoidance. Common in political, ethical, journalistic, and moral contexts. Carries a critical or accusatory tone. | [CORE] |
| 진실 (jinsil) | 진실을 왜곡하다 | jinsil-eul waegokada | to distort the truth, to twist the truth | Used in journalistic and political discourse to describe the misrepresentation of facts. 왜곡하다 (to distort, to warp) implies deliberate or systematic misrepresentation rather than simple error. The pairing of 진실 with 왜곡하다 is highly conventionalized. | [CORE] |
| 진실 (jinsil) | 진실이 밝혀지다 | jinsil-i balkhyeojida | the truth comes to light, the truth is revealed, the truth is uncovered | Passive/resultative construction describing the emergence of truth, typically after a period of concealment or uncertainty. The passive/spontaneous counterpart to 진실을 밝히다. Common in narrative, journalistic, and legal contexts. The subject is 진실 rather than an agent. Carries a slightly more agentive implication than 진실이 드러나다 — the truth is uncovered through investigation or disclosure rather than simply emerging. | [CORE] |
| 진실 (jinsil) | 진실이 드러나다 | jinsil-i deureonada | the truth emerges, the truth is exposed, the truth comes to light | Predicative collocation describing the emergence of truth, often in contexts where it had previously been concealed. Similar to 진실이 밝혀지다 but 드러나다 (to be exposed, to surface) carries a slightly stronger implication of something previously hidden becoming visible. Common in investigative, journalistic, and narrative contexts. The subject is 진실 and the verb 드러나다 is intransitive. | [CORE] |
| 진실 (jinsil) | 진실과 거짓을 가리다 | jinsil-gwa geojit-eul garida | to distinguish truth from falsehood, to separate truth from lies | Fixed collocation framing the epistemic task of discrimination between true and false. 가리다 (to distinguish, to determine) is the standard verb in this construction. Appears in legal, journalistic, and philosophical contexts. | [CORE] |
| 진실 (jinsil) | 진실과 거짓을 구별하다 | jinsil gwa geojit-eul gubyeolhada | to distinguish truth from falsehood | Verb-object collocation framing the epistemic task of differentiating true from false claims. Common in educational, philosophical, and journalistic discourse. The paired structure 진실과 거짓 (truth and falsehood) is itself a fixed nominal pair. | [CORE] |
| 진실 (jinsil) | 진실과 거짓 사이 | jinsil-gwa geojit sai | between truth and falsehood | A fixed framing construction used in titles, discourse, and analytical contexts to position something in the space between truth and lie. The pairing 진실과 거짓 is highly conventionalized as a polar opposition. | [CORE] |
| 진실 (jinsil) | 진실의 순간 | jinsil-ui sungan | moment of truth | Used to describe a decisive moment when the true nature of something or someone is revealed. Likely influenced by the Spanish/English expression "moment of truth." Used in everyday and journalistic Korean to describe situations where the true state of affairs becomes apparent or a critical decision must be made. Documented in P1b; cross-referenced here as a fixed nominal construction. | [CORE] |
| 진실 (jinsil) | 진실은 하나다 | jinsil-eun hanada | there is only one truth, the truth is singular | A formulaic assertion used in investigative, legal, and popular culture contexts (notably associated with the detective fiction genre in Korean popular culture) to assert that facts are determinate and singular. Functions as a rhetorical claim rather than a philosophical proposition in most uses. | [CORE] |
| 진실 (jinsil) | 진실만이 살 길이다 | jinsil-mani sal gil-ida | truth is the only way to survive, only the truth will save us | Proverbial expression used in moral and political discourse to assert that honesty is the only viable course of action in a difficult situation. The construction is formulaic and appears in public speech and media commentary. | [CORE] |
| 진실 (jinsil) | 진실 규명 | jinsil gyumyeong | truth investigation, clarification of the truth, truth-finding | Noun-noun compound used in political, legal, and historical contexts to refer to formal processes of establishing what actually happened. Cross-referenced from P1b. | [CORE] |
| 사실 (sasil) | 사실을 확인하다 | sasil-eul hwagin-hada | to verify the facts, to confirm the facts | Standard collocation for the act of checking whether reported information is accurate. Common in journalistic, administrative, legal, and everyday contexts. The basis for the compound 사실확인 (fact-checking) documented in P1b; here as a verbal construction. | [CORE] |
| 사실 (sasil) | 사실에 근거하다 | sasil-e geungeo-hada | to be based on facts, to be grounded in fact | Used to characterize a claim, argument, or representation as having factual foundation. Common in academic, journalistic, and legal discourse. The prepositional phrase 사실에 (on/in fact) combines with the verb 근거하다 (to be grounded in). Connects 사실 and 근거 from P1. | [CORE] |
| 사실 (sasil) | 사실을 왜곡하다 | sasil-eul waegokada | to distort the facts, to misrepresent the facts | Used in journalistic and political discourse to describe deliberate misrepresentation of factual information. Parallel in structure to 진실을 왜곡하다 but focuses on specific facts rather than truth as an abstract value. Connects 사실 and 왜곡 from P1. | [CORE] |
| 사실 (sasil) | 사실을 직시하다 | sasil-eul jiksihada | to face the facts squarely, to look the facts in the face | Used to describe the act of confronting reality without avoidance or self-deception. 직시하다 (to look directly at) implies a deliberate, unflinching engagement with what is actually the case. Common in motivational, political, and analytical discourse. Carries a connotation of unflinching acknowledgment. | [CORE] |
| 사실 (sasil) | 사실이 아니다 | sasil-i anida | it is not a fact, that is not true, that is not the case | The standard negation construction for factual claims. Extremely common across all registers as a denial or correction. More neutral in register than 거짓말이다 (that is a lie). Fully compositional but highly conventionalized as a denial formula. | [CORE] |
| 사실 (sasil) | 사실로 드러나다 | sasil-ro deureonada | to turn out to be true, to be revealed as fact | Used to describe the confirmation of a claim that was previously uncertain or disputed. The construction marks a transition from alleged or suspected to established. Common in journalistic and narrative contexts. | [CORE] |
| 사실 (sasil) | 사실로 밝혀지다 | sasil-ro balkhyeojida | to turn out to be true, to be revealed as fact | Used in journalistic and everyday contexts to describe the confirmation of a claim as factually accurate. The construction 사실로 + 밝혀지다/드러나다 is highly conventionalized. | [CORE] |
| 사실 (sasil) | 사실 여부를 따지다 | sasil yeobu-reul ttajida | to examine whether something is factual, to question the factual basis of something | Used in evaluative, investigative, legal, and journalistic contexts to describe the act of scrutinizing whether a claim is actually true. 따지다 (to examine closely, to question) implies critical scrutiny. 여부 (whether or not) is a productive element in formal Korean discourse. | [CORE] |
| 사실 (sasil) | 사실을 말하자면 | sasil-eul malhajamyeon | to tell the truth, if I'm being honest, speaking factually, the fact of the matter is | Discourse-framing construction used to introduce a frank or corrective statement. Functions as a hedging or truth-marking device at the beginning of an utterance, signaling that what follows is the speaker's genuine assessment. Common in spoken and written registers. | [CORE] |
| 사실 (sasil) | 엄연한 사실 | eomyeonhan sasil | an undeniable fact, a plain fact, an incontrovertible fact | A fixed nominal collocation using 엄연한 (undeniable/plain/manifest) as an intensifying modifier. Used to assert that something is unambiguously factual and cannot be disputed. Common in argumentative and rhetorical contexts. | [CORE] |
| 사실 (sasil) | 사실과 다르다 | sasil-gwa dareuda | to differ from the facts, to be contrary to the facts | A standard construction used in legal, journalistic, and everyday contexts to characterize a claim as factually incorrect without necessarily implying deliberate deception. Appears in formal corrections and rebuttals. | [CORE] |
| 사실 (sasil) | 사실을 은폐하다 | sasil-eul eunpye hada | to conceal the facts, to cover up the facts | Verb-object collocation describing deliberate suppression of factual information. Common in journalistic, legal, and political discourse; carries a stronger implication of institutional or deliberate concealment than 사실을 숨기다. | [CORE] |
| 사실 (sasil) | 사실관계를 파악하다 | sasil gwangye-reul paakada | to grasp the factual circumstances, to establish the facts of the case | Verb-object collocation used in legal and journalistic contexts to describe the act of determining the factual basis of a situation. 사실관계 is documented in P1b; this collocation with 파악하다 is its most common verbal partner. | [CORE] |

| 진짜 (jinjja) | 이게 진짜야? | ige jinjjaya? | Is this real? | 진짜 (jinjja) | 진짜 맞다 | jinjja matda | it's really true, that's genuinely correct | Colloquial emphatic collocation used to assert that something is genuinely the case. 진짜 functions as an adverbial intensifier modifying 맞다 (to be correct/right).
