# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2b COMPOSITIONAL CONSTRUCTIONS
# Language: Turkish (tr)

---

## METHODOLOGY NOTE

This analysis documents **attested multi-word constructions** involving P1 lexical items. Constructions are included only if they:
1. Are natural and commonly used in Turkish
2. Form recognizable units beyond free combination
3. Have specific pragmatic or semantic functions
4. Are not purely derivational (covered in P2a)

Absence of constructions is explicitly noted where expected patterns are not attested.

## BASE FORM 1: doğru

### Construction 1.1: doğru mu yanlış mı
**Form:** doğru mu yanlış mı
**Transliteration:** doğru mu yanlış mı
**Literal breakdown:** *doğru* (true) + question particle *mu* + *yanlış* (false) + question particle *mı*
**English translation:** true or false?, right or wrong?
**Usage:** Question format used to request binary evaluation or to express uncertainty about correctness. Common in educational contexts and when seeking confirmation.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### Construction 1.2: doğru yanlış sorusu
**Form:** doğru yanlış sorusu
**Transliteration:** doğru yanlış sorusu
**Literal breakdown:** *doğru* (true) + *yanlış* (false) + *soru* (question) + possessive *-su*
**English translation:** true-false question
**Usage:** Educational term for binary test questions requiring true/false answers. Standard in Turkish educational assessment.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### Construction 1.3: doğru kabul etmek
**Form:** doğru kabul etmek
**Transliteration:** doğru kabul etmek
**Literal breakdown:** *doğru* (true/correct) + *kabul* (acceptance) + *etmek* (to do)
**English translation:** to accept as true, to acknowledge as correct
**Usage:** Used for accepting or acknowledging the truth or correctness of something, often in formal or argumentative contexts. Example: "Bu iddiayı doğru kabul ediyorum" (I accept this claim as true).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### Construction 1.4: doğru yere koymak
**Form:** doğru yere koymak
**Transliteration:** doğru yere koymak
**Literal breakdown:** *doğru* (right/correct) + *yer* (place) + dative *-e* + *koymak* (to put)
**English translation:** to put in the right place, to use correctly, to apply appropriately
**Usage:** Idiomatic expression for using something appropriately or placing something where it belongs, both literally (physical placement) and figuratively (appropriate use of resources, words, etc.).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### Construction 1.5: doğru düzgün
**Form:** doğru düzgün
**Transliteration:** doğru düzgün
**Literal breakdown:** *doğru* (correct/proper) + *düzgün* (proper/orderly)
**English translation:** proper, decent, respectable, in good order
**Usage:** Fixed alliterative collocation emphasizing propriety and orderliness. Often used with negation to mean "not even proper/adequate." Example: "Doğru düzgün bir iş yok" (There's not even a proper job).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### Construction 1.6: doğru dürüst
**Form:** doğru dürüst
**Transliteration:** doğru dürüst
**Literal breakdown:** *doğru* (proper/correct) + *dürüst* (honest, from Persian)
**English translation:** proper, decent, respectable, honest
**Usage:** Fixed collocation used to evaluate something as proper, adequate, or respectable. Often used with negation. Example: "Doğru dürüst bir açıklama yapmadı" (He/she didn't give a proper explanation).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### Construction 1.7: doğrudan doğruya
**Form:** doğrudan doğruya
**Transliteration:** doğrudan doğruya
**Literal breakdown:** *doğru* (straight/direct) + ablative *-dan* + *doğru* (straight) + dative *-ya*
**English translation:** directly, straight away, immediately, frankly
**Usage:** Emphatic adverbial expression stressing complete directness or immediacy. More emphatic than simple *doğrudan*. Example: "Doğrudan doğruya söyledim" (I told him/her straight out).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### Construction 1.8: doğru ile yanlış arasında
**Form:** doğru ile yanlış arasında
**Transliteration:** doğru ile yanlış arasında
**Literal breakdown:** *doğru* (true/right) + *ile* (with) + *yanlış* (false/wrong) + *ara* (between) + locative *-sında*
**English translation:** between right and wrong, between true and false
**Usage:** Expression for moral or epistemic ambiguity, situations where the distinction between right/wrong or true/false is unclear.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### Construction 1.9: ne doğru ne yanlış
**Form:** ne doğru ne yanlış
**Transliteration:** ne doğru ne yanlış
**Literal breakdown:** *ne* (neither) + *doğru* (true/right) + *ne* (nor) + *yanlış* (false/wrong)
**English translation:** neither right nor wrong, neither true nor false
**Usage:** Expression for situations that cannot be clearly categorized as true/false or right/wrong. Used in philosophical or nuanced discussions.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## BASE FORM 3: gerçek

### Construction 3.1: gerçek mi hayal mi
**Form:** gerçek mi hayal mi
**Transliteration:** gerçek mi hayal mi
**Literal breakdown:** *gerçek* (real) + question *mi* + *hayal* (dream/fantasy) + question *mi*
**English translation:** real or fantasy?, is it real or a dream?
**Usage:** Rhetorical question expressing uncertainty about whether something is real or imaginary. Used in contexts of disbelief or wonder.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### Construction 3.2: gerçek gibi
**Form:** gerçek gibi
**Transliteration:** gerçek gibi
**Literal breakdown:** *gerçek* (real) + *gibi* (like, as if)
**English translation:** like real, as if real, realistic, lifelike
**Usage:** Expression for something that appears very realistic or lifelike. Example: "Gerçek gibi görünüyor" (It looks real / It looks like the real thing).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### Construction 3.3: gerçek sandım
**Form:** gerçek sandım
**Transliteration:** gerçek sandım
**Literal breakdown:** *gerçek* (real) + *san-* (to suppose) + past *-dım*
**English translation:** I thought it was real
**Usage:** Common expression for mistaking something unreal for reality. Often used in surprise or disappointment. Example: "Gerçek sandım, şaka çıktı" (I thought it was real, turned out to be a joke).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### Construction 3.4: gerçek mi gerçek
**Form:** gerçek mi gerçek
**Transliteration:** gerçek mi gerçek
**Literal breakdown:** *gerçek* (real) + question *mi* + *gerçek* (real)
**English translation:** is it really real?, for real?
**Usage:** Emphatic question expressing surprise or seeking confirmation of reality. Colloquial and emphatic.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### Construction 3.5: acı gerçek
**Form:** acı gerçek
**Transliteration:** acı gerçek
**Literal breakdown:** *acı* (bitter/painful) + *gerçek* (truth/reality)
**English translation:** harsh truth, bitter reality, painful truth
**Usage:** Fixed collocation for unpleasant or difficult truths. Example: "Acı gerçeklerle yüzleşmek zorundayız" (We must face harsh truths).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### Construction 3.6: çıplak gerçek
**Form:** çıplak gerçek
**Transliteration:** çıplak gerçek
**Literal breakdown:** *çıplak* (naked/bare) + *gerçek* (truth/reality)
**English translation:** naked truth, bare truth, unvarnished truth
**Usage:** Expression for truth without embellishment or concealment. Literary and formal register.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### Construction 3.7: gerçeğin ta kendisi
**Form:** gerçeğin ta kendisi
**Transliteration:** gerçeğin ta kendisi
**Literal breakdown:** *gerçek* (reality) + genitive *-in* + *ta* (emphatic particle) + *kendi* (self) + possessive *-si*
**English translation:** reality itself, the very truth, the real thing itself
**Usage:** Emphatic expression for something that is quintessentially real or true. Example: "Bu gerçeğin ta kendisi" (This is reality itself / the real thing).

### Construction 3.8: gerçek ortaya çıkmak
**Form:** gerçek ortaya çıkmak
**Transliteration:** gerçek ortaya çıkmak
**Literal breakdown:** *gerçek* (truth) + *ortaya* (into the open) + *çıkmak* (to come out)
**English translation:** the truth comes out, reality emerges, truth is revealed
**Usage:** Common expression for truth being revealed or discovered. Example: "Sonunda gerçek ortaya çıktı" (Finally the truth came out).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### Construction 3.9: gerçek ortaya çıkarmak
**Form:** gerçek ortaya çıkarmak
**Transliteration:** gerçek ortaya çıkarmak
**Literal breakdown:** *gerçek* (truth) + *ortaya* (into the open) + *çıkarmak* (to bring out, causative)
