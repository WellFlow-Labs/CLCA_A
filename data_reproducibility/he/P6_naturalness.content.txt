# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P6 NATURALNESS: Change, Causation, and Stability
# Language: Hebrew (he)

---

## 1. BECOMING TRUE / ACQUIRING TRUTH STATUS

### 1.1 Direct "Become True" Constructions

**Pattern:** X + nihyah/ne'esah + emet/nakhon
**Status:** [MARGINAL] — Unnatural in Hebrew

Hebrew lacks a natural direct construction for "X becomes true." The verb *nihyah* "became" or *ne'esah* "was made/became" can theoretically combine with truth-terms, but this is highly marked and uncommon.

**Attempted examples:**
- ❌ *ha-davar nihyah emet* — "the thing became true" — **UNNATURAL**
- ❌ *ha-te'anah ne'estah nkhonah* — "the claim became correct" — **UNNATURAL**

**Why unnatural:** Hebrew conceptualizes truth as a property that is discovered or revealed, not as something that comes into being. Truth is seen as pre-existing and becoming known, rather than becoming true.

---

### 1.2 Natural Alternatives: Discovery/Revelation

**Pattern:** ha-emet + hitgaltah/hitbarah/nimtse'ah
**Status:** [CORE] — Standard way to express truth emerging

Hebrew uses verbs of revelation, discovery, or clarification to express what English might render as "became true" or "turned out to be true."

**Examples:**

**האמת התגלתה**
*ha-emet hitgaltah*
"the truth was revealed / came to light"
[CORE] — Very common in narrative and investigative contexts

**התברר שהוא צודק**
*hitbarar she-hu tsodek*
"it became clear that he was right / it turned out that he was right"
[CORE] — Extremely frequent for expressing that something proved to be correct

**נמצא שזה נכון**
*nimtsa she-ze nakhon*
"it was found that this is correct / it turned out to be correct"
[CORE] — Common in formal and investigative contexts

**התברר שהסיפור אמיתי**
*hitbarar she-ha-sipur amiti*
"it became clear that the story is true / the story turned out to be true"
[CORE] — Standard expression for truth being established

**הדבר התברר**
*ha-davar hitbarar*
"the matter became clear / was clarified"
[CORE] — Common for matters being resolved or clarified

---

### 1.3 Verification/Confirmation Constructions

**Pattern:** X + ishtarer/itbarar/hukhakh
**Status:** [CORE] — Standard for truth being confirmed

**Examples:**

**הדבר אושר**
*ha-davar ushar*
"the thing was confirmed / verified"
[CORE] — Formal/official confirmation

**העובדה אוששה**
*ha-uvdah ushshah*

[CORE] — Formal verification of facts

**הוכח שזה אמת**
*hukhakh she-ze emet*
"it was proved that this is true"
[CORE] — Standard for proof being established

## 3. CAUSING TRUTH / MAKING TRUE

### 3.1 Direct Causative: "Make True"

**Pattern:** Y + asah/sam + X + emet/nakhon
**Status:** [MARGINAL] — Unnatural for most truth-terms

Direct causative constructions with "make" are generally unnatural with truth-terms, because truth is not seen as something that can be made or caused.

**Attempted examples:**
- ❌ *hu asah et ze emet* — "he made it true" — **UNNATURAL**
- ❌ *hi samah et ze nakhon* — "she made it correct" — **UNNATURAL**

**Exception:** Correction/fixing can use causative:

**הוא תיקן את הטעות**
*hu tiken et ha-ta'ut*
"he corrected the mistake / fixed the error"
[CORE] — Standard for correction

**היא תיקנה את התשובה**
*hi tiknah et ha-tshuvah*
"she corrected the answer"
[CORE] — Common in educational contexts

---

### 3.2 Natural Alternatives: Proving/Demonstrating

**Pattern:** Y + hokhiakh/her'ah + she-X
**Status:** [CORE] — Standard way to express causing truth to be known

**Examples:**

**הוא הוכיח שזה אמת**
*hu hokhiakh she-ze emet*
"he proved that this is true"
[CORE] — Standard for establishing truth through proof

**היא הראתה שהוא צודק**
*hi her'atah she-hu tsodek*
"she showed that he is right"
[CORE] — Common for demonstrating correctness

**הם הוכיחו את נכונות הטענה**
*hem hokhikhu et nekhonut ha-ta'anah*
"they proved the correctness of the claim"
[CORE] — Formal proof construction

**הניסוי אישר את התיאוריה**
*ha-nisuy ishar et ha-te'oryah*
"the experiment confirmed the theory"
[CORE] — Scientific/formal confirmation

---

### 3.3 Revelation/Disclosure

**Pattern:** Y + gilah/hevi le-or + et ha-emet

**Examples:**

## 10. ASPECTUAL DISTINCTIONS

### 10.1 Gradual vs. Sudden Change

**Gradual revelation:**

**האמת התגלתה בהדרגה**
*ha-emet hitgaltah be-hadraga*
"the truth was revealed gradually"
[CORE] — Gradual emergence

**לאט לאט התברר שהוא צודק**
*le'at le'at hitbarar she-hu tsodek*
"slowly it became clear that he is right"
[CORE] — Gradual clarification

**Sudden revelation:**

**האמת התגלתה בבת אחת**
*ha-emet hitgaltah be-bat akhat*
"the truth was revealed all at once / suddenly"
[CORE] — Sudden revelation

**פתאום התברר שזה לא נכון**
*pit'om hitbarar she-ze lo nakhon*
"suddenly it became clear that this is not correct"
[CORE] — Sudden realization

---

### 10.2 Completed vs. Ongoing Process

**Completed:**

**האמת התגלתה**
*ha-emet hitgaltah*
"the truth was revealed" (perfective)
[CORE] — Completed revelation

**Ongoing:**

**האמת מתגלה**
*ha-emet mitgalah*

[CORE] — Ongoing revelation

**האמת ממשיכה להתגלות**

"the truth continues to be revealed"
[CORE] — Continuing process

## 11. SUMMARY OF KEY PATTERNS

### 11.1 What Hebrew DOES NOT Naturally Express:

1. **Direct "become true/false"** — Hebrew lacks natural constructions for truth-values coming into being
2. **Direct "make true/false"** — Causative constructions with truth-terms are generally unnatural
3. **Truth as mutable property** — Hebrew conceptualizes truth as discovered, not created or changed

### 11.2 What Hebrew DOES Naturally Express:

1. **Discovery/Revelation** — *hitgalah*, *hitbarar*, *nimtsa* for truth emerging
2. **Proof/Demonstration** — *hokhiakh*, *her'ah* for establishing truth
3. **Refutation/Disproof** — *hifrikh*, *satar* for disproving claims
4. **Misleading/Error** — *hit'ah*, *garam le-ta'ut* for causing error
5. **Doubt manipulation** — *hetil safek*, *hesir safek* for raising/removing doubt
6. **Persistence** — *nish'ar*, *amad be-* for truth enduring

### 11.3 Conceptual Framework:

Hebrew treats truth as:
- **Pre-existing** (discovered, not created)
- **Revealed** (brought to light, not made true)
- **Stable** (enduring, not changing)
- **Knowable** (proven, demonstrated, clarified)

This contrasts with a framework where truth-values could be directly caused or changed. The language encodes a realist epistemology where truth exists independently and human actions relate to it through discovery, demonstration, or concealment—not through creation or alteration.
