# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P1b ROOT EXPANSION
# Language: Basque (eu)

---

## Step 1 — Identify Central Roots

---

### Root 1: **egia** (*egia*)

**Participation in P1 items:** 7 items — *egia* (1), *egiazkoa* (2), *egiazkotasuna* (11), *egiaztatzea* (25), *egiaztatu* (26), and indirectly through the *egia-* stem in the derivational family.

**Core meaning contribution:** The primary nominal root for "truth" in Basque. It functions as the base for adjectival, abstract nominal, and verbal derivations across the truth-evaluation domain. It is the most morphologically productive root in the P1 set.

---

### Root 2: **balio** (*balio*)

**Participation in P1 items:** 3 items — *balioa* (12), *baliozkoa* (13), *baliogabea* (14).

**Core meaning contribution:** Encodes value, worth, and validity. It anchors the sub-domain of formal and legal validity within the broader truth-evaluation field, and participates in productive compounding with privative and adjectival suffixes.

---

### Root 3: **zintzo** (*zintzo*)

**Participation in P1 items:** 2 items — *zintzoa* (22), *zintzotasuna* (23).

**Note:** *zintzo* participates in fewer P1 items than *egia* or *balio*, but it is the most productive root for personal moral integrity and honesty as character traits, a domain that is underrepresented by the other two roots. It is included here as the primary root for the moral-character sub-domain.

### ROOT 1: egia

---

**1.**
- **Form:** egia esan
- **Transliteration:** egia esan
- **Literal breakdown:** egia (truth) + esan (to say)
- **Natural English translation:** to tell the truth
- **Context of use:** The standard colloquial and formal expression for telling the truth. Used across all registers, from everyday conversation to courtroom testimony.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**2.**
- **Form:** egia da
- **Transliteration:** egia da
- **Literal breakdown:** egia (truth) + da (it is, 3sg present of *izan*)
- **Natural English translation:** it is true; that is true
- **Context of use:** The most basic predicative assertion of truth in Basque. Used in everyday speech, argumentation, and written discourse to affirm the truth of a proposition.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**3.**
- **Form:** egia esan nahi bada
- **Transliteration:** egia esan nahi bada
- **Literal breakdown:** egia (truth) + esan (say) + nahi (want) + bada (if it is)
- **Natural English translation:** to tell the truth; if truth be told; frankly speaking
- **Context of use:** A fixed hedging or framing phrase used to introduce a candid or frank statement, often signaling that what follows may be uncomfortable or surprising. Common in spoken and written registers.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**4.**
- **Form:** egiaz
- **Transliteration:** egiaz
- **Literal breakdown:** egia (truth) + -z (instrumental/adverbial suffix)
- **Natural English translation:** truly, in truth, really
- **Context of use:** An adverbial form used to assert that something is genuinely or truly the case. Used in both spoken and written registers as an intensifying or affirming device.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**5.**

- **Transliteration:** egitan

- **Natural English translation:** truly, in reality, in fact, actually

- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]
