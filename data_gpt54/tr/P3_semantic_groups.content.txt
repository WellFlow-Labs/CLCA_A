Below are usage-based groupings that emerge from the Turkish items in P1 and P2. I keep them loose and overlapping, since several expressions are used across more than one practical pattern.

## 1. **Factual truth / saying what is actually the case**

These expressions are used when speakers talk about whether a statement, account, or report matches reality. In practice they appear in everyday honesty talk, journalism, argument, and testimony, and they often contrast with concealment, distortion, or fabrication.

**Representative expressions**
- **doğru** — doğru  
- **gerçek** — gerçek  
- **hakikat** — hakikat  
- **doğruluk** — doğruluk  
- **gerçeği söylemek** — gerçeği söylemek  
- **doğruyu söylemek** — doğruyu söylemek  
- **hakikati söylemek** — hakikati söylemek  
- **gerçeği yansıtmak** — gerçeği yansıtmak  
- **gerçeği çarpıtmak** — gerçeği çarpıtmak  
- **hakikati gizlemek** — hakikati gizlemek  
- **hakikatten uzak olmak** — hakikatten uzak olmak  
- **acı gerçek** — acı gerçek  

**Ambiguous / overlapping items**
- **dürüst / dürüstlük** — often overlaps with moral honesty, not only factual truth
- **gerçekten / hakikaten** — often work as discourse emphasis, not only truth marking
- **doğru söz** — can also praise aptness or candor

## 12. **Honesty, frankness, and truthful conduct**

These expressions are used mainly for people, answers, and ways of speaking rather than for propositions alone. In usage they combine truthfulness with character evaluation and often imply openness, fairness, or lack of deception.

**Representative expressions**
- **dürüst** — dürüst  
- **dürüstlük** — dürüstlük  
- **dürüstçe** — dürüstçe  
- **dürüst olmak gerekirse** — dürüst olmak gerekirse  
- **dürüst davranmak** — dürüst davranmak  
- **dürüst cevap vermek** — dürüst cevap vermek  
- **dürüst bir insan** — dürüst bir insan  
- **doğru dürüst** — doğru dürüst  

**Ambiguous / overlapping items**
- **doğruluk** — can refer either to factual accuracy or truthfulness of character/speech
- **gerçeği söylemek / doğruyu söylemek** — overlap strongly with honesty
- **doğru dürüst** — often means “properly / adequately,” so it does not fit cleanly with honesty alone

## Items that do not fit cleanly into one practical grouping

Some expressions are clearly attested but sit at the edge of the main usage patterns above, or shift away from truth-related evaluation in ordinary use:

- **doğruca** — mostly directional/directness
- **doğrudan / doğrudan doğruya** — mostly immediacy or lack of mediation
- **doğru orantı** — mathematical/technical
- **doğru akım** — electrical term
- **gerçek sayı** — mathematical term
- **gerçek zamanlı / gerçek zamanlı sistem** — technical computing/engineering
- **gerçeküstü / gerçeküstücü / gerçeküstücülük** — art/literary movement and surreal description
- **özdeş / özdeşlik** — identity/sameness rather than ordinary truth evaluation
- **öznel / öznellik / öznelcilik** — perspective/subjectivity vocabulary, only indirectly related
- **hakikatten uzak** fits well in truth-evaluation, but **hakikat ışığı**, **ehl-i hakikat**, **Hak yolunda**, **Hak** are more specifically literary/religious
- **hak etmek**, **hakkını vermek** — common and related to right/due, but not central to truth/falsity usage
