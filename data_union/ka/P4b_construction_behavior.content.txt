## Summary Notes

- **Naturalness ratings:** All constructions rated [CORE] except: არაგულწრფელი ბოდიში [MARGINAL]; სიყალბეზე წასვლა [MARGINAL]; -oba suffix nominalizations (namdviloba, gulc̣rpelobaˈ, avtentiuroba) [MARGINAL]; მართლმადიდებელი [MARGINAL]; მიმბაძველი [MARGINAL]; სიმართლის მაძიებელი [MARGINAL]; მოჩვენებითი ხასიათი აქვს [MARGINAL]; ფიქტიურად ირიცხება [MARGINAL]; productive causative pattern applied to truth-domain adjectives as a class [UNCERTAIN].

- **Register differentiation** is most clearly observable in Construction 6 (abstract nominalization), where si-...-e/le nominals pattern with colloquial use and -eba/-oba nominals pattern with formal and legal use.

- **Construction 11 (genitive modification)** is identified in P4a as the most productive multi-word pattern in this domain and generates the majority of legal and philosophical collocations.

- **The u- vs. ara- distinction** is documented as categorical, not stylistic: u- yields lexicalized forms with specific and not fully predictable meanings; ara- yields compositional negations. The two prefixes are not freely interchangeable.

- **The enclitic -a copula is categorically excluded under negation**; the full copula aris is obligatory in negative predicative constructions. This is a morphosyntactic restriction, not a stylistic choice.

- **No dedicated agentive nominal for "truth-teller"** is a common lexicalized form; the concept is expressed through martali used nominally or through verbal constructions (simartle ambobs).

- **The concessive formula მართალია, მაგრამ...** is specific to martali and does not extend to č̣ešmariteba or simartle.

- **The discourse-framing constructions** (სიმართლე რომ ვთქვათ; სიმართლე ისაა, რომ...) are specific to simartle; no structural parallel in the falseness domain is attested, and no parallel with č̣ešmariteba or other truth-domain nouns is documented.

- **No constructions have been added beyond those identified in P4a.** All examples are preserved exactly as given in the probes.
