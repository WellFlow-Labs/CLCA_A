# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2a Derivational System
# Language: Chinese (zh)

---

## Preliminary Note on Derivational Morphology in Chinese

Chinese derivational morphology differs substantially from that of Indo-European languages. The primary productive derivational processes attested in the P1 vocabulary are:

- **Suffixation with 性 (-xìng):** nominalizes adjectives/stative verbs to abstract property nouns ("the property of being X")
- **Suffixation with 化 (-huà):** forms verbs meaning "to become X" or "to make X" (comparable to English -ize/-ify)
- **Suffixation with 者 (-zhě):** forms agentive nouns ("one who does/is X")
- **Suffixation with 主义 (-zhǔyì):** forms ideological or doctrinal nouns ("the doctrine/ism of X")

Compounding (the dominant word-formation process in Chinese) is treated in P1b and is not repeated here. Only suffixal and other clearly derivational processes are documented below.

Gaps are preserved. Where no derivation is attested, this is stated explicitly.

### Base: 真实 (zhēnshí)

---

**1.**
**Base Form:** 真实 (zhēnshí)
**Derived Form:** 真实性
**Transliteration:** zhēnshíxìng
**Literal Breakdown:** true + solid/actual + -ness (nominalizing suffix 性)
**Natural English Translation:** truthfulness, authenticity, factual accuracy, veracity
**Usage:** The nominalized abstract property form of 真实. Used in formal, legal, journalistic, and academic contexts to refer to the property of being truthful or factually accurate ("核实其真实性" — to verify its authenticity/accuracy; "新闻的真实性" — the truthfulness of news reporting). Highly productive and extremely common.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**2.**
**Base Form:** 真实 (zhēnshí)
**Derived Form:** 真实化
**Transliteration:** zhēnshíhuà
**Literal Breakdown:** true + solid/actual + -ize (verbalizing suffix 化)
**Natural English Translation:** to make realistic, to render authentic, to bring closer to reality
**Usage:** Used in aesthetic and media contexts to describe the process of making a representation more realistic or authentic ("角色真实化" — making characters more realistic; "场景真实化" — rendering scenes more authentic). Less common than 真实性 and more restricted in register.
**Epistemic Status:** [MARGINAL]

## Summary Note on Productivity

The suffix **性 (-xìng)** is the most productive derivational process attested across this vocabulary set, yielding nominalizations from stative verbs and adjectives. It applies most freely to disyllabic bases (真实性, 准确性, 可靠性, 合法性, 违法性, 实用性) and is well attested for bases in the correctness, reliability, and legality domains.

The suffix **化 (-huà)** is attested for bases that describe states amenable to change or process (合法化, 道德化), but is not productive across the full set.

The suffix **者 (-zhě)** is attested only where the base describes an action with a clear human agent (伪造者, 欺骗者), and even there, competing colloquial forms (骗子) may be preferred in informal registers.

The suffix **主义 (-zhǔyì)** is attested only for 实用主义, where it translates an established philosophical doctrine. It does not apply freely across the set.
