# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2a Derivational System
# Language: Chinese (zh)

---

## Preliminary Note on Scope

Chinese morphology differs structurally from Indo-European languages in ways that bear directly on this task. The primary word-formation mechanism in Modern Chinese is **compounding** — the combination of two or more morphemes into a new lexical item — rather than affixation in the classical sense. Derivational affixes in the strict sense (bound morphemes that attach to a base to produce a new word of a different category or meaning) are limited in number and productivity. The most relevant for this corpus are:

- **-性 (-xìng)**: nominalizing suffix, roughly "the property/quality of X"
- **-化 (-huà)**: verbalizing/processual suffix, roughly "to become X" or "to make X"
- **-者 (-zhě)**: agentive suffix, roughly "one who X-es"
- **-性 and -化** are the most productive with the roots in this corpus

Reduplication, negation with **不- (bù-)** and **非- (fēi-)**, and the prefix **伪- (wěi-)** functioning as "pseudo-" are also documented where attested.

Forms already fully documented in P1 and P1b are cross-referenced but not re-described in full.

## SECTION 1 — Derivations from 真实 (zhēnshí)

---

**D-01**
**Base Form:** 真实 (zhēnshí)
**Derived Form:** 真实性
**Transliteration:** zhēnshíxìng
**Literal Breakdown:** true + solid/real + property/nature (-性 nominalizing suffix)
**Natural English Translation:** truthfulness, authenticity, veracity, factual accuracy
**Usage:** Abstract noun formed by attaching the nominalizing suffix -性 to 真实. Used in legal, journalistic, and academic contexts to refer to the property of being truthful or authentic as an assessable quality (e.g., 核实信息的真实性 "verify the truthfulness of the information," 证据的真实性 "the authenticity of the evidence"). This is one of the most productive and well-attested -性 nominalizations in the truth domain.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**D-02**
**Base Form:** 真实 (zhēnshí)
**Derived Form:** 不真实
**Transliteration:** bù zhēnshí
**Literal Breakdown:** not + true + solid/real
**Natural English Translation:** untrue, inauthentic, not factual
**Usage:** Negation formed with the standard negator 不. Used to evaluate accounts, representations, or depictions as failing to correspond to reality (e.g., 报道内容不真实 "the content of the report is not factual"). Less formal than 虚假 and more directly compositional.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

## Summary Note on Productivity

The suffix **-性** is the most productive derivational element in this corpus, attaching to evaluative adjectives and compound adjectives to produce abstract nouns denoting assessable properties. It is attested with: 真实, 虚假, 正确, 准确, 客观, 可靠, 可信, 公正, 正当, 虚伪, 真理, 实证. The suffix **-化** is attested with 客观 and 合法, both of which describe processes of transformation into a state. The suffix **-者** is attested with 伪造 and (marginally) 假冒, both of which describe agents of deliberate deception. The suffix **-度** is attested with 可信 and 失真, both of which involve scalar measurement. The suffix **-主义** is attested with 实证 and 写实, both of which name doctrinal positions. The negators **不-** and **非-** are productive with most evaluative adjectives in this corpus, though 非- is more restricted and formal than 不-.

No evidence was found for productive use of reduplication, diminutive suffixes, or other derivational processes with the items in this corpus.
