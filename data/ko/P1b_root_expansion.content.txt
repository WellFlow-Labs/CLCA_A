# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P1b ROOT EXPANSION
# Language: Korean (ko)

## Step 1 — Identify Central Roots

### Root 1: 진 (jin) 眞
**Native script + transliteration:** 진 (jin)
**P1 participation count:** 11 items
**Core meaning contribution:** "true, genuine, authentic, real" — denotes correspondence to reality, authenticity, or genuineness. Sino-Korean character 眞 used in formal and philosophical contexts.

### Root 2: 거짓 (geojit)
**Native script + transliteration:** 거짓 (geojit)
**P1 participation count:** 5 items
**Core meaning contribution:** "false, fake, deceptive" — denotes lack of correspondence to reality or intentional deception. Native Korean root used across all registers.

### Root 3: 실 (sil) 實
**Native script + transliteration:** 실 (sil)
**P1 participation count:** 5 items
**Core meaning contribution:** "reality, actuality, substance" — denotes concrete reality, actual existence, or substantive nature. Sino-Korean character 實 emphasizing material reality.
