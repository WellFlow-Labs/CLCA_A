# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P4a_CONSTRUCTIONS
# Language: Korean (ko)

---

## 1. ATTRIBUTION

### Pattern
**X-이다 / X-하다** — Predicative constructions asserting truth-domain properties

Korean uses two main predicative patterns:
- Copula **이다** with nouns: NP + 이다/아니다
- Adjectival verb **하다** with Sino-Korean stems: STEM + 하다

### Examples
- 이것은 **진실이다** (igeoseun jinsilida) — "This is the truth"
- 그 사람은 **정직하다** (geu sarameun jeongjikada) — "That person is honest"

### Notes
Sino-Korean nouns require copula 이다 for predication (진실이다, 사실이다), while Sino-Korean adjective stems take 하다 suffix (정직하다, 확실하다). Native Korean adjectives like 참 appear in limited predicative uses.

---

## 2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION

### Pattern
**X-이/가 아니다** (copula negation) or **안/못 X-하다** (verbal negation)

Two negation strategies:
- Copula negation: NP + 이/가 아니다
- Verbal negation: 안/못 + VERB-하다 or VERB-하지 않다

### Examples
- 이것은 **거짓이 아니다** (igeoseun geojisi anida) — "This is not false"
- 그는 **정직하지 않다** (geuneun jeongjikaji anta) — "He is not honest"

### Notes
Copula negation (아니다) used with nominal predicates; verbal negation (안/지 않다) used with adjectival verbs. Double negation (거짓이 아니다 "not false") commonly used to assert truth indirectly.

---

## 3. ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION

### Pattern
**X-(으)ㄴ/는 N** — Adnominal forms modifying nouns

Korean uses adnominal endings to create modifying constructions:
- Past/perfective: -(으)ㄴ
- Present/imperfective: -는
- Sino-Korean stems: STEM + 한/된

### Examples
- **진실한 마음** (jinsilhan maeum) — "truthful heart, sincere heart"
- **거짓된 말** (geojitdoen mal) — "false words, lying speech"

### Notes
Sino-Korean adjective stems take -한 (진실한, 정직한); native Korean adjectives use -(으)ㄴ. The form -된 (from 되다 "become") creates stative modifiers (거짓된 "falsified, false").

---

## 4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE

### Pattern
**X-이/가 되다** — "become X" construction using auxiliary 되다

Standard inchoative uses copula-like auxiliary 되다 "become":
- NP + 이/가 되다

### Examples
- 거짓말이 **진실이 되다** (geojinmari jinsiri doeda) — "A lie becomes truth"
- **확실해지다** (hwaksilhaejida) — "to become certain"

### Notes
Nominal predicates use 되다 (진실이 되다); adjectival verbs use -아/어지다 suffix (확실하다 → 확실해지다). Common in proverbial expressions about transformation of truth/falsehood.

---

## 5. CAUSATIVE

### Pattern
**X-하게 하다** (analytic causative) or **-시키다** (lexical causative)

Korean forms causatives analytically or with dedicated causative suffixes:
- Analytic: VERB-게 하다 "make/let X happen"
- Lexical: STEM-시키다 "cause to X"

### Examples
- 진실을 **믿게 하다** (jinsireul mitge hada) — "to make (someone) believe the truth"
- **진정시키다** (jinjeongsikida) — "to calm down, to pacify"

### Notes
Truth-domain terms rarely form productive causatives. 믿게 하다 "make believe" is common; 진정시키다 "calm/pacify" uses 시키다 but with specialized meaning. No common causatives like *"진실하게 하다" (make truthful).

---

## 6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION

### Pattern
**STEM-성** — Abstract quality nominalization with suffix -성 (性)

Highly productive pattern creating abstract nouns denoting qualities:
- Sino-Korean STEM + 성

### Examples
- 진실 → **진실성** (jinsilseong) — "truthfulness, veracity"
- 신뢰 → **신뢰성** (sinroeseong) — "reliability, trustworthiness"

### Notes
Extremely productive with Sino-Korean stems in truth domain (정직성, 확실성, 타당성, 진정성). Creates abstract quality nouns systematically. Not applicable to native Korean roots (no *거짓성).

---

## 7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION

### Pattern
**X-쟁이** — Agentive/habitual person marker (limited productivity)

Limited agentive nominalization using native Korean suffix -쟁이:
- Action noun + 쟁이 (person characterized by habitual action)

### Examples
- 거짓말 → **거짓말쟁이** (geojinmaljaengi) — "liar, habitual liar"
- (no common expression for "truth-teller")

### Notes
Very limited productivity in truth domain. 거짓말쟁이 "liar" is common and productive, but no corresponding positive agentive exists (*진실쟁이 not attested). Asymmetry reflects focus on marking deviant behavior rather than expected truthfulness.

---

## 8. VERBAL COLLOCATIONS

### Pattern
**Truth-domain noun + action verb** — Light verb constructions

Korean extensively uses noun + verb collocations for truth-domain expressions:
- Object + 말하다 "speak"
- Object + 밝히다 "reveal"
- Object + 숨기다 "hide"

### Examples
- **진실을 말하다** (jinsireul malhada) — "to tell the truth"
- **사실을 밝히다** (sasireul balkhida) — "to reveal the facts"

### Notes
Highly productive pattern. Core verbs include 말하다 (speak), 밝히다 (reveal), 숨기다 (hide), 알다 (know), 찾다 (seek), 확인하다 (verify). Different nouns show preferences for different verbs (진위 strongly prefers 가리다 "distinguish").

---

## 9. ADVERBIAL EXPRESSIONS

### Pattern
**X-(으)로** — Instrumental/manner adverbial with postposition -(으)로

Productive adverbialization using instrumental/manner postposition:
- Noun + (으)로

### Examples
- **진실로** (jinsilro) — "truly, truthfully"
- **거짓으로** (geojiseuro) — "falsely, by deception"

### Notes
Productive with both Sino-Korean (진실로, 실제로) and native Korean (거짓으로, 진짜로) roots. Creates manner adverbs indicating how action is performed. Very common in everyday speech.

---

## 10. CONTRASTIVE CONSTRUCTIONS

### Pattern
**X-와/과 Y** or **X-인지 Y-인지** — Explicit truth/falsehood contrasts

Korean frequently uses explicit contrastive constructions pairing truth and falsehood terms:
- Coordination: X와/과 Y "X and Y"
- Disjunction: X인지 Y인지 "whether X or Y"

### Examples
- **진실과 거짓** (jinsilgwa geojit) — "truth and falsehood"
- **진짜인지 가짜인지** (jinjjainji gajjainji) — "whether real or fake"

### Notes
Highly conventionalized pairings: 진실과 거짓, 참과 거짓, 진짜와 가짜. Used in philosophical discourse, investigations, and everyday evaluation. Reflects cultural emphasis on distinguishing truth from falsehood.

---

## 11. DISCOURSE MARKERS

### Pattern
**Truth-domain term + topic/contrast marker** — Discourse-organizing functions

Several truth-domain terms grammaticalized as discourse markers:
- 사실은 (topic marker)
- 솔직히 말하면 (conditional frame)

### Examples
- **사실은** (sasireun) — "in fact, actually, to tell the truth"
- **솔직히 말하면** (soljikhi malhamyeon) — "frankly speaking, to be honest"

### Notes
사실은 extremely common as discourse marker introducing factual information or corrections. 솔직히 (말하면) introduces candid statements. These function beyond truth-evaluation as discourse organizers.

---

## 12. INTENSIFICATION CONSTRUCTIONS

### Pattern
**Reduplication or emphatic particles** — Emphasis and intensification

Korean uses reduplication and emphatic particles for intensification:
- Reduplication: X X
- Emphatic suffixes: -배기, -투성이

### Examples
- **진짜 진짜** (jinjja jinjja) — "really really, for real for real"
- **거짓투성이** (geojittuseongi) — "full of lies, riddled with falsehood"

### Notes
Reduplication (진짜 진짜) common in colloquial speech for emphasis. Suffix -투성이 "full of/covered with" creates saturation expressions (거짓투성이). Suffix -배기 creates emphatic forms (진짜배기 "the real deal").

---

## 13. EVIDENTIAL/EPISTEMIC CONSTRUCTIONS

### Pattern
**X-ㄹ 틀림없다** or **분명히 X** — Certainty and evidentiality marking

Korean marks epistemic stance through modal constructions:
- Modality: -(으)ㄹ 틀림없다 "must be, certainly"
- Adverbial: 분명히/확실히 "clearly/certainly"

### Examples
- **진실일 틀림없다** (jinsiril teullimeopda) — "must be true, certainly true"
- **확실히 알다** (hwaksilhi alda) — "to know for certain"

### Notes
틀림없다 (literally "without mistake") expresses strong epistemic certainty. Adverbs 확실히, 분명히 modify verbs to indicate speaker's confidence level. Common in assertions and predictions.

---

## 14. RESULTATIVE CONSTRUCTIONS

### Pattern
**X-이/가 V-어지다** — Passive/resultative with -어지다

Korean forms resultatives using -어지다 suffix or passive constructions:
- Intransitive resultative: V-어지다
- Passive: V-어지다

### Examples
- **진실이 밝혀지다** (jinsiri balkhyeojida) — "the truth is revealed"
- **진실이 드러나다** (jinsiri deureonada) — "the truth emerges, comes to light"

### Notes
Common in investigative and narrative contexts describing truth being revealed. 밝혀지다 (passive of 밝히다 "reveal") and 드러나다 (intransitive "emerge") both express truth coming to light. Reflects cultural narrative of hidden truth being uncovered.

---

## 15. COMPARATIVE CONSTRUCTIONS

### Pattern
**X-보다 더 Y** or **X-만큼 Y** — Degree comparisons

(no common expression identified)

### Notes
Truth-domain terms rarely appear in degree comparisons in Korean. Truth is typically treated as categorical rather than gradable. Expressions like *"더 진실하다" (more truthful) or *"가장 정확하다" (most accurate) are grammatical but uncommon. Precision terms (정확하다) show some gradability (더 정확하다 "more accurate"), but core truth terms resist comparison.

---

## 16. CONDITIONAL CONSTRUCTIONS

### Pattern
**X-(이)라면** — Conditional "if X"

Conditional constructions using -(이)라면 "if it is X":
- Noun + (이)라면

### Examples
- **진실이라면** (jinsiramyeon) — "if it is true, if it is the truth"
- **거짓이라면** (geojitramyeon) — "if it is false, if it is a lie"

### Notes
Common in hypothetical reasoning and conditional arguments. Used when reasoning from assumption of truth or falsehood. Frequently appears in investigative and analytical discourse.

---

## 17. PERFORMATIVE CONSTRUCTIONS

### Pattern
**X-을/를 V-ㄹ 것을 맹세하다** — Oath/promise formulas

Formal performative constructions for oaths and promises:
- Object + future marker + 맹세하다 "swear"

### Examples
- **진실을 말할 것을 맹세합니다** (jinsireul malhal geoseul maengsehamnida) — "I swear to tell the truth"
- **진실만을 말하겠다고 약속하다** (jinsilmaneul malhagessdago yaksokhada) — "to promise to tell only the truth"

### Notes
Highly formulaic legal and ceremonial language. 맹세하다 "swear/vow" used in court testimony. 약속하다 "promise" used in less formal pledges. Reflects institutional contexts requiring explicit truth commitments.

---

## 18. QUOTATIVE/REPORTATIVE CONSTRUCTIONS

### Pattern
**X-이다** (assertive) or **X-라고 하다** (quotative)

Korean uses copula for assertions and quotative constructions for reports:
- Direct assertion: X이다
- Quotative: X라고 하다 "say that X"

### Examples
- **사실이다** (sasirida) — "it is a fact, it is true"
- **진실이라고 하다** (jinsilrago hada) — "to say that it is true"

### Notes
Simple copula 이다 makes direct assertions (사실이다, 진실이다). Quotative -라고 하다 reports others' claims. Common in contexts of verification and testimony where distinguishing direct knowledge from reported information matters.

---

## 19. NEGATION WITH PRIVATIVE EXPRESSIONS

### Pattern
**X-없이** — "without X" privative construction

Privative adverbial using 없이 "without":
- Noun + 없이

### Examples
- **거짓없이** (geojiseobsi) — "truthfully, honestly, without deception"
- (진실없이 not commonly used)

### Notes
거짓없이 "without falsehood" common for emphasizing truthfulness. Asymmetric: positive term 진실없이 "without truth" rare, while negative term 거짓없이 productive. Reflects marking of absence of deception rather than presence of truth.

---

## 20. COMPOUND VERB CONSTRUCTIONS

### Pattern
**V1-아/어 V2** — Compound verb sequences

Korean uses compound verbs where V2 modifies or extends V1:
- V1-아/어 + V2 (resultative, directional, aspectual)

### Examples
- **속아 넘어가다** (soga neomeogada) — "to be deceived, to fall for deception"
- **들키다** (deulkida) — "to be caught (in a lie)"

### Notes
속아 넘어가다 combines 속다 "be deceived" with 넘어가다 "fall over/for" creating idiomatic expression for being successfully deceived. 들키다 (from 들다 "be heard/caught") specifically used for lies being discovered. These are lexicalized compounds specific to deception contexts.
