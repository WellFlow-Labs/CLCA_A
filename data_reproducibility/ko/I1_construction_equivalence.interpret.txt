## 2. Substitution Patterns (Narrative)

### REVELATION substitutes for BECOME
Korean shows a clear preference for REVELATION constructions over BECOME constructions in the truth domain. While 되다 "become" is grammatically possible (진실이 되다 "to become truth"), P4b marks this as **[MARGINAL]** and notes that "specialized verbs (드러나다, 밝혀지다) for truth emergence" are preferred. The construction 거짓말이 들통나다 "a lie becomes exposed" is marked **[CORE]**, indicating that Korean conceptualizes truth-value changes as processes of revelation/discovery rather than simple state transitions.

### VERIFICATION encompasses DISCONFIRMATION
Korean does not distinguish VERIFICATION from DISCONFIRMATION constructionally. Both use the same pattern: TRUTH-TERM + 을/를 + VERIFICATION-VERB. The same verbs (확인하다 "verify/confirm," 가리다 "distinguish/determine") apply regardless of whether the outcome confirms or disconfirms. The distinction is semantic (in the result) rather than constructional.

### CAUSATION requires periphrasis
Korean lacks productive morphological causatives for truth-domain predicates. P4a explicitly states: "Korean lacks productive morphological causatives for truth-domain predicates. Causation expressed periphrastically." P4b marks this as **[UNCERTAIN]** and notes it is a "Gap in paradigm compared to other semantic domains." 

For factual causation, Korean uses transitive revelation verbs: 진실을 밝히다 (jinsireul balkhida) "to reveal/bring to light the truth" (literally "to brighten the truth"). This functions as a light verb construction where 밝히다 carries the causative semantics.

For character causation, Korean uses the auxiliary construction -게 하다: 거짓말을 하게 하다 "to make someone lie."

### NEGATION is analytic
Korean employs analytic negation strategies:
- For nominal predicates: 아니다 "not be" (이것은 사실이 아니다)
- For adjectival predicates: -지 않다 pattern (그 말은 정확하지 않다)
- Alternative lexical negation: 틀리다 "to be wrong," 거짓이다 "to be false"

No morphological negative affixes attach directly to truth-domain terms.

### Asymmetries in agentive nominalization
Korean shows a striking asymmetry in agentive nominalization. The negative agent term 거짓말쟁이 "liar" is **[CORE]** and uses the productive suffix -쟁이. However, P4a notes: "No parallel *진실쟁이 or *사실쟁이. 'Truth-teller' expressed periphrastically: 진실을 말하는 사람 (jinsireul malhaneun saram) 'person who speaks truth.'" This represents an **asymmetric gap** where only the negative form has morphological expression.

### Asymmetries in intensification
Intensification patterns apply primarily to negative terms. P4b notes that 새빨간 거짓말 "blatant lie" and 하얀 거짓말 "white lie" are attested, but "No parallel intensification for truth terms (*새빨간 진실 not idiomatic)." This is marked **[MARGINAL]** with "Limited and asymmetric pattern."

### Domain-specific constructions
Several constructions show domain specialization:
- **Authentication constructions** (진짜인지 가짜인지) are "Domain-specific to material authenticity" and "Less commonly applied to propositional truth"
- **Correctness evaluation** (맞다/틀리다) is "Domain-specific to educational, testing, and evaluative contexts" and "Distinct from moral evaluation (옳다/그르다)"
- **Legitimacy assessment** (정당하다/타당하다) is "Specialized to legal, political, and argumentative discourse"

## 3. Missingness Summary

### Structural Gaps
None identified. All canonical operators have some constructional realization, though some require substitution or periphrasis.

### Morphosyntactic Gaps
1. **CAUSATION (factual)**: No productive morphological causative. Only periphrastic expressions using transitive revelation verbs (밝히다) or light verb constructions.
2. **CAUSATION (character)**: No productive morphological causative. Only auxiliary construction -게 하다.

### Lexical Gaps
1. **Positive agentive nominalization**: No single-word term for "truth-teller" (requires periphrastic 진실을 말하는 사람).
2. **Truth-term intensifiers**: No idiomatic intensification patterns for positive truth terms (only for 거짓말).

### Conceptual Gaps
None identified. All truth-domain concepts can be expressed, though some require circumlocution.

### Asymmetric Gaps
1. **Agentive nominalization**: Productive only for negative agent (거짓말쟁이 "liar"); positive agent requires periphrasis.
2. **Intensification**: Productive primarily for negative terms (거짓말); positive terms lack parallel constructions.
3. **BECOME vs. REVELATION**: While 되다 "become" is grammatically possible, it is marginal; REVELATION constructions are strongly preferred for truth emergence.

## 4. Canonicalized Operator Inventory

### Fully Available
- **ATTR**: Standard copular construction (NP + 이/가 + TRUTH-TERM + 이다)
- **ADNOM**: Productive adnominal modification (TRUTH-TERM-(으)ㄴ/는 + NOUN)
- **NEGATION**: Analytic negation (아니다, -지 않다)
- **NOMINALIZATION**: Productive abstract nominalization (-성 suffix)
- **VERIFICATION**: Light verb construction (TRUTH-TERM + 을/를 + VERIFICATION-VERB)
- **REVELATION**: Specialized intransitive verbs (드러나다, 밝혀지다)

### Available with Substitution
- **BECOME**: Grammatically possible (되다) but functionally substituted by REVELATION constructions in actual usage
- **DISCONFIRMATION**: No distinct construction; uses VERIFICATION construction with negative semantic outcome
- **CAUSATION (factual)**: Expressed through transitive REVELATION verbs (밝히다 "reveal/bring to light") functioning as light verb causatives

### Partially Available
- **CAUSATION (character)**: Only periphrastic auxiliary construction (-게 하다); no morphological causative
- **Agentive nominalization**: Productive only for negative agents (거짓말쟁이); positive agents require periphrasis
- **Intensification**: Productive only for negative terms (거짓말); positive terms lack morphological intensification

### Missing Entirely
None. All canonical operators have some form of expression, though some require circumlocution or substitution.

## Additional Observations

### Light Verb Constructions
Korean extensively uses light verb constructions in the truth domain:
- **VERIFICATION**: 사실을 확인하다 (sasireul hwaginhada) - 확인하다 "verify" functions as light verb
- **REVELATION (transitive)**: 진실을 밝히다 (jinsireul balkhida) - 밝히다 "brighten/reveal" functions as light verb
- **CAUSATION (factual)**: Same as transitive REVELATION (밝히다 carries causative semantics)

These constructions behave syntactically as transitive verbs with truth-domain nouns as objects.

### Discourse-Level Grammaticalization
Several truth-domain terms have grammaticalized into discourse markers:
- 사실은 (sasireun) "actually, in fact"
- 솔직히 말하면 (soljiki malhamyeon) "frankly speaking"

P4b marks these as **[CORE]** and notes they are "Fully grammaticalized as discourse markers beyond compositional meaning."

### Register Stratification
P4b identifies register-specific patterns:
- **Formal registers**: Legitimacy Assessment (정당하다/타당하다), Credibility Constructions (신빙성)
- **Colloquial registers**: Intensification Patterns (새빨간 거짓말), Agentive suffix -쟁이

### Evidential Marking
Korean shows obligatory epistemic stance marking in many contexts. P4b notes: "Korean obligatorily marks epistemic stance in many contexts" with multiple constructions (것 같다, 듯하다, 나 보다) showing "subtle differences in evidential source."
