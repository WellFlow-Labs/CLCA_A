# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P4a CONSTRUCTIONS
## Language: Korean (ko)
### Transliteration Standard: Revised Romanization of Korean (RR)

---

## 1. ATTRIBUTION

### Pattern
**[Noun] + 이다/이 아니다** — copular predication asserting or denying that something belongs to the truth domain

**N이/가 + Adj** (predicative attribution via copular adjective)
The truth-domain adjective or nominal predicate follows the subject with subject particle 이/가. Korean has no copula separate from the predicate adjective itself; the adjective inflects for tense and mood directly.

### Examples
- 사실이다 → *sasil-ida* → "It is a fact / That is true"
- 거짓말이 아니다 → *geojitmal-i anida* → "It is not a lie / That is not false"
- 그것은 사실이다 / geugeoseun sasirida / "That is a fact."
- 이것은 거짓말이다 / igeoseun geojitmarida / "This is a lie."
- 그 말이 사실이다 → *geu mali sasirida* → "That statement is a fact / That is true."
- 그의 주장이 타당하다 → *geuui jujang i tadanghada* → "His claim is valid/reasonable."

### Notes
Korean uses the copula 이다 (positive) and 이 아니다 (negative) to predicate truth-domain nouns directly of a subject. No separate "to be true" verb exists; the nominal 사실, 진실, 거짓말, etc. is predicated via copula. The negative form 이 아니다 is the standard denial construction across all registers.

The most productive frame for truth-domain attribution. Nominal predicates (사실이다, 진실이다) use the copula 이다; adjectival predicates (타당하다, 정확하다, 옳다) inflect directly. Both patterns are common; the choice depends on whether the base form is a noun or adjective.

The copula 이다 attaches directly to the nominal base. Negation is formed with 이/가 아니다 (e.g., 사실이 아니다, "it is not a fact"), which is the standard construction for denying factual status. No verb inflection for tense or agreement beyond the copula is required.

## 2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION

### Pattern
**[Noun] + 이/가 + 없다** — existential negation asserting the absence of a truth-domain property

**N이/가 + Neg-Adj** or **N이/가 + 아니다**
Negation is achieved either by prefixing 부-/비-/무- to the predicate base, or by using the negative copula 아니다 with nominal predicates. Verbal negation uses 안 or -지 않다.

Noun + 이/가 아니다 — copular negation asserting that the subject does not instantiate a truth-domain category.

### Examples
- 근거가 없다 → *geungeo-ga eopda* → "There is no basis / It is groundless"
- 신빙성이 없다 → *sinbyeongseong-i eopda* → "It lacks credibility / It is implausible"
- 그 주장은 사실이 아니다 / geu jujangeun sasiri anida / "That claim is not a fact."
- 이건 진짜가 아니다 / igeon jinjjaga anida / "This is not genuine."
- 그 정보가 정확하지 않다 → *geu jeongboga jeonghwakaji anta* → "That information is not accurate."
- 그것은 사실이 아니다 → *geugeoseun sasiri anida* → "That is not a fact / That is not true."

### Notes
For abstract quality nouns (근거, 신빙성, 진정성, 타당성), the primary negative construction is existential 없다 ("to not exist/have") rather than copular negation. The positive counterpart uses 있다 ("to exist/have"): 근거가 있다, 신빙성이 있다. This 있다/없다 pattern is the standard evaluative frame for this class of nouns; -하다 attachment is not available for most of them.

이/가 아니다 is the standard negation of 이다 and is the primary construction for denying factual or authenticity status in Korean. It is more neutral in register than 거짓이다 ("it is a falsehood") and is preferred in formal corrections and public statements.

Two structurally distinct negation strategies coexist: morphological (부정확하다, 무효하다) and syntactic (-지 않다, 아니다). The syntactic strategy is more broadly productive and more common in spoken registers; morphological negation tends toward formal and written use.

## 3. ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION

### Pattern
**[Truth-domain adjective stem] + -(으)ㄴ + [Noun]** — attributive modification of a noun by a truth-domain predicate

**Adj-stem + -(으)ㄴ + N** (adnominal form of adjective modifying noun)
Korean adjectives precede the nouns they modify in their adnominal form, marked by the suffix -(으)ㄴ for stative adjectives. Sino-Korean bases take -한 (from 하다); native Korean adjectives take -은/-ㄴ directly from the stem.

### Examples
- 근거 없는 주장 → *geungeo eomneun jujang* → "An unsubstantiated claim / A groundless assertion"
- 진정성 있는 사과 → *jinjeonseong inneun saga* → "A sincere apology / A genuine apology"
- 정확한 정보 / jeonghwakan jeongbo / "accurate information"
- 올바른 판단 / olbareun pandan / "correct judgment"
- 정확한 정보 → *jeonghwakhan jeongbo* → "accurate information"
- 올바른 판단 → *olbareun pandan* → "a correct/right judgment"

### Notes
Attributive modification in Korean is formed by the adnominal ending -(으)ㄴ on adjective stems or the participial form of 있다/없다 (있는/없는). Truth-domain nouns used attributively (진짜, 거짓, 허위, 올바른, 정확한) precede the head noun directly. The construction 근거 없는 is particularly fixed and functions as a near-compound modifier meaning "unsubstantiated."

Korean truth-domain adjectives (형용사) form their attributive (adnominal) form with the suffix -(으)ㄴ attached to the stem. For 하다-adjectives (e.g., 정확하다, 정직하다), the stem is the Sino-Korean base plus 한 (e.g., 정확한, 정직한). Native adjectives (e.g., 올바르다, 옳다, 틀리다) follow standard irregular or regular conjugation patterns (올바른, 옳은, 틀린).

This is the primary attributive position in Korean; there is no postnominal adjectival modification. The adnominal suffix differs by base type: Sino-Korean -하다 adjectives use -한, native Korean adjectives use -(으)ㄴ directly on the stem. Both patterns are fully productive across the truth-domain vocabulary.

## 4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE

### Pattern
**[Noun] + (으)로 + 드러나다 / 밝혀지다 / 판명되다** — inchoative constructions marking the transition of a truth-domain state into public knowledge

**N + 이/가 + 되다** or **N + (으)로 + 되다 / 판명되다**
Inchoative meaning ("becomes X," "turns out to be X") is expressed periphrastically. The verb 되다 ("to become") follows the nominal predicate with subject particle, or the nominal appears in the -로 instrumental frame with result verbs such as 판명되다 (to be determined to be) and 밝혀지다 (to be revealed as).

Noun + (으)로 밝혀지다 / 드러나다 / 판명되다 — inchoative construction expressing that a truth-domain status comes to be established or revealed.

### Examples
- 진실이 드러나다 → *jinsil-i deureonada* → "The truth emerges / The truth is exposed"
- 가짜로 판명되다 → *gajja-ro panmyeong-doeda* → "To be determined to be fake / To be found counterfeit"
- 그 주장이 허위로 밝혀졌다 / geu jujangi heowiro balkyeojyeossda / "That claim was revealed to be false."
- 가짜로 판명되었다 / gajjaro panmyeongdoeeossda / "It was determined to be fake."
- 계약이 무효가 되다 → *gyeyagi muhyoga doeda* → "The contract becomes void."

### Notes
Korean expresses the coming-to-be-known of truth-domain states through intransitive verbs of emergence and determination: 드러나다 (to surface, be exposed), 밝혀지다 (to be illuminated, come to light), 판명되다 (to be officially determined). The -로 particle marks the resultant state in 판명되다 constructions. These are not morphological inchoatives but lexically distinct verbs; no single inchoative suffix covers this domain.

Korean does not have a single inchoative morpheme equivalent to "become + adjective." In the truth domain, the inchoative meaning is characteristically expressed through the passive/spontaneous verbs 밝혀지다 ("to be revealed/come to light"), 드러나다 ("to be exposed"), and 판명되다 ("to be determined"), combined with the nominal + (으)로 ("as") construction. The pattern Noun + (이)라는 것이 밝혀지다 is an alternative with an embedded clause.

Korean lacks a single inchoative morpheme; the pattern is periphrastic. 되다 is the general inchoative verb; 판명되다 and 밝혀지다 are more specific to contexts of investigation and verification, where the change of state results from an epistemic process rather than a natural transition.

## 5. CAUSATIVE (OR ABSENCE)

### Pattern
**[Agent] + 이/가 + [Truth-domain noun] + 을/를 + 밝히다 / 숨기다 / 왜곡하다** — agentive causative expressed through transitive verb selection

**N + 을/를 + 무효화하다 / 합법화하다 / 정당화하다** (causative via -화하다 suffix)
A productive causative-like pattern in the truth domain uses the suffix -화하다 (化하다, "to make into / to cause to become") attached to Sino-Korean nominal bases. This produces transitive verbs meaning "to render X" or "to cause something to become X."

Noun + 하다 (transitive) — the 하다 verb with a truth-domain nominal base functions as the primary causative-like construction, expressing the deliberate performance of a truth-domain act.

### Examples
- 진실을 밝히다 → *jinsil-eul balkhida* → "To reveal the truth / To bring the truth to light"
- 진실을 숨기다 → *jinsil-eul sumgida* → "To conceal the truth / To hide the truth"
- 계약을 무효화하다 → *gyeyageul muhyohwa hada* → "To invalidate the contract / to render the contract void."
- 마리화나를 합법화하다 → *marihwanareul hapbeophwa hada* → "To legalize marijuana."
- 거짓말하다 / geojitmal-hada / "to lie" (lit. "to do lying")
- 위조하다 / wijo-hada / "to forge / to counterfeit" (lit. "to do forgery")

### Notes
Korean has no productive morphological causative suffix in this domain. Causative meaning — causing a truth-domain state to become known or to remain hidden — is expressed entirely through transitive verb selection: 밝히다 (reveal), 숨기다 (conceal), 왜곡하다 (distort), 외면하다 (avert from). These verbs take the truth-domain noun as direct object. No form like *진실-이키다 or *사실-히다 is attested.

The -화하다 pattern is the closest Korean equivalent to a morphological causative in this domain. It is productive with Sino-Korean bases (무효화, 합법화, 정당화, 실체화) but not with native Korean bases (no *진짜화하다, *옳화하다). A separate periphrastic causative using -게 하다 exists but is not specific to the truth domain.

Korean does not have a productive morphological causative suffix that applies systematically to truth-domain adjectives or nouns. The closest functional equivalent is the 하다 verb construction, which converts a nominal base into a transitive verb expressing deliberate action. For stative adjectives (e.g., 정확하다, 진실하다), no causative form is identified in the data; causation is expressed periphrastically (e.g., 정확하게 만들다, "to make accurate"). This pattern is therefore limited to nominal bases that denote acts rather than states.

## 6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION

### Pattern
**[Sino-Korean nominal base] + 성(性)** — derivation of an abstract quality noun

**(a) Noun/Adjective stem + 성 (性)** — nominalizing suffix yielding an abstract quality noun.
**(b) Adjective stem + -(으)ㅁ** — verbal/adjectival nominalizing suffix.
**(c) Verb-stem + -기** — nominalizing suffix.

### Examples
- 진실성 → *jinsilseong* → "Truthfulness / The quality of being truthful"
- 타당성 → *tadangseong* → "Validity / Soundness as a quality"
- 신뢰성 / sinloeseong / "reliability / trustworthiness"
- 올바름 / olbareum / "rightness / correctness"
- 옳음 / oreum / "rightness / correctness" (more formal/philosophical)
- 정확성을 높이다 → *jeonghwakseong eul nopida* → "To improve accuracy / to raise the level of precision."
- 올바름의 기준 → *olbareum ui gijun* → "The standard of rightness/correctness."

### Notes
The suffix -성 (性) attaches productively to Sino-Korean nominal bases to derive abstract quality nouns: 진실성, 정확성, 신뢰성, 공정성, 진정성, 유사성, 객관성, 주관성. Native Korean bases (진짜, 거짓말, 맞다) do not take -성. A second nominalization pattern uses -음 on native verbal stems: 올바름 (rightness), 틀림 (error). These two patterns are in complementary distribution by base type.

The suffix 성 (性) is the dominant abstract nominalizer in the truth domain and attaches productively to Sino-Korean bases (진실성, 정확성, 타당성, 공정성, 신빙성, 진정성, 정직성). The native Korean nominalizer -(으)ㅁ applies to native adjective stems (올바름, 옳음) and yields forms that appear primarily in philosophical and formal written registers. The two patterns are not interchangeable: 성 attaches to nominal/Sino-Korean bases; -(으)ㅁ attaches to verbal/adjectival stems.

-성 is highly productive with Sino-Korean bases in this domain (진실성, 타당성, 유효성, 합법성, 신뢰성, 신빙성, 진정성, 허위성). Native Korean adjective stems nominalize via -(으)ㅁ (올바름, 옳음, 그름), but these forms are less common in everyday use and tend toward philosophical or literary registers.

## 7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION

### Pattern
**[거짓말] + 쟁이 / 꾼** — pejorative agentive suffix deriving a person-noun from a lying-related base

**Base + -자 (者)** (Sino-Korean agentive) or **Base + -쟁이** (native Korean colloquial agentive)
Agentive nominalizations referring to persons defined by their relationship to truth or falsehood are formed by -자 (者, formal) or -쟁이 (colloquial, habitual agent).

### Examples
- 거짓말쟁이 → *geojitmal-jaengi* → "Liar (habitual, pejorative)"
- 위조범 → *wijobom* → "Forger / Counterfeiter (legal register)"
- 거짓말쟁이 / geojitmaljaengi / "liar / habitual liar"
- 위선자 → *wiseonja* → "Hypocrite" (person of false virtue; formal)

### Notes
Agentive nominalization in this domain is sparse and register-stratified. The native suffix -쟁이 (pejorative, habitual agent) attaches to 거짓말 to yield 거짓말쟁이; -꾼 (habitual agent, slightly less pejorative) yields 거짓말꾼. In formal/legal registers, the Sino-Korean -범 (犯, criminal) attaches to 위조 to yield 위조범. No agentive form meaning "truth-teller" is lexicalized; the concept is expressed periphrastically (진실을 말하는 사람, 정직한 사람).

The suffix 쟁이 is the only productive agentive nominalizer identified in the truth domain in the P1–P3 data, and it attaches only to 거짓말 in common usage. No parallel agentive form for "truth-teller" (e.g., *진실쟁이) is attested as a common expression. The suffix 범 (犯, criminal/offender) yields 위조범 ("forger"), but this is a legal category label rather than a general agentive pattern. The asymmetry — a common agentive for liars but not for truth-tellers — is preserved as found.

The asymmetry is structurally significant: Korean has no common agentive nominalization for "truth-teller" parallel to 거짓말쟁이. The concept is expressed periphrastically (진실을 말하는 사람, "a person who tells the truth") rather than by a single lexicalized form. Agentive -자 forms in this domain (위선자, 위조범) tend toward the negative/false pole.

## 8. LIGHT VERB CONSTRUCTION WITH 하다

### Pattern
**[Sino-Korean nominal base] + 하다** — conversion of a nominal into a predicate

**N + 을/를 + V** where V is a semantically light or collocationally fixed verb
A large proportion of truth-domain predication in Korean is expressed through verb-object collocations in which a nominal base takes a collocationally fixed verb. The nominal carries the semantic content; the verb (밝히다, 숨기다, 쌓다, 잃다, 왜곡하다, etc.) specifies the action type.

### Examples
- 위조하다 → *wijo-hada* → "To forge / To counterfeit"
- 정직하다 → *jeongjik-hada* → "To be honest / To have integrity"
- 진실을 밝히다 → *jinsileul balkhida* → "To reveal the truth."
- 신뢰를 잃다 → *sinloereul ilta* → "To lose trust/credibility."

### Notes
The light verb 하다 attaches to Sino-Korean nominal bases to produce predicates across the domain: 진실하다, 정직하다, 위조하다, 신뢰하다, 타당하다, 유사하다, 근거하다. This is the primary verbalization strategy for Sino-Korean bases. Native Korean bases (진짜, 가짜, 거짓) do not take 하다 in this pattern; 거짓말하다 is an exception where the full compound 거짓말 (not 거짓 alone) takes 하다.

This pattern is the primary means of expressing truth-domain actions in Korean. The same nominal base participates in multiple collocations with different verbs (진실을 밝히다 / 숨기다 / 왜곡하다 / 외면하다), and the verb choice encodes the specific action. This is structurally distinct from derivational morphology and from simple predication; it constitutes the most productive construction type in the domain.

## 9. STATIVE PREDICATE WITH 되다

### Pattern
**[Nominal base] + 되다** — stative or passive predicate expressing an inherent or resultant truth-domain state

### Examples
- 모순되다 → *mosun-doeda* → "To be contradictory / To contradict"
- 거짓되다 → *geojit-doeda* → "To be false / To be deceitful"

### Notes
Where both -하다 and -되다 forms exist (진실하다 / 진실되다), the -하다 form tends toward active or agentive meaning and the -되다 form toward stative or inherent quality. 모순되다 is the standard predicate for contradiction (모순하다 is not attested). 참되다 (to be genuinely true/authentic) appears in literary and religious registers. The -되다 pattern is less productive than -하다 in this domain.

## 10. EXISTENTIAL PREDICATION OF ABSTRACT QUALITY

### Pattern
**Abstract quality noun + 이/가 있다 / 없다** — existential construction asserting the presence or absence of a truth-domain quality.

**N + 이/가 + 있다 / 없다** or **N + 이/가 + 높다 / 낮다**
Several truth-domain nominals — particularly those denoting properties or qualities — are predicated not through adjectives but through the existential verbs 있다 (to exist/have) and 없다 (to not exist/lack), or through scalar adjectives 높다 (high) and 낮다 (low).

### Examples
- 진정성이 있다 / jinjeongseong-i itda / "to have sincerity / to be authentic"
- 신빙성이 없다 / sinbyeongseong-i eopda / "to lack credibility / to be implausible"
- 신빙성이 있다 / 없다 → *sinbyeongseong i itda / eopda* → "To be credible / to lack credibility."

### Notes
This construction is the primary predication strategy for abstract quality nouns formed with 성 (性) in the truth domain. Because these nominalizations (진정성, 신뢰성, 신빙성, 타당성) do not take 하다 as a predicative suffix in their nominalized form, the existential predicates 있다/없다 serve as the standard affirmative and negative predicators. This pattern is highly productive across the truth domain and has no direct structural parallel in English.

This construction is specific to nominal bases that denote assessable properties (신빙성, 타당성, 진정성, 근거, 신뢰도). It is not available for all truth-domain nominals — 사실이 있다 does not mean "to be factual" in the same way. The 있다/없다 frame treats the property as a binary possession; the 높다/낮다 frame treats it as a scalar quantity. Both are productive in evaluative and journalistic discourse.

## 11. SCALAR PREDICATION OF ABSTRACT QUALITY

### Pattern
**Abstract quality noun + 이/가 높다 / 낮다** — scalar predication expressing degree of a truth-domain quality.

### Examples
- 신뢰성이 높다 / sinloeseong-i nopda / "credibility is high"
- 정확도가 낮다 / jeonghwakdoga natda / "accuracy is low"
- 신뢰도가 높다 → *sinloedo ga nopda* → "To have high credibility/trustworthiness."

### Notes
Truth-domain quality nouns, particularly those formed with 성 (性) and 도 (度), combine productively with the scalar adjectives 높다 ("high") and 낮다 ("low") to express degree of a quality. The 도 (度) suffix (정확도, 신뢰도) is specifically associated with quantifiable degree and appears more frequently in this scalar construction than 성 (性) forms. This construction is characteristic of journalistic, legal, and scientific evaluation contexts.

## 12. ADVERBIAL FORMATION

### Pattern
Three distinct adverbial patterns in complementary distribution by base type:
- **[Sino-Korean base] + 히** → 정확히, 정직히
- **[-하다 stem] + 게** → 정직하게, 공정하게, 올바르게
- **[Nominal] + 로/으로** → 진실로, 진정으로, 실제로

**(a) Adjective stem + 히** — adverbializing suffix (applies to select Sino-Korean adjective bases).
**(b) Adjective stem + 하게** — adverbializing suffix via 하다 form (applies to 하다-adjectives).
**(c) Noun + (으)로** — instrumental/manner particle yielding adverbial expression.

### Examples
- 정확히 말하면 → *jeonghwaki malhamyeon* → "To be precise / Strictly speaking"
- 실제로 일어난 일 → *siljero ileonan il* → "What actually happened"
- 정확히 말하다 / jeonghwaki malhada / "to speak exactly / to state precisely"
- 정직하게 말하다 / jeongjik-hage malhada / "to speak honestly"
- 진정으로 사과하다 / jinjeong-euro sagwa-hada / "to apologize sincerely"
- 진심으로 감사하다 / jinsimeuro gamsahada / "to thank sincerely / from the heart"

### Notes
Korean truth-domain adverbs are formed by three non-interchangeable strategies. The -히 suffix attaches to a subset of Sino-Korean bases (정확히, 정직히) and is not freely productive. The -게 ending on -하다 stems is more broadly available and common in spoken registers. The -로/으로 particle on nominals produces adverbials of manner or perspective (진실로, 진정으로) or of actuality (실제로, 사실상). Register and base type determine which form is used; they are not synonymous.

Three distinct adverbial formation strategies are attested in the truth domain. The 히 suffix applies to a restricted set of Sino-Korean bases (정확히 is the primary truth-domain example). The 하게 suffix applies productively to all 하다-adjectives. The (으)로 construction applies to nominal bases (진정으로, 진심으로, 허위로, 가짜로) and is particularly common with nouns that do not form 하다-adjectives. All three patterns are productive and non-interchangeable.

## 13. DISCOURSE-FRAMING CONSTRUCTION

### Pattern
**Noun/Adverb + (을/를) 말하자면 / 말하다면** — conditional framing construction introducing a frank, precise, or corrective statement.

### Examples
- 정확히 말하자면 / jeonghwaki malhajamyeon / "to be precise / strictly speaking"
- 사실을 말하자면 / sasireul malhajamyeon / "to tell the truth / the fact of the matter is"

### Notes
This construction functions as a pragmatic marker signaling that what follows is the speaker's more precise or frank assessment, often correcting or refining a prior statement. It is not a morphological derivation but a syntactic pattern that is highly conventionalized in the truth domain. The construction combines a truth-domain adverb or noun with the conditional form of 말하다 ("to speak/say") and is common across spoken and written registers.

## 14. POLAR OPPOSITION FRAMING

### Pattern
**Noun₁ + 과/와 + Noun₂** — coordinated nominal pair expressing the binary opposition of truth-domain poles.

### Examples
- 진실과 거짓 / jinsil-gwa geojit / "truth and falsehood"
- 옳고 그름 / olko geureum / "right and wrong"

### Notes
Coordinated polar pairs are a structurally distinct and highly conventionalized pattern in the Korean truth domain. They function as compound noun phrases or as framing constructions for moral and epistemic evaluation. The pair 진짜인지 가짜인지 ("whether real or fake") extends this pattern into an alternative-question construction used to express authenticity doubt. These pairs are not productive in the sense of generating new combinations freely; the attested pairs are fixed collocations.
