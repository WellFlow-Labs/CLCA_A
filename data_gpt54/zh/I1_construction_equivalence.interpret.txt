## 2. Substitution Patterns (Narrative)

Chinese shows direct support for attribution, adnominal modification, change-of-state, analytic negation, verification, and abstract nominalization.

For **BECOME**, the language uses general change-of-state syntax rather than a specialized truth-domain form: **变得 + Adj / 变 + Adj / 越来越 + Adj**. Here **变(得)** is the light/change verb carrying the operator. P4a/P4b do not list any separate **REVELATION** construction, so there is no evidence here that revelation replaces becoming. The appropriate flag is therefore **REVELATION: MISSING (structural gap)**, not substitution.

For **CAUSATION**, Chinese does not use dedicated causative morphology in this domain. Instead it uses analytic carriers such as **使 / 让 / 把** plus a result state: **使事实变得不清楚**, **让图像失真**. This means causation is available, but through general analytic syntax. In addition, for deliberate falsity/fabrication meanings, Chinese often prefers lexical eventive expressions rather than causativized truth adjectives: **发布虚假信息**, **伪造证件**, **弄虚作假**. Descriptively, some causative meanings are therefore **substituted by lexical falsehood/fabrication events**.

There is no listed evidence that **DISCOVERY substitutes for CAUSATION**. No discovery construction appears in P4a/P4b, so no such substitution can be stated.

For **NEGATION**, the dominant pattern is **analytic**, especially **不 + Adj**. Alongside this, there are lexicalized or semi-lexicalized negative predicates such as **不实** and **失实**, especially in formal rebuttal language. So the system is asymmetrical: ordinary negation is analytic, but formal falsity judgments often prefer specialized negative lexical items.

For **VERIFICATION** and **DISCONFIRMATION**, the data show a close relation. **属实** is the positive formal verification predicate, while **不属实** is its direct negative counterpart. At the same time, **不实** appears as an additional formal negative predicate. So these two operators **partly share the same constructional base**, especially in the **属实 / 不属实** pair, but disconfirmation also has its own lexicalized negative form (**不实**).

There is also a positive/negative asymmetry in register:
- positive verification is strongly specialized and institutional: **属实 / 经查属实 / 查证属实**
- negative evaluation can be either general analytic (**不可信**) or formal lexicalized rebuttal (**不实**, **失实**)

For agentive meanings, P4a/P4b explicitly note no common productive pair matching “liar / truth-teller.” This does not map directly to the requested canonical operators, but it shows a lexical gap in the broader domain: Chinese often uses paraphrase (**说真话的人**) or unrelated lexical items, rather than a productive derivational pattern.

## 3. Missingness Summary

### Structural Gaps
- **REVELATION**: **MISSING (structural gap)**
  - No revelation-type construction is provided in P4a/P4b.
- No dedicated morphological causative construction for truth-domain predicates
  - Causation is expressed analytically with **使 / 让 / 把**.

### Lexical Gaps
- No common productive compact agentive pair equivalent to “liar / truth-teller”
  - P4a/P4b: **(no common expression identified)**
  - Available forms are lexical or paraphrastic: **伪造者**, **说真话的人**
- For **CAUSATION (character)**, no dedicated lexicalized operator is shown for “make X true/reliable/credible” as a regular construction
  - Often requires circumlocution or different lexical verbs.

### Conceptual Gaps
- No explicit evidence in P4a/P4b for a distinct **REVELATION** operator.
- No explicit evidence in P4a/P4b for **DISCOVERY** as a substitute operator.
- No evidence for a distinct operator specifically targeting “character” causation as separate from factual/state causation; available data only show general analytic causation and lexical falsification/correction verbs.

### Asymmetric Gaps
- **DISCONFIRMATION** is better lexicalized/formalized than many positive counterparts in rebuttal contexts:
  - **不实**, **失实** are especially common in formal negative judgments.
- **VERIFICATION** is strongly specialized around **属实**, especially with reports/allegations, rather than a broad everyday predicate.
- **CAUSATION** is available for negative/result-state meanings like **失真** and **不清楚**, but deliberate falsity often shifts to lexical event verbs (**伪造**, **发布虚假信息**) rather than parallel causativized adjective constructions.
- Agentive nominalization is asymmetric:
  - paraphrastic **说真话的人** is natural
  - no equally productive compact derivational pair is identified.
