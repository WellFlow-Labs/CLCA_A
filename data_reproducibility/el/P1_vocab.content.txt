# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P1 CORE VOCABULARY COLLECTION
# Language: Greek (el)

---

## 1. αλήθεια
**Transliteration:** alítheia  
**Literal meaning:** un-hiddenness (α- privative + λήθω "to hide")  
**Typical English translations:** truth, reality, truthfulness

Used to denote truth as correspondence to facts or reality, and also sincerity or truthfulness in speech. Common in everyday discourse, philosophical texts, and religious contexts where it refers to ultimate reality or divine truth.

**[CORE]**

---

## 2. αληθινός
**Transliteration:** alithinós  
**Literal meaning:** true, genuine (derived from αλήθεια)  
**Typical English translations:** true, genuine, real, authentic

Describes something as genuine or authentic rather than false or imitation. Used for objects, statements, and persons to indicate they conform to what they claim or appear to be.

**[CORE]**

---

## 3. ψέμα
**Transliteration:** pséma  
**Literal meaning:** lie, falsehood  
**Typical English translations:** lie, falsehood, untruth

Refers to a deliberate false statement or deception. Common in everyday speech to denote intentional misrepresentation of facts.

**[CORE]**

---

## 4. ψεύτης
**Transliteration:** pséftis  
**Literal meaning:** liar (agent noun from ψεύδομαι "to lie")  
**Typical English translations:** liar

Denotes a person who tells lies or habitually deceives. Used in both casual and serious moral condemnation of dishonest persons.

**[CORE]**

---

## 5. σωστός
**Transliteration:** sostós  
**Literal meaning:** correct, right (from σῴζω "to save, preserve")  
**Typical English translations:** correct, right, proper, accurate

Indicates correctness in statements, answers, procedures, or conduct. Widely used in educational, technical, and everyday contexts to affirm accuracy or appropriateness.

**[CORE]**

## 6. λάθος
**Transliteration:** láthos

## 7. ορθός

**Typical English translations:** correct, right, proper, orthodox
