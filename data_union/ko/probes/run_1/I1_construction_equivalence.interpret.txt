## 2. Substitution Patterns (Narrative)

### 2.1 REVELATION substitutes for BECOME

The BECOME operator — expressing the transition of a truth-domain state into existence or public knowledge — is not realized by any morphological inchoative suffix in Korean. Instead, the function is covered entirely by a set of lexically distinct intransitive verbs: 드러나다 (to surface/be exposed), 밝혀지다 (to come to light), and 판명되다 (to be officially determined). These verbs are not morphological inchoatives derived from truth-domain adjectives; they are independent lexical items whose semantics include the transition component. The REVELATION operator therefore absorbs the BECOME function in this domain. The two are not formally distinguished: the same verbs (드러나다, 밝혀지다) serve both the "coming-to-be-known" (BECOME) and the "being-revealed" (REVELATION) readings. 판명되다 is distinguished from the others by its official or authoritative determination sense, but it remains within the same constructional frame.

### 2.2 Transitive verb selection substitutes for morphological CAUSATION

Korean has no productive morphological causative suffix in the truth domain. The causative function — causing a truth-domain state to become known or to remain hidden — is expressed entirely through the selection of transitive verbs: 밝히다 (reveal), 숨기다 (conceal), 왜곡하다 (distort), 외면하다 (avert from). These verbs carry the causative meaning as part of their lexical semantics rather than through a morphological operator. The light verb 하다 in constructions like 위조하다 (to forge) also carries causative-like meaning for Sino-Korean bases, but this is a verbalization strategy rather than a dedicated causative morpheme.

### 2.3 NEGATION is analytic, not morphological

Negation in the truth domain is expressed through three distinct analytic strategies rather than a single morphological operator:
- The negative copula 이 아니다 for truth-domain noun predication (거짓말이 아니다)
- The existential negative 없다 for abstract quality nouns (근거가 없다, 신빙성이 없다)
- The participial 없는 for adnominal modification (근거 없는 주장)

These three forms are not interchangeable; their distribution is conditioned by the syntactic position and the noun class involved. There is no single morphological negation operator that covers all three positions.

### 2.4 DISCONFIRMATION is expressed through NEGATION and REVELATION, not a dedicated construction

No construction in P4a or P4b is described as specifically encoding disconfirmation — the act of showing that a claim is false or unsupported. The closest available expressions are:
- Negative attribution: 근거가 없다 (there is no basis), which asserts the absence of evidential support
- Revelation with a false-domain resultant: 거짓으로 밝혀지다 (to be revealed as false), attested as an alternate in P4b

Neither of these is a dedicated disconfirmation construction. The function is distributed across NEGATION and REVELATION operators.

### 2.5 VERIFICATION has no attested construction

No construction in P4a or P4b describes the act of checking or confirming a claim against evidence. 판명되다 (to be officially determined) approaches this in its authoritative-determination reading, but it is classified in P4a under BECOME/REVELATION and describes the outcome of a determination process rather than the act of verification itself. VERIFICATION as a distinct operator is not represented in the attested data.

### 2.6 Asymmetry between deception and truth-telling in CAUSATION (character) and NOMINALIZATION

The data show a consistent asymmetry between the deception side and the truth-telling side of the domain:
- Agentive nominals for habitual deceivers are lexicalized: 거짓말쟁이 (liar, informal), 거짓말꾼 (liar, slightly less pejorative), 위조범 (forger, legal)
- No agentive nominal for "truth-teller" is lexicalized; the concept requires periphrastic expression: 진실을 말하는 사람, 정직한 사람

This asymmetry is noted in both P4a §7 and P4b and is confirmed as a genuine lexical gap rather than an oversight.

### 2.7 Register stratification within constructions

Several constructions that are formally available show internal register stratification that functions as a partial substitution pattern:
- 정직히 (formal/written) vs. 정직하게 (spoken/colloquial) for the same adverbial meaning
- 진실로 / 진정으로 (formal/emphatic) vs. 실제로 / 사실상 (neutral/everyday) for actuality adverbials
- 거짓말쟁이 (informal) vs. 위조범 (legal/formal) for agentive nominals

These are not substitutions between different operators but register-conditioned alternations within the same operator.

### 2.8 The -하다 / -되다 distinction within LIGHT VERB constructions

Where both -하다 and -되다 forms exist (e.g., 진실하다 / 진실되다), P4a and P4b describe a consistent semantic split: -하다 tends toward active or agentive meaning, -되다 toward stative or inherent quality. This is not a substitution between operators but a semantic differentiation within the same constructional type. 모순되다 is the only available form for "to be contradictory" (모순하다 is not attested), making -되다 the sole carrier of that predicate.

## 3. Missingness Summary

### 3.1 Structural Gaps
*(Missing constructions — no form of the construction exists)*

- **VERIFICATION**: No construction in the attested data encodes the act of checking or confirming a claim against evidence. The concept is not expressed by any dedicated construction. **MISSING (structural gap).**
- **Morphological inchoative**: No inchoative suffix covers the BECOME function in the truth domain. The function is covered by lexical substitution (드러나다, 밝혀지다, 판명되다). **MISSING (structural gap); function covered by REVELATION.**
- **Morphological causative**: No causative suffix is available in the truth domain. The function is covered by transitive verb selection. **MISSING (structural gap); function covered by lexical verb selection.**
- **Dedicated DISCONFIRMATION construction**: No single construction encodes disconfirmation as a distinct operator. **MISSING (structural gap); function distributed across NEGATION and REVELATION.**

### 3.2 Lexical Gaps
*(Missing words within otherwise available constructions)*

- **Agentive nominal for "truth-teller"**: No lexicalized noun meaning "habitual truth-teller" or "person who tells the truth" is attested. The concept requires periphrastic expression (진실을 말하는 사람, 정직한 사람). The deception-side equivalents (거짓말쟁이, 거짓말꾼) are fully lexicalized. **Lexical gap on the truth-telling side of CAUSATION (character) and NOMINALIZATION.**
- **-하다 attachment to native bases**: Native Korean bases (진짜, 가짜, 거짓) do not take -하다 in the light verb construction. This is a lexical distributional gap within an otherwise productive pattern. **Lexical gap by base type.**
- **-성 attachment to native bases**: The abstract nominalization suffix -성 does not attach to native Korean bases (진짜, 거짓말, 맞다). **Lexical gap by base type; complementary -음 pattern covers native bases.**

### 3.3 Conceptual Gaps
*(Operations that appear to be blocked or unavailable as a category)*

- **VERIFICATION as a distinct conceptual category**: The data provide no evidence that Korean grammaticalizes a distinct VERIFICATION operation (checking a claim against evidence) separately from REVELATION (a claim becoming known) or ATTRIBUTION (asserting truth/falsity). Whether this reflects a conceptual gap or simply a lexical gap that would require circumlocution cannot be determined from the P4a/P4b data alone; the data support only the observation that no dedicated construction is attested.

### 3.4 Asymmetric Gaps
*(Only one polarity — positive or negative, truth or falsity — is missing)*

- **Agentive nominalization**: Deception-side agentive nominals are lexicalized (거짓말쟁이, 거짓말꾼, 위조범); truth-telling-side agentive nominals are not. **Asymmetric lexical gap: falsity side present, truth side absent.**
- **-되다 predicate availability**: 모순되다 is the only available form for "to be contradictory" (모순하다 unattested); 거짓되다 is [MARGINAL] and restricted to literary registers, while 참되다 is restricted to literary/religious registers. The -되다 pattern is less productive than -하다 overall. **Partial asymmetry within the stative predicate construction.**
- **Adverbial register asymmetry**: Actuality adverbials (실제로, 사실상) are neutral-register [CORE] forms; the corresponding truth-domain adverbials (진실로, 진정으로) are restricted to formal or emphatic contexts. **Asymmetric register availability: actuality side more broadly available than truth-domain side.**
