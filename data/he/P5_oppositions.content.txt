# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P5 OPPOSITIONS
# Language: Hebrew (he)

## IDENTIFIED OPPOSITIONS

### OPPOSITION 1
**Term A:** אמת (emet)  
**Term B:** שקר (sheker)  
**Transliteration:** emet vs. sheker  
**Translation:** truth vs. falsehood/lie  
**Description:** Primary lexical opposition in Hebrew truth domain. אמת denotes correspondence with reality or factual accuracy, while שקר denotes falsehood, deception, or non-correspondence with facts. This is the most fundamental and frequently attested opposition across all data.  
**Contrast type:** lexical  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]
