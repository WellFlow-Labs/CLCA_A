| 真实 (zhēnshí) | 真实性 | zhēnshíxìng | true + solid/real + -ness/-ity (nominalizing suffix) | truthfulness, authenticity, veracity, factual accuracy (as a property) | The nominalized form of 真实, used to name the property of being truthful or authentic as an abstract quality subject to evaluation or measurement. Extremely common in legal, journalistic, literary-critical, and heritage discourse: 新闻报道的真实性 (the truthfulness of news reporting), 历史真实性 (historical authenticity), 证据的真实性 (the authenticity of evidence), 核实其真实性 (to verify its authenticity/accuracy). This is one of the most productive and well-established 性-nominalizations in the truth domain. | [CORE] |
| 虚假 (xūjiǎ) | 虚假性 | xūjiǎxìng | empty/hollow + false + -ness/-ity | falseness, spuriousness, fraudulent nature, the quality of being fictitious or deceptive | Nominalizes 虚假 to name the abstract property of being false or deceptive, used in legal, philosophical, and analytical discourse: 陈述的虚假性 (the falseness of a statement), 广告的虚假性 (the fraudulent nature of the advertisement). Less frequent than 真实性 but attested in regulatory and academic writing. | [CORE] |

Well-attested across academic and technical registers. | [CORE] |
