## 1. Per-language extraction of main oppositional / contrastive structures

### zh — Chinese
**Lexical contrasts**
- **真 / 假** — basic true/real vs false/fake
- **真实 / 虚假** — factual reality vs false/deceptive
- **正确 / 错误** — correct vs wrong
- **属实 / 失实** — verified true vs not in accord with facts
- **属实 / 不实** — verified true vs untrue/unfounded
- **真话 / 假话** — truthful words vs lies
- **真品 / 假货** — genuine article vs counterfeit goods
- **正品 / 冒牌(货)** — genuine product vs bogus/counterfeit
- **正牌 / 冒牌** — legitimate brand/status vs counterfeit/bogus
- **如实 / 弄虚作假** — report according to facts vs falsify/fraud
- **伪造 / 真迹** — forged vs authentic original
- **仿真 / 逼真** vs **真 / 真实** — resemblance/lifelikeness vs actual reality
- **失真** vs **真实 / 真实感** — distortion vs faithful reality/realism

**Morphological contrasts**
- **可信 / 不可信**
- **可靠 / 不可靠**
- **正确 / 不正确**
- **准确 / 不准确**
- **真实 / 不真实**

**Constructional contrasts**
- **真伪 / 真假** — internally contrastive paired structures
- **经查属实 / 尚未证实是否属实** — verified truth vs unresolved verification
- Positive vs negative attribution:
  - **这是真的** vs **报道不实**
  - broader **X + 是/很 + Adj** vs **X + 不 + Adj / 不实 / 不属实**
- Dynamic restriction:
  - ordinary change-of-state works for **真实, 可信, 准确, 失实**
  - but **属实** resists ordinary *变得属实* and instead uses verification formulas

### he — Hebrew

- **אמין / מהימן** — colloquial/broad vs formal/source-oriented
- **אמיתי / אותנטי**

**Morphological contrasts**

**Constructional contrasts**

- **תואם את העובדות / אינו תואם למציאות**

**F6-relevant asymmetry**
- No common general inchoative truth-domain pattern identified.

### el — Greek

- **ουσία / τύπος**
- **κατ’ ουσίαν / κατά γράμμα**
- **αυθεντικός / πλαστός**
- **γνήσιος / πλαστός**
- **γνησιότητα / αυθεντικότητα** vs **παραποίηση / πλαστογραφία / πλαστότητα**

- **πιστός / πιστότητα** vs **απαράλλακτος / κατά γράμμα**
- **παραποιημένος / πλαστός**
- **σωστός / ορθός**
- **αληθινός / αληθής**
- **ειλικρινής / ψέμα**

**Morphological contrasts**
- Positive vs negative attribution with **δεν είναι**
- Not many core lexicalized negative pairs in F5 beyond constructional negation

**Constructional contrasts**
- **λέω την αλήθεια / λέω ψέματα**

- **[NP] είναι + ADJ/N / [NP] δεν είναι + ADJ/N**

- **επί της ουσίας** vs procedural/peripheral matters

**F6-relevant asymmetry**
- Dynamic truth/falsity often uses result/discovery:
  - **αληθεύει**
  - **αποδεικνύεται ψευδής**
  - **αποδείχθηκε πλαστή**
- No common dedicated “become authentic/genuine/valid” pattern.

- **pantay / pantasqa** vs **llulla / llullasqa** — mistake/error vs lie/deception

**Constructional contrasts**
- **chiqaq simi / llulla simi**
- **allinmi / mana allin**
- **pantachiy / pantachisqa** — causative action vs resulting state
- **pantachiy / pantayman churay** — compact causative vs periphrastic causative
- **llullaq / llulla runa** — agentive vs descriptive person-label
- **chiqap / chiqaqta** — alternate adverbial truth-assertion forms

**F6-relevant asymmetry**
- Repeated absence of common “become true/false/wrong” expressions.
- Dynamicity is clearest in error-causation (**pantachiy**) and lying (**llullakuy**), not in truth-state change.

## 3. Patterns from F6 naturalness judgments

### A. Strongly natural across multiple languages

#### 1. **Static attribution and negated attribution**
Strong recurrent pattern.
- **zh**: 这是真的, 这个消息很可靠, 报道不实
- **he**: זה לא נכון, לא מדויק, לא אמין
- **el**: Αυτό είναι αληθές, Αυτό δεν είναι αληθές, Το έγγραφο είναι έγκυρο
- **en**: broad positive/negative attribution system
- **tl**: Totoo iyan, Hindi pa tiyak ang resulta, Tama ang sagot mo
- **sw**: Ni kweli, Si kweli, Hii ni sahihi
- **qu**: allinmi, mana allin

This is the most stable cross-language pattern.

#### 2. **Truth/falsehood as speech action**
Strong recurrent pattern.
- **he**: לומר את האמת / לספר שקר
- **sa**: सत्यं वद / मिथ्या वदति / असत्यं वदति
- **el**: λέω την αλήθεια / λέω ψέματα
- **en**: tell the truth
- **sw**: sema ukweli / alisema uongo
- **zh**: 如实回答 / 说实话 / 说真的
- **qu**: llullakuy, speech-specific simi phrases

#### 3. **Verification / proof / result-of-checking instead of simple “become true”**
Strong recurrent pattern.
- **zh**: 属实 / 经查属实 / 查证属实
- **he**: לגלות את האמת, להוציא את האמת לאור, בדיקת אמינות, מבחן האמת
- **sa**: प्रमाणेन सिद्धम्, प्रमाणसिद्ध
- **el**: αποδεικνύεται ψευδής, αποδείχθηκε πλαστή
- **en**: prove true, authenticate, validate
- **tl**: beripikahin, beripikadong
- **sw**: weaker, but credibility checking via kuaminika-family is closest

#### 4. **Specialized lexical verbs for falsification / correction / authentication**
Strong recurrent pattern.
- **zh**: 伪造, 发布虚假信息, 纠正错误
- **he**: להצדיק, להוכיח, להוציא את האמת לאור
- **el**: παραποιώ, πλαστογραφώ
- **en**: authenticate, validate, invalidate, correct
- **tl**: beripikahin, wastuhin
- **qu**: pantachiy

---

### B. Combinations consistently weak, awkward, marked, or absent

#### 1. **A single general “become true” or “become false” verb across the whole domain**
Strong recurrent restriction.
- Explicitly absent or not identified in **he, sw, qu**
- weak/marginal in **sa**
- only subdomain-specific in **el**
- distributed across different constructions in **en**
- concentrated in one family in **tl**
- restricted in **zh**

#### 2. **General causative “make X true / make X authentic / make X correct”**
Strong recurrent restriction.
- **zh**: often replaced by lexical verbs, not broad causative
- **he**: no general pattern; uses revelation/justification/testing verbs
- **sa**: no single common productive causative
- **el**: no common dedicated causative for authentic/valid/genuine
- **en**: no single general causative; uses domain-specific verbs
- **tl**: explicitly no common general causative
- **sw**: explicitly no common general causative
- **qu**: causative exists mainly for error, not truth

#### 3. **Dynamic inchoatives for authenticity/genuineness**
Moderate pattern of blocking.
- **he**: no common “become authentic”
- **el**: no common “become authentic/genuine”
- **en**: *become authentic/genuine* not strongly favored
- **tl**: no common dynamic “become genuine/fake”
- **sw**: no common dynamic “become fake/genuine”
- **qu**: no such pattern identified

#### 4. **Dynamic inchoatives for formal verification terms**
Single-language but clear:
- **zh 属实** resists *变得属实* and *让X属实*

---

### C. Recurrent patterns of restriction, substitution, or blocking

#### 1. **Verification/result constructions substitute for inchoatives**
Strong recurrent pattern.
Instead of “X becomes true,” languages often use:
- “X was verified”
- “X proved true”
- “truth came out”
- “it was shown false”
- “it was authenticated”

This is especially clear in **zh, he, sa, el, en, tl**.

#### 2. **Lexical verbs substitute for general causatives**
Strong recurrent pattern.
Instead of “make false / make valid / make authentic,” languages use:
- falsify / forge / authenticate / validate / correct / mislead
- 伪造 / πλαστογραφώ / authenticate / pantachiy / beripikahin

#### 3. **Some semantic domains support dynamic constructions better than others**
Strong recurrent pattern.
- **truth-confirmation** often supports dynamic forms
  - en *prove true, come true*
  - tl *magkatotoo, nagkatotoo*
  - zh with some adjectives and result formulas
- **credibility** sometimes supports dynamic/state-shift forms
  - zh 变得可信
  - sw inaaminika / haaminiki
  - he has evaluation/testing rather than inchoative
- **authenticity** often resists ordinary inchoatives
- **validity** often prefers institutional/procedural evaluation rather than ordinary “become” verbs

#### 4. **Negative states are often easier to lexicalize than positive transitions**
Moderate pattern.
- fake/forged/falsified terms are often strong and lexicalized
- positive “become genuine/authentic” is often weak or absent
- liar terms are often stronger than truth-teller terms

---

## 4. Cross-language asymmetries

### A. One side richly lexicalized, the other analytic or weak

#### 1. **Liar vs truth-teller**
Strong recurrent pattern.
- **en**: *liar* strong, *truth-teller* marginal
- **el**: ψεύτης strong, no equally common positive counterpart
- **he**: no common productive positive agentive in F4
- **qu**: llullaq / llulla runa strong; positive counterpart weaker
- **zh**: 说真话的人 paraphrastic; negative side more compact in some domains

#### 2. **Fake/forged side richer than “become genuine” side**
Strong recurrent pattern.
Many languages have rich lexicalization for:
- fake
- forged
- falsified
- counterfeit
But weak or absent ordinary dynamic expressions for:
- become genuine
- become authentic

Seen in **he, el, en, tl, sw, qu**, and partly **zh**.

#### 3. **Formal verification richer than ordinary inchoative truth**
Strong recurrent pattern.
- **zh 属实** family is highly specialized
- **sa प्रमाण** family is highly elaborated
- **en** has *prove true / authenticate / validate*
- **tl** has *beripikahin / beripikado*
- **el** has result/discovery patterns

---

### B. Productive derivation in some contrasts, weak derivation in others

#### 1. **Negation is productive; positive dynamic derivation often is not**
Strong recurrent pattern.
- Many languages productively form or use negatives:
  - zh 不实, 不可信
  - he לא נכון, לא אמין
  - sa असत्य, अयथार्थ
  - en untrue, invalid, unreliable
  - sw si kweli, si sahihi
  - qu mana allin
- But productive “become true/authentic/correct” derivation is much less general.

#### 2. **Error-causation may be productive where truth-causation is not**
Moderate pattern.
- **qu**: pantachiy strong
- **sa**: causative/proof family stronger than truth-inchoatives
- **en**: invalidate / mislead / correct / authenticate stronger than “make true”
- **zh**: 纠正错误, 伪造 stronger than broad truth-causatives

---

### C. Semantic domains resisting construction types natural elsewhere

#### 1. **Verification adjectives resisting ordinary change-of-state**
Single-language observation, but very clear:
- **zh 属实** resists ordinary inchoative/causative patterns.

#### 2. **Authenticity resisting ordinary inchoatives**
Moderate pattern.
- Natural in some languages to say something “proved fake/forged”
- less natural to say it “became authentic/genuine”
- especially blocked in **he, el, en, tl, sw**

#### 3. **Correctness often lacks dedicated dynamic verbs**
Moderate pattern.
- **he**: no common general inchoative
- **el**: no clearly common specialized dynamic expression beyond ordinary γίνεται
- **sw**: static **sahihi** only
- **qu**: no common “become right/correct”
- **tl**: only limited **wastuhin**
- **zh/en** are stronger here because they have correction/result verbs

#### 4. **Truth vs credibility asymmetry**
Moderate pattern.
Some languages allow dynamic credibility more readily than dynamic truth:
- **sw**: kuaminika-family is clearer than any “become true” pattern
- **he**: testing/reliability constructions are clearer than inchoative truth
- **zh**: 可信 is easier dynamically than 属实

---

## Final synthesis

### Strong recurrent patterns
- Basic **lexical truth vs falsity** contrasts are robust across languages.
- **Morphological or analytic negation** is widely available.
- **Constructional speech contrasts** (“tell the truth” vs “lie”) recur strongly.
- **Verification/result constructions** recur across many languages as substitutes for broad inchoatives.
- **Lexical verbs for falsification, correction, authentication, validation, or misleading** are common cross-linguistically.

### Moderate patterns
- Dynamic truth-state expressions exist, but are usually **restricted to specific lexical families or subdomains**.
- Authenticity and validity often have **strong stative vocabularies** but **weak ordinary inchoatives**.
- Credibility sometimes supports dynamic/state-shift constructions more readily than truth proper.

### Single-language observations
- **zh 属实** strongly resists ordinary “become/cause to become” patterns.
- **tl** has a distinctive seriousness shift in the **totoo** family.
- **qu** shows especially strong asymmetry toward error-causation rather than truth-state change.

Overall, the F5 and F6 data support a cross-language picture in which **opposition is usually easy to lexicalize**, but **dynamic transition is often uneven, blocked, or rerouted through verification, correction, falsification, or evidential constructions rather than a single general “become true/false” system**.
