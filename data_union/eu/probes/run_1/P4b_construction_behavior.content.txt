# Language: Basque (eu)

## CONSTRUCTION USAGE TABLE

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness Rating | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. ATTRIBUTION** | *Egia da* | N/A | "It is true." | [CORE] | Ubiquitous in everyday speech as a bare propositional evaluation. No register restriction; functions as a complete utterance. |
| **1. ATTRIBUTION** | *Gezurra da* | N/A | "It is a lie / It is false." | [CORE] | Equally common as the negative pole of truth attribution. Functions as a complete utterance in conversational and formal registers alike. |
| **2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION** | *Ez da egia* | N/A | "It is not true." | [CORE] | Standard negation of propositional truth; preverbal *ez* is obligatory and not subject to variation in this function. |
| **2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION** | *Ziurra ez da* | N/A | "It is not certain." | [MARGINAL] | The postposed-predicate word order (*ziurra ez da*) is attested but less frequent than the canonical *ez da ziurra*; the reduced form *ziurra ez* is further restricted to informal or elliptical contexts. |
| **3. ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION** | *Egiazko lekukotasuna* | N/A | "True / genuine testimony." | [CORE] | Standard in formal, legal, and written registers; the *-zko* relational adjective is the expected adnominal modifier in these domains. |
| **3. ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION** | *Gezurrezko agiria* | N/A | "A false / forged document." | [CORE] | Common in legal and administrative contexts; *gezurrezko* is the regular adnominal form of *gezur*. No attested variation in the modifier-precedes-head order. |
| **4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE** | *Zintzotu* | N/A | "To become honest; to reform." | [CORE] | Used in moral and characterological contexts; intransitive use with *da* auxiliary yields the inchoative reading without ambiguity in context. |
| **4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE** | *Okertu* | N/A | "To go wrong; to become distorted; to err." | [CORE] | Broadly attested across moral, physical, and epistemic senses; the range of meaning is wider than *zintzotu* and extends beyond the strictly moral domain. |
| **5. CAUSATIVE** | *Gezurra sinetsarazi* | N/A | "To make someone believe a lie; to get someone to believe a falsehood." | [CORE] | The *-arazi* productive causative is the expected form when the causing of a belief state is expressed; common in narrative and argumentative registers. |
| **5. CAUSATIVE** | *Egiaztatu* | N/A | "To cause to be verified; to have [something] confirmed." | [CORE] | Standard in administrative, legal, and journalistic registers; the implicit causative reading is institutionally established rather than compositionally derived in active use. |
| **6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION** | *Ziurtasun* | N/A | "Certainty; sureness; assurance." | [CORE] | Highly productive and unrestricted across registers; *-tasun* nominals in this domain are fully lexicalized and function as ordinary abstract nouns. |
| **6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION** | *Zintzotasun* | N/A | "Honesty; uprightness; sincerity." | [CORE] | Common in moral discourse, educational, and religious registers; no observable restriction on use. |
| **7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION** | *Zuzentzaile* | N/A | "Corrector; editor; proofreader." | [CORE] | Standard professional and occupational label; fully lexicalized in publishing and educational contexts. |
| **7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION** | *Gezurti* | N/A | "Liar; lying person." | [CORE] | Common and idiomatic as a characterizing nominal; the *-ti* suffix in this instance is fully lexicalized and carries a clear pejorative moral evaluation. |
| **8. TRANSLATIVE CONSTRUCTION** | *Egiazkotzat jo* | N/A | "To consider as true; to take as genuine." | [CORE] | Characteristic of legal, administrative, and formal written registers; marks attribution as institutional judgment rather than direct assertion. |
| **8. TRANSLATIVE CONSTRUCTION** | *Baliozkotzat jo* | N/A | "To consider as valid; to regard as legitimate." | [CORE] | Parallel to *egiazkotzat jo* in distribution and register; both are productive with *jo*, *hartu*, and *onartu* as the evaluative verb. |
| **9. EPISTEMIC HEDGE** | *Ziurrenik etorriko da* | N/A | "He/she will probably come; most likely he/she will come." | [CORE] | Common across spoken and written registers as a sentence-level probability marker; the semantic shift from "certainly" to "probably" is fully established and not marked as unusual. |
| **9. EPISTEMIC HEDGE** | *Antza denez, egia da* | N/A | "Apparently it is true; it would seem to be true." | [CORE] | Attested in both spoken and written registers; *antza denez* and *antza du* function as parenthetical evidential frames and are not restricted to formal contexts. |
| **10. PEJORATIVE PRACTICE NOMINALIZATION** | *Gezurkeria* | N/A | "Lying; deceitfulness; dishonesty as a habitual practice." | [CORE] | Common in moral evaluation contexts; the pejorative and habitual reading is inherent to the suffix and not dependent on context for disambiguation. |
| **10. PEJORATIVE PRACTICE NOMINALIZATION** | *Itxurakeria* | N/A | "Pretense; affectation; hypocrisy; keeping up appearances." | [CORE] | Attested in everyday and literary registers; the range of meaning is broader than *gezurkeria* and covers social performance as well as deliberate deception. |

## ALTERNATE CONSTRUCTIONS

| Construction Type | Alternate Form | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION** | *Ez da ziurra* | Canonical word order alternative to *ziurra ez da*; more frequent than the postposed-predicate variant. |
| **2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION** | *Ziurra ez* (reduced) | Elliptical form; restricted to informal or discourse-internal contexts where the copula is recoverable. |
| **4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE** | *Bihurtu* (periphrastic) | Periphrastic inchoative with *bihurtu* ("to become") is attested as an alternate to *-tu* intransitive; noted in P4a but not exemplified with a truth-domain item. |
| **5. CAUSATIVE** | *-tu* transitive (e.g., *egiaztatu*, *ziurtatu*, *zuzendu*) | Alternate to *-arazi* productive causative; these forms are lexicalized rather than productively derived and carry implicit rather than compositional causative meaning. |
| **6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION** | *Egiaztapen*, *ziurtapen* (*-pen* result nominals) | Secondary nominalization pattern on verbal stems; yields result nominals rather than quality nominals; attested in formal and administrative registers. |
| **6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION** | *Egiaztatze*, *ziurtatze* (*-tze* process nominals) | Process nominalization pattern; distinct from *-tasun* (quality) and *-pen* (result); attested but not the primary pattern in this domain. |
| **7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION** | *Egia esaten duena* (periphrastic) | Periphrastic construction used in place of an unattested *-tzaile* "truth-teller" form; noted in P4a as the expected substitute for a lexical gap. |
| **8. TRANSLATIVE CONSTRUCTION** | *-tzat hartu*, *-tzat onartu* | Alternate evaluative verbs in the translative construction; parallel in structure and register to *-tzat jo*. |

## SUMMARY OBSERVATIONS

- All ten constructions identified in P4a receive a **[CORE]** rating with the exception of the postposed-predicate variant in Construction 2 (*ziurra ez da*), which is rated **[MARGINAL]** due to its lower frequency relative to canonical word order.
- No construction identified in P4a has been rated **[UNCERTAIN]**; all have sufficient attestation to support a usage characterization.
- Register variation is the primary axis of differentiation: the translative construction (8) and relational adnominal modification (3) are concentrated in formal and legal registers, while attribution (1), negative attribution (2), and pejorative nominalization (10) are unrestricted across registers.
- No new constructions have been introduced in this step; all alternates listed above are drawn from P4a notes.
