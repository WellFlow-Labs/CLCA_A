1. **Term A:** òtítọ́  
   **Term B:** irọ́  
   **Transliteration:** òtítọ́ ~ irọ́  
   **English translation:** truth ~ lie / falsehood  
   **Description of contrast:** The context explicitly identifies *irọ́* as one of the basic opposites of *òtítọ́* in ordinary discourse. This is the clearest lexical truth-vs-falsehood contrast in the data.  
   **Contrast type:** lexical  
   **Epistemic status:** [CORE]
