# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P4b Construction Usage & Constraints
# Language: Chinese (zh)
# Source: P4a output

## Construction 1: ATTRIBUTION

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Epistemic Status | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Subject + 是 + truth/authenticity adjective + 的 | 这是真的。 | Zhè shì zhēn de. | "This is true / genuine." | [CORE] | Default predicative pattern across all registers; applies to the full truth-domain vocabulary (真, 假, 正确, 错误, 可靠, 客观, etc.). Degree adverbs (很, 完全, 根本) insert before the adjective. |
| Subject + 是 + truth/authenticity adjective + 的 | 这份报告是虚假的。 | Zhè fèn bàogào shì xūjiǎ de. | "This report is false / fraudulent." | [CORE] | More formal register than the 这是真的 example; 虚假 is characteristic of written, administrative, and journalistic contexts. No restriction on noun type as subject. |

**Attested alternates:** Direct adjectival predication without 是…的 is possible for some adjectives (e.g., 这报告很虚假), but the 是…的 frame is the dominant pattern for categorical truth-value attribution.

## Construction 2: NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Epistemic Status | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Subject + 不是 + truth/authenticity adjective + 的 | 这不是真的。 | Zhè bú shì zhēn de. | "This is not true / not genuine." | [CORE] | Emphatic or contrastive negation; commonly used in spoken and written registers when contradicting a prior claim. More pragmatically marked than direct negation. |
| Subject + 不 + adjective | 他的判断不准确。 | Tā de pànduàn bù zhǔnquè. | "His judgment is not accurate." | [CORE] | Neutral negation; more common in analytical and evaluative prose. Applies productively across gradable truth-domain adjectives. 没有 is not used for property negation in this domain. |

**Attested alternates:** Both patterns (不是…的 and 不 + adjective) are independently productive; choice reflects pragmatic emphasis rather than grammatical restriction.

## Construction 3: ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Epistemic Status | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Truth/authenticity adjective + 的 + Noun | 可靠的证据 | kěkào de zhèngjù | "reliable evidence" | [CORE] | Highly productive across the full vocabulary; attested with 真实的报道, 虚假的信息, 正确的方法, 准确的描述, 道地的北京话. Standard pattern in all registers. |
| Truth/authenticity adjective + 的 + Noun | 客观的评价 | kèguān de píngjià | "an objective evaluation" | [CORE] | Same pattern; 客观 is characteristic of formal, analytical, and academic registers. No register restriction on the construction itself. |

**Attested alternates:** Monosyllabic roots (真, 假) appear without 的 in tight compounds (真相, 假话) but require 的 as free attributive modifiers. The compound and free-modifier uses are in complementary distribution by lexical context, not free variation.

## Construction 4: BECOME / INCHOATIVE

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Epistemic Status | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 变得 + adjective | 情况变得更加复杂，真相变得难以查明。 | Qíngkuàng biàn de gèngjiā fùzá, zhēnxiàng biàn de nányǐ chámíng. | "The situation became more complex; the truth became harder to establish." | [CORE] | Applies to gradable truth-domain adjectives (准确, 可靠, 客观); semantically odd with categorical 真/假, which do not readily admit of becoming. Characteristic of narrative and analytical registers. |
| Lexicalized inchoative: 弄假成真 | 弄假成真 | nòng jiǎ chéng zhēn | "What began as pretense became real." | [CORE] | Fixed four-character expression; encodes the specific inchoative meaning of a false state transitioning to a genuine one. Not decomposable as a productive construction. |
| Lexicalized inchoative: 假戏真做 | 假戏真做 | jiǎ xì zhēn zuò | (fiction/pretense enacted as real) | [CORE] | Fixed four-character expression; parallel to 弄假成真 in encoding pretense-to-reality transition. No productive morphological inchoative exists for this domain. |

**Attested alternates:** No productive morphological inchoative is attested for this domain. 变得 is the only productive periphrastic inchoative; 弄假成真 and 假戏真做 are lexicalized and not generative.

## Construction 5: CAUSATIVE

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Epistemic Status | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 使/让 + NP + adjective/verb | 这种做法使报道失真。 | Zhè zhǒng zuòfǎ shǐ bàodào shīzhēn. | "This practice causes the reporting to become distorted." | [CORE] | Productive periphrastic causative; 使 is more formal than 让 in this domain. Characteristic of critical, analytical, and regulatory registers (使数据失真, 让判断更客观). |
| Lexicalized causative: 以假乱真 | 以假乱真 | yǐ jiǎ luàn zhēn | "using fakes to confuse [what is] genuine" | [CORE] | Fixed four-character expression encoding causative meaning lexically; not a productive open construction. Attested in contexts of deliberate deception and fraud. |
| Resultative complement: verb + 真/实 | 逼真 | bīzhēn | "lifelike" (approach + genuine) | [MARGINAL] | Attested in 逼真 and 属实 but not a productive open construction in this domain. These are better treated as lexicalized compounds than as instances of a generative pattern. |

**Attested alternates:** No dedicated morphological causative exists. The periphrastic 使/让 pattern is the only productive causative construction; 以假乱真 and the resultative complement pattern (逼真, 属实) are lexicalized and not generative.

## Construction 6: ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Epistemic Status | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Adjective/verb base + 性 | 新闻报道的真实性 | xīnwén bàodào de zhēnshíxìng | "the truthfulness / authenticity of news reporting" | [CORE] | Most productive abstract nominalizer in this domain; attested with 真实性, 正确性, 准确性, 可靠性, 客观性, 主观性, 实质性, 真诚性, 诚实性, 虚假性. Characteristic of formal, academic, and regulatory registers. Does not attach to nouns like 真相 or 事实. |
| Adjective/verb base + 上 | 本质上是一样的 | běnzhìshàng shì yīyàng de | "essentially the same" | [CORE] | Applied to 本质 and 实质, produces highly frequent adverbial phrases functioning as discourse markers (see also Construction 10). Distinct from 性-nominalization in syntactic function: 上-forms are adverbials, not noun-phrase heads. |
| Adjective/verb base + 度 | 失真度 | shīzhēndù | "degree of distortion" | [MARGINAL] | Attested only in the technical term 失真度; not a productive open construction in this domain. Restricted to technical and measurement contexts. |

**Attested alternates:** 性 is the dominant nominalizer for evaluative adjectives in this domain. 度 is attested only in 失真度 and should not be treated as productive here. 上-forms are adverbial, not nominal, and overlap with Construction 10.

## Construction 7: AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Epistemic Status | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Verb/adjective base + 者 | 伪善者 | wěishànzhě | "a hypocrite; one who practices false virtue" | [CORE] | Productive agentive suffix in Chinese; in this domain attaches predominantly to negative-pole bases (伪善者, 造假者, 作假者). Characteristic of formal, written, and critical registers. |
| Verb/adjective base + 者 | 造假者 | zàojiǎzhě | "a fabricator; one who manufactures fakes or falsifies data" | [CORE] | Same pattern; 造假者 is frequent in journalistic, legal, and regulatory contexts. The asymmetry — agentives for falsifiers but not for truth-tellers via 者-suffixation — is supported by the data. |

**Attested alternates:** A positive-pole agentive ("truth-teller") is not lexicalized via 者-suffixation. Nearest equivalents are periphrastic: 说真话的人, 如实陈述的人, or the compound 诚实的人. These are not 者-suffixed forms and represent a distinct construction type.

## Construction 8: PROHIBITIVE / NORMATIVE CONSTRUCTION

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Epistemic Status | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 严禁 + truth-domain verb phrase | 严禁弄虚作假。 | Yánjìn nòng xū zuò jiǎ. | "It is strictly forbidden to engage in fraud and falsification." | [CORE] | Restricted to the deliberate-falsification cluster (弄虚作假, 造假, 作假, 伪造); does not appear with truth-evaluation adjectives or authenticity nouns. Characteristic of regulatory, administrative, and anti-corruption discourse. |
| 不得 + truth-domain verb phrase | 不得造假。 | Bùdé zào jiǎ. | "Fabrication is not permitted." | [CORE] | Same domain restriction as 严禁; 不得 is slightly less emphatic and more broadly used in formal written regulations. Both 严禁 and 不得 are restricted to the falsification sub-domain and do not extend to truth-evaluation adjectives (正确, 真实) or authenticity nouns (真相, 事实). |

**Attested alternates:** No alternate constructions attested for this prohibitive/normative function within the falsification sub-domain. The restriction to deliberate-falsification vocabulary is a stable feature of this construction type.

## Construction 9: VERB + TRUTH-NOUN COLLOCATION (INVESTIGATIVE / EPISTEMIC)

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Epistemic Status | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Epistemic/investigative verb + 真相/事实/真伪 (revealing set) | 揭露真相 | jiēlù zhēnxiàng | "to expose the truth / reveal the true state of affairs" | [CORE] | Most productive construction type in the domain. Revealing/establishing verbs (揭露, 查明, 还原, 尊重) collocate with 真相, 事实, or 是非 as object. Stable across legal, journalistic, and political registers. |
| Epistemic/investigative verb + 真相/事实/真伪 (concealing/distorting set) | 歪曲事实 | wāiqū shìshí | "to distort the facts / misrepresent the truth" | [CORE] | Concealing/distorting verbs (掩盖, 歪曲, 颠倒, 混淆) collocate with the same set of truth-nouns. Contrastive pairing with the revealing set (揭露真相 / 掩盖真相; 尊重事实 / 歪曲事实) is a stable discourse pattern. |

**Attested alternates:** Contrastive pairing of one verb from each semantic set is a stable discourse pattern; this is not a separate construction but a collocational tendency within the same construction type.

## Construction 10: DISCOURSE MARKER (FRAME-SETTING ADVERBIAL)

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Epistemic Status | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Truth/reality noun + 上 → sentence-initial adverbial | 事实上，情况并非如此。 | Shìshíshàng, qíngkuàng bìng fēi rúcǐ. | "In fact, the situation is not like that." | [CORE] | Among the most frequent discourse-framing devices in Chinese across all registers. Functions to introduce a correction or clarification of a prior claim. Syntactically an adverbial, not a noun-phrase head (distinct from 性-nominalization in §6). |
| Truth/reality noun + 上 → sentence-initial adverbial | 本质上，这两个问题是一样的。 | Běnzhìshàng, zhè liǎng gè wèntí shì yīyàng de. | "Essentially, these two questions are the same." | [CORE] | 本质上 and 实质上 function as discourse markers introducing a claim about underlying reality. Attested across formal, analytical, and journalistic registers. Distinct from the locative-adverbial nominalization use noted in §6 in that these forms function as sentence-level adverbials rather than as modifiers within a noun phrase. |

**Attested alternates:** No alternate constructions attested for this discourse-marker function. The 上-forms (事实上, 本质上, 实质上) are the attested set; no other truth-domain nouns are documented in this construction in the P4a data.
