# Patent Notice

The discovery method, findings, reference materials, and data in this repository are
released openly under the licenses in [`README.md`](README.md) (code: Apache 2.0;
data, prompts, and documents: CC-BY-4.0).

Separately, the author has filed U.S. **provisional patent applications** on
**computational systems and methods that apply the semantic constraint architecture**
described in this work. They cover:

- **tagging, validating, and enforcing** the semantic constraints in software and AI
  systems, including runtime constraint tagging, substrate-level enforcement, and
  reflective-stability mechanisms;
- **marking data records with their epistemic standing**, such as whether a value is a
  measurement, a prediction, a report, or a conjecture, carrying that mark through
  computation, and gating actions on it; and
- **machine inquiry, repair, and revalidation**: systems that turn an unmet condition on
  an inference or action into a question to pursue, repair what the answer licenses,
  revalidate the conclusions that depended on it, and gate action on the result.
