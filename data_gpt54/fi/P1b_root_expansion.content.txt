## Step 1 — Central roots

### 1. **oike- / oikea**
- Transliteration: —
- Participates in P1 items: **5**
  - **oikea, oikein, oikeus, oikeudenmukainen, lainmukainen** (indirectly not from the same root, so not counted)
- Core meaning contribution: correctness, rightness, propriety, legitimacy; in some uses also entitlement and justice.

### 2. **tod-/tos-/tot-** (the **tosi / totta / tod-** family)
- Transliteration: —
- Participates in P1 items: **5**
  - **totta, tosi, totuus, todellinen, todellisuus**
- Core meaning contribution: truth, reality, actuality, correspondence with what is real or the case.

### 3. **aito-**
- Transliteration: —
- Participates in P1 items: **3**
  - **aito, aidosti, epäaito**
- Core meaning contribution: genuineness, authenticity, non-imitation, sincerity.

## Root: **oike- / oikea**

This root is highly productive. Below are the **most common 20–30 expressions**; additional forms exist.

### 1. **oikea**
- Transliteration: —
- Literal meaning / morphology: basic adjective ‘right, correct, proper’
- Natural English translation: right, correct, proper; sometimes real/genuine
- Context of use: Very common in everyday speech, education, administration, and moral evaluation. Used for correct answers, proper procedures, suitable choices, and sometimes the “real” or intended person/thing.
- Epistemic status: **[CORE]**

### 2. **oikein**
- Transliteration: —
- Literal meaning / morphology: adverb from **oikea**
- Natural English translation: correctly, properly, right
- Context of use: Common in instructions, assessment, and practical evaluation. Also frequent in spoken Finnish as an intensifier in some contexts.
- Epistemic status: **[CORE]**

### 3. **oikeus**
- Transliteration: —
- Literal meaning / morphology: **oikea** + **-us**
- Natural English translation: right; justice; law court
- Context of use: Central in legal, moral, and political language. Depending on context it refers to entitlement, justice, or the judicial institution.
- Epistemic status: **[CORE]**

### 4. **oikeudenmukainen**
- Transliteration: —
- Literal meaning / morphology: **oikeuden** ‘of justice’ + **mukainen** ‘in accordance with’
- Natural English translation: just, fair
- Context of use: Used for treatment, decisions, laws, and social arrangements. Common in public debate, education, ethics, and law.
- Epistemic status: **[CORE]**

### 5. **oikeudenmukaisuus**
- Transliteration: —
- Literal meaning / morphology: **oikeudenmukainen** + **-uus**
- Natural English translation: justice, fairness
- Context of use: Standard abstract noun in moral, political, legal, and philosophical discussion. Used for both institutional justice and interpersonal fairness.
- Epistemic status: **[CORE]**

### 6. **oikeudellinen**
- Transliteration: —
- Literal meaning / morphology: **oikeus** + **-llinen**
- Natural English translation: legal, juridical
- Context of use: Very common in legal and administrative language. Refers to legal status, legal consequences, legal interpretation, and juridical frameworks.
- Epistemic status: **[CORE]**

### 7. **oikeudellisesti**
- Transliteration: —
- Literal meaning / morphology: **oikeudellinen** + **-sti**

## Root: **tod-/tos-/tot-** (**tosi / totta / tod-** family)

This root family is highly productive. Below are the **most common 20–30 expressions**; additional forms exist.

### 1. **tosi**
- Transliteration: —
- Literal meaning / morphology: basic adjective ‘true, real’
- Natural English translation: true, real
- Context of use: Common in everyday speech and standard Finnish. Used for truth, authenticity, and in colloquial speech also as an intensifier.
- Epistemic status: **[CORE]**

### 2. **totta**
- Transliteration: —
- Literal meaning / morphology: predicate/adverbial form in the same family
- Natural English translation: true; that’s true
- Context of use: Very common as a response or predicate in conversation and writing. Used to affirm that something matches reality.
- Epistemic status: **[CORE]**

### 3. **totuus**
- Transliteration: —
- Literal meaning / morphology: **tosi/totta** + **-uus**
- Natural English translation: truth
- Context of use: Standard noun in everyday, philosophical, moral, and legal contexts. Used for abstract truth and the truth of a matter.
- Epistemic status: **[CORE]**

### 4. **todellinen**
- Transliteration: —
- Literal meaning / morphology: **tod(e)-** + **-llinen**
- Natural English translation: real, actual
- Context of use: Used to distinguish what is actual from what is apparent, supposed, or fictional. Common in everyday, legal, and philosophical language.
- Epistemic status: **[CORE]**

### 5. **todellisuus**
- Transliteration: —
- Literal meaning / morphology: **todellinen** + **-uus**
- Natural English translation: reality
- Context of use: Standard noun for reality in general language and intellectual discourse. Often contrasted with illusion, image, or fiction.
- Epistemic status: **[CORE]**

### 6. **todellakin**
- Transliteration: —
- Literal meaning / morphology: **todella** + emphatic particle **-kin**
- Natural English translation: indeed, really
- Context of use: Common emphatic adverb in speech and writing. Used to confirm or strengthen a statement.
- Epistemic status: **[CORE]**

### 7. **todella**
- Transliteration: —
- Literal meaning / morphology: adverb from the same family
- Natural English translation: really, truly, very
- Context of use: Very common in everyday Finnish. Used both for genuine affirmation and as a degree intensifier.
- Epistemic status: **[CORE]**

### 8. **totisesti**
- Transliteration: —
- Literal meaning / morphology: **tosi/totta** + **-sti**
- Natural English translation: truly, verily, indeed
- Context of use: Literary, rhetorical, biblical, or elevated style. Less common in everyday speech.
- Epistemic status: **[MARGINAL]**

### 9. **totuudenmukainen**
- Transliteration: —
- Literal meaning / morphology: **totuuden** ‘of truth’ + **mukainen** ‘in accordance with’
- Natural English translation: truthful, true to fact
- Context of use: Used for descriptions, depictions, testimony, and representations that accord with truth. Common in journalism, criticism, and legal or historical discussion.
- Epistemic status: **[CORE]**

### 10. **totuudenmukaisuus**
- Transliteration: —
- Literal meaning / morphology: **totuudenmukainen** + **-uus**
- Natural English translation: truthfulness; factual accuracy
- Context of use: Used in criticism, historiography, law, and media analysis. Refers to the degree to which an account or depiction accords with truth.
- Epistemic status: **[CORE]**

### 11. **totuudenmukaisesti**
- Transliteration: —
- Literal meaning / morphology: **totuudenmukainen** + **-sti**
- Natural English translation: truthfully, in accordance with the facts
- Context of use: Used in formal writing and testimony-related contexts. Refers to presenting something in a factually faithful way.
- Epistemic status: **[CORE]**

### 12. **totuudenmukaisempi**
- Transliteration: —
- Literal meaning / morphology: comparative of **totuudenmukainen**
- Natural English translation: more truthful, more true to fact
- Context of use: Common enough in criticism and comparison, but this is a regular inflectional/comparative form rather than a separate lexical item.

### 13. **totuudenrakkaus**

- Context of use: Attested in philosophical, theological, and literary language, but less common than **totuudenmukainen**. Often used in elevated or specialized prose.

### 15. **totuudellisuus**

## Root: **aito-**

This root has **fewer than 5 very common core derivatives**, but a broader set of established compounds and expressions exists.

### 1. **aito**
- Transliteration: —
- Literal meaning / morphology: basic adjective ‘genuine, authentic’
- Natural English translation: genuine, authentic
- Context of use: Very common for materials, emotions, style, and persons. Used to distinguish the genuine from the fake, artificial, or insincere.
- Epistemic status: **[CORE]**

### 2. **aidosti**
- Transliteration: —
- Literal meaning / morphology: **aito** + **-sti**
- Natural English translation: genuinely, authentically
- Context of use: Common in speech and writing for sincerity, authenticity, and non-pretended feeling or interest. Also used in branding and cultural commentary.
- Epistemic status: **[CORE]**

### 3. **aitous**
- Transliteration: —
- Literal meaning / morphology: **aito** + **-us**
- Natural English translation: authenticity, genuineness
- Context of use: Standard abstract noun in cultural criticism, marketing, psychology, and everyday evaluation. Refers to the quality of being genuine.
- Epistemic status: **[CORE]**

### 4. **epäaito**
- Transliteration: —
- Literal meaning / morphology: **epä-** ‘un-, non-’ + **aito**
- Natural English translation: inauthentic, fake, ungenuine
- Context of use: Used for expressions, performances, styles, and behavior judged not genuine. Common in criticism and psychology, though less frequent than **aito**.
- Epistemic status: **[CORE]**

### 5. **epäaidosti**
- Transliteration: —
- Literal meaning / morphology: **epäaito** + **-sti**
