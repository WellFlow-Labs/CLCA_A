## 1. TRUTH / FACT BECOMES REVEALED
### Pattern
**진실/사실/실체 + 이/가 드러나다**
### Examples
- 진실이 드러나다. → *jinsiri deureonada* → “The truth comes out.”
- 실체가 드러나다. → *silchega deureonada* → “The true nature is revealed.”
### Notes
Korean very naturally encodes dynamic truth not as “become true” but as **becoming revealed**. With **진실**, **사실**, and especially **실체**, the common dynamic is emergence into view. This is central in journalism, investigation, and public controversy.

---

## 2. FACTS / TRUTH GET CLARIFIED
### Pattern
**진실/사실/실체 + 을/를 밝히다**
### Examples
- 진실을 밝히다. → *jinsireul balkhida* → “Reveal the truth.”
- 사실을 밝히다. → *sasireul balkhida* → “Clarify/reveal the facts.”
### Notes
This is a major causative pattern. Korean often uses **밝히다** rather than a dedicated verb meaning “make true.” The causation is epistemic: someone causes hidden truth or facts to become known.

---

## 3. FACTS GET CHECKED / VERIFIED
### Pattern
**사실/정품 여부 + 를 확인하다**
### Examples
- 사실을 확인하다. → *sasireul hwaginhada* → “Confirm the facts.”
- 정품 여부를 확인하다. → *jeongpum yeobureul hwaginhada* → “Check whether an item is genuine.”
### Notes
Another common dynamic is procedural verification. Korean often encodes truth-related change through **확인하다**: not “make X true,” but “establish whether X is true/genuine.”

---

## 4. FALSEHOOD BECOMES APPARENT
### Pattern
**거짓/허위 + 으로 드러나다**
### Examples
- 그 주장은 거짓으로 드러났다. → *geu jujangeun geojiseuro deureonatda* → “That claim turned out to be false.”
- 허위로 드러나다. → *heowiro deureonada* → “Turn out to be false.”
### Notes
For movement into a non-truth state in discourse, Korean commonly uses **드러나다** with a result phrase: something is shown to be **거짓** or **허위**. This is revelation of falsity, not necessarily a real change in the thing itself.

---

## 5. VALIDITY IS LOST
### Pattern
**무효 + 가 되다**
### Examples
- 계약이 무효가 되었다. → *gyeyagi muhyoga doeeotda* → “The contract became invalid.”
- 무효가 되다. → *muhyoga doeda* → “Become invalid.”
### Notes
This is one of the clearest “become X” patterns in the dataset. In legal and institutional language, Korean directly uses **N이/가 되다** with **무효** for loss of validity.

---

## 6. SOMETHING IS MADE INVALID
### Pattern
**N + 을/를 무효로 하다**
### Examples
- 결정을 무효로 했다. → *gyeoljeongeul muhyoro haetda* → “They invalidated the decision.”
- 무효로 하다. → *muhyoro hada* → “Invalidate; render null.”
### Notes
This is the corresponding causative pattern for validity. Korean uses a periphrastic construction with **무효로 하다**, not a special single verb dedicated to this domain.

---

## 7. SOMETHING REMAINS VALID
### Pattern
**아직 유효하다**
### Examples
- 아직 유효하다. → *ajik yuhyohada* → “Still be valid.”
- 유효한 계약. → *yuhyohan gyeyak* → “a valid contract”
### Notes
For stability rather than change, **유효하다** is important. The dataset gives clear evidence for continued validity with **아직 유효하다**.  
A common “become valid” expression is **not clearly provided in the context**, so it should not be assumed here.

## 8. TRUTH IS HIDDEN VS REVEALED
### Pattern
**진실 + 을/를 숨기다 / 밝히다**
### Examples
- 진실을 숨기다. → *jinsireul sumgida* → “Hide the truth.”
- 진실을 밝히다. → *jinsireul balkhida* → “Reveal the truth.”
### Notes
This is a strong dynamic opposition. Korean often frames truth in terms of concealment and disclosure rather than simple state change.

---

## 9. FACTUAL STATUS IS ASSERTED OR DENIED
### Pattern
**~는 것은 사실이다 / 사실이 아니다 / 사실과 다르다**
### Examples
- 그가 떠난 것은 사실이다. → *geuga tteonan geoseun sasirida* → “It is true that he left.”
- 그 말은 사실이 아니다. → *geu mareun sasiri anida* → “That statement is not true.”
### Notes
These are not change-of-state expressions, but they are central dynamic frames in discourse because they assign or reject factual status. **사실과 다르다** adds a mismatch reading rather than simple negation.

---

## 10. AUTHENTICITY IS CHECKED, CERTIFIED, OR DENIED
### Pattern
**정품 여부를 확인하다 / 정품 인증을 받다 / 정품이 아니다**
### Examples
- 정품 여부를 확인하다. → *jeongpum yeobureul hwaginhada* → “Check whether an item is genuine.”
- 정품 인증을 받다. → *jeongpum injeungeul batda* → “Receive authenticity certification.”
### Notes
In product-authenticity contexts, Korean often encodes dynamics through institutional actions: checking, certifying, denying. This is different from everyday **진짜/가짜**, which is more impressionistic.

---

## 11. APPEARANCE SHIFTS TO REVEALED REALITY
### Pattern
**진짜/가짜 같다, ~처럼 보이다 ↔ 실체가 드러나다**
### Examples
- 진짜처럼 보인다. → *jinjjacheoreom boinda* → “It looks real.”
- 그 조직의 실체가 드러났다. → *geu jojigui silchega deureonatda* → “The true nature of that organization was revealed.”
### Notes
A very important Korean dynamic is movement from **appearance** to **revealed reality**. The language often contrasts seeming authenticity with later exposure of **실체**.

---

## 12. ACCURACY IS IMPROVED OR TESTED
### Pattern
**정확성 + 을/를 높이다 / 검증하다**
### Examples
- 정확성을 높이다. → *jeonghwakseongeul nopida* → “Improve accuracy.”
- 정확성을 검증하다. → *jeonghwakseongeul geomjeunghada* → “Verify accuracy.”
### Notes
With **정확성**, the dynamic is not truth-vs-falsehood but degree and evaluation. Korean commonly encodes change here as raising accuracy, and causation as testing or verifying it.

---

## 13. RELIABILITY IS GAINED OR SECURED
### Pattern
**신뢰를 얻다 / 신뢰성을 확보하다**
### Examples
- 신뢰를 얻다. → *silloereul eotda* → “Gain trust.”
- 신뢰성을 확보하다. → *silloeseongeul hwakbohada* → “Secure reliability.”
### Notes
These concern trustworthiness rather than truth directly. Still, they are dynamic truth-adjacent expressions in the dataset: a person or system can come to be regarded as dependable.

---

## 14. SINCERITY / AUTHENTICITY IS FELT OR DOUBTED
### Pattern
**진실성/진정성 + 이/가 느껴지다 / 을/를 의심하다 / 인정하다**
### Examples
- 진실성이 느껴지다. → *jinsilseongi neukkyeojida* → “Sincerity/authenticity can be felt.”
- 진실성을 의심하다. → *jinsilseongeul uisimhada* → “Doubt someone’s sincerity/authenticity.”
### Notes
For **진실성** and related sincerity vocabulary, Korean often encodes dynamics as perception and evaluation, not literal state change. The issue is whether authenticity becomes perceptible, questioned, or acknowledged.

## 19. SUMMARY OF MAIN DYNAMIC STRATEGIES
### Pattern
**드러나다 / 밝히다 / 확인하다 / 무효가 되다 / 무효로 하다**
### Examples
- 진실이 드러나다. → *jinsiri deureonada* → “The truth comes out.”
- 결정을 무효로 했다. → *gyeoljeongeul muhyoro haetda* → “They invalidated the decision.”
### Notes
From the provided data, Korean encodes truth-related dynamics mainly through:
1. **revelation** — **드러나다**
2. **disclosure** — **밝히다**
3. **verification** — **확인하다**
4. **institutional status change** — **무효가 되다 / 무효로 하다**
5. **perception/evaluation** — **느껴지다, 의심하다, 인정하다**

So the most natural dynamic grammar is often not “truth changes,” but “truth is revealed, checked, hidden, or institutionally recognized.”
