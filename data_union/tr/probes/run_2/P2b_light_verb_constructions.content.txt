# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2b Compositional Constructions
# Language: Turkish (tr)

---

> **Scope note:** This step documents attested multi-word constructions involving P1 lexical items. Derivational forms already covered in P2a are excluded. Constructions already cited as examples within P1 or P1b are included here where they constitute genuine multi-word units warranting documentation; they are cross-referenced accordingly. Absence of common constructions is recorded explicitly. Epistemic statuses reflect confidence in attestation and centrality.

---

## P1 Item 1 — doğru

**Base Form:** doğru

---

**Construction 1**
**Example:** doğruyu söylemek
**Transliteration:** doğruyu söylemek
**English Translation:** to tell the truth
**Usage Notes:** The canonical collocation for the act of making a true statement; *doğru* takes the accusative as a definite noun object ("the truth"). Used in moral exhortation, legal contexts, and everyday speech. Documented in P1b Root 1, item 15; included here as a core multi-word unit.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 2**
**Example:** doğru söylemek
**Transliteration:** doğru söylemek
**English Translation:** to say something correct, to tell the truth
**Usage Notes:** Differs from *doğruyu söylemek* in that *doğru* here functions as a predicative adverb rather than a definite noun object; the construction is slightly less emphatic. Both forms are in common use. Documented in P1b Root 1, item 14.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 3**
**Example:** doğru yanlış sorusu
**Transliteration:** doğru yanlış sorusu
**English Translation:** a true/false question
**Usage Notes:** A fixed nominal phrase used in educational contexts to describe a question format requiring a binary true-or-false judgment. The coordinate pair *doğru yanlış* functions as a compound modifier.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 4**
**Example:** doğruyu yanlıştan ayırt etmek
**Transliteration:** doğruyu yanlıştan ayırt etmek
**English Translation:** to distinguish the true from the false, to tell right from wrong
**Usage Notes:** A standard collocation in moral, logical, and educational discourse for the capacity to discriminate between what is correct and what is not. The construction uses the ablative of separation (*yanlıştan*) with *ayırt etmek* (to distinguish).
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 5**
**Example:** doğru yoldan sapmak
**Transliteration:** doğru yoldan sapmak
**English Translation:** to deviate from the right path, to go astray
**Usage Notes:** A fixed collocation used in moral and religious discourse to describe departure from correct conduct or belief. *Doğru yol* (the right path) is itself a stable nominal compound; *sapmak* (to deviate) is the standard verbal complement.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 6**
**Example:** doğru yolu bulmak
**Transliteration:** doğru yolu bulmak
**English Translation:** to find the right path, to find the correct way
**Usage Notes:** The positive counterpart to *doğru yoldan sapmak*; used in moral, religious, and practical contexts to describe finding the correct course of action or belief. Common in everyday and literary registers.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 7**
**Example:** doğruluk payı var
**Transliteration:** doğruluk payı var
**English Translation:** there is a grain of truth (in it)
**Usage Notes:** A fixed predicative construction used in everyday and journalistic discourse to acknowledge partial truth in a claim. The full form is typically *Bu iddiada bir doğruluk payı var* (there is a grain of truth in this claim). Documented in P1b Root 1, item 28.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 8**
**Example:** doğrudan doğruya söylemek
**Transliteration:** doğrudan doğruya söylemek
**English Translation:** to say it straight out, to say it directly and without intermediary
**Usage Notes:** A reinforced adverbial construction emphasizing complete directness; more emphatic than *doğrudan söylemek* alone. The reduplicated structure *doğrudan doğruya* is a fixed adverbial phrase. Documented in P1b Root 1, item 10.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 9**
**Example:** doğru bilgiye ulaşmak
**Transliteration:** doğru bilgiye ulaşmak
**English Translation:** to access correct information, to reach accurate knowledge
**Usage Notes:** Used in journalistic, educational, and philosophical contexts to describe the goal of obtaining accurate information. *Doğru bilgi* (correct information/true knowledge) is a stable nominal compound; *ulaşmak* (to reach) is the standard verbal complement in this construction.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 10**
**Example:** doğruluk beyanı
**Transliteration:** doğruluk beyanı
**English Translation:** declaration of accuracy, declaration of truthfulness
**Usage Notes:** A fixed nominal phrase used in administrative and legal contexts for a formal statement attesting to the accuracy of information provided. *Beyan* (declaration, from Arabic *bayān*) is the standard noun in this administrative collocation.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 11**
**Example:** doğrulama kodu
**Transliteration:** doğrulama kodu
**English Translation:** verification code, authentication code
**Usage Notes:** A fixed nominal phrase in digital security and administrative contexts for a code used to verify identity or confirm a transaction. *Doğrulama* (verification) + *kod* (code, from French/English) is a standard technical compound.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 12**
**Example:** kimlik doğrulama
**Transliteration:** kimlik doğrulama
**English Translation:** identity verification, authentication
**Usage Notes:** A fixed nominal compound used in digital security, administrative, and legal contexts. *Kimlik* (identity) + *doğrulama* (verification) is the standard Turkish term for authentication procedures. Documented in P1b Root 1, item 3.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 13**
**Example:** doğruluk tablosu oluşturmak
**Transliteration:** doğruluk tablosu oluşturmak
**English Translation:** to construct a truth table
**Usage Notes:** A standard collocation in formal logic and digital circuit design. *Doğruluk tablosu* (truth table) is a fixed technical compound; *oluşturmak* (to construct, to form) is the standard verbal complement. Documented in P1b Root 1, item 23.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 14**
**Example:** doğruluk değeri atamak
**Transliteration:** doğruluk değeri atamak
**English Translation:** to assign a truth value
**Usage Notes:** A technical collocation in formal logic and philosophy of language. *Doğruluk değeri* (truth value) is a fixed compound; *atamak* (to assign) is the standard verbal complement in logical and computational contexts.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 15**
**Example:** bu karar doğrultusunda hareket etmek
**Transliteration:** bu karar doğrultusunda hareket etmek
**English Translation:** to act in accordance with this decision, to act in line with this decision
**Usage Notes:** A standard formal and administrative construction using *doğrultusunda* (in the direction/line of) as a postpositional phrase. The construction is productive: any noun can replace *karar* (decision). Documented in P1b Root 1, item 26.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 16**
**Example:** doğru dürüst çalışmamak
**Transliteration:** doğru dürüst çalışmamak
**English Translation:** to not work properly, to not function adequately
**Usage Notes:** A colloquial construction using the fixed compound *doğru dürüst* as an adequacy/degree modifier; the negated form is more common than the positive. Used in mild reproach or complaint. Documented in P1b Root 1, item 11.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 17**
**Example:** doğruya en yakın açıklama
**Transliteration:** doğruya en yakın açıklama
**English Translation:** the explanation closest to the truth
**Usage Notes:** Used in evaluative and epistemic contexts to describe the best available approximation when certainty is not achievable. The superlative construction *en yakın* (closest) with the dative *doğruya* is productive and not fixed to a single noun. Documented in P1b Root 1, item 29.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 18**
**Example:** doğruluk testi uygulamak
**Transliteration:** doğruluk testi uygulamak
**English Translation:** to apply a truth test, to fact-check
**Usage Notes:** Used in journalistic fact-checking and scientific contexts. *Doğruluk testi* (truth/accuracy test) is a fixed compound; *uygulamak* (to apply) is the standard verbal complement. Documented in P1b Root 1, item 30.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 19**
**Example:** doğru parçası
**Transliteration:** doğru parçası
**English Translation:** line segment
**Usage Notes:** A fixed mathematical term in which *doğru* functions in its geometric sense (straight line) rather than its epistemic sense. *Parça* (piece, segment) + possessive suffix. Used in geometry education and technical contexts.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

> **Absence note:** No common construction of the type "to be true" using *doğru* as a predicate with a propositional subject has been identified as a fixed multi-word unit; this function is typically expressed through *doğru olmak* (to be correct/true), which is a regular predicative construction rather than a fixed collocation. No fixed idiom using *doğru* to mean "sincere" (as opposed to "correct") has been identified; this function is covered by *dürüst* and *özü sözü bir*.

## P1 Item 2 — gerçek

**Base Form:** gerçek

---

**Construction 1**
**Example:** gerçeği söylemek
**Transliteration:** gerçeği söylemek
**English Translation:** to tell the truth, to say what is real
**Usage Notes:** The canonical collocation for stating what is factually the case; *gerçek* takes the accusative as a definite noun object. Used in everyday and formal discourse. Documented in P1b Root 3, item 15.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 2**
**Example:** gerçeği yansıtmak
**Transliteration:** gerçeği yansıtmak
**English Translation:** to reflect reality, to represent the truth
**Usage Notes:** Used in journalistic, artistic, and evaluative contexts to assess whether a representation accurately mirrors what is real. *Yansıtmak* (to reflect, to mirror) is the standard verbal complement in this construction. Documented in P1b Root 3, item 16.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 3**
**Example:** gerçeğe aykırı beyan
**Transliteration:** gerçeğe aykırı beyan
**English Translation:** false declaration, statement contrary to fact
**Usage Notes:** A fixed legal phrase for a statement that contradicts what is real or true. *Gerçeğe aykırı* (contrary to reality) is a stable adjectival phrase used as a modifier in legal documents. Documented in P1b Root 3, item 17.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 4**
**Example:** gerçeğe uygun beyan
**Transliteration:** gerçeğe uygun beyan
**English Translation:** truthful declaration, statement conforming to fact
**Usage Notes:** The positive counterpart to *gerçeğe aykırı beyan*; used in legal and administrative contexts to describe a statement that accurately corresponds to reality. Documented in P1b Root 3, item 18.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 5**
**Example:** gerçeği çarpıtmak
**Transliteration:** gerçeği çarpıtmak
**English Translation:** to distort the truth, to twist reality
**Usage Notes:** Used in journalistic, legal, and moral contexts to describe misrepresentation of what is real. *Çarpıtmak* (to distort, to twist) is the standard verbal complement in this construction. Documented in P1b Root 3, item 29.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 6**
**Example:** gerçeği gizlemek
**Transliteration:** gerçeği gizlemek
**English Translation:** to conceal the truth, to hide reality
**Usage Notes:** Used in moral, legal, and journalistic contexts to describe withholding or suppressing what is true. *Gizlemek* (to hide, to conceal) is the standard verbal complement. Documented in P1b Root 3, item 30.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 7**
**Example:** gerçek ile kurgu arasındaki sınır
**Transliteration:** gerçek ile kurgu arasındaki sınır
**English Translation:** the boundary between reality and fiction
**Usage Notes:** A standard nominal construction used in literary, journalistic, and philosophical contexts to frame the distinction between what is real and what is invented. The coordinate phrase *gerçek ile kurgu* (reality and fiction) is stable; *arasındaki sınır* (the boundary between) is the standard postpositional complement. Documented in P1b Root 3, item 24.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 8**
**Example:** gerçek yüzünü göstermek
**Transliteration:** gerçek yüzünü göstermek
**English Translation:** to show one's true face, to reveal one's true colors
**Usage Notes:** An idiomatic expression used in moral and evaluative contexts to describe the revelation of someone's true character beneath a surface appearance. *Gerçek yüz* (true face) is a stable compound; *göstermek* (to show) is the standard verbal complement. Documented in P1b Root 3, item 27.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 9**
**Example:** gerçek anlamda özgür olmak
**Transliteration:** gerçek anlamda özgür olmak
**English Translation:** to be free in the true sense of the word
**Usage Notes:** A standard adverbial construction using *gerçek anlamda* (in the true/real sense) to assert that a predicate applies in a full or genuine sense rather than nominally. The phrase *gerçek anlamda* is productive and not fixed to *özgür*. Documented in P1b Root 3, item 11.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 10**
**Example:** gerçek zamanlı veri
**Transliteration:** gerçek zamanlı veri
**English Translation:** real-time data
**Usage Notes:** A fixed technical compound in computing and telecommunications. *Gerçek zamanlı* (real-time) functions as a stable adjectival modifier; *veri* (data) is the most common head noun but the modifier is productive with other technical nouns. Documented in P1b Root 3, item 12.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 11**
**Example:** sanal gerçeklik gözlüğü
**Transliteration:** sanal gerçeklik gözlüğü
**English Translation:** virtual reality headset
**Usage Notes:** A fixed technical compound in consumer technology. *Sanal gerçeklik* (virtual reality) is a stable compound; *gözlük* (glasses, headset) is the standard head noun for the device. Documented in P1b Root 3, item 13.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 12**
**Example:** gerçek mi bu?
**Transliteration:** gerçek mi bu?
**English Translation:** Is this real? Is this true?
**Usage Notes:** A highly frequent colloquial interrogative construction expressing disbelief or surprise. The question particle *mi* follows *gerçek* directly; *bu* (this) is the subject. Extremely common in spoken Turkish.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 13**
**Example:** gerçek neden nedir?
**Transliteration:** gerçek neden nedir?
**English Translation:** What is the real reason?
**Usage Notes:** A standard analytical question used in investigative and everyday contexts to distinguish the actual cause from stated or apparent reasons. The construction *gerçek* + noun is productive as a modifier meaning "the real/true X." Documented in P1b Root 3, item 26.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 14**
**Example:** gerçeklik algısı
**Transliteration:** gerçeklik algısı
**English Translation:** perception of reality
**Usage Notes:** A fixed nominal compound used in psychological, philosophical, and everyday contexts. *Gerçeklik* (reality) + *algı* (perception) + possessive suffix is a stable compound in psychological and philosophical discourse. Documented in P1b Root 3, item 2.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 15**
**Example:** gerçeklik ilkesi
**Transliteration:** gerçeklik ilkesi
**English Translation:** reality principle
**Usage Notes:** A fixed technical term in psychoanalytic theory (Freud's *Realitätsprinzip*), used in Turkish translations of psychoanalytic literature. *Gerçeklik* + *ilke* (principle) + possessive suffix.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 16**
**Example:** gerçeklik testi
**Transliteration:** gerçeklik testi
**English Translation:** reality testing
**Usage Notes:** A fixed term in clinical psychology for the cognitive process of distinguishing internal mental states from external reality. Used in Turkish psychiatric and psychological literature.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

> **Absence note:** No fixed idiomatic construction using *gerçek* to mean "sincere" (as a personal quality) has been identified; this function is covered by *özü sözü bir* and *dürüst*. No common construction of the type "the whole truth" using *gerçek* as a fixed phrase has been identified; the concept is expressed through *tüm gerçek* or *bütün gerçek* as regular modifier-noun constructions rather than fixed idioms.

## P1 Item 3 — hakikat

**Base Form:** hakikat

---

**Construction 1**
**Example:** hakikati söylemek
**Transliteration:** hakikati söylemek
**English Translation:** to tell the truth, to speak the truth
**Usage Notes:** A formal or literary collocation for stating what is true, especially in contexts of moral or philosophical weight. *Hakikat* takes the accusative as a definite noun object. Documented in P1b Root 2, item 16.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 2**
**Example:** hakikate ulaşmak
**Transliteration:** hakikate ulaşmak
**English Translation:** to reach the truth, to attain truth
**Usage Notes:** Used in philosophical, religious, and literary discourse to describe the goal of inquiry or spiritual seeking. *Hakikat* takes the dative; *ulaşmak* (to reach, to attain) is the standard verbal complement. Documented in P1b Root 2, item 17.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 3**
**Example:** hakikat arayışı
**Transliteration:** hakikat arayışı
**English Translation:** the search for truth, the quest for truth
**Usage Notes:** A fixed nominal compound used in philosophical, literary, and journalistic contexts. *Hakikat* + *arayış* (search, quest) + possessive suffix is a stable compound. Documented in P1b Root 2, item 18.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 4**
**Example:** hakikat arayışı felsefenin özüdür
**Transliteration:** hakikat arayışı felsefenin özüdür
**English Translation:** the search for truth is the essence of philosophy
**Usage Notes:** A representative sentence illustrating the use of *hakikat arayışı* in philosophical discourse; included to document the predicative construction rather than as a fixed idiom. The sentence structure is regular.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 5**
**Example:** hakikate aykırı
**Transliteration:** hakikate aykırı
**English Translation:** contrary to the truth, at odds with reality
**Usage Notes:** A formal adjectival phrase used in literary and philosophical registers, parallel to *gerçeğe aykırı* but with a more elevated register. Less common in legal documents than *gerçeğe aykırı*.
**Epistemic Status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**Construction 6**
**Example:** hakikat sonrası
**Transliteration:** hakikat sonrası
**English Translation:** post-truth
**Usage Notes:** A calque of English *post-truth*, used in journalistic and academic discourse to describe an epistemic environment in which objective facts are less influential than emotional appeals. *Sonrası* (after, post-) is a productive postpositional element. Attested in contemporary Turkish journalism and academic writing.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

> **Absence note:** No fixed idiomatic construction using *hakikat* in legal registers (as opposed to *gerçek*) has been identified beyond *hakikati söylemek* and *hakikate ulaşmak*. The legal domain strongly prefers *gerçek* and *doğru* in formal constructions. No common construction of the type "the whole truth" using *hakikat* as a fixed phrase has been identified.

## P1 Item 4 — hak

**Base Form:** hak

---

**Construction 1**
**Example:** hak vermek
**Transliteration:** hak vermek
**English Translation:** to concede that someone is right, to agree with someone
**Usage Notes:** A standard collocation in everyday and formal discourse for acknowledging that another person's position is correct. *Sana hak veriyorum* (I agree with you / I concede you are right). Documented in P1b Root 2, item 6.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 2**
**Example:** hakkını teslim etmek
**Transliteration:** hakkını teslim etmek
**English Translation:** to give someone their due, to acknowledge someone's merit
**Usage Notes:** A fixed collocation used in moral and evaluative contexts to acknowledge that someone deserves credit or recognition. *Hak* takes the possessive-accusative; *teslim etmek* (to deliver, to hand over) is the verbal complement. Documented in P1b Root 2, item 7.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 3**
**Example:** hak etmek
**Transliteration:** hak etmek
**English Translation:** to deserve, to merit, to earn
**Usage Notes:** A light-verb construction used in moral and evaluative contexts to assert that someone deserves a particular outcome. *Bunu hak etti* (he/she deserved this). Extremely common in everyday speech. Documented in P1b Root 2, item 8.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 4**
**Example:** hak aramak
**Transliteration:** hak aramak
**English Translation:** to seek one's rights, to pursue justice
**Usage Notes:** Used in legal and political contexts to describe the act of pursuing one's legal or moral entitlements. *Hak arama özgürlüğü* (freedom to seek one's rights / access to justice) is a fixed legal phrase. Documented in P1b Root 2, item 13.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 5**
**Example:** hak kazanmak
**Transliteration:** hak kazanmak
**English Translation:** to earn a right, to acquire an entitlement
**Usage Notes:** Used in legal and everyday contexts to describe the acquisition of a right or entitlement through action, time, or procedure. Documented in P1b Root 2, item 9.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 6**
**Example:** hak ihlali
**Transliteration:** hak ihlali
**English Translation:** rights violation, infringement of rights
**Usage Notes:** A standard legal and human rights term. *İnsan hakları ihlali* (human rights violation) is the most common extended form. Documented in P1b Root 2, item 10.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 7**
**Example:** hak yemek
**Transliteration:** hak yemek
**English Translation:** to deprive someone of their rights, to take what is not one's due
**Usage Notes:** An idiomatic expression using *yemek* (to eat) in its figurative sense of wrongful taking. *Kimsenin hakkını yemem* (I will not deprive anyone of their due). Documented in P1b Root 2, item 22.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 8**
**Example:** hak tanımak
**Transliteration:** hak tanımak
**English Translation:** to recognize a right, to acknowledge an entitlement
**Usage Notes:** Used in legal and political contexts to describe the formal or informal recognition of a right or claim. *Azınlıklara hak tanımak* (to recognize rights for minorities). Documented in P1b Root 2, item 23.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 9**
**Example:** hak sahibi olmak
**Transliteration:** hak sahibi olmak
**English Translation:** to be a rights holder, to be entitled
**Usage Notes:** A standard legal construction for holding a particular right or entitlement. *Hak sahibi* (rights holder) is a fixed nominal compound; *olmak* (to be) is the predicative complement. Documented in P1b Root 2, item 24.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 10**
**Example:** insan hakları ihlali
**Transliteration:** insan hakları ihlali
**English Translation:** human rights violation
**Usage Notes:** A fixed legal and journalistic compound. *İnsan hakları* (human rights) + *ihlal* (violation) + possessive suffix is the standard Turkish phrase for human rights violations. Used in legal documents, journalism, and political discourse.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 11**
**Example:** telif hakkı ihlali
**Transliteration:** telif hakkı ihlali
**English Translation:** copyright infringement
**Usage Notes:** A fixed legal term. *Telif hakkı* (copyright) + *ihlal* (infringement) + possessive suffix is the standard Turkish phrase for copyright infringement. Used in legal, publishing, and creative industry contexts. Documented in P1b Root 2, item 25.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 12**
**Example:** oy hakkını kullanmak
**Transliteration:** oy hakkını kullanmak
**English Translation:** to exercise the right to vote
**Usage Notes:** A standard political and legal collocation. *Oy hakkı* (right to vote) takes the possessive-accusative; *kullanmak* (to use, to exercise) is the standard verbal complement for exercising rights. Documented in P1b Root 2, item 26.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 13**
**Example:** hak ve adalet
**Transliteration:** hak ve adalet
**English Translation:** right and justice, truth and justice
**Usage Notes:** A fixed coordinate phrase used in moral, political, and legal discourse. Common in political rhetoric and legal writing. Documented in P1b Root 2, item 27.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 14**
**Example:** hakkını vermek
**Transliteration:** hakkını vermek
**English Translation:** to give someone what they are due, to do justice to something
**Usage Notes:** A collocation used in moral and evaluative contexts to describe giving someone or something the recognition or treatment they deserve. *Konunun hakkını vermek* (to do justice to the subject/topic). Distinct from *hakkını teslim etmek* in being slightly less formal.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 15**
**Example:** hakkını aramak
**Transliteration:** hakkını aramak
**English Translation:** to seek one's own rights, to stand up for one's rights
**Usage Notes:** A collocation used in legal and everyday contexts to describe an individual's pursuit of their own entitlements. Distinct from *hak aramak* in that the possessive *-kını* specifies that the rights sought are one's own. *Hakkını aramaktan çekinmemek* (not to hesitate to seek one's rights).
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 16**
**Example:** hak mı haksız mı?
**Transliteration:** hak mı haksız mı?
**English Translation:** right or wrong? justified or unjust?
**Usage Notes:** A formulaic evaluative question used in everyday and journalistic discourse to frame a binary moral or legal judgment. Documented in P1b Root 2, item 28.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

> **Absence note:** No fixed idiomatic construction using *hak* to mean "truth" in a propositional sense (as opposed to "right" or "entitlement") has been identified in contemporary secular registers; this semantic function is covered by *gerçek*, *doğru*, and *hakikat*. The theological use of *Hak* as a divine name is a proper noun usage rather than a compositional construction.

## P1 Item 5 — haklı

**Base Form:** haklı

---

**Construction 1**
**Example:** haklı çıkmak
**Transliteration:** haklı çıkmak
**English Translation:** to be proven right, to turn out to be correct
**Usage Notes:** A fixed collocation describing the outcome in which someone's position is vindicated by events or evidence. *Sonunda haklı çıktı* (in the end, he/she was proven right). Extremely common in spoken and written Turkish. Documented in P2a Item 5.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 2**
**Example:** haklı olmak
**Transliteration:** haklı olmak
**English Translation:** to be right, to be justified
**Usage Notes:** The standard predicative construction for asserting that a person's claim or position is correct. *Haklısın* (you are right) is the contracted everyday form. Extremely common across all registers.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 3**
**Example:** haklı gerekçe

**Usage Notes:** A fixed legal term for the instruments or methods used to establish proof in legal proceedings. *İspat vasıtaları* (means of proof) is a standard term in Turkish procedural law. Used in legal documents and academic legal writing.
