# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P5 Oppositions
# Language: Chinese (zh)

---

## Opposition 1

**Term A:** 真 (zhēn)
**Term B:** 假 (jiǎ)
**Transliteration:** zhēn / jiǎ
**English translation:** genuine, real / fake, false
**Description of contrast:** These are the primary positive and negative poles of the genuineness-evaluation domain, explicitly documented as direct contrasts throughout P1, P1b, and P3. They appear together in the binomial 真假 and in paired constructions such as 说真话 vs. 说假话.
**Contrast type:** lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## Opposition 2

**Term A:** 真 (zhēn)
**Term B:** 伪 (wěi)
**Transliteration:** zhēn / wěi
**English translation:** genuine / spurious, falsified
**Description of contrast:** These two roots co-occur in the binomial 真伪 (authenticity vs. falsity) and in the fixed phrase 真伪难辨. P1b notes that 伪 functions as a marker of deliberate falsification, making this contrast more specifically associated with intentional fraud than the 真/假 opposition.
**Contrast type:** lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## Opposition 3

**Term A:** 真实 (zhēnshí)
**Term B:** 虚假 (xūjiǎ)
**Transliteration:** zhēnshí / xūjiǎ
**English translation:** truthful, factual, real / false, fictitious, fraudulent
**Description of contrast:** Documented in P1 as contrasting compounds used to evaluate statements, representations, and claims. 虚假 carries a stronger negative connotation than 假 alone and is more specifically associated with deliberate deception or legal fraud, while 真实 asserts correspondence with reality.
**Contrast type:** lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## Opposition 4

**Term A:** 真实 (zhēnshí)
**Term B:** 失实 (shīshí)
**Transliteration:** zhēnshí / shīshí
**English translation:** truthful, accurate / inaccurate, not in accordance with the facts
**Description of contrast:** Both terms evaluate the factual accuracy of statements and representations (P3 Group 2), but 失实 specifically marks a departure from factual accuracy — whether through error or distortion — without necessarily implying deliberate deception, while 真实 asserts positive correspondence with reality.
**Contrast type:** lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## Opposition 5

**Term A:** 真实 (zhēnshí)
**Term B:** 失真 (shīzhēn)
**Transliteration:** zhēnshí / shīzhēn
**English translation:** truthful, accurate / distorted, having lost fidelity
**Description of contrast:** Both evaluate fidelity to reality or an original, but 失真 specifically marks departure from an original state or source (as in audio distortion or historical distortion), while 真实 asserts positive correspondence. P3 notes that 失真 emphasizes departure from an original rather than from an external fact, distinguishing it from 失实.
**Contrast type:** lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## Opposition 6

**Term A:** 正确 (zhèngquè)
**Term B:** 错误 (cuòwù)
**Transliteration:** zhèngquè / cuòwù
**English translation:** correct, right / error, mistake, wrong
**Description of contrast:** Documented in P1 as the standard antonym pair for correctness relative to a norm or standard. P1 explicitly states that 错误 "contrasts with 正确 as its standard antonym," and P3 Group 3 treats them as the central positive and negative poles of correctness evaluation.
**Contrast type:** lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 13

**Term A:** 真实性 (zhēnshíxìng)
**Term B:** 虚假性 (xūjiǎxìng)
**Transliteration:** zhēnshíxìng / xūjiǎxìng
**English translation:** truthfulness, factual accuracy (as a property) / falseness, fraudulent nature (as a property)
**Description of contrast:** Both are -性 nominalizations documented in P2a Section 1, used in legal and analytical discourse to refer to the abstract properties of being truthful and being false/fraudulent respectively. P2a notes that 虚假性 is less common than 真实性 but is attested in legal and academic writing.
**Contrast type:** morphological
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 14

**Term A:** 名副其实 (míng fù qí shí)
**Term B:** 名不副实 (míng bù fù shí)
**Transliteration:** míng fù qí shí / míng bù fù shí
**English translation:** living up to one's name, worthy of the name / in name only, failing to match the description
**Description of contrast:** Documented in P1 as a fixed antonym pair: 名副其实 asserts that a label accurately corresponds to actual qualities, while 名不副实 criticizes a mismatch between label and reality. P1 explicitly states that "its antonym 名不副实 is equally common."
**Contrast type:** morphological
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 15

**Term A:** 表里如一 (biǎo lǐ rú yī)
**Term B:** 表里不一 (biǎo lǐ bù yī)
**Transliteration:** biǎo lǐ rú yī / biǎo lǐ bù yī
**English translation:** consistent inside and out, genuine / inconsistent inside and out, hypocritical
**Description of contrast:** Documented in P1 and P2b as a fixed antonym pair formed by replacing 如 (like, as) with 不 (not). P2b item 91 explicitly describes 表里不一 as "the antonymous construction" of 表里如一, equally common in moral evaluation.
**Contrast type:** morphological
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 33

**Term A:** 真阳性 / 假阳性 (zhēn yángxìng / jiǎ yángxìng)

**Note:** (no clear opposition identified as a pair within the data)

P1b documents 假阳性 (false positive) and 假阴性 (false negative) as a contrasting pair (Root 3, items 20 and 21), but neither is contrasted with a 真阳性 (true positive) or 真阴性 (true negative) form in the data. The opposition between 假阳性 and 假阴性 is documented; the opposition between 假阳性 and 真阳性 is inferrable from scientific usage but not explicitly supported in the context.

## Opposition 36

**Term A:** 以假乱真 (yǐ jiǎ luàn zhēn)
**Term B:** 真金不怕火炼 (zhēnjīn bù pà huǒ liàn)
**Transliteration:** yǐ jiǎ luàn zhēn / zhēnjīn bù pà huǒ liàn
**English translation:** to pass off the fake as genuine / true gold fears no fire; genuine quality withstands any test
**Description of contrast:** Both are fixed expressions documented in P1b (Root 3, item 13 and Root 1, item 19) that address the relationship between genuine and counterfeit under conditions of scrutiny, but from opposite perspectives: 以假乱真 describes the successful deception of the genuine by the fake; 真金不怕火炼 asserts that what is genuinely good will resist such deception and prove itself under testing.
**Contrast type:** lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 37

**Term A:** 弄假成真 (nòngjiǎ chéngzhēn)
**Term B:** 以假乱真 (yǐ jiǎ luàn zhēn)
**Transliteration:** nòngjiǎ chéngzhēn / yǐ jiǎ luàn zhēn

**Note:** (no clear opposition identified)

Both expressions involve the relationship between 假 and 真 in dynamic situations, but they describe different processes rather than opposing ones: 弄假成真 describes a transformation from feigned to genuinely real; 以假乱真 describes the use of the fake to confuse people about what is real. They are not documented as a contrastive pair in the data.
