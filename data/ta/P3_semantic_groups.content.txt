# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P3 NATURAL SEMANTIC GROUPING
# Language: Tamil (ta)

---

## METHODOLOGY STATEMENT

This analysis identifies natural groupings among Tamil truth-domain expressions based solely on **attested patterns of usage, co-occurrence, contrast, and contextual distribution** documented in P1 and P2. Groupings reflect how terms are actually used and distinguished in Tamil discourse, not imposed conceptual categories.

The groupings are **descriptive** rather than prescriptive, based on:
- Contexts where terms appear
- Terms that contrast or substitute for each other
- Collocational patterns
- Register and domain restrictions
- Functional similarities in discourse

---

## GROUP 1: FACTUAL CORRESPONDENCE EXPRESSIONS

**Definition:** Terms used to evaluate whether statements, information, or representations correspond to actual states of affairs. These expressions appear in contexts of verification, fact-checking, reporting, and distinguishing accurate from inaccurate information. They are commonly used with verbs of speaking, telling, and reporting.

### Representative expressions:

**Core members:**
- உண்மை (uṇmai) "truth, fact"
- பொய் (poy) "lie, falsehood"
- உண்மையான (uṇmaiyāṉa) "true, factual"
- பொய்யான (poyyāṉa) "false, untrue"
- நிஜம் (nijam) "truth, reality, fact"
- நிஜமான (nijamāṉa) "true, real, actual"

**Common collocations:**
- உண்மை சொல்லு (uṇmai collu) "tell the truth"
- பொய் சொல்லு (poy collu) "tell a lie"
- உண்மைத்தகவல் (uṇmaittakaval) "true information"
- பொய்த்தகவல் (poyttakaval) "false information"
- உண்மைக்கதை (uṇmaikkatai) "true story"
- பொய்க்கதை (poykatai) "false story"

### Ambiguous or overlapping items:

- மெய் (mey) "truth, reality" — overlaps with this group but also appears in more philosophical/metaphysical contexts
- சத்தியம் (cattiyam) "truth, oath" — overlaps but more restricted to oath-taking and solemn contexts
- வாஸ்தவம் (vāstavam) "reality, actuality" — overlaps but more formal register

---

## GROUP 2: CORRECTNESS/ACCURACY EXPRESSIONS

**Definition:** Terms used to evaluate whether answers, calculations, procedures, or actions are correct or accurate according to established standards or rules. These expressions are particularly common in educational, technical, and administrative contexts, and frequently appear with verbs of checking, verifying, and correcting.

### Representative expressions:

**Core members:**
- சரி (cari) "correct, right"
- தப்பு (tappu) "wrong, mistake"
- தவறு (tavaṟu) "error, mistake"
- சரியான (cariyāṉa) "correct, accurate"
- தவறான (tavaṟāṉa) "wrong, incorrect"
- தப்பான (tappāṉa) "wrong, mistaken"

**Common collocations:**
- சரி பார் (cari pār) "check, verify"
- சரி செய் (cari cey) "correct, fix"
- தப்பு செய் (tappu cey) "make a mistake"
- தவறு திருத்து (tavaṟu tiruttu) "correct an error"

### Ambiguous or overlapping items:

- துல்லியம் (tulliyam) "accuracy, precision" — overlaps but more technical/formal
- பொருத்தம் (poruttam) "appropriateness, suitability" — overlaps but emphasizes contextual fit rather than absolute correctness

---

## GROUP 3: AUTHENTICITY/GENUINENESS EXPRESSIONS

**Definition:** Terms used to distinguish genuine, original, or authentic items from fake, counterfeit, or imitation ones. These expressions are particularly common in commercial contexts, discussions of documents and certificates, and evaluations of material objects. They frequently appear in contexts of authentication and fraud detection.

### Representative expressions:

**Core members:**
- அசல் (acal) "original, genuine"
- நகல் (nakal) "copy, imitation"
- உண்மையான (uṇmaiyāṉa) "genuine, authentic" [when modifying objects]
- பொய்யான (poyyāṉa) "fake, counterfeit" [when modifying objects]

**Common collocations:**
- அசல் சாமான் (acal cāmāṉ) "genuine goods"
- நகல் சாமான் (nakal cāmāṉ) "imitation goods"
- பொய் நாணயம் (poy nāṇayam) "counterfeit currency"
- பொய் சான்று (poyccāṉṟu) "false certificate"
- உண்மைச்சான்று (uṇmaiccāṉṟu) "certificate of authenticity"

### Ambiguous or overlapping items:

- மெய்யான (meyyāṉa) "genuine, real" — overlaps but more literary register
- உண்மைப்பொருள் (uṇmaipporuḷ) "genuine article" — overlaps but can also mean "true meaning"

---

## GROUP 4: HONESTY/INTEGRITY EXPRESSIONS

**Definition:** Terms used to evaluate the moral character of persons in terms of truthfulness, straightforwardness, and ethical conduct. These expressions focus on personal qualities and habitual behavior rather than individual statements or actions. They are common in character descriptions, moral evaluations, and discussions of trustworthiness.

### Representative expressions:

**Core members:**
- நேர்மை (nērmai) "honesty, integrity"
- நேர்மையான (nērmaiyāṉa) "honest, upright"
- நேர்மையாக (nērmaiyāka) "honestly"
- நம்பகம் (nampakam) "trustworthiness, reliability"
- நம்பகமான (nampakamāṉa) "trustworthy, reliable"
- நம்பிக்கை (nampikkai) "trust, faith"

**Common collocations:**
- நேர்மையான ஆள் (nērmaiyāṉa āḷ) "honest person"
- நம்பகமான ஆள் (nampakamāṉa āḷ) "trustworthy person"
- நேர்மையாக நடந்துக்கொள் (nērmaiyāka naṭantukkoḷ) "behave honestly"

**Contrasts with:**
- கள்ளம் (kaḷḷam) "deceit, fraud"
- கபடம் (kapaṭam) "hypocrisy, duplicity"
- ஏமாற்று (ēmāṟṟu) "deception, cheating"

### Ambiguous or overlapping items:

- உண்மையாளர் (uṇmaiyāḷar) "truthful person" — overlaps but focuses more on truth-telling than general integrity
- பொய்யன் (poyyan) "liar" — overlaps as character evaluation but more specific to lying behavior

---

## GROUP 5: JUSTICE/FAIRNESS EXPRESSIONS

**Definition:** Terms used to evaluate whether actions, decisions, or situations are just, fair, or reasonable according to moral or legal standards. These expressions are common in legal discourse, moral arguments, and complaints about unfair treatment. They often appear in contexts of rights, entitlements, and moral evaluation.

### Representative expressions:

**Core members:**
- நியாயம் (niyāyam) "justice, fairness, rightness"
- அநியாயம் (aṉiyāyam) "injustice, unfairness"
- நியாயமான (niyāyamāṉa) "just, fair, reasonable"
- உரிமை (urimai) "right, entitlement"
- தகுதி (takuti) "worthiness, deservingness"

**Common collocations:**
- நியாயம் இல்லை (niyāyam illai) "it's not fair"
- நியாயம் கேள் (niyāyam kēḷ) "demand justice"
- நியாயமான முடிவு (niyāyamāṉa muṭivu) "just decision"

### Ambiguous or overlapping items:

- அறம் (aṟam) "virtue, righteousness, dharma" — overlaps but broader moral/religious scope
- தருமம் (tarumam) "dharma, righteousness" — overlaps but more religious context

---

## GROUP 6: APPROPRIATENESS/SUITABILITY EXPRESSIONS

**Definition:** Terms used to evaluate whether something fits, matches, or is appropriate to a particular context, purpose, or situation. These expressions emphasize contextual correctness and compatibility rather than absolute truth or accuracy. They are common in assessments of matching, compatibility, and contextual rightness.

### Representative expressions:

**Core members:**
- பொருத்தம் (poruttam) "appropriateness, suitability, compatibility"
- பொருத்தமான (poruttamāṉa) "appropriate, suitable"
- பொருந்தும் (poruntum) "fits, suits, is appropriate"
- ஒப்பு (oppu) "resemblance, agreement, match"
- தகுதி (takuti) "fitness, qualification"

**Common collocations:**
- பொருத்தம் பார் (poruttam pār) "check compatibility"
- பொருந்தி வருகிறது (poruntu varukiratu) "it matches, it fits"
- ஒப்பிட்டுப் பார் (oppiṭṭup pār) "compare and see"

### Ambiguous or overlapping items:

- சரி (cari) "correct, right" — overlaps when used for contextual appropriateness
- இயல்பு (iyalpu) "nature, natural state" — overlaps in contexts of natural fit

---

## GROUP 7: VERIFICATION/PROOF EXPRESSIONS

**Definition:** Terms and constructions used in contexts of proving, verifying, demonstrating, or establishing truth through evidence or investigation. These expressions are particularly common in legal, scientific, investigative, and administrative discourse. They emphasize the process and means of establishing truth.

### Representative expressions:

**Core members:**
- மெய்ப்பு (meyppu) "proof, verification"
- மெய்ப்பிக்க (meypikka) "to prove, to verify"
- சான்று (cāṉṟu) "proof, evidence"
- ஆதாரம் (ātāram) "evidence, basis"
- சாட்சி (cāṭci) "witness, testimony"
- உறுதி (uṟuti) "certainty, confirmation"

**Common collocations:**
- மெய்ப்பிக்க முடியும் (meypikka muṭiyum) "can prove"
- சான்று காட்டு (cāṉṟu kāṭṭu) "show proof"
- ஆதாரம் இருக்கா (ātāram irukkā) "is there evidence?"
- சாட்சி சொல் (cāṭci col) "give testimony"
- உண்மையை நிரூபி (uṇmaiyai nirūpi) "prove the truth"

### Ambiguous or overlapping items:

- உண்மைப்படுத்து (uṇmaippaṭuttu) "to verify, to authenticate" — overlaps but also used in administrative authentication
- மெய்யெனக் காட்டு (meyyeṉak kāṭṭu) "show to be true" — overlaps with demonstration

---

## GROUP 8: DECEPTION/FRAUD EXPRESSIONS

**Definition:** Terms used to describe deliberate acts of deceiving, lying, or defrauding others. These expressions emphasize intentional dishonesty and are common in moral condemnation, legal contexts involving fraud, and discussions of manipulative behavior. They often appear with verbs of doing, making, or committing.

### Representative expressions:

**Core members:**
- பொய் (poy) "lie, falsehood" [when referring to deliberate lying]
- கள்ளம் (kaḷḷam) "deceit, fraud"
- கபடம் (kapaṭam) "hypocrisy, duplicity"
- ஏமாற்று (ēmāṟṟu) "deception, cheating"

**Common collocations:**
- பொய் கட்டு (poy kaṭṭu) "fabricate a lie"
- கள்ளம் செய் (kaḷḷam cey) "commit fraud"
- ஏமாற்று செய் (ēmāṟṟu cey) "deceive, cheat"
- கள்ளம் கபடம் இல்லாமல் (kaḷḷam kapaṭam illāmal) "without deceit or hypocrisy"

**Specific fraud types:**
- பொய் சாட்சி (poyccāṭci) "false testimony, perjury"
- பொய் குற்றச்சாட்டு (poykuṟṟaccāṭṭu) "false accusation"
- பொய் ஆதாரம் (poyyātāram) "false evidence"
- பொய் கணக்கு (poy kaṇakku) "false accounts, cooked books"

---

## GROUP 9: REVELATION/DISCOVERY EXPRESSIONS

**Definition:** Constructions describing the process of truth becoming known, being revealed, or being discovered. These expressions emphasize the transition from hidden to known and are common in narrative contexts, investigative discourse, and discussions of exposure of deception. They typically involve verbs of coming out, emerging, or being found.

### Representative expressions:

**Core constructions:**
- உண்மை வெளிப்படு (uṇmai veḷippaṭu) "truth comes out, is revealed"
- உண்மை தெரிய வரு (uṇmai teriya varu) "truth comes to be known"
- பொய் வெளிப்படு (poy veḷippaṭu) "lie is exposed"
- உண்மையை கண்டுபிடி (uṇmaiyai kaṇṭupiṭi) "discover the truth"
- உண்மை வெளிச்சத்துக்கு வரு (uṇmai veḷiccattukku varu) "truth comes to light"

**Idiomatic expressions:**
- உண்மை ஒரு நாள் வெளிவரும் (uṇmai oru nāḷ veḷivarum) "truth will come out one day"
- பொய் ஒரு நாள் வெளிவரும் (poy oru nāḷ veḷivarum) "lies will be exposed one day"

---

## GROUP 10: CONCEALMENT/HIDING EXPRESSIONS

**Definition:** Constructions describing deliberate hiding, covering up, or suppressing of truth. These expressions emphasize intentional concealment and are common in contexts of corruption, cover-ups, and moral condemnation of dishonesty. They typically involve verbs of hiding, covering, or suppressing.

### Representative expressions:

**Core constructions:**
- உண்மையை மறை (uṇmaiyai maṟai) "hide the truth"
- உண்மையை மூடி மறை (uṇmaiyai mūṭi maṟai) "cover up and hide the truth"
- உண்மையை மறைக்க முயற்சி செய் (uṇmaiyai maṟaikka muyaṟci cey) "try to hide the truth"

---

## GROUP 11: REALIZATION/AWARENESS EXPRESSIONS

**Definition:** Terms and constructions describing the mental process of coming to know, realize, or become aware of truth or reality. These expressions emphasize cognitive or perceptual grasp of truth and are common in philosophical, spiritual, and educational contexts. They often involve verbs of knowing, perceiving, or realizing.

### Representative expressions:

**Core constructions:**
- உண்மையை உணர் (uṇmaiyai uṇar) "realize the truth"
- உண்மையை அறி (uṇmaiyai aṟi) "know the truth"
- மெய்யை உணர் (meyyai uṇar) "perceive reality"
- மெய் உணர்வு பெறு (mey uṇarvu peṟu) "attain true awareness"
- மெய் ஞானம் பெறு (mey ñāṉam peṟu) "attain true wisdom"

**Related terms:**
- உண்மையுணர்வு (uṇmaiyuṇarvu) "awareness of truth"
- மெய்யுணர்வு (meyyuṇarvu) "true awareness, enlightenment"
- உண்மையறிவு (uṇmaiyaṟivu) "knowledge of truth"
- மெய்யறிவு (meyyaṟivu) "true knowledge"
- மெய்ஞ்ஞானம் (meyññāṉam) "true wisdom, spiritual knowledge"

### Note on register:
This group shows clear register stratification, with மெய்-based expressions more common in philosophical/spiritual discourse and உண்மை-based expressions more common in everyday contexts.

---

## GROUP 12: ACCEPTANCE/ACKNOWLEDGMENT EXPRESSIONS

**Definition:** Constructions describing the act of accepting, admitting, or acknowledging truth, often after initial denial or resistance. These expressions emphasize the transition from denial to acceptance and are common in contexts of confession, apology, and coming to terms with reality.

### Representative expressions:

**Core constructions:**
- உண்மையை ஒப்புக்கொள் (uṇmaiyai oppukkoḷ) "admit the truth"
- உண்மையை ஏற்று (uṇmaiyai ēṟṟu) "accept the truth"
- தப்பை ஒப்புக்கொள் (tappai oppukkoḷ) "admit the mistake"
- தப்பு என்று ஒப்புக்கொள் (tappu eṉṟu oppukkoḷ) "acknowledge it as a mistake"

---

## GROUP 13: PHILOSOPHICAL/METAPHYSICAL TRUTH EXPRESSIONS

**Definition:** Terms and constructions used in philosophical, religious, and metaphysical discourse about ultimate reality, essential truth, or spiritual enlightenment. These expressions are more abstract and elevated than everyday truth-talk and are particularly associated with classical Tamil literature and religious texts.

### Representative expressions:

**Core members:**
- மெய் (mey) "truth, reality" [in philosophical contexts]
- மெய்ப்பொருள் (meypporuḷ) "true meaning, ultimate reality, essence"
- மெய்ம்மை (meymai) "truth, reality, genuineness"
- மெய்யியல் (meyyiyal) "metaphysics, ontology"
- மெய்ஞ்ஞானம் (meyññāṉam) "true wisdom, spiritual knowledge"

**Common collocations:**
- மெய்ப்பொருள் அறி (meypporuḷ aṟi) "know the ultimate reality"
- மெய் உணர்வு (mey uṇarvu) "true awareness, enlightenment"

### Note on contrast:
This group contrasts with Group 1 (Factual Correspondence) in that it deals with metaphysical/essential truth rather than correspondence of statements to facts.

---

## GROUP 14: LEGAL/TESTIMONIAL EXPRESSIONS

**Definition:** Terms and constructions specific to legal contexts, particularly involving testimony, oaths, evidence, and legal proceedings. These expressions are restricted to judicial and legal discourse and often involve specialized legal vocabulary.

### Representative expressions:

**Core members:**
- சாட்சி (cāṭci) "witness, testimony"
- பொய் சாட்சி (poyccāṭci) "false testimony, perjury"
- சத்தியம் (cattiyam) "oath, truth" [in oath-taking contexts]
- பொய் சத்தியம் (poy cattiyam) "false oath"
- ஆதாரம் (ātāram) "evidence"
- பொய் ஆதாரம் (poyyātāram) "false evidence"

**Common collocations:**
- சாட்சி சொல் (cāṭci col) "give testimony"
- சத்தியம் செய் (cattiyam cey) "take an oath"
- பொய் குற்றச்சாட்டு (poykuṟṟaccāṭṭu) "false accusation"
- பொய் வழக்கு (poyvaḻakku) "false case, fabricated lawsuit"

---

## GROUP 15: COMMERCIAL/DOCUMENTARY FRAUD EXPRESSIONS

**Definition:** Terms describing specific types of counterfeit items, forged documents, or commercial fraud. These expressions are common in commercial, administrative, and legal contexts involving authentication of goods and documents.

### Representative expressions:

**Counterfeit goods:**
- பொய் சாமான் (poy cāmāṉ) "fake goods"
- பொய் நாணயம் (poy nāṇayam) "counterfeit currency"
- பொய் மருந்து (poy maruntu) "fake medicine"
- பொய் பொருள் (poypporuḷ) "counterfeit items"

**Forged documents:**
- பொய் சான்று (poyccāṉṟu) "false certificate"
- பொய் ஆவணம் (poyyāvaṇam) "false document"
- பொய் எழுத்து (poyeḻuttu) "forged writing"
- பொய் கையெழுத்து (poy kaiyeḻuttu) "forged signature"
- பொய் முத்திரை (poy muttirai) "false seal"

**Identity fraud:**
- பொய் அடையாளம் (poyaṭaiyāḷam) "false identity"
- பொய் பெயர் (poy peyar) "false name, alias"

**Financial fraud:**
- பொய் கணக்கு (poy kaṇakku) "false accounts, cooked books"
- பொய் பில் (poy bil) "false invoice"

---

## GROUP 16: EMOTIONAL/BEHAVIORAL SINCERITY EXPRESSIONS

**Definition:** Terms describing sincerity or insincerity of emotions, affections, and interpersonal behaviors. These expressions evaluate authenticity of feelings and relationships rather than factual truth. They are common in discussions of personal relationships and character evaluation.

### Representative expressions:

**Sincere emotions:**
- மெய்யன்பு (meyyaṉpu) "true love, genuine affection"
- மெய் நட்பு (meynaṭpu) "true friendship"
- மெய் நண்பன் (mey naṇpaṉ) "true friend"

**Insincere emotions:**
- பொய் நேசம் (poynēcam) "false love, insincere affection"
- பொய் நட்பு (poynaṭpu) "false friendship"
- பொய் கண்ணீர் (poykaṇṇīr) "crocodile tears, false tears"
- பொய் அழுகை (poyaḻukai) "false crying, feigned weeping"

**Pretense/performance:**
- பொய் முகம் (poymukam) "false face, mask, pretense"
- பொய் நாடகம் (poynāṭakam) "false drama, pretense, act"
- பொய் நடிப்பு (poynaṭippu) "false acting, pretense"

---

## GROUP 17: INFORMATION/MEDIA EXPRESSIONS

**Definition:** Terms describing true or false information, news, and media content. These expressions are particularly common in journalistic, media, and information technology contexts. They have become increasingly prominent with concerns about misinformation and fake news.

### Representative expressions:

**True information:**
- உண்மைத்தகவல் (uṇmaittakaval) "true information, factual information"
- உண்மை செய்தி (uṇmai ceyti) "true news"

**False information:**
- பொய்த்தகவல் (poyttakaval) "false information, misinformation"
- பொய் செய்தி (poyceyti) "false news, fake news"
- பொய் வதந்தி (poy vatanti) "false rumor"
- பொய் பிரச்சாரம் (poyppiracāram) "false propaganda, disinformation"

---

## GROUP 18: CHECKING/CORRECTION EXPRESSIONS

**Definition:** Constructions involving the process of checking, verifying, correcting, or fixing errors and mistakes. These expressions are particularly common in educational, technical, and quality control contexts. They emphasize the practical activity of error detection and correction.

### Representative expressions:

**Checking/verifying:**
- சரி பார் (cari pār) "check, verify"
- சரி பார்த்துக்கொள் (cari pārttukkoḷ) "check for oneself"
- சரி பார்ப்பு நடத்து (cari pārppu naṭattu) "conduct an inspection"

**Correcting/fixing:**
- சரி செய் (cari cey) "correct, fix, repair"
- தப்பை சரி செய் (tappai cari cey) "correct the mistake"
- தவறு திருத்து (tavaṟu tiruttu) "correct the error"

## GROUP 19: PROVERBIAL/IDIOMATIC TRUTH EXPRESSIONS

**Definition:** Fixed or semi-fixed proverbial expressions and idioms about truth, lies, and their consequences. These expressions encode cultural wisdom and moral teachings about truthfulness and are common in moral instruction and folk wisdom.

### Representative expressions:

**About truth:**
- உண்மை கசக்கும் (uṇmai kaṟakkum) "truth is bitter"
- உண்மை எப்போதும் வெல்லும் (uṇmai eppōtum vellum) "truth always wins"
- உண்மை ஒரு நாள் வெளிவரும் (uṇmai oru nāḷ veḷivarum) "truth will come out one day"

**About lies:**
- பொய்க்கு கால் இல்லை (poykku kāl illai) "lies have no legs" (lies don't last)
- பொய் சொன்னால் கொம்பு முளைக்கும் (poy coṉṉāl kompu muḷaikkum) "if you tell lies, horns will sprout"
- பொய் ஒரு நாள் வெளிவரும் (poy oru nāḷ veḷivarum) "lies will be exposed one day"
- பொய் மேல் பொய் (poy mēl poy) "lie upon lie"

---

## ITEMS WITH AMBIGUOUS OR MULTIPLE GROUP MEMBERSHIP

### உண்மை (uṇmai) "truth"
- **Primary membership:** Group 1 (Factual Correspondence)
- **Secondary membership:** Group 9 (Revelation/Discovery), Group 12 (Acceptance/Acknowledgment)
- **Reason:** This is the most general and versatile truth-term in Tamil, appearing across multiple functional contexts

### பொய் (poy) "falsehood, lie"
- **Primary membership:** Group 1 (Factual Correspondence)
- **Secondary membership:** Group 8 (Deception/Fraud), Group 9 (Revelation/Discovery)
- **Reason:** Functions both as simple negation of truth and as term for deliberate deception

### மெய் (mey) "truth, reality"
- **Primary membership:** Group 13 (Philosophical/Metaphysical Truth)
- **Secondary membership:** Group 1 (Factual Correspondence), Group 7 (Verification/Proof)
- **Reason:** Shows clear register stratification between philosophical and everyday uses

### சரி (cari) "correct, right"
- **Primary membership:** Group 2 (Correctness/Accuracy)
- **Secondary membership:** Group 6 (Appropriateness/Suitability)
- **Reason:** Can indicate both absolute correctness and contextual appropriateness

### நியாயம் (niyāyam) "justice, fairness"
- **Primary membership:** Group 5 (Justice/Fairness)
- **Secondary membership:** Group 2 (Correctness/Accuracy) [in some contexts]
- **Reason:** Can indicate both moral rightness and logical reasonableness

### தகுதி (takuti) "worthiness, qualification"
- **Primary membership:** Group 6 (Appropriateness/Suitability)
- **Secondary membership:** Group 5 (Justice/Fairness)
- **Reason:** Overlaps between contextual fitness and deserved entitlement

### ஆதாரம் (ātāram) "evidence, basis"
- **Primary membership:** Group 7 (Verification/Proof)
- **Secondary membership:** Group 14 (Legal/Testimonial)
- **Reason:** Used in both general evidential contexts and specific legal contexts

### சாட்சி (cāṭci) "witness, testimony"
- **Primary membership:** Group 14 (Legal/Testimonial)
- **Secondary membership:** Group 7 (Verification/Proof)
- **Reason:** Primarily legal but also used in general evidential contexts

## ITEMS THAT DO NOT FIT CLEANLY INTO GROUPS

### இயல்பு (iyalpu) "nature, natural state"
**Reason for non-grouping:** While this term appears in truth-domain contexts when discussing what is natural or essential, it functions more as a general term for inherent nature than as a truth-evaluation term. It doesn't pattern consistently with other truth-domain expressions.

### உரிமை (urimai) "right, ownership, entitlement"
**Reason for non-grouping:** While related to justice and fairness, this term is more specifically about legal/moral rights and ownership rather than truth-evaluation per se. It appears in Group 5 contexts but has broader usage beyond truth-domain.

### அறம் (aṟam) "virtue, righteousness, dharma"
### தருமம் (tarumam) "dharma, righteousness"
### பாவம் (pāvam) "sin, wrongdoing"
### புண்ணியம் (puṇṇiyam) "merit, virtue"
**Reason for non-grouping:** These terms belong to the broader moral/religious domain rather than specifically to truth-evaluation. While they can appear in contexts involving truthfulness as a virtue, they are not primarily truth-domain terms.

### சந்தேகம் (cantēkam) "doubt, suspicion"
**Reason for non-grouping:** This term expresses epistemic uncertainty but doesn't directly evaluate truth or falsity. It represents a different dimension of epistemic evaluation (certainty/uncertainty) rather than truth/falsity.

### நடப்பு (naṭappu) "actuality, happening"
**Reason for non-grouping:** While this term can be used to distinguish what actually happens from what is claimed, it functions more as a general term for current events or actuality than as a truth-evaluation term per se.

## NOTES ON GROUPING PATTERNS

### Register stratification:
Several groups show clear register differences:
- **Literary/formal:** Group 13 (Philosophical/Metaphysical), மெய்-based expressions
- **Legal/technical:** Group 14 (Legal/Testimonial), Group 7 (Verification/Proof)
- **Colloquial:** Group 2 (Correctness/Accuracy) with சரி/தப்பு, Group 1 with நிஜம்
- **Commercial:** Group 15 (Commercial/Documentary Fraud), Group 3 (Authenticity)

### Domain restrictions:
Some groups are restricted to specific domains:
- Group 14 is largely restricted to legal contexts
- Group 15 is restricted to commercial/administrative contexts
- Group 13 is restricted to philosophical/religious discourse
- Group 17 is increasingly restricted to media/information contexts

### Asymmetries:
- **Negative bias:** Groups involving பொய் (falsehood) show much greater elaboration and specificity than positive truth-groups, particularly in fraud and deception contexts (Groups 8, 15)
- **Process vs. state:** Some groups emphasize processes (Groups 9, 10, 11, 12, 18) while others emphasize states or qualities (Groups 1, 2, 4, 5)

### Overlapping patterns:
- Groups 1, 7, and 14 show significant overlap in evidential/verification contexts
- Groups 2 and 6 overlap in contexts of evaluating correctness and appropriateness
- Groups 4 and 8 represent opposite poles of character evaluation (honesty vs. deception)
