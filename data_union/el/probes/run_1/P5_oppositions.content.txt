# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P5 Oppositions
# Language: Greek (el)

---

## Opposition 1

**Term A:** αληθής / αλήθεια / αληθινός
**Term B:** ψευδής / ψεύδος / ψεύτικος
**Transliteration A:** alithís / alíthia / alithinós
**Transliteration B:** psevdhís / psévdhos / psévtikos
**English translation:** true / truth / genuine (colloquial) vs. false / falsehood / fake (colloquial)
**Description of contrast:** These two clusters form the primary lexical opposition in the propositional truth-evaluation domain. The αληθ- cluster encodes correspondence with reality; the ψευδ-/ψευτ- cluster encodes departure from it. The opposition is explicitly framed in the fixed binary expression αλήθεια ή ψέμα ("truth or lie") and in the paired formula αλήθεια και ψέμα ("truth and falsehood"). Within each cluster, register distinctions operate in parallel: αληθής / ψευδής are formal and legal; αληθινός / ψεύτικος are colloquial.
**Contrast type:** lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 2

**Term A:** αλήθεια
**Term B:** αναλήθεια
**Transliteration A:** alíthia
**Transliteration B:** analíthia
**English translation:** truth vs. untruth / falsehood
**Description of contrast:** A morphological negation formed by the privative prefix ἀν- applied to the primary truth noun. αναλήθεια is documented as a formal or literary alternative to ψέμα, used in philosophical and journalistic writing. The opposition is asymmetric: αλήθεια is [CORE] across all registers, while αναλήθεια is restricted to elevated registers.
**Contrast type:** morphological
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 8

**Term A:** έγκυρος
**Term B:** άκυρος
**Transliteration A:** éngiros
**Transliteration B:** ákiros
**English translation:** valid / authoritative vs. invalid / void / null
**Description of contrast:** These two terms share the root κῦρος (authority, validity) but are formed with different prefixes (ἐν- vs. ἀ-). The opposition is explicitly noted in the data as a root-level contrast rather than a simple privative negation of έγκυρος itself. Both are [CORE] in legal and administrative registers, where the validity or invalidity of documents, contracts, and decisions is at issue. The opposition generates a productive cluster on the negative side (άκυρος → ακυρότητα → ακυρώνω → ακύρωση) that has no full parallel on the positive side.
**Contrast type:** lexical (shared root, distinct prefixes)
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 15

**Term A:** επαληθεύσιμος
**Term B:** ανεπαλήθευτος
**Transliteration A:** epalithéfsimos
**Transliteration B:** anepalítheftos
**English translation:** verifiable vs. unverified
**Description of contrast:** These two terms are morphologically related to the same verb (επαληθεύω) but encode different aspects of the verification dimension: επαληθεύσιμος describes the in-principle possibility of verification (a property of claims), while ανεπαλήθευτος describes the actual state of not having been verified (a property of information at a given time). The opposition is not a simple privative pair — the suffixes differ (-σιμος vs. -τος) — and the semantic contrast is between potential and actuality rather than between presence and absence of a single property.
**Contrast type:** morphological
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 16

**Term A:** αληθοφανής / αληθοφάνεια
**Term B:** αληθής / αλήθεια
**Transliteration A:** alithofanís / alithofánia
**Transliteration B:** alithís / alíthia
**English translation:** truth-seeming / verisimilitude vs. true / truth
**Description of contrast:** A lexical opposition between appearing to be true and actually being true. αληθοφανής (from αληθ- + φαίνομαι, "to appear") describes something that has the appearance of truth without necessarily being true; αληθής describes something that actually is true. The contrast is between the φαίνεσθαι (appearing) and the εἶναι (being) dimensions of truth. This opposition is documented in rhetorical and literary contexts and is [CORE] in those registers.
**Contrast type:** lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 17

**Term A:** αυθεντικός / γνήσιος
**Term B:** πλαστός
**Transliteration A:** afthentikós / gníssios
**Transliteration B:** plastós
**English translation:** authentic / genuine vs. forged / fabricated
**Description of contrast:** A lexical opposition in the domain of object and document authenticity. αυθεντικός and γνήσιος both affirm that something is what it purports to be; πλαστός asserts that it has been deliberately fabricated or falsified. The contrast is between genuine origin and deliberate falsification. πλαστός is the standard legal term for forgery; αυθεντικός and γνήσιος are the positive counterparts used in authentication contexts.
**Contrast type:** lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 24

**Term A:** πρωτότυπο έργο / αυθεντικό έργο τέχνης

**Transliteration A:** protótipo érgo / afthentikó érgo téchnis
**Transliteration B:** andígrafo / plastó érgo

## Opposition 26

**Term A:** Αυτό είναι αληθινό / Αυτό είναι σωστό
**Term B:** Αυτό δεν είναι αληθινό / Αυτό δεν είναι σωστό
**Transliteration A:** Aftó íne alithinó / Aftó íne sostó
**Transliteration B:** Aftó dhen íne alithinó / Aftó dhen íne sostó
**English translation:** This is true / This is correct vs. This is not true / This is not correct
**Description of contrast:** A constructional opposition between affirmative and negative copular attribution using truth-domain adjectives. The data documents both as [CORE] constructions. The negative is formed by inserting the negation particle δεν before the copula; no other structural change occurs. This is the default negation strategy in everyday speech, as opposed to the use of lexicalized privative adjectives (Opposition 2, 3, 6).
**Contrast type:** constructional
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 27

**Term A:** ψευδοεπιστήμη
**Term B:** επιστήμη
**Transliteration A:** psevdhoepistími
**Transliteration B:** epistími
**English translation:** pseudoscience vs. science
**Description of contrast:** A morphological opposition formed by the ψευδο- prefix applied to a domain noun. The data explicitly notes that ψευδο- compounds assert a positive category of simulacrum — something that has the form but not the substance of the base category — rather than simply negating it. This distinguishes the opposition from simple negation (δεν είναι επιστήμη, "it is not science"): ψευδοεπιστήμη asserts that the entity presents itself as science while lacking its defining properties. The same pattern applies to ψευδοδημοκρατία / δημοκρατία, ψευδοδιανοούμενος / διανοούμενος, etc.
**Contrast type:** morphological
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 28

**Term A:** αληθής / αληθινός (applied to persons, objects, states)
**Term B:** εικονικός (applied to the same domains)
**Transliteration A:** alithís / alithinós
**Transliteration B:** ikonikós
**English translation:** true / real / genuine vs. simulated / nominal / fictitious
**Description of contrast:** A lexical opposition documented in the data between terms affirming that something is what it appears to be and εικονικός, which asserts that something has the appearance or form of a category without its substance (εικονική σύμβαση = sham contract; εικονική πραγματικότητα = virtual reality). The opposition is not as tightly paired as the αληθής / ψευδής opposition, and εικονικός is restricted to specific legal and technical registers, but the data explicitly notes its evaluative force as marking that "something appears to be what it is not."
**Contrast type:** lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 29

**Term A:** η αλήθεια (propositional noun)
**Term B:** Αλήθεια; (discourse particle)
**Transliteration A:** i alíthia
**Transliteration B:** Alíthia?
**English translation:** truth (propositional) vs. Really? / By the way (discourse particle)
**Description of contrast:** A constructional opposition within the same lexical item. The data explicitly documents two distinct uses of αλήθεια: as a propositional noun evaluating correspondence with reality (Group 1 in P3), and as a discourse particle expressing surprise or introducing a topic shift (Group 5 in P3), where "the truth-evaluation meaning is bleached." The opposition is between a full propositional use and a pragmatically bleached discourse use of the same form.
**Contrast type:** constructional
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 30

**Term A:** αλήθεια (objective, propositional)
**Term B:** η δική μου αλήθεια
**Transliteration A:** alíthia
**Transliteration B:** i dhikí mu alíthia
**English translation:** truth (propositional / objective) vs. my truth (personal / subjective)
**Description of contrast:** A constructional opposition documented in P2b and flagged in P3 as a usage pattern distinct from propositional truth evaluation. η δική μου αλήθεια applies αλήθεια to personal experience or perspective rather than to propositions about external states of affairs, reflecting a relativist or subjectivist framing. The data notes this as a contemporary usage in psychological and social media registers that is "distinct from the other groups." The opposition is between the bare noun (objective truth) and the possessive construction (subjective truth).
**Contrast type:** constructional
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]
