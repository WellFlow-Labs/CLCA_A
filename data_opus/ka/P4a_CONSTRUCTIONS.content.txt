# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P4a_CONSTRUCTIONS
## Language: Georgian (ka)
## Transliteration: ISO 9984 (1996)

---

## 1. ATTRIBUTION
### Pattern
Predicate-adjective frame: Subject + evaluative adjective + copula (verb-final), affirming that a person/statement holds a truth-domain property. Concentrated on the propositional-correctness items (მართალი, სწორი).
### Examples
- მართალი ხარ → martali xar → "you are right"
- სწორი ხარ → sc̣ori xar → "you are correct"
### Notes
The copula (xar "you are") follows the adjective; usage distinguishes მართალი (more moral/propositional) from სწორი (more factual/accuracy-based). This frame is not generalizable to the genuineness or reliability adjectives.

---

## 2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION
### Pattern
Privative prefix **ara-** prefixed to a trust/honesty adjective to form a lexicalized negative attribute. Available only for a subset of the field (reliability and honesty items), not uniformly across it.
### Examples
- არასანდო წყარო → arasando c̣q̇aro → "an unreliable source"
- არაპატიოსანი ადამიანი → arap̣aṭiosani adamiani → "a dishonest person"
### Notes
The **ara-** prefix is attested for arasando, arasaimedo, arap̣aṭiosani but is not available across the whole field; the truth/lie and genuineness items do not regularly take it. Sentential negation (e.g., *არ ხარ მართალი*) is the alternative for those.

---

## 3. ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION
### Pattern
Attributive **Adj + N** collocation, the most productive constructional frame in the field. The adjective precedes the noun; available across most evaluative adjectives at varying registers.
### Examples
- ნამდვილი მეგობარი → namdvili megobari → "a true/real friend"
- ყალბი ფული → q̇albi puli → "counterfeit money"
### Notes
Available across genuineness (namdvili, q̇albi), reliability (sando c̣q̇aro), accuracy (zusṭi dro), and honesty (p̣aṭiosani adamiani) items; register varies from everyday to formal/technical (avtenṭuri doḳumenṭi).

---

## 4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE
### Pattern
No dedicated inchoative construction is attested in the data for truth-domain adjectives. Where change-of-state is expressed, it surfaces via independent perfective verbs (e.g., ṭq̇uili gamovida "it turned out a lie"), not a regular Adj→inchoative pattern.
### Examples
- ტყუილი გამოვიდა → ṭq̇uili gamovida → "it turned out to be a lie"
- (no second example identified)
### Notes
Only one borderline frame is attested (the *gamovida* "turned out" frame), and it is lexical rather than a productive inchoative derivation. No common Adj-based "become true/genuine/honest" construction is identified.

---

## 5. CAUSATIVE (OR ABSENCE)
### Pattern
A productive causative-style derivation exists only at the falsehood/fakeness pole, via prefix + root + **-eba** verbs meaning "make false / forge / deceive" (ga-, mo-). No symmetric "make true/genuine" causative is attested.
### Examples
- გაყალბება → gaq̇albeba → "to falsify, to forge"
- მოტყუება → moṭq̇ueba → "to deceive"
### Notes
Causative formation is asymmetric: it is robust on the negative pole (q̇alb-, ṭq̇u-) but absent for the truth/genuineness adjectives, which have no common "cause-to-be-true" verb.

---

## 6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION
### Pattern
Two recurring abstract-noun patterns: **si- … -e** and suffix **-eba / -oba** on adjectival bases, yielding quality nouns.
### Examples
- სიმართლე → simartle → "truth" (si- + martl- + -e)
- სანდოობა → sandooba → "reliability, trustworthiness" (sando- + -oba)
### Notes
The -eba/-oba and si-…-e patterns recur broadly (sizusṭe, gulc̣rpeloba, p̣aṭiosneba, namdviloba). Several P1 items are themselves such derivatives and show no further nominalization.

---

## 7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION (liar, truth-teller)
### Pattern
Agentive nouns formed at the falsehood pole via **ma- … -ara** and via compounding with mc̣me/mkneli; concentrated on lying/forging. No common single-word agentive for "truth-teller" is attested.
### Examples
- მატყუარა → maṭq̇uara → "liar, deceiver"
- ცრუმოწმე → crumoc̣me → "false witness"
### Notes
Agentive formation is asymmetric: robust for the negative pole (maṭq̇uara "liar," q̇albismkneli "forger," crumoc̣me "false witness"), but no lexicalized positive counterpart ("truth-teller") emerges; truth-telling is expressed phrasally (simartlis tkma).

---

## 8. SPEECH-ACT (GENITIVE + VERBAL-NOUN)
### Pattern
**N-genitive + verbal-noun (tkma "telling")** construction naming the act of truth-telling or lying; clusters on the truth/lie noun items. Also surfaces as accusative + speech verb (ambob "you say").
### Examples
- სიმართლის თქმა → simartlis tkma → "telling the truth"
- ტყუილს ამბობ → ṭq̇uils ambob → "you are lying / that's a lie"
### Notes
This frame is shared by the truth-pole (simartle) and lie-pole (ṭq̇uili, cru-) items, which are mutually contrastive within it; it does not extend to genuineness or reliability terms.

---

## 9. ESSENCE / GENITIVE COLLOCATION
### Pattern
**N-genitive + essence noun** construction expressing the core/nature of something; clusters on arsi, buneba.
### Examples
- საქმის არსი → sakmis arsi → "the essence/gist of the matter"
- ადამიანის ბუნება → adamianis buneba → "human nature"
### Notes
A distinct genitive-collocation cluster forming its own sub-field; it connects loosely to genuineness (what something fundamentally is) but shares no frames with the truth/lie or honesty constructions.
