# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2b Compositional Constructions
# Language: Chinese (zh)

---

## Preliminary Note on Scope and Method

This step documents attested multi-word constructions involving the P1 lexical items. "Multi-word construction" is understood here as a syntactically-driven or periphrastic pattern — a combination of a P1 item with verbs, particles, coverbs, aspect markers, or other lexical material — that constitutes a recurrent, natural unit of usage. This step excludes:

- Derivational forms already documented in P2a
- Compounds and four-character expressions already documented in P1 and P1b (those are lexical items, not constructions)
- Invented or hypothetical combinations

The constructions documented here are organized by base form. Where a P1 item does not participate in common multi-word constructions beyond its basic predicative or attributive use, this is explicitly stated.

Register variation is noted where relevant. Asymmetries between positive and negative forms, and between different P1 items, are preserved.

## SECTION 1 — Constructions with 真 (zhēn)

---

**Construction 1.1**
**Base Form:** 真 (zhēn)
**Example:** 这是真的。
**Transliteration:** Zhè shì zhēn de.
**English Translation:** This is real / This is true.
**Usage Notes:** The copular construction X + 是 + 真的 is the basic predicative pattern for asserting that something is genuine or true. The particle 的 nominalizes 真, making it function as a predicate nominal. Ubiquitous across all registers.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 1.2**
**Base Form:** 真 (zhēn)
**Example:** 这是真的吗？
**Transliteration:** Zhè shì zhēn de ma?
**English Translation:** Is this real? / Is this true?
**Usage Notes:** The interrogative form of Construction 1.1, formed by appending the sentence-final question particle 吗. The most basic way of questioning the truth or genuineness of something in everyday speech.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 1.3**
**Base Form:** 真 (zhēn)
**Example:** 说真的，我不知道。
**Transliteration:** Shuō zhēn de, wǒ bù zhīdào.
**English Translation:** To tell the truth, I don't know. / Honestly, I don't know.
**Usage Notes:** The construction 说真的 ("speaking truly") functions as a discourse-initial adverbial phrase signaling that the speaker is about to make a candid or frank statement. Common in conversational registers.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 1.4**
**Base Form:** 真 (zhēn)
**Example:** 真不真，你自己判断。
**Transliteration:** Zhēn bù zhēn, nǐ zìjǐ pànduàn.
**English Translation:** Whether it's true or not, you judge for yourself.
**Usage Notes:** The A-不-A interrogative construction 真不真 is used to frame the question of truth or genuineness as an open question for the interlocutor to resolve. Common in everyday speech.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

## SECTION 2 — Constructions with 假 (jiǎ)

---

**Construction 2.1**
**Base Form:** 假 (jiǎ)
**Example:** 这是假的。
**Transliteration:** Zhè shì jiǎ de.
**English Translation:** This is fake. / This is false.
**Usage Notes:** The copular construction X + 是 + 假的 is the basic predicative pattern for asserting that something is counterfeit or false. Direct counterpart to Construction 1.1. Ubiquitous across all registers.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 2.2**
**Base Form:** 假 (jiǎ)
**Example:** 他在装假。
**Transliteration:** Tā zài zhuāng jiǎ.
**English Translation:** He is pretending / putting on a false front.
**Usage Notes:** The construction 装假 ("feign falseness / put on a pretense") uses 装 ("to feign, to pretend") as the main verb with 假 as a resultative or manner complement. Used to describe deliberate performance of a false state.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 2.3**
**Base Form:** 假 (jiǎ)
**Example:** 以假乱真
**Transliteration:** yǐ jiǎ luàn zhēn
**English Translation:** to pass off the fake as genuine; to use fakes to confuse the real
**Usage Notes:** A fixed four-character expression functioning as a verb phrase, meaning to deliberately substitute or mix counterfeits with genuine items so as to deceive. Used in authentication, legal, and commercial contexts. Already noted in P1b context; included here as it functions as a periphrastic construction rather than a simple compound.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 2.4**
**Base Form:** 假 (jiǎ)
**Example:** 真真假假，难以分辨。
**Transliteration:** Zhēn zhēn jiǎ jiǎ, nányǐ fēnbiàn.
**English Translation:** With the true and false all mixed together, it is hard to distinguish them.
**Usage Notes:** The reduplication pattern 真真假假 ("true-true-false-false") describes a situation in which genuine and counterfeit elements are intermingled and difficult to separate. Functions as a predicate-initial adverbial in this construction. Used in evaluative and descriptive contexts.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

## SECTION 3 — Constructions with 真实 (zhēnshí)

**Usage Notes:** The predicative construction 内容不真实 ("the content is not truthful") is the standard way of asserting that a report, account, or representation fails to correspond to reality. Common in legal, journalistic, and administrative evaluation.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Usage Notes:** The verb 还原 ("to restore, to reconstruct") combined with 真实情况 forms a recurrent phrase in journalistic and investigative contexts, describing the act of recovering an accurate account of events from distorted or incomplete records.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 3.3**
**Base Form:** 真实 (zhēnshí)
**Example:** 反映真实情况
**Transliteration:** fǎnyìng zhēnshí qíngkuàng
**English Translation:** to reflect / accurately represent the true situation
**Usage Notes:** The verb 反映 ("to reflect, to represent") combined with 真实情况 is a recurrent construction in administrative, journalistic, and legal contexts, used to describe the requirement or achievement of accurate representation of facts.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 3.4**
**Base Form:** 真实 (zhēnshí)
**Example:** 保证内容真实
**Transliteration:** bǎozhèng nèiróng zhēnshí
**English Translation:** to guarantee that the content is truthful
**Usage Notes:** The verb 保证 ("to guarantee, to ensure") combined with a 真实 predicate is a recurrent construction in legal and administrative contexts, particularly in declarations, contracts, and regulatory compliance statements.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 3.5**
**Base Form:** 真实 (zhēnshí)
**Example:** 这部电影真实地再现了那段历史。
**Transliteration:** Zhè bù diànyǐng zhēnshí de zàixiàn le nà duàn lìshǐ.
**English Translation:** This film faithfully recreates that period of history.
**Usage Notes:** 真实地 (with the adverbial particle 地) functions as a manner adverbial modifying a verb of representation or depiction. This adverbial construction is productive with verbs such as 再现 ("recreate"), 描绘 ("depict"), 记录 ("record"), and 反映 ("reflect").
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

**Construction 5.3**

**Transliteration:** zhèngquè lǐjiě zhèngcè
**English Translation:** to correctly understand the policy
**Usage Notes:** 正确 used as a pre-verbal adverbial (without 地) modifying verbs of cognition or action is a recurrent pattern in administrative and political discourse. Common collocates include 理解 ("understand"), 执行 ("implement"), 处理 ("handle"), and 对待 ("treat/approach").

---

**Construction 5.4**

**Transliteration:** jiūzhèng cuòwù, zǒuxiàng zhèngquè
**English Translation:** to correct errors and move toward what is correct
**Usage Notes:** The pairing of 纠正错误 ("correct errors") with 走向正确 ("move toward correctness") is a recurrent contrastive construction in self-criticism, political, and educational discourse.

**Construction 6.2**
**Base Form:** 错误 (cuòwù)
**Example:** 纠正错误
**Transliteration:** jiūzhèng cuòwù

**Usage Notes:** The verb 纠正 ("to correct, to rectify") combined with 错误 is a recurrent construction in administrative, political, and educational contexts. One of the most common collocations of 错误 as a direct object.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 6.3**
**Base Form:** 错误 (cuòwù)
**Example:** 承认错误
**Transliteration:** chéngrèn cuòwù
**English Translation:** to admit an error / to acknowledge a mistake
**Usage Notes:** The verb 承认 ("to admit, to acknowledge") combined with 错误 is a recurrent construction in moral, political, and interpersonal contexts. Contrasts with 否认错误 ("deny an error") and 掩盖错误 ("cover up an error").

---

**Construction 6.4**
**Base Form:** 错误 (cuòwù)
**Example:** 这种观点是错误的。
**Transliteration:** Zhè zhǒng guāndiǎn shì cuòwù de.
**English Translation:** This view is erroneous / mistaken.
**Usage Notes:** The copular predicative construction X + 是 + 错误的 is the standard way of asserting that a view, claim, or action is wrong. Widely used in political, educational, and logical contexts.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 6.5**
**Base Form:** 错误 (cuòwù)
**Example:** 错误地理解了问题
**Transliteration:** cuòwù de lǐjiě le wèntí
**English Translation:** to have mistakenly / erroneously understood the problem
**Usage Notes:** 错误地 (with the adverbial particle 地) functions as a manner adverbial modifying verbs of cognition or action. Productive with verbs such as 理解 ("understand"), 判断 ("judge"), 引导 ("guide"), and 处理 ("handle").
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]
