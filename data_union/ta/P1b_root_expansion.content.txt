# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P1b ROOT EXPANSION
# Language: Tamil (ta)

---

## Step 1 — Identify Central Roots

---

**Root 1**

**Form:** உண்மை / *uṇmai*
**P1 participation:** 8 items (items 1, 4, 10, 17, 18, 19, 24, 29; also *uṇmai*, *uṇmaiyāna*, *uṇmaiyil*, *uṇmait-taṉmai*, *uṇmaikkum māṟāna*, and as the base of *meyppiкka* via semantic overlap)
**Core meaning contribution:** The primary Tamil root for truth, reality, and genuine existence. Supplies the domain's central nominal anchor and generates the most productive derivational and collocational network in the truth-evaluation field. Functions as both an epistemic term (truth of statements) and an ontological term (reality of states of affairs).

---

**Root 2**

**Form:** மெய் / *mey*
**P1 participation:** 3 items (*mey*, *meyppiкka*, and as the classical/literary counterpart underlying *uṇmai* compounds in formal registers)
**Core meaning contribution:** "Body, substance, reality" — the classical root encoding truth as tangible reality or substance; highly productive in literary, philosophical, and formal compound formation. Participates in two distinct semantic streams — "truth/reality" and "body/physical" — both of which are well-attested in classical and modern Tamil.

---

**Root 3**

**Form:** நம்பு / *nampu*
**P1 participation:** 4 items (*nampakamāna*, *nampikkai*, and the base of *nampakam*; also items 7, 8, 27, and implicitly 28 via the trust-evaluation cluster)
**Core meaning contribution:** "To trust, to believe" — the primary verbal root encoding epistemic trust, credibility, and belief; productive in both nominal and adjectival derivation. Generates the domain's vocabulary for credibility, reliability, and the epistemic relationship between a person and a claim or source.

---

**Root 4**

**Form:** நேர் / *nēr*
**P1 participation:** 4 items (items 3, 9, 28, 29; *nēṟmai*, *nēṟtti*, *nēṟmaiyāna*)
**Core meaning contribution:** Straightness, directness, and proper alignment — the root from which honesty and integrity are conceptualized as absence of deviation. Generates a cluster of evaluative terms for personal conduct and formal correctness. Also participates in spatial and geometric senses that extend beyond the truth domain.

---

## Step 2 — Expand Each Root

---

### Root 1: உண்மை / *uṇmai*

*Note: This root is highly productive. The following compounds, collocations, derived expressions, and fixed phrases represent the most common and well-attested forms; additional compositional forms exist in formal and literary registers.*

---

**1.**
**Form:** உண்மை
**Transliteration:** uṇmai
**Morpheme breakdown:** *uḷ* (to be/exist) + *-mai* (abstract nominalizer)
**Natural English translation:** truth, reality, fact
**Context of use:** The base nominal form, used across all registers — everyday assertion, philosophical argument, legal testimony, and moral evaluation — to denote correspondence with reality. Appears in moral exhortation (*uṇmai pēcu* — speak the truth), philosophical discourse, and legal testimony.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**2.**
**Form:** உண்மையான
**Transliteration:** uṇmaiyāna
**Morpheme breakdown:** *uṇmai* + *-āna* (adjectival copular suffix)
**Natural English translation:** true, genuine, real, authentic
**Context of use:** Attributive adjective applied to persons, objects, feelings, and propositions to assert genuineness or factual accuracy; extremely common in spoken and written Tamil. Used to distinguish genuine instances from false or imitation ones: *uṇmaiyāna kaṭal* (genuine love), *uṇmaiyāna tகவல்* (accurate information).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**3.**
**Form:** உண்மையாக
**Transliteration:** uṇmaiyāka
**Morpheme breakdown:** *uṇmai* + *-āka* (adverbial/predicative copular suffix)
**Natural English translation:** truly, genuinely, honestly, in truth
**Context of use:** Adverbial form used to assert that an action or state is genuine or sincere. Common in everyday speech: *uṇmaiyāka colla* (to speak truthfully). Also used predicatively: *itu uṇmaiyāka irukku* (this is genuinely the case).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**4.**
**Form:** உண்மையில்
**Transliteration:** uṇmaiyil
**Morpheme breakdown:** *uṇmai* + *-il* (locative case suffix)
**Natural English translation:** in truth, actually, in reality
**Context of use:** Adverbial expression used to correct a misconception or contrast appearance with reality; common in both spoken and written Tamil. Used as a sentence-initial or mid-sentence frame to introduce a correction or clarification: *uṇmaiyil avaṉ varāttāṉ* (in fact, he did not come).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**5.**
**Form:** உண்மையிலேயே
**Transliteration:** uṇmaiyilēyē
**Morpheme breakdown:** *uṇmai* + *-il* (locative) + *-ē* (emphatic) + *-yē* (further emphatic)
**Natural English translation:** truly indeed, in actual fact, really and truly
**Context of use:** Emphatic adverbial used in spoken Tamil to strongly assert that something is genuinely the case, often in contexts of surprise or insistence. Functions as an intensifier: *avaḷ uṇmaiyilēyē nalla āḷ* (she is genuinely a good person).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**6.**
**Form:** உண்மைத்தன்மை
**Transliteration:** uṇmait-taṉmai
**Morpheme breakdown:** *uṇmai* + *taṉmai* (nature, inherent quality)
**Natural English translation:** truthfulness, authenticity, veracity, genuineness
**Context of use:** Abstract nominal used in philosophical, literary, and formal evaluative discourse to refer to the property of being true or authentic. Used in literary criticism, philosophy, and formal prose; appears in evaluations of literary works, testimonies, or representations.
**Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**7.**
**Form:** உண்மைத் தன்மையுள்ள
**Transliteration:** uṇmai-t-taṉmaiyuḷḷa
**Morpheme breakdown:** *uṇmai* + *taṉmai* + *uḷḷa* (possessing, having)
**Natural English translation:** authentic, genuinely possessing (a quality), truthful (attributive)
**Context of use:** Attributive participial phrase in formal and literary written Tamil, used to characterize persons, works, or representations as genuinely possessing a stated quality. More compositionally transparent than *uṇmaiyāna*.
**Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**8.**
**Form:** உண்மைப்படுத்துதல்
**Transliteration:** uṇmaippaṭuttutal
**Morpheme breakdown:** *uṇmai* + causative *-paṭuttu-* + verbal noun *-tal*
**Natural English translation:** authentication, verification, substantiation
**Context of use:** Formal and administrative verbal noun denoting the act of establishing the truth or genuineness of a document, claim, or identity. Used in official Tamil in document-processing and legal contexts.
**Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**9.**
**Form:** உண்மைப்படுத்து
**Transliteration:** uṇmaippaṭuttu
**Morpheme breakdown:** *uṇmai* + causative *-paṭuttu* (finite/imperative stem)
**Natural English translation:** authenticate, verify, substantiate, confirm
**Context of use:** The finite verb form of *uṇmaippaṭuttutal*, used in administrative and legal instructions: *ātāratai uṇmaippaṭuttu* (substantiate/verify the evidence). Appears in official written Tamil.
**Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**10.**
**Form:** உண்மைக்கு மாறான
**Transliteration:** uṇmaikkum māṟāna
**Morpheme breakdown:** *uṇmai* + dative *-kku* + *māṟāna* (contrary to, deviating from)
**Natural English translation:** contrary to fact, false, counterfactual
**Context of use:** Fixed evaluative expression used in legal and formal written contexts to characterize a statement or representation as factually false.
**Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**11.**
**Form:** உண்மையான அர்த்தத்தில்

**Natural English translation:** in the true sense, properly speaking, in the genuine meaning
**Context of use:** A semi-fixed phrase used in formal and philosophical written Tamil to specify that a term is being used in its proper or strict sense, distinguishing genuine from nominal instantiation of a category.
**Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**12.**
