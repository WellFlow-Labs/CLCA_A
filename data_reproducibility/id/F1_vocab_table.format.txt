# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — F1 Vocabulary Table
# Language: Indonesian (id)

| Form | Transliteration | Literal Meaning | Common Translations | Usage Description | Epistemic Status |
|------|-----------------|-----------------|---------------------|-------------------|------------------|
| benar | N/A (Latin script) | true, correct | true, correct, right, accurate | Used to evaluate statements as factually accurate or actions as morally/procedurally correct. Appears in both epistemic contexts ("itu benar" = "that's true") and normative contexts ("cara yang benar" = "the right way"). Highly productive in derivations (kebenaran = truth, membenarkan = to confirm/justify). | [CORE] |
| salah | N/A (Latin script) | wrong, mistaken | wrong, incorrect, mistaken, false | Primary term for negating correctness or accuracy of statements, actions, or judgments. Used in epistemic contexts ("jawaban salah" = "wrong answer") and moral contexts ("perbuatan salah" = "wrongdoing"). Forms productive derivatives (kesalahan = error/mistake, menyalahkan = to blame). | [CORE] |
| asli | N/A (Latin script) | original, genuine | genuine, authentic, original, real | Evaluates objects or representations as authentic rather than counterfeit or copied. Commonly used for documents ("dokumen asli" = "original document"), products ("barang asli" = "genuine goods"), and identity ("orang asli" = "indigenous person/native"). Distinguished from imitations or fakes. | [CORE] |
| palsu | N/A (Latin script) | false, fake | fake, counterfeit, false, forged | Evaluates objects, documents, or representations as inauthentic or deliberately deceptive. Used for counterfeit goods ("uang palsu" = "counterfeit money"), forged documents, and false identities. Carries connotation of intentional deception. | [CORE] |
| bohong | N/A (Latin script) | lie, false | lie, false, untrue, dishonest | Specifically evaluates statements or speech acts as deliberately false or deceptive. Functions as both noun ("itu bohong" = "that's a lie") and verb ("dia bohong" = "he/she is lying"). Strongly implies intentional deception rather than mere error. | [CORE] |
| jujur | N/A (Latin script) | honest, truthful | honest, truthful, sincere, frank | Evaluates persons or statements as truthful and without deception. Applied to character ("orang jujur" = "honest person") and speech ("berkata jujur" = "speak honestly"). Forms abstract noun kejujuran (honesty). | [CORE] |
| tepat | N/A (Latin script) | precise, exact | accurate, precise, exact, appropriate | Evaluates accuracy, precision, or appropriateness of statements, measurements, or actions. Used for temporal precision ("tepat waktu" = "on time"), spatial accuracy, and appropriateness of choices. Emphasizes exactness and suitability. | [CORE] |
| akurat | N/A (Latin script) | accurate (from Dutch/English "accurate") | accurate, precise | Borrowed term evaluating precision and correctness, especially in technical, scientific, or measurement contexts. Used for data accuracy ("data akurat" = "accurate data") and precise descriptions. More formal register than "tepat." | [CORE] |
| nyata | N/A (Latin script) | real, actual | real, actual, tangible, evident | Evaluates existence or reality of entities, events, or states. Contrasts with imaginary, hypothetical, or illusory ("dunia nyata" = "real world"). Also used for evidence being manifest or clear ("bukti nyata" = "tangible evidence"). | [CORE] |
| sejati | N/A (Latin script) | true, genuine (from Sanskrit "sajāti") | true, genuine, real, authentic | Evaluates essential nature or genuine quality, often with philosophical or elevated register. Used for authentic identity ("cinta sejati" = "true love"), essential nature, or genuine exemplars. More literary than "asli." | [CORE] |
| sungguh | N/A (Latin script) | truly, really | truly, really, genuinely, seriously | Functions as adverb or adjective evaluating sincerity, reality, or intensity. Used to emphasize truthfulness ("sungguh-sungguh" = "seriously/earnestly") or reality of situations. Forms derivatives like kesungguhan (seriousness/earnestness). | [CORE] |
| keliru | N/A (Latin script) | mistaken, erroneous | mistaken, wrong, erroneous, confused | Evaluates statements or beliefs as incorrect, typically through misunderstanding rather than deception. Softer than "salah," suggesting error rather than moral wrongness. Used for misunderstandings ("saya keliru" = "I was mistaken"). | [CORE] |
| sah | N/A (Latin script) | valid, legal | valid, legal, legitimate, lawful | Evaluates legal or procedural validity of documents, actions, or status. Used in legal contexts ("pernikahan sah" = "legal marriage"), official documents ("surat sah" = "valid document"). Emphasizes conformity to rules or law. | [CORE] |
| absah | N/A (Latin script) | valid, legitimate (from Arabic) | valid, legitimate, lawful | Synonym of "sah" with slightly more formal register, evaluating legal or procedural validity. Used interchangeably with "sah" in many contexts but may carry more formal or religious connotation due to Arabic origin. | [CORE] |
| batal | N/A (Latin script) | void, cancelled | void, invalid, cancelled, nullified | Evaluates legal or procedural status as invalid or nullified. Used for cancelled agreements ("kontrak batal" = "void contract"), invalidated actions, or nullified status. Opposite of "sah." | [CORE] |
| mirip | N/A (Latin script) | similar, resembling | similar, resembling, alike, look-alike | Evaluates degree of resemblance between entities. Used for physical similarity ("mirip sekali" = "very similar"), resemblance in appearance or characteristics. Forms basis for comparison judgments (kemiripan = similarity). | [CORE] |
| sama | N/A (Latin script) | same, equal | same, equal, identical | Evaluates identity or equivalence between entities. Used for sameness ("sama dengan" = "same as"), equality, or identity. Highly productive in compounds (kesamaan = similarity/equality, menyamakan = to equate). | [CORE] |
| hakikat | N/A (Latin script) | essence, reality (from Arabic "ḥaqīqah") | essence, true nature, reality, truth | Evaluates or refers to essential nature or fundamental reality of things. Used in philosophical and religious contexts ("hakikat manusia" = "human nature/essence"). Emphasizes underlying truth beyond appearances. | [CORE] |
| sebenarnya | N/A (Latin script) | actually, in truth (se- + benar + -nya) | actually, in fact, really, truly | Adverbial form evaluating actual state of affairs versus appearances or assumptions. Used to introduce corrections or reveal true situations ("sebenarnya saya tidak setuju" = "actually I disagree"). Emphasizes reality versus perception. | [CORE] |
| sesungguhnya | N/A (Latin script) | truly, in reality (se- + sungguh + -nya) | truly, indeed, in reality, verily | More formal or literary variant of "sebenarnya," evaluating truth or reality. Common in religious texts and formal discourse. Used to emphasize genuine truth or reality of statements. | [CORE] |
| betul | N/A (Latin script) | correct, true | correct, right, true, accurate | Near-synonym of "benar," evaluating correctness or truth. Used interchangeably with "benar" in many contexts, though may be slightly more colloquial. Common in confirmations ("betul sekali" = "exactly right"). | [CORE] |
| cocok | N/A (Latin script) | suitable, matching | suitable, matching, fitting, appropriate | Evaluates fit, compatibility, or appropriateness between entities or for purposes. Used for matching ("cocok dengan" = "matches with"), suitability for purpose, or compatibility in relationships. Emphasizes proper fit. | [CORE] |
| pas | N/A (Latin script) | exact, fitting (from Dutch "pas") | exact, fitting, just right, precise | Evaluates exact fit, precision, or appropriateness. Used for physical fit ("pas ukurannya" = "the size fits exactly"), temporal precision, or exact amounts. More colloquial than "tepat." | [CORE] |
| valid | N/A (Latin script) | valid (from English/Dutch) | valid | Borrowed term evaluating logical or procedural validity, especially in academic, technical, or formal contexts. Used for valid arguments, valid data, or valid methods ("argumen valid" = "valid argument"). More technical register. | [CORE] |
| dusta | N/A (Latin script) | lie, falsehood (from Sanskrit) | lie, falsehood, deceit | More literary or formal term for lies or falsehood. Used in elevated register or religious contexts ("pendusta" = "liar"). Carries strong moral condemnation. Less common in everyday speech than "bohong." | [MARGINAL] |
| khilaf | N/A (Latin script) | error, mistake (from Arabic "khilāf") | error, mistake, lapse | Evaluates unintentional errors or lapses, especially in religious or formal contexts. Implies mistake without malicious intent ("saya khilaf" = "I made an error/lapse"). More formal than "keliru." | [MARGINAL] |
| tulen | N/A (Latin script) | pure, genuine | pure, genuine, authentic, unadulterated | Evaluates purity or genuineness, especially of materials or bloodlines. Used for pure gold ("emas tulen" = "pure gold"), purebred animals, or authentic heritage. Emphasizes unmixed or unadulterated quality. | [CORE] |
| faktual | N/A (Latin script) | factual (from English/Dutch) | factual, based on facts | Borrowed term evaluating basis in facts rather than opinion or fiction. Used in formal or academic contexts ("informasi faktual" = "factual information"). Emphasizes empirical or documentary basis. | [CORE] |
| wajar | N/A (Latin script) | reasonable, natural | reasonable, natural, proper, fair | Evaluates reasonableness, naturalness, or propriety of situations, actions, or expectations. Used for what is normal or expected ("itu wajar" = "that's natural/reasonable"). Implies conformity to norms or expectations. | [CORE] |
| pantas | N/A (Latin script) | appropriate, fitting | appropriate, fitting, proper, deserved | Evaluates appropriateness or deservingness of actions, treatment, or outcomes. Used for what is fitting ("pantas saja" = "no wonder/serves right") or proper behavior. Can imply moral deservingness. | [CORE] |
| kebenaran | N/A | ke- + benar + -an (nominalization) | truth, correctness, the truth | Abstract noun for truth as concept or property. Used in philosophical discourse ("mencari kebenaran" = "seeking truth"), religious contexts ("kebenaran Tuhan" = "God's truth"), and everyday speech ("katakan kebenaran" = "tell the truth"). | [CORE] |
| membenarkan | N/A | meN- + benar + -kan (causative verb) | to confirm, to verify, to justify, to validate | Transitive verb meaning to confirm something as true or to justify an action. Used in statements of confirmation ("dia membenarkan laporan itu" = "he confirmed that report") and justification contexts ("membenarkan tindakan" = "to justify an action"). | [CORE] |
| dibenarkan | N/A | di- + benar + -kan (passive voice) | to be confirmed, to be permitted, to be justified | Passive form indicating something is confirmed or permitted. Used in permissions ("tidak dibenarkan" = "not permitted") and validation contexts ("telah dibenarkan" = "has been confirmed"). | [CORE] |
| pembenaran | N/A | peN- + benar + -an (nominalization of action) | justification, validation, confirmation | Noun referring to act or process of justifying or validating. Common in formal discourse ("pembenaran atas tindakan" = "justification for action") and legal contexts ("pembenaran hukum" = "legal justification"). | [CORE] |
| sebenar-benarnya | N/A | se- + benar + reduplication + -nya (superlative/emphatic) | most truly, in absolute truth, as truthfully as possible | Emphatic form used in oaths, legal testimony, and solemn declarations. Common in legal formulas ("dengan sebenar-benarnya" = "most truthfully") and sworn statements. | [CORE] |
| sebenar | N/A | se- + benar (prefix indicating "as/truly") | as true, truly | Less common variant, appears in some fixed expressions and older or formal texts. Sometimes used as shortened form in compounds. | [MARGINAL] |
| benar-benar | N/A | benar + reduplication (intensification) | really, truly, genuinely, very | Adverbial intensifier used to emphasize reality or degree. Very common in everyday speech ("saya benar-benar lelah" = "I'm really tired") and to emphasize genuineness ("dia benar-benar jujur" = "he's truly honest"). | [CORE] |
| membenar | N/A | meN- + benar (intransitive verb) | to be correct, to turn out right | Less common intransitive form. Used when something proves correct or comes true ("ramalannya membenar" = "his prediction came true"). More common in formal or literary register. | [MARGINAL] |
| kebenar-benaran | N/A | ke- + benar + reduplication + -an | excessive correctness, overly serious | Rare form suggesting excessive emphasis on correctness or taking things too seriously. Limited usage in colloquial contexts. | [UNCERTAIN] |
| memperbenarkan | N/A | meN- + per- + benar + -kan (causative with per-) | to justify, to vindicate, to prove right | More formal variant of membenarkan with emphasis on proving or demonstrating correctness. Used in formal discourse ("memperbenarkan argumen" = "to vindicate an argument"). | [CORE] |
| terbenar | N/A | ter- + benar (accidental/superlative) | most correct, truest | Superlative form indicating highest degree of correctness. Used in comparisons ("yang terbenar" = "the most correct one") and philosophical contexts. | [MARGINAL] |
| paling benar | N/A | paling (most) + benar | most correct, most true | Analytic superlative construction, more common than terbenar in everyday speech. Used in comparisons and evaluations ("cara paling benar" = "the most correct way"). | [CORE] |
| kebenarannya | N/A | ke- + benar + -an + -nya (possessive/definite) | its truth, the truth of it | Definite form referring to the truth of a specific matter. Common in discourse ("kebenarannya masih diragukan" = "its truth is still doubted"). | [CORE] |
| yang benar | N/A | yang (relativizer) + benar | the correct one, the true one, what is right | Relative clause construction identifying correct item or truth. Very common in questions ("yang mana yang benar?" = "which one is correct?") and statements. | [CORE] |
| sungguh-sungguh | N/A | sungguh + reduplication (intensification) | seriously, earnestly, in earnest, with full effort | Intensified form emphasizing seriousness or earnestness of action or state. Very common in everyday speech ("belajar sungguh-sungguh" = "study seriously") and exhortations ("bekerja sungguh-sungguh" = "work earnestly"). | [CORE] |
| kesungguhan | N/A | ke- + sungguh + -an (nominalization) | seriousness, earnestness, sincerity | Abstract noun for quality of being serious or earnest. Used in evaluations of character or effort ("kesungguhan hatinya" = "his sincerity of heart") and formal assessments. | [CORE] |
| bersungguh-sungguh | N/A | ber- + sungguh + reduplication (stative verb) | to be serious, to be earnest, to act seriously | Verbal form indicating state or action of being serious. Used in descriptions of behavior ("dia bersungguh-sungguh dalam pekerjaannya" = "he is serious in his work"). | [CORE] |
| menyungguhkan | N/A | meN- + sungguh + -kan (causative verb) | to make serious, to take seriously, to actualize | Less common causative form meaning to make something serious or real. Used in formal contexts ("menyungguhkan niat" = "to make intention real/serious"). | [MARGINAL] |
| sesungguh-sungguhnya | N/A | se- + sungguh + reduplication + -nya (superlative/emphatic) | most truly, in absolute truth, most earnestly | Emphatic superlative form used in solemn declarations and formal statements. Similar usage to sebenar-benarnya in oaths and testimony. | [CORE] |
| sungguhpun | N/A | sungguh + pun (concessive particle) | although, even though, despite | Concessive conjunction, more literary or formal register. Used to introduce contrasting clauses ("sungguhpun demikian" = "even so/nevertheless"). Less common in colloquial speech. | [MARGINAL] |
| kesungguh-sungguhan | N/A | ke- + sungguh + reduplication + -an | excessive seriousness, overly earnest | Rare form suggesting excessive or exaggerated seriousness. Limited colloquial usage. | [UNCERTAIN] |
| tersungguh | N/A | ter- + sungguh (accidental/superlative) | most genuine, most real | Rare superlative form. Occasionally appears in literary or formal texts but not common in everyday usage. | [MARGINAL] |
| mengesahkan | N/A | meN- + sah + -kan (causative verb) | to validate, to legalize, to ratify, to authorize | Transitive verb meaning to make something legally valid or official. Very common in legal and administrative contexts ("mengesahkan dokumen" = "to validate documents," "mengesahkan undang-undang" = "to ratify a law"). | [CORE] |
| pengesahan | N/A | peN- + sah + -an (nominalization of action) | validation, legalization, ratification, authorization | Noun referring to act or process of validation. Common in legal and administrative discourse ("pengesahan kontrak" = "contract validation," "pengesahan pernikahan" = "marriage legalization"). | [CORE] |
| disahkan | N/A | di- + sah + -kan (passive voice) | to be validated, to be legalized, to be ratified | Passive form indicating something is made valid or legal. Standard in official announcements ("telah disahkan" = "has been validated/ratified"). | [CORE] |
| keabsahan | N/A | ke- + absah + -an (nominalization) | validity, legitimacy, legality | Abstract noun for quality of being valid or legitimate. Used in legal and formal contexts ("keabsahan dokumen" = "validity of documents," "mempertanyakan keabsahan" = "to question the legitimacy"). | [CORE] |
| kesahan | N/A | ke- + sah + -an (nominalization) | validity, legitimacy | Synonym of keabsahan, though keabsahan is more common. Used interchangeably in formal contexts ("kesahan hukum" = "legal validity"). | [CORE] |
| sahih | N/A | sahih (from Arabic ṣaḥīḥ, related root) | valid, authentic, sound (especially of hadith) | Related term from Arabic, primarily used in Islamic religious contexts to evaluate authenticity of hadith ("hadis sahih" = "authentic hadith"). | mensahkan | N/A | meN- + sah + -kan (alternative form) | to validate, to legalize | Alternative form of mengesahkan, less common but still used. Same meaning and contexts as mengesahkan. | [MARGINAL] |
| tersah | N/A | ter- + sah (accidental passive) | accidentally validated, happened to be valid | Rare form suggesting accidental or unintentional validation. Very limited usage. | [UNCERTAIN] |
| sahkan | N/A | sah + -kan (imperative/bare causative) | validate!, legalize! (command form) | Imperative or bare causative form. Used in commands or instructions ("sahkan dokumen ini" = "validate this document"). | [CORE] |
| pengesah | N/A | peN- + sah (agent noun) | validator, authorizer, one who validates | Agent noun referring to person or entity that validates. Used in administrative contexts ("pengesah dokumen" = "document validator"). | [CORE] |
| sah-mengesahkan | N/A | sah + meN- + sah + -kan (compound) | mutual validation, to validate each other | Reciprocal form indicating mutual validation. Used in contexts where parties validate each other's positions or documents. | [MARGINAL] |
| kesahihan | N/A | ke- + sahih + -an (nominalization) | validity, authenticity, soundness | Abstract noun from sahih, particularly common in Islamic scholarship contexts ("kesahihan hadis" = "authenticity of hadith") but also used more broadly. | [CORE] |
| yang sah | N/A | yang (relativizer) + sah | the valid one, the legal one, the legitimate one | Relative clause construction identifying valid or legal item. Common in legal discourse ("dokumen yang sah" = "the valid document"). | [CORE] |
