# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P5 Oppositions
# Language: Chinese (zh)

---

## Opposition 1

**Term A:** 真 (zhēn)
**Term B:** 假 (jiǎ)
**Transliteration:** zhēn / jiǎ
**English Translation:** true, genuine / fake, false
**Description of Contrast:** Explicitly identified in P1 as direct antonyms across everyday, philosophical, and legal registers; 假 is described as "direct antonym of 真 in most contexts." The pair is lexicalized as a binomial 真假 (P1b A-11) used to frame the question of authenticity as an open question.
**Contrast Type:** lexical
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

## Opposition 2

**Term A:** 真 (zhēn)
**Term B:** 伪 (wěi)
**Transliteration:** zhēn / wěi
**English Translation:** genuine / false, counterfeit, spurious
**Description of Contrast:** The pair is lexicalized as the binomial 真伪 (P1 #17), described as "a fixed binomial used to frame the question of whether something is genuine or counterfeit." 伪 carries connotations of deliberate fabrication that 假 does not always imply; the contrast is specifically associated with authentication of artifacts, documents, and texts.
**Contrast Type:** lexical
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

## Opposition 3

**Term A:** 真实 (zhēnshí)
**Term B:** 虚假 (xūjiǎ)
**Transliteration:** zhēnshí / xūjiǎ
**English Translation:** truthful, authentic, factual / false, fictitious, fraudulent
**Description of Contrast:** These are the primary formal-register antonyms for evaluating whether accounts and representations correspond to reality; P1 describes 虚假 as used in legal and regulatory language (虚假广告, 虚假陈述) while 真实 is the positive evaluative term in the same contexts. The pair appears in contrastive constructions throughout P2b (e.g., 保证内容真实 vs. 作出虚假陈述).
**Contrast Type:** lexical
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

## Opposition 4

**Term A:** 真实 (zhēnshí)
**Term B:** 不真实 (bù zhēnshí)
**Transliteration:** zhēnshí / bù zhēnshí
**English Translation:** truthful, factual / not truthful, not factual
**Description of Contrast:** Standard morphological negation of 真实 with 不, documented in P2a (D-02) as the direct compositional negation used in legal and journalistic evaluation. Less formal than 虚假 and more directly compositional; the two negative forms (不真实 and 虚假) are not fully interchangeable.
**Contrast Type:** morphological
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

## Opposition 5

**Term A:** 正确 (zhèngquè)
**Term B:** 错误 (cuòwù)
**Transliteration:** zhèngquè / cuòwù
**English Translation:** correct, right / wrong, erroneous, mistaken
**Description of Contrast:** Explicitly identified in P1 as a contrastive pair: 正确 "contrasts with 错误 'wrong/erroneous'" and the two are described as "frequently paired in contrastive contexts." Both function as adjectives and nouns; the pair appears in the construction 纠正错误，走向正确 (P2b Construction 5.4).
**Contrast Type:** lexical
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

## Opposition 6

**Term A:** 正确 (zhèngquè)
**Term B:** 不正确 (bù zhèngquè)
**Transliteration:** zhèngquè / bù zhèngquè
**English Translation:** correct / incorrect, wrong
**Description of Contrast:** Standard morphological negation of 正确 with 不, documented in P2a (D-05) as "functionally equivalent to 错误 in many contexts but more directly compositional." The existence of both a lexical antonym (错误) and a morphological negation (不正确) for the same base is explicitly noted in P2a.
**Contrast Type:** morphological
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

## Opposition 7

**Term A:** 准确 (zhǔnquè)
**Term B:** 不准确 (bù zhǔnquè)
**Transliteration:** zhǔnquè / bù zhǔnquè
**English Translation:** accurate, precise / inaccurate, imprecise
**Description of Contrast:** Standard morphological negation of 准确 with 不, documented in P2a (D-08). Unlike 正确/错误, 准确 does not have a dedicated lexical antonym in the P1 data; its primary negation is morphological. The scalar nature of 准确 is reflected in degree constructions (数据不够准确, P2b Construction 9).
**Contrast Type:** morphological
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 8

**Term A:** 可靠 (kěkào)
**Term B:** 不可靠 (bù kěkào)
**Transliteration:** kěkào / bù kěkào
**English Translation:** reliable, dependable / unreliable, undependable
**Description of Contrast:** Standard morphological negation of 可靠 with 不, documented in P1 ("contrasts with 不可靠 'unreliable'") and P2a (D-12). P2a notes that "the negation is as frequent as the positive form," indicating that both poles are equally established in usage.
**Contrast Type:** morphological
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 9

**Term A:** 可信 (kěxìn)
**Term B:** 不可信 (bù kěxìn)
**Transliteration:** kěxìn / bù kěxìn
**English Translation:** credible, believable / not credible, unbelievable
**Description of Contrast:** Standard morphological negation of 可信 with 不, documented in P2a (D-15). Used in legal and journalistic evaluation of testimony and claims; the negated form evaluates sources or statements as failing to merit belief.
**Contrast Type:** morphological
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 10

**Term A:** 合法 (héfǎ)
**Term B:** 非法 (fēifǎ)
**Transliteration:** héfǎ / fēifǎ
**English Translation:** lawful, legal / illegal, unlawful
**Description of Contrast:** P1 explicitly identifies 非法 as the standard contrast term for 合法: "contrasts with 非法 'illegal/unlawful'." The negation uses 非- rather than 不-, which P2a notes is "more formal and restricted." 非法 is the established lexical antonym; 非合法 (D-18) is a more technical variant distinguished from it.
**Contrast Type:** lexical
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

## Opposition 11

**Term A:** 合法 (héfǎ)
**Term B:** 非合法 (fēi héfǎ)
**Transliteration:** héfǎ / fēi héfǎ
**English Translation:** lawful, legal / non-legal (not necessarily criminal)
**Description of Contrast:** Documented in P2a (D-18) as a more technical negation distinct from 非法: "used in more technical or analytical contexts to mark something as falling outside legal frameworks without necessarily implying criminal illegality." The distinction between 非合法 and 非法 is explicitly noted as "subtle and context-dependent."
**Contrast Type:** morphological
**Epistemic Status:** [MARGINAL]

## Opposition 12

**Term A:** 真诚 (zhēnchéng)
**Term B:** 虚伪 (xūwěi)
**Transliteration:** zhēnchéng / xūwěi
**English Translation:** sincere, genuine / hypocritical, insincere, two-faced
**Description of Contrast:** P1 explicitly identifies 虚伪 as the contrast term for 真诚: 真诚 "contrasts with 虚伪 'hypocritical/insincere'." The opposition is between outward expression that reflects genuine inner state (真诚) and outward expression that conceals a contrary inner state (虚伪). P3 places them in the same semantic group (Group 6).
**Contrast Type:** lexical
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 13

**Term A:** 客观 (kèguān)
**Term B:** 主观 (zhǔguān)
**Transliteration:** kèguān / zhǔguān
**English Translation:** objective, impartial / subjective
**Description of Contrast:** P1 explicitly identifies 主观 as the contrast term for 客观: 客观 "contrasts with 主观 'subjective'." The opposition is between assessments based on external facts (客观) and those based on personal perspective or bias (主观). The pair is a standard binary in Chinese journalistic, scholarly, and legal evaluation.
**Contrast Type:** lexical
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 14

**Term A:** 公正 (gōngzhèng)
**Term B:** 不公正 (bù gōngzhèng)
**Transliteration:** gōngzhèng / bù gōngzhèng
**English Translation:** just, fair, impartial / unjust, unfair, partial
**Description of Contrast:** Standard morphological negation of 公正 with 不, documented in P2a (D-21). No dedicated lexical antonym for 公正 is identified in the P1 data; the primary negation is morphological. Used in moral and legal evaluation of decisions, conduct, and persons.
**Contrast Type:** morphological
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 15

**Term A:** 名副其实 (míng fù qí shí)
**Term B:** 名不副实 (míng bù fù shí)
**Transliteration:** míng fù qí shí / míng bù fù shí
**English Translation:** worthy of the name, genuinely what it claims to be / not living up to its name, more nominal than real
**Description of Contrast:** P1 (#19) explicitly notes that "its negation 名不副实 ('the name does not match the reality') is equally common," and P1b (B-22) documents 名不副实 as a separate entry. The negation is internal to the four-character structure (不 inserted before 副), making this a morphological negation within a fixed expression rather than a free compositional negation.
**Contrast Type:** morphological
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

## Opposition 16

**Term A:** 名实相符 (míngshí xiāngfú)
**Term B:** 名实不符 (míngshí bù fú)
**Transliteration:** míngshí xiāngfú / míngshí bù fú
**English Translation:** the name and reality correspond / the name and reality do not correspond
**Description of Contrast:** Both forms are documented in P2b (Constructions 28.1 and 28.2) as a contrastive pair in philosophical and evaluative discourse. The negation is constructional: 不 is inserted before the verb 符 within the fixed frame 名实 + [correspondence verb]. More classical in register than 名副其实 / 名不副实.
**Contrast Type:** constructional
**Epistemic Status:** [MARGINAL]

---

## Opposition 17

**Term A:** 与事实相符 (yǔ shìshí xiāngfú)
**Term B:** 与事实不相符 / 与事实不符 (yǔ shìshí bù xiāngfú)
**Transliteration:** yǔ shìshí xiāngfú / yǔ shìshí bù xiāngfú
**English Translation:** consistent with the facts / not consistent with the facts
**Description of Contrast:** Documented in P2b (Constructions 18.1 and 18.2) and P4a (Construction 10) as the positive and negative poles of the Correspondence Frame. The negation is constructional: 不 is inserted before 相符 within the fixed 与 + X + 相符 frame. This is the primary syntactic mechanism in Chinese institutional discourse for asserting or denying correspondence between a claim and reality.
**Contrast Type:** constructional
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

## Opposition 18

**Term A:** 揭露真相 (jiēlù zhēnxiàng)
**Term B:** 掩盖真相 (yǎngài zhēnxiàng)
**Transliteration:** jiēlù zhēnxiàng / yǎngài zhēnxiàng
**English Translation:** to expose / reveal the truth / to cover up / conceal the truth
**Description of Contrast:** Documented in P2b (Constructions 8.1 and 8.3) and P4a (Construction 12) as the two poles of the Concealment/Revelation Frame. The contrast is constructional: the same nominal object (真相) is combined with semantically opposed verb classes — reveal-verbs (揭露, 查明, 还原) vs. conceal-verbs (掩盖, 隐瞒). P4a notes that "the verb slot is not freely filled" and that the construction "presupposes that the truth is hidden."
**Contrast Type:** constructional
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 19

**Term A:** 实话 (shíhuà)
**Term B:** 假话 (jiǎhuà)
**Transliteration:** shíhuà / jiǎhuà
**English Translation:** truthful words, honest speech / false words, lies
**Description of Contrast:** P1b (B-11) explicitly notes that 实话 "contrasts with 假话 'false words'." Both are colloquial compound nouns formed by the same pattern ([truth-value morpheme] + 话 "speech/words"), making this a lexical opposition with parallel morphological structure. The pair frames the truth-value of speech at the level of individual utterances.
**Contrast Type:** lexical
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

## Opposition 20

**Term A:** 真相大白 (zhēnxiàng dàbái)
**Term B:** 不明真相 (bù míng zhēnxiàng)
**Transliteration:** zhēnxiàng dàbái / bù míng zhēnxiàng
**English Translation:** the truth comes to light, the true facts are fully revealed / not knowing the true situation, unaware of the facts
**Description of Contrast:** Both are documented in P2b (Constructions 8.4 and 8.6) as fixed constructions involving 真相. They represent opposite epistemic states with respect to the same object: in 真相大白 the truth has become known (resultative); in 不明真相 the truth remains unknown to the subject. The contrast is constructional rather than a simple lexical or morphological negation.
**Contrast Type:** constructional
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 21

**Term A:** 经查属实 (jīng chá shǔshí)
**Term B:** 经查失实 (jīng chá shīshí)
**Transliteration:** jīng chá shǔshí / jīng chá shīshí
**English Translation:** verified as true upon investigation / found to be inaccurate upon investigation
**Description of Contrast:** Both are documented in P4a (Construction 11) and P2b (Constructions 15.3 and 30.3) as formulaic administrative outcome phrases sharing the same verification frame (经 + investigation verb) but with opposed result predicates (属实 "confirmed as accurate" vs. 失实 "found to be inaccurate"). The contrast is constructional: the frame is identical; only the result predicate differs.
**Contrast Type:** constructional
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 22

**Term A:** 伪造者 (wěizàozhě)
**Term B:** *(no attested parallel truth-telling agentive)*
**Transliteration:** wěizàozhě / —
**English Translation:** forger, counterfeiter / —
**Description of Contrast:** P4b explicitly documents an asymmetry in the agentive -者 construction: "No parallel truth-telling agentive (e.g., *真实者, *如实者) is attested in common usage." The -者 agentive is productive for deception-role agents (伪造者, 假冒者) but not for truth-telling roles. This is a one-sided opposition: a role is lexicalized on one pole but not the other.
**Contrast Type:** morphological
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 23

**Term A:** 真实性 (zhēnshíxìng) — property as categorical
**Term B:** 可信度 (kěxìndù) — property as scalar and measurable
**Transliteration:** zhēnshíxìng / kěxìndù
**English Translation:** truthfulness, authenticity (as a property) / degree of credibility, credibility level (as a scalar measure)
**Description of Contrast:** P2a documents both -性 and -度 as nominalizing suffixes but explicitly states they "are not interchangeable: -性 denotes the property as such; -度 denotes its measurable extent." P4b reiterates that "their distribution is determined by whether the property is treated as categorical (-性) or scalar and measurable (-度)." This is a constructional contrast between two nominalizing strategies applied to related but distinct bases.
**Contrast Type:** constructional
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 24

**Term A:** 去伪存真 (qù wěi cún zhēn)
**Term B:** 以假乱真 (yǐ jiǎ luàn zhēn)
**Transliteration:** qù wěi cún zhēn / yǐ jiǎ luàn zhēn
**English Translation:** eliminate the false and retain the true / use fakes to confuse the genuine
**Description of Contrast:** Both are four-character expressions documented in P1b (C-16) and P2b (Construction 2.3) respectively. They represent opposed orientations toward the genuine/false distinction: 去伪存真 describes a process of critical discrimination that preserves the genuine, while 以假乱真 describes a process of deliberate confusion that undermines the ability to distinguish genuine from false. The contrast is lexical at the level of fixed expressions.
**Contrast Type:** lexical
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 25

**Term A:** 写实 (xiěshí) / 写实主义 (xiěshí zhǔyì)
**Term B:** 写意 (xiěyì)
**Transliteration:** xiěshí / xiěshí zhǔyì / xiěyì
**English Translation:** realistic depiction, realism / impressionistic or expressive style
**Description of Contrast:** P1b (B-26) explicitly notes that 写实 "contrasts with 写意 'impressionistic/expressive style'" in aesthetic and literary criticism. The opposition is between an aesthetic approach that aims to represent reality faithfully (写实) and one that prioritizes subjective expression or impression over faithful representation (写意). This is a lexical opposition within the domain of aesthetic evaluation.
**Contrast Type:** lexical
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 26

**Term A:** 真理 (zhēnlǐ) — abstract universal truth
**Term B:** 真相 (zhēnxiàng) — specific factual truth of a situation
**Transliteration:** zhēnlǐ / zhēnxiàng
**English Translation:** truth as a universal principle / the true facts of a particular situation
**Description of Contrast:** P1b (A-08) explicitly distinguishes these: 真理 "refers to abstract or universal propositions rather than specific facts," while 真相 "refers to the actual state of affairs behind appearances." P3 (Group 7 notes) reiterates: "真理 refers to abstract or universal propositions while 真相 refers to the specific facts of a particular situation." Both contain the root 真 but occupy distinct semantic positions within the truth domain.
**Contrast Type:** lexical
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 27

**Term A:** 本质 (běnzhì) — philosophical essence
**Term B:** 实质 (shízhì) — practical or legal substance
**Transliteration:** běnzhì / shízhì
**English Translation:** essence, fundamental nature / substance, real nature (practical/legal)
**Description of Contrast:** P1 explicitly distinguishes these: 本质 "tends to foreground philosophical essence" while 实质 "tends to foreground practical or legal substance over philosophical essence." P3 places them together as items that "distinguish fundamental or substantive reality from surface or formal appearance" but notes they are not fully interchangeable. The contrast is between two near-synonyms differentiated by register and domain of application.
**Contrast Type:** lexical
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 28

**Term A:** 可靠 (kěkào) — reliability of sources and systems
**Term B:** 可信 (kěxìn) — credibility of statements and testimony
**Transliteration:** kěkào / kěxìn
**English Translation:** reliable, dependable (of sources, systems, persons) / credible, believable (of statements, testimony, claims)
**Description of Contrast:** P1 and P3 (Group 10 notes) explicitly distinguish these by their typical objects: 可靠 "applies to sources, systems, channels, and persons as dependable entities" while 可信 "applies more specifically to statements, testimony, and claims as meriting belief." P3 notes this distinction is "supported by their different collocates." The two terms are near-synonyms that are not fully interchangeable.
**Contrast Type:** lexical
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 29

**Term A:** 失实 (shīshí) — inaccuracy of verbal/written accounts
**Term B:** 失真 (shīzhēn) — distortion of representations (verbal and technical)
**Transliteration:** shīshí / shīzhēn
**English Translation:** inaccurate, contrary to fact (of reports and statements) / distorted, loss of fidelity (of reports and technical signals)
**Description of Contrast:** P3 (Group 2 notes) explicitly distinguishes these: "失实 and 失真 overlap in media criticism contexts but 失真 extends into technical domains (audio distortion) where 失实 does not appear." Both describe deviation from accurate representation but 失真 has a broader domain of application that includes technical signal processing, while 失实 is restricted to verbal and written accounts.
**Contrast Type:** lexical
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 30

**Term A:** 形式 (xíngshì) — formal or nominal appearance
**Term B:** 实质 (shízhì) — substantive reality
**Transliteration:** xíngshì / shízhì
**English Translation:** form, formality, nominal appearance / substance, substantive reality
**Description of Contrast:** P2b (Construction 10.3) documents the fixed contrastive construction 流于形式，缺乏实质 ("to become merely formal, lacking substance") and describes it as "used to criticize procedures or actions that are formally correct but substantively empty." The 形式/实质 contrast is a recurrent structural opposition in administrative and critical discourse, not merely an incidental pairing.
**Contrast Type:** lexical
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 31

**Term A:** 真凭实据 (zhēn píng shí jù)
**Term B:** *(no attested fixed-expression antonym)*
**Transliteration:** zhēn píng shí jù / —
**English Translation:** solid evidence, concrete proof / —
**Description of Contrast:** P1b (A-13) documents 真凭实据 as a four-character expression used "to demand or assert that claims are backed by genuine, verifiable evidence." No fixed-expression antonym for the absence of such evidence is identified in the data. This is a one-sided entry: the expression asserts an evidential standard but no parallel fixed expression for the failure to meet that standard is attested in the corpus.
**Contrast Type:** lexical
**Epistemic Status:** [UNCERTAIN]
