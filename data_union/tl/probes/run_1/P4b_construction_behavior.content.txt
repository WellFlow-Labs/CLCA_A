ATTRIBUTION** | Totoo ang balita. | Totoo ang balita. | "The news is true." | [CORE] | The predicate-initial pattern is the default declarative structure for truth-domain adjectives in Tagalog; attested across all registers and media contexts. No observable restriction on which P1 item occupies the predicate slot. |
| **1. ATTRIBUTION** | Tama ang sagot mo. | Tama ang sagot mo. | "Your answer is correct." | [CORE] | Equally natural in spoken and written registers; *tama* is among the highest-frequency items in this slot. |

NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION** | Hindi totoo ang alegasyon. | Hindi totoo ang alegasyon. | "The allegation is not true." | [CORE] | *Hindi* negation of predicative truth-domain adjectives is fully productive and attested in news, legal, and everyday discourse. | **2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION** | Hindi tapat ang kanyang mga salita. | Hindi tapat ang kanyang mga salita. | "His/her words are not honest." | [CORE] | No restriction observed; *hindi* + truth-domain adjective applies across the full P1 inventory in this construction. |
| **3. ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION** | Tunay na kaibigan | Tunay na kaibigan | "A genuine friend" | [CORE] | Prenominal position is strongly preferred; the pattern is productive across the full P1 inventory. Postnominal order is grammatical but less frequent in attested usage. |
| **3. ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION** | Huwad na dokumento | Huwad na dokumento | "A forged document" | [CORE] | *Huwad* and *peke* in this slot are strongly associated with physical objects (documents, currency, goods); their use with abstract nouns is less attested. |
| **4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE** | Maging tapat ka sa akin. | Maging tapat ka sa akin. | "Be honest with me." | [CORE] | Most naturally attested with *tapat* and *totoo* in interpersonal and moral contexts; functions as a directive or exhortation. |
| **4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE** | Maging totoo ka. | Maging totoo ka. | "Be real / Be truthful." | [CORE] | Common in spoken registers as a direct appeal. The construction is [MARGINAL] with *huwad* and *peke*, which describe object properties rather than states a person enters into. |
| **5. CAUSATIVE** | Patunayan mo ang iyong sinasabi. | Patunayan mo ang iyong sinasabi. | "Prove what you are saying." | [CORE] | *Patunayan* is a high-frequency lexical item in argumentative, legal, and journalistic discourse; the causative-verificational meaning is fully conventionalized. |
| **5. CAUSATIVE** | Itama ang iyong mga pagkakamali. | Itama ang iyong mga pagkakamali. | "Correct your mistakes." | [CORE] | *Itama* and *iwasto* are attested in instructional, editorial, and corrective contexts. These are lexically specific forms; the pattern does not extend productively to all P1 roots. |
| **6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION** | Ang katotohanan ay mahalaga. | Ang katotohanan ay mahalaga. | "Truth is important." | [CORE] | *Ka-...-an* nominalizations (*katotohanan*, *katapatan*, *katunayan*, *kawastuhan*) are fully lexicalized and attested in formal, philosophical, and everyday registers. The pattern is productive across the P1 inventory. |
| **6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION** | Ang pagtapat niya ay nagulat sa lahat. | Ang pagtapat niya ay nagulat sa lahat. | "His/her honest disclosure surprised everyone." | [CORE] | *Pag-* action nominalizations (*pagtapat*, *pagtotoo*, *pagpapatunay*) are attested in narrative and reportorial contexts; they foreground the act rather than the abstract property. |
| **7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION** | Sinungaling ka! | Sinungaling ka! | "You are a liar!" | [CORE] | *Sinungaling* is a fully lexicalized root used freely in accusatory and colloquial contexts; it is not transparently derived from a P1 truth-domain adjective. No productive agentive morpheme is attested for this domain. |
| **7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION** | Ang mamamahayag ay tagapagtanggol ng katotohanan. | Ang mamamahayag ay tagapagtanggol ng katotohanan. | "The journalist is a defender of truth." | [MARGINAL] | *Tagapagtanggol ng katotohanan* is a compound construction rather than a simple derivation; it is attested in formal and rhetorical registers but is not a routine everyday expression. |
| **8. EMPHATIC INTENSIFICATION (REDUPLICATION)** | Totoong-totoo ito, hindi biro. | Totoong-totoo ito, hindi biro. | "This is absolutely real, not a joke." | [CORE] | Full reduplication with linker is productive across the P1 inventory and is attested in spoken and informal written registers; it characteristically occurs in contexts of expressed or anticipated doubt. |
| **8. EMPHATIC INTENSIFICATION (REDUPLICATION)** | Tunay na tunay ang kanyang pagmamahal. | Tunay na tunay ang kanyang pagmamahal. | "His/her love is absolutely genuine." | [CORE] | The *na* linker variant (rather than *-ng*) appears where the adjective is vowel-initial or where phonological context requires it; the emphatic function is identical across both linker forms. |
| **9. APPEARANCE / EVIDENTIAL FRAMING** | Mukhang totoo ang produkto pero peke pala. | Mukhang totoo ang produkto pero peke pala. | "The product looked real but turned out to be fake." | [CORE] | *Mukhang* is attested as a visual-evidential marker in consumer, journalistic, and everyday contexts; the construction frequently co-occurs with a corrective clause (*pero pala*) that resolves the appearance against the truth-domain standard. |
| **9. APPEARANCE / EVIDENTIAL FRAMING** | Parang totoo ang kanyang pagiyak. | Parang totoo ang kanyang pagiyak. | "Her crying seemed real." | [CORE] | *Parang* marks resemblance without asserting truth; it is attested in evaluative and narrative contexts. Both *mukhang* and *parang* occupy a distinct evidential slot not present in the standard attribution pattern. |
| **10. DISCOURSE-FRAMING CONSTRUCTIONS** | Sa totoo lang, hindi ko alam. | Sa totoo lang, hindi ko alam. | "To tell the truth, I don't know." | [CORE] | *Sa totoo lang* is a high-frequency sentence-initial frame in spoken and informal written Tagalog; it signals candor and is not restricted by topic or register within conversational contexts. |
| **10. DISCOURSE-FRAMING CONSTRUCTIONS** | Ang totoo nito, mas kumplikado ang sitwasyon. | Ang totoo nito, mas kumplikado ang sitwasyon. | "The truth of the matter is, the situation is more complicated." | [CORE] | *Ang totoo* and *ang totoo nito* function as corrective discourse operators and are attested in journalistic, analytical, and argumentative writing. No equivalent discourse-framing construction is attested for *mali*, *huwad*, or *peke*. |

## Cross-Construction Observations

- The [MARGINAL] ratings cluster around two conditions: (a) constructions that require a human subject entering a state (*maging* + object-property adjectives *huwad*, *peke*) and (b) agentive nominalizations, where no productive morphological pattern exists and attested forms are either lexicalized (*sinungaling*) or compound (*tagapagtanggol ng katotohanan*).
- Discourse-framing constructions (10) are asymmetric: they are attested only for positive truth-domain items (*totoo*, *katunayan*), not for *mali*, *huwad*, or *peke*. This asymmetry is reported as observed, not explained.
- No [UNCERTAIN] ratings were assigned; all constructions in P4a are supported by attested examples with sufficient contextual information to assign a naturalness rating.
