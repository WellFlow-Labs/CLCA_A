# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2a Derivational System
# Language: Finnish (fi)
# Task: Attested derivational morphology from P1 base items

---

## Methodological note

The following documents derivational forms attested for each P1 base item. Where a P1 item is itself already a derived form (e.g., *totuus* derived from *tosi*, *aitous* from *aito*), derivations from that derived form are included if independently attested. Forms already appearing in P1 are cross-referenced rather than re-documented. Asymmetries in derivational productivity are preserved and noted where evident.

## P1 Item 1: *tosi*

---

**1.**
**Base Form:** tosi
**Derived Form:** totuus
**Transliteration:** N/A
**Literal Breakdown:** *tot-* (allomorph of *tosi* before *-uus*) + *-uus* (abstract nominal suffix)
**Natural English translation:** truth
**Usage:** Abstract noun denoting the property of being true or a true state of affairs. Already documented as P1 item 2; cross-referenced here as the primary nominal derivation from *tosi*.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

**2.**
**Base Form:** tosi

**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**3.**

**Usage:** Used in technical, administrative, and IT security contexts for the process of verifying that something is genuine or that a claim is accurate. *Todentaa henkilöllisyys* (to verify identity); *kaksivaiheinen todentaminen* (two-factor authentication).

**Note on *tosi*:** This base shows unusually high compounding productivity, particularly with nouns (*tosimaailma*, *tosielämä*, *tositapahtuma*, *tosiseikka*, *tosipohjainen*). The allomorphic variation (*tosi-* / *tod-* / *tot-* / *toden-*) is conditioned by the following suffix and is a regular feature of this root. The verbal derivation *todistaa* is itself highly productive (see P1 item 16 and its own derivational network below).

**Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**Note on *totuus*:** Derivational productivity from *totuus* falls into two distinct clusters: (a) genitive-compound forms (*totuuden-* + *mukainen/vastainen*) used in legal and evaluative registers, and (b) direct compounds (*totuus* + noun) concentrated in technical philosophical and logical vocabulary. The *-llinen* derivation (*totuudellinen*) is marginal, suggesting that this adjectival suffix is not fully productive on this base.

## P1 Item 3: *aito*

---

**1.**
**Base Form:** aito
**Derived Form:** aitous
**Transliteration:** N/A
**Literal Breakdown:** *aito* + *-us* (abstract nominal suffix; vowel harmony variant *-uus* not applicable here due to front vowel environment — note: *aitous* uses *-us* with the stem)
**Natural English translation:** authenticity; genuineness
**Usage:** Abstract noun denoting the quality of being genuine or authentic. Already documented as P1 item 4; cross-referenced here as the primary nominal derivation from *aito*.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**2.**
**Base Form:** aito
**Derived Form:** epäaito
**Transliteration:** N/A
**Literal Breakdown:** *epä-* (negating prefix) + *aito*
**Natural English translation:** inauthentic; fake; spurious; not genuine
**Usage:** Already documented as P1 item 29; cross-referenced here as the primary prefixal negation of *aito*. Applied to objects, emotions, and conduct.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**3.**
**Base Form:** aito
**Derived Form:** epäaitous
**Transliteration:** N/A
**Literal Breakdown:** *epä-* + *aitous*
**Natural English translation:** inauthenticity; spuriousness
**Usage:** Abstract noun corresponding to *epäaito*. Used in philosophical and aesthetic contexts to name the quality of being inauthentic. Less frequent than *epäaito* itself.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**4.**
**Base Form:** aito
**Derived Form:** aidonkaltainen
**Transliteration:** N/A
**Literal Breakdown:** *aidon* (genitive of *aito*) + *kaltainen* (resembling, of the kind of)
**Natural English translation:** genuine-seeming; resembling the authentic; authentic-looking
**Usage:** Used to describe something that resembles or appears to be genuine without necessarily being so. Appears in evaluative and critical contexts, including art authentication and consumer protection.
**Epistemic status:** [UNCERTAIN]

---

**Note on *aito*:** The derivational network of *aito* is relatively limited compared to *tosi* or *oikea*. The primary productive pattern is the *epä-* negation (*epäaito*, *epäaitous*). The *-us* abstract nominal (*aitous*) is well-attested. No verbal derivation directly from *aito* is identified; the related verbal concept is expressed periphrastically or through *todentaa*.

## P1 Item 4: *aitous*

---

**1.**
**Base Form:** aitous
**Derived Form:** aitoustodistus
**Transliteration:** N/A
**Literal Breakdown:** *aitous* + *todistus* (certificate, proof)
**Natural English translation:** certificate of authenticity

---

**1.**
**Base Form:** oikea

---

**4.**
**Base Form:** oikea

**Transliteration:** N/A
**Literal Breakdown:** *oikea* + *-sti* (adverbial suffix)
**Natural English translation:** really; genuinely; actually; truly
**Usage:** Colloquial adverb used to assert that something is genuinely or actually the case, often contrasting with appearance or expectation. *Oikeasti se on niin* (it really is so). Very common in spoken Finnish.

**5.**

**Usage:** Used to assert that something provides sufficient grounds or authorization for an action or claim. *Mikään ei oikeuta tätä* (nothing justifies this). Common in legal, moral, and argumentative contexts.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**6.**
**Base Form:** oikea
**Derived Form:** oikeakielisyys
**Transliteration:** N/A
**Literal Breakdown:** *oikea* + *kielinen* (language-related, from *kieli*, language) + *-yys*
**Natural English translation:** linguistic correctness; standard language use; prescriptive correctness
**Usage:** Refers to conformity with prescriptive norms of standard Finnish. *Oikeakielisyyden vaatimukset* (the requirements of linguistic correctness). Used in language planning and education contexts.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**7.**
**Base Form:** oikea
**Derived Form:** oikeaoppinen
**Transliteration:** N/A
**Literal Breakdown:** *oikea* + *oppinen* (doctrine-holding, from *oppi*, doctrine/teaching)

**8.**
**Base Form:** oikea

**Natural English translation:** orthodoxy; doctrinal correctness
**Usage:** Abstract noun for the quality of being orthodox or doctrinally correct. Used in religious, political, and intellectual discourse. *Oikeaoppisuuden vaatimus* (the demand for orthodoxy).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**9.**
**Base Form:** oikea
**Derived Form:** oikeamielinen
**Transliteration:** N/A
**Literal Breakdown:** *oikea* + *mielinen* (minded, from *mieli*, mind)

**Usage:** Used to describe a person as having correct moral attitudes or a sense of justice. *Oikeamielinen ihminen* (a right-minded, fair person). Somewhat literary.
**Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**10.**
**Base Form:** oikea
**Derived Form:** oikeamielisyys
**Transliteration:** N/A
**Literal Breakdown:** *oikeamielinen* + *-yys*
**Natural English translation:** right-mindedness; fair-mindedness; uprightness
**Usage:** Abstract noun for the quality of being right-minded or fair. Used in moral and character evaluation contexts.
**Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**Note on *oikea*:** This base is exceptionally productive. The derivation *oikeus* is itself a major derivational base generating an extensive legal vocabulary network (documented separately under P1 item 5 notes). The adverbial forms *oikein* and *oikeasti* are both high-frequency. Compound formations with *oikea-* as first element (*oikeakielinen*, *oikeaoppinen*, *oikeamielinen*) follow a consistent pattern but vary in frequency and register.
