# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — F4 Construction Table
# Language: Turkish (tr)

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Usage Notes | Epistemic Status |
|------------------|---------|-----------------|-------------|-------------|------------------|
| **1. ATTRIBUTION** | Bu doğru | bu doğru | This is true | Extremely common in everyday speech and writing. Zero copula is the unmarked pattern for present tense third person. Used across all registers. | [CORE] |
| **1. ATTRIBUTION** | Söylediğin gerçek değil | söylediğin gerçek değil | What you said is not true/real | Standard negative attribution. No register restrictions. | [CORE] |
| **1. ATTRIBUTION (Alternate)** | Bu doğrudur (with overt copula -dır) | bu doğrudur | This is true | Formal/written register variant. Adds emphatic or authoritative tone. Less common in speech. | [MARGINAL] |
| **2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION** | Bu doğru değil | bu doğru değil | This is not true/correct | The standard and only productive negative copula pattern for present tense. No register or context restrictions. | [CORE] |
| **2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION** | Gerçek değil, yalan | gerçek değil, yalan | It's not true, it's a lie | Common contrastive pattern. Used in argumentative and explanatory contexts. | [CORE] |
| **3. ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION** | doğru bilgi | doğru bilgi | correct information, true information | Highly productive across all noun types. No restrictions on noun class (abstract or concrete). Used in all registers. | [CORE] |
| **3. ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION** | yanlış anlama | yanlış anlama | misunderstanding, wrong understanding | Productive with both concrete and abstract nouns. Common in everyday usage. | [CORE] |
| **4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE** | Gerçeğe dönüştü | gerçeğe dönüştü | It turned into reality | More formal/literary. Emphasizes transformation process. Less common than *çıkmak* pattern. | [MARGINAL] |
| **4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE** | Doğru çıktı | doğru çıktı | It turned out to be true | Very common in everyday speech. Emphasizes revelation or discovery of truth-value. Preferred over *dönüşmek* in colloquial contexts. | [CORE] |
| **5. CAUSATIVE** | Gerçekleştirmek | gerçekleştirmek | to realize, to make real | The only productive morphological causative in truth domain. Common in all registers. Specifically means "bring about, actualize." | [CORE] |
| **5. CAUSATIVE** | Doğru göstermek | doğru göstermek | to show to be true, to demonstrate truth | Light verb construction is preferred over morphological causative for most truth-domain causation. Common in argumentative and evidential contexts. | [CORE] |
| **5. CAUSATIVE (Alternates)** | Doğru kabul etmek, gerçeği ortaya çıkarmak | doğru kabul etmek, gerçeği ortaya çıkarmak | to accept as true, to reveal the truth | Multiple light verb strategies are more productive than morphological causatives. Each has specific semantic nuances. | [CORE] |
| **6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION** | doğruluk | doğruluk | truth, truthfulness, correctness | Extremely productive pattern. Used across all registers. Creates quality/state nouns from virtually all truth-domain adjectives. | [CORE] |
| **6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION** | gerçeklik | gerçeklik | reality, realness | Fully productive. No restrictions. Common in both everyday and philosophical/academic discourse. | [CORE] |
| **7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION** | yalancı | yalancı | liar | The only lexicalized agentive form in truth domain. Common in everyday speech. | [CORE] |
| **7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION** | doğru söyleyen (kişi) | doğru söyleyen (kişi) | truth-teller, one who tells the truth | Periphrastic construction is the productive pattern. *Kişi* "person" is often optional. Used when agentive meaning needed for truth-tellers. | [CORE] |
| **8. VERBAL NOUN FORMATION** | doğrulama | doğrulama | verification, confirmation | Highly productive across all truth-domain verbs. Common in technical and everyday contexts. Denotes process/action rather than state. | [CORE] |
| **8. VERBAL NOUN FORMATION** | gerçekleşme | gerçekleşme | realization, occurrence | Productive pattern. Used in all registers. Clearly distinguished from *-lık/-lik* abstract nouns by process/event semantics. | [CORE] |
| **9. LIGHT VERB: *etmek*** | hak etmek | hak etmek | to deserve, to merit | Extremely common. Used with loan nouns (Arabic/Persian) primarily. Productive pattern for verbalizing abstract nouns. | [CORE] |
| **9. LIGHT VERB: *etmek*** | kabul etmek | kabul etmek | to accept | Very common in constructions like *doğru kabul etmek* "accept as true." No register restrictions. | [CORE] |
| **10. LIGHT VERB: *olmak*** | doğru olmak | doğru olmak | to be true | Functions as copula (stative). Often zero in present tense third person, but overt in other contexts. Used across all registers. | [CORE] |
| **10. LIGHT VERB: *olmak*** | gerçek olmak | gerçek olmak | to be real | Copular function is primary. Inchoative reading ("become real") possible in some contexts but less common than dedicated inchoative patterns. | [CORE] |
| **10. LIGHT VERB: *olmak* (Alternate)** | Zero copula in present tense | Bu doğru | This is true | Zero copula (*Bu doğru*) is more common than overt *olmak* in present tense third person. | [CORE] |
| **11. LIGHT VERB: *çıkmak*** | doğru çıkmak | doğru çıkmak | to turn out to be true, to prove correct | Very common in everyday speech. Emphasizes revelation/discovery of previously uncertain truth-value. Preferred inchoative pattern for truth-domain. | [CORE] |
| **11. LIGHT VERB: *çıkmak*** | sahte çıkmak | sahte çıkmak | to turn out to be fake, to be revealed as counterfeit | Productive with all truth-value adjectives. Common in narrative and evidential contexts. | [CORE] |
| **12. LIGHT VERB: *vermek*** | hak vermek | hak vermek | to agree with, to acknowledge someone is right | Very common idiom. Used in argumentative and interpersonal contexts. Means "to side with, to agree with." | [CORE] |
| **12. LIGHT VERB: *vermek*** | hakkını vermek | hakkını vermek | to give credit, to do justice to | Common expression. Requires possessive marking on noun. Used when acknowledging merit or giving due recognition. | [CORE] |
| **13. LIGHT VERB: *almak*** | hakkını almak | hakkını almak | to get one's due, to obtain justice | Common expression. Contrasts with *hakkını vermek* by emphasizing active claiming rather than granting. Used in justice/fairness contexts. | [CORE] |
| **14. LIGHT VERB: *söylemek*** | doğruyu söylemek | doğruyu söylemek | to tell the truth | Very common. Shows accusative marking (*-yu*). Standard expression for truthful speech. | [CORE] |
| **14. LIGHT VERB: *söylemek*** | yalan söylemek | yalan söylemek | to tell a lie | Extremely common. Notably lacks accusative marking (unlike *doğruyu söylemek*). This asymmetry is lexicalized. | [CORE] |
| **14. LIGHT VERB: *söylemek* (Alternate)** | doğrusunu söylemek | doğrusunu söylemek | to tell the truth, to be frank | Idiomatic variant with possessive + accusative. Slightly more emphatic or colloquial than *doğruyu söylemek*. | [CORE] |
| **15. LIGHT VERB: *bulmak*** | doğru bulmak | doğru bulmak | to find correct, to approve of, to consider right | Common in evaluative contexts. Expresses subjective judgment rather than objective discovery. Used across registers. | [CORE] |
| **15. LIGHT VERB: *bulmak* (Alternates)** | uygun bulmak, yerinde bulmak | uygun bulmak, yerinde bulmak | to find appropriate, to find justified | Similar evaluative constructions with other adjectives. Pattern is productive for judgment expressions. | [CORE] |
| **16. LIGHT VERB: *görmek*** | uygun görmek | uygun görmek | to deem appropriate, to consider suitable | Common in formal/official contexts. More formal register than *bulmak* equivalent. | [CORE] |
| **16. LIGHT VERB: *görmek*** | doğru görmek | doğru görmek | to consider right, to deem correct | Less common than *doğru bulmak*. Tends toward formal register. Used in official/bureaucratic contexts. | [MARGINAL] |
| **17. POSTPOSITION: *hakkında*** | bu konu hakkında | bu konu hakkında | about this subject | Fully grammaticalized postposition. No truth-evaluation function. Common across all registers for expressing "aboutness." | [CORE] |
| **17. POSTPOSITION: *hakkında*** | gerçek hakkında | gerçek hakkında | about the truth/reality | Standard postpositional use. No special truth-domain semantics. | [CORE] |
| **18. ABLATIVE ADVERBIAL** | gerçekten | gerçekten | really, truly | Extremely common adverb. Used as intensifier and manner adverb. No register restrictions. | [CORE] |
| **18. ABLATIVE ADVERBIAL** | doğrudan | doğrudan | directly | Common adverb meaning "directly, straight." Semantic shift from "true" to "direct" is complete. | [CORE] |
| **18. ABLATIVE ADVERBIAL (Alternate)** | hakikaten | hakikaten | truly, really | Synonym of *gerçekten*. Slightly more formal/literary. Both are common. | [CORE] |
| **19. INSTRUMENTAL ADVERBIAL** | hakkıyla | hakkıyla | properly, duly, as it deserves | Common manner adverb. Requires possessive marking. Used to emphasize proper or complete manner of action. | [CORE] |
| **19. INSTRUMENTAL ADVERBIAL** | tam anlamıyla | tam anlamıyla | in the full sense, completely | Common emphatic expression. Shows productive instrumental pattern with possessed abstract nouns. | [CORE] |
| **20. COMPARATIVE** | yalandan daha kötü | yalandan daha kötü | worse than a lie | Truth-value terms appear in standard of comparison but rarely as compared element. Used for rhetorical emphasis. | [MARGINAL] |
| **20. COMPARATIVE** | gerçekten daha garip | gerçekten daha garip | stranger than reality/truth | Idiomatic expression. Truth-value terms function as comparison standard, not as gradable qualities themselves. | [MARGINAL] |
| **21. EQUATIVE: *gibi*** | gerçek gibi | gerçek gibi | like real, as if real, realistic | Common simile construction. Used to evaluate verisimilitude. No register restrictions. | [CORE] |
| **21. EQUATIVE: *gibi*** | yalan gibi | yalan gibi | like a lie, unbelievable, incredible | Idiomatic expression meaning "unbelievable" (often for surprising truths). Very common in everyday speech. | [CORE] |
| **22. QUESTION PARTICLE** | Doğru mu? | doğru mu | Is it true? Is it correct? | Standard yes/no question formation. Extremely common. Used across all registers. | [CORE] |
| **22. QUESTION PARTICLE** | Gerçek mi? | gerçek mi | Is it real? Is it true? | Standard interrogative pattern. No restrictions. Common in verification contexts. | [CORE] |
| **22. QUESTION PARTICLE (Alternate)** | doğru mu yanlış mı | doğru mu yanlış mı | true or false? | Alternative question with double question particles. Common in binary choice contexts. | [CORE] |
| **23. EMPHATIC REDUPLICATION** | doğru düzgün | doğru düzgün | proper, decent | Lexicalized alliterative pair. Common in everyday speech. Second element (*düzgün* "proper") is semantically related. | [CORE] |
| **23. EMPHATIC REDUPLICATION** | yalan dolan | yalan dolan | full of lies | Lexicalized rhyming pair. Common colloquial expression. Second element (*dolan*) is phonologically motivated. | [CORE] |
| **23. EMPHATIC REDUPLICATION (Alternate)** | doğru dürüst | doğru dürüst | proper, honest | Alternative alliterative pair with *doğru*. Semantically similar to *doğru düzgün*. | [CORE] |
| **24. NEGATIVE POLARITY** | Doğru değil | doğru değil | It's not true | Standard negative copula. Extremely common. No restrictions. | [CORE] |
| **24. NEGATIVE POLARITY** | Gerçek olamaz | gerçek olamaz | It can't be real | Negative potential construction. Common for expressing impossibility or disbelief. Used in exclamatory contexts. | [CORE] |
| **24. NEGATIVE POLARITY (Alternate)** | Verbal negation with *-ma/-me* | doğrulamadı, gerçekleşmedi | didn't verify, didn't come true | Productive on all verbs: *doğrulamadı* "didn't verify," *gerçekleşmedi* "didn't come true." | [CORE] |
| **25. CONDITIONAL** | Doğruysa | doğruysa | If it's true | Standard conditional formation. Common in hypothetical reasoning. No register restrictions. | [CORE] |
| **25. CONDITIONAL** | Gerçek olsaydı | gerçek olsaydı | If it were real | Counterfactual conditional. Common for contrary-to-fact statements. Used across registers. | [CORE] |
| **26. CONCESSIVE** | Doğru olsa bile | doğru olsa bile | Even if it's true | Standard concessive construction. Common in argumentative contexts. *Bile* is colloquial variant. | [CORE] |
| **26. CONCESSIVE** | Gerçek olsa dahi | gerçek olsa dahi | Even if it were real | Formal/literary variant with *dahi*. Less common in everyday speech. Used in written/formal registers. | [MARGINAL] |
| **27. CAUSAL/REASON** | Doğru olduğu için | doğru olduğu için | Because it's true | Standard causal construction. Very common. Used across all registers for expressing reasons. | [CORE] |
| **27. CAUSAL/REASON** | Gerçek için | gerçek için | For the truth, for reality | Purpose/dedication construction. Common in ideological or motivational contexts. Different function from causal *olduğu için*. | [CORE] |
| **28. TEMPORAL: *zaman*** | Gerçeği öğrendiği zaman | gerçeği öğrendiği zaman | When he/she learned the truth | Standard temporal subordination. Very common. No register restrictions. | [CORE] |
| **28. TEMPORAL: *zaman*** | Doğru olduğu zaman | doğru olduğu zaman | When it's true | Productive temporal pattern. Common in conditional-temporal contexts. | [CORE] |
| **28. TEMPORAL: *zaman* (Alternate)** | *-dığında* suffix | öğrendiğinde | when s/he learned | Alternative temporal marker: *öğrendiğinde* "when s/he learned." Semantically equivalent to *zaman* construction. | [CORE] |
| **29. RELATIVE CLAUSE** | doğru söyleyen kişi | doğru söyleyen kişi | person who tells the truth | Standard participial relative clause. Very productive. *Kişi* often optional. Common across registers. | [CORE] |
| **29. RELATIVE CLAUSE** | gerçeği bilen | gerçeği bilen | one who knows the truth | Headless relative clause (no overt noun). Very common. Productive pattern for creating nominal expressions. | [CORE] |
| **29. RELATIVE CLAUSE (Alternate)** | Past participle *-dık/-dik* | öğrendiği gerçek | the truth that s/he learned | Alternative participle for completed action: *öğrendiği gerçek* "the truth that s/he learned." | [CORE] |
| **30. COMPLEMENT CLAUSE** | Doğru olduğunu biliyorum | doğru olduğunu biliyorum | I know that it's true | Standard complement clause construction. Extremely common with verbs of cognition and speech. No restrictions. | [CORE] |
| **30. COMPLEMENT CLAUSE** | Gerçek olduğunu söyledi | gerçek olduğunu söyledi | He/she said that it's real | Productive with all propositional attitude verbs. Common across all registers. | [CORE] |
