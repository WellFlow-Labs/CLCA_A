## ALTERNATE CONSTRUCTIONS ATTESTED

### Attribution (Construction 1)
- **Alternate with overt copula**: *Bu doğrudur* (with evidential *-dır*) — more formal/emphatic register
- **Past tense**: *Bu doğruydu* "This was true" — obligatory copula in past
- **Future**: *Bu doğru olacak* "This will be true" — obligatory *olmak* in future

### Negative Attribution (Construction 2)
- **Double negation**: *yanlış değil* vs. *doğru* — semantically distinct (negation vs. affirmation)
- **Verbal negation**: *güvenmemek* "not to trust" vs. *güvenilmez* "untrustworthy" — different syntactic functions

### Manner Adverbials (Construction 9)
- **Ablative**: *gerçekten* "really" (lexicalized ablative)
- **Instrumental**: *kesinlikle* "certainly," *yanlışlıkla* "by mistake" (lexicalized instrumental)
- **Suffix -ca/-ce**: *dürüstçe* "honestly" (productive manner derivation)

### Intensification (Construction 10)
- **Particle intensifiers**: *çok*, *tam*, *son derece*, *oldukça* — all productive with truth-domain adjectives
- **Reduplication**: *doğru düzgün*, *yalan dolan* — lexically specific, not freely productive
- **Confirmation questions**: *doğru mu?* "is it true?" — interrogative particle *mu/mı/mu/mü*

## GENERAL OBSERVATIONS

1. **Register variation**: Formal/written registers show preference for denominative verbs (*doğrulamak*) and abstract nominalizations (*güvenilirlik*), while colloquial speech favors light verb constructions (*yalan söylemek*) and resultative patterns (*doğru çıkmak*).

2. **Productivity**: Constructions 1-6 and 9-13 show high productivity with minimal lexical restrictions. Constructions 14-15 are productive but show more lexical specificity.

3. **No marginal patterns**: All 15 constructions identified in P4a are [CORE] — fully natural and productive in Turkish. No [MARGINAL] or [UNCERTAIN] patterns were identified in the P4a data.

4. **Semantic distinctions**: Multiple constructions may express related meanings with subtle distinctions (e.g., *yalanlamak* "to deny/refute" vs. *yalan söylemek* "to tell a lie").
