1. **உண்மை**  
   **Transliteration:** uṇmai  
   **Literal meaning / breakdown:** from **உண் / உள்**-related Dravidian root associated with being/reality + abstract noun suffix **-மை**  
   **Typical English translations:** truth, reality, fact, genuineness  

   **Usage:** This is one of the most common Tamil words for “truth” or “what is real.” It is used for factual correctness, sincerity, and ontological reality, as in discussions of whether a statement is true, whether a report reflects reality, or what the true nature of something is.  
   **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

2. **உண்மையான**  
   **Transliteration:** uṇmaiyāṉa  
   **Literal meaning / breakdown:** **உண்மை** “truth, reality” + adjectival **-ஆன**  
   **Typical English translations:** true, real, genuine, authentic  

   **Usage:** This adjective is used for things judged to be genuine or actually so, such as a true story, a real friend, or an authentic object. It is common in both everyday and formal registers when evaluating whether something is not false, fake, or merely apparent.  
   **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

3. **உண்மைத்தன்மை**  
   **Transliteration:** uṇmaittaṉmai  
   **Literal meaning / breakdown:** **உண்மை** “truth” + linking **-த்-** + **தன்மை** “quality, nature”  
   **Typical English translations:** truthfulness, authenticity, veracity, genuineness  

   **Usage:** This form refers to the quality of being true or genuine. It is often used in formal writing, criticism, journalism, and philosophical or legal discussion when assessing the truth-status or authenticity of a claim, document, portrayal, or testimony.  
   **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

4. **மெய்**  
   **Transliteration:** mey  
   **Literal meaning / breakdown:** body; real; true  
   **Typical English translations:** true, real, genuine  

   **Usage:** This is an old and still productive Tamil word meaning “true” or “real,” especially in compounds and elevated usage. It appears in literary, philosophical, and religious contexts, and also in contrasts such as true versus false or real versus illusory.  
   **Epistemic status:** [CORE]
