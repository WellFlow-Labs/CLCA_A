# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P4a_CONSTRUCTIONS
# Language: Chinese (zh)

---

## 1. ATTRIBUTION

### Pattern
**X 是 Y** (X shì Y) - "X is Y"
Standard copular construction using 是 (shì) "to be" to predicate truth-domain properties.

### Examples
- **这是真的** (zhè shì zhēn de)
  - Transliteration: zhè shì zhēn de
  - Translation: "This is true/real"

- **他的话是假的** (tā de huà shì jiǎ de)
  - Transliteration: tā de huà shì jiǎ de
  - Translation: "His words are false"

### Notes
Chinese uses the copula 是 (shì) with nominalizer 的 (de) converting adjectives to predicates. The pattern is fully productive across all truth-domain terms.

---

## 2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION

### Pattern
**X 不是 Y** (X bù shì Y) - "X is not Y"
Negation using 不 (bù) before copula 是 (shì).

### Examples
- **这不是真的** (zhè bù shì zhēn de)
  - Transliteration: zhè bù shì zhēn de
  - Translation: "This is not true/real"

- **答案不对** (dá'àn bù duì)
  - Transliteration: dá'àn bù duì
  - Translation: "The answer is not correct"

### Notes
Two patterns exist: 不是 + adjective+的 for copular negation, or 不 + bare adjective for direct negation. Both are fully productive.

---

## 3. ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION

### Pattern
**Y 的 X** (Y de X) - "Y X" / "X that is Y"
Attributive modification using particle 的 (de) between modifier and head noun.

### Examples
- **真实的情况** (zhēnshí de qíngkuàng)
  - Transliteration: zhēnshí de qíngkuàng
  - Translation: "the true/actual situation"

- **正确的答案** (zhèngquè de dá'àn)
  - Transliteration: zhèngquè de dá'àn
  - Translation: "the correct answer"

### Notes
The particle 的 (de) marks attributive modification. For monosyllabic modifiers or tight compounds, 的 may be omitted (e.g., 真相 "truth" without 的).

---

## 4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE

### Pattern
**变 Y** (biàn Y) - "become Y"
**变得 Y** (biàn de Y) - "become Y" (with resultative particle)

### Examples
- **变真** (biàn zhēn) / **弄假成真** (nòng jiǎ chéng zhēn)
  - Transliteration: nòng jiǎ chéng zhēn
  - Translation: "what starts as pretense becomes real"

- **变得不正常** (biàn de bù zhèngcháng)
  - Transliteration: biàn de bù zhèngcháng
  - Translation: "become abnormal"

### Notes
The verb 变 (biàn) "change, become" combines with truth-domain adjectives. The resultative particle 得 (de) is optional. The idiom 弄假成真 lexicalizes this pattern with 成 (chéng) "become."

---

## 5. CAUSATIVE

### Pattern
**使/让 X Y** (shǐ/ràng X Y) - "make X Y"
**把 X 弄 Y** (bǎ X nòng Y) - "make X Y" (disposal construction)

### Examples
- **让事实说话** (ràng shìshí shuōhuà)
  - Transliteration: ràng shìshí shuōhuà
  - Translation: "let the facts speak"

- **把错误改正** (bǎ cuòwù gǎizhèng)
  - Transliteration: bǎ cuòwù gǎizhèng
  - Translation: "correct the mistakes"

### Notes
Chinese uses causative verbs 使 (shǐ), 让 (ràng) or the disposal construction 把...弄/改 to express causation. Resultative verb compounds (改正, 纠正) are more common than periphrastic causatives for truth-domain terms.

## 6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION

### Pattern
**Y + 性** (Y-xìng) - "Y-ness, quality of being Y"
**Y + 度** (Y-dù) - "degree of Y"

### Examples
- **真实性** (zhēnshíxìng)
  - Transliteration: zhēnshíxìng
  - Translation: "authenticity, truthfulness"

- **准确度** (zhǔnquèdù)
  - Transliteration: zhǔnquèdù
  - Translation: "degree of accuracy"

### Notes
The suffix -性 (-xìng) creates abstract quality nouns; -度 (-dù) creates degree nouns. Both are productive with truth-domain adjectives. See P2a for full inventory.

### Pattern
**(no common expression identified)**

### Examples
N/A

### Notes
Chinese lacks productive agentive derivation specific to truth-domain terms. While general agentive suffix -者 (-zhě) exists (e.g., 欺骗者 "deceiver"), it is not specific to this semantic domain and does not productively generate agent nouns from truth-domain roots. Terms like "truth-teller" or "falsifier" are expressed periphrastically (说真话的人 "person who tells truth") rather than through derivational morphology.

## 8. VERIFICATION / CONFIRMATION

### Pattern
**是不是 Y?** (shì bu shì Y?) - "Is it Y or not?"
**Y 不 Y?** (Y bu Y?) - "Y or not Y?"

### Examples
- **是不是真的?** (shì bu shì zhēn de?)
  - Transliteration: shì bu shì zhēn de?
  - Translation: "Is it true or not?"

- **对不对?** (duì bu duì?)
  - Transliteration: duì bu duì?
  - Translation: "Right or not? / Is that correct?"

### Notes
Chinese uses A-not-A question pattern for yes/no questions. This is extremely productive with truth-domain terms, creating verification questions without question particles.

## 9. DEGREE MODIFICATION

### Pattern
**很/非常/十分 Y** (hěn/fēicháng/shífēn Y) - "very Y"
**更/最 Y** (gèng/zuì Y) - "more/most Y"

### Examples
- **很真实** (hěn zhēnshí)
  - Transliteration: hěn zhēnshí
  - Translation: "very authentic/realistic"

- **最正确** (zuì zhèngquè)
  - Transliteration: zuì zhèngquè
  - Translation: "most correct"

### Notes
Standard degree adverbs modify truth-domain adjectives productively. No specialized degree modification exists for this domain.

## 10. COMPARATIVE CONSTRUCTIONS

### Pattern
**X 比 Y (更) Z** (X bǐ Y (gèng) Z) - "X is (more) Z than Y"
**X 和 Y 一样 Z** (X hé Y yīyàng Z) - "X is as Z as Y"

### Examples
- **这个比那个更真实** (zhège bǐ nàge gèng zhēnshí)
  - Transliteration: zhège bǐ nàge gèng zhēnshí
  - Translation: "This is more authentic than that"

- **他和我一样诚实** (tā hé wǒ yīyàng chéngshí)
  - Transliteration: tā hé wǒ yīyàng chéngshí
  - Translation: "He is as honest as I am"

### Notes
Standard comparative (比 bǐ) and equative (一样 yīyàng) constructions apply productively to truth-domain terms without specialized forms.

---

## 11. EVIDENTIAL / REPORTATIVE

### Pattern
**据说 X** (jùshuō X) - "it is said that X"
**听说 X** (tīngshuō X) - "I heard that X"

### Examples
- **据说是真的** (jùshuō shì zhēn de)
  - Transliteration: jùshuō shì zhēn de
  - Translation: "It is said to be true"

- **听说不是事实** (tīngshuō bù shì shìshí)
  - Transliteration: tīngshuō bù shì shìshí
  - Translation: "I heard it's not factual"

### Notes
Chinese has limited grammaticalized evidentials. Reportative markers 据说, 听说 are general and not specific to truth-domain. They indicate hearsay evidence for any proposition.

## 12. MODAL CONSTRUCTIONS (OBLIGATION)

### Pattern
**应该/必须 V** (yīnggāi/bìxū V) - "should/must V"

### Examples
- **应该说实话** (yīnggāi shuō shíhuà)
  - Transliteration: yīnggāi shuō shíhuà
  - Translation: "should tell the truth"

- **必须面对事实** (bìxū miànduì shìshí)
  - Transliteration: bìxū miànduì shìshí
  - Translation: "must face the facts"

### Notes
Standard deontic modals combine with truth-domain predicates. No specialized modal constructions exist for this semantic domain.

## 13. INTENSIFICATION / EMPHASIS

### Pattern
**Reduplication**: Y-Y or AABB pattern
**真/确实 + Y**: "really/indeed Y"

### Examples
- **实实在在** (shíshízàizài)
  - Transliteration: shíshízàizài
  - Translation: "really and truly, genuinely"

- **真诚实** (zhēn chéngshí)
  - Transliteration: zhēn chéngshí
  - Translation: "really honest"

### Notes
Reduplication (AABB pattern) intensifies meaning. The adverb 真 (zhēn) "really" also functions as intensifier before truth-domain adjectives.

---

## 14. RESULTATIVE VERB COMPOUNDS

### Pattern
**V + 正** (V-zhèng) - "V to correctness"
**V + 实** (V-shí) - "V to solidity/reality"

### Examples
- **改正** (gǎizhèng)
  - Transliteration: gǎizhèng
  - Translation: "correct, amend" (change + correct)

- **落实** (luòshí)
  - Transliteration: luòshí
  - Translation: "implement, actualize" (fall/settle + solid)

### Notes
Truth-domain roots (especially 正, 实) function as resultative complements indicating the result state. This is a productive pattern creating verbs of correction and actualization.

---

## 15. DISCOURSE MARKERS

### Pattern
**说真的/老实说** (shuō zhēn de / lǎoshí shuō) - "honestly speaking"
**真的吗?** (zhēn de ma?) - "Really?"

### Examples
- **说真的，我不知道** (shuō zhēn de, wǒ bù zhīdào)
  - Transliteration: shuō zhēn de, wǒ bù zhīdào
  - Translation: "Honestly speaking, I don't know"

- **真的吗?** (zhēn de ma?)
  - Transliteration: zhēn de ma?
  - Translation: "Really? Is that so?"

### Notes
Truth-domain terms grammaticalize as discourse markers managing speaker stance and interpersonal communication. These function pragmatically rather than semantically.
