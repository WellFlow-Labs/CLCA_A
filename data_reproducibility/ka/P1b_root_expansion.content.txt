# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P1b ROOT EXPANSION
# Language: Georgian (ka)

---

## STEP 1 — IDENTIFY CENTRAL ROOTS

### Root 1: მართ- (mart'-)
**Native script:** მართ-  
**Transliteration:** mart'-  
**P1 participation:** 3 items (მართალი mart'ali, სიმართლე simart'le, სამართლიანი samart'liani)  
**Core meaning contribution:** Rightness, correctness, truth; encompasses both factual accuracy and moral/legal rightness. This root is the primary native Georgian morpheme for truth-domain concepts.

### Root 2: ნამდვილ- (namdvil-)
**Native script:** ნამდვილ-  
**Transliteration:** namdvil-  
**P1 participation:** 1 item (ნამდვილი namdvili)  
**Core meaning contribution:** Genuineness, authenticity, reality; emphasizes the quality of being real rather than fake, actual rather than apparent.

### Root 3: ტყუ- (t'q'u-)
**Native script:** ტყუ-  
**Transliteration:** t'q'u-  
**P1 participation:** 2 items (ტყუილი t'q'uili, უტყუარი ut'q'uari)  
**Core meaning contribution:** Falsehood, lying, deception; the primary native Georgian root for falsity and deliberate untruth.

### ROOT 1: მართ- (mart'-)

#### 1. მართებული (mart'ebuli)
**Transliteration:** mart'ebuli  
**Literal morpheme breakdown:** mart'- (right) + -ebul- (participle suffix) + -i (adjective)  
**Natural English translation:** justified, well-founded, legitimate  
**Context of use:** Used in formal and legal contexts to describe claims, demands, or actions that are justified or have legitimate grounds. Common in administrative and argumentative discourse to evaluate whether something is warranted.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 3. გაამართლა (gamart'la)

## STEP 3 — PERIPHERAL EXTENSIONS

### Root მართ- (mart'-)

**Peripheral extensions noted:**

1. **მართლმადიდებელი (mart'lmadidebeli)** — "Orthodox (Christian)": The root's contribution shifts from general rightness/truth to specifically religious orthodoxy. The term literally means "right-glorifying" but functions as a fixed religious designation rather than a truth-evaluation term.

2. **მართლზომიერი (mart'lzomieri)** — "moderate, temperate": The root extends from truth/correctness into the domain of degree and moderation. Here მართ- contributes a sense of proper measure rather than factual accuracy.

3. **მართებს (mart'ebs)** — "it is fitting, it befits": The root shifts from truth-evaluation to propriety and social appropriateness. This is a modal/deontic use rather than epistemic.

4. **მართლწერა (mart'lts'era)** — "orthography": While related to correctness, this is a technical linguistic term where the root functions in a specialized domain of spelling conventions rather than general truth-evaluation.

5. **მართალი გული (mart'ali guli)** — "righteous heart": The root extends into moral character evaluation, where it describes virtue rather than factual accuracy.

6. **მართლმსაჯულება (mart'lmsajuleba)** — "justice, jurisprudence": The root participates in legal-institutional terminology where it refers to the justice system as an institution rather than truth-evaluation per se.

### Root ნამდვილ- (namdvil-)

**Peripheral extensions noted:**

1. **ნამდვილი ოსტატი (namdvili ost'at'i)** — "true master": The root extends from authenticity of objects to evaluation of skill level and expertise, functioning as an intensifier of mastery.

2. **ნამდვილი ქართველი (namdvili kartveli)** — "true Georgian": The root shifts to cultural identity evaluation, where it assesses conformity to cultural ideals rather than factual authenticity.

3. **ნამდვილი ხელოვნება (namdvili khelovneba)** — "true art": The root extends into aesthetic evaluation, distinguishing genuine artistic merit from pretension, which involves value judgment beyond simple authenticity.

4. **ნამდვილი ღირებულება (namdvili ghirebuleba)** — "true value": The root participates in value assessment that may involve both factual and normative dimensions.

### Root ტყუ- (t'q'u-)

**Peripheral extensions noted:**

1. **ტყუილად (t'q'uilad)** — "in vain, for nothing": The root shifts from deliberate falsehood to futility and lack of purpose. This is a significant semantic extension where the falsity sense becomes metaphorical for emptiness or purposelessness.

2. **ტყუილუბრალოდ (t'q'uilubralod)** — "simply for nothing, pointlessly": Similar to above, emphasizing futility rather than deception.

3. **ტყუილი იმედი (t'q'uili imedi)** — "false hope, vain hope": The root extends to describe hopes that will not be realized, which involves both falsity and futility.

4. **ტყუილი სიხარული (t'q'uili sikharuli)** — "false joy": The root evaluates emotional authenticity, distinguishing genuine from feigned emotions.

5. **უტყუარი (ut'q'uari)** and **უტყუარობა (ut'q'uaroba)** — "infallible, infallibility": The negated form extends to describe absolute reliability and inability to err, which goes beyond simple truth-telling to encompass perfect accuracy.
