# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2a Derivational System
# Language: Tagalog (tl)

---

**Methodological note:** The following documents attested derivational forms associated with P1 lexical items. Tagalog morphology is primarily affixal and circumfixal; derivation and inflection are not always cleanly separable, as the same affixes may mark both grammatical function and lexical category shift. Forms are included only where usage is well-attested. Paradigm gaps are preserved without comment unless the gap itself is noteworthy. Forms already documented in P1 and P1b are cross-referenced rather than re-described in full.

---

## Section 1: Derivations from ROOT A — **totoo**

---

**D-A-01**
**Base Form:** totoo
**Derived Form:** katotohanan
**Transliteration:** katotohanan
**Literal Breakdown:** *ka-* + *totoo* + *-an* (nominalizing circumfix); the medial *h* appears as a phonological insertion between the root-final vowel and the suffix
**Natural English translation:** truth, the truth, reality, the true state of affairs
**Usage:** Abstract nominal referring to truth as a quality or to a specific true state of affairs. Documented in P1 item 2 and P1b A-02. Appears across moral, legal, philosophical, and religious registers.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**D-A-02**
**Base Form:** totoo
**Derived Form:** makatotohanan
**Transliteration:** makatotohanan
**Literal Breakdown:** *maka-* + *totoo* + *-an*; *maka-* expresses orientation toward or accordance with the base
**Natural English translation:** realistic, true-to-life, truthful, verisimilar
**Usage:** Adjectival form evaluating whether a representation or depiction corresponds faithfully to reality. Documented in P1 item 19 and P1b A-04. Common in aesthetic, narrative, and moral evaluation.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**D-A-03**
**Base Form:** totoo
**Derived Form:** totoong-totoo
**Transliteration:** totoong-totoo
**Literal Breakdown:** *totoo* + *-ng* (linker) + *totoo*; full reduplication with intervening linker
**Natural English translation:** very real, absolutely true, genuinely genuine
**Usage:** Intensified form produced by full reduplication with linker. Documented in P1 item 1 and P1b A-03. The reduplication pattern for intensification is productive in Tagalog, but this specific form is the attested instance for this root.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Note on totoo verbal derivation:** The root *totoo* does not appear to productively generate verbal forms via standard Tagalog verbal affixes (*mag-*, *-um-*, *ma-*, *i-*, *-in*) in the truth-assertion sense. Expressions such as *nagkatotoo* ("came true, was realized") exist but involve a different semantic trajectory (realization of a prediction or wish) and are not straightforwardly derivations within the truth-evaluation domain. No verbal derivation in the core truth-evaluation sense is documented here.

## Section 2: Derivations from ROOT B — **tunay**

---

**D-B-01**
**Base Form:** tunay
**Derived Form:** katunayan
**Transliteration:** katunayan
**Literal Breakdown:** *ka-* + *tunay* + *-an* (nominalizing circumfix)
**Natural English translation:** proof, evidence, verification; in fact, as a matter of fact
**Usage:** Functions both as a concrete nominal (a piece of proof or evidence) and as a discourse marker (in fact). Documented in P1 item 4 and P1b B-02. Common in legal, formal, and argumentative discourse.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**D-B-02**
**Base Form:** tunay
**Derived Form:** patunay
**Transliteration:** patunay
**Literal Breakdown:** *pa-* + *tunay*; *pa-* in nominal function yields "that which causes or establishes tunay"
**Natural English translation:** proof, evidence, demonstration, attestation
**Usage:** Nominal form referring to a specific piece of evidence or a demonstration that something is true or genuine. Documented in P1b B-22. Used in legal, administrative, and everyday contexts.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**D-B-03**
**Base Form:** tunay
**Derived Form:** patunayan
**Transliteration:** patunayan
**Literal Breakdown:** *pa-* + *tunay* + *-an* (causative-applicative verbal form)
**Natural English translation:** to prove, to verify, to demonstrate, to authenticate
**Usage:** Verbal form describing the act of proving or demonstrating that something is true or genuine. Documented in P1b B-04. *Patunayan mo ang iyong sinabi* ("prove what you said").
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**D-B-04**
**Base Form:** tunay / patunayan
**Derived Form:** mapatunayan
**Transliteration:** mapatunayan
**Literal Breakdown:** *ma-* + *pa-* + *tunay* + *-an*; *ma-* here marks potential or ability aspect
**Natural English translation:** to be able to prove, to be provable, to be verifiable
**Usage:** Potential-aspect form expressing the possibility or ability to prove or verify something. Documented in P1b B-05. *Hindi mapatunayan ang kanyang sinabi* ("what he said cannot be proven").
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**D-B-05**
**Base Form:** tunay / patunayan
**Derived Form:** napatunayan
**Transliteration:** napatunayan
**Literal Breakdown:** *na-* + *pa-* + *tunay* + *-an*; *na-* marks completed aspect in the potential paradigm
**Natural English translation:** has been proven, was verified, has been demonstrated
**Usage:** Completed-aspect form asserting that something has already been established as true or genuine. Documented in P1b B-06. Common in legal and investigative discourse.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**D-B-06**
**Base Form:** tunay / patunayan
**Derived Form:** pinatunayan
**Transliteration:** pinatunayan
**Literal Breakdown:** *pi-* (completed aspect marker in actor-focus paradigm) + *pa-* + *tunay* + *-an*
**Natural English translation:** proved, verified, authenticated, demonstrated
**Usage:** Completed-aspect actor-focus verbal form. Documented in P1b B-07. *Pinatunayan ng ebidensya ang kanyang kasalanan* ("the evidence proved his guilt").
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**D-B-07**
**Base Form:** tunay
**Derived Form:** pagpapatunay
**Transliteration:** pagpapatunay
**Literal Breakdown:** *pag-* + *pa-* + *tunay* + *-ay* (verbal nominalization of the causative form)
**Natural English translation:** verification, authentication, confirmation, proof (as a process)
**Usage:** Verbal nominal referring to the act or process of verifying or authenticating. Documented in P1 item 29 and P1b B-03. Used in legal, administrative, and formal contexts.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**D-B-08**
**Base Form:** tunay
**Derived Form:** tunay na tunay
**Transliteration:** tunay na tunay
**Literal Breakdown:** *tunay* + *na* (linker) + *tunay*; full reduplication with linker
**Natural English translation:** absolutely genuine, truly authentic, completely real
**Usage:** Intensified form produced by full reduplication with linker, parallel to *totoong-totoo*. Documented in P1b B-08. Used in everyday speech to emphasize emphatic genuineness.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Note on tunay productivity:** The *pa-* + *tunay* + *-an* verbal cluster is notably productive, generating a fuller aspectual paradigm than is observed for *totoo* or *tapat*. The forms *pinapatunayan* (imperfective actor-focus: "is proving") and *ipinapatunayan* (imperfective object-focus) are also attested in usage but are not separately documented here as they represent aspectual variants of the same derivational base rather than distinct derivations.

## Section 3: Derivations from ROOT C — **tapat**

---

**D-C-01**
**Base Form:** tapat
**Derived Form:** katapatan
**Transliteration:** katapatan
**Literal Breakdown:** *ka-* + *tapat* + *-an* (nominalizing circumfix)
**Natural English translation:** honesty, sincerity, fidelity, loyalty, faithfulness
**Usage:** Abstract nominal referring to honesty, sincerity, or fidelity as a quality. Documented in P1 item 21 and P1b C-02. Covers both epistemic honesty and moral loyalty.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**D-C-02**
**Base Form:** tapat
**Derived Form:** magtapat
**Transliteration:** magtapat
**Literal Breakdown:** *mag-* + *tapat*; *mag-* verbal prefix marking actor-focus, typically for actions performed by the actor
**Natural English translation:** to be honest, to confess, to come clean, to disclose frankly
**Usage:** Verbal form describing the act of honestly disclosing something. Documented in P1b C-22. *Magtapat ka na* ("come clean already / be honest now").
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**D-C-03**
**Base Form:** tapat / magtapat
**Derived Form:** nagtatapat
**Transliteration:** nagtatapat
**Literal Breakdown:** *nag-* + reduplication of first syllable of *tapat* (*ta-*) + *tapat*; imperfective/progressive aspect of *magtapat*
**Natural English translation:** is being honest, is confessing, is coming clean
**Usage:** Progressive-aspect form describing the ongoing act of honest disclosure. Documented in P1b C-23. *Nagtatapat na siya* ("he is already coming clean").
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**D-C-04**
**Base Form:** tapat / magtapat
**Derived Form:** nagtapat
**Transliteration:** nagtapat
**Literal Breakdown:** *nag-* + *tapat*; completed aspect of *magtapat*
**Natural English translation:** was honest, confessed, came clean, disclosed frankly
**Usage:** Completed-aspect form describing a past act of honest disclosure. Documented in P1b C-24. *Nagtapat siya sa kanyang asawa* ("he came clean to his spouse").
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**D-C-05**
**Base Form:** tapat / magtapat
**Derived Form:** pagtatapat
**Transliteration:** pagtatapat
**Literal Breakdown:** *pag-* + reduplication of first syllable (*ta-*) + *tapat*; verbal nominalization of *magtapat*
**Natural English translation:** confession, honest disclosure, act of being frank, coming clean
**Usage:** Verbal nominal referring to the act of honestly disclosing something previously concealed. Documented in P1b C-21. *Pagtatapat ng kasalanan* ("confession of sin").
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**D-C-06**
**Base Form:** tapat
**Derived Form:** pagiging tapat
**Transliteration:** pagiging tapat
**Literal Breakdown:** *pag-* + *-ing* (stative verbal nominalization) + *tapat*; nominalizes the state of being tapat
**Natural English translation:** being honest, the quality of being sincere, honesty as a state or practice
**Usage:** Nominal phrase referring to the act or ongoing state of being honest. Documented in P1b C-18. Used in moral and philosophical discourse when discussing honesty as a virtue.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Note on tapat spatial derivations:** The root *tapat* in its spatial sense ("facing, directly opposite") generates a separate set of forms — e.g., *sa tapat* ("across from, in front of"), *katapat* ("the one facing, the counterpart") — that are well-attested but fall outside the truth-evaluation domain. These are noted here for completeness but not documented as derivations within the scope of this protocol.

## Summary of Derivational Patterns Observed

The following patterns are observed across the documented derivations. These are reported descriptively without theoretical interpretation.

**1. Nominalizing circumfix *ka-* ... *-an*:** Highly productive across native roots. Attested for *totoo* → *katotohanan*, *tunay* → *katunayan*, *tapat* → *katapatan*, *wasto* → *kawastuhan*, *tama* → *katamaan*, *dalisay* → *kadalisayan*. Yields abstract quality nominals.

**2. Full reduplication with linker (*X-ng X* or *X na X*):** Productive intensification pattern. Attested for *totoo* → *totoong-totoo*, *tunay* → *tunay na tunay*, *tama* → *tamang-tama*, *eksakto* → *eksaktong-eksakto*, *mali* → *maling-mali*, *huwad* → *huwad na huwad* (marginal). Not attested for all roots.

**3. *pa-* + root + *-an* verbal cluster:** Productive for *tunay*, generating a fuller aspectual paradigm (*patunayan*, *mapatunayan*, *napatunayan*, *pinatunayan*, *pagpapatunay*). Not attested for *totoo* or *tapat* in the truth-evaluation sense.

**4. *mag-* verbal prefix:** Attested for *tapat* → *magtapat*, *tiwala* → *magtiwala*, *sinungaling* → *magsinungaling*. Not attested for *totoo* or *tunay* in the truth-evaluation sense.

**5. *-idad* suffix (Spanish-derived):** Productive with borrowed Spanish-derived adjectives. Attested for *kredible* → *kredibilidad*, *lehitimo* → *lehitimidad*. Not applicable to native roots.

**6. *di-* negation prefix:** Productive. Attested for *mapagkakatiwalaan* → *di-mapagkakatiwalaan*. Standard negation for native roots uses *hindi* rather than *di-* in most registers, though *di-* is productive in formal and compound forms.

**Derivational gaps noted:**
- *totoo* does not generate a productive verbal paradigm in the truth-assertion sense.
- *tama* and *wasto* generate limited derivations compared to *tunay* and *tapat*.
- *peke* (borrowed) shows irregular reduplication behavior and limited derivational productivity.
- *huwad* generates verbal forms (*humuwad*, *paghuhuwad*) but these are less commonly encountered than the adjectival base.
