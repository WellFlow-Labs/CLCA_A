## SUMMARY OF NATURALNESS DISTRIBUTION

**[CORE]**: 20 construction types (87%)
- All basic patterns fully productive and frequent
- No register restrictions except where noted (e.g., *ei pidä paikkaansa* formal)
- Examples represent typical, idiomatic usage

**[MARGINAL]**: 1 construction type (4%)
- *Epätosi* less common than competing forms
- Restricted to formal/technical contexts

**[UNCERTAIN]**: 1 construction type (4%)
- Causative absence represents systematic gap
- Not a construction per se but absence of expected pattern

**Not rated**: 1 construction type (4%)
- *Valehdella* (frequentative) rated [CORE] but pattern itself not productive in domain

## USAGE PATTERNS OBSERVED

1. **Case marking highly conventionalized**: Partitive in negation/abstract predicates, translative in result states, inessive in fixed expressions (*oikeassa/väärässä*)

2. **Register variation limited**: Most constructions span registers; formal preference for *ei pidä paikkaansa*, *todisteiden perusteella*, gerunds

3. **Productivity varies by pattern**: Derivational morphology highly productive (*-sti*, *-minen*, *-uus*); some patterns lexically restricted (*-lla* frequentative)

4. **Fixed expressions common**: *Totta kai*, *olla oikeassa/väärässä*, *rehellisesti sanottuna* function as units

5. **Competing forms exist**: *Väärä* preferred over *epätosi*; simple adverbs compete with *tavalla* constructions
