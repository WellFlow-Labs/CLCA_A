# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2a Derivational Morphology
# Language: Chinese (zh)

---

## PRELIMINARY NOTE ON CHINESE DERIVATION

Chinese is an isolating language with limited affixal derivation. Most word-formation is **compounding** (already documented extensively in P1b and excluded here as it is not derivation proper). Genuine derivational morphology in Chinese is restricted to a small set of affix-like morphemes, principally:

- **-性 (-xìng)** — nominalizing suffix ("-ness / -ity")
- **-化 (-huà)** — verbalizing suffix ("-ize / -ify")
- **-度 (-dù)** — degree/measure nominalizer ("degree of")
- **-者 (-zhě)** — agentive nominalizer ("one who")
- **可- (kě-)** — modal prefix ("-able")
- **不- (bù-)** — negative prefix (productive but arguably inflectional/syntactic; included only where lexicalized)

Only forms meeting the criterion of genuine attestation are listed below. Many P1 items support **no** affixal derivation; these gaps are preserved and stated explicitly.

### 2. 假 (jiǎ)

**Base Form:** 假 (jiǎ)
**Derived Form:** 假性
**Transliteration:** jiǎxìng
**Literal Breakdown:** 假 (false) + 性 (-ness/nature)
**Natural English:** pseudo-, false (as a property)
**Usage:** Bound prefixal use in medical/technical terms (假性近视 "pseudo-myopia"). Restricted to compounded technical vocabulary.
**Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

**Usage:** Used in journalism, scholarship, and law to assess the reliability of accounts or evidence (材料的真实性). High-frequency, fully lexicalized.

## SUMMARY OF ATTESTED DERIVATION

**Dominant attested pattern: -性 (-xìng) nominalization.**
Attested on evaluative adjectives:
- 真实 → 真实性 [CORE]
- 正确 → 正确性 [CORE]
- 准确 → 准确性 [CORE]
- 可靠 → 可靠性 [CORE]
- 相似 → 相似性 [CORE]
- 虚伪 → 虚伪性 [MARGINAL]
- 本质 → 本质性 [MARGINAL]
- 可信 → 可信性 [MARGINAL]

**Secondary attested pattern: -度 (-dù) degree nominalization.**
Restricted to gradable evaluative adjectives:
- 可信 → 可信度 [CORE]
- 准确 → 准确度 [CORE]
- 相似 → 相似度 [CORE]

**Marginal/technical patterns:**
- 假 → 假性 (prefixal, technical) [MARGINAL]
- 错误 → 错误率 (rate nominalization) [MARGINAL]

## OBSERVED ASYMMETRIES AND GAPS (preserved, not filled)

1. **-性 attaches to bisyllabic adjectives, not to the monosyllabic core roots.** 真, 假, 对, 错, 像 take **no** -性 derivative; the abstract notion is carried by compounds instead (e.g., 真 → 真假/真伪 rather than *真性 in the truth sense).

2. **-度 is restricted to clearly gradable properties.** It is attested for 可信, 准确, 相似 but **not** for 正确 or 可靠 in robust everyday usage (正确度, 可靠度 are rare/dispreferred relative to -性), and **not** for non-gradable factual terms (事实, 真相).

3. **Moral-character adjectives resist -性 here.** 真诚, 诚实 yield **no** standard nominalizer despite being prototypical evaluative terms; this is a genuine gap, not an oversight.

4. **All idioms and fixed binomials (名副其实, 货真价实, 真伪, 名不副实) derive nothing.** Expected, but stated to preserve the asymmetry.

5. **-化 (-huà) verbalization is not attested** for any P1 item in this domain (no *真实化, *正确化, etc. as lexicalized forms). This whole derivational slot is **empty** for this vocabulary set.

6. **-者 (-zhě) agentive is not attested** for any P1 item (no *诚实者, *可靠者 as standard words).
