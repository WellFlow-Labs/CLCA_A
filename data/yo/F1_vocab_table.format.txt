| òtítọ́ | òtítọ́ | Noun derived from "tọ́" (to be straight, correct) | truth, truthfulness, honesty, accuracy | This term is used to describe statements that correspond to reality and to characterize persons who habitually speak accurately. | irọ́ | irọ́ | Noun meaning "lie, falsehood" | lie, falsehood, untruth, deception | This is the standard term for statements that do not correspond to reality, whether intentionally false or mistaken. | tọ́ | tọ́ | Verb meaning "to be straight, to be correct, to be true" | to be true, to be correct, to be right, to be straight | This verb is used to affirm that something is accurate or corresponds to reality. | purọ́ | purọ́ | Verb meaning "to lie, to tell a falsehood" | to lie, to deceive, to speak falsely | This verb describes the act of making false statements, typically with awareness of their falsity. | òdodo | òdodo | Noun meaning "righteousness, justice, correctness" | righteousness, justice, uprightness, correctness | This term evaluates conduct, judgments, and character with respect to moral and legal standards. | gidi | gidi | Adjective/ideophone meaning "genuine, real, authentic" | genuine, real, authentic, true, actual | This term emphasizes the genuine or authentic nature of something, distinguishing it from imitations or false representations. | ìbámu | ìbámu | Noun meaning "agreement, correspondence, harmony" | agreement, correspondence, conformity, harmony | This term describes the relationship between things that match or correspond to each other.
