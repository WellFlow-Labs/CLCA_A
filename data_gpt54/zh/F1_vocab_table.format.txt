| 假 | jiǎ | “false; fake” | false; fake; counterfeit | 假 is widely used for things judged not genuine, not real, or not true, including 假话 “lies/falsehoods,” 假货 “counterfeit goods,” and 假证 “false evidence.” In everyday and legal-commercial contexts, it often marks deception or lack of authenticity. | [CORE] |
| 真实 | zhēnshí | 真 “true, real” + 实 “solid, actual” | real; true; authentic | 真实 is commonly used for statements, reports, experiences, and representations that correspond to actual facts or conditions. | 虚假 | xūjiǎ | 虚 “empty, unreal” + 假 “false” | false; fraudulent; bogus | 虚假 is common in formal and regulatory language for claims, advertising, documents, or testimony that are false or deceptive. | 正确 | zhèngquè | 正 “correct, proper” + 确 “certain, accurate” | correct; right; accurate | 正确 is a standard term for judgments about whether an answer, method, statement, or action is right. | 准确 | zhǔnquè | 准 “exact, standard” + 确 “certain, precise” | accurate; exact; precise | 准确 is commonly used when evaluating measurements, descriptions, predictions, wording, or data for precision and correctness. | 失实 | shīshí | 失 “lose, miss” + 实 “fact, reality” | inaccurate; not in accordance with the facts | 失实 is used mainly in formal contexts for reports, accounts, or descriptions that do not match the facts, whether by error or distortion. | 可信 | kěxìn | 可 “can, may” + 信 “believe, trust” | credible; believable; trustworthy | 可信 is used to evaluate whether information, testimony, a source, or sometimes a person deserves belief. | 可靠 | kěkào | 可 “can” + 靠 “lean on, rely on” | reliable; dependable | 可靠 is widely used for persons, machines, information, methods, and evidence that can be depended on. Compared with 可信, it often emphasizes practical dependability or consistency rather than merely being believable. | [CORE] |
| 伪造 | wěizào | 伪 “false, forged” + 造 “make” | forge; counterfeit; falsify | 伪造 is a common verb in legal and forensic contexts for making false documents, signatures, artworks, or evidence. It directly concerns genuineness and fraud, as in 伪造证件 “forge documents.” | [CORE] |
| 仿真 | fǎngzhēn | 仿 “imitate” + 真 “real” | simulation; lifelike; realistic imitation | 仿真 is used for objects, models, images, or systems made to resemble reality closely, such as 仿真花 “artificial flowers that look real” or 仿真技术 “simulation technology.” It concerns resemblance rather than actual genuineness. | [CORE] |
| 逼真 | bīzhēn | 逼 “close to, approach” + 真 “real” | lifelike; vivid; realistic | 逼真 is common in aesthetic and representational contexts for paintings, acting, models, or digital images that closely resemble reality. It evaluates convincing resemblance, not actual truth or authenticity. | [CORE] |
| 名副其实 | míng fù qí shí | 名 “name” + 副 “match” + 其 “its” + 实 “reality” | be worthy of the name; truly live up to one’s name | This fixed expression is used when a title, reputation, or description genuinely matches the actual person or thing. | 货真价实 | huò zhēn jià shí | 货 “goods” + 真 “genuine” + 价 “price” + 实 “solid, honest” | genuine and fairly priced; authentic and good value | This fixed expression is common in commercial and figurative use for goods that are genuine and worth their stated price. | 如实 | rúshí | 如 “according to” + 实 “fact, reality” | truthfully; according to the facts | 如实 is a common adverb in legal, administrative, and everyday contexts meaning to report or state things as they really are. | 失真 | shīzhēn | 失 “lose” + 真 “truth, reality” | distortion; lose fidelity; be distorted | 失真 is used for sound, images, data, and representations that no longer faithfully reproduce the original. | 本质 | běnzhì | 本 “root, fundamental” + 质 “substance, quality” | essence; nature; substance | 本质 is widely used in philosophical, political, and ordinary analytical contexts for what something fundamentally is. | 实质 | shízhì | 实 “real, actual” + 质 “substance” | substance; essence; actual nature | 实质 is common in formal and analytical language for the real content or substantive nature behind appearances, wording, or procedure. | 正当 | zhèngdàng | 正 “proper, right” + 当 “appropriate” | proper; legitimate; justified | 正当 is used in legal, moral, and practical contexts to evaluate conduct, reasons, rights, or procedures as proper and justified. | 不实 | bùshí | 不 “not” + 实 “real, factual” | untrue; unfounded; not factual | 不实 is frequent in formal written Chinese for allegations, reports, or statements that are not true or not supported by facts. It is common in phrases like 内容不实 “the content is untrue” and 不实之词 “false claims.” | [CORE] |
| 冒牌 | màopái | 冒 “pass off as” + 牌 “brand, label” | counterfeit; bogus; knockoff | 冒牌 is used for goods, identities, or credentials falsely presented as belonging to a recognized brand or status.
