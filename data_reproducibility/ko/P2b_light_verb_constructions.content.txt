# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2b Compositional Constructions
# Language: Korean (ko)

---

## METHODOLOGICAL NOTE

Korean compositional constructions involving truth-domain vocabulary include:
1. **Verb phrase constructions** (e.g., noun + verb combinations)
2. **Idiomatic expressions** (fixed multi-word units)
3. **Collocational patterns** (statistically significant co-occurrences)
4. **Grammaticalized constructions** (syntactic patterns with specialized meanings)

This analysis documents only attested, natural constructions. Absence of constructions for particular semantic patterns is explicitly noted.

## CONSTRUCTIONS BY BASE FORM

### 1. Base: 진실 (jinsil) "truth"

**Construction 1:**
- **Base Form:** 진실 (jinsil)
- **Construction:** 진실을 말하다 (jinsireul malhada)
- **Transliteration:** jinsireul malhada
- **Translation:** to tell the truth, to speak the truth
- **Usage:** Standard expression for truthful speech. Very common across all registers. Example: "진실을 말해 주세요" (Please tell me the truth).
- **Status:** [CORE]

**Construction 2:**
- **Base Form:** 진실 (jinsil)
- **Construction:** 진실을 밝히다 (jinsireul balkhida)
- **Transliteration:** jinsireul balkhida
- **Translation:** to reveal the truth, to bring the truth to light
- **Usage:** Common in investigative, journalistic, and legal contexts. Emphasizes uncovering hidden truth. Example: "사건의 진실을 밝히다" (to reveal the truth of the incident).
- **Status:** [CORE]

**Construction 3:**
- **Base Form:** 진실 (jinsil)
- **Construction:** 진실을 숨기다 (jinsireul sumgida)
- **Transliteration:** jinsireul sumgida
- **Translation:** to hide the truth, to conceal the truth
- **Usage:** Common expression for deliberate concealment of truth. Used in moral and investigative contexts.
- **Status:** [CORE]

**Construction 4:**
- **Base Form:** 진실 (jinsil)
- **Construction:** 진실을 알다 (jinsireul alda)
- **Transliteration:** jinsireul alda
- **Translation:** to know the truth
- **Usage:** Standard expression for possessing knowledge of truth. Common across all contexts.
- **Status:** [CORE]

**Construction 5:**
- **Base Form:** 진실 (jinsil)
- **Construction:** 진실을 찾다 (jinsireul chatda)
- **Transliteration:** jinsireul chatda
- **Translation:** to seek the truth, to search for the truth
- **Usage:** Common in investigative and philosophical contexts. Emphasizes active pursuit of truth.
- **Status:** [CORE]

**Construction 6:**
- **Base Form:** 진실 (jinsil)
- **Construction:** 진실이 드러나다 (jinsiri deureonada)
- **Transliteration:** jinsiri deureonada
- **Translation:** the truth comes to light, the truth is revealed
- **Usage:** Intransitive construction describing truth becoming known. Common in narrative and journalistic contexts.
- **Status:** [CORE]

**Construction 7:**
- **Base Form:** 진실 (jinsil)
- **Construction:** 진실 게임 (jinsil geim)
- **Transliteration:** jinsil geim
- **Translation:** truth game (game where participants must tell the truth)
- **Usage:** Colloquial expression for social games involving truthful disclosure. Borrowed from English "truth game."
- **Status:** [CORE]

**Construction 8:**
- **Base Form:** 진실 (jinsil)
- **Construction:** 진실의 순간 (jinsirui sungan)
- **Transliteration:** jinsirui sungan
- **Translation:** moment of truth
- **Usage:** Idiomatic expression for critical moment when truth is revealed or tested. Calque from English.
- **Status:** [CORE]

### 2. Base: 거짓 (geojit) "falsehood"

**Construction 1:**
- **Base Form:** 거짓 (geojit)
- **Construction:** 거짓말을 하다 (geojinmareul hada)
- **Transliteration:** geojinmareul hada
- **Translation:** to tell a lie, to lie
- **Usage:** Most common expression for lying. Standard across all registers. Example: "거짓말하지 마" (Don't lie).
- **Status:** [CORE]

**Construction 2:**
- **Base Form:** 거짓 (geojit)
- **Construction:** 거짓말이 들통나다 (geojinmari deultongada)
- **Transliteration:** geojinmari deultongada
- **Translation:** a lie is exposed, a lie is found out
- **Usage:** Idiomatic expression for lies being discovered. Common in everyday speech. "들통나다" means "to be exposed/found out."
- **Status:** [CORE]

**Construction 3:**
- **Base Form:** 거짓 (geojit)
- **Construction:** 거짓말을 꾸미다 (geojinmareul kkumida)
- **Transliteration:** geojinmareul kkumida
- **Translation:** to fabricate a lie, to concoct a lie
- **Usage:** Emphasizes deliberate construction of false story. "꾸미다" means "to devise/fabricate."
- **Status:** [CORE]

**Construction 4:**
- **Base Form:** 거짓 (geojit)
- **Construction:** 거짓말을 밝히다 (geojinmareul balkhida)
- **Transliteration:** geojinmareul balkhida
- **Translation:** to expose a lie, to reveal a lie
- **Usage:** Common in investigative contexts. Emphasizes uncovering falsehood.
- **Status:** [CORE]

**Construction 5:**
- **Base Form:** 거짓 (geojit)
- **Construction:** 새빨간 거짓말 (saeppalgan geojinmal)
- **Transliteration:** saeppalgan geojinmal
- **Translation:** blatant lie, outright lie (literally "bright red lie")
- **Usage:** Idiomatic intensifier emphasizing obviousness or brazenness of lie. Very common in colloquial speech.
- **Status:** [CORE]

**Construction 6:**
- **Base Form:** 거짓 (geojit)
- **Construction:** 하얀 거짓말 (hayan geojinmal)
- **Transliteration:** hayan geojinmal
- **Translation:** white lie
- **Usage:** Calque from English "white lie." Refers to harmless or well-intentioned lies. Common in contemporary usage.
- **Status:** [CORE]

**Construction 7:**
- **Base Form:** 거짓 (geojit)
- **Construction:** 거짓말처럼 (geojinmalcheoreom)
- **Transliteration:** geojinmalcheoreom
- **Translation:** like a lie, unbelievably
- **Usage:** Idiomatic expression meaning something is so surprising it seems unreal. Example: "거짓말처럼 성공했다" (succeeded unbelievably).
- **Status:** [CORE]

- **Translation:** it is not a fact, it is not true

**Construction 4:**
- **Base Form:** 사실 (sasil)
- **Construction:** 사실은 (sasireun)
- **Transliteration:** sasireun
- **Translation:** actually, in fact, the truth is
- **Usage:** Discourse marker introducing factual correction or revelation. Extremely common in spoken Korean. Example: "사실은 나도 몰랐어" (Actually, I didn't know either).
- **Status:** [CORE]

**Construction 5:**
- **Base Form:** 사실 (sasil)
- **Construction:** 사실 여부 (sasil yeobu)
- **Transliteration:** sasil yeobu
- **Translation:** truth or falsehood, factuality
- **Usage:** Formal expression for whether something is factual. Common in legal and investigative contexts. Example: "사실 여부를 가리다" (to determine truth or falsehood).
- **Status:** [CORE]

**Construction 6:**
- **Base Form:** 사실 (sasil)
- **Construction:** 사실관계 (sasilgwangye)
- **Transliteration:** sasilgwangye
- **Translation:** factual relationship, facts of the case
- **Usage:** Legal and administrative term for factual circumstances. Common in "사실관계를 파악하다" (to ascertain the facts).
- **Status:** [CORE]

**Construction 7:**
- **Base Form:** 사실 (sasil)
- **Construction:** 사실무근 (sasilmugeun)
- **Transliteration:** sasilmugeun
- **Translation:** groundless, baseless, unfounded
- **Usage:** Formal four-character compound meaning "without factual basis." Common in official denials. Example: "사실무근한 소문" (groundless rumor).
- **Status:** [CORE]

- **Transliteration:** jinjjainji gajjainji

**Construction 3:**
- **Base Form:** 진짜 (jinjja)
- **Construction:** 진짜 같다 (jinjja gatda)
- **Transliteration:** jinjja gatda
- **Translation:** seems real, looks genuine
- **Usage:** Common expression for something appearing authentic. Can be used positively or with suspicion.
- **Status:** [CORE]

**Construction 4:**
- **Base Form:** 진짜 (jinjja)
- **Construction:** 진짜배기 (jinjjabaegi)
- **Transliteration:** jinjjabaegi
- **Translation:** the real deal, the genuine article
- **Usage:** Colloquial intensified form emphasizing authenticity. "배기" is an intensifying suffix. Example: "진짜배기 실력" (genuine skill).
- **Status:** [CORE]

## SUMMARY OBSERVATIONS

### Productive Construction Patterns:

1. **Noun + 을/를 + Verb patterns:** Highly productive with truth-domain nouns (진실을 말하다, 사실을 확인하다, 거짓말을 하다)

2. **Adverb + Verb patterns:** Common with manner adverbs (정확하게 말하다, 솔직히 말하면, 진정으로 사랑하다)

3. **Adjective + Noun collocations:** Frequent and natural (정당한 이유, 타당한 근거, 확실한 증거)

4. **Abstract noun + 이/가 있다/없다:** Standard pattern for quality attribution (신빙성이 있다/없다)

5. **Discourse markers:** Several items function as discourse markers (사실은, 솔직히 말하면)

### Domain-Specific Patterns:

- **Legal/investigative domain:** Rich in constructions involving verification, testimony, and evidence (진위를 가리다, 허위로 진술하다, 진상을 밝히다)

- **Interpersonal domain:** Constructions emphasizing honesty and frankness (솔직히 말하면, 정직하게 말하다)

- **Evaluative domain:** Constructions for assessing correctness and validity (옳고 그름, 맞는지 틀리는지, 타당성을 검토하다)

### Register Variation:

- Sino-Korean bases (진실, 사실, 정당, 타당) tend toward formal constructions
- Native Korean bases (참, 맞다, 옳다) appear in both formal and colloquial constructions
- Colloquial items (진짜, 거짓말) dominate informal constructions

### Asymmetries:

- Truth-asserting constructions are more numerous than falsehood-asserting ones
- Verification and revelation constructions are highly productive
- Some bases (진위, 진본) have limited construction patterns, functioning primarily in specialized contexts
