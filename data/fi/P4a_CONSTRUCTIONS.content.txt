# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P4a CONSTRUCTIONS
# Language: Finnish (fi)

## 1. ATTRIBUTION

### Pattern
**X on TRUTH-TERM** (X is TRUTH-TERM)
Standard predicative construction using copula *olla* "to be" + nominative or partitive case of truth-domain term. Partitive case (*totta*, *valhetta*) is required for abstract truth evaluation; nominative for concrete/countable entities.

### Examples
- se on totta
  - se on totta
  - that is true
  
- väite on epätosi
  - väite on epätosi
  - the claim is false

### Notes
Partitive case obligatory for mass/abstract truth (*totta*, not *\*tosi*). Nominative used for countable entities (*tosiasia* "fact", *valhe* "lie"). Negation: *ei ole totta* "is not true".

---

## 2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION

### Pattern
**X ei ole TRUTH-TERM** (X is not TRUTH-TERM)
Standard negation using negative auxiliary *ei* + copula stem *ole* + partitive or nominative of truth-term. Partitive case maintained under negation for abstract terms.

### Examples
- se ei ole totta
  - se ei ole totta
  - that is not true
  
- tieto ei ole luotettavaa
  - tieto ei ole luotettavaa
  - the information is not reliable

### Notes
Partitive case required under negation for abstract/mass nouns (*totta*, *luotettavaa*). Nominative for countable nouns (*valhe*). Construction *ei pidä paikkaansa* "does not hold true" is fixed alternative.

---

## 3. ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION

### Pattern
**TRUTH-TERM + NOUN** (TRUTH-TERM + NOUN)
Direct adjectival modification in attributive position. Truth-domain adjectives precede noun and agree in case/number. Compounds (*tosi-*, *vale-*) are also productive.

### Examples
- tosi väite
  - tosi väite
  - true claim
  
- väärä todistus
  - väärä todistus
  - false testimony

### Notes
Adjectives (*tosi*, *väärä*, *oikea*, *aito*, *todellinen*, *luotettava*) modify directly. Compound forms (*tositarina* "true story", *valepassi* "fake passport") are lexicalized. Genitive constructions rare (*totuuden hetki* "moment of truth").

---

## 4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE

### Pattern
**X + INCHOATIVE-VERB** (X becomes TRUTH-STATE)
Inchoative formations using *-stua/-styä* suffixes (become) or light verb *tulla* "become" + translative case (*-ksi*). Marks transition into truth-state.

### Examples
- väite osoittautui vääräksi
  - väite osoittautui vääräksi
  - the claim proved (to be) false
  
- totuus paljastui
  - totuus paljastui
  - the truth was revealed

### Notes
Translative case (*oikeaksi*, *vääräksi*) with *osoittautua* "prove to be". Inchoative verbs: *todellistua* "be realized", *toteutua* "come true", *vääristyä* "become distorted", *todentua* "be confirmed". Light verb *tulla* + translative also possible.

---

## 5. CAUSATIVE

### Pattern
**AGENT + CAUSATIVE-VERB + OBJECT** (AGENT makes X TRUTH-STATE)
Causative formations using *-ttaa/-staa* suffixes or light verb constructions. Agent causes transition to truth-state or establishes truth-value.

### Examples
- todistaa väite vääräksi
  - todistaa väite vääräksi
  - to prove the claim false
  
- vääristää totuutta
  - vääristää totuutta
  - to distort the truth

### Notes
Causative verbs: *todistaa* "prove", *todentaa* "verify", *todellistaa* "realize/actualize", *toteuttaa* "implement", *vääristää* "distort", *väärentää* "forge", *oikeuttaa* "justify". Translative case marks result state (*vääräksi* "false", *oikeaksi* "right").

---

## 6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION

### Pattern
**ROOT + -uus/-yys/-suus** (quality of being TRUTH-STATE)
Productive abstract noun formation from adjectival bases. Suffixes *-uus/-yys* (after vowel-final stems) or *-suus* (after consonant clusters) create quality/state nominals.

### Examples
- totuus
  - totuus
  - truth (< tosi "true")
  
- rehellisyys
  - rehellisyys
  - honesty (< rehellinen "honest")

### Notes
Highly productive: *totuus* "truth", *todellisuus* "reality", *oikeellisuus* "correctness", *luotettavuus* "reliability", *aitous* "authenticity", *rehellisyys* "honesty", *todennäköisyys* "probability". Suffix selection phonologically conditioned.

---

## 7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION

### Pattern
**VERB-STEM + -ja/-jä** (one who performs TRUTH-ACTION)
Agent noun formation using *-ja/-jä* suffix attached to verb stem. Marks person who performs truth-related action (testifying, lying, etc.).

### Examples
- todistaja
  - todistaja
  - witness (< todistaa "testify/prove")
  
- väärentäjä
  - väärentäjä
  - forger (< väärentää "forge")

### Notes
Productive for action verbs: *todistaja* "witness", *väärentäjä* "forger", *väittelijä* "debater". No common agent noun for "liar" from *valehdella* (uses *valehtelija* marginally or descriptive *valehteleva henkilö*). "Truth-teller" expressed periphrastically (*totuuden puhuja*).

---

## 8. RESULT NOMINALIZATION

### Pattern
**VERB-STEM + -e/-us/-os** (result/product of TRUTH-ACTION)
Result noun formation marking product or outcome of truth-related action. Multiple suffixes depending on verb class and semantic nuance.

### Examples
- todiste
  - todiste
  - proof, evidence (< todistaa "prove")
  
- väärennös
  - väärennös
  - forgery, counterfeit (< väärentää "forge")

### Notes
Productive patterns: *todiste* "evidence" (*-e*), *todistus* "certificate/testimony" (*-us*), *väärennös* "forgery" (*-ös*), *väite* "claim" (*-e*), *toteutus* "implementation" (*-us*). Suffix choice partly lexicalized.

---

## 9. ACTION NOMINALIZATION

### Pattern
**VERB-STEM + -minen** (act/process of TRUTH-ACTION)
Productive action/process nominalization using *-minen* suffix. Creates gerund-like nouns denoting ongoing or completed action.

### Examples
- todistaminen
  - todistaminen
  - proving, testifying (< todistaa)
  
- vääristäminen
  - vääristäminen
  - distorting (< vääristää)

### Notes
Fully productive for all verbs: *todistaminen* "proving", *toteuttaminen* "implementing", *todellistuminen* "being realized", *vääristäminen* "distorting". Functions as verbal noun in all syntactic positions.

---

## 10. MANNER ADVERBIAL

### Pattern
**ADJECTIVE-STEM + -sti** (in TRUTH-MANNER)
Productive manner adverb formation from adjectives using *-sti* suffix. Describes manner of action according to truth-domain quality.

### Examples
- rehellisesti
  - rehellisesti
  - honestly (< rehellinen "honest")
  
- todenmukaisesti
  - todenmukaisesti
  - truthfully, accurately (< todenmukainen "truthful")

### Notes
Fully productive: *oikein* "correctly", *väärin* "wrongly", *todella* "really", *todennäköisesti* "probably", *rehellisesti* "honestly", *vilpittömästi* "sincerely". Some forms irregular (*oikein*, *väärin* < *oikea*, *väärä*).

---

## 11. LIGHT VERB CONSTRUCTIONS (TRUTH-TELLING)

### Pattern
**SPEECH-VERB + TRUTH-TERM (partitive/genitive)** (to speak/tell TRUTH-TERM)
Light verb constructions with verbs of speaking (*puhua*, *sanoa*, *kertoa*) + truth-domain noun in partitive or genitive case.

### Examples
- puhua totuutta
  - puhua totuutta
  - to speak the truth
  
- puhua valhetta
  - puhua valhetta
  - to tell a lie

### Notes
Common verbs: *puhua* "speak", *sanoa* "say", *kertoa* "tell". Partitive case standard (*totuutta*, *valhetta*). Fixed expressions: *puhua totta* "speak truth", *valehdella* (dedicated verb "to lie" from *valhe*).

---

## 12. LIGHT VERB CONSTRUCTIONS (ERROR)

### Pattern
**tehdä + virhe** (to make + error)
Fixed construction with *tehdä* "make/do" + *virhe* "error/mistake" for error commission. Correction uses *korjata* "correct" or *oikaista* "rectify".

### Examples
- tehdä virhe
  - tehdä virhe
  - to make a mistake
  
- korjata virhe
  - korjata virhe
  - to correct an error

### Notes
Standard error construction: *tehdä virhe(itä)* "make error(s)". Correction: *korjata virhe* "correct error", *oikaista virhe* "rectify error". Discovery: *huomata virhe* "notice error", *löytää virhe* "find error".

---

## 13. LIGHT VERB CONSTRUCTIONS (EVIDENCE)

### Pattern
**EVIDENCE-VERB + todiste(ita)** (to VERB + evidence)
Light verb constructions with *todiste* "evidence" and various verbs marking different evidence-related actions (presenting, gathering, evaluating).

### Examples
- esittää todisteita
  - esittää todisteita
  - to present evidence
  
- kerätä todisteita
  - kerätä todisteita
  - to collect evidence

### Notes
Common verbs: *esittää* "present", *tuoda* "bring", *hankkia* "obtain", *kerätä* "collect", *arvioida* "evaluate", *punnita* "weigh". Partitive plural (*todisteita*) standard. Legal register concentration.

---

## 14. PREDICATIVE STATE CONSTRUCTIONS

### Pattern
**olla + LOCATIVE-CASE** (to be in STATE-LOCATION)
Idiomatic constructions using copula + locative cases (inessive *-ssa*, adessive *-lla*) to express being in truth-related state.

### Examples
- olla oikeassa
  - olla oikeassa
  - to be right (lit: to be in right)
  
- olla väärässä
  - olla väärässä
  - to be wrong (lit: to be in wrong)

### Notes
Fixed expressions: *olla oikeassa* "be right", *olla väärässä* "be wrong", *olla tosissaan* "be serious" (inessive case). Contrast with predicative nominative/partitive (*olla totta* "be true").

---

## 15. FIXED IDIOMATIC EXPRESSIONS

### Pattern
**TRUTH-TERM + pitää paikkansa** (TRUTH-TERM holds its place)
Fixed idiomatic expression *pitää paikkansa* "to hold true, be accurate" (lit: "hold its place"). Subject can be statement, claim, or information.

### Examples
- väite pitää paikkansa
  - väite pitää paikkansa
  - the claim is true/accurate
  
- tarina pitää paikkansa
  - tarina pitää paikkansa
  - the story is true

### Notes
Highly frequent idiom for affirming truth/accuracy. Negation: *ei pidä paikkaansa* "is not true". Related: *paikkansapitävä* "accurate" (adjectival derivative). Literal meaning opaque to most speakers.

---

## 16. INTENSIFIER CONSTRUCTIONS (COLLOQUIAL)

### Pattern
**tosi + ADJECTIVE/ADVERB** (really/very + ADJECTIVE/ADVERB)
Colloquial use of *tosi* as degree intensifier meaning "really" or "very". Restricted to informal registers.

### Examples
- tosi hyvä
  - tosi hyvä
  - really good
  
- tosi paljon
  - tosi paljon
  - really much, a lot

### Notes
Grammaticalized from truth-domain adjective to degree modifier. Informal only; formal equivalent *todella* "really" or *hyvin* "very". Productive with any gradable adjective/adverb. Parallel: *oikein* "really, quite" (more acceptable in formal contexts).

---

## 17. DISCOURSE MARKERS

### Pattern
**TRUTH-TERM + PARTICIPLE/ADVERB** (TRUTH-TERM speaking/said)
Grammaticalized discourse markers introducing frank or honest statements. Fixed expressions functioning as sentence adverbials.

### Examples
- rehellisesti sanottuna
  - rehellisesti sanottuna
  - honestly speaking, to be honest
  
- totta puhuen
  - totta puhuen
  - to tell the truth, truthfully speaking

### Notes
Fixed discourse markers: *rehellisesti sanottuna* "honestly speaking", *totta puhuen* "truthfully speaking", *totta kai* "of course". Grammaticalized from truth-evaluation to discourse-pragmatic functions. High frequency in spoken language.

---

## 18. COMPARATIVE CONSTRUCTIONS

### Pattern
**TRUTH-TERM + COMPARATIVE-SUFFIX (-mpi)** (more TRUTH-QUALITY)
Standard comparative formation using *-mpi* suffix. Superlative uses *-in* suffix. Some truth-domain terms resist comparison.

### Examples
- todenmukaisempi
  - todenmukaisempi
  - more truthful, more accurate
  
- luotettavampi
  - luotettavampi
  - more reliable

### Notes
Comparative productive for gradable adjectives: *todenmukaisempi* "more truthful", *luotettavampi* "more reliable", *uskottavampi* "more credible". Absolute terms resist comparison: *\*tosempi* "more true", *\*valhempi* "more false" ungrammatical. Use *enemmän totta* "more true" periphrastically.

---

## 19. NEGATIVE DERIVATION (epä-)

### Pattern
**epä- + TRUTH-TERM** (un-/non- + TRUTH-TERM)
Productive negative prefix *epä-* creates antonyms. Applies to adjectives and some nouns. Semantically marks absence or negation of quality.

### Examples
- epätosi
  - epätosi
  - untrue, false
  
- epärehellinen
  - epärehellinen
  - dishonest

### Notes
Productive with adjectives: *epätosi* "untrue", *epärehellinen* "dishonest", *epäluotettava* "unreliable", *epäuskottava* "incredible". Less productive with nouns. Competes with dedicated antonyms (*väärä* "false" vs. *epätosi* "untrue").

---

## 20. PRIVATIVE DERIVATION (-tön)

### Pattern
**ROOT + -tön/-ttömyys** (without QUALITY / lack of QUALITY)
Privative suffix *-tön* "without" creates adjectives marking absence. Abstract noun *-ttömyys* marks state of lacking quality.

### Examples
- virheetön
  - virheetön
  - error-free, flawless
  
- vilpitön
  - vilpitön
  - sincere, without deceit (< vilppi "deceit")

### Notes
Productive: *virheetön* "error-free", *virheettömyys* "flawlessness", *vilpitön* "sincere", *vilpittömyys* "sincerity". Marks absence of negative quality. *Vilpitön* lexicalized with positive meaning "sincere" (lit: "without deceit").

---

## 21. COMPOUND FORMATIONS (tosi-)

### Pattern
**tosi- + NOUN** (true/real + NOUN)
Productive compound formation with *tosi-* prefix marking authenticity or factuality. Creates compounds distinguishing real from fictional/simulated.

### Examples
- tositarina
  - tositarina
  - true story
  
- tosielämä
  - tosielämä
  - real life

### Notes
Productive compounds: *tositarina* "true story", *tositapahtumat* "real events", *tosielämä* "real life", *tosiasia* "fact". Marks factual basis or actual occurrence. Contrast with fictional or theoretical.

---

## 22. COMPOUND FORMATIONS (vale-)

### Pattern
**vale- + NOUN** (false/fake + NOUN)
Productive compound formation with *vale-* prefix (< *valhe* "lie") marking falseness or counterfeit status. Creates compounds for fake/fraudulent entities.

### Examples
- valepassi
  - valepassi
  - fake passport
  
- valenimi
  - valenimi
  - pseudonym, false name

### Notes
Highly productive: *valepassi* "fake passport", *valenimi* "pseudonym", *valeraha* "counterfeit money", *valetieto* "false information". Marks deliberate falsification or inauthenticity. Legal/criminal terminology concentration.

---

## 23. TRANSLATIVE RESULT CONSTRUCTIONS

### Pattern
**VERB + OBJECT + TRANSLATIVE-CASE** (VERB + OBJECT + into STATE)
Translative case (*-ksi*) marks result state in causative constructions. Common with *todistaa* "prove" and *osoittautua* "prove to be".

### Examples
- todistaa oikeaksi
  - todistaa oikeaksi
  - to prove right, to vindicate
  
- todistaa vääräksi
  - todistaa vääräksi
  - to prove wrong, to disprove

### Notes
Translative marks result: *oikeaksi* "into right/correct", *vääräksi* "into wrong/false", *todeksi* "into true/real". Common with *todistaa* "prove", *osoittautua* "turn out", *kääntyä* "turn". Fixed expressions: *todistaa oikeaksi/vääräksi*.

---

## 24. PARTITIVE OBJECT CONSTRUCTIONS

### Pattern
**VERB + TRUTH-TERM (partitive)** (VERB + TRUTH-TERM)
Partitive case marks object in truth-domain constructions, especially with mass/abstract nouns. Required with certain verbs and under negation.

### Examples
- etsiä totuutta
  - etsiä totuutta
  - to seek the truth
  
- vääristää totuutta
  - vääristää totuutta
  - to distort the truth

### Notes
Partitive required for mass/abstract objects: *totuutta* "truth", *valhetta* "lies", *tosiasioita* "facts". Verbs: *etsiä* "seek", *puhua* "speak", *vääristää* "distort", *salata* "conceal". Accusative for countable: *todistaa väite* "prove claim".

---

## 25. GENITIVE ATTRIBUTE CONSTRUCTIONS

### Pattern
**TRUTH-TERM (genitive) + NOUN** (TRUTH-TERM's + NOUN)
Genitive case marks possessive or attributive relationship. Common in abstract compounds and fixed expressions.

### Examples
- totuuden hetki
  - totuuden hetki
  - moment of truth
  
- totuuden etsintä
  - totuuden etsintä
  - search for truth

### Notes
Genitive constructions: *totuuden hetki* "moment of truth", *totuuden etsintä* "truth-seeking", *totuudenmukainen* "truthful" (< *totuuden mukainen* "according to truth"). Less productive than compounds. Some lexicalized (*todennäköinen* < *toden näköinen* "truth-appearing").
