# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P4b Construction Usage & Constraints
## Language: Georgian (ka)
## Transliteration: ISO 9984 (1996)

---

## CONSTRUCTION USAGE TABLE

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Epistemic Status | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Attribution | მართალი ხარ | martali xar | "you are right" | [CORE] | Used to affirm propositional/moral correctness of a person; copula follows the adjective (verb-final). Restricted to the propositional-correctness items (მართალი, სწორი); not generalizable to genuineness or reliability adjectives. Alternate: სწორი ხარ (sc̣ori xar, "you are correct"), distinguished as more factual/accuracy-based. |
| 2. Negative Attribution | არასანდო წყარო | arasando c̣q̇aro | "an unreliable source" | [MARGINAL] | Lexicalized negative attribute via privative prefix **ara-**; typical of reliability and honesty items. Restricted: attested only for arasando, arasaimedo, arap̣aṭiosani; not regularly available for truth/lie or genuineness items. Alternate: sentential negation, e.g. *არ ხარ მართალი* (ar xar martali) for items lacking the ara- form. |
| 3. Adnominal Modification | ნამდვილი მეგობარი | namdvili megobari | "a true/real friend" | [CORE] | Most productive frame in the field; Adj precedes N. Available across genuineness, reliability, accuracy, and honesty items at varying registers (everyday to formal/technical). Alternate: ყალბი ფული (q̇albi puli, "counterfeit money"); register variant avtenṭuri doḳumenṭi ("authentic document"). |
| 4. Become / Inchoative | ტყუილი გამოვიდა | ṭq̇uili gamovida | "it turned out to be a lie" | [MARGINAL] | No productive inchoative pattern; change-of-state surfaces only via independent perfective verbs. Restricted to a single borderline lexical frame (*gamovida* "turned out"); not a regular Adj→inchoative derivation. Alternate: none identified (only one example attested). |
| 5. Causative (or Absence) | გაყალბება | gaq̇albeba | "to falsify, to forge" | [CORE] | Productive only at the falsehood/fakeness pole via prefix + root + **-eba** (ga-, mo-). Asymmetric: robust for q̇alb-/ṭq̇u- roots but absent for truth/genuineness adjectives (no "cause-to-be-true" verb). Alternate: მოტყუება (moṭq̇ueba, "to deceive"). |
| 6. Abstract Nominalization | სიმართლე | simartle | "truth" | [CORE] | Quality nouns via **si-…-e** and **-eba/-oba** suffixation on adjectival bases. Recurs broadly (sizusṭe, gulc̣rpeloba, p̣aṭiosneba, namdviloba); some P1 items are already such derivatives and take no further nominalization. Alternate: სანდოობა (sandooba, "reliability, trustworthiness") via -oba. |
| 7. Agentive Nominalization | მატყუარა | maṭq̇uara | "liar, deceiver" | [CORE] | Agentive nouns at the falsehood pole via **ma-…-ara** and compounding (mc̣me/mkneli). Asymmetric: robust for the negative pole (maṭq̇uara, q̇albismkneli, crumoc̣me) but no lexicalized "truth-teller." Alternate: ცრუმოწმე (crumoc̣me, "false witness"); truth-telling expressed phrasally as simartlis tkma. |
| 8. Speech-Act (Genitive + Verbal-Noun) | სიმართლის თქმა | simartlis tkma | "telling the truth" | [CORE] | **N-genitive + verbal-noun (tkma)** naming the act of truth-telling/lying; clusters on truth/lie noun items, which are mutually contrastive. Does not extend to genuineness or reliability terms. Alternate: accusative + speech verb, e.g. ტყუილს ამბობ (ṭq̇uils ambob, "you are lying"). |
| 9. Essence / Genitive Collocation | საქმის არსი | sakmis arsi | "the essence/gist of the matter" | [CORE] | **N-genitive + essence noun** (arsi, buneba) expressing the core/nature of something; forms its own sub-field. Connects loosely to genuineness but shares no frames with truth/lie or honesty constructions. Alternate: ადამიანის ბუნება (adamianis buneba, "human nature"). |

---

## SUMMARY NOTES

- **CORE constructions (7):** Attribution (1), Adnominal Modification (3), Causative (5), Abstract Nominalization (6), Agentive Nominalization (7), Speech-Act (8), Essence/Genitive Collocation (9). These are common and idiomatic within their respective scopes, though several (5, 7) are pole-restricted to the falsehood/fakeness side.

- **MARGINAL constructions (2):** Negative Attribution (2) — restricted to a subset of reliability/honesty items; Become/Inchoative (4) — only a single borderline lexical frame attested, not a productive pattern.

- **UNCERTAIN constructions (0):** None.

- **Observed asymmetries (preserved, not inferred):** Causative (5) and Agentive Nominalization (7) are robust at the falsehood pole with no attested positive counterpart. Attribution (1) and Speech-Act (8) cluster on specific item subsets and do not generalize across the field.
