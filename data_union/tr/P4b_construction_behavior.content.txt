| **10. SINCERITY / CORRECTION DISCOURSE MARKER** | Doğrusu, bilmiyorum. | *Doğrusu, bilmiyorum.* | "Honestly, I don't know." | [CORE] | Fully grammaticalized as a sentence-initial sincerity hedge; common across spoken and written registers. | **10. SINCERITY / CORRECTION DISCOURSE MARKER** | Aslında öyle değil. | *Aslında öyle değil.* | "Actually, it's not like that." | [CORE] | *Aslında* is among the most frequent discourse markers in Turkish; used as a corrective or contrastive frame across all registers. | **10. | **11. APPROXIMATION CONSTRUCTION** | Doğruya en yakın cevap | *doğruya en yakın cevap* | "the answer closest to correct" | [CORE] | Productive and compositional; the spatial metaphor (*yakın/uzak* + dative truth noun) is natural and unrestricted. | **11. | **12. | **12. | **13. | **13. LIGHT-VERB CONSTRUCTION (etmek)** | Bunu hak etti. | *Bunu hak etti.* | "He/she deserved this." | [CORE] | *Hak etmek* is fully lexicalized and common across all registers. | **14. RIGHTS-DEPRIVATION / FULFILLMENT (yemek)** | Kimsenin hakkını yemem. | *Kimsenin hakkını yemem.* | "I will not deprive anyone of their due." | [CORE] | *Hakkını yemek* is a fully lexicalized idiom common in everyday speech and ethical/moral discourse. | **14. RIGHTS-DEPRIVATION / FULFILLMENT (vermek)** | İşinin hakkını verdi. | *İşinin hakkını verdi.* | "He/she did justice to his/her work." | [CORE] | *Hakkını vermek* is fully lexicalized and common across registers; the genitive-possessive frame (*işinin hakkını*) specifying the domain of entitlement is a characteristic extension of the basic construction. The set of verbs entering this frame (*vermek*, *aramak*, *yemek*, *gözetmek*) appears closed in P4a. |

| **4. INCHOATIVE** | *[adjective] + olmak* | Periphrastic alternate where *-leş-* is not conventionalized (*geçerli olmak*, *haklı olmak*); attested in P4a notes. |

| **5. CAUSATIVE (related)** | *doğrulamak*, *kanıtlamak*, *ispatlamak* | Factitive *-la-* verbalizations noted in P4a as a related but distinct pattern; not strict causatives of adjectival predicates. |

| **7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION** | *-cı* (noun-based habitual agent) vs. *-ıcı* (verb-based participial agent) | Both attested; distinct bases and distinct primary functions (nominal vs. adjectival). |
| **7. AGENTIVE (deceptive)** | *sahtekâr* (Persian *-kâr*) | Alternate agentive form with Persian suffix; lexicalized, not productively derived; attested in P4a notes alongside *sahteci*. |
