# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P1b ROOT EXPANSION
# Language: Turkish (tr)

---

## Step 1 — Identify Central Roots

---

**Root 1**

**Form:** doğru / *doğru*
**Participating P1 items:** 3 — *doğru* (item 1), *doğrulamak* (item 12), and implicitly the derivational family noted under item 1 (*doğruluk*, *doğrultmak*)
**Core meaning contribution:** Straightness, directness, correctness; the primary native Turkic root for propositional and moral rightness. Highly productive in derivation and compounding.

---

**Root 2**

**Form:** hak / *hak* (from Arabic *ḥaqq*)
**Participating P1 items:** 4 — *hak* (item 4), *haklı* (item 5), *hakikat* (item 3), *hakiki* (item 24)
**Core meaning contribution:** Right, truth, entitlement, due; the primary Arabic-origin root covering both epistemic truth and normative rightness. Appears in both direct borrowings and derived Turkish forms.

---

**Root 3**

**Form:** gerçek / *gerçek*
**Participating P1 items:** 3 — *gerçek* (item 2), *gerçekçi* (item 11), and the derivational family noted under item 2 (*gerçeklik*, *gerçekleşmek*)
**Core meaning contribution:** Reality, factual existence, what is actually the case; the primary native or old Turkic root for ontological realness as opposed to appearance or fiction.

## Step 2 — Root Expansion

---

### ROOT 1: doğru / *doğru*

---

**1.**
**Form:** doğruluk
**Transliteration:** doğruluk
**Literal breakdown:** *doğru* (correct/straight) + *-luk* (abstract noun suffix)
**Natural English translation:** truth, correctness, accuracy, integrity
**Context of use:** Used in philosophical, moral, and everyday contexts to name the property of being true or correct; also used to describe personal integrity in the sense of consistency between word and deed. *Doğruluğa bağlılık* (commitment to truth/integrity).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**2.**
**Form:** doğrulamak
**Transliteration:** doğrulamak
**Literal breakdown:** *doğru* + *-la-* (factitive/verbalizer) + *-mak* (infinitive)
**Natural English translation:** to verify, to confirm, to validate, to authenticate
**Context of use:** Used in administrative, journalistic, and scientific registers for the act of checking and confirming the truth or correctness of a claim, document, or identity. Already listed in P1 (item 12); included here as the primary verbal derivative.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**3.**
**Form:** doğrulama
**Transliteration:** doğrulama
**Literal breakdown:** *doğru* + *-la-* + *-ma* (verbal noun suffix)
**Natural English translation:** verification, confirmation, validation, authentication
**Context of use:** The nominalized form of *doğrulamak*; used in technical, administrative, and journalistic contexts. *Kimlik doğrulama* (identity verification/authentication) is a standard term in digital security and administrative procedure.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**4.**
**Form:** doğrulanmak
**Transliteration:** doğrulanmak
**Literal breakdown:** *doğru* + *-la-* + *-n-* (passive) + *-mak*
**Natural English translation:** to be verified, to be confirmed, to be validated
**Context of use:** The passive form; used in scientific and journalistic registers to describe claims or findings that have been confirmed by an external process or authority. *İddia doğrulandı* (the claim was confirmed/verified).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**5.**
**Form:** doğrulanabilir
**Transliteration:** doğrulanabilir
**Literal breakdown:** *doğru* + *-la-* + *-n-* + *-abil-* (potential) + *-ir*
**Natural English translation:** verifiable, confirmable
**Context of use:** Used in scientific and philosophical discourse, particularly in discussions of empirical methodology and the philosophy of science. *Doğrulanabilir hipotez* (verifiable hypothesis).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**6.**
**Form:** doğrulanabilirlik
**Transliteration:** doğrulanabilirlik
**Literal breakdown:** *doğrulanabilir* + *-lik* (abstract noun suffix)
**Natural English translation:** verifiability
**Context of use:** A technical term in philosophy of science and epistemology, used in Turkish translations of logical positivism and related literature. *Doğrulanabilirlik ilkesi* (the principle of verifiability).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**7.**
**Form:** doğrultmak
**Transliteration:** doğrultmak
**Literal breakdown:** *doğru* + *-lt-* (causative) + *-mak*
**Natural English translation:** to straighten, to correct, to align, to aim (a weapon)
**Context of use:** Used in physical and figurative senses: physically straightening something, correcting a course of action, or aiming a weapon or instrument. *Silahı doğrultmak* (to aim the weapon); *yanlışı doğrultmak* (to correct the error).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**8.**
**Form:** doğrultu
**Transliteration:** doğrultu
**Literal breakdown:** *doğru* + *-ltu* (nominal derivative of *doğrultmak*)
**Natural English translation:** direction, alignment, line (of direction)
**Context of use:** Used in technical, navigational, and everyday contexts to indicate a direction or line of orientation. *Kuzey doğrultusu* (northward direction); *doğru doğrultuda ilerlemek* (to advance in the right direction).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**9.**
**Form:** doğrudan
**Transliteration:** doğrudan
**Literal breakdown:** *doğru* + *-dan* (ablative/adverbial suffix) — "from the straight/direct"
**Natural English translation:** directly, straightforwardly, without intermediary
**Context of use:** Used as an adverb in everyday, administrative, and journalistic registers to indicate directness or the absence of mediation. *Doğrudan iletişim* (direct communication); *doğrudan seçim* (direct election).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**10.**
**Form:** doğrudan doğruya
**Transliteration:** doğrudan doğruya
**Literal breakdown:** *doğrudan* + *doğruya* (dative of *doğru*) — reduplicated directional construction
**Natural English translation:** directly, straight, point-blank, without any intermediary
**Context of use:** A reinforced adverbial phrase used in everyday and formal speech to emphasize complete directness or immediacy. More emphatic than *doğrudan* alone. *Doğrudan doğruya söylemek* (to say it straight out).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**11.**
**Form:** doğru dürüst
**Transliteration:** doğru dürüst
**Literal breakdown:** *doğru* (correct/straight) + *dürüst* (upright, honest — a near-synonym of Persian origin used here as a rhyming compound)
**Natural English translation:** properly, decently, adequately; (of a person) honest and upright
**Context of use:** A fixed collocation used in everyday speech, often with a slightly negative framing to indicate that something is not being done properly. *Doğru dürüst çalışmıyor* (it's not working properly); *doğru dürüst bir insan* (a decent, honest person).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**12.**
**Form:** dürüst
**Transliteration:** dürüst
**Literal breakdown:** from Persian *dorost* — correct, sound, honest (historically related to *doğru* through semantic convergence, now an independent lexeme)
**Natural English translation:** honest, upright, straightforward
**Context of use:** Used in moral and character evaluation to describe a person who is honest and does not deceive. *Dürüst bir insan* (an honest person). Participates in *dürüstlük* (honesty, integrity).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**13.**
**Form:** dürüstlük
**Transliteration:** dürüstlük
**Literal breakdown:** *dürüst* + *-lük* (abstract noun suffix)
**Natural English translation:** honesty, integrity, uprightness
**Context of use:** Used in moral, professional, and everyday evaluative contexts to name the quality of being honest and straightforward. *Dürüstlük ilkesi* (the principle of honesty/integrity).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**14.**
**Form:** doğru söylemek
**Transliteration:** doğru söylemek
**Literal breakdown:** *doğru* (true/correct) + *söylemek* (to say, to tell)
**Natural English translation:** to tell the truth, to say what is correct
**Context of use:** A standard collocation in everyday speech for the act of making a true statement. *Doğruyu söyle* (tell the truth) is a common imperative in interpersonal and legal contexts.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**15.**
**Form:** doğruyu söylemek
**Transliteration:** doğruyu söylemek
**Literal breakdown:** *doğru-yu* (accusative of *doğru*, used as noun "the truth") + *söylemek*
**Natural English translation:** to tell the truth (with definite reference to "the truth")
**Context of use:** Slightly more emphatic than *doğru söylemek*; the accusative marks *doğru* as a definite noun object ("the truth"). Used in moral exhortation and legal contexts. *Doğruyu söylemek zorundayım* (I must tell the truth).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**16.**
**Form:** doğru yanlış
**Transliteration:** doğru yanlış
**Literal breakdown:** *doğru* (correct) + *yanlış* (wrong) — coordinate pair
**Natural English translation:** right and wrong, true and false, correct and incorrect
**Context of use:** Used as a coordinate pair in educational, moral, and logical contexts to frame binary evaluation. *Doğru yanlış sorusu* (a true/false question); *doğruyu yanlıştan ayırt etmek* (to distinguish right from wrong / true from false).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**17.**
**Form:** doğru yol
**Transliteration:** doğru yol
**Literal breakdown:** *doğru* (straight/correct) + *yol* (road, path, way)
**Natural English translation:** the right path, the straight and narrow, the correct way
**Context of use:** Used in moral, religious, and everyday contexts to describe the correct course of conduct or belief. *Doğru yoldan sapmak* (to deviate from the right path) is a common moral expression; also used in navigation.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**18.**
**Form:** doğrusal
**Transliteration:** doğrusal
**Literal breakdown:** *doğru* (straight/line) + *-sal* (adjectival suffix for scientific/technical use)
**Natural English translation:** linear
**Context of use:** A technical term in mathematics, physics, and engineering meaning "linear" (pertaining to a straight line or linear relationship). *Doğrusal denklem* (linear equation); *doğrusal programlama* (linear programming).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**19.**
**Form:** doğru açı
**Transliteration:** doğru açı
**Literal breakdown:** *doğru* (straight/right) + *açı* (angle)
**Natural English translation:** right angle
**Context of use:** A standard mathematical and geometric term. *Doğru açılı üçgen* (right-angled triangle). Used in educational and technical contexts.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**20.**
**Form:** doğrulayıcı
**Transliteration:** doğrulayıcı
**Literal breakdown:** *doğrula-* (stem of *doğrulamak*) + *-yıcı* (agentive/adjectival suffix)
**Natural English translation:** verifying, confirmatory, validator
**Context of use:** Used in technical and administrative contexts to describe a person, process, or mechanism that verifies or confirms. *Doğrulayıcı kanıt* (confirmatory evidence); used in software and administrative systems.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**21.**
**Form:** doğruluk değeri
**Transliteration:** doğruluk değeri
**Literal breakdown:** *doğruluk* (truth/correctness) + *değer* (value) + *-i* (possessive/genitive construction)
**Natural English translation:** truth value
**Context of use:** A technical term in formal logic and philosophy of language. *Bir önermenin doğruluk değeri* (the truth value of a proposition). Used in Turkish logic textbooks and philosophical writing.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**22.**
**Form:** doğruluk koşulu
**Transliteration:** doğruluk koşulu
**Literal breakdown:** *doğruluk* + *koşul* (condition) + *-u* (possessive)
**Natural English translation:** truth condition
**Context of use:** A technical term in formal semantics and philosophy of language. *Doğruluk koşulları teorisi* (truth-conditional theory of meaning). Used in academic linguistic and philosophical writing.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**23.**
**Form:** doğruluk tablosu
**Transliteration:** doğruluk tablosu
**Literal breakdown:** *doğruluk* + *tablo* (table) + *-su* (possessive)
**Natural English translation:** truth table
**Context of use:** A standard term in formal logic and computer science. *Doğruluk tablosu oluşturmak* (to construct a truth table). Used in logic courses and digital circuit design.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**24.**
**Form:** doğru bilgi
**Transliteration:** doğru bilgi
**Literal breakdown:** *doğru* (correct/true) + *bilgi* (knowledge, information)
**Natural English translation:** correct information, accurate knowledge, true knowledge
**Context of use:** Used in educational, journalistic, and philosophical contexts to distinguish accurate information from misinformation. *Doğru bilgiye ulaşmak* (to access correct information/true knowledge).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**25.**
**Form:** doğru haber
**Transliteration:** doğru haber
**Literal breakdown:** *doğru* (correct/true) + *haber* (news, report)
**Natural English translation:** accurate news, correct report, true information
**Context of use:** Used in journalistic and everyday contexts to distinguish accurate reporting from misinformation or rumor. *Doğru haberi kaynağından almak* (to get accurate news from the source).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**26.**
**Form:** doğrultusunda
**Transliteration:** doğrultusunda
**Literal breakdown:** *doğrultu* (direction/alignment) + *-su* (possessive) + *-nda* (locative)
**Natural English translation:** in the direction of, in line with, in accordance with
**Context of use:** Used in formal, administrative, and journalistic registers as a postpositional phrase meaning "in accordance with" or "along the lines of." *Bu karar doğrultusunda hareket etmek* (to act in accordance with this decision).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**27.**
**Form:** doğru düzgün
**Transliteration:** doğru düzgün
**Literal breakdown:** *doğru* (correct/straight) + *düzgün* (orderly, proper) — rhyming/reinforcing compound
**Natural English translation:** properly, decently, in an orderly and correct manner
**Context of use:** A colloquial reinforcing compound used in everyday speech to indicate that something is done properly and correctly. *Doğru düzgün konuşmak* (to speak properly); often used in mild reproach.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**28.**
**Form:** doğruluk payı
**Transliteration:** doğruluk payı
**Literal breakdown:** *doğruluk* (truth/correctness) + *pay* (share, portion) + *-ı* (possessive)
**Natural English translation:** grain of truth, element of truth
**Context of use:** Used in everyday and journalistic discourse to acknowledge that a claim, though not entirely accurate, contains some truth. *Bu iddiada bir doğruluk payı var* (there is a grain of truth in this claim).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**29.**
**Form:** doğruya en yakın
**Transliteration:** doğruya en yakın
**Literal breakdown:** *doğru-ya* (dative of *doğru*) + *en* (most) + *yakın* (close, near)
**Natural English translation:** closest to the truth, nearest to correct
**Context of use:** Used in evaluative and epistemic contexts to describe an approximation or the best available account when certainty is not achievable. *Doğruya en yakın açıklama* (the explanation closest to the truth).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**30.**
**Form:** doğruluk testi
**Transliteration:** doğruluk testi
**Literal breakdown:** *doğruluk* + *test* (test, from English) + *-i* (possessive)
**Natural English translation:** truth test, accuracy test, fact-check
**Context of use:** Used in journalistic fact-checking and scientific contexts to describe a procedure for evaluating the accuracy of a claim or measurement. *Doğruluk testi uygulamak* (to apply a truth/accuracy test).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

> **Note:** The *doğru* root is highly productive. Additional forms exist beyond those listed here, including further technical compounds in mathematics (*doğru parçası*, line segment), computing (*doğrulama kodu*, verification code), and administrative language (*doğruluk beyanı*, declaration of accuracy). The 30 items above represent the most commonly attested forms.

### ROOT 2: hak / *hak* (and its Arabic-origin derivatives *hakikat*, *hakiki*)

---

**1.**
**Form:** hak
**Transliteration:** hak
**Literal breakdown:** from Arabic *ḥaqq* — right, truth, due
**Natural English translation:** right, entitlement, truth, justice
**Context of use:** Already listed in P1 (item 4); included here as the base form. Used in legal, moral, and religious contexts. *Hak aramak* (to seek one's rights); *hakkı teslim etmek* (to give someone their due).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**2.**
**Form:** haklı
**Transliteration:** haklı
**Literal breakdown:** *hak* + *-lı* (possessive/adjectival suffix)
**Natural English translation:** right, justified, in the right
**Context of use:** Already listed in P1 (item 5). Predicates that a person's claim or position is correct or warranted. *Haklısın* (you are right).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**3.**
**Form:** haksız
**Transliteration:** haksız
**Literal breakdown:** *hak* + *-sız* (privative suffix) — "without right"
**Natural English translation:** unjust, wrong, unwarranted, in the wrong
**Context of use:** The direct antonym of *haklı*; used in moral, legal, and interpersonal contexts to evaluate a claim, action, or person as lacking justification or being contrary to what is right. *Haksız yere suçlamak* (to accuse unjustly).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**4.**
**Form:** haksızlık
**Transliteration:** haksızlık
**Literal breakdown:** *haksız* + *-lık* (abstract noun suffix)
**Natural English translation:** injustice, wrong, unfairness
**Context of use:** Used in moral, legal, and political discourse to name the condition or act of being unjust or wrongful. *Haksızlığa uğramak* (to suffer injustice); *haksızlıkla mücadele etmek* (to fight injustice).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**5.**
**Form:** haklılık
**Transliteration:** haklılık
**Literal breakdown:** *haklı* + *-lık* (abstract noun suffix)
**Natural English translation:** justification, rightness, being in the right
**Context of use:** Used in legal and philosophical contexts to name the property of being justified or in the right. *Haklılığını kanıtlamak* (to prove that one is in the right/justified).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**6.**
**Form:** hak vermek
**Transliteration:** hak vermek
**Literal breakdown:** *hak* (right/truth) + *vermek* (to give)
**Natural English translation:** to concede that someone is right, to agree with someone, to grant someone's point
**Context of use:** A standard collocation in everyday and formal discourse for acknowledging that another person's position or claim is correct. *Sana hak veriyorum* (I agree with you / I concede you are right).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**7.**
**Form:** hakkını teslim etmek
**Transliteration:** hakkını teslim etmek
**Literal breakdown:** *hak-kı-nı* (accusative of *hak* with 3rd person possessive) + *teslim etmek* (to deliver, to hand over)
**Natural English translation:** to give someone their due, to acknowledge someone's merit, to render what is owed
**Context of use:** A fixed collocation used in moral and evaluative contexts to acknowledge that someone deserves credit or recognition. *Hakkını teslim etmek gerekir* (one must give credit where it is due).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**8.**
**Form:** hak etmek
**Transliteration:** hak etmek
**Literal breakdown:** *hak* + *etmek* (light verb, "to do/make")
**Natural English translation:** to deserve, to merit, to earn
**Context of use:** Used in moral and evaluative contexts to assert that someone deserves a particular outcome, reward, or punishment. *Bunu hak etti* (he/she deserved this). Extremely common in everyday speech.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**9.**
**Form:** hak kazanmak
**Transliteration:** hak kazanmak
**Literal breakdown:** *hak* + *kazanmak* (to earn, to win)
**Natural English translation:** to earn a right, to acquire an entitlement
**Context of use:** Used in legal and everyday contexts to describe the acquisition of a right or entitlement through action, time, or procedure. *İzin hakkı kazanmak* (to earn the right to leave/vacation).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**10.**
**Form:** hak ihlali
**Transliteration:** hak ihlali
**Literal breakdown:** *hak* (right) + *ihlal* (violation, from Arabic *ikhlāl*) + *-i* (possessive)
**Natural English translation:** rights violation, infringement of rights
**Context of use:** A standard legal and human rights term. *İnsan hakları ihlali* (human rights violation). Used in legal documents, journalism, and political discourse.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**11.**
**Form:** insan hakları
**Transliteration:** insan hakları
**Literal breakdown:** *insan* (human being) + *hak-lar-ı* (plural of *hak* with possessive)
**Natural English translation:** human rights
**Context of use:** The standard Turkish term for human rights, used in legal, political, and journalistic contexts. *İnsan Hakları Evrensel Beyannamesi* (Universal Declaration of Human Rights).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**12.**
**Form:** haklar
**Transliteration:** haklar
**Literal breakdown:** *hak* + *-lar* (plural suffix)
**Natural English translation:** rights, entitlements
**Context of use:** The plural form, used in legal and political discourse to refer to a set of rights. *Temel haklar* (fundamental rights); *haklar ve özgürlükler* (rights and freedoms).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**13.**
**Form:** hak arama
**Transliteration:** hak arama
**Literal breakdown:** *hak* + *aramak* (to seek, to look for) — verbal noun construction
**Natural English translation:** seeking one's rights, pursuit of justice
**Context of use:** Used in legal and political contexts to describe the act of pursuing one's legal or moral entitlements. *Hak arama özgürlüğü* (freedom to seek one's rights / access to justice).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

**14.**

**15.**

**Context of use:** Used as an adverb in formal and literary registers to assert that something is genuinely the case. *Hakikaten öyle* (it is truly so). Also used in everyday speech as an emphatic affirmation.

## Step 3 — Peripheral Extensions

The following are expressions where the root's contribution appears to shift meaning or function relative to its core truth-domain use. These are flagged without interpretation or categorization.

---

**From the *doğru* root:**

- *doğrusal* (linear): The root *doğru* contributes the geometric sense of "straight line" rather than any epistemic or moral sense; the term operates entirely within mathematics and engineering.

- *doğrultu* / *doğrultusunda*: The root contributes a spatial sense of "direction" or "alignment"; in administrative use (*bu karar doğrultusunda*), the connection to truth or correctness is not operative.

- *doğru açı* (right angle): The root functions as a geometric descriptor; no epistemic content is present.

- *doğrultmak* in the sense of aiming a weapon (*silahı doğrultmak*): The root contributes a physical directional sense with no connection to truth or correctness.

- *doğru dürüst* and *doğru düzgün*: In colloquial use, these compounds often function as degree or adequacy markers (*doğru dürüst çalışmıyor*, "it's not working properly") rather than as truth or moral evaluations.

---

**From the *hak* root:**

- *hak etmek* (to deserve): The root contributes a sense of desert or merit rather than truth or factual correctness; the connection is to normative entitlement rather than epistemic status.

- *Hak* as a divine name: In Islamic usage, *Hak* (capitalized) is one of the names of God, functioning as a proper noun with theological rather than propositional content.

- *hak yemek* (to deprive of rights): The root operates in a purely normative-legal domain; the idiomatic use of *yemek* (to eat) shifts the expression into a domain of wrongful taking rather than truth evaluation.

- *telif hakkı* (copyright), *oy hakkı* (right to vote): The root functions as a legal entitlement marker with no direct connection to truth or falsity.

- *hak kazandırmak* (to confer a right): The root operates in a procedural-legal domain concerning the acquisition of entitlements.

---

**From the *gerçek* root:**

- *gerçek zamanlı* (real-time): The root contributes a temporal-technical sense; the expression operates in computing and broadcasting with no epistemic content.

- *sanal gerçeklik* (virtual reality) and *artırılmış gerçeklik* (augmented reality): The root *gerçeklik* is used in compound technical terms where the "reality" in question is explicitly mediated or simulated; the relationship to unmediated truth is inverted or complicated.

- *gerçek üstü* / *gerçek üstücülük* (surreal / surrealism): The root contributes a baseline of "reality" against which the aesthetic movement defines itself by transgression; the root's function is as a reference point rather than an assertion.

- *gerçek yüz* (true face): The root operates in a domain of character revelation and moral unmasking rather than propositional truth.

- *gerçekleşmek* / *gerçekleştirmek* (to come true / to realize): The root contributes a sense of actualization or coming-into-being; the connection is to ontological realization rather than epistemic correctness.
