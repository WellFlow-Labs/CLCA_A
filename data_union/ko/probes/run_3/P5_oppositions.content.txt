# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P5 Oppositions
## Language: Korean (ko)
### Transliteration Standard: Revised Romanization of Korean (RR)

---

## Preliminary Note on Method

Oppositions below are extracted exclusively from patterns attested in P1–P4. Each opposition is supported by explicit evidence from those steps: direct statements of contrast, collocational pairing, morphological negation, or constructional alternation. No opposition has been inferred beyond what the data supports. Where asymmetries exist between the two poles of a contrast, they are reported rather than smoothed over.

## Opposition 1

**Term A:** 진짜 (jinjja)
**Term B:** 가짜 (gajja)
**Transliteration:** jinjja / gajja
**English translation:** genuine, real / fake, counterfeit
**Description of contrast:** P1 explicitly states that 진짜 and 가짜 are direct contrasts, with 진짜 marking the positive pole of the genuine/fake distinction and 가짜 marking the negative pole. The pair appears together in fixed constructions: 진짜인지 가짜인지 모르다 (not knowing whether it is real or fake), 진짜와 가짜를 구별하다 (to distinguish the genuine from the fake).
**Contrast type:** lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]
