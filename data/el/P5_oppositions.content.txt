## IDENTIFIED OPPOSITIONS

### OPPOSITION 1
**Term A:** αλήθεια (alítheia)  
**Term B:** ψέμα (pséma)  
**Transliteration:** alítheia vs. pséma  
**Translation:** truth vs. lie  
**Description:** Core lexical opposition between truth and falsehood in speech acts. Both terms function as nouns denoting the content of statements, with αλήθεια marking correspondence to facts and ψέμα marking deliberate misrepresentation.  
**Contrast type:** lexical  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## SUMMARY OBSERVATIONS

**Total clear oppositions identified:** 45 (excluding 3 uncertain cases where implied contrasts lack documented terms)

**Distribution by contrast type:**
- Lexical oppositions: 28
- Morphological oppositions: 4 (using α- privative prefix)
- Constructional oppositions: 13

**Key patterns observed:**

1. **Morphological negation limited:** Only 4 clear morphological oppositions using α- privative (δίκαιος/άδικος, έγκυρος/άκυρος, and their derivatives). Most oppositions are lexically distinct roots.

2. **Asymmetries in opposition:**
   - Truth-telling (λέω την αλήθεια) vs. lying (λέω ψέματα) shows number asymmetry (singular vs. plural)
   - Doing right (κάνω το σωστό) vs. making mistake (κάνω λάθος) shows article asymmetry (article present vs. absent)
   - Agent nouns productive for negative behaviors (ψεύτης) but not positive (no *αληθευτής)

3. **Multiple contrasts for single terms:**
   - αληθινός contrasts with ψευδής, ψεύτικος, and αληθοφανής
   - γνήσιος contrasts with πλαστός, κίβδηλος, and νόθος
   - λάθος contrasts with σωστός, ορθός, and ακριβής

4. **Productive oppositional patterns:**
   - ψευδ- prefix creates systematic oppositions with base terms (science/pseudoscience, democracy/pseudo-democracy, etc.)
   - Color metaphors create evaluative oppositions (άσπρο/μαύρο ψέμα)
   - Revelation/concealment constructions oppose truth emergence with truth hiding

5. **Register stratification:**
   - Formal oppositions: ψευδής/αληθινός, κίβδηλος/γνήσιος
   - Colloquial oppositions: ψεύτικος/αληθινός, κοτσάνα ψέματα
   - Legal/administrative: έγκυρος/άκυρος, βεβαιώνω/ακυρώνω

6. **Domain-specific oppositions:**
   - Legal: γνήσια/πλαστή υπογραφή, έγκυρο/άκυρο έγγραφο
   - Epistemic: βέβαιος/αμφίβολος, βεβαιότητα/αμφιβολία
   - Digital/contemporary: πραγματικός/εικονικός κόσμος, πληροφορία/ψευδοπληροφορία

7. **Implied but undocumented oppositions:**
   - Several terms suggest oppositions not explicitly documented in P1-P4 (ορθοδοξία implies heterodoxy, ορθολογικός implies irrationality, etc.)
   - These are marked [UNCERTAIN] as they lack clear attestation in the provided data
