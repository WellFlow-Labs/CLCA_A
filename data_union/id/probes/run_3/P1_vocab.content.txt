# Language: Indonesian (id)

---

**1.**
**Form:** benar
**Transliteration:** N/A (Latin script)
**Literal meaning:** true, correct
**Typical English translations:** true, correct, right, accurate

Used to affirm that a statement, fact, or claim corresponds to reality or is without error. Also used in moral and evaluative contexts to indicate that an action or position is right or proper. Participates in derivation: *kebenaran* (truth, correctness), *membenarkan* (to justify, to confirm), *sebenarnya* (in truth, actually).

**2.**
**Form:** salah
**Transliteration:** N/A
**Literal meaning:** wrong, mistaken
**Typical English translations:** wrong, incorrect, mistaken, false

Used to indicate that a statement, action, or judgment is in error or does not correspond to fact. Also used in moral contexts to mean that conduct is improper or blameworthy. Participates in derivation: *kesalahan* (error, fault, mistake), *menyalahkan* (to blame, to fault).

**[CORE]**

**3.**
**Form:** asli
**Transliteration:** N/A
**Literal meaning:** original, native
**Typical English translations:** genuine, authentic, original, real

Applied to objects, documents, persons, or cultural items to indicate that they are not copies, forgeries, or imitations. Contrasts with *palsu* and *tiruan*. Commonly used in legal, commercial, and cultural contexts.

**4.**
**Form:** palsu
**Transliteration:** N/A
**Literal meaning:** false, fake (from Portuguese *falso*)
**Typical English translations:** fake, counterfeit, false, forged, spurious

Used of documents, currency, goods, or identities that are fraudulently presented as genuine. Also used of statements or claims that are deliberately untrue. Participates in derivation: *memalsukan* (to falsify, to forge), *pemalsuan* (falsification, forgery).

**5.**

**Transliteration:** N/A

**Typical English translations:** real, actual, evident, concrete, tangible

Used to indicate that something exists in fact or is perceptibly present, as opposed to being imagined, hypothetical, or illusory. Also used to emphasize that a claim or situation is clearly the case. Participates in derivation: *kenyataan* (reality, fact), *menyatakan* (to state, to declare).

**6.**

**Transliteration:** N/A

**Typical English translations:** truly, really, genuinely, seriously

Used as an intensifier affirming the sincerity or reality of a statement or state of affairs. Often used in expressions of genuine feeling or to assert that something is not pretended. Participates in reduplication: *sungguh-sungguh* (earnestly, in all seriousness).

**15.**
**Form:** tiruan
**Transliteration:** N/A

**Typical English translations:** imitation, copy, replica, fake

Used of objects or representations that are made to resemble something else, without necessarily implying fraudulent intent. Contrasts with *asli*. The morphological base *tiru* participates in further derivation: *meniru* (to imitate), *peniruan* (imitation, copying).
