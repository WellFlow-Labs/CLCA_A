## SUMMARY OBSERVATIONS

### High-frequency CORE constructions:
- Zero copula attribution (Construction 1)
- Negative copula *değil* (Construction 2)
- Adnominal modification (Construction 3)
- *Çıkmak* inchoative (Construction 11)
- Abstract nominalization *-lık/-lik* (Construction 6)
- Light verb *söylemek* constructions (Construction 14)
- Question particle *mı/mi/mu/mü* (Construction 22)
- Complement clauses with *-dığını* (Construction 30)

### MARGINAL constructions (restricted register or lower frequency):
- *Dönüşmek* inchoative (Construction 4) — more formal/literary
- *Görmek* light verb (Construction 16) — formal register
- Comparative constructions (Construction 20) — truth-values rarely gradable
- Concessive with *dahi* (Construction 26) — formal/literary

### Notable asymmetries:
- *Doğruyu söylemek* (with ACC) vs. *yalan söylemek* (no ACC) — lexicalized difference
- Morphological causative limited to *gerçekleştirmek*; other causatives use light verbs
- Agentive morphology only for *yalancı* "liar"; truth-tellers require periphrasis
- Truth-value adjectives appear in comparison standards but rarely as compared elements

### Productive patterns:
- Light verb constructions (especially *etmek*, *olmak*, *çıkmak*, *söylemek*)
- Nominalization (*-lık/-lik* for abstract nouns, *-ma/-me* for verbal nouns)
- Participial relative clauses
- Complement clauses with accusative nominalization
