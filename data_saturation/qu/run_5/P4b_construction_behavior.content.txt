## Summary Notes

**Constructions rated [CORE]:** 1, 2, 3, 4 (both examples), 5, 6 (infinitive-as-nominal only), 7, 8, 9, 10 — these represent the dominant, productive, and cross-registerally attested patterns in the truth domain.

**Constructions rated [MARGINAL]:** 6 (*cheqa kay* abstract nominalization with *kay*) — restricted to formal and reflective registers; the everyday nominalization strategy (infinitive in *-y*) is [CORE].

**Constructions rated [UNCERTAIN]:** None identified on the basis of the P4a data. No construction in the P4a output was flagged as doubtful or unattested.

**Cross-cutting asymmetry noted:** The *cheqa* root behaves differently from other truth-domain roots in two constructions: (4) it does not attest a direct *cheqayay* inchoative form; (5) it uses *-ncha-* rather than *-chi-* for causative/verbalizing derivation. These asymmetries are reported as attested in P4a and are preserved here without theoretical interpretation.
