# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2a Derivational System
# Language: Korean (ko)

---

**Transliteration standard:** Revised Romanization of Korean (국립국어원 표준 로마자 표기법), applied consistently throughout.

---

**Scope note:** This document records attested derivational morphology for the 30 base lexical items identified in P1. Derivations already documented in P1b are cross-referenced rather than re-described in full. Forms are listed under each base item; no predefined derivational categories are imposed. Gaps and asymmetries are preserved as found.

---

## 1. 진실 (jinsil)

---

**1.1**
**Base Form:** 진실 (jinsil)
**Derived Form:** 진실성
**Transliteration:** jinsilseong
**Literal Breakdown:** 진실 (jinsil, truth) + 성 (性, nominalizing suffix -ness/-ity)
**Natural English Translation:** truthfulness, veracity, truth-value
**Usage:** Used in legal testimony evaluation and academic discourse to assess whether a statement or person possesses the quality of being truthful. Appears in constructions like 진실성을 검증하다 (to verify truthfulness). [Cross-referenced from P1b Root 1, item 2.]
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**1.2**
**Base Form:** 진실 (jinsil)
**Derived Form:** 진실하다
**Transliteration:** jinsilhada
**Literal Breakdown:** 진실 (jinsil, truth) + 하다 (hada, verbal/adjectival copula)
**Natural English Translation:** to be truthful, honest, sincere
**Usage:** Used to describe a person as honest and sincere in their dealings. Common in moral and interpersonal evaluation. [Cross-referenced from P1b Root 1, item 4.]
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**1.3**
**Base Form:** 진실 (jinsil)
**Derived Form:** 진실되다
**Transliteration:** jinsiltoeda
**Literal Breakdown:** 진실 (jinsil, truth) + 되다 (toeda, to be/become — passive/stative verbal suffix)
**Natural English Translation:** to be truthful, to be genuine
**Usage:** Predicative form used to evaluate a person or statement as genuinely truthful in character. Slightly more stative in connotation than 진실하다; common in moral and interpersonal evaluation. [Cross-referenced from P1b Root 1, item 3.]
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**1.4**
**Base Form:** 진실 (jinsil)
**Derived Form:** 진실되게
**Transliteration:** jinsiltoege
**Literal Breakdown:** 진실되다 (jinsiltoeda, to be truthful) + 게 (ge, adverbializing suffix)
**Natural English Translation:** truthfully, genuinely
**Usage:** Adverbial form derived from 진실되다. Used to describe how something is said or done — e.g., 진실되게 말하다 (to speak truthfully). Less frequent than 진실하게 but attested in written and spoken moral discourse.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**1.5**
**Base Form:** 진실 (jinsil)
**Derived Form:** 진실하게
**Transliteration:** jinsilhage
**Literal Breakdown:** 진실하다 (jinsilhada, to be truthful) + 게 (ge, adverbializing suffix)
**Natural English Translation:** truthfully, honestly, sincerely
**Usage:** Adverbial form derived from 진실하다. Used to describe the manner of speech or conduct as honest and sincere. Appears in 진실하게 대하다 (to treat someone sincerely).
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Note on 진실:** The suffix 성 (性) attaches to the nominal base 진실 to yield 진실성. The verbal/adjectival forms 진실하다 and 진실되다 are both attested and in common use; their adverbial forms in 게 are also attested. No passive or causative verbal derivations from 진실 are identified.

## 2. 사실 (sasil)

---

**2.1**
**Base Form:** 사실 (sasil)
**Derived Form:** 사실상
**Transliteration:** sasilsang
**Literal Breakdown:** 사실 (sasil, fact) + 상 (上, in terms of/on the level of)
**Natural English Translation:** in fact, in practice, effectively, de facto
**Usage:** Used as an adverbial expression to indicate that something is the case in practice even if not formally or officially so. Extremely common across legal, journalistic, and everyday registers. [Cross-referenced from P1b Root 2, item 2.]
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**2.2**
**Base Form:** 사실 (sasil)
**Derived Form:** 사실적
**Transliteration:** sasiljeok
**Literal Breakdown:** 사실 (sasil, fact/reality) + 적 (的, adjectival suffix -ic/-al)
**Natural English Translation:** realistic, factual, true-to-life
**Usage:** Used in aesthetic and descriptive contexts to evaluate whether a representation or account corresponds closely to reality. Common in literary and art criticism. [Cross-referenced from P1b Root 2, item 6.]
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**2.3**
**Base Form:** 사실 (sasil)
**Derived Form:** 사실적으로
**Transliteration:** sasiljeogeuro
**Literal Breakdown:** 사실적 (sasiljeok, realistic) + 으로 (euro, manner/instrumental particle)
**Natural English Translation:** realistically, factually, in a true-to-life manner
**Usage:** Adverbial form of 사실적. Used in critical and descriptive discourse to characterize how something is depicted or described. Appears in 사실적으로 묘사하다 (to depict realistically).
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**2.4**
**Base Form:** 사실 (sasil)
**Derived Form:** 사실주의
**Transliteration:** sasil juui
**Literal Breakdown:** 사실 (sasil, fact/reality) + 주의 (juui, 主義, -ism/doctrine)
**Natural English Translation:** realism (in art, literature, or philosophy)
**Usage:** Used in literary, artistic, and philosophical contexts to refer to the movement or approach that aims to depict reality as it actually is. [Cross-referenced from P1b Root 2, item 5.]
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**2.5**
**Base Form:** 사실 (sasil)
**Derived Form:** 사실주의적
**Transliteration:** sasil juuijeok
**Literal Breakdown:** 사실주의 (sasil juui, realism) + 적 (的, adjectival suffix)
**Natural English Translation:** realist (adjective), characteristic of realism
**Usage:** Used in literary and art criticism to characterize works, styles, or approaches as belonging to or consistent with the realist tradition. Appears in 사실주의적 기법 (realist technique).
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**2.6**
**Base Form:** 사실 (sasil)
**Derived Form:** 사실관계
**Transliteration:** sasil gwangye
**Literal Breakdown:** 사실 (sasil, fact) + 관계 (gwangye, 關係, relationship/circumstances)
**Natural English Translation:** the facts of the matter, factual circumstances
**Usage:** Used in legal and journalistic contexts to refer to the factual circumstances of a case, as distinct from legal interpretation. [Cross-referenced from P1b Root 2, item 3.]
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**2.7**
**Base Form:** 사실 (sasil)
**Derived Form:** 사실확인
**Transliteration:** sasil hwagin
**Literal Breakdown:** 사실 (sasil, fact) + 확인 (hwagin, 確認, confirmation/verification)
**Natural English Translation:** fact-checking, verification of facts
**Usage:** Used in journalistic, legal, and administrative contexts to describe the process of verifying whether a claim corresponds to the facts. [Cross-referenced from P1b Root 2, item 4.]
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Note on 사실:** 사실 functions both as a noun and as a sentence-level adverb meaning "in fact / actually." The adverbial use is not a morphological derivation but a pragmatic extension of the nominal form. The suffix 적 (的) attaches productively to yield 사실적 and its further adverbial form 사실적으로. The suffix 주의 (主義) yields 사실주의, from which 사실주의적 is further derived. No verbal derivation of 사실 via 하다 is identified as a common independent form.

## 3. 거짓 (geojit)

---

**3.1**
**Base Form:** 거짓 (geojit)
**Derived Form:** 거짓말
**Transliteration:** geojitmal
**Literal Breakdown:** 거짓 (geojit, falsehood) + 말 (mal, speech/word)
**Natural English Translation:** lie, falsehood
**Usage:** A conventionalized compound specifically denoting a false utterance or deliberate deception. Extremely common in everyday moral evaluation of speech acts. Already listed as a separate P1 item (item 4). [Cross-referenced from P1.]
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**3.2**
**Base Form:** 거짓 (geojit)
**Derived Form:** 거짓되다
**Transliteration:** geojittoeda
**Literal Breakdown:** 거짓 (geojit, falsehood) + 되다 (toeda, stative verbal suffix)
**Natural English Translation:** to be false, to be untrue
**Usage:** Predicative form used to evaluate a statement or representation as false. Less common than 거짓말하다 in everyday speech; appears more in written and formal registers.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**3.3**
**Base Form:** 거짓 (geojit)
**Derived Form:** 거짓되게
**Transliteration:** geojittoege
**Literal Breakdown:** 거짓되다 (geojittoeda, to be false) + 게 (ge, adverbializing suffix)
**Natural English Translation:** falsely, deceptively
**Usage:** Adverbial form of 거짓되다. Used to describe how something is represented or stated — e.g., 거짓되게 꾸미다 (to fabricate falsely). Appears in written and formal registers.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Note on 거짓:** The primary productive derivation from 거짓 proceeds through the compound 거짓말 (item 4 below), which then serves as a further base for verbal and nominal derivation. Direct derivation from 거짓 itself (without 말) is more limited. No 성 (性) nominalization of bare 거짓 is identified as a common independent form.

---

## 4. 거짓말 (geojitmal)

---

**4.1**
**Base Form:** 거짓말 (geojitmal)
**Derived Form:** 거짓말하다
**Transliteration:** geojitmal-hada
**Literal Breakdown:** 거짓말 (geojitmal, lie) + 하다 (hada, to do)
**Natural English Translation:** to lie, to tell a lie
**Usage:** The standard verbal form meaning "to lie." Extremely common in everyday speech and moral discourse. The most frequent verbal expression of the act of lying in Korean.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**4.2**
**Base Form:** 거짓말 (geojitmal)
**Derived Form:** 거짓말쟁이
**Transliteration:** geojitmaljaengi
**Literal Breakdown:** 거짓말 (geojitmal, lie) + 쟁이 (jaengi, colloquial agentive suffix denoting a habitual doer or person characterized by something)
**Natural English Translation:** liar, habitual liar
**Usage:** A colloquial agentive noun denoting a person who lies habitually. Common in everyday speech, particularly in informal and child-directed language. Carries a mildly pejorative tone. Already noted in P1 item 3.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**4.3**
**Base Form:** 거짓말 (geojitmal)
**Derived Form:** 거짓말쟁이짓
**Transliteration:** geojitmaljaengijit
**Literal Breakdown:** 거짓말쟁이 (geojitmaljaengi, liar) + 짓 (jit, colloquial suffix denoting an act or behavior, often pejorative)
**Natural English Translation:** the behavior of a liar, lying behavior
**Usage:** A colloquial compound used to characterize someone's conduct as that of a liar. Informal register; appears in everyday speech and informal writing.
**Epistemic Status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**4.4**
**Base Form:** 거짓말 (geojitmal)
**Derived Form:** 새빨간 거짓말
**Transliteration:** saepalgan geojitmal
**Literal Breakdown:** 새빨간 (saepalgan, intensified bright red — colloquial intensifier meaning "outright/blatant") + 거짓말 (geojitmal, lie)
**Natural English Translation:** outright lie, blatant lie, barefaced lie
**Usage:** A fixed collocation used to intensify the characterization of a lie as completely and obviously false. The color intensifier 새빨간 functions idiomatically here. Extremely common in everyday speech.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Note on 거짓말:** 새빨간 거짓말 is included here as a fixed collocation rather than a morphological derivation; it is documented because it represents the primary intensification strategy for this lexical item and is highly conventionalized. The verbal form 거짓말하다 is the most productive derivation.

## 5. 진짜 (jinjja)

---

**5.1**
**Base Form:** 진짜 (jinjja)
**Derived Form:** 진짜배기
**Transliteration:** jinjjabaegi
**Literal Breakdown:** 진짜 (jinjja, genuine) + 배기 (baegi, colloquial intensifying suffix denoting the genuine article)
**Natural English Translation:** the real thing, the genuine article, the real deal
**Usage:** Colloquial and emphatic form of 진짜, used to stress that something is unambiguously authentic. Common in everyday speech and advertising. [Cross-referenced from P1b Root 1, item 8.]
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Note on 진짜:** 진짜 functions as both an adjective and an adverb in Korean without morphological change (진짜 좋다, "really good"; 진짜 물건, "real thing"). No 하다 verbal form or 성 nominalization of 진짜 is identified as a common independent form. The primary intensification strategy is 진짜배기. The adverbial use of 진짜 is a pragmatic extension, not a morphological derivation.

---

## 6. 가짜 (gajja)

---

**6.1**
**Base Form:** 가짜 (gajja)
**Derived Form:** 가짜뉴스
**Transliteration:** gajja nyuseu
**Literal Breakdown:** 가짜 (gajja, fake) + 뉴스 (nyuseu, news — loanword from English)
**Natural English Translation:** fake news
**Usage:** Used in journalistic, political, and everyday contexts to refer to deliberately false or misleading news content. Entered widespread use in Korean public discourse from approximately 2016–2017 onward, parallel to international usage.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**6.2**
**Base Form:** 가짜 (gajja)
**Derived Form:** 가품
**Transliteration:** gapum
**Literal Breakdown:** 가 (假, false/fake) + 품 (品, goods/article)
**Natural English Translation:** fake goods, counterfeit item
**Usage:** Used in consumer, legal, and art contexts to designate an item as not genuine. Contrasts with 진품 (jinpum, genuine article). More formal than 가짜 alone; common in legal and commercial contexts.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Note on 가짜:** 가짜 does not productively take 하다 to form a verb (one does not say *가짜하다 to mean "to fake"). The primary derivational extension is through compounding with nouns (가짜뉴스, 가짜 서류 "fake documents," etc.). 가품 is listed here as a morphologically related form sharing the 가 (假) morpheme, though it is formed directly from 가 rather than from 가짜 as a base.

## 7. 진정 (jinjeong)

---

**7.1**
**Base Form:** 진정 (jinjeong)
**Derived Form:** 진정성
**Transliteration:** jinjeongseong
**Literal Breakdown:** 진정 (jinjeong, genuine/true) + 성 (性, nominalizing suffix -ness/-ity)
**Natural English Translation:** authenticity, genuineness, sincerity
**Usage:** Already listed as a separate P1 item (item 8). Used in public discourse, criticism, and ethical evaluation to assess whether a person, act, or expression reflects genuine inner conviction. [Cross-referenced from P1.]
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**7.2**
**Base Form:** 진정 (jinjeong)
**Derived Form:** 진정하다
**Transliteration:** jinjeonghada
**Literal Breakdown:** 진정 (jinjeong, genuine/true) + 하다 (hada, verbal/adjectival copula)
**Natural English Translation:** to be genuine, sincere, true
**Usage:** Predicative form used to evaluate whether a person's feelings, intentions, or expressions are authentic. Common in interpersonal and public discourse. [Cross-referenced from P1b Root 1, item 11.]
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**7.3**
**Base Form:** 진정 (jinjeong)
**Derived Form:** 진정한
**Transliteration:** jinjeonghan
**Literal Breakdown:** 진정하다 (jinjeonghada, to be genuine) + ㄴ/은 (attributive adjectival ending)
**Natural English Translation:** true, genuine, real (attributive adjective)
**Usage:** Extremely common attributive adjective. Appears in expressions like 진정한 사랑 (true love), 진정한 친구 (true friend), 진정한 의미 (true meaning). [Cross-referenced from P1b Root 1, item 12.]
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**7.4**
**Base Form:** 진정 (jinjeong)
**Derived Form:** 진정으로
**Transliteration:** jinjeong-euro
**Literal Breakdown:** 진정 (jinjeong, genuine/true) + 으로 (euro, manner/instrumental particle)
**Natural English Translation:** truly, genuinely, sincerely
**Usage:** Adverbial expression used to assert that something is done or felt genuinely. Appears in 진정으로 사과하다 (to apologize sincerely), 진정으로 원하다 (to truly want). Common across registers.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Note on 진정:** There is a homophonous 진정 (鎭靜, meaning "calming/suppression") that takes 하다 to mean "to calm down" or "to suppress." This is a distinct lexical item and is not included here. The 진정 under analysis is 眞正 (genuine/true). The two forms are distinguished by context.

## 8. 진정성 (jinjeongseong)

---

**Note on 진정성:** 진정성 is itself a nominalization (진정 + 성). No further common morphological derivations from 진정성 as a base are identified. The form 진정성 있다 (jinjeongseong itda, "to have authenticity") and 진정성 없다 (jinjeongseong eopda, "to lack authenticity") are extremely common collocations, but these involve the existential predicates 있다/없다 rather than morphological derivation from 진정성 itself.

(no common derivation identified beyond the base form)

## 9. 정확 (jeonghwak)

---

**9.1**
**Base Form:** 정확 (jeonghwak)
**Derived Form:** 정확하다
**Transliteration:** jeonghwak-hada
**Literal Breakdown:** 정확 (jeonghwak, accurate/precise) + 하다 (hada, verbal/adjectival copula)
**Natural English Translation:** to be accurate, precise, exact
**Usage:** The standard predicative adjectival form. Used across all registers to evaluate whether a statement, measurement, or representation is correct and precise. The base form 정확 does not typically appear without 하다 in predicative position.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**9.2**
**Base Form:** 정확 (jeonghwak)
**Derived Form:** 정확한
**Transliteration:** jeonghwakan
**Literal Breakdown:** 정확하다 (jeonghwak-hada, to be accurate) + ㄴ/은 (attributive adjectival ending)
**Natural English Translation:** accurate, precise, exact (attributive adjective)
**Usage:** Extremely common attributive form. Appears in 정확한 정보 (accurate information), 정확한 수치 (exact figure), 정확한 시간 (exact time). Already noted in P1 item 9.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**9.3**
**Base Form:** 정확 (jeonghwak)
**Derived Form:** 정확히
**Transliteration:** jeonghwaki
**Literal Breakdown:** 정확하다 (jeonghwak-hada, to be accurate) + 히 (hi, adverbializing suffix)
**Natural English Translation:** accurately, exactly, precisely
**Usage:** Extremely common adverb used to assert that something is stated or done with precision. Appears across all registers. Already noted in P1 item 9 and P1b Root 3, item 3.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**9.4**
**Base Form:** 정확 (jeonghwak)
**Derived Form:** 정확성
**Transliteration:** jeonghwakseong
**Literal Breakdown:** 정확 (jeonghwak, accurate) + 성 (性, nominalizing suffix -ness/-ity)
**Natural English Translation:** accuracy, precision
**Usage:** Used in scientific, journalistic, and legal contexts to evaluate the degree to which a measurement, statement, or representation is exact. Already noted in P1 item 9 and P1b Root 3, item 2.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**9.5**
**Base Form:** 정확 (jeonghwak)
**Derived Form:** 정확도
**Transliteration:** jeonghwakdo
**Literal Breakdown:** 정확 (jeonghwak, accurate) + 도 (度, degree/level)
**Natural English Translation:** degree of accuracy, accuracy rate
**Usage:** Used in scientific, technical, and evaluative contexts to quantify or assess how accurate something is. Common in statistics, medicine, and engineering. [Cross-referenced from P1b Root 3, item 4.]
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**9.6**
**Base Form:** 정확 (jeonghwak)
**Derived Form:** 부정확
**Transliteration:** bujeonghwak
**Literal Breakdown:** 부 (不, negating prefix) + 정확 (jeonghwak, accurate)
**Natural English Translation:** inaccuracy, imprecision
**Usage:** The negated nominal form, used to characterize a statement, measurement, or representation as lacking accuracy. Appears in 부정확한 정보 (inaccurate information), 부정확성 (bujeonghwakseong, inaccuracy as a quality).
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**9.7**
**Base Form:** 정확 (jeonghwak)
**Derived Form:** 부정확하다
**Transliteration:** bujeonghwak-hada
**Literal Breakdown:** 부정확 (bujeonghwak, inaccurate) + 하다 (hada, verbal/adjectival copula)
**Natural English Translation:** to be inaccurate, imprecise
**Usage:** Predicative form of 부정확. Used to evaluate a statement or measurement as lacking precision. Common in scientific, journalistic, and everyday contexts.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**9.8**
**Base Form:** 정확 (jeonghwak)
**Derived Form:** 부정확성
**Transliteration:** bujeonghwakseong
**Literal Breakdown:** 부정확 (bujeonghwak, inaccurate) + 성 (性, nominalizing suffix)
**Natural English Translation:** inaccuracy (as a quality)
**Usage:** Used in evaluative and critical contexts to name the property of being inaccurate. Less frequent than 정확성 but attested in academic and journalistic writing.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

## 10. 올바르다 (olbareuda)

---

**10.1**
**Base Form:** 올바르다 (olbareuda)
**Derived Form:** 올바른
**Transliteration:** olbareun
**Literal Breakdown:** 올바르다 (olbareuda, to be right/correct/proper) + ㄴ/은 (attributive adjectival ending)
**Natural English Translation:** right, correct, proper, upright (attributive adjective)
**Usage:** Very common attributive form used in moral and civic discourse. Appears in 올바른 행동 (proper conduct), 올바른 판단 (correct judgment), 올바른 가치관 (proper values). Already noted in P1 item 10.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**10.2**
**Base Form:** 올바르다 (olbareuda)
**Derived Form:** 올바르게
**Transliteration:** olbareuge
**Literal Breakdown:** 올바르다 (olbareuda, to be right/correct) + 게 (ge, adverbializing suffix)
**Natural English Translation:** rightly, correctly, properly
**Usage:** Adverbial form used to describe how something is done or judged. Appears in 올바르게 행동하다 (to act properly), 올바르게 이해하다 (to understand correctly). Common in educational and moral discourse.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**10.3**
**Base Form:** 올바르다 (olbareuda)
**Derived Form:** 올바름
**Transliteration:** olbareum
**Literal Breakdown:** 올바르다 (olbareuda, to be right/correct) + ㅁ/음 (nominalizing suffix)
**Natural English Translation:** rightness, correctness, propriety
**Usage:** Nominalized form used to refer to the quality or state of being right or proper. Appears in philosophical and educational discourse, though less frequent than the adjectival and adverbial forms.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Note on 올바르다:** 올바르다 is an adjective (형용사) in Korean and does not take 하다 as a suffix (it is not a 하다 verb). Its derivational paradigm consists of the attributive form 올바른, the adverbial form 올바르게, and the nominalization 올바름. No 성 (性) nominalization (*올바름성, *올바르성) is identified as a common form.

**11.3**
**Base Form:** 옳다 (olta)
