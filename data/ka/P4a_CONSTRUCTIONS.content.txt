# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P4a_CONSTRUCTIONS
# Language: Georgian (ka)

---

## 1. ATTRIBUTION

### Pattern
Predicative constructions using copula არის (aris) "is" or its negation არ არის (ar aris) "is not" with truth-domain adjectives or nouns. Subject typically in nominative case, predicate in nominative case for adjectives or with copula for nouns.

### Examples
- ეს ჭეშმარიტია (es č'ešmarit'ia) → "this is true"
- ეს სიმართლეა (es simart'lea) → "this is the truth"

### Notes
Standard predicative pattern. Adjectives take copular suffix -ა (-a), nouns take copula separately. Negation with particle არ (ar) before copula.

---

## 2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION

### Pattern
Negation using particle არ (ar) before copula, or negative prefix უ- (u-) on adjectives to create antonyms.

### Examples
- ეს არ არის სიმართლე (es ar aris simart'le) → "this is not the truth"
- ეს უსამართლოა (es usamart'loa) → "this is unjust"

### Notes
Two strategies: syntactic negation with არ (ar) or lexical negation with უ- prefix. Prefix უ- creates stable antonyms (სამართლიანი → უსამართლო).

---

## 3. ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION

### Pattern
Truth-domain adjectives directly modify nouns in attributive position, agreeing in case. Adjective precedes noun.

### Examples
- ჭეშმარიტი სიტყვა (č'ešmarit'i sit'q'va) → "true word"
- ნამდვილი მეგობარი (namdvili megobari) → "true friend"

### Notes
Adjectives inflect for case matching head noun. Very productive pattern across all truth-domain adjectives.

---

## 4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE

### Pattern
Verb გახდა (gaxda) "became" with instrumental case of adjective, or intransitive verbs with preverb გა- (ga-) indicating change of state.

### Examples
- სიმართლე გამოვლინდა (simart'le gamovlinda) → "the truth was revealed / came to light"
- მართალი გახდა (mart'ali gaxda) → "became right/correct"

### Notes
Two patterns: გახდა + instrumental for general "become," or specialized verbs like გამოვლინდა "was revealed" for truth-specific inchoatives.

---

## 5. CAUSATIVE

### Pattern
(no common dedicated causative construction identified)

### Examples
(no examples)

### Notes
Georgian lacks a productive morphological causative for truth-domain predicates. Causation expressed periphrastically with verbs like გააკეთა "made" + adjective, but no fixed construction specific to truth domain.

---

## 6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION

### Pattern
Suffix -ობა (-oba) or -ება (-eba) creates abstract nouns from adjectival bases. Prefix სი- (si-) also forms abstract nouns from some roots.

### Examples
- ნამდვილი → ნამდვილობა (namdvili → namdviloba) → "real → reality/genuineness"
- მართალი → სიმართლე (mart'ali → simart'le) → "right → truth"

### Notes
Highly productive pattern. Choice between -ობა/-ება lexically determined. Prefix სი- appears with some core roots (სიმართლე, სიყალბე).

---

## 7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION

### Pattern
Prefix მა- (ma-) + root + suffix -არა (-ara) or -ებელი (-ebeli) creates agent nouns, particularly for negative agents (liars, deceivers).

### Examples
- მატყუარა (maṭq'uara) → "liar, deceiver"
- მატყუარებელი (maṭq'uarebeli) → "deceiver, one who deceives"

### Notes
Pattern primarily attested with deception root ტყუ-. Positive agents less commonly lexicalized (no single word for "truth-teller," expressed periphrastically).

---

## 8. VERBAL NOUN FORMATION WITH PREVERBS

### Pattern
Preverb + verbal root + suffix -ება (-eba) creates action nouns. Preverbs (მო-, გა-, შე-, etc.) add aspectual/spatial meaning.

### Examples
- მოტყუება (moṭq'ueba) → "deception, deceiving" (მო- + ტყუ- + -ება)
- გამართლება (gamart'leba) → "justification" (გა- + მართ- + -ება)

### Notes
Highly productive pattern. Different preverbs create aspectual distinctions (მოტყუება vs. გატყუება vs. ატყუება all "deceiving" with different nuances).

---

## 9. ADVERBIALIZATION

### Pattern
Suffix -ად (-ad) creates manner adverbs from adjectives.

### Examples
- ნამდვილად (namdvilad) → "really, truly, actually"
- ზუსტად (zustad) → "precisely, exactly"

### Notes
Extremely productive pattern applying to all truth-domain adjectives. Adverbs function both as manner modifiers and as discourse particles.

---

## 10. LIGHT VERB CONSTRUCTIONS: TRUTH-TELLING

### Pattern
Truth/lie noun in accusative + verb of saying (თქვა t'qva "say," ამბობს ambobs "tells").

### Examples
- სიმართლე თქვა (simart'le t'qva) → "to tell the truth"
- ტყუილი თქვა (ṭq'uili t'qva) → "to tell a lie"

### Notes
Standard construction for truth-telling/lying. Verb თქვა for perfective, ამბობს for habitual aspect. Highly frequent in everyday speech.

---

## 11. LIGHT VERB CONSTRUCTIONS: TRUTH-SEEKING

### Pattern
Truth noun in accusative + verbs of searching/discovering (გაარკვია gaark'via "find out," იძიოს iʒios "search").

### Examples
- სიმართლე გაარკვია (simart'le gaark'via) → "to find out the truth"
- სიმართლე იძიოს (simart'le iʒios) → "to search for the truth"

### Notes
Multiple verbs available for different aspects of truth-seeking. Preverbs on verbs add aspectual meaning (გა- completive, გამო- "out from").

---

## 12. LIGHT VERB CONSTRUCTIONS: VERIFICATION

### Pattern
Truth noun in accusative + verbs of confirming/verifying (დაადასტურა daadast'ura "confirmed," დაამოწმა daamoc'ma "verified").

### Examples
- სიმართლე დაადასტურა (simart'le daadast'ura) → "confirmed the truth"
- დაამოწმა (daamoc'ma) → "verified, attested"

### Notes
Formal register constructions common in legal and evidentiary contexts. Verbs typically have preverb და- (da-).

---

## 13. CORRESPONDENCE CONSTRUCTIONS

### Pattern
Truth/reality noun in dative case + verb შეესაბამება (sheesabameba) "corresponds to."

### Examples
- სიმართლეს შეესაბამება (simart'les sheesabameba) → "corresponds to the truth"
- რეალობას შეესაბამება (realobas sheesabameba) → "corresponds to reality"

### Notes
Formal construction for truth-as-correspondence. Dative case marks the standard of comparison. Common in academic and formal discourse.

---

## 14. EMPHATIC AFFIRMATION CONSTRUCTIONS

### Pattern
Truth-domain adverb + conjunction რომ (rom) "that" creates emphatic construction.

### Examples
- მართლაც რომ (mart'lac rom) → "really that" (emphatic)
- ნამდვილად რომ (namdvilad rom) → "really that" (emphatic)

### Notes
Highly frequent conversational pattern. Functions as discourse intensifier. Can also form questions by intonation alone (მართლაც? "really?").

---

## 15. CONCEALMENT CONSTRUCTIONS

### Pattern
Truth noun in accusative + verbs of hiding/covering (დამალა damala "hid," დაფარა dap'ara "covered").

### Examples
- სიმართლე დამალა (simart'le damala) → "hid the truth"
- სიმართლე დაფარა (simart'le dap'ara) → "covered the truth"

### Notes
Metaphorical extension of physical hiding/covering to truth concealment. Both verbs have preverb და- (da-) marking completion.

---

## 16. REVELATION CONSTRUCTIONS

### Pattern
Truth noun as subject + intransitive verb გამოვლინდა (gamovlinda) "was revealed," or truth noun as object + transitive გამოაჩინა (gamoač'ina) "revealed."

### Examples
- სიმართლე გამოვლინდა (simart'le gamovlinda) → "the truth was revealed"
- სიმართლე გამოაჩინა (simart'le gamoač'ina) → "revealed the truth"

### Notes
Preverb გამო- (gamo-) "out from" metaphorically represents bringing truth from concealment to light. Transitive/intransitive pair.

---

## 17. COMPARATIVE PROXIMITY CONSTRUCTIONS

### Pattern
Truth noun in postpositional phrase with -თან (-tan) "with/near" + spatial adverb ახლოს (axlos) "close" or შორს (shors) "far."

### Examples
- სიმართლესთან ახლოს (simart'lestan axlos) → "close to the truth"
- სიმართლესთან შორს (simart'lestan shors) → "far from the truth"

### Notes
Spatial metaphor for epistemic distance. Postposition -თან takes genitive form of noun. Common in hedged or approximate claims.

---

## 18. DISJUNCTIVE QUESTIONING

### Pattern
Predicative adjective + interrogative particle თუ (tu) "or/whether" + negation არა (ara) "not."

### Examples
- სწორია თუ არა (sc'oria tu ara) → "is it correct or not"
- მართალია თუ არა (mart'alia tu ara) → "is it true or not"

### Notes
Standard yes/no question formation for truth-domain predicates. Particle თუ marks alternative, არა provides negative option.

---

## 19. DOUBT MARKING WITH ვითომ

### Pattern
Particle ვითომ (vitom) "supposedly, allegedly" + predicative construction marks epistemic distance or doubt.

### Examples
- ვითომ მართალია (vitom mart'alia) → "supposedly it's true"
- ვითომ ნამდვილია (vitom namdvilia) → "supposedly it's real"

### Notes
Particle ვითომ marks reported or alleged status without speaker commitment. Common in skeptical or distancing discourse.

---

## 20. REFLEXIVE CONSTRUCTIONS

### Pattern
Prefix თვით- (tviṭ-) "self" + verbal noun creates reflexive meaning.

### Examples
- თვითმოტყუება (tviṭmoṭq'ueba) → "self-deception"
- თვითმოტყუებაში იმყოფება (tviṭmoṭq'uebashi imq'opeba) → "is in self-deception"

### Notes
Prefix თვით- productively creates reflexive forms. Pattern attested with deception but potentially applicable to other truth-domain verbs.

---

## 21. NEGATION WITH უ- PREFIX

### Pattern
Negative prefix უ- (u-) + adjectival base creates antonym, often with suffix -ო (-o).

### Examples
- სამართლიანი → უსამართლო (samart'liani → usamart'lo) → "just → unjust"
- მართებული → უმართებულო (mart'ebuli → umart'ebulo) → "correct → incorrect"

### Notes
Productive lexical negation pattern. Suffix -ო often appears on negated forms. Creates stable antonyms distinct from syntactic negation.

---

## 22. PARTICIPIAL MODIFICATION

### Pattern
Participial suffix -ული (-uli) or -ებული (-ebuli) creates adjectives from verbal stems, often with passive/resultative meaning.

### Examples
- მართებული (mart'ebuli) → "correct, proper, justified"
- მოტყუებული (moṭq'uebuli) → "deceived, deluded"

### Notes
Highly productive pattern. Suffix -ებული typically follows preverb + root combinations. Creates adjectives describing states resulting from actions.

---

## 23. COMPOUND FORMATION

### Pattern
Two roots combine, often with linking element, to create specialized compounds.

### Examples
- მართლმადიდებელი (mart'lmadidebeli) → "Orthodox (Christian)" (მართ- + მა- + დიდებელ-)
- მართლწერა (mart'lc'era) → "orthography" (მართ- + წერ-)

### Notes
Productive with მართ- root in particular. Compounds often lexicalized with specialized meanings beyond compositional semantics.

---

## 24. GENITIVE + NOMINALIZATION CONSTRUCTIONS

### Pattern
Abstract noun in genitive case + another nominalization creates complex nominal expressing process or quality.

### Examples
- ჭეშმარიტების ძიება (č'ešmarit'ebis ʒieba) → "search for truth"
- სამართლიანობის აღდგენა (samart'lianobis aɣdgena) → "restoration of justice"

### Notes
Formal register construction. Genitive marks possessor/theme relationship. Common in academic and legal discourse.

---

## 25. COLLOCATION WITH ABSTRACT NOUNS

### Pattern
Truth-domain adjective + abstract noun creates fixed or semi-fixed collocations expressing essential properties.

### Examples
- ნამდვილი ბუნება (namdvili buneba) → "true nature"
- ნამდვილი ღირებულება (namdvili ɣirebuleba) → "true value"

### Notes
Highly productive pattern. Adjectives like ნამდვილი and ჭეშმარიტი freely combine with abstract nouns to distinguish essential from apparent properties.

---

## 26. MANNER ADVERB + VERB CONSTRUCTIONS

### Pattern
Truth-domain adverb in -ად + verb creates manner construction.

### Examples
- სწორად გააკეთა (sc'orad gaak'et'a) → "did it correctly"
- ზუსტად იცის (zustad icis) → "knows precisely"

### Notes
Standard manner modification. Adverbs in -ად freely combine with verbs. Very frequent in everyday speech for evaluating action correctness.

---

## 27. POSTPOSITIONAL CONSTRUCTIONS

### Pattern
Truth-domain noun + postposition creates locative or relational expression.

### Examples
- რეალობაში (realobashi) → "in reality" (-ში "in")
- სამართლის წინაშე (samart'lis c'inashe) → "before the law" (წინაშე "before")

### Notes
Postpositions take genitive case of preceding noun. Different postpositions create different spatial/relational meanings applied metaphorically to truth domain.

---

## 28. CONCESSIVE CONSTRUCTIONS

### Pattern
Predicative form + conjunction რომ (rom) "that" creates concessive meaning "it is true that..."

### Examples
- მართალია რომ (mart'alia rom) → "it is true that, admittedly"
- სწორია რომ (sc'oria rom) → "it is correct that"

### Notes
Common in argumentative discourse. Acknowledges truth while often preparing to qualify or contrast. Functions as discourse marker.

---

## 29. BIBLICAL/SOLEMN FORMULAS

### Pattern
Adverb ჭეშმარიტად (č'ešmarit'ad) "truly" + verb of saying with 2nd person indirect object creates solemn affirmation.

### Examples
- ჭეშმარიტად გეუბნები (č'ešmarit'ad geubnеbi) → "truly I tell you"
- ჭეშმარიტად გეტყვი (č'ešmarit'ad get'q'vi) → "verily I say unto you"

### Notes
Fixed formula from biblical/religious register. Marks solemn or authoritative speech. Indirect object marking (გე-) indicates addressee.

---

## 30. EVALUATIVE COLLOCATIONS

### Pattern
Truth-domain adjective + evaluative noun creates character or quality assessment.

### Examples
- სანდო ადამიანი (sando adamiani) → "reliable person"
- გულწრფელი გრძნობები (gulc'rpeli grʒnobebi) → "sincere feelings"

### Notes
Semi-fixed collocations common in character evaluation and emotional assessment. Adjectives select for compatible noun classes (persons, feelings, sources).
