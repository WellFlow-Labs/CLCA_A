Below are attested derivationally related forms associated with the P1 lexical items. I include only forms that are common or clearly supported by usage, and I leave gaps where no common derivation is identifiable.

### 1. Base Form: **진실**
- **Derived Form:** 진실하다
- **Transliteration:** jinsilhada
- **Literal Breakdown:** 眞實 “truth” + 하다
- **Natural English translation:** be truthful; be sincere; be true
- **Usage description:** Used for persons, speech, attitudes, or emotional expression judged to be truthful or sincere. It is more literary and evaluative than plain everyday spoken wording.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### 2. Base Form: **진실**
- **Derived Form:** 진실성
- **Transliteration:** jinsilseong
- **Literal Breakdown:** 眞實 “truthfulness” + 性 “quality”
- **Natural English translation:** sincerity; truthfulness; authenticity
- **Usage description:** Common in criticism, politics, ethics, and public discourse. It evaluates whether a person, statement, or expression feels honest and genuine.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### 3. Base Form: **진실**
- **Derived Form:** 진실되다
- **Transliteration:** jinsildoeda
- **Literal Breakdown:** 眞實 “truth” + 되다
- **Natural English translation:** be truthful; be genuine
- **Usage description:** Attested but less common than 진실하다. It appears mainly in literary, moral, or descriptive prose for character, speech, or attitude.
- **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

### 4. Base Form: **진실**
- **Derived Form:** 진실로
- **Transliteration:** jinsillo
- **Literal Breakdown:** 眞實 “truth” + 로
- **Natural English translation:** truly; indeed
- **Usage description:** A literary or formal adverbial form found in elevated prose, sermons, and essays. It is not a neutral everyday spoken equivalent of “really.”
- **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]
