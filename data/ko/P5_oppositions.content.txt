## SUMMARY NOTES

### Asymmetries Identified

Multiple asymmetric oppositions were found where one pole is lexicalized or productive while the expected opposite is not:

1. **Agentive asymmetry**: 거짓말쟁이 "liar" exists, but no *진실쟁이 "truth-teller"
2. **Privative asymmetry**: 거짓없이 "without falsehood" common, but 진실없이 "without truth" rare
3. **Saturation asymmetry**: 거짓투성이 "full of lies" exists, but no *진실투성이
4. **Verbal asymmetry**: 속이다 "deceive" exists as single verb, but "undeceive" requires construction
5. **Constructional asymmetries**: Several terms (실제, 진상, 실력, 실적) mark actual reality in implicit contrast to unstated alternatives

These asymmetries reflect cultural and cognitive patterns where:
- Deviant behavior (lying) is marked more explicitly than expected behavior (truth-telling)
- Absence of negative qualities (거짓없이) is marked rather than presence of positive qualities
- Actual reality is explicitly marked in contrast to various implicit alternatives (appearance, theory, claims)

### Opposition Types Distribution

- **Lexical oppositions**: 15 core pairs (진실/거짓, 참/거짓, 진짜/가짜, etc.)
- **Morphological oppositions**: 10 pairs (using prefixes 부-/불- or suffixes)
- **Constructional oppositions**: 15 patterns (verbal collocations, adverbial expressions, etc.)
- **Asymmetric patterns**: 8 identified (lacking expected counterpart)

### Register Variation

Oppositions show register stratification:
- **Formal/legal**: 진본/위조, 사실/허위, 진위 constructions
- **Colloquial**: 진짜/가짜, 진짜배기/가짜배기
- **Academic**: 이론/실제, 명목상/실질적
- **Universal**: 맞다/틀리다, 진실/거짓

All oppositions listed are supported by attested usage patterns in the provided context data.
