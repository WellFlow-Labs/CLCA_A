## OPPOSITION 1

**Term A:** სიმართლე (simart'le)  
**Term B:** ტყუილი (t'q'uili)  
**Transliteration:** simart'le vs. t'q'uili  
**English translation:** truth vs. lie  
**Description of contrast:** Core lexical opposition between truth as correspondence to reality and lie as deliberate falsehood. This is the primary native Georgian opposition in the truth domain, appearing in fixed phrases like სიმართლე და ტყუილი (simart'le da t'q'uili) "truth and lie" and contrasting honest speech with deception.  
**Contrast type:** lexical  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## SUMMARY

This analysis identifies **30 attested oppositions** in Georgian truth-domain vocabulary, comprising:

- **11 core lexical oppositions** (e.g., სიმართლე vs. ტყუილი, ნამდვილი vs. ყალბი)
- **4 morphological oppositions** through negation prefixes უ- and არა-
- **15 constructional oppositions** through contrasting adjectives with shared nouns or parallel structures

The oppositions show both symmetric pairs (e.g., ობიექტური vs. სუბიექტური) and asymmetric contrasts where one pole is more lexicalized than the other. The data reveals that Georgian expresses truth-domain oppositions through multiple strategies: dedicated lexical items, productive morphological negation, and compositional constructions with contrasting modifiers.
