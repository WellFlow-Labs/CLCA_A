# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P5 Oppositions
# Language: Korean (ko)

---

**Transliteration standard:** Revised Romanization of Korean (국립국어원 표준 로마자 표기법), applied consistently throughout.

---

## Preliminary Note on Method

Oppositions below are extracted exclusively from patterns attested in P1–P4. Each opposition is supported by explicit co-occurrence, contrastive framing, morphological pairing, or constructional alternation documented in the prior steps. No opposition has been inferred from general semantic knowledge not present in the data. Where the data supports multiple contrasts for a single term, each is listed separately. Where the data does not support a clear opposition, this is stated explicitly.

## Opposition 1

**Term A:** 진실 (jinsil)
**Term B:** 거짓 (geojit)
**Transliteration:** jinsil / geojit
**English translation:** truth / falsehood
**Description of contrast:** These two terms are explicitly paired as polar opposites in the construction 진실과 거짓 사이 ("between truth and falsehood") and in 거짓과 진실을 구별하다 ("to distinguish falsehood from truth"), both attested in P2b. P3 identifies 진실 and 거짓 as the positive and negative poles of the truth-evaluation axis, and P1 notes that 진실 "appears in moral, legal, and everyday contexts, often in opposition to 거짓."
**Contrast type:** Lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 2

**Term A:** 진실 (jinsil)
**Term B:** 거짓말 (geojitmal)
**Transliteration:** jinsil / geojitmal
**English translation:** truth / lie
**Description of contrast:** P3 places 진실 as the positive pole contrasted with 거짓말 in everyday moral discourse about speech acts. The construction 진실을 말하다 ("to tell the truth") is the direct counterpart to 거짓말하다 ("to lie"), both documented in P2b and P2a respectively.
**Contrast type:** Lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 3

**Term A:** 사실 (sasil)
**Term B:** 허위 (heowi) / 허위사실 (heowi sasil)
**Transliteration:** sasil / heowi, heowi sasil
**English translation:** fact / falsehood; false fact
**Description of contrast:** P3 identifies 사실 and 허위 as the primary poles of the factual-status assertion group, noting that 허위사실 is explicitly formed by combining 허위 with 사실 to denote a claim presented as factual but false. The construction 사실이 아니다 ("it is not a fact") and 허위로 밝혀지다 ("to be revealed as false") are documented as contrasting constructions in P2b.
**Contrast type:** Lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 4

**Term A:** 진짜 (jinjja)
**Term B:** 가짜 (gajja)
**Transliteration:** jinjja / gajja
**English translation:** real / fake
**Description of contrast:** P1 explicitly states that 진짜 "contrasts directly with 가짜." The construction 진짜인지 가짜인지 모르다 ("not to know whether it is real or fake") and 가짜를 진짜로 속이다 ("to pass off a fake as genuine") are both attested in P2b. P3 identifies these as the most colloquial pair in the authenticity-of-objects group.
**Contrast type:** Lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 5

## Opposition 8

**Term A:** 진위 (jinwi) — the 진 (眞) component
**Term B:** 진위 (jinwi) — the 위 (僞) component

**Contrast type:** Lexical (internal to compound)

## Opposition 11

**Term A:** 옳다 (olta)
**Term B:** 틀리다 (teullida)
**Transliteration:** olta / teullida
**English translation:** to be right / to be wrong (in everyday factual contexts)

P2b (item 13.1) documents 틀린 게 없다 ("there is nothing wrong") as the everyday negation of correctness.
