### Base: αλήθεια (alítheia) "truth"

#### Derived Form 1: αληθινός
**Transliteration:** alithinós
**Literal breakdown:** αληθ- (true) + -ινός (adjectival suffix indicating material/quality)
**Translation:** true, genuine, real, authentic
**Usage:** Adjectival form describing something as genuine or authentic. Used attributively and predicatively in all registers.
**[CORE]**

#### Derived Form 2: αληθεύω
**Transliteration:** alithévo
**Literal breakdown:** αληθ- (true) + -εύω (denominative verb suffix)
**Translation:** to tell the truth, to be truthful
**Usage:** Denominative verb formed from the noun. Used in everyday and formal contexts for truthful speaking.
**[CORE]**

#### Derived Form 3: αληθώς
**Transliteration:** alithós
**Literal breakdown:** αληθ- (true) + -ώς (adverbial suffix)
**Translation:** truly, really, indeed
**Usage:** Adverbial form used to emphasize truth or reality of statements. Common in confirmations and emphatic assertions.
**[CORE]**

#### Derived Form 4: αληθινά
**Transliteration:** alithiná
**Literal breakdown:** αληθιν- (true) + -ά (neuter plural/adverbial ending)
**Translation:** truly, really, genuinely
**Usage:** Adverbial form derived from the adjective αληθινός. Interchangeable with αληθώς in many contexts.
**[CORE]**

#### Derived Form 5: αληθινότητα
**Transliteration:** alithinótita
**Literal breakdown:** αληθιν- (true) + -ότητα (abstract noun suffix)
**Translation:** genuineness, authenticity, truthfulness
**Usage:** Abstract noun derived from αληθινός referring to the quality of being genuine. Used in formal discourse.
**[CORE]**

#### Derived Form 6: αληθής
**Transliteration:** alithís
**Literal breakdown:** αληθ- (true) + -ής (adjectival suffix)
**Translation:** true, truthful, veracious
**Usage:** Alternative adjectival form, more formal than αληθινός. Common in philosophical and formal writing.
**[CORE]**

#### Derived Form 7: αληθεύομαι
**Transliteration:** alithévome
**Literal breakdown:** αληθ- (true) + -εύομαι (passive/middle denominative verb)
**Translation:** to be verified, to prove true
**Usage:** Passive/middle voice form indicating verification or confirmation of truth. Used when predictions or claims are confirmed.
**[CORE]**

#### Derived Form 8: αλήθω
**Transliteration:** alítho
**Literal breakdown:** αληθ- (true) + -ω (verb ending)
**Translation:** to tell the truth, to speak truthfully
**Usage:** Alternative verb form, less common than αληθεύω but still in regular use.
**[CORE]**

## COMPOUND FORMATIONS

### Compounds with ψευδ- (pseud-) prefix:

The root ψευδ- shows exceptional productivity as a prefix forming compounds. These are documented in P1b but merit note here as a distinct derivational pattern:

**Pattern:** ψευδ- + noun/adjective → compound indicating falsity, resemblance, or spuriousness

**Examples from P1b:**
- ψευδομαρτυρία (false testimony)
- ψευδομάρτυρας (false witness)
- ψευδαίσθηση (illusion)
- ψευδοεπιστήμη (pseudoscience)
- ψευδοπροφήτης (false prophet)
- ψευδορκία (perjury)
- ψευδόρκος (perjurer)

This pattern remains productive in modern Greek for creating new terms, particularly in scientific and critical discourse.

---

### Compounds with ορθ- (orth-) prefix:

The root ορθ- similarly shows high productivity as a prefix, documented in P1b:

**Pattern:** ορθ- + noun → compound indicating correctness, straightness, or proper alignment

**Examples from P1b:**
- ορθόδοξος (orthodox)
- ορθοδοξία (orthodoxy)
- ορθογραφία (orthography)
- ορθοπεδικός (orthopedic)
- ορθολογισμός (rationalism)
- ορθογώνιος (right-angled)

This pattern remains productive, particularly in technical and religious terminology.

---

### Compounds with αληθ- (alith-) root:

The root αληθ- shows more limited compounding productivity compared to ψευδ- and ορθ-:

**Examples from P1b:**
- αληθοφανής (apparently true)
- αληθολογώ (to speak truth)
- αληθοφιλής (truth-loving)

This pattern is less productive in modern Greek, with most compounds having formal or literary register.

## NOTES ON DERIVATIONAL PATTERNS

### Adverbial Formation:

**Pattern 1:** Adjective + -α (neuter plural) → adverb
- Common, productive pattern
- Examples: σωστά, ορθά, δίκαια, άδικα, πραγματικά

**Pattern 2:** Adjective + -ώς → adverb
- More formal variant
- Examples: ορθώς, δικαίως, βεβαίως, ψευδώς

**Pattern 3:** Adjective stem + -ά (from -ινός adjectives) → adverb
- Examples: αληθινά, αυθεντικά, ειλικρινά

### Abstract Noun Formation:

**Pattern 1:** Adjective stem + -ότητα → abstract noun
- Highly productive pattern
- Examples: ορθότητα, σωστότητα, αληθινότητα, πραγματικότητα, αυθεντικότητα, εγκυρότητα, ακυρότητα, βεβαιότητα, πλαστότητα

**Pattern 2:** Adjective stem + -ία → abstract noun
- Common pattern, particularly for negative qualities
- Examples: αδικία, αμφιβολία, αξιοπιστία

**Pattern 3:** Adjective stem + -εια/-ία → abstract noun
- Examples: ακρίβεια, ειλικρίνεια, νοθεία

**Pattern 4:** Adjective stem + -οσύνη → abstract noun
- Less common, often with ethical/moral qualities
- Example: δικαιοσύνη

### Diminutive Formation:

**Pattern:** Noun + -άκι → diminutive
- Productive pattern for nouns
- Examples: ψεματάκι, λαθάκι
- Function: minimizes severity or expresses affection

### Causative Verb Formation:

**Pattern:** Adjective/noun stem + -ώνω → causative verb
- Productive pattern
- Examples: ακυρώνω (to invalidate), βεβαιώνω (to certify)

### Action Noun Formation:

**Pattern:** Verb stem + -ωση → action noun
- Productive pattern from -ώνω verbs
- Examples: ακύρωση (cancellation), βεβαίωση (certification)
