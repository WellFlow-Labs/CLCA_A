Below are **attested derivational morphology forms** associated with the P1 lexical items. I include only forms that are genuinely established in English or strongly supported by usage. Where no common derivation is identifiable from the P1 base, I state that explicitly.

### 1. Base Form: **truth**
1. **Derived Form:** truthful  
   - **Transliteration:** N/A  
   - **Literal Breakdown:** truth + -ful  
   - **Natural English translation:** truthful; honest  
   - **Usage description:** Used for persons, statements, and testimony that conform to truth and do not deceive. It is common in moral, interpersonal, and legal contexts.  
   - **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

2. **Derived Form:** truthfully  
   - **Transliteration:** N/A  
   - **Literal Breakdown:** truthful + -ly  
   - **Natural English translation:** truthfully; honestly  
   - **Usage description:** Common as an adverb for speaking or reporting honestly. It also appears as a discourse marker, as in “Truthfully, I was surprised.”  
   - **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

3. **Derived Form:** truthfulness  
   - **Transliteration:** N/A  
   - **Literal Breakdown:** truthful + -ness  
   - **Natural English translation:** truthfulness; honesty  
   - **Usage description:** Refers to the quality of being truthful in speech or character. It is well established, though somewhat less common than *honesty* in everyday use.  
   - **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

4. **Derived Form:** untruth  
   - **Transliteration:** N/A  
   - **Literal Breakdown:** un- + truth  
   - **Natural English translation:** untruth; falsehood  
   - **Usage description:** Used for a false statement, often with a somewhat formal or euphemistic tone. It is established but less common than *lie* or *falsehood*.  
   - **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

5. **Derived Form:** untruthful
