## Preliminary Note on Method

Oppositions below are extracted from patterns explicitly documented in P1, P1b, P2a, P2b, P3, P4a, and P4b. Each opposition is supported by direct textual evidence from the CONTEXT. Where the CONTEXT explicitly names a contrast, that is noted. Where the opposition is implicit but clearly supported by co-occurrence, antonym labelling, or structural pairing in the data, that is also noted. No opposition is inferred beyond what the data supports.

## Opposition 1

1. **Term A:** *tosi*
2. **Term B:** *epätosi*
3. **Transliteration:** tosi / epätosi
4. **English translation:** true / untrue, false
5. **Description of contrast:** *Epätosi* is formed by applying the negating prefix *epä-* directly to *tosi*, producing its logical negation. The contrast is used in propositional truth attribution contexts (*se on tosi* — "that is true" vs. *väite on epätosi* — "the claim is false"). The CONTEXT explicitly identifies *epätosi* as "the direct negation of *tosi*" and notes it is more formal and explicitly propositional than *väärä*; *epätosi* is more characteristic of written and academic registers than *tosi*, which is common across all registers including colloquial speech.
6. **Contrast type:** morphological
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## Opposition 2

1. **Term A:** *tosi*
2. **Term B:** *väärä*
3. **Transliteration:** tosi / väärä
4. **English translation:** true / wrong, false
5. **Description of contrast:** The CONTEXT identifies *väärä* as "the primary antonym of both *oikea* and *tosi* in evaluative contexts." While *epätosi* is the formal propositional negation, *väärä* is the general-purpose negative evaluative term used across propositional, normative, and identity-evaluation contexts. The CONTEXT does not document a single direct lexical antonym of *tosi* with a morphologically parallel negative form within the same root; the negative pole is covered by *väärä* in propositional contexts, making this opposition asymmetric.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical (asymmetric)
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 3

1. **Term A:** *tosi*
2. **Term B:** *taru*
3. **Transliteration:** tosi / taru
4. **English translation:** true, real / legend, myth, tale
5. **Description of contrast:** The fixed contrastive phrase *tosi vai tarua* ("fact or fiction / true or legend") pairs *tosi* with *taru* (legend, myth, tale) as a framing device in journalism and popular media. This is a lexical opposition between factual reality and legendary or fictional narrative, not a morphological negation. The CONTEXT documents *tosi vai tarua* as "a fixed binary construction contrasting factual truth with legend or myth (*taru* — legend, myth)."
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 5

3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** to be true, to hold / to not be true, to not hold

## Opposition 6

1. **Term A:** *totuus*
2. **Term B:** *epätotuus*
3. **Transliteration:** totuus / epätotuus
4. **English translation:** truth / untruth, falsehood
5. **Description of contrast:** *Totuus* is the standard nominal for truth as an abstract property; *epätotuus* is its morphological negation, referring to a statement or proposition that is not true. The CONTEXT notes that *epätotuus* does not necessarily imply deliberate deception, distinguishing it from *valhe* (lie); it is somewhat less formal than *valhe* and is used in everyday and journalistic contexts (*puhua epätotuuksia* — "to say untruths/falsehoods").
6. **Contrast type:** morphological
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 7

1. **Term A:** *totuus*
2. **Term B:** *valhe*
3. **Transliteration:** totuus / valhe
4. **English translation:** truth / lie
5. **Description of contrast:** The context notes *epätotuus* as "somewhat less formal than *valhe* (lie)" when describing falsehoods, implying *valhe* as the lexical counterpart to *totuus* in the domain of deliberate untruth. *Valhe* is referenced in the context as a contrasting term. The opposition is between truth as a property of statements and lying as a deliberate act.

## Opposition 8

1. **Term A:** *totuudenmukainen* / *totuudenmukaisuus*
2. **Term B:** *totuudenvastainen* / *totuudenvastaisuus*
3. **Transliteration:** totuudenmukainen / totuudenvastainen; totuudenmukaisuus / totuudenvastaisuus
4. **English translation:** truthful, in accordance with truth / contrary to truth, untruthful; truthfulness / untruthfulness, falsity
5. **Description of contrast:** These are compound-derived antonyms built on the genitive of *totuus* with *mukainen* (in accordance with) and *vastainen* (contrary to) respectively. The CONTEXT documents both pairs and notes their use in legal and formal discourse to evaluate whether statements correspond to or contradict the truth. The CONTEXT also notes this as a productive antonym pattern in which *mukainen* (conforming to) and *vastainen* (contrary to) substitute on the same genitive base to produce opposing compound adjectives, described as productive across the domain in P4a and P4b.
6. **Contrast type:** morphological
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 10

1. **Term A:** *oikeaoppinen*
2. **Term B:** *vääräoppinen*
3. **Transliteration:** oikeaoppinen / vääräoppinen
4. **English translation:** orthodox, doctrinally correct / heretical, heterodox, doctrinally incorrect
5. **Description of contrast:** The context documents these as direct contrasts in religious and ideological contexts, formed by substituting *oikea* (right/correct) with *väärä* (wrong/false) on the same second element *-oppinen* (doctrine-holding). The context notes *oikeaoppinen* can be used ironically. The CONTEXT also notes that *oikeaoppinen* involves "doctrinal conformity, which may involve ideological or religious rather than factual correctness, and can carry ironic connotations," distinguishing it from the factual or normative correctness of *oikea*. Both are documented in P1b and P2a and are used in religious and historical contexts.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical (parallel compound formation)
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 12

1. **Term A:** *oikea*
2. **Term B:** *väärä*
3. **Transliteration:** oikea / väärä
4. **English translation:** right, correct, genuine / wrong, false, incorrect, counterfeit
5. **Description of contrast:** The CONTEXT explicitly pairs these as antonyms in normative and evaluative contexts (*oikea vastaus* / *väärä vastaus*; *olla oikeassa* / *olla väärässä*). *Oikea* evaluates conformity to a standard or norm; *väärä* evaluates deviation from it. The context documents these as the primary adjectival opposition in the domain, covering both epistemic correctness (*oikea vastaus* vs. *väärä vastaus*) and inauthenticity (*väärä seteli* — counterfeit banknote). The CONTEXT explicitly documents *oikein vai väärin* ("right or wrong") as a contrastive pair. The opposition is noted as covering multiple semantic dimensions simultaneously.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 13

1. **Term A:** *olla oikeassa*
2. **Term B:** *olla väärässä*
3. **Transliteration:** olla oikeassa / olla väärässä
4. **English translation:** to be right / to be wrong
5. **Description of contrast:** These are the standard periphrastic constructions for asserting that a person's belief or claim is correct or incorrect respectively. The CONTEXT explicitly identifies *olla väärässä* as "the direct antonym of *olla oikeassa*" and notes both are very high frequency across all registers. They are lexicalized inessive constructions using *olla* (to be) with *oikeassa* (inessive of *oikea*) and *väärässä* (inessive of *väärä*) respectively; the inessive case is lexicalized in both forms and does not carry its usual locative meaning. Documented in P2b and P4a Construction 10.
6. **Contrast type:** constructional
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 14

1. **Term A:** *oikein*
2. **Term B:** *väärin*
3. **Transliteration:** oikein / väärin
4. **English translation:** correctly, rightly / wrongly, incorrectly
5. **Description of contrast:** These are the adverbial forms of *oikea* and *väärä* respectively, documented as a contrastive pair (*oikein vai väärin* — "right or wrong") in P2b. Both combine productively with verbs: *tehdä oikein* ("to do correctly/rightly") contrasts with *tehdä väärin* ("to do wrong/incorrectly"). *Väärin* is additionally highly productive in forming misaction compounds (*ymmärtää väärin*, *tulkita väärin*, *käyttää väärin*), a productivity not paralleled by *oikein*. The CONTEXT notes in P4a that this construction type "covers semantic ground handled by English *mis-* prefixation."
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 23

## Opposition 28

1. **Term A:** *aito*
2. **Term B:** *epäaito*
3. **Transliteration:** aito / epäaito
4. **English translation:** genuine, authentic / inauthentic, not genuine
5. **Description of contrast:** *Aito* asserts that something is genuine rather than an imitation or counterfeit; *epäaito* is its direct morphological negation. The CONTEXT documents both as P1 items and notes that *epäaito* generates its own nominal *epäaitous* (inauthenticity). The context further notes that *epäaito* is used more in philosophical and personal identity contexts (existential inauthenticity) than in object-authentication contexts, where *väärennös* or *väärä* are more common; this asymmetry in domain of use distinguishes this opposition from a simple logical negation pair.
6. **Contrast type:** morphological
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 30

1. **Term A:** *aito*
2. **Term B:** *väärennös*
3. **Transliteration:** aito / väärennös
4. **English translation:** genuine / forgery, counterfeit
5. **Description of contrast:** The CONTEXT explicitly states that *väärennös* "contrasts with *aito* (genuine) and *alkuperäinen* (original)." *Aito* asserts non-counterfeit status; *väärennös* names the product of deliberate falsification that lacks that status. The fixed contrastive phrase *aito vai väärennös* ("genuine or a forgery") is used in art authentication, legal, and journalistic contexts. Unlike *jäljitelmä*, *väärennös* implies deliberate fraudulent production. The opposition is between an adjective and a noun, making it structurally asymmetric.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]
