# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2b Compositional Constructions
## Language: Georgian (ka)
## Transliteration: ISO 9984 (1996), consistent with P1/P1b/P2a

This document records **attested multi-word constructions** involving P1 items. Derivational forms (P2a) and semantically-shifted compounds/single-word forms (P1b) are excluded. Light-verb constructions, fixed collocations, idioms, and syntactic frames are included only where natural and attested. Absences are recorded explicitly.

## Summary observations (supported by the data above)

- **The most productive constructional frame is the attributive Adj + N collocation** (e.g., sando c̣q̇aro, ʔq̇albi puli, namdvili megobari, p̣aṭiosani adamiani), available across most evaluative adjectives in the field, at varying register levels.
- **A genitive + verbal-noun pattern** clusters around the truth/lie speech-act items: *simartlis tkma* "telling the truth," *ṭq̇uilis tkma* "telling a lie," *č̣ešmaritebis dzieba* "search for truth," *namdvilobis dadgena* "establishing authenticity." This pattern is concentrated on the noun items denoting acts and abstractions.
- **Predicate/discourse frames** appear specifically with მართალი and სწორი (*martali xar*, *martals ambob*, *sc̣ori xar*) and with truth/lie nouns (*simartles vambob*, *ṭq̇uils ambob*); these are not generalizable to the genuineness or reliability adjectives.
- **Idioms proper are scarce.** The clearest idiomatic material clusters around **არსი** ("getting to the essence") and the discourse parenthetical *martali gitxra* "to tell you the truth"; most other items participate only in transparent collocations.
- **Asymmetries / absences preserved:** no common multi-word construction was identified for **სიცრუე (#10)**, **იგივეობა (#18)**, or **მართებულობა (#27)**; abstract -oba/-eba nouns in general yield few constructions beyond genitive collocations.
- **Sense-shift within constructions** is recorded where relevant: *naɣdi puli* and *cocxali arseba* exercise the "cash" and "creature" senses respectively, not the truth-evaluation sense; *bunebit* "by nature" is borderline inflectional.
