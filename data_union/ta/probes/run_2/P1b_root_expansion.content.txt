# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P1b ROOT EXPANSION
# Language: Tamil (ta)

---

## Step 1 — Identify Central Roots

---

**Root 1**

**Form:** உண்மை / *uṇmai* (and its base *uḷ-* / *uṇ-*)

**P1 items participating:** 6
Items 1 (*uṇmai*), 4 (*uṇmaiyāna*), 9 (*uṇmaitaṉmai*), 20 (*uṇmaiyil*), 21 (*uṇmaiyilēyē*), 30 (*uṇmaikkup puṟampāna*)

**Core meaning contribution:** The primary Tamil root for truth, reality, and genuine existence. Supplies the domain's central nominal anchor and generates the most productive derivational and collocational network in the truth-evaluation field.

---

**Root 2**

**Form:** நேர் / *nēr*

**P1 items participating:** 4
Items 3 (*nēṟmai*), 10 (*nēṟtti*), 19 (*nēṟmaiyāna*), 29 (*nēr*)

**Core meaning contribution:** Straightness, directness, and proper alignment — the root from which honesty and integrity are conceptualized as absence of deviation. Generates a cluster of evaluative terms for personal conduct and formal correctness.

---

**Root 3**

**Form:** நம்பு / *nampu*

**P1 items participating:** 4
Items 7 (*nampakamāna*), 8 (*nampakattaṉmai*), 28 (*nampikkai*), and implicitly the base of *nampakam*

**Core meaning contribution:** The act and state of trusting or believing. Generates the domain's vocabulary for credibility, reliability, and the epistemic relationship between a person and a claim or source.

---

## Step 2 — Expand Each Root

### Root 1: உண்மை / *uṇmai*

*This root is highly productive. The 25 most common compounds, collocations, and fixed expressions are listed below; additional rarer forms exist.*

---

**1.**
**Form:** உண்மையான
**Transliteration:** uṇmaiyāna
**Morpheme breakdown:** *uṇmai* (truth) + *-āna* (adjectival copular suffix)
**Natural English translation:** true, genuine, real, authentic
**Context of use:** Attributive adjective applied to persons, feelings, objects, and statements to assert genuineness. Extremely frequent across all registers of spoken and written Tamil.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**2.**
**Form:** உண்மையாக
**Transliteration:** uṇmaiyāka
**Morpheme breakdown:** *uṇmai* (truth) + *-āka* (adverbial copular suffix)
**Natural English translation:** truly, genuinely, honestly, in truth
**Context of use:** Adverbial form used to assert that an action or state is genuine or sincere. Common in everyday speech: *uṇmaiyāka colla* (to speak truthfully).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**3.**
**Form:** உண்மையில்
**Transliteration:** uṇmaiyil
**Morpheme breakdown:** *uṇmai* (truth) + *-il* (locative case suffix)
**Natural English translation:** in truth, actually, in reality
**Context of use:** Fixed adverbial expression used to introduce a correction or clarification of what is actually the case. Extremely common in spoken and written Tamil.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

**4.**

**Natural English translation:** truly, really, genuinely, in actual fact
**Context of use:** Emphatic adverbial form stressing the reality of a state of affairs. More emphatic than *uṇmaiyil*; common in spoken Tamil and informal writing.

## Step 3 — Peripheral Extensions

The following expressions show the identified roots appearing in contexts where the connection to truth, falsity, or credibility is not straightforward. These are flagged without interpretation.

---

**From *uṇmai*:**

- *uṇmaiyāna aṉpu* (true love) — the root evaluates the intensity or depth of an emotion rather than its correspondence to fact.
- *uṇmaiyāna vilai* (the real price) — the root functions to distinguish an actual quantity from a stated one, in a commercial rather than epistemic frame.
- *uṇmai vaḻi* (the path of truth) — the root appears in a moral-conduct frame where "truth" names a way of living rather than a property of propositions.
- *uṇmaiyāna mukam* (the true face) — the root is applied to identity and character revelation rather than to the accuracy of a statement.

---

**From *nēr*:**

- *nērkōṭu* (straight line) — the root contributes purely geometric meaning with no evaluative or epistemic content.
- *nērkōṇam* (right angle) — same as above; the root functions as a technical geometric descriptor.
- *nēraṭi olvipārppu* (live broadcast) — the root contributes the sense of directness or immediacy in a media-transmission context.
- *nērmāṟu* (opposite, inverse) — the root contributes a logical/mathematical sense of direct opposition rather than honesty or correctness.
- *nērnilai* (upright position) — the root describes physical verticality with no necessary evaluative content.
- *nēṟtti* (neatness, precision of form) — the root contributes an aesthetic sense of proper execution that is distinct from moral honesty.

---

**From *nampu*:**

- *nampavē muṭiyāta* (unbelievable, incredible) — the root functions as an intensity or degree marker in contexts of surprise, not necessarily involving credibility assessment.
- *nampikkai ūṭṭu* (to instill confidence) — the root appears in a motivational or psychological frame where confidence rather than epistemic trust is the primary sense.
- *nampikkai* in religious contexts — the root is used for religious faith (*kaṭavuḷ mēl nampikkai*, faith in God), where the object of trust is not an empirical claim subject to verification.
