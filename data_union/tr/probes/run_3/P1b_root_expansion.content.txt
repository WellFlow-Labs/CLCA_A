# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P1b ROOT EXPANSION
# Language: Turkish (tr)

---

## Step 1 — Identify Central Roots

---

**Root 1**

**Form:** doğru / *doğr-*
**Transliteration:** doğru / doğr-
**P1 participation:** 3 items — *doğru* (item 1), *doğruluk* (item 12), *doğrulamak* (item 13)
**Core meaning contribution:** Correctness, straightness, alignment with fact or norm. The root supplies the primary native Turkic stem for evaluating statements, directions, and conduct as accurate or true. Highly productive derivationally.

---

**Root 2**

**Form:** gerçek / *gerçek-*
**Transliteration:** gerçek / gerçek-
**P1 participation:** 2 items — *gerçek* (item 2), *gerçeklik* (item 11)
**Core meaning contribution:** Reality, genuineness, non-illusoriness. The root supplies the primary colloquial stem for asserting that something has genuine existence or is not fabricated. Moderately productive derivationally.

---

**Root 3**

**Form:** hak / *hak-*
**Transliteration:** hak / hak-
**P1 participation:** 5 items — *hak* (item 4), *haklı* (item 5), *hakikat* (item 3), *hakiki* (item 19), *sahih* (item 20, via Arabic *ṣaḥīḥ*, cognate domain though not morphologically related)
**Note:** *hakikat* and *hakiki* are Arabic-origin forms built on the same Arabic root *ḥ-q-q* as *hak*; they function as a morphological family in Turkish even though the Arabic internal structure is not natively transparent to Turkish speakers. They are counted together here because Turkish speakers and lexicographers treat *hak*, *hakikat*, and *hakiki* as a related cluster.
**Core meaning contribution:** Right, truth, entitlement, essential nature. The root (from Arabic *ḥaqq*) supplies the primary formal/legal/philosophical stem for evaluating claims, persons, and states as correct, justified, or genuinely what they are.

## Step 2 — Expand Each Root

---

### ROOT 1: *doğr-* (doğru and its derivatives)

---

**1.**
**Form:** doğru
**Transliteration:** doğru
**Morpheme breakdown:** *doğr-* (root: straight, correct) + *-u* (adjectival/nominal ending)
**Natural English translation:** straight, correct, true, right, direct
**Context of use:** Used as adjective, adverb, and noun across all registers. *Bu doğru mu?* ("Is this correct?"); *doğru yol* ("the right path/road").
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**2.**
**Form:** doğruluk
**Transliteration:** doğruluk
**Morpheme breakdown:** *doğru* + *-luk* (abstract noun suffix)
**Natural English translation:** truthfulness, accuracy, correctness, integrity
**Context of use:** Used in both epistemic contexts (accuracy of a measurement or statement) and moral contexts (honesty of a person's character). *Doğruluk her zaman ödüllendirilmez.* ("Truthfulness is not always rewarded.")
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**3.**
**Form:** doğrulamak
**Transliteration:** doğrulamak
**Morpheme breakdown:** *doğru* + *-la-* (denominative/factitive verb suffix) + *-mak* (infinitive)
**Natural English translation:** to verify, to confirm, to validate, to authenticate
**Context of use:** Used in journalistic, legal, and technical registers to describe the act of checking and establishing the truth of a claim or the validity of a document. *Haberi doğrulamak gerekiyor.* ("The news needs to be verified.")
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**4.**
**Form:** doğrulanmak
**Transliteration:** doğrulanmak
**Morpheme breakdown:** *doğru* + *-la-* + *-n-* (passive) + *-mak*
**Natural English translation:** to be verified, to be confirmed, to be validated
**Context of use:** Passive counterpart of *doğrulamak*; used when the subject of verification is the claim or document rather than the agent. *İddia henüz doğrulanmadı.* ("The claim has not yet been confirmed.")
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**5.**
**Form:** doğrultmak
**Transliteration:** doğrultmak
**Morpheme breakdown:** *doğru* + *-lt-* (causative) + *-mak*
**Natural English translation:** to straighten, to correct, to aim, to direct
**Context of use:** Used in physical contexts (straightening an object) and figurative contexts (correcting a course of action or aiming a weapon or instrument). *Silahı hedefe doğrulttular.* ("They aimed the weapon at the target.")
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**6.**
**Form:** doğrulmak
**Transliteration:** doğrulmak
**Morpheme breakdown:** *doğru* + *-l-* (intransitive/reflexive) + *-mak*
**Natural English translation:** to straighten up, to sit up straight, to rise upright
**Context of use:** Used in physical contexts to describe a person or object becoming upright or straight. *Yataktan doğruldu.* ("He/she sat up straight in bed.")
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**7.**
**Form:** doğrultu
**Transliteration:** doğrultu
**Morpheme breakdown:** *doğru* + *-ltu* (deverbal/denominal noun suffix, variant of *-ltı*)
**Natural English translation:** direction, line of direction, alignment
**Context of use:** Used in technical, navigational, and spatial contexts to denote a line or direction of orientation. *Kuzey doğrultusunda ilerlediler.* ("They advanced in the direction of north.")
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**8.**
**Form:** doğrudan
**Transliteration:** doğrudan
**Morpheme breakdown:** *doğru* + *-dan* (ablative/adverbial suffix, here lexicalized as adverb)
**Natural English translation:** directly, straight, without intermediary
**Context of use:** Used as an adverb across all registers to indicate that something is done without mediation or detour. *Doğrudan konuya gelelim.* ("Let's get straight to the point.")
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**9.**
**Form:** doğrudan doğruya
**Transliteration:** doğrudan doğruya
**Morpheme breakdown:** *doğrudan* + *doğruya* (dative of *doğru*) — reduplicated adverbial phrase
**Natural English translation:** directly, straight, point-blank, without any intermediary
**Context of use:** Emphatic adverbial phrase used to stress the absence of any mediation or indirection. More emphatic than *doğrudan* alone. *Doğrudan doğruya ona söyledim.* ("I told him/her directly, straight out.")
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**10.**
**Form:** doğru dürüst
**Transliteration:** doğru dürüst
**Morpheme breakdown:** *doğru* (correct, proper) + *dürüst* (upright, honest — from Persian *durust*) — fixed binomial
**Natural English translation:** properly, decently, adequately; (of a person) decent and honest
**Context of use:** Used as an adverbial or adjectival phrase to indicate that something is done properly or that a person is genuinely decent. *Doğru dürüst bir iş yapamıyor.* ("He/she can't do a proper job.")
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**11.**
**Form:** dürüst
**Transliteration:** dürüst
**Morpheme breakdown:** from Persian *durust* (correct, sound, intact) — borrowed and lexicalized independently
**Natural English translation:** honest, upright, straightforward, decent
**Context of use:** Used to evaluate persons as genuinely honest and morally upright. Frequently collocates with *doğru* in the binomial *doğru dürüst*. *Dürüst bir insan.* ("An honest person.")
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**12.**
**Form:** dürüstlük
**Transliteration:** dürüstlük
**Morpheme breakdown:** *dürüst* + *-lük* (abstract noun suffix)
**Natural English translation:** honesty, uprightness, integrity
**Context of use:** Used as an abstract noun in moral and character evaluation. *Dürüstlük en önemli erdemlerden biridir.* ("Honesty is one of the most important virtues.")
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**13.**
**Form:** doğrulayıcı
**Transliteration:** doğrulayıcı
**Morpheme breakdown:** *doğrula-* + *-yıcı* (agentive/adjectival suffix)
**Natural English translation:** verifying, confirmatory, validating; a verifier
**Context of use:** Used in technical and administrative contexts to describe a person, process, or mechanism that performs verification. *Doğrulayıcı kod* ("verification code") is common in digital/technical usage.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**14.**
**Form:** doğrulama
**Transliteration:** doğrulama
**Morpheme breakdown:** *doğrula-* + *-ma* (verbal noun suffix)
**Natural English translation:** verification, confirmation, validation
**Context of use:** Used as a noun in legal, technical, and journalistic contexts to name the act or process of verifying. *Kimlik doğrulama* ("identity verification") is a standard technical term.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**15.**
**Form:** kimlik doğrulama
**Transliteration:** kimlik doğrulama
**Morpheme breakdown:** *kimlik* (identity) + *doğrulama* (verification)
**Natural English translation:** identity verification, authentication
**Context of use:** Standard technical and administrative term for the process of verifying a person's identity, used in digital security, banking, and official procedures.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**16.**
**Form:** doğru söylemek
**Transliteration:** doğru söylemek
**Morpheme breakdown:** *doğru* (true/correct) + *söylemek* (to say, to tell)
**Natural English translation:** to tell the truth, to speak correctly
**Context of use:** Common collocation used in everyday speech and moral evaluation. *Doğruyu söyle.* ("Tell the truth.")
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**17.**
**Form:** doğruyu söylemek
**Transliteration:** doğruyu söylemek
**Morpheme breakdown:** *doğru-yu* (accusative of *doğru*) + *söylemek*
**Natural English translation:** to tell the truth (with direct object marking)
**Context of use:** Variant of *doğru söylemek* with accusative marking on *doğru*, emphasizing the truth as the direct object of the speech act. Both forms are in common use.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**18.**
**Form:** doğru yol
**Transliteration:** doğru yol
**Morpheme breakdown:** *doğru* (right, correct) + *yol* (road, path, way)
**Natural English translation:** the right path, the correct way, the straight and narrow
**Context of use:** Used in moral, religious, and practical contexts to refer to the correct course of action or life. *Doğru yoldan çıkmak* ("to stray from the right path") is a common expression.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**19.**
**Form:** doğru yolda olmak
**Transliteration:** doğru yolda olmak
**Morpheme breakdown:** *doğru yol* + *-da* (locative) + *olmak* (to be)
**Natural English translation:** to be on the right track, to be on the right path
**Context of use:** Used in moral, practical, and evaluative contexts to indicate that a person or process is proceeding correctly. *Doğru yoldasınız.* ("You are on the right track.")
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**20.**
**Form:** doğru düzgün
**Transliteration:** doğru düzgün
**Morpheme breakdown:** *doğru* (correct, proper) + *düzgün* (orderly, neat, proper) — binomial
**Natural English translation:** properly, decently, in an orderly and correct manner
**Context of use:** Colloquial binomial used to indicate that something is done in a fully proper and adequate manner. *Doğru düzgün konuşamıyor.* ("He/she can't speak properly.")
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**21.**
**Form:** doğrusal
**Transliteration:** doğrusal
**Morpheme breakdown:** *doğru* + *-sal* (adjectival suffix, "pertaining to")
**Natural English translation:** linear, rectilinear
**Context of use:** Used in mathematical and scientific contexts to describe relationships, functions, or forms that are linear (proceeding in a straight line). *Doğrusal denklem* ("linear equation").
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**22.**
**Form:** doğrusal olmayan
**Transliteration:** doğrusal olmayan
**Morpheme breakdown:** *doğrusal* + *olmayan* (not being, negative participle of *olmak*)
**Natural English translation:** nonlinear
**Context of use:** Used in mathematics, physics, and engineering to describe systems or relationships that are not linear. *Doğrusal olmayan dinamikler* ("nonlinear dynamics").
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**23.**
**Form:** doğru açı
**Transliteration:** doğru açı
**Morpheme breakdown:** *doğru* (right, correct) + *açı* (angle)
**Natural English translation:** right angle
**Context of use:** Standard geometric term for a 90-degree angle. Used in mathematics education and technical drawing.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**24.**
**Form:** doğru orantılı
**Transliteration:** doğru orantılı
**Morpheme breakdown:** *doğru* (direct) + *orantılı* (proportional)
**Natural English translation:** directly proportional
**Context of use:** Standard mathematical and scientific term indicating that two quantities increase or decrease together. *Hız ve mesafe doğru orantılıdır.* ("Speed and distance are directly proportional.")
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**25.**
**Form:** doğruluk payı
**Transliteration:** doğruluk payı
**Morpheme breakdown:** *doğruluk* (truthfulness, accuracy) + *payı* (share, portion — possessive)
**Natural English translation:** grain of truth, element of truth
**Context of use:** Used in evaluative discourse to acknowledge that a claim, though not entirely accurate, contains some truth. *Bu iddiada bir doğruluk payı var.* ("There is a grain of truth in this claim.")
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**26.**
**Form:** doğruluk testi
**Transliteration:** doğruluk testi
**Morpheme breakdown:** *doğruluk* (accuracy, truthfulness) + *testi* (test — from English/French)
**Natural English translation:** truth test, accuracy test, fact-check
**Context of use:** Used in journalistic and analytical contexts to describe the process of checking whether a claim is accurate. Common in media literacy and fact-checking discourse.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**27.**
**Form:** doğru bulmak
**Transliteration:** doğru bulmak
**Morpheme breakdown:** *doğru* (correct) + *bulmak* (to find, to consider)
**Natural English translation:** to find correct, to consider right, to approve of
**Context of use:** Used to express that a person judges something to be correct or appropriate. *Bunu doğru bulmuyorum.* ("I don't find this right / I don't approve of this.")
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**28.**
**Form:** doğru çıkmak
**Transliteration:** doğru çıkmak
**Morpheme breakdown:** *doğru* (correct, true) + *çıkmak* (to come out, to turn out)
**Natural English translation:** to turn out to be correct, to prove true
**Context of use:** Used to describe a prediction, claim, or suspicion that is subsequently confirmed by events. *Tahmini doğru çıktı.* ("His/her prediction turned out to be correct.")
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**29.**
**Form:** doğruya doğru, eğriye eğri demek
**Transliteration:** doğruya doğru, eğriye eğri demek
**Morpheme breakdown:** *doğru-ya doğru* (to the straight/right, [call it] right) + *eğri-ye eğri* (to the crooked, [call it] crooked) + *demek* (to say)
**Natural English translation:** to call a spade a spade, to call things as they are
**Context of use:** A proverbial expression used to praise or prescribe honest, unvarnished evaluation. *O doğruya doğru, eğriye eğri der.* ("He/she calls things as they are.")
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**30.**
**Form:** eğri
**Transliteration:** eğri
**Morpheme breakdown:** *eğri* (crooked, bent, curved) — antonym of *doğru* in spatial and moral senses
**Natural English translation:** crooked, bent, curved; (figuratively) wrong, dishonest
**Context of use:** Functions as the primary antonym of *doğru* in both physical (a crooked line) and moral (a dishonest person or action) contexts. Appears in the proverb above and in many collocations.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

> **Note:** The *doğr-* root is highly productive. Additional attested forms include *doğruca* (straightforwardly, directly), *doğrucuk* (diminutive/affectionate: quite right), *doğrulayamamak* (to be unable to verify), and various compound noun phrases in technical domains. The 30 items above represent the most common and widely attested.

### ROOT 2: *gerçek-* (gerçek and its derivatives)

---

**1.**
**Form:** gerçek
**Transliteration:** gerçek
**Morpheme breakdown:** *gerçek* (root: real, genuine)
**Natural English translation:** real, true, genuine, actual; (as noun) reality, truth
**Context of use:** Used as adjective and noun across all registers. *Gerçek bir dost* ("a true friend"); *gerçeği söylemek* ("to tell the truth").
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**2.**
**Form:** gerçeklik
**Transliteration:** gerçeklik
**Morpheme breakdown:** *gerçek* + *-lik* (abstract noun suffix)
**Natural English translation:** reality, realness, genuineness, authenticity
**Context of use:** Used in philosophical, psychological, and everyday contexts to refer to the property of being real or to the domain of reality. *Sanal gerçeklik* ("virtual reality") is a standard technical term.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**3.**
**Form:** gerçekleşmek
**Transliteration:** gerçekleşmek
**Morpheme breakdown:** *gerçek* + *-leş-* (inchoative/reciprocal verb suffix) + *-mek*
**Natural English translation:** to come true, to materialize, to be realized, to happen
**Context of use:** Used to describe dreams, plans, predictions, or events that become actual. *Hayali gerçekleşti.* ("His/her dream came true.")
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**4.**
**Form:** gerçekleştirmek
**Transliteration:** gerçekleştirmek
**Morpheme breakdown:** *gerçek* + *-leş-* + *-tir-* (causative) + *-mek*
**Natural English translation:** to realize, to carry out, to bring about, to accomplish
**Context of use:** Used to describe the act of making something actual or bringing a plan to fruition. *Projeyi gerçekleştirdiler.* ("They carried out the project.")
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**5.**
**Form:** gerçekleştirilmek
**Transliteration:** gerçekleştirilmek
**Morpheme breakdown:** *gerçek* + *-leş-* + *-tir-* + *-il-* (passive) + *-mek*
**Natural English translation:** to be realized, to be carried out, to be accomplished
**Context of use:** Passive form used in formal and administrative language when the agent of realization is backgrounded. *Proje zamanında gerçekleştirildi.* ("The project was carried out on time.")
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**6.**
**Form:** gerçekten
**Transliteration:** gerçekten
**Morpheme breakdown:** *gerçek* + *-ten* (ablative, here lexicalized as adverb)
**Natural English translation:** really, truly, genuinely, indeed
**Context of use:** High-frequency adverb used across all registers to assert or emphasize that something is genuinely the case. *Gerçekten mi?* ("Really?"); *Gerçekten güzel.* ("It's truly beautiful.")
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**7.**
**Form:** gerçek anlamda
**Transliteration:** gerçek anlamda
**Morpheme breakdown:** *gerçek* (real) + *anlam* (meaning) + *-da* (locative)
**Natural English translation:** in the true sense, in the real sense of the word
**Context of use:** Used to specify that a term is being used in its full, genuine sense rather than loosely or metaphorically. *Gerçek anlamda bir lider.* ("A leader in the true sense of the word.")
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**8.**
**Form:** gerçek dışı
**Transliteration:** gerçek dışı
**Morpheme breakdown:** *gerçek* (real) + *dışı* (outside of, beyond — postpositional)
**Natural English translation:** unreal, surreal, beyond reality, fictitious
**Context of use:** Used to describe things that lie outside the bounds of reality — surreal experiences, fictional scenarios, or implausible claims. *Gerçek dışı bir durum.* ("An unreal / surreal situation.")
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**9.**
**Form:** gerçeküstü
**Transliteration:** gerçeküstü
**Morpheme breakdown:** *gerçek* (real) + *üstü* (above, beyond — postpositional compound)
**Natural English translation:** surreal, surrealistic
**Context of use:** Used in aesthetic and literary contexts to describe the surrealist movement or surrealistic qualities in art, literature, and experience. *Gerçeküstücülük* is the corresponding term for Surrealism as a movement.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**10.**
**Form:** gerçeküstücülük
**Transliteration:** gerçeküstücülük
**Morpheme breakdown:** *gerçeküstü* + *-cü-* (agentive) + *-lük* (abstract noun)
**Natural English translation:** Surrealism (as an artistic/literary movement)
**Context of use:** Standard term in Turkish art history and literary criticism for the Surrealist movement. *Gerçeküstücülük 20. yüzyılın önemli sanat akımlarından biridir.* ("Surrealism is one of the important art movements of the 20th century.")
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

**11.**

**Morpheme breakdown:** *gerçek* (real) + *anlam* (meaning) + *-ı* (possessive) + *-yla* (instrumental)
**Natural English translation:** in the true sense, literally, in the full meaning of the word
**Context of use:** Used to specify that a word or expression is being used in its full, literal, or genuine sense. *Gerçek anlamıyla bir trajedi.* ("A tragedy in the true sense of the word.")
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

> **Note:** The *gerçek-* root is moderately productive. Additional attested forms include *gerçekleşememek* (to fail to materialize), *gerçekleşme* (materialization, realization as a noun), and *gerçekçice* (realistically, as an adverb). The items above represent the most common and widely attested.

## Step 3 — Peripheral Extensions

The following expressions involve the three roots identified above but appear in contexts where the root's contribution shifts away from its core truth-evaluation or rights-entitlement function. These are flagged without interpretation or categorization.

---

**From *doğr-*:**

- *doğrusal* / *doğrusal olmayan* — the root contributes "straight/linear" in mathematical and scientific contexts where truth-evaluation is not at issue; the relevant property is geometric or functional linearity.

- *doğrultu* — the root contributes "direction" or "line of orientation" in spatial and navigational contexts; the relevant property is physical alignment rather than epistemic correctness.

- *doğrulmak* — the root contributes "becoming upright" in purely physical contexts (sitting up, standing straight); no epistemic or moral evaluation is involved.

- *doğru orantılı* — the root contributes "direct" in the mathematical sense of direct proportionality; the relevant relationship is quantitative rather than evaluative.

- *doğru açı* — the root contributes "right" in the geometric sense (right angle = 90°); the relevant property is angular measure.

---

**From *gerçek-*:**

- *gerçekleşmek* / *gerçekleştirmek* — the root contributes "becoming actual" or "bringing into actuality" in contexts of plans, events, and projects; the relevant notion is actualization or accomplishment rather than truth-evaluation of a statement.

- *gerçek zamanlı* — the root contributes "real" in the technical sense of real-time processing; the relevant property is temporal immediacy in computational systems.

- *sanal gerçeklik* / *artırılmış gerçeklik* — the root contributes "reality" as a technical domain name in computing; the compound names a simulated or enhanced environment rather than evaluating a claim as true.

- *gerçeküstü* / *gerçeküstücülük* — the root contributes "reality" as a baseline from which the aesthetic movement departs; the relevant context is artistic and aesthetic rather than epistemic.

- *gerçekçi* / *gerçekçilik* — the root contributes "real" in the context of aesthetic realism as a movement or style; the relevant evaluation concerns artistic representation rather than the truth of a proposition.

---

**From *hak-* / *hakk-*:**

- *hak etmek* — the root contributes "due" in the sense of desert or merit; the relevant evaluation concerns whether an outcome is deserved rather than whether a statement is true.

- *hak yemek* — the root contributes "due" in an idiomatic expression where *yemek* (to eat) frames the deprivation of entitlement; the relevant context is moral and interpersonal rather than epistemic.

- *telif hakkı* / *fikri mülkiyet hakkı* — the root contributes "right" in the legal-commercial sense of intellectual property; the relevant context is property law rather than truth-evaluation.

- *hak-hukuk* — the root appears in a binomial with *hukuk* (law), where both elements refer to legal entitlements; the relevant context is legal rights rather than epistemic correctness.

- *hakkaniyet* — the root contributes "due" in the sense of equitable treatment; the relevant context is distributive justice rather than truth-evaluation of statements.

- *hakikat* in religious contexts — the root cluster contributes "essential nature" in Sufi and Islamic theological discourse, where *hakikat* names a level of spiritual reality or divine truth distinct from surface religious law (*şeriat*); the relevant context is metaphysical and soteriological rather than propositional.
