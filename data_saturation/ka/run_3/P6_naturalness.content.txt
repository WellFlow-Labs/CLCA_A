# CLCA Protocol v3.3 — P6 CHANGE, CAUSATION, AND STABILITY
# Language: Georgian (ka)

---

**Methodological note:** This step documents how Georgian encodes dynamic relationships — becoming, causing, and maintaining — involving truth-related vocabulary established in P1–P5. All vocabulary is drawn from the CONTEXT. Auxiliary verbs, aspect markers, copulas, and case markers are added only as needed to form natural Georgian expressions. Where no common expression exists, this is stated explicitly. Each truth-related term is treated separately where its behavior differs. Epistemic status reflects confidence in attestation and naturalness based on patterns documented in the CONTEXT.

---

## 1. BECOMING A TRUTH-RELATED STATE

### 1.1 Truth / correctness coming to light or being established

**Pattern:** Truth-domain nominal as subject + resultative verb expressing disclosure or establishment.

---

**Expression 1**
- **Georgian:** სიმართლე გამოვლინდა
- **Transliteration:** simartle gamovlinda
- **English translation:** the truth came to light / the truth was revealed
- **Source:** P2b (č̣ešmariteba gamovlinda is documented; the parallel with simartle is directly supported by the simartle gamovlena collocation in P1b and the gamovlena pattern documented throughout P2b)
- **Notes:** The verb gamovlinda is the perfective of gamovlindeba ("to be revealed, to come to light"), documented in P2b under č̣ešmariteba. The subject is the truth nominal; no agent need be expressed. The construction encodes the transition from a hidden to a disclosed state of truth.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Expression 2**
- **Georgian:** ჭეშმარიტება გამოვლინდა
- **Transliteration:** č̣ešmariteba gamovlinda
- **English translation:** the truth came to light / the truth was revealed
- **Source:** P2b (documented directly: č̣ešmariteba gamovlinda)
- **Notes:** Parallel to Expression 1 but in the elevated philosophical/theological register associated with č̣ešmariteba (P3 Group 7). The same verb applies to both truth nominals.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Expression 3**
- **Georgian:** სიმართლე დადგინდა
- **Transliteration:** simartle dadginda
- **English translation:** the truth was established / the truth was determined
- **Source:** P1b (simartlis dadgena, "determination of the truth," documented in mart- root expansion item 7); the perfective verb dadginda is the resultative form corresponding to the verbal noun dadgena.
- **Notes:** Encodes the transition from an undetermined to a determined state of truth, specifically in legal and investigative contexts. The verbal noun dadgena is documented; the corresponding finite verb form dadginda is the natural perfective.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Expression 4**
- **Georgian:** სიმართლე გამარჯვებს
- **Transliteration:** simartle gamarjvebs
- **English translation:** truth prevails / truth wins out
- **Source:** P1b (mart- root expansion item 27, documented directly)
- **Notes:** Encodes a dynamic process — truth moving from a contested to a victorious state. The present tense gamarjvebs expresses ongoing or habitual prevailing. Documented as a proverbial/rhetorical expression.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Expression 5**
- **Georgian:** ჭეშმარიტება გამარჯვებს
- **Transliteration:** č̣ešmariteba gamarjvebs
- **English translation:** truth prevails / truth is victorious
- **Source:** P1b (č̣ešmar- root expansion item 22, documented directly)
- **Notes:** Parallel to Expression 4 in the elevated register. P5 Opposition 17 notes both as proverbial expressions asserting the ultimate triumph of truth.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### 1.2 Falsity / fraud coming to light or being exposed

**Pattern:** Falsity-domain nominal as subject + resultative verb expressing exposure or disclosure.

---

**Expression 6**
- **Georgian:** სიყალბე გამოაშკარავდა
- **Transliteration:** siq̇albe gamoašk'aravda
- **English translation:** the fraud was exposed / the falsity came to light
- **Source:** P2b (documented directly under P1 item #11)
- **Notes:** The verb gamoašk'aravda ("was exposed, was unmasked") is documented in P2b as "a standard collocation partner for siq̇albe." The construction encodes the transition from a hidden to a disclosed state of fraud or falsity. No agent need be expressed.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Expression 7**
- **Georgian:** სიყალბე გამოვლინდა
- **Transliteration:** siq̇albe gamovlinda
- **English translation:** the falsity came to light / the fraud was revealed
- **Source:** P1b (siq̇albis gamovlena, "exposure of fraud," documented in q̇alb- root expansion item 21); the perfective gamovlinda corresponds to the verbal noun gamovlena.
- **Notes:** Parallel in structure to Expression 1 but with the falsity nominal as subject. The verbal noun gamovlena is documented for both simartle (P1b mart- item 10) and siq̇albe (P1b q̇alb- item 21), supporting the parallel finite verb construction.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### 1.3 A person becoming a liar / becoming untruthful

**Pattern:** No single grammaticalized inchoative construction is attested for this meaning. The data supports only the resultative/evaluative constructions documented below.

---

**Expression 8**
- **Georgian:** მტყუარი აღმოჩნდა
- **Transliteration:** mt'q̇uari aɣmočnda
- **English translation:** turned out to be a liar / proved to be untruthful
- **Source:** P2b (documented directly under P1 item #13)
- **Notes:** The verb aɣmočnda ("turned out to be, proved to be") encodes the retrospective discovery of a state rather than a transition into it. P2b describes this as used "in narrative and evaluative contexts to describe the retrospective discovery that a person was lying." This is the closest attested expression to "becoming a liar" in the data; it encodes revelation of an existing state rather than a change of state.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### 1.4 Something becoming falsified / forged

**Pattern:** Resultative participial construction encoding the completed state of falsification.

---

**Expression 9**
- **Georgian:** დოკუმენტი გაყალბდა
- **Transliteration:** dok'ument'i gaq̇albda
- **English translation:** the document was forged / the document became falsified
- **Source:** P2a (gaq̇albeba, "the act of making false/forging," documented as a verbal noun from the verb gaq̇albebs; the perfective passive gaq̇albda is the natural finite form corresponding to this verbal noun)
- **Notes:** The verbal noun gaq̇albeba is documented in P2a as the standard legal term for the act of falsification. The perfective form gaq̇albda encodes the completed transition of an object into a falsified state. The subject is the document or object; no agent need be expressed in the passive construction.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### 1.5 Something becoming confirmed / verified

**Pattern:** Resultative construction encoding the completed state of confirmation.

---

**Expression 10**
- **Georgian:** ინფორმაცია დადასტურდა
- **Transliteration:** in'p'ormacia dadasturda
- **English translation:** the information was confirmed / the information became verified
- **Source:** P2a (dadasturebuli, "confirmed, verified," documented as the resultative participial adjective from dadastureba; the perfective dadasturda is the natural finite form)
- **Notes:** The resultative participial dadasturebuli is documented in P2a. The perfective finite form dadasturda encodes the completed transition of information from unconfirmed to confirmed status. Consistent with the journalistic and legal contexts documented for dadastureba in P2b.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Expression 11**
- **Georgian:** ინფორმაცია დარჩა დაუდასტურებელი
- **Transliteration:** in'p'ormacia darča daudasturebeli
- **English translation:** the information remained unconfirmed / the information stayed unverified
- **Source:** P2a (daudasturebeli, "unconfirmed, unverified," documented directly); the verb darča ("remained, stayed") is a standard copular verb for expressing persistence of a state.
- **Notes:** This construction encodes the persistence of an unconfirmed state rather than a change of state. It is included here as the stability counterpart to Expression 10. See Section 3 (Stability) below for further discussion.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## 2. CAUSING A TRUTH-RELATED STATE

### 2.1 Causing truth to be established or revealed

**Pattern:** Agent nominal + causative verbal construction with truth-domain nominal as object or subject of the embedded predicate.

---

**Expression 12**
- **Georgian:** სიმართლე დაადგინა
- **Transliteration:** simartle daadgina
- **English translation:** established the truth / determined the truth
- **Source:** P1b (simartlis dadgena, "determination of the truth," documented in mart- root expansion item 7); the transitive perfective daadgina is the natural finite form corresponding to the verbal noun dadgena.
- **Notes:** The verbal noun dadgena is documented in P1b in legal and investigative contexts. The transitive verb daadgina takes the truth nominal as its object and an agent (court, investigator, journalist) as its subject. This is the primary causative construction for establishing truth in legal and investigative contexts.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Expression 13**
- **Georgian:** სიმართლე გამოავლინა
- **Transliteration:** simartle gamoavlina
- **English translation:** revealed the truth / brought the truth to light
- **Source:** P1b (simartlis gamovlena, "bringing truth to light," documented in mart- root expansion item 10); the transitive perfective gamoavlina is the natural finite form corresponding to the verbal noun gamovlena.
- **Notes:** The verbal noun gamovlena is documented in P1b for both simartle and č̣ešmariteba. The transitive verb gamoavlina takes the truth nominal as its object and an agent (journalist, investigator) as its subject. Attested in journalistic and legal contexts.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Expression 14**
- **Georgian:** ჭეშმარიტება გამოავლინა
- **Transliteration:** č̣ešmariteba gamoavlina
- **English translation:** revealed the truth / manifested the truth
- **Source:** P1b (č̣ešmaritebis gamovlena, "the revelation of truth," documented in č̣ešmar- root expansion item 10)
- **Notes:** Parallel to Expression 13 in the elevated register. P1b notes this appears in "theological and philosophical contexts to refer to the disclosure of truth, whether through divine revelation or rational inquiry."
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Expression 15**
- **Georgian:** სიმართლე დამალა
- **Transliteration:** simartle damala
- **English translation:** concealed the truth / hid the truth
- **Source:** P1b (simartlis damalva, "concealment of the truth," documented in mart- root expansion item 8); the transitive perfective damala is the natural finite form corresponding to the verbal noun damalva.
- **Notes:** The verbal noun damalva is documented in P1b as used "in moral, legal, and journalistic contexts to characterize the act of withholding or suppressing true information." The transitive verb damala takes the truth nominal as its object and an agent as its subject. This is the causative construction for suppressing truth, directly opposing Expression 13.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### 2.2 Causing falsification / forgery

**Pattern:** Agent nominal + transitive verb of falsification with document/object nominal as object.

---

**Expression 16**
- **Georgian:** დოკუმენტი გაყალბა
- **Transliteration:** dok'ument'i gaq̇alba
- **English translation:** forged the document / falsified the document
- **Source:** P2a (gaq̇albeba documented as the verbal noun from gaq̇albebs, "to falsify, to forge, to counterfeit"; the transitive perfective gaq̇alba is the natural finite form)
- **Notes:** The verbal noun gaq̇albeba is documented in P2a as "the standard legal term for the act of forgery." The transitive verb gaq̇alba takes the document as its object and the forger (gamaq̇albebeli, P2a) as its subject. This is the primary causative construction for document falsification.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Expression 17**
- **Georgian:** ხელმოწერა გაყალბა
- **Transliteration:** xelmc̣era gaq̇alba
- **English translation:** forged the signature / falsified the signature
- **Source:** P1b (q̇albi xelmc̣era, "forged signature," documented in q̇alb- root expansion item 4); the transitive verb gaq̇alba applies to the signature as object.
- **Notes:** The same transitive verb gaq̇alba applies across the range of objects documented in P1b for q̇albi (documents, signatures, certificates, currency). The object varies; the causative verb is constant.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### 2.3 Causing confirmation / verification

**Pattern:** Agent nominal + transitive verb of confirmation with information/claim nominal as object.

---

**Expression 18**

- **Notes:** The verb daadasturebs is explicitly documented in P2a as the productive verbal form corresponding to the verbal noun dadastureba. The transitive perfective takes the information or claim as its object and the confirming agent as its subject.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

- **Notes:** The verbal noun gadamomc̣meba is documented in P2b as the primary term in fact-checking discourse. The transitive verb takes the facts as its object and the journalist or investigator as its subject.

### 2.4 Causing a person or claim to be characterized as credible or incredible

**Pattern:** No single grammaticalized causative construction for making something credible is attested in the data. The data supports evaluative predicative constructions rather than causative ones for sarcmuno and sando.

---

**Expression 20**
- **Georgian:** სარწმუნოდ მიაჩნია
- **Transliteration:** sarcmunod miacnia
- **English translation:** considers it credible / regards it as reliable
- **Source:** P2b (documented directly under P1 item #14)
- **Notes:** This construction encodes an agent's evaluative judgment that something is credible, rather than a causative act of making something credible. P2b describes it as used "in legal and journalistic contexts to describe a party's or court's assessment of the credibility of evidence or information." It is the closest attested construction to a causative of credibility in the data.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### 2.6 Causing acquittal / justification

**Pattern:** Agent nominal (court) + transitive verb of acquittal with person nominal as object.

---

**Expression 22**
- **Georgian:** სასამართლომ გაამართლა
- **Transliteration:** sasamartlom gaamarтla
- **English translation:** the court acquitted / the court vindicated
- **Source:** P1b (gamartleba, "justification, vindication, acquittal," documented in mart- root expansion item 19, with the note that it derives from the verb gamarтlebs, "to justify, to acquit, to vindicate"); the transitive perfective gaamarтla is the natural finite form with the court as ergative subject.
- **Notes:** The verbal noun gamartleba is documented in P1b as used "in legal contexts for acquittal or exoneration." The transitive verb takes the accused person as its object and the court (sasamartlo, P1b) as its ergative subject. This is the primary causative construction for legal acquittal.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## 3. STABILITY — PERSISTENCE OF A TRUTH-RELATED STATE

### 3.1 Truth remaining hidden or contested

**Pattern:** Truth-domain nominal as subject + verb of persistence (darča, "remained") + state predicate.

---

**Expression 23**
- **Georgian:** სიმართლე დამალული დარჩა
- **Transliteration:** simartle damaluli darča
- **English translation:** the truth remained hidden / the truth stayed concealed
- **Source:** P1b (simartlis damalva, "concealment of the truth," documented in mart- root expansion item 8); the resultative participial damaluli ("hidden, concealed") and the persistence verb darča ("remained") are both natural Georgian forms.
- **Notes:** This construction encodes the persistence of a concealed state of truth. It is the stability counterpart to Expression 13 (revealing the truth). The resultative participial damaluli encodes the state; darča encodes its persistence.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Expression 24**
- **Georgian:** სიმართლე უცნობი დარჩა
- **Transliteration:** simartle ucnobi darča
- **English translation:** the truth remained unknown / the truth stayed undiscovered
- **Source:** The pattern is supported by the documented constructions for simartlis dadgena ("establishing the truth," P1b) and the persistence verb darča; ucnobi ("unknown") is a standard Georgian adjective not specific to this domain but compatible with the construction.
- **Notes:** Encodes the persistence of an undiscovered state of truth, contrasting with Expression 3 (truth being established). The construction is compositionally transparent.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### 3.2 Falsity / fraud persisting undetected

**Pattern:** Falsity-domain nominal as subject + verb of persistence + state predicate.

---

**Expression 25**
- **Georgian:** სიყალბე დაფარული დარჩა
- **Transliteration:** siq̇albe dap'aruli darča
- **English translation:** the fraud remained covered up / the falsity stayed concealed
- **Source:** The pattern is supported by the documented constructions for siq̇albe gamoašk'aravda (P2b, "the fraud was exposed") as the change-of-state counterpart; dap'aruli ("covered, concealed") and darča ("remained") are standard Georgian forms.
- **Notes:** Encodes the persistence of an undetected fraudulent state, contrasting with Expression 6 (fraud being exposed). The construction is compositionally transparent and consistent with the patterns documented for siq̇albe in P2b.
- **Epistemic status:** [UNCERTAIN]

### 3.3 Information remaining unconfirmed

**Pattern:** Information nominal as subject + persistence verb + unconfirmed state predicate.

---

**Expression 26**
- **Georgian:** ინფორმაცია დაუდასტურებელი დარჩა
- **Transliteration:** in'p'ormacia daudasturebeli darča
- **English translation:** the information remained unconfirmed / the information stayed unverified
- **Source:** P2a (daudasturebeli, "unconfirmed, unverified," documented directly with the note that it "appears in journalistic contexts when reporting on unverified information"); the persistence verb darča is standard.
- **Notes:** This is the stability counterpart to Expression 10 (information becoming confirmed). The resultative participial daudasturebeli encodes the unconfirmed state; darča encodes its persistence. The construction is consistent with journalistic usage documented in P2a.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### 3.4 Legal force persisting

**Pattern:** Legal-force nominal as subject + persistence verb.

---

**Expression 27**

## 4. SUMMARY OF PATTERNS

The following observations are directly supported by the data documented above:

**4.1 Asymmetry between truth and falsity in becoming-constructions.** The data documents becoming-constructions for both truth (gamovlinda, dadginda) and falsity (gamoašk'aravda, gaq̇albda), but the constructions are not parallel in form. Truth coming to light uses gamovlinda or dadginda; fraud being exposed uses gamoašk'aravda (a distinct verb). This asymmetry is supported by the distinct collocational partners documented in P2b.

**4.2 The gamovlena verbal family applies to both truth and falsity.** The verbal noun gamovlena and its finite forms (gamovlinda, gamoavlina) are documented for simartle (P1b item 10), č̣ešmariteba (P1b č̣ešmar- item 10), and siq̇albe (P1b q̇alb- item 21). This root is not restricted to one polarity.

**4.3 Causative constructions are primarily transitive verb constructions.** No morphological causative suffix specific to truth-domain predicates is attested. Causation is encoded through transitive verbs (daadgina, gamoavlina, gaq̇alba, daadastура, gadaamc̣ma, gaasс̣ora, gaamarтla) with the affected nominal as object.

**4.4 Stability is encoded through the persistence verb darča + resultative participial.** The pattern darča + resultative participial (damaluli, daudasturebeli) is the primary construction for encoding the persistence of a truth-related state. This pattern is compositionally transparent and not restricted to the truth domain.

**4.5 The inchoative adessive construction (siq̇albeze c̣asvla, P4a) is the only volitional becoming-construction attested.** All other becoming-constructions encode non-volitional transitions (truth coming to light, fraud being exposed, document being forged). The adessive + motion verb construction is rated [MARGINAL] in P4b, consistent with its restricted attestation.

**4.6 No causative of credibility is attested.** The data does not support a construction meaning "X made Y credible." The closest attested construction is the evaluative sarcmunod miacnia ("considers it credible"), which encodes judgment rather than causation.
