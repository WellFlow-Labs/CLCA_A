## 2. Substitution Patterns (Narrative)

**Revelation replacing becoming:** No independent REVELATION construction is attested in P4a/P4b. The only realization/actualization sense appears embedded in the fixed inchoative idiom *come true* (P4a #4), classed under BECOME. Thus revelation does not substitute for becoming; rather, a peripheral realization meaning is lexicalized within the BECOME slot itself. There is no productive revelation operator distinct from becoming.

**Verification / Disconfirmation sharing one construction:** VERIFICATION and DISCONFIRMATION are expressed by the **same** morphosyntactic device — the productive suffixal causative layer in P4a #5. *verify, validate, authenticate* (confirm truth) and *falsify* (disconfirm) are formed identically by *-ify/-ate* suffixation on truth-domain bases. The construction is shared; only the lexical base differs in polarity. DISCONFIRMATION additionally has the negative-predication route (P4a #2: *that is untrue / that is false*).

**Verification vs. causation:** VERIFICATION does not replace CAUSATION; they occupy the same suffixal construction (P4a #5). *falsify* is simultaneously a factual-causation verb ("make the records false") and a disconfirmation verb. The language does not formally distinguish "making something false" from "showing something to be false" within this layer — both are carried by the same *-ify* morphology.

**Avoidance of native-core causation:** A systematic asymmetry appears in CAUSATION: Latinate/suffixal bases form simple causative verbs freely (*falsify, validate, rectify*), but native core adjectives (*true, real, right*) lack a simple causative verb and are forced into periphrasis (*make true*) or particle constructions (*true up*) (P4a/P4b #5).

**Negation — analytic and morphological coexist:** NEGATION (P4a #2) is realized by **both** a morphological strategy (prefixed *un-/in-/im-/il-/dis-*, lexically unpredictable) and an analytic strategy (*not* + positive ADJ). Suppletive antonyms (*false* for *true*, *fake* for *genuine/real*) compete with and are often preferred over prefixed forms in predication.

**Positive vs. negative asymmetries:**
- AUTHENTICATION FRAME (P4a #10): fixed positive NPs cluster on *real/genuine* (*the genuine article*, *the real McCoy*), but **no negative-pole equivalent** exists (no fixed "*the fake article*").
- AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION (P4a #7): positive agentives are compounds (*truth-teller*); the negative agent is the suppletive *liar*; deception agentives are suffixal (*faker, forger, counterfeiter*). Most evaluative adjectives have no direct agentive and need periphrasis.
- NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION favors suppletion (*false/fake*) over prefixation (*untrue/*unreal*).

## 3. Missingness Summary

**Structural Gaps (missing constructions):**
- **REVELATION** — no independent construction; realization sense survives only inside the fixed BECOME idiom *come true*.
- **CAUSATION (character)** — no construction distinguishing character-causation from factual causation.
- BECOME — no dedicated inchoative morphology; construction is restricted/idiomatic (morphosyntactic gap, partial availability).

**Lexical Gaps (missing words):**
- Native core adjectives (*true, real, right*) lack a simple causative verb → periphrasis required (*make true*) (P4a #5).
- Most evaluative adjectives (*true, honest, valid*) lack a direct agentive noun → periphrasis ("an honest person") (P4a #7).

**Conceptual Gaps (blocked operations):**
- None explicitly indicated as blocked in P4a/P4b. All attested meanings are expressible via at least one construction or circumlocution.

**Asymmetric Gaps (only one polarity available):**
- AUTHENTICATION NOMINAL FRAME: positive pole only (*the genuine article*); negative pole missing (no fixed "*the fake article*").
- AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION: positive uses compound *truth-teller*; negative uses suppletive *liar* — formed by different strategies (asymmetric formation).
- NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION: prefixed forms exist but are dispreferred relative to suppletive antonyms.

## 4. Canonicalized Operator Inventory

**Fully available (direct construction, [CORE]):**
- ATTR — copular *be* + truth ADJ
- ADNOM — prenominal ADJ + N
- NEGATION — morphological (prefix) and analytic (*not*), plus suppletion
- NOMINALIZATION (abstract) — productive suffixal (*-th/-ity/-cy/-ness/-hood*)
- VERIFICATION — suffixal causative (*verify, validate, authenticate*)
- DISCONFIRMATION — suffixal *falsify* + negative predication (shares construction with VERIFICATION)

**Available with substitution:**
- CAUSATION (factual) — fully available for Latinate/suffixal bases; **SUBSTITUTED BY periphrasis/particle** (*make true*, *true up*) for native core
- REVELATION — **SUBSTITUTED BY [BECOME idiom]** (realization sense lexicalized only in *come true*)

**Partially available:**
- BECOME — restricted, idiomatic only (*come true*, *get real*); general use uncommon
- NOMINALIZATION (agentive) — compounds and suppletion only; **NO COMMON EXPRESSION – circumlocution required** for most evaluative adjectives

**Missing entirely:**
- CAUSATION (character) — **MISSING (structural gap)**; no distinct construction
- REVELATION as an independent operator — **MISSING (structural gap)** (only the BECOME-embedded substitution above exists)
