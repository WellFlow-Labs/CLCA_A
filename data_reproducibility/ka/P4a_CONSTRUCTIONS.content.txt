# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P4a_CONSTRUCTIONS
# Language: Georgian (ka)

---

## 1. ATTRIBUTION

### Pattern
**Copular construction:** NP + **არის** (aris) "is" + ADJECTIVE

The standard attributive construction uses the copula არის (aris) "is" with predicative adjectives. Adjectives agree in number with the subject (singular vs. plural forms).

### Examples
- **ეს მართალია** (es mart'alia) → "this is true/correct"
- **ინფორმაცია სანდოა** (inpormatsia sandoa) → "the information is reliable"

### Notes
Georgian uses predicative forms of adjectives with incorporated copula for present tense (e.g., მართალია mart'alia = მართალი + არის). Past and future tenses use separate copula forms (იყო iq'o "was", იქნება ikneba "will be").

---

## 2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION

### Pattern
**Negated copular:** NP + **არ არის** (ar aris) "is not" + ADJECTIVE

Negation is formed by placing the particle არ (ar) before the copula. The adjective remains in predicative form.

### Examples
- **ეს არ არის მართალი** (es ar aris mart'ali) → "this is not true/correct"
- **ინფორმაცია არ არის სანდო** (inpormatsia ar aris sando) → "the information is not reliable"

### Notes
The negative particle არ (ar) is obligatory and precedes the copula. Alternative negation with არა (ara) is used for constituent negation but not for standard predicate negation.

---

## 3. ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION

### Pattern
**Attributive adjective + Noun:** ADJECTIVE + NOUN

Adjectives precede nouns in attributive position. No agreement marking or special forms required.

### Examples
- **ნამდვილი მეგობარი** (namdvili megobari) → "true friend"
- **სანდო წყარო** (sando ts'q'aro) → "reliable source"

### Notes
Georgian adjectives are invariant in attributive position. The same form is used regardless of case, number, or definiteness of the head noun. Genitive constructions (e.g., სიმართლის ძიება simart'lis dzieba "search for truth") use the genitive case marker -ის (-is) on the noun.

---

## 4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE

### Pattern
**Verb გახდომა (gakhdoma) "become":** NP + **გახდა** (gakhda) + ADJECTIVE-**ად** (-ad)

The verb გახდომა (gakhdoma) "become" combines with adjectives in adverbial form (suffix -ად -ad) to express change of state.

### Examples
- **ეს მართალი გახდა** (es mart'ali gakhda) → "this became true/turned out to be true"
- **ინფორმაცია საეჭვო გახდა** (inpormatsia saech'vo gakhda) → "the information became doubtful"

### Notes
Alternative construction uses the verb ხდება (khdeba) "becomes" with predicative adjectives. The -ად (-ad) adverbial form is optional but common. Some contexts prefer specific verbs (e.g., გამოვლინდა gamovlinda "was revealed" for truth emergence).

---

## 5. CAUSATIVE

### Pattern
**Preverb გა- (ga-) + verb root + causative morphology**

Causative constructions typically use the preverb გა- (ga-) with transitive verbs. For truth-domain concepts, causatives often involve "making known" or "proving."

### Examples
- **გაამართლა** (gamart'la) → "justified, vindicated" (made right/true)
- **დაამტკიცა** (daamt'k'itsa) → "proved, confirmed" (made firm/certain)

### Notes
Direct causatives of truth-adjectives are rare. Instead, Georgian uses specific verbs like დამტკიცება (damt'k'itseba) "proving," გამოვლენა (gamovlena) "revealing," or ჩვენება (ch'veneba) "showing" to express causation in the truth domain.

---

## 6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION

### Pattern
**Prefix სი- (si-) + root + suffix -ე (-e):** OR **Root + suffix -ობა (-oba)** OR **Root + suffix -ება (-eba)**

Georgian has multiple productive nominalization patterns. The სი-...-ე (si-...-e) frame is common for quality nouns, while -ობა (-oba) and -ება (-eba) create abstract nouns from adjectives and verbs.

### Examples
- **სიმართლე** (simart'le) → "truth" (from მართალი mart'ali "true")
- **სანდოობა** (sandooba) → "reliability" (from სანდო sando "reliable")

### Notes
The სი-...-ე pattern is highly productive for native roots (სიზუსტე sizuste "precision," სისწორე sists'ore "correctness"). The -ობა suffix is productive for both native and borrowed adjectives (ობიექტურობა obiek't'uroba "objectivity"). Verbal nouns use -ება (მოტყუება mot'q'ueba "deception").

---

## 7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION

### Pattern
**Prefix მა- (ma-) + root + suffix -არა (-ara):** OR **Root + suffix -ელი (-eli)**

Agent nouns are formed with the prefix მა- (ma-) and suffix -არა (-ara) for habitual agents, or with -ელი (-eli) for general agents.

### Examples
- **მატყუარა** (mat'q'uara) → "liar" (from ტყუილი t'q'uili "lie")
- **დამაჯერებელი** (damajerebeli) → "convincing one" (from ჯერება jereba "believing")

### Notes
The მა-...-არა pattern is productive for habitual or characteristic agents (მატყუარა "liar," მაყალბებელი maq'albebeli "forger"). The -ელი suffix creates agent/instrument nouns from verbs (მთქმელი mt'q'meli "speaker," დამამტკიცებელი damamt'k'itsebeli "prover"). Some agents use participle forms (გამყალბებელი gamq'albebeli "falsifier").

## 8. EVIDENTIAL/EPISTEMIC MARKING

### Pattern
**Particle თურმე (turme) "apparently/reportedly":** OR **Particle ხომ (khom) "you know/surely"**

Georgian uses discourse particles rather than grammatical evidentials to mark epistemic stance. These particles modify entire propositions.

### Examples
- **მართალი თურმე იყო** (mart'ali turme iq'o) → "apparently it was true" (reportative)
- **მართალი ხომ არის** (mart'ali khom aris) → "it is true, you know" (confirmative)

### Notes
თურმე (turme) marks information learned indirectly or surprisingly. ხომ (khom) seeks confirmation or presents information as shared knowledge. These particles are syntactically independent and can combine with any predicate. Additional particles include მეთქი (metki) "allegedly" and ჰოდა (hoda) "so/then" for inference.

---

## 9. DEGREE MODIFICATION

### Pattern
**Intensifier + ADJECTIVE:** OR **Comparative უფრო (upro) "more" + ADJECTIVE**

Degree is expressed through adverbial intensifiers or the comparative particle უფრო (upro). Superlatives use ყველაზე (q'velaze) "most."

### Examples
- **ძალიან მართალი** (dzalian mart'ali) → "very true/correct"
- **უფრო ზუსტი** (upro zusti) → "more precise"

### Notes
Common intensifiers include ძალიან (dzalian) "very," ძლიერ (dzlier) "strongly," სრულიად (sruliad) "completely," and აბსოლუტურად (absolut'urad) "absolutely." The comparative უფრო (upro) is invariant. Superlative ყველაზე (q'velaze) literally means "than all." No morphological comparison exists for adjectives.

---

## 10. CONTRASTIVE/CORRECTIVE CONSTRUCTIONS

### Pattern
**არა X, არამედ Y** (ara X, aramed Y) "not X, but Y"

Contrastive correction uses არა (ara) "not" for the rejected element and არამედ (aramed) "but rather" introducing the correction.

### Examples
- **არა მცდარია, არამედ მართალია** (ara mts'daria, aramed mart'alia) → "it's not false, but true"
- **არა ყალბი, არამედ ნამდვილი** (ara q'albi, aramed namdvili) → "not fake, but genuine"

### Notes
This construction explicitly contrasts two alternatives. The particle არა (ara) negates constituents (not clauses), while არამედ (aramed) introduces the corrected alternative. Alternative construction uses კი არა... არამედ (ki ara... aramed) "not... but rather" for emphasis.

## 11. METALINGUISTIC TRUTH PREDICATES

### Pattern
**Verb თქმა (t'q'ma) "saying" + truth noun in dative**

Metalinguistic constructions about truth-telling use the verb თქმა (t'q'ma) "to say" with truth/lie nouns in dative case.

### Examples
- **სიმართლეს ამბობს** (simart'les ambobs) → "tells the truth" (lit. "says to truth")
- **ტყუილს ამბობს** (t'q'uils ambobs) → "tells lies" (lit. "says to lie")

### Notes
The dative case on the truth/lie noun is obligatory. Alternative verbs include თხრობა (t'khroba) "telling" and ლაპარაკი (lap'arak'i) "speaking." Fixed expressions like სიმართლე რომ ვთქვათ (simart'le rom vt'q'vat) "to tell the truth" use subjunctive mood (რომ rom + subjunctive).

---

## 12. APPEARANCE/SEEMING CONSTRUCTIONS

### Pattern
**Verb ჩანს (ch'ans) "appears/seems":** OR **Adjective მოჩვენებითი (moch'venebiti) "apparent"**

Appearance is expressed through the verb ჩანს (ch'ans) "appears" or the adjective მოჩვენებითი (moch'venebiti) "apparent, seeming."

### Examples
- **მართალი ჩანს** (mart'ali ch'ans) → "it seems true/appears correct"
- **მოჩვენებითი სიმართლე** (moch'venebiti simart'le) → "apparent truth"

### Notes
ჩანს (ch'ans) is an impersonal verb taking dative experiencer (მე მართალი მეჩვენება me mart'ali mech'veneba "it seems true to me"). The adjective მოჩვენებითი explicitly marks something as merely apparent. Alternative expressions use როგორც ჩანს (rogorc ch'ans) "as it appears" or თითქოს (titkos) "as if."

---

## 13. VERIFICATION/CONFIRMATION CONSTRUCTIONS

### Pattern
**Verb დამტკიცება (damt'k'itseba) "proving/confirming":** OR **Verb გადამოწმება (gadamots'meba) "checking/verifying"**

Verification is expressed through specific verbs rather than constructions with truth-adjectives.

### Examples
- **დაამტკიცა სიმართლე** (daamt'k'itsa simart'le) → "proved the truth"
- **გადაამოწმა ინფორმაცია** (gadaamots'ma inpormatsia) → "verified the information"

### Notes
Key verification verbs include დამტკიცება (damt'k'itseba) "proving," გადამოწმება (gadamots'meba) "checking," დადასტურება (dadast'ureba) "confirming," and გამოვლენა (gamovlena) "revealing." These verbs take truth/information nouns as direct objects. No simple construction exists with truth-adjectives alone.

---

## 14. DOUBT/QUESTIONING CONSTRUCTIONS

### Pattern
**Noun ეჭვი (ech'vi) "doubt" + verb ქონა (kona) "have":** OR **Adjective საეჭვო (saech'vo) "doubtful"**

Doubt is expressed through the noun ეჭვი (ech'vi) with possessive constructions, or the adjective საეჭვო (saech'vo).

### Examples
- **ეჭვი მაქვს** (ech'vi makvs) → "I have doubt" (I doubt)
- **საეჭვო ინფორმაციაა** (saech'vo inpormatsiaa) → "it is doubtful information"

### Notes
The construction ეჭვი მაქვს (ech'vi makvs) literally means "doubt exists to me" (dative experiencer). The verb ეჭვი შემეპარა (ech'vi shemep'ara) "doubt seized me" expresses sudden doubt. Questions use particle ხომ (khom) or მართლა (mart'la) "really?" for seeking confirmation.

---

## 15. CONCESSIVE CONSTRUCTIONS

### Pattern
**მართალია, მაგრამ...** (mart'alia, magram...) "it's true, but..."

Concessive constructions acknowledge truth while introducing contrasting information using მაგრამ (magram) "but" or თუმცა (tumtsa) "however."

### Examples
- **მართალია, მაგრამ არასრული** (mart'alia, magram arasruli) → "it's true, but incomplete"
- **სწორია, თუმცა არაზუსტი** (sts'oria, tumtsa arazusti) → "it's correct, however imprecise"

### Notes
This construction explicitly concedes a point before contrasting. The truth-adjective appears in predicative form with incorporated copula. Alternative concessive markers include თუმცა (tumtsa) "however," მაინც (maints) "still," and მიუხედავად ამისა (miukhedavad amisa) "despite this."
