Below is a derivational inventory anchored in the P1 base forms only. I include only forms already strongly supported by the P1b material or clearly attested in standard Hebrew usage. Where no common derivation is identified from a given P1 item itself, I state that explicitly.

### Base Form: **אמת**
**Derived Form:** **באמת**  
**Transliteration:** *beʾemet*  
**Literal Breakdown:** ב־ “in / really” + אמת  
**Natural English translation:** really, truly, in truth  
**Usage description:** Very common adverb in speech and writing. It marks sincerity, emphasis, surprise, or actual truth.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### Base Form: **אמת**
**Derived Form:** **האמת**  
**Transliteration:** *ha-ʾemet*  
**Literal Breakdown:** definite form of אמת  
**Natural English translation:** the truth  
**Usage description:** Used referentially and also as a discourse marker, especially in colloquial speech. A common pattern is *האמת ש־* “actually / the truth is that...”.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### Base Form: **אמת**
**Derived Form:** **האמת היא ש־**  
**Transliteration:** *ha-ʾemet hi she-*  
**Literal Breakdown:** “the truth is that—”  
**Natural English translation:** the truth is that; actually  
**Usage description:** Fixed phrase introducing what the speaker presents as the real situation. Common in conversation, interviews, and argumentative prose.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### Base Form: **אמת**
**Derived Form:** **למען האמת**  
**Transliteration:** *lemaʿan ha-ʾemet*  
**Literal Breakdown:** “for the sake of the truth”  
**Natural English translation:** to tell the truth; honestly  
**Usage description:** Common discourse marker for candid admission or correction. Used in both speech and writing.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### Base Form: **אמת**
**Derived Form:** **אמת לאמיתה**  
**Transliteration:** *ʾemet la-ʾamitah*  
**Literal Breakdown:** “truth to its truth”  
**Natural English translation:** the full truth; truth in the deepest sense  
**Usage description:** Literary and rabbinic expression emphasizing complete or essential truth rather than partial correctness. Not common in casual speech.  
**Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]
