# G6 — Cross-Language Opposition and Asymmetry Comparison

---

## 1. Per-Language Extraction of Oppositional Structures

### zh (Chinese)

**Lexical oppositions:**
- 真 / 假 — true/genuine vs. false/fake; highly productive in compounds (真品/假货, 真丝/假丝) and fixed expressions
- 对 / 错 — correct vs. wrong; primary pair for answers and judgments
- 实 / 虚 — solid/real/substantial vs. empty/void/false; philosophical and strategic contexts
- 真实 / 虚假 — authentic vs. fraudulent; formal and legal register
- 诚实 / 欺骗 — honest vs. deceive; moral evaluation
- 事实 / 谎言 — fact vs. lie; objective vs. deliberately false statement

**Morphological oppositions:**
- 正常 / 不正常 — normal vs. abnormal (不 negation)
- 真实 / 不真实 — authentic vs. inauthentic (不 negation)
- 正 / 不正 — correct/upright vs. improper (不 negation)
- 名副其实 / 名不副实 — worthy of the name vs. unworthy (不 inserted into fixed expression)
- 言行一致 / 言行不一 — words match deeds vs. words don't match deeds (不 inserted)

**Constructional oppositions:**
- 说实话 / 说谎 — tell the truth vs. tell lies (parallel verb-object)
- 符合事实 / 不符合事实 — conform to facts vs. not conform (negation of construction)
- 揭露真相 / 隐瞒真相 — reveal the truth vs. conceal the truth (antonymous verbs, same object)
- 是真的 / 不是真的 — it is true vs. it is not true (copular negation)
- 对不对? — right or not? (A-not-A interrogative embedding both poles)
- 是不是真的? — is it true or not? (A-not-A interrogative)
- 弄假成真 — fake becomes real (internal transformation, single expression)
- 以假乱真 — fake confuses real (internal opposition, single expression)
- 真假难辨 — difficult to distinguish true from false (compound subject embedding both poles)
- 半真半假 — half true half false (parallel structure, mixed)
- 虚虚实实 — mix of feint and reality (AABB reduplication embedding both poles)

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Primary truth/falsity** | zh | 真 (zhēn) | 假 (jiǎ) | Lexical | Highly productive in compounds and fixed expressions; both poles equally productive |
| **Primary truth/falsity** | he | אמת (emet) | שקר (sheker) | Lexical | Primary pair; שקר generates fuller derivational family (שקרן, שקרנות, לשקר) |
| **Primary truth/falsity** | sa | satya | asatya | Morphological (a- privative) | asatya is lexicalized antonym, not merely negation; both poles generate compounds |
| **Primary truth/falsity** | sa | satya | mithyā | Lexical | mithyā is the philosophical antonym; covers illusoriness as well as falsity |
| **Primary truth/falsity** | el | αλήθεια | ψέμα | Lexical | Both poles productive; ψέμα generates ψεύτης, ψεύδομαι, ψευδ- compounds |
| **Primary truth/falsity** | en | true | false | Lexical | Both poles productive; false generates falsify, falsification, falsifiable chain |
| **Primary truth/falsity** | tl | totoo | kasinungalingan | Lexical | Asymmetric: totoo is simple root; kasinungalingan is derived abstract noun |

- he: הדבר הלך והתברר [CORE], האמת התגלתה בהדרגה [CORE], הדבר נעשה ברור יותר ויותר [CORE]
- el: βγαίνει σιγά σιγά η αλήθεια [CORE], αποκαλύπτεται σταδιακά η αλήθεια [CORE]
- sw: Habari zinakuwa na ukweli zaidi [MARGINAL], Taarifa zake zinaongezeka kwa usahihi [MARGINAL]

Gradual change is [CORE] in zh, he, and el; [MARGINAL] in sw. The pattern is natural in the languages with richer inchoative vocabulary.
