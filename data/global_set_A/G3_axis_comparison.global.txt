# G3 — Emergent Cross-Language Semantic Dimensions

## Methodology

This analysis extracts semantic dimensions from F3 semantic grouping tables and supporting F1-F2 evidence across eight languages. Each dimension represents a recurring pattern of lexical or semantic organization visible in multiple languages. Dimensions are defined strictly from observed patterns in the data.

## Language-Specific Patterns

### Major Splits (Dimensions Maintained as Lexically Distinct)

**Finnish**: Clearest separation across multiple dimensions:
- DIM-1 (truth-value: tosi/totuus) distinct from
- DIM-2 (authenticity: aito) distinct from
- DIM-3 (honesty: rehellinen) distinct from
- DIM-4 (correctness: oikea) distinct from
- DIM-9 (deliberate lie: valhe vs. unintentional error: virhe)

**Korean**: Extreme register-based splitting within DIM-1:
- 진실 (jinsil): formal/moral truth
- 사실 (sasil): objective/factual truth
- 진짜 (jinjja): colloquial/authenticity truth

## Conclusion

The analysis reveals **12 strongly supported dimensions** appearing in 6-8 languages with clear F3 documentation, **8 moderately supported dimensions** appearing in 3-5 languages, and **3 weakly supported dimensions** appearing in 1-2 languages or primarily through constructional means.

**Core universal structure**: All languages distinguish:
- Statement correspondence (DIM-1)
- Material authenticity (DIM-2)
- Character honesty (DIM-3)
- Evidential establishment (DIM-14)
- Truth visibility/revelation (DIM-15)

These five dimensions form the cross-linguistic core of truth-related semantic organization, documented in all F3 tables.

**Variable elaboration**: Languages differ significantly in:
- Register-based truth term differentiation (DIM-11): extensive in Korean/Tamil/Turkish, minimal in Yoruba/Basque
- Procedural correctness integration (DIM-4): merged with truth in Georgian/Yoruba/Turkish, separate in Finnish/Korean
- Realization vocabulary (DIM-18): elaborated in Finnish/Turkish/Korean, less developed in others
- Practice/theory distinction (DIM-19): highly elaborated in Korean only

**Systematic polysemy**: The spatial-epistemic polysemy (DIM-17) appears in 6/8 languages, suggesting widespread metaphorical mapping, but is not universal (absent in Indonesian and Korean, which maintain separate spatial and epistemic vocabulary).
