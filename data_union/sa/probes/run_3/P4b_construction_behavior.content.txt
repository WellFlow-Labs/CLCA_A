Attested in *Taittirīya Upaniṣad* 1.11.1 and *Manusmṛti* 4.138 as formulaic. | Optative construction (weaker normative force, same domain) |

## Summary Notes

1. All nine construction types identified in P4a are rated [CORE] with the exception of the denominative causative *satyāpayati* (§5, [MARGINAL]) and the *-jña* agentive nominalization (§7, [MARGINAL]), both of which show observable domain or register restrictions relative to the other attested patterns.
2. No construction has been rated [UNCERTAIN]; all examples are drawn from attested sources as reported in P4a, and usage notes reflect only patterns stated or implied there.
3. Alternate constructions listed are restricted to those already attested within the P4a data; no additional alternates have been generated.
