# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P1 CORE VOCABULARY COLLECTION
# Language: Yoruba (yo)

## Lexical Items

### 1. òtítọ́
**Form:** òtítọ́  
**Literal meaning:** Noun derived from *tọ́* (to be straight, correct)  
**Typical English translations:** truth, truthfulness, honesty, correctness

Used to refer to statements or representations that correspond to reality, or to characterize persons who habitually speak accurately. Also used in moral contexts to describe integrity and honesty in conduct.

**[CORE]**

---

### 2. irọ́
**Form:** irọ́  
**Literal meaning:** Noun referring to falsehood  
**Typical English translations:** lie, falsehood, untruth

Refers to statements known to be false or to deliberate misrepresentation. Commonly used in everyday discourse to identify false claims or deceptive speech.

**[CORE]**

---

### 3. tọ́
**Form:** tọ́  
**Literal meaning:** Verb root meaning "to be straight, correct, true"  
**Typical English translations:** to be true, to be correct, to be right

Used to affirm the accuracy of statements or the correctness of actions. Can be used predicatively to evaluate whether something corresponds to fact or proper standards.

**[CORE]**

---

### 4. purọ́
**Form:** purọ́  
**Literal meaning:** Verb meaning "to tell lies"  
**Typical English translations:** to lie, to tell falsehoods

Describes the act of deliberately making false statements. Commonly used to accuse or describe someone engaged in deception.

**[CORE]**

---

### 5. òdodo
**Form:** òdodo  
**Literal meaning:** Noun related to straightness, uprightness  
**Typical English translations:** righteousness, justice, uprightness, correctness

Used in moral and legal contexts to describe conduct that conforms to proper standards. Also refers to fairness in judgment and ethical behavior.

**[CORE]**

---

### 6. ẹ̀tàn
**Form:** ẹ̀tàn  
**Literal meaning:** Noun meaning "deception, fraud"  
**Typical English translations:** deception, fraud, trickery, deceit

Refers to deliberate acts of misleading others, often for personal gain. Used in contexts involving dishonest schemes or manipulative behavior.

**[CORE]**

---

### 7. gbàgbọ́
**Form:** gbàgbọ́  
**Literal meaning:** Verb meaning "to believe, to trust"  
**Typical English translations:** to believe, to trust, to have faith in

Used to express acceptance of statements as true or confidence in the reliability of persons or things. Central to epistemic evaluation in everyday discourse.

**[CORE]**

---

### 8. jẹ́
**Form:** jẹ́  
**Literal meaning:** Verb meaning "to be, to exist, to affirm"  
**Typical English translations:** to be, to affirm, to confirm as true

Used to affirm existence or to confirm the truth of statements. Often appears in constructions verifying or validating claims.

**[CORE]**

### 9. dájú
**Form:** dájú  
**Literal meaning:** Verb meaning "to be certain, sure"  
**Typical English translations:** to be certain, to be sure, to be definite

Expresses epistemic certainty about statements or situations. Used when the speaker has confidence in the accuracy or reliability of information.

**[CORE]**

### 10. ṣe déédé
**Form:** ṣe déédé  
**Literal meaning:** "to do correctly/properly" (*ṣe* = do, *déédé* = correctly)  
**Typical English translations:** to do correctly, to do properly, to be accurate

Fixed expression used to describe actions performed according to proper standards or statements made with accuracy. Emphasizes conformity to correct procedures or facts.
