## 1. ATTRIBUTION

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Predicate-initial verbless clause: truth-adjective + ang-phrase / pronoun | *Totoo ang balita.* | Totoo ang balita. | "The news is true." | [CORE] | The unmarked, default declarative structure for truth-predication across all registers from casual speech to formal writing. No restriction on which truth-domain adjective occupies the predicate slot; the construction is fully productive across the truth-domain set. |
| Predicate-initial verbless clause: truth-adjective + ang-phrase / pronoun | *Tama ang sagot mo.* | Tama ang sagot mo. | "Your answer is correct." | [CORE] | Equally natural in spoken and written registers; *tama* is among the highest-frequency items in this slot. Equally natural with *mali*, *tunay*, *tapat*, *huwad*, *kredible*. |
| Predicate-initial verbless clause: truth-adjective + ang-phrase / pronoun | *Tama ang sagot niya.* | Tama ang sagot niya. | "His/her answer is correct." | [CORE] | Equally unmarked; used in evaluative contexts from casual correction to formal assessment. |
| Predicate-initial verbless clause: truth-adjective + ang-phrase / pronoun | *Totoo iyan.* | Totoo iyan | "That is true." | [CORE] | The unmarked structure for truth-predication across all registers; no restrictions on formality or context are attested. |
| Predicate-initial verbless clause: truth-adjective + ang-phrase / pronoun | *Mali ang sagot.* | Mali ang sagot | "The answer is wrong / incorrect." | [CORE] | Equally unmarked; used in evaluative contexts from casual correction to formal assessment. |

**Attested alternates:**
- Topic-comment inversion with *ay*: *Ang balita ay totoo.* ("The news is true.") — grammatical but more formal or written in register; the predicate-initial order is the unmarked form.

**Attested alternates:**
- The hedging nuance of *hindi mali* is noted as a pragmatic tendency, not a distinct construction.
- The two patterns (*hindi* + adjective vs. *wala* + nominal) are not alternates of each other; they occupy different structural slots.

Postnominal order is grammatical but less frequent in attested usage. |
| Truth-adjective + *na* (linker, consonant-final modifier) + noun | *Huwad na dokumento* | Huwad na dokumento | "A forged document" | [CORE] | *Huwad* and *peke* in this slot are strongly associated with physical objects (documents, currency, goods); their use with abstract nouns is less attested. |

| Truth-adjective + *na* (linker, consonant-final modifier) + noun | *maling impormasyon* | maling impormasyon | "wrong information / misinformation" | [CORE] | Equally productive; attested in both everyday and formal/journalistic contexts. |
| Truth-adjective + *na* (linker, consonant-final modifier) + noun | *Tunay na ginto* | Tunay na ginto | "Genuine gold" | [CORE] | Fully productive modifier-head order with obligatory linker; attested across all truth-domain adjectives (*tapat na tao*, *huwad na dokumento*, *wastong paraan*, *kredible na saksi*). |

**Attested alternates:**
- P4a notes additional attested instances following the same pattern: *tunay na kaibigan*, *huwad na dokumento*, *kredibleng saksi*, *totoong kwento*. These are not separate constructions but confirm the productivity of the single pattern.
- Head-modifier order (noun preceding adjective) is not documented for this construction type in the data.

## 5. CAUSATIVE

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| *Pa-* + *tunay* + *-an* verbal paradigm | *Patunayan mo ang iyong sinasabi.* | Patunayan mo ang iyong sinasabi. | "Prove what you are saying." | [CORE] | *Patunayan* is a high-frequency lexical item in argumentative, legal, and journalistic discourse; the causative-verificational meaning is fully conventionalized. Productive verbal paradigm (*patunayan*, *pinatunayan*, *pagpapatunay*) attested across formal and informal registers; the most grammaticalized causative in the truth domain. |
| *Pa-* + *tunay* + *-an* verbal paradigm | *Patunayan mo ang iyong sinabi.* | Patunayan mo ang iyong sinabi. | "Prove what you said." | [CORE] | Same paradigm; attested across formal and informal registers. |
| *i-* + correctness adjective | *Itama ang iyong mga pagkakamali.* | Itama ang iyong mga pagkakamali. | "Correct your mistakes." | [CORE] | *Itama* and *iwasto* are attested in instructional, editorial, and corrective contexts. These are lexically specific forms; the pattern does not extend productively to all P1 roots. Productive for correctness adjectives (*tama*, *wasto*, *akma*); used across registers in instructional and corrective contexts. |
| *i-* + correctness adjective | *Itama ang pagkakamali.* | Itama ang pagkakamali | "Correct the mistake." | [CORE] | Productive for correctness adjectives (*tama*, *wasto*, *akma*); used across registers in instructional and corrective contexts. |
| *i-* + correctness adjective | *Iwasto ang mali.* | Iwasto ang mali | "Set the wrong right. / Correct the error." | [CORE] | Same pattern; no register restrictions attested for this sub-type. |
| *i-* + correctness adjective | *Iniwasto na ang pagkakamali.* | Iniwasto na ang pagkakamali. | "The mistake has already been corrected." | [CORE] | Standard causative-corrective construction for *wasto*; natural in contexts of error correction. Structurally distinct from the *pa-…-an* paradigm. |

**Attested alternates:**
- The *pa-…-an* causative is reported as a separate morphological pattern from the *i-* construction, not an alternate of it.
- The *i-* causative is explicitly noted as **not attested** for truth-predicate adjectives (*totoo*, *tunay*).
- No productive causative verbal paradigm is attested in P4a data for *totoo* or *tapat* in the truth-evaluation domain. This asymmetry is noted in P4a and is preserved here without inference.

## 6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Circumfix *ka-*…*-an* / *-han* + root (native roots) | *Ang katotohanan ay mahalaga.* | Ang katotohanan ay mahalaga. | "Truth is important." | [CORE] | *Ka-...-an* nominalizations (*katotohanan*, *katapatan*, *katunayan*, *kawastuhan*) are fully lexicalized and attested in formal, philosophical, and everyday registers. The pattern is productive across the P1 inventory. Primary mechanism for abstract noun formation in this field; highly consistent across both indigenous roots and integrated borrowings. |
| Circumfix *ka-*…*-an* / *-han* + root (native roots) | *Ang katotohanan ay mapait.* | Ang katotohanan ay mapait. | "The truth is bitter." | [CORE] | Primary nominalizing strategy for native truth-domain roots; fully productive across *katotohanan*, *katunayan*, *katapatan*, *kawastuhan*, *kadalisayan*. Common in all registers. |
| Circumfix *ka-*…*-an* / *-han* + root (native roots) | *kasinungalingan* | kasinungalingan | "lie / falsehood / untruth" | [CORE] | Same pattern; no restrictions on use attested. |
| *Pag-* action nominalization (verbal stem) | *Ang pagtapat niya ay nagulat sa lahat.* | Ang pagtapat niya ay nagulat sa lahat. | "His/her honest disclosure surprised everyone." | [CORE] | *Pag-* action nominalizations (*pagtapat*, *pagtotoo*, *pagpapatunay*) are attested in narrative and reportorial contexts; they foreground the act rather than the abstract property. Distinct in meaning from *ka-…-an* forms. |
| *Pag-* + reduplication (process nominal) | *(attested in P4a notes: pagtatapat, pagpapatunay, paghahanap ng katotohanan)* | pagtatapat / pagpapatunay / paghahanap ng katotohanan | "act of confessing / act of verifying / truth-seeking" | [CORE] | Yields process nominals rather than state nominals; distinct in meaning from *ka-…-an* forms. Attested across formal and informal contexts. |
| *-idad* suffix (Spanish-borrowed adjectives) | *Ang kredibilidad ng saksi ay pinag-aralan ng hukuman.* | Ang kredibilidad ng saksi ay pinag-aralan ng hukuman. | "The credibility of the witness was examined by the court." | [CORE] | Standard nominalization for Spanish-origin adjectives; most frequent in formal, legal, journalistic, and academic registers. |

**Attested alternates:**
- P4a attests a second nominalization pattern, **pag- + [verbal stem]**, producing action nominals: *pagpapatunay* ("the act of verifying/proving"), *pagsisinungaling* ("the act of lying"), *pagwawasto* ("the act of correcting"), *pagkakamali* ("a specific instance of error"). This is reported as a distinct pattern with a different semantic output (action / event nominal vs. abstract state/quality nominal), not a direct alternate of the *ka-…-an* circumfix.
- The *ka-…-an* and *-idad* strategies are not interchangeable; they apply to distinct lexical strata (native vs. Spanish-borrowed). No overlap is documented in P4a data.

## 7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Root form used directly as agentive nominal / predicate | *Sinungaling ka!* | Sinungaling ka! | "You are a liar!" | [CORE] | *Sinungaling* is a fully lexicalized root used freely in accusatory and colloquial contexts; it is not transparently derived from a P1 truth-domain adjective. No productive agentive morpheme is attested for this domain. Functions directly as predicate or nominal without additional affixation; natural across all registers including casual accusation and formal characterization. |
| Root form used directly as agentive nominal / predicate | *Sinungaling ka.* | Sinungaling ka | "You are a liar." | [CORE] | *Sinungaling* is lexically supplied rather than productively derived; used freely across registers as both noun and adjective. |
| Root form used directly as agentive nominal / predicate | *Huwag kang maging sinungaling.* | Huwag kang maging sinungaling | "Don't be a liar." | [CORE] | Same item in nominal predicate position; no restrictions attested. |
| *Mang-* / *man-* + root (habitual agentive) | *(attested in P4a notes: manlinlang)* | manlinlang | "one who deceives habitually" | [MARGINAL] | Attested for deception (*manlinlang*); no parallel habitual agentive for truth-telling is documented in P4a data. Restricted to the deception sub-domain within the truth cluster. |
| *Tagapag-* + verbal base (institutional / role agentive) | *Ang mamamahayag ay tagapagtanggol ng katotohanan.* | Ang mamamahayag ay tagapagtanggol ng katotohanan. | "The journalist is a defender of truth." | [MARGINAL] | *Tagapagtanggol ng katotohanan* is a compound construction rather than a simple derivation; it is attested in formal and rhetorical registers but is not a routine everyday expression. Yields formal institutional or role-based agentives; more common in formal, legal, or organizational discourse than in casual speech. |

**Attested alternates:**
- P4a notes that no parallel single-word agentive for "truth-teller" is attested. The concept is expressed periphrastically: *ang nagsasabi ng katotohanan* ("one who tells the truth"). The *taga-* prefix is noted as capable of forming agentives (*tagapagtanggol ng katotohanan*) but these are not reported as lexicalized stable single-word items in this field.
- For the concept "honest person," no dedicated agentive affix construction is attested in P4a data; the adnominal construction *tapat na tao* serves this function instead. This is a documented asymmetry and is not inferred beyond what P4a reports.
- No productive agentive morpheme is attested for the truth-domain as a whole.

| *Sa* + truth-nominal as sentence-initial frame | *Sa katunayan, mas mataas ang bilang.* | Sa katunayan, mas mataas ang bilang | "In fact, the count is higher." | [CORE] | Used to introduce factual correction or emphasis; functions analogously to English "in fact." |

**Attested alternates:**
- *Sa katunayan* ("in fact") is documented in P4a as near-synonymous with *sa katotohanan* in this discourse-marker function.
- Discourse-framing constructions are asymmetric: they are attested only for positive truth-domain items (*totoo*, *katunayan*), not for *mali*, *huwad*, or *peke*. This asymmetry is reported as observed, not explained.
- The *Ang totoo...* pattern is reported as a distinct surface form within the same pragmatic function (correction, emphasis, concession) as the *Sa* + nominal pattern; its pragmatic force is reported as similarly non-compositional.

## Cross-Construction Observations

- The [MARGINAL] ratings cluster around two conditions: (a) constructions that require a human subject entering a state (*maging* + object-property adjectives *huwad*, *peke*) and (b) agentive nominalizations, where no productive morphological pattern exists and attested forms are either lexicalized (*sinungaling*) or compound (*tagapagtanggol ng katotohanan*) or restricted in distribution (*manlinlang*, *nagkatotoo*).
- Discourse-framing constructions are asymmetric: they are attested only for positive truth-domain items (*totoo*, *katunayan*), not for *mali*, *huwad*, or *peke*. This asymmetry is reported as observed, not explained.
- No [UNCERTAIN] ratings were assigned; all constructions are supported by attested examples with sufficient contextual information to assign a naturalness rating.
- The *i-* causative is explicitly noted as not attested for truth-predicate adjectives (*totoo*, *tunay*); no productive causative verbal paradigm
