# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P1b ROOT EXPANSION
# Language: Quechua (qu)

## Step 1 — Identify Central Roots

### Root 1: **chiq-** (truth/correctness root)
**Form:** chiq-  
**Participates in:** 5 P1 items (chiqaq, cheqaq, chiqan, chiqamanta, and related to chiqay)  
**Core meaning contribution:** Truth, correctness, genuineness, accuracy; the fundamental correspondence between representation and reality

### Root 2: **allin** (goodness/rightness root)
**Form:** allin  
**Participates in:** 2 P1 items (allin, mana allin)  
**Core meaning contribution:** Goodness, rightness, propriety; positive evaluation across moral, practical, and aesthetic domains

### Root 3: **panta-** (error/mistake root)
**Form:** panta-  
**Participates in:** 2 P1 items (pantay, pantasqa)  
**Core meaning contribution:** Error, mistake, deviation from correctness; going astray from truth or proper course

## Step 2 — Expand Each Root

### ROOT 1: **chiq-** (truth/correctness)

#### 1. chiqay
**Form:** chiqay  
**Literal breakdown:** chiq-a-y (truth-VBLZ-INF)  
**Translation:** to be true, to be real  
**Context:** Verbal form used to assert or verify the truth of something. Used in statements like "kayqa chiqanmi" (this is true). Common in everyday verification and validation contexts.  
**[CORE]**

#### 2. chiqalla
**Form:** chiqalla  
**Literal breakdown:** chiq-a-lla (truth-VBLZ-LIM)  
**Translation:** only the truth, just true, merely true  
**Context:** Used with the limitative suffix to emphasize that something is purely or simply true, without exaggeration or addition. Appears in contexts where truthfulness alone is being emphasized.  
**[CORE]**

#### 3. chiqap
**Form:** chiqap  
**Literal breakdown:** chiq-a-p (truth-VBLZ-GEN)  
**Translation:** of truth, truth's  
**Context:** Genitive form used in possessive constructions, such as "chiqap siminwan" (with the word of truth). Common in formal and religious discourse.  
**[CORE]**

#### 4. chiqapuni
**Form:** chiqapuni  
**Literal breakdown:** chiq-a-pu-ni (truth-VBLZ-BEN-EMPH)  
**Translation:** truly indeed, really and truly  
**Context:** Emphatic form used to strongly affirm truth. The -puni suffix adds certainty and emphasis. Used when speaker wants to stress absolute truthfulness.  
**[CORE]**

#### 5. chiqapta
**Form:** chiqapta  
**Literal breakdown:** chiq-a-p-ta (truth-VBLZ-GEN-ACC)  
**Translation:** the truth (as object), truthfully  
**Context:** Accusative form used when truth is the direct object, or adverbially to mean "truthfully." Common in phrases like "chiqapta rimay" (speak truthfully).  
**[CORE]**

#### 6. chiqaqlla
**Form:** chiqaqlla  
**Literal breakdown:** chiq-aq-lla (truth-AG-LIM)  
**Translation:** only true, just the true one  
**Context:** Combines the agentive/adjectival -q with limitative -lla. Used to indicate something is exclusively or purely true, distinguishing it from false alternatives.  
**[CORE]**

#### 7. chiqaqnin
**Form:** chiqaqnin  
**Literal breakdown:** chiq-aq-ni-n (truth-AG-POSS-3)  
**Translation:** its truth, the true one of it  
**Context:** Possessive form indicating the true version or true aspect of something. Used when distinguishing authentic from inauthentic versions.  
**[CORE]**

#### 8. chiqaqninchik
**Form:** chiqaqninchik  
**Literal breakdown:** chiq-aq-ni-nchik (truth-AG-POSS-1PL.INCL)  
**Translation:** our truth, what is true for us  
**Context:** First person plural inclusive possessive. Used in communal contexts to refer to shared truths or collective authentic traditions.  
**[CORE]**

#### 9. chiqaqta
**Form:** chiqaqta  
**Literal breakdown:** chiq-aq-ta (truth-AG-ACC)  
**Translation:** the true one (as object), truly  
**Context:** Accusative of the adjectival form. Used as direct object or adverbially. Common in phrases meaning "to do truly" or "to seek the true one."  
**[CORE]**

#### 10. chiqaqman
**Form:** chiqaqman  
**Literal breakdown:** chiq-aq-man (truth-AG-ALL)  
**Translation:** toward the true, to the truth  
**Context:** Allative case indicating direction or goal. Used metaphorically for moving toward truth or striving for authenticity.  
**[CORE]**

#### 11. chiqaqmanta
**Form:** chiqaqmanta  
**Literal breakdown:** chiq-aq-manta (truth-AG-ABL)  
**Translation:** from the true, from truth  
**Context:** Ablative indicating source or origin. Used to indicate something derives from truth or is based on genuine foundations. Variant of chiqamanta in P1.  
**[CORE]**

#### 12. chiqaqpi
**Form:** chiqaqpi  
**Literal breakdown:** chiq-aq-pi (truth-AG-LOC)  
**Translation:** in the true, in truth  
**Context:** Locative case. Used to indicate location within truth or truthful context. Appears in phrases meaning "in reality" or "in the true situation."  
**[CORE]**

#### 13. chiqaqpaq
**Form:** chiqaqpaq  
**Literal breakdown:** chiq-aq-paq (truth-AG-BEN)  
**Translation:** for the true, for truth's sake  
**Context:** Benefactive case indicating purpose or benefit. Used when actions are done for the sake of truth or to serve truthfulness.  
**[CORE]**

#### 14. chiqaqwan
**Form:** chiqaqwan  
**Literal breakdown:** chiq-aq-wan (truth-AG-INST)  
**Translation:** with truth, by means of truth  
**Context:** Instrumental case. Used to indicate truth as the means or instrument. Common in phrases about acting with truthfulness or using truth as a tool.  
**[CORE]**

#### 15. chiqaqyay
**Form:** chiqaqyay  
**Literal breakdown:** chiq-aq-ya-y (truth-AG-INCH-INF)  
**Translation:** to become true, to be verified  
**Context:** Inchoative verb indicating the process of becoming true or being verified. Used when something is in the process of being confirmed or proven true.  
**[CORE]**

#### 16. chiqaqchay
**Form:** chiqaqchay  
**Literal breakdown:** chiq-aq-cha-y (truth-AG-DIM-INF)  
**Translation:** to make somewhat true, to verify partially  
**Context:** Diminutive verbal form suggesting partial or approximate truth-making. Used in contexts of partial verification or making something more truthful.  
**[MARGINAL]**

#### 17. chiqaqkuna
**Form:** chiqaqkuna  
**Literal breakdown:** chiq-aq-kuna (truth-AG-PL)  
**Translation:** true ones, truths  
**Context:** Plural form referring to multiple true things or truths. Used when discussing various truths or multiple authentic items.  
**[CORE]**

#### 18. chiqaqkunamanta
**Form:** chiqaqkunamanta  
**Literal breakdown:** chiq-aq-kuna-manta (truth-AG-PL-ABL)  
**Translation:** from the truths, from true things  
**Context:** Ablative plural. Used when discussing derivation from multiple truths or sources of truth.  
**[CORE]**

#### 19. chiqanchay
**Form:** chiqanchay  
**Literal breakdown:** chiq-a-ncha-y (truth-VBLZ-CAUS-INF)  
**Translation:** to make true, to verify, to authenticate  
**Context:** Causative form meaning to cause something to be true or to establish truth. Used in contexts of verification, authentication, and validation.  
**[CORE]**

#### 20. chiqanchakuy
**Form:** chiqanchakuy  
**Literal breakdown:** chiq-a-ncha-ku-y (truth-VBLZ-CAUS-REFL-INF)  
**Translation:** to verify for oneself, to authenticate oneself  
**Context:** Reflexive causative. Used when someone verifies something for their own benefit or authenticates their own claims.  
**[CORE]**

#### 21. chiqanchana
**Form:** chiqanchana  
**Literal breakdown:** chiq-a-ncha-na (truth-VBLZ-CAUS-NMLZ)  
**Translation:** means of verification, authenticator  
**Context:** Nominalizer indicating instrument or means. Refers to tools, methods, or persons used for verification or authentication.  
**[CORE]**

#### 22. chiqanchaq
**Form:** chiqanchaq  
**Literal breakdown:** chiq-a-ncha-q (truth-VBLZ-CAUS-AG)  
**Translation:** verifier, one who authenticates  
**Context:** Agentive form referring to a person who verifies or authenticates. Used for officials, witnesses, or anyone who establishes truth.  
**[CORE]**

#### 23. chiqanchapakuy
**Form:** chiqanchapakuy  
**Literal breakdown:** chiq-a-ncha-pa-ku-y (truth-VBLZ-CAUS-BEN-REFL-INF)  
**Translation:** to have verified for oneself, to get authenticated  
**Context:** Benefactive reflexive causative. Used when someone has something verified or authenticated for their own benefit.  
**[CORE]**

#### 24. chiqaqsapa
**Form:** chiqaqsapa  
**Literal breakdown:** chiq-aq-sapa (truth-AG-ABUND)  
**Translation:** full of truth, very truthful  
**Context:** Abundance suffix indicating something or someone characterized by much truth. Used to describe truthful persons or situations rich in authenticity.  
**[CORE]**

#### 25. chiqaqniyuq
**Form:** chiqaqniyuq  
**Literal breakdown:** chiq-aq-ni-yuq (truth-AG-POSS-POSSESSOR)  
**Translation:** having truth, possessing authenticity  
**Context:** Possessor suffix indicating something that has or possesses truth. Used for claims, documents, or persons that have genuine truth.  
**[CORE]**

#### 26. chiqaqninnaq
**Form:** chiqaqninnaq  
**Literal breakdown:** chiq-aq-ni-nnaq (truth-AG-POSS-STILL)  
**Translation:** still its truth, still the true one  
**Context:** Continuative suffix indicating something remains true or authentic despite circumstances. Used when affirming continued truthfulness.  
**[MARGINAL]**

### ROOT 2: **allin** (goodness/rightness)

**Form:** allinlla

#### 2. allinpuni
**Form:** allinpuni

## Step 3 — Peripheral Extensions

### ROOT 1: **chiq-** (truth/correctness)

**Peripheral extensions noted:**

1. **chiqaqsapa** — The abundance suffix construction appears to extend beyond simple truth-evaluation into character assessment, describing persons as "truthful" as a moral quality rather than just accurate in statements.

2. **chiqanchay** and related causatives — These forms shift from describing truth-states to active processes of verification and authentication, entering administrative and legal procedural domains.

3. **chiqaqman** (allative) — The directional use suggests metaphorical extension where truth becomes a goal or destination, moving beyond simple correspondence evaluation into aspirational or teleological contexts.

4. **chiqaqninchik** (1pl.incl possessive) — The communal possessive form suggests extension into social/collective domains where "our truth" may involve shared cultural authenticity rather than propositional truth.

### ROOT 2: **allin** (goodness/rightness)

**Peripheral extensions noted:**

1. **allinpi** in health contexts — While "good" is evaluative, the specific use for health states ("allinpi kashani" = I am well/healthy) extends into physical/medical domains beyond moral or practical evaluation.

2. **allinyachiy** — The causative forms extend into technical repair and medical healing contexts, where "making good" involves physical restoration rather than moral or evaluative judgment.

3. **allinchakuy** — This reflexive diminutive has specialized into personal grooming/presentation contexts, a specific domain extension from general goodness.

4. **allinsapa** — The abundance form appears to function as an intensifier for quality assessment that may extend beyond moral goodness into aesthetic or technical excellence.

5. **allinyay** — The inchoative form's use in healing contexts suggests extension from evaluative states into physical/medical process domains.

### ROOT 3: **panta-** (error/mistake)

**Peripheral extensions noted:**

1. **pantachiy** causative series — These forms extend from simple error-description into active deception and misleading, entering moral culpability domains beyond mere mistake.

2. **pantarqo** — The sudden action suffix suggests extension into aspectual/temporal marking where the root contributes error-meaning but the construction emphasizes manner of action.

3. **pantasapa** — The abundance form may extend into character evaluation (error-prone person) beyond simple mistake-counting.

4. **pantakuy** — The reflexive form appears to add nuances of personal responsibility or self-affectedness that extend beyond simple error-description into agency and accountability domains.
