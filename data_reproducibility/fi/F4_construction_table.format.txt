| ATTRIBUTION | Se on totta. | se on totta | That is true. | Highly frequent in everyday and formal discourse. Partitive *totta* obligatory with abstract noun; nominative adjectives (*tosi*, *väärä*) equally common. No register restrictions. | [CORE] |
| ATTRIBUTION | Väite on väärä. | väite on väärä | The claim is false. | Standard pattern for evaluating statements. Adjective form preferred in formal/written contexts. | [CORE] |
| NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION | Se ei ole totta. | se ei ole totta | That is not true. | Standard negation pattern, fully productive. Partitive case automatic in negation. Used across all registers. | [CORE] |
| NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION | Väite ei pidä paikkaansa. | väite ei pidä paikkaansa | The claim does not hold. | Fixed expression common in formal/academic discourse. Preferred over simple negation in careful argumentation. Less frequent in casual speech. | [CORE] |
| ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION | tosi asia | tosi asia | true matter/fact | Fully productive pattern. *Tosiasia* lexicalized as single word "fact." Adjectives freely modify statement-nouns (*väite*, *käsitys*, *vastaus*). | [CORE] |
| ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION | väärä käsitys | väärä käsitys | wrong impression | Common collocation. Pattern unrestricted across truth-domain vocabulary. | [CORE] |

| TRANSLATIVE CONSTRUCTIONS | Väite todistettiin vääräksi. | väite todistettiin vääräksi | The claim was proven false. | Standard pattern in formal/academic discourse. Common with *todistaa*, *osoittaa* "prove/show." Passive voice typical. | [CORE] |

| EMPHATIC CONSTRUCTIONS | Se on ihan tosi. | se on ihan tosi | It's completely true. | Very common in casual speech. *Ihan* frequent intensifier with *tosi*. Less formal than *täysin totta*. | [CORE] |
