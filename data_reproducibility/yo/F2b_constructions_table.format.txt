| purọ́ (to lie) | Ó purọ́ | ó purọ́ | He/she lied / is lying | Simple verbal construction. Most direct way to accuse someone of lying. Common in confrontational contexts. | [CORE] |

| dájú (to be certain) | Mo dájú pé ó tọ́ | mo dájú pé ó tọ́ | I am certain that it is true | Construction using complementizer pé to introduce embedded clause. Very common for expressing epistemic certainty about propositions. | [CORE] |
| dájú (to be certain) | Ṣé o dájú? | ṣé o dájú | Are you certain? / Are you sure? | Interrogative construction using question particle ṣé. Extremely common in conversation when seeking confirmation or challenging claims. | [CORE] |
