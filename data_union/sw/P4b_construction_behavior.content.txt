## SUMMARY NOTES

**Asymmetries flagged:**
- The "tell the truth" directive uses *sema ukweli* (light verb *kusema*), not a *piga* construction; *piga* is restricted to *uongo* in this domain.
- No agentive nominal for "truth-teller" is directly derived from *kweli* or *ukweli*; periphrastic expression only (*mtu anayesema ukweli*, *mtu wa kweli*).
- The possessive epistemic construction (*nina/sina uhakika*) does not extend to propositional truth (*kweli*); the two sub-domains use distinct constructions.
- The inchoative periphrastic (*kuwa na uhakika*) is rated [MARGINAL] relative to the lexicalized *-ika* forms and the formulaic *nina/sina uhakika*.

**Passive vs. Inchoative distinction (§8 / §4):**
- The contrast between *kudanganyika* (inchoative, no agent) and *kudanganywa* (passive, agent expressible) is semantically significant and both forms are [CORE]; they are not interchangeable alternates but distinct constructions with different agent-foregrounding properties.

**Complementizer alternation (§11):**
- Both *kwamba* and *kuwa* are attested as complementizers in the *ukweli ni kwamba/kuwa* frame; no distributional restriction between them is reported.

**Reduplicated intensification (§9):**
- *Kwa kweli kweli* is attested as an intensified variant of *kwa kweli*; no other morphological variants are reported.

**Pattern 4 / Pattern 8 overlap:**
- *-ika* (stative, no external agent) and *-wa* (passive, external agent implied) are both attested for resultant-state readings; the distinction rests on implied agentivity and is not a free alternation.

**Absence of attested alternates for §12 (periphrastic verbal construction):**
- No alternate constructions are listed for the periphrastic verbal construction because no single-word lexicalized verbs for the relevant actions ("to truth-tell," "to evidence-gather") are attested; the periphrastic pattern is the primary attested form for these meanings.

**No constructions have been added beyond those identified in P4a.**
