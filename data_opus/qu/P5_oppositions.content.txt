# CLCA P5 — OPPOSITIONS
## Language: Quechua (qu)

Based solely on contrasts attested in the CONTEXT (P1–P4b). Each opposition is grounded in explicit statements or structural parallels in the data.

---

## OPPOSITION 1

1. **Term A:** chiqaq
2. **Term B:** llulla
3. **Transliteration:** chiqaq / llulla
4. **English:** true, real, genuine / lie, false, liar
5. **Description of contrast:** The core truth-vs-falsehood opposition of the domain. P1 describes *llulla* as "the negative pole of the truth domain"; P3 notes a "recurrent contrast pair" between the chiqa- truth-affirmation group and the llulla falsehood group.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## OPPOSITION 2

1. **Term A:** chiqaq simi
2. **Term B:** llulla simi
3. **Transliteration:** chiqaq simi / llulla simi
4. **English:** true word, truthful speech / false word, falsehood in speech
5. **Description of contrast:** A minimal pair at the constructional level using the [QUALITY + simi] frame; explicitly identified in P2b and P4a/P4b as "the clearest attested truth/falsity contrast pair at the construction level."
6. **Contrast type:** constructional
7. **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

## OPPOSITION 3

1. **Term A:** allin
2. **Term B:** mana allin
3. **Transliteration:** allin / mana allin
4. **English:** good, right, proper / bad, wrong, not good, improper
5. **Description of contrast:** Morphological negation of the broad evaluative root via preposed *mana* "not," yielding the opposite (negative) evaluative polarity. Documented as a fixed negated expression in P1 (#28) and P4a/P4b.
6. **Contrast type:** morphological
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## OPPOSITION 4

1. **Term A:** llulla / llullaq
2. **Term B:** mana llullaq
3. **Transliteration:** llulla / mana llullaq
4. **English:** lie, liar / truthful, honest (one who does not lie)
5. **Description of contrast:** Morphological negation of the agentive *llullaq* via *mana*, reversing polarity from a negative trait (liar) to a positive one (honest person). P4a notes *mana* + AGENTIVE "yields positive polarity by negating a negative trait."
6. **Contrast type:** morphological
7. **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

## OPPOSITION 5

1. **Term A:** mana allin
2. **Term B:** mana llullaq
3. **Transliteration:** mana allin / mana llullaq
4. **English:** bad (not good) / honest (not lying)
5. **Description of contrast:** Both use the negator *mana* but yield opposite evaluative polarities because they negate different word classes — *mana* + ADJ gives negative polarity, *mana* + AGENTIVE gives positive polarity. Explicitly noted in P2b ("negate different word classes... opposite evaluative polarity") and P4a/P4b.
6. **Contrast type:** constructional
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## OPPOSITION 6

1. **Term A:** llullakuy
2. **Term B:** pantay
3. **Transliteration:** llullakuy / pantay
4. **English:** to lie, to deceive / to err, to make a mistake
5. **Description of contrast:** Intentional falsehood vs. unintentional error. P1 (#10) explicitly states *pantay* is "distinct from intentional lying," and P3 keeps Group 11 (error) separate from Group 4 (lying) on this basis.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## OPPOSITION 7

1. **Term A:** chiqachay / allinchay (causative, *-cha-*)
2. **Term B:** llullachiy (causative, *-chi-*)
3. **Transliteration:** chiqachay / llullachiy
4. **English:** to set right, make good / to cause to believe a falsehood, deceive
5. **Description of contrast:** A constructional/derivational contrast within the causative system: *-cha-* renders something into a good/true state (rectification), whereas *-chi-* on *llulla* causes a third party to be deceived. The two causatives diverge in both affix and evaluative direction.
6. **Contrast type:** constructional
7. **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

## OPPOSITION 8

1. **Term A:** chiqaq sunqu
2. **Term B:** (no balanced "bad-hearted" term attested)
3. **Transliteration:** chiqaq sunqu / allin sunqu
4. **English:** sincere/true-hearted; good-hearted, kind
5. **Description of contrast:** Within the [QUALITY + sunqu] character-evaluation frame, *chiqaq sunqu* and *allin sunqu* are positive variants; the CONTEXT attests no negated/opposite *sunqu* compound. This is an asymmetry rather than a clean opposition. (no clear oppositional partner identified for a negative *sunqu* term)
6. **Contrast type:** constructional
7. **Epistemic status:** [UNCERTAIN]

---

## OPPOSITION 9

1. **Term A:** chiqaq (genuine/authentic sense) / kikin ("the very same")
2. **Term B:** rikch'akuq / rikch'ay (mere appearance, resemblance)
3. **Transliteration:** chiqaq / rikch'ay
4. **English:** genuine, the very one / appearance, likeness, resembling
5. **Description of contrast:** P1 (#18) notes *rikch'ay* "relates to how something appears versus what it is," implying a being-vs-seeming contrast against the genuine/self-same senses of *chiqaq*/*kikin*. The contrast is suggested by the data but not stated as an explicit pair.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [UNCERTAIN]

---

## OPPOSITION 10

1. **Term A:** chiqaqchu (interrogative, *-chu*)
2. **Term B:** chiqaqmi (affirmative, *-mi*)
3. **Transliteration:** chiqaqchu / chiqaqmi
4. **English:** is it true? / it's true (I affirm)
5. **Description of contrast:** A constructional contrast on the same truth root between questioning a claim (*-chu* interrogative truth-confirmation) and affirming it with direct evidential commitment (*-mi*). Both are documented (P4a #9, #1).
6. **Contrast type:** constructional
7. **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

## Notes and limitations

- Oppositions 1, 3, and 6 are the most strongly and explicitly supported by the CONTEXT (described directly as poles/distinctions in P1/P3).
- Opposition 5 is a notable constructional asymmetry explicitly flagged in the data: identical negator, opposite resulting polarity, due to differing host word class.
- Oppositions 8 and 9 are flagged [UNCERTAIN] because the CONTEXT does not attest a balanced contrasting partner; they reflect asymmetries or implied (not stated) contrasts and are not forced into symmetry.
- The truth/error/lie three-way distinction (chiqaq vs. pantay vs. llulla) is supported: error (*pantay*) is distinguished from both truth and intentional lying, but the data presents it primarily as opposed to lying (Opposition 6) rather than as a clean binary with *chiqaq*.
- No opposition was constructed for the evidential enclitic system (-mi/-si/-chá), which the CONTEXT explicitly excludes as grammatical material outside lexical scope.
