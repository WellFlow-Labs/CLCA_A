# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2a Derivational System
# Language: Georgian (ka)

---

**Methodological note:** The following documents attested derivational morphology for the 30 P1 lexical items. Where P1b root expansion has already established derivatives of the same roots, those forms are cross-referenced rather than duplicated, and only forms not already documented are added. Epistemic status reflects confidence in attestation and currency of use. Forms already fully documented in P1b are noted as such and not re-described.

---

## 1. Base Form: ჭეშმარიტი (č̣ešmariti)

---

**Derived Form 1**

1. **Base Form:** ჭეშმარიტი (č̣ešmariti)
2. **Derived Form:** ჭეშმარიტება (č̣ešmariteba)
3. **Transliteration:** č̣ešmariteba
4. **Literal Breakdown:** č̣ešmariti + -eba (nominalizing suffix)
5. **Natural English translation:** truth, truthfulness
6. **Usage:** The standard abstract noun for truth in Georgian, documented as P1 item #2. Fully described in P1.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

*Note: This is the primary and most productive derivation from č̣ešmariti. No further well-attested morphological derivatives of č̣ešmariti beyond č̣ešmariteba have been identified. The root does not appear to generate adverbial, agentive, or privative forms in current standard usage. Collocational extensions are documented in P1b.*

## 3. Base Form: სიმართლე (simartle)

---

**Derived Form 1**

1. **Base Form:** სიმართლე (simartle)
2. **Derived Form:** სიმართლის თქმა (simartlis t̕q̇ma)
3. **Transliteration:** simartlis t̕q̇ma
4. **Literal Breakdown:** simartle (genitive: simartlis) + t̕q̇ma (verbal noun of t̕q̇ven-, "to say")
5. **Natural English translation:** truth-telling, the telling of the truth
6. **Usage:** Documented in P1b as a fixed nominal phrase for the act of stating what is true. Highly frequent in legal oath contexts and moral discourse.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

*Note: simartle does not appear to generate morphological derivatives through suffixation in the way that mart- root forms do. Its derivational relationship is upstream (it is itself derived from mart- via si-...-e circumfix). The genitive construction simartlis is productive in collocations but this is inflectional rather than derivational.*

3. **Transliteration:** siq̇albe

5. **Natural English translation:** falseness, falsehood, forgery, inauthenticity
6. **Usage:** Documented as P1 item #5. The standard abstract noun for the quality of being false or counterfeit, used in legal and moral contexts.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2**

1. **Base Form:** ყალბი (q̇albi)
2. **Derived Form:** ყალბობა (q̇alboba)
3. **Transliteration:** q̇alboba
4. **Literal Breakdown:** q̇alb- + -oba (nominalizing suffix)
5. **Natural English translation:** falseness, the quality or practice of being fake or counterfeit
6. **Usage:** Documented in P1b. Less common than siq̇albe but used in moral and evaluative discourse as an abstract noun for the property or practice of falseness.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 3**

1. **Base Form:** ყალბი (q̇albi)
2. **Derived Form:** გაყალბება (gaq̇albeba)
3. **Transliteration:** gaq̇albeba
4. **Literal Breakdown:** ga- (completive preverb) + q̇alb- + -eba (verbal/nominal suffix)
5. **Natural English translation:** falsification, forgery, fabrication
6. **Usage:** Documented in P1b. The standard verbal noun for the act of falsifying or forging, used in legal contexts for the criminal act of forgery.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 4**

1. **Base Form:** ყალბი (q̇albi)
2. **Derived Form:** გაყალბებული (gaq̇albebuli)
3. **Transliteration:** gaq̇albebuli
4. **Literal Breakdown:** ga- + q̇alb- + -ebuli (participial/adjectival suffix)
5. **Natural English translation:** falsified, forged, fabricated
6. **Usage:** Documented in P1b. Participial adjective describing documents, evidence, or data that have been deliberately falsified. Widely used in legal and investigative contexts.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

*Note on q̇alb- productivity: The root generates both a si-...-e abstract noun (siq̇albe) and an -oba abstract noun (q̇alboba), as well as a ga- prefixed verbal form (gaq̇albeba) and its participial derivative (gaq̇albebuli). This makes it moderately productive. No agentive, privative, or adverbial derivatives have been identified.*

## 9. Base Form: სიცრუე (sicrue)

---

**Derived Form 1**

1. **Base Form:** სიცრუე (sicrue)
2. **Derived Form:** მცრუ (mcru)
3. **Transliteration:** mcru
4. **Literal Breakdown:** m- (adjectival/participial prefix) + cru- (root: false, lying)
5. **Natural English translation:** false, lying, deceptive
6. **Usage:** An adjectival form derived from the same cru- root as sicrue, used to describe persons or statements as false or deceptive. Appears in formal and literary registers; less common in everyday speech than q̇albi.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2**

1. **Base Form:** სიცრუე (sicrue)
2. **Derived Form:** მცდარი (mcdari)
3. **Transliteration:** mcdari
4. **Literal Breakdown:** m- (adjectival prefix) + cd- (root related to error, trial, testing) + -ari (adjectival suffix)
5. **Natural English translation:** erroneous, mistaken, wrong, incorrect
6. **Usage:** Used to describe statements, beliefs, or conclusions that are factually incorrect or mistaken, with a nuance of error rather than deliberate deception. Appears in academic, scientific, and formal discourse. Distinct from q̇albi (which implies deliberate falsification) in that mcdari can describe honest mistakes.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

*Note on the cru- root: The root generates sicrue (si-...-e abstract noun) and the adjectival mcru. The form mcdari, while semantically adjacent, derives from a distinct root (cd-) and is included here because of its close functional relationship to sicrue in the domain of falsehood and error. These are separate roots that overlap in the semantic field.*

*Correction: mcdari is derived from a different root (cd-, related to error/trial) and should not be listed as a derivation of sicrue. It is retained here as a semantically adjacent form with a note on its distinct etymology, but it does not constitute a morphological derivative of sicrue.*

## 10. Base Form: ტყუილი (ẗq̇uili)

---

**Derived Form 1**

1. **Base Form:** ტყუილი (ẗq̇uili)
2. **Derived Form:** ტყუილუბრალოდ (ẗq̇uilubralo d)
3. **Transliteration:** ẗq̇uilubralod
4. **Literal Breakdown:** ẗq̇uili + u- (privative) + bral- (fault, cause) + -o + -d (adverbial suffix); "without cause, for nothing"
5. **Natural English translation:** in vain, for no reason, needlessly, without cause
6. **Usage:** A fixed adverbial compound used in everyday speech to describe actions done without purpose or result. This form extends the "in vain" sense of ẗq̇uili (noted in P1) into a fully lexicalized compound adverb. Extremely common in spoken Georgian.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2**

1. **Base Form:** ტყუილი (ẗq̇uili)
2. **Derived Form:** ტყუილად (ẗq̇uilad)
3. **Transliteration:** ẗq̇uilad
4. **Literal Breakdown:** ẗq̇uili + -ad (adverbial/instrumental suffix)
5. **Natural English translation:** falsely, in vain, for nothing, without reason
6. **Usage:** Adverbial form used in everyday speech to describe actions done falsely, without justification, or to no effect. Overlaps with the "in vain" sense of ẗq̇uili noted in P1.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 3**

1. **Base Form:** ტყუილი (ẗq̇uili)
2. **Derived Form:** უტყუარი (uẗq̇uari)
3. **Transliteration:** uẗq̇uari
4. **Literal Breakdown:** u- (privative prefix) + ẗq̇uar- (lie, falsehood) + -i
5. **Natural English translation:** infallible, indubitable, certain, irrefutable
6. **Usage:** Documented as P1 item #15. The privative formation explicitly marks the absence of falsehood or error. Used in legal and philosophical contexts for evidence or proofs beyond doubt.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

*Note: The ẗq̇uil- root generates the privative uẗq̇uari (P1 #15), the adverbial ẗq̇uilad, and the compound ẗq̇uilubralod. No agentive or further nominal derivatives have been identified.*
