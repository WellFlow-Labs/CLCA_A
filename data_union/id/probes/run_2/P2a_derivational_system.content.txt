# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2a Derivational System
**Language: Indonesian (id)**

---

## Preliminary Note on Method

The following documents attested derivational morphology for each lexical item identified in P1. Forms are drawn from the P1 entries and P1b root expansion where derivational information was already noted, supplemented by analysis of those forms. No paradigm completion has been performed. Gaps are preserved. Where a P1 item is itself a derived form (e.g., *kebenaran*, *sebenarnya*), its base is identified and its derivational relationship is described, but it is not re-derived unless further derivatives are independently attested.

Epistemic status reflects confidence in attestation: [CORE] = well-attested in standard usage across multiple registers; [MARGINAL] = attested but restricted in register, frequency, or distribution; [UNCERTAIN] = reported or inferrable but attestation is not fully secure.

## Item 1: benar

**Base Form:** benar

---

**Derived Form 1:**
1. **Base Form:** benar
2. **Derived Form:** kebenaran
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *ke-benar-an* — circumfix *ke-...-an* (abstract nominalizer) + *benar*
5. **Natural English Translation:** truth, correctness, rightness
6. **Usage:** Refers to truth as an abstract quality or to the correctness of a specific claim or state of affairs. Used across moral, legal, philosophical, and everyday registers; one of the most frequent nominalizations in the truth domain.
7. **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2:**
1. **Base Form:** benar
2. **Derived Form:** membenarkan
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *me(m)-benar-kan* — active transitive prefix *me(m)-* + *benar* + causative suffix *-kan*
5. **Natural English Translation:** to confirm, to justify, to validate; also to correct (someone or something)
6. **Usage:** Used in two related senses: epistemic (to confirm that something is true) and moral/legal (to justify or excuse an action). Both senses are well established across journalistic, legal, and everyday registers.
7. **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 3:**
1. **Base Form:** benar
2. **Derived Form:** dibenarkan
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *di-benar-kan* — passive prefix *di-* + *benar* + *-kan*
5. **Natural English Translation:** to be confirmed, to be justified, to be permitted
6. **Usage:** Passive counterpart of *membenarkan*. The negative form *tidak dibenarkan* ("not permitted / not justified") is a standard formula in official regulations and legal documents.
7. **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 4:**
1. **Base Form:** benar
2. **Derived Form:** pembenaran
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *pe(m)-benar-an* — nominalizing circumfix *pe(m)-...-an* + *benar*
5. **Natural English Translation:** justification, confirmation, validation, rationalization
6. **Usage:** Refers to the act or process of justifying something. Used in moral, psychological, and legal discourse; the collocation *pembenaran diri* (self-justification, rationalization) is stable and frequent in psychological and editorial commentary.
7. **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 5:**
1. **Base Form:** benar
2. **Derived Form:** sebenarnya
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *se-benar-nya* — prefix *se-* (in the manner/state of) + *benar* + enclitic *-nya* (definiteness/3sg)
5. **Natural English Translation:** actually, in truth, in fact, really
6. **Usage:** A fixed adverbial form functioning as a discourse marker that introduces a correction, clarification, or disclosure of what is actually the case. Extremely frequent in both spoken and written Indonesian across all registers.
7. **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 6:**
1. **Base Form:** benar
2. **Derived Form:** sebenar-benarnya
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *se-benar-benar-nya* — prefix *se-* + full reduplication of *benar* + enclitic *-nya*
5. **Natural English Translation:** in all truth, truly and completely, the absolute truth of the matter
6. **Usage:** An emphatic intensified form of *sebenarnya*, used to stress that what follows is the complete and unvarnished truth. Appears in formal declarations, sworn testimony, and emphatic speech.
7. **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 7:**
1. **Base Form:** benar
2. **Derived Form:** benar-benar
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *benar-benar* — full reduplication of *benar*
5. **Natural English Translation:** truly, really, genuinely, absolutely
6. **Usage:** An intensified adverbial form used to emphasize the reality or sincerity of a state or action. Extremely common in spoken and written Indonesian across all registers; functions as a degree intensifier.
7. **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 8:**
1. **Base Form:** benar
2. **Derived Form:** membenarkan diri
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *membenarkan* (to justify) + *diri* (self/oneself)
5. **Natural English Translation:** to justify oneself, to rationalize one's own actions
6. **Usage:** A stable collocation used in moral and psychological discourse to describe the act of excusing or rationalizing one's own behavior. Closely related to *pembenaran diri* but in verbal rather than nominal form.
7. **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Note on benar:** The root *benar* is the most derivationally productive root in the P1 inventory. The prefix-suffix combinations *ke-...-an*, *me(m)-...-kan*, *di-...-kan*, and *pe(m)-...-an* are all productively attested. The *se-...-nya* form (*sebenarnya*) is a fixed lexicalized adverb rather than a fully productive pattern. Reduplication (*benar-benar*) functions as an intensifier. No agentive *pe(m)-* form (*pembenar*, "one who justifies/confirms") is listed in P1 or P1b; this gap is preserved.

The form *menyalahi* (to violate, to go against — *me(ny)-salah-i*) is attested in legal and moral contexts (*menyalahi aturan*, "to violate the rules") but was not listed in P1; it is noted here as an additional attested derivative.
