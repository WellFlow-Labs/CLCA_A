## Summary Observations

- All construction types identified across the probes receive a **[CORE]** naturalness rating based on the attested data, with the exception of the **postnominal attributive** variant within Construction 3, which is rated [MARGINAL] as a sub-pattern, consistent with the P4a note that it is marked and restricted to emphatic or literary use.
- The absence of a lexicalized agentive noun for "truth-teller" (Construction 7) is a notable asymmetry in the domain, attested explicitly across probes and preserved here without inference.
- The pragmatic distinction between δήθεν/τάχα (speaker believes claim is false) and φερόμενος ως (claim is unconfirmed) within the epistemic hedge sub-type of Construction 8 is the only case where two sub-patterns within a single construction type carry observably different epistemic force.
- Privative-prefixed adjectives (αναξιόπιστος, αναληθής, ανακριβής) are preferred in formal, journalistic, and legal registers for negative attribution, but not all positive adjectives have a lexicalized privative counterpart, limiting productivity.
- No productive morphological causative exists in the truth domain; all causative constructions are periphrastic or lexically distinct verbs, each semantically restricted to its own sub-domain rather than being interchangeable.
- Suffix choice for abstract nominalizations (-εια, -ία, -τητα, -ση) is lexically determined and not rule-governed; -ση nominalizations denote acts or processes rather than abstract properties and are distinct in meaning from -ότητα nominalizations.
- The ψευδο- prefix is invariant and combines freely with domain nouns across registers; new formations are coined as needed with no apparent morphological restriction on the base noun class.
