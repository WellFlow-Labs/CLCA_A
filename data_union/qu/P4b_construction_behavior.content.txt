# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P4b Construction Usage & Constraints
# Language: Quechua (Southern: Cusco-Collao / Ayacucho-Chanka)

---

**Scope note:** Ratings and usage notes are based solely on patterns documented in P1–P3 and described in P4a. No additional constructions are generated. Naturalness ratings reflect attestation density and contextual breadth as reported in the source data.

**Attested alternate constructions:**
- *Cheqa kasqam* — past or reported context, with overt copula *kasqa*; attested but contextually restricted to third-person past and reported speech frames.
- *Cheqa sonqo* ("true-hearted, sincere") and *llulla sonqo* ("false-hearted, deceitful") — extension of the same prenominal pattern to body-part nominals encoding inner states; attested but noted as a specific collocation subtype rather than a distinct construction.
- Paired rhetorical construction: *cheqam, manan llullachu* ("it is true, it is not a lie") — positive assertion combined with negative denial of the opposite pole; attested as a rhetorical pattern but not described as an independent construction type.

**Naturalness: [CORE]**

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness Rating | Usage Notes | Attested Alternates |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Negative Attribution (prenominal) | mana cheqa qelqa | *mana cheqa qelqa* | 'inauthentic document / not a genuine text' | [CORE] | *Mana* precedes the adjective-noun sequence; *-chu* does not appear in prenominal attributive position. | Negative Attribution (predicative) | mana allinchu | *mana allinchu* | 'it is not good / it is wrong / it is bad' | [CORE] | *-chu* is obligatory on the predicate in negative declarative clauses; its absence would be ungrammatical. | Negative attribution | Manan cheqachu | *mana-n cheqa-chu* | 'It is not true / That is not correct' | [CORE] | The *mana...chu* frame is described as the standard negation pattern for predicates; attested without register restriction. | Negative attribution | Manan llullachu | *mana-n llulla-chu* | 'It is not false / That is not a lie' | [CORE] | Parallel construction to *Manan cheqachu*; attested as part of the paired rhetorical pattern. | Paired rhetorical construction: *cheqam, manan llullachu* |
| *Mana* + predicate + *-chu* | `Mana cheqachu.` | *mana cheqa-chu* | 'It is not true. / That is not the case.' | [CORE] | Standard negation pattern in Southern Quechua; applies to truth-domain predicates without any special construction. | *Mana* + predicate + *-mi* + *-chu* | `Mana chiqanmi chay ruway.` | *mana chiqan-mi chay ruway* | 'That action is not right/just.' | [CORE] | Same *mana … -chu* frame; here *-mi* attaches to the predicate rather than to *mana*. **Attested alternate constructions:**
- Paired rhetorical construction: *cheqam, manan llullachu* ("it is true, it is not a lie") — positive assertion combined with negative denial of the opposite pole; attested as a rhetorical pattern but not described as an independent construction type.
- Evidential clitic may attach to *mana* rather than to the predicate; both placements are noted in P4a as attested options.

| Become / Inchoative | allinkay | *allinkay* | 'to become good / to get well / to improve / to recover' | [CORE] | Common in everyday speech including health and recovery contexts. - *Cheqakay* (non-accusative nominalized form) — the base nominalization from which *cheqakayta* is derived; implied by the P4a data but the accusative form is the attested example.

## 5. CAUSATIVE

**Naturalness: [CORE]**

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness Rating | Usage Notes | Attested Alternates |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Causative (*-ncha-*) | cheqanchay | *cheqanchay* | 'to verify / to confirm / to make true / to correct' | [CORE] | Applies to adjectival roots (*cheqa*, *allin*, *sut'i*, *sumaq*); yields transitive verbs of making or establishing. Appears in legal, administrative, and everyday registers. | Reflexive extension: *cheqanchakuy* |
| Causative (*-chi-*) | pantachiy | *pantachiy* | 'to mislead / to cause someone to err' | [CORE] | Applies to verbal roots (*panta-*, *llulla-*, *yacha-*); yields verbs of causing an action in another. *Llulla* uses *-chi-* (*llullachiy*) rather than *-ncha-*; this asymmetry is preserved as attested. | *llullachiy* 'to cause to lie' (same pattern) |
| Causative verb | Cheqachiy | *cheqa-chi-y* | 'To verify / To confirm / To prove / To correct' | [CORE] | Described as highly productive across all roots in this domain; no contextual restriction noted beyond the general transitive causative meaning. | *Cheqachikuy* ("to verify for oneself") — causative + reflexive *-ku*; attested. |
| Causative verb | Llullachiy | *llulla-chi-y* | 'To deceive (someone) / To cause to lie / To mislead' | [CORE] | Parallel to *Cheqachiy*; attested without restriction. *Llullachiy* is the most specifically attested form among truth-domain causatives. | *Llullachikuy* ("to deceive oneself") — causative + reflexive *-ku*; attested. |
| Morphological causative: root + *-chay/-chiy* | `Kay pantasqata chiqanchay tiyan.` | *kay pantasqa-ta chiqancha-y tiyan* | 'This mistake needs to be corrected.' | [CORE] | Productive morphological pattern; *-chay/-chiy* applies directly to the adjectival/nominal root to produce a transitive verb. Attested for *cheqaqchay*, *chiqanchay*, and *llullachiy*. | Periphrastic causative with *-chiy* on a verbal stem is noted in P4a as a general Quechua strategy but is not separately documented for truth-domain items beyond *llullachiy*. |
| Morphological causative: root + *-chay/-chiy* | `Llullachirqan payta.` | *llullachiqa-n pay-ta* | 'He/she deceived him/her / caused him/her to believe a lie.' | [CORE] | Same morphological strategy; *llullachiy* is the most specifically attested form among truth-domain causatives. | Periphrastic causative noted in P4a as a general Quechua strategy but not separately documented for truth-domain items beyond *llullachiy*. |

**Attested alternate constructions:**
- *Cheqachikuy* ("to verify for oneself") — causative + reflexive *-ku*; attested.
- *Llullachikuy* ("to deceive oneself") — causative + reflexive *-ku*; attested.
- *Sut'ichay* ("to clarify"), *allinchay* ("to correct"), *pantachiy* ("to mislead") — parallel causative forms on other roots in the domain; attested and listed in P4a notes. The causative-reflexive combination is described as attested on all four major roots.
- Periphrastic causative with *-chiy* on a verbal stem is noted in P4a as a general Quechua strategy but is not separately documented for truth-domain items beyond *llullachiy*.

## 6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness Rating | Usage Notes | Attested Alternates |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Abstract Nominalization | cheqa kay | *cheqa kay* | 'truth / reality / the state of being true / genuineness' | [CORE] | Productive across evaluative roots. Appears in philosophical, religious, and legal registers as well as everyday speech. | Possessive extension: *cheqa kaynin* |
| Abstract Nominalization | allin kay | *allin kay* | 'goodness / well-being / the state of being good or right' | [CORE] | Same productive pattern; no restrictions noted beyond those of the lexical root. | None attested beyond possessive extension pattern |
| Possessed abstract nominal | Cheqan | *cheqa-n* | 'The truth / What is right / The correct one' | [CORE] | Described as the primary abstract nominal for "truth"; takes case suffixes freely (*cheqanta*, *cheqanmanta*). Attested in the collocation *cheqanta rimay* ("to speak the truth"). | *Cheqanta* (accusative of *cheqan*) — attested in *cheqanta rimay*; *Cheqanmanta* (ablative of *cheqan*) — attested; meaning "about the truth / truly." |
| Possessed abstract nominal (accusative) | Cheqanta | *cheqanta* | 'the truth' (accusative) | [CORE] | Case-inflected form of the primary abstract nominal; attested in *cheqanta rimay* ("to speak the truth"). | *Cheqan* (base form); *Cheqanmanta* (ablative) |
| Possessed abstract nominal (ablative) | Cheqanmanta | *cheqanmanta* | 'about the truth / truly' | [CORE] | Ablative case-inflected form of *cheqan*; attested. | *Cheqan* (base form); *Cheqanta* (accusative) |
| Verbal nominalization (abstract, accusative) | Cheqakayta | *cheqa-ka-y-ta* | 'The being true / The truth of (something)' (as object of epistemic verbs) | [MARGINAL] | Attested as object of epistemic verbs; contextually restricted to that position in the data reported. | Bare infinitive nominalization | `Cheqaqta rimay allinmi.` | *cheqaqta rimay allin-mi* | 'Speaking the truth is good.' | [CORE] | Bare infinitive nominalization; *cheqaqta* functions adverbially/nominally here. Attested in a wide range of clause-argument contexts, not restricted to truth-domain vocabulary. | Bare infinitive nominalization |

**Attested alternate constructions:**
- *Cheqanta* (accusative of *cheqan*) — attested in *cheqanta rimay*; case-inflected form of the primary abstract nominal.
- *Cheqanmanta* (ablative of *cheqan*) — attested; meaning "about the truth / truly."
- No parallel abstract nominal for *llulla* is attested in the sources consulted; the concept of "falsehood" as an abstract noun is expressed through the bare adjective or verbal nominalizations according to P4a.

## 7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| Agentive Nominalization (habitual) | llullakuq | *llulla-ku-q* | 'habitual liar / chronic deceiver / one who habitually deceives' | [CORE] | The reflexive *-ku* + agentive *-q* combination adds a habitual or self-directed nuance; attested and described as distinct from the bare agentive. | Agentive Nominalization | cheqanchaq | *cheqanchaq* | 'one who verifies / a verifier / one who confirms' | [CORE] | Applied to the *-ncha-* verbal base; appears in formal and legal registers. | *cheqaq* 'truthful / genuine' (direct adjectival root + *-q*) |

- *Yachaq* ("one who knows"), *pantaq* ("one who errs"), *riqsiq* ("one who recognizes") — parallel agentive forms on other roots; listed in P4a notes as attested.
- *Llullakuq* (from verbal stem *llullakuy* + *-q*) is noted in P4a as near-synonymous with *llullaq* and attested alongside it.
