# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P4b Construction Usage & Constraints
# Language: Greek (el)

---

## CONSTRUCTION NATURALNESS & USAGE ANALYSIS

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Epistemic Status | Usage Notes |
|------------------|---------|-----------------|-------------|------------------|-------------|
| **1. ATTRIBUTION** | αυτό είναι αλήθεια | aftó íne alítheia | this is truth/true | [CORE] | Standard predication pattern used across all registers. No restrictions on subject type. Works equally with nominal (αλήθεια) and adjectival (αληθινός) predicates. |
| **2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION** | αυτό δεν είναι αλήθεια | aftó den íne alítheia | this is not true | [CORE] | Both analytic negation (δεν + positive) and lexical negation (ψέμα, λάθος) are equally productive. Choice depends on emphasis and register. |
| **3. ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION** | αληθινή ιστορία | alithiní istoría | true story | [CORE] | Standard attributive modification with full agreement. No restrictions. Used across all domains (narrative, legal, epistemic). |
| **4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE** | γίνεται σωστό | gínete sostó | it becomes correct | [MARGINAL] | More natural with correctness/clarity terms (σωστός, σαφής) than with core truth terms (αλήθεια). Truth is typically conceptualized as static rather than emergent. |
| **5. CAUSATIVE** | το κάνει σωστό | to káni sostó | it makes it correct | [MARGINAL] | Analytic causative (κάνω + adj) is less common than morphological causatives (ακυρώνω, βεβαιώνω) in formal contexts. More natural with correctness than with truth per se. |
| **6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION** | ορθότητα | orthótita | correctness | [CORE] | Highly productive derivational pattern across all truth-domain adjectives. Standard in formal and technical registers. Pattern extends freely to new adjectives. |
| **7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION** | ψεύτης | pséftis | liar | [CORE] | Extremely productive for negative agents (ψεύτης, απατεώνας). Asymmetric: no common positive equivalent for "truth-teller" in everyday use. |
| **8. TRUTH-TELLING CONSTRUCTIONS** | λέω την αλήθεια | léo tin alítheia | I tell the truth | [CORE] | Primary construction for truth-telling/lying. Light verb λέω is obligatory; no single-word equivalent exists in colloquial Greek. Used across all registers. |
| **9. ERROR-MAKING CONSTRUCTIONS** | κάνω λάθος | káno láthos | I make a mistake | [CORE] | Standard and obligatory construction for expressing error. Extremely common in all contexts. Highly productive with modifiers (μεγάλο λάθος, σοβαρό λάθος). |
| **10. PREPOSITIONAL TRUTH PHRASES** | στην αλήθεια | stin alítheia | in truth, actually | [CORE] | Functions as discourse marker introducing corrections or frank statements. Very common in both spoken and written Greek. Productive with abstract truth nouns. |
| **11. INTERROGATIVE TRUTH EXPRESSIONS** | αλήθεια; | alítheia? | really? | [CORE] | Extremely common in colloquial speech for expressing surprise or seeking confirmation. Bare noun with interrogative intonation functions as complete question. |
| **12. EXCLAMATORY TRUTH EXPRESSIONS** | ψέματα! | psémata! | lies! no way! | [CORE] | Productive in colloquial speech for emphasis or denial. Plural ψέματα functions specifically as denial. Possessive forms (αλήθεια μου) emphasize sincerity. |
| **13. CATCHING IN FALSEHOOD** | τον έπιασα στο ψέμα | ton épiasa sto pséma | I caught him in a lie | [CORE] | Common construction in everyday speech for expressing discovery of deception or error. Works with both ψέμα and λάθος. |
| **14. TRUTH REVELATION** | βγαίνει η αλήθεια στο φως | vgéni i alítheia sto fos | the truth comes to light | [CORE] | Both intransitive (βγαίνω) and transitive (αποκαλύπτω) patterns are common. Metaphorical extension στο φως is conventional. Used in narrative and investigative contexts. |
| **15. TRUTH CONCEALMENT** | κρύβει την αλήθεια | krývi tin alítheia | he/she hides the truth | [CORE] | Standard construction for expressing concealment. Common in accusations and discussions of deception. Contrasts productively with revelation constructions. |
| **16. LIVING IN FALSEHOOD** | ζει στο ψέμα | zi sto pséma | he/she lives a lie | [CORE] | Idiomatic construction expressing sustained inauthenticity. Common in discussions of authenticity and self-deception. Specific to ψέμα (not used with λάθος). |
| **17. VERIFICATION/CONFIRMATION** | αληθεύτηκε η πρόβλεψη | alithéftike i próvlepsi | the prediction proved true | [MARGINAL] | Less common than active forms but productive for expressing confirmation of predictions or claims. More formal register than active constructions. |
| **18. COLOR-MODIFIED LIES** | άσπρο ψέμα | áspro pséma | white lie | [CORE] | Well-established idiomatic pattern using color metaphors to grade lie severity. Productive in colloquial usage. Specific colors have conventional meanings (άσπρο = minor, μαύρο = serious). |
| **19. DIMINUTIVE MITIGATION** | ψεματάκι | psematáki | little lie, fib | [CORE] | Productive pattern for minimizing severity or expressing affection. Common in everyday speech for mitigating face-threatening acts. Works with both ψέμα and λάθος. |
| **20. PSEUDO-COMPOUNDS** | ψευδοεπιστήμη | psevdoepistími | pseudoscience | [CORE] | Exceptionally productive prefix across multiple domains (scientific, legal, religious). Remains productive for new coinages. Standard in formal and technical registers. |
| **21. ORTHO-COMPOUNDS** | ορθογραφία | orthografía | orthography | [CORE] | Highly productive in technical, religious, and educational domains. Creates compounds indicating both physical straightness and abstract correctness. Standard in formal registers. |
| **22. INVALIDATION/CANCELLATION** | ακυρώνω το έγγραφο | akyróno to éngrafo | I invalidate the document | [CORE] | Standard construction for official invalidation in legal and administrative contexts. Highly productive with institutional objects (documents, decisions, contracts). |
| **23. CERTIFICATION/CONFIRMATION** | βεβαιώνω την ταυτότητα | veveóno tin taftótita | I certify the identity | [CORE] | Standard in administrative and legal contexts. Takes both nominal objects and clausal complements (ότι-clauses). Formal register. |
| **24. ADVERBIAL AFFIRMATION** | ακριβώς! | akrivós! | exactly! | [CORE] | Productive pattern using truth-domain adverbs as standalone affirmative responses. Common in conversational Greek across all registers. Functions as complete utterance. |
| **25. FABRICATION ACCUSATION** | κατασκευάζει στοιχεία | kataskevázi stichía | he/she fabricates evidence | [CORE] | Common in legal contexts and accusations of frame-ups. Specifically used for false evidence or accusations (not general falsehood). Participle κατασκευασμένος functions adjectivally. |

## ALTERNATE CONSTRUCTIONS ATTESTED

### For TRUTH-TELLING (Construction 8):
- **ψεύδομαι** (pséfdome) "I lie" — More formal alternative to λέω ψέματα, less common in everyday speech
- **λέω αλήθειες** (léo alítheies) "I tell truths" (plural) — Emphasizes multiple true statements

### For NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION (Construction 2):
- **X είναι ψέμα** (X íne pséma) "X is a lie" — Lexical negation alternative
- **X είναι λάθος** (X íne láthos) "X is wrong/mistaken" — Error-focused alternative
- **X είναι άδικος** (X íne ádikos) "X is unjust" — Justice-domain alternative

### For PREPOSITIONAL TRUTH PHRASES (Construction 10):
- **στην πραγματικότητα** (stin pragmatikótita) "in reality" — Reality-focused alternative
- **στ' αλήθεια** (st' alítheia) — Contracted colloquial form

### For INTERROGATIVE EXPRESSIONS (Construction 11):
- **σοβαρά;** (sovará?) "seriously?" — Seriousness-focused alternative
- **πραγματικά;** (pragmatiká?) "really?" — Reality-focused alternative

### For EXCLAMATORY EXPRESSIONS (Construction 12):
- **αλήθεια μου!** (alítheia mou!) "honestly!" — Possessive form emphasizing sincerity
- **ψέματα και τρέλα!** (psémata ke tréla!) "lies and madness!" — Intensified denial

## USAGE RESTRICTIONS OBSERVED

### Semantic Restrictions:
1. **Inchoative constructions** (γίνομαι + truth-term) resist core truth vocabulary; prefer correctness/clarity terms
2. **Comparative modification** not attested with core truth terms (αλήθεια, ψέμα)
3. **Degree modification** not productive with core truth terms; requires lexical alternatives (απόλυτη αλήθεια)

### Lexical Gaps:
1. **No single-word causative** for "make true" (*αληθώνω not attested)
2. **No common positive agentive** for "truth-teller" (αληθολόγος marginal/literary)
3. **No single-word verb** for "lie" in colloquial Greek (*ψεμίζω not standard)

### Register Restrictions:
1. **ψεύδομαι** (lie-VERB) more formal than **λέω ψέματα** (tell lies)
2. **αληθεύομαι** (be-verified) more formal than active alternatives
3. **Morphological causatives** (ακυρώνω, βεβαιώνω) restricted to formal/institutional contexts

### Distributional Asymmetries:
1. **Agentive nominalization** highly productive for negative agents, limited for positive
2. **Color modification** specific to ψέμα (not productive with other truth terms)
3. **Living construction** (ζω στο X) specific to ψέμα (not used with λάθος)
