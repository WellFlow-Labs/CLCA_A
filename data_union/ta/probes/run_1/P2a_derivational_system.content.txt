# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2a Derivational System
# Language: Tamil (ta)

---

**Methodological note:** The following documents attested derivational morphology for lexical items identified in P1. Forms are included only where usage is supported by the P1 data and by well-attested Tamil morphological patterns. Paradigm gaps are preserved. Uneven productivity across roots is reported as found, not normalized.

---

## Item 1: உண்மை / *uṇmai*

---

**Derived Form 1**

1. **Base Form:** உண்மை / *uṇmai*
2. **Derived Form:** உண்மையான
3. **Transliteration:** uṇmaiyāna
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *uṇmai* + *-āna* (adjectival copular suffix, from copula *ā-* + participial *-na*)
5. **Natural English translation:** true, genuine, real, authentic
6. **Usage:** Attributive adjective modifying nouns to assert genuineness or factual accuracy; applied to persons, objects, and propositions. Extremely productive in both spoken and written Tamil.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2**

1. **Base Form:** உண்மை / *uṇmai*
2. **Derived Form:** உண்மையில்
3. **Transliteration:** uṇmaiyil
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *uṇmai* + *-il* (locative case suffix)
5. **Natural English translation:** in truth, actually, in reality
6. **Usage:** Adverbial expression used to assert that what follows reflects the actual state of affairs, often correcting a misconception or contrasting appearance with reality. Common in both spoken and written registers.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 3**

1. **Base Form:** உண்மை / *uṇmai*
2. **Derived Form:** உண்மையிலேயே
3. **Transliteration:** uṇmaiyilēyē
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *uṇmai* + *-il* (locative) + *-ē* (emphatic particle) + *-yē* (further emphatic reinforcement)
5. **Natural English translation:** truly indeed, in actual fact, really and truly
6. **Usage:** Emphatic adverbial used in spoken Tamil to strongly assert that something is genuinely the case, often in contexts of surprise or insistence. More emphatic than *uṇmaiyil*.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 4**

1. **Base Form:** உண்மை / *uṇmai*
2. **Derived Form:** உண்மைத்தன்மை
3. **Transliteration:** uṇmait-taṉmai
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *uṇmai* + *taṉmai* (nature, inherent quality; itself from *taṉ* + *-mai*)
5. **Natural English translation:** truthfulness, authenticity, veracity, the quality of being true
6. **Usage:** Abstract nominal used in philosophical, literary, and formal evaluative discourse to refer to the property of being true or authentic. Less common in casual speech than *uṇmai* alone.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 5**

1. **Base Form:** உண்மை / *uṇmai*
2. **Derived Form:** உண்மைக்கு மாறான
3. **Transliteration:** uṇmaikkum māṟāna
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *uṇmai* + dative *-kku* + *māṟāna* (contrary to, deviating from; participial adjective from *māṟu*, to deviate/change)
5. **Natural English translation:** contrary to fact, false, counterfactual
6. **Usage:** Fixed evaluative expression used in legal and formal written contexts to characterize a statement or representation as factually false. More formal and explicit than *poy* alone.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Note on *uṇmai* productivity:** The *-āna* adjectival suffix and the locative *-il* are the two most productive derivational operations on *uṇmai*. The dative + *māṟāna* construction is a fixed legal phrase rather than a freely productive derivation. The *taṉmai* compound is a lexicalized nominal. No verbal derivation directly from *uṇmai* is attested in P1; verbal expressions involving *uṇmai* are periphrastic (e.g., *uṇmaiyai maṟaittal*, *uṇmaiyai aṟital*) rather than morphologically derived.

## Item 2: பொய் / *poy*

---

**Derived Form 1**

1. **Base Form:** பொய் / *poy*
2. **Derived Form:** பொய்யான
3. **Transliteration:** poyyāna
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *poy* + *-āna* (adjectival copular suffix; note gemination of *y* before vowel-initial suffix)
5. **Natural English translation:** false, lying, untrue, deceptive
6. **Usage:** Attributive adjective applied to statements, persons, and representations judged to be false or deceptive. Parallel in formation to *uṇmaiyāna* and used contrastively with it.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2**

1. **Base Form:** பொய் / *poy*
2. **Derived Form:** பொய்மை
3. **Transliteration:** poymai
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *poy* + *-mai* (abstract nominalizer)
5. **Natural English translation:** falsehood, falsity, the quality of being false
6. **Usage:** Abstract nominal form denoting the quality or state of being false; used in philosophical and moral discourse as the direct antonym of *uṇmai*. Appears in classical and formal Tamil writing.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Note on *poy* productivity:** *poy* participates in the same *-āna* adjectival and *-mai* nominalizing derivations as other evaluative roots in this domain. No verbal derivation directly from *poy* is documented in P1. The root functions as both noun and attributive modifier without further morphological change in many contexts.

## Item 3: நேர்மை / *nērmai*

---

**Derived Form 1**

1. **Base Form:** நேர்மை / *nērmai*
2. **Derived Form:** நேர்மையான
3. **Transliteration:** nērmaiyāna
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *nērmai* + *-āna* (adjectival copular suffix)
5. **Natural English translation:** honest, upright, straightforward, of integrity
6. **Usage:** Attributive adjective applied to persons and conduct evaluated as honest and non-deceptive. Common in moral evaluation in personal, professional, and civic contexts.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2**

1. **Base Form:** நேர்மை / *nērmai*
2. **Derived Form:** நேர்மையில்லாத
3. **Transliteration:** nērmaiyillāta
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *nērmai* + *illāta* (lacking, without; negative existential participial adjective from *il-*)
5. **Natural English translation:** dishonest, lacking integrity, not straightforward
6. **Usage:** Negative attributive adjective used to evaluate persons or conduct as lacking honesty or integrity. Formed by the productive negative existential construction rather than a dedicated privative suffix.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Note on *nērmai* and its base *nēr*:** *nērmai* is itself a derivation from the root *nēr* (straight, direct) via the *-mai* nominalizer. The root *nēr* also participates independently in compounds such as *nēr-mukaṃ* (face-to-face), but these are not further derivations of *nērmai* itself. No verbal derivation from *nērmai* is attested in P1.

## Item 4: நம்பு / *nampu* (root underlying *nampikkai* and *nampakamāna*)

---

**Derived Form 1**

1. **Base Form:** நம்பு / *nampu* (verbal root)
2. **Derived Form:** நம்பிக்கை
3. **Transliteration:** nampikkai
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *nampu* + *-ikkai* (action/state nominalizer)
5. **Natural English translation:** trust, belief, faith, confidence
6. **Usage:** The primary nominal derivation from *nampu*; used across religious, interpersonal, and institutional contexts. Functions as an independent base for further compounding (see below).
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2**

1. **Base Form:** நம்பு / *nampu* (verbal root)
2. **Derived Form:** நம்பகம்
3. **Transliteration:** nampakam
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *nampu* + *-akam* (nominalizing infix)
5. **Natural English translation:** trustworthiness, reliability, credibility
6. **Usage:** Nominal form denoting the quality of being trustworthy; used in formal and written Tamil. Functions as the base for *nampakamāna*.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 3**

1. **Base Form:** நம்பு / *nampu* (verbal root)
2. **Derived Form:** நம்பகமான
3. **Transliteration:** nampakamāna
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *nampakam* + *-āna* (adjectival copular suffix)
5. **Natural English translation:** reliable, trustworthy, credible, dependable
6. **Usage:** Attributive adjective applied to persons, sources, institutions, and information judged worthy of trust. Common in journalistic, legal, and everyday evaluative contexts.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 4**

1. **Base Form:** நம்பு / *nampu* (verbal root)
2. **Derived Form:** நம்பத்தக்க
3. **Transliteration:** nampattakka
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *nampu* + *-att-* (potential/dative infix) + *takka* (worthy of, fit for; participial adjective from *taku*)
5. **Natural English translation:** trustworthy, believable, credible, worth trusting
6. **Usage:** Participial adjective evaluating whether a person, source, or claim is worthy of trust. Common in journalistic and legal evaluation of sources and testimony.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 5**

1. **Base Form:** நம்பு / *nampu* (verbal root)
2. **Derived Form:** நம்பத்தகாத
3. **Transliteration:** nampattakāta
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *nampu* + *-att-* + *takāta* (not worthy of; negative participial adjective from *taku* + negative *-āta*)
5. **Natural English translation:** untrustworthy, not credible, not worth trusting
6. **Usage:** Negative participial adjective evaluating persons, sources, or claims as unworthy of trust. Common in legal and journalistic contexts as the direct negative counterpart of *nampattakka*.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 6**

1. **Base Form:** நம்பிக்கை / *nampikkai* (nominal derivation from *nampu*)
2. **Derived Form:** நம்பிக்கையான
3. **Transliteration:** nampikkaiāna
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *nampikkai* + *-āna* (adjectival copular suffix)
5. **Natural English translation:** trustworthy, reliable, faithful
6. **Usage:** Attributive adjective applied to persons or things judged worthy of trust; used in everyday and formal Tamil to evaluate reliability. Derived from the nominal *nampikkai* rather than directly from the verbal root.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 7**

1. **Base Form:** நம்பிக்கை / *nampikkai*
2. **Derived Form:** நம்பிக்கையின்மை
3. **Transliteration:** nampikkaiinmai
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *nampikkai* + *iṉmai* (absence, lack; nominal from *il-* negative root + *-mai*)
5. **Natural English translation:** lack of trust, distrust, absence of faith
6. **Usage:** Nominal form denoting the absence of trust or faith; used in moral, psychological, and political contexts. Formed by the productive *iṉmai* privative nominal construction.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 8**

1. **Base Form:** நம்பிக்கை / *nampikkai*
2. **Derived Form:** நம்பிக்கையாளர்
3. **Transliteration:** nampikkaiāḷar
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *nampikkai* + *āḷar* (person/agent suffix, honorific plural form of *āḷ*)
5. **Natural English translation:** believer, person of faith, the faithful
6. **Usage:** Agent nominal used in religious contexts to refer to believers or the faithful; common in Tamil Christian and Islamic discourse as a term for members of the faith community.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 9**

1. **Base Form:** நம்பு / *nampu* (verbal root)
2. **Derived Form:** நம்பல்
3. **Transliteration:** nampal
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *nampu* + *-al* (verbal noun suffix)
5. **Natural English translation:** trusting, believing, the act of trust
6. **Usage:** Verbal noun form of *nampu*; used in philosophical and formal written Tamil to refer to the act or process of trusting or believing. Less common than *nampikkai* in most registers.
7. **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**Note on *nampu* productivity:** *nampu* is the most morphologically productive root in this domain. It generates derivations at two levels: directly from the verbal root (*nampikkai*, *nampakam*, *nampattakka*, *nampattakāta*, *nampal*) and secondarily from the nominal *nampikkai* (*nampikkaiāna*, *nampikkaiinmai*, *nampikkaiāḷar*). The *-ikkai* nominalization is the most productive single derivation, as *nampikkai* itself functions as a compounding base for a large number of further forms documented in P1b.

## Item 5: மெய் / *mey*

---

**Derived Form 1**

1. **Base Form:** மெய் / *mey*
2. **Derived Form:** மெய்ம்மை
3. **Transliteration:** meymmai
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *mey* + *-m-* (euphonic consonant) + *-mai* (abstract nominalizer)
5. **Natural English translation:** truth, reality, truthfulness, genuineness
6. **Usage:** Classical and literary nominal form for truth or truthfulness; used in philosophical and poetic contexts as a more emphatic or formal variant of *mey* itself. Appears in classical Tamil texts.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2**

1. **Base Form:** மெய் / *mey*
2. **Derived Form:** மெய்யான
3. **Transliteration:** meyyāna
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *mey* + *-āna* (adjectival copular suffix; note gemination of *y*)
5. **Natural English translation:** true, real, genuine
6. **Usage:** Attributive adjective used in literary and formal Tamil as a more classical variant of *uṇmaiyāna*; appears in literary criticism, philosophical writing, and formal prose.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 3**

1. **Base Form:** மெய் / *mey*
2. **Derived Form:** மெய்யே
3. **Transliteration:** meyyē
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *mey* + *-ē* (emphatic particle)
5. **Natural English translation:** truly, really, indeed
6. **Usage:** Emphatic assertion of truth used in literary and formal spoken Tamil; appears in classical poetry and formal prose to strongly affirm a statement.
7. **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**Derived Form 4**

1. **Base Form:** மெய் / *mey*
2. **Derived Form:** மெய்ப்பிக்க / மெய்ப்பித்தல்
3. **Transliteration:** meyppiкka / meyppittal
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *mey* + causative *-ppi-* + infinitive *-kka* / verbal noun *-ttal*
5. **Natural English translation:** to prove, to verify, to demonstrate; proving, verification
6. **Usage:** Causative verbal derivation meaning "to cause to be real/true," used in legal and argumentative contexts for the act of proving or establishing the truth of a claim. The verbal noun *meyppittal* appears in formal written Tamil.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Note on the causative derivation:** The causative *-ppi-* applied to *mey* is notable as the only verbal derivation from a truth-domain root in this inventory that is directly morphologically derived rather than periphrastic. This derivation is not paralleled by *uṇmai*, which lacks a corresponding causative verbal form.

---

**Derived Form 5**

1. **Base Form:** மெய் / *mey*
2. **Derived Form:** மெய்யியல்
3. **Transliteration:** meyyiyal
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *mey* (truth/reality) + *iyal* (nature, science, study of; from verbal root *iyal*, to be natural)
5. **Natural English translation:** philosophy, metaphysics, the study of reality
6. **Usage:** Used as a Tamil equivalent for philosophy or metaphysics in academic and intellectual writing; appears in Tamil philosophical and educational texts.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Note on *mey* and the body/truth split:** As documented in P1b, *mey* participates in two distinct semantic streams — "truth/reality" and "body/physical." Derivations in the body sense (*meykāppu*, *meypāṭu*, *mey cilirttal*) are attested and productive but fall outside the truth-domain scope of this inventory. They are noted here as a structural fact about the root without being fully documented as truth-domain derivations.

## Item 6: சரி / *cari*

**No common derivation identified.**

*cari* functions primarily as an uninflected evaluative particle and discourse marker in spoken Tamil. It does not participate in the *-mai*, *-āna*, or *-ikkai* derivational patterns attested for other items in this inventory. No nominal, adjectival, or verbal derivation from *cari* is documented in P1 or P1b.

**No common derivation identified.**

## Item 11: தகுதி / *takuti*

---

**Derived Form 1**

1. **Base Form:** தகுதி / *takuti*
2. **Derived Form:** தகுதியான
3. **Transliteration:** takutiyāna
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *takuti* + *-āna* (adjectival copular suffix)
5. **Natural English translation:** qualified, worthy, eligible, fit
6. **Usage:** Attributive adjective applied to persons or things that meet a required standard for a role, claim, or judgment. Common in legal, professional, and moral contexts.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2**

1. **Base Form:** தகுதி / *takuti*
2. **Derived Form:** தகுதியற்ற
3. **Transliteration:** takutiyaṟṟa
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *takuti* + *aṟṟa* (privative participial suffix, from *aṟu*, to cut off/lack)
5. **Natural English translation:** unqualified, ineligible, unworthy, unfit
6. **Usage:** Privative participial adjective applied to persons or things that fail to meet a required standard. Used in legal disqualification, moral condemnation, and professional assessment.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Note on the *aṟṟa* privative:** The *aṟṟa* privative participial suffix is a distinct derivational strategy from the *illāta* negative existential construction documented for *nērmai*. Both are productive in Tamil, but they are not interchangeable; *aṟṟa* tends to be used with nouns denoting qualities or attributes, while *illāta* is more broadly applicable. The coexistence of both strategies in this inventory is a structural fact worth noting.

## Item 12: நியாயம் / *niyāyam*

---

**Derived Form 1**

2. **Derived Form:** நியாயமான
3. **Transliteration:** niyāyamāna
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *niyāyam* + *-āna* (adjectival copular suffix)
5. **Natural English translation:** just, fair, reasonable, justified
6. **Usage:** Attributive adjective used in moral and legal contexts to evaluate whether an action, decision, or claim is just or well-founded. Common in everyday speech and formal argumentation.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Note on *niyāyam* and *aniyāyam*:** The antonym *aniyāyam* (injustice) is formed by the Sanskrit privative prefix *a-* applied to the Sanskrit-origin base *nyāya* before borrowing into Tamil, rather than by a Tamil-native derivational suffix applied to *niyāyam* within Tamil. This is a lexical antonym pair rather than a productive Tamil-internal derivation. It is documented in P1 as a separate lexical item rather than a derivation.

## Item 19: பொருத்தம் / *poruttam*

---

**Derived Form 1**

1. **Base Form:** பொருத்தம் / *poruttam*
2. **Derived Form:** பொருத்தமான

## Summary Observations

The following structural patterns emerge from the attested derivations documented above. These observations are reported only insofar as they are directly supported by the data.

**1. The *-āna* adjectival suffix is the single most productive derivational operation in this domain.** It applies to *uṇmai*, *poy*, *nērmai*, *nampakam*, *nampikkai*, *mey*, *tavaṟu*, *pōli*, *takuti*, *niyāyam*, *ātāram*, and *poruttam*. It is the default adjectival derivation for nominal bases in this semantic field.

**2. The *aṟṟa* privative participial suffix and the *illāta* negative existential construction represent two distinct privative strategies.** *aṟṟa* is attested with *takuti*, *ātāram*, and *poruttam*; *illāta* is attested with *nērmai*. These are not interchangeable and their distribution is not fully predictable from the data available.

**3. The *-mai* abstract nominalizer is attested on *poy* (*poymai*) and on *mey* (*meymmai*), but not directly on *uṇmai* (which is itself already a *-mai* derivation) or on most other items.** This reflects the fact that *uṇmai* and *nērmai* are already *-mai* nominals and do not reduplicate the suffix.

**4. Verbal derivation is rare in this domain.** The only morphologically derived verbal form documented is *meyppiкka* (causative from *mey*). All other verbal expressions involving these roots are periphrastic.

**5. Several loanword items (*acal*, *nakal*, *cāṭci*, *niyāyam*, *ātāram*) show limited or no Tamil-native derivational productivity**, with the exception of the *-āna* adjectival suffix, which applies broadly regardless of etymological origin.

**6. *nampu* is the most morphologically productive root in this inventory**, generating derivations at two levels (from the verbal root and from the nominal *nampikkai*) and participating in the *takka/takāta* participial construction not attested for other roots.

**7. Paradigm gaps are real and preserved.** No verbal derivation from *uṇmai*, *poy*, *nērmai*, *tavaṟu*, or *pōli* is documented. No *-mai* nominalization from *tavaṟu*, *pōli*, *takuti*, *niyāyam*, *ātāram*, or *poruttam* is documented. These gaps are reported as found.
