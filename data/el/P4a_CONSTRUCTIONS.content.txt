# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P4a_CONSTRUCTIONS
# Language: Greek (el)

---

## 1. ATTRIBUTION

### Pattern
**[NP] + [COP] + [TRUTH-DOMAIN ADJECTIVE/NOUN]**

Predicative constructions using copula είμαι (íme) "to be" to attribute truth-domain properties to subjects. Adjectives agree in gender, number, and case with subject.

### Examples
- **Αυτό είναι αλήθεια** (Aftó íne alítheia) — "This is truth/true"
- **Είναι ψέμα** (Íne pséma) — "It is a lie/false"
- **Η ιστορία είναι αληθινή** (I istoría íne alithiní) — "The story is true"
- **Το έγγραφο είναι πλαστό** (To éngrafo íne plastó) — "The document is forged"

### Notes
Standard predicative pattern with copula. Nouns (αλήθεια, ψέμα) used predicatively without article in many contexts. Adjectives show full agreement morphology.

---

## 2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION

### Pattern
**[NP] + [NEG] + [COP] + [TRUTH-DOMAIN ADJECTIVE/NOUN]**

Negation with δεν (den) preceding copula. Alternative construction with negative copula δεν είναι (den íne).

### Examples
- **Δεν είναι αλήθεια** (Den íne alítheia) — "It is not true/truth"
- **Δεν είναι σωστό** (Den íne sostó) — "It is not correct"
- **Αυτό δεν είναι ψέμα** (Aftó den íne pséma) — "This is not a lie"

### Notes
Standard negation with preverbal particle δεν. No special negative forms of truth-domain predicates; negation is purely syntactic.

---

## 3. ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION

### Pattern
**[TRUTH-DOMAIN ADJECTIVE] + [NOUN]**

Attributive adjectives precede or follow noun, agreeing in gender, number, and case. Prenominal position more common for truth-domain adjectives.

### Examples
- **αληθινή ιστορία** (alithiní istoría) — "true story"
- **ψευδής μαρτυρία** (psevdís martyría) — "false testimony"
- **σωστή απάντηση** (sostí apántisi) — "correct answer"
- **πλαστά έγγραφα** (plastá éngrafa) — "forged documents"

### Notes
Adjectives show full agreement. Position generally prenominal for truth-domain terms, though postnominal possible for emphasis or with certain fixed expressions.

---

## 4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE

### Pattern
**[NP] + γίνομαι (gínome) + [TRUTH-DOMAIN ADJECTIVE]**

Inchoative construction with γίνομαι "become" + adjective. Less common with truth-domain vocabulary than with other semantic domains.

### Examples
- **Η αλήθεια γίνεται γνωστή** (I alítheia gínete gností) — "The truth becomes known"
- **Το ψέμα γίνεται φανερό** (To pséma gínete faneró) — "The lie becomes apparent"

### Notes
γίνομαι + adjective is standard inchoative pattern, but truth-domain terms more commonly appear in revelation/discovery constructions (βγαίνει στη φόρα "comes to light") than pure inchoative frames. The pattern exists but is not highly productive with core truth-domain vocabulary.

---

## 5. CAUSATIVE

### Pattern
**[AGENT] + κάνω (káno) + [NP-ACC] + [TRUTH-DOMAIN ADJECTIVE-ACC]**

Causative constructions with κάνω "make/do" + object + adjective, or with specialized causative verbs formed with -ώνω suffix.

### Examples
- **Αυτό με κάνει βέβαιο** (Aftó me káni vévaio) — "This makes me certain"
- **Διορθώνω το λάθος** (Diorthóno to láthos) — "I correct the mistake" (causative verb)
- **Ακυρώνω το έγγραφο** (Akyróno to éngrafo) — "I invalidate the document"
- **Βεβαιώνω την πληροφορία** (Vevaióno tin pliροforía) — "I confirm the information"

### Notes
Two patterns: (1) analytic causative with κάνω + adjective; (2) morphological causatives with -ώνω suffix (διορθώνω "correct," ακυρώνω "invalidate," βεβαιώνω "confirm"). The -ώνω pattern is productive with truth-domain adjectives.

---

## 6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION

### Pattern
**[ADJECTIVE STEM] + -ότητα / -εια / -ία / -ση**

Abstract nouns formed from adjectives via suffixation. Multiple productive suffixes depending on base.

### Examples
- **αληθινότητα** (alithinótita) from αληθινός — "truthfulness, genuineness"
- **ειλικρίνεια** (eilikríneia) from ειλικρινής — "sincerity"
- **ακρίβεια** (akríveia) from ακριβής — "accuracy, precision"
- **βεβαιότητα** (vevaiótita) from βέβαιος — "certainty"
- **δικαιοσύνη** (dikaiosýni) from δίκαιος — "justice"

### Notes
Highly productive pattern. Suffix -ότητα most common with -ός/-ή/-ό adjectives; -εια with -ής adjectives; -οσύνη with moral/character adjectives. These abstract nouns are fundamental to truth-domain discourse.

---

## 7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION

### Pattern
**[VERB/ROOT STEM] + -της / -τρια (m./f.) / -ας**

Agent nouns marking persons characterized by truth-domain behaviors. Productive with lying/deception vocabulary, less so with truth-telling.

### Examples
- **ψεύτης** (pséftis) from ψεύδομαι — "liar" (masculine)
- **ψεύτρα** (pséftra) — "liar" (feminine)
- **υποκριτής** (ipokritís) from υποκρίνομαι — "hypocrite"
- **απατεώνας** (apateónas) from απατώ — "swindler, fraud"

### Notes
Agent nouns highly productive for negative truth-domain behaviors (lying, deceiving, hypocrisy). No common single-word agent noun for "truth-teller" (*αληθευτής not standard; λέω την αλήθεια used instead). Asymmetry reflects cultural/linguistic focus on marking deviations from truth-telling norm.

---

## 8. LIGHT VERB CONSTRUCTIONS (TRUTH/LIE)

### Pattern
**λέω (léo) "say/tell" + [TRUTH/LIE NOUN-ACC]**

Light verb λέω combines with truth/lie nouns to express speech acts. Extremely productive and high-frequency pattern.

### Examples
- **λέω την αλήθεια** (léo tin alítheia) — "tell the truth"
- **λέω ψέματα** (léo psémata) — "tell lies" (note plural)
- **λέω το ψέμα** (léo to pséma) — "tell the lie" (specific lie)

### Notes
λέω + αλήθεια/ψέμα is the standard construction for truth-telling/lying speech acts. Note ψέματα typically plural in this construction. Alternative verbs: αληθεύω "tell truth" (less common), ψεύδομαι "lie" (more formal).

---

## 9. LIGHT VERB CONSTRUCTIONS (ERROR)

### Pattern
**κάνω (káno) "make/do" + λάθος (láthos) "mistake"**

Light verb κάνω combines with λάθος to express making errors. Standard construction for error commission.

### Examples
- **κάνω λάθος** (káno láthos) — "make a mistake, be wrong"
- **κάνω μεγάλο λάθος** (káno megálo láthos) — "make a big mistake"
- **έκανα λάθος** (ékana láthos) — "I made a mistake" (past)

### Notes
κάνω λάθος is the unmarked expression for error-making. Alternative: έχω λάθος (écho láthos) "be wrong" (stative). The construction is extremely high-frequency across all registers.

---

## 10. PREPOSITIONAL PHRASES (MANNER/ACCORDANCE)

### Pattern
**[PREPOSITION] + [TRUTH-DOMAIN NOUN-ACC]**

Prepositional phrases functioning as adverbials or discourse markers. Multiple prepositions combine with truth-domain nouns.

### Examples
- **στην αλήθεια** (stin alítheia) — "in truth, actually" (σε + την)
- **κατά την αλήθεια** (katá tin alítheia) — "according to truth, truthfully"
- **κατά λάθος** (katá láthos) — "by mistake, accidentally"
- **εκ λάθους** (ek láthous) — "by mistake" (formal, genitive)

### Notes
Prepositional phrases with truth-domain nouns grammaticalize as manner adverbials and discourse markers. στην αλήθεια and κατά λάθος are extremely high-frequency. Genitive case with εκ (archaic/formal).

---

## 11. REVELATION/DISCOVERY CONSTRUCTIONS

### Pattern
**[TRUTH NOUN-NOM] + βγαίνω (vgaíno) "come out" / φέρνω (férno) "bring" + [LOCATIVE]**

Metaphorical constructions presenting truth as hidden entity that emerges or is brought to light.

### Examples
- **βγαίνει η αλήθεια στη φόρα** (vgaíni i alítheia sti fóra) — "the truth comes out/to light"
- **φέρνω την αλήθεια στο φως** (férno tin alítheia sto fos) — "bring the truth to light"
- **αποκαλύπτω την αλήθεια** (apokalýpto tin alítheia) — "reveal the truth"

### Notes
Truth conceptualized as hidden entity requiring revelation. βγαίνω στη φόρα (literally "come out to the surface") is idiomatic. φως "light" common metaphorical target. These constructions are culturally salient and high-frequency.

---

## 12. DETECTION/EXPOSURE CONSTRUCTIONS

### Pattern
**[AGENT] + πιάνω (piáno) "catch" / ξεσκεπάζω (xeskepázo) "uncover" + [OBJECT] + [LOCATIVE/MANNER]**

Constructions for detecting lies or exposing falsehood, using verbs of catching or uncovering.

### Examples
- **πιάνω κάποιον στο ψέμα** (piáno kápion sto pséma) — "catch someone in a lie"
- **ξεσκεπάζω το ψέμα** (xeskepázo to pséma) — "expose/uncover the lie"

### Notes
Lies conceptualized as coverings or traps that can be detected. πιάνω "catch" + στο ψέμα is idiomatic. ξεσκεπάζω literally "un-cover," metaphorically "expose." These constructions are common in narrative and investigative contexts.

---

## 13. INTENSIFICATION (ADJECTIVE + TRUTH/LIE)

### Pattern
**[INTENSIFYING ADJECTIVE] + [TRUTH/LIE NOUN]**

Adjectival modification of truth/lie nouns for emphasis or qualification. Specific adjectives conventionalized with each noun.

### Examples
- **η γυμνή αλήθεια** (i gymní alítheia) — "the naked truth"
- **η πικρή αλήθεια** (i pikrí alítheia) — "the bitter truth"
- **η σκληρή αλήθεια** (i sklirí alítheia) — "the harsh truth"
- **άσπρο ψέμα** (áspro pséma) — "white lie"
- **κοτσάνα ψέματα** (kotsána psémata) — "whopping lies" (colloquial)

### Notes
Truth collocates with adjectives emphasizing unpleasantness (πικρή "bitter," σκληρή "harsh") or bareness (γυμνή "naked," καθαρή "pure"). Lies show color metaphors (άσπρο "white" = harmless) and colloquial intensifiers (κοτσάνα "big/whopping").

---

## 14. PROVERBIAL/GNOMIC CONSTRUCTIONS

### Pattern
**[TRUTH/LIE NOUN-NOM] + [VERB] + [COMPLEMENT]**

Fixed proverbial expressions about truth and lies. Subject-verb-complement structure with generic/gnomic meaning.

### Examples
- **το ψέμα έχει κοντά ποδάρια** (to pséma échi kontá podária) — "lies have short legs" (lies don't get far)
- **η αλήθεια πονάει** (i alítheia ponáei) — "the truth hurts"
- **η αλήθεια θα σε ελευθερώσει** (i alítheia tha se eleftherósi) — "the truth will set you free"

### Notes
Proverbial expressions personify truth/lies with agency and effects. These are culturally salient fixed expressions used in moral discourse. Biblical influence evident in third example.

---

## 15. COMPOUND FORMATIONS (ψευδ- PREFIX)

### Pattern
**ψευδ(ο)- + [NOUN/ADJECTIVE]**

Highly productive compounding with ψευδ- "false/pseudo-" prefix to create critical terms marking false pretenders to categories.

### Examples
- **ψευδοεπιστήμη** (psevdoepistími) — "pseudoscience" (ψευδ- + επιστήμη "science")
- **ψευδοδημοκρατία** (psevdodimokratía) — "pseudo-democracy" (ψευδ- + δημοκρατία)
- **ψευδοδιανοούμενος** (psevdodianoúmenos) — "pseudo-intellectual"
- **ψευδοπληροφορία** (psevdopliροforía) — "misinformation"

### Notes
Extremely productive pattern for creating critical/polemical terms. ψευδ- marks false claim to category membership. Pattern extends to contemporary domains (fake news, misinformation). Linking vowel -ο- appears before consonant-initial bases.

---

## 16. COMPOUND FORMATIONS (ορθ- PREFIX)

### Pattern
**ορθ(ο)- + [NOUN]**

Productive compounding with ορθ- "correct/straight/ortho-" prefix. Creates both technical terms and correctness vocabulary.

### Examples
- **ορθογραφία** (orthografía) — "orthography, correct spelling" (ορθ- + γραφία "writing")
- **ορθοδοξία** (orthodoxía) — "orthodoxy" (ορθ- + δοξία "belief")
- **ορθολογισμός** (orthologismós) — "rationalism" (ορθ- + λογισμός "reasoning")
- **ορθοπεδική** (orthopedikí) — "orthopedics" (ορθ- + παιδ- "child")

### Notes
ορθ- productive in multiple domains: linguistic (orthography), religious (orthodoxy), rational (rationalism), medical (orthopedics). Linking vowel -ο- before consonants. Pattern creates both everyday and technical vocabulary.

---

## 17. DISCOURSE MARKERS

### Pattern
**[TRUTH-DOMAIN NOUN/ADVERB] → PRAGMATIC MARKER**

Grammaticalization of truth-domain expressions as discourse markers for emphasis, confirmation, or surprise.

### Examples
- **αλήθεια;** (alítheia?) — "really? is that so?" (question particle)
- **πραγματικά** (pragmatiká) — "really, actually" (confirmation/emphasis)
- **ουσιαστικά** (ousiastiká) — "basically, essentially" (reformulation)
- **ακριβώς** (akrivós) — "exactly" (agreement marker)

### Notes
Several truth-domain terms grammaticalize as discourse markers. αλήθεια as question particle expresses surprise/doubt. πραγματικά and ουσιαστικά function as epistemic/reformulation markers. ακριβώς marks agreement/confirmation. These are extremely high-frequency in spoken Greek.

---

## 18. CORRECTION CONSTRUCTIONS

### Pattern
**[AGENT] + διορθώνω (diorthóno) / σωστεύω (sostévo) + [ERROR-ACC]**

Constructions for correcting errors using specialized correction verbs.

### Examples
- **διορθώνω το λάθος** (diorthóno to láthos) — "I correct the mistake"
- **σωστεύω την απάντηση** (sostévo tin apántisi) — "I correct the answer"
- **διόρθωση λαθών** (diórthosi lathón) — "correction of errors" (nominal)

### Notes
Two main correction verbs: διορθώνω (formal, from δι- + ορθ-) and σωστεύω (from σωστός). Both take error/incorrect item as direct object. Nominalization διόρθωση common in editorial and educational contexts.

---

## 19. VALIDATION/INVALIDATION CONSTRUCTIONS

### Pattern
**[AGENT] + βεβαιώνω (vevaióno) / ακυρώνω (akyróno) + [DOCUMENT/CLAIM-ACC]**

Legal/administrative constructions for validating or invalidating documents, claims, or procedures.

### Examples
- **βεβαιώνω την πληροφορία** (vevaióno tin pliροforía) — "I confirm the information"
- **ακυρώνω το έγγραφο** (akyróno to éngrafo) — "I invalidate/cancel the document"
- **βεβαίωση γνησιότητας** (vevaíosi gnisiótitas) — "certificate of authenticity"

### Notes
βεβαιώνω "confirm/certify" and ακυρώνω "invalidate/cancel" are standard administrative verbs. Nominalizations (βεβαίωση "confirmation," ακύρωση "cancellation") common in official documents. These constructions mark institutional authority over validity.

---

## 20. ADMISSION/ACKNOWLEDGMENT CONSTRUCTIONS

### Pattern
**[AGENT] + παραδέχομαι (paradéchome) / ομολογώ (omologó) + [TRUTH/ERROR-ACC]**

Constructions for admitting truth or acknowledging error. Verbs mark speaker's acceptance of previously denied/hidden information.

### Examples
- **παραδέχομαι το λάθος μου** (paradéchome to láthos mou) — "I admit my mistake"
- **ομολογώ την αλήθεια** (omologó tin alítheia) — "I confess the truth"
- **παραδέχομαι ότι είχα άδικο** (paradéchome óti ícha ádiko) — "I admit that I was wrong"

### Notes
παραδέχομαι "admit/acknowledge" and ομολογώ "confess" mark speaker's acceptance of truth or error. Common in interpersonal conflict resolution and public apologies. Constructions often include possessive (το λάθος μου "my mistake").

---

## SUMMARY OBSERVATIONS

Greek shows rich constructional resources for truth-domain meanings:

**Highly productive patterns:**
- Light verb constructions (λέω + truth/lie, κάνω + error)
- Compounding (ψευδ-, ορθ- prefixes)
- Abstract nominalization (-ότητα, -εια suffixes)
- Prepositional phrases as discourse markers

**Metaphorical constructions:**
- Truth as hidden entity requiring revelation (βγαίνει στη φόρα)
- Lies as coverings requiring exposure (ξεσκεπάζω)
- Truth as painful but liberating (η αλήθεια πονάει)

**Asymmetries:**
- Agent nouns productive for negative behaviors (ψεύτης, απατεώνας) but not truth-telling
- ψευδ- compounds highly productive for critical terms
- Truth vocabulary shows richer idiomatic/proverbial expressions than lie vocabulary

**Grammaticalization:**
- Truth-domain adverbs → discourse markers (πραγματικά, ουσιαστικά, ακριβώς)
- Prepositional phrases → manner adverbials (κατά λάθος, στην αλήθεια)

**Register variation:**
- Formal: εκ λάθους, κατά την αλήθεια, ομολογώ
- Colloquial: στ' αλήθεια, κοτσάνα ψέματα, πιάνω στο ψέμα
