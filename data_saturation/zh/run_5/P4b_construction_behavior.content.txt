## Summary Notes

**No constructions from P4a have been added, removed, or restructured.** All naturalness ratings are based strictly on information provided in P4a (frequency statements, register notes, and the presence or absence of attested examples).

**Marginal ratings** are assigned in exactly two cases:
- The periphrastic inchoative (变得 + ADJ / 成为 + N), reported as grammatically available but infrequent, with no attested example provided.
- Lower-frequency 性 nominalizations (诚实性, 真诚性, 虚假性), explicitly described in P4a as restricted to formal or analytical registers and lower in frequency.

**No [UNCERTAIN] ratings** are assigned, as P4a provides sufficient information to distinguish core from marginal patterns in all cases.
