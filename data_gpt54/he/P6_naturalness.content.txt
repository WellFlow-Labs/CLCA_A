## 2. RESULTATIVE CHANGE: later turning out to be right

### Pattern
**יצא + truth-related predicate**

### Examples
- **יצא צודק** → *yatsa tsodek* → “turned out to be right”

### Notes
This is the clearest dynamic expression in the CONTEXT for a change-over-time reading. It does not mean that someone “became morally right”; rather, later events showed that the person’s earlier position was right.  
This pattern is attested only with **צודק** in the provided material. I do **not** extend it automatically to other predicates.

---

## 3. REVELATION / DISCOVERY OF TRUTH

### Pattern
**Verb of discovery/revelation + את האמת**

### Examples
- **לגלות את האמת** → *legalot et ha-ʾemet* → “to discover / reveal the truth”
- **להוציא את האמת לאור** → *lehotsi et ha-ʾemet la-ʾor* → “to bring the truth to light”

### Notes
These are dynamic because truth is presented as something hidden that becomes known or exposed.  
The change is not encoded as “truth becomes true,” but as access to truth changing over time.

Related expression:
- **לדעת את האמת** → *ladaʿat et ha-ʾemet* → “to know the truth”  
  This is more stative, but it naturally pairs with the dynamic expressions above.

## 4. SPEECH ACTS THAT PRODUCE OR OPPOSE TRUTHFULNESS

### Pattern
**Verb + truth/falsehood noun**

### Examples
- **לומר את האמת** → *lomar et ha-ʾemet* → “to tell the truth”
- **לספר שקר** → *lesaper sheqer* → “to tell a lie”

### Notes
These are dynamic event expressions involving truthful vs. false speech.  
They do not mean that the proposition itself changes state; rather, a speaker performs an act of truthful reporting or lying.

This is one of the strongest dynamic oppositions in the dataset:
- **לומר את האמת** vs. **לספר שקר**

---

## 5. CAUSATIVE: causing a claim or position to count as justified/right

### Pattern
**להצדיק + NP**

### Examples
- **להצדיק את הטענה** → *lehatsdiq et ha-taʿanah* → “to justify the claim”

### Notes
This is the clearest causative morphology in the CONTEXT.  
It does not mean “make true”; it means “make justified / show as warranted.”  
So this belongs specifically to the **צ־ד־ק** cluster, not to all truth terms in general.

Related forms in the same semantic area:
- **הצדקה** → *hatsdaqah* → “justification”
- **מוצדק** → *mutsdaq* → “justified”
- **בלתי מוצדק** → *bilti mutsdaq* → “unjustified”

These support a dynamic interpretation where argument or evidence can lead to a status of justification.

---

## 6. PROVING OR VINDICATING SOMEONE AS RIGHT

### Pattern
**להוכיח ש־ + מישהו צודק**

### Examples
- **להוכיח שמישהו צודק** → *lehokhiaḥ she-mishehu tsodek* → “to prove that someone is right”

### Notes
This is causation in an evidential/argumentative sense: evidence or reasoning establishes that a person’s position is right.  
Again, this is tied specifically to **צודק**, not to **נכון** or **אמת** in the dataset.

It is closely related to:
- **יצא צודק** → later events vindicate someone
- **אתה צודק** → stative acknowledgment

## 7. UNDERMINING RELIABILITY / CREDIBILITY

### Pattern
**Verb + באמינות / במהימנות**

### Examples
- **לפגוע באמינות** → *lifgoaʿ be-aminut* → “to damage credibility / reliability”
- **להטיל ספק במהימנות** → *lehatil safeq bi-mehemanut* → “to cast doubt on the reliability/credibility of—”

### Notes
These are important dynamic expressions for movement toward a **non-truth-related evaluative state**, especially in the reliability domain.

They do not mean that a source becomes false. Rather:
- **לפגוע באמינות** lowers credibility
- **להטיל ספק במהימנות** challenges assessed trustworthiness

This is one of the clearest “becomes less reliable” areas in the dataset, though expressed causatively/periphrastically rather than by a simple inchoative verb.

---

## 8. TESTING STABILITY OF RELIABILITY

### Pattern
**test/check noun + reliability noun**

### Examples
- **בדיקת אמינות** → *bediqat aminut* → “reliability check / credibility check”
- **מבחן אמינות** → *mivḥan aminut* → “reliability test”
- **מבחן האמת** → *mivḥan ha-ʾemet* → “real test / test of reality”

### Notes
These expressions encode dynamics of **stability under examination**.

- **בדיקת אמינות / מבחן אמינות**: a system, person, or source is checked for dependable performance or credibility.
- **מבחן האמת**: a claim, plan, or idea faces the real-world test that shows whether it holds up.

These are not simple state changes, but they are strongly dynamic: truth-related status is evaluated through a process over time.

## 9. PRECISION AS REFINEMENT OR CORRECTION

### Pattern
**לדייק / ליתר דיוק / אם לדייק**

### Examples
- **לדייק** → *ledayeq* → “to be precise; to refine; to state more exactly”
- **אם לדייק** → *ʾim ledayeq* → “to be precise”
- **ליתר דיוק** → *le-yeter diyuq* → “more precisely”

### Notes
This is a dynamic area centered on **improving exactness** rather than changing truth value.

The relevant change is:
- from a rougher formulation
- to a more exact formulation

Related negative/stability expressions:
- **חוסר דיוק** → “lack of precision”
- **אי-דיוק** → “inaccuracy”
- **לא מדויק / לא לגמרי מדויק** → “not accurate / not entirely accurate”

So the **דיוק** cluster strongly supports dynamic refinement, but not a simple “become accurate” construction in the dataset.

## 10. SHIFT TO FORMAL NEGATIVE EVALUATION

### Pattern
**formal labeling of falsehood rather than simple denial**

### Examples
- **הצהרה כוזבת** → *hatsaharah kozevet* → “false declaration”
- **טענה שקרית** → *taʿanah sheqrit* → “false claim”
- **עדות כוזבת** → *ʿedut kozevet* → “false testimony”
- **מצג כוזב** → *matsag kozév* → “false representation”

### Notes
These are not inchoatives, but they encode an evaluative outcome: a statement, testimony, or representation is classified as false in a formal register.

This contrasts with the more immediate dynamic denial:
- **זה לא נכון** → “that is not true”

So Hebrew here often encodes change in discourse stance not by “becoming false,” but by moving from neutral mention to explicit institutional classification.

## 11. CORRESPONDENCE TO FACTS OR REALITY AS A DYNAMIC ASSESSMENT

### Pattern
**תואם / אינו תואם + facts/reality**

### Examples
- **תואם את העובדות** → *toʾem et ha-uvdot* → “matches the facts”
- **תואם למציאות** → *toʾem la-metsiʾut* → “corresponds to reality”
- **אינו תואם למציאות** → *eino toʾem la-metsiʾut* → “does not correspond to reality”
- **לא לגמרי תואם למציאות** → *lo le-gamrei toʾem la-metsiʾut* → “not entirely true to reality”

### Notes
These expressions are usually evaluative outcomes of comparison.  
The dataset does **not** provide a dedicated “become corresponding / cease to correspond” construction.  
Still, they are dynamic in use because a description, report, or representation is assessed against facts or reality.

This is especially common in formal correction and public argument.

## 12. AUTHENTICITY AND FORGERY: dynamic opposition, but no clear inchoative pattern

### Pattern
**classification as original/authentic vs. forged/fake**

### Examples
- **המסמך המקורי** → *ha-mismakh ha-meqori* → “the original document”
- **אותנטי / לא אותנטי** → *otenti / lo otenti* → “authentic / inauthentic”
- **מסמך מזויף** → *mismakh mezuyaf* → “forged document”
- **מוצר מזויף** → *mutsar mezuyaf* → “counterfeit product”
- **זיוף מסמכים** → *ziyuf mismakhim* → “document forgery”
- **זיוף חתימה** → *ziyuf ḥatimah* → “signature forgery”

### Notes
This domain clearly encodes a dynamic contrast between genuine and falsified states, but the dataset mainly gives:
- nouns of process: **זיוף**
- result-state adjectives: **מזויף**
- authenticity labels: **אותנטי, מקורי**

What is **not** clearly provided is a common inchoative pattern like “become forged” or “become authentic.”  
So the dynamic meaning is present mainly through **process/result classification**, not through a dedicated become-construction.

## 13. STABILITY VS. FAILURE OF TRUTH-RELATED STATUS

### Pattern
**scalar or tested status**

### Examples
- **אמינות גבוהה / נמוכה** → *aminut gvohah / nemukhah* → “high / low reliability”
- **מדויק מאוד** → *meduyaq meʾod* → “very accurate”
- **לא לגמרי מדויק** → *lo le-gamrei meduyaq* → “not entirely accurate”
- **זהה לחלוטין** → *zehe la-ḥalutin* → “completely identical”
- **כמעט זהה ל־** → *kimʿat zehe le-* → “almost identical to—”

### Notes
The dataset often encodes stability not by verbs of becoming, but by:
- scalar grading
- testing
- comparison
- negated evaluation

So truth-related meanings in Hebrew here are often **stable assessed states** rather than overtly inchoative ones.

---

## 14. SUMMARY

### Strongly attested dynamic patterns
- **יצא צודק** — later turned out to be right
- **לגלות את האמת / להוציא את האמת לאור** — truth becomes known/revealed
- **לומר את האמת / לספר שקר** — truthful vs. false speech acts
- **להצדיק את הטענה** — cause a claim to count as justified
- **להוכיח שמישהו צודק** — prove someone right
- **לפגוע באמינות / להטיל ספק במהימנות** — undermine credibility
- **בדיקת אמינות / מבחן אמינות / מבחן האמת** — test stability of reliability/truth
- **לדייק / ליתר דיוק / אם לדייק** — refine toward greater precision

### Explicit absences
- No general common pattern in the dataset for:
  - “X becomes true”
  - “X becomes false”
  - “X becomes reliable”
  - “X becomes authentic”
  - “X becomes corresponding to reality”

### General note
In this dataset, Hebrew tends to encode truth-related dynamics through:
- **revelation**
- **testing**
- **justification**
- **correction/refinement**
- **credibility damage**
- **resultative vindication**

more than through a single productive “become + truth-state” construction.
