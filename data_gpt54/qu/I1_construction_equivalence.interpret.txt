## 2. Substitution Patterns (Narrative)

The dataset shows clear support for **ATTR**, **ADNOM**, **NEGATION**, and parts of **NOMINALIZATION**.

**BECOME** is explicitly absent: P4a states “no common expression identified,” and P4b repeats that no clear common inchoative construction is attested. There is therefore no basis here for saying that **REVELATION** replaces **BECOME**, because **REVELATION** is also not attested.

For **CAUSATION**, the available material is restricted to the **error** domain:
- **pantachiy** is a compact causative verb.
- **pantayman churay** is a multi-word periphrastic construction, with **churay** functioning as a light verb/carrier and **pantayman** supplying the directional/error content.

This means causation is only partially represented, and only for causing error or misleading. No corresponding truth-establishing causative is given, and no character-changing causative is given.

**DISCONFIRMATION** does not have a dedicated construction in the supplied material. Instead, rejection of truth/correctness is expressed through **NEGATION**, especially **mana allin** and more uncertainly **mana chiqaq**. So in this dataset, disconfirmative meaning is descriptively **substituted by negation**, not by a separate operator.

There is no evidence that **DISCOVERY** substitutes for **CAUSATION**, because no discovery/revelation/verification construction is provided at all.

**NEGATION** is clearly **analytic**, using the separate particle **mana** before the adjective. No morphological negative marking is given in P4a/P4b for this domain.

**VERIFICATION** and **DISCONFIRMATION** do not share the same construction, because **VERIFICATION** is unattested, while **DISCONFIRMATION** is only indirectly represented through analytic negation.

There are also asymmetries:
- **Truth/falsity speech expressions** are better represented on the falsehood side: **llulla simi** is [CORE], while **chiqaq simi** is only [MARGINAL].
- **Agentive person reference** is available for the liar/deception side: **llullaq**, **llulla runa**. No equally secure truth-teller counterpart is provided.
- **Causation** is available for causing error, but not for causing truth or causing truthful character.

### Structural Gaps

- **REVELATION**: **MISSING (structural gap)**
- **CAUSATION (character)**: **MISSING (structural gap)**
- **VERIFICATION**: **MISSING (structural gap)**
- Dedicated **DISCONFIRMATION** operator: **SUBSTITUTED BY NEGATION**

### Lexical Gaps
- No attested truth-teller counterpart to **llullaq** / **llulla runa**
- No attested positive truth-causative counterpart to **pantachiy**
- No attested inchoative lexeme/construction for ‘become true’, ‘become mistaken’, or ‘become a liar’

### Conceptual Gaps
- None can be established from P4a/P4b alone. The materials show missing or unattested constructions, but do not state that these meanings are conceptually blocked.

### Asymmetric Gaps
- **Negative vs. positive**: negative evaluation is clearly available via **mana allin**, while no dedicated verification operator is given on the positive side.
- **Truth vs. falsity**: falsity/deception is better represented than truth in agentive and causative domains.
- **Speech domain**: **llulla simi** is core, while **chiqaq simi** is marginal.
- **Character domain**: liar expressions are attested; no parallel truth-teller expression is securely attested.
