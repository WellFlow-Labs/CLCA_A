# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P4a CONSTRUCTIONS
# Language: Chinese (zh)

---

## 1. ATTRIBUTION

### Pattern
Subject + 是 + [truth-domain adjective/noun] + 的
*or*
Subject + [truth-domain adjective] (predicative, no copula required)

### Examples
- 这个消息是真实的。/ Zhège xiāoxi shì zhēnshí de. / "This piece of news is authentic."
- 他的陈述完全正确。/ Tā de chénshù wánquán zhèngquè. / "His statement is completely correct."

### Notes
The copular frame X + 是 + ADJ + 的 is the standard attributive predication for stative truth-domain adjectives (真实, 虚假, 正确, 准确, 可靠). The bare predicative frame (subject + adjective, no 是) is equally grammatical and common, especially with degree adverbs (完全, 非常). Both frames are used across all registers.

---

## 2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION

### Pattern
Subject + 不 + [truth-domain adjective]
*or*
Subject + 是 + 假的 / 虚假的 / 错误的

### Examples
- 这种说法不正确。/ Zhè zhǒng shuōfǎ bù zhèngquè. / "This formulation is incorrect."
- 这是假的。/ Zhè shì jiǎ de. / "This is fake."

### Notes
The prefix 不 is the productive negation morpheme for adjectives in this domain (不真实, 不正确, 不准确, 不可靠, 不诚实). The copular frame X + 是 + 假/虚假/错误 + 的 is used for nominal or more emphatic predications. No suppletive negative forms exist; negation is fully regular.

---

## 3. ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION

### Pattern
[truth-domain adjective/noun] + 的 + Noun

### Examples
- 真实的情况 / zhēnshí de qíngkuàng / "the actual situation, the true state of affairs"
- 虚假的陈述 / xūjiǎ de chénshù / "a false statement, a fraudulent representation"

### Notes
Adnominal modification in Chinese is uniformly pre-nominal, marked by the particle 的 between modifier and head noun. Truth-domain adjectives (真实, 虚假, 正确, 准确, 可靠, 诚实) all follow this pattern without exception. Some collocations are highly lexicalized (虚假广告, 可靠来源) and may appear without 的 in fixed attributive compounds.

---

## 4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE

### Pattern
变得 + [truth-domain adjective]
*or*
成为 + [truth-domain noun]
*or*
[fixed inchoative expressions specific to this domain]

### Examples
- 真相大白。/ Zhēnxiàng dàbái. / "The truth has come to light." (lit. "the true facts have become fully clear")
- 弄假成真。/ Nòng jiǎ chéng zhēn. / "What started as a pretense has become real." (lit. "manipulate-fake-become-true")

### Notes
General inchoative constructions (变得 + ADJ, 成为 + N) are grammatically available but infrequent with truth-domain adjectives. The domain instead favors lexicalized inchoative expressions: 真相大白 (truth comes to light), 弄假成真 (fake becomes real), 坐实 (a claim becomes confirmed fact). These fixed forms carry the inchoative meaning more naturally than periphrastic constructions.

---

## 5. CAUSATIVE

### Pattern
让/使 + NP + [truth-domain predicate]
*or*
[verb of revealing/exposing] + NP (causative-resultative)

### Examples
- 让他原形毕露。/ Ràng tā yuánxíng bì lù. / "Cause him to show his true colors / expose him for what he really is."
- 这一调查使真相大白。/ Zhè yī diàochá shǐ zhēnxiàng dàbái. / "This investigation caused the truth to come to light."

### Notes
Periphrastic causatives with 让 (permissive/causative) and 使 (causative, more formal) are the standard productive frames. In this domain, causative meaning is also frequently encoded in the verb itself (揭露, 揭示, 戳穿, 坐实), which take a truth-domain noun as direct object and imply a causer-agent subject. No morphological causative exists in Chinese.

---

## 6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION

### Pattern
[truth-domain adjective/verb] + 性

### Examples
- 真实性 / zhēnshíxìng / "authenticity, veracity (as an abstract property)"
- 准确性 / zhǔnquèxìng / "accuracy, precision (as an abstract property)"

### Notes
The suffix 性 is the productive nominalizer for abstract properties in this domain, attested on 真实性, 准确性, 可靠性, 正确性, 欺骗性, 实质性, 假设性, and others. Frequency varies: 真实性, 准确性, 可靠性, and 实质性 are high-frequency; 诚实性, 真诚性, and 虚假性 are lower-frequency and more restricted to formal or analytical registers. The suffix attaches to adjectival and some verbal bases; it does not attach to bare roots (真, 假, 伪) directly.

---

## 7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION

### Pattern
[verbal base] + 者

### Examples
- 欺骗者 / qīpiànzhě / "deceiver, one who deceives"
- 伪造者 / wěizàozhě / "forger, counterfeiter"

### Notes
The suffix 者 derives agentive nouns from verbal bases in this domain: 欺骗者, 伪造者, 伪装者, 伪善者. It does not attach to purely adjectival bases. There is no productive agentive nominalization for "truth-teller" as a single lexical item; the concept is expressed periphrastically (说真话的人, "a person who tells the truth") or via character-evaluation adjectives (诚实的人). The asymmetry between available agentive nominalizations for deceivers and the absence of one for truth-tellers is a structural feature of the domain.

---

## 8. VERB-OBJECT COLLOCATIONS WITH TRUTH-DOMAIN NOUNS

### Pattern
[Action verb] + [truth-domain noun]

### Examples
- 揭露真相 / jiēlù zhēnxiàng / "to expose the truth"
- 歪曲事实 / wāiqū shìshí / "to distort the facts"

### Notes
Truth-domain nouns (真相, 事实, 谎言, 真理) participate in a dense network of verb-object collocations that encode the full range of epistemic actions: disclosure (揭露, 查明, 还原), concealment (掩盖, 隐瞒), distortion (歪曲, 捏造), pursuit (追求, 坚持), and verification (核实, 查实). These collocations are highly conventionalized and constitute the primary syntactic environment for truth-domain nouns.

---

## 9. ADVERBIAL TRUTH-FRAME

### Pattern
[truth-domain adverb] + Verb
*or*
[truth-domain noun] + 上 + 是 + …

### Examples
- 如实陈述 / rúshí chénshù / "to state truthfully, to give an accurate account"
- 实质上是欺骗。/ Shízhì shàng shì qīpiàn. / "In substance it is deception."

### Notes
Two distinct adverbial patterns are attested. First, the adverb 如实 (truthfully, as it actually is) pre-modifies action verbs (反映, 陈述, 汇报) to assert accurate correspondence in reporting. Second, the frame NOUN + 上 + 是 (本质上是, 实质上是, 实际上是) asserts what something fundamentally or actually amounts to, contrasting surface appearance with underlying reality. Both patterns are productive and common across formal registers.
