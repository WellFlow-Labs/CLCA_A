# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2a Derivational System
# Language: Tagalog (tl)

---

**Note on scope:** This step documents attested derivational morphology for the P1 lexical items. Forms already fully analyzed in P1 or P1b (e.g., *katotohanan*, *katunayan*, *pagkakamali*, *patunayan*, *patunayan*, *napatunayan*, etc.) are cross-referenced rather than re-derived where they were already the output of a derivational process. The focus here is on derivations not yet documented, plus systematic documentation of the derivational relationships already identified. Verbal aspect paradigms are included where the forms are conventionalized and attested; nonce inflectional forms are excluded.

---

## Item 1 — Base Form: totoo

---

**Derived Form 1**
- **Base Form:** totoo
- **Derived Form:** katotohanan
- **Transliteration:** katotohanan
- **Literal Breakdown:** *ka-* + *totoo* (with internal vowel adjustment: *totoo* → *totohan*) + *-an*; circumfix nominalizing the root into an abstract state or domain
- **Natural English Translation:** truth, reality, the truth of the matter
- **Usage:** Refers to truth as an abstract property or to a specific true state of affairs; used across formal, moral, philosophical, and legal registers. Fully documented in P1 item 2.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2**
- **Base Form:** totoo
- **Derived Form:** totoong-totoo
- **Transliteration:** totoong-totoo
- **Literal Breakdown:** *totoo* + *-ng* (linker) + *totoo*; full reduplication with intervening linker, producing emphatic intensification
- **Natural English Translation:** absolutely true, really and truly real
- **Usage:** Emphatic form asserting the unambiguous reality or truth of something, typically in response to doubt. Documented in P1b.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 3**
- **Base Form:** totoo
- **Derived Form:** makatotohanan
- **Transliteration:** makatotohanan
- **Literal Breakdown:** *maka-* + *katotohanan* + *-an*; the prefix *maka-* expresses conformity with or orientation toward the base; the base here is the derived nominal *katotohanan*
- **Natural English Translation:** realistic, truthful, true-to-life
- **Usage:** Applied to representations, narratives, or portrayals that accurately reflect reality; common in aesthetic and critical evaluation. Fully documented in P1 item 18.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 4**
- **Base Form:** totoo
- **Derived Form:** pagkatotoo
- **Transliteration:** pagkatotoo
- **Literal Breakdown:** *pagka-* (nominalizing prefix expressing state or quality of being) + *totoo*
- **Natural English Translation:** the state of being true, the quality of being real, the fact of something being the case
- **Usage:** Refers to the abstract quality or state of being true or real; used in formal and philosophical registers (*Ang pagkatotoo ng pangyayari ay hindi mapapabulaanan* "The reality of the event cannot be denied"). Less frequent than *katotohanan* but attested in written Tagalog.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 5**
- **Base Form:** totoo
- **Derived Form:** itotoo
- **Transliteration:** itotoo
- **Literal Breakdown:** *i-* (object-focus verbal prefix) + *totoo*; future/contemplated aspect
- **Natural English Translation:** will make real, will actualize, will realize (bring into reality)
- **Usage:** Verbal form meaning to make something real or to actualize something previously planned or promised (*Itotoo ko ang aking pangako* "I will make my promise real / I will fulfill my promise"). Attested in spoken and written Tagalog in contexts of commitment and actualization.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 6**
- **Base Form:** totoo
- **Derived Form:** itinotoo
- **Transliteration:** itinototoo
- **Literal Breakdown:** *i-* + *-in-* (completive infix) + *totoo* + reduplication (imperfective); actor-focus imperfective of the *i-* verbal form
- **Natural English Translation:** is making real, is actualizing, is fulfilling
- **Usage:** Imperfective form of *itotoo*, describing an ongoing process of making something real or fulfilling a commitment (*Itinototoo niya ang kanyang mga pangako* "He/she is making his/her promises real"). Attested in written and spoken Tagalog.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 7**
- **Base Form:** totoo
- **Derived Form:** itinotoo
- **Transliteration:** itinotoo
- **Literal Breakdown:** *i-* + *-in-* (completive infix) + *totoo*; completed object-focus form
- **Natural English Translation:** made real, actualized, fulfilled, realized
- **Usage:** Completive form describing the accomplished actualization of something (*Itinotoo niya ang kanyang pangarap* "He/she made his/her dream real"). Attested in narrative and motivational contexts.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 8**
- **Base Form:** totoo
- **Derived Form:** magtotoo
- **Transliteration:** magtotoo
- **Literal Breakdown:** *mag-* (actor-focus verbal prefix) + *totoo*; future/contemplated aspect
- **Natural English Translation:** will tell the truth, will be truthful, will be honest
- **Usage:** Verbal form meaning to tell the truth or to be truthful in one's statements (*Magtotoo ka ba?* "Will you tell the truth?"). Attested in moral and interpersonal contexts.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 9**
- **Base Form:** totoo
- **Derived Form:** nagtotoo
- **Transliteration:** nagtotoo
- **Literal Breakdown:** *nag-* (actor-focus, imperfective) + *totoo* + reduplication
- **Natural English Translation:** is telling the truth, is being truthful
- **Usage:** Imperfective actor-focus form describing someone in the act of telling the truth (*Nagtotoo ba siya?* "Is he/she telling the truth?"). Common in interpersonal and evaluative discourse.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 10**
- **Base Form:** totoo
- **Derived Form:** nagtotoo
- **Transliteration:** nagtotoo

**Note:** The completive actor-focus form would be *nagtotoo* in some analyses but *nagtotoo* is more commonly the imperfective. The completive is *nagtotoo* with stress shift in some dialects, but this distinction is not consistently marked in writing. The form is attested; the aspectual distinction in writing is not always recoverable.

---

**Derived Form 11**
- **Base Form:** totoo
- **Derived Form:** pagtotoo
- **Transliteration:** pagtotoo
- **Literal Breakdown:** *pag-* (action nominalization) + *totoo*
- **Natural English Translation:** the act of telling the truth, truth-telling, being truthful
- **Usage:** Nominal form referring to the act or practice of telling the truth (*Ang pagtotoo ay isang katangian ng mabuting tao* "Truth-telling is a quality of a good person"). Attested in moral and didactic discourse.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 12**
- **Base Form:** totoo
- **Derived Form:** katotohanang-katotohanan
- **Transliteration:** katotohanang-katotohanan
- **Literal Breakdown:** *katotohanan* + *-ng* + *katotohanan*; full reduplication of the derived nominal with linker, producing emphatic intensification
- **Natural English Translation:** absolute truth, the very truth itself
- **Usage:** Emphatic form of *katotohanan* used in philosophical, religious, or rhetorical contexts to assert the highest or most fundamental level of truth. Documented in P1b as [MARGINAL].
- **Epistemic Status:** [MARGINAL]

- **Derived Form:** katotohanang-katotohanan

## Item 3 — Base Form: tunay

---

**Derived Form 1**
- **Base Form:** tunay
- **Derived Form:** katunayan
- **Transliteration:** katunayan
- **Literal Breakdown:** *ka-* + *tunay* (with adjustment: *tunay* → *tunay-an*) + *-an*; circumfix nominalizing the root
- **Natural English Translation:** proof, evidence, authenticity, the fact that
- **Usage:** Refers to evidence or proof establishing genuineness or factual truth; also functions as a conjunctive expression (*sa katunayan* "in fact"). Fully documented in P1 item 4.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2**
- **Base Form:** tunay
- **Derived Form:** tunay na tunay
- **Transliteration:** tunay na tunay
- **Literal Breakdown:** *tunay* + *na* (linker) + *tunay*; full reduplication with linker
- **Natural English Translation:** absolutely genuine, truly authentic
- **Usage:** Emphatic form asserting the highest degree of genuineness. Documented in P1b.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 3**
- **Base Form:** tunay
- **Derived Form:** patunay
- **Transliteration:** patunay
- **Literal Breakdown:** *pa-* (prefix with causative/directional/instrumental function) + *tunay*; "that which makes something genuine/proven," or "the means of proving"
- **Natural English Translation:** proof, evidence, attestation, verification (as a concrete item)
- **Usage:** Refers to a specific piece of evidence or a document that proves or verifies a claim; slightly more concrete than *katunayan*, often referring to a specific document or item (*Ipakita ang patunay ng pagbabayad* "Show proof of payment"). Documented in P1b.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 4**
- **Base Form:** tunay
- **Derived Form:** patunayan
- **Transliteration:** patunayan
- **Literal Breakdown:** *pa-* + *tunay* + *-an*; verbal form with *-an* suffix indicating the goal or locus of the action
- **Natural English Translation:** to prove, to verify, to demonstrate, to establish
- **Usage:** Verbal form meaning to demonstrate the truth or genuineness of something (*Patunayan mo ang iyong sinasabi* "Prove what you are saying"). Extremely common across all registers. Documented in P1b.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 5**
- **Base Form:** tunay
- **Derived Form:** napatunayan
- **Transliteration:** napatunayan
- **Literal Breakdown:** *na-* (completive aspect marker) + *patunayan*
- **Natural English Translation:** has been proven, was verified, has been established
- **Usage:** Completive form of *patunayan*, asserting that something has already been demonstrated or verified. Documented in P1b.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 6**
- **Base Form:** tunay
- **Derived Form:** mapatunayan
- **Transliteration:** mapatunayan
- **Literal Breakdown:** *ma-* (potential aspect prefix) + *patunayan*
- **Natural English Translation:** to be able to prove, can be proven
- **Usage:** Potential form expressing the ability or possibility of proving something. Documented in P1b.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 7**
- **Base Form:** tunay
- **Derived Form:** pinatunayan
- **Transliteration:** pinatunayan
- **Literal Breakdown:** *p-in-atunayan*; infix *-in-* inserted after initial consonant of *patunayan*, completive actor-focus
- **Natural English Translation:** proved, verified, demonstrated (by a specific agent)
- **Usage:** Completive actor-focus form describing the act of proving as completed by a specific agent. Documented in P1b.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 8**
- **Base Form:** tunay
- **Derived Form:** pinapatunayan
- **Transliteration:** pinapatunayan
- **Literal Breakdown:** *p-in-a-* + *patunayan* + reduplication of first syllable of root; imperfective actor-focus of *patunayan*
- **Natural English Translation:** is proving, is verifying, is demonstrating (ongoing action by a specific agent)
- **Usage:** Imperfective actor-focus form of *patunayan*, describing an ongoing act of proving (*Pinapatunayan niya ang kanyang inosensya* "He/she is proving his/her innocence"). Attested in legal and argumentative discourse.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 9**
- **Base Form:** tunay
- **Derived Form:** pagpapatunay
- **Transliteration:** pagpapatunay
- **Literal Breakdown:** *pag-* + *pa-* + *tunay* + reduplication; nominalization of the *patunayan* verbal action
- **Natural English Translation:** the act of proving, verification, demonstration, substantiation
- **Usage:** Nominal form referring to the process or act of proving or verifying something (*Ang pagpapatunay ng kanyang pagkakakilanlan ay kinakailangan* "Verification of his/her identity is required"). Common in administrative, legal, and formal contexts.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 10**
- **Base Form:** tunay
- **Derived Form:** pagpapatunayan
- **Transliteration:** pagpapatunayan
- **Literal Breakdown:** *pag-* + *pa-* + *tunay* + *-an* + reduplication; nominalization with goal/locus suffix
- **Natural English Translation:** the proving of something, the verification of something (with reference to a specific object)
- **Usage:** Nominal form with *-an* suffix, referring to the proving or verification of a specific thing (*Ang pagpapatunayan ng katotohanan ay mahirap* "The proving of the truth is difficult"). Attested in formal and written registers; less common than *pagpapatunay*.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 11**
- **Base Form:** tunay
- **Derived Form:** di-tunay
- **Transliteration:** di-tunay
- **Literal Breakdown:** *di-* (negation prefix, clitic form of *hindi*) + *tunay*
- **Natural English Translation:** inauthentic, not genuine, false
- **Usage:** Negated form of *tunay*, used to evaluate something as lacking genuine character or authenticity. Documented in P1 item 30 as [MARGINAL].
- **Epistemic Status:** [MARGINAL]

## Item 4 — Base Form: katunayan

*katunayan* is itself a derived form (from *tunay*). Further derivations from it as a base are limited.

---

**Derived Form 1**
- **Base Form:** katunayan
- **Derived Form:** sa katunayan
- **Transliteration:** sa katunayan
- **Literal Breakdown:** *sa* (oblique/locative marker) + *katunayan*; fixed prepositional phrase functioning as a discourse connector
- **Natural English Translation:** in fact, as a matter of fact, in truth
- **Usage:** Highly conventionalized discourse connector introducing a confirming or strengthening statement. Documented in P1 item 4 and P1b. Included here as a fixed phrasal derivation from the nominal base.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Note:** No further productive morphological derivations from *katunayan* as an immediate base are identified. The form participates in collocations (documented in P1b) but does not appear to generate new morphologically derived forms beyond those already captured under *tunay*.

## Item 5 — Base Form: tama

---

**Derived Form 1**
- **Base Form:** tama
- **Derived Form:** katamaan
- **Transliteration:** katamaan
- **Literal Breakdown:** *ka-* + *tama* + *-an*; circumfix nominalizing the root into an abstract quality
- **Natural English Translation:** correctness, rightness, appropriateness
- **Usage:** Abstract nominal referring to the quality of being correct or appropriate (*Ang katamaan ng kanyang desisyon ay napatunayan* "The correctness of his/her decision was proven"). Used in formal and evaluative discourse; less frequent in casual speech than the root *tama*.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2**
- **Base Form:** tama
- **Derived Form:** tama-tama
- **Transliteration:** tama-tama
- **Literal Breakdown:** *tama* + *tama*; full reduplication
- **Natural English Translation:** just right, exactly right, precisely correct, fitting
- **Usage:** Reduplication producing a meaning of exact or precise correctness, often with a sense of something being just the right amount or degree (*Tama-tama ang sukat* "The measurement is just right"). Common in everyday speech.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 3**
- **Base Form:** tama
- **Derived Form:** tamang-tama
- **Transliteration:** tamang-tama
- **Literal Breakdown:** *tama* + *-ng* (linker) + *tama*; full reduplication with linker
- **Natural English Translation:** exactly right, just right, perfectly timed, spot on
- **Usage:** Emphatic form of *tama-tama*, often used to describe something that is perfectly timed or exactly appropriate (*Tamang-tama ang pagdating niya* "His/her arrival was perfectly timed"). Very common in spoken Tagalog.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 4**
- **Base Form:** tama
- **Derived Form:** itama
- **Transliteration:** itama
- **Literal Breakdown:** *i-* (object-focus verbal prefix) + *tama*
- **Natural English Translation:** to correct (something), to fix, to set right
- **Usage:** Verbal form meaning to correct an error or to set something right (*Itama ang iyong mga pagkakamali* "Correct your mistakes"). Common in educational, editorial, and interpersonal contexts.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 5**
- **Base Form:** tama
- **Derived Form:** itinama
- **Transliteration:** itinama
- **Literal Breakdown:** *i-* + *-in-* (completive infix) + *tama*; completive object-focus form
- **Natural English Translation:** corrected, fixed, set right
- **Usage:** Completive form of *itama*, describing the accomplished correction of something (*Itinama niya ang kanyang sagot* "He/she corrected his/her answer"). Common in educational and editorial contexts.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 6**
- **Base Form:** tama
- **Derived Form:** itinatama
- **Transliteration:** itinatama
- **Literal Breakdown:** *i-* + *-in-* + *tama* + reduplication; imperfective object-focus form
- **Natural English Translation:** is correcting, is fixing, is setting right
- **Usage:** Imperfective form describing an ongoing act of correction (*Itinatama niya ang kanyang mga gawi* "He/she is correcting his/her habits"). Attested in educational and behavioral contexts.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 7**
- **Base Form:** tama
- **Derived Form:** pagtama
- **Transliteration:** pagtama
- **Literal Breakdown:** *pag-* (action nominalization) + *tama*
- **Natural English Translation:** the act of correcting, correction
- **Usage:** Nominal form referring to the act of correcting something (*Ang pagtama sa mga kamalian ay kailangan* "Correcting mistakes is necessary"). Attested in formal and didactic discourse.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 8**
- **Base Form:** tama
- **Derived Form:** magtama
- **Transliteration:** magtama
- **Literal Breakdown:** *mag-* (actor-focus verbal prefix) + *tama*
- **Natural English Translation:** to correct, to make right, to rectify
- **Usage:** Actor-focus verbal form meaning to correct or rectify something (*Magtama ka ng iyong mga gawi* "Correct your habits"). Attested in moral and instructional discourse.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 9**
- **Base Form:** tama
- **Derived Form:** nagtama
- **Transliteration:** nagtama
- **Literal Breakdown:** *nag-* (actor-focus, completive) + *tama*
- **Natural English Translation:** corrected, rectified (by an agent)
- **Usage:** Completive actor-focus form (*Nagtama siya ng kanyang mga pagkakamali* "He/she corrected his/her mistakes"). Attested in narrative and evaluative contexts.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 10**
- **Base Form:** tama
- **Derived Form:** tamain
- **Transliteration:** tamain
- **Literal Breakdown:** *tama* + *-in* (patient-focus verbal suffix)
- **Natural English Translation:** to hit (a target), to strike accurately; to be correct about (something)
- **Usage:** Patient-focus verbal form with a primary meaning of hitting a target accurately (*Tamain ang target* "Hit the target"). The sense of accuracy connects to the root meaning of correctness. Also used figuratively (*Natamain ang punto* "The point was hit/struck home"). Common in everyday and figurative speech.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 11**
- **Base Form:** tama
- **Derived Form:** natamaan
- **Transliteration:** natamaan
- **Literal Breakdown:** *na-* (completive, patient-focus) + *tama* + *-an*
- **Natural English Translation:** was hit, was struck; was affected (emotionally)
- **Usage:** Completive patient-focus form of *tamain* with *-an* suffix; used both literally (to be struck by something) and figuratively (to be emotionally affected by a remark or situation) (*Natamaan siya ng kanyang mga salita* "He/she was struck/affected by his/her words"). Very common in everyday speech.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

## Item 6 — Base Form: mali

---

**Derived Form 1**
- **Base Form:** mali
- **Derived Form:** pagkakamali
- **Transliteration:** pagkakamali
- **Literal Breakdown:** *pagkaka-* (prefix nominalizing an accidental or completed action/state) + *mali*; "an instance of being wrong"
- **Natural English Translation:** mistake, error, fault
- **Usage:** Refers to a specific instance of error, whether factual, moral, or practical. Fully documented in P1 item 7.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2**
- **Base Form:** mali
- **Derived Form:** kamali-an
- **Transliteration:** kamalian
- **Literal Breakdown:** *ka-* + *mali* + *-an*; circumfix nominalizing the root
- **Natural English Translation:** error, mistake, wrongness, fault
- **Usage:** Abstract nominal referring to the quality or instance of being wrong or mistaken (*Ang kamalian ay bahagi ng pag-aaral* "Error is part of learning"). Slightly more abstract than *pagkakamali*, which emphasizes a specific instance. Common in formal and didactic discourse.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 3**
- **Base Form:** mali
- **Derived Form:** mali-mali
- **Transliteration:** mali-mali
- **Literal Breakdown:** *mali* + *mali*; full reduplication
- **Natural English Translation:** full of errors, riddled with mistakes, all wrong
- **Usage:** Reduplication producing a meaning of multiple or pervasive errors (*Mali-mali ang kanyang sagot* "His/her answer is full of mistakes"). Common in everyday evaluative speech.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 4**
- **Base Form:** mali
- **Derived Form:** magkamali
- **Transliteration:** magkamali
- **Literal Breakdown:** *magka-* (prefix indicating accidental or unintended occurrence) + *mali*
- **Natural English Translation:** to make a mistake, to err, to be wrong (unintentionally)
- **Usage:** Verbal form meaning to commit an error, with the *magka-* prefix emphasizing the unintentional or accidental nature of the mistake (*Huwag kang magkamali* "Don't make a mistake"). Very common in everyday speech.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 5**
- **Base Form:** mali
- **Derived Form:** nagkamali
- **Transliteration:** nagkamali
- **Literal Breakdown:** *nagka-* (completive of *magka-*) + *mali*
- **Natural English Translation:** made a mistake, erred, was wrong
- **Usage:** Completive form of *magkamali*, describing the completed act of making an error (*Nagkamali ako* "I made a mistake"). Extremely common in everyday speech and in apology contexts.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 6**
- **Base Form:** mali
- **Derived Form:** nagkakamali
- **Transliteration:** nagkakamali
- **Literal Breakdown:** *nagka-* + *mali* + reduplication; imperfective of *magkamali*
- **Natural English Translation:** is making a mistake, keeps making errors, is erring
- **Usage:** Imperfective form describing an ongoing or repeated pattern of making mistakes (*Nagkakamali siya palagi* "He/she keeps making mistakes"). Common in evaluative and instructional discourse.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 7**
- **Base Form:** mali
- **Derived Form:** maling-mali
- **Transliteration:** maling-mali
- **Literal Breakdown:** *mali* + *-ng* (linker) + *mali*; full reduplication with linker
- **Natural English Translation:** completely wrong, absolutely incorrect, totally mistaken
- **Usage:** Emphatic form asserting the complete or total wrongness of something (*Maling-mali ang iyong pag-iisip* "Your thinking is completely wrong"). Common in spoken Tagalog for strong correction or disagreement.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 8**
- **Base Form:** mali
- **Derived Form:** imali
- **Transliteration:** imali
- **Literal Breakdown:** *i-* (object-focus verbal prefix) + *mali*
- **Natural English Translation:** to use wrongly, to apply incorrectly, to misuse
- **Usage:** Verbal form meaning to use or apply something incorrectly (*Huwag imali ang salita* "Don't use the word incorrectly"). Attested but less frequent than other verbal forms of *mali*.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 9**
- **Base Form:** mali
- **Derived Form:** maling-akala
- **Transliteration:** maling-akala
- **Literal Breakdown:** *mali* + *-ng* (linker) + *akala* (belief, assumption, thought)
- **Natural English Translation:** misconception, mistaken belief, false assumption
- **Usage:** Fixed compound referring to a belief or assumption that is incorrect (*Maling-akala ang iniisip mo* "What you're thinking is a misconception"). Common in corrective and educational discourse.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

## Item 8 — Base Form: huwad

---

**Derived Form 1**
- **Base Form:** huwad
- **Derived Form:** huwad na huwad
- **Transliteration:** huwad na huwad
- **Literal Breakdown:** *huwad* + *na* (linker) + *huwad*; full reduplication with linker
- **Natural English Translation:** completely fake, absolutely counterfeit, thoroughly forged
- **Usage:** Emphatic form asserting the complete inauthenticity of something (*Huwad na huwad ang dokumento* "The document is completely forged"). Attested in spoken and written Tagalog in contexts of strong condemnation of forgery or fraud.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2**
- **Base Form:** huwad
- **Derived Form:** maghuwad
- **Transliteration:** maghuwad
- **Literal Breakdown:** *mag-* (actor-focus verbal prefix) + *huwad*
- **Natural English Translation:** to forge, to counterfeit, to fake (something)
- **Usage:** Verbal form meaning to produce a counterfeit or forged item (*Bawal maghuwad ng pera* "It is forbidden to counterfeit money"). Attested in legal and moral discourse.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 3**
- **Base Form:** huwad
- **Derived Form:** naghuwad
- **Transliteration:** naghuwad
- **Literal Breakdown:** *nag-* (actor-focus, completive) + *huwad*
