## SUMMARY OBSERVATIONS

### High-Frequency Core Constructions
The following patterns show unrestricted productivity and high frequency across registers:
- Attribution (both word orders)
- Negation with **hindi** and **walang**
- Adnominal modification with linkers
- **Ka-...-an** abstract nominalization
- Question formation with **ba**
- Conditional with **kung**
- Emphatic particles (**nga**, **naman**, **talaga**)
- Imperative/directive forms

### Restricted or Specialized Patterns
- Agentive nominalization: Limited productivity; **sinungaling** is lexicalized
- Reciprocal **mag-...-an**: Productive with honesty/dishonesty terms but not factual truth terms
- Cessation marker **tama na**: Semantic specialization restricted to **tama** and **hustó**
- Comparison forms (**katulad**, **kamukha**): Appear lexicalized rather than transparently productive

### Discourse Functions
Several constructions have grammaticalized into discourse markers:
- **Sa totoo lang** (epistemic framing)
- **Ang totoo** (correction/clarification)
- **Tama na** (sufficiency/cessation)
- **May tama ka** (concession in argument)

### Morphological Productivity
The following affixation patterns show full productivity:
- **Mag-ka-** (inchoative)
- **Pa-...-an** and **i-** (causative)
- **Ka-...-an** (abstract nominalization)
- **Napaka-** (intensification)
- Progressive aspect marking
