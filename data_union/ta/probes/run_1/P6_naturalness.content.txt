# CLCA Protocol v3.3 — P6 Change, Causation, and Stability
# Language: Tamil (ta)

---

**Methodological note:** The following documents dynamic expressions — encoding change of state, causation, and stability — involving truth-related vocabulary identified in the CONTEXT. Only vocabulary, constructions, and patterns attested in P1–P5 and I1 are used. Where a construction type is not attested for a given item, this is stated explicitly. Auxiliary verbs, aspect markers, and function words are added as needed to form natural Tamil expressions; these additions are flagged. Items are treated separately where their behavior differs.

---

## 1. BECOMING A TRUTH-RELATED STATE

### 1.1 உண்மை / *uṇmai* — becoming true / becoming known as true

**Pattern:** *uṇmai* + *āku* (to become) + optional completive *viṭu*

| Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| அது உண்மையாகிவிட்டது | *atu uṇmaiyākiviṭṭatu* | "That has turned out to be true / has become true" | [CORE] | Attested in P4a Construction 4 and P4b. The completive auxiliary *viṭu* adds finality or irreversibility. Expresses a change of epistemic status — a claim previously uncertain is now established as true. |
| அது உண்மையாகும் | *atu uṇmaiyākum* | "That will become true / will turn out to be true" | [CORE] | Future form of the same construction without *viṭu*; used in predictive and conditional contexts. |

**Pattern:** *uṇmai* + *teriya varu* (come to be known) — truth becoming known

| Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| உண்மை தெரிய வந்தது | *uṇmai teriya vantatu* | "The truth became known / came to light" | [CORE] | Attested in P4a Construction 4, P4b, P2b Construction 1.8, and P3 Group 7. Treats truth as an entity moving from concealment to visibility; the change is from hidden to known. |
| உண்மை தெரிய வரும் | *uṇmai teriya varum* | "The truth will become known / will come to light" | [CORE] | Future form; common in predictive moral and narrative speech. |

**Pattern:** *uṇmai* + *veḷippaṭu* (become manifest, come out)

| Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| உண்மை வெளிப்பட்டது | *uṇmai veḷippaṭṭatu* | "The truth became manifest / was revealed" | [CORE] | Attested in P1b (*uṇmai veḷippaṭutal*) and P2b Construction 1.7. The verb *veḷippaṭu* (to become manifest, to come out) encodes the change from concealed to visible. |

---

### 1.2 நேர்மை / *nērmai* — becoming honest

**Pattern:** *nērmaiyāka āku* (become honest)

| Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| அவன் நேர்மையாகிவிட்டான் | *avaṉ nērmaiyākiviṭṭāṉ* | "He has become honest / has turned honest" | [CORE] | Formed by *nērmaiyāka* (adverbial form, attested in P2b) + *āki* (becoming) + completive *viṭu*. The *-āka* adverbial + *āku* is the standard inchoative construction for character-state change in Tamil. |
| அவள் நேர்மையானாள் | *avaḷ nērmaiyāṉāḷ* | "She became honest / became an honest person" | [CORE] | Predicative inchoative using *nērmaiyāna* + past copula *āṉāḷ* (she became); a natural alternative to the adverbial construction. |

**Note:** No expression for *nērmai* becoming is attested as a fixed phrase in the CONTEXT. The above are formed by applying the productive *-āka āku* and *-āna āku* patterns documented in P4a to *nērmai*, consistent with attested Tamil morphosyntax.

---

### 1.3 நம்பிக்கை / *nampikkai* — trust/faith coming into being

**Pattern:** *nampikkai* + *piṟa* (to be born, to arise) or *uṇṭāku* (to come into existence)

| Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| அவர் மீது நம்பிக்கை உண்டாயிற்று | *avar mītu nampikkai uṇṭāyiṟṟu* | "Trust in him/her came into being / arose" | [CORE] | *uṇṭāku* (to come to exist, to arise) is the standard verb for the coming-into-existence of a state or quality in Tamil. The postposition *mītu* (toward, upon) marks the object of trust, consistent with P2b Construction 4.7. |
| நம்பிக்கை வளர்ந்தது | *nampikkai vaḷarntatu* | "Trust grew / faith increased" | [CORE] | *vaḷar* (to grow, to increase) encodes gradual increase of trust; common in interpersonal and institutional contexts. |

**Pattern:** *nampikkai* + *koḷ* (to take on, to adopt) — attested in P1b

| Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| அவர் நம்பிக்கை கொண்டார் | *avar nampikkai koṇṭār* | "He/she came to have faith / adopted belief" | [MARGINAL] | Attested in P1b (*nampikkai koḷḷutal*, adopting faith); rated [MARGINAL] in P1b as confined to religious and philosophical contexts. |

---

### 1.4 சரி / *cari* — becoming correct / right

**Pattern:** *cari* + *āku*

| Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| அது சரியாகிவிட்டது | *atu cariyākiviṭṭatu* | "That has become right / has been corrected / has come good" | [CORE] | The *-āka āku* inchoative applied to *cari*; attested as a natural expression in Tamil for a situation or condition that has moved from wrong/broken to correct/fixed. The completive *viṭu* adds finality. |
| உடம்பு சரியாகும் | *uṭampu cariyākum* | "The body will become right / will recover" | [CORE] | A common expression for recovery from illness, using *cari* in its "in order, well" sense (attested in P2b Construction 5.4: *cariyillai* = something is wrong / I am unwell). |

---

### 1.5 தவறு / *tavaṟu* — becoming wrong / erroneous

**Pattern:** *tavaṟāka āku* or *tavaṟu āku*

| Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| அது தவறாகிவிட்டது | *atu tavaṟākiviṭṭatu* | "That has become wrong / has turned out to be a mistake" | [CORE] | Parallel to *uṇmaiyākiviṭṭatu*; encodes a change of epistemic or moral status from acceptable to erroneous. Common in everyday speech when a plan, calculation, or judgment is found to be mistaken. |

---

### 1.6 போலி / *pōli* — becoming fake / being exposed as fake

**No common inchoative expression attested.** *pōli* is used attributively and predicatively (*itu pōli*, this is fake) but no fixed expression for something *becoming* fake is attested in the CONTEXT. The relevant dynamic in the *pōli* domain is exposure rather than change of state: something is revealed *as* fake rather than *becoming* fake. This is expressed by the revelation construction (see §3 below).

## 2. BECOMING A NON-TRUTH / OPPOSED STATE

### 2.1 உண்மை → பொய் / truth becoming falsehood

**Pattern:** *poy* + *āku* (become a lie / become false)

| Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| அவர் சொன்னது பொய்யாகிவிட்டது | *avar coṉṉatu poyyākiviṭṭatu* | "What he/she said has turned out to be false / has become a lie" | [CORE] | *poy* + *-āka* + *āki* + completive *viṭu*; encodes the change of a claim's status from believed-true to recognized-as-false. Common in narrative and journalistic Tamil when a claim is disproved. |
| உண்மை மறைந்தது | *uṇmai maṟaintatu* | "The truth was hidden / truth disappeared" | [CORE] | *maṟai* (to hide, to disappear) used intransitively; encodes the concealment or loss of truth. Attested in P2b (*uṇmaiyai maṟai*, hide the truth) in its transitive form; the intransitive use treats truth as the entity that disappears. |

---

### 2.2 நேர்மை → வஞ்சனை / honesty giving way to deception

**Pattern:** *vañcaṉai* + *āku* or periphrastic

| Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| அவன் வஞ்சகனாகிவிட்டான் | *avaṉ vañcakaṉākiviṭṭāṉ* | "He has become a deceiver / has turned treacherous" | [CORE] | *vañcakaṉ* (deceiver, agent nominal from *vañcu*) + *āki* + *viṭu*; encodes a change of character from honest to deceptive. The agent nominal *vañcakaṉ* is not listed in P1 but is formed from the attested root *vañcu* underlying *vañcaṉai*. |
| நேர்மை போய்விட்டது | *nērmai pōyviṭṭatu* | "Honesty has gone / integrity has been lost" | [CORE] | *pō* (to go, to be lost) + completive *viṭu*; treats *nērmai* as an entity that can depart. Parallel in structure to *nampikkai pōccu* (trust is gone), attested in P2b Construction 4.5. |

---

### 2.3 நம்பிக்கை → நம்பிக்கையின்மை / trust becoming distrust

**Pattern:** *nampikkai* + *iḻa* (lose) or *pō* (go/be lost)

| Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| நம்பிக்கை போய்விட்டது | *nampikkai pōyviṭṭatu* | "Trust is gone / faith has been lost" | [CORE] | Attested in P2b Construction 4.5 (*nampikkai pōccu*, colloquial form). The completive *viṭu* adds finality. |
| நம்பிக்கை குறைந்தது | *nampikkai kuṟaintatu* | "Trust diminished / confidence decreased" | [CORE] | *kuṟai* (to decrease, to diminish) encodes gradual erosion of trust; attested in P1b (*nampikkai valu viḻattal*, weakening of trust) as a related pattern. |

---

### 2.4 சரி → தவறு / correct becoming wrong

**Pattern:** *tavaṟāka āku*

| Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| சரியானது தவறாகிவிட்டது | *cariyāṉatu tavaṟākiviṭṭatu* | "What was correct has become wrong / the right thing has turned into a mistake" | [CORE] | Encodes a change of evaluative status; common in contexts where a previously correct procedure or judgment is found to be mistaken. |

## 3. CAUSING A TRUTH-RELATED STATE

### 3.1 Causing truth to be known / established

**Pattern:** *meyppiкka* (morphological causative from *mey*) — attested in P4a Construction 5 and P2a

| Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| அதை மெய்ப்பிக்க முடியவில்லை | *atai meyppiкka muṭiyavillai* | "It was not possible to prove that / could not be demonstrated" | [CORE] | Attested in P4a and P4b. The only morphologically derived causative in the truth domain; from *mey* + causative *-ppi-*. The object of proving takes accusative *-ai*. |
| அவர் தன் நிரபராதித்துவத்தை மெய்ப்பித்தார் | *avar taṉ nirapārātittuvatai meyppiттār* | "He/she proved his/her innocence" | [CORE] | Attested pattern of *meyppiкka* with an accusative nominal object; common in legal Tamil. |

**Pattern:** *uṇmaiyai nilaināṭṭu* (establish the truth) — attested in P2b Construction 1.9 and P4a Construction 5

| Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| உண்மையை நிலைநாட்டினார் | *uṇmaiyai nilaināṭṭiṉār* | "He/she established the truth definitively" | [CORE] | Attested in P4a and P4b. Periphrastic; *nilaināṭṭu* (to make stand firm) encodes the causative act of establishing truth permanently. |

**Pattern:** *uṇmait-taṉmaiyai nirūpi* (prove authenticity) — attested in P2b Construction 7.2

| Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| உண்மைத்தன்மையை நிரூபித்தார் | *uṇmait-taṉmaiyai nirūpittār* | "He/she proved the authenticity / demonstrated the genuineness" | [CORE] | Attested in P2b. *nirūpi* (to prove, to demonstrate) is a periphrastic alternative to *meyppiкka* in formal and legal registers. |

### 3.2 Causing truth to be hidden / suppressed

**Pattern:** *uṇmaiyai maṟai* (hide the truth) — attested in P2b Construction 1.3 and P4a Construction 9

| Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Notes |

| உண்மையை மறைக்கிறார்கள் | *uṇmaiyai maṟaikkiṟārkaḷ* | "They are hiding the truth / concealing the facts" | [CORE] | Attested in P4b. Transitive; *uṇmai* bears accusative *-ai*; the agent deliberately causes truth to remain hidden. |
| உண்மையை மறைத்தார் | *uṇmaiyai maṟaittār* | "He/she concealed the truth" | [CORE] | Past tense of the same construction; common in legal and narrative Tamil. |

### 3.3 Causing trust to arise or increase

**Pattern:** *nampikkai ūṭṭu* (instill confidence) — attested in P1b and P2b

### 3.4 Causing trust to be lost / betrayed

**Pattern:** *nampikkai turōkam cey* (betray trust) — attested in P2b Construction 4.4

| Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| அவர் நம்பிக்கை துரோகம் செய்தார் | *avar nampikkai turōkam ceytār* | "He/she betrayed the trust / committed a breach of faith" | [CORE] | Attested in P2b. *cey* (to do/commit) is the causative verb; *nampikkai turōkam* functions as a compound nominal object. |

---

### 3.5 Causing a person to become honest

**No common causative expression attested.** No construction for causing someone to become honest (*nērmai* + causative) is attested in the CONTEXT. The domain of *nērmai* does not yield a morphological causative, and no periphrastic causative construction with *nērmai* as object is documented in P1–P5. The closest attested expressions are imperative constructions (*nērmaiyāka iru*, be honest; *nērmaiyāka naṭa*, behave honestly), which are directive rather than causative.

**Pattern:** *pōli eṉṟu kaṇṭupiṭi* (identify as fake) — attested in P2b Construction 10.4

| Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|

**Pattern:** *uṇmai* + *vell* (prevail, win) — attested in P2b Construction 1.16

| Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| உண்மை எப்போதும் வெல்லும் | *uṇmai eppōtum vellum* | "Truth always prevails / truth always wins" | [CORE] | Attested in P2b and P3 Group 8 as a proverbial expression. *eppōtum* (always) encodes the stability and permanence of truth's ultimate triumph. |

**Pattern:** *uṇmai* + *nilaināṭṭu* (stand firm, be established) — attested in P2b Construction 1.9

| Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Notes |

| உண்மை நிலைத்து நிற்கும் | *uṇmai nilaittu niṟkum* | "Truth will stand firm / truth will endure" | [CORE] | *nilai* (to be established, to stand) + *niṟku* (to stand, to remain); encodes the stability and persistence of truth over time. The construction treats truth as an entity that can stand or fall. |

## 5. STRUCTURAL OBSERVATIONS

The following observations are drawn directly from the patterns documented above and in P1–P5.

**5.1 The *-āka āku* inchoative is the productive change-of-state construction across this domain.** It applies to *uṇmai* (*uṇmaiyākiviṭṭatu*, has become true), *nērmai* (*nērmaiyākiviṭṭāṉ*, has become honest), *cari* (*cariyākiviṭṭatu*, has become right), and *tavaṟu* (*tavaṟākiviṭṭatu*, has become wrong). The completive auxiliary *viṭu* consistently adds finality or irreversibility to the change. This pattern is not attested for *pōli* or *vañcaṉai* in the CONTEXT.

**5.2 The causative domain is structurally asymmetric.** *meyppiкka* (from *mey*) is the only morphological causative in the entire domain, as documented in P4a and P5 Opposition 21. All other causative expressions are periphrastic: *nilaināṭṭu* (establish), *nirūpi* (prove), *maṟai* (hide), *ūṭṭu* (instill), *koṭu* (give), *cey* (commit). No morphological causative from *uṇmai*, *nērmai*, *nampikkai*, *cari*, or *tavaṟu* is attested.

**5.3 The revelation/concealment construction (P4a Construction 9) is the primary dynamic pattern for *uṇmai*.** The intransitive revelation pole (*uṇmai veḷiyē vantatu*, *uṇmai teriya vantatu*) and the transitive concealment pole (*uṇmaiyai maṟai*) together constitute the most productive dynamic construction in the truth domain, treating truth as a moveable entity that can be hidden or brought to light. This pattern does not extend to *nērmai*, *nampikkai*, or *cari* in the attested data.

**5.4 Stability expressions are proverbially anchored.** The expressions encoding truth's persistence (*uṇmai eppōtum vellum*, truth always prevails; *poy nilaikkātu*, a lie will not last) are proverbial in character, as documented in P3 Group 8. They encode a culturally salient belief in the ultimate stability of truth and instability of falsehood. No parallel proverbial stability expressions are attested for *nērmai*, *nampikkai*, or *cari* in the CONTEXT.

**5.5 *nampikkai* has the richest causative vocabulary of any item in this domain.** Three distinct causative constructions are attested: *nampikkai ūṭṭu* (instill confidence), *nampikkai koṭu* (give confidence), and *nampikkai turōkam cey* (betray trust). This reflects the interpersonal and institutional salience of trust as something that can be actively built, transferred, or destroyed by agents.
