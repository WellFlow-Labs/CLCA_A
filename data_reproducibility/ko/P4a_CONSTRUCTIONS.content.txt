# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P4a_CONSTRUCTIONS
# Language: Korean (ko)

---

## 1. ATTRIBUTION

### Pattern
**NP + 이/가 + TRUTH-TERM + 이다**
Subject marked with nominative particle 이/가, followed by truth-domain noun or adjectival predicate with copula 이다.

### Examples
- 이것은 사실이다 (igeoseun sasirida) "This is a fact"
- 그의 말은 진실이다 (geuui mareun jinsilida) "His words are truth"

### Notes
Standard copular construction. Truth-domain nouns (사실, 진실, 거짓) function as predicates. Adjectival forms use -하다 verbs without copula: 이것은 정확하다 (igeoseun jeonghwakhada) "This is accurate."

---

## 2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION

### Pattern
**NP + 이/가 + TRUTH-TERM + 이/가 아니다**
Negation using 아니다 "not be" for nouns, or 않다/없다 for adjectival predicates.

### Examples
- 이것은 사실이 아니다 (igeoseun sasiri anida) "This is not a fact"
- 그 말은 정확하지 않다 (geu mareun jeonghwakhaji anta) "That statement is not accurate"

### Notes
Nominal predicates negate with 아니다. Adjectival predicates use -지 않다 pattern. Alternative: 틀리다 (teullida) "to be wrong" or 거짓이다 (geojisida) "to be false."

---

## 3. ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION

### Pattern
**TRUTH-TERM-(으)ㄴ/는 + NOUN**
Adjectival forms modify nouns using -(으)ㄴ (past/perfective) or -는 (present) adnominal endings.

### Examples
- 진실한 사람 (jinsilhan saram) "truthful person"
- 정확한 정보 (jeonghwakhan jeongbo) "accurate information"

### Notes
Sino-Korean nouns require -하다 verbalization before adnominal marking: 정직하다 → 정직한. Native Korean adjectives use stem + -(으)ㄴ: 옳다 → 옳은 "right/correct."

---

## 4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE

### Pattern
**TRUTH-TERM + 이/가 되다**
"Become" construction using auxiliary verb 되다 with truth-domain nouns or adjectival stems.

### Examples
- 진실이 되다 (jinsiri doeda) "to become truth"
- 거짓말이 들통나다 (geojinmari deultongada) "a lie becomes exposed"

### Notes
되다 marks change of state. Specialized inchoatives exist: 드러나다 (deureonada) "to be revealed" for truth emergence, 밝혀지다 (balkhyeojida) "to be brought to light." No simple "become true/false" for propositions—Korean uses evidential or discovery verbs instead.

## 5. CAUSATIVE

### Pattern

### Examples
N/A

### Notes
Korean lacks productive morphological causatives for truth-domain predicates. Causation expressed periphrastically: 진실을 밝히다 (jinsireul balkhida) "to reveal/bring to light the truth" (literally "to brighten the truth"), 거짓말을 하게 하다 (geojinmareul hage hada) "to make someone lie" (using auxiliary -게 하다). No single-word causatives like *"to truthify" or *"to falsify" (in sense of making false).

## 6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION

### Pattern
**TRUTH-TERM + 성 (-seong)**
Sino-Korean suffix -성 derives abstract quality nouns from adjectival bases.

### Examples
- 진실성 (jinsilseong) "truthfulness, veracity"
- 정확성 (jeonghwakseong) "accuracy, precision"

### Notes
Highly productive with Sino-Korean bases: 정직성 (jeongjikseong) "honesty," 타당성 (tadangseong) "validity," 신빙성 (sinbingseong) "credibility." Native Korean bases use different patterns: 옳음 (oleum) "rightness" (stem + -음 nominalizer). Some items already nominalized: 진실 (jinsil) itself is a noun.

---

## 7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION

### Pattern
**TRUTH-TERM + 쟁이 (-jaengi) / -자 (-ja)**
Agentive suffixes create person nouns. Limited productivity in truth domain.

### Examples
- 거짓말쟁이 (geojinmaljjaengi) "liar" (literally "lie-person")
- (no common term for "truth-teller" using productive morphology)

### Notes
-쟁이 suffix (colloquial, often pejorative) attaches to 거짓말. No parallel *진실쟁이 or *사실쟁이. "Truth-teller" expressed periphrastically: 진실을 말하는 사람 (jinsireul malhaneun saram) "person who speaks truth." Sino-Korean -자 suffix not productive here (진술자 "testifier" exists but is not truth-domain specific).

---

## 8. EVIDENTIAL/EPISTEMIC MARKING

### Pattern
**TRUTH-TERM + (으)ㄴ/는 것 같다**
Evidential construction using 것 같다 "seems/appears" with adnominal forms.

### Examples
- 사실인 것 같다 (sasirin geot gatda) "seems to be a fact"
- 거짓말인 것 같다 (geojinmarin geot gatda) "seems to be a lie"

### Notes
Korean extensively marks epistemic stance. 것 같다 indicates inference or appearance. Alternatives: -(으)ㄴ/는 듯하다 (deuthada) "seems," -나 보다 (na boda) "appears." Certainty marked by 틀림없다 (teullimeopda) "without doubt," 확실하다 (hwaksilhada) "certain."

---

## 9. DISCOURSE MARKERS

### Pattern
**TRUTH-TERM + (으)로 / 은/는**
Truth-domain items grammaticalized as discourse markers introducing statements.

### Examples
- 사실은 (sasireun) "actually, in fact, the truth is"
- 솔직히 말하면 (soljiki malhamyeon) "frankly speaking, to be honest"

### Notes
Highly productive pattern. 사실 (sasil) and 사실은 (sasireun) function as discourse markers for factual correction or revelation. 솔직히 (soljiki) "frankly" introduces candid statements. 진실로 (jinsilro) "truly" marks sincerity. These are grammaticalized beyond compositional meaning.

---

## 10. VERIFICATION CONSTRUCTIONS

### Pattern
**TRUTH-TERM + 을/를 + VERIFICATION-VERB**
Object-marking construction with verbs of verification, revelation, or concealment.

### Examples
- 진실을 밝히다 (jinsireul balkhida) "to reveal the truth"
- 사실을 확인하다 (sasireul hwaginhada) "to verify the facts"

### Notes
Rich domain-specific pattern. Common verbs: 밝히다 "reveal/bring to light," 확인하다 "verify/confirm," 가리다 "distinguish/determine," 숨기다 "hide/conceal," 찾다 "seek/search for," 알다 "know." Intransitive counterpart: 진실이 드러나다 (jinsiri deureonada) "truth comes to light."

---

## 11. AUTHENTICATION CONSTRUCTIONS

### Pattern
**AUTHENTICITY-TERM + 인지 + OPPOSITE-TERM + 인지**
"Whether X or Y" construction using -인지 conditional/interrogative marker.

### Examples
- 진짜인지 가짜인지 (jinjjainji gajjainji) "whether real or fake"
- 진본인지 모조품인지 (jinboninji mojopuminji) "whether authentic or counterfeit"

### Notes
Standard pattern for questioning authenticity. -인지 marks embedded questions. Often followed by verbs like 구별하다 (gubyeolhada) "distinguish," 판별하다 (panbyeolhada) "identify," 가리다 (garida) "determine." Used in authentication contexts for objects, not typically for statement truth.

---

## 12. CORRECTNESS EVALUATION

### Pattern
**맞다/틀리다 + ADNOMINAL + NOUN**
Binary correct/incorrect evaluation using native Korean verbs.

### Examples
- 맞는 답 (manneun dap) "correct answer"
- 틀린 답 (teullin dap) "wrong answer"

### Notes
Domain-specific to educational and evaluative contexts. 맞다 (matda) "to be correct/right" vs. 틀리다 (teullida) "to be wrong/incorrect." Often in coordinate construction: 맞고 틀리고 (matgo teulligo) "right and wrong," 맞는지 틀리는지 (manneunji teullineunji) "whether right or wrong." Distinct from moral rightness (옳다/그르다).

---

## 13. INTENSIFICATION PATTERNS

### Pattern
**COLOR/INTENSIFIER + TRUTH-TERM**
Intensifying modifiers with truth-domain nouns, often idiomatic.

### Examples
- 새빨간 거짓말 (saeppalgan geojinmal) "blatant lie" (literally "bright red lie")
- 하얀 거짓말 (hayan geojinmal) "white lie"

### Notes
Limited pattern. 새빨간 (saeppalgan) "bright red" intensifies obviousness or brazenness of lies. 하얀 (hayan) "white" calqued from English "white lie." Intensifier suffix -배기 in 진짜배기 (jinjjabaegi) "the real deal." No parallel intensification for truth terms (*새빨간 진실 not idiomatic).

---

## 14. LEGITIMACY ASSESSMENT

### Pattern
**LEGITIMACY-TERM + (으)ㄴ/는 + ABSTRACT-NOUN**
Adnominal modification with legitimacy/validity predicates.

### Examples
- 정당한 이유 (jeongdanghan iyu) "legitimate reason"
- 타당한 근거 (tadanghan geungeo) "valid grounds"

### Notes
Specialized to legal, political, and argumentative discourse. 정당하다 (jeongdanghada) "legitimate/justified" vs. 타당하다 (tadanghada) "valid/reasonable." Common collocations: 정당한 권리 (jeongdanghan gwonri) "legitimate right," 타당한 주장 (tadanghan jujang) "valid claim." Abstract nominalizations (정당성, 타당성) frequently appear with 확보하다 "secure," 검토하다 "examine."

---

## 15. CREDIBILITY CONSTRUCTIONS

### Pattern
**신빙성 + 이/가 + EXISTENCE/DEGREE-VERB**
Credibility noun with verbs marking presence, absence, or degree.

### Examples
- 신빙성이 있다 (sinbingseongi itda) "to be credible" (literally "credibility exists")
- 신빙성이 떨어지다 (sinbingseongi tteoreojida) "to lose credibility" (literally "credibility falls")

### Notes
Fixed construction pattern. 신빙성 (sinbingseong) "credibility" takes existential verbs: 있다 "exist," 없다 "not exist," or degree verbs: 높다 "be high," 낮다 "be low," 떨어지다 "fall/drop," 높이다 "raise." Distinct from truth-value—assesses trustworthiness before verification.
