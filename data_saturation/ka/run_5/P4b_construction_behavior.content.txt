## Summary Notes

**No constructions were rated [MARGINAL] overall**, though the following restrictions are observable from the data:

- **Construction 7 (Agentive Nominalization)**: The -ebeli pattern is productive primarily on the falsification/deception side. No positive agentive noun (e.g., "truth-teller") is attested in the data. This asymmetry is noted but not explained by the data.
- **Construction 7, mimbadzvelilit**: Rated [UNCERTAIN] due to the apparent irregularity in the transliteration as given in P4a. No correction has been applied; the form is reported as given.
- **Construction 9 (Postpositional Conformity/Mismatch)**: Register is observably restricted to formal, legal, and administrative contexts. It is not attested in colloquial speech in the data.
- **Construction 4 (Inchoative)**: The gamovida variant is observably more colloquial than the aɣmočnda variant; both are [CORE] but differ in register distribution.
