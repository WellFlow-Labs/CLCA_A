# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2a Derivational System
# Language: Chinese (zh)

## PRELIMINARY NOTE ON CHINESE DERIVATIONAL MORPHOLOGY

Chinese derivational morphology differs fundamentally from Indo-European patterns. The language primarily uses:
1. **Compounding** (combining morphemes/characters)
2. **Affixation** (limited productive affixes)
3. **Reduplication** (limited patterns)

Most P1 items are already compounds (e.g., 真实 = 真 + 实). "Derivation" in Chinese typically means:
- Adding productive affixes (化, 性, 者, etc.)
- Forming new compounds with the base
- Verbal/nominal conversions (often zero-marked)

The P1b expansion already documented most productive compounding. This section focuses on **additional derivational patterns** not covered in P1b.

## DERIVATIONAL PATTERNS IDENTIFIED

### PATTERN 1: Nominalization with -性 (-xìng) "quality, -ness, -ity"

This suffix derives abstract nouns denoting qualities or properties.

#### From 真 (zhēn)

**真实性 (zhēnshíxìng)**
- Base: 真实 (zhēnshí) "real, true"
- Literal: true-actual-QUALITY = authenticity, truthfulness
- Translation: authenticity, truthfulness, veracity
- Usage: Abstract noun referring to the quality of being authentic or true. Common in academic and analytical discourse evaluating the authenticity of claims, documents, or representations.
- [CORE]

**真正性 (zhēnzhèngxìng)**
- Base: 真正 (zhēnzhèng) "genuine, real"
- Literal: true-correct-QUALITY = genuineness
- Translation: genuineness, authenticity
- Usage: Abstract noun for the quality of being genuine. Less common than 真实性, used in formal contexts.
- [MARGINAL]

#### From 实 (shí)

**真实性** (see above - derived from compound 真实)

**现实性 (xiànshíxìng)**
- Base: 现实 (xiànshí) "reality, realistic" [not in P1 but common compound with 实]
- Literal: appear-actual-QUALITY = reality, practicality
- Translation: reality, practicality, feasibility
- Usage: Abstract noun referring to realistic quality or practical feasibility. Common in evaluating whether plans or ideas are realistic.
- [CORE]

**实际性 (shíjìxìng)**
- Base: 实际 (shíjì) "actual, practical"
- Literal: actual-boundary-QUALITY = practicality, actuality
- Usage: Abstract noun for practical or actual quality. Used in formal contexts evaluating practical aspects.
- [MARGINAL]

**实用性 (shíyòngxìng)**
- Base: 实用 (shíyòng) "practical, functional"
- Literal: actual-use-QUALITY = practicality, utility
- Translation: practicality, utility, usefulness
- Usage: Abstract noun for practical utility or usefulness. Very common in product evaluation and design contexts.
- [CORE]

#### From 假 (jiǎ)

**虚假性 (xūjiǎxìng)**
- Base: 虚假 (xūjiǎ) "false, fake"
- Literal: empty-false-QUALITY = falsity, falseness
- Translation: falsity, falseness, fraudulence
- Usage: Abstract noun for the quality of being false or fraudulent. Used in formal analysis of deceptive claims or materials.
- [CORE]

#### From 对 (duì)

**正确性 (zhèngquèxìng)**
- Base: 正确 (zhèngquè) "correct, right"
- Literal: correct-exact-QUALITY = correctness, validity
- Translation: correctness, validity, rightness
- Usage: Abstract noun for the quality of being correct. Common in academic and technical contexts evaluating validity of statements or methods.
- [CORE]

#### From 错 (cuò)

**错误性 (cuòwùxìng)**
- Base: 错误 (cuòwù) "error, mistake" [错 + 误]
- Literal: wrong-mistake-QUALITY = erroneousness
- Translation: erroneousness, incorrectness
- Usage: Abstract noun for the quality of being erroneous. Used in formal analysis of errors or mistakes.
- [MARGINAL]

#### From 准确 (zhǔnquè)

**准确性 (zhǔnquèxìng)**
- Base: 准确 (zhǔnquè) "accurate, precise"
- Literal: standard-exact-QUALITY = accuracy, precision
- Translation: accuracy, precision, exactness
- Usage: Abstract noun for accuracy or precision. Very common in technical, scientific, and informational contexts.
- [CORE]

#### From 可靠 (kěkào)

**可靠性 (kěkàoxìng)**
- Base: 可靠 (kěkào) "reliable, dependable"
- Literal: can-rely-QUALITY = reliability, dependability
- Translation: reliability, dependability, trustworthiness
- Usage: Abstract noun for reliability. Extremely common in technical, scientific, and quality assessment contexts.
- [CORE]

#### From 客观 (kèguān)

**客观性 (kèguānxìng)**
- Base: 客观 (kèguān) "objective"
- Literal: object-observe-QUALITY = objectivity
- Translation: objectivity, impartiality
- Usage: Abstract noun for objectivity. Common in academic, scientific, and analytical discourse about unbiased evaluation.
- [CORE]

#### From 主观 (zhǔguān)

**主观性 (zhǔguānxìng)**
- Base: 主观 (zhǔguān) "subjective"
- Literal: subject-observe-QUALITY = subjectivity
- Translation: subjectivity, personal bias
- Usage: Abstract noun for subjectivity. Common in academic and analytical contexts discussing bias or personal perspectives.
- [CORE]

### PATTERN 7: Negation with 不 (bù) "not"

- Base: 真 (zhēn) "true, real"

- Translation: untrue, false, not genuine
- Usage: Simple negation indicating lack of truth or genuineness. Less common than positive antonyms like 假.
- [MARGINAL]

**不真实 (bù zhēnshí)**
- Base: 真实 (zhēnshí) "real, true"
- Literal: not-true-actual = unreal, untrue
- Translation: unreal, untrue, inauthentic
- Usage: Negation indicating lack of reality or truth. Common in evaluative contexts.
- [CORE]

#### From 实 (shí)

- Translation: untrue, false, inaccurate, unfounded
- Usage: Indicates lack of factual basis. Common in formal contexts, especially legal and journalistic (不实报道 = false report).

**不实际 (bù shíjì)**
- Base: 实际 (shíjì) "actual, practical"
- Literal: not-actual-boundary = impractical, unrealistic

#### From 准确 (zhǔnquè)

## SUMMARY OF PRODUCTIVE PATTERNS

### Highly Productive Patterns:
1. **-性 (-xìng)** nominalization: Applies broadly to adjectives in truth domain
2. **-度 (-dù)** degree nominalization: Applies to gradable qualities
3. **-地 (-de)** adverbialization: Applies to most adjectives
4. **AABB reduplication**: Limited to specific bases but productive within those
5. **Resultative complements with 实**: Very productive (落实, 查实, 核实, 证实, etc.)
6. **Resultative complements with 对/错**: Highly productive with action verbs
7. **Negation with 不**: Applies broadly

### Moderately Productive Patterns:
1. **-化 (-huà)** verbalization: Selective application
2. **-者 (-zhě)** agent nominalization: Limited to certain bases

### Limited Productivity:
1. **Resultative complements with 真**: Regional or restricted usage
2. **Potential complements**: Grammatical rather than derivational

### No Common Derivation Identified:
- **虚 (xū)**: While it appears in compounds (虚假, 虚伪), it does not show productive derivational patterns beyond those already documented in P1b
- **似 (sì)**: Functions primarily in comparative constructions; no clear derivational patterns
- **像 (xiàng)**: Functions primarily as verb/preposition; no clear derivational patterns
- **谎言 (huǎngyán)**: Already a compound; no further derivation attested
- **本质 (běnzhì)**: Shows 本质上 (essentially) but this is grammatical rather than derivational

## NOTES ON METHODOLOGY

1. **Distinction from P1b**: P1b documented productive compounding with the three main roots. This section focuses on affixal derivation and other morphological processes.

2. **Grammatical vs. Derivational**: Chinese has fuzzy boundaries between grammar and derivation. Patterns included here show some degree of lexicalization or conventionalization beyond pure syntax.

3. **Productivity Assessment**: Based on:
   - Frequency of attested forms
   - Range of bases accepting the pattern
   - Conventionalization of meaning

4. **Gaps Preserved**: Not all P1 items show derivational patterns. This asymmetry is preserved rather than forcing paradigmatic completeness.
