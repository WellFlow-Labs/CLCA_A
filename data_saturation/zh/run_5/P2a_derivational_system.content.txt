# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2a Derivational System
# Language: Chinese (zh)

---

## Preliminary Note on Derivational Morphology in Chinese

Chinese derivational morphology differs substantially from that of Indo-European languages. The primary productive derivational processes attested in Mandarin Chinese are:

- **Suffixation with nominalizing/categorizing suffixes:** 性 (-xìng, abstract property), 者 (-zhě, agentive), 化 (-huà, verbalization/transformation), 式 (-shì, type/mode), 感 (-gǎn, feeling/sense), 论 (-lùn, theory/discourse), 主义 (-zhǔyì, -ism)
- **Prefixation:** Limited; 非- (fēi-, non-/un-), 反- (fǎn-, anti-/counter-), 超- (chāo-, super-/ultra-) are the most productive
- **Reduplication:** Attested for some adjectives and verbs, with aspectual or intensifying effects

Compounding is the dominant word-formation process in Chinese and has been extensively documented in P1 and P1b. The present step documents only **derivational** processes — those involving the addition of a bound morpheme to a base to produce a new lexical item of a different category or with a systematically shifted meaning — as distinct from compounding.

Many P1 items show **no productive derivational morphology** beyond what is already documented. This is reported explicitly where it applies. The inventory below is restricted to forms that are genuinely attested in use.

### Base: 真实 (zhēnshí)

---

**Entry 1**
1. **Base Form:** 真实 (zhēnshí)
2. **Derived Form:** 真实性
3. **Transliteration:** zhēnshíxìng
4. **Literal Breakdown:** true + solid/real + nature/property (nominalizing suffix 性)
5. **Natural English Translation:** authenticity, veracity, truthfulness (as an abstract property)
6. **Usage:** The suffix 性 nominalizes the adjective 真实, producing a noun denoting the abstract property of being truthful or authentic. Appears in assessments of evidence, testimony, documents, and representations: 核实真实性 (verify the authenticity), 质疑其真实性 (question its veracity). Common in legal, journalistic, and academic registers.
7. **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Entry 2**
1. **Base Form:** 真实 (zhēnshí)
2. **Derived Form:** 真实感
3. **Transliteration:** zhēnshígǎn
4. **Literal Breakdown:** true + solid/real + feeling/sense (suffix 感)
5. **Natural English Translation:** sense of reality, feeling of authenticity, realism (as an aesthetic quality)
6. **Usage:** The suffix 感 derives a noun denoting the subjective impression or aesthetic quality of seeming real or authentic. Used in literary criticism, film criticism, and everyday aesthetic evaluation: 这部电影缺乏真实感 (this film lacks a sense of reality), 增强真实感 (enhance the feeling of authenticity).
7. **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

**Entry 3**

## Forms with No Common Derivation Identified

The following P1 items were examined for derivational morphology. No productive or commonly attested derivational forms beyond those already documented in P1 and P1b were identified. This is reported explicitly.

---

**真 (zhēn)** — as a free morpheme and root, 真 participates extensively in compounding (documented in P1b) but does not take derivational suffixes directly. The form 真性 exists in technical usage (e.g., 真性近视, true myopia, as opposed to pseudomyopia) but functions as a bound attributive modifier in medical terminology rather than as a productive derivational form within the truth-evaluation domain. No common derivation identified within the domain of this study.

**假 (jiǎ)** — as a free morpheme, 假 participates in compounding and in grammaticalized conditional constructions (documented in P1b) but does not take derivational suffixes productively within the truth-evaluation domain. No common derivation identified.

**伪 (wěi)** — as a bound root, 伪 functions productively as a prefixal element in compounding (documented in P1b) but does not take suffixes such as 性 or 者 directly. Agentive and property nominalizations are formed on compounds containing 伪 (e.g., 伪造者, 伪善者, documented above) rather than on 伪 alone. No common derivation of bare 伪 identified.

**真相 (zhēnxiàng)** — no common derivation identified. The form 真相性 is not attested. Nominal and adverbial uses are handled by the base form itself.

**真理 (zhēnlǐ)** — no common derivation identified. 真理性 is occasionally encountered in philosophical writing (e.g., 真理性认识, knowledge of a truth-bearing character) but is not a standard or widely attested form. Not included.

**真伪 (zhēnwěi)** — no common derivation identified. Functions as a binary nominal; does not take productive suffixes.

**谎言 (huǎngyán)** — no common derivation identified. The base form functions as a count noun; no productive suffixation with 性, 者, or 化 is attested.

## Note on Productivity

The suffix **性 (-xìng)** is the most productive derivational morpheme attested across this domain. It applies to adjectives and some nouns to produce abstract property nouns, and its application is broadly regular across the items examined. However, not all adjectives in the domain take it with equal frequency: 真实性, 准确性, 可靠性, and 实质性 are high-frequency forms, while 诚实性, 真诚性, and 虚假性 are lower-frequency and more restricted to formal or analytical registers.

The suffix **者 (-zhě)** applies productively to verbal bases to produce agentive nouns. It is attested on 欺骗者, 伪造者, 伪装者, and 伪善者 within this domain. Its application is constrained to verbal or verbal-nominal bases; it does not attach to purely adjectival bases.

The prefix **反- (fǎn-)** applies productively to verbal and nominal bases to produce anti- or counter- compounds. Within this domain it is attested on 欺骗, 伪造, and 假冒. Its productivity in contemporary administrative and commercial language is well-supported.
