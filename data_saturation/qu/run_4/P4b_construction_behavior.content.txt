# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P4b Construction Usage & Constraints
# Language: Quechua (qu)

---

**Scope note:** Ratings and usage notes are based strictly on patterns documented in P1–P3 and described in P4a. No constructions are added by analogy. Where P4a notes ambiguity or restricted attestation, that is reflected in the rating.

---

## 1. ATTRIBUTION

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness Rating | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Predicative attribution (direct evidential) | `Cheqam kay.` | cheqa-m kay | "This is true." (I assert from direct knowledge) | [CORE] | Standard assertive predication in Quechua discourse; the evidential clitic *-m/-mi* is grammatically required for first-hand assertion. Bare *cheqa* without an evidential is documented as grammatically incomplete as a standalone assertion. |
| Predicative attribution (direct evidential) | `Llullam chay simi.` | llulla-m chay simi | "That statement is a lie." (I assert from direct knowledge) | [CORE] | Same pattern as above; applies equally to *llulla*. Predicate-initial and predicate-final orders are both attested; the evidential clitic attaches to the focused constituent. |

**Attested alternate constructions:** Evidential variation is attested: *Cheqas.* (reportative), *Cheqacha.* (conjectural) — documented under Construction 8 (Evidential Predication). These are not alternates in the sense of being optional; they encode distinct epistemic sources.

---

## 2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness Rating | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Negated predicative attribution | `Mana cheqachu.` | mana cheqa-chu | "It is not true. / That is not correct." | [CORE] | The discontinuous frame *mana* … *-chu* is the standard negation pattern across all predicative constructions in this domain. Evidential clitic is absent in the basic negated form. |
| Negated predicative attribution | `Mana llullachu.` | mana llulla-chu | "It is not false. / That is not a lie." | [CORE] | Same pattern as above; applies symmetrically to *llulla*. |

**Attested alternate constructions:** When the speaker adds evidential commitment to a negation, *-m* attaches to *mana* (*manam … -chu*), documented in P4a notes. This variant is attested but not illustrated with a standalone example in P4a; it is noted here as an observed variant, not a constructed one.

## 3. ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness Rating | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pre-nominal adjectival modifier | `cheqa simi` | cheqa simi | "true word / true statement / honest testimony" | [CORE] | Highly productive pattern; *cheqa* combines freely with nominal heads. No agreement morphology; modifier is invariant. Attested with *simi*, *runa*, *sunqu*, *kawsay*, *yuyay*, *ñan*. |
| Pre-nominal adjectival modifier | `llulla runa` | llulla runa | "lying person / dishonest person" | [CORE] | Same pattern; *llulla* is equally productive in pre-nominal position. *Sut'i* participates in the same pattern (*sut'i simi* "clear statement"), also documented in P4a. |

**Attested alternate constructions:** *sut'i simi* ("clear statement") is documented as a parallel instance of the same pattern (P4a notes). No other alternates reported.

## 4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness Rating | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Adjective + *tukuy* (inchoative/pretense) | `cheqa tukuy` | cheqa tuku-y | "to pretend to be genuine / to act as if true" | [MARGINAL] | The pretense reading is documented as the more frequent interpretation in the truth-evaluation domain (P1b, P3 Group 10); the genuine inchoative reading ("to become true") is possible but less commonly attested. Context is required for disambiguation. |
| Adjective + *tukuy* (inchoative/pretense) | `llulla tukuy` | llulla tuku-y | "to feign / to play false / to pretend to be false" | [MARGINAL] | Same ambiguity as above; pretense reading is primary in attested data. Rated [MARGINAL] because the construction is attested but its use in the truth-evaluation domain is predominantly restricted to the deception/pretense reading rather than genuine change of state. |

**Attested alternate constructions:** None reported in P4a for this construction type.

## 5. CAUSATIVE

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness Rating | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Adjectival base + *-ncha-* causative | `cheqanchay` | cheqa-ncha-y | "to make true / to verify / to correct / to rectify" | [CORE] | The suffix *-ncha-* applies to nominal/adjectival bases; *cheqanchay* and *sut'ichay* are both attested. P4a explicitly notes this causative is well-attested across the truth-evaluation domain and is not absent. |
| Verbal base + *-chi-* causative | `pantachiy` | panta-chi-y | "to cause to err / to mislead" | [CORE] | The suffix *-chi-* applies to verbal bases; *pantachiy*, *yachachiy*, *qawachiy* are all attested. Both causative suffixes are described as productive. |

**Attested alternate constructions:** *sut'ichay* (sut'i + *-ncha-* + *-y*) is documented as a parallel instance of the *-ncha-* pattern. *yachachiy* and *qawachiy* are documented as parallel instances of the *-chi-* pattern.

## 6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness Rating | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Adjective + *-n* (possessive/nominalizing) | `cheqan` | cheqa-n | "the truth / what is correct / its truth" | [CORE] | Documented as the closest equivalent to an abstract noun "truth" in Quechua; attested in the construction *cheqanta rimay* ("speak the truth"). P4a notes that Quechua lacks a single dedicated abstract nominalizer; this is the primary strategy for abstract reference to truth. |
| Case-marked adverbial form | `cheqaqpaq` | cheqaq-paq | "truly / in truth / for real" | [CORE] | Attested adverbial expression; the *-paq* case suffix on the participial/adjectival form yields a fixed adverbial. Documented as one of the primary strategies for abstract reference alongside *cheqan*. |

**Attested alternate constructions:** *cheqa kasqan* ("its being true") is documented in P4a notes as a third strategy for abstract reference, via nominalized clause. It also appears as the embedded predicate in Construction 9. No additional alternates reported.

---

## 7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness Rating | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Verbal stem + *-q* (agentive nominalizer) | `llullakuq` | llullaku-q | "liar / one who lies / habitual deceiver" | [CORE] | The suffix *-q* is described as fully productive across verbal stems. *llullakuq*, *yachaq*, *qawaq*, *ruraq* are all attested in the truth-evaluation domain. |
| Verbal stem + *-q* (agentive nominalizer) | `yachaq` | yacha-q | "one who knows / knowledgeable person / wise person" | [CORE] | Same pattern; attested and productive. |

**Attested alternate constructions:** A truth-teller agentive is documented as not lexicalized; the attested periphrastic form is *cheqata rimaq* ("one who speaks truly"). The adjectival compound *cheqa runa* ("honest person") is also documented as an alternative strategy for this concept. Both are reported in P4a; neither is a single-morpheme agentive form.

## 8. EVIDENTIAL PREDICATION

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness Rating | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Predicate + *-m* (direct evidential) | `Cheqam.` | cheqa-m | "It is true (I know directly)" | [CORE] | Grammatically obligatory in assertive discourse; the choice among evidential clitics encodes epistemic source as a grammatical category, not optional stylistic variation. Applies to all predicates in the truth-evaluation domain. |
| Predicate + *-s* (reportative evidential) | `Cheqas.` | cheqa-s | "It is apparently true (I was told)" | [CORE] | Same grammatical status as *-m*; encodes reported/hearsay source. Attested for both *cheqa* and *llulla* (*Llullas.*). |
| Predicate + *-chá* (conjectural evidential) | `Cheqacha.` | cheqa-chá | "It is probably true (I conjecture)" | [CORE] | Encodes conjectural epistemic source. Attested for *cheqa*; P4a does not document a conjectural form for *llulla* — no such form is reported here. |
| Predicate + *-m* (direct evidential) | `Llullam.` | llulla-m | "It is false (I know directly)" | [CORE] | Direct evidential form for *llulla*; parallel to *Cheqam*. |
| Predicate + *-s* (reportative evidential) | `Llullas.` | llulla-s | "It is reportedly false" | [CORE] | Reportative form for *llulla*; attested in P4a. |

**Attested alternate constructions:** P4a notes that this pattern cross-cuts all other construction types (attribution, nominalization, etc.). The *manam … -chu* variant (evidential commitment added to negation) is documented under Construction 2.

## 9. NOMINALIZED COMPLEMENT CONSTRUCTION

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness Rating | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nominalized embedded clause as object of epistemic verb (*yachay*) | `Cheqa kasqanta yachani.` | cheqa kasqa-n-ta yacha-ni | "I know that it is true." (lit. "I know its being-true") | [CORE] | Standard pattern for expressing propositional knowledge about truth or falsity; Quechua lacks a *that*-complementizer and uses *-sqa* + possessive + accusative instead. Documented as the standard pattern for *yachay* and *iñiy*. |
| Nominalized embedded clause as object of epistemic verb (*yachay*) | `Llulla kasqanta yachani.` | llulla kasqa-n-ta yacha-ni | "I know that it is false." (lit. "I know its being-false") | [CORE] | Same pattern applied to *llulla*; parallel to the *cheqa* form above. |

**Attested alternate constructions:** The contrast between *yachay* (knowledge) and *iñiy* (belief) in this construction is explicitly attested and epistemically significant (P2b: *Iñinim, mana yachanichu* — "I believe it, I do not know it"). The belief variant (*cheqa kasqanta iñini*) is documented as a parallel instance of the same nominalized complement construction with a different matrix verb.
