# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P1b ROOT EXPANSION
# Language: Turkish (tr)

---

## Step 1 — Identify Central Roots

---

**Root 1**

**Form:** doğru / doğr-
**Transliteration:** doğru / doğr-
**P1 participation:** 3 items (*doğru*, *doğruluk*, and implicitly *yanlış* as its antonym defined against it)
**Core meaning contribution:** Straightness, directness, correctness — the primary native Turkic root encoding correspondence between a claim or action and a standard (factual, moral, or normative). The most productive native root in the truth-evaluation domain.

---

**Root 2**

**Form:** hak / hak-
**Transliteration:** hak / hak-
**P1 participation:** 5 items (*haksız*, *haklı*, *hakkaniyetli*, *hakikat*, and *hak* as base of several)
**Core meaning contribution:** Right, justice, entitlement, truth — from Arabic *ḥaqq*. Contributes both epistemic senses (what is true, what is real) and normative senses (what is rightful, what is owed). Highly productive across legal, moral, philosophical, and everyday registers.

---

**Root 3**

**Form:** gerçek / gerçek-
**Transliteration:** gerçek / gerçek-
**P1 participation:** 3 items (*gerçek*, *gerçek dışı*, and *gerçeklik* implied in the entry)
**Core meaning contribution:** Reality, genuineness, factual existence — the primary root encoding ontological correspondence (what is real as opposed to fake, illusory, or fictional). Productive in compounds and derivations across everyday, philosophical, and legal registers.

---

## Step 2 — Expand Each Root

---

### ROOT 1: doğru / doğr-

---

**1.**
**Form:** doğruluk
**Transliteration:** doğruluk
**Morpheme breakdown:** doğru (straight/correct) + -luk (nominal suffix)
**Natural English translation:** truthfulness, correctness, accuracy
**Context of use:** Used in moral discourse to name the virtue of being truthful, and in technical contexts (e.g., measurement accuracy). Standard in both philosophical writing and everyday moral evaluation.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**2.**
**Form:** doğrulamak
**Transliteration:** doğrulamak
**Morpheme breakdown:** doğru (correct) + -la- (verbalizer) + -mak (infinitive)
**Natural English translation:** to verify, to confirm, to validate
**Context of use:** Used in scientific, journalistic, and administrative contexts to describe the act of checking and confirming that something is accurate or true. *Bilgiyi doğrulamak* (to verify information) is a standard collocation.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**3.**
**Form:** doğrulama
**Transliteration:** doğrulama
**Morpheme breakdown:** doğru + -la- + -ma (verbal noun)
**Natural English translation:** verification, confirmation, validation
**Context of use:** The nominalized process of verifying or confirming. Used in technical, administrative, and journalistic registers: *kimlik doğrulama* (identity verification), *doğrulama süreci* (verification process).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**4.**
**Form:** doğrulanmak
**Transliteration:** doğrulanmak
**Morpheme breakdown:** doğru + -la- + -n- (passive) + -mak
**Natural English translation:** to be verified, to be confirmed
**Context of use:** Passive form; used when a claim, identity, or fact is confirmed by an external process or authority. *İddia doğrulandı* (the claim was confirmed/verified).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**5.**
**Form:** doğrulatmak
**Transliteration:** doğrulatmak
**Morpheme breakdown:** doğru + -la- + -t- (causative) + -mak
**Natural English translation:** to have (something) verified, to cause to be confirmed
**Context of use:** Causative form; used when a person causes or arranges for something to be verified by another party. *Belgeyi doğrulatmak* (to have the document verified).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**6.**
**Form:** doğrultmak
**Transliteration:** doğrultmak
**Morpheme breakdown:** doğru + -lt- (causative/directional) + -mak
**Natural English translation:** to straighten, to correct, to align, to aim (a weapon)
**Context of use:** Used in physical and figurative senses: straightening an object, correcting a course of action, or aiming a weapon. *Silahı doğrultmak* (to aim the weapon), *yanlışı doğrultmak* (to correct the error).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**7.**
**Form:** doğrultu
**Transliteration:** doğrultu
**Morpheme breakdown:** doğru + -ltu (nominal suffix, directional)
**Natural English translation:** direction, alignment, line (of direction)
**Context of use:** Used in technical, navigational, and figurative contexts to indicate a direction or line of orientation. *Bu doğrultuda* (in this direction/along these lines) is a very common discourse marker in formal writing.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**8.**
**Form:** doğrudan
**Transliteration:** doğrudan
**Morpheme breakdown:** doğru + -dan (ablative/adverbial suffix)
**Natural English translation:** directly, straightforwardly
**Context of use:** Used as an adverb meaning "directly" or "without intermediary." *Doğrudan iletişim* (direct communication), *doğrudan söylemek* (to say directly/straightforwardly). Extremely common across all registers.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**9.**
**Form:** doğrudan doğruya
**Transliteration:** doğrudan doğruya
**Morpheme breakdown:** doğru + -dan + doğru + -ya (reduplicative adverbial)
**Natural English translation:** directly, straight, without any intermediary
**Context of use:** A reinforced adverbial form of *doğrudan*, emphasizing the absence of any mediation or indirection. Used in formal and emphatic speech: *doğrudan doğruya söylemek* (to say it straight out).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**10.**
**Form:** doğru dürüst
**Transliteration:** doğru dürüst
**Morpheme breakdown:** doğru (correct) + dürüst (upright, honest — a separate but semantically related word)
**Natural English translation:** properly, decently, adequately; (of a person) honest and upright
**Context of use:** A fixed collocation used to mean "properly" or "decently" in everyday speech (*doğru dürüst yemek yememek*, not eating properly) and to describe a person of honest, upright character.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**11.**
**Form:** dürüst
**Transliteration:** dürüst
**Morpheme breakdown:** (from Persian *durust*, correct, sound — closely associated with *doğru* in the semantic field)
**Natural English translation:** honest, upright, straightforward
**Context of use:** Used to characterize persons and conduct as honest and morally upright. *Dürüst bir insan* (an honest person). Frequently collocates with *doğru* and participates in the same moral-epistemic field, though it is etymologically distinct.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**12.**
**Form:** dürüstlük
**Transliteration:** dürüstlük
**Morpheme breakdown:** dürüst + -lük (nominal suffix)
**Natural English translation:** honesty, integrity, uprightness
**Context of use:** Names the virtue or quality of being honest and upright. Used in moral, professional, and everyday discourse: *dürüstlük ilkesi* (the principle of honesty).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**13.**
**Form:** doğrulayıcı
**Transliteration:** doğrulayıcı
**Morpheme breakdown:** doğru + -la- + -yıcı (agentive/adjectival suffix)
**Natural English translation:** verifying, confirmatory, validator
**Context of use:** Used as an adjective or noun to describe something or someone that verifies or confirms. *Doğrulayıcı kanıt* (confirmatory evidence). More common in technical and academic registers.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**14.**
**Form:** doğrulanabilir
**Transliteration:** doğrulanabilir
**Morpheme breakdown:** doğru + -la- + -n- + -abilir (potential/ability suffix)
**Natural English translation:** verifiable
**Context of use:** Used in scientific, philosophical, and legal contexts to describe claims or facts that can in principle be verified. *Doğrulanabilir iddia* (verifiable claim). Associated with positivist and empirical discourse.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**15.**
**Form:** doğrulanabilirlik
**Transliteration:** doğrulanabilirlik
**Morpheme breakdown:** doğru + -la- + -n- + -abilir + -lik (nominal suffix)
**Natural English translation:** verifiability
**Context of use:** The abstract property of being verifiable. Used in philosophy of science and epistemology: *doğrulanabilirlik ilkesi* (the principle of verifiability, associated with logical positivism).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**16.**
**Form:** doğrusal
**Transliteration:** doğrusal
**Morpheme breakdown:** doğru (straight) + -sal (adjectival suffix)
**Natural English translation:** linear
**Context of use:** Used in mathematics, physics, and technical fields to mean "linear" (as in linear function, linear progression). *Doğrusal denklem* (linear equation), *doğrusal ilişki* (linear relationship).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**17.**
**Form:** doğru söylemek
**Transliteration:** doğru söylemek
**Morpheme breakdown:** doğru (true/correct) + söylemek (to say)
**Natural English translation:** to tell the truth, to speak correctly
**Context of use:** A basic collocation used in everyday moral and epistemic contexts to describe the act of telling the truth. *Doğruyu söyle* (tell the truth) is a common imperative.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**18.**
**Form:** doğruyu bulmak
**Transliteration:** doğruyu bulmak
**Morpheme breakdown:** doğru + -yu (accusative) + bulmak (to find)
**Natural English translation:** to find the truth, to arrive at the correct answer
**Context of use:** Used in investigative, philosophical, and everyday contexts to describe the process of arriving at what is true or correct. *Doğruyu bulmak zordur* (it is difficult to find the truth).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**19.**
**Form:** doğru yanlış
**Transliteration:** doğru yanlış
**Morpheme breakdown:** doğru (correct) + yanlış (wrong)
**Natural English translation:** right and wrong, true and false
**Context of use:** Used as a paired expression to refer to the distinction between correct and incorrect, or true and false. *Doğru yanlış ayrımı* (the distinction between right and wrong/true and false). Common in educational and moral discourse.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**20.**
**Form:** doğruluk payı
**Transliteration:** doğruluk payı
**Morpheme breakdown:** doğruluk (truthfulness/correctness) + payı (share/portion of)
**Natural English translation:** grain of truth, degree of accuracy
**Context of use:** Used to acknowledge that a claim contains some element of truth even if not entirely accurate. *Bu iddiada bir doğruluk payı var* (there is a grain of truth in this claim).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**21.**
**Form:** doğru çıkmak
**Transliteration:** doğru çıkmak
**Morpheme breakdown:** doğru (correct) + çıkmak (to come out/emerge)
**Natural English translation:** to turn out to be correct, to prove right
**Context of use:** Used to describe a prediction, claim, or person being proven correct by subsequent events. *Tahmini doğru çıktı* (his prediction turned out to be correct).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**22.**
**Form:** doğruluk testi
**Transliteration:** doğruluk testi
**Morpheme breakdown:** doğruluk (accuracy/truthfulness) + testi (test)
**Natural English translation:** truth test, accuracy test, fact-check
**Context of use:** Used in journalistic and analytical contexts to describe the process of checking the accuracy of claims. Associated with fact-checking practices.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**23.**
**Form:** doğru bilgi
**Transliteration:** doğru bilgi
**Morpheme breakdown:** doğru (correct) + bilgi (information/knowledge)
**Natural English translation:** correct information, accurate knowledge
**Context of use:** A standard collocation used in educational, administrative, and journalistic contexts to emphasize the importance of accurate information. *Doğru bilgiye ulaşmak* (to access correct information).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**24.**
**Form:** doğruluk değeri
**Transliteration:** doğruluk değeri
**Morpheme breakdown:** doğruluk (truth) + değeri (value of)
**Natural English translation:** truth value
**Context of use:** A technical term in logic and philosophy of language referring to the truth value (true or false) of a proposition. *Önermenin doğruluk değeri* (the truth value of the proposition).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**25.**
**Form:** doğruluk koşulu
**Transliteration:** doğruluk koşulu
**Morpheme breakdown:** doğruluk (truth) + koşulu (condition of)
**Natural English translation:** truth condition
**Context of use:** A technical term in formal semantics and philosophy of language. *Cümlenin doğruluk koşulları* (the truth conditions of the sentence). Used in academic linguistic and philosophical writing.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**26.**
**Form:** doğruluk işlevi
**Transliteration:** doğruluk işlevi
**Morpheme breakdown:** doğruluk (truth) + işlevi (function of)
**Natural English translation:** truth function
**Context of use:** A technical term in logic referring to a function whose value is determined by the truth values of its arguments. Used in formal logic and philosophy.
**Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**27.**
**Form:** doğru orantılı
**Transliteration:** doğru orantılı
**Morpheme breakdown:** doğru (direct/straight) + orantılı (proportional)
**Natural English translation:** directly proportional
**Context of use:** A standard mathematical and scientific expression. *İki değişken doğru orantılıdır* (the two variables are directly proportional).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**28.**
**Form:** doğruluk yemini
**Transliteration:** doğruluk yemini
**Morpheme breakdown:** doğruluk (truthfulness) + yemini (oath of)
**Natural English translation:** oath of truthfulness, truth oath
**Context of use:** Used in legal contexts to refer to an oath to tell the truth. Related to the standard legal oath formula.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**29.**
**Form:** doğruya en yakın
**Transliteration:** doğruya en yakın
**Morpheme breakdown:** doğru + -ya (dative) + en (most) + yakın (close/near)
**Natural English translation:** closest to the truth, nearest to correct
**Context of use:** Used in evaluative and epistemic contexts to describe the best available approximation to truth or correctness. *Doğruya en yakın cevap* (the answer closest to correct).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**30.**
**Form:** doğruluk adına
**Transliteration:** doğruluk adına
**Morpheme breakdown:** doğruluk (truth/truthfulness) + adına (in the name of)
**Natural English translation:** in the name of truth, for the sake of truthfulness
**Context of use:** Used in rhetorical and moral contexts to invoke truth as a justification for a statement or action. *Doğruluk adına söylemeliyim ki...* (In the name of truth, I must say that...).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

> **Note:** The *doğru-* root is highly productive. Additional attested forms exist beyond the 30 listed here, including further technical compounds in mathematics (*doğru* as "line" in geometry: *doğru parçası*, line segment; *doğru denklem*, linear equation) and extended verbal derivatives (*doğrulaştırmak*, to make more correct/accurate). These are noted in Step 3.

### ROOT 2: hak / hak-

---

**1.**
**Form:** hak
**Transliteration:** hak
**Morpheme breakdown:** hak (right, entitlement, truth — from Arabic *ḥaqq*)
**Natural English translation:** right, entitlement, truth, justice
**Context of use:** Used as a standalone noun meaning "right" or "entitlement" (*insan hakları*, human rights) and in philosophical/religious contexts meaning "truth" or "the Real" (as a name/attribute of God in Islamic tradition). Foundational to a large derivational family.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**2.**
**Form:** haklı
**Transliteration:** haklı
**Morpheme breakdown:** hak + -lı (possessing)
**Natural English translation:** right, justified, in the right
**Context of use:** Used to affirm that a person's position or claim is justified. *Haklısın* (you are right/justified). Carries both epistemic and moral senses.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**3.**
**Form:** haksız
**Transliteration:** haksız
**Morpheme breakdown:** hak + -sız (without)
**Natural English translation:** unjust, wrongful, unfair
**Context of use:** Applied to actions, decisions, or persons that violate standards of justice or rightful entitlement. *Haksız muamele* (unjust treatment).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**4.**
**Form:** haklılık
**Transliteration:** haklılık
**Morpheme breakdown:** hak + -lı + -lık (nominal suffix)
**Natural English translation:** justifiedness, rightfulness, being in the right
**Context of use:** Names the quality or state of being justified or in the right. *Haklılığını kanıtlamak* (to prove one's rightfulness/that one is right). Used in legal and everyday evaluative contexts.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**5.**
**Form:** haksızlık
**Transliteration:** haksızlık
**Morpheme breakdown:** hak + -sız + -lık
**Natural English translation:** injustice, wrongfulness, unfairness
**Context of use:** Names the quality or act of being unjust or wrongful. *Haksızlığa uğramak* (to suffer an injustice). Very common in moral, legal, and everyday discourse.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**6.**
**Form:** haklı çıkmak
**Transliteration:** haklı çıkmak
**Morpheme breakdown:** haklı (justified/right) + çıkmak (to come out/emerge)
**Natural English translation:** to be proven right, to turn out to be justified
**Context of use:** A very common collocation describing the outcome where someone's position is vindicated by events or evidence. *Sonunda haklı çıktı* (in the end, he was proven right).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**7.**
**Form:** hakkaniyet
**Transliteration:** hakkaniyet
**Morpheme breakdown:** hak + -kaniyet (Arabic-derived nominal suffix, via *ḥaqq* + *-āniyya*)
**Natural English translation:** equity, fairness, equitableness
**Context of use:** Used in legal and administrative contexts to refer to the principle of equitable treatment. *Hakkaniyet ilkesi* (the principle of equity). More formal than *adalet*.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**8.**
**Form:** hakkaniyetli
**Transliteration:** hakkaniyetli
**Morpheme breakdown:** hakkaniyet + -li (possessing)
**Natural English translation:** equitable, fair
**Context of use:** Adjectival form; used in legal and administrative writing. *Hakkaniyetli karar* (equitable decision).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**9.**
**Form:** hakkaniyetsiz
**Transliteration:** hakkaniyetsiz
**Morpheme breakdown:** hakkaniyet + -siz (without)
**Natural English translation:** inequitable, unfair
**Context of use:** The antonym of *hakkaniyetli*; used in legal and formal contexts to describe decisions or treatments that fail the standard of equity.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**10.**
**Form:** hakikat
**Transliteration:** hakikat
**Morpheme breakdown:** from Arabic *ḥaqīqa* (true nature, reality), related to *ḥaqq*
**Natural English translation:** truth, reality, the true nature of something
**Context of use:** Used in philosophical, religious, and literary contexts for deep or ultimate truth. *Hakikati aramak* (to seek the truth). More elevated in register than *gerçek* or *doğru*.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**11.**
**Form:** hakikatli
**Transliteration:** hakikatli
**Morpheme breakdown:** hakikat + -li (possessing)
**Natural English translation:** truthful, veracious, in accordance with truth
**Context of use:** Used in literary and formal contexts to describe persons or statements that are truthful or in accordance with deep truth. Less common than *doğru* or *dürüst* in everyday speech.
**Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**12.**
**Form:** hakikaten
**Transliteration:** hakikaten
**Morpheme breakdown:** hakikat + -en (adverbial suffix, from Arabic *-an*)
**Natural English translation:** truly, really, in truth
**Context of use:** An adverb used to affirm the truth of a statement, often with an emphatic or somewhat formal tone. *Hakikaten güzel bir eserdi* (it was truly a beautiful work). Somewhat more formal than *gerçekten*.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**13.**
**Form:** hak etmek
**Transliteration:** hak etmek
**Morpheme breakdown:** hak (right/desert) + etmek (to do/make)
**Natural English translation:** to deserve, to merit, to earn
**Context of use:** Used to express that someone has earned or merited something by their actions. *Bunu hak etti* (he deserved this). Extremely common across all registers.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**14.**
**Form:** hak vermek
**Transliteration:** hak vermek
**Morpheme breakdown:** hak (right) + vermek (to give)
**Natural English translation:** to concede that someone is right, to grant someone's point
**Context of use:** Used to acknowledge that another person's position is correct or justified. *Sana hak veriyorum* (I grant you are right / I concede your point). Very common in conversational and argumentative contexts.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**15.**
**Form:** hakkını vermek
**Transliteration:** hakkını vermek
**Morpheme breakdown:** hak + -kını (possessive accusative) + vermek (to give)
**Natural English translation:** to give someone their due, to do justice to something
**Context of use:** Used to mean giving someone what they are rightfully owed, or doing full justice to a topic or task. *Konunun hakkını vermek* (to do the topic justice).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**16.**
**Form:** hak ihlali
**Transliteration:** hak ihlali
**Morpheme breakdown:** hak (right) + ihlali (violation of)
**Natural English translation:** rights violation, infringement of rights
**Context of use:** A standard legal and human rights collocation. *İnsan hakları ihlali* (human rights violation). Ubiquitous in legal, journalistic, and political discourse.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**17.**
**Form:** insan hakları
**Transliteration:** insan hakları
**Morpheme breakdown:** insan (human) + hakları (rights of, plural possessive)
**Natural English translation:** human rights
**Context of use:** The standard Turkish term for human rights. Used in legal, political, and journalistic discourse. *İnsan hakları ihlali* (human rights violation), *insan hakları örgütü* (human rights organization).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**18.**
**Form:** hak arama
**Transliteration:** hak arama
**Morpheme breakdown:** hak (right) + arama (seeking)
**Natural English translation:** seeking one's rights, pursuit of justice
**Context of use:** Used in legal and political contexts to describe the act of pursuing one's legal rights or seeking redress. *Hak arama özgürlüğü* (the freedom to seek one's rights) is a constitutional concept.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**19.**
**Form:** haksız kazanç
**Transliteration:** haksız kazanç
**Morpheme breakdown:** haksız (unjust/wrongful) + kazanç (gain/profit)
**Natural English translation:** unjust enrichment, wrongful gain
**Context of use:** A fixed legal term referring to enrichment obtained without legal justification. *Haksız kazanç elde etmek* (to obtain unjust enrichment). Standard in civil law discourse.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

**20.**
**Form:** haksız rekabet
**Transliteration:** haksız rekabet
**Morpheme breakdown:** haksız (unfair/wrongful) + rekabet (competition)
**Natural English translation:** unfair competition
**Context of use:** A standard legal and commercial term. *Haksız rekabet yasası* (unfair competition law). Used in commercial law and business contexts.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**21.**
**Form:** haksız fiil
**Transliteration:** haksız fiil
**Morpheme breakdown:** haksız (wrongful) + fiil (act/deed)
**Natural English translation:** tort, wrongful act
**Context of use:** A legal term for a wrongful act giving rise to civil liability. *Haksız fiilden doğan sorumluluk* (liability arising from a wrongful act). Standard in civil law.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

**Form:** hak sahibi
**Transliteration:** hak sahibi
**Morpheme breakdown:** hak (right) + sahibi (owner/holder of)
**Natural English translation:** rights holder, entitled party
**Context of use:** Used in legal and administrative contexts to identify the person who holds a particular right. *Telif hakkı sahibi* (copyright holder).

## Step 3 — Peripheral Extensions

The following are expressions where the root's contribution appears to shift meaning or function compared to its core truth-domain use. These are flagged without interpretation or categorization.

---

**From doğru / doğr-:**

- *doğrusal* (linear) — the root contributes a geometric/mathematical sense of straightness rather than truth or correctness
- *doğrultu* (direction, line of orientation) — the root functions as a spatial/directional concept in navigation, physics, and discourse structure (*bu doğrultuda*, "along these lines")
- *doğru orantılı* (directly proportional) — the root marks a mathematical relationship of proportionality
- *doğrultmak* in the sense of aiming a weapon (*silahı doğrultmak*) — the root contributes a physical directional sense
- *doğru* as a geometric line (*doğru parçası*, line segment) — the root names a geometric object
- *doğru dürüst* as an adverb of adequacy (*doğru dürüst yapamamak*, to be unable to do something properly) — the root contributes a sense of adequacy or sufficiency rather than truth

---

**From hak / hak-:**

- *hak etmek* (to deserve, to merit) — the root contributes a sense of desert or merit rather than truth or factual correctness
- *Hak* as a divine name/attribute — the root functions as a theological proper noun
- *hakkını vermek* in the sense of doing justice to a task (*konunun hakkını vermek*) — the root contributes a sense of adequate performance or full treatment
- *telif hakkı* (copyright), *mülkiyet hakkı* (property right) — the root functions as a legal entitlement marker with no direct epistemic content
- *hak kazanmak* (to earn a right) — the root contributes a sense of earned entitlement through performance
- *hakkaniyetli* in the sense of procedural fairness — the root contributes a procedural rather than epistemic sense
- *hak düşürücü süre* (limitation period that extinguishes a right) — the root functions in a purely procedural legal sense

---

**From gerçek / gerçek-:**

- *gerçekleşmek* (to come true, to happen, to materialize) — the root contributes a sense of actualization or occurrence rather than truth-evaluation
- *gerçekleştirmek* (to carry out, to accomplish) — the root contributes a sense of bringing into existence or completing a task
- *sanal gerçeklik* (virtual reality) and *artırılmış gerçeklik* (augmented reality) — the root participates in naming technological systems that simulate or modify perceptual reality
- *gerçeküstücülük* (surrealism) — the root contributes to naming an artistic movement defined by its relationship to and departure from ordinary reality
- *gerçek kişi* (natural person) — the root functions as a legal classificatory term distinguishing types of legal subjects
- *gerçek zamanlı* (real-time) — the root contributes a temporal/technical sense of simultaneity rather than truth
- *gerçek ötesi* (post-truth) — the root participates in a compound where truth is treated as a historical or political condition rather than a property of statements
- *gerçeklik ilkesi* (reality principle, in psychoanalytic discourse) — the root contributes to a psychological/psychoanalytic technical term
