## 2. Substitution Patterns (Narrative)

### 2.1 REVELATION as Resultative Construction
Korean does not have a dedicated REVELATION operator distinct from resultative constructions. What CLCA might categorize as REVELATION is expressed through:
- **Passive resultatives**: 밝혀지다 (passive of 밝히다 "reveal")
- **Intransitive resultatives**: 드러나다 "emerge, come to light"

These are Construction 14 (RESULTATIVE CONSTRUCTIONS) in the P4a inventory. They are [CORE] and highly productive in investigative and narrative contexts. The language treats revelation as a resultative state-change rather than a separate operator.

### 2.2 CAUSATION Split: Factual vs. Character
Korean shows a clear split in causative strategies:

**Factual causation** (causing truth to be revealed/known):
- Uses light verb collocations (Construction 8): 진실을 밝히다 "reveal the truth", 사실을 알리다 "make facts known"
- [CORE] productivity
- No morphological causative required

**Character causation** (making someone truthful):
- Blocked for direct causatives: *진실하게 하다 is not attested
- Only indirect causation available: 믿게 하다 "make believe"
- [MARGINAL] productivity
- Shows **conceptual gap**: Korean resists the idea of directly causing someone to have truth-domain character traits

### 2.3 VERIFICATION and DISCONFIRMATION Share Light Verb Strategy
Both operators use Construction 8 (VERBAL COLLOCATIONS):
- **VERIFICATION**: 확인하다 "verify", 가리다 "distinguish", 알아보다 "check"
- **DISCONFIRMATION**: Same verbs + negation, or 드러내다 "expose" with negative complement

The language does not distinguish these operators constructionally; they share the same light verb frame with different semantic content in the complement.

### 2.4 NEGATION: Dual Analytic Strategies
Korean employs two fully productive analytic negation strategies:
- **Copula negation**: NP + 이/가 아니다 (for nominal predicates)
- **Verbal negation**: 안/못 + VERB or VERB-지 않다 (for verbal predicates)

No morphological negation exists for truth-domain terms. Both strategies are [CORE] and show no gaps.

### 2.5 Asymmetric Patterns

**Agentive nominalization asymmetry**:
- Negative: 거짓말쟁이 "liar" [CORE]
- Positive: *진실쟁이 "truth-teller" **MISSING**
- Reflects cultural focus on marking deviant behavior

**Privative asymmetry**:
- Negative privative: 거짓없이 "without falsehood" [CORE]
- Positive privative: 진실없이 "without truth" [MARGINAL]
- Emphasizes absence of deception over presence of truth

**Abstract nominalization asymmetry**:
- Sino-Korean: 진실성, 정직성, 확실성 [CORE]
- Native Korean: *거짓성 **MISSING (structural gap)**
- -성 suffix only attaches to Sino-Korean stems

### 2.6 Light Verb Strategy Dominance
Korean strongly prefers light verb collocations (Construction 8) over morphological operations for:
- CAUSATION (factual): 밝히다, 알리다, 숨기다
- VERIFICATION: 확인하다, 가리다, 알아보다
- DISCONFIRMATION: 밝히다 + negation, 드러내다

This is a systematic substitution pattern where verbal collocations replace what might be morphological causatives or applicatives in other languages.

## 3. Missingness Summary

### 3.1 Structural Gaps

**Construction 15 (COMPARATIVE CONSTRUCTIONS)**:
- **MISSING (structural gap)** for truth-domain terms
- Truth treated as categorical, not gradable
- *더 진실하다 "more truthful" grammatical but not attested in natural usage
- Precision terms (정확하다 "accurate") show limited gradability, but core truth terms resist comparison

**Abstract nominalization for native Korean roots**:
- **MISSING (structural gap)**: *거짓성 not possible
- -성 suffix restricted to Sino-Korean stems only
- Represents morphological constraint, not lexical gap

### 3.2 Lexical Gaps

**Positive agentive nominalization**:
- **MISSING (lexical gap)**: No word for "truth-teller" corresponding to 거짓말쟁이 "liar"
- *진실쟁이 not attested
- Asymmetric: only negative agentive exists

**Positive privative expression**:
- **MARGINAL**: 진실없이 "without truth" rarely used
- Functional gap: language prefers 거짓없이 "without falsehood" to express truthfulness

### 3.3 Conceptual Gaps

**Direct character causation**:
- **BLOCKED**: *진실하게 하다 "make truthful" not attested
- Korean resists direct causation of character traits in truth domain
- Only indirect causation available: 믿게 하다 "make believe"
- Represents conceptual constraint on causative operations

**Gradability of truth**:
- **BLOCKED**: Core truth terms resist degree modification
- Truth conceptualized as categorical, not scalar
- Comparative constructions grammatical but pragmatically odd

### 3.4 Asymmetric Gaps

**Negative-only forms**:
- Agentive: 거짓말쟁이 exists, *진실쟁이 missing
- Privative: 거짓없이 common, 진실없이 rare

**Sino-Korean-only forms**:
- Abstract nominalization: -성 suffix only with Sino-Korean stems
- Native Korean roots cannot form abstract quality nouns this way

**Pattern**: Korean systematically marks deviance (lying, deception) more explicitly than expected behavior (truthfulness, honesty) in agentive and privative constructions.

## 5. Functional Notes

### 5.1 Light Verb Strategy as Systematic Substitution
Korean systematically uses Construction 8 (VERBAL COLLOCATIONS) where other languages might use:
- Morphological causatives (CAUSATION factual)
- Applicative constructions (VERIFICATION)
- Dedicated revelation verbs (DISCONFIRMATION)

This is not a gap but a typological preference. The light verb strategy is [CORE] and fully productive.

### 5.2 Resultative as Revelation
Construction 14 (RESULTATIVE) serves the function of REVELATION. Korean does not distinguish "becoming true" from "being revealed as true" constructionally; both use resultative morphology (-어지다) or intransitive change-of-state verbs (드러나다).

### 5.3 Categorical Truth Conceptualization
The absence of productive comparative constructions (Construction 15) reflects a conceptual pattern: Korean treats truth as categorical rather than gradable. This is consistent across all truth-domain terms except precision terms (정확하다 "accurate"), which show limited gradability.

### 5.4 Asymmetric Marking of Deviance
Korean shows systematic asymmetry in marking:
- **Deviance marked**: 거짓말쟁이 "liar", 거짓없이 "without falsehood"
- **Expected behavior unmarked**: No *진실쟁이 "truth-teller", 진실없이 rare

This reflects a cultural-linguistic pattern of explicitly marking deviation from truth-norms rather than conformity to them.
