| 真 (zhēn) | 假 (jiǎ) | zhēn vs. jiǎ | "true, real, genuine" vs. "false, fake, counterfeit" | This is the fundamental lexical opposition in the Chinese truth domain, contrasting genuine/authentic items and true statements with counterfeit/fake items and false statements.
