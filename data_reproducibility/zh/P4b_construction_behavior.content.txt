# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P4b Construction Usage & Constraints
# Language: Chinese (zh)

---

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness Rating | Usage Notes |
|------------------|---------|-----------------|-------------|-------------------|-------------|
| **1. ATTRIBUTION** | 这个说法是真的 | zhè ge shuō fǎ shì zhēn de | "This statement is true" | [CORE] | Extremely common in all registers. The 是...的 frame is the default predicative structure. Bare adjective predicates (without 是) occur but are less emphatic and more restricted to informal speech. |
| **2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION** | 这不是真的 | zhè bù shì zhēn de | "This is not true" | [CORE] | Standard negation pattern across all contexts. The alternative pattern with 不 directly before adjectives (不真, 不对) is also common but slightly less categorical in force. Both patterns freely alternate. |
| **3. ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION** | 真实的情况 | zhēn shí de qíng kuàng | "the true/actual situation" | [CORE] | Highly productive across all registers. The 的 particle is generally required except in lexicalized compounds (真相, 事实). Deletion of 的 is restricted to fixed, high-frequency noun-noun combinations. |
| **4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE** | 假的变成真的了 | jiǎ de biàn chéng zhēn de le | "The false became true" | [CORE] | Common in narrative and descriptive contexts. 变(成) and 成为 are both productive. The pattern appears frequently in idiomatic expressions (弄假成真, 假戏真做) which may be more common than productive uses. |
| **5. CAUSATIVE** | 证据使这个说法变得可靠 | zhèng jù shǐ zhè ge shuō fǎ biàn de kě kào | "The evidence makes this claim reliable" | [MARGINAL] | Grammatically well-formed but less idiomatic than resultative verb compounds (证实, 查实, 核实). The periphrastic causative with 使/让 is more formal/literary; resultative compounds dominate in actual usage. |
| **6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION** | 真实性 | zhēn shí xìng | "authenticity, truthfulness" | [CORE] | Extremely productive with -性 suffix across formal and academic registers. The -度 suffix is also productive but more restricted to contexts emphasizing measurability or gradability. Both patterns are standard in written Chinese. |
| **7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION** | 欺骗者 | qī piàn zhě | "deceiver, liar" | [MARGINAL] | Productive but restricted to formal/literary registers. Colloquial speech strongly prefers compound nouns (骗子) or descriptive phrases (说谎的人). The -者 suffix marks elevated register and is rare in spoken language. |
| **8. RESULTATIVE COMPLEMENTS** | 说对了 | shuō duì le | "said correctly, got it right" | [CORE] | Extremely productive and common across all registers. This is a fundamental construction type in Chinese grammar. The pattern freely combines action verbs with truth-domain complements (对, 错, 实) to encode achieved results. |
| **9. POTENTIAL COMPLEMENTS** | 说得对 | shuō de duì | "can say correctly, be right in saying" | [CORE] | Highly productive pattern expressing ability or possibility of achieving a result. Common in both spoken and written language. The 得/不 infix systematically derives potential forms from resultative compounds. |
| **10. COMPARATIVE CONSTRUCTIONS** | 这个说法比那个更真实 | zhè ge shuō fǎ bǐ nà ge gèng zhēn shí | "This statement is more true/authentic than that one" | [CORE] | Standard comparative structure used across all contexts. The 比 pattern with 更/还 is the default comparative construction. The alternative 没有 pattern for negative comparison is equally common and productive. |
| **11. QUESTION FORMATIONS** | 对不对？ | duì bù duì | "Right or not? Correct?" | [CORE] | Multiple question strategies all highly productive. A-not-A pattern (对不对, 是不是) is extremely common in spoken Chinese. Question particle 吗 and alternative questions with 还是 are equally natural and freely used. |
| **12. ADVERBIAL MODIFICATION** | 真诚地道歉 | zhēn chéng de dào qiàn | "sincerely apologize" | [CORE] | Productive pattern across registers, though some high-frequency adverbs (确实, 的确) have lexicalized without 地. The 地 particle is generally required for productive adverbial derivation from adjectives. |
| **13. REDUPLICATION PATTERNS** | 老老实实 | lǎo lǎo shí shí | "very honest, obediently" | [MARGINAL] | Productive but restricted to certain adjectives and adds colloquial/informal flavor. Not all truth-domain adjectives permit AABB reduplication. Common with 真实, 老实, 实在, 认真, but lexically restricted overall. |
| **14. DISCOURSE MARKERS** | 说实话 | shuō shí huà | "to tell the truth, honestly speaking" | [CORE] | Extremely common in spoken discourse and informal writing. These expressions have grammaticalized as pragmatic markers with reduced literal meaning. They function to frame utterances and signal speaker stance rather than make truth claims. |
| **15. EVIDENTIAL CONSTRUCTIONS** | 查明真相 | chá míng zhēn xiàng | "ascertain the truth, investigate the facts" | [CORE] | Common in formal, journalistic, and legal contexts. The pattern combines verification verbs with truth-domain objects productively. Highly frequent in news reporting and official communications. |
| **16. CONFORMITY CONSTRUCTIONS** | 符合事实 | fú hé shì shí | "conform to facts, accord with reality" | [CORE] | Standard in formal, legal, and journalistic registers. The 符合 pattern and its variant 与...相符/不符 are both productive and common in written Chinese. Less frequent in casual spoken language. |
| **17. INTENSIFICATION WITH FIXED EXPRESSIONS** | 千真万确 | qiān zhēn wàn què | "absolutely true, completely certain" | [MARGINAL] | These are conventionalized idioms rather than productive patterns. Each expression is lexically fixed. Common in emphatic contexts but not freely generative. Usage is restricted to specific idiomatic combinations. |
| **18. NEGATION OF EVIDENCE/BASIS** | 没有根据 | méi yǒu gēn jù | "groundless, without basis, unfounded" | [CORE] | Common pattern for criticizing unsupported claims across all registers. Both 没有 and the emphatic 毫无 are productive with evidential nouns (根据, 证据, 事实根据). Frequent in argumentative and evaluative contexts. |

## ALTERNATE CONSTRUCTIONS ATTESTED

### For Construction 1 (Attribution):
- **Bare adjective predicate**: 这个说法真 (zhè ge shuō fǎ zhēn) "This statement [is] true" — less emphatic, more informal

### For Construction 2 (Negative Attribution):
- **Direct negation**: 不真 (bù zhēn) "not true", 不对 (bù duì) "not correct" — slightly less categorical

### For Construction 10 (Comparative):
- **Negative comparison**: 这个没有那个真实 (zhè ge méi yǒu nà ge zhēn shí) "This is not as true as that" — equally common alternative

### For Construction 11 (Questions):
- **Question particle**: 真的吗？(zhēn de ma) "Really?" — alternative to A-not-A pattern
- **Alternative question**: 真的还是假的？(zhēn de hái shì jiǎ de) "True or false?" — explicit choice question

### For Construction 16 (Conformity):
- **Variant pattern**: 与事实不符 (yǔ shì shí bù fú) "not correspond with facts" — alternative structure with 与...相符/不符

---

## GENERAL OBSERVATIONS

1. **Register variation**: Constructions 5, 7, 15, and 16 show clear formal/literary bias, while constructions 13 and 14 lean informal/colloquial.

2. **Productivity gradient**: Constructions 1-4, 6, 8-12, 18 are highly productive. Constructions 13 and 17 are lexically restricted to specific items.

3. **Spoken vs. written**: Constructions 14 (discourse markers) and 11 (A-not-A questions) are particularly characteristic of spoken language, while 15-16 (evidential/conformity) are more typical of written formal registers.

4. **Compound preference**: Construction 5 notes that resultative verb compounds (证实, 核实) are strongly preferred over periphrastic causatives in actual usage, suggesting lexicalized patterns dominate over productive syntax in this domain.
