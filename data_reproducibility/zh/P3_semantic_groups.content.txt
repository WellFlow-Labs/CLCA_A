# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P3 NATURAL SEMANTIC GROUPING
# Language: Chinese (zh)

---

## METHODOLOGICAL NOTE

This grouping is based strictly on observed usage patterns, typical contexts, and natural contrasts in Chinese discourse. Groups reflect how speakers actually use and distinguish these expressions, not imposed theoretical categories. Overlaps, asymmetries, and ungrouped items are preserved as found in the data.

---

## GROUP 1: FACTUAL CORRESPONDENCE

**Definition:** Expressions used to evaluate whether statements, claims, or representations correspond to actual states of affairs or objective reality. These terms appear in contexts of verification, fact-checking, and distinguishing claims from reality. Commonly used when assessing the accuracy of reports, accounts, or descriptions against what actually exists or occurred.

**Representative expressions:**

**Core members:**
- 真实 (zhēnshí) "real, true" — evaluates whether something corresponds to reality
- 事实 (shìshí) "fact, reality" — refers to actual states of affairs
- 符合 (fúhé) "conform to, accord with" — marks correspondence relationship
- 真相 (zhēnxiàng) "truth, the real facts" — the actual situation behind appearances
- 实际 (shíjì) "actual, practical, reality" — what actually is the case
- 实情 (shíqíng) "actual situation, true state of affairs"
- 属实 (shǔshí) "be true, be factual"
- 证实 (zhèngshí) "confirm, verify, prove"
- 核实 (héshí) "verify, check, confirm"
- 查实 (cháshí) "verify, ascertain"

**Phrasal constructions:**
- 符合事实 (fú hé shì shí) "conform to facts"
- 与事实相符 (yǔ shì shí xiāng fú) "correspond with facts"
- 不符合事实 (bù fú hé shì shí) "not conform to facts"
- 实事求是 (shí shì qiú shì) "seek truth from facts"
- 真相大白 (zhēn xiàng dà bái) "the truth comes to light"
- 查明真相 (chá míng zhēn xiàng) "ascertain the truth"
- 揭露真相 (jiē lù zhēn xiàng) "reveal the truth"

**Negations:**
- 不实 (bùshí) "untrue, false, inaccurate"
- 不真实 (bù zhēnshí) "unreal, untrue"
- 虚假 (xūjiǎ) "false, fake, fraudulent"

---

## GROUP 2: CORRECTNESS IN JUDGMENT OR ACTION

**Definition:** Expressions evaluating whether judgments, answers, decisions, or actions are correct or mistaken. These terms appear in educational contexts (right/wrong answers), practical decision-making (correct/incorrect choices), and evaluations of whether someone did something properly. Distinguished from factual correspondence by focus on normative correctness rather than correspondence to reality.

**Representative expressions:**

**Core members:**
- 对 (duì) "correct, right" — marks correctness of answers, judgments, actions
- 错 (cuò) "wrong, mistaken, error" — marks incorrectness or mistakes
- 正确 (zhèngquè) "correct, right, proper" — indicates approved or proper positions
- 错误 (cuòwù) "error, mistake"

**Resultative constructions:**
- 说对 (shuōduì) "say correctly"
- 做对 (zuòduì) "do correctly"
- 猜对 (cāiduì) "guess correctly"
- 说错 (shuōcuò) "say wrongly"
- 做错 (zuòcuò) "do wrongly"
- 看错 (kàncuò) "see wrongly, misjudge"
- 认错 (rèncuò) "admit mistake"

**Phrasal constructions:**
- 对不对？(duì bù duì?) "right? isn't it?"
- 没错 (méi cuò) "that's right, exactly"
- 不对劲 (bù duì jìn) "something's wrong"
- 大错特错 (dà cuò tè cuò) "completely wrong"
- 对症下药 (duì zhèng xià yào) "prescribe the right medicine"
- 将错就错 (jiāng cuò jiù cuò) "make the best of a mistake"
- 知错能改，善莫大焉 (zhī cuò néng gǎi, shàn mò dà yān) "knowing mistakes and correcting them is the greatest virtue"

**Derived forms:**
- 正确性 (zhèngquèxìng) "correctness, validity"
- 错误性 (cuòwùxìng) "erroneousness"
- 完全正确 (wán quán zhèng què) "completely correct"
- 绝对正确 (jué duì zhèng què) "absolutely correct"

## GROUP 3: PRECISION AND ACCURACY

**Definition:** Expressions evaluating the degree of exactness, precision, or accuracy in measurements, statements, or representations. These terms appear in technical, scientific, and informational contexts where fine-grained correspondence matters. Focus is on how closely something matches a standard or target, with emphasis on measurable precision rather than simple truth/falsity.

**Representative expressions:**

**Core members:**
- 准确 (zhǔnquè) "accurate, precise, exact"
- 准 (zhǔn) "accurate, standard, correct"
- 精确 (jīngquè) "precise, exact" [not in P1 but related]

**Derived forms:**
- 准确性 (zhǔnquèxìng) "accuracy, precision"
- 准确度 (zhǔnquèdù) "degree of accuracy"
- 不准确 (bù zhǔnquè) "inaccurate, imprecise"
- 准确无误 (zhǔn què wú wù) "accurate and error-free"
- 更准确 (gèng zhǔnquè) "more accurate"

**Related constructions:**
- 失真 (shīzhēn) "distorted, lose fidelity" — loss of accuracy in reproduction

---

## GROUP 4: AUTHENTICITY AND GENUINENESS

**Definition:** Expressions distinguishing genuine items, materials, or identities from counterfeits, imitations, or false versions. These terms appear in commercial contexts (genuine vs. fake products), art authentication (original vs. copy), and identity verification (real vs. assumed identity). Focus is on whether something is what it claims or appears to be, rather than on propositional truth.

**Representative expressions:**

**Core members:**
- 真 (zhēn) "true, real, genuine" — when applied to objects/materials
- 假 (jiǎ) "false, fake, counterfeit"
- 真品 (zhēnpǐn) "genuine article, authentic item"
- 真迹 (zhēnjì) "authentic work, original"
- 真丝 (zhēnsī) "real silk, pure silk"
- 真金 (zhēnjīn) "real gold, pure gold"
- 真本 (zhēnběn) "authentic edition, original text"

**Phrasal constructions:**
- 真假 (zhēnjiǎ) "true or false, authenticity"
- 真伪 (zhēnwěi) "authenticity, genuineness"
- 以假乱真 (yǐ jiǎ luàn zhēn) "pass off the fake as genuine"
- 真伪莫辨 (zhēn wěi mò biàn) "unable to distinguish true from false"
- 货真价实 (huò zhēn jià shí) "genuine goods at fair prices"

**Counterfeit-related:**
- 假冒 (jiǎmào) "counterfeit, pass off as"
- 假造 (jiǎzào) "fabricate, forge, counterfeit"
- 假证 (jiǎzhèng) "false certificate, forged document"

**Artificial/prosthetic (different semantic domain):**
- 假牙 (jiǎyá) "false tooth, dentures"
- 假发 (jiǎfà) "wig, hairpiece"
- 假肢 (jiǎzhī) "artificial limb, prosthesis"

## GROUP 5: SINCERITY AND HONESTY IN EXPRESSION

**Definition:** Expressions evaluating whether people's words, emotions, or intentions genuinely reflect their inner states, or whether they are pretending, deceiving, or being insincere. These terms appear in interpersonal contexts, moral evaluation of character, and assessment of emotional authenticity. Focus is on alignment between inner reality and outward expression.

**Representative expressions:**

**Core members:**
- 诚 (chéng) "sincere, honest, truthful"
- 诚实 (chéngshí) "honest, truthful, sincere"
- 真诚 (zhēnchéng) "sincere, genuine, heartfelt"
- 真心 (zhēnxīn) "sincere, wholehearted, genuine"
- 真挚 (zhēnzhì) "sincere, genuine, earnest"
- 真情 (zhēnqíng) "true feelings, genuine emotion"

**Phrasal constructions:**
- 真心诚意 (zhēn xīn chéng yì) "wholehearted sincerity"
- 真心实意 (zhēn xīn shí yì) "wholehearted sincerity"
- 诚心诚意 (chéng xīn chéng yì) "with complete sincerity"
- 真情实感 (zhēn qíng shí gǎn) "genuine feelings and emotions"
- 开诚布公 (kāi chéng bù gōng) "be frank and open"
- 精诚所至，金石为开 (jīng chéng suǒ zhì, jīn shí wèi kāi) "complete sincerity can move heaven and earth"

**Derived forms:**
- 诚实地 (chéngshí de) "honestly, truthfully"
- 真诚地 (zhēnchéng de) "sincerely, genuinely"
- 真心地 (zhēnxīn de) "sincerely, wholeheartedly"
- 不诚实 (bù chéngshí) "dishonest, untruthful"

**Insincerity/pretense:**
- 假装 (jiǎzhuāng) "pretend, feign, fake"
- 假意 (jiǎyì) "pretense, feigned intention"
- 虚情假意 (xū qíng jiǎ yì) "hypocritical, insincere"
- 假仁假义 (jiǎ rén jiǎ yì) "hypocritical benevolence"
- 假装不知道 (jiǎ zhuāng bù zhī dào) "pretend not to know"

## GROUP 6: DECEPTION AND LYING

**Definition:** Expressions describing deliberate acts of deceiving, lying, or misleading others through false statements or fraudulent behavior. These terms appear in moral condemnation, legal contexts, and warnings about dishonest behavior. Distinguished from mere error or mistake by emphasis on intentional deception.

**Representative expressions:**

**Core members:**
- 谎言 (huǎngyán) "lie, falsehood, untruth"
- 欺骗 (qīpiàn) "deceive, cheat, defraud"
- 假话 (jiǎhuà) "lie, falsehood, untruth"
- 撒谎 (sā huǎng) "tell lies, lie"
- 说谎话 (shuō huǎng huà) "tell lies"

**Phrasal constructions:**
- 弄虚作假 (nòng xū zuò jiǎ) "practice fraud, engage in deception"
- 自欺欺人 (zì qī qī rén) "deceive oneself and others"
- 欺世盗名 (qī shì dào míng) "deceive the public to gain fame"
- 欺上瞒下 (qī shàng mán xià) "deceive superiors and hide truth from subordinates"
- 假公济私 (jiǎ gōng jì sī) "use public office for private gain"
- 欺骗别人 (qī piàn bié rén) "deceive others"
- 被骗了 (bèi piàn le) "was deceived"
- 上当受骗 (shàng dàng shòu piàn) "be taken in, be deceived"
- 隐瞒真相 (yǐn mán zhēn xiàng) "conceal the truth"

**Agent forms:**
- 欺骗者 (qīpiànzhě) "deceiver, cheater, fraud"

**Contrasts with honesty:**
- 说真话 (shuō zhēn huà) "tell the truth"
- 讲真话 (jiǎng zhēn huà) "tell the truth"
- 实话实说 (shí huà shí shuō) "speak the plain truth"
- 童叟无欺 (tóng sǒu wú qī) "honest dealing with all"

## GROUP 7: RELIABILITY AND TRUSTWORTHINESS

**Definition:** Expressions evaluating whether sources, people, information, or systems can be depended upon for accuracy, consistency, or proper functioning. These terms appear in contexts of source evaluation, quality assessment, and trust judgments. Focus is on dependability over time rather than single-instance truth.

**Representative expressions:**

**Core members:**
- 可靠 (kěkào) "reliable, dependable, trustworthy"

**Derived forms:**
- 可靠性 (kěkàoxìng) "reliability, dependability"
- 可靠度 (kěkàodù) "degree of reliability"
- 不可靠 (bù kěkào) "unreliable, undependable"

**Related constructions:**
- 值得信赖 (zhí dé xìn lài) "trustworthy, reliable"
- 可以信赖 (kě yǐ xìn lài) "can be trusted"
- 不可信 (bù kě xìn) "untrustworthy, unreliable"

---

## GROUP 8: OBJECTIVITY AND SUBJECTIVITY

**Definition:** Expressions distinguishing perspectives, judgments, or claims based on whether they are independent of personal bias (objective) or influenced by personal viewpoint (subjective). These terms appear in academic discourse, analytical contexts, and methodological discussions. Used to qualify the epistemic status of claims or to critique bias.

**Representative expressions:**

**Core members:**
- 客观 (kèguān) "objective, impartial"
- 主观 (zhǔguān) "subjective, personal"

**Derived forms:**
- 客观性 (kèguānxìng) "objectivity, impartiality"
- 主观性 (zhǔguānxìng) "subjectivity, personal bias"
- 客观化 (kèguānhuà) "objectify, make objective"
- 客观地 (kèguān de) "objectively"
- 主观地 (zhǔguān de) "subjectively"

**Phrasal constructions:**
- 客观事实 (kè guān shì shí) "objective facts"
- 主观意见 (zhǔ guān yì jiàn) "subjective opinion"
- 主观臆断 (zhǔ guān yì duàn) "subjective conjecture"
- 客观地看 (kè guān de kàn) "objectively speaking"
- 客观地说 (kè guān de shuō) "objectively speaking"
- 从客观角度 (cóng kè guān jiǎo dù) "from an objective perspective"

---

## GROUP 9: SUBSTANCE VERSUS APPEARANCE

**Definition:** Expressions contrasting what something actually is (its substance, essence, or reality) with how it appears, is named, or is represented. These terms appear in critical analysis, philosophical discourse, and evaluations of whether things live up to their names or reputations. Focus is on the gap or alignment between surface and depth.

**Representative expressions:**

**Core members:**
- 实 (shí) "solid, real, actual" — emphasizing substance
- 虚 (xū) "empty, void, false, unreal" — lacking substance
- 本质 (běnzhì) "essence, nature, essential quality"
- 实质 (shízhì) "substance, essence, substantive"

**Phrasal constructions:**
- 名副其实 (míng fù qí shí) "worthy of the name"
- 名不副实 (míng bù fù shí) "unworthy of the name"
- 华而不实 (huá ér bù shí) "showy but lacking substance"
- 徒有虚名 (tú yǒu xū míng) "have an undeserved reputation"
- 名实相符 (míng shí xiāng fú) "name matches reality"
- 名实不符 (míng shí bù fú) "name doesn't match reality"
- 表里如一 (biǎo lǐ rú yī) "the same inside and out"
- 表里不一 (biǎo lǐ bù yī) "inconsistent in word and deed"
- 言行一致 (yán xíng yī zhì) "words match deeds"
- 言行不一 (yán xíng bù yī) "words don't match deeds"

**Related:**
- 真才实学 (zhēn cái shí xué) "real talent and genuine learning"
- 真凭实据 (zhēn píng shí jù) "solid evidence"

---

## GROUP 10: PRACTICAL IMPLEMENTATION AND ACTUALIZATION

**Definition:** Expressions describing the process of making plans, ideas, or potentials into actual reality, or evaluating whether something exists in practice versus theory. These terms appear in administrative, organizational, and policy contexts. Focus is on the transition from abstract to concrete, from plan to execution.

**Representative expressions:**

**Core members:**
- 实践 (shíjiàn) "practice, put into practice"
- 实现 (shíxiàn) "realize, achieve, bring about"
- 实施 (shíshī) "implement, put into effect"
- 实行 (shíxíng) "implement, practice, put into effect"
- 落实 (luòshí) "implement, carry out, put into practice"

**Phrasal constructions:**
- 实事求是 (shí shì qiú shì) "seek truth from facts, be practical"
- 脚踏实地 (jiǎo tà shí dì) "down-to-earth, practical and realistic"
- 实践出真知 (shí jiàn chū zhēn zhī) "practice produces true knowledge"
- 实践是检验真理的唯一标准 (shí jiàn shì jiǎn yàn zhēn lǐ de wéi yī biāo zhǔn) "practice is the sole criterion for testing truth"

**Related:**
- 实际 (shíjì) "actual, practical, reality" — contrasting practice with theory
- 实用 (shíyòng) "practical, pragmatic, functional"
- 实际上 (shí jì shàng) "actually, in fact, in reality"
- 不实际 (bù shíjì) "impractical, unrealistic"

## GROUP 11: PHYSICAL SOLIDITY AND MATERIAL PROPERTIES

**Definition:** Expressions describing physical density, structural integrity, or material composition of objects. These terms appear in descriptions of construction, manufacturing, and physical objects. While etymologically related to truth-domain terms through the morpheme 实, they function primarily in the material/physical domain rather than epistemic evaluation.

**Representative expressions:**

**Core members:**
- 实心 (shíxīn) "solid (not hollow)"
- 结实 (jiēshi) "sturdy, durable, strong"
- 厚实 (hòushí) "thick, solid, substantial"
- 坚实 (jiānshí) "solid, firm, substantial"
- 扎实 (zhāshi) "solid, sturdy, sound"

**Note:** These terms share morphology with truth-domain expressions but function in a distinct semantic domain focused on physical properties.

---

## GROUP 12: CHARACTER TRAITS AND PERSONAL QUALITIES

**Definition:** Expressions describing personality characteristics related to honesty, simplicity, reliability, or seriousness as enduring traits rather than specific acts. These terms appear in character evaluation and personality description. Distinguished from Group 5 (sincerity in expression) by focus on stable character traits rather than momentary alignment of inner/outer states.

**Representative expressions:**

**Core members:**
- 老实 (lǎoshi) "honest, frank, well-behaved, simple"
- 朴实 (pǔshí) "simple, plain, down-to-earth, unpretentious"
- 踏实 (tāshi) "steady, dependable, at ease, down-to-earth"
- 认真 (rènzhēn) "serious, earnest, conscientious"
- 真率 (zhēnshuài) "frank, candid, natural"

**Intensified forms:**
- 老老实实 (lǎolǎo shíshí) "very honest, obediently"
- 认认真真 (rènrèn zhēnzhēn) "very seriously, very conscientiously"
- 踏踏实实 (tàtà shíshí) "very steady, very dependable"
- 实实在在 (shíshí zàizài) "very real, absolutely genuine"

**Related:**
- 实在 (shízài) "real, honest" — when describing character
- 诚实者 (chéngshízhě) "honest person"

---

## GROUP 13: HYPOTHETICAL AND SUPPOSITIONAL REASONING

**Definition:** Expressions introducing hypothetical scenarios, assumptions, or conditional reasoning. These terms appear in logical argumentation, planning, and theoretical discussion. While using the morpheme 假 (false), they function to mark epistemic stance (hypothetical) rather than to evaluate truth-value.

**Representative expressions:**

**Core members:**
- 假如 (jiǎrú) "if, supposing, in case"
- 假设 (jiǎshè) "suppose, assume, hypothesis"
- 假定 (jiǎdìng) "assume, suppose, given that"
- 假想 (jiǎxiǎng) "imagine, suppose, hypothetical"

**Phrasal constructions:**
- 假如说 (jiǎ rú shuō) "suppose, if we say"
- 假以时日 (jiǎ yǐ shí rì) "given time, in due course"

**Note:** These expressions are semantically distinct from other 假-based terms that mark falsity or deception.

---

## GROUP 14: ARTISTIC AND REPRESENTATIONAL REALISM

**Definition:** Expressions evaluating how realistically or vividly artistic representations, performances, or simulations capture reality. These terms appear in aesthetic evaluation, art criticism, and assessment of simulations. Focus is on verisimilitude and lifelike quality rather than factual truth.

**Representative expressions:**

**Core members:**
- 逼真 (bīzhēn) "lifelike, realistic, true to life"
- 写真 (xiězhēn) "portrait, photograph, realistic depiction"
- 真切 (zhēnqiè) "vivid, distinct, genuine"

**Related:**
- 失真 (shīzhēn) "distorted, lose fidelity" — in technical reproduction

---

## GROUP 15: DISCOURSE MARKERS AND EMPHATIC EXPRESSIONS

**Definition:** Expressions functioning as discourse markers to introduce statements, emphasize truth-value, express surprise, or manage conversational flow. These terms appear in everyday speech to frame utterances or express speaker stance. Grammaticalized uses of truth-domain vocabulary.

**Representative expressions:**

**Truth-affirming markers:**
- 说真的 (shuō zhēn de) "to tell the truth, honestly speaking"
- 说实话 (shuō shí huà) "to tell the truth, honestly speaking"
- 老实说 (lǎo shí shuō) "honestly speaking, to be frank"
- 说实在的 (shuō shí zài de) "to be honest, frankly speaking"
- 实际上 (shí jì shàng) "actually, in fact"
- 事实上 (shì shí shàng) "in fact, actually"

**Confirmation/verification:**
- 真的吗？(zhēn de ma?) "really? is that true?"
- 当真 (dāng zhēn) "take seriously, really mean it"
- 确实 (quèshí) "indeed, really, truly"
- 的确 (díquè) "indeed, really, certainly"

**Exclamations:**
- 真是的 (zhēn shì de) "really! good grief!"

**Tag questions:**
- 对不对？(duì bù duì?) "right? isn't it?"

---

## GROUP 16: EVIDENTIAL AND VERIFICATION PROCESSES

**Definition:** Expressions describing processes of investigating, verifying, confirming, or establishing facts through evidence. These terms appear in investigative, legal, and journalistic contexts. Focus is on the procedural aspects of establishing truth rather than the truth-value itself.

**Representative expressions:**

**Verification processes:**
- 证实 (zhèngshí) "confirm, verify, prove"
- 核实 (héshí) "verify, check, confirm"
- 查实 (cháshí) "verify, ascertain"
- 坐实 (zuòshí) "confirm, establish as fact"
- 属实 (shǔshí) "be true, be factual"

**Investigation:**
- 查明真相 (chá míng zhēn xiàng) "ascertain the truth"
- 弄清真相 (nòng qīng zhēn xiàng) "clarify the truth"
- 揭露真相 (jiē lù zhēn xiàng) "reveal the truth"
- 核实情况 (hé shí qíng kuàng) "verify the situation"
- 证实消息 (zhèng shí xiāo xi) "confirm the news"

**Evidence-related:**
- 真凭实据 (zhēn píng shí jù) "solid evidence"
- 查无实据 (chá wú shí jù) "no concrete evidence found"
- 实证 (shízhèng) "empirical evidence, positive proof"
- 实录 (shílù) "faithful record, veritable record"
- 实例 (shílì) "concrete example, actual case"

---

## GROUP 17: INTENSIFICATION AND EMPHASIS OF TRUTH-VALUE

**Definition:** Expressions that intensify or emphasize the degree of truth, certainty, or genuineness. These terms appear when speakers want to strengthen assertions or remove doubt. Often involve reduplication, hyperbolic imagery, or emphatic modifiers.

**Representative expressions:**

**Emphatic affirmations:**
- 千真万确 (qiān zhēn wàn què) "absolutely true, completely certain"
- 真真实实 (zhēnzhēn shíshí) "very real, absolutely true"
- 实打实 (shí dǎ shí) "absolutely real, genuine, solid"
- 真刀真枪 (zhēn dāo zhēn qiāng) "for real, in earnest"

**Metaphorical emphasis:**
- 真金不怕火炼 (zhēn jīn bù pà huǒ liàn) "real gold fears no fire"
- 铁一般的事实 (tiě yī bān de shì shí) "iron-clad facts"
- 不容置疑的事实 (bù róng zhì yí de shì shí) "indisputable facts"

**Absolute modifiers:**
- 完全正确 (wán quán zhèng què) "completely correct"
- 绝对正确 (jué duì zhèng què) "absolutely correct"
- 准确无误 (zhǔn què wú wù) "accurate and error-free"

---

## GROUP 18: AMBIGUITY AND MIXED TRUTH-VALUE

**Definition:** Expressions describing situations where truth and falsehood are mixed, unclear, or difficult to distinguish. These terms appear when discussing complex situations, ambiguous evidence, or deliberate mixing of truth and lies. Focus is on the difficulty of determination rather than clear truth-value.

**Representative expressions:**

**Core members:**
- 真真假假 (zhēn zhēn jiǎ jiǎ) "mix of truth and falsehood"
- 半真半假 (bàn zhēn bàn jiǎ) "half true and half false"
- 亦真亦假 (yì zhēn yì jiǎ) "both true and false, ambiguous"
- 真伪莫辨 (zhēn wěi mò biàn) "unable to distinguish true from false"
- 真假难辨 (zhēn jiǎ nán biàn) "hard to distinguish real from fake"

**Related:**
- 假象 (jiǎxiàng) "false appearance, illusion"

---

## GROUP 19: CONCRETE OBJECTS AND MATERIAL REALITY

**Definition:** Expressions referring to physical objects, material entities, or tangible reality as opposed to abstract concepts, representations, or monetary equivalents. These terms appear in commercial, administrative, and philosophical contexts distinguishing material from immaterial.

**Representative expressions:**

**Core members:**
- 实体 (shítǐ) "entity, substance, physical object"
- 实物 (shíwù) "physical object, material object, goods in kind"
- 实数 (shíshù) "real number (mathematics), actual number"

**Related:**
- 实体化 (shítǐhuà) "materialize, embody, instantiate"

## GROUP 20: FULFILLMENT AND ENRICHMENT

**Definition:** Expressions describing states of being fulfilled, enriched, or having substantial content, or the process of enriching something. These terms appear in describing life experiences, content quality, or psychological states. Extended from material substance to experiential richness.

**Core members:**

- 殷实 (yīnshí) "well-off, substantial, prosperous"

## AMBIGUOUS AND OVERLAPPING ITEMS

**Items belonging to multiple groups:**

1. **真实 (zhēnshí)** — appears in both Group 1 (factual correspondence) and Group 4 (authenticity), depending on whether applied to statements or objects

2. **实际 (shíjì)** — appears in both Group 1 (factual correspondence, as "actual reality") and Group 10 (practical implementation, as "practical vs. theoretical")

3. **实在 (shízài)** — appears in Group 1 (factual correspondence, as "real, true"), Group 12 (character traits, as "honest"), and functions as discourse marker in Group 15

4. **认真 (rènzhēn)** — appears in Group 12 (character traits) but also functions as evaluative term for work quality beyond truth-domain

5. **真正 (zhēnzhèng)** — can mark both authenticity (Group 4) and emphasis (Group 17)

6. **假 (jiǎ)** — appears in Group 4 (counterfeit objects), Group 6 (deception), Group 13 (hypothetical reasoning), and in prosthetic/artificial items (separate domain)

7. **对 (duì)** — appears in Group 2 (correctness) but also has spatial/relational meanings outside truth-domain

8. **实 (shí)** — appears across Groups 1, 9, 10, 11, 19 depending on context

**Items that don't fit cleanly into groups:**

1. **真理 (zhēnlǐ) "truth, verity"** — refers to fundamental philosophical/ideological truths; more abstract and universal than factual correspondence, but not clearly groupable with other items

2. **真知 (zhēnzhī) "true knowledge, genuine understanding"** — epistemological term that doesn't fit neatly into any group

3. **真传 (zhēnchuán) "authentic teaching, true transmission"** — specialized usage in traditional learning contexts

4. **真人 (zhēnrén)** — has two distinct meanings: actual person (vs. representation) and Daoist perfected person; neither fits clearly into established groups

5. **真空 (zhēnkōng) "vacuum, true emptiness"** — technical/philosophical term in separate domain

6. **真身 (zhēnshēn) "true body, actual person"** — specialized religious/philosophical usage

7. **真言 (zhēnyán) "true words, mantra"** — religious term in separate domain

8. **真谛 (zhēndì) "true meaning, real truth"** — philosophical/profound truth, similar issue to 真理

9. **纯真 (chúnzhēn) "pure, innocent, naive"** — describes innocence/purity, peripheral to truth-domain

10. **实况 (shíkuàng) "actual situation, live broadcast"** — technical media term

11. **实验 (shíyàn) "experiment, test"** — scientific methodology term

12. **实习 (shíxí) "internship, field work"** — educational term

13. **实力 (shílì) "actual strength, real capability"** — power/capability assessment

14. **平实 (píngshí) "plain, simple, down-to-earth"** — stylistic evaluation

15. **翔实 (xiángshí) "detailed and accurate"** — documentation quality

16. **似 (sì) "resemble, seem"** — comparative/epistemic marker, not clearly groupable

17. **像 (xiàng) "resemble, be like"** — comparative marker, not clearly groupable

18. **假借 (jiǎjiè)** — has both deceptive usage and linguistic technical usage (phonetic borrowing)

19. **假托 (jiǎtuō) "use as pretext, falsely claim"** — specialized usage

20. **假释 (jiǎshì) "parole"** — legal term where "temporary" rather than "false" is salient

21. **假期/假日/假条 (jiàqī/jiàrì/jiàtiáo)** — vacation/leave terms, completely different semantic domain despite same character

22. **不假思索 (bù jiǎ sī suǒ) "without thinking"** — idiomatic, not clearly in deception domain

23. **错综复杂 (cuò zōng fù zá) "intricate and complex"** — 错 here means "interlaced" not "wrong"

24. **阴差阳错 (yīn chā yáng cuò) "by coincidence"** — idiomatic usage of 错

25. **诚惶诚恐 (chéng huáng chéng kǒng) "with trepidation"** — 诚 here intensifies fear, not about sincerity

## NOTES ON GROUPING PATTERNS

1. **Overlapping domains:** Many expressions can function in multiple groups depending on context. This reflects actual usage patterns rather than deficiency in categorization.

2. **Morphological vs. semantic grouping:** Items sharing morphemes (真, 实, 假) don't necessarily belong to the same semantic group, as these morphemes have extended into diverse domains.

3. **Grammaticalization:** Some truth-domain vocabulary has grammaticalized into discourse markers (Group 15), losing direct truth-evaluative function.

4. **Domain extensions:** Several groups (11, 19, 20) represent extensions of truth-domain morphology into physical, material, or experiential domains.

5. **Asymmetries preserved:** Groups are uneven in size and scope, reflecting actual distribution of expressions rather than imposed balance.

6. **Ungrouped items:** Approximately 25 items don't fit cleanly into any group, often due to specialized, technical, or idiomatic usage.
