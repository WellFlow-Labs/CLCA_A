## 2. Substitution Patterns (Narrative)

### 2.1 VERIFICATION substitutes for CAUSATION (factual)

The P4a/P4b data do not attest a single productive causative morpheme that applies across the full P1 truth-domain inventory. Instead, causative-factual meaning is expressed through lexically specific forms: *patunayan* (from *tunay*, "to cause to be verified/proven") and *itama*/*iwasto* (from *tama*/*wastó*, "to cause to be correct/rectified"). These forms are conventionalized and high-frequency in their respective domains (argumentative/legal for *patunayan*; instructional/editorial for *itama*, *iwasto*), but they do not constitute a single productive pattern. The VERIFICATION function (*patunayan*) and the CORRECTION function (*itama*, *iwasto*) together cover the semantic space that a unified CAUSATION (factual) operator would occupy in other languages. No equivalent causative form is reported for *tapat*, *huwad*, or *peke*.

### 2.2 NEGATION and EVIDENTIAL FRAMING substitute for DISCONFIRMATION

No dedicated DISCONFIRMATION construction is attested. The function is distributed across two constructions:

- **Propositional disconfirmation** (asserting that a claim is false) is handled by the NEGATION construction: *Hindi totoo ang alegasyon.* The *hindi* negator applies to the predicative truth-domain adjective and directly denies the truth of the proposition.
- **Appearance-vs-reality disconfirmation** (asserting that something appeared true but is not) is handled by the APPEARANCE/EVIDENTIAL FRAMING construction: *Mukhang totoo ang produkto pero peke pala.* The *mukhang* evidential marker establishes an appearance, and the corrective clause (*pero peke pala*) resolves it against the truth-domain standard. P4b notes that this co-occurrence pattern (*mukhang* + corrective *pero pala*) is specifically attested in this function.

These two constructions are formally distinct and occupy different syntactic positions, but together they cover the functional range of DISCONFIRMATION.

### 2.3 REVELATION is not attested as a distinct construction

No construction in P4a/P4b corresponds to a REVELATION operator (a construction expressing that a truth-domain property is discovered or comes to light, with the subject as experiencer rather than agent). The BECOME construction (*maging*) expresses entry into a state by a volitional subject, not the discovery or revelation of a pre-existing state. The APPEARANCE/EVIDENTIAL FRAMING construction (*mukhang*, *parang*) expresses appearance without commitment to truth, and its corrective variant approaches revelation in meaning, but it is not structurally equivalent to a dedicated REVELATION construction. No substitution is reported; the gap is structural.

### 2.4 BECOME is restricted by adjective type

The BECOME construction (*maging* + truth-domain adjective) is [CORE] for *tapat* and *totoo*, which describe states that a person can enter volitionally. It is [MARGINAL] for *huwad* and *peke*, which P4b characterizes as describing object properties rather than states a person adopts. This restriction is reported as a distributional asymmetry within the BECOME construction, not as a separate construction type.

### 2.5 DISCOURSE-FRAMING constructions are asymmetric across truth-domain polarity

The DISCOURSE-FRAMING constructions (*sa totoo lang*, *ang totoo*, *ang totoo nito*, *sa katunayan*) are attested only for positive truth-domain items (*totoo*, *katunayan*). No equivalent discourse-framing construction is attested for *mali*, *huwad*, or *peke*. P4b reports this asymmetry as observed without explanation. The positive-polarity frames function as candor markers (*sa totoo lang*) or corrective operators (*ang totoo*, *ang totoo nito*); no parallel negative-polarity frame (e.g., a conventionalized sentence-initial marker meaning "to tell you it's false" or "the falsehood of the matter is") is attested.

### 2.6 NEGATION: analytic form is primary; morphological form is register-restricted

The primary NEGATION strategy is analytic: *hindi* precedes the predicative truth-domain adjective. The morphological clitic *di-* (hyphenated before the adjective: *di-tunay*, *di-lehitimo*) is restricted to formal written registers. These are not two competing constructions of equal status; the analytic form is the default across all registers, and the morphological form is a register variant. Both are [CORE] in their respective registers per P4b.

### 2.7 AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION is lexically suppletive, not morphologically productive

The AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION slot is filled by a lexicalized root (*sinungaling*, "liar") that is not transparently derived from any P1 truth-domain adjective, and by a compound construction (*tagapagtanggol ng katotohanan*, "defender of truth") that uses the *taga-* agentive prefix on a verbal base. No productive agentive morpheme deriving "truth-teller," "deceiver," or "verifier" directly from P1 roots is attested. This is reported as a lexical gap within an otherwise morphologically productive system.

## 3. Missingness Summary

### 3.1 Structural Gaps
*(No construction of any kind is attested for this operator)*

- **REVELATION**: No construction expressing the discovery or coming-to-light of a truth-domain property is attested in P4a/P4b. The BECOME construction (*maging*) is not equivalent: it requires a volitional subject entering a state. The EVIDENTIAL FRAMING construction (*mukhang* + corrective clause) approaches this function in specific contexts but is not structurally equivalent to a REVELATION operator.
- **CAUSATION (character)**: No construction expressing the causing of a person to become honest, truthful, or genuine (as a character-directed causative, distinct from the factual/verificational causative) is attested.

### 3.2 Lexical Gaps
*(The construction type exists but specific lexical items are missing)*

- **CAUSATION (factual)**: The causative-verificational function is covered by *patunayan* (from *tunay*) and the correction function by *itama*/*iwasto* (from *tama*/*wastó*), but no equivalent causative forms are attested for *tapat*, *huwad*, or *peke*. The gap is in the lexical inventory, not in the construction type itself.
- **AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION**: No productive agentive morpheme derives agent nouns from P1 truth-domain roots. *Sinungaling* is lexicalized; *tagapagtanggol ng katotohanan* is a compound. The agentive nominalization slot exists in Tagalog morphology (*taga-* prefix) but is not productively filled across the P1 inventory.
- **VERIFICATION**: *Patunayan* is attested for *tunay*-based verification. No equivalent verificational verbal form is reported for *tama*, *tapat*, or other P1 items.

### 3.3 Conceptual Gaps
*(No evidence of the conceptual operation being available in any form)*

- None identified on the basis of P4a/P4b data alone. All gaps identified above are structural or lexical; no operation is blocked at the conceptual level on the available evidence.

### 3.4 Asymmetric Gaps
*(Only one polarity — positive or negative — is attested)*

- **DISCOURSE-FRAMING (positive only)**: Conventionalized sentence-initial truth-framing constructions (*sa totoo lang*, *ang totoo*, *ang totoo nito*, *sa katunayan*) are attested only for positive truth-domain items. No equivalent construction is attested for *mali*, *huwad*, or *peke*. The asymmetry is in the positive direction: positive-polarity discourse frames exist; negative-polarity discourse frames do not.
- **BECOME (partial asymmetry by adjective type)**: *Maging* + truth-domain adjective is [CORE] for person-state adjectives (*tapat*, *totoo*) and [MARGINAL] for object-property adjectives (*huwad*, *peke*). This is an asymmetry within the BECOME construction rather than a full gap.
