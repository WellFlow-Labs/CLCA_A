# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P1b ROOT EXPANSION
# Language: Korean (ko)

---

## STEP 1 — IDENTIFY CENTRAL ROOTS

### Root 1: 진 (jin)
**Occurrences in P1:** 8 items (진실, 진짜, 진정, 진위, 진실성, 진본, and as component in 정직)
**Core meaning contribution:** "true, genuine, real, authentic" — marks correspondence to reality, authenticity, or genuineness

### Root 2: 거짓 (geojit)
**Occurrences in P1:** 3 items (거짓, 가짜, 거짓말)
**Core meaning contribution:** "false, fake, deceptive" — marks deviation from truth, inauthenticity, or deliberate deception

### Root 3: 실 (sil) / 실제 (silje)
**Occurrences in P1:** 5 items (진실, 사실, 실제, 확실하다, 진실성)
**Core meaning contribution:** "real, actual, factual" — marks actuality, concrete existence, or factual status as opposed to theoretical or nominal

## STEP 3 — PERIPHERAL EXTENSIONS

### Root 진 (jin):

**Directional/Movement Extensions:**
Items 10-14 (진보, 진화, 진행, 진출, 진입) use 진 with the meaning "advance/progress" rather than "true/genuine." This appears to be a homophonous root with different semantic origin, though written with the same character. The contribution shifts from truth-evaluation to forward movement or development.

**Military/Positional Extensions:**
Items 16-17 (진지, 진지하다) show 진 in military/positional contexts. While 진지하다 (serious, earnest) may connect to authenticity, 진지 (military camp) appears to be a distinct usage related to position or ground.

**Medical/Physical Extensions:**
Items 8-9, 28-30 (진단, 진술, 진맥, 진료, 진찰) show 진 in medical examination contexts. The connection to "truth" may be through the sense of "determining the true state" but the usage has specialized into technical medical terminology.

**Effort/Intensity Extensions:**
Items 22, 26 (진력, 진땀) use 진 to mark intensity or extremity rather than authenticity per se—"utmost effort," "profuse sweat."

**Calming/Pacification Extensions:**
Items 23-25 (진혼, 진정하다, 진정서) show 진 with meanings related to calming or settling, which appears semantically distinct from the truth-domain core.

### Root 거짓 (geojit):

The 거짓 root shows relatively consistent usage within the falsity/deception domain. Most extensions maintain the core meaning of "false" or "fake" applied to various objects (statements, emotions, relationships, identities). No major semantic shifts observed beyond straightforward compositional combinations.

### Root 실 (sil):

**Practical/Applied Extensions:**
Items 8-23 (실력, 실적, 실효, 실익, 실용, 실천, 실행, 실현, 실감, 실황, 실시간, 실험, 실습, 실기, 실무, 실전) show 실 emphasizing practical application, actual performance, or concrete manifestation. While connected to "reality," the focus shifts from truth-evaluation to practical efficacy and implementation.

**Negative Outcome Extensions:**
Items 24-27 (실패, 실수, 실종, 실업) use 실 in contexts of failure, error, or loss. The connection to "actual/real" is maintained but applied to negative outcomes.

**Spatial Extensions:**
Items 28-29 (실내, 실외) use 실 (室) meaning "room" rather than "real"—this is a homophonous character with distinct meaning.

**Material/Physical Extensions:**
Items 30-32 (실크, 실타래, 실오라기) use 실 (絲) meaning "thread/silk"—another homophonous character with distinct etymology and meaning unrelated to truth or reality.

**Substance/Content Extensions:**
Items 33-35 (실없다, 실없이, 실속) use 실 to mark presence or absence of substance/content, which connects to reality but functions more as evaluation of depth or practical value than truth per se.
