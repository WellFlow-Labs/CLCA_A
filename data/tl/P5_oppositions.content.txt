### OPPOSITION 1
- **Term A:** totoo
- **Term B:** hindi totoo
- **Transliteration A:** totoo
- **Transliteration B:** hindi totoo
- **Translation A:** true, real
- **Translation B:** not true, untrue
- **Description of contrast:** Standard morphological negation using the negative particle *hindi* to negate the truth-value of the base term. This is the unmarked, most emphatic negation pattern.

## SUMMARY

**Total oppositions identified: 50**

### Distribution by contrast type:
- **Lexical:** 20 oppositions
- **Morphological:** 9 oppositions  
- **Constructional:** 17 oppositions
- **Mixed (lexical/constructional):** 4 oppositions

### Key patterns observed:

1. **Morphological negation with *hindi*:** Highly productive pattern creating oppositions across all major truth-domain terms (totoo, tunay, tama, wasto, tapat, akma, makatotohanan).

2. **Existential constructions (*may* vs. *walang*):** Productive pattern with abstract nouns (katotohanan, katunayan, katapatan, lihim, dáyà, kasinungalingan).

3. **Primary lexical antonym pairs:**
   - tama ↔ mali (correctness domain)
   - lantád ↔ lihim (concealment/revelation domain)
   - totoo ↔ multiple opposites (kasinungalingan, kathâ, kathang-isip, gawâ-gawâ, linlang)
   - tunay ↔ multiple opposites (peke, huwad, palabás, kunwari, gawâ-gawâ)

4. **Concealment/revelation domain:** Shows rich systematic oppositions across multiple derivational forms (ilihim ↔ ilantad, paglilihim ↔ paglalantad, inililihim ↔ inilalantad, lihiman ↔ lantaran).

5. **Particle-based constructional oppositions:**
   - Interrogative *ba* ↔ Affirmative *nga*
   - Discourse markers: totoo lang ↔ kunwari lang
   - Aspectual: *na* (already) ↔ *pa* (still)

6. **Asymmetries noted:**
   - Some existential negatives (*walang X*) are explicitly attested while positive counterparts (*may X*) are implied but not documented
   - Truth terms (totoo, tunay) have multiple lexical opposites reflecting different types of non-truth (lies, fiction, fakes, pretense)
   - Not all oppositions are balanced or symmetrical
