| Factual truth assertion and denial | These expressions are used in everyday and formal discourse to affirm or deny that something is true or real as a matter of fact — that it actually happened, actually exists, or actually is the case. | Marking insincerity in social and emotional performance | These expressions are used to evaluate a person's expressed emotions, social displays, or interpersonal conduct as not reflecting genuine inner states — as performed rather than felt. | Evaluating honesty and directness in persons | These expressions are used to evaluate persons, their speech, or their conduct as honest, frank, and without concealment — or as lacking these qualities.
