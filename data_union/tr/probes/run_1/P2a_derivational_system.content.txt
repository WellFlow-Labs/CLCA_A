# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2a Derivational System
# Language: Turkish (tr)

---

## Preliminary Note on Scope

The P1 and P1b outputs have already documented a substantial portion of the derivational system for the three central roots (*doğru-*, *hak-*, *gerçek-*). This P2a output therefore focuses on the remaining P1 lexical items not yet fully treated, while avoiding duplication of forms already documented. Where a P1b form is referenced for completeness, it is marked as such rather than re-documented in full.

### Base: **doğru**

*Core derivations already documented in P1b: doğruluk, doğrulamak, doğrulama, doğrulanmak, doğrulatmak, doğrultmak, doğrultu, doğrudan, doğrusal, doğrulayıcı, doğrulanabilir, doğrulanabilirlik.*

---

**Derived Form 1**

1. **Base Form:** doğru
2. **Derived Form:** doğrucu
3. **Transliteration:** doğrucu
4. **Literal Breakdown:** doğru (correct/true) + -cu (agentive/habitual suffix)
5. **Natural English translation:** truth-teller, one who always tells the truth
6. **Usage:** Used to describe a person habitually committed to telling the truth; can carry a slightly ironic or naive connotation in colloquial speech. *Doğrucu Davut* is a proverbial figure name for someone who tells uncomfortable truths.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2**

1. **Base Form:** doğru
2. **Derived Form:** doğrucuk
3. **Transliteration:** doğrucuk
4. **Literal Breakdown:** doğru + -cuk (diminutive suffix)
5. **Natural English translation:** a small truth, a little bit true
6. **Usage:** A diminutive form used in colloquial and affective speech, often in addressing children or in softened assertions. *Doğrucuk söylüyorum* (I'm telling a little truth / I'm being honest, said softly). Not common in formal registers.
7. **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**Derived Form 3**

1. **Base Form:** doğru
2. **Derived Form:** doğrulaştırmak
3. **Transliteration:** doğrulaştırmak
4. **Literal Breakdown:** doğru + -laş- (inchoative verbalizer) + -tır- (causative) + -mak (infinitive)
5. **Natural English translation:** to make more accurate, to render correct
6. **Usage:** Used in technical and editorial contexts to describe the process of improving the accuracy or correctness of something. Less common than *doğrulamak* and tends to appear in more specialized registers.
7. **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

### Base: **yanlış**

---

**Derived Form 1**

1. **Base Form:** yanlış
2. **Derived Form:** yanlışlık
3. **Transliteration:** yanlışlık
4. **Literal Breakdown:** yanlış (wrong/incorrect) + -lık (nominal suffix)
5. **Natural English translation:** error, mistake, incorrectness, wrongness
6. **Usage:** Names the quality or instance of being wrong or incorrect. *Yanlışlık yapmak* (to make a mistake) is a common collocation in everyday and formal speech.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2**

1. **Base Form:** yanlış
2. **Derived Form:** yanlışlamak
3. **Transliteration:** yanlışlamak
4. **Literal Breakdown:** yanlış (wrong) + -la- (verbalizer) + -mak (infinitive)
5. **Natural English translation:** to falsify, to show to be wrong, to refute
6. **Usage:** Used in philosophical, scientific, and argumentative contexts to describe demonstrating that a claim is incorrect. Associated with Popperian falsificationism in Turkish academic discourse: *bir hipotezi yanlışlamak* (to falsify a hypothesis).
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 3**

1. **Base Form:** yanlış
2. **Derived Form:** yanlışlama
3. **Transliteration:** yanlışlama
4. **Literal Breakdown:** yanlış + -la- + -ma (verbal noun)
5. **Natural English translation:** falsification, refutation
6. **Usage:** The nominalized process of falsifying or refuting. Used in philosophy of science: *yanlışlama ilkesi* (the principle of falsification). Directly parallels *doğrulama* in structure and register.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 4**

1. **Base Form:** yanlış
2. **Derived Form:** yanlışlanabilir
3. **Transliteration:** yanlışlanabilir
4. **Literal Breakdown:** yanlış + -la- + -n- (passive) + -abilir (potential suffix)
5. **Natural English translation:** falsifiable
6. **Usage:** Used in philosophy of science to describe claims that are in principle capable of being shown to be false. *Yanlışlanabilir hipotez* (falsifiable hypothesis). Directly parallels *doğrulanabilir* in structure and domain.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 5**

1. **Base Form:** yanlış
2. **Derived Form:** yanlışlanabilirlik
3. **Transliteration:** yanlışlanabilirlik
4. **Literal Breakdown:** yanlış + -la- + -n- + -abilir + -lik (nominal suffix)
5. **Natural English translation:** falsifiability
6. **Usage:** The abstract property of being falsifiable. Used in philosophy of science: *yanlışlanabilirlik ölçütü* (the criterion of falsifiability, associated with Popper). Directly parallels *doğrulanabilirlik*.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 6**

1. **Base Form:** yanlış
2. **Derived Form:** yanlışlanmak
3. **Transliteration:** yanlışlanmak
4. **Literal Breakdown:** yanlış + -la- + -n- (passive) + -mak
5. **Natural English translation:** to be falsified, to be shown to be wrong
6. **Usage:** Passive form; used when a claim or hypothesis is demonstrated to be incorrect by evidence or argument. *Hipotez yanlışlandı* (the hypothesis was falsified).
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 7**

1. **Base Form:** yanlış
2. **Derived Form:** yanlışlıkla
3. **Transliteration:** yanlışlıkla
4. **Literal Breakdown:** yanlış + -lık + -la (instrumental/adverbial suffix)
5. **Natural English translation:** by mistake, accidentally, erroneously
6. **Usage:** An extremely common adverbial expression used to indicate that an action was performed in error rather than intentionally. *Yanlışlıkla gönderdi* (he sent it by mistake). Used across all registers.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 8**

1. **Base Form:** yanlış
2. **Derived Form:** yanlış anlamak
3. **Transliteration:** yanlış anlamak
4. **Literal Breakdown:** yanlış (wrong) + anlamak (to understand)
5. **Natural English translation:** to misunderstand
6. **Usage:** A standard collocation for misunderstanding. *Beni yanlış anladın* (you misunderstood me). Extremely common in everyday speech and writing.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 9**

1. **Base Form:** yanlış
2. **Derived Form:** yanlış anlaşılmak
3. **Transliteration:** yanlış anlaşılmak
4. **Literal Breakdown:** yanlış + anlaşılmak (to be understood, passive)
5. **Natural English translation:** to be misunderstood
6. **Usage:** Passive form of *yanlış anlamak*; used when a person or statement is the object of misunderstanding. *Yanlış anlaşılmak istemiyorum* (I don't want to be misunderstood).
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 10**

1. **Base Form:** yanlış
2. **Derived Form:** yanlış bilgi
3. **Transliteration:** yanlış bilgi
4. **Literal Breakdown:** yanlış (wrong/incorrect) + bilgi (information/knowledge)
5. **Natural English translation:** misinformation, incorrect information
6. **Usage:** Used in journalistic, educational, and administrative contexts to describe information that is factually incorrect. *Yanlış bilgi yaymak* (to spread misinformation). Distinct from *dezenformasyon* (disinformation) in not necessarily implying intent.

**Derived Form 11**

**Derived Form 3**

1. **Base Form:** sahte
