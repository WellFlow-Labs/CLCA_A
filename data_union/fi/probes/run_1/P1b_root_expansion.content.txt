# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P1b ROOT EXPANSION
# Language: Finnish (fi)

---

## Step 1 — Identify Central Roots

---

**Root 1: tosi / tode- / tod-**

- **Root form:** tosi / tode- / tod-
- **P1 items participating:** tosi (1), totuus (2), todellinen (6), todellisuus (7), todistaa (21), todiste (22) — **6 items**
- **Core meaning contribution:** Propositional truth; correspondence between a claim or representation and reality; the factual or actual state of affairs. This root supplies the primary epistemic vocabulary for truth as a property of statements and states of the world.

---

**Root 2: oikea / oikeus / oikeel-**

- **Root form:** oikea / oikeu- / oikeel-
- **P1 items participating:** oikea (4), oikeellisuus (5), oikeudenmukainen (26) — **3 items**
- **Core meaning contribution:** Normative and identificatory rightness; correctness relative to a standard, norm, or proper identity; justice and entitlement. This root covers both evaluative correctness and the domain of rights and legal order.

---

**Root 3: väärä / vääre- / väären-**

- **Root form:** väärä / vääre- / väären-
- **P1 items participating:** väärä (13), väärentää (15), väärennös (16) — **3 items**
- **Core meaning contribution:** Falsity, wrongness, and deliberate falsification; the antonym cluster covering incorrect, false, crooked, and fraudulent. This root anchors the negative pole of the truth-evaluation domain.

---

*Note: The roots tosi/tode- and oikea/oikeu- are both highly productive. The root väärä/vääre- is moderately productive. All three are expanded below. The tosi/tode- root is the most productive of the three and yields more than 30 attested forms; the 25 most common are listed with a note that additional forms exist.*

## Step 2 — Expand Each Root

---

### ROOT 1: tosi / tode- / tod-

---

**1.**
- **Form:** tosi
- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Morpheme breakdown:** tosi (root: true, real)
- **English translation:** true; real; really
- **Context of use:** Used predicatively (*se on tosi* — "it is true") and as an adverbial intensifier in colloquial speech (*tosi hyvä* — "really good"). The base form from which the entire cluster derives.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**2.**
- **Form:** totuus
- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Morpheme breakdown:** tosi/totu- + -uus (nominalizing suffix)
- **English translation:** truth
- **Context of use:** The standard nominal for truth as an abstract property or a specific true state of affairs. Used in philosophical, legal, and everyday discourse.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**3.**
- **Form:** totuudenmukaisuus
- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Morpheme breakdown:** totuuden (genitive of totuus) + mukainen (in accordance with) + -uus
- **English translation:** truthfulness; accuracy; fidelity to truth
- **Context of use:** Used in journalistic, legal, and academic contexts to evaluate whether a representation, account, or statement faithfully corresponds to the facts (*uutisoinnin totuudenmukaisuus* — "the truthfulness of the reporting").
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**4.**
- **Form:** totuudenmukainen
- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Morpheme breakdown:** totuuden (genitive of totuus) + mukainen (in accordance with)
- **English translation:** truthful; accurate; faithful to the truth
- **Context of use:** Applied to accounts, descriptions, and representations to assert that they correspond to the facts (*totuudenmukainen kuvaus* — "a truthful/accurate description"). Used in legal testimony evaluation and journalistic standards.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**5.**
- **Form:** totuudenvastaisuus
- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Morpheme breakdown:** totuuden (genitive of totuus) + vastainen (contrary to) + -uus
- **English translation:** untruthfulness; falsity; contrariness to truth
- **Context of use:** Used in legal and formal contexts to characterize statements or representations that contradict the truth (*todistajan totuudenvastaisuus* — "the witness's untruthfulness"). More formal than *valheellisuus*.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**6.**
- **Form:** totuudenvastainen
- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Morpheme breakdown:** totuuden (genitive of totuus) + vastainen (contrary to)
- **English translation:** contrary to truth; untruthful; false
- **Context of use:** Applied to statements, claims, and representations in legal and formal discourse to assert that they contradict the truth (*totuudenvastainen väite* — "a claim contrary to truth").
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**7.**
- **Form:** totuusjärjestelmä
- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Morpheme breakdown:** totuus + järjestelmä (system)
- **English translation:** truth system; regime of truth
- **Context of use:** Used in philosophical and social-theoretical discourse (particularly in discussions of Foucault and epistemology) to refer to the set of norms and institutions that determine what counts as true in a given context.
- **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**8.**
- **Form:** totuusarvo
- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Morpheme breakdown:** totuus + arvo (value)
- **English translation:** truth value
- **Context of use:** Technical term in logic and formal semantics referring to the truth or falsity of a proposition (*lauseen totuusarvo* — "the truth value of a sentence"). Used in logic, mathematics, and linguistics.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**9.**
- **Form:** totuuskomissio
- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Morpheme breakdown:** totuus + komissio (commission)
- **English translation:** truth commission
- **Context of use:** Used in political and legal discourse to refer to bodies established to investigate historical injustices or atrocities (*Etelä-Afrikan totuuskomissio* — "the South African Truth Commission").
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**10.**
- **Form:** totuuspuhe
- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Morpheme breakdown:** totuus + puhe (speech, talk)
- **English translation:** truth-telling; discourse of truth; parrhesia
- **Context of use:** Used in philosophical discourse, particularly in discussions of parrhesia and frank speech, to refer to the practice of speaking truth. Also used more broadly for honest public discourse.
- **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**11.**
- **Form:** totuusserum
- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Morpheme breakdown:** totuus + serum (serum, loanword)
- **English translation:** truth serum
- **Context of use:** Used in legal, journalistic, and popular discourse to refer to substances alleged to compel truthful disclosure (*totuusseerumin käyttö kuulusteluissa* — "the use of truth serum in interrogations").
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**12.**
- **Form:** todellinen
- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Morpheme breakdown:** tode- (stem of tosi) + -llinen (adjectival suffix)
- **English translation:** real; actual; genuine; true
- **Context of use:** Asserts that something exists or is the case in reality as opposed to being apparent or imagined. Used across everyday, philosophical, and scientific discourse.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**13.**
- **Form:** todellisuus
- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Morpheme breakdown:** todellinen + -uus
- **English translation:** reality; actuality
- **Context of use:** The standard term for reality as a domain. Used in philosophical, psychological, and everyday contexts (*todellisuudessa* — "in reality").
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**14.**
- **Form:** todellisuudentaju
- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Morpheme breakdown:** todellisuuden (genitive of todellisuus) + taju (sense, grasp)
- **English translation:** sense of reality; grasp of reality
- **Context of use:** Used in psychological and everyday discourse to evaluate whether a person has an adequate perception of what is real (*häneltä puuttuu todellisuudentaju* — "he lacks a sense of reality").
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**15.**
- **Form:** todellisuuspako
- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Morpheme breakdown:** todellisuus + pako (escape, flight)
- **English translation:** escape from reality; escapism
- **Context of use:** Used in psychological and cultural criticism to describe the tendency to avoid confronting reality through fantasy, entertainment, or denial (*elokuvat voivat olla todellisuuspakoa* — "films can be an escape from reality").
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**16.**
- **Form:** todistaa
- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Morpheme breakdown:** tode- + -staa (causative/factitive suffix)
- **English translation:** to prove; to testify; to witness; to attest
- **Context of use:** Used in legal contexts for giving testimony and in logical/epistemic contexts for proving a claim. Central to both legal and epistemic evaluation.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**17.**
- **Form:** todistus
- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Morpheme breakdown:** todista- + -us (result nominalization)
- **English translation:** proof; certificate; testimony; attestation
- **Context of use:** Used in legal contexts for testimony and in administrative contexts for certificates and credentials (*koulutodistus* — "school certificate"; *todistus syyllisyydestä* — "proof of guilt").
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**18.**
- **Form:** todistaja
- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Morpheme breakdown:** todista- + -ja (agent suffix)
- **English translation:** witness
- **Context of use:** Used in legal discourse for a person who gives testimony (*silminnäkijätodistaja* — "eyewitness"; *todistaja oikeudessa* — "witness in court"). Also used in religious contexts (*Jehovan todistaja* — "Jehovah's Witness").
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**19.**
- **Form:** todiste
- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Morpheme breakdown:** todista- + -e (result nominalization)
- **English translation:** piece of evidence; proof
- **Context of use:** Refers to a specific item of evidence used to establish the truth or falsity of a claim, particularly in legal contexts.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**20.**
- **Form:** todistusaineisto
- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Morpheme breakdown:** todistus + aineisto (material, body of material)
- **English translation:** body of evidence; evidentiary material
- **Context of use:** Used in legal and investigative discourse to refer to the totality of evidence assembled in a case (*todistusaineiston riittävyys* — "the sufficiency of the body of evidence").
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**21.**
- **Form:** todistustaakka
- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Morpheme breakdown:** todistus + taakka (burden)
- **English translation:** burden of proof
- **Context of use:** Legal term referring to the obligation to prove a claim (*todistustaakka on syyttäjällä* — "the burden of proof lies with the prosecutor"). Standard term in Finnish legal discourse.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**22.**
- **Form:** todistusvoima
- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Morpheme breakdown:** todistus + voima (force, power)
- **English translation:** probative force; evidentiary weight
- **Context of use:** Legal and logical term for the degree to which a piece of evidence or an argument establishes a claim (*todisteen todistusvoima* — "the probative force of the evidence").
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**23.**
- **Form:** todistetusti
- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Morpheme breakdown:** todistettu (past passive participle of todistaa) + -sti (adverbial suffix)
- **English translation:** demonstrably; provably; as has been proven
- **Context of use:** Used as a sentence adverb in legal, academic, and journalistic discourse to assert that something has been established by evidence (*todistetusti väärä* — "demonstrably false").
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**24.**
- **Form:** todentaa
- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Morpheme breakdown:** tode- + -ntaa (causative suffix)
- **English translation:** to verify; to authenticate; to confirm; to validate
- **Context of use:** Used in administrative, technical, and legal contexts to refer to the act of confirming that something is real, correct, or authentic (*todentaa henkilöllisyys* — "to verify identity"; *todentaa tiedot* — "to verify the data"). Also used in computing for authentication.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**25.**
- **Form:** todennus
- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Morpheme breakdown:** todenna- + -us (result nominalization)
- **English translation:** verification; authentication; validation
- **Context of use:** The nominal form of *todentaa*. Used in administrative, technical, and computing contexts (*kaksivaiheinen todennus* — "two-factor authentication"; *tietojen todennus* — "data verification").
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Step 3 — Peripheral Extensions

The following expressions involve the identified roots in contexts where the meaning contribution appears to shift away from the core truth-evaluation domain. These are flagged without interpretation or categorization.

---

**From tosi / tode- / tod-:**

- **tosi** as an adverbial intensifier in colloquial speech (*tosi hyvä* — "really good"; *tosi paljon* — "really a lot") — the root appears in a degree-marking function.

- **toteuttaa** (to implement, to carry out, to realize) and **toteutua** (to be realized, to come true, to materialize) — the root appears in contexts of practical execution and project management, not obviously involving truth or falsity.

- **toteuma** (realization, outcome, actual result as opposed to planned result) — used in budgeting and project management to contrast actual outcomes with plans.

- **todellisuuspako** (escapism, escape from reality) — the root appears in a psychological and cultural-critical context involving desire and avoidance.

- **todennäköinen / todennäköisyys** (probable / probability) — the root appears in a probabilistic rather than binary truth context, and in mathematical and statistical usage.

- **totuusserum** (truth serum) — the root appears in a context involving pharmacological compulsion rather than epistemic evaluation.

- **totuuskomissio** (truth commission) — the root appears in a political and transitional-justice context.

---

**From oikea / oikeu-:**

- **oikea** in directional use (*käänny oikealle* — "turn right") — the root appears as a spatial orientation term with no evaluative or truth-related content.

- **oikeaoppinen** (orthodox) — the root appears in contexts of doctrinal conformity, which may involve ideological or religious rather than factual correctness, and can carry ironic connotations.

- **oikeakielisyys** (linguistic correctness) — the root appears in prescriptive language evaluation, where "correctness" is defined by social norms rather than correspondence to fact.

- **oikeuttaa** (to justify, to entitle, to authorize) — the root appears in contexts of moral and legal authorization that extend beyond simple correctness.

- **oikeusvoima** (legal force, res judicata) — the root appears in a context where a judgment has binding force regardless of its factual accuracy.

- **oikeushenkilö** (legal person) — the root appears in a technical legal context where "person" is a constructed category rather than a factual one.

---

**From väärä / vääre-:**

- **väärä** in spatial/physical use (*väärässä asennossa* — "in a crooked/wrong position") — the root appears in a physical orientation or posture context.

- **vääristymä** (distortion, cognitive bias) — the root appears in psychological and statistical contexts involving systematic deviation rather than deliberate falsification.

- **vääristää kilpailua** (to distort competition) — the root appears in economic and regulatory discourse involving market distortion.

- **vääräuskoinen** (heterodox, of false belief) — the root appears in a religious context where "falsity" is defined by doctrinal rather than factual standards.

- **väärämielinen** (malicious, ill-intentioned) — the root appears in a context of moral character and intent rather than factual incorrectness.
