## 2. Substitution Patterns (Narrative)

Basque, as represented in P4a/P4b, shows a strong preference for direct copular attribution and analytic negation in the truth domain.

For **ATTR**, the language uses a straightforward copular pattern: **X [egia / gezurra / zuzena / okerra] da**. This is fully attested and central.

For **NEGATION**, the language uses analytic negation with **ez** before the copula: **X ez da [egia / zuzena / benetakoa]**. P4a explicitly notes that Basque often prefers simple copular negation rather than a special antonym construction. So negation is descriptively **analytic**, not morphological.

For **ADNOM**, the language has a clear prenominal modifier construction: **[egiazko / benetako / gezurrezko / faltsu / fidagarri / sinesgarri] + N**. These multi-word and derived forms behave syntactically as adjectives modifying nouns.

For **BECOME**, P4a/P4b explicitly state that no common expression was securely identified. Therefore there is no attested dedicated inchoative construction here. Since no revelation construction is provided either, **REVELATION does not substitute for BECOME in the supplied material**. Both are absent from the present evidence.

For **CAUSATION (factual)**, the dataset does not show a productive causative built from the attribution pattern. Instead, it uses lexical verbs such as **egiaztatu** ‘verify’ and **zuzendu** ‘correct’. Descriptively, this means factual causation is not represented by a dedicated truth-causative construction, but by lexical operations of verification and correction. In that sense, **VERIFICATION partly substitutes for CAUSATION (factual)** in the available material.

For **VERIFICATION**, **egiaztatu** is directly attested and behaves as a lexical verb. It is not a light-verb construction.

For **DISCONFIRMATION**, no dedicated construction is listed. The available way to reject truth is through **NEGATION**: **Hori ez da egia.** / **Albiste hori ez da benetakoa.** Therefore **DISCONFIRMATION is substituted by NEGATION** in the supplied material. VERIFICATION and DISCONFIRMATION do **not** share the same construction in the dataset: verification uses lexical **egiaztatu**, while disconfirmation is expressed by analytic negation.

For **NOMINALIZATION**, abstract nominalization is well supported by derivational morphology: **-tasun**, **-pen**, **-tze**. This is a direct morphological resource. Agentive nominalization is asymmetric: **gezurti** is a lexical noun for ‘liar’, but no equally secure noun for ‘truth-teller’ is established. Instead, Basque uses the light-verb expression **egia esan** ‘tell the truth’. So there is a **lexical gap** in the parallel agent noun, but not a total conceptual gap in expressing truth-telling.

The truth/lie expressions **egia esan** and **gezurra esan** are normalized as **light-verb constructions**: the semantic core is carried by the noun (**egia**, **gezurra**), while **esan** functions as the verbal carrier. In **Egia esan, nekatuta nago.**, the expression functions pragmatically as a discourse-marking adverbial expression rather than a simple literal predicate.

## 3. Missingness Summary

### Structural Gaps
- **BECOME**: **NO COMMON EXPRESSION - circumlocution required**
- **REVELATION**: **MISSING (structural gap)**
- **CAUSATION (character)**: **MISSING (structural gap)**
- **DISCONFIRMATION** as a dedicated operator: **SUBSTITUTED BY NEGATION**

### Lexical Gaps
- No securely established lexicalized noun for **“truth-teller”** in parallel to **gezurti**.
- P4a/P4b support **gezurti** but only the light-verb expression **egia esan** for truth-telling.

### Conceptual Gaps
- No conceptual gap is directly established for truth, falsity, verification, negation, or nominalization, since these meanings are expressible.
- No dedicated conceptual operation for **BECOME** is established in the supplied material.
- No dedicated conceptual operation for **REVELATION** is established in the supplied material.

### Asymmetric Gaps
- **Positive/negative asymmetry**: negative truth assessment is clearly available via **ez da**, but no corresponding dedicated **BECOME** construction is attested for either positive or negative change.
- **Agentive asymmetry**: **gezurti** exists for ‘liar’, but no equally secure lexical noun for ‘truth-teller’ is attested.
- **Verification/disconfirmation asymmetry**: verification has lexical **egiaztatu**, while disconfirmation lacks a dedicated operator and is expressed through negation.
