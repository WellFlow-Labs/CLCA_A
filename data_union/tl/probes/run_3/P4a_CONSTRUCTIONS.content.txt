# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P4a CONSTRUCTIONS
# Language: Tagalog (tl)

---

## 1. ATTRIBUTION

### Pattern
Predicate-initial clause: **[truth-adjective] + ang + [noun phrase]**
(Tagalog is predicate-initial; the truth-evaluating adjective or nominal precedes the topic-marked noun phrase)

### Examples
- *Totoo ang balita.* → Totoo ang balita. → "The news is true."
- *Tama ang sagot niya.* → Tama ang sagot niya. → "His/her answer is correct."

### Notes
The standard attributive construction places the predicate (truth adjective) before the topic-marked noun phrase introduced by *ang*. No copula is used. The same pattern holds for *mali*, *tunay*, *tapat*, *huwad*, *kredible*, etc.

---

## 2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION

### Pattern
**Hindi + [truth-adjective] + ang + [noun phrase]**
(*Hindi* is the general negation particle for non-verbal predicates; *wala* is used for existential negation of nominal truth-terms)

### Examples
- *Hindi totoo ang kanyang sinabi.* → Hindi totoo ang kanyang sinabi. → "What he/she said is not true."
- *Walang katotohanan ang akusasyon.* → Walang katotohanan ang akusasyon. → "The accusation has no truth to it."

### Notes
*Hindi* negates adjectival predicates (*hindi totoo*, *hindi tama*, *hindi tunay*, *hindi tapat*). *Wala* + *-ng* linker negates existential constructions with abstract nominals (*walang katotohanan*, *walang katunayan*, *walang katapatan*); these two negation patterns are structurally distinct and not interchangeable.

---

## 3. ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION

### Pattern
**[truth-adjective] + na/ng (linker) + [noun]**
(The linker *na* follows consonant-final forms; *-ng* is suffixed to vowel-final forms; the modifier precedes the head noun)

### Examples
- *Tunay na ginto* → Tunay na ginto → "Genuine gold"
- *Mapagkakatiwalaang pinagkukunan ng impormasyon* → Mapagkakatiwalaang pinagkukunan ng impormasyon → "Reliable source of information"

### Notes
The linker (*na*/*-ng*) is obligatory between modifier and head noun in Tagalog; it does not mark grammatical case. This pattern is productive across all truth-domain adjectives (*tapat na tao*, *huwad na dokumento*, *wastong paraan*, *kredible na saksi*). Modifier-head order is the standard order for adnominal modification.

---

## 4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE

### Pattern
**Maging + [truth-adjective]**
(*Maging* is the infinitive/imperative form of the copular-inchoative verb "to become/to be"; it introduces a change-of-state or injunctive reading)

### Examples
- *Maging tapat sa iyong sarili.* → Maging tapat sa iyong sarili. → "Be honest with yourself."
- *Naging totoo ang kanyang pangarap.* → Naging totoo ang kanyang pangarap. → "His/her dream became real / came true."

### Notes
*Maging* + truth-adjective is the standard inchoative/injunctive construction. *Naging* is the completed-aspect form ("became"). The construction is attested for *tapat* (moral injunction) and *totoo* (realization of a state); it is less conventionalized for *tama*, *tunay*, or borrowed adjectives in truth-evaluation contexts. The form *nagkatotoo* ("came true," from *nagka-* + *totoo*) is an alternative inchoative specifically for the realization of predictions or wishes.

---

## 5. CAUSATIVE

### Pattern
**Pa- + [root] + -an verbal paradigm** (for *tunay*); **Iwasto** (*i-* + *wasto*) for correctness

### Examples
- *Patunayan mo ang iyong sinabi.* → Patunayan mo ang iyong sinabi. → "Prove what you said." (cause it to be verified)
- *Iniwasto na ang pagkakamali.* → Iniwasto na ang pagkakamali. → "The mistake has already been corrected."

### Notes
A productive causative paradigm is attested only for *tunay* via *pa-* + *tunay* + *-an* (*patunayan*, *pinatunayan*, *pagpapatunay*), meaning "to cause something to be verified/proven." For *wasto*, the prefix *i-* yields *iwasto* ("to correct/rectify"). No productive causative verbal paradigm is attested for *totoo* or *tapat* in the truth-evaluation domain; this is a notable asymmetry in the data.

---

## 6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION

### Pattern
***Ka-* + [root] + *-an* circumfix** (native roots); ***-idad* suffix** (Spanish-borrowed adjectives)

### Examples
- *Ang katotohanan ay mapait.* → Ang katotohanan ay mapait. → "The truth is bitter."
- *Ang kredibilidad ng saksi ay pinag-aralan ng hukuman.* → Ang kredibilidad ng saksi ay pinag-aralan ng hukuman. → "The credibility of the witness was examined by the court."

### Notes
The circumfix *ka-…-an* is the primary nominalizing strategy for native truth-domain roots: *katotohanan* (truth), *katunayan* (proof/fact), *katapatan* (honesty/fidelity), *kawastuhan* (correctness), *kadalisayan* (purity). Spanish-borrowed adjectives take *-idad*: *kredibilidad*, *lehitimidad*. The *pag-* + reduplication verbal nominalization pattern yields process nominals: *pagtatapat* (act of confessing), *pagpapatunay* (act of verifying), *paghahanap ng katotohanan* (truth-seeking).

---

## 7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION

### Pattern
**[root] alone** (for *sinungaling*); ***mang-/man-* + [root]** (for agentive deception terms); ***tagapag-* + [verbal base]** (for defender/advocate roles)

### Examples
- *Sinungaling ka.* → Sinungaling ka. → "You are a liar."
- *Tagapagtanggol ng katotohanan* → Tagapagtanggol ng katotohanan → "Defender of truth"

### Notes
*Sinungaling* (liar) functions directly as an agentive nominal/predicate without additional affixation. *Manlinlang* (*man-* + *linlang*) is the agentive form for "one who deceives habitually." No common agentive nominal for "truth-teller" or "honest person" is formed by a dedicated agentive affix; instead, the adnominal construction *tapat na tao* ("an honest person") serves this function. The *tagapag-* prefix yields institutional/role agentives (*tagapagtanggol* — defender/advocate).

---

## 8. DISCOURSE-MARKER CONSTRUCTIONS

### Pattern
**Ang + [truth-nominal]** or **Sa + [truth-nominal]** functioning as sentence-initial pragmatic frames

### Examples
- *Ang totoo, hindi ko alam.* → Ang totoo, hindi ko alam. → "The truth is, I don't know."
- *Sa katotohanan, mas kumplikado ang sitwasyon.* → Sa katotohanan, mas kumplikado ang sitwasyon. → "In truth, the situation is more complicated."

### Notes
Both *ang totoo* and *sa katotohanan* (and the near-synonymous *sa katunayan* — "in fact") function as conventionalized sentence-initial discourse markers introducing corrective or clarifying statements. This construction type is specific to truth-domain nominals and has no parallel in the correctness (*tama*) or authenticity (*tunay*) clusters at the same level of conventionalization.

---

## 9. PARTICLE-MARKED EPISTEMIC STANCE

### Pattern
**Totoo + [discourse particle]** (*ba*, *nga*, *pala*, *naman*, *pa rin*)

### Examples
- *Totoo nga pala.* → Totoo nga pala. → "So it really is true after all." (confirmed realization)
- *Totoo naman ang sinabi ko.* → Totoo naman ang sinabi ko. → "What I said is indeed true." (responsive affirmation against doubt)

### Notes
This construction type is specific to *totoo* among the truth-domain items; no parallel conventionalized particle-cluster constructions are attested for *tunay*, *tapat*, or *tama* at the same density. The particles encode distinct epistemic stances: *ba* (interrogative), *nga* (confirmation), *pala* (realization/discovery), *naman* (contrast/responsive), *pa rin* (persistence). These are not freely compositional in all combinations; *totoo nga pala* as a unit marks a specific epistemic update not reducible to the sum of its particles.
