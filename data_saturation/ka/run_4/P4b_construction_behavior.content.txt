# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P4b Construction Usage & Constraints
# Language: Georgian (ka)

---

## 1. ATTRIBUTION

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| NP + ADJ-i (copula omitted, enclitic -ia) | ეს ამბავი მართალია | es ambavi martalia | "This story is true / correct" | [CORE] | Extremely common in both spoken and written Georgian; the fused enclitic -ia is the default form in informal and standard written registers. No observable restriction on which truth-domain adjective appears in this slot. |
| NP + ADJ-i (copula omitted, enclitic -ia) | ეს დოკუმენტი ნამდვილია | es dokumenti namdvilia | "This document is genuine" | [CORE] | Same pattern as above; namdvili and martali are interchangeable in this frame. č̣ešmariti is attested but carries a more formal or elevated register. |

**Attested alternates:** Full copula form NP + ADJ-i + aris is also attested (more formal or emphatic); enclitic -ia is the reduced/fused variant of aris.

## 2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| NP + ara-ADJ-i (lexicalized prefix negation) | ეს ინფორმაცია არასანდოა | es inpormacia arasandoa | "This information is unreliable" | [CORE] | Common for adjectives denoting stable properties; ara- forms carry a slightly more categorical or permanent sense than syntactic negation. Productive but not universal across all truth-domain adjectives. |
| NP + ADJ-i + ar aris (syntactic negation) | ეს ამბავი მართალი არ არის | es ambavi martali ar aris | "This story is not true / not correct" | [CORE] | The general negation strategy; applies to all adjectives without restriction. Interchangeable with ara- forms in most contexts, but lacks the categorical nuance of lexicalized negation. |

**Attested alternates:** Both strategies are described as largely interchangeable in everyday speech. No additional alternates attested beyond these two in P4a.

## 3. ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ADJ-i + NOUN (prenominal, adjective invariant) | ყალბი დოკუმენტი | q̇albi dokumenti | "a forged document" | [CORE] | The dominant pattern for adnominal modification in Georgian; the adjective is invariant in attributive position and the noun alone carries case inflection. No exceptions attested among truth-domain adjectives. |
| ADJ-i + NOUN (prenominal, adjective invariant) | ნამდვილი მეგობარი | namdvili megobari | "a genuine / true friend" | [CORE] | Same pattern; namdvili in this slot carries both the "genuine" and "true (friend)" senses. All truth-domain adjectives listed in P4a follow this pattern without exception. |

**Attested alternates:** None attested in P4a. The pattern is described as applying without exception across all truth-domain adjectives.

## 4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| NP + ADJ-i + gamodga (resultative/discovery) | ეს ამბავი ყალბი გამოდგა | es ambavi q̇albi gamodga | "This story turned out to be false" | [CORE] | Standard construction for expressing the discovery that something has a property; extremely common in the truth domain. Expresses resultative discovery rather than a transition into a state. |
| NP + ADJ-i + q̇opila (evidential/retrospective) | ეს ყალბი ყოფილა | es q̇albi q̇opila | "This was fake all along (evidential, often with surprise)" | [CORE] | Also extremely common; carries an additional evidential nuance of surprise or belated recognition not present in gamodga. Treated separately and in more detail under Construction 8. |

**Attested alternates:** A true inchoative ("become true/false") is explicitly noted as not a natural category in Georgian truth-domain expression. No additional alternates attested.

## 5. CAUSATIVE

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| შეცდომაში შეყვანა (periphrastic causative of falsity) | შეცდომაში შეიყვანა | šecodomashi šeiq̇vana | "He/she misled (someone) / led (someone) into error" | [CORE] | Standard periphrastic expression for causing someone to hold a false belief; common in everyday speech. Not a morphological causative; no productive causative suffix applies across truth-domain adjectives. |
| გაყალბება (verbal noun of falsification) | დოკუმენტების გაყალბება | dokumentebis gaq̇albeba | "falsification of documents / making documents false" | [CORE] | Common in legal, administrative, and everyday contexts involving documents or records. Root-specific to q̇alb-; does not generalize across other truth-domain adjectives. |

**Attested alternates:** No morphological causative applying generally across truth-domain adjectives is attested. The two periphrastic forms above are the only attested strategies in P4a.

## 6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| si-ROOT-e frame | სიმართლე | simartle | "truth, correctness" | [CORE] | One of the two most productive nominalization patterns in the truth domain; tends to produce nouns with a more concrete or situational sense. Attested forms include simartle, siq̇albe, sicrue, sizuste. |
| ADJ + -oba suffix | ნამდვილობა | namdviloba | "genuineness, authenticity" | [CORE] | The other most productive pattern; tends to denote stable abstract properties. Attested forms include namdviloba, sandooba, gulc̣rpeloba, avṭenturoba, martebuloba. |
| ROOT + -eba suffix | č̣ešmariteba, sarc̣munoeba | — | "truth (elevated); reliability" | [MARGINAL] | Attested but produces nouns of more elevated register; less common in everyday speech than si-...-e or -oba forms. Restricted to specific roots and does not appear to be freely productive across the full truth-domain adjective set. |

**Attested alternates:** Three patterns are attested (si-...-e, -oba, -eba); all three are described as fully productive, though -eba forms are register-restricted.

## 7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lexicalized compound | თვალთმაქცი | tvaltmaq̇ci | "deceiver, fraud, impostor" | [CORE] | A fully lexicalized compound (lit. "eye-tricker"); common in everyday speech. Not morphologically transparent as a productive agentive formation. |
| cru + ROLE NOUN (compound) | ცრუ მოწმე | cru moq̇me | "false witness, perjurer" | [CORE] | Common in legal and religious contexts; the pattern cru + role noun is productive for forming compounds denoting false occupants of a role. |
| Lexicalized compound | pirmoṭne | — | "hypocrite" | [CORE] | Fully lexicalized; common in everyday speech. Listed in P4a but no Georgian script example provided for this item specifically in Construction 7. |
| Periphrastic description | sicrueze mosaubre | — | "one who speaks falsehood" | [MARGINAL] | Periphrastic; used when no single lexicalized agentive noun is available. No common single-word equivalent for "liar" or "truth-teller" is attested as a lexicalized item. |

**Attested alternates:** No single productive agentive suffix applying uniformly across truth-domain roots is attested. The verbal form cruobs ("he/she lies") is noted as an alternative to a nominal "liar" form. No lexicalized agentive noun for "truth-teller" is attested.

## 8. EVIDENTIAL / RETROSPECTIVE DISCOVERY CONSTRUCTION

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| NP + ADJ-i + q̇opila (evidential copula) | ყალბი ყოფილა | q̇albi q̇opila | "It was fake all along / it turns out it was fake" | [CORE] | Extremely frequent in the truth domain; the discovery of falsity or inauthenticity is a paradigmatic context for evidential marking in Georgian. Grammatically distinct from the simple past iyo. |
| NP + ADJ-i + q̇opila (evidential copula) | პირმოთნე ყოფილა | pirmoṭne q̇opila | "He/she turned out to be a hypocrite (evidential)" | [CORE] | Same construction applied to an agentive noun rather than a property adjective; equally natural. Carries connotations of surprise, disillusionment, or belated recognition. |

**Attested alternates:** gamodga (Construction 4) covers overlapping semantic ground but lacks the evidential/surprise nuance of q̇opila. The two are not fully interchangeable: q̇opila marks speaker non-witnessing and inference; gamodga marks resultative discovery without the same evidential commitment.

## 9. FORMULAIC CANDOR INTRODUCTION

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Parenthetical subordinate clause (conditional/subjunctive) | სიმართლე რომ ვთქვა | simartle rom vtq̇va | "To tell the truth / if I'm being honest / truth be told" | [CORE] | Extremely common in spoken Georgian; functions as a discourse marker of candor or hedging. Structurally a conditional/subjunctive clause but pragmatically a sincerity-signaling device with no single-word equivalent. |
| Adverbial + speech verb | გულწრფელად გეუბნები | gulc̣rpelaد geubnebi | "I'm telling you sincerely / frankly speaking" | [CORE] | Common in spoken Georgian; uses the adverbial form of gulc̣rpeli with a speech verb. Parallel in pragmatic function to simartle rom vtq̇va but uses an adverbial rather than a subordinate clause frame. |

**Attested alternates:** No additional alternates attested in P4a beyond these two patterns. Both are described as extremely common in spoken Georgian.
