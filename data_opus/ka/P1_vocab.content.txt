# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P1 Core Vocabulary Collection
## Language: Georgian (ka)

Transliteration standard used throughout: **ISO 9984 (1996)** for Georgian.

---

**1. Form:** ჭეშმარიტი
**Transliteration:** č̣ešmariti
**Literal/morphology:** root denoting "true/genuine"; adjectival form
**English:** true, veritable, genuine
**Usage:** Used in elevated, philosophical, and religious registers to denote deep or absolute truth, as opposed to mere factual correctness. Frequently appears in theological and moral discourse (e.g., "the true path"). Productive in forming ჭეშმარიტება (truth).
**Status:** [CORE]

---

**2. Form:** ჭეშმარიტება
**Transliteration:** č̣ešmariteba
**Literal/morphology:** č̣ešmarit- + nominalizing suffix -eba
**English:** truth, verity
**Usage:** The abstract noun for truth in philosophical, moral, and religious contexts. Distinct from everyday "fact-correctness," often carrying weight of ultimate or essential truth.
**Status:** [CORE]

---

**3. Form:** სიმართლე
**Transliteration:** simartle
**Literal/morphology:** si- (abstract prefix) + martl- ("right/straight") + -e
**English:** truth, justice, rightness
**Usage:** The most common everyday word for "truth" and also "justice/rightness." Used when telling the truth, demanding fairness, or affirming what really happened. Highly productive.
**Status:** [CORE]

---

**4. Form:** მართალი
**Transliteration:** martali
**Literal/morphology:** root martl- "right/straight"
**English:** true, right, correct; righteous
**Usage:** Applied to statements ("you are right") and to persons (a righteous, just person). Bridges factual correctness and moral uprightness. Very common.
**Status:** [CORE]

---

**5. Form:** სწორი
**Transliteration:** sc̣ori
**Literal/morphology:** root meaning "straight/even/correct"
**English:** correct, right, accurate, straight
**Usage:** Everyday word for correctness of answers, calculations, and conduct. Less moral than მართალი, more about accuracy and conformity to a standard.
**Status:** [CORE]

---

**6. Form:** ნამდვილი
**Transliteration:** namdvili
**Literal/morphology:** root denoting "real/genuine"
**English:** real, genuine, authentic, true
**Usage:** Marks authenticity and genuineness of objects and persons ("a real friend," "genuine gold"). Contrasts directly with the fake or imitation. Productive: ნამდვილად (really, indeed).
**Status:** [CORE]

---

**7. Form:** ნამდვილობა
**Transliteration:** namdviloba
**Literal/morphology:** namdvil- + -oba (abstract suffix)
**English:** authenticity, genuineness, reality
**Usage:** Abstract noun used for the quality of being genuine or authentic, e.g., the authenticity of a document or artwork. More formal/technical.
**Status:** [CORE]

---

**8. Form:** ყალბი
**Transliteration:** q̇albi
**Literal/morphology:** borrowing (ultimately Arabic *qalb*)
**English:** fake, counterfeit, false, forged
**Usage:** Applied to counterfeit money, forged documents, and false/insincere persons. Strong negative evaluation of genuineness. Productive: გაყალბება (to falsify/forge).
**Status:** [CORE]

---

**9. Form:** ცრუ
**Transliteration:** cru
**Literal/morphology:** root "false/lying"
**English:** false, lying, deceitful, mendacious
**Usage:** Describes false statements and lying persons ("false prophet," "false testimony"). Common in moral and religious framing. Productive: სიცრუე (falsehood), ცრუობა (to lie).
**Status:** [CORE]

---

**10. Form:** სიცრუე
**Transliteration:** sicrue
**Literal/morphology:** si- + cru- + -e
**English:** falsehood, lie, mendacity
**Usage:** Abstract noun for falsehood and lying as opposed to truth. Used in moral and rhetorical contexts.
**Status:** [CORE]

---

**11. Form:** ტყუილი
**Transliteration:** ṭq̇uili
**Literal/morphology:** deverbal noun from "to lie"
**English:** lie, falsehood; (adverbially) in vain
**Usage:** The everyday word for "a lie." Also used adverbially to mean pointlessly/in vain. Very common in speech.
**Status:** [CORE]

---

**12. Form:** სანდო
**Transliteration:** sando
**Literal/morphology:** from root of "to trust" + adjectival formation
**English:** reliable, trustworthy, dependable
**Usage:** Applied to sources, persons, and information of which one can be confident. Common in journalistic and everyday assessment of reliability.
**Status:** [CORE]

---

**13. Form:** საიმედო
**Transliteration:** saimedo
**Literal/morphology:** sa- + imed- ("hope") + -o
**English:** reliable, dependable, secure
**Usage:** Denotes something that can be relied upon/counted on (a reliable car, dependable person). Overlaps with სანდო but emphasizes dependability over honesty.
**Status:** [CORE]

---

**14. Form:** ზუსti / ზუსტი
**Transliteration:** zusṭi
**Literal/morphology:** root "exact"
**English:** exact, precise, accurate
**Usage:** Used for precision and exactness of measurements, statements, and descriptions. Common in technical and everyday contexts.
**Status:** [CORE]

---

**15. Form:** უტყუარი
**Transliteration:** uṭq̇uari
**Literal/morphology:** u- (privative) + ṭq̇u- ("lie") + -ari → "lacking falsehood"
**English:** unmistakable, infallible, indubitable, reliable
**Usage:** Describes signs, proofs, or facts that admit no doubt or falsehood. Somewhat elevated register, used for incontrovertible evidence.
**Status:** [CORE]

---

**16. Form:** მსგავსი
**Transliteration:** msgavsi
**Literal/morphology:** root denoting "similar/like"
**English:** similar, like, resembling
**Usage:** The standard word for resemblance and similarity between objects or persons. Productive: მსგავსება (similarity, resemblance).
**Status:** [CORE]

---

**17. Form:** მსგავსება
**Transliteration:** msgavseba
**Literal/morphology:** msgavs- + -eba
**English:** similarity, resemblance, likeness
**Usage:** Abstract noun denoting resemblance, used in description, comparison, and aesthetic/representational contexts.
**Status:** [CORE]

---

**18. Form:** იგივეობა
**Transliteration:** igiveoba
**Literal/morphology:** igive ("the same") + -oba
**English:** identity, sameness
**Usage:** Used in philosophical, logical, and legal contexts to denote identity/sameness of a thing with itself or another. Somewhat technical.
**Status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**19. Form:** არსი
**Transliteration:** arsi
**Literal/morphology:** root of "to be"
**English:** essence, substance, gist, the core of
**Usage:** Denotes the essence or fundamental nature of a thing ("the essence of the matter"). Common in philosophical discourse and in the idiom of "getting to the essence."
**Status:** [CORE]

---

**20. Form:** არსება
**Transliteration:** arseba
**Literal/morphology:** ars- + -eba
**English:** essence, being; (also) creature
**Usage:** Means both "essence/being" in abstract/philosophical use and "creature/being." In evaluative contexts refers to fundamental nature.
**Status:** [CORE]

---

**21. Form:** ბუნება
**Transliteration:** buneba
**Literal/morphology:** borrowing + -eba; "nature"
**English:** nature, character, fundamental quality
**Usage:** Denotes the inherent nature/character of a person or thing ("by nature," "human nature"). Used in moral and philosophical evaluation of what something fundamentally is.
**Status:** [CORE]

---

**22. Form:** ნაღდი
**Transliteration:** naɣdi
**Literal/morphology:** borrowing (ultimately Arabic *naqd*, "cash")
**English:** genuine, real, solid; cash
**Usage:** Colloquial term for the genuine, real-deal, or solid/dependable (a "real man," genuine article). Also means "cash." Informal register.
**Status:** [CORE]

---

**23. Form:** გულწრფელი
**Transliteration:** gulc̣rpeli
**Literal/morphology:** gul- ("heart") + c̣rpel- ("pure/sincere")
**English:** sincere, candid, heartfelt
**Usage:** Describes sincerity and honesty of feeling/expression. Evaluates the genuineness of a person's stance or words. Productive: გულწრფელობა (sincerity).
**Status:** [CORE]

---

**24. Form:** გულახდილი
**Transliteration:** gulaxdili
**Literal/morphology:** gul- ("heart") + axdil- ("opened")
**English:** frank, open, candid
**Usage:** Describes openness and frankness in speech; an honest, hiding-nothing manner. Common in describing candid conversation.
**Status:** [CORE]

---

**25. Form:** პატიოსანი
**Transliteration:** p̣aṭiosani
**Literal/morphology:** from "honor" + adjectival suffix
**English:** honest, honorable, decent
**Usage:** Describes an honest, honorable person in moral and conduct evaluation. Very common in everyday moral assessment. Productive: პატიოსნება (honesty).
**Status:** [CORE]

---

**26. Form:** მართებული
**Transliteration:** martebuli
**Literal/morphology:** from martl-/marteb- "be due/proper"
**English:** proper, appropriate, correct, fitting
**Usage:** Used for what is proper, fitting, or correct in a given situation (a "proper decision," "appropriate response"). Somewhat formal register.
**Status:** [CORE]

---

**27. Form:** მართებულობა
**Transliteration:** martebuloba
**Literal/morphology:** marteb- + -ul- + -oba
**English:** correctness, appropriateness, validity
**Usage:** Abstract noun for the property of being correct/proper. Used in formal, legal, and argumentative contexts to assess rightness.
**Status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**28. Form:** დამაჯერებელი
**Transliteration:** damaǰerebeli
**Literal/morphology:** causative from ǰer- "to believe" → "causing to believe"
**English:** convincing, persuasive, credible
**Usage:** Describes arguments, accounts, or persons that are credible/convincing. Common in evaluation of testimony, performance, and argumentation.
**Status:** [CORE]

---

**29. Form:** ავთენტური
**Transliteration:** avtenṭuri
**Literal/morphology:** European borrowing ("authentic") + -uri
**English:** authentic
**Usage:** Technical/modern borrowing used for authenticity of documents, artworks, and cultural objects. Found in scholarly, legal, and journalistic registers rather than colloquial speech.
**Status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**30. Form:** ჯეროვანი
**Transliteration:** ǰerovani
**Literal/morphology:** from ǰer- ("due/proper amount/time") + -ovani
**English:** due, proper, appropriate, adequate
**Usage:** Formal/legal term meaning "due and proper" (e.g., "due care," "proper consideration"). Used in legal and administrative texts to assess conformity to a standard.
**Status:** [MARGINAL]

---

### Notes
- Item 14 (ზუსტი) contained an inadvertent script artifact in the form line; the correct form is **ზუსტი** (zusṭi).
- ISO 9984 transliteration applied consistently; ejective/aspirate distinctions marked with subscript dot (e.g., ṭ, ḳ, p̣, c̣, č̣, q̇) per the standard.
- Several abstract nouns (e.g., ჭეშმარიტება, ნამდვილობა, მსგავსება) are derived via the productive suffixes **-eba/-oba**, and several adjectives via **-uri/-ovani/-uli**; these patterns are noted where they bear on the listed items.
