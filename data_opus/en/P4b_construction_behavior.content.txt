# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P4b Construction Usage & Constraints
## Language: English (en)

---

## NATURALNESS RATINGS & USAGE NOTES

### 1. ATTRIBUTION
**Rating:** [CORE]
**Usage Note:** The default and most frequent strategy for predicating statement-truth; copular *be* dominates across all registers. The perception-copula substitutes (*ring true*, *seem true*, *sound right*) are restricted to marking apparent rather than asserted truth.
**Alternate Constructions:** Perception-copulas *ring true*, *seem true*, *sound right*; nominal attribution with abstract noun (SUBJECT + *be* + (the) NOUN).

### 2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION
**Rating:** [CORE]
**Usage Note:** Both strategies are common; suppletive antonyms (*false*, *fake*) are frequently preferred over prefixed forms (*untrue*, *unreal*) in predication. The negating prefix is lexically unpredictable.
**Alternate Constructions:** Sentential *not* + positive ADJ (*not correct*); suppletive antonyms (*false*, *fake*).

### 3. ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION
**Rating:** [CORE]
**Usage Note:** Canonically prenominal across all truth-domain adjectives; Latinate items collocate freely but form no fixed idiomatic units. Postnominal use is restricted to delimiting *proper*.
**Alternate Constructions:** Postnominal delimiting *proper* ("the city proper").

### 4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE
**Rating:** [MARGINAL]
**Usage Note:** Restricted and largely idiomatic; *come true* (actualization) and *get real* (informal imperative) are fixed, while general *become true/false* is compositional but uncommon. No dedicated inchoative morphology exists.
**Alternate Constructions:** Fixed *come true*; informal imperative *get real*; compositional *become true/false*.

### 5. CAUSATIVE (OR ABSENCE)
**Rating:** [CORE] (for suffixal layer); [MARGINAL] (for native-core periphrasis)
**Usage Note:** A productive suffixal causative layer (*falsify, verify, validate, authenticate, rectify, legitimize, justify*) is well established, but native core adjectives (*true, real, right*) lack a simple causative verb and require periphrasis (*make true*) or particle constructions (*true up*).
**Alternate Constructions:** Periphrastic *make/render* + ADJ; particle construction *true up*.

### 6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION
**Rating:** [CORE]
**Usage Note:** Highly productive but the suffix is lexically selected and unpredictable (*-ity/-cy/-ness/-th/-hood*). Several bases support two nominals with distinct nuance (*falsity* vs *falsehood*; *trueness* vs *truth*).
**Alternate Constructions:** Competing suffixes on shared bases (*falsity* / *falsehood*; *trueness* / *truth*).

### 7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION
**Rating:** [MARGINAL]
**Usage Note:** Positive agentives are compounds (*truth-teller*) with suppletive *liar* for the negative; deception agentives are suffixal (*faker, forger, counterfeiter*). Most evaluative adjectives (*true, honest, valid*) lack a direct agentive noun and require periphrasis.
**Alternate Constructions:** Compound *-teller*; suppletive *liar*; suffixal *-er/-or* on fabricator verbs; periphrasis ("an honest person").

### 8. EVIDENTIAL / BELIEF-WORTHINESS PREDICATION
**Rating:** [CORE]
**Usage Note:** Structurally identical to attribution but semantically distinct (predicates deservedness-of-belief, not truth); lexically Latinate. Forms no idioms except the fixed term *plausible deniability*.
**Alternate Constructions:** ADJ + N collocations (*credible threat*, *plausible explanation*); fixed term *plausible deniability*.

### 9. CANDOR-MARKING DISCOURSE FRAMES
**Rating:** [CORE]
**Usage Note:** Function as sentence adverbials/hedges rather than predicates; cluster lexically on the native core (*honest, truth*) plus precision (*exact*). Distinct construction class operating at clause level.
**Alternate Constructions:** *to be honest*, *truth be told*, *to tell the truth*, *to be exact*, *correct me if I'm wrong*.

### 10. AUTHENTICATION NOMINAL FRAME
**Rating:** [MARGINAL]
**Usage Note:** A near-synonymous cluster of fixed NPs concentrated on *real/genuine*, used to assert non-counterfeit status; shows notable lexical redundancy. No productive equivalent exists for the negative pole (no fixed "*the fake article*").
**Alternate Constructions:** *the real McCoy*, *the real deal*, *the genuine article*.
