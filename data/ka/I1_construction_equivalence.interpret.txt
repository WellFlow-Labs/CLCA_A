## 2. Substitution Patterns (Narrative)

### BECOME → REVELATION Substitution
The language shows a clear preference for specialized revelation constructions over generic "become" constructions in the truth domain. While გახდა (gaxda) "became" + instrumental case exists as a general inchoative strategy, P4b rates it as **[MARGINAL]** for truth-domain predicates. Instead, the specialized verb გამოვლინდა (gamovlinda) "was revealed" serves as the functional equivalent of BECOME for truth predicates. This represents a **semantic specialization** where the metaphor of truth emerging from concealment replaces the generic change-of-state operator.

### CAUSATION → DISCOVERY/SEEKING Substitution
Georgian lacks a productive morphological causative for truth-domain predicates (P4a Construction 5 notes "no common dedicated causative construction identified"). Instead, the language employs light verb constructions with verbs of discovery and seeking:
- გაარკვია (gaark'via) "find out" (completive aspect)
- იძიოს (iʒios) "search for" (processual aspect)

These constructions express the **process of arriving at truth** rather than causing someone to believe or know truth. This represents a **functional substitution** where epistemic discovery replaces causative semantics.

### VERIFICATION vs. DISCONFIRMATION Asymmetry
The language has robust, dedicated constructions for VERIFICATION (დაადასტურა "confirmed," დაამოწმა "verified"), rated **[CORE]** in P4b. However, no parallel construction for DISCONFIRMATION appears in the data. This suggests an **asymmetric gap** where positive verification is lexicalized but negative disconfirmation requires circumlocution (likely negation + verification verb, though this is not attested in the data).

### Dual NEGATION Strategies
Georgian employs two distinct negation strategies that are **not freely interchangeable**:
1. **Syntactic negation**: არ (ar) + copula for predicative negation
2. **Morphological negation**: უ- (u-) prefix creating stable antonyms

P4b notes these create "distinct lexical items, not compositional negation." The უ- prefix forms (უსამართლო "unjust," უმართებულო "incorrect") have specialized meanings beyond simple negation of their positive counterparts.

### REVELATION Voice Alternation
The REVELATION construction shows a productive **transitive/intransitive alternation**:
- Intransitive: სიმართლე გამოვლინდა "the truth was revealed" (truth as subject)
- Transitive: სიმართლე გამოაჩინა "revealed the truth" (truth as object)

Both are rated **[CORE]** with equal frequency, suggesting this is a systematic voice alternation rather than separate constructions.

### Truth-Telling vs. Truth-Seeking Asymmetry
Light verb constructions show different patterns:
- **Truth-telling**: Uses simple verb თქვა (t'qva) "say" (სიმართლე თქვა "tell the truth")
- **Truth-seeking**: Uses multiple specialized verbs with aspectual distinctions (გაარკვია completive, იძიოს processual)

This suggests **greater elaboration** in the seeking/discovery domain than in the telling domain.

## 3. Missingness Summary

### Structural Gaps
1. **CAUSATION (character)**: No morphological or dedicated syntactic construction for causing someone to become truthful/honest or deceptive. P4a Construction 5 explicitly notes "Georgian lacks a productive morphological causative for truth-domain predicates."

2. **Generic BECOME for truth predicates**: While the construction exists (გახდა + instrumental), it is **[MARGINAL]** in the truth domain, functionally replaced by specialized REVELATION constructions.

### Lexical Gaps
1. **DISCONFIRMATION**: No dedicated verb or light verb construction attested for disconfirming or refuting truth claims. The verification verbs (დაადასტურა, დაამოწმა) have no negative counterparts in the data.

2. **Positive agentive nominalizations**: P4a Construction 7 notes "Positive agents less commonly lexicalized (no single word for 'truth-teller,' expressed periphrastically)." The pattern exists for negative agents (მატყუარა "liar") but not positive ones.

### Conceptual Gaps
None clearly identified. All semantic domains appear expressible through some construction, even if requiring circumlocution.

### Asymmetric Gaps
1. **Verification/Disconfirmation asymmetry**: Positive verification fully lexicalized; negative disconfirmation absent.

2. **Agentive nominalization asymmetry**: Negative agents (liars, deceivers) have dedicated lexical forms; positive agents (truth-tellers) require periphrasis.

3. **BECOME asymmetry**: Generic "become" construction marginal for truth predicates; specialized "reveal" construction preferred.

## 4. Canonicalized Operator Inventory

### Fully Available
- **ATTR**: Predicative constructions with copula (Construction 1)
- **ADNOM**: Attributive adjective modification (Construction 3)
- **REVELATION**: Dedicated intransitive/transitive verb pair (Construction 16)
- **VERIFICATION**: Light verb constructions with specialized verbs (Construction 12)
- **NEGATION**: Both syntactic (არ + copula) and morphological (უ- prefix) strategies (Constructions 2, 21)
- **NOMINALIZATION**: Multiple productive patterns (-ობა/-ება suffixes, სი- prefix) (Construction 6)

### Available with Substitution
- **BECOME**: Functionally replaced by REVELATION constructions
  - Generic გახდა exists but rated **[MARGINAL]**
  - Specialized გამოვლინდა serves as functional equivalent
  
- **CAUSATION (factual)**: Functionally replaced by DISCOVERY/SEEKING constructions
  - No morphological causative
  - Light verbs გაარკვია "find out" and იძიოს "search for" express related semantics

### Partially Available
- **DISCONFIRMATION**: No dedicated construction attested
  - Likely expressible through negation + verification verbs
  - Requires circumlocution; no common fixed expression

### Missing Entirely
- **CAUSATION (character)**: No construction for causing someone to become truthful/deceptive
  - Not expressible through dedicated morphosyntactic means
  - Would require periphrastic expression with generic causative verbs (e.g., გააკეთა "made") + adjective, but P4a notes this is "not fixed construction specific to truth domain"

## Summary Statistics

- **Direct equivalents**: 6/10 operators (ATTR, ADNOM, REVELATION, VERIFICATION, NEGATION, NOMINALIZATION)
- **Functional substitutions**: 2/10 operators (BECOME, CAUSATION-factual)
- **Partial availability**: 1/10 operators (DISCONFIRMATION)
- **Structural gaps**: 1/10 operators (CAUSATION-character)

The language shows **robust coverage** of core truth-domain operations with some systematic gaps in causative semantics and asymmetries favoring positive verification over negative disconfirmation.
