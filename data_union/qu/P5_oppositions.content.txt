# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P5 Oppositions
# Language: Quechua (qu)

---

**Preliminary note on method:** Oppositions below are extracted exclusively from contrasts explicitly stated or structurally demonstrated in the CONTEXT (P1–P4b). No opposition is inferred from English translation equivalents or from general linguistic knowledge not supported by the data. Where the CONTEXT explicitly marks a contrast (e.g., "contrasts directly with", "negative counterpart", "contrasts with"), that contrast is included. Where a structural opposition is demonstrable from the paradigmatic data (e.g., two forms differing by a single morpheme with documented opposing meanings), that contrast is included. Asymmetries are preserved; no symmetry is imposed.

---

## OPPOSITION 1

1. **Term A:** cheqa
2. **Term B:** llulla
3. **Transliteration:** cheqa / llulla
4. **English translation:** 'true, real, correct, genuine' / 'false, lying, deceptive, fake'
5. **Description of contrast:** These are described in P1 as the primary antonym pair in the truth-evaluation domain; *llulla* "contrasts directly with *cheqa*" (P1, item 7/10). The opposition is attested across adjectival, nominal, and verbal uses, and is explicitly described as the foundational positive/negative polarity of the domain. They are used as direct predicative antonyms in the same construction type (e.g., *Cheqam kay* "This is true" vs. *Llullam chay* "That is a lie") and appear as contrasting modifiers in parallel nominal phrases (*cheqa simi* "true word" vs. *llulla simi* "false speech"; *cheqa sonqo* "honest at heart" vs. *llulla sonqo* "false-hearted"). *Cheqa* predicates truth or genuineness of a statement, person, or thing; *llulla* characterizes statements, persons, or representations as dishonest or counterfeit. They are the primary antonyms in the truth-evaluation domain.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## OPPOSITION 2

1. **Term A:** cheqa
2. **Term B:** mana cheqa
3. **Transliteration:** cheqa / mana cheqa
4. **English translation:** 'true, real, genuine' / 'untrue, false, not genuine, inauthentic, incorrect'
5. **Description of contrast:** *Mana cheqa* is described in P1 as "the standard negated form used to predicate falsity or inauthenticity" and functions as "a fixed evaluative expression" contrasting with *cheqa*. P1b and P2a describe it as "the standard negated form of *cheqa*" and "widely attested in Southern Quechua." The negator *mana* produces a phrasal negative that operates as a fixed evaluative unit rather than a transparent compositional negation.
6. **Contrast type:** morphological
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## OPPOSITION 3

1. **Term A:** allin
2. **Term B:** mana allin
3. **Transliteration:** allin / mana allin
4. **English translation:** 'good, right, well, proper, fine, correct' / 'bad, not good, wrong, improper, incorrect'
5. **Description of contrast:** P1 describes *mana allin* as "standard negated evaluative expression for moral, practical, and aesthetic badness or wrongness" and the "negative counterpart" to *allin*, functioning as a conventionalized fixed expression. The CONTEXT treats this as a fixed expression parallel in status to *mana cheqa*. Both appear in the same predicate-plus-evidential construction (*Allinmi chay ruway* vs. *Mana allinmi chay*). The opposition covers moral, practical, and accuracy-related contexts.
6. **Contrast type:** morphological
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## OPPOSITION 4

1. **Term A:** cheqam
2. **Term B:** mana cheqachu
3. **Transliteration:** cheqam / mana cheqachu
4. **English translation:** 'it is true (I know directly)' / 'it is not true / it is false'
5. **Description of contrast:** P2b documents these as a direct predicative pair: *cheqam* is "the most basic predicative truth assertion" and *mana cheqachu* is its "standard negative predication of falsity," described as "extremely common in discourse as a direct denial." The *-chu* enclitic is described as obligatory in negative declarative clauses, making the structural asymmetry between the two forms grammatically conditioned. P4a and P4b document both as the standard forms for truth assertion and denial across Southern Quechua registers.
6. **Contrast type:** constructional
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## OPPOSITION 5

1. **Term A:** llullam
2. **Term B:** mana llullachu
3. **Transliteration:** llullam / mana llullachu
4. **English translation:** 'it is a lie / that is false' / 'it is not a lie / that is not false'
5. **Description of contrast:** P2b documents *llullam* as "the most direct predicative assertion of falsehood or deception" and *mana llullachu* as its negative counterpart (Construction 17), described as "the standard negative predication of *llulla*," used "in rebuttal and defense contexts." The same obligatory *-chu* asymmetry applies as in Opposition 4. This is the morphological negation of the negative evaluative root, functioning as a positive characterization by negating the negative term.
6. **Contrast type:** constructional
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## OPPOSITION 6

1. **Term A:** cheqam
2. **Term B:** cheqas
3. **Transliteration:** cheqam / cheqas
4. **English translation:** 'it is true (I know directly)' / 'it is true (reportedly / I heard that)'
5. **Description of contrast:** P2b (Construction 25.1) and P4a (§8) explicitly document this as a grammatically encoded contrast between first-hand and reported knowledge of truth. The CONTEXT states: "The contrast between the direct evidential *-m* and the reportative *-s* on the same predicate *cheqa* constitutes a grammatically encoded distinction between first-hand and reported knowledge of truth." The two forms are identical except for the evidential enclitic.
6. **Contrast type:** morphological
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## OPPOSITION 7

1. **Term A:** cheqam
2. **Term B:** cheqachá
3. **Transliteration:** cheqam / cheqachá
4. **English translation:** 'it is true (I know directly)' / 'it is probably true / perhaps it is genuine'
5. **Description of contrast:** P4a (§8) documents the three-way evidential contrast; *-m/-mi* marks direct knowledge while *-chá* marks "conjecture or inference rather than certain knowledge" (P1, item 27). The CONTEXT treats these as part of a single grammatical system with three distinct values.
6. **Contrast type:** morphological
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## OPPOSITION 8

1. **Term A:** cheqas
2. **Term B:** cheqachá
3. **Transliteration:** cheqas / cheqachá
4. **English translation:** 'it is true (reportedly)' / 'it is probably true (I conjecture)'
5. **Description of contrast:** Within the three-way evidential system documented in P1 (items 25–27) and P4a (§8), *-s/-si* marks a claim as based on reported information from another source, while *-chá* marks it as the speaker's own inference or conjecture. Both contrast with the direct evidential *-m/-mi* (Oppositions 6 and 7) and with each other.
6. **Contrast type:** morphological
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## OPPOSITION 9

1. **Term A:** cheqanchay
2. **Term B:** cheqakay
3. **Transliteration:** cheqanchay / cheqakay
4. **English translation:** 'to verify / to confirm / to make true (transitive)' / 'to become true / to be realized / to come true (intransitive)'
5. **Description of contrast:** P2a explicitly states: "*cheqakay* is intransitive ('it becomes true'), while *cheqanchay* is transitive ('to make something true / to verify')." P4a (§4 and §5) formalizes this as a systematic contrast between the *-ncha-* causative verbalizer (transitive, externally directed) and the *-ka-* intransitive verbalizer (inchoative, internally directed). Both derive from the same root *cheqa*.
6. **Contrast type:** morphological
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## OPPOSITION 13

1. **Term A:** pantay
2. **Term B:** pantachiy
3. **Transliteration:** pantay / pantachiy
4. **English translation:** 'to err / to be mistaken (intransitive)' / 'to mislead / to cause to err (causative, transitive)'
5. **Description of contrast:** P2a documents *pantachiy* as the causative derivative of *pantay* via *-chi-*, meaning "to cause another to hold a false belief or make an error." P3 (Group 4 vs. Group 6) distinguishes expressions of causing error in another from expressions of simply being in error. The two forms share the root *panta-* but differ in transitivity and in the distribution of agency.
6. **Contrast type:** morphological
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## OPPOSITION 14

1. **Term A:** llullakuy
2. **Term B:** llullachiy
3. **Transliteration:** llullakuy / llullachiy
4. **English translation:** 'to lie / to deceive (self-initiated act)' / 'to cause to lie / to induce falsehood in another (causative)'
5. **Description of contrast:** P2a documents *llullachiy* as the causative derivative of *llulla* via *-chi-*, used "when an agent causes or induces another person to lie or deceive." *Llullakuy* describes the act of lying as performed by the agent themselves; *llullachiy* describes causing another to perform that act.

## OPPOSITION 15

1. **Term A:** cheqanchay (via -ncha-)
2. **Term B:** llullachiy (via -chi-)
3. **Transliteration:** cheqanchay / llullachiy
4. **English translation:** 'to verify / to make true' (adjectival root + *-ncha-*) / 'to cause to lie' (root + *-chi-*)
5. **Description of contrast:** P2a explicitly documents an asymmetry: "The root *llulla* does not show derivation via the *-ncha-* causative verbalizer, which is the primary transitive derivational pathway for *cheqa* and *allin*. Instead, it uses *-ku-* as its primary verbalizer and *-chi-* for causative meaning." P4a (§5) preserves this as an attested structural asymmetry. The contrast is between two different causative derivational pathways available to roots in this domain.
6. **Contrast type:** morphological
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## OPPOSITION 19

1. **Term A:** cheqa Dios
2. **Term B:** llulla Dios
3. **Transliteration:** cheqa Dios / llulla Dios
4. **English translation:** 'the true God / the genuine God' / 'false god / a deity that is not genuine / idol / counterfeit deity'
5. **Description of contrast:** P1b documents both compounds in Christian religious discourse in Quechua, where *cheqa Dios* distinguishes the Christian deity from others and *llulla Dios* characterizes non-Christian spiritual beings as counterfeit. P1b explicitly describes *llulla Dios* as "the direct antonym of *cheqa Dios*" (P1b, item 12 under llulla). P3 notes them as a parallel pair under "ambiguous and overlapping items." Both apply an evaluative root to the same nominal in the same register. Both are attested specifically in Christian Quechua religious discourse.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]
