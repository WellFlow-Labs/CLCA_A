# CLCA Protocol v3.3 — P6 NATURALNESS: CHANGE, CAUSATION, AND STABILITY
# Language: Greek (el)

---

## 1. BECOMING TRUE / ACQUIRING TRUTH STATUS

### 1.1 With αλήθεια (truth)

**Pattern:** βγαίνει η αλήθεια (the truth comes out)
**Transliteration:** vgéni i alítheia
**Translation:** the truth comes out, the truth emerges
**Usage:** Standard expression for truth being revealed or becoming known. Uses intransitive verb βγαίνω "come out, emerge" rather than γίνομαι "become."
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — This is the natural way to express truth emerging or becoming known.

**Pattern:** αποδεικνύεται αλήθεια
**Transliteration:** apodikníete alítheia
**Translation:** it proves to be true, it turns out to be true
**Usage:** Formal expression for something being proven true. Uses passive voice of αποδεικνύω "prove."
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Common in formal and investigative contexts.

**Pattern:** *γίνεται αλήθεια
**Transliteration:** gínete alítheia
**Translation:** *it becomes truth
**Usage:** NOT ATTESTED as natural expression. Greek does not use γίνομαι "become" with αλήθεια in this way.
**Naturalness:** [UNNATURAL] — This construction is not used. Truth is conceptualized as being revealed rather than becoming.

**Note:** Greek strongly prefers revelation/emergence metaphors (βγαίνω, αποκαλύπτομαι) over change-of-state metaphors (γίνομαι) for truth.

---

### 1.2 With αληθινός (true, genuine)

**Pattern:** αποδεικνύεται αληθινός/αληθινή/αληθινό
**Transliteration:** apodikníete alithinós/alithiní/alithinó
**Translation:** it proves to be true/genuine
**Usage:** Formal expression for something being proven genuine or true. Adjective agrees with subject.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Common in formal contexts, particularly for authentication.

**Pattern:** γίνεται αληθινός/αληθινή/αληθινό
**Transliteration:** gínete alithinós/alithiní/alithinó
**Translation:** it becomes true/genuine
**Usage:** MARGINALLY ATTESTED. Can be used but sounds somewhat awkward. More natural with specific subjects (e.g., φίλος "friend").
**Naturalness:** [MARGINAL] — Possible but not preferred. Example: γίνεται αληθινός φίλος "becomes a true friend" is acceptable.

**Pattern:** αληθεύομαι
**Transliteration:** alithévome
**Translation:** to be verified, to prove true, to turn out to be true
**Usage:** Middle/passive voice verb expressing verification or confirmation. More formal register.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Standard formal expression for verification.

**Example:** αληθεύτηκε η πρόβλεψη (alithéftike i próvlepsi) "the prediction proved true"

### 1.3 With σωστός (correct, right)

**Transliteration:** gínete sostó/sostí/sostós

**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Natural expression for correction or becoming accurate.

**Example:** γίνεται σωστό με τη διόρθωση (gínete sostó me ti diórthosi) "it becomes correct with the correction"

**Pattern:** διορθώνεται
**Transliteration:** diorthónete
**Translation:** it is corrected, it gets corrected
**Usage:** Passive voice of διορθώνω "correct." Standard expression for correction.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Primary way to express something being corrected.

### 1.4 With πραγματικός (real, actual)

**Usage:** Passive voice of πραγματοποιώ "realize, accomplish." Standard expression for realization.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Primary way to express something being realized or coming true.

### 1.5 With ορθός (correct, right, straight)

**Transliteration:** diorthónete
**Translation:** it is corrected, it is straightened

**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Natural in institutional contexts.

### 1.7 With βέβαιος (certain, sure)

**Transliteration:** gínome véveos/vévei

**Transliteration:** veveónome

**Usage:** Middle voice of βεβαιώνω. Expresses active verification or making sure.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Standard expression for verification.

## 3. CAUSATIVE: MAKING TRUE / CAUSING TRUTH

### 3.1 With αλήθεια (truth)

**Pattern:** αποκαλύπτω την αλήθεια
**Transliteration:** apokalýpto tin alítheia
**Translation:** I reveal the truth
**Usage:** Standard transitive expression for causing truth to be known. Does not make something true, but reveals existing truth.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Primary way to express truth revelation.

**Pattern:** φέρνω την αλήθεια στο φως
**Transliteration:** férno tin alítheia sto fos
**Translation:** I bring the truth to light
**Usage:** Idiomatic expression for revealing truth. Metaphorical causative.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Common idiomatic expression.

**Pattern:** *κάνω κάτι αλήθεια
**Transliteration:** káno káti alítheia
**Translation:** *I make something truth
**Usage:** NOT ATTESTED as natural expression. Greek does not use κάνω "make" to cause truth.
**Naturalness:** [UNNATURAL] — This construction is not used.

**Note:** Greek lacks a simple causative for "make true" because truth is conceptualized as pre-existing and revealed rather than created.

---

### 3.2 With αληθινός (true, genuine)

**Pattern:** κάνω κάτι αληθινό
**Transliteration:** káno káti alithinó
**Translation:** I make something real/genuine
**Usage:** MARGINALLY ATTESTED. Can be used in specific contexts (e.g., making dreams real) but sounds somewhat awkward.
**Naturalness:** [MARGINAL] — Possible but not preferred.

**Pattern:** πραγματοποιώ
**Transliteration:** pragmatopiό
**Translation:** I realize, I make real, I accomplish
**Usage:** Standard verb for making something real or accomplishing it. Derived from πραγματικός "real."
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Primary way to express realization or making real.

**Example:** πραγματοποιώ το όνειρό μου (pragmatopiό to óneiró mou) "I realize my dream"

---

### 3.3 With σωστός (correct, right)

**Pattern:** κάνω κάτι σωστό
**Transliteration:** káno káti sostó
**Translation:** I make something correct, I do something right
**Usage:** ATTESTED and natural. Can mean either "I correct something" or "I do the right thing."
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Natural expression with dual meaning.

**Pattern:** διορθώνω
**Transliteration:** diorthóno
**Translation:** I correct, I fix
**Usage:** Standard transitive verb for correcting errors or fixing mistakes.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Primary way to express correction.

**Example:** διορθώνω το λάθος (diorthóno to láthos) "I correct the mistake"

---

### 3.4 With ορθός (correct, right, straight)

**Pattern:** κάνω κάτι ορθό
**Transliteration:** káno káti orthó
**Translation:** I make something correct/straight
**Usage:** MARGINALLY ATTESTED. More natural in physical sense (straightening) than epistemic sense.
**Naturalness:** [MARGINAL] — Possible but not preferred for epistemic correctness.

**Pattern:** διορθώνω
**Transliteration:** diorthóno
**Translation:** I correct, I straighten
**Usage:** Same verb as with σωστός. Standard expression for correction.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Primary way to express correction.

**Pattern:** ορθώνω
**Transliteration:** orthóno
**Translation:** I straighten, I erect, I raise upright
**Usage:** Causative verb specifically for physical straightening or raising upright. Not used for epistemic correctness.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Standard for physical straightening; [UNNATURAL] for epistemic correctness.

---

### 3.5 With έγκυρος (valid, legitimate)

**Pattern:** κάνω κάτι έγκυρο
**Transliteration:** káno káti énkyro
**Translation:** I make something valid
**Usage:** MARGINALLY ATTESTED. More natural to use specialized causative verb.
**Naturalness:** [MARGINAL] — Possible but not preferred.

**Pattern:** επικυρώνω
**Transliteration:** epikyrόno
**Translation:** I validate, I certify, I ratify
**Usage:** Standard causative verb for making something officially valid. Legal/administrative term.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Primary way to express validation in formal contexts.

**Example:** επικυρώνω το έγγραφο (epikyrόno to éngrafo) "I validate the document"

**Pattern:** κυρώνω
**Transliteration:** kyrόno
**Translation:** I ratify, I validate, I approve officially
**Usage:** Formal causative verb for official validation or ratification.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Standard in legal/administrative contexts.

---

### 3.6 With βέβαιος (certain, sure)

**Pattern:** κάνω κάποιον βέβαιο
**Transliteration:** káno kápion véveo
**Translation:** I make someone certain
**Usage:** MARGINALLY ATTESTED. More natural to use specialized causative verb.

**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Primary way to express confirmation or certification.

**Usage:** Intensified form of βεβαιώνω, specifically for reassuring or strongly assuring someone.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Standard for personal assurance.

---

### 3.7 With δίκαιος (just, fair)

**Pattern:** κάνω κάτι δίκαιο
**Transliteration:** káno káti díkaio
**Translation:** I make something just/fair, I do justice

## 10. SUMMARY OF PATTERNS

### 10.1 Preferred change-of-state verbs by domain:

**Truth domain (αλήθεια):**
- Revelation: βγαίνω "come out," αποκαλύπτομαι "be revealed"
- Verification: αποδεικνύομαι "be proven," αληθεύομαι "be verified"
- NOT: γίνομαι "become" (unnatural with αλήθεια)

**Correctness domain (σωστός, ορθός):**
- Change: γίνομαι "become" (natural)
- Correction: διορθώνομαι "be corrected"

**Reality domain (πραγματικός):**
- Realization: γίνομαι "become," πραγματοποιούμαι "be realized"

**Validity domain (έγκυρος, άκυρος):**
- Change: γίνομαι "become" (natural)
- Validation: επικυρώνομαι "be validated"
- Invalidation: ακυρώνομαι "be invalidated"

**Justice domain (δίκαιος, άδικος):**
- Change: γίνομαι "become" (natural)
- Vindication: δικαιώνομαι "be vindicated"

---

### 10.2 Causative strategies:

**Morphological causatives (productive):**
- -ώνω suffix: ακυρώνω "invalidate," βεβαιώνω "certify," επικυρώνω "validate," δικαιώνω "vindicate"

**Specialized verbs (lexical causatives):**
- διορθώνω "correct"
- πραγματοποιώ "realize"
- πλαστογραφώ "forge"
- κατασκευάζω "fabricate"
- διαψεύδω "disprove"
- παραποιώ "falsify"

**Analytic causatives (limited):**
- κάνω + adjective (marginally productive, not preferred for core truth terms)

**Absent causatives:**
- No simple causative for "make true" (*αληθώνω not attested)
- No simple causative for "make false" (*ψευδώνω not attested)

---

### 10.3 Conceptual patterns:

**Truth is revealed, not created:**
- Greek strongly prefers revelation metaphors (βγαίνω, αποκαλύπτω) over change-of-state metaphors (γίνομαι) for truth
- Truth is conceptualized as pre-existing and discovered rather than becoming true

**Correctness can be achieved:**
- Correctness terms (σωστός, ορθός) naturally take γίνομαι "become"
- Correctness is conceptualized as achievable state rather than revealed fact

**Validity is institutional:**
- Validity terms have specialized causative verbs (επικυρώνω, ακυρώνω)
- Validity is conceptualized as conferred by authority rather than inherent

**Falsity is proven, not made:**
- No simple causative for "make false"
- Falsity is proven (διαψεύδω) or fabricated (κατασκευάζω, πλαστογραφώ) but not simply "made"
