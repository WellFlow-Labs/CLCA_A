## Summary Notes

1. **All constructions rated [CORE] except the adessive + motion verb inchoative ([MARGINAL]) and the causative ([UNCERTAIN]).** The inchoative adessive construction is attested but less frequent and more restricted in context than the resultative verb construction. The causative is unattested as a productive pattern.

2. **Register differentiation is most clearly observable in Construction 6** (abstract nominalization), where **si-...-e/le** nominals pattern with colloquial use and **-eba/-oba** nominals pattern with formal and legal use.

3. **Construction 8 (genitive modification) is identified in P4a as the most productive multi-word pattern** in this domain and generates the majority of legal and philosophical collocations; this is consistent with its [CORE] rating across both examples.

4. **No constructions have been added or inferred beyond those identified in P4a.** All examples are preserved exactly as given.
