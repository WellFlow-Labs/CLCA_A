| Propositional Affirmation and Denial | Expressions used to affirm or deny the truth-value of statements or propositions in discourse. These forms appear primarily in responses, confirmations, corrections, and evaluative contexts where speakers assess whether something said or claimed corresponds to reality. | Correctness and Accuracy Evaluation | Expressions used to evaluate the correctness, accuracy, or precision of answers, information, procedures, calculations, and documents. | Authenticity and Genuineness | Expressions used to distinguish genuine, authentic, or real entities from fake, counterfeit, or superficial ones. | Moral Character and Uprightness | Expressions used to evaluate the moral character of persons, particularly regarding honesty, integrity, fairness, and uprightness.
