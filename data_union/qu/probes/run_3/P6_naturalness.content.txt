# CLCA Protocol v3.3 — P6 Change, Causation, and Stability
# Language: Quechua (Southern, Cusco-Collao / Ayacucho-Chanka)

---

**Preliminary note on scope and method:** This step documents how Southern Quechua encodes dynamic truth-related meanings — becoming, causing, and maintaining truth-related states — using only vocabulary attested in P1–P5. The primary construction types available in the data are: (a) the inchoative *tukuy* ("to become") construction documented in P4a §4; (b) the morphological causative with *-chay/-chiy* documented in P4a §5 and P2a; (c) the reflexive/passive verbalizing forms (*-siy*, *-kuy*) documented in P2a; (d) the participial *-sqa* forms used to describe resultant states; and (e) periphrastic constructions with *tiyan* ("it is necessary, there is") documented in P2b. Each truth-domain root is treated separately where its behavior differs. Gaps are preserved as gaps. Epistemic statuses are carried forward from prior steps.

---

## 1. BECOMING A TRUTH-RELATED STATE (*tukuy* constructions)

### Pattern
[truth-domain adjective/nominal] + *tukuy* ("to become, to turn into"), with tense and person inflection carried by *tukuy*. Documented in P4a §4 as a structurally regular pattern in Southern Quechua; specific attestation for truth-domain items is limited and both examples carry [UNCERTAIN] status from prior steps.

## 2. CAUSING A TRUTH-RELATED STATE (morphological causatives)

### Pattern
Root + *-chay/-chiy* (causative verbalizing suffix), producing a transitive verb meaning "to cause [root]-state" or "to make [something] [root]." Documented in P4a §5 and P2a as the primary causative strategy for truth-domain items. Three forms are attested with [CORE] status: *cheqaqchay*, *chiqanchay*, and *llullachiy*.

## 5. STABILITY AND MAINTENANCE OF TRUTH-RELATED STATES

### Pattern
The data in P1–P5 do not document a dedicated construction for maintaining or preserving a truth-related state (e.g., "X remains true," "X stays honest"). The closest available constructions involve the emphatic suffix *-puni* ("absolutely, always, invariably") attached to *cheqaq*, producing *cheqaqpuni* ("absolutely true, certainly genuine"), which asserts that something is unquestionably and without exception the case. This is documented in P2a and P4a as [CORE].

---

### 5.1 Emphatic / absolute assertion of stable truth

| Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `Cheqaqpunim chay.` | cheqaqpuni-m chay | "That is absolutely true. / That is certainly genuine." | [CORE] | Documented in P2b C2.3 and P4a §1 as [CORE]; the *-puni* suffix asserts that the truth-state is absolute and without exception. Carried forward. |
| `Cheqaqpunim cheqaq.` | cheqaqpuni-m cheqaq | "Truth is absolutely truth. / What is true is invariably true." | [UNCERTAIN] | Extension of the *cheqaqpuni* pattern to a tautological assertion of stable truth; not specifically attested in prior steps. Flagged accordingly. |

**Note on absence:** No construction specifically encoding the maintenance or persistence of a truth-related state over time (e.g., a progressive or continuative aspect applied to truth-domain predicates) is documented in P1–P5. This absence is preserved. The *-puni* suffix encodes absoluteness rather than temporal persistence specifically.

## 6. SUMMARY OF ATTESTED DYNAMIC PATTERNS

The following observations are directly supported by the data above and are reported without interpretation beyond what the data warrant.

**Differential attestation of causative forms:** Three morphological causatives are attested with [CORE] status: *cheqaqchay* (causing truth/authentication), *chiqanchay* (causing correction/rectification), and *llullachiy* (causing deception/false belief). *Sut'ichay* (causing clarity) is attested as [CORE] in P2a but its specific dynamic constructions carry [UNCERTAIN] status in P2b. No morphological causative is attested for *allin*, *pantay*, *kikin*, *sut'i*, or *yachay* within the truth-evaluation domain in available sources.

**Asymmetry between cheqaq and llulla in dynamic constructions:** The *cheqaq* root has an attested reflexive/passive form (*cheqaqsiy*, "to be confirmed, to turn out to be true") that describes a process of becoming verified, though this form carries [UNCERTAIN] status. No parallel reflexive/passive form is documented for *llulla* beyond *llullakuy* itself, which is the primary verbal form for the act of lying rather than a derived passive. This asymmetry is preserved without explanation.

**Pantay as inherently dynamic:** *Pantay* ("to err, to make a mistake, to go astray") is itself a verbal root encoding a dynamic event (the act of going wrong), unlike *cheqa*, *llulla*, and *chiqan*, which are primarily adjectival/nominal roots that require *tukuy* or a causative suffix to express dynamic meanings. The *tukuy* inchoative construction with *pantasqa* would therefore be structurally redundant in many contexts, as noted in §1.3.

**Tukuy constructions:** The *tukuy* inchoative is documented as structurally available for truth-domain adjectives (*cheqaq tukuy*, *chiqan tukuy*) but all specific examples carry [UNCERTAIN] status from P4a. No *tukuy* construction for *llulla* is specifically attested in P1–P5; the absence is preserved.

**No dedicated stability construction:** No construction specifically encoding the maintenance or persistence of a truth-related state is documented in P1–P5. The *-puni* emphatic suffix (*cheqaqpuni*) is the closest available form, encoding absoluteness rather than temporal persistence.
