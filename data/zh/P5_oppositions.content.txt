# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P5 OPPOSITIONS
# Language: Chinese (zh)

## OPPOSITION 1

**Term A**: 真 (zhēn)  
**Term B**: 假 (jiǎ)  
**Transliteration**: zhēn vs. jiǎ  
**Translation**: "true, real, genuine" vs. "false, fake, artificial"  
**Description**: Core lexical opposition evaluating authenticity of objects, products, and statements. Extremely productive in compounds (真品/假货, 真丝/假丝) and idiomatic expressions (真假难辨, 以假乱真).  
**Contrast type**: lexical  
**Epistemic status**: [CORE]

## SUMMARY

**Total oppositions identified**: 51

**Distribution by contrast type**:
- Lexical oppositions: 18
- Morphological oppositions: 9
- Constructional oppositions: 24

**Key patterns observed**:
