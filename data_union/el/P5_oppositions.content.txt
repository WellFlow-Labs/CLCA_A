## Opposition 1

**Term A:** αληθής / αλήθεια / αληθινός
**Term B:** ψευδής / ψεύδος / ψεύτικος
**Transliteration A:** alithís / alíthia / alithinós
**Transliteration B:** psevdhís / psévdhos / psévtikos
**English translation:** true / truth / genuine (colloquial) vs. false / falsehood / fake (colloquial)
**Description of contrast:** These two clusters form the primary lexical opposition in the propositional truth-evaluation domain. The αληθ- cluster encodes correspondence with reality; the ψευδ-/ψευτ- cluster encodes departure from it. The opposition is explicitly framed in the fixed binary expression αλήθεια ή ψέμα ("truth or lie") and in the paired formula αλήθεια και ψέμα ("truth and falsehood"). Within each cluster, register distinctions operate in parallel: αληθής / ψευδής are formal and legal; αληθινός / ψεύτικος are colloquial. The two base adjectives αληθής and ψευδής appear together in the fixed binary construction αληθής ή ψευδής ("true or false"), used in logic, philosophy, and educational testing. Both apply primarily to statements, claims, and documents. They are documented as the most symmetrical pair in the truth-value predication domain and appear in parallel passive predicative constructions: *Ο ισχυρισμός αποδείχθηκε αληθής* vs. *Ο ισχυρισμός κρίθηκε ψευδής*. The primary abstract noun pair αλήθεια / ψεύδος appears as a direct contrast in moral, philosophical, and everyday discourse, and each generates a large derivational family oriented around the same opposition.
**Contrast type:** lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 2

**Term A:** αλήθεια
**Term B:** αναλήθεια
**Transliteration A:** alíthia
**Transliteration B:** analíthia
**English translation:** truth vs. untruth / falsehood
**Description of contrast:** A morphological negation formed by the privative prefix ἀν- applied to the primary truth noun. αναλήθεια is documented as a formal or literary alternative to ψέμα, used in philosophical and journalistic writing, and as a formal and philosophical abstract noun used in contexts of explicit philosophical discussion of truth-values. Less common than ψεύδος or ψέμα for the same meaning. The opposition is asymmetric: αλήθεια is [CORE] across all registers, while αναλήθεια is restricted to elevated registers.
**Contrast type:** morphological
**Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL] (αναλήθεια is [MARGINAL]; αλήθεια is [CORE])

## Opposition 12

**Term A:** έγκυρος
**Term B:** άκυρος
**Transliteration A:** éngiros
**Transliteration B:** ákiros
**English translation:** valid / authoritative vs. invalid / void / null
**Description of contrast:** These two terms share the root κῦρος (authority, validity) but are formed with different prefixes (ἐν- vs. ἀ-). The opposition is noted as a root-level contrast rather than a simple privative negation of έγκυρος itself. Both are [CORE] in legal and administrative registers, where the validity or invalidity of documents, contracts, and decisions is at issue. The opposition generates a productive cluster on the negative side (άκυρος → ακυρότητα → ακυρώνω → ακύρωση) that has no full parallel on the positive side. The data explicitly notes that άκυρος is the preferred legal term rather than the periphrastic δεν είναι έγκυρος, indicating that the lexical opposition is institutionally fixed. They appear in parallel constructions: *Το συμβόλαιο παραμένει έγκυρο* vs. *Άκυρο εισιτήριο*; *άκυρη ψήφος*.
**Contrast type:** lexical (shared root, distinct prefixes; co-derivatives from shared base; not direct morphological derivation of one from the other)
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 15

**Term A:** γνήσιος
**Term B:** νόθος
**Transliteration A:** gníssios
**Transliteration B:** nóthos
**English translation:** genuine / of legitimate origin vs. spurious / of illegitimate origin / adulterated

They appear in parallel philological and commercial contexts: *γνήσιο προϊόν* vs. *νόθα προϊόντα*; *γνήσιο έργο* vs. *νόθο κείμενο*.

## Opposition 27

**Term A:** επαληθεύσιμος
**Term B:** ανεπαλήθευτος
**Transliteration A:** epalithéfsimos
**Transliteration B:** anepalítheftos
**English translation:** verifiable vs. unverified
**Description of contrast:** These two terms are morphologically related to the same verb (επαληθεύω) but encode different aspects of the verification dimension: επαληθεύσιμος describes the in-principle possibility of verification (a property of claims), while ανεπαλήθευτος describes the actual state of not having been verified (a property of information at a given time). The opposition is not a simple privative pair — the suffixes differ (-σιμος vs. -τος) — and the semantic contrast is between potential and actuality rather than between presence and absence of a single property.

## Opposition 29

**Term A:** αληθοφανής / αληθοφάνεια
**Term B:** αληθής / αλήθεια
**Transliteration A:** alithofanís / alithofánia
**Transliteration B:** alithís / alíthia
**English translation:** truth-seeming / verisimilitude vs. true / truth
**Description of contrast:** A lexical opposition between appearing to be true and actually being true. αληθοφανής (from αληθ- + φαίνομαι, "to appear") describes something that has the appearance of truth without necessarily being true; αληθής describes something that actually is true. The contrast is between the φαίνεσθαι (appearing) and the εἶναι (being) dimensions of truth. The data explicitly identifies αληθοφάνεια and αληθοφανής as describing the quality of appearing true rather than being true, and flags this as a peripheral extension of the αληθ- root where "the root contributes to a concept of appearing true rather than being true — a shift from truth to the aesthetics of plausibility." This opposition is documented in rhetorical, literary, and aesthetic contexts. The contrast is described as aesthetic and rhetorical rather than purely propositional.
**Contrast type:** lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]
