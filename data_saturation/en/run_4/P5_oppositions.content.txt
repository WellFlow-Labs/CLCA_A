# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P5 OPPOSITIONS
**Language: English (en)**

## Preliminary Note on Method

Oppositions below are extracted exclusively from patterns documented in P1, P1b, P2a, P2b, P3, P4a, and P4b. Each opposition is supported by explicit textual evidence from the CONTEXT. Where the CONTEXT notes an asymmetry, gap, or qualification, this is preserved. No opposition has been inferred beyond what the data support. The ordering follows the P1 item sequence where possible.

## Opposition 1

**Term A:** true (P1-1)
**Term B:** false (P1-9)
**Transliteration:** N/A
**English translation:** true / false
**Description of contrast:** The CONTEXT explicitly identifies these as direct contrasts: P1-9 states that *false* "contrasts directly with *true*." Both appear in the canonical frame *that is true / that is false* (P2b-1.2, P2b-9.1) and in the fixed binary interrogative *true or false?* (P2b-9.8). They anchor the propositional truth/falsity opposition in Group 1 (P3).
**Contrast type:** lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]
