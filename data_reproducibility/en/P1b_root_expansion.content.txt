# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P1b ROOT EXPANSION
# Language: English (en)

## Step 1 — Identify Central Roots

### Root 1: TRUE
**Form:** true (Old English trēowe)
**Participates in:** 3 P1 items directly (true, truthful, untrue [implied])
**Core meaning contribution:** Correspondence to reality; faithfulness; genuineness

### Root 2: FALSE
**Form:** false (Latin falsus)
**Participates in:** 2 P1 items directly (false, fallacious)
**Core meaning contribution:** Lack of correspondence to reality; deception; error

### Root 3: RIGHT
**Form:** right (Old English riht)
**Participates in:** 2 P1 items directly (right, wrong [as antonym])
**Core meaning contribution:** Correctness; moral propriety; straightness/alignment

**Note:** While "real," "valid," and "genuine" are frequent in P1, they function more as independent lexical items rather than productive roots generating multiple compounds in modern English. The three roots above show the greatest productivity in generating truth-evaluation expressions.

## Step 2 — Expand Each Root

### ROOT 1: TRUE

#### 1. untrue
**Form:** untrue
**Literal breakdown:** un- (negation) + true
**Translation:** not true, false
**Context:** Used to evaluate statements as not corresponding to reality. Common in everyday and formal discourse as a direct negation of truth claims.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 2. truth
**Form:** truth
**Literal breakdown:** true + -th (abstract noun suffix)
**Translation:** truth, the truth, veracity
**Context:** Nominal form referring to the quality of being true or to true statements/facts themselves. Fundamental term in philosophical, legal, religious, and everyday discourse.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 3. untruth
**Form:** untruth
**Literal breakdown:** un- (negation) + truth
**Translation:** falsehood, lie, untruth
**Context:** Nominal form for false statements or the quality of being false. Somewhat formal or literary; "lie" or "falsehood" more common in everyday speech.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 4. truly
**Form:** truly
**Literal breakdown:** true + -ly (adverbial suffix)
**Translation:** truly, really, genuinely
**Context:** Adverbial form used to emphasize truth or genuineness of statements or qualities. Common in everyday speech and writing across all registers.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 5. true-blue
**Form:** true-blue
**Literal breakdown:** true + blue (color)
**Translation:** loyal, faithful, steadfast
**Context:** Evaluates persons as completely loyal or faithful to a cause, party, or principle. Used in political and personal contexts. Historical reference to color fastness.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 6. true to form
**Form:** true to form

#### 7. true to life

#### 9. come true

### ROOT 2: FALSE

**Form:** falsehood
**Literal breakdown:** false + -hood (state/condition suffix)
**Translation:** lie, untruth, false statement

#### 3. falsify
**Form:** falsify
**Literal breakdown:** false + -ify (verb-forming suffix)
**Translation:** make false, forge, distort
**Context:** Verb meaning to alter something to make it false or to prove something false. Used in legal, scientific, and philosophical contexts.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 4. falsification
**Form:** falsification
**Literal breakdown:** falsify + -ation (noun suffix)
**Translation:** act of falsifying, forgery, distortion

**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 5. falsifiable
**Form:** falsifiable
**Literal breakdown:** falsify + -able (capable of being)

#### 6. falsifiability
**Form:** falsifiability

## Step 3 — Note Peripheral Extensions

### ROOT 1: TRUE

**Directional/spatial extensions:**
- "true north" — extends from correspondence-to-reality to precise spatial/directional reference
- "true" in carpentry/construction (e.g., "true up," "out of true") — extends to physical alignment and straightness

**Essential nature extensions:**
- "true self," "true colors," "true nature" — extends from factual truth to authentic essence or identity
- "true love," "true friend" — extends to genuine/authentic instances of categories

**Loyalty/faithfulness extensions:**
- "true-blue," "true to one's word," "true believer" — extends from correspondence to reality to constancy and loyalty
- "faithful" (related form) — bridges truth and loyalty domains

**Realization/actualization extensions:**
- "come true" — extends from static truth to dynamic realization of potential states

### ROOT 2: FALSE

**Artificiality/substitution extensions:**
- "false teeth," "false bottom" — extends from deceptive statements to artificial or substitute objects
- "false dawn" — extends to natural phenomena that mislead

**Premature/invalid action extensions:**
- "false start," "false move," "false step" — extends from incorrect statements to invalid or premature actions

**Technical/diagnostic extensions:**
- "false positive," "false negative" — extends to technical errors in detection/classification systems
- These maintain truth-evaluation function but in specialized measurement contexts

**Aesthetic/tonal extensions:**
- "false note" — extends from factual incorrectness to aesthetic inappropriateness or discord

### ROOT 3: RIGHT

**Directional/spatial extensions:**
- "right" as direction (opposite of left) — homophone/homograph with distinct etymology but potential semantic blending
- "right side" — can refer to correct side OR directional side

**Moral/justice extensions:**
- "righteous," "righteousness" — extends from correctness to moral virtue
- "wrongdoing," "wrongdoer" — extends from incorrectness to moral transgression
- "rightful" — bridges correctness and legitimate entitlement

**Degree/completeness extensions:**
- "right" as intensifier (e.g., "right away," "right here") — extends to immediacy and precision of location/time
- "all right" — extends from correctness to general acceptability

**Legal/entitlement extensions:**
- "rights" (noun, plural) — distinct but related sense referring to entitlements and legal claims
- "rightful owner/heir" — bridges correctness and legitimate possession

**Physical condition extensions:**
- "set right," "put right," "make right" — extends from abstract correctness to physical repair
- "right as rain" — extends to physical health and proper functioning

---

**General observation:** All three roots show extensions from epistemic evaluation (truth/correctness of statements) into:
1. Authenticity and essential nature (true self, genuine articles)
2. Moral evaluation (righteous, wrongdoing)
3. Physical properties (alignment, construction, health)
4. Social relations (loyalty, legitimacy, entitlement)
5. Technical/specialized domains (navigation, diagnostics, law)

The boundaries between truth-evaluation and these other domains are often fluid, with many expressions carrying multiple resonances simultaneously.
