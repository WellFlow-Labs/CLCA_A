# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P5 OPPOSITIONS
# Language: Quechua (qu)

## ATTESTED OPPOSITIONS

### OPPOSITION 1
**Term A:** chiqan (#1)
**Term B:** llulla (#3)
**Transliteration:** chiqan / llulla
**Translation:** true, real, genuine / false, lie, deceptive
**Description:** Primary lexical opposition between truth and falsehood. These terms contrast in all contexts: statements, objects, persons, and situations. Both are equally productive in derivations and constructions.
**Contrast type:** lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## SUMMARY OF OPPOSITION PATTERNS

### Distribution by Contrast Type:
- **Lexical oppositions:** 5 (chiqan/llulla, hunt'a/ch'ulla, hunt'a/ch'usaq, yachay/yuyay, and the primary truth/falsity pair)
- **Morphological oppositions:** 8 (primarily negation with mana, plus causative and reflexive-causative pairs)
- **Constructional oppositions:** 22 (parallel structures with contrasting quality terms)

### Key Observations:

1. **Asymmetry in opposition:** Not all terms have clear opposites. Notably, pantay "to err" and the inchoative/causative forms of allin lack attested opposites.

2. **Productivity of parallel constructions:** The chiqan/llulla opposition generates numerous parallel constructions across different domains (speech, character, documents, perception, etc.).

3. **Negation as primary strategy:** For allin and related terms, morphological negation with mana is the standard strategy for creating opposites rather than dedicated antonyms.

4. **Constructional symmetry:** When oppositions exist, they typically show parallel constructional patterns (same case marking, same verb, same derivational structure).

5. **No gaps in core oppositions:** All [CORE] oppositions show balanced, productive contrasts. [MARGINAL] status appears only for metaphorical locative constructions.
