## Step 1 — Central roots

### 1. **მართ-** — *mart-*
**Participates in:** 6 P1 items  
(**სიმართლე, მართალი, მართლმსაჯულება, მართებულობა, მართებული**, and historically/derivationally the same root family)

**Core meaning contribution:**  
Contributes the sense of rightness, truth, correctness, justice, and properness. In Georgian this root is central both for factual “being right/true” and for moral, legal, or procedural rightness.

---

### 2. **ჭეშმარიტ-** — *č’ešmarit’-*
**Participates in:** 2 P1 items  
(**ჭეშმარიტება, ჭეშმარიტი**)

**Core meaning contribution:**  
Contributes the sense of true in a fuller, stronger, often elevated sense: genuine truth, verity, doctrinal or metaphysical truth, authenticity of essence.

---

### 3. **ნამდვილ-** — *namdvil-*
**Participates in:** 3 P1 items  
(**ნამდვილი, ნამდვილად, ნამდვილობა**)

**Core meaning contribution:**  
Contributes the sense of realness, genuineness, authenticity, and actual rather than apparent status. It is especially common in everyday distinctions between real and fake, genuine and nominal.

---

## Step 2 — Root expansion

## Root 1: **მართ-** — *mart-*

This root is highly productive. Below are the most common and well-attested expressions; additional forms exist.

---

### 1. **სიმართლე**
**Transliteration:** *simartle*  
**Literal breakdown:** si- + *martl-* + -e (abstract noun formation)  
**Natural English translation:** truth; correctness; justice  
**Context of use:** Used for truth in statements, but also fairness and moral rightness. Common in everyday speech, public discourse, religion, and literature.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### 2. **მართალი**
**Transliteration:** *martali*  
**Literal breakdown:** root adjective  
**Natural English translation:** true; right; just  
**Context of use:** Used for a person who is right, a statement that is true, or someone morally in the right. Very common in conversation, argument, and ethical evaluation.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### 3. **მართლა**
**Transliteration:** *martla*  
**Literal breakdown:** from *მართალ-* > adverbial/discourse form  
**Natural English translation:** really; truly; indeed
