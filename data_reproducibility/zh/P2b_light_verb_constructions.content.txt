# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2b Compositional Constructions
# Language: Chinese (zh)

---

## PRELIMINARY NOTE ON CHINESE MULTI-WORD CONSTRUCTIONS

Chinese multi-word constructions relevant to the truth domain include:

1. **Chengyu (成语)**: Four-character idiomatic expressions with historical/literary origins
2. **Suyu (俗语)**: Common sayings and proverbs
3. **Guanyongyu (惯用语)**: Conventional expressions and set phrases
4. **Periphrastic constructions**: Multi-word analytical expressions
5. **Syntactic patterns**: Conventionalized sentence structures

This analysis excludes:
- Simple compounds already documented in P1/P1b
- Derivational forms documented in P2a
- Purely compositional phrases with no conventionalization

## CONSTRUCTION TYPE 1: CHENGYU (Four-Character Idioms)

### Based on 真 (zhēn)

**1. 千真万确 (qiān zhēn wàn què)**
- Base: 真 (zhēn) "true"
- Transliteration: qiān zhēn wàn què
- Literal: thousand-true-ten.thousand-certain
- Translation: absolutely true, completely certain, beyond any doubt
- Usage: Emphatic idiom affirming complete truthfulness or certainty of something. Very common in both spoken and written discourse when strongly asserting truth.
- [CORE]

**2. 去伪存真 (qù wěi cún zhēn)**
- Base: 真 (zhēn) "true"
- Transliteration: qù wěi cún zhēn
- Literal: remove-false-preserve-true
- Translation: eliminate the false and retain the true, separate truth from falsehood
- Usage: Describes the process of critical analysis to distinguish truth from falsehood. Common in analytical and investigative contexts.
- [CORE]

**3. 返璞归真 (fǎn pǔ guī zhēn)**
- Base: 真 (zhēn) "true"
- Transliteration: fǎn pǔ guī zhēn
- Literal: return-uncarved.jade-return-true
- Translation: return to simplicity and authenticity, return to one's true nature
- Usage: Philosophical/aesthetic idiom about returning to natural, authentic state. Common in discussions of lifestyle, art, and personal development.
- [CORE]

**4. 以假乱真 (yǐ jiǎ luàn zhēn)**
- Base: 真 (zhēn) "true", 假 (jiǎ) "false"
- Transliteration: yǐ jiǎ luàn zhēn
- Literal: use-false-confuse-true
- Translation: pass off the fake as genuine, confuse the false with the true
- Usage: Describes counterfeits or imitations so good they're mistaken for the real thing. Common in discussions of forgery, imitation, and deception.
- [CORE]

**5. 弄假成真 (nòng jiǎ chéng zhēn)**
- Base: 真 (zhēn) "true", 假 (jiǎ) "false"
- Transliteration: nòng jiǎ chéng zhēn
- Literal: make-false-become-true
- Translation: what starts as pretense becomes real, fake becomes genuine
- Usage: Describes situations where pretense or acting becomes reality, especially in romantic contexts. Common in narrative and relationship discussions.
- [CORE]

**6. 真凭实据 (zhēn píng shí jù)**
- Base: 真 (zhēn) "true", 实 (shí) "actual"
- Transliteration: zhēn píng shí jù
- Literal: true-evidence-actual-proof
- Translation: solid evidence, conclusive proof, hard facts
- Usage: Emphasizes concrete, irrefutable evidence. Common in legal, investigative, and argumentative contexts.
- [CORE]

**7. 真知灼见 (zhēn zhī zhuó jiàn)**
- Base: 真 (zhēn) "true"
- Transliteration: zhēn zhī zhuó jiàn
- Literal: true-knowledge-penetrating-insight
- Translation: true knowledge and penetrating insight, profound understanding
- Usage: Praises genuine understanding and deep insight. Common in intellectual and academic contexts.
- [CORE]

**8. 真心诚意 (zhēn xīn chéng yì)**
- Base: 真 (zhēn) "true", 诚 (chéng) "sincere"
- Transliteration: zhēn xīn chéng yì
- Literal: true-heart-sincere-intention
- Translation: wholehearted sincerity, genuine and sincere
- Usage: Emphasizes complete sincerity of intentions and feelings. Common in expressing or evaluating sincerity.
- [CORE]

**9. 真相大白 (zhēn xiàng dà bái)**
- Base: 真相 (zhēnxiàng) "truth"
- Transliteration: zhēn xiàng dà bái
- Literal: true-appearance-greatly-white/clear
- Translation: the truth comes to light, the facts are revealed
- Usage: Describes revelation of truth or facts. Common in investigative and narrative contexts.
- [CORE]

**10. 真伪莫辨 (zhēn wěi mò biàn)**

- Transliteration: zhēn wěi mò biàn
- Literal: true-false-cannot-distinguish

## CONSTRUCTION TYPE 2: COMMON SAYINGS AND PROVERBS (俗语)

### Based on 真 (zhēn)

**41. 真金不怕火炼 (zhēn jīn bù pà huǒ liàn)**
- Base: 真 (zhēn) "real"
- Transliteration: zhēn jīn bù pà huǒ liàn
- Literal: real-gold-not-fear-fire-refine
- Translation: real gold fears no fire, genuine quality withstands testing
- Usage: Proverb expressing confidence that genuine quality will be proven under testing. Very common in various contexts.
- [CORE]

**42. 是真的假不了，是假的真不了 (shì zhēn de jiǎ bù liǎo, shì jiǎ de zhēn bù liǎo)**
- Base: 真 (zhēn) "true", 假 (jiǎ) "false"
- Transliteration: shì zhēn de jiǎ bù liǎo, shì jiǎ de zhēn bù liǎo
- Literal: be-true-[particle]-fake-not-can, be-fake-[particle]-true-not-can
- Translation: what's true can't be made false, what's false can't be made true; truth will out
- Usage: Proverb asserting that truth and falsehood will ultimately be revealed. Common in reassuring contexts.
- [CORE]

**43. 真人不露相 (zhēn rén bù lòu xiàng)**
- Base: 真 (zhēn) "true/real"
- Transliteration: zhēn rén bù lòu xiàng
- Literal: true-person-not-reveal-appearance
- Translation: truly capable people don't show off, real experts are modest
- Usage: Proverb praising modesty and criticizing ostentation. Common in character evaluation.
- [CORE]

### Based on 实 (shí)

**44. 耳听为虚，眼见为实 (ěr tīng wéi xū, yǎn jiàn wéi shí)**
- Base: 虚 (xū) "empty/false", 实 (shí) "actual"
- Transliteration: ěr tīng wéi xū, yǎn jiàn wéi shí
- Literal: ear-hear-be-empty, eye-see-be-actual
- Translation: hearing is uncertain, seeing is believing; don't believe everything you hear

**45. 实践出真知 (shí jiàn chū zhēn zhī)**

- Transliteration: shí jiàn chū zhēn zhī
- Literal: practice-produce-true-knowledge
- Translation: practice produces true knowledge, knowledge comes from practice
- Usage: Slogan emphasizing learning through practice. Common in educational and political contexts.
- [CORE]

**46. 实践是检验真理的唯一标准 (shí jiàn shì jiǎn yàn zhēn lǐ de wéi yī biāo zhǔn)**
- Base: 实践 (shíjiàn) "practice", 真理 (zhēnlǐ) "truth"
- Transliteration: shí jiàn shì jiǎn yàn zhēn lǐ de wéi yī biāo zhǔn
- Literal: practice-be-test-verify-truth-[possessive]-only-one-standard
- Translation: practice is the sole criterion for testing truth
- Usage: Important political slogan from 1978 reform era. Common in political and philosophical discourse.
- [CORE]

### Based on 假 (jiǎ)

**47. 假的真不了，真的假不了 (jiǎ de zhēn bù liǎo, zhēn de jiǎ bù liǎo)**
- Base: 假 (jiǎ) "false", 真 (zhēn) "true"

## GAPS AND ABSENCES

### Constructions NOT commonly found:

1. **Degree modifiers with 虚 (xū)**: While 虚假 exists, there are no common multi-word constructions specifically intensifying or modifying 虚 beyond those already documented in P1/P1b.

2. **Periphrastic constructions with 似 (sì) and 像 (xiàng)**: These function primarily in comparative syntax (X 像/似 Y) rather than forming conventionalized multi-word expressions in the truth domain.

3. **Idiomatic expressions specifically with 准 (zhǔn)**: While 准确 forms many constructions, 准 alone doesn't appear in conventionalized multi-word truth-domain expressions beyond those already documented.

4. **Specialized constructions with 本质 (běnzhì)**: While 本质 appears in analytical discourse, it doesn't form distinctive conventionalized multi-word constructions beyond compositional phrases.

5. **Fixed expressions with 谎言 (huǎngyán)**: While 谎言 is common, it appears in compositional phrases (说谎言 "tell lies") rather than conventionalized multi-word constructions.
