# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P5 Oppositions
# Language: Basque (eu)

---

## Preliminary Note on Method

Oppositions are extracted only where the CONTEXT explicitly documents a contrast, names one term as the antonym of another, or shows two forms appearing in paired or disjunctive constructions. Where the CONTEXT notes an asymmetry (e.g., one side of a pair is absent or expressed differently), this is recorded as part of the description rather than suppressed. No oppositions have been inferred beyond what the data supports.

## Opposition 1

**Term A:** *egia*
**Term B:** *gezurra*
**Transliteration:** egia / gezurra
**English translation:** truth / lie, falsehood
**Description of contrast:** *Egia* is described in P1 as the primary nominal for truth; *gezurra* is described as its primary antonym. The pair appears explicitly in disjunctive constructions (*egia ala gezurra?*, C1.3; *egia da ala gezurra?*, C1.4) and in collocations that presuppose their opposition (*gezurra eta egia nahastu*, C4.4; *gezurra eta egia bereiztea*, C4.8).
**Contrast type:** Lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 2

**Term A:** *egiazkoa*
**Term B:** *gezurrezkoa*
**Transliteration:** egiazkoa / gezurrezkoa
**English translation:** true, truthful, genuine / false, lying, based on lies
**Description of contrast:** P2a (D4.2) explicitly states that *gezurrezkoa* contrasts directly with *egiazkoa*. Both are adjectival forms derived from their respective nominal roots via the *-zko* suffix and appear in the same attributive and predicative positions (e.g., *egiazko lekukotasuna* vs. *gezurrezko lekukotasuna*, P2a D4.1).
**Contrast type:** Morphological (parallel *-zko* derivation from opposing roots)
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 3

**Term A:** *egiazko* (attributive)
**Term B:** *gezurrezko* (attributive)
**Transliteration:** egiazko / gezurrezko
**English translation:** true, genuine (attributive) / false, lying (attributive)
**Description of contrast:** The indefinite attributive forms of Opposition 2, used before nouns in the same syntactic position. P2a (D4.1) documents *gezurrezko* as the attributive counterpart to *egiazko* in pre-nominal position, with the example *gezurrezko lekukotasuna* (false testimony) contrasting with *egiazko lekukotasuna* (true testimony, P1b).
**Contrast type:** Morphological (parallel attributive forms)
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 4

**Term A:** *baliozkoa*
**Term B:** *baliogabea*

**English translation:** valid, legitimate / invalid, null, void
**Description of contrast:** P1 (items 13 and 14) explicitly identifies *baliogabea* as the direct antonym of *baliozkoa*. *Baliozkoa* is formed with the adjectival suffix *-zko*; *baliogabea* is formed with the privative suffix *-gabe*, both on the root *balio*. The pair appears in legal and administrative contexts.
**Contrast type:** Morphological (*-zko* vs. *-gabe* on the same root)

## Opposition 7

**Term A:** *frogagarria*
**Term B:** *frogaezina*
**Transliteration:** frogagarria / frogaezina
**English translation:** provable, demonstrable / unprovable, impossible to prove
**Description of contrast:** P2a (D28.4 and D28.5) documents both forms from the *froga-* root. *Frogagarria* uses the *-garri* potential suffix; *frogaezina* uses the *ezin* impossibility marker. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 12

**Term A:** *benetan*
**Term B:** *itxuraz*
**Transliteration:** benetan / itxuraz
**English translation:** truly, really, genuinely / apparently, seemingly, ostensibly
**Description of contrast:** P2b (C3.2) documents the construction *benetan ala itxuraz?* (really or just apparently?) as a binary interrogative that directly contrasts the two adverbials. **Term B:** *benetan* (implicit)

## Opposition 15

**Term A:** *egia esan* (to tell the truth)
**Term B:** *gezur esan* (to tell a lie)
**Transliteration:** egia esan / gezur esan
**English translation:** to tell the truth / to tell a lie
**Description of contrast:** Both are verbal collocations with the same verb *esan* (to say) and contrasting nominal objects (*egia* vs. *gezur*). P2a (D4.7) documents *gezur esan* as the standard verbal collocation for lying; P1b (C1.1 and related) documents *egia esan* as the standard collocation for truth-telling. The parallel structure makes the contrast constructional as well as lexical.
**Contrast type:** Constructional (parallel [nominal + *esan*] collocations with opposing nominals)
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 18

**Term A:** *gezurti* / *gezurtia*
**Term B:** (no single-word form attested)
**Transliteration:** gezurtia / —
**English translation:** liar / (truth-teller: no single-word form)
**Description of contrast:** P4a (§7) and P4b (§7) document an asymmetry: *gezurti* (liar) is a fully lexicalized agentive nominal formed with the *-ti* suffix, but no parallel *-ti* agentive for the truth-telling agent is attested. The truth-telling agent is expressed periphrastically (*egia esaten duena*, the one who tells the truth) or through character adjectives (*zintzoa*, *jatorra*). The opposition is asymmetric: one side is lexicalized, the other is not.
**Contrast type:** Lexical (asymmetric: one term lexicalized, the other expressed periphrastically)
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 20

**Term A:** *egiaztagarria* (verifiable)
**Term B:** *egiaztaezina* (unverifiable)
**Term C:** *egiaztatu gabe* (not yet verified / left unverified)

**Note:** These three forms from the *egiazta-* stem represent distinct contrasts that are not fully reducible to a single binary. *Egiaztagarria* vs. *egiaztaezina* is a contrast of possibility (can be verified / cannot be verified). *Egiaztatu gabe* expresses that verification has not occurred, which is distinct from impossibility. P4a (§9) notes that *-gabe* expresses absence while *ezin* expresses impossibility, and these are not interchangeable. The three-way structure is documented here rather than collapsed.

**Contrast type:** Morphological (three-way: *-garri* / *ezin* / *-gabe*)
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 22

**Term A:** *egia osoa*
**Term B:** *egia erdiak*
**Transliteration:** egia osoa / egia erdiak
**English translation:** the whole truth / half-truths
**Description of contrast:** P1b documents both collocations. *Egia osoa* (the whole truth) asserts complete truthfulness; *egia erdiak* (half-truths) describes statements that are technically true but misleading through omission. P3 notes that neither fits a single group cleanly and that *egia erdiak* is distinct from outright lying (*gezurra*). The contrast is between complete and partial truth rather than between truth and falsehood.
**Contrast type:** Constructional (opposing nominal collocations with *egia* + quantifying modifier)
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 28

**Term A:** *-tze* verbal noun (process)
**Term B:** *-pen* result nominal (outcome)
**Transliteration:** e.g., *egiaztatzea* / *egiaztapen*; *frogatzea* / *frogapen*
**English translation:** verifying (as process) / verification (as result); proving (as process) / proof (as result)
**Description of contrast:** P4a (§8) and P4b (§8) document that *-tze* nominals denote the ongoing process or act, while *-pen* nominals denote the result or outcome. Both are derived from the same verbal stems and are not interchangeable. The contrast is aspectual (process vs. result) rather than evaluative.
**Contrast type:** Morphological (*-tze* vs. *-pen* on the same verbal stem)
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

**Term B:** *ezin* construction (impossibility)

**Contrast type:** Morphological (*-gabe* vs. *ezin* as competing privative strategies with distinct meanings)
