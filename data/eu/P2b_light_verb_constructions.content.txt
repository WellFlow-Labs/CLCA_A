## GAPS AND ABSENCES NOTED

### No common constructions identified for:

1. **Causative constructions with egia:** No attested construction like *"egia eragin" (to cause truth) or *"egia sortu" (to create truth). Truth is found, told, or revealed, but not caused or created in standard usage.

2. **Benefactive constructions:** No common construction like *"egiaren mesedetan" (for the benefit of truth). While "egiaren alde" (on the side of truth) exists, benefactive marking is not attested.

3. **Comitative constructions:** No common construction like *"egiarekin" (with truth) as a fixed expression. The instrumental "egiaz" exists but comitative is not attested as a fixed construction.

4. **Temporal constructions:** No common construction like *"egiaren garaian" (in the time of truth). Temporal marking of truth is not a productive pattern.

5. **Locative constructions with gezur:** While "gezurretan" (in lies) exists with specific verbs, no general locative construction like *"gezurrean" is attested as a productive pattern.

6. **Manner constructions with zuzen:** While "zuzenean" (directly) and "zuzenki" (correctly) exist as derivations, no productive multi-word manner construction like *"zuzen moduan" (in a correct manner) is commonly attested.

7. **Purpose constructions:** No common construction like *"egiaren asmoz" (with the purpose of truth) or *"egiaren helburuarekin" (with the goal of truth). Purpose is not productively marked with these roots.

8. **Degree constructions:** While intensifiers like "oso" (very) can modify these terms, no fixed degree constructions like *"egia neurri batean" (truth to some degree) are attested as standard expressions.

## SUMMARY

This analysis documents **over 100 attested multi-word constructions** involving truth-related lexical items in Basque. The most productive construction types are:

1. **Light verb constructions** (especially with egia and gezurra)
2. **Case-marked prepositional phrases** (especially genitive and locative)
3. **Fixed collocations** (especially with egiazko and gezurrezko)
4. **Comparative constructions** (binary oppositions)
5. **Discourse markers and emphatics**

Notable patterns include:
- High productivity of egia in multiple construction types
- Asymmetry between egia (many constructions) and gezur (fewer constructions)
- Specialized constructions for zuzeneko in media/technical domains
- Rich system of case-marked phrases for epistemic and moral evaluation
- Extensive fixed collocations distinguishing genuine from false instances

The analysis preserves attested asymmetries and explicitly notes gaps where expected constructions are not found in standard usage.
