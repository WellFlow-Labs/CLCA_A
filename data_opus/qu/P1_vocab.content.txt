# CLCA P1 — CORE VOCABULARY COLLECTION
## Language: Quechua (qu)

Note on orthography: I use the standardized trivocalic (3-vowel) Southern Quechua orthography (a, i, u) as adopted in Peru/Bolivia academic convention, applied consistently throughout. Items reflect mainly Southern Quechua (Cusco-Collao / Ayacucho) usage.

---

**1. Form:** chiqaq
**Transliteration:** chiqaq
**Literal/morphology:** root *chiqa-* "true, straight" + agentive/attributive
**English:** true, real, correct, genuine
**Usage:** Used to affirm that a statement corresponds to fact or that something is authentic. Common in everyday assertions and in contrast to falsehood. Participates in derivation (chiqaq-chay "to verify/straighten out").
**Status:** [CORE]

**2. Form:** chiqap
**Transliteration:** chiqap
**Literal/morphology:** variant of *chiqaq*
**English:** true, truly, real
**Usage:** Dialectal/variant form of chiqaq, used adverbially and adjectivally to mark truth or reality. Common in Cusco-area speech. Often heard as "chiqaptachu?" ("is it true?").
**Status:** [CORE]

**3. Form:** chiqap kay
**Transliteration:** chiqap kay
**Literal/morphology:** *chiqap* "true" + *kay* "to be / being"
**English:** truth, the truth, reality
**Usage:** A nominalized fixed expression referring to truth or what is real. Used in moral and discursive contexts to invoke veracity.
**Status:** [CORE]

**4. Form:** cheqaq
**Transliteration:** cheqaq
**Literal/morphology:** 5-vowel orthographic variant of *chiqaq*
**English:** true, correct
**Usage:** Same meaning as chiqaq; included as an attested spelling but noted as orthographic overlap. Listed for completeness given common written usage.
**Status:** [MARGINAL]

**5. Form:** sut'i
**Transliteration:** sut'i
**Literal/morphology:** root meaning "clear, evident"
**English:** clear, evident, manifest, plain
**Usage:** Describes that which is evident or self-evidently the case, often in the sense that truth is plainly visible. Used both perceptually (clear light) and epistemically (a clear fact). Derives sut'ichay "to clarify/explain."
**Status:** [CORE]

**6. Form:** sut'inchay
**Transliteration:** sut'inchay
**Literal/morphology:** *sut'i* "clear" + *-n-* + *-chay* causative
**English:** to clarify, to explain, to make evident
**Usage:** Used when making something clear or demonstrating its truth. Common in pedagogical, legal, and explanatory contexts.
**Status:** [CORE]

**7. Form:** cheqachay / chiqachay
**Transliteration:** chiqachay
**Literal/morphology:** *chiqa* "true/straight" + *-chay* causative
**English:** to correct, to straighten, to verify, to set right
**Usage:** Used for correcting errors or setting something straight, both physically and morally/factually. Appears in contexts of justice and accuracy.
**Status:** [CORE]

**8. Form:** llulla
**Transliteration:** llulla
**Literal/morphology:** root "lie, false"
**English:** lie, liar, false
**Usage:** A central term for falsehood and deceit; can name the lie, the act, or the liar. Strongly evaluative and morally charged. Derives llullakuy "to lie," llullasapa "habitual liar."
**Status:** [CORE]

**9. Form:** llullakuy
**Transliteration:** llullakuy
**Literal/morphology:** *llulla* + *-ku-* reflexive + *-y* infinitive
**English:** to lie, to deceive
**Usage:** The verb for telling untruths or deceiving. Used in moral evaluation of conduct and reliability of speakers.
**Status:** [CORE]

**10. Form:** pantay
**Transliteration:** pantay
**Literal/morphology:** root "to err"
**English:** to err, to make a mistake, to be wrong
**Usage:** Indicates mistaken belief or action, distinct from intentional lying. Common in evaluating accuracy of statements or conduct. Derives pantasqa "mistaken, erroneous."
**Status:** [CORE]

**11. Form:** allin
**Transliteration:** allin
**Literal/morphology:** root "good"
**English:** good, well, right, proper, correct
**Usage:** Broad evaluative term for goodness, rightness, and correctness across moral, practical, and qualitative domains. Highly productive (allinchay "to improve/repair," allillanmi "fine/well"). Used to judge conduct and quality.
**Status:** [CORE]

**12. Form:** allinchay
**Transliteration:** allinchay
**Literal/morphology:** *allin* "good" + *-chay* causative
**English:** to improve, to repair, to make right, to fix
**Usage:** Used for setting things right or improving them, including correcting wrongs. Appears in moral and practical evaluation.
**Status:** [CORE]

**13. Form:** cheqan / chiqan
**Transliteration:** chiqan
**Literal/morphology:** *chiqa* + *-n*; "straight, right, direct"
**English:** straight, right, correct, just
**Usage:** Denotes straightness/rightness, extended to justice and correctness of direction or conduct. Used in moral-legal contexts (a just/right path).
**Status:** [CORE]

**14. Form:** cheqaqta / chiqaqta
**Transliteration:** chiqaqta
**Literal/morphology:** *chiqaq* + *-ta* adverbial/accusative
**English:** truly, really, rightly
**Usage:** Adverbial form affirming that something is done or is the case in truth. Frequent in emphatic assertions.
**Status:** [CORE]

**15. Form:** kikin
**Transliteration:** kikin
**Literal/morphology:** root "self, same"
**English:** same, identical, the very same, itself
**Usage:** Marks identity and sameness, including the genuine/self-same nature of a thing ("the very one"). Used in contexts of resemblance and authenticity. Derives kikinchay "to compare/equate/identify."
**Status:** [CORE]

**16. Form:** kikinchay
**Transliteration:** kikinchay
**Literal/morphology:** *kikin* "same" + *-chay* causative
**English:** to compare, to identify, to equate, to liken
**Usage:** Used when establishing sameness or correspondence between things. Relevant to resemblance and identification.
**Status:** [MARGINAL]

**17. Form:** rikch'akuy
**Transliteration:** rikch'akuy
**Literal/morphology:** *rikch'a-* "appearance/likeness" + *-ku-* reflexive + *-y*
**English:** to resemble, to be similar, to look like
**Usage:** Used to express that something resembles or appears like another. Central to judgments of likeness and appearance.
**Status:** [CORE]

**18. Form:** rikch'ay
**Transliteration:** rikch'ay
**Literal/morphology:** root "appearance, aspect, likeness"
**English:** appearance, likeness, image, form
**Usage:** Denotes the look or semblance of something, the basis for comparison and resemblance. Also relates to how something appears versus what it is.
**Status:** [CORE]

**19. Form:** rikch'akuq
**Transliteration:** rikch'akuq
**Literal/morphology:** *rikch'akuy* + agentive *-q*
**English:** similar, resembling, alike
**Usage:** Attributive form describing things that resemble one another. Used in comparisons.
**Status:** [MARGINAL]

**20. Form:** cheqaqchu / chiqaqchu
**Transliteration:** chiqaqchu
**Literal/morphology:** *chiqaq* "true" + *-chu* interrogative
**English:** is it true?, really?
**Usage:** A conventionalized interrogative expression questioning the truth of a claim. Very common in conversation as a request for confirmation.
**Status:** [CORE]

**21. Form:** chaniyuq
**Transliteration:** chaniyuq
**Literal/morphology:** *chani* "price, value, worth" + *-yuq* possessive
**English:** valuable, worthy, of worth
**Usage:** Describes things or persons having worth or value, used in evaluative (aesthetic, moral, economic) judgments. Derives from chani "value/price."
**Status:** [CORE]

**22. Form:** chani
**Transliteration:** chani
**Literal/morphology:** root "price, value, worth"
**English:** price, value, worth
**Usage:** Names the worth or value of something, basis for evaluative judgments. Used in economic and moral-aesthetic contexts.
**Status:** [CORE]

**23. Form:** mana llullaq
**Transliteration:** mana llullaq
**Literal/morphology:** *mana* "not" + *llulla* + agentive *-q*
**English:** truthful, one who does not lie, honest
**Usage:** A fixed negated expression denoting a truthful, reliable person. Used in moral evaluation of character and reliability.
**Status:** [MARGINAL]

**24. Form:** iñiy
**Transliteration:** iñiy
**Literal/morphology:** root "to believe"
**English:** to believe, faith, belief
**Usage:** Used for believing or trusting a statement or person, and (in Christian contexts) religious faith. Relates to reliability and acceptance of truth. Derives iñiq "believer."
**Status:** [CORE]

**25. Form:** sunqu chiqaq / chiqaq sunqu
**Transliteration:** chiqaq sunqu
**Literal/morphology:** *chiqaq* "true" + *sunqu* "heart"
**English:** sincere, true-hearted, honest
**Usage:** A fixed expression for sincerity and uprightness of character. Used in moral evaluation of persons and conduct.
**Status:** [MARGINAL]

**26. Form:** hina
**Transliteration:** hina
**Literal/morphology:** comparative particle "like, as"
**English:** like, as, similar to, thus
**Usage:** Functional element marking likeness/resemblance and manner, e.g. "X hina" = "like X." Highly productive in comparisons (kikin hina "just the same"). Borderline grammatical vs. lexical.
**Status:** [CORE]

**27. Form:** hinalla / hinallataq
**Transliteration:** hinallataq
**Literal/morphology:** *hina* "thus/like" + *-lla* + *-taq*
**English:** likewise, the same way, similarly
**Usage:** Conventionalized expression for sameness of manner or correspondence. Used in comparisons and affirmations of equivalence.
**Status:** [MARGINAL]

**28. Form:** mana allin
**Transliteration:** mana allin
**Literal/morphology:** *mana* "not" + *allin* "good"
**English:** bad, wrong, improper, not good
**Usage:** Fixed negated expression for badness or wrongness, in moral and qualitative evaluation. Very common in everyday judgment.
**Status:** [CORE]

**29. Form:** pantasqa
**Transliteration:** pantasqa
**Literal/morphology:** *panta-* "err" + *-sqa* participle
**English:** mistaken, wrong, erroneous
**Usage:** Participial form describing a mistaken statement, answer, or belief. Used in evaluating accuracy.
**Status:** [CORE]

**30. Form:** -puni
**Transliteration:** -puni
**Literal/morphology:** suffix "certainly, definitely, for sure"
**English:** certainly, definitely, surely, indeed
**Usage:** An enclitic marking certainty/definiteness of a claim (chiqaqpuni "definitely true"). Highly productive; relates to reliability and assertion strength. Borderline grammatical (suffix) rather than lexical, hence flagged.
**Status:** [UNCERTAIN]

---

### Notes on uncertainty and limitations
- Items **4** and **2/13** reflect orthographic and dialectal variation (5-vowel vs. 3-vowel; Cusco vs. Ayacucho); I have flagged the variant spelling forms accordingly.
- The evidential suffix system (-mi/-si/-chá) is highly relevant to evaluation of truth/reliability but consists of grammatical enclitics, not lexical items, so I excluded them per the inclusion criteria; **-puni** is included only as a borderline [UNCERTAIN] case.
- Fixed expressions (**3, 23, 25, 28**) are attested but show variable word order and composition; status reflects this.
- Meanings span dialects (Cusco-Collao, Ayacucho); a given form may be more [CORE] in one variety than another.
