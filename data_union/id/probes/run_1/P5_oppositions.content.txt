# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P5 OPPOSITIONS
# Language: Indonesian (id)

---

## Preliminary Note on Method

Oppositions are extracted only where the CONTEXT explicitly documents a contrast, names both terms as contrasting, or provides collocational evidence (e.g., *A atau B*, *A tetapi B*, *A maupun B*) that the two items function as poles of a contrast in attested use. Where the CONTEXT notes a relationship without providing sufficient evidence of contrastive use, this is stated. Oppositions are listed individually; no grouping or categorization is imposed.

## Opposition 1

**Term A:** benar
**Term B:** salah
**Transliteration:** N/A
**English Translation:** true / correct — wrong / incorrect
**Description of Contrast:** Documented in P1 as the primary antonym pair in the truth-evaluation domain; *salah* is explicitly described as "the primary antonym of *benar*." The pair appears in the fixed binary construction *benar atau salah* ("right or wrong, true or false"), attested in P4a and P2b as a standard logical, moral, and legal framing.
**Contrast Type:** lexical
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]
