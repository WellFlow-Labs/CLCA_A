# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P4b Construction Usage & Constraints
**Language: Indonesian (id)**

---

## 1. ATTRIBUTION

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Subject + truth-adjective (no copula) | *Informasi itu benar.* | Informasi itu benar. | "That information is correct." | [CORE] | Neutral declarative register; no contextual restriction. Standard across spoken and written Indonesian. |
| Subject + truth-adjective (no copula) | *Dokumen ini palsu.* | Dokumen ini palsu. | "This document is fake." | [CORE] | Equally unrestricted; attested in legal, journalistic, and everyday registers. |

**Attested alternates:** Negated forms *Informasi itu tidak benar*; categorical denial *Ini bukan yang asli*. See Construction 2.

---

## 2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Subject + *tidak* + adjective | *Berita itu tidak akurat.* | Berita itu tidak akurat. | "That news is not accurate." | [CORE] | Standard negation of adjectival predicates; unrestricted across registers. |
| Subject + *bukan* + NP | *Ini bukan asli.* | Ini bukan asli. | "This is not genuine." | [CORE] | Used for categorical denial of identity or category membership; common in legal and everyday contexts. |
| Softened negation: *kurang* + adjective | — | *kurang tepat* / *kurang benar* | "not quite right" / "not entirely correct" | [CORE] | Pragmatically softened; frequent in polite, face-saving, and editorial contexts. Implies partial rather than total negation. |

**Attested alternates:** *kurang tepat*, *kurang benar* as softened variants of *tidak tepat*, *tidak benar*.

---

## 3. ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Noun + *yang* + adjective | *Informasi yang benar dan akurat.* | Informasi yang benar dan akurat. | "Information that is correct and accurate." | [CORE] | Productive default for postnominal modification; unrestricted across registers and item types. |
| Adjective + noun (direct juxtaposition, no *yang*) | *Hadis sahih.* | Hadis sahih. | "A sound hadith." | [MARGINAL] | Restricted to fixed collocations and technical terms (*hadis sahih*, *emas murni*, *uang palsu*); not the productive default. Not freely generalized to new combinations. |

**Attested alternates:** *yang* construction is the productive default; direct juxtaposition is lexically restricted.

## 4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| *menjadi* + NP/adjective | *Kebohongan itu akhirnya menjadi kenyataan yang diketahui publik.* | Kebohongan itu akhirnya menjadi kenyataan yang diketahui publik. | "That lie eventually became a reality known to the public." | [CORE] | General inchoative; unrestricted. *Menjadi kenyataan* is a fixed collocation within this pattern. |
| *terungkap* as inchoative | *Kebenaran akan terungkap pada akhirnya.* | Kebenaran akan terungkap pada akhirnya. | "The truth will come to light in the end." | [CORE] | Conventionalized for truth-coming-to-light contexts; frequent in journalistic and narrative registers. Subject is typically *kebenaran* or a concealed fact. |
| *terbukti* as inchoative | — | — | "to be proven" | [CORE] | Closely related to *terungkap*; used when proof or evidence is foregrounded rather than mere revelation. Frequent in legal and journalistic registers. |

**Attested alternates:** *menjadi kenyataan* (fixed collocation); *terungkap* and *terbukti* are distinct conventionalized forms within the same semantic space.

## 5. CAUSATIVE

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Active causative: *me(N)-* + base + *-kan* | *Pernyataan itu mengelirukan publik.* | Pernyataan itu mengelirukan publik. | "That statement misled the public." | [CORE] | Productive across the truth-domain inventory; unrestricted in register. Attested: *membenarkan*, *mengelirukan*, *memalsukan*, *mengesahkan*, *memurnikan*, *mengikhlaskan*. |
| Passive causative: *di-* + base + *-kan* | *Dokumen itu telah dipalsukan.* | Dokumen itu telah dipalsukan. | "That document has been forged." | [CORE] | Equally productive; particularly frequent in formal, legal, and journalistic registers where the agent is backgrounded or unknown. |

**Attested alternates:** Active and passive forms are both fully productive and co-attested; choice is governed by discourse-pragmatic factors (agent prominence, register).

## 6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| *ke-* + base + *-an* | *Kejujuran adalah nilai yang dijunjung tinggi.* | Kejujuran adalah nilai yang dijunjung tinggi. | "Honesty is a highly valued principle." | [CORE] | Primary abstract nominalizer; productive across the inventory. Attested: *kebenaran*, *kesalahan*, *keaslian*, *kejujuran*, *kebohongan*, *keabsahan*, *kemurnian*, *ketulusan*, *keikhlasan*, *kenyataan*, *ketepatan*, *keakuratan*. |
| *ke-* + base + *-an* | *Keabsahan pemilu itu dipertanyakan.* | Keabsahan pemilu itu dipertanyakan. | "The legitimacy of that election was called into question." | [CORE] | Frequent in legal, political, and journalistic registers; no restriction on subject type. |
| Loanword substitution: *validitas* | — | *validitas* | "validity" | [MARGINAL] | Used in technical/academic registers in place of *\*kevalidan*; reflects incomplete morphological integration of Dutch/Latin loanwords. *\*Keotentikan* is noted as marginal. |

**Attested alternates:** *validitas* (technical register) as a non-integrated loanword alternative where *ke-...-an* derivation is blocked or marginal.

## 7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| *pe(N)-* + base (negative-role agent) | *Dia dikenal sebagai pembohong ulung.* | Dia dikenal sebagai pembohong ulung. | "He is known as a skilled liar." | [CORE] | Well established for negative agentive roles (*pembohong*, *pendusta*, *pemalsu*, *peniru*); unrestricted in register. |
| *pe(N)-* + base (negative-role agent) | *Pemalsu dokumen itu akhirnya ditangkap.* | Pemalsu dokumen itu akhirnya ditangkap. | "The document forger was finally arrested." | [CORE] | Frequent in legal and journalistic registers; *pemalsu* is fully lexicalized. |
| Periphrastic positive-role agent: *orang yang* + adjective | — | *orang yang jujur* | "an honest person" | [CORE] | Used where *pe(N)-* derivation is absent or marginal for positive roles; *\*penjujur* is unattested. Asymmetry is noted: agentive nominals are established for negative roles but not positive ones in this domain. |

**Attested alternates:** *orang yang jujur* as periphrastic substitute for the unattested *\*penjujur*; *pendusta* as a formal/religious register variant of *pembohong*.

## 8. PROCESS NOMINALIZATION

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| *pe(N)-* + base + *-an* | *Pemalsuan dokumen adalah tindak pidana.* | Pemalsuan dokumen adalah tindak pidana. | "Document forgery is a criminal act." | [CORE] | Productive process/event nominalizer; frequent in legal and journalistic registers. Attested: *pemalsuan*, *pengesahan*, *pembenaran*, *pemurnian*, *peniruan*, *penyimpangan*. |
| *pe(N)-* + base + *-an* | *Pengesahan undang-undang itu berlangsung kemarin.* | Pengesahan undang-undang itu berlangsung kemarin. | "The ratification of that law took place yesterday." | [CORE] | Particularly frequent in formal, legal, and administrative registers; refers to the act or event rather than the abstract quality. |

**Attested alternates:** Distinct from *ke-...-an* abstracts (Construction 6); the two circumfixes are not interchangeable — *pemalsuan* (act of forging) vs. *\*kemalsuan* (unattested); *kebenaran* (truth as quality) vs. *pembenaran* (act of justifying/confirming).

## 9. DISCOURSE-MARKER CONSTRUCTIONS (CORRECTION / DISCLOSURE)

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| *Sebenarnya* + clause | *Sebenarnya, saya tidak setuju dengan keputusan itu.* | Sebenarnya, saya tidak setuju dengan keputusan itu. | "Actually, I don't agree with that decision." | [CORE] | High-frequency in everyday spoken and written Indonesian; sentence-initial pragmatic operator for correction and disclosure. No register restriction. |
| *Pada hakikatnya* + clause | *Pada hakikatnya, masalah ini jauh lebih kompleks.* | Pada hakikatnya, masalah ini jauh lebih kompleks. | "In essence, this problem is far more complex." | [MARGINAL] | Restricted to formal, philosophical, and academic registers; introduces statements about essential rather than surface reality. Less frequent than *sebenarnya* in everyday use. |
| *Sebenar-benarnya* (emphatic variant) | — | *sebenar-benarnya* | "truly, in the fullest sense" | [MARGINAL] | Restricted to formal declarations and sworn testimony; not used in casual speech. Emphatic and register-marked. |

**Attested alternates:** *Sebenarnya* and *pada hakikatnya* occupy overlapping but register-differentiated positions; *sebenar-benarnya* is a further emphatic variant restricted to formal contexts.

## 10. PARTIAL CONCESSION CONSTRUCTION

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| *Ada benarnya* (+ *juga*) | *Ada benarnya juga pendapat itu.* | Ada benarnya juga pendapat itu. | "There is something to that view too." | [CORE] | Fixed partial-concession construction; frequent in conversational and editorial discourse. *Juga* co-occurs frequently as a further softener. No productive generalization to other adjectives is noted. |
| *Memang benar* + clause | *Memang benar bahwa situasinya sulit.* | Memang benar bahwa situasinya sulit. | "It is indeed true that the situation is difficult." | [CORE] | Confirmatory/concessive construction; frequent in argumentative, editorial, and conversational contexts. *Memang* adds a concessive or evidential nuance ("as is indeed the case"). |

**Attested alternates:** No single-word equivalents noted for either construction. *Ada benarnya* and *memang benar* are functionally distinct: the former acknowledges partial validity; the latter concedes or affirms a full proposition.

## 11. INTENSIFIED ASSERTION

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| *Benar-benar* + predicate | *Dia benar-benar tidak tahu tentang hal itu.* | Dia benar-benar tidak tahu tentang hal itu. | "He truly did not know about that." | [CORE] | Productive intensifier; unrestricted across registers. Intensifies degree or reality of the predicate. |
| *Nyata-nyata* + predicate | *Tindakan itu nyata-nyata melanggar hukum.* | Tindakan itu nyata-nyata melanggar hukum. | "That action clearly and manifestly violated the law." | [CORE] | Intensifies evidential certainty ("manifestly, clearly"); frequent in journalistic and legal registers. Carries a stronger evidential implication than *benar-benar*. |
| *Sungguh-sungguh* + predicate | — | *sungguh-sungguh* | "earnestly, sincerely" | [CORE] | Intensifies sincerity and earnestness rather than factual certainty; frequent in interpersonal and motivational contexts. Functionally distinct from *benar-benar* and *nyata-nyata*. |
| *Sebenar-benarnya* (emphatic formal) | — | *sebenar-benarnya* | "in the truest sense" | [MARGINAL] | Related emphatic form; restricted to formal declarations. See also Construction 9. |

**Attested alternates:** *Benar-benar*, *sungguh-sungguh*, and *nyata-nyata* are co-attested but functionally differentiated: degree/reality (*benar-benar*), sincerity/earnestness (*sungguh-sungguh*), evidential manifestness (*nyata-nyata*). *Sebenar-benarnya* is a register-restricted emphatic variant.
