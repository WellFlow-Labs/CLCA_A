## SUMMARY OBSERVATIONS

### Distribution of Naturalness Ratings:
- **[CORE]**: 38 constructions (95%)
- **[MARGINAL]**: 2 constructions (5%)
- **[UNCERTAIN]**: 0 constructions (0%)

### Key Patterns:
1. The vast majority of constructions are core/idiomatic in Basque
2. Only generic inchoative constructions with *bihurtu* show marginal status, competing with more specialized lexicalized verbs
3. Derivational morphology (causatives, nominalizations, adverbials) is highly productive and core to the language
4. Light verb constructions and locative+verb combinations are fully productive
5. No theoretical or unattested constructions were included in the analysis
