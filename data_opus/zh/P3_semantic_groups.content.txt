# P3 — Natural Semantic Groupings (Chinese)

Based solely on observed usage patterns documented in P1 and P2 (collocations, registers, constructional frames, and contrasts), the following groupings emerge.

---

## Group 1: Colloquial truth/error confirmation in speech

**Definition:** Monosyllabic terms used as predicates, complements, or interjections to affirm or deny the correctness of a statement or action in everyday spoken exchange. They appear in resultative/complement frames (V+得+对, V+错+了) and tag/confirmatory frames (对不对, 没错) rather than in verb–object or attributive collocations.

**Representative expressions:**
- 对 (duì) — "说得对," "对不对?"
- 错 (cuò) — "说错了," "没错"
- 真的 (zhēn de) — "是真的吗"
- 真是 (zhēn shì)

**Note:** 对/错 are explicitly contrasted in P2b with their bisyllabic counterparts (正确/错误), which do *not* share these construction types.

---

## Group 2: Formal/written evaluation of correctness and error

**Definition:** Bisyllabic terms evaluating answers, judgments, and actions as correct or mistaken, used in educational, technical, and political registers. They build verb–object (犯错误, 纠正错误) and attributive (正确的选择) collocations, and support -性 nominalization (正确性).

**Representative expressions:**
- 正确 (zhèngquè)
- 错误 (cuòwù)
- 错误率 (cuòwùlǜ)

---

## Group 3: Factual reality and "what is actually the case"

**Definition:** Terms anchoring discourse to actual facts, frequently entering discourse-connective frames (事实上, 本质上) and evidential sentential frames (事实证明). Common in legal, journalistic, and argumentative registers, often contrasted with opinion, appearance, or fabrication.

**Representative expressions:**
- 事实 (shìshí) — "事实上," "摆事实," "事实证明"
- 真相 (zhēnxiàng) — "揭露真相," "查明真相"
- 真理 (zhēnlǐ) — "追求真理"
- 本质 (běnzhì) — "本质上," "透过现象看本质"
- 实情 (shíqíng), 实话 (shíhuà), 现实 (xiànshí), 实际 (shíjì)

**Internal distinctions noted in usage:** 真相 takes *revealing* verbs (揭露/查明); 真理 takes *pursuit* verbs (追求/掌握); 事实 specializes into discourse connectives. These are different collocational behaviors despite shared grouping.

---

## Group 4: Authentication — genuine vs. fake (objects, artifacts, products)

**Definition:** Terms used to assess whether a concrete object, document, or product is genuine or counterfeit, occurring with verbs of discrimination (辨别真伪, 鉴定真伪) and in commercial/authentication contexts. Register is legal, scholarly, or commercial.

**Representative expressions:**
- 真伪 (zhēnwěi) — "辨别真伪"
- 真假 (zhēnjiǎ) — "分辨真假"
- 真迹 (zhēnjì), 真品 (zhēnpǐn)
- 真假难辨 (zhēn jiǎ nán biàn)
- 货真价实 (huò zhēn jià shí)

**Overlapping item:** 假 (jiǎ) — functions as the counterpart pole here but is broader (see Group 8).

---

## Group 5: Authentic-to-type (food, language, tradition, craft)

**Definition:** Terms evaluating whether something is faithful to an original lineage, type, or standard, used attributively with food/culture/language nouns (正宗川菜, 地道的北京话) and in V+得+地道 complement frames. Register ranges from culinary/cultural to colloquial.

**Representative expressions:**
- 正宗 (zhèngzōng) — "正宗川菜"
- 地道 (dìdao) — "地道的北京话," "说得地道"
- 名副其实 (míng fù qí shí)
- 名不副实 (míng bù fù shí) — negative counterpart
- 名存实亡 (míng cún shí wáng), 名实相符 (míng shí xiāng fú)

**Note:** P2b explicitly notes no overlap in construction type between this group's attributive frames and Group 4's discrimination-verb frames, despite some shared roots.

---

## Group 6: Sincerity and honesty of persons and feelings

**Definition:** Terms applied to persons, attitudes, and emotions to evaluate moral honesty or heartfelt genuineness, carrying positive (or, for the antonym, negative) moral valuation. Used in interpersonal, moral, and educational contexts; several resist -性 nominalization (真诚, 诚实 yield no standard nominalizer).

**Representative expressions:**
- 真诚 (zhēnchéng) — "真诚的态度"
- 诚实 (chéngshí) — "诚实守信"
- 真心 (zhēnxīn), 真情 (zhēnqíng)
- 真挚 (zhēnzhì) — locked to emotion/relationship nouns (真挚的感情/友谊)
- 老实 (lǎoshi)
- 虚伪 (xūwěi) — negatively valued counterpart, "虚伪的人"

**Internal distinction noted in usage:** 真挚 collocates narrowly (emotion/friendship); 真诚 with attitude/conduct; the shared 真 root does not predict shared collocational range.

---

## Group 7: Accuracy, precision, and certainty (measured against a standard)

**Definition:** Terms evaluating measurements, descriptions, and conclusions for exactness or certainty, with heavy use in technical, scientific, and procedural registers. Gradable members support both -性 and -度 nominalization (准确性/准确度); several express psychological conviction or confirmation rather than correspondence-truth.

**Representative expressions:**
- 准确 (zhǔnquè) — "准确地说," "准确无误"
- 精确 (jīngquè), 确切 (quèqiè), 明确 (míngquè)
- 确定 (quèdìng), 确认 (quèrèn), 确信 (quèxìn)
- 确凿 (quèzáo) — narrowly fixed to 证据/事实 ("证据确凿")
- 的确 (díquè), 确实 (quèshí), 千真万确 (qiān zhēn wàn què)

**Note:** P1b/P2a flag these as shifting toward exactness/certainty rather than truth-as-correspondence.

---

## Group 8: Reliability and credibility of sources/information

**Definition:** Terms evaluating sources, people, equipment, testimony, or information as trustworthy or believable, used across professional and everyday registers. Productivity is chiefly derivational (可信度, 可信性, 可靠性) rather than constructional.

**Representative expressions:**
- 可靠 (kěkào) — "可靠消息"
- 可信 (kěxìn) — "可信度"
- 可信度 (kěxìndù), 可靠性 (kěkàoxìng)
- 确实 (quèshí) — overlaps with Group 7

---

## Group 9: Resemblance and likeness

**Definition:** Terms asserting that two or more things resemble each other, built into comparative frames (像…一样, 与…相似) and likeness-nominal frames (相似之处). 逼真 specifically evaluates a representation's closeness to reality rather than genuineness.

**Representative expressions:**
- 像 (xiàng) — "很像," "像…一样"
- 好像 (hǎoxiàng) — borders on epistemic/evidential marker
- 相似 (xiāngsì) — "相似之处," "相似度"
- 逼真 (bīzhēn) — "画得逼真" (emphasizes resemblance, not genuineness, per P1)

---

## Group 10: Faithful reporting / verification procedures

**Definition:** Terms describing the faithful representation or factual verification of information, occurring in administrative, legal, and journalistic registers. Adverbial members modify verbs of reporting (如实汇报); verbal members denote checking against fact.

**Representative expressions:**
- 如实 (rúshí) — "如实汇报," "如实回答"
- 据实 (jùshí)
- 核实 (héshí), 证实 (zhèngshí), 属实 (shǔshí)
- 实事求是 (shí shì qiú shì)
- 真凭实据 (zhēn píng shí jù)

---

## Group 11: Intensifier / degree-marking uses (function shift)

**Definition:** Forms in which the truth-domain morpheme functions as a preverbal degree adverb or emphatic marker rather than asserting truth. Flagged in P1b/P2b as a distinct function from the truth sense.

**Representative expressions:**
- 真 (zhēn) — "真有意思," "真好吃"
- 真的 (zhēn de) / 真是 (zhēn shì) — intensifier use
- 实在 (shízài) — "实在太累"

**Note:** These overlap formally with Groups 1 and 6 but are separated because the documented function is degree-marking, not truth-assertion.

---

## Items that do not fit cleanly / remain ungrouped

- **真切 (zhēnqiè)** — vividness of perception (真切地感受到); straddles Group 3 (reality) and Group 6 (felt genuineness) but collocates too narrowly to sit firmly in either. [MARGINAL in source]
- **真 (zhēn) as bare root** — functions as the positive pole underlying many groups but, per P2a, takes no -性 derivative in the truth sense and distributes across Groups 1, 4, 6, and 11 rather than belonging to one.
- **假 (jiǎ)** — spans Group 4 (counterfeit objects), feigned conduct (假装), and pretense idioms (弄假成真, 假戏真做); too broad to assign to a single group.
- **本质 (běnzhì)** — placed in Group 3 but distinctive in contrasting with 现象/表象 (appearance), a contrast not shared by other Group 3 members.

---

## Explicitly preserved asymmetries

- **Monosyllabic vs. bisyllabic correctness terms** (Group 1 vs. Group 2) do **not** share construction types — a usage split, not a conceptual one.
- **Two authentication groups** (Group 4 vs. Group 5) are kept separate because they take entirely different construction types (discrimination verbs vs. attributive food/culture frames), per P2b.
- **Shared root 真 does not predict shared grouping:** its members spread across Groups 1, 3, 4, 6, 9, and 11. No attempt was made to merge them on root identity.
- Groups were **not** balanced for size or symmetry; Group 11 (function shift) and the ungrouped items are retained as documented in the source rather than forced into the truth-evaluation groups.
