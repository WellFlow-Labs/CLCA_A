# F6 — NATURALNESS TABLE

| Prompt # | Expression (Native Script) | Transliteration | Naturalness Rating | Notes |
|----------|---------------------------|-----------------|-------------------|-------|
| 1.1a | तत् सत्यं भवति | tat satyaṃ bhavati | [CORE] | Standard construction for acquiring truth-status or being verified. Bhavati serves inchoative function. Context disambiguates stative vs. dynamic reading. |
| 1.1b | वचनं सत्यं जायते | vacanaṃ satyaṃ jāyate | [MARGINAL] | Jāyate emphasizes origination. Less common than bhavati. Restricted to contexts emphasizing fulfillment of predictions/promises. |
| 1.1c | (compound verb satyī-bhū) | — | [UNCERTAIN] | Theoretically possible but not attested in corpus. Sanskrit prefers predicative adjective + bhavati. |
| 1.2a | कथा तथ्यं भवति | kathā tathyaṃ bhavati | [CORE] | Used when narrative/testimony is verified as factual. Emphasizes correspondence to actual events. |
| 1.2b | ज्ञानं यथार्थं भवति | jñānaṃ yathārthaṃ bhavati | [CORE] | Describes cognition achieving correspondence to reality. Yathārtha functions adverbially or predicatively. |
| 1.3a | ज्ञानं प्रमा जायते | jñānaṃ pramā jāyate | [CORE] | Standard epistemological construction for genesis of valid cognition. Jāyate preferred for epistemic states. |
| 1.3b | बुद्धिः प्रमा भवति | buddhiḥ pramā bhavati | [CORE] | Alternative to jāyate. Bhavati emphasizes result/state rather than process. Both constructions productive. |
| 2.1a | वचनम् असत्यं भवति | vacanam asatyaṃ bhavati | [CORE] | Describes statement being revealed as false. Parallel construction to satyaṃ bhavati. |
| 2.1b | ज्ञानं मिथ्या भवति | jñānaṃ mithyā bhavati | [CORE] | Common in Vedāntic contexts for characterizing empirical knowledge as ultimately illusory. |
| 2.1c | जगत् मिथ्या प्रतीयते | jagat mithyā pratīyate | [CORE] | Passive construction emphasizing appearance/cognition of falsity. Common in Advaita Vedānta. |
| 2.2a | ज्ञानं भ्रमः भवति | jñānaṃ bhramaḥ bhavati | [CORE] | Describes cognition being revealed as erroneous. Bhrama emphasizes cognitive mistake. |
| 2.2b | बुद्धिः अप्रमा भवति | buddhiḥ apramā bhavati | [CORE] | Technical epistemological construction for cognition failing to achieve validity. |
| 2.3 | प्रत्ययः अयथार्थं भवति | pratyayaḥ ayathārthaṃ bhavati | [CORE] | Describes cognition losing/lacking correspondence to reality. Parallel to yathārthaṃ bhavati. |
| 3.1a | स तद् वचनं सत्यं करोति | sa tad vacanaṃ satyaṃ karoti | [CORE] | Used for fulfilling promises, realizing predictions. Emphasizes agent's role in bringing about truth-status. |
| 3.1b | स प्रतिज्ञां सत्यीकरोति | sa pratijñāṃ satyīkaroti | [CORE] | Compound causative more emphatic than simple karoti. Common for promise-fulfillment. Modern usage extends to "verify, authenticate." |
| 3.1c | स वचनं साधयति | sa vacanaṃ sādhayati | [CORE] | Sādhayati means "accomplish, prove, establish." Used for proving claims through demonstration. |
| 3.1d | प्रमाणेन तत् सत्यं सिध्यते | pramāṇena tat satyaṃ sidhyate | [CORE] | Passive construction with instrumental means. Emphasizes epistemic warrant rather than agentive causation. |
| 3.2a | (mithyā/asatyam karoti) | — | [UNCERTAIN] | Theoretically possible but not commonly attested. Falsification typically expressed through negation or deception verbs. |
| 3.3a | प्रत्यक्षेण ज्ञानं प्रमा जायते | pratyakṣeṇa jñānaṃ pramā jāyate | [CORE] | Standard epistemological construction. Instrumental marks epistemic means causing valid cognition. |
| 3.3b | प्रत्यक्षं प्रमां जनयति | pratyakṣaṃ pramāṃ janayati | [MARGINAL] | Active causative with pramāṇa as subject. Less common than instrumental + jāyate. Somewhat personifies pramāṇa. |
| 3.3c | आचार्यः शिष्यं प्रमाणेन ज्ञापयति | ācāryaḥ śiṣyaṃ pramāṇena jñāpayati | [CORE] | Causative of √jñā with instrumental pramāṇa. Used for pedagogical contexts. |
| 3.4a | अविद्यया भ्रमः जायते | avidyayā bhramaḥ jāyate | [CORE] | Instrumental marks cause of error. Standard construction in epistemology for explaining error-genesis. |
| 3.4b | मायिनः जनं भ्रमयति | māyinaḥ janaṃ bhramayati | [CORE] | Causative verb bhramayati means "cause to err, deceive." Used for intentional deception. |
| 4.1a | राजा प्रजां धर्मे स्थापयति | rājā prajāṃ dharme sthāpayati | [CORE] | Sthāpayati "establishes" with locative dharme. Common in political/ethical discourse. |
| 4.1b | गुरुः शिष्यं धर्मम् आचारयति | guruḥ śiṣyaṃ dharmam ācārayati | [CORE] | Causative verbs ācārayati "causes to practice" or śikṣayati "teaches." Used in pedagogical contexts. |
| 4.2 | (adharma causation) | — | [UNCERTAIN] | Direct causation of adharma rarely expressed through simple causative. Typically described through verbs of leading astray. |
| 5.1a | आचार्यः शिष्यं तत्त्वं ज्ञापयति | ācāryaḥ śiṣyaṃ tattvaṃ jñāpayati | [CORE] | Jñāpayati (causative of √jñā) standard for causing knowledge. Double accusative construction. |
| 5.1b | गुरुः शिष्यं बोधयति | guruḥ śiṣyaṃ bodhayati | [CORE] | Bodhayati emphasizes awakening/enlightenment. Common in spiritual contexts. |

| 5.2a | (avidyā causation) | — | [UNCERTAIN] | Avidyā typically treated as beginningless in Vedāntic philosophy, not caused by specific agent. |
| 5.2b | माया जनं मोहम् आपादयति | māyā janaṃ moham āpādayati | [CORE] | Āpādayati "causes to fall into" with moha "delusion." Māyā common subject. |
| 6.1a | ज्ञानं भ्रमं नाशयति | jñānaṃ bhramaṃ nāśayati | [CORE] | Nāśayati "destroys" or nivartayati "causes to cease" for removing error. Knowledge common subject. |
| 6.1b | प्रमाणेन भ्रमः नश्यति | pramāṇena bhramaḥ naśyati | [CORE] | Passive/intransitive construction with instrumental means. Common in epistemological discourse. |
| 6.2a | ज्ञानम् अविद्यां नाशयति | jñānam avidyāṃ nāśayati | [CORE] | Standard soteriological construction. Central to Vedāntic liberation doctrine. |
| 6.2b | विद्यया अविद्या नश्यति | vidyayā avidyā naśyati | [CORE] | Instrumental construction emphasizing means of removal. Common in Upaniṣadic texts. |
| 6.2c | अविद्यानिवृत्तौ मोक्षः | avidyānivṛttau mokṣaḥ | [CORE] | Nominal compound in locative absolute. Common in soteriological discourse linking ignorance-removal to liberation. |

| 7.2a | राजा धर्मे तिष्ठति | rājā dharme tiṣṭhati | [CORE] | Parallel to satye tiṣṭhati. Common in political/ethical discourse. |
| 7.2b | धर्मात् न चलेत् | dharmāt na calet | [CORE] | Optative construction expressing moral obligation. Common prescriptive formula in dharmaśāstra. |
| 7.3a | तत् सत्यम् एव भवति | tat satyam eva bhavati | [CORE] | Emphatic particle eva stresses exclusive/persistent truth. Common in philosophical discourse. |
| 7.3b | ब्रह्म नित्यं सत्यम् | brahma nityaṃ satyam | [CORE] | Nityam "eternally" emphasizes permanence. Common in Vedāntic discourse about eternal reality. |
| 8.1a | अधिकारी तथ्यं सत्यापयति | adhikārī tathyaṃ satyāpayati | [CORE] | Modern administrative term for official verification. Extension of classical vocabulary. |
| 8.1b | समितिः दस्तावेजं प्रमाणीकरोति | samitiḥ dastāvejaṃ pramāṇīkaroti | [CORE] | Modern term for authentication/certification. Used in administrative/legal contexts. |
