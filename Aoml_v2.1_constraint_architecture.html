LAYER 1C — CORE OPERATORS · answer: what structural transformation or relation is being asserted?
ATTRATTRIBUTION Assign property "X is [axis]" — assigns a truth-kind property to a subject. The most basic assertion. Universally licensed across all primary axes. — ✓ All axes. No restrictions. Universal cross-linguistically
CAUSECAUSATION Bring about state "Make X [axis]" — external agent produces or destroys a state. Licensing is polarity-conditioned and target-type-conditioned. The most restricted operator in the system. — Restricted: target-type governs licensing. Polarity asymmetry for AUTH. See R1, R3
BECOMEBECOMING Change of state "X becomes [axis]" — subject transitions into an axis-state. Directionally asymmetric: positive emergence is natural, negative emergence is marked or blocked in most languages. — Biased: positive states (become-true/real) >> negative (become-false/fake). See R4
RESULTRESULT State established "X proved/turned out to be [axis]" — marks an achieved or discovered state. Universally licensed. The pathway through which REVEAL-type constructions are often realized. — ✓ All axes. Near-universal licensing
ABSTRABSTRACTION Nominalize concept "Truth / justice / authenticity" — converts an axis-property into a standalone concept. Universally productive except for LIFELIKENESS (blocked) and ESSENCE (marginal). — ✓ Most axes. Blocked for LIFE; marginal for ESS (cross-linguistically)
STRATSTRATIFICATION Layered distinction "Inner vs outer truth / surface vs deep sincerity" — marks internal contrast within an axis. Universally attested. The operation through which levels become linguistically accessible. — ✓ All axes. Restricted for AUTH and SINC (surface stratification less natural)
CONTRCONTRAST Opposition "True vs false / genuine vs fake" — marks binary opposition or differentiated contrast. Universally licensed. Failures of contrast generate Type 5 (Contrast Frame Collapse) in UFA. — ✓ All axes. False or collapsed contrast = UFA Type 5
PROVISIONAL — REVEAL · status contested; empirically strong but structurally ambiguous
REVEALREVELATION
Provisional		 Disclosure of latent truth "X was revealed to be [axis]" — presupposes a latent pre-existing state that was already true before disclosure. Emerged unprompted across 4+ unrelated language families (Georgian, Korean, Turkish, Finnish) in both CLCA protocols. Key diagnostic: LIFELIKENESS blocks REVEAL (constructed things cannot be revealed, only discovered), confirming the presupposition is structural, not stylistic. — Provisionally treated as a primitive operator rather than a derived RESULT + STRAT pathway, because the LIFE/REVEAL diagnostic suggests it encodes a structural presupposition that RESULT + STRAT cannot reconstruct. Status to be confirmed by cross-architectural validation (non-Claude LLM families).

LAYER 2 — TARGET-TYPE CLASSES · cross-cutting structural partition over the primary-axis space; governs differential operator licensing
FACTFACTUAL
Target Type		 States of reality VER, AUTH, LIFE — properties of things in the world. Externally observable and, for some axes, externally producible. The paradigm domain for OBSERVATION and EXPERIMENT. Cross-cutting CAUSE generally licensed (subject to polarity conditions). BECOME natural in both directions for VER and LIFE, asymmetric for AUTH
EPISTEPISTEMIC
Target Type		 Knowledge states VER (as belief state), VERIF — properties of claims or belief-states relative to evidence. VERIF runs claim→world (testing); VER can run world→claim (discovery). Both resist direct causation of the epistemic state itself. Cross-cutting RESULT and ATTR strongly licensed. CAUSE restricted (cannot directly cause knowledge — only conditions that facilitate it)
DISPDISPOSITIONAL
Target Type		 Character states SINC — stable inner tendencies of agents. Not observable states of the world, not epistemic states — they are cultivated through practice and revealed through pattern of conduct. Universal causation block (R1) applies exclusively to this type. Cross-cutting CAUSE universally blocked (p < 10⁻⁹). BECOME marginal. RESULT and REVEAL licensed. This is the deepest structural constraint in the system.

§ III — Structural Rules · Empirical Constraints on the Core Legality Matrix

Layer distinction These rules are not primitive — they are empirical constraints discovered through CLCA that overlay the core legality matrix. They capture polarity effects, target-type conditioning, directional biases, and meta-axis substitution prohibitions that cannot be reduced to simple ✓/✗/△ cells in the matrix above. Each rule maps to one or more UFA violation types.
Rule Formal Statement Empirical Basis UFA Mapping
R1 Dispositional Causation Block
CAUSE × DISPOSITIONAL → universally blocked
Direct causation of dispositional character traits is structurally prohibited across all tested languages. Applies to SINC and all subregions (reliability, faithfulness). Not a prohibition on causation generally — FACTUAL states are causally accessible.
"Make someone honest" — blocked. "Encourage honesty" — natural. "Make the claim true" — natural.
 16/16 languages. Fisher's exact p < 10⁻⁹. Both CLCA protocols. The strongest single empirical finding in the system. Type 2 — Operator Overextension
CAUSE applied beyond its licensed scope to a dispositional target
R2 Essence Stability
CAUSE × ESS → blocked · BECOME × ESS → blocked
Essential properties are treated as intrinsic and non-manipulable. Neither external causation nor gradual transition applies to essence. Things have or lack essential properties — they do not acquire or lose them through process.
"Make it essentially X" — blocked. "Become essentially X" — blocked. "Reveal its essential nature" — licensed.
 15/16 (CAUSE), 15/16 (BECOME) across CLCA data. ESSENCE is the most restricted axis in the operator space. Type 2 — Operator Overextension
R3 Authenticity Polarity Asymmetry
CAUSE(+) × AUTH → blocked · CAUSE(−) × AUTH → licensed
Authenticity cannot be manufactured or externally created (CAUS+ blocked), but can be destroyed or forged (CAUS− licensed). This polarity asymmetry is the primary empirical evidence for the ONTOLOGICAL target-type class: ontological properties cannot be produced de novo externally, though they can be corrupted.
"Manufacture authentic goods" — blocked. "Forge/destroy authenticity" — natural in all tested languages.
 10/11 languages for CAUS+ block; 8/11 for CAUS− license. Asymmetry consistent across language families. Drove the CAUS+/CAUS− clarification in v2.1 (modeled as polarity conditioning on CAUSE, not two separate operators). Type 2 — Operator Overextension
Positive polarity of CAUSE extends beyond licensed scope for AUTH
R4 Positive Emergence Bias
BECOME(+state) >> BECOME(−state) across truth-domain axes
Positive emergence is natural and linguistically productive ("become true," "become real"). Negative emergence is marked, dispreferred, or blocked in most languages — conceptualized as degradation or corruption rather than an autonomous developmental process. Aligns with privation theory: negative states are absences, not destinations.
"The rumor became true" — natural. "The statement became false" — marked or blocked in most languages. Exceptions: Yoruba, Indonesian (analytic flexibility).
 14/16 languages show asymmetry. Two exceptions (Yoruba, Indonesian) are analytic languages where morphological asymmetries manifest pragmatically rather than morphologically. Type 2 — Operator Overextension
BECOME(−) extended beyond its licensed polarity
R5 Degree Meta-Axis Substitution
DEG[A] ↛ A in any inference step
The degree of a primary-axis claim cannot substitute for the claim itself. A claim about how much or how intensely something is the case does not establish that it is the case. The derivative is not the function. Natural language encodes DEG ordinally (more/less, enough/too much) not quantitatively — the prohibition applies regardless of the precision of the degree claim.
High probability (DEG[VER]) ≠ truth (VER). Very sincere-seeming (DEG[SINC]) ≠ veridical evidence (VER). Highly probable ≠ certain.
 Structural consequence of meta-axis architecture. DEG confirmed as meta-axis by both CLCA protocols. Explains a large class of classical fallacies under one rule. Type 2 — Operator Overextension (Meta-Axis Substitution subtype)
R6 Temporal Meta-Axis Substitution
TEMP[A] ↛ A in any inference step
Temporal position, recency, or historical precedence cannot validate a claim on its host primary axis without independent method. Additionally, TEMP introduces internal level stratification (past / present / future) — inference across these levels without justification constitutes a Type 4 error. Note: TEMP has provisional meta-axis status (CLCA-Revision only, D21).
Recent = better (TEMP[NORM] → NORM): Appeal to Novelty. Past investment = future obligation (∫past → future): Sunk Cost. Present standards applied to past (TEMP level crossing): Presentism / Historian's Fallacy.
 Structural derivation from BECOME, STRAT, and inchoative asymmetry data. TEMP emerged as D21 in CLCA-Revision (6/8 Set A) but not in CLCA-1.1. Cross-architectural confirmation pending. Type 2 (substitution) / Type 4 (level crossing)

§ V — Deep Hypothesis · The Hidden Constraint Behind the Three CLCA Discoveries

Status of this section This is not a formal claim but a structural hypothesis derivable from the CLCA data. The three strongest empirical findings may not be independent — they may all project from a single deeper constraint on how languages categorize the relationship between external agency and semantic targets. If confirmed, this hypothesis would make the target-type ontology explanatorily primary in the architecture.

The External / Internal Causation Hypothesis

The three most robust CLCA discoveries are currently treated as separate empirical findings:

Discovery 1

Universal blocking of direct character-trait causation (CAUSE × DISPOSITIONAL, p < 10⁻⁹)

Discovery 2

Positive emergence bias — becoming true/real is natural; becoming false/fake is marked or blocked (14/16 languages)

Discovery 3

REVEAL preference — truth-state transitions prefer revelatory constructions over transformative ones (15/16 languages, unprompted)

These may not be independent. They may all be surface projections of a single, deeper constraint:

Languages treat some semantic targets as externally producible and others as internally emergent or discoverable. The category to which a target belongs determines which operators are licensed — not the specific axis, but the target type.

Under this hypothesis, the three discoveries decompose cleanly:

FACTUAL targets

States of reality. External causation generally applies. "Make X true" = produce the fact. CAUSE licensed.

EPISTEMIC targets

Knowledge / belief states. Truth-values are discovered or revealed, not produced. REVEAL preferred. BECOME biased toward positive emergence (truth comes to light; falsehood doesn't emerge — it's an absence).

DISPOSITIONAL targets

Character states. Internally cultivated, not externally imposed. External causation universally blocked. Cultivation through practice and pattern, not direct intervention.

If this is correct, then the real discovery isn't three findings — it's one: the target-type ontology is structurally prior to the operator licensing matrix. The axes don't determine what operators are allowed; the target types do. The axes are just the named semantic territories within which targets of each type are found.

This would have a significant implication for Paper II (AOML / UFA): the primary structural layer for explaining fallacies may not be axis-to-axis substitution errors but target-type crossing errors — treating a dispositional target as if it were a factual one, or an epistemic target as if it were normative. The five UFA types would remain valid but their derivation would be deeper.
