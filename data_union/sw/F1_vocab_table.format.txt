| ukweli hudumu | ukweli hudumu | *ukweli* + *hu-* (habitual prefix) + *dumu* (to last, to endure) | truth endures; truth lasts | A proverbial or semi-fixed expression used in moral and religious discourse to assert the permanence of truth over falsehood. | [MARGINAL] |

| ni kweli | ni kweli | *ni* (it is, copula) + *kweli* (truth/true) | it is true; that's true; it's real; indeed | The standard copular assertion of truth. | si kweli | si kweli | *si* (it is not, negative copula) + *kweli* | it is not true; that's not true; it's false; untrue | The standard negation of a truth claim. Used across all registers to deny the accuracy of a statement. | [CORE] |

Common in spoken debate and formal testimony. | [CORE] |
| ukweli uchi | ukweli uchi | *u-* (abstract noun prefix) + *kweli* (truth) + *uchi* (nakedness, bare state) | the naked truth; the bare truth | Used rhetorically to assert that something is the unvarnished, unembellished truth.
