# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2b Compositional Constructions
# Language: Chinese (zh)

---

## Preliminary Note on Scope and Method

This step documents attested multi-word constructions involving the P1 base items. The focus is on constructions that are not single lexical items (already covered in P1 and P1b) and not derivational forms (covered in P2a), but rather periphrastic, collocational, and syntactically-driven patterns that are conventionalized in use. These include:

- **Verb-object collocations** (e.g., 说真话, 讲实话)
- **Predicate + complement constructions** (e.g., 证明是真的)
- **Topic-comment and evaluative frames** (e.g., X是真实的)
- **Coverb/prepositional phrases** (e.g., 如实地陈述)
- **Serial verb constructions** with truth-evaluation verbs
- **Fixed periphrastic expressions** not already listed as 成语 in P1/P1b

Constructions already fully documented as single lexical items or 成语 in P1/P1b (e.g., 真相大白, 实话实说, 以假乱真) are not repeated here unless they participate in a larger productive constructional pattern worth documenting separately.

Absence of constructions for a given base item is recorded explicitly.

## Section 1: Constructions with 真 (zhēn)

---

**1.**
- **Base Form:** 真 (zhēn)
- **Example:** 说真的
- **Transliteration:** shuō zhēn de
- **English Translation:** to be honest; honestly speaking; for real
- **Usage Notes:** A highly common conversational phrase used to introduce a candid or sincere statement, or to assert that one is speaking seriously rather than jokingly. Functions as a discourse marker across all registers of spoken Chinese.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**2.**
- **Base Form:** 真 (zhēn)
- **Example:** 是真的
- **Transliteration:** shì zhēn de
- **English Translation:** it is true; it is real; it is genuine
- **Usage Notes:** The basic predicative construction asserting truth or genuineness of a proposition or object. Extremely common across all registers as the standard way to affirm that something is the case or is authentic.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**3.**
- **Base Form:** 真 (zhēn)
- **Example:** 不是真的
- **Transliteration:** bù shì zhēn de
- **English Translation:** it is not true; it is not real; it is not genuine
- **Usage Notes:** The standard predicative negation of 是真的, used to deny the truth or authenticity of a proposition or object. Equally common across all registers.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**4.**
- **Base Form:** 真 (zhēn)
- **Example:** 当真吗？
- **Transliteration:** dàng zhēn ma?
- **English Translation:** Are you serious? Do you really mean it? Is it for real?
- **Usage Notes:** A common conversational question used to ask whether a statement or situation should be taken as genuine rather than as a joke or exaggeration. Distinct from the lexicalized 当真 (P1b) in that it functions as a full interrogative construction.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**5.**
- **Base Form:** 真 (zhēn)
- **Example:** 半真半假
- **Transliteration:** bàn zhēn bàn jiǎ
- **English Translation:** half true, half false; partly genuine, partly fabricated
- **Usage Notes:** A fixed periphrastic construction using the 半X半Y pattern to describe something that is a mixture of genuine and false elements. Used in evaluative discourse about accounts, claims, or representations.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**6.**
- **Base Form:** 真 (zhēn)
- **Example:** 说真话
- **Transliteration:** shuō zhēnhuà
- **English Translation:** to tell the truth; to speak truthfully
- **Usage Notes:** A common verb-object collocation meaning to speak truthfully. Contrasts directly with 说假话 (to tell lies). Used across moral, legal, and everyday contexts.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

## Cross-cutting Observations

**1. Verb-object collocations are the dominant construction type.** The most productive pattern across the domain is [verb] + [P1 noun], particularly with 真相, 真理, 本质, 核实, 伪造, and 鉴定 as objects. The verbs that most frequently collocate with truth-evaluation nouns as objects are: 揭露/揭示 (to expose/reveal), 掩盖 (to conceal), 查明 (to investigate and establish), 追求 (to pursue), 坚持 (to uphold), 还原 (to reconstruct), 辨别/鉴别 (to distinguish/authenticate), 核实 (to verify), and 认清 (to clearly recognize).

**2. Prepositional constructions with 与...相符 and 如实 + verb are highly productive** in legal and administrative registers, reflecting the domain's strong presence in formal institutional discourse.

**3. Degree modification is productive for adjectival P1 items.** 严重 (serious) collocates with both 失实 and 失真; 完全 and 基本 collocate with 正确; 准确无误 is a fixed intensified form. This pattern reflects the evaluative gradient inherent in accuracy and truth assessments.

**4. The 无法/无从 + [verification verb] construction** (items 47, 79) is a productive epistemic frame expressing the impossibility of authentication or verification, reflecting the domain's concern with epistemic access to truth.

**5. Register asymmetry is marked.** Items associated with legal and institutional discourse (伪造, 鉴定, 核实, 相符, 如实) show constructions concentrated in formal registers. Items associated with everyday moral evaluation (诚实, 真, 假) show constructions distributed across all registers. Items in specialized domains (原真性) show very few constructions and restricted register distribution.
