   **Term B:** 假  
   **Transliteration:** zhēn / jiǎ  
   **English translation:** true, real, genuine / false, fake, counterfeit  
   **Description of contrast:** This is the most basic lexical opposition in the dataset, used for judging whether something is genuine or not, true or false. The contrast is explicitly noted in forms like 真/假 and underlies many later compounds.
