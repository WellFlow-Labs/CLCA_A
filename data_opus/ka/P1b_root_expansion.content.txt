# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P1b ROOT EXPANSION
## Language: Georgian (ka)
## Transliteration: ISO 9984 (1996), consistent with P1

---

## STEP 1 — Central Roots

After examining the P1 items, three root morphemes emerge as the primary building blocks of the truth-evaluation domain in Georgian.

### Root 1: მართ- / მართლ- (mart- / martl-)
- **Root form:** მართ-/მართლ- (mart-/martl-)
- **P1 items it participates in:** 5 — სიმართლე (simartle, #3), მართალი (martali, #4), მართებული (martebuli, #26), მართებულობა (martebuloba, #27); also relevant to სწორი's semantic field but სწორი is a distinct root (sc̣or-). Counting strictly mart-/martl- forms: #3, #4, #26, #27 = 4 direct items.
- **Core meaning contribution:** "right/straight/just/proper" — spans factual correctness, moral righteousness, and procedural propriety. The most pervasive native truth-evaluation root, bridging the factual and the moral.

### Root 2: ცრუ- / ტყუ- (cru- / ṭq̇u-) — "false/lie"
*(Treated together as the falsehood pole, but note they are two distinct roots.)*
- **Root form:** ცრუ- (cru-) and ტყუ- (ṭq̇u-)
- **P1 items:** ცრუ- in cru (#9), sicrue (#10); ṭq̇u- in ṭq̇uili (#11), uṭq̇uari (#15). Total = 4 items across the two roots.
- **Core meaning contribution:** ცრუ- denotes moral/abstract falsehood and mendacity; ტყუ- denotes the concrete act/instance of lying. Together they form the negative pole of the truth field.

### Root 3: ნამდვ- (namdv-) — "real/genuine"
- **Root form:** ნამდვილ- (namdvil-)
- **P1 items:** 2 — ნამდვილი (namdvili, #6), ნამდვილობა (namdviloba, #7).
- **Core meaning contribution:** authenticity/genuineness; the "real vs. fake" axis, distinct from the "true vs. false" (statement) axis and the "right/just" (moral) axis.

> **Note:** მართ-/მართლ- is by far the most productive and central; it receives the fullest expansion. ცრუ-/ტყუ- and ნამდვ- are moderately productive.

## STEP 3 — Peripheral Extensions (flagged, not interpreted)

The following are flagged where the root's contribution appears to shift away from its core truth/falsity use:

**From მართ-/მართლ-:**
- **მართვა** (martva) — "driving/steering/governing" — shift to physical control/management.
- **მმართველი / მმართველობა** (mmartveli / mmartveloba) — "ruler / governance" — shift to political administration.
- **გამართვა / გამართება** (gamarteba) — "to set up / arrange / repair / fix" — shift to physical arrangement and repair.
- **გამართული** (gamartuli) — "fluent / smoothly-running / well-organized" — shift to quality of organization/fluency.
- **გამართლება** (gamartleba) — colloquial "it worked out / paid off / good luck" — shift to outcome/fortune.
- **მართებს / მართება** (martebs / marteba) — "it behooves / is owed / one ought" — shift to moral obligation and debt/owing.
- **მართმადიდებელი / მართმადიდებლობა** (martmadidebeli / martmadidebloba) — "Orthodox / Orthodoxy" — shift to religious confession (via "right-glorifying").
- **მართლწერა** (martlc̣era) — "orthography" — shift to linguistic correctness of writing.
- **სამართალი / სამართლებრივი** (samartali / samartlebrivi) — "law / legal" — shift to the juridical domain.

**From ცრუ-:**
- **ცრურწმენა / ცრუმორწმუნე** (crurc̣mena / crumorc̣mune) — "superstition / superstitious" — shift toward belief/epistemic-error domain rather than lying per se.

**From ტყუ-:**
- **ტყუილად / ტყუილუბრალოდ** (ṭq̇uilad / ṭq̇uilubralod) — "in vain / for nothing" — shift to futility/purposelessness rather than falsity.

**From ნამდვ-:**
- **ნამდვილად** (namdvilad) — "really/certainly" — shift to adverbial intensification/confirmation rather than predicating authenticity.
