# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P5 OPPOSITIONS
# Language: Yoruba (yo)

---

## OPPOSITION 1

**Term A:** òtítọ́  
**Term B:** irọ́  
**Transliteration:** òtítọ́ / irọ́  
**Translation:** truth / lie, falsehood  
**Description of contrast:** òtítọ́ refers to statements or representations that correspond to reality, while irọ́ refers to statements known to be false or deliberate misrepresentation. This is the primary lexical opposition in the truth domain.  
**Contrast type:** lexical  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## OPPOSITION 2

**Term A:** tọ́  
**Term B:** kò tọ́  
**Transliteration:** tọ́ / kò tọ́  
**Translation:** to be correct, true / to not be correct, not true  
**Description of contrast:** tọ́ affirms the correctness or truth of statements, while kò tọ́ negates it using the standard negative marker kò. This is the basic morphological negation of the stative verb.  
**Contrast type:** morphological  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## SUMMARY

A total of **45 oppositions** have been identified in the Yoruba truth domain data:

- **Lexical oppositions:** 20 (including scalar contrasts and verb/noun oppositions)
- **Morphological oppositions:** 17 (primarily using negative markers kò, àì-, kì í ṣe)
- **Constructional oppositions:** 5 (copular, imperative, comparative, manner, existential)
- **Combined lexical and morphological:** 3
- **Combined lexical and constructional:** 2

All identified oppositions are marked as **[CORE]** except for one term in Opposition 28, which is marked **[MARGINAL]** because the second construction is inferred rather than explicitly attested.

The most productive opposition pattern is the primary lexical pair **òtítọ́/irọ́** (truth/lie), which appears in multiple constructions and compounds throughout the data. The second most productive pattern is morphological negation using **kò** (standard negation), **àì-** (negative prefix), and **kì í ṣe** (emphatic negative copula).
