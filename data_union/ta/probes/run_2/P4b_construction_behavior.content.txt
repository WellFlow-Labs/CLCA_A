# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P4b Construction Usage & Constraints
# Language: Tamil (ta)

---

**Transliteration standard:** ISO 15919

---

## Construction Usage Table

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness Rating | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. ATTRIBUTION** (*-āna* prenominal) | உண்மையான செய்தி | *uṇmaiyāna ceytī* | "a true/genuine news report" | [CORE] | Fully productive across registers; the standard adjectival modification strategy for truth-domain nominals. No observable register restriction. |
| **1. ATTRIBUTION** (*-āna* prenominal) | நம்பகமான ஆதாரம் | *nampakamāna ātāram* | "a reliable source/evidence" | [CORE] | Equally productive; common in journalistic, legal, and everyday contexts. No attested alternate construction displaces this pattern. |
| **2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION** (*-aṟṟa*) | ஆதாரமற்ற வதந்தி | *ātāramaṟṟa vatanti* | "an unsubstantiated rumor" | [CORE] | Formal register tendency; attested in written, legal, and journalistic contexts. *-aṟṟa* is the preferred privative form in formal prose. |
| **2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION** (*-illāta*) | கள்ளமில்லாத மனிதன் | *kaḷḷamillāta maṉitaṉ* | "a person without guile" | [CORE] | Cross-register; attested in both spoken and written Tamil. *-illāta* is the more colloquially accessible of the two privative forms. |
| **3. ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION** (genitive *-iṉ*) | ஆவணத்தின் உண்மைத்தன்மை | *āvaṇattiṉ uṇmaitaṉmai* | "the authenticity of the document" | [CORE] | Preferred with abstract nominals; common in formal, legal, and philosophical registers. The genitive construction is more explicit than juxtaposition. |
| **3. ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION** (juxtaposition) | மனத் தூய்மை | *maṉat tūymai* | "purity of mind / mental integrity" | [CORE] | Attested in fixed compounds; more common in literary and moral discourse. Juxtaposition is restricted to tighter, more lexicalized compounds. |
| **4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE** (*-āki* + *viṭu*) | நம்பகத்தன்மை கேள்விக்குறியாகிவிட்டது | *nampakattaṉmai kēḷvikkuṟiyākiviṭṭatu* | "His credibility has become a question mark" | [CORE] | Marks completed change of state; common across spoken and written registers. The completive auxiliary *viṭu* adds a sense of finality or irreversibility. |
| **4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE** (*veḷippaṭu* collocation) | உண்மை வெளிப்பட்டது | *uṇmai veḷippaṭṭatu* | "The truth came out / became revealed" | [CORE] | Distinct collocation from the *āku* construction; attested as a fixed inchoative expression specifically for truth being revealed. Not interchangeable with *āki* in this collocation. |
| **5. CAUSATIVE** (*paṭuttu*) | ஆதாரப்படுத்து | *ātārappaṭuttu* | "to substantiate / to support with evidence" | [CORE] | Morphological causative; productive across truth-domain nominals. Attested forms include *uṟutippaṭuttu*, *niyāyappaṭuttu*, *tūymaippaṭuttu*. |
| **5. CAUSATIVE** (lexical: *niṟuvu* / *nirūpikku*) | உண்மை நிறுவு | *uṇmai niṟuvu* | "to establish the truth" | [CORE] | Lexical rather than morphological causative; nominal functions as direct object. Attested alongside the *paṭuttu* pattern without apparent register restriction between them. |
| **6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION** (*-mai*) | நேர்மை | *nēṟmai* | "honesty, integrity" | [CORE] | Primary abstract nominalizing suffix; fully productive and attested across all registers. These forms (*nēṟmai*, *tūymai*, *poymai*, *meymmai*) are the base lexical items, not derived on the fly. |
| **6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION** (*taṉmai* compound) | உண்மைத்தன்மை | *uṇmaitaṉmai* | "truthfulness, authenticity" | [MARGINAL] | Consistently more formal and less frequent than the *-mai* forms; restricted to philosophical, legal, and formal written registers. Not a productive spoken-register pattern. |
| **7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION** (negative agents) | பொய்யன் | *poyyan* | "liar (masculine)" | [CORE] | Agentive forms for negative truth-domain properties are more frequent than positive counterparts; attested across registers including colloquial speech. |
| **7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION** (negative agents) | வஞ்சகன் | *vañcakaṉ* | "deceiver, traitor (masculine)" | [CORE] | Attested in literary and everyday registers; the masculine form is the base form, with feminine *vañcaki* also attested. |
| **7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION** (positive agents, honorific) | (உண்மையாளர்) | (*uṇmaiyāḷar*) | "person of truth (honorific)" | [MARGINAL] | Less frequent than negative agentive forms; the honorific *-āḷar* form is restricted to formal or elevated registers. Asymmetry with negative forms is explicitly noted in P4a data. |
| **8. DEONTIC CONSTRUCTION** (*-āṇum*, colloquial) | உண்மை சொல்லணும் | *uṇmai collāṇum* | "(One) must tell the truth" | [CORE] | Spoken/colloquial form; highly productive in everyday moral and interpersonal discourse. The *-āṇum* suffix is the standard spoken obligative. |
| **8. DEONTIC CONSTRUCTION** (*-vēṇṭum*, formal) | (formal equivalent) | (*uṇmai collvēṇṭum*) | "(One) must tell the truth" | [CORE] | Formal written equivalent of *-āṇum*; attested in written registers. Both forms are productive; register determines selection rather than semantic difference. |
| **9. EPISTEMIC DISCOURSE MARKER** (*uṇmaiyil*) | உண்மையில் அவன் அங்கே இல்லை | *uṇmaiyil avaṉ aṅkē illai* | "In truth, he was not there" | [CORE] | Grammaticalized sentence-initial adverb; functions as a fixed marker of correction or strong factual assertion. Not compositionally interpreted. |
| **9. EPISTEMIC DISCOURSE MARKER** (*uṇmaiyilēyē*, emphatic) | உண்மையிலேயே இப்படி நடந்துச்சா? | *uṇmaiyilēyē ippaṭi naṭantuccā?* | "Did this really happen like this?" | [CORE] | Emphatic form of *uṇmaiyil*; attested in spoken registers, particularly in questions expressing surprise or disbelief. No parallel forms from *poy* or *nēṟmai* are attested in this function. |
| **10. BINARY QUESTION CONSTRUCTION** (*X-ā illai Y-ā*) | இது உண்மையா இல்லை பொய்யா? | *itu uṇmaiyā illai poyyā?* | "Is this true or false?" | [CORE] | Productive interrogative construction across all registers; the *uṇmai/poy* pair is the most frequent binary in the truth domain. |
| **10. BINARY QUESTION CONSTRUCTION** (*X-ā illai Y-ā*) | இது அசலா நகலா? | *itu acalā nakalā?* | "Is this the original or a copy?" | [CORE] | Attested with multiple truth-domain pairs (*acal/nakal*, *cariyā/illaiyā*, *niyāyamā/illaiyā*); the *illai* element may be omitted in the *acal/nakal* example as given, which represents a tighter binary form. |
| **11. PROVERBIAL / SCALAR ASSERTION** (*tāṉ vellum*) | உண்மை தான் வெல்லும் | *uṇmai tāṉ vellum* | "Truth alone will prevail" | [CORE] | Fixed proverbial pattern; attested in speeches, proverbs, and popular culture. Restricted to moral assertion contexts; not a productive open syntactic pattern. |
| **11. PROVERBIAL / SCALAR ASSERTION** (*tāṉ vellum*) | நியாயம் தான் வெல்லும் | *niyāyam tāṉ vellum* | "Justice alone will prevail" | [CORE] | Parallel to the *uṇmai* form; also attested with literary *mey* (*meyyē vellum*). The pattern is lexically restricted to a small set of moral-domain nominals. |

## Summary of Attested Alternate Constructions

| Construction Type | Alternates Attested |
|---|---|
| **1. Attribution** | Predicative alternative: nominal + *-āka* + *iru* (noted in P4a but no example provided in P4b scope) |
| **2. Negative Attribution** | Two forms: *-aṟṟa* (formal) and *-illāta* (cross-register); both attested, register-differentiated |
| **3. Adnominal Modification** | Genitive *-iṉ* vs. juxtaposition; distribution conditioned by degree of lexicalization |
| **4. Inchoative** | *āki(viṭu)* construction vs. *veḷippaṭu* collocation; not interchangeable |
| **5. Causative** | Morphological (*paṭuttu*) vs. lexical (*niṟuvu*, *nirūpikku*); both attested |
| **6. Abstract Nominalization** | *-mai* (productive, all registers) vs. *taṉmai* compound (formal only) |
| **7. Agentive Nominalization** | Masculine *-aṉ* / feminine *-aḷ* / honorific *-āḷar*; gender and register conditioned |
| **8. Deontic** | *-āṇum* (spoken) vs. *-vēṇṭum* (written formal); register-conditioned alternates |
| **9. Epistemic Marker** | *uṇmaiyil* vs. emphatic *uṇmaiyilēyē*; no alternates from *poy* or *nēṟmai* attested |
| **10. Binary Question** | Full form (*X-ā illai Y-ā*) vs. reduced form (*X-ā Y-ā*) as suggested by *acalā nakalā* example |
| **11. Proverbial Assertion** | *uṇmai tāṉ vellum* / *niyāyam tāṉ vellum* / *meyyē vellum*; lexically restricted set |
