# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P5 OPPOSITIONS
# Language: Chinese (zh)

---

## Preliminary Note

Oppositions below are extracted directly from patterns documented in P1, P1b, P2a, P2b, and P3. Each opposition is supported by explicit co-occurrence, contrastive framing, or antonym pairing attested in the CONTEXT. No opposition has been inferred from general linguistic knowledge alone. Where the CONTEXT documents an asymmetry (e.g., one pole is more productive, more formal, or more restricted than the other), this is noted in the description.

## OPPOSITION 1

**Term A:** 真 (zhēn)
**Term B:** 假 (jiǎ)
**Transliteration:** zhēn / jiǎ
**English translation:** genuine, real / fake, false, counterfeit
**Description of contrast:** The CONTEXT explicitly identifies these as antonyms and as the positive and negative poles of the authenticity axis (P1 items 1 and 2; P1b Root 1 and Root 3). They co-occur in the compound 真假 (P1b Root 1, item 6) and in the interrogative frame 是真是假 (P2b construction 2), which frames the question of authenticity as a binary between these two poles.
**Contrast type:** Lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## OPPOSITION 2

**Term A:** 真 (zhēn)
**Term B:** 伪 (wěi)
**Transliteration:** zhēn / wěi
**English translation:** genuine, true / false, spurious, counterfeit
**Description of contrast:** The CONTEXT documents these as the two poles of the compound 真伪 (P1 item 13), which frames the authentication question as a binary. P1b notes that 伪 and 假 function as near-synonyms in the falsity domain, but 伪 is more restricted to formal and legal registers and appears specifically in 伪造 (to forge) and 真伪 (genuine or fake).
**Contrast type:** Lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## OPPOSITION 3

**Term A:** 真实 (zhēnshí)
**Term B:** 虚假 (xūjiǎ)
**Transliteration:** zhēnshí / xūjiǎ
**English translation:** truthful, factual, accurate / false, fraudulent, fictitious
**Description of contrast:** The CONTEXT treats these as the formal antonyms in the domain of statements and representations (P1 items 3 and 4; P3 Group 3 and Group 5). 真实 evaluates a representation as accurately reflecting reality; 虚假 characterizes a statement or claim as deliberately misleading or fabricated. The CONTEXT notes that 虚假 is stronger and more formal than 假 alone.
**Contrast type:** Lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## OPPOSITION 4

**Term A:** 正确 (zhèngquè)
**Term B:** 错误 (cuòwù)
**Transliteration:** zhèngquè / cuòwù
**English translation:** correct, right, accurate / wrong, erroneous, mistaken
**Description of contrast:** The CONTEXT documents these as antonyms evaluating conformity to a standard (P1 items 5 and 6; P3 Group 3). 正确 asserts that something conforms to a standard or is free from error; 错误 asserts deviation from what is correct. Both appear in the same evaluative contexts (educational, technical, moral) and are treated as a contrasting pair throughout the CONTEXT.
**Contrast type:** Lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## OPPOSITION 5

**Term A:** 真实性 (zhēnshíxìng)
**Term B:** 虚假性 (xūjiǎxìng)
**Transliteration:** zhēnshíxìng / xūjiǎxìng
**English translation:** truthfulness, factual accuracy, veracity / falseness, fraudulence, the quality of being fictitious or deceptive
**Description of contrast:** These are the 性-nominalized abstract property forms of 真实 and 虚假 respectively (P2a items 1 and 3). They stand in the same oppositional relationship as their bases but at the level of abstract property nouns. The CONTEXT notes that 真实性 is more frequent and broadly distributed than 虚假性, which is more restricted to legal and regulatory discourse.
**Contrast type:** Morphological
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## OPPOSITION 6

**Term A:** 正确性 (zhèngquèxìng)
**Term B:** 错误性 (cuòwùxìng)
**Transliteration:** zhèngquèxìng / cuòwùxìng
**English translation:** correctness, the property of being right / erroneousness, the quality of being mistaken or wrong
**Description of contrast:** These are the 性-nominalized forms of 正确 and 错误 (P2a items 4 and 5). The CONTEXT explicitly notes an asymmetry: 正确性 is highly productive and common across registers, while 错误性 is considerably less common because 错误 itself already functions as a noun in many contexts, reducing the need for the 性 form.
**Contrast type:** Morphological
**Epistemic status:** [CORE] for 正确性; [MARGINAL] for 错误性

## OPPOSITION 7

**Term A:** 合法 (héfǎ)
**Term B:** 违法 (wéifǎ)
**Transliteration:** héfǎ / wéifǎ
**English translation:** lawful, legal, legitimate / illegal, unlawful, in violation of the law
**Description of contrast:** The CONTEXT documents these as the positive and negative poles of legal evaluation (P1 items 22 and 23; P3 Group 8). 合法 evaluates conduct or status as conforming to legal requirements; 违法 evaluates it as contravening legal norms. Both are standard terms in Chinese legal, administrative, and journalistic registers.
**Contrast type:** Lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## OPPOSITION 8

**Term A:** 合法性 (héfǎxìng)
**Term B:** 违法性 (wéifǎxìng)
**Transliteration:** héfǎxìng / wéifǎxìng
**English translation:** legality, legitimacy, lawfulness / illegality, unlawfulness, the quality of being in violation of the law
**Description of contrast:** These are the 性-nominalized forms of 合法 and 违法 (P2a items 9 and 11). Both are documented as [CORE] in legal and analytical discourse, and they stand in the same oppositional relationship as their bases. The CONTEXT notes that 合法性 also functions as a translation of "legitimacy" in the Weberian political-philosophical sense, an extension not shared by 违法性.
**Contrast type:** Morphological
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## OPPOSITION 9

**Term A:** 合法化 (héfǎhuà)
**Term B:** (no clear opposition identified)
**Transliteration:** héfǎhuà
**English translation:** to legalize, to legitimize
**Description of contrast:** The CONTEXT documents 合法化 as a productive 化-derivation (P2a item 10) but does not attest a corresponding *违法化 form. The inchoative/process opposition for this pair is therefore asymmetric: the process of becoming lawful is lexicalized, but the process of becoming unlawful is not expressed by a parallel 化-form in the attested data.
**Contrast type:** Morphological
**Epistemic status:** [UNCERTAIN]

## OPPOSITION 10

**Term A:** 名副其实 (míng fù qí shí)
**Term B:** 名不副实 (míng bù fù shí)
**Transliteration:** míng fù qí shí / míng bù fù shí
**English translation:** living up to one's name, worthy of the title / not living up to one's name, undeserving of the title, nominal only
**Description of contrast:** The CONTEXT explicitly identifies 名不副实 as the negative counterpart of 名副其实 (P1 items 10 and 20; P3 Group 2). The opposition is formed by the insertion of 不 (negation) into the fixed four-character expression. 名副其实 is commendatory; 名不副实 is critical. Both are used to evaluate the correspondence between a label or title and actual qualities.
**Contrast type:** Morphological (negation within a fixed expression)
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## OPPOSITION 11

**Term A:** 这是真的 (zhè shì zhēn de)
**Term B:** 这不是真的 (zhè bù shì zhēn de)
**Transliteration:** zhè shì zhēn de / zhè bù shì zhēn de
**English translation:** "This is true / genuine." / "This is not true / not genuine."
**Description of contrast:** The CONTEXT documents both the affirmative and negated forms of the canonical attribution construction (P2b constructions 1 and 6; P4a Construction 1 and 2). The negated form is produced by inserting 不 before 是. The CONTEXT notes that the two forms have distinct pragmatic uses: the affirmative asserts truth or genuineness, while the negated form is used for denial, correction, or reassurance.
**Contrast type:** Constructional
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## OPPOSITION 12

**Term A:** 属实 (shǔshí) / 经查属实 (jīng chá shǔshí)
**Term B:** 失实 (shīshí) / 报道失实 (bàodào shīshí)
**Transliteration:** shǔshí / jīng chá shǔshí — shīshí / bàodào shīshí
**English translation:** confirmed as factual, verified / inaccurate, departing from the facts; the report is inaccurate
**Description of contrast:** The CONTEXT documents these as contrasting outcomes of the evaluation of statements and reports (P1 items 15 and 16; P3 Group 3 and Group 4). 属实 is the formal assertion that a claim has been verified as true; 失实 characterizes a report or account as departing from the facts. The CONTEXT notes that 失实 does not necessarily imply deliberate deception, distinguishing it from 虚假.
**Contrast type:** Lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## OPPOSITION 13

**Term A:** 逼真 (bīzhēn)
**Term B:** 失真 (shīzhēn)
**Transliteration:** bīzhēn / shīzhēn
**English translation:** lifelike, true to life, realistic / distortion, loss of fidelity, inaccurate representation
**Description of contrast:** The CONTEXT documents these as contrasting evaluations of representational fidelity (P1 items 25 and 26; P3 Group 3). 逼真 asserts that a representation closely approaches its subject; 失真 asserts that a representation has departed from the original or from reality. Both share the root 真 but with opposite directional prefixes: 逼 (to approach/press toward) vs. 失 (to lose/depart from).
**Contrast type:** Lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## OPPOSITION 14

**Term A:** 诚实 (chéngshí)
**Term B:** 欺骗 (qīpiàn)
**Transliteration:** chéngshí / qīpiàn
**English translation:** honest, truthful, sincere / to deceive, to cheat, to defraud
**Description of contrast:** The CONTEXT places these at opposite poles of the moral evaluation of persons and conduct (P1 items 27 and 28; P3 Group 6 and Group 5). 诚实 evaluates a person's character as marked by truthfulness and absence of deception; 欺骗 describes deliberate acts of misleading others. The opposition is between a character virtue and a deceptive act, which creates a structural asymmetry: 诚实 is a stative adjective, 欺骗 is primarily a verb.
**Contrast type:** Lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## OPPOSITION 15

**Term A:** 真诚 (zhēnchéng) / 真心 (zhēnxīn)
**Term B:** 虚情假意 (xū qíng jiǎ yì) / 假意 (jiǎyì)
**Transliteration:** zhēnchéng / zhēnxīn — xū qíng jiǎ yì / jiǎyì
**English translation:** sincere, genuine, heartfelt / wholehearted, genuine in feeling — false sentiments and feigned intentions / false pretense, feigned intention
**Description of contrast:** The CONTEXT documents these as contrasting evaluations of the genuineness of expressed emotion and intention (P1b Root 1 items 3 and 4; P1b Root 3 items 10 and 11; P3 Group 6). 真诚 and 真心 assert that an emotion or intention is genuinely felt; 虚情假意 and 假意 characterize expressed feeling as performed rather than real. The CONTEXT notes that 虚情假意 is used critically in moral and interpersonal evaluations.
**Contrast type:** Lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## OPPOSITION 16

**Term A:** 真话 (zhēnhuà) / 实话 (shíhuà)
**Term B:** 假话 (jiǎhuà)
**Transliteration:** zhēnhuà / shíhuà — jiǎhuà
**English translation:** truthful words, honest speech / the truth, straight talk — lies, false statements, untruths
**Description of contrast:** The CONTEXT documents 真话, 实话, and 假话 as a three-way set in which 真话 and 实话 occupy the positive pole (honest speech) and 假话 occupies the negative pole (false speech) (P1b Root 1 item 5; P1b Root 2 item 5; P1b Root 3 item 3; P3 Group 3). The CONTEXT notes that 真话 and 实话 are used interchangeably in many contexts, while 假话 is their shared antonym.
**Contrast type:** Lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## OPPOSITION 17

**Term A:** 说真话 / 说实话 (shuō zhēnhuà / shuō shíhuà)
**Term B:** 说假话 (shuō jiǎhuà)
**Transliteration:** shuō zhēnhuà / shuō shíhuà — shuō jiǎhuà
**English translation:** to tell the truth, to speak honestly — to tell lies, to say false things
**Description of contrast:** These are the verb-object constructional realizations of the 真话/实话 vs. 假话 opposition (P2b constructions 4 and 78; P1b Root 3 item 3). The construction 说 + truth/falsity noun is the standard frame for describing honest or dishonest speech acts. The CONTEXT documents 说真话 and 说实话 as [CORE] in everyday speech.
**Contrast type:** Constructional
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## OPPOSITION 18

**Term A:** 真相 (zhēnxiàng)
**Term B:** 假象 (jiǎxiàng)
**Transliteration:** zhēnxiàng / jiǎxiàng
**English translation:** the truth of a matter, the real situation, the facts — false appearance, illusion, misleading phenomenon
**Description of contrast:** The CONTEXT documents these as contrasting compounds sharing the second morpheme 象 (appearance/phenomenon) but with opposite first morphemes 真 (true) and 假 (false) (P1b Root 1 item 2; P1b Root 3 item 4; P3 Group 3 and Group 5). 真相 refers to the actual state of affairs behind appearances; 假象 refers to a surface appearance that gives a misleading impression of the underlying reality.
**Contrast type:** Lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## OPPOSITION 19

**Term A:** 名副其实的专家 (míng fù qí shí de zhuānjiā)
**Term B:** 名不副实的头衔 (míng bù fù shí de tóuxián)
**Transliteration:** míng fù qí shí de zhuānjiā — míng bù fù shí de tóuxián
**English translation:** "a genuine expert (one who truly deserves the title)" — "a title that doesn't live up to its name; an undeserved title"
**Description of contrast:** These are the adnominal constructional realizations of the 名副其实 / 名不副实 opposition (P2b constructions 29 and 49; P4a Construction 3). Both use the modifier + 的 + noun frame, but the commendatory expression (名副其实) is typically applied to persons, while the critical expression (名不副实) is more typically applied to titles and labels in the attested examples. The CONTEXT does not claim this distributional difference is categorical.
**Contrast type:** Constructional
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## OPPOSITION 20

**Term A:** 经查属实 (jīng chá shǔshí)
**Term B:** 无法核实 (wúfǎ héshí)
**Transliteration:** jīng chá shǔshí — wúfǎ héshí
**English translation:** "verified upon investigation; confirmed as factual after inquiry" — "cannot be verified; impossible to confirm"
**Description of contrast:** The CONTEXT explicitly identifies 无法核实 as the negative counterpart of the verification/confirmation construction (P2b construction 81; P4a Construction 8; P4b table). 经查属实 asserts that a formal verification process has produced a positive outcome; 无法核实 asserts that verification cannot be completed. Both are conventionalized formulas in journalistic and administrative registers.
**Contrast type:** Constructional
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## OPPOSITION 21

**Term A:** 务实 (wùshí)
**Term B:** 脱离实际 / 脱离现实 (tuōlí shíjì / tuōlí xiànshí)
**Transliteration:** wùshí — tuōlí shíjì / tuōlí xiànshí
**English translation:** pragmatic, practical, down-to-earth — divorced from reality, out of touch with actual conditions
**Description of contrast:** The CONTEXT documents these as contrasting evaluations of whether an approach or attitude is grounded in actual conditions (P1b Root 2 item 13; P2b constructions 75 and 77; P3 Group 7). 务实 commends engagement with practical realities; 脱离实际 and 脱离现实 criticize failure to engage with actual conditions. The CONTEXT notes that 脱离实际 is more common in administrative and political discourse, while 脱离现实 is more common in everyday speech.
**Contrast type:** Lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## OPPOSITION 22

**Term A:** 从实际出发 (cóng shíjì chūfā)
**Term B:** 脱离实际 (tuōlí shíjì)
**Transliteration:** cóng shíjì chūfā — tuōlí shíjì
**English translation:** "to proceed from actual conditions; to start from reality" — "divorced from reality; out of touch with actual conditions"
**Description of contrast:** These are contrasting constructional expressions of the same evaluative dimension documented in P2b (constructions 76 and 77) and P3 Group 7. 从实际出发 describes the correct epistemic starting point (actual conditions); 脱离实际 describes the failure to maintain contact with those conditions. Both are fixed expressions in Chinese political and administrative discourse.
**Contrast type:** Constructional
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## OPPOSITION 23

**Term A:** 以假乱真 (yǐ jiǎ luàn zhēn)
**Term B:** 辨别真伪 / 鉴定真伪 (biànbié zhēnwěi / jiàndìng zhēnwěi)
**Transliteration:** yǐ jiǎ luàn zhēn — biànbié zhēnwěi / jiàndìng zhēnwěi
**English translation:** "to pass off the fake as genuine, to use counterfeits to confuse the real" — "to distinguish the genuine from the false; to authenticate"
**Description of contrast:** The CONTEXT documents these as opposing activities in the authentication domain (P1b Root 1 item 11; P1 items 13 and 14; P3 Group 1 and Group 4). 以假乱真 describes the act of deliberately confusing genuine and fake items; 辨别真伪 and 鉴定真伪 describe the counteracting act of distinguishing them. The opposition is between a deceptive act and a verification act directed at the same object.
**Contrast type:** Lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## OPPOSITION 24

**Term A:** 弄虚作假 (nòng xū zuò jiǎ)
**Term B:** 实事求是 (shíshì qiúshì)
**Transliteration:** nòng xū zuò jiǎ — shíshì qiúshì
**English translation:** "to engage in fraud and deception, to falsify" — "to seek truth from facts; to be realistic and empirical"
**Description of contrast:** The CONTEXT documents these as opposing normative orientations in administrative and political discourse (P1b Root 3 item 13; P1b Root 2 item 24; P3 Group 5 and Group 7). 弄虚作假 describes deliberate falsification of records and conduct; 实事求是 prescribes an empirical, non-falsifying approach to problems. Both are extremely common in Chinese political and administrative discourse and are treated as opposing conduct norms.
**Contrast type:** Lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## OPPOSITION 25

**Term A:** 伪造者 (wěizàozhě)
**Term B:** (no clear opposition identified)
**Transliteration:** wěizàozhě
**English translation:** forger, counterfeiter
**Description of contrast:** The CONTEXT documents 伪造者 as the agentive nominalization of 伪造 (P2a item 15) but does not attest a corresponding positive-pole agentive nominalization (a "truth-teller" or "authenticator" formed with 者). The CONTEXT explicitly notes this asymmetry: "no agentive nominalization with 者 is attested for the positive (truth-telling) pole" (P4a Construction 7). The concept of an honest person is expressed periphrastically (说真话的人) or by character adjectives (诚实的人) rather than by a parallel 者-form.
**Contrast type:** Morphological
**Epistemic status:** [UNCERTAIN]

## OPPOSITION 26

**Term A:** 假设 / 假如 / 假若 / 假定 (jiǎshè / jiǎrú / jiǎruò / jiǎdìng)
**Term B:** 事实上 / 实际上 / 实质上 (shìshí shàng / shíjì shàng / shízhì shàng)
**Transliteration:** jiǎshè / jiǎrú / jiǎruò / jiǎdìng — shìshí shàng / shíjì shàng / shízhì shàng
**English translation:** hypothesis, if, supposing that, to assume — in fact, in reality, in substance
**Description of contrast:** The CONTEXT documents the hypothetical/conditional 假 expressions (P1b Root 3 items 5–8; P3 Group 9) and the factual adverbial frames (P2b constructions 69, 47; P3 Group 7) as occupying opposite ends of an epistemic dimension: stipulated or imagined conditions vs. assertions about what is actually the case. The CONTEXT notes in P1b Step 3 that the 假 root in hypothetical expressions "does not assert that something is false or counterfeit, but rather introduces a stipulated or imagined condition."
**Contrast type:** Constructional
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## OPPOSITION 27

**Term A:** 尊重事实 (zūnzhòng shìshí)
**Term B:** 歪曲事实 / 捏造事实 (wāiqū shìshí / niēzào shìshí)
**Transliteration:** zūnzhòng shìshí — wāiqū shìshí / niēzào shìshí
**English translation:** "to respect the facts; to be guided by the facts" — "to distort the facts / to fabricate facts"
**Description of contrast:** The CONTEXT documents these as contrasting orientations toward factual reality in journalistic, legal, and political discourse (P2b constructions 70, 71, and 72). 尊重事实 asserts adherence to factual reality; 歪曲事实 describes deliberate distortion of facts; 捏造事实 describes deliberate invention of false facts. The CONTEXT notes that 捏造事实 is legally significant in Chinese defamation law.
**Contrast type:** Constructional
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## OPPOSITION 28

**Term A:** 半真半假 (bàn zhēn bàn jiǎ) / 真真假假 (zhēn zhēn jiǎ jiǎ)
**Term B:** 千真万确 (qiān zhēn wàn què)
**Transliteration:** bàn zhēn bàn jiǎ / zhēn zhēn jiǎ jiǎ — qiān zhēn wàn què
**English translation:** "half true and half false; a mixture of truth and falsehood" — "absolutely true, beyond all doubt, one hundred percent certain"
**Description of contrast:** The CONTEXT documents these as contrasting evaluations of the degree of truth in a claim or account (P1b Root 1 items 10 and 15; P1b Root 1 item 9). 千真万确 emphatically asserts complete and undeniable truth; 半真半假 and 真真假假 describe intermediate or mixed states where genuine and false elements are intermingled. The opposition is between emphatic certainty and epistemic mixture.
**Contrast type:** Lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## OPPOSITION 29

**Term A:** 严禁弄虚作假 (yánjìn nòng xū zuò jiǎ)
**Term B:** 诚实守信 (chéngshí shǒuxìn)
**Transliteration:** yánjìn nòng xū zuò jiǎ — chéngshí shǒuxìn
**English translation:** "it is strictly forbidden to engage in fraud and falsification" — "honest and trustworthy; to be honest and keep one's word"
**Description of contrast:** The CONTEXT documents these as contrasting normative expressions in institutional and moral discourse (P2b construction 90; P2b construction 62; P3 Group 5 and Group 6). 严禁弄虚作假 is a prohibition construction in regulatory and administrative registers; 诚实守信 is a positive moral principle in educational and civic registers. The opposition is between a formal prohibition of dishonest conduct and a positive prescription of honest conduct, with a register asymmetry: the prohibition is administrative/legal, the prescription is moral/educational.
**Contrast type:** Constructional
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## OPPOSITION 30

**Term A:** 真知灼见 (zhēn zhī zhuó jiàn)
**Term B:** 错误 (cuòwù) / 失实 (shīshí)
**Transliteration:** zhēn zhī zhuó jiàn — cuòwù / shīshí
**English translation:** "genuine insight, penetrating and well-founded views" — "wrong, erroneous, mistaken / inaccurate, departing from the facts"
**Description of contrast:** The CONTEXT documents 真知灼见 as a commendatory expression for views that are both accurate and perceptive (P1b Root 1 item 13), used "to commend views or analyses that are both accurate and perceptive, as opposed to superficial or mistaken opinions." The CONTEXT does not pair it with a single fixed antonym, but the contrast with 错误 and 失实 is implied by the definition. This opposition is less tightly attested than others in this list.
**Contrast type:** Lexical
**Epistemic status:** [UNCERTAIN]
