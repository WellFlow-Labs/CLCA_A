## SUMMARY OBSERVATIONS

**CORE constructions (39/40):** Nearly all attested constructions show high naturalness and frequency. They are productive, unrestricted, and used across registers.

**MARGINAL constructions (1/40):** Only Construction 19 (emphatic reduplication אמת לאמיתה) shows limited productivity and restricted usage, being somewhat idiomatic and colloquial.

**UNCERTAIN constructions (0/40):** No constructions show unclear or doubtful status based on the data provided.

**General patterns:**
- Morphological patterns (binyanim, nominalization) are highly productive
- Light verb constructions are extremely common
- Metaphorical extensions show consistent conceptual patterns
- Discourse markers derived from truth-terms are frequent in spoken Hebrew
- No significant register restrictions except for some legal/biblical terms (עד שקר, צדק וחסד)
