## BASE FORM 1: benar (true, correct)

### Construction 1.1: yang benar
**Example:** yang benar
**Transliteration:** N/A (Latin script)
**English translation:** the correct one, the true one, what is right
**Usage notes:** Used to identify or select the correct option among alternatives. Also functions as exclamation expressing disbelief ("Yang benar saja!" = "Really?!/You must be joking!").
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### Construction 1.2: apa benar
**Example:** apa benar
**Transliteration:** N/A (Latin script)
**English translation:** is it true, is it really
**Usage notes:** Question formula seeking confirmation of truth. Very common in conversational Indonesian for verifying information.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### Construction 1.3: kalau benar
**Example:** kalau benar
**Transliteration:** N/A (Latin script)
**English translation:** if it's true, if correct
**Usage notes:** Conditional construction introducing hypothetical truth conditions. Common in reasoning and argumentation.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### Construction 1.4: tidak benar
**Example:** tidak benar
**Transliteration:** N/A (Latin script)
**English translation:** not true, incorrect, false
**Usage notes:** Standard negation of benar. Used to deny truth or correctness across all contexts.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### Construction 1.5: kurang benar
**Example:** kurang benar
**Transliteration:** N/A (Latin script)
**English translation:** not quite correct, somewhat inaccurate
**Usage notes:** Polite or hedged negation indicating partial incorrectness. Common in formal and polite discourse to soften disagreement.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

**Transliteration:** N/A (Latin script)
**English translation:** not factual, unfactual
**Usage notes:** Standard negation indicating lack of factual basis. Common in analytical and critical contexts.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### Construction 32.2: kurang faktual
**Example:** kurang faktual
**Transliteration:** N/A (Latin script)
**English translation:** not quite factual, somewhat unfactual
**Usage notes:** Polite or hedged criticism indicating questionable factual basis. Common in formal evaluations.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### Construction 32.3: sangat faktual
**Example:** sangat faktual
**Transliteration:** N/A (Latin script)
**English translation:** very factual, highly factual
**Usage notes:** Intensified form emphasizing strong factual basis. Common in positive evaluations of information.
