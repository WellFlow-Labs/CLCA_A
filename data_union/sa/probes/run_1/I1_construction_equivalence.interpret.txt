## 2. Substitution Patterns (Narrative)

### 2.1 REVELATION and BECOME

No REVELATION construction is attested in P4a or P4b. The data provide no construction in which a truth-domain state is framed as coming to light, being uncovered, or being disclosed to a perceiver. The BECOME operator is directly attested (*pramā jāyate*, *mithyājñānaṃ nivartate*), but it is framed as production or cessation of an epistemic state, not as disclosure or revelation of a pre-existing fact. There is therefore no evidence that REVELATION substitutes for BECOME or vice versa; BECOME is present and REVELATION is absent, with no substitution relationship attested.

### 2.2 VERIFICATION and CAUSATION (factual)

The closest attested construction to VERIFICATION is *satyāpana* (morphological causative, legal-administrative register), which carries the meaning "causing the truth of a statement to be established, attestation." This construction is rated [MARGINAL] and is explicitly restricted to legal and administrative Sanskrit. In the philosophical and epistemological registers, no dedicated VERIFICATION construction is attested. The *pramāṇīkaraṇa* periphrastic causative covers "making something into a valid authority" (validation, authentication) but is described as more prominent in modern Sanskrit-influenced administrative usage rather than classical philosophical Sanskrit. The data therefore support the following characterization: in the legal-administrative register, VERIFICATION is expressed through the CAUSATION (factual) construction (*satyāpana*); in the philosophical-epistemological register, neither VERIFICATION nor CAUSATION (factual) is productively attested, and both are effectively absent from that domain.

### 2.3 DISCONFIRMATION

No DISCONFIRMATION construction is attested. The data include constructions for negating a predication (*bhramo na pramā*), for existential negation of an epistemic ground (*pramāṇaṃ nāsti*), and for the cessation of a false epistemic state (*mithyājñānaṃ nivartate*), but none of these constitutes a construction in which a claim is shown to be false or refuted by evidence. The existential negation *pramāṇaṃ nāsti* functions as a dismissal formula in philosophical debate (denying that any valid epistemic ground exists for a claim), which is functionally adjacent to DISCONFIRMATION, but P4b explicitly characterizes it as a denial of the existence of an epistemic ground rather than a demonstration that a claim is false. No substitution relationship for DISCONFIRMATION is attested in the data.

### 2.4 CAUSATION (character)

No construction expressing "X causes someone to be a truth-teller / to have a truth-related character property" is attested in P4a or P4b. The agentive nominalization *satyavādin* names the character type, and the prescriptive/injunctive construction (*satyaṃ brūyāt*) prescribes the conduct, but neither constitutes a causative construction targeting character. This gap is not filled by any substitution attested in the data.

### 2.5 Asymmetries: Positive vs. Negative Forms

The data show consistent symmetry between positive and negative forms in several constructions:

- **ATTR**: Both *satyaṃ paraṃ dharmaḥ* (positive) and *asatyaṃ pāpam* (negative subject) are attested as [CORE].
- **AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION**: Both *satyavādin* and *asatyavādin* / *mithyāvādin* are explicitly described as well-attested.
- **PRESCRIPTIVE/INJUNCTIVE**: Both the positive optative (*satyaṃ brūyāt*) and the negative prohibitive (*asatyaṃ na vadet*) are attested as [CORE].
- **ABLATIVE DEVIATION**: Both the negated-deviation form (*satyāt na pracalati*) and the positive-deviation form (*aucityāt cyutam*) are attested as [CORE].

One asymmetry is noted in the agentive nominalization domain: the *-vādin* pattern covers speech/conduct agents (both positive and negative), but the epistemological knower-of-truth role is filled by a structurally distinct form (*pramātṛ*, using the *-tṛ* suffix). No negative counterpart to *pramātṛ* (an agentive form for "one who produces false cognition" via the *-tṛ* suffix) is attested in the data.

### 2.6 Register and Domain Restrictions as Substitution Boundaries

A consistent cross-construction restriction is attested in P4b: the PRESCRIPTIVE/INJUNCTIVE (§8) and ABLATIVE DEVIATION (§9) constructions are explicitly restricted to speech-evaluating and conduct-evaluating contexts and do not extend to epistemological or ontological groups. This means that the constructions available in the ethical-normative domain are not available as substitutes in the epistemological domain, and the epistemological constructions (e.g., *pramā jāyate*, *pramāṇaṃ nāsti*) do not extend into the ethical-normative domain. The two domains use largely non-overlapping construction sets, with no cross-domain substitution attested.

### 2.7 NEGATION: Analytic vs. Morphological

Two distinct negation mechanisms are attested:

- **Analytic (syntactic) negation**: *na* + predicate nominal (property negation) and *nāsti* (existential negation). These are syntactic constructions operating at the sentence level.
- **Morphological negation**: The prefix *a-*/*an-* is attested in lexical items (*asatya*, *asatyavādin*, *mithyājñāna*), but P4a treats these as lexicalized compounds handled in P2a rather than as productive morphosyntactic negation constructions in the truth-domain construction inventory.

The data therefore support the characterization that productive NEGATION in this domain is primarily analytic (syntactic), with morphological negation present but treated as lexical rather than constructional.

### 2.8 VERIFICATION and DISCONFIRMATION: Shared Construction?

The data provide no evidence that VERIFICATION and DISCONFIRMATION share the same construction. VERIFICATION has a marginal, register-restricted attested form (*satyāpana*). DISCONFIRMATION has no attested form. They do not share a construction.

## 3. Missingness Summary

### 3.1 Structural Gaps
*(The meaning is not expressed by any attested construction in P4a/P4b)*

- **REVELATION**: No construction attested in which a truth-domain state is framed as coming to light or being disclosed. Not substituted by BECOME (which is present but distinct in framing).
- **CAUSATION (character)**: No construction attested for causing someone to have a truth-related character property.
- **DISCONFIRMATION**: No construction attested for showing a claim to be false or refuting it by evidence. The existential negation *nāsti* is functionally adjacent but is characterized in P4b as denial of an epistemic ground, not demonstration of falsity.

### 3.2 Lexical Gaps
*(The construction type exists but specific lexical items are absent)*

- No morphological causative on bare *satya-*, *pramā-*, or *dharma-* roots is attested in the philosophical register. The morphological causative is lexically restricted to the *satyā-* + causative-of-*āp-* pattern (*satyāpana*).
- No agentive form for "one who produces false cognition" via the *-tṛ* suffix is attested (the negative counterpart to *pramātṛ*).

### 3.3 Conceptual Gaps
*(The data provide no evidence that the relevant conceptual operation is available in this domain)*

- No evidence in P4a or P4b that REVELATION (truth coming to light, disclosure of a pre-existing fact) is a conceptually operative framing in the Sanskrit truth domain as documented. The BECOME operator is present but framed as production/cessation of epistemic states, not as disclosure.
- No evidence that DISCONFIRMATION (demonstrating falsity through evidence or argument) is expressed as a distinct construction type, as opposed to negation of a predication or denial of an epistemic ground.

### 3.4 Asymmetric Gaps
*(Only one polarity is attested)*

- **Agentive nominalization, epistemological domain**: *Pramātṛ* (positive: the knowing subject, one who produces valid cognition) is attested; no parallel *-tṛ*-suffix agentive for the producer of false cognition is attested in the data.
- **CAUSATION (factual)**: The attested causative constructions (*satyāpana*, *pramāṇīkaraṇa*) both operate in the positive direction (establishing truth, validating). No causative construction for "causing something to be false" or "causing an error to arise" is attested in P4a or P4b.
- **BECOME / inchoative**: *Pramā jāyate* (valid cognition arises, positive) and *mithyājñānaṃ nivartate* (false knowledge ceases, negative-domain cessation) are both attested, but the inverse pair — arising of false cognition and cessation of valid cognition — is not attested as a construction in P4a/P4b. The cessation form is restricted to the soteriological removal of a negative epistemic state.
