# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P6 CHANGE, CAUSATION, AND STABILITY
# Language: Tamil (ta)

## 1. BECOMING TRUE / ACQUIRING TRUTH STATUS

### 1.1 Standard Inchoative Construction

**Pattern:** TERM + ஆகு (āku) "become"

**Examples:**
- உண்மை ஆகு (uṇmai āku) → "become truth" → "become true, turn out to be true"
- உண்மையாக ஆகு (uṇmaiyāka āku) → "become as truth" → "become true"

**Usage:** ஆகு is the standard inchoative verb in Tamil. With truth-domain terms, it expresses change of state into truth or correctness. Common in contexts where something previously uncertain or false is revealed to be true.

**Attestation:** [CORE] - Standard productive pattern

---

### 1.2 Emergence/Revelation Construction

**Pattern:** உண்மை + வெளிப்படு (veḷippaṭu) "emerge, be revealed"

**Examples:**
- உண்மை வெளிப்படு (uṇmai veḷippaṭu) → "truth emerges" → "truth comes out, truth is revealed"
- உண்மை வெளிச்சத்திற்கு வரு (uṇmai veḷiccattiṟku varu) → "truth comes to light" → "truth is revealed"

**Usage:** Emphasizes the process of truth emerging from concealment or obscurity. வெளிப்படு "emerge, be revealed" is particularly common with உண்மை. வரு "come" in metaphorical constructions (வெளிச்சத்திற்கு "to light") expresses similar revelation dynamics.

**Attestation:** [CORE] - Very frequent in narrative and investigative contexts

---

### 1.3 Coming to be Known Construction

**Pattern:** உண்மை + தெரிய வரு (teriya varu) "come to be known"

**Examples:**
- உண்மை தெரிய வரு (uṇmai teriya varu) → "truth known come" → "truth comes to be known, truth becomes clear"
- உண்மை தெரிந்தது (uṉmai terintatu) → "truth became known" → "the truth became known"

**Usage:** Focuses on epistemic change - truth becoming known to observers. தெரி "appear, be visible, be known" + வரு "come" expresses gradual or eventual coming to knowledge. Common in contexts of discovery and investigation.

**Attestation:** [CORE] - Common in epistemic contexts

---

### 1.4 Becoming Clear/Evident Construction

**Pattern:** தெளிவு + ஆகு (āku) "become clear"

**Examples:**
- தெளிவாகு (teḷivāku) → "clarity-become" → "become clear"
- தெளிவு ஆகு (teḷivu āku) → "clarity become" → "become clear, be clarified"

**Usage:** Expresses change from unclear to clear state. தெளிவு "clarity" with ஆகு "become" is a common collocation for clarification processes. Can refer to both understanding becoming clear and situations becoming evident.

**Attestation:** [CORE] - Very common in explanatory contexts

---

### 1.5 Becoming Correct Construction

**Pattern:** சரி + ஆகு (āku) "become correct"

**Examples:**
- சரியாகு (cariyāku) → "correct-become" → "become correct, be corrected"
- சரி ஆகு (cari āku) → "correct become" → "become right, be fixed"

**Usage:** Expresses correction or fixing of errors. Common in contexts of repair, correction, and problem-solving. Can refer to both factual correction and situational improvement.

**Attestation:** [CORE] - Extremely common in everyday speech

---

### 1.6 Becoming Certain/Confirmed Construction

**Pattern:** உறுதி + ஆகு (āku) "become certain"

**Examples:**
- உறுதியாகு (uṟutiyāku) → "certainty-become" → "become certain, be confirmed"
- உறுதி ஆகு (uṟuti āku) → "certainty become" → "be confirmed, become definite"

**Usage:** Expresses change from uncertain to certain state. Common in contexts of confirmation, verification, and establishing facts. Frequently used in formal and legal contexts.

**Attestation:** [CORE] - Common in formal discourse

---

## 2. BECOMING FALSE / ACQUIRING FALSITY STATUS

### 2.1 Becoming Exposed as False Construction

**Pattern:** பொய் + அம்பலமாகு (ampalamāku) "become public/exposed"

**Examples:**
- பொய் அம்பலமாகு (poy ampalamāku) → "lie public-become" → "lie is exposed, lie becomes public"
- பொய் அம்பலம் ஆகு (poy ampalam āku) → "lie public become" → "lie is exposed"

**Usage:** Expresses lies being revealed or exposed. அம்பலம் "public, open" + ஆகு "become" is a common collocation for exposure of hidden things. Particularly common with பொய் for exposure of deception.

**Attestation:** [CORE] - Common in contexts of revelation and scandal

---

### 2.2 Being Caught/Discovered as False Construction

**Pattern:** பொய் + பிடிபடு (piṭipaṭu) "be caught"

**Examples:**
- பொய் பிடிபடு (poy piṭipaṭu) → "lie catch-become" → "lie is caught, lie is discovered"
- பொய் பிடிபட்டது (poy piṭipaṭṭatu) → "lie was caught" → "the lie was discovered"

**Usage:** Treats lies as objects that can be physically caught or captured. பிடிபடு "be caught, be captured" is a resultative verb. Common in everyday speech about deception being discovered.

**Attestation:** [CORE] - Very frequent in narrative contexts

---

### 2.3 Becoming Revealed as False Construction

**Pattern:** பொய் + வெளியாகு (veḷiyāku) "become exposed"

**Examples:**
- பொய் வெளியாகு (poy veḷiyāku) → "lie exposed-become" → "lie becomes exposed, lie comes out"
- பொய் வெளியானது (poy veḷiyāṉatu) → "lie became exposed" → "the lie was exposed"

**Usage:** Similar to அம்பலமாகு but emphasizes the process of coming out or being exposed. வெளி "outside" + ஆகு "become" expresses emergence from concealment. Common in investigative and journalistic contexts.

**Attestation:** [CORE] - Common in formal and narrative registers

---

### 2.4 Becoming Wrong/Mistaken Construction

**Pattern:** தப்பு/தவறு + ஆகு (āku) "become wrong"

**Examples:**
- தப்பாகு (tappāku) → "wrong-become" → "become wrong, go wrong"
- தவறாகு (tavaṟāku) → "mistake-become" → "become mistaken, turn out wrong"

**Usage:** Expresses something becoming incorrect or going wrong. Common in contexts where correct situations deteriorate or errors emerge. Can refer to both factual errors and moral wrongs.

**Attestation:** [CORE] - Common in everyday speech

---

### 2.5 Becoming Unclear/Ambiguous Construction

**Pattern:** தெளிவற்ற + ஆகு (āku) "become unclear"

**Examples:**
- தெளிவற்றதாகு (teḷivaṟṟatāku) → "unclear-become" → "become unclear, become ambiguous"
- தெளிவு இழ (teḷivu iḻa) → "clarity lose" → "lose clarity, become unclear"

**Usage:** Expresses loss of clarity or increase in ambiguity. Less common than positive "become clear" construction. Alternative construction uses இழ "lose" with தெளிவு "clarity."

**Attestation:** [MARGINAL] - Less frequent than positive counterpart

---

## 3. CAUSING TRUTH / MAKING TRUE

### 3.1 Standard Causative Construction

**Pattern:** TERM + accusative + VERB-causative (-உத்து -uttu)

**Examples:**
- உண்மையை வெளிப்படுத்து (uṇmaiyai veḷippaṭuttu) → "truth-ACC reveal-CAUS" → "reveal the truth, cause truth to be revealed"
- உண்மையை நிரூபி (uṇmaiyai nirūpi) → "truth-ACC prove" → "prove the truth"

**Usage:** Standard causative formation with -உத்து suffix on verb stems. வெளிப்படுத்து "reveal" (causative of வெளிப்படு "emerge") is the most common verb for causing truth to be revealed. நிரூபி "prove" (from Sanskrit) is used for establishing truth through evidence.

**Attestation:** [CORE] - Very common in investigative and argumentative contexts

---

### 3.2 Making Clear/Clarifying Construction

**Pattern:** தெளிவுபடுத்து (teḷivupaṭuttu) "make clear, clarify"

**Examples:**
- தெளிவுபடுத்து (teḷivupaṭuttu) → "clarity-become-CAUS" → "make clear, clarify"
- விஷயத்தை தெளிவுபடுத்து (viṣayattai teḷivupaṭuttu) → "matter-ACC clarify" → "clarify the matter"

**Usage:** NOUN + படுத்து (paṭuttu) "cause to become" pattern. தெளிவுபடுத்து is extremely common for causing clarity or making things clear. Very frequent in explanatory and instructional contexts.

**Attestation:** [CORE] - Extremely frequent across all registers

---

### 3.3 Confirming/Establishing as Certain Construction

**Pattern:** உறுதிபடுத்து (uṟutipaṭuttu) "confirm, establish as certain"

**Examples:**
- உறுதிபடுத்து (uṟutipaṭuttu) → "certainty-become-CAUS" → "confirm, make certain"
- உண்மையை உறுதிபடுத்து (uṇmaiyai uṟutipaṭuttu) → "truth-ACC confirm" → "confirm the truth"

**Usage:** Causative construction for establishing certainty or confirming facts. உறுதிபடுத்து is very common in formal, legal, and administrative contexts for verification and confirmation.

**Attestation:** [CORE] - Very common in formal discourse

---

### 3.4 Correcting/Making Right Construction

**Pattern:** சரி + செய் (cey) "make correct" / திருத்து (tiruttu) "correct"

**Examples:**
- சரி செய் (cari cey) → "correct make" → "correct, fix, make right"
- திருத்து (tiruttu) → "correct, amend, fix"

**Usage:** செய் "do, make" is the general causative verb. சரி செய் "make correct" is extremely common for fixing errors or correcting mistakes. திருத்து is a dedicated verb for correction and amendment. Both very frequent in everyday speech.

**Attestation:** [CORE] - Extremely common in everyday contexts

---

### 3.5 Proving/Demonstrating Truth Construction

**Pattern:** உண்மையை + நிரூபி (nirūpi) / நிறுவு (niṟuvu) "prove, establish"

**Examples:**
- உண்மையை நிரூபி (uṇmaiyai nirūpi) → "truth-ACC prove" → "prove the truth"
- உண்மையை நிறுவு (uṇmaiyai niṟuvu) → "truth-ACC establish" → "establish the truth"

**Usage:** நிரூபி (from Sanskrit) and நிறுவு are verbs for proving or establishing truth through evidence or demonstration. Common in argumentative, legal, and scientific contexts.

**Attestation:** [CORE] - Common in formal and argumentative discourse

---

### 3.6 Bringing Truth to Light Construction

**Pattern:** உண்மையை + வெளிச்சத்திற்கு கொண்டு வரு (veḷiccattiṟku koṇṭu varu)

**Examples:**
- உண்மையை வெளிச்சத்திற்கு கொண்டு வரு (uṇmaiyai veḷiccattiṟku koṇṭu varu) → "truth-ACC light-to bring come" → "bring the truth to light"

**Usage:** Metaphorical causative construction. கொண்டு வரு "bring" (literally "taking come") is a compound verb for bringing/causing to come. வெளிச்சம் "light" is the metaphorical destination. Common in journalistic and investigative contexts.

**Attestation:** [CORE] - Common in narrative and journalistic registers

---

## 4. CAUSING FALSITY / MAKING FALSE

### 4.1 Exposing Lies Construction

**Pattern:** பொய்யை + அம்பலப்படுத்து (ampalapaṭuttu) "expose"

**Examples:**
- பொய்யை அம்பலப்படுத்து (poyyai ampalapaṭuttu) → "lie-ACC public-become-CAUS" → "expose the lie"
- பொய்யை வெளிப்படுத்து (poyyai veḷippaṭuttu) → "lie-ACC reveal-CAUS" → "reveal the lie, expose the lie"

**Usage:** Causative constructions for exposing or revealing lies. அம்பலப்படுத்து "expose" (causative of அம்பலமாகு "become public") is common for deliberate exposure. வெளிப்படுத்து "reveal" also used with பொய்.

**Attestation:** [CORE] - Common in investigative and confrontational contexts

---

### 4.2 Catching/Discovering Lies Construction

**Pattern:** பொய்யை + பிடி (piṭi) "catch"

**Examples:**
- பொய்யை பிடி (poyyai piṭi) → "lie-ACC catch" → "catch the lie, discover the lie"
- பொய் பிடித்தேன் (poy piṭittēṉ) → "lie caught-I" → "I caught the lie"

**Usage:** Treats lies as objects that can be caught. பிடி "catch, capture" is the transitive causative counterpart to பிடிபடு "be caught." Common in everyday speech about discovering deception.

**Attestation:** [CORE] - Common in everyday speech

---

### 4.3 Creating/Fabricating Falsehood Construction

**Pattern:** பொய் + உருவாக்கு (uruvākku) "create, fabricate"

**Examples:**
- பொய் உருவாக்கு (poy uruvākku) → "lie create" → "create lies, fabricate falsehood"
- பொய் கட்டு (poy kaṭṭu) → "lie tie/construct" → "fabricate lies, concoct lies"

**Usage:** Causative constructions for creating or fabricating lies. உருவாக்கு "create, form" is formal. கட்டு "tie, construct" is colloquial and metaphorical (literally "tie" or "construct"). Both common for deliberate lie creation.

**Attestation:** [CORE] - Common across registers

---

### 4.4 Making Something Wrong/Incorrect Construction

**Pattern:** OBJECT + தப்பாக்கு (tappākku) "make wrong"

**Examples:**
- தப்பாக்கு (tappākku) → "wrong-make-CAUS" → "make wrong, cause to be wrong"
- கணக்கை தப்பாக்கு (kaṇakkai tappākku) → "calculation-ACC make-wrong" → "make the calculation wrong"

**Usage:** Causative construction with ஆக்கு (causative of ஆகு "become"). Less common than positive "make correct" construction. Used when someone deliberately or accidentally causes something to become wrong.

**Attestation:** [MARGINAL] - Less frequent than positive counterpart

---

### 4.5 Confusing/Making Unclear Construction

**Pattern:** OBJECT + குழப்பு (kuḻappu) "confuse, make unclear"

**Examples:**
- குழப்பு (kuḻappu) → "confuse, make unclear, muddle"
- விஷயத்தை குழப்பு (viṣayattai kuḻappu) → "matter-ACC confuse" → "confuse the matter, make unclear"

**Usage:** குழப்பு is a dedicated verb for causing confusion or making things unclear. Common in contexts of deliberate obfuscation or accidental confusion. Not directly derived from truth-domain vocabulary but functionally relevant.

**Attestation:** [CORE] - Very common in everyday speech

---

## 5. MAINTAINING TRUTH / KEEPING TRUE

### 5.1 Maintaining/Preserving Truth Construction

**Pattern:** உண்மையை + காத்து (kāttu) "protect, maintain"

**Examples:**
- உண்மையை காத்து (uṇmaiyai kāttu) → "truth-ACC protect" → "protect the truth, maintain truth"
- உண்மையை காப்பாற்று (uṇmaiyai kāppāṟṟu) → "truth-ACC save/preserve" → "preserve the truth"

**Usage:** காத்து "protect, guard, maintain" and காப்பாற்று "save, preserve" are verbs for maintaining or preserving truth. Common in contexts of defending truth against attack or distortion.

**Attestation:** [CORE] - Common in moral and protective contexts

---

### 5.2 Maintaining Honesty/Integrity Construction

**Pattern:** நேர்மையை + காத்து (kāttu) / கடைப்பிடி (kaṭaipiṭi)

**Examples:**
- நேர்மையை காத்து (nērmaiyai kāttu) → "honesty-ACC protect" → "maintain honesty, preserve integrity"
- நேர்மையை கடைப்பிடி (nērmaiyai kaṭaipiṭi) → "honesty-ACC adhere-to" → "practice honesty, adhere to integrity"

**Usage:** காத்து "protect, maintain" and கடைப்பிடி "adhere to, practice" are common verbs with நேர்மை. Both express maintaining or upholding honesty as a practice or principle. Very common in moral instruction.

**Attestation:** [CORE] - Very common in moral discourse

---

### 5.3 Keeping/Holding to Truth Construction

**Pattern:** உண்மையில் + இரு (iru) "be, remain"

**Examples:**
- உண்மையில் இரு (uṇmaiyil iru) → "truth-in remain" → "remain in truth, stay true"
- உண்மையாக இரு (uṇmaiyāka iru) → "truth-as be" → "be truthful, remain true"

**Usage:** இரு "be, remain, stay" expresses stative maintenance of truth. உண்மையில் இரு "remain in truth" and உண்மையாக இரு "be truthful" are constructions for maintaining truthful state. Common in moral and spiritual discourse.

**Attestation:** [CORE] - Common in moral contexts

---

### 5.4 Upholding/Supporting Truth Construction

**Pattern:** உண்மையை + ஆதரி (ātari) "support" / தாங்கு (tāṅku) "uphold"

**Examples:**
- உண்மையை ஆதரி (uṇmaiyai ātari) → "truth-ACC support" → "support the truth"
- உண்மையை தாங்கு (uṇmaiyai tāṅku) → "truth-ACC uphold" → "uphold the truth"

**Usage:** ஆதரி "support" and தாங்கு "uphold, bear" are verbs for actively supporting or upholding truth. Common in contexts of defending truth or taking a stand for truth.

**Attestation:** [CORE] - Common in argumentative and moral contexts

---

## 6. MAINTAINING FALSITY / KEEPING FALSE

### 6.1 Maintaining Lies/Deception Construction

**Pattern:** பொய்யை + தொடர் (toṭar) "continue"

**Examples:**
- பொய்யை தொடர் (poyyai toṭar) → "lie-ACC continue" → "continue the lie, maintain the deception"
- பொய்யில் தொடர் (poyyil toṭar) → "lie-in continue" → "continue in lies"

**Usage:** தொடர் "continue" expresses ongoing maintenance of lies or deception. Less common than truth-maintenance constructions, but attested in contexts of sustained deception.

**Attestation:** [MARGINAL] - Less frequent than truth-maintenance

---

### 6.2 Hiding/Concealing Truth (Maintaining Concealment) Construction

**Pattern:** உண்மையை + மறை (maṟai) "hide, conceal"

**Examples:**
- உண்மையை மறை (uṇmaiyai maṟai) → "truth-ACC hide" → "hide the truth, conceal the truth"
- உண்மையை மறைத்து வை (uṇmaiyai maṟaittu vai) → "truth-ACC hide-and keep" → "keep the truth hidden"

**Usage:** மறை "hide, conceal" is the primary verb for concealing truth. வை "keep, put" in compound மறைத்து வை "keep hidden" emphasizes ongoing concealment. Very common in contexts of deliberate suppression of truth.

**Attestation:** [CORE] - Very common in investigative and moral contexts

---

### 6.3 Covering Up/Suppressing Truth Construction

**Pattern:** உண்மையை + மூடி மறை (mūṭi maṟai) "cover and hide"

**Examples:**
- உண்மையை மூடி மறை (uṇmaiyai mūṭi maṟai) → "truth-ACC cover hide" → "cover up the truth"
- உண்மையை அடக்கு (uṇmaiyai aṭakku) → "truth-ACC suppress" → "suppress the truth"

**Usage:** மூடி மறை "cover and hide" is an intensified compound for covering up. அடக்கு "suppress, repress" is used for forceful suppression of truth. Both common in contexts of deliberate concealment or cover-up.

**Attestation:** [CORE] - Common in investigative and political contexts

---

## 7. LOSING TRUTH / CEASING TO BE TRUE

### 7.1 Losing Truth/Veracity Construction

**Pattern:** உண்மை + இழ (iḻa) "lose"

**Examples:**
- உண்மை இழ (uṇmai iḻa) → "truth lose" → "lose truth, lose veracity"
- உண்மைத்தன்மை இழ (uṇmaittaṉmai iḻa) → "truthfulness lose" → "lose truthfulness"

**Usage:** இழ "lose" expresses loss of truth or truthfulness. Less common than positive acquisition constructions. Used when something previously true becomes false or loses credibility.

**Attestation:** [MARGINAL] - Less frequent than positive counterparts

---

### 7.2 Becoming Doubtful/Uncertain Construction

**Pattern:** சந்தேகம் + வரு (varu) "come" / ஏற்படு (ēṟpaṭu) "arise"

**Examples:**
- சந்தேகம் வரு (cantēkam varu) → "doubt come" → "doubt arises, doubt comes"
- சந்தேகம் ஏற்படு (cantēkam ēṟpaṭu) → "doubt arise" → "doubt arises, uncertainty occurs"

**Usage:** வரு "come" and ஏற்படு "arise, occur" express emergence of doubt or uncertainty. Common in contexts where previously certain things become doubtful. Represents loss of certainty rather than direct loss of truth.

**Attestation:** [CORE] - Common in epistemic contexts

---

### 7.3 Losing Clarity Construction

**Pattern:** தெளிவு + இழ (iḻa) "lose"

**Examples:**
- தெளிவு இழ (teḷivu iḻa) → "clarity lose" → "lose clarity, become unclear"
- தெளிவு போ (teḷivu pō) → "clarity go" → "clarity is lost"

**Usage:** இழ "lose" and போ "go" express loss of clarity. Common in contexts where understanding or situation becomes less clear. போ "go" is colloquial alternative to இழ "lose."

**Attestation:** [CORE] - Common in everyday speech

---

## 8. RESISTING CHANGE / STABILITY

### 8.1 Truth Remaining/Persisting Construction

**Pattern:** உண்மை + நிலை (nilai) "remain, stand"

**Examples:**
- உண்மை நிலைக்கு (uṇmai nilaikkum) → "truth will-stand" → "truth will remain, truth will persist"
- உண்மை நிலைத்து நிற்கு (uṇmai nilaitttu niṟku) → "truth standing stand" → "truth stands firm"

**Usage:** நிலை "stand, remain, persist" expresses stability and persistence of truth. நிற்கு "stand" in compound நிலைத்து நிற்கு "stand firm" emphasizes endurance. Common in proverbial and philosophical contexts about truth's persistence.

**Attestation:** [CORE] - Common in philosophical and proverbial discourse

---

### 8.2 Truth Prevailing/Triumphing Construction

**Pattern:** உண்மை + வெல்லு (vellu) "win, triumph"

**Examples:**
- உண்மை வெல்லும் (uṇmai vellum) → "truth will-win" → "truth will triumph, truth will prevail"
- உண்மை இறுதியில் வெல்லும் (uṇmai iṟutiyil vellum) → "truth finally will-win" → "truth will triumph in the end"

**Usage:** வெல்லு "win, conquer, triumph" expresses truth's ultimate victory. உண்மை இறுதியில் வெல்லும் "truth will triumph in the end" is a proverbial expression. Common in moral and philosophical discourse about truth's ultimate persistence.

**Attestation:** [CORE] - Common in proverbial and moral discourse

---

### 8.3 Unchanging Truth Construction

**Pattern:** மாறாத (māṟāta) + உண்மை (uṇmai) "unchanging truth"

**Examples:**
- மாறாத உண்மை (māṟāta uṇmai) → "unchanging truth" → "unchanging truth, eternal truth"
- நிலையான உண்மை (nilaiyāṉa uṇmai) → "stable truth" → "stable truth, permanent truth"

**Usage:** மாறாத "unchanging" (negative participle of மாறு "change") and நிலையான "stable, permanent" modify உண்மை to express unchanging or eternal truth. Common in philosophical and religious discourse.

**Attestation:** [CORE] - Common in philosophical contexts

---

## 9. PREVENTING CHANGE / BLOCKING DYNAMICS

### 9.1 Preventing Truth from Emerging Construction

**Pattern:** உண்மை + வெளிப்பட + விட (viṭa) + மாட்டேன் (māṭṭēṉ) "won't let"

**Examples:**
- உண்மை வெளிப்பட விடமாட்டேன் (uṇmai veḷippaṭa viṭamāṭṭēṉ) → "truth emerge let-won't-I" → "I won't let the truth emerge"
- உண்மை தெரிய விடாதே (uṇmai teriya viṭātē) → "truth known let-not" → "don't let the truth be known"

**Usage:** விட "let, allow" in negative constructions expresses prevention. மாட்டேன் "won't" (negative future) and negative imperative விடாதே "don't let" are common patterns for blocking truth emergence.

**Attestation:** [CORE] - Common in contexts of suppression

---

### 9.2 Preventing Lies from Being Exposed Construction

**Pattern:** பொய் + வெளியாக + விட (viṭa) + மாட்டேன் (māṭṭēṉ) "won't let"

**Examples:**
- பொய் வெளியாக விடமாட்டேன் (poy veḷiyāka viṭamāṭṭēṉ) → "lie exposed let-won't-I" → "I won't let the lie be exposed"
- பொய் பிடிபட விடாதே (poy piṭipaṭa viṭātē) → "lie caught let-not" → "don't let the lie be caught"

**Usage:** Same negative விட "let" pattern applied to preventing exposure of lies. Common in contexts of maintaining deception or avoiding discovery.

**Attestation:** [CORE] - Common in narrative contexts

---

### 9.3 Blocking Clarification Construction

**Pattern:** தெளிவுபட + விட (viṭa) + மாட்டேன் (māṭṭēṉ) "won't let become clear"

**Examples:**
- தெளிவுபட விடமாட்டேன் (teḷivupaṭa viṭamāṭṭēṉ) → "clear-become let-won't-I" → "I won't let it become clear"
- தெளிவாக விடாதே (teḷivāka viṭātē) → "clearly let-not" → "don't let it be clear"

**Usage:** Negative விட pattern applied to blocking clarification. Used in contexts of deliberate obfuscation or maintaining ambiguity.

**Attestation:** [MARGINAL] - Less common than truth/lie prevention constructions

---

## 10. GRADUAL VS. SUDDEN CHANGE

### 10.1 Gradual Emergence of Truth Construction

**Pattern:** உண்மை + கசந்து வரு (kacantu varu) "leak out"

**Examples:**
- உண்மை கசந்து வரு (uṇmai kacantu varu) → "truth leak-and come" → "truth leaks out, truth gradually emerges"
- உண்மை மெதுவாக வெளிப்படு (uṇmai metuvāka veḷippaṭu) → "truth slowly emerge" → "truth emerges slowly"

**Usage:** கசந்து வரு "leak out" (கசி "leak" + வரு "come") expresses gradual emergence. மெதுவாக "slowly" with வெளிப்படு "emerge" explicitly marks gradual process. Common in contexts where truth emerges piece by piece.

**Attestation:** [CORE] - Common in narrative contexts

---

### 10.2 Sudden Revelation of Truth Construction

**Pattern:** உண்மை + திடீரென்று (tiṭīreṉṟu) + வெளிப்படு (veḷippaṭu) "suddenly emerge"

**Examples:**
- உண்மை திடீரென்று வெளிப்படு (uṇmai tiṭīreṉṟu veḷippaṭu) → "truth suddenly emerge" → "truth suddenly emerges"
- உண்மை ஒரே நேரத்தில் தெரிந்தது (uṇmai orē nērattil terintatu) → "truth one time-in became-known" → "the truth became known all at once"

**Usage:** திடீரென்று "suddenly" and ஒரே நேரத்தில் "at one time, all at once" mark sudden revelation. Contrasts with gradual emergence constructions. Common in narrative contexts of dramatic revelation.

**Attestation:** [CORE] - Common in narrative contexts

---

### 10.3 Gradual Clarification Construction

**Pattern:** படிப்படியாக (paṭippaṭiyāka) + தெளிவாகு (teḷivāku) "gradually become clear"

**Examples:**
- படிப்படியாக தெளிவாகு (paṭippaṭiyāka teḷivāku) → "step-by-step clarity-become" → "gradually become clear"
- மெதுவாக தெளிவு வரு (metuvāka teḷivu varu) → "slowly clarity come" → "clarity comes slowly"

**Usage:** படிப்படியாக "step by step, gradually" and மெதுவாக "slowly" mark gradual process of clarification. Common in contexts of progressive understanding or explanation.

**Attestation:** [CORE] - Common in explanatory contexts

---

## 11. ASPECTUAL DISTINCTIONS

### 11.1 Completed Change: Truth Has Emerged

**Pattern:** உண்மை + வெளிப்பட்டது (veḷippaṭṭatu) "has emerged" (perfective)

**Examples:**
- உண்மை வெளிப்பட்டது (uṇmai veḷippaṭṭatu) → "truth emerged-it" → "the truth has emerged"
- உண்மை தெரிந்து விட்டது (uṇmai terintu viṭṭatu) → "truth known-and finished-it" → "the truth has become known (completely)"

**Usage:** Past tense forms with perfective aspect (marked by -ட்ட- -ṭṭa- past marker). விட்டது (viṭṭatu) "has finished" adds completive aspect. Common for reporting completed revelation or discovery.

**Attestation:** [CORE] - Very common in narrative and reportage

---

### 11.2 Ongoing Process: Truth Is Emerging

**Pattern:** உண்மை + வெளிப்படுகிறது (veḷippaṭukiṟatu) "is emerging" (progressive)

**Examples:**
- உண்மை வெளிப்படுகிறது (uṇmai veḷippaṭukiṟatu) → "truth is-emerging" → "the truth is emerging"
- உண்மை தெரிய வருகிறது (uṇmai teriya varukiṟatu) → "truth known is-coming" → "the truth is coming to be known"

**Usage:** Present progressive forms with -கிறது (-kiṟatu) present marker. Expresses ongoing process of emergence or revelation. Common in contexts of investigation or gradual discovery.

**Attestation:** [CORE] - Common in progressive contexts

---

### 11.3 Habitual/Repeated Change

**Pattern:** உண்மை + எப்போதும் (eppōtum) + வெல்லும் (vellum) "always triumphs"

**Examples:**
- உண்மை எப்போதும் வெல்லும் (uṇmai eppōtum vellum) → "truth always will-win" → "truth always triumphs"
- பொய் இறுதியில் பிடிபடும் (poy iṟutiyil piṭipaṭum) → "lie finally will-be-caught" → "lies are eventually caught"

**Usage:** எப்போதும் "always" and இறுதியில் "finally, eventually" mark habitual or inevitable patterns. Future tense -உம் (-um) expresses general truth. Common in proverbial and philosophical discourse.

**Attestation:** [CORE] - Common in proverbial contexts

---

## 12. EXPRESSIONS NOT COMMONLY ATTESTED

While grammatically possible to construct expressions like *பகுதியாக உண்மையாகு "become partially true," no such fixed expressions were attested in the data. Truth is generally treated as categorical rather than gradable in dynamic constructions.

**Status:** [UNCERTAIN] - No common expression identified

No evidence for comparative causative constructions like *"make X more true" or *"increase truthfulness." Truth-domain terms resist degree modification in causative constructions, consistent with their categorical nature.

## SUMMARY OBSERVATIONS

### High-Frequency Dynamic Patterns

**Most common change constructions:**
1. **Inchoative with ஆகு:** TERM + ஆகு "become" - highly productive
2. **Emergence with வெளிப்படு:** உண்மை வெளிப்படு "truth emerges" - very frequent
3. **Coming to knowledge:** உண்மை தெரிய வரு "truth comes to be known" - common
4. **Becoming clear:** தெளிவாகு "become clear" - extremely frequent

**Most common causative constructions:**
1. **Revealing truth:** உண்மையை வெளிப்படுத்து "reveal the truth" - very frequent
2. **Clarifying:** தெளிவுபடுத்து "make clear" - extremely frequent
3. **Confirming:** உறுதிபடுத்து "confirm" - common in formal contexts
4. **Correcting:** சரி செய் "make correct" - extremely frequent

**Most common maintenance constructions:**
1. **Hiding truth:** உண்மையை மறை "hide the truth" - very frequent
2. **Maintaining honesty:** நேர்மையை கடைப்பிடி "practice honesty" - common
3. **Protecting truth:** உண்மையை காத்து "protect the truth" - common

### Notable Asymmetries

1. **Truth emergence vs. lie exposure:** Truth "emerges" (வெளிப்படு) naturally; lies are "caught" (பிடிபடு) or "exposed" (அம்பலமாகு) - different metaphorical framings

2. **Positive vs. negative causatives:** Constructions for making true/clear are more frequent and productive than constructions for making false/unclear

3. **Maintenance asymmetry:** Truth-maintenance constructions (காத்து "protect," கடைப்பிடி "practice") are more common than falsity-maintenance constructions

4. **Aspectual marking:** Completed change (perfective) more commonly marked than ongoing process for truth emergence

### Metaphorical Patterns

1. **Light metaphor:** Truth "comes to light" (வெளிச்சத்திற்கு வரு) - common across languages
2. **Capture metaphor:** Lies are "caught" (பிடிபடு) like physical objects
3. **Covering metaphor:** Truth is "hidden" (மறை) or "covered up" (மூடி மறை)
4. **Victory metaphor:** Truth "triumphs" (வெல்லு) or "prevails" over falsehood

### Gaps and Restrictions

1. **No degree modification in dynamics:** Truth doesn't "become more true" or "increase in truthfulness"
2. **No reciprocal constructions:** No fixed expressions for mutual truth-telling
3. **Limited benefactive causation:** No idiomatic "reveal truth for X's sake" constructions
4. **Categorical treatment:** Truth-domain terms resist gradual or partial change expressions in most contexts
