## 2. Substitution Patterns (Narrative)

### 2.1 BECOME ↔ REVELATION Overlap

The language shows **functional overlap** between BECOME and REVELATION operators, particularly through the verb வரு (varu) "come":

- **உண்மை தெரிய வரு** (uṇmai teriya varu) "truth comes to be known" can be analyzed as either:
  - BECOME: "truth comes into a state of being known" (inchoative)
  - REVELATION: "truth is revealed/becomes apparent" (epistemic emergence)

The construction is ambiguous between pure inchoative change-of-state and epistemic revelation. This suggests that **Tamil does not sharply distinguish becoming-true from being-revealed** in certain constructions.

Additionally:
- Pure BECOME uses ஆகு (āku): **பொய் அம்பலமாகு** "lie becomes exposed"
- Pure REVELATION uses வெளிப்படு (veḷippaṭu): **உண்மை வெளிப்படு** "truth is revealed"
- Overlapping construction uses வரு (varu): **உண்மை வெளிச்சத்திற்கு வரு** "truth comes to light" (both inchoative and revelatory)

**Pattern**: REVELATION constructions frequently incorporate inchoative semantics; BECOME constructions with வரு frequently incorporate epistemic/revelatory semantics. The boundary is permeable.

### 2.2 VERIFICATION Uses சரி "Correct" Not உண்மை "Truth"

The VERIFICATION operator systematically uses **சரி** (cari) "correct, right" rather than **உண்மை** (uṇmai) "truth":

- **சரி பார்** (cari pār) "check, verify" (lit. "see correct")
- **உறுதிப்படுத்து** (uṟutipaṭuttu) "confirm" (lit. "cause to become certain")

This represents a **lexical substitution**: verification operations target correctness/certainty rather than truth per se. The language does not have a common expression *"உண்மை பார்" for "verify truth" — instead, it "checks correctness" or "confirms certainty."

**Pattern**: VERIFICATION domain prefers சரி and உறுதி over உண்மை.

### 2.3 DISCONFIRMATION Asymmetry: Applies to Falsehood Only

DISCONFIRMATION constructions are **asymmetrically restricted to பொய்** (poy) "lie/falsehood":

- **பொய் பிடிபடு** (poy piṭipaṭu) "lie is caught"
- **பொய் வெளியாகு** (poy veḷiyāku) "lie becomes exposed"

There are **no parallel constructions** for "truth is caught" or "truth is exposed" — these would be semantically anomalous. The metaphor of "catching" or "exposing" applies specifically to hidden falsehoods, not to truths.

**Pattern**: DISCONFIRMATION is conceptually restricted to negative truth-values. The language does not support symmetric disconfirmation of truth.

### 2.4 Agentive Nominalization: Productive Only for "Liar"

Agentive derivation (CAUSATION-character) shows **severe asymmetry**:

- **பொய்யன்** (poyyan) "liar" — productive agentive nominalization from பொய் "lie"
- **NO FORM** for "truth-teller" from உண்மை — requires periphrasis: **உண்மை பேசுபவர்** (uṇmai pēcupavar) "person who speaks truth"

This is a **structural gap**: the agentive nominalization pattern (TERM + person marker) is restricted to பொய் and does not extend to உண்மை. The language lacks a morphological strategy for deriving "truth-teller" and must use multi-word circumlocution.

**Pattern**: Character-based causation (habitual truth-telling/lying) is morphologically available only for negative pole (lying).

### 2.5 NEGATION: Dual Strategy (Analytic + Morphological)

Tamil employs **two parallel negation strategies** without functional distinction:

1. **Analytic negation**: TERM + இல்லை (illai) — predicative
   - **அது உண்மை இல்லை** (atu uṇmai illai) "that is not true"

2. **Morphological negation**: TERM + அற்ற (aṟṟa) — attributive
   - **உண்மையற்ற** (uṇmaiyaṟṟa) "untrue, without truth"

The choice depends on **syntactic position**: இல்லை for predicates, -அற்ற for attributive adjectives. Both are fully productive and freely available.

**Pattern**: NEGATION is not a single construction but a syntactically conditioned alternation between analytic and morphological strategies.

### 2.6 Emphatic Particle Stacking

Tamil shows a **productive emphatic intensification pattern** not directly mappable to CLCA operators but pervasive across truth-domain constructions:

- **உண்மையிலேயே** (uṇmaiyi-l-ē-y-ē) "truly indeed, really and truly"
- **நிஜமாகவே** (nijamāka-v-ē) "really indeed"

This involves **particle stacking** (-ஏ emphatic, -ய்/-வ் euphonic) and is extremely frequent in colloquial speech. It functions as an **intensification operator** applicable to multiple base constructions (locative, adverbial, etc.).

**Pattern**: Emphasis/intensification is a productive cross-cutting operation, not a discrete CLCA category but a modifier applicable to ATTR, ADNOM, and other operators.

## 3. Missingness Summary

### 3.1 Structural Gaps

**CAUSATION (character) — Agentive nominalization for "truth-teller"**
- **Gap**: No productive morphological derivation for agent nouns from உண்மை "truth"
- **Evidence**: Agentive pattern (TERM + person marker) restricted to பொய் only: பொய்யன் "liar" exists, but *உண்மையன் "truth-teller" does not
- **Circumlocution required**: உண்மை பேசுபவர் (uṇmai pēcupavar) "person who speaks truth" (multi-word periphrasis)
- **Type**: Morphosyntactic gap — the derivational pattern exists but is lexically restricted

### 3.2 Lexical Gaps

**None identified**. All core CLCA operators have lexical realizations in Tamil, though some show restricted productivity (see structural gaps above).

### 3.3 Conceptual Gaps

**DISCONFIRMATION of truth (symmetric to disconfirmation of falsehood)**
- **Gap**: No construction for "catching" or "exposing" truth parallel to பொய் பிடிபடு "lie is caught"
- **Evidence**: Disconfirmation metaphors (catching, exposing) apply only to பொய் "lie/falsehood," not to உண்மை "truth"
- **Reason**: Conceptual asymmetry — truths are revealed/discovered, not "caught" or "exposed" (which presuppose hiddenness or deception)
- **Type**: Conceptual gap — the operation is semantically blocked for positive truth-values

### 3.4 Asymmetric Gaps

**3.4.1 Agentive Nominalization (CAUSATION-character)**
- **Available for**: பொய் "lie" → பொய்யன் "liar"
- **Missing for**: உண்மை "truth" → *உண்மையன் (unattested)
- **Asymmetry type**: Morphological productivity restricted to negative pole

**3.4.2 Disconfirmation**
- **Available for**: பொய் "lie" → பொய் பிடிபடு "lie is caught"
- **Missing for**: உண்மை "truth" → *உண்மை பிடிபடு (semantically anomalous)
- **Asymmetry type**: Conceptual restriction to negative truth-values

**3.4.3 Locative/Discourse Marker Productivity**
- **Highly productive for**: உண்மையில் (uṇmaiyil) "in truth, actually" — extremely frequent
- **Marginal for**: பொய்யில் (poyyil) "in falsehood" — literary/formal only
- **Asymmetry type**: Frequency/register asymmetry favoring positive pole

### 3.5 Register-Based Marginal Availability

**Classical/Literary Nominalizations**
- **-மை (-mai) abstract nominalization**: பொய்மை (poymai) "falsehood" — classical/literary, marginal in colloquial
- **-இன்மை (-iṉmai) absence nominalization**: உண்மையின்மை (uṇmaiyiṉmai) "absence of truth" — formal/literary, less common than -அற்ற alternative

**Type**: Not true gaps, but register-restricted availability. Modern colloquial Tamil prefers -தன்மை (-taṉmai) for abstractions and -அற்ற (-aṟṟa) for negation.

## 5. Summary of Key Findings

1. **BECOME ↔ REVELATION overlap**: வரு (varu) "come" constructions blur the boundary between inchoative and epistemic emergence.

2. **VERIFICATION lexical substitution**: Uses சரி "correct" and உறுதி "certainty" rather than உண்மை "truth."

3. **Asymmetric productivity**:
   - Agentive nominalization: productive for பொய் "lie" (பொய்யன் "liar"), not for உண்மை "truth"
   - Disconfirmation: applies to பொய் only (பொய் பிடிபடு "lie is caught")
   - Locative discourse marker: உண்மையில் highly frequent, பொய்யில் marginal

4. **Dual negation strategy**: Analytic (இல்லை) for predicates, morphological (-அற்ற) for attributive adjectives.

5. **No true structural gaps**: All operators realized, but some require circumlocution (CAUSATION-character for உண்மை) or show conceptual restrictions (DISCONFIRMATION for உண்மை).

6. **Register stratification**: Classical patterns (-மை, -இன்மை) marginal in colloquial speech; modern patterns (-தன்மை, -அற்ற) preferred.
