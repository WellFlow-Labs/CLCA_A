# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2a DERIVATIONAL SYSTEM
# Language: Tamil (ta)

---

## DERIVATIONAL PATTERNS IDENTIFIED

Based on the P1 vocabulary and P1b root expansions, the following derivational patterns are attested in Tamil truth-domain vocabulary:

---

### PATTERN 1: Abstract Noun Formation with -மை (-mai)

**Pattern description:** The suffix -மை (-mai) attaches to roots or adjectives to form abstract nouns denoting qualities or states.

#### 1.1
**Base form:** உண்மை (uṇmai) — already contains -mai suffix
**Derived form:** உண்மைத்தன்மை
**Transliteration:** uṇmaittaṉmai
**Literal breakdown:** uṇmai (truth) + taṉmai (quality, nature)
**Translation:** truthfulness, the quality of being true
**Usage:** Used in formal discourse to refer to the abstract quality or property of truthfulness, especially when discussing the nature of truth itself or evaluating whether something possesses the quality of truth.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 1.2
**Base form:** பொய் (poy)
**Derived form:** பொய்ம்மை
**Transliteration:** poymai
**Literal breakdown:** poy (false) + -mai (abstract noun suffix)
**Translation:** falseness, falsehood, the quality of being false
**Usage:** Abstract noun referring to the quality or state of being false. More formal and literary than simple poy, used in philosophical and analytical discourse about the nature of falsity.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 1.3
**Base form:** மெய் (mey)
**Derived form:** மெய்ம்மை
**Transliteration:** meymai
**Literal breakdown:** mey (truth) + -mai (abstract noun suffix)
**Translation:** truth, reality, genuineness
**Usage:** Abstract noun emphasizing the quality of being true or real. More formal and literary than simple mey, commonly used in classical Tamil literature and philosophical discourse.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 1.4
**Base form:** நேர் (nēr) "straight, direct"
**Derived form:** நேர்மை
**Transliteration:** nērmai
**Literal breakdown:** nēr (straight) + -mai (abstract noun suffix)
**Translation:** honesty, integrity, uprightness
**Usage:** Refers to the quality of moral honesty and straightforwardness in character and conduct. Very common in evaluating personal integrity and ethical behavior.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### PATTERN 2: Adjectival Formation with -ஆன (-āṉa)

**Pattern description:** The suffix -ஆன (-āṉa) attaches to nouns to form adjectives meaning "that which is X" or "being X."

#### 2.1
**Base form:** உண்மை (uṇmai)
**Derived form:** உண்மையான
**Transliteration:** uṇmaiyāṉa
**Literal breakdown:** uṇmai (truth) + -āṉa (adjectival suffix)
**Translation:** true, genuine, real, authentic
**Usage:** Adjectival form used attributively to modify nouns, indicating that something is true, genuine, or authentic. Extremely common across all registers and contexts.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 2.2
**Base form:** பொய் (poy)
**Derived form:** பொய்யான
**Transliteration:** poyyāṉa
**Literal breakdown:** poy (false) + -āṉa (adjectival suffix)
**Translation:** false, fake, untrue, counterfeit
**Usage:** Adjectival form used to describe false or fake things. Very common in evaluating statements, objects, and behaviors as inauthentic or deceptive.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 2.3
**Base form:** மெய் (mey)
**Derived form:** மெய்யான
**Transliteration:** meyyāṉa
**Literal breakdown:** mey (truth) + -āṉa (adjectival suffix)
**Translation:** true, real, genuine
**Usage:** Adjectival form indicating truth or genuineness. Used across registers to describe authentic things or true statements, though somewhat more formal than uṇmaiyāṉa.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 2.4
**Base form:** சரி (cari)
**Derived form:** சரியான
**Transliteration:** cariyāṉa
**Literal breakdown:** cari (correct) + -āṉa (adjectival suffix)
**Translation:** correct, right, proper, accurate
**Usage:** Adjectival form indicating correctness or rightness. Extremely common in everyday speech and writing to describe correct answers, proper behavior, or appropriate actions.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 2.5
**Base form:** தவறு (tavaṟu)
**Derived form:** தவறான
**Transliteration:** tavaṟāṉa
**Literal breakdown:** tavaṟu (error) + -āṉa (adjectival suffix)
**Translation:** wrong, incorrect, mistaken, erroneous
**Usage:** Adjectival form describing incorrect or mistaken things. Common in both formal and informal contexts for evaluating errors and mistakes.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 2.6
**Base form:** நேர்மை (nērmai)
**Derived form:** நேர்மையான
**Transliteration:** nērmaiyāṉa
**Literal breakdown:** nērmai (honesty) + -āṉa (adjectival suffix)
**Translation:** honest, upright, straightforward, sincere
**Usage:** Adjectival form describing honest or upright persons and conduct. Very common in character evaluation and moral discourse.

#### 2.7
**Base form:** நம்பகம் (nampakam)
**Derived form:** நம்பகமான
**Transliteration:** nampakamāṉa
**Literal breakdown:** nampakam (trustworthiness) + -āṉa (adjectival suffix)

#### 2.8
**Base form:** மெய்ம்மை (meymai)
**Derived form:** மெய்ம்மையான
**Transliteration:** meymaiyāṉa

### PATTERN 4: Emphatic Adverbial Formation with -ஆகவே (-ākavē)

**Pattern description:** The suffix combination -ஆகவே (-ākavē) = -āka (adverbial) + -ē (emphatic particle) creates emphatic adverbs.

**Base form:** உண்மை (uṇmai)
**Derived form:** உண்மையாகவே
**Transliteration:** uṇmaiyākavē
**Literal breakdown:** uṇmai (truth) + -āka (adverbial) + -ē (emphatic)
**Translation:** truly indeed, really and truly
**Usage:** Doubly emphatic adverbial form for strong affirmation of truth. Used for emphasis in both formal and informal contexts when strongly asserting truthfulness.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 4.2
**Base form:** பொய் (poy)
**Derived form:** பொய்யாகவே
**Transliteration:** poyyākavē
**Literal breakdown:** poy (false) + -āka (adverbial) + -ē (emphatic)
**Translation:** falsely indeed, certainly as a lie
**Usage:** Doubly emphatic adverbial form for strong assertion of falseness. Used when emphatically condemning something as false.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 4.3
**Base form:** மெய் (mey)
**Derived form:** மெய்யாகவே
**Transliteration:** meyyākavē
**Literal breakdown:** mey (truth) + -āka (adverbial) + -ē (emphatic)
**Translation:** truly indeed, really and truly
**Usage:** Doubly emphatic adverbial form for strong affirmation. Used for emphasis when strongly asserting genuineness or truth.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## NOTES ON DERIVATIONAL PRODUCTIVITY

### High Productivity Patterns:

1. **Adjectival -ஆன (-āṉa)**: Extremely productive with truth-domain nouns. Nearly all truth-domain nouns can take this suffix to form adjectives.

2. **Adverbial -ஆக (-āka)**: Highly productive with truth-domain nouns to form manner adverbs.

3. **Emphatic -ஏ (-ē)**: Very productive, can attach to most truth-domain nouns and adverbs for emphasis.

4. **Compound formation with common nouns**: Highly productive patterns include compounding with:
   - கதை (katai) "story"
   - நிலை (nilai) "state"
   - பொருள் (poruḷ) "meaning/object"
   - உணர்வு (uṇarvu) "awareness"
   - அறிவு (aṟivu) "knowledge"

### Moderate Productivity Patterns:

1. **Agent noun formation**: Multiple suffixes (-ஆளர், -ஆன், -ஆளி, -அர்) exist but show some lexical restrictions and register differences.

2. **Abstract noun -மை (-mai)**: Productive but often lexicalized in specific forms.

3. **Causative -படுத்து (-paṭuttu)**: Productive with truth-domain nouns but semantically restricted to verification/authentication meanings.

### Limited Productivity Patterns:

1. **Compound with ஞானம் (ñāṉam) "wisdom"**: Restricted to philosophical/religious register, primarily with மெய் (mey).

2. **Compound with இயல் (iyal) "science/study"**: Restricted to academic/technical terminology.

### Asymmetries Noted:

1. **பொய் (poy)** shows much higher productivity in forming compounds denoting specific types of falsehood (false documents, false testimony, etc.) compared to உண்மை (uṇmai) and மெய் (mey).

2. **மெய் (mey)** shows higher productivity in philosophical/spiritual compounds (மெய்ஞ்ஞானம், மெய்யுணர்வு, மெய்யியல்) compared to the other roots.

3. **உண்மை (uṇmai)** shows balanced productivity across administrative, legal, and everyday domains.

4. Negative/pejorative compounds (false X, fake X) are much more numerous and productive than positive/authenticating compounds (true X, genuine X).

## GAPS AND NON-DERIVATIONS

The following expected derivations are **NOT attested** or are **extremely marginal**:

1. **No productive diminutive formation**: Tamil truth-domain vocabulary does not show productive diminutive derivation.

2. **No productive augmentative formation**: No augmentative suffixes apply productively to truth-domain vocabulary.

3. **Limited reciprocal formation**: No productive reciprocal derivations identified in truth-domain.

4. **No productive iterative/frequentative formation**: Truth-domain verbs do not show productive iterative derivation.

5. **Restricted negative prefix formation**: While அநியாயம் (aṉiyāyam) "injustice" exists (from அ- negative + நியாயம்), this pattern is not productive across other truth-domain terms. Forms like *அஉண்மை or *அமெய் are not attested.
