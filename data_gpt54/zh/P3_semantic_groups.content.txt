Below are **natural usage-based groupings** that emerge from the P1 and P2 material.

---

## 1. Everyday true / false contrast terms

These expressions are used in ordinary speech and writing to distinguish whether something is real, true, or fake. They often appear in direct contrast sets and in simple judgments about statements, goods, or events.

**Representative expressions**
- 真 (zhēn)
- 假 (jiǎ)
- 真假 (zhēnjiǎ)
- 真话 (zhēnhuà)
- 假话 (jiǎhuà)
- 真有此事 (zhēn yǒu cǐ shì)
- 说真的 (shuō zhēn de)
- 当真 (dàngzhēn)

**Ambiguous or overlapping items**
- 真实 (zhēnshí) — overlaps with more formal factual-reporting language
- 真伪 (zhēnwěi) — similar contrast, but more formal than 真假
- 千真万确 (qiān zhēn wàn què) — stronger, emphatic affirmation rather than neutral classification

---

## 2. Formal factual verification and rebuttal language

These items are common in official statements, investigations, journalism, and administrative writing when claims are checked against facts. They are often used in formulas for confirming, denying, or criticizing reports and allegations.

**Representative expressions**
- 属实 (shǔshí)
- 经查属实 (jīng chá shǔshí)
- 查证属实 (cházhèng shǔshí)
- 情况是否属实 (qíngkuàng shìfǒu shǔshí)
- 如实 (rúshí)
- 如实回答 (rúshí huídá)
- 如实报告 (rúshí bàogào)
- 如实说明情况 (rúshí shuōmíng qíngkuàng)
- 不实 (bùshí)
- 内容不实 (nèiróng bùshí)
- 报道不实 (bàodào bùshí)
- 纯属不实 (chúnshǔ bùshí)
- 失实 (shīshí)
- 报道严重失实 (bàodào yánzhòng shīshí)
- 与事实不符，内容失实 (yǔ shìshí bù fú, nèiróng shīshí)

**Ambiguous or overlapping items**
- 真实 (zhēnshí) — often used in the same domains, but broader than verification formulas
- 虚假 (xūjiǎ) — overlaps when the issue is not just inaccuracy but deception
- 准确 (zhǔnquè) — overlaps where factual fit and precision are both relevant

---

## 3. Falsehood, deception, and fabrication in legal-regulatory use

These expressions are used where falsity is treated as deceptive conduct, often in law, regulation, policing, or public criticism. They commonly refer to false information, forged materials, misleading promotion, or deliberate fabrication.

**Representative expressions**
- 虚假 (xūjiǎ)
- 发布虚假信息 (fābù xūjiǎ xìnxī)
- 虚假陈述 (xūjiǎ chénshù)
- 存在虚假宣传 (cúnzài xūjiǎ xuānchuán)
- 伪造 (wěizào)
- 伪造证件 (wěizào zhèngjiàn)
- 伪造签名 (wěizào qiānmíng)
- 伪造事实 (wěizào shìshí)
- 伪造者 (wěizàozhě)
- 伪造罪 (wěizàozuì)
- 弄虚作假 (nòng xū zuò jiǎ)

**Ambiguous or overlapping items**
- 假货 (jiǎhuò), 假证 (jiǎzhèng) — also fit counterfeit-goods/document groupings
- 不实 (bùshí), 失实 (shīshí) — may describe false content without necessarily implying deliberate fraud
- 冒牌 (màopái) — overlaps when deception involves brand or identity passing-off

---

## 4. Correctness and error in answers, judgments, and handling

These expressions are used to evaluate whether an answer, judgment, method, or response is right or wrong. They are common in education, management, public discourse, and practical instruction.

**Representative expressions**
- 正确 (zhèngquè)
- 错误 (cuòwù)
- 正误 (zhèngwù)
- 正解 (zhèngjiě)
- 作出正确判断 (zuòchū zhèngquè pànduàn)
- 树立正确观念 (shùlì zhèngquè guānniàn)
- 正确对待 (zhèngquè duìdài)
- 犯错误 (fàn cuòwù)
- 承认错误 (chéngrèn cuòwù)
- 纠正错误 (jiūzhèng cuòwù)
- 错误地 (cuòwù de)

**Ambiguous or overlapping items**
- 准确 (zhǔnquè) — often close to 正确, but commonly stresses precision
- 正当 (zhèngdàng) — concerns justified conduct rather than simply correct answers
- 正确性 (zhèngquèxìng) — more formal evaluative noun use

---

## 5. Precision, exact expression, and error-free reporting

These items are used when the issue is not just being right in general, but being exact, precise, or free from distortion in wording, data, measurement, or description. They are common in technical, educational, and analytical settings.

**Representative expressions**
- 准确 (zhǔnquè)
- 准确性 (zhǔnquèxìng)
- 准确地 (zhǔnquè de)
- 准确把握 (zhǔnquè bǎwò)
- 准确表达 (zhǔnquè biǎodá)
- 准确无误 (zhǔnquè wúwù)
- 失真 (shīzhēn)
- 声音失真 (shēngyīn shīzhēn)
- 图像失真 (túxiàng shīzhēn)
- 信息传递过程中发生失真 (xìnxī chuándì guòchéng zhōng fāshēng shīzhēn)

**Ambiguous or overlapping items**
- 真实 (zhēnshí) — may also evaluate faithfulness to reality
- 失实 (shīshí) — overlaps when a report departs from facts rather than from an original signal or wording
- 逼真 (bīzhēn) — also concerns resemblance, but usually in aesthetic depiction rather than precision

---

## 6. Credibility and dependability of information, evidence, and sources

These expressions are used when speakers evaluate whether something deserves belief or can be relied on. In practice, they often apply to testimony, evidence, reports, sources, methods, or systems.

**Representative expressions**
- 可信 (kěxìn)
- 可信度 (kěxìndù)
- 可信性 (kěxìnxìng)
- 来源可信 (láiyuán kěxìn)
- 这一说法不太可信 (zhè yī shuōfǎ bù tài kěxìn)
- 可信度不高 (kěxìndù bù gāo)
- 可靠 (kěkào)
- 可靠性 (kěkàoxìng)
- 证据可靠 (zhèngjù kěkào)
- 消息可靠吗？(xiāoxi kěkào ma)
- 提供可靠依据 (tígōng kěkào yījù)

**Ambiguous or overlapping items**
- 属实 (shǔshí) — stronger, since it indicates verified truth rather than mere believability
- 准确 (zhǔnquè) — concerns correctness/precision rather than trustworthiness
- 正当 (zhèngdàng) — not about belief, but about legitimacy

---

## 7. Genuine goods, authentic products, and counterfeit contrasts

These expressions are used in commerce, appraisal, consumer talk, and figurative extension to distinguish authentic goods or credentials from fake ones. They often cluster around branded products, documents, collectibles, and market trust.

**Representative expressions**
- 真货 / 真品 (zhēnpǐn)
- 假货 (jiǎhuò)
- 冒牌 (màopái)
- 冒牌货 (màopáihuò)
- 冒牌产品 (màopái chǎnpǐn)
- 正品 (zhèngpǐn)
- 正牌 (zhèngpái)
- 正货 (zhènghuò)
- 货真价实 (huò zhēn jià shí)
- 东西货真价实 (dōngxi huò zhēn jià shí)
- 货真价实的产品 (huò zhēn jià shí de chǎnpǐn)
- 真伪 (zhēnwěi)
- 辨别真伪 implied by P2a usage note

**Ambiguous or overlapping items**
- 伪造品 (wěizàopǐn) — overlaps with legal-forensic forgery language
- 假证 (jiǎzhèng) — document-focused, partly legal rather than commercial
- 货真价实 used figuratively for people or qualifications, not only goods

---

## 8. Authentic identity, title, or reputation matching reality

These expressions are used when a name, title, qualification, or identity is judged to match what the person or thing really is. They often appear in praise, appraisal, or contrast with imposture and inflated claims.

**Representative expressions**
- 名副其实 (míng fù qí shí)
- 名副其实的专家 (míng fù qí shí de zhuānjiā)
- 可谓名副其实 (kěwèi míng fù qí shí)
- 这才是名副其实的…… (zhè cái shì míng fù qí shí de ...)
- 真名实姓 (zhēn míng shí xìng)
- 真身 (zhēnshēn)
- 真容 (zhēnróng)
- 他可是货真价实的博士 (tā kě shì huò zhēn jià shí de bóshì)

**Ambiguous or overlapping items**
- 冒牌专家 (màopái zhuānjiā) — belongs here and also in counterfeit/deceptive passing-off
- 正宗 (zhèngzōng) — can also fit cultural-style authenticity
- 真正 (zhēnzhèng) — broad and often used for “real” in many contexts, not only title matching

---

## 9. Lifelike resemblance, simulation, and realistic depiction

These expressions are used for things that look, sound, or feel close to reality without necessarily being real. They are common in art, acting, design, media, engineering, and product description.

**Representative expressions**
- 仿真 (fǎngzhēn)
- 仿真度 (fǎngzhēndù)
- 仿真器 (fǎngzhēnqì)
- 仿真模型 (fǎngzhēn móxíng)
- 仿真机器人 (fǎngzhēn jīqìrén)
- 高仿真 (gāo fǎngzhēn)
- 逼真 (bīzhēn)
- 逼真地 (bīzhēn de)
- 形象逼真 (xíngxiàng bīzhēn)
- 描写得很逼真 (miáoxiě de hěn bīzhēn)
- 效果逼真 (xiàoguǒ bīzhēn)
- 真实感 (zhēnshígǎn)
- 逼真感 (bīzhēngǎn)

**Ambiguous or overlapping items**
- 失真 (shīzhēn) — related through representation fidelity, but marks failure of faithful reproduction rather than successful realism
- 以假乱真 (yǐ jiǎ luàn zhēn) — overlaps when imitation becomes hard to distinguish from the real
- 真实 (zhēnshí) — can describe realism in representation as well as factual truth

---

## 10. Original/local-style authenticity in food, culture, and presentation

These expressions are used when something is valued for retaining an original style, local character, or traditional standard. They are especially common in food writing, tourism, cultural presentation, and some specialist product domains.

**Representative expressions**
- 原汁原味 (yuán zhī yuán wèi)
- 保留原汁原味 (bǎoliú yuán zhī yuán wèi)
- 原汁原味地呈现 (yuán zhī yuán wèi de chéngxiàn)
- 充满原汁原味的地方特色 (chōngmǎn yuán zhī yuán wèi de dìfāng tèsè)
- 道地 (dàodì)
- 味道很道地 (wèidào hěn dàodì)
- 做得很道地 (zuò de hěn dàodì)
- 道地药材 (dàodì yàocái)
- 正宗 (zhèngzōng)

**Ambiguous or overlapping items**
- 正牌 / 正品 — authentic, but more commercial-brand oriented
- 名副其实 — can praise something as genuinely deserving its label, but not specifically original-style authenticity
- 货真价实 — emphasizes genuineness and value, not necessarily local/traditional character

---

## 11. Substance, essence, and looking past surface form

These expressions are used in analytical, philosophical, legal, and policy-oriented language when speakers focus on what something really is underneath appearances, labels, or procedures. They often occur in argumentative frames like “from the standpoint of essence/substance.”

**Representative expressions**
- 本质 (běnzhì)
- 从本质上看 (cóng běnzhì shàng kàn)
- 揭示本质 (jiēshì běnzhì)
- 看清问题的本质 (kànqīng wèntí de běnzhì)
- 本质上 (běnzhì shàng)
- 实质 (shízhì)
- 从实质上看 (cóng shízhì shàng kàn)
- 没有实质区别 (méiyǒu shízhì qūbié)
- 取得实质进展 (qǔdé shízhì jìnzhǎn)
- 实质上 (shízhì shàng)
- 实质性 (shízhìxìng)

**Ambiguous or overlapping items**
- 真实 (zhēnshí) — may also point to reality behind appearances, but is broader
- 名副其实 (míng fù qí shí) — also compares name and reality, though in a more evaluative idiomatic way
- 真相 (zhēnxiàng) from root expansion is related, but not part of P1 proper

---

## 12. Legitimacy, justified conduct, and proper rights

These expressions are used where the issue is whether an action, reason, right, or procedure is proper or justified. They are especially common in legal, moral, and institutional contexts rather than in simple truth/falsity judgments.

**Representative expressions**
- 正当 (zhèngdàng)
- 正当理由 (zhèngdàng lǐyóu)
- 正当权益 (zhèngdàng quányì)
- 属于正当防卫 (shǔyú zhèngdàng fángwèi)
- 正当性 (zhèngdàngxìng)
- 正当地 (zhèngdàng de)

**Ambiguous or overlapping items**
- 正确 (zhèngquè) — sometimes nearby in practical advice, but usually about rightness/correctness rather than legitimacy
- 公正 (gōngzhèng), 正义 (zhèngyì) from root expansion — related in broader normative use, though not P1 items
- 正宗 (zhèngzōng) — shares 正 but belongs to authenticity-of-origin usage

---

## Items that do not fit cleanly into one group

Some expressions are too broad, too idiomatic, or too context-dependent to place neatly in a single usage cluster:

- **真实 (zhēnshí)** — used for factual truth, realistic representation, and authenticity more broadly
- **真 (zhēn)** — basic and highly flexible across many contexts
- **真正 (zhēnzhèng)** — broad contrastive use for “real/genuine” in many domains
- **货真价实 (huò zhēn jià shí)** — commercial authenticity, but also figurative for qualifications and persons
- **正宗 (zhèngzōng)** — can refer to authentic origin, orthodox lineage, or culturally accepted “real version”
- **以假乱真 (yǐ jiǎ luàn zhēn)** — sits between counterfeit, imitation, and realistic resemblance
- **失真 (shīzhēn)** — technical distortion, representational unfaithfulness, and extended discourse distortion
