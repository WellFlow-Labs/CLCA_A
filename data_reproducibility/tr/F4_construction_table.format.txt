| ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION | doğru bilgi | doğru bilgi | "correct information" | Completely natural and productive. Used across all registers. No restrictions on truth-domain adjectives modifying nouns. | [CORE] |

| VERBAL PHRASE (LIGHT VERBS) | haksızlık etmek | haksızlık etmek | "to wrong someone, to commit injustice" | Natural in formal and everyday contexts. Light verb constructions are highly productive in Turkish across truth domain. | [CORE] |
| MANNER ADVERBIALS | dürüstçe | dürüstçe | "honestly, in an honest manner" | Productive pattern for manner adverbs. Common in both spoken and written registers. No restrictions on formation from truth-domain adjectives. | [CORE] |

| RESULTATIVE/OUTCOME | doğru çıkmak | doğru çıkmak | "to turn out to be true" | Very common in everyday speech and narrative contexts. Expresses outcome of verification or revelation. Natural with truth-domain predicates. | [CORE] |
| RESULTATIVE/OUTCOME | yalan çıkmak | yalan çıkmak | "to turn out to be a lie" | Frequent in narrative and evaluative contexts. Implies prior uncertainty or claim that is now resolved. Fully idiomatic. | [CORE] |

| FIXED COLLOCATIONS (BODY/SPATIAL) | gerçek yüz | gerçek yüz | "true face, real nature" | Semi-lexicalized metaphorical expression. Common in evaluative/character assessment contexts. Natural in both spoken and written registers. | [CORE] |
| FIXED COLLOCATIONS (BODY/SPATIAL) | doğru yol | doğru yol | "right path, correct way" | Frequent in both literal (spatial) and metaphorical (moral/methodological) senses. Fully natural in all registers. | [CORE] |
| EVALUATIVE (BULMAK/GÖRMEK) | doğru bulmak | doğru bulmak | "to find correct, to deem appropriate" | Common in evaluative and deliberative contexts. Expresses considered judgment. Natural across registers. | [CORE] |
| EVALUATIVE (BULMAK/GÖRMEK) | şüpheli görünmek | şüpheli görünmek | "to look suspicious" | Frequent in everyday speech and narrative. Expresses subjective impression or appearance. Productive with truth-domain adjectives. | [CORE] |
