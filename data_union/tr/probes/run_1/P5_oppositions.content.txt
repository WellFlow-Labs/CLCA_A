# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P5 Oppositions
# Language: Turkish (tr)

---

## Opposition 1

1. **Term A:** doğru
2. **Term B:** yanlış
3. **Transliteration:** doğru / yanlış
4. **English translation:** correct, true / wrong, incorrect, false
5. **Description of contrast:** The primary antonym pair in the truth-evaluation domain; *doğru* affirms correspondence of a statement or action to fact or norm, while *yanlış* denotes deviation from correctness. The data explicitly identifies *yanlış* as "the primary antonym of *doğru*" and the pair appears together in the formulaic construction *doğru mu yanlış mı?* and in *doğruyu yanlıştan ayırt etmek*.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## Opposition 2

1. **Term A:** doğru
2. **Term B:** doğru değil
3. **Transliteration:** doğru / doğru değil
4. **English translation:** correct / not correct
5. **Description of contrast:** The morphosyntactic negation of *doğru* via the invariant negation particle *değil*; the data documents *Bu bilgi doğru değil* as the standard negation construction for adjectival predicates in this domain. This is a constructional negation rather than a lexical antonym, and it coexists with the lexical antonym *yanlış*.
6. **Contrast type:** constructional
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 3

1. **Term A:** doğruluk
2. **Term B:** yanlışlık
3. **Transliteration:** doğruluk / yanlışlık
4. **English translation:** truthfulness, correctness, accuracy / error, mistake, incorrectness, wrongness
5. **Description of contrast:** The nominalized property forms of the *doğru*/*yanlış* pair; *doğruluk* names the quality of being true or accurate, while *yanlışlık* names the quality or instance of being wrong or incorrect. Both are documented as [CORE] derivations of their respective bases.
6. **Contrast type:** morphological
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 4

1. **Term A:** doğrulamak / doğrulama / doğrulanabilir / doğrulanabilirlik
2. **Term B:** yanlışlamak / yanlışlama / yanlışlanabilir / yanlışlanabilirlik
3. **Transliteration:** doğrulamak / yanlışlamak; doğrulama / yanlışlama; doğrulanabilir / yanlışlanabilir; doğrulanabilirlik / yanlışlanabilirlik
4. **English translation:** to verify / to falsify; verification / falsification; verifiable / falsifiable; verifiability / falsifiability
5. **Description of contrast:** A parallel morphological series in which the *doğru-* and *yanlış-* bases generate structurally identical derivations encoding opposite epistemic operations. The data explicitly notes that *yanlışlama ilkesi* (the principle of falsification) "directly parallels *doğrulama*" and that *yanlışlanabilirlik* "directly parallels *doğrulanabilirlik*." The pair encodes the logical positivist / Popperian distinction in Turkish academic discourse.
6. **Contrast type:** morphological
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE] for the verbal and process nominal forms; [MARGINAL] for the ability/potential nominalizations (*doğrulanabilirlik*, *yanlışlanabilirlik*)

## Opposition 5

1. **Term A:** gerçek
2. **Term B:** sahte
3. **Transliteration:** gerçek / sahte
4. **English translation:** real, genuine, actual / fake, counterfeit, forged, spurious
5. **Description of contrast:** The central antonym pair in the ontological genuineness domain; *gerçek* affirms factual or ontological reality, while *sahte* denotes objects, documents, or identities presented as genuine but not. The data identifies these as the "central antonym pair" in Group 2 and documents the construction *gerçeği sahteden ayırt etmek* (to distinguish the genuine from the fake).
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]
