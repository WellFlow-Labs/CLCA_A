## Alternate Constructions

| Construction Type | Alternate Construction | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **1. ATTRIBUTION** (truth) | *Se pitää paikkansa.* | Positive counterpart of *ei pidä paikkaansa*; attested and common for asserting propositional truth colloquially. |
| **1. ATTRIBUTION** (truth, negative) | *ei ole totta* | The negative of *olla totta* retains the partitive: *se ei ole totta*. Covered under Pattern 2 but noted here as the direct negative alternate. |
| **2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION** | *ei ole tosi* / *ei ole oikein* | Compositional negated copula; grammatical and attested but less idiomatic than *ei pidä paikkaansa* for propositional falsity in spoken Finnish. |
| **2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION** | *ei ole totta* | Direct negative copular alternate to the idiomatic *ei pidä paikkaansa*; both are attested and in common use. |
| **2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION** | *epä-* prefixation ↔ *ei ole* + adjective | Both strategies are fully grammatical; *epä-* forms tend toward formal/written use, *ei ole* toward spoken registers. Neither is restricted to a subset of truth-domain adjectives. |
| **4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE** | *käydä toteen* | Attested periphrastic for predictions or claims "proving true"; more formal or literary in register than *toteutua*. |
| **4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE** | *käydä* + TRANSLATIVE; *tulla* + TRANSLATIVE | Attested as alternates to *osoittautua* + translative for change-of-state. |
| **4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE** | *osoittautua* + translative ↔ *paljastua* + translative or bare | *Paljastua* is attested as an alternate inchoative specifically for the revelation of hidden truth; it is not interchangeable with *osoittautua* in all contexts. |
| **5. CAUSATIVE** | *todentaa*, *oikaista* | Individually lexicalized morphological causatives; not instances of a productive suffix pattern across the domain. |
| **5. CAUSATIVE** | Morphological causative ↔ periphrastic *saada* + passive participle | The periphrastic causative with *saada* is noted as existing but is not the dominant pattern in the truth domain; no attested examples are provided in P4a. |
| **6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION** | *-uus/-yys* ↔ *-minen* ↔ *-us/-ys* | Three nominalization suffixes are attested with distinct semantic functions: *-uus/-yys* for abstract property nouns, *-minen* for process/event nominals, *-us/-ys* for result or act nominals (*todistus*, *oikeutus*, *todiste*). |
| **11. PERIPHRASTIC STANCE** | *kokea* + translative (*kokea väite vääräksi*) | Attested but less frequent; encodes subjective experiential stance rather than evaluative or official determination. |
| **13. CORRESPONDENCE CONSTRUCTION** | *pitää paikkansa* (with subject NP) | Overlaps functionally with the correspondence construction when the subject is a proposition-denoting NP; encodes truth-holding rather than explicit two-place correspondence. |
| **14. CONFORMITY COMPOUND ADJECTIVE** | NP-GEN + *vastainen* (contrary to) | Antonym pattern: *totuudenvastainen* (contrary to truth); *totuudenvastaisuus* (untruthfulness). Productive across the domain. |
