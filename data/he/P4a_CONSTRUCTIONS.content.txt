# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P4a_CONSTRUCTIONS
# Language: Hebrew (he)

## 1. ATTRIBUTION

### Pattern
**Subject + copula היה/הוא + truth-domain adjective/noun**
Predicative constructions using present-tense copula (pronoun) or past/future forms of היה "to be."

### Examples
- זה אמת
  - ze emet
  - "this is truth / this is true"

- הדבר נכון
  - hadavar nakhon
  - "the thing is correct"

### Notes
Hebrew present-tense copula is typically a pronoun (הוא, היא, זה) or zero. Past/future use conjugated forms of היה. Adjectives agree in gender/number with subject.

---

## 2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION

### Pattern
**Subject + לא + adjective/noun** OR **אין + subject + adjective**
Negation with לא before predicate or existential negation with אין.

### Examples
- זה לא נכון
  - ze lo nakhon
  - "this is not correct"

- אין בזה אמת
  - eyn baze emet
  - "there is no truth in this"

### Notes
Standard negation uses לא. Existential negation אין common with abstract nouns (אין אמת "there is no truth"). Both patterns highly productive.

---

## 3. ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION

### Pattern
**Noun + attributive adjective** (with gender/number agreement)
Adjectives follow nouns and agree in definiteness, gender, and number.

### Examples
- סיפור אמיתי
  - sipur amiti
  - "true story"

- עדות שקר
  - edut sheker
  - "false testimony"

### Notes
Adjectives follow nouns. Definiteness spreads (הסיפור האמיתי "the true story"). Some truth-terms function as nouns in construct state (עד שקר "false witness").

---

## 4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE

### Pattern
**להיות + adjective** OR **להפוך ל- + noun**
"To become" expressed with להיות or להפוך ל- (literally "to turn into").

### Examples
- להיות כנה
  - lihyot kene
  - "to become honest / to be honest"

- הפך לשקרן
  - hafakh leshakran
  - "became a liar"

### Notes
להיות ambiguous between stative "be" and inchoative "become" (context-dependent). להפוך ל- explicitly inchoative. Common with agent nouns (שקרן "liar").

---

## 5. CAUSATIVE

### Pattern
**Hif'il binyan verbs** (causative conjugation pattern)
Root-based causative formation through hif'il pattern (ה- prefix, -i- vowel).

### Examples
- להאמין
  - leha'amin
  - "to cause to be firm → to believe, to make believe"

- להצדיק
  - lehatsדik
  - "to cause to be right → to justify"

### Notes
Hif'il binyan creates causatives from roots: א-מ-נ → האמין "made firm/believed," צ-ד-ק → הצדיק "made right/justified." Productive morphological pattern.

---

## 6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION

### Pattern
**Adjective/root + suffix -ות (-ut)**
Abstract noun formation via feminine plural suffix -ות.

### Examples
- אמיתי → אמיתות
  - amiti → amitiyut
  - "true → authenticity/truthfulness"

- נאמן → נאמנות
  - ne'eman → ne'emanut
  - "faithful → faithfulness/loyalty"

### Notes
Highly productive pattern: adjective + -ות creates abstract quality noun. Also applies to participles (מהימן → מהימנות "reliable → reliability").

---

## 7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION

### Pattern
**Root/noun + suffix -ן (-an)** OR **active participles**
Agent nouns via -ן suffix (habitual) or participial forms.

### Examples
- שקר → שקרן
  - sheker → shakran
  - "lie → liar (habitual)"

- מאמין
  - ma'amin
  - "believer" (active participle of האמין)

### Notes
Suffix -ן indicates habitual/characteristic agent (שקרן "liar"). Active participles (בניין פעיל) also function as agent nouns (מאמין "believer," דובר שקרים "speaker of lies").

---

## 8. VERBAL CONSTRUCTIONS WITH LIGHT VERBS

### Pattern
**Light verb + truth-domain noun** (object or complement)
Common verbs: לומר "say," לדבר "speak," לעשות "do," לחפש "seek."

### Examples
- לומר את האמת
  - lomar et ha'emet
  - "to say the truth → to tell the truth"

- לעשות צדק
  - la'asot tsedek
  - "to do justice"

### Notes
Light verb constructions highly productive. Common patterns: לומר/לדבר + אמת/שקר (speech), לחפש/לגלות + אמת (discovery), לעשות + צדק (action).

---

## 9. INTENSIFICATION / DEGREE MODIFICATION

### Pattern
**Adjective + intensifier** OR **construct compounds**
Intensifiers: מאוד "very," לחלוטין "completely," גמור "complete," מוחלט "absolute."

### Examples
- ברור לחלוטין
  - barur leḥalutin
  - "clear completely → completely clear"

- שקר גמור
  - sheker gamur
  - "lie complete → complete lie"

### Notes
Post-adjectival intensifiers (מאוד, לחלוטין) or attributive adjectives (גמור, מוחלט, צרוף). Construct state creates emphatic compounds (אמת לאמיתה "truth to its truth").

---

## 10. COMPARATIVE CONSTRUCTIONS

### Pattern
**יותר + adjective** (more) OR **הכי + adjective** (most)
Analytic comparative/superlative with יותר/הכי + base adjective.

### Examples
- יותר נכון
  - yoter nakhon
  - "more correct"

- הכי מדויק
  - hakhi meduyak
  - "most precise"

### Notes
Hebrew uses analytic comparison: יותר "more" + adjective (comparative), הכי "most" + adjective (superlative). No synthetic forms. Common in precision/accuracy contexts.

---

## 11. PREPOSITIONAL PHRASE ADVERBIALS

### Pattern
**Preposition ב- "in/with" + noun**
Creates adverbial expressions from abstract nouns.

### Examples
- באמת
  - be'emet
  - "in truth → truly, really"

- בצדק
  - betsedek
  - "in justice → justly, rightly"

### Notes
Highly productive: ב- + truth-noun creates manner adverb. Also functions as discourse marker (באמת "really?"). Common with אמת, אמונה, צדק, יושר, כנות.

---

## 12. DOMAIN-SPECIFIC COMPOUNDS

### Pattern
**Truth-noun + domain adjective** (attributive modification)
Specifies type/domain of truth/justice through adjectival modification.

### Examples
- אמת מדעית
  - emet mada'it
  - "truth scientific → scientific truth"

- צדק חברתי
  - tsedek ḥevrati
  - "justice social → social justice"

### Notes
Productive pattern for creating domain-specific terms: base noun (אמת, צדק) + domain adjective (מדעית "scientific," היסטורית "historical," חברתי "social," משפטי "legal").

---

## 13. CONSTRUCT STATE COMPOUNDS

### Pattern
**Noun (construct form) + noun (absolute form)**
Genitive relationship through construct state (סמיכות).

### Examples
- אמת מידה
  - amat mida
  - "truth-of measure → criterion, standard"

- עד שקר
  - ed sheker
  - "witness-of falsehood → false witness"

### Notes
First noun takes construct form (אמת → אמת, עד unchanged). Creates fixed compounds. Common in legal/formal register (עדות שקר "false testimony," נביא שקר "false prophet").

---

## 14. DISCOURSE MARKERS AND TAG QUESTIONS

### Pattern
**Truth-term as sentence-final particle** OR **standalone question**
Pragmaticized uses seeking confirmation or framing discourse.

### Examples
- נכון?
  - nakhon?
  - "correct? → right?, isn't it?"

- האמת היא ש...
  - ha'emet hi she...
  - "the truth is that..."

### Notes
Several terms grammaticalized as discourse markers: נכון? (tag question), באמת? (surprise), ברור? (comprehension check), האמת היא ש... (discourse frame). High frequency in spoken Hebrew.

---

## 15. NEGATION OF POSSIBILITY/ABILITY

### Pattern
**אי אפשר ל- + infinitive** OR **קשה ל- + infinitive**
Impossibility/difficulty constructions with truth-verbs.

### Examples
- אי אפשר להאמין
  - i efshar leha'amin
  - "impossible to believe → unbelievable"

- קשה להאמין
  - kashe leha'amin
  - "difficult to believe → hard to believe"

### Notes
Modal constructions expressing epistemic stance. Common with להאמין "to believe." אי אפשר "impossible," קשה "difficult" + infinitive. Frequent in expressions of incredulity.

---

## 16. EXISTENTIAL CONSTRUCTIONS

### Pattern
**יש/אין + (ב-) + noun**
Existential affirmation/negation with truth-nouns.

### Examples
- יש בזה אמת
  - yesh baze emet
  - "there-is in-this truth → there's truth in this"

- אין זה שקר
  - eyn ze sheker
  - "there-is-not this lie → this is not a lie"

### Notes
יש "there is" / אין "there is not" with truth-nouns. Often with locative ב- (בזה "in this"). Common in concessions (יש בזה אמת) and denials (אין בזה אמת).

---

## 17. BINYAN-BASED VOICE ALTERNATIONS

### Pattern
**Active (pa'al/pi'el) ↔ Passive (nif'al/pu'al/huf'al)**
Voice alternations through verbal conjugation patterns.

### Examples
- לזייף → מזויף
  - lezayef → mezuyaf
  - "to forge → forged/fake" (pi'el → pu'al)

- להצדיק → מוצדק
  - lehatsדik → mutsдak
  - "to justify → justified" (hif'il → huf'al)

### Notes
Hebrew binyan system creates systematic voice alternations. Passive participles (מזויף "forged," מוכח "proven," מוצדק "justified") function as adjectives. Productive morphological pattern.

---

## 18. COORDINATE COMPOUNDS

### Pattern
**Noun + ו- + noun** (coordinating conjunction)
Paired truth-domain terms creating emphatic or contrastive expressions.

### Examples
- אמת או שקר
  - emet o sheker
  - "truth or falsehood"

- צדק וחסד
  - tsedek vaḥesed
  - "justice and mercy"

### Notes
Coordination with ו- "and" or או "or." Creates binary oppositions (אמת או שקר) or value pairings (צדק וחסד, אמת ושלום). Some fixed in religious/ethical discourse.

---

## 19. EMPHATIC REDUPLICATION

### Pattern
**Noun + prepositional phrase with possessive suffix**
Emphatic construction through prepositional elaboration.

### Examples
- אמת לאמיתה
  - emet la'amita
  - "truth to-its-truth → absolute truth"

### Notes
Emphatic pattern: noun + ל- + same noun with possessive suffix. Creates intensified meaning. Limited productivity (mainly with אמת). Colloquial register.

---

## 20. METAPHORICAL EXTENSIONS

### Pattern
**Truth-noun + metaphorical verb**
Verbs of revelation, concealment, emergence applied to truth.

### Examples
- האמת תצא לאור
  - ha'emet tetse la'or
  - "the truth will-exit to-light → the truth will come to light"

- להסתיר את האמת
  - lehasתir et ha'emet
  - "to hide the truth"

### Notes
Truth conceptualized as object that can be hidden (להסתיר), revealed (לגלות), sought (לחפש), or emerge (לצאת לאור). Productive metaphorical pattern across truth-domain nouns.
