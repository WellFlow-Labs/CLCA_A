## 2. Substitution Patterns (Narrative)

Georgian shows several descriptive substitution patterns in the supplied material.

For **BECOME**, no common dedicated construction meaning simply “become true/correct” is identified. Instead, the language uses eventive predicates with a truth noun, especially **სიმართლე გაირკვა.** and **სიმართლე დადგინდა.** These express emergence, clarification, or establishment of truth. Descriptively, **REVELATION substitutes for BECOME**.

For **CAUSATION (factual)**, the material does not show a general causative equivalent of “make true.” Instead, Georgian uses lexical and procedural expressions such as **სიმართლის დადგენა** and **ნამდვილობის დადგენა**. These are establishment/verification constructions rather than a simple causative operator. Descriptively, **VERIFICATION substitutes for factual CAUSATION**.

For **CAUSATION (character)**, the only relevant material is **გაამართლა მოლოდინი.**, which expresses justification or living up to expectations. This is narrower than a general character-oriented causative construction. So this area is only partially represented and depends on lexical circumlocution.

For **NEGATION**, the dominant pattern is analytic negation with **არ**, as in **ეს სიმართლეს არ შეესაბამება.** The material also shows a lexical negative-value adjective, **ყალბი**, in **ეს დოკუმენტი ყალბია.** So negation is **primarily analytic**, but falsity/authenticity can also be expressed lexically rather than by negating a positive adjective.

For **VERIFICATION** and **DISCONFIRMATION**, the material does not establish a separate dedicated disconfirmation construction parallel to verification. Instead, disconfirmation is expressed by negating a correspondence predicate: **ეს სიმართლეს არ შეესაბამება.** Thus, **VERIFICATION and DISCONFIRMATION do not share the same positive construction**, but disconfirmation is formed through **analytic negation of a truth-correspondence predicate**.

There is also an asymmetry between positive and negative domains. Positive truth evaluation has several direct resources: predicative adjectives, adnominal adjectives, abstract nouns, truth-telling constructions, and verification nouns. Negative evaluation is available, but often through either analytic negation (**არ**) or a lexical adjective like **ყალბი**, rather than through a fully parallel positive/negative operator pair in every category.

On normalization of multi-word expressions:
- **სიმართლის თქმა** behaves as a **verbal-nominal/light-verb construction**, with **თქმა** as the carrier of the event.
- **სიმართლე გითხრათ.** and **მართალი რომ ვთქვა.** are clause-like discourse-marking formulas; functionally they behave as **discourse markers / stance expressions**, not simple adjectives.
- **ეს სიმართლეს შეესაბამება / არ შეესაბამება** is a **verbal predicate construction** expressing correspondence to truth.
- **ნამდვილობის დადგენა**, **სიზუსტის შემოწმება**, **სიმართლის დადგენა** are **procedural nominal constructions**, with **დადგენა / შემოწმება** carrying the verification/establishment operator.
