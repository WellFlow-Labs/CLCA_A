# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2a Derivational System
# Language: Tagalog (tl)
# Layer: P2a_derivational_system

---

**Note on scope:** This step documents attested derivational morphology for the P1 lexical items. Forms already fully analyzed in P1 or P1b are cross-referenced rather than re-derived where they were already the output of a derivational process. The focus here is on derivations not yet documented, plus systematic documentation of the derivational relationships already identified. Verbal aspect paradigms are included where the forms are conventionalized and attested; nonce inflectional forms are excluded.

**Preliminary note on Tagalog morphology:** Tagalog derivational morphology operates primarily through prefixes, suffixes, circumfixes, and reduplication, often in combination. The same morphological material can serve multiple functions (e.g., *-an* as locative applicative, as abstract nominalizer, as benefactive marker), and the boundary between derivation and inflection is not always sharp. The forms documented below are restricted to those with clear attestation in the P1 and P1b data or in well-established general usage. Forms generated by regular verbal aspect morphology are noted where they are lexically significant but are not treated as independent derivations unless they show semantic specialization.

**Methodological note:** Forms are included only where usage is well-attested. Paradigm gaps are preserved without comment unless the gap itself is noteworthy. Forms already documented in P1 and P1b are cross-referenced rather than re-described in full.

## Item 1 — Base Form: totoo

---

**Derived Form 1**
- **Base Form:** totoo
- **Derived Form:** katotohanan
- **Transliteration:** katotohanan
- **Literal Breakdown:** *ka-* + *totoo* + *-an* (nominalizing circumfix); the medial *h* appears as a phonological insertion between the root-final vowel and the suffix; also analyzed as *ka-* + *totoo* (with internal vowel adjustment: *totoo* → *totohan*) + *-an*; circumfix nominalizing the root into an abstract state or domain
- **Natural English Translation:** truth, the truth, reality, the true state of affairs
- **Usage:** Abstract nominal referring to truth as an abstract property or to a specific true state of affairs. The standard abstract noun for truth across philosophical, religious, legal, and everyday registers. It is the expected translation equivalent of "truth" in Bible translations and legal oaths, and appears in formal moral exhortations (*Sabihin ang katotohanan* — "Tell the truth"). Used across formal, moral, philosophical, and legal registers. Fully documented in P1 item 2 and P1b A-02.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2**
- **Base Form:** totoo
- **Derived Form:** totoong-totoo
- **Transliteration:** totoong-totoo
- **Literal Breakdown:** *totoo* + *-ng* (linker) + *totoo*; full reduplication with intervening linker, producing emphatic intensification
- **Natural English Translation:** absolutely true, really and truly real, very real, genuinely genuine
- **Usage:** Emphatic form asserting the unambiguous reality or truth of something, typically in response to doubt. An intensified form used in emphatic speech to stress that something is unambiguously real or true. Common in emotional or emphatic registers of everyday speech. Documented in P1 item 1 and P1b A-03.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 3**
- **Base Form:** totoo
- **Derived Form:** makatotohanan
- **Transliteration:** makatotohanan
- **Literal Breakdown:** *maka-* + *katotohanan* + *-an*; also analyzed as *maka-* + *totoo* + *-an*; the prefix *maka-* expresses conformity with or orientation toward or accordance with the base; the base here is the derived nominal *katotohanan* or the root *totoo*
- **Natural English Translation:** realistic, truthful, true-to-life, verisimilar
- **Usage:** Applied to representations, narratives, or portrayals that accurately reflect reality; common in aesthetic and critical evaluation. Applied in aesthetic and narrative contexts to evaluate whether a representation corresponds faithfully to reality (*makatotohanang pagkukuwento* — "realistic/truthful storytelling"). Less common in purely propositional contexts than *totoo* itself. Documented in P1 item 18 and P1b A-04.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 4**
- **Base Form:** totoo
- **Derived Form:** pagkatotoo
- **Transliteration:** pagkatotoo
- **Literal Breakdown:** *pagka-* (nominalizing prefix expressing state or quality of being) + *totoo*
- **Natural English Translation:** the state of being true, the quality of being real, the fact of something being the case
- **Usage:** Refers to the abstract quality or state of being true or real; used in formal and philosophical registers (*Ang pagkatotoo ng pangyayari ay hindi mapapabulaanan* "The reality of the event cannot be denied"). Less frequent than *katotohanan* but attested in written Tagalog.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 5**
- **Base Form:** totoo
- **Derived Form:** itotoo
- **Transliteration:** itotoo
- **Literal Breakdown:** *i-* (object-focus verbal prefix) + *totoo*; future/contemplated aspect
- **Natural English Translation:** will make real, will actualize, will realize (bring into reality)
- **Usage:** Verbal form meaning to make something real or to actualize something previously planned or promised (*Itotoo ko ang aking pangako* "I will make my promise real / I will fulfill my promise"). Attested in spoken and written Tagalog in contexts of commitment and actualization.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 6**
- **Base Form:** totoo
- **Derived Form:** itinototoo
- **Transliteration:** itinototoo
- **Literal Breakdown:** *i-* + *-in-* (completive infix) + *totoo* + reduplication (imperfective); actor-focus imperfective of the *i-* verbal form
- **Natural English Translation:** is making real, is actualizing, is fulfilling
- **Usage:** Imperfective form of *itotoo*, describing an ongoing process of making something real or fulfilling a commitment (*Itinototoo niya ang kanyang mga pangako* "He/she is making his/her promises real"). Attested in written and spoken Tagalog.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 7**
- **Base Form:** totoo
- **Derived Form:** itinotoo
- **Transliteration:** itinotoo
- **Literal Breakdown:** *i-* + *-in-* (completive infix) + *totoo*; completed object-focus form
- **Natural English Translation:** made real, actualized, fulfilled, realized
- **Usage:** Completive form describing the accomplished actualization of something (*Itinotoo niya ang kanyang pangarap* "He/she made his/her dream real"). Attested in narrative and motivational contexts.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 8**
- **Base Form:** totoo
- **Derived Form:** magtotoo
- **Transliteration:** magtotoo
- **Literal Breakdown:** *mag-* (actor-focus verbal prefix) + *totoo*; future/contemplated aspect
- **Natural English Translation:** will tell the truth, will be truthful, will be honest
- **Usage:** Verbal form meaning to tell the truth or to be truthful in one's statements (*Magtotoo ka ba?* "Will you tell the truth?"). Attested in moral and interpersonal contexts.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 9**
- **Base Form:** totoo
- **Derived Form:** nagtotoo
- **Transliteration:** nagtotoo
- **Literal Breakdown:** *nag-* (actor-focus, imperfective) + *totoo* + reduplication
- **Natural English Translation:** is telling the truth, is being truthful
- **Usage:** Imperfective actor-focus form describing someone in the act of telling the truth (*Nagtotoo ba siya?* "Is he/she telling the truth?"). Common in interpersonal and evaluative discourse.

**Note:** The completive actor-focus form would be *nagtotoo* in some analyses but *nagtotoo* is more commonly the imperfective. The completive is *nagtotoo* with stress shift in some dialects, but this distinction is not consistently marked in writing. The form is attested; the aspectual distinction in writing is not always recoverable.

- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 10**
- **Base Form:** totoo
- **Derived Form:** pagtotoo
- **Transliteration:** pagtotoo
- **Literal Breakdown:** *pag-* (action nominalization) + *totoo*
- **Natural English Translation:** the act of telling the truth, truth-telling, being truthful
- **Usage:** Nominal form referring to the act or practice of telling the truth (*Ang pagtotoo ay isang katangian ng mabuting tao* "Truth-telling is a quality of a good person"). Attested in moral and didactic discourse.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 11**
- **Base Form:** totoo
- **Derived Form:** katotohanang-katotohanan
- **Transliteration:** katotohanang-katotohanan
- **Literal Breakdown:** *katotohanan* + *-ng* + *katotohanan*; full reduplication of the derived nominal with linker, producing emphatic intensification
- **Natural English Translation:** absolute truth, the very truth itself
- **Usage:** Emphatic form of *katotohanan* used in philosophical, religious, or rhetorical contexts to assert the highest or most fundamental level of truth. Documented in P1b as [MARGINAL].
- **Epistemic Status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**Derived Form 12**
- **Base Form:** totoo
- **Derived Form:** nagkatotoo
- **Transliteration:** nagkatotoo
- **Literal Breakdown:** *nagka-* + *totoo*; *nagka-* marks an unintended or spontaneous coming-about of a state
- **Natural English Translation:** came true, was realized, turned out to be real
- **Usage:** Used to describe a prediction, wish, or fear that was realized (*Nagkatotoo ang kanyang pangarap* "his dream came true"). The semantic trajectory here is realization of a future state rather than assertion of present truth, placing this form at the periphery of the truth-evaluation domain.
- **Epistemic Status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**Note on totoo verbal derivation:** The root *totoo* does not appear to productively generate verbal forms via standard Tagalog verbal affixes (*mag-*, *-um-*, *ma-*, *i-*, *-in*) in the truth-assertion sense. Expressions such as *nagkatotoo* ("came true, was realized") exist but involve a different semantic trajectory (realization of a prediction or wish) and are not straightforwardly derivations within the truth-evaluation domain. The verbal function in the truth-assertion sense is served by *magsabi ng katotohanan* and similar phrases rather than a derived verb from the root itself.

## Item 3 — Base Form: tunay

---

**Derived Form 1**
- **Base Form:** tunay
- **Derived Form:** katunayan
- **Transliteration:** katunayan
- **Literal Breakdown:** *ka-* + *tunay* + *-an* (nominalizing circumfix); "the state of being *tunay*; proof"
- **Natural English Translation:** proof, evidence, authenticity, genuineness, verification, the fact that
- **Usage:** Functions both as a concrete nominal (a piece of proof or evidence) and as a discourse marker (*sa katunayan* — "in fact"). Refers to evidence or proof establishing genuineness or factual truth; also functions as a conjunctive expression. Used in legal, formal, and argumentative discourse. Fully documented in P1 item 4 and P1b B-02.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2**
- **Base Form:** tunay
- **Derived Form:** tunay na tunay
- **Transliteration:** tunay na tunay
- **Literal Breakdown:** *tunay* + *na* (linker) + *tunay*; full reduplication with linker
- **Natural English Translation:** absolutely genuine, truly authentic, completely real
- **Usage:** Emphatic form asserting the highest degree of genuineness, parallel to *totoong-totoo*. Used in everyday speech to emphasize emphatic genuineness. Documented in P1b B-08.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 3**
- **Base Form:** tunay
- **Derived Form:** patunay
- **Transliteration:** patunay
- **Literal Breakdown:** *pa-* (prefix with causative/directional/instrumental function) + *tunay*; *pa-* in nominal function yields "that which causes or establishes tunay"; "that which makes something genuine/proven," or "the means of proving"
- **Natural English Translation:** proof, evidence, attestation, verification, demonstration (as a concrete item)
- **Usage:** Nominal form referring to a specific piece of evidence or a document that proves or verifies a claim; slightly more concrete than *katunayan*, often referring to a specific document or item (*Ipakita ang patunay ng pagbabayad* "Show proof of payment"). Used in legal, administrative, and everyday contexts. Documented in P1b B-22.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 4**
- **Base Form:** tunay
- **Derived Form:** patunayan
- **Transliteration:** patunayan
- **Literal Breakdown:** *pa-* (causative prefix) + *tunay* + *-an* (applicative/locative suffix); verbal form with *-an* suffix indicating the goal or locus of the action; "to cause to be proven; to prove to someone"
- **Natural English Translation:** to prove, to verify, to demonstrate, to establish, to authenticate
- **Usage:** The standard verb for the act of proving or demonstrating something to be true or genuine. Used in legal argumentation, scientific discourse, and everyday persuasion (*Patunayan mo ang iyong sinasabi* "Prove what you are saying"; *Patunayan mo* — "Prove it"). Extremely common across all registers. Documented in P1b B-04.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 5**
- **Base Form:** tunay
- **Derived Form:** napatunayan
- **Transliteration:** napatunayan
- **Literal Breakdown:** *na-* (completive aspect marker / perfective/completed aspect marker) + *pa-* + *tunay* + *-an*; "has been proven"
- **Natural English Translation:** has been proven, was verified, has been established
- **Usage:** Completive form of *patunayan*, asserting that something has already been demonstrated or verified. The completed-aspect form indicating that something has been successfully proven or verified. Common in legal verdicts, scientific conclusions, and formal declarations. Documented in P1b B-05 and B-06.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 6**
- **Base Form:** tunay
- **Derived Form:** mapatunayan
- **Transliteration:** mapatunayan
- **Literal Breakdown:** *ma-* (potential aspect prefix) + *pa-* + *tunay* + *-an*; "to be able to prove; to be provable"
- **Natural English Translation:** to be able to prove, can be proven, to be provable, to be verifiable, to manage to verify
- **Usage:** Potential form expressing the ability or possibility of proving something. Common in legal and argumentative discourse (*Hindi mapatunayan* — "cannot be proven"). Documented in P1b B-05.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 7**
- **Base Form:** tunay
- **Derived Form:** pinatunayan
- **Transliteration:** pinatunayan
- **Literal Breakdown:** *p-in-atunayan*; infix *-in-* inserted after initial consonant of *patunayan*, completive actor-focus; also analyzed as *pi-* (completed aspect marker in actor-focus paradigm) + *pa-* + *tunay* + *-an*; "was proven/caused to be verified"
- **Natural English Translation:** proved, verified, demonstrated, authenticated (by a specific agent)
- **Usage:** Completive actor-focus form describing the act of proving as completed by a specific agent. Common in legal and argumentative contexts (*Pinatunayan ng ebidensya ang kanyang kasalanan* "the evidence proved his guilt"). Documented in P1b B-07.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 8**
- **Base Form:** tunay
- **Derived Form:** pinapatunayan
- **Transliteration:** pinapatunayan
- **Literal Breakdown:** *p-in-a-* + *patunayan* + reduplication of first syllable of root; imperfective actor-focus of *patunayan*
- **Natural English Translation:** is proving, is verifying, is demonstrating (ongoing action by a specific agent)
- **Usage:** Imperfective actor-focus form of *patunayan*, describing an ongoing act of proving (*Pinapatunayan niya ang kanyang inosensya* "He/she is proving his/her innocence"). Attested in legal and argumentative discourse.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 9**
- **Base Form:** tunay
- **Derived Form:** pagpapatunay
- **Transliteration:** pagpapatunay
- **Literal Breakdown:** *pag-* (verbal noun prefix) + *pa-* (causative) + *tunay* + *-an* + reduplication; nominalization of the *patunayan* verbal action; "the act of causing something to be verified/proven"
- **Natural English Translation:** the act of proving, verification, authentication, confirmation, proof (as an act or process)
- **Usage:** Nominal form referring to the process or act of verifying, authenticating, or proving something. Used in legal, administrative, and formal contexts (*pagpapatunay ng dokumento* — "authentication/verification of a document"; *Ang pagpapatunay ng kanyang pagkakakilanlan ay kinakailangan* "Verification of his/her identity is required"). Common in administrative, legal, and formal contexts. Documented in P1 item 29 and P1b B-03.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 10**
- **Base Form:** tunay
- **Derived Form:** pagpapatunayan
- **Transliteration:** pagpapatunayan
- **Literal Breakdown:** *pag-* + *pa-* + *tunay* + *-an* + reduplication; nominalization with goal/locus suffix
- **Natural English Translation:** the proving of something, the verification of something (with reference to a specific object)
- **Usage:** Nominal form with *-an* suffix, referring to the proving or verification of a specific thing (*Ang pagpapatunayan ng katotohanan ay mahirap* "The proving of the truth is difficult"). Attested in formal and written registers; less common than *pagpapatunay*.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 11**
- **Base Form:** tunay
- **Derived Form:** di-tunay
- **Transliteration:** di-tunay
- **Literal Breakdown:** *di-* (negation prefix, clitic form of *hindi*) + *tunay*
- **Natural English Translation:** inauthentic, not genuine, false
- **Usage:** Negated form of *tunay*, used to evaluate something as lacking genuine character or authenticity. Documented in P1 item 30 as [MARGINAL].
- **Epistemic Status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**Note on tunay productivity:** The *pa-* + *tunay* + *-an* verbal cluster is notably productive, generating a fuller aspectual paradigm than is observed for *totoo* or *tapat*. The forms *pinapatunayan* (imperfective actor-focus: "is proving") and *ipinapatunayan* (imperfective object-focus) are also attested in usage. The root does not appear to generate a standard agentive noun (e.g., "one who proves") as a lexicalized item; that function is served by periphrastic constructions.

## Item 5 — Base Form: tama

---

**Derived Form 1**
- **Base Form:** tama
- **Derived Form:** katamaan
- **Transliteration:** katamaan
- **Literal Breakdown:** *ka-* + *tama* + *-an* (circumfix; abstract nominalizer); "the state or quality of being *tama*"
- **Natural English Translation:** correctness, rightness, appropriateness, accuracy
- **Usage:** Abstract nominal referring to the quality of being correct or appropriate (*Ang katamaan ng kanyang desisyon ay napatunayan* "The correctness of his/her decision was proven"; *Ang katamaan ng sagot* "the correctness of the answer"). Used in formal and evaluative discourse; less frequent in casual speech than the root *tama*.

**Derived Form 2**

- **Usage:** Reduplication producing a meaning of exact or precise correctness, often with a sense of something being just the right amount or degree (*Tama-tama ang sukat* "The measurement is just right"). Common in everyday speech.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 3**
