# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2a Derivational System
# Language: Quechua (qu)

## Derivational Analysis

### BASE: chiqan (#1)

#### Derived Form 1: chiqap
**Base:** chiqan (true, real, genuine)
**Derived Form:** chiqap
**Transliteration:** chiqap
**Literal Breakdown:** chiqan + -p (adverbializer/genitive)
**Translation:** truly, really, indeed
**Usage:** Adverbial form used to emphasize the truth or reality of a statement. Functions as an intensifier in discourse to confirm veracity.
**Status:** [CORE]

#### Derived Form 2: chiqamanta
**Base:** chiqan (true, real, genuine)
**Derived Form:** chiqamanta
**Transliteration:** chiqamanta
**Literal Breakdown:** chiqan + -manta (ablative: from/out of)
**Translation:** from truth, truly, genuinely
**Usage:** Ablative form emphasizing origin in truth. Used to indicate that something proceeds from or is grounded in truth rather than falsehood.
**Status:** [CORE]

#### Derived Form 3: chiqanchay
**Base:** chiqan (true, real, genuine)
**Derived Form:** chiqanchay
**Transliteration:** chiqanchay
**Literal Breakdown:** chiqan + -cha- (causative/factitive) + -y (infinitive)
**Translation:** to verify, to authenticate, to make true, to confirm
**Usage:** Causative derivation indicating the action of making something true or establishing its truth. Common in administrative and legal contexts for verification.
**Status:** [CORE]

#### Derived Form 4: chiqanchakuy
**Base:** chiqan (true, real, genuine)
**Derived Form:** chiqanchakuy
**Transliteration:** chiqanchakuy
**Literal Breakdown:** chiqan + -cha- (causative) + -ku- (reflexive/middle) + -y (infinitive)
**Translation:** to verify for oneself, to confirm personally, to ascertain
**Usage:** Reflexive-causative form emphasizing personal verification or self-directed confirmation. Used when someone personally establishes truth.
**Status:** [CORE]

## Summary of Derivational Patterns

### Productive Derivational Suffixes Identified:

1. **-cha-** (causative/factitive): Attested with chiqan → chiqanchay, allin → allinchay, llulla → llullachiy
   - Function: Creates verbs meaning "to make X" or "to cause to be X"

2. **-ku-** (reflexive/middle): Attested with llulla → llullakuy, rikch'ay → rikch'akuy
   - Function: Creates verbs with reflexive or middle voice semantics

3. **-cha- + -ku-** (causative + reflexive): Attested with chiqan → chiqanchakuy, allin → allinchakuy, llulla → llullachakuy
   - Function: Creates verbs meaning "to make oneself X" or "to X for oneself"

4. **-ya-** (intensifier/continuative/inchoative): Attested with allin → allinyay, llulla → llullayay
   - Function: Creates verbs indicating becoming, intensification, or habitual action

5. **-q** (agentive/nominalizer): Attested with llulla → llullakuq
   - Function: Creates agent nouns from verbs

6. **-sqa** (past participle/resultative): Attested with pantay → pantasqa
   - Function: Creates participial forms indicating result or state

7. **-p** (adverbializer/genitive): Attested with chiqan → chiqap
   - Function: Creates adverbial forms

8. **-manta** (ablative): Attested with chiqan → chiqamanta
   - Function: Creates forms indicating source or manner (though -manta is technically a case suffix, it functions derivationally here)

9. **-naya-** (potential): Attested with yachay → yachanayay
   - Function: Creates forms indicating possibility or potential

### Asymmetries and Gaps:

- The causative -cha- is highly productive with quality terms (chiqan, allin, llulla) but not attested with other bases
- The reflexive -ku- appears both independently and in combination with causatives
- Many bases show no derivational morphology in the attested materials
- Verbal bases (pantay, takyay, musyay, riqsiy) show fewer derivations than quality terms
- Particles and interrogatives (k'ari, imayna) show no derivation, as expected
- The substantivizer kaq, being already derived, shows no further derivation

### Notes on Productivity:

The most productive pattern is the causative -cha-, particularly with quality/state terms that can be "made to be" or "caused to become." The combination of causative + reflexive (-cha- + -ku-) is also notably productive, creating a semantic pattern of self-directed causation or personal verification/improvement.
