## 1. ATTRIBUTION

### Pattern
Predicate truth-assertion via the 是…的 frame, or bare adjectival predication (Subj + (是) + Adj). Used to assert that something is true/real/correct.

### Examples
- 这是真的吗？ → zhè shì zhēn de ma? → "Is this true/real?"
- 你说得对。 → nǐ shuō de duì → "What you say is right."

### Notes
Two distinct predication strategies: the nominal 是…的 frame (是真的 / 是假的) for genuineness, and bare/complement predication for monosyllabic 对/错 (V+得+对, 没错). Bisyllabic terms (正确, 可靠) predicate directly (消息可靠).

## 2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION

### Pattern
Negation via 不 (不是真的, 不对, 不可靠) or 没 (没错), plus the lexicalized negative pole 假 in the 是假的 frame.

### Examples
- 这是假的。 → zhè shì jiǎ de → "This is fake."
- 不可靠 → bù kěkào → "unreliable / not dependable."

### Notes
Falsity is expressed both by negating the positive term (不真实, 不正确) and by dedicated lexical antonyms (假, 错, 错误, 虚伪). The 没错 form is a fixed negated affirmation rather than a denial.

## 3. ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION

### Pattern
Adjective + 的 + Noun attributive frame; some collocations are conventionally restricted by noun class.

### Examples
- 真实的情况 → zhēnshí de qíngkuàng → "the real situation"
- 正宗川菜 → zhèngzōng chuāncài → "authentic Sichuan cuisine"

### Notes
Productive modifier slot, but collocational ranges differ sharply: 真挚 locks to emotion/relationship nouns (真挚的感情), 正宗/地道 to food/culture nouns, 可靠 to source/information nouns. Authenticity-to-type terms often drop 的 in fixed N-N collocations (正宗川菜).

## 4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE

### Pattern
Change-of-state expressed lexically via 成/成真 ("become real") within fixed idioms; no productive general inchoative frame on truth adjectives.

### Examples
- 弄假成真 → nòng jiǎ chéng zhēn → "what began as pretense becomes real"
- 真相大白 → zhēnxiàng dàbái → "the truth comes fully to light"

### Notes
Inchoative meaning is carried by lexicalized idioms (…成真, 真相大白) rather than a productive [Adj + become] template. No common *变真实 / *变正确 inchoative construction is attested.

## 5. CAUSATIVE (or absence)

### Pattern
No productive morphological or light-verb causative on truth-domain adjectives. Related notions are expressed by distinct lexical verbs of verification/revelation, not by causativizing the adjective.

### Examples
- 揭露真相 → jiēlù zhēnxiàng → "to expose the truth"
- 核实情况 → héshí qíngkuàng → "to verify the situation"

### Notes
No common expression identified for "make X true/correct" as a causative of the adjective (no *使真化, no productive 使+Adj frame in this domain). The functional space is filled by independent transitive verbs (揭露, 查明, 核实, 证实, 纠正).

## 6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION

### Pattern
Suffixal -性 (-ness/-ity) on bisyllabic evaluative adjectives, and -度 (degree) on gradable ones; monosyllabic roots and moral-character terms resist suffixation and use compounds instead.

### Examples
- 真实性 → zhēnshíxìng → "authenticity, truthfulness (as a property)"
- 可信度 → kěxìndù → "credibility, degree of trustworthiness"

### Notes
-性 attaches to 真实, 正确, 准确, 可靠, 相似 (CORE); -度 to gradable 可信, 准确, 相似. Monosyllabic 真/假/对/错 take no -性; abstract notions are instead carried by binomial compounds (真假, 真伪). Moral terms 真诚/诚实 yield no standard nominalizer (genuine gap).

## 7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION (liar, truth-teller)

### Pattern
No productive -者 agentive on truth-domain adjectives. Agentive/character reference uses [Adj + 人] (老实人, 虚伪的人) rather than a derived agent noun.

### Examples
- 老实人 → lǎoshi rén → "an honest person"
- 虚伪的人 → xūwěi de rén → "a hypocritical person"

### Notes
The -者 slot is empty for this vocabulary set (no *诚实者, *可靠者 as standard words). "Liar / truth-teller" type agents are not derived from these adjectives; they are expressed by separate lexemes or by the [Adj(+的)+人] phrasal frame.

## 8. DISCOURSE-CONNECTIVE / STANCE FRAMES

### Pattern
Fixed adverbial frames [N/Adj + 上] and [Adj + 地说] functioning as discourse connectives or hedges that anchor a claim to fact, essence, or precision.

### Examples
- 事实上 → shìshí shàng → "in fact, as a matter of fact"
- 准确地说 → zhǔnquè de shuō → "to put it precisely / strictly speaking"

### Notes
A distinct constructional type emerging from the data: 事实/本质 specialize into 上-connectives, while accuracy terms enter 地说 clarification frames. These do not assert truth predicatively but frame the speaker's stance toward it.

## 9. VERB–OBJECT COLLOCATIONS (truth-handling predicates)

### Pattern
Conventionalized [Verb + truth-noun] collocations, with the verb class selected by the noun's concreteness.

### Examples
- 追求真理 → zhuīqiú zhēnlǐ → "to pursue truth"
- 辨别真伪 → biànbié zhēnwěi → "to distinguish genuine from fake"

### Notes
Verb classes split by noun type: 真理 takes pursuit verbs (追求/掌握), 真相 takes revealing verbs (揭露/查明), 真伪/真假 take discrimination verbs (辨别/鉴定), 错误 takes 犯/纠正. Despite shared semantic field, these are non-interchangeable collocational sets.

## 10. RESEMBLANCE / LIKENESS FRAMES

### Pattern
Comparative templates [像…一样] and [与/和…相似], plus the lexicalized epistemic 好像.

### Examples
- 像他一样 → xiàng tā yíyàng → "(just) like him"
- 与原作相似 → yǔ yuánzuò xiāngsì → "similar to the original"

### Notes
像 builds a productive manner/likeness comparative frame and extends to the epistemic marker 好像 ("it seems"); 相似 takes the more formal 与…相似 frame and a likeness-nominal (相似之处). 逼真 evaluates a representation's closeness to reality in V+得 complements (画得逼真).
