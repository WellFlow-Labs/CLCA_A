# CLCA Protocol v3.3 — P6 DYNAMIC EXPRESSIONS
# Language: Tamil (ta)

---

**Transliteration standard:** ISO 15919

---

## Preliminary Note on Scope

This document analyzes how Tamil encodes change, causation, and stability with respect to truth-related vocabulary documented in P1–P5. All vocabulary is drawn exclusively from the CONTEXT. Auxiliary verbs, aspect markers, and function words are added as needed to form natural expressions. Where no common expression is attested for a given term or dynamic type, this is explicitly stated. Asymmetry across terms is preserved throughout.

---

---

## Section 1: X Becomes a Truth-Related State

This section covers inchoative expressions — situations where something transitions into a truth-related state. The primary grammatical resources are: (a) the *āku* construction (nominal + *-āka* + *āku*, "to become X"), (b) the completive auxiliary *viṭu* marking irreversible change, and (c) lexicalized inchoative collocations documented in P1b and P2b.

---

### 1.1 Truth / Genuineness becoming apparent or established

**Expression 1**

**Form:** உண்மை வெளிப்படுகிறது
**Transliteration:** uṇmai veḷippaṭukiṟatu
**Morpheme breakdown:** *uṇmai* (truth) + *veḷippaṭu* (to come out, to be revealed; inchoative passive verb)
**English translation:** The truth is coming out / The truth is being revealed
**Dynamic type:** Inchoative — truth transitioning from hidden to revealed state
**Notes:** Documented in P1b Root 1 item 9 and P4a (inchoative section) as a lexicalized collocation. The verb *veḷippaṭu* is specifically inchoative-passive; it cannot be replaced by *āku* in this collocation without loss of naturalness. Present tense form given here; past *veḷippaṭṭatu* (the truth came out) is equally attested (P4b).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Expression 2**

**Form:** உண்மை தெரிய வருகிறது
**Transliteration:** uṇmai teriya varukiṟatu
**Morpheme breakdown:** *uṇmai* (truth) + *teriya* (infinitive of *teri*, to become known) + *varu* (to come; here: directional auxiliary marking approach/emergence)
**English translation:** The truth is coming to be known / The truth is emerging
**Dynamic type:** Inchoative — truth transitioning from unknown to known
**Notes:** *teri* (to become known, to come to light) is documented in P1b Root 1 item 20 as a common collocation with *uṇmai*. The directional auxiliary *varu* (to come) adds a sense of gradual emergence. This construction is slightly more gradual in aspect than *veḷippaṭu* (which implies a more sudden revelation).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Expression 3**

**Form:** உண்மை நிலைபெறுகிறது
**Transliteration:** uṇmai nilaipeṟukiṟatu
**Morpheme breakdown:** *uṇmai* (truth) + *nilaipeṟu* (to become established, to take hold; from *nilai* "state/position" + *peṟu* "to obtain/attain")
**English translation:** The truth is becoming established / Truth is taking hold
**Dynamic type:** Inchoative — truth transitioning into a stable, established state
**Notes:** *nilai* (state, condition) is documented in P1b Root 1 item 8 (*uṇmai nilai*, the true state of affairs). The verb *peṟu* (to obtain, to attain) combines with *nilai* to produce an inchoative meaning of becoming established. This construction appears in formal and philosophical registers.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Expression 4**

**Form:** இது உண்மையாகிவிட்டது
**Transliteration:** itu uṇmaiyākiviṭṭatu
**Morpheme breakdown:** *itu* (this) + *uṇmaiyāki* (*uṇmai* + *-āka* adverbial + *āki* conjunctive of *āku*, "to become") + *viṭṭatu* (completive auxiliary *viṭu*, past)
**English translation:** This has turned out to be true / This has become a reality
**Dynamic type:** Inchoative with completive — transition into truth-state marked as completed and irreversible
**Notes:** The *-āki viṭu* construction is documented in P4a (inchoative section) as marking completed change of state. The completive *viṭu* adds a sense of finality. This construction is used when a prediction, suspicion, or possibility has been confirmed as actually the case.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### 1.2 Credibility / Trustworthiness becoming established or lost

**Expression 5**

**Form:** அவரது நம்பகத்தன்மை கேள்விக்குறியாகிவிட்டது
**Transliteration:** avaratu nampakattaṉmai kēḷvikkuṟiyākiviṭṭatu
**Morpheme breakdown:** *avaratu* (his/her) + *nampakattaṉmai* (credibility) + *kēḷvikkuṟiyāki* (becoming a question mark; *kēḷvikkuṟi* "question mark" + *-āki*) + *viṭṭatu* (completive)
**English translation:** His/Her credibility has come into question / His/Her trustworthiness has become a question mark
**Dynamic type:** Inchoative with completive — credibility transitioning into a questioned/undermined state
**Notes:** Documented directly in P2b Construction 1 under *nampakattaṉmai*. The compound predicate *kēḷvikkuṟiyāki* (becoming a question mark) is a fixed journalistic expression. The completive *viṭu* marks the change as having occurred and being difficult to reverse.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Expression 6**

**Form:** நம்பிக்கை குறைந்துவிட்டது
**Transliteration:** nampikkai kuṟaintuViṭṭatu
**Morpheme breakdown:** *nampikkai* (trust, confidence) + *kuṟaintu* (conjunctive of *kuṟai*, to decrease, to diminish) + *viṭṭatu* (completive)
**English translation:** Trust has diminished / Confidence has eroded
**Dynamic type:** Inchoative — trust transitioning into a diminished state
**Notes:** *nampikkai* is documented in P1 item 28 and P1b Root 3. The verb *kuṟai* (to decrease, to diminish) is a natural inchoative verb for the erosion of trust; it describes a gradual process, in contrast to *iḻa* (to lose, documented in P1b Root 3 item 7), which describes a more complete loss. The completive *viṭu* marks the diminishment as having occurred.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Expression 7**

**Form:** நம்பிக்கை வலுப்பெறுகிறது
**Transliteration:** nampikkai valuppeṟukiṟatu
**Morpheme breakdown:** *nampikkai* (trust, confidence) + *valu* (strength) + *peṟu* (to obtain, to attain; here: to gain)
**English translation:** Trust is growing stronger / Confidence is being strengthened
**Dynamic type:** Inchoative — trust transitioning into a stronger state
**Notes:** *nampikkai* is documented throughout P1b Root 3. The compound *valuppeṟu* (to gain strength, to become stronger) is a natural inchoative expression for the growth of trust. This contrasts with *kuṟaintu viṭu* (diminished) and *iḻa* (lost) documented elsewhere.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### 1.3 Honesty / Integrity becoming established

**Expression 8**

**Form:** நேர்மை நிலைபெறுகிறது
**Transliteration:** nēṟmai nilaipeṟukiṟatu
**Morpheme breakdown:** *nēṟmai* (honesty, integrity) + *nilaipeṟu* (to become established, to take hold)
**English translation:** Honesty is becoming established / Integrity is taking hold
**Dynamic type:** Inchoative — honesty transitioning into a stable, established state
**Notes:** *nēṟmai* is documented in P1 item 3 and throughout P1b Root 2. The *nilaipeṟu* construction is parallel to Expression 3 above (*uṇmai nilaipeṟukiṟatu*). Appears in formal and political discourse about the establishment of honest governance.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### 1.4 Something becoming false, fake, or discredited

**Expression 9**

**Form:** இது பொய்யாகிவிட்டது
**Transliteration:** itu poyyākiviṭṭatu
**Morpheme breakdown:** *itu* (this) + *poyyāki* (*poy* + *-āka* + *āki*) + *viṭṭatu* (completive)
**English translation:** This has turned out to be false / This has proven to be a lie
**Dynamic type:** Inchoative with completive — transition into falseness, marked as completed
**Notes:** *poy* is documented in P1 item 2 and P2a. The *-āki viṭu* construction is the standard inchoative-completive pattern documented in P4a. This construction is used when something previously believed or asserted has been shown to be false.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Expression 10**

**Form:** அவன் பொய்யனாகிவிட்டான்
**Transliteration:** avaṉ poyyaṉākiviṭṭāṉ
**Morpheme breakdown:** *avaṉ* (he) + *poyyaṉāki* (*poyyan* "liar" + *-āka* + *āki*) + *viṭṭāṉ* (completive, 3sg masculine)
**English translation:** He has become a liar / He has turned into a liar
**Dynamic type:** Inchoative with completive — person transitioning into the identity of a liar
**Notes:** *poyyan* (liar, masculine agentive) is documented in P2a under *poy*. The *-āki viṭu* construction applied to an agentive nominal describes a person taking on a characterological identity. The completive *viṭu* marks this as a definitive change.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Expression 11**

**Form:** போலி என்று தெரிந்துவிட்டது
**Transliteration:** pōli eṉṟu terintuViṭṭatu
**Morpheme breakdown:** *pōli* (fake) + *eṉṟu* (quotative: as, that it is) + *terintu* (conjunctive of *teri*, to become known) + *viṭṭatu* (completive)
**English translation:** It has become known to be fake / It has been found out to be counterfeit
**Dynamic type:** Inchoative with completive — fakeness transitioning from hidden to known
**Notes:** *pōli* is documented in P1 item 13 and P2a. The quotative *eṉṟu* frames *pōli* as the content of what has become known. The verb *teri* (to become known) is documented in P1b Root 1 item 20 in the *uṇmai* context; here it applies to the revelation of fakeness. The completive *viṭu* marks the discovery as final.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Expression 12**

**Form:** தவறு என்று தெரிந்துவிட்டது
**Transliteration:** tavaṟu eṉṟu terintuViṭṭatu
**Morpheme breakdown:** *tavaṟu* (wrong, error) + *eṉṟu* (quotative) + *terintu* (conjunctive of *teri*) + *viṭṭatu* (completive)
**English translation:** It has become known to be wrong / It has been found out to be an error
**Dynamic type:** Inchoative with completive — wrongness transitioning from unrecognized to recognized
**Notes:** *tavaṟu* is documented in P1 item 6 and P2a. Parallel in structure to Expression 11. The construction describes the moment of recognition that something is an error, often with a sense of finality.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Section 2: X Becomes a Non-Truth / Opposed State

This section covers transitions away from truth-related states — the erosion, loss, or reversal of truth, honesty, credibility, or correctness.

---

**Expression 13**

**Form:** நம்பிக்கை இழந்துவிட்டார்கள்
**Transliteration:** nampikkai iḻantuViṭṭārkaḷ
**Morpheme breakdown:** *nampikkai* (trust) + *iḻantu* (conjunctive of *iḻa*, to lose) + *viṭṭārkaḷ* (completive, 3pl honorific)
**English translation:** They have lost trust / They have lost faith (in something/someone)
**Dynamic type:** Inchoative with completive — trust transitioning into a lost state
**Notes:** *nampikkai iḻa* (to lose trust) is documented in P1 item 28 and P1b Root 3 item 7 as a common collocation. The completive *viṭu* marks the loss as having occurred and being difficult to reverse. This is the most natural expression for the loss of trust in Tamil.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Expression 14**

**Form:** நேர்மை மறைந்துவிட்டது
**Transliteration:** nēṟmai maṟaintuViṭṭatu
**Morpheme breakdown:** *nēṟmai* (honesty, integrity) + *maṟaintu* (conjunctive of *maṟai*, to disappear, to be hidden) + *viṭṭatu* (completive)
**English translation:** Honesty has disappeared / Integrity has vanished
**Dynamic type:** Inchoative with completive — honesty transitioning into an absent state
**Notes:** *nēṟmai* is documented in P1 item 3 and P1b Root 2. The verb *maṟai* (to hide, to disappear) is documented in P1b Root 1 item 12 in the *uṇmai* context (*uṇmai maṟai*, to conceal the truth); here it applies intransitively to the disappearance of honesty as a quality. The completive *viṭu* marks the disappearance as complete.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Expression 15**

**Form:** தூய்மை கெட்டுவிட்டது
**Transliteration:** tūymai keṭṭuviṭṭatu
**Morpheme breakdown:** *tūymai* (purity, integrity) + *keṭṭu* (conjunctive of *keṭu*, to be ruined, to be spoiled) + *viṭṭatu* (completive)
**English translation:** Integrity has been ruined / Purity has been corrupted
**Dynamic type:** Inchoative with completive — purity/integrity transitioning into a corrupted state
**Notes:** *tūymai* is documented in P1 item 25 and P2a. The verb *keṭu* (to be ruined, to spoil) is a natural inchoative verb for the corruption of moral integrity; it is commonly used in Tamil for the deterioration of moral or physical states. The completive *viṭu* marks the corruption as having occurred.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Expression 16**

**Form:** சரியில்லாமல் போய்விட்டது
**Transliteration:** cariyillāmal pōyviṭṭatu
**Morpheme breakdown:** *cariyillāmal* (without being correct; privative adverbial of *cari*) + *pōy* (conjunctive of *pō*, to go; here: directional auxiliary marking departure from a state) + *viṭṭatu* (completive)
**English translation:** It has gone wrong / It has ended up being incorrect
**Dynamic type:** Inchoative with completive — correctness transitioning into an incorrect state
**Notes:** *cari* is documented in P1 item 5 and P2a. The construction *illāmal pōyviṭu* (to go without X, to end up lacking X) is a natural Tamil expression for the loss of a desirable state. The directional auxiliary *pō* (to go) marks departure from the correct state.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Expression 17**

**Form:** நம்பகத்தன்மை குறைந்துவிட்டது
**Transliteration:** nampakattaṉmai kuṟaintuViṭṭatu
**Morpheme breakdown:** *nampakattaṉmai* (credibility) + *kuṟaintu* (conjunctive of *kuṟai*, to decrease) + *viṭṭatu* (completive)
**English translation:** Credibility has diminished / Trustworthiness has declined
**Dynamic type:** Inchoative with completive — credibility transitioning into a diminished state
**Notes:** *nampakattaṉmai* is documented in P1 item 8 and P2b. The verb *kuṟai* (to decrease) describes gradual erosion, in contrast to the more sudden *kēḷvikkuṟiyāki viṭu* (becoming a question mark, Expression 5). Both are attested for *nampakattaṉmai*.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Section 3: Y Causes X to Be a Truth-Related State

This section covers causative expressions — situations where an agent or event brings about a truth-related state. The primary grammatical resources are: (a) the morphological causative *paṭuttu* documented in P4a, (b) lexical causative verbs (*niṟuvu*, *nirūpikku*, *ūṭṭu*, *taru*), and (c) the conjunctive participle construction (*V-i* + main verb) expressing means or instrument.

---

### 3.1 Causing truth to be established or revealed

**Expression 18**

**Form:** உண்மையை வெளிப்படுத்தினார்கள்
**Transliteration:** uṇmaiyai veḷippaṭuttināṟkaḷ
**Morpheme breakdown:** *uṇmaiyai* (truth, accusative) + *veḷippaṭuttu* (causative of *veḷippaṭu*, to reveal; *veḷi* "outside" + *paṭuttu* "causative") + *-ināṟkaḷ* (past, 3pl honorific)
**English translation:** They revealed the truth / They brought the truth to light
**Dynamic type:** Causative — agent causing truth to transition from hidden to revealed
**Notes:** *uṇmai veḷippaṭu* (truth comes out) is documented in P1b Root 1 item 9 as an inchoative collocation. The causative form *veḷippaṭuttu* (to cause to come out, to reveal) is the natural transitive counterpart. This is the standard expression for deliberately revealing hidden facts.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Expression 19**

**Form:** உண்மையை நிறுவினார்கள்
**Transliteration:** uṇmaiyai niṟuviṉārkaḷ
**Morpheme breakdown:** *uṇmaiyai* (truth, accusative) + *niṟuvi* (conjunctive of *niṟuvu*, to establish, to prove) + *-ṉārkaḷ* (past, 3pl honorific)
**English translation:** They established the truth / They proved the truth
**Dynamic type:** Causative — agent causing truth to be established
**Notes:** *uṇmai niṟuvu* (to establish the truth) is documented in P1b Root 1 item 14 and P4a (causative section). This is the standard legal and argumentative expression for proving that something is true.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Expression 20**

**Form:** விசாரணை உண்மையை வெளிக்கொண்டுவந்தது
**Transliteration:** vicāraṇai uṇmaiyai veḷikkoṇṭuvantatu
**Morpheme breakdown:** *vicāraṇai* (inquiry, investigation) + *uṇmaiyai* (truth, accusative) + *veḷi* (outside) + *koṇṭu vā* (to bring; directional compound verb)
**English translation:** The investigation brought the truth to light
**Dynamic type:** Causative — event (investigation) causing truth to be revealed
**Notes:** *uṇmai vicāraṇai* (truth inquiry) is documented in P1b Root 1 item 23. The compound verb *veḷikkoṇṭu vā* (to bring out, to bring to light) is a natural causative expression for the revelation of facts through investigation. The subject here is an event rather than a person.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Expression 21**

**Form:** சான்றுகள் உண்மையை நிரூபித்தன
**Transliteration:** cāṉṟukaḷ uṇmaiyai nirūpittaṉa
**Morpheme breakdown:** *cāṉṟukaḷ* (evidence, plural) + *uṇmaiyai* (truth, accusative) + *nirūpitta* (past participial of *nirūpikku*, to prove) + *-ṉa* (3pl neuter past)
**English translation:** The evidence proved the truth / The evidence established the facts
**Dynamic type:** Causative — evidence causing truth to be established
**Notes:** *cāṉṟu* (evidence, proof) is documented in P1 item 16 and P2b. *nirūpikku* (to prove) is documented in P2b Construction 1 under *cāṉṟu* and Construction 3 under *ātāram*. The construction combines both evidence-domain vocabulary and truth-domain vocabulary in a causative frame.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### 3.2 Causing trust or credibility to be established

**Expression 22**

**Form:** நம்பிக்கையை ஊட்டினார்கள்
**Transliteration:** nampikkaiyai ūṭṭināṟkaḷ
**Morpheme breakdown:** *nampikkaiyai* (trust/confidence, accusative) + *ūṭṭi* (conjunctive of *ūṭṭu*, to instill, to feed) + *-ṉārkaḷ* (past, 3pl honorific)
**English translation:** They instilled confidence / They inspired trust
**Dynamic type:** Causative — agent causing trust to be established in others
**Notes:** *nampikkai ūṭṭu* (to instill confidence) is documented in P1b Root 3 item 15 as a common collocation. This is the standard expression for the deliberate building of trust or confidence in a person or group.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Expression 23**

**Form:** நம்பிக்கையை தந்தார்கள்
**Transliteration:** nampikkaiyai tantārkaḷ
**Morpheme breakdown:** *nampikkaiyai* (trust/confidence, accusative) + *tantu* (conjunctive of *taru*, to give) + *-ārkaḷ* (past, 3pl honorific)
**English translation:** They gave confidence / They provided reassurance
**Dynamic type:** Causative — agent causing trust to be given to others
**Notes:** *nampikkai taru* (to give confidence) is documented in P1b Root 3 item 17. This construction is slightly less formal than *ūṭṭu* and emphasizes the giving of reassurance rather than the building of confidence from scratch.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Expression 24**

**Form:** நம்பகத்தன்மையை நிலைநாட்டினார்கள்
**Transliteration:** nampakattaṉmaiyai nilainātṭināṟkaḷ
**Morpheme breakdown:** *nampakattaṉmaiyai* (credibility, accusative) + *nilainātṭi* (conjunctive of *nilainātṭu*, to establish, to maintain) + *-ṉārkaḷ* (past, 3pl honorific)
**English translation:** They established credibility / They built up trustworthiness
**Dynamic type:** Causative — agent causing credibility to be established
**Notes:** *nampakattaṉmaiyai nilainātṭāṇum* (credibility must be established) is documented in P2b Construction 2 under *nampakattaṉmai*. The past tense form here describes the completed act of establishing credibility.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### 3.3 Causing honesty or integrity to be established

**Expression 25**

**Form:** தூய்மையை நிலைநாட்டினார்கள்
**Transliteration:** tūymaiyai nilainātṭināṟkaḷ
**Morpheme breakdown:** *tūymaiyai* (purity/integrity, accusative) + *nilainātṭi* (conjunctive of *nilainātṭu*, to establish) + *-ṉārkaḷ* (past, 3pl honorific)
**English translation:** They established integrity / They upheld purity
**Dynamic type:** Causative — agent causing integrity to be established or maintained
**Notes:** *tūymai* is documented in P1 item 25 and P2a. The verb *nilainātṭu* (to establish, to maintain) is documented in P2b under *nampakattaṉmai*; it applies equally naturally to *tūymai* in formal and political discourse about maintaining moral integrity.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Expression 26**

**Form:** தூய்மைப்படுத்தினார்கள்
**Transliteration:** tūymaippaṭuttināṟkaḷ
**Morpheme breakdown:** *tūymai* (purity) + *paṭuttu* (causative) + *-ināṟkaḷ* (past, 3pl honorific)
**English translation:** They purified (it/them) / They cleansed (it/them)
**Dynamic type:** Causative — agent causing something to become pure
**Notes:** *tūymaippaṭuttu* (to purify, to cleanse) is documented in P2a under *tūymai* as a productive morphological causative. This is the standard expression for the act of purification in both literal and figurative senses.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### 3.4 Causing something to be verified or confirmed

**Expression 27**

**Form:** உறுதிப்படுத்தினார்கள்
**Transliteration:** uṟutippaṭuttināṟkaḷ
**Morpheme breakdown:** *uṟuti* (firmness, certainty) + *paṭuttu* (causative) + *-ināṟkaḷ* (past, 3pl honorific)
**English translation:** They confirmed (it) / They verified (it) / They certified (it)
**Dynamic type:** Causative — agent causing a fact or state to be confirmed
**Notes:** *uṟutippaṭuttu* (to confirm, to verify, to certify) is documented in P1 item 27 and P2a. This is the standard formal expression for the act of official confirmation or certification.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Expression 28**

**Form:** ஆதாரப்படுத்தினார்கள்
**Transliteration:** ātārappaṭuttināṟkaḷ
**Morpheme breakdown:** *ātāram* (evidence, basis) + *paṭuttu* (causative) + *-ināṟkaḷ* (past, 3pl honorific)
**English translation:** They substantiated (it) / They provided evidence for (it)
**Dynamic type:** Causative — agent causing a claim to be supported with evidence
**Notes:** *ātārappaṭuttu* (to substantiate, to support with evidence) is documented in P2a under *ātāram*. This is the standard expression for the act of providing evidential support for a claim in legal and journalistic contexts.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Expression 29**

**Form:** நியாயப்படுத்தினார்கள்
**Transliteration:** niyāyappaṭuttināṟkaḷ
**Morpheme breakdown:** *niyāyam* (justice, justification) + *paṭuttu* (causative) + *-ināṟkaḷ* (past, 3pl honorific)
**English translation:** They justified (it) / They rationalized (it)
**Dynamic type:** Causative — agent causing an action or claim to be justified
**Notes:** *niyāyappaṭuttu* (to justify, to rationalize) is documented in P2a under *niyāyam*. This expression is used in moral and legal contexts for the act of providing justification; it can carry either a neutral sense (legitimate justification) or a negative sense (rationalization of wrongdoing), depending on context.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### 3.5 Causing truth to be concealed or falsified

**Expression 30**

**Form:** உண்மையை மறைத்தார்கள்
**Transliteration:** uṇmaiyai maṟaittārkaḷ
**Morpheme breakdown:** *uṇmaiyai* (truth, accusative) + *maṟaitti* (conjunctive of *maṟai*, to conceal) + *-ārkaḷ* (past, 3pl honorific)
**English translation:** They concealed the truth / They hid the facts
**Dynamic type:** Causative — agent causing truth to be hidden
**Notes:** *uṇmai maṟai* (to conceal the truth) is documented in P1b Root 1 item 12 and P2b Construction 6 under *uṇmai*. This is the standard accusatory expression for deliberate suppression of facts.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Expression 31**

**Form:** சான்றுகளை மறைத்தார்கள்
**Transliteration:** cāṉṟukaḷai maṟaittārkaḷ
**Morpheme breakdown:** *cāṉṟukaḷai* (evidence, accusative plural) + *maṟaitti* (conjunctive of *maṟai*) + *-ārkaḷ* (past, 3pl honorific)
**English translation:** They concealed the evidence / They suppressed the proof
**Dynamic type:** Causative — agent causing evidence to be hidden
**Notes:** *cāṉṟukaḷai maṟai* (to conceal evidence) is documented in P2b Construction 4 under *cāṉṟu*. This is the standard legal expression for suppression of evidence. Parallel in structure to Expression 30 but with *cāṉṟu* (evidence) rather than *uṇmai* (truth) as the object.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

**Expression 32**

**Form:** பொய் சொல்லி ஏமாற்றினான்
**Transliteration:** poy colli ēmāṟṟiṉāṉ
**Morpheme breakdown:** *poy* (lie) + *colli* (conjunctive of *col*, to say) + *ēmāṟṟi* (conjunctive of *ēmāṟṟu*, to deceive, to cheat) + *-ṉāṉ* (past, 3sg masculine)
**English translation:** He deceived (someone) by lying / He lied and cheated
**Dynamic type:** Causative — agent causing a false belief through lying
**Notes:** Documented directly in P2b Construction 8 under *poy*. The conjunctive participle *colli* (having said) expresses the means by which the deception was accomplished. This is the standard expression for deception through lying.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### 3.6 Causing trust to be broken

**Expression 33**

**Form:** நம்பிக்கையை துரோகம் செய்தான்
**Transliteration:** nampikkaiyai turōkam ceytāṉ
**Morpheme breakdown:** *nampikkaiyai* (trust, accusative) + *turōkam* (betrayal, treason) + *ceytāṉ* (past, 3sg masculine of *cey*, to do)

**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Section 4: Stability — Expressions of Maintaining or Resisting Change

This section covers expressions where a truth-related state is maintained, preserved, or resisted against change. These are documented in P1–P5 but were not explicitly analyzed as a dynamic type in earlier steps.

---

**Expression 34**

**Form:** உண்மையில் நிலைத்திருக்கிறார்
**Transliteration:** uṇmaiyil nilaittirukkirār
**Morpheme breakdown:** *uṇmaiyil* (in truth, locative) + *nilaittu* (conjunctive of *nilai*, to stand firm, to remain stable) + *irukkirār* (present continuous of *iru*, to be; 3sg honorific)
**English translation:** He/She stands firm in truth / He/She remains committed to truth
**Dynamic type:** Stability — maintaining a truth-related state against pressure
**Notes:** *uṇmaiyil* (in truth) is documented in P1 item 20 and P2b. The verb *nilai* (to stand firm, to remain stable) combined with the durative auxiliary *iru* (to be) expresses the maintenance of a state over time. This construction appears in moral and political discourse about commitment to truth.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Expression 35**

**Form:** நேர்மையை கைவிடவில்லை
**Transliteration:** nēṟmaiyai kaivitavillai
**Morpheme breakdown:** *nēṟmaiyai* (honesty, accusative) + *kaivita* (infinitive of *kaivitu*, to abandon, to let go of) + *-villai* (negative present/general)
**English translation:** (He/She) has not abandoned honesty / (He/She) has not given up integrity
**Dynamic type:** Stability — resistance to abandoning a truth-related state
**Notes:** *nēṟmai* is documented in P1 item 3 and P1b Root 2. The verb *kaivitu* (to abandon, to let go of) is a natural expression for the relinquishing of a value or commitment; its negation expresses the maintenance of that commitment. This construction appears in moral and political discourse about principled conduct under pressure.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Expression 36**

**Form:** நம்பிக்கையை தக்கவைத்துக்கொண்டார்கள்
**Transliteration:** nampikkaiyai takkavaittukkoṇṭārkaḷ
**Morpheme breakdown:** *nampikkaiyai* (trust, accusative) + *takka* (worthy, fitting; here: to keep/maintain) + *vaittukkoḷ* (to keep for oneself, to maintain; reflexive-benefactive compound) + *-ṭārkaḷ* (past, 3pl honorific)
**English translation:** They maintained trust / They kept faith (with someone)
**Dynamic type:** Stability — maintaining a trust relationship over time
**Notes:** *nampikkai* is documented in P1 item 28 and P1b Root 3. The compound *takkavaittukkoḷ* (to keep/maintain something for oneself) is a natural expression for the deliberate maintenance of a relational state. This construction appears in institutional and interpersonal contexts about sustaining trust.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Expression 37**

**Form:** உண்மை மாறாமல் இருக்கிறது
**Transliteration:** uṇmai māṟāmal irukkiṟatu
**Morpheme breakdown:** *uṇmai* (truth) + *māṟāmal* (without changing; privative adverbial of *māṟu*, to change) + *irukkiṟatu* (present of *iru*, to be/remain)
**English translation:** The truth remains unchanged / The truth stays the same
**Dynamic type:** Stability — truth persisting without change
**Notes:** *uṇmai* is documented throughout P1 and P1b. The construction *māṟāmal iru* (to remain without changing) is a natural expression for the persistence of a state. This construction appears in philosophical and moral discourse asserting the unchanging nature of truth, often in contrast to changing circumstances or opinions.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Section 6: Observations on Asymmetries

The following asymmetries are supported by the data documented in P1–P5 and the expressions above. They are reported without interpretation beyond what the data supports.

---

**Asymmetry 1: *uṇmai* has a richer inchoative vocabulary than *poy***

*uṇmai* participates in multiple distinct inchoative collocations (*veḷippaṭu*, *teri*, *nilaipeṟu*, *-āki viṭu*) describing different aspects of truth becoming apparent or established. *poy* participates primarily in the generic *-āki viṭu* construction for becoming false. No lexicalized inchoative collocations parallel to *uṇmai veḷippaṭu* or *uṇmai teri* are attested for *poy* in the CONTEXT data.

---

**Asymmetry 2: Causative expressions for concealment are concentrated around *uṇmai* and *cāṉṟu***

The causative verb *maṟai* (to conceal) is documented in P1b and P2b specifically in collocations with *uṇmai* (*uṇmai maṟai*) and *cāṉṟu* (*cāṉṟukaḷai maṟai*). No parallel *maṟai* collocations are documented for *nēṟmai*, *nampikkai*, or *ātāram* in the CONTEXT data. This asymmetry is preserved here.

---

**Asymmetry 3: The morphological causative *paṭuttu* is productive for abstract nominals but not for *uṇmai* itself**

The *paṭuttu* causative is documented in P2a and P4a for *ātāram* (*ātārappaṭuttu*), *niyāyam* (*niyāyappaṭuttu*), *tūymai* (*tūymaippaṭuttu*), and *uṟuti* (*uṟutippaṭuttu*). No *uṇmaippaṭuttu* form is documented in the CONTEXT data for *uṇmai* itself. The causative for *uṇmai* is expressed lexically (*niṟuvu*, *nirūpikku*, *veḷippaṭuttu*) rather than through the *paṭuttu* morphological pattern.

---

**Asymmetry 4: Stability expressions are attested for *uṇmai* and *nēṟmai* but not for *poy*, *pōli*, or *tavaṟu***

Stability expressions (maintaining a state against change) are documented for *uṇmai* (*māṟāmal iru*, *nilai*) and *nēṟmai* (*kaivitavillai*) and *nampikkai* (*takkavaittukkoḷ*). No stability expressions are documented in the CONTEXT data for *poy*, *pōli*, or *tavaṟu*. This asymmetry may reflect the evaluative polarity of these terms: stability is a valued property associated with positive truth-domain states, not with their negations.
