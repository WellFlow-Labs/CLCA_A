# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2b Compositional Constructions
## Language: Korean (ko)
### Transliteration Standard: Revised Romanization of Korean (RR)

---

## Preliminary Note on Scope and Method

This step documents attested multi-word constructions involving the 30 P1 base forms. "Multi-word construction" is understood here as a syntactically composite expression — including verb-object collocations, predicative collocations, adverbial phrases, and idiomatic expressions — that is not reducible to simple derivational morphology (handled in P2a) and that functions as a recognizable unit in Korean usage.

The following categories are represented where attested:
- **Verb-object collocations** (N + 을/를 + V): e.g., 진실을 말하다
- **Predicative collocations** (N + 이/가 + Adj/V): e.g., 신빙성이 있다
- **Adverbial collocations** (N + particle + adverbial function)
- **Idiomatic expressions** (fixed multi-word units with non-compositional or semi-compositional meaning)
- **Syntactic frames** (productive patterns in which the base form participates)

Forms already fully documented in P1b or P2a are cross-referenced rather than re-described in full. The focus is on constructions not yet entered into the record, or on constructions that warrant additional usage documentation.

**Asymmetry is preserved throughout.** Where no common construction exists for a given base form or construction type, this is explicitly stated.

## 1. 진실 (jinsil) — truth, truthfulness

---

**1.1**
- **Base Form:** 진실 (jinsil)
- **Example:** 진실을 말하다
- **Transliteration:** jinsilеul malhada
- **English translation:** to tell the truth
- **Usage notes:** Standard verb-object collocation for the act of speaking truthfully. Used across all registers, from everyday conversation to legal testimony. *Cross-referenced from P1b.*
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**1.2**
- **Base Form:** 진실 (jinsil)
- **Example:** 진실을 밝히다
- **Transliteration:** jinsilеul balkhida
- **English translation:** to reveal the truth, to bring the truth to light
- **Usage notes:** Used in journalistic, legal, and investigative contexts to describe the act of uncovering or disclosing what is actually true. *Cross-referenced from P1b.*
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**1.3**
- **Base Form:** 진실 (jinsil)
- **Example:** 진실을 숨기다
- **Transliteration:** jinsilеul sumgida
- **English translation:** to hide the truth, to conceal the truth
- **Usage notes:** Verb-object collocation describing deliberate concealment of what is true. Common in journalistic, legal, and interpersonal discourse as the counterpart to 진실을 밝히다.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**1.4**
- **Base Form:** 진실 (jinsil)
- **Example:** 진실을 알다
- **Transliteration:** jinsilеul alda
- **English translation:** to know the truth
- **Usage notes:** Verb-object collocation describing the epistemic state of knowing what is actually the case. Common across all registers; frequently appears in narrative and investigative contexts.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**1.5**
- **Base Form:** 진실 (jinsil)
- **Example:** 진실을 외면하다
- **Transliteration:** jinsilеul oemyeonhada
- **English translation:** to turn away from the truth, to ignore the truth
- **Usage notes:** Used in journalistic, political, and ethical discourse to describe the act of deliberately avoiding or refusing to acknowledge what is true. Carries a critical or accusatory tone.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**1.6**
- **Base Form:** 진실 (jinsil)
- **Example:** 진실이 드러나다
- **Transliteration:** jinsili deureonada
- **English translation:** the truth comes to light, the truth is revealed
- **Usage notes:** Predicative collocation describing the emergence of truth, often in contexts where it had previously been concealed. Common in journalistic and narrative discourse. The subject is 진실 and the verb 드러나다 (to be revealed, to emerge) is intransitive.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**1.7**
- **Base Form:** 진실 (jinsil)
- **Example:** 진실이 밝혀지다
- **Transliteration:** jinsili balkhyeojida
- **English translation:** the truth is uncovered, the truth comes out
- **Usage notes:** Passive predicative collocation. Functionally similar to 진실이 드러나다 but with a slightly more agentive implication — the truth is uncovered through investigation or disclosure rather than simply emerging. Common in journalistic and legal discourse.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**1.8**
- **Base Form:** 진실 (jinsil)
- **Example:** 진실 규명
- **Transliteration:** jinsil gyumyeong
- **English translation:** truth investigation, clarification of the truth, truth-finding
- **Usage notes:** Noun-noun compound used in political, legal, and historical contexts to refer to formal processes of establishing what actually happened. *Cross-referenced from P1b.*
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**1.9**
- **Base Form:** 진실 (jinsil)
- **Example:** 진실과 거짓을 구별하다
- **Transliteration:** jinsil gwa geojiteul gubyeolhada
- **English translation:** to distinguish truth from falsehood
- **Usage notes:** Verb-object collocation framing the epistemic task of differentiating true from false claims. Common in educational, philosophical, and journalistic discourse. The paired structure 진실과 거짓 (truth and falsehood) is itself a fixed nominal pair.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**1.10**
- **Base Form:** 진실 (jinsil)
- **Example:** 진실의 순간
- **Transliteration:** jinsil ui sungan
- **English translation:** the moment of truth
- **Usage notes:** Nominal phrase used to describe a decisive or revealing moment. Likely influenced by the Spanish/English expression "moment of truth" (originally from bullfighting). Used in everyday and journalistic Korean to describe situations where the true state of affairs becomes apparent or a critical decision must be made.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**1.11**
- **Base Form:** 진실 (jinsil)
- **Example:** 진실을 왜곡하다
- **Transliteration:** jinsilеul waegokada
- **English translation:** to distort the truth
- **Usage notes:** Verb-object collocation describing the act of misrepresenting what is true. Common in journalistic and political discourse. Connects the 진실 and 왜곡 entries from P1.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## 2. 사실 (sasil) — fact, actuality

---

**2.1**
- **Base Form:** 사실 (sasil)
- **Example:** 사실을 확인하다
- **Transliteration:** sasilеul hwagin hada
- **English translation:** to verify the facts, to confirm the facts
- **Usage notes:** Verb-object collocation describing the act of checking whether something is factually accurate. Extremely common in journalistic, legal, and everyday discourse.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**2.2**
- **Base Form:** 사실 (sasil)
- **Example:** 사실에 근거하다
- **Transliteration:** sasile geungeo hada
- **English translation:** to be based on fact, to be grounded in fact
- **Usage notes:** Predicative collocation asserting that a claim or representation has a factual basis. Common in journalistic, academic, and legal discourse. Connects 사실 and 근거 from P1.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**2.3**
- **Base Form:** 사실 (sasil)
- **Example:** 사실을 왜곡하다
- **Transliteration:** sasilеul waegokada
- **English translation:** to distort the facts
- **Usage notes:** Verb-object collocation describing the misrepresentation of factual information. Common in journalistic and political discourse. Connects 사실 and 왜곡 from P1.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**2.4**
- **Base Form:** 사실 (sasil)
- **Example:** 사실을 은폐하다
- **Transliteration:** sasilеul eunpye hada
- **English translation:** to conceal the facts, to cover up the facts
- **Usage notes:** Verb-object collocation describing deliberate suppression of factual information. Common in journalistic, legal, and political discourse; carries a stronger implication of institutional or deliberate concealment than 사실을 숨기다.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**2.5**
- **Base Form:** 사실 (sasil)
- **Example:** 사실이 아니다
- **Transliteration:** sasili anida
- **English translation:** it is not a fact, that is not true
- **Usage notes:** Standard predicative negation collocation used to deny the factual accuracy of a claim. Extremely common across all registers as a direct denial formula.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**2.6**
- **Base Form:** 사실 (sasil)
- **Example:** 사실로 밝혀지다
- **Transliteration:** sasillo balkhyeojida
- **English translation:** to be revealed as fact, to turn out to be true
- **Usage notes:** Predicative collocation describing the confirmation of something as factually accurate, often after a period of uncertainty or investigation. Common in journalistic discourse.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**2.7**
- **Base Form:** 사실 (sasil)
- **Example:** 사실 여부를 따지다
- **Transliteration:** sasil yeobureul ttajida
- **English translation:** to examine whether something is factual, to question the factual basis
- **Usage notes:** Collocation used in legal, journalistic, and analytical contexts to describe the act of scrutinizing whether a claim is factually accurate. 여부 (whether or not) is a productive element in formal Korean discourse.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**2.8**
- **Base Form:** 사실 (sasil)
- **Example:** 사실을 직시하다
- **Transliteration:** sasilеul jiksihada
- **English translation:** to face the facts squarely, to look the facts in the face
- **Usage notes:** Verb-object collocation describing the act of confronting reality without evasion. Common in motivational, journalistic, and analytical discourse. 직시하다 (to look directly at) carries a connotation of unflinching acknowledgment.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**2.9**
- **Base Form:** 사실 (sasil)
- **Example:** 엄연한 사실
- **Transliteration:** eomyeonhan sasil
- **English translation:** an undeniable fact, an incontrovertible fact
- **Usage notes:** Fixed nominal collocation using the modifier 엄연한 (undeniable, incontrovertible) to emphasize that something is unquestionably factual. Common in argumentative and journalistic discourse.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**2.10**
- **Base Form:** 사실 (sasil)
- **Example:** 사실관계를 파악하다
- **Transliteration:** sasil gwangyereul paakada
- **English translation:** to grasp the factual circumstances, to establish the facts of the case
- **Usage notes:** Verb-object collocation used in legal and journalistic contexts to describe the act of determining the factual basis of a situation. 사실관계 is documented in P1b; this collocation with 파악하다 is its most common verbal partner.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## 3. 진짜 (jinjja) — real, genuine, authentic

---

**3.1**
- **Base Form:** 진짜 (jinjja)
- **Example:** 진짜인지 가짜인지 모르다
- **Transliteration:** jinjjainji gajjainji moreuda
- **English translation:** not knowing whether it is real or fake
- **Usage notes:** Fixed interrogative frame expressing uncertainty about authenticity. Common in everyday discourse when evaluating the genuineness of objects, claims, or situations. The paired structure 진짜인지 가짜인지 is a productive and frequently attested construction.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**3.2**
- **Base Form:** 진짜 (jinjja)
- **Example:** 진짜 같다
- **Transliteration:** jinjja gatda
- **English translation:** it seems real, it looks genuine, it appears authentic
- **Usage notes:** Predicative collocation expressing the appearance of genuineness without asserting it. Common in everyday discourse when evaluating imitations, performances, or claims. The -같다 construction is productive with many nominal bases.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**3.3**
- **Base Form:** 진짜 (jinjja)
- **Example:** 진짜 맞다
- **Transliteration:** jinjja matda
- **English translation:** it's really true, that's genuinely correct
- **Usage notes:** Colloquial emphatic collocation used to assert that something is genuinely the case. 진짜 functions as an adverbial intensifier modifying 맞다 (to be correct/right). Extremely common in informal spoken Korean.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Note on 진짜:** The base form functions as both adjective and adverb and participates freely in nominal and adverbial positions. Its multi-word constructions are largely productive syntactic combinations rather than fixed idioms. The constructions above represent the most frequently attested fixed or semi-fixed patterns.

## 4. 가짜 (gajja) — fake, counterfeit, imitation

---

**4.1**
- **Base Form:** 가짜 (gajja)
- **Example:** 가짜로 판명되다
- **Transliteration:** gajjaro panmyeong doeda
- **English translation:** to be determined to be fake, to be found to be counterfeit
- **Usage notes:** Predicative collocation describing the outcome of an authenticity investigation. Common in journalistic and legal discourse when the result of verification is that something is not genuine.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**4.2**
- **Base Form:** 가짜 (gajja)
- **Example:** 가짜 뉴스
- **Transliteration:** gajja nyuseu
- **English translation:** fake news
- **Usage notes:** Nominal collocation referring to deliberately false or misleading news content. Entered Korean public discourse prominently from approximately 2016–2017 onward, largely parallel to international usage. Now well-established in journalistic and political discourse.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**4.3**
- **Base Form:** 가짜 (gajja)
- **Example:** 가짜인 줄 몰랐다
- **Transliteration:** gajjain jul mollassda
- **English translation:** I didn't know it was fake
- **Usage notes:** Colloquial construction expressing ignorance of inauthenticity. The -인 줄 몰랐다 frame (didn't know that it was X) is productive; this collocation with 가짜 is among the most common instantiations in everyday discourse.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**4.4**
- **Base Form:** 가짜 (gajja)
- **Example:** 진짜와 가짜를 구별하다
- **Transliteration:** jinjja wa gajjareul gubyeolhada
- **English translation:** to distinguish the genuine from the fake
- **Usage notes:** Verb-object collocation framing the task of authenticity discrimination. The paired nominal structure 진짜와 가짜 (the genuine and the fake) is a fixed nominal pair that appears frequently in this and related constructions.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## 5. 정확 (jeonghwak) — accuracy, precision

---

**5.1**
- **Base Form:** 정확 (jeonghwak)
- **Example:** 정확히 말하면
- **Transliteration:** jeonghwaki malhamyeon
- **English translation:** to be precise, strictly speaking, if one speaks accurately
- **Usage notes:** Fixed adverbial frame used to introduce a more precise or corrected formulation of a previous statement. Extremely common across all registers as a discourse marker for precision. *Cross-referenced from P2a.*
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**5.2**
- **Base Form:** 정확 (jeonghwak)
- **Example:** 정확한 정보를 제공하다
- **Transliteration:** jeonghwakhan jeongboreul jegonghada
- **English translation:** to provide accurate information
- **Usage notes:** Verb-object collocation describing the act of supplying factually correct information. Common in journalistic, educational, and administrative discourse.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**5.3**
- **Base Form:** 정확 (jeonghwak)
- **Example:** 정확성을 기하다
- **Transliteration:** jeonghwakseong eul gihada
- **English translation:** to ensure accuracy, to aim for precision
- **Usage notes:** Verb-object collocation using 기하다 (to aim for, to ensure) with 정확성 as object. Common in journalistic, academic, and administrative discourse as a statement of commitment to accuracy.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**5.4**
- **Base Form:** 정확 (jeonghwak)
- **Example:** 정확을 기하다
- **Transliteration:** jeonghwak eul gihada
- **English translation:** to ensure accuracy, to be precise
- **Usage notes:** Variant of 5.3 using the base nominal form 정확 rather than 정확성 as the object of 기하다. Both forms are attested; 정확성을 기하다 is somewhat more common in formal writing.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## 6. 올바르다 (olbareuda) — to be correct, right, proper

---

**6.1**
- **Base Form:** 올바르다 (olbareuda)
- **Example:** 올바른 길을 가다
- **Transliteration:** olbareun gireul gada
- **English translation:** to go the right path, to take the correct course
- **Usage notes:** Idiomatic collocation using the metaphor of a path (길) to describe living or acting in accordance with correct or moral principles. Common in ethical, educational, and motivational discourse.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**6.2**
- **Base Form:** 올바르다 (olbareuda)
- **Example:** 올바른 판단을 내리다
- **Transliteration:** olbareun pandaneul naerida
- **English translation:** to make a correct judgment, to reach a right decision
- **Usage notes:** Verb-object collocation describing the act of arriving at a correct or appropriate judgment. Common in legal, ethical, and analytical discourse. 판단을 내리다 (to make a judgment) is itself a fixed collocation.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**6.3**
- **Base Form:** 올바르다 (olbareuda)
- **Example:** 올바른 방향으로 나아가다
- **Transliteration:** olbareun banghyang euro naagada
- **English translation:** to move in the right direction
- **Usage notes:** Directional collocation used in policy, organizational, and motivational discourse to describe progress toward a correct or appropriate goal. The 방향 (direction) frame is productive with 올바른.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Note on 올바르다:** This adjective participates primarily in productive syntactic combinations as a prenominal modifier (올바른 + N) rather than in fixed idiomatic constructions. The constructions above represent the most frequently attested semi-fixed patterns.

## 7. 옳다 (olta) — to be right, correct, just

---

**7.1**
- **Base Form:** 옳다 (olta)
- **Example:** 옳고 그름을 가리다
- **Transliteration:** olko geureumеul garida
- **English translation:** to distinguish right from wrong
- **Usage notes:** Verb-object collocation using the fixed nominal pair 옳고 그름 (right and wrong) as the object of 가리다 (to distinguish). Common in ethical, educational, and legal discourse.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**7.2**
- **Base Form:** 옳다 (olta)
- **Example:** 옳고 그름을 따지다
- **Transliteration:** olko geureumеul ttajida
- **English translation:** to examine right and wrong, to question what is right and wrong
- **Usage notes:** Variant of 7.1 using 따지다 (to examine, to question closely) rather than 가리다. Carries a slightly more argumentative or analytical tone. Common in ethical and everyday discourse.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**7.3**
- **Base Form:** 옳다 (olta)
- **Example:** 네 말이 옳다
- **Transliteration:** ne mali olta
- **English translation:** what you say is right, you are correct
- **Usage notes:** Standard predicative construction affirming the correctness of a statement or position. The subject-predicate frame N이/가 옳다 is the primary syntactic pattern for this adjective. Common across all registers.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**7.4**
- **Base Form:** 옳다 (olta)
- **Example:** 옳은 일을 하다
- **Transliteration:** oreun ireul hada
- **English translation:** to do the right thing
- **Usage notes:** Verb-object collocation describing action in accordance with moral correctness. Common in ethical and everyday discourse. The phrase 옳은 일 (the right thing) is a frequently attested nominal unit.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## 8. 그르다 (geureuda) — to be wrong, incorrect

---

**8.1**
- **Base Form:** 그르다 (geureuda)
- **Example:** 옳고 그름
- **Transliteration:** olko geureum
- **English translation:** right and wrong
- **Usage notes:** Fixed nominal pair in which 그름 (the nominalized form of 그르다) appears almost exclusively. This is the primary attested construction for 그르다 in contemporary Korean. *Cross-referenced from P1.*
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**8.2**
- **Base Form:** 그르다 (geureuda)
- **Example:** 그것은 그르다
- **Transliteration:** geugeoseun geureuda
- **English translation:** that is wrong, that is incorrect
- **Usage notes:** Standard predicative construction asserting incorrectness or moral wrongness. Somewhat formal or literary in contemporary usage, consistent with the base form's register. Less common than 틀리다 (to be mistaken) or 잘못되다 (to be wrong) in everyday speech for factual incorrectness.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Note on 그르다:** This adjective is notably less productive in multi-word constructions than its antonym 옳다. Its primary attested multi-word context is the fixed pair 옳고 그름. Independent predicative use (그것은 그르다) is attested but formal. No common idiomatic constructions beyond these patterns were identified.

- **Transliteration:** heowiimi deureonada
- **English translation:** it is revealed to be false, the falseness comes to light
- **Usage notes:** Predicative collocation describing the exposure of something as false. Common in journalistic discourse when a claim or representation is shown to be untrue.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**10.4**

- **Transliteration:** heowiro kkumida

## 11. 거짓 (geojit) — lie, falsehood, falseness

---

**11.1**
- **Base Form:** 거짓 (geojit)
- **Example:** 거짓말을 하다
- **Transliteration:** geojitmalеul hada
- **English translation:** to tell a lie
- **Usage notes:** Standard verb-object collocation. *Cross-referenced from P1 and P2a.*
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**11.2**
- **Base Form:** 거짓 (geojit)
- **Example:** 거짓말을 들키다
- **Transliteration:** geojitmalеul deulkida
- **English translation:** to be caught in a lie, to have one's lie found out
- **Usage notes:** Verb-object collocation describing the exposure of a lie. Common in everyday discourse. 들키다 (to be caught, to be found out) is the standard verb for this meaning.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**11.3**
- **Base Form:** 거짓 (geojit)
- **Example:** 거짓말로 둘러대다
