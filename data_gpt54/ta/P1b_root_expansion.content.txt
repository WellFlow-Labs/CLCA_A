## Step 1 — Central roots

The most recurrent building blocks in the P1 list are:

1. **உண்மை — uṇmai**  
   **Participates in:** 3 P1 items  
   (**உண்மை, உண்மையான, உண்மைத்தன்மை**)  
   **Core meaning contribution:** truth, reality, factual or genuine status

2. **மெய் — mey**  
   **Participates in:** 3 P1 items  
   (**மெய், மெய்யான, மெய்ப்பொருள்**)  
   **Core meaning contribution:** true, real, genuine; also essential reality / what is not false or illusory

3. **சரி — cari**  
   **Participates in:** 3 P1 items  
   (**சரி, சரியான, சரித்தன்மை**)  
   **Core meaning contribution:** correctness, rightness, propriety, practical validity

These are the clearest repeated stems in the P1 set. Other items such as **பொய்**, **தவறு**, **நிஜம்**, **உறுதி**, and **நம்பக-** are important semantically, but they recur less as visible derivational bases within the provided P1 inventory.

## Step 2 — Root expansion

### Root 1: **உண்மை — uṇmai**

This root is productive, but not extremely so in everyday compounding. Common forms are concentrated around truth, reality, factuality, and sincerity.

1. **உண்மை**  
   **Transliteration:** uṇmai  
   **Breakdown:** root noun  
   **Translation:** truth; reality  
   **Context:** Basic and very common word for truth or what is real. Used in ordinary speech, journalism, argument, and philosophy.  
   **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

2. **உண்மையான**  
   **Transliteration:** uṇmaiyāṉa  
   **Breakdown:** உண்மை + -ஆன  
   **Translation:** true; real; genuine  
   **Context:** Used for true statements, real situations, genuine persons or objects. Common across spoken and written Tamil.  
   **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

3. **உண்மைத்தன்மை**  
   **Transliteration:** uṇmaittaṉmai  
   **Breakdown:** உண்மை + -த்- + தன்மை  
   **Translation:** truthfulness; veracity; authenticity  
   **Context:** Common in formal evaluation of reports, testimony, representation, and authenticity. Often used in journalism, criticism, and legal-administrative writing.  
   **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

4. **உண்மையில்**  
   **Transliteration:** uṇmaiyil  
   **Breakdown:** உண்மை + -இல்  
   **Translation:** in truth; actually; in fact  
   **Context:** Very common discourse form in speech and writing. Used to correct appearances, introduce the real situation, or emphasize factuality.  
   **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

5. **உண்மையாக**  
   **Transliteration:** uṇmaiyāka  
   **Breakdown:** உண்மை + -ஆக  
   **Translation:** truly; really; genuinely  
   **Context:** Used adverbially to stress sincerity or factual reality. Common in both conversational and formal registers.  
   **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

6. **உண்மையிலே / உண்மையிலேயே**  
   **Transliteration:** uṇmaiyilē / uṇmaiyilēyē  
   **Breakdown:** உண்மை + -இல் + emphatic extension  
   **Translation:** truly; really indeed; actually  
   **Context:** Common emphatic spoken and written forms. Used to intensify assertion that something is genuinely the case.  
   **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

7. **உண்மையா?**  
   **Transliteration:** uṇmaiyā?  
   **Breakdown:** உண்மை + interrogative colloquial usage  
   **Translation:** really?; is it true?  
   **Context:** Very common in speech for surprise, verification, or doubt. It tests factuality or sincerity.  
   **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

8. **உண்மையில்லை**  
   **Transliteration:** uṇmaiyillai  
   **Breakdown:** உண்மை + இல்லை  
   **Translation:** not true; untrue  
   **Context:** Used to deny factual correctness or reality. Common in argument, reporting, and everyday contradiction.  
   **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

9. **உண்மையற்ற**  
   **Transliteration:** uṇmaiyaṟṟa  
   **Breakdown:** உண்மை + அற்ற  
   **Translation:** untrue; baseless; lacking truth  
   **Context:** Common in formal prose for allegations, claims, or depictions judged not grounded in fact.  
   **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

10. **உண்மைக்குப் புறம்பான**  
    **Transliteration:** uṇmaikkup puṟampāṉa  
    **Breakdown:** உண்மை + -க்கு + புறம்பான  
    **Translation:** contrary to truth; contrary to fact  
    **Context:** Used in formal criticism, legal language, and public discourse for statements or representations that conflict with facts.  
    **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

11. **உண்மைக்கு மாறான**  
    **Transliteration:** uṇmaikku māṟāṉa  
    **Breakdown:** உண்மை + -க்கு + மாறான  
    **Translation:** opposed to truth; factually contrary  
    **Context:** Similar to the previous item, often used in evaluative prose and public argument.  
    **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

12. **உண்மை நிலை**  
    **Transliteration:** uṇmai nilai  
    **Breakdown:** உண்மை + நிலை  
    **Translation:** actual state; real condition  
    **Context:** Used in administration, reporting, and analysis to refer to the real state of affairs as distinct from claims or appearances.  
    **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

13. **உண்மை நிலவரம்**  
    **Transliteration:** uṇmai nilavaram  
    **Breakdown:** உண்மை + நிலவரம்  
    **Translation:** actual situation; true state of affairs  
    **Context:** Common in journalism, official reporting, and public discussion when clarifying what the situation really is.  
    **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

14. **உண்மை சம்பவம்**  
    **Transliteration:** uṇmai campavam  
    **Breakdown:** உண்மை + சம்பவம்  
    **Translation:** true incident; real event  
    **Context:** Common in media, storytelling, and film promotion to indicate that an event actually occurred.  
    **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

15. **உண்மை கதை**  
    **Transliteration:** uṇmai katai  
    **Breakdown:** உண்மை + கதை  
    **Translation:** true story  
    **Context:** Used in publishing, cinema, journalism, and conversation for narratives based on actual events.  
    **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

16. **உண்மை தகவல்**  
    **Transliteration:** uṇmai takaval  
    **Breakdown:** உண்மை + தகவல்  
    **Translation:** true information; factual information  
    **Context:** Used in media, administration, and education to distinguish verified information from rumor or falsehood.  
    **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

17. **உண்மை அறிதல்**  
    **Transliteration:** uṇmai aṟital  
    **Breakdown:** உண்மை + அறிதல்  
    **Translation:** knowing the truth; realization of reality  
    **Context:** Used in philosophical, religious, and reflective contexts for coming to know what is really the case.  
    **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

18. **உண்மை தேடல்**  
    **Transliteration:** uṇmai tēṭal  
    **Breakdown:** உண்மை + தேடல்  
    **Translation:** search for truth  
    **Context:** Common in intellectual, journalistic, and philosophical contexts. Also used rhetorically in essays and public discourse.  
    **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

19. **உண்மை வெளிப்பாடு**  
    **Transliteration:** uṇmai veḷippāṭu  
    **Breakdown:** உண்மை + வெளிப்பாடு  
    **Translation:** revelation of truth; truthful expression  
    **Context:** Used in literary criticism, testimony, and reflective prose for the bringing out of what is true or real.  
    **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

20. **உண்மைச் சாட்சி**  
    **Transliteration:** uṇmaic cāṭci  
    **Breakdown:** உண்மை + சாட்சி  
    **Translation:** true testimony; truthful witness  
    **Context:** Used in legal and moral contexts for testimony that corresponds to fact.  
    **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

21. **உண்மை பேசு / உண்மையை பேசு**  
    **Transliteration:** uṇmai pēcu / uṇmaiyai pēcu  
    **Breakdown:** உண்மை + பேசு  
    **Translation:** speak the truth  
    **Context:** Very common in everyday moral instruction and interpersonal confrontation.  
    **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

22. **உண்மையைச் சொல்**  
    **Transliteration:** uṇmaiyaic col  
    **Breakdown:** உண்மை + சொல்  
    **Translation:** tell the truth  
    **Context:** Common in speech, parenting, narrative dialogue, and legal-dramatic contexts.  
    **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

23. **உண்மை முகம்**  
    **Transliteration:** uṇmai mukam  
    **Breakdown:** உண்மை + முகம்  
    **Translation:** true face; real nature  
    **Context:** Used figuratively in journalism and public discourse for the real character of a person, institution, or situation becoming visible.  
    **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

24. **உண்மை உருவம்**  
    **Transliteration:** uṇmai uruvam  
    **Breakdown:** உண்மை + உருவம்  
    **Translation:** true form; real form  
    **Context:** Used in literary, philosophical, and descriptive prose for actual form as opposed to appearance or distortion.  
    **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

25. **உண்மை உணர்வு**  
    **Transliteration:** uṇmai uṇarvu  
    **Breakdown:** உண்மை + உணர்வு  
    **Translation:** sense of reality; awareness of truth  
    **Context:** Used in reflective, educational, and philosophical writing.  
    **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

### Root 2: **மெய் — mey**

This root is highly productive and extends into literary, philosophical, grammatical, bodily, and religious usage. More forms exist beyond the list below.

1. **மெய்**  
   **Transliteration:** mey  
   **Breakdown:** root form  
   **Translation:** true; real; body  
   **Context:** Classical and still active lexical item. Used in literary, philosophical, grammatical, and religious contexts, and also in compounds.  
   **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

2. **மெய்யான**  
   **Transliteration:** meyyāṉa  
   **Breakdown:** மெய் + -ஆன  
   **Translation:** true; real; genuine  
   **Context:** Elevated or formal adjective for what is truly so. Common in religious and philosophical prose.  
   **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

3. **மெய்ப்பொருள்**  
   **Transliteration:** meypporuḷ  
   **Breakdown:** மெய் + பொருள்  
   **Translation:** true meaning; essential reality  
   **Context:** Common in literary exegesis, philosophy, and religious interpretation for the real import or essential substance of something.  
   **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

4. **மெய்மை**  
   **Transliteration:** meymai  
   **Breakdown:** மெய் + -மை  
   **Translation:** truth; truthfulness; reality  
   **Context:** Literary and formal noun parallel to உண்மை. Found in classical-style prose, ethical writing, and philosophical discourse.  
   **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

5. **மெய்யாக**  
   **Transliteration:** meyyāka  
   **Breakdown:** மெய் + -ஆக  
   **Translation:** truly; really  
   **Context:** Literary or elevated adverbial form. Used in formal prose and devotional or philosophical expression.  
   **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

6. **மெய்யென்று**  
   **Transliteration:** meyyeṉṟu  
   **Breakdown:** மெய் + என்று  
   **Translation:** as true; to be true  
   **Context:** Common in literary and argumentative prose when something is taken or declared as true.  
   **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

7. **மெய்யல்ல**  
   **Transliteration:** meyyalla  
   **Breakdown:** மெய் + அல்ல  
   **Translation:** not true; not real  
   **Context:** Used in formal or literary denial of truth or reality. Less colloquial than உண்மையில்லை.  
   **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

8. **மெய்ப்பாடு**  
   **Transliteration:** meyppāṭu  
   **Breakdown:** மெய் + பாடு  
   **Translation:** bodily expression of emotion; manifest emotional state  
   **Context:** Important in classical poetics and literary studies. Refers to outward bodily manifestation, especially in Tamil aesthetic theory.  
   **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

9. **மெய்ப்பொருளியல்**  
   **Transliteration:** meypporuḷiyal  
   **Breakdown:** மெய்ப்பொருள் + இயல்  
   **Translation:** metaphysics; study of ultimate reality  
   **Context:** Used in philosophy and academic writing for metaphysical inquiry into real being or ultimate substance.  
   **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

10. **மெய்யியல்**  
    **Transliteration:** meyyiyal  
    **Breakdown:** மெய் + இயல்  
    **Translation:** metaphysics; ontology; philosophy of reality  
    **Context:** Standard philosophical term in Tamil for inquiry into reality or being.  
    **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

11. **மெய்யுணர்வு**  
    **Transliteration:** meyyuṇarvu  
    **Breakdown:** மெய் + உணர்வு  
    **Translation:** true realization; realization of reality  
    **Context:** Common in religious, spiritual, and philosophical discourse for direct apprehension of truth or ultimate reality.  
    **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

12. **மெய்ஞ்ஞானம்**  
    **Transliteration:** meyññāṉam  
    **Breakdown:** மெய் + ஞானம்  
    **Translation:** true knowledge; spiritual knowledge  
    **Context:** Very common in Saiva and other religious-philosophical traditions for higher or genuine knowledge.  
    **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

13. **மெய்ஞ்ஞானி**  
    **Transliteration:** meyññāṉi  
    **Breakdown:** மெய்ஞ்ஞானம் + -ி  
    **Translation:** knower of true wisdom; realized sage  
    **Context:** Used in religious and devotional discourse for one possessing true spiritual knowledge.  
    **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

14. **மெய்சாட்சி**  
    **Transliteration:** meycāṭci  
    **Breakdown:** மெய் + சாட்சி  
    **Translation:** true witness; inner witness; conscience  
    **Context:** Used in moral, religious, and reflective discourse. In some contexts it refers to conscience or inward testimony.  
    **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

15. **மெய்சிலிர்ப்பு**  
    **Transliteration:** meycilirppu  
    **Breakdown:** மெய் + சிலிர்ப்பு  
    **Translation:** goosebumps; bodily thrill  
    **Context:** Very common in everyday and literary usage for a physical shiver or thrill, often from emotion, fear, or aesthetic impact.  
    **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

16. **மெய்மறத்தல்**  
    **Transliteration:** meymaṟattal  
    **Breakdown:** மெய் + மறத்தல்  
    **Translation:** loss of bodily awareness; ecstasy; being beside oneself  
    **Context:** Used in literary, devotional, and emotional contexts for overwhelming feeling or trance-like absorption.  
    **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

17. **மெய்மறந்து**  
    **Transliteration:** meymaṟantu  
    **Breakdown:** மெய் + மறந்து  
    **Translation:** beside oneself; in ecstasy; oblivious  
    **Context:** Common in literary and spoken usage for intense emotional absorption.  
    **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

18. **மெய்யுரை**  
    **Transliteration:** meyyurai  
    **Breakdown:** மெய் + உரை  
    **Translation:** true statement; authoritative exposition  
    **Context:** Used in literary, religious, and formal contexts for a truthful or genuine exposition.  
    **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

19. **மெய்நிலை**  
    **Transliteration:** meynilai  
    **Breakdown:** மெய் + நிலை  
    **Translation:** true state; real condition  
    **Context:** Used in philosophy, literary criticism, and formal prose for actual or essential state.  
    **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

20. **மெய்நிகர்**  
    **Transliteration:** meynikar  
    **Breakdown:** மெய் + நிகர்  
    **Translation:** virtual  
    **Context:** Standard modern technical term, especially in computing and media, as in virtual reality and virtual learning.

21. **மெய்நிகர் உண்மை**  
    **Transliteration:** meynikar uṇmai

23. **மெய்நிகர் சந்திப்பு**  
    **Transliteration:** meynikar cantippu  
    **Breakdown:** மெய்நிகர் + சந்திப்பு  
    **Translation:** virtual meeting  
    **Context:** Common in office, institutional, and technological usage.  
    **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

24. **மெய்யெழுத்து**

    **Breakdown:** மெய் + எழுத்து  
    **Translation:** consonant  
    **Context:** Standard grammatical term in Tamil orthography, contrasting with உயிரெழுத்து. Here மெய் does not directly mean truth but is part of the traditional letter classification.  
    **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

25. **உயிர்மெய்**  
    **Transliteration:** uyirmey  
    **Breakdown:** உயிர் + மெய்  
    **Translation:** compound letter of vowel + consonant  
    **Context:** Standard grammatical and pedagogical term in Tamil script instruction.  
    **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

26. **மெய்க்கீர்த்தி**  
    **Transliteration:** meykkīrtti  
    **Breakdown:** மெய் + கீர்த்தி  
    **Translation:** royal eulogy; formal inscriptional praise text  
    **Context:** Historical and literary term for inscriptional panegyric, especially in Chola epigraphy.  
    **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

27. **மெய்ப்படம்**  
    **Transliteration:** meyppaṭam  
    **Breakdown:** மெய் + படம்  
    **Translation:** realistic image; lifelike depiction  
    **Context:** Used in artistic and descriptive contexts for a depiction that appears true to life. Attestation is more limited than some other items.  
    **Epistemic status:** [UNCERTAIN]

28. **மெய்யுரு**  
    **Transliteration:** meyyuru  
    **Breakdown:** மெய் + உரு  
    **Translation:** true form; real form  
    **Context:** Found in literary and religious contexts for essential or actual form.  
    **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

29. **மெய்வழி**  
    **Transliteration:** meyvazhi  
    **Breakdown:** மெய் + வழி  
    **Translation:** true path; right path  
    **Context:** Used in religious and moral discourse for the genuine or proper spiritual path.  
    **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

30. **மெய்வருத்தம்**

### Root 3: **சரி — cari**

This root is very common in speech and practical evaluation, but derivational expansion is somewhat limited compared with its frequency in use.

1. **சரி**  
   **Transliteration:** cari  
   **Breakdown:** root form  
   **Translation:** correct; right; okay  
   **Context:** Extremely common in spoken and written Tamil for correctness, agreement, adequacy, or conversational acknowledgment.  
   **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

2. **சரியான**  
   **Transliteration:** cariyāṉa  
   **Breakdown:** சரி + -ஆன  
   **Translation:** correct; proper; accurate  
   **Context:** Used for correct answers, proper procedure, accurate information, and appropriate conduct.  
   **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

3. **சரித்தன்மை**  
   **Transliteration:** carittaṉmai  
   **Breakdown:** சரி + தன்மை  
   **Translation:** correctness; propriety  
   **Context:** Formal noun used in evaluative prose, though less common than other ways of expressing correctness.  
   **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

4. **சரியாக**  
   **Transliteration:** cariyāka  
   **Breakdown:** சரி + -ஆக  
   **Translation:** correctly; properly; exactly  
   **Context:** Very common adverb in speech, education, administration, and instruction.  
   **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

5. **சரியில்லை**  
   **Transliteration:** cariyillai  
   **Breakdown:** சரி + இல்லை  
   **Translation:** not right; not correct; not okay  
   **Context:** Very common in everyday speech for factual, practical, moral, or interpersonal disapproval.  
   **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

6. **சரியல்ல**  
   **Transliteration:** cariyalla  
   **Breakdown:** சரி + அல்ல  
   **Translation:** not correct; improper  
   **Context:** Common in somewhat more explicit or formal negation than சரியில்லை.  
   **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

7. **சரியா?**  
   **Transliteration:** cariyā?  
   **Breakdown:** சரி + interrogative usage  
   **Translation:** right?; okay?; correct?  
   **Context:** Extremely common in conversation for checking agreement, correctness, or acceptability.  
   **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

8. **சரிதான்**  
   **Transliteration:** caritāṉ  
   **Breakdown:** சரி + தான்  
   **Translation:** that is right; exactly so  
   **Context:** Common emphatic confirmation in speech and writing.  
   **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

9. **சரிபார்**  
   **Transliteration:** caripār  
   **Breakdown:** சரி + பார்  
   **Translation:** check; verify; inspect  
   **Context:** Very common in administration, education, editing, and technology for checking whether something is correct.  
   **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

10. **சரிபார்ப்பு**  
    **Transliteration:** caripārppu  
    **Breakdown:** சரிபார் + -ப்பு  
    **Translation:** verification; checking  
    **Context:** Standard term in bureaucratic, technical, educational, and procedural contexts.  
    **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Step 3 — Peripheral extensions

The following expressions show shifts or extensions away from straightforward truth/falsity evaluation:

- **மெய்** meaning **body** rather than truth/reality
- **மெய்ப்பாடு** in classical poetics for bodily manifestation of emotion
- **மெய்சிலிர்ப்பு** for physical thrill or goosebumps
- **மெய்மறத்தல் / மெய்மறந்து** for ecstasy, loss of bodily awareness, emotional absorption
- **மெய்யெழுத்து** and **உயிர்மெய்** in grammatical/orthographic terminology
- **மெய்நிகர்** in technical usage meaning virtual
- **மெய்க்கீர்த்தி** in inscriptional-historical usage
- **சரிநிகர்**, **சரிசமம்** where **சரி** contributes equivalence or parity rather than correctness
- **சரிகட்டு** where **சரி** contributes settlement/regularization rather than truth or correctness
- **உண்மை முகம்**, **உண்மை உருவம்** where the root extends toward “real nature” or “true character”
- **உண்மை அறிதல்** and **உண்மை உணர்வு** in philosophical or reflective usage beyond ordinary factual truth
