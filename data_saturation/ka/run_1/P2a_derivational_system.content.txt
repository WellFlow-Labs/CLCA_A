# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2a Derivational System
# Language: Georgian (ka)

---

**Methodological note:** The following documents attested derivational morphology for each P1 base form. Forms already fully documented in P1 or P1b are cross-referenced rather than re-described in full. Derivations are listed per base form; where a derived form has itself been listed as a P1 base, this is noted. No paradigm completion has been attempted. Gaps are preserved.

---

## 1. Base Form: ჭეშმარიტი / č̣ešmariti

---

**Derived Form 1**

1. **Base Form:** ჭეშმარიტი / č̣ešmariti
2. **Derived Form:** ჭეშმარიტება / č̣ešmariteba
3. **Transliteration:** č̣ešmariteba
4. **Literal Breakdown:** č̣ešmariti + nominalizing suffix -eba; "the state/quality of being true"
5. **Natural English Translation:** truth, truthfulness, veracity
6. **Usage:** The standard abstract noun for truth in philosophical, theological, and everyday discourse. Already documented as P1 item #2; listed here to anchor the derivational relationship.
7. **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2**

1. **Base Form:** ჭეშმარიტი / č̣ešmariti
2. **Derived Form:** ჭეშმარიტად / č̣ešmaritad
3. **Transliteration:** č̣ešmaritad
4. **Literal Breakdown:** č̣ešmariti + adverbial suffix -ad; "in a true manner, truly"
5. **Natural English Translation:** truly, in truth, verily, genuinely
6. **Usage:** Adverb used in formal, literary, religious, and philosophical discourse to assert or emphasize the truth of a statement. Documented in P1b (Root 3, item 2); listed here to anchor the adverbial derivation.
7. **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Note on further derivation:** No productive derivation from č̣ešmariteba itself (e.g., a further suffixed form beyond the adverb and noun) is attested with sufficient frequency to document here. The root č̣ešmar- does not appear to generate verbal forms in contemporary Georgian.

## 3. Base Form: სიმართლე / simartle

---

**Derived Form 1**

1. **Base Form:** სიმართლე / simartle
2. **Derived Form:** სიმართლის თქმა / simarṭlis tqma
3. **Transliteration:** simarṭlis tqma
4. **Literal Breakdown:** simarṭle (truth, genitive -is) + tqma (verbal noun of tkva, "to say"); "the saying of truth"
5. **Natural English Translation:** truth-telling, telling the truth
6. **Usage:** Used in moral, legal, and everyday contexts to describe the act of speaking truthfully. Documented in P1b (Root 1, item 6). This is a genitive nominal compound rather than a suffixal derivation; included here because it functions as a lexicalized unit.
7. **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Note:** simartle is itself a circumfix derivation (si-...-e) from the root martal-/marṭ-. It does not appear to generate further suffixal derivations as a base in its own right. The productive derivational work in this family is done from the root marṭ- directly (see item #4, martali, below).

**Note on the marṭ- family:** The root marṭ- is the most derivationally productive root in this domain. ## Summary of Derivational Patterns Observed

The following patterns are documented across the P1 set. These observations are drawn directly from the attested forms above and are not projected onto unattested items.

---

**1. Adverbial -ad suffix**
Attested productively across native and borrowed adjectives: č̣ešmaritad, namdvilad, marṭla (variant form), sc̣orad, zustad, piqtiurad, gulc̣rpeladˈ, marṭebuladˈ, arsebitad, šinaˈarsobiˈrivad. This is the most consistent derivational pattern across the set.

**2. Nominalizing -oba suffix**
Attested from adjectives: namdviloba, gulc̣rpelobaˈ, marṭebuloba, avtentiuroba, utyuaroba, damajereblobaˈ. Also attested from compound bases: marṭlmadideblobaˈ. Less consistently attested from privative forms (uzustoba: [UNCERTAIN]).

**3. Circumfix si-...-e nominalization**
Attested from roots: simartle (marṭ-), siq̇albe (q̇alb-), sicrue (cru-), sizuste (zust-), sisc̣ore (sc̣or-). This is a productive native pattern for forming abstract quality nouns, but it operates from roots rather than from the P1 adjectives directly.

**4. Resultative participial -uli**
Attested from verbal nouns: dadasturebuli, daudasturebeliˈ, gaq̇albebuli, gamarṭlebuli, gasc̣orebuli, dazustebuli. Consistently productive from preverb + root + -eba verbal nouns.

**5. Privative ara- prefix**
Attested: arasc̣ori, arasc̣orad, arasc̣muno, aragulc̣rpeli. Productive with native adjectives.

**6. Privative u-...-o circumfix**
Attested: uzusto (from zusti). Less productive than ara- in this set; only one clear instance documented.

**7. Agentive -eli / -veli suffix**
Attested: tvaltmaqci (from tvaltmaqcoba), mimbadzveliˈ (from mibadzva/badz-). Limited attestation in this set.

**8. Relational -obr-iv- suffix**
Attested: šinaˈarsobiˈrivi (from šinaˈarsi). Documented as productive in formal registers for forming relational adjectives from nominal bases.
