# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2a Derivational System
# Language: Tamil (ta)

---

**Transliteration standard:** ISO 15919 (applied consistently throughout)

---

## Preliminary Note on Scope

This document records attested derivational morphology for lexical items identified in P1. Forms already fully documented in P1 or P1b are cross-referenced rather than re-described. The inventory below is organized by base form. Where no derivation beyond forms already documented is identified, this is explicitly stated. Asymmetry across entries is preserved and not corrected.

## 1. Base Form: உண்மை / *uṇmai*

---

**Derived Form 1**

**Base Form:** உண்மை / *uṇmai*
**Derived Form:** உண்மையான
**Transliteration:** uṇmaiyāna
**Literal Breakdown:** *uṇmai* + *-āna* (adjectival copular suffix)
**Natural English translation:** true, genuine, real, authentic
**Usage:** Attributive adjective applied to persons, objects, feelings, and statements to assert genuineness or correspondence to fact. Extremely frequent across all registers of spoken and written Tamil.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

*(Cross-reference: documented in P1 item 4 and P1b Root 1 item 1)*

---

**Derived Form 2**

**Base Form:** உண்மை / *uṇmai*
**Derived Form:** உண்மையாக
**Transliteration:** uṇmaiyāka
**Literal Breakdown:** *uṇmai* + *-āka* (adverbial copular suffix)
**Natural English translation:** truly, genuinely, honestly, in truth
**Usage:** Adverbial form used to assert that an action or state is genuine or sincere; appears in constructions such as *uṇmaiyāka colla* (to speak truthfully). Common in everyday speech and formal writing.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

*(Cross-reference: documented in P1b Root 1 item 2)*

---

**Derived Form 3**

**Base Form:** உண்மை / *uṇmai*
**Derived Form:** உண்மையில்
**Transliteration:** uṇmaiyil
**Literal Breakdown:** *uṇmai* + *-il* (locative case suffix)
**Natural English translation:** in truth, actually, in reality
**Usage:** Fixed adverbial expression used to introduce a correction or clarification of what is actually the case; functions as an epistemic discourse marker. Extremely common in spoken and written Tamil.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

*(Cross-reference: documented in P1 item 20 and P1b Root 1 item 3)*

---

**Derived Form 4**

**Base Form:** உண்மை / *uṇmai*
**Derived Form:** உண்மையிலேயே
**Transliteration:** uṇmaiyilēyē
**Literal Breakdown:** *uṇmaiyil* + emphatic particle *-ē* + *-yē*
**Natural English translation:** truly, really, genuinely, in actual fact
**Usage:** Emphatic adverbial form stressing the reality of a state of affairs; marks stronger epistemic commitment than *uṇmaiyil*. Common in spoken Tamil and informal written registers.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

*(Cross-reference: documented in P1 item 21 and P1b Root 1 item 4)*

---

**Derived Form 5**

**Base Form:** உண்மை / *uṇmai*
**Derived Form:** உண்மைத்தன்மை
**Transliteration:** uṇmaitaṉmai
**Literal Breakdown:** *uṇmai* + *taṉmai* (quality, nature; a nominal compounding element)
**Natural English translation:** truthfulness, authenticity, genuineness, veracity
**Usage:** Formal and philosophical nominal referring to the property of being true or genuine; used in literary criticism, philosophy, and formal prose. Less frequent than *uṇmai* itself.
**Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

*(Cross-reference: documented in P1 item 9 and P1b Root 1 item 5)*

---

**Derived Form 6**

**Base Form:** உண்மை / *uṇmai*
**Derived Form:** உண்மையாளர்
**Transliteration:** uṇmaiyāḷar
**Literal Breakdown:** *uṇmai* + *āḷar* (agentive/person suffix, plural-honorific)
**Natural English translation:** truthful persons, people of truth, truth-tellers
**Usage:** Used to refer to persons characterized by truthfulness or commitment to truth; appears in formal, literary, and moral discourse. The singular *uṇmaiyāḷan* (masculine) and *uṇmaiyāḷ* (general/honorific) are also attested, though less frequent than the base noun.
**Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**Derived Form 7**

**Base Form:** உண்மை / *uṇmai*
**Derived Form:** உண்மையற்ற
**Transliteration:** uṇmaiyaṟṟa
**Literal Breakdown:** *uṇmai* + *aṟṟa* (privative participial: without, lacking)
**Natural English translation:** untrue, lacking truth, false, inauthentic
**Usage:** Used to evaluate persons, statements, or representations as lacking truth or genuineness; appears in moral and formal evaluative contexts. Less common than *poy* or *poyyana* for simple falsehood but attested in formal registers.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Note on productivity:** The *uṇmai* root is the most derivationally productive root in this domain. The suffixes *-āna*, *-āka*, *-il*, *-taṉmai*, and *-aṟṟa* all attach productively. The agentive *-āḷar* form is attested but less frequent.

## 2. Base Form: பொய் / *poy*

---

**Derived Form 1**

**Base Form:** பொய் / *poy*
**Derived Form:** பொய்யன்
**Transliteration:** poyyan
**Literal Breakdown:** *poy* (falsehood) + *-an* (masculine agentive suffix)
**Natural English translation:** liar (masculine), a man who lies
**Usage:** Used to characterize a male person as one who habitually tells lies; appears in everyday moral evaluation and in narrative contexts. The form is informal and carries strong negative evaluative force.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

*(Cross-reference: noted in P1 item 2)*

---

**Derived Form 2**

**Base Form:** பொய் / *poy*
**Derived Form:** பொய்யள்
**Transliteration:** poyyaḷ
**Literal Breakdown:** *poy* + *-aḷ* (feminine agentive suffix)
**Natural English translation:** liar (feminine), a woman who lies
**Usage:** Feminine counterpart of *poyyan*; used in everyday moral evaluation to characterize a female person as a liar. Carries the same strong negative evaluative force.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 3**

**Base Form:** பொய் / *poy*
**Derived Form:** பொய்யான
**Transliteration:** poyyāna
**Literal Breakdown:** *poy* + *-āna* (adjectival copular suffix)
**Natural English translation:** false, fake, untrue, deceptive
**Usage:** Adjectival form used to evaluate statements, objects, or persons as false or not genuine; directly contrasts with *uṇmaiyāna* (true, genuine). Common across all registers.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

*(Cross-reference: noted in P1 item 2)*

---

**Derived Form 4**

**Base Form:** பொய் / *poy*
**Derived Form:** பொய்யாக
**Transliteration:** poyyāka
**Literal Breakdown:** *poy* + *-āka* (adverbial copular suffix)
**Natural English translation:** falsely, deceptively, untruthfully
**Usage:** Adverbial form used to characterize the manner of speech or action as false or deceptive; appears in constructions such as *poyyāka colluvatu* (speaking falsely). Common in moral and legal discourse.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 5**

**Base Form:** பொய் / *poy*
**Derived Form:** பொய்மை
**Transliteration:** poymai
**Literal Breakdown:** *poy* + *-mai* (abstract nominal suffix)
**Natural English translation:** falsehood, untruthfulness, the quality of being false
**Usage:** Abstract nominal referring to the property or state of being false; used in formal, literary, and philosophical contexts. Less frequent in everyday speech than *poy* itself but attested in classical and modern formal Tamil.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 6**

**Base Form:** பொய் / *poy*
**Derived Form:** பொய்த்தன்மை
**Transliteration:** poyttaṉmai
**Literal Breakdown:** *poy* + *taṉmai* (quality, nature)
**Natural English translation:** falseness, the quality of being untrue, spuriousness
**Usage:** A more formal and abstract nominal than *poymai*; used in philosophical and literary critical contexts to refer to the property of being false or inauthentic. Rare in everyday speech.
**Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**Derived Form 7**

**Base Form:** பொய் / *poy*
**Derived Form:** பொய்யுரை
**Transliteration:** poyyurai
**Literal Breakdown:** *poy* (false) + *urai* (speech, statement, utterance)
**Natural English translation:** false statement, lie, false testimony
**Usage:** A compound nominal used in formal and legal contexts to refer to a false statement or false testimony; appears in legal proceedings and formal written Tamil. More formal than *poy* alone.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 8**

**Base Form:** பொய் / *poy*
**Derived Form:** பொய்சாட்சி
**Transliteration:** poycāṭci
**Literal Breakdown:** *poy* (false) + *cāṭci* (witness, testimony)
**Natural English translation:** false witness, perjury, false testimony
**Usage:** Used in legal and moral contexts to refer to false testimony given by a witness; appears in legal proceedings and in moral discourse about the wrongness of lying under oath.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 9**

**Base Form:** பொய் / *poy*
**Derived Form:** பொய்யில்லாத
**Transliteration:** poyyillāta
**Literal Breakdown:** *poy* + *illāta* (without, not having; privative participial)
**Natural English translation:** without falsehood, truthful, free from lies
**Usage:** Used to evaluate persons or statements as entirely free from falsehood; appears in moral instruction, religious contexts, and formal evaluative discourse. Carries a strong positive evaluative force.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Note on productivity:** *poy* is moderately productive. The suffixes *-āna*, *-āka*, *-mai*, and agentive *-an*/*-aḷ* attach productively. Compounding with *urai* (speech) and *cāṭci* (testimony) produces attested legal vocabulary.

## 4. Base Form: சரி / *cari*

---

**Derived Form 1**

**Base Form:** சரி / *cari*
**Derived Form:** சரியான
**Transliteration:** cariyāna
**Literal Breakdown:** *cari* + *-āna* (adjectival copular suffix)
**Natural English translation:** correct, proper, right, appropriate
**Usage:** Adjectival form used to evaluate statements, actions, or conduct as correct or appropriate; extremely common across all registers of spoken and written Tamil.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

*(Cross-reference: noted in P1 item 5)*

---

**Derived Form 2**

**Base Form:** சரி / *cari*
**Derived Form:** சரியாக
**Transliteration:** cariyāka
**Literal Breakdown:** *cari* + *-āka* (adverbial copular suffix)
**Natural English translation:** correctly, properly, rightly, accurately
**Usage:** Adverbial form used to characterize the manner of an action or the accuracy of a result as correct; very common in everyday speech, educational, and technical contexts.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 3**

**Base Form:** சரி / *cari*
**Derived Form:** சரிபார்
**Transliteration:** caripār
**Literal Breakdown:** *cari* (correct) + *pār* (to see, look; here: to check)
**Natural English translation:** to verify, to check, to confirm, to validate
**Usage:** Verb used to describe the act of checking or verifying whether something is correct or accurate; common in everyday, administrative, and technical contexts.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

*(Cross-reference: documented in P1 item 26)*

---

**Derived Form 4**

**Base Form:** சரி / *cari*
**Derived Form:** சரிபார்ப்பு
**Transliteration:** caripārppu
**Literal Breakdown:** *caripār* (to verify) + *-ppu* (verbal noun suffix)
**Natural English translation:** verification, checking, validation, review
**Usage:** Verbal noun derived from *caripār*; used to refer to the act or process of verification. Common in administrative, technical, and journalistic contexts.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 5**

**Base Form:** சரி / *cari*
**Derived Form:** சரியில்லாத
**Transliteration:** cariyillāta
**Literal Breakdown:** *cari* + *illāta* (without, not having; privative participial)
**Natural English translation:** incorrect, improper, not right, faulty
**Usage:** Used to evaluate something as not correct or not in proper order; common in everyday speech and in technical and administrative contexts. Slightly less formal than *tavaṟāna* for the same meaning.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 6**

**Base Form:** சரி / *cari*
**Derived Form:** சரிசெய்
**Transliteration:** caricey
**Literal Breakdown:** *cari* (correct) + *cey* (to do, to make)
**Natural English translation:** to correct, to fix, to set right, to repair
**Usage:** Verb used to describe the act of correcting an error or repairing something that is not functioning properly; extremely common in everyday speech across all registers.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 7**

**Base Form:** சரி / *cari*
**Derived Form:** சரிசெய்தல்
**Transliteration:** cariceyttal
**Literal Breakdown:** *caricey* (to correct) + *-tal* (verbal noun suffix)
**Natural English translation:** correction, fixing, repair, rectification
**Usage:** Verbal noun of *caricey*; used in formal and administrative contexts to refer to the act of correction or repair. Appears in official documents and technical writing.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Note on productivity:** *cari* is productive in compounding with verbs (*pār*, *cey*) and in taking the standard adjectival and adverbial suffixes. The privative *illāta* construction is also attested. The base also functions as a discourse particle (agreement/confirmation), but this is not a derivational form and is not listed here.

**Derived Form:** தவறில்லாத
**Transliteration:** tavaṟillāta
**Literal Breakdown:** *tavaṟu* + *illāta* (without, not having; privative participial)
**Natural English translation:** without error, faultless, correct, flawless

**Derived Form 4**

**Base Form:** தவறு / *tavaṟu*

**Derived Form 5**

**Base Form:** தவறு / *tavaṟu*
**Derived Form:** தவறிழை
**Transliteration:** tavaṟiḻai
**Literal Breakdown:** *tavaṟu* (error, wrong) + *iḻai* (to commit, to do; here: to commit an error)
**Natural English translation:** to commit an error, to do wrong, to make a mistake
**Usage:** Verb used in formal and literary contexts to describe the act of committing a wrong or making an error; appears in moral and legal discourse. Less common in everyday speech.
**Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**Derived Form 6**

**Base Form:** தவறு / *tavaṟu*
**Derived Form:** தவறுதலான
**Transliteration:** tavaṟutalaāna
**Literal Breakdown:** *tavaṟutal* (erring) + *-āna* (adjectival suffix)
**Natural English translation:** erroneous, mistaken, faulty
**Usage:** Adjectival form derived from the verbal noun *tavaṟutal*; used in formal written Tamil to evaluate something as involving error. Less common than *tavaṟāna* and confined to formal registers.
**Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**Note on productivity:** *tavaṟu* takes the standard adjectival and adverbial suffixes productively. The verbal noun *-tal* form and the compounding with *iḻai* are attested but less frequent. The agentive construction *tavaṟu ceytavar* (one who has done wrong), noted in P1, is a participial relative clause construction rather than a derivational form and is not listed here.

**Base Form:** உறுதிப்படுத்து / *uṟutippaṭuttu*
**Derived Form:** உறுதிப்படுத்தல்
**Transliteration:** uṟutippaṭuttal
**Literal Breakdown:** *uṟutippaṭuttu* (to confirm, to verify) + *-al* (verbal noun suffix)
**Natural English translation:** confirmation, verification, certification, establishment
**Usage:** Verbal noun referring to the act of confirming or verifying; used in formal, legal, and administrative contexts. Appears in official documents and procedural writing.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

**Derived Form 2**

**Base Form:** உறுதி / *uṟuti* (base nominal underlying *uṟutippaṭuttu*)
**Derived Form:** உறுதியான
**Transliteration:** uṟutiyāna
**Literal Breakdown:** *uṟuti* (firmness, certainty) + *-āna* (adjectival copular suffix)
**Natural English translation:** firm, certain, definite, confirmed, reliable
**Usage:** Adjectival form of the base nominal *uṟuti*; used to evaluate persons, statements, or situations as firm, certain, or reliable. Common across all registers of Tamil.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 3**

**Base Form:** உறுதி / *uṟuti*
**Derived Form:** உறுதியாக
**Transliteration:** uṟutiyāka
**Literal Breakdown:** *uṟuti* + *-āka* (adverbial copular suffix)
**Natural English translation:** firmly, certainly, definitively, with certainty
**Usage:** Adverbial form characterizing the manner of assertion or action as firm or certain; common in everyday speech and formal writing.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 4**

**Base Form:** உறுதி / *uṟuti*
**Derived Form:** உறுதிமொழி
**Transliteration:** uṟutimoḻi
**Literal Breakdown:** *uṟuti* (firmness, certainty) + *moḻi* (speech, statement)
**Natural English translation:** oath, pledge, solemn declaration, vow
**Usage:** Used to refer to a solemn statement or pledge; the standard Tamil term for an oath or formal vow. Common in legal, political, and religious contexts.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

**Note:** The base *uṟuti* is more derivationally active than the compound verbal form *uṟutippaṭuttu*. Forms derived from *uṟuti* are documented here because they are directly relevant to the truth-evaluation domain and because *uṟutippaṭuttu* is itself derived from *uṟuti*.
