# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P1 CORE VOCABULARY COLLECTION
# Language: Finnish (fi)

## Core Vocabulary Items

### 1. tosi
**Form:** tosi  
**Literal meaning:** true  
**Typical English translations:** true, real, genuine  
**Usage:** Used to describe statements that correspond to reality or facts ("tosi väite" = true claim). Also used colloquially as an intensifier meaning "really" or "very" ("tosi hyvä" = really good). Common in both everyday and formal discourse.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### 2. totuus
**Form:** totuus  
**Literal meaning:** truth (derived from *tosi* + abstract noun suffix *-uus*)  
**Typical English translations:** truth  
**Usage:** Abstract noun referring to the quality or state of being true, or to true statements/facts ("puhua totuutta" = speak the truth). Used in philosophical, legal, and everyday contexts when discussing veracity or reality.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### 3. totta
**Form:** totta  
**Literal meaning:** partitive case of *tosi*  
**Typical English translations:** true (predicative)  
**Usage:** Predicative form used in statements like "se on totta" (that is true) or "onko se totta?" (is that true?). Standard way to affirm or question the truth of a statement in Finnish.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### 4. valhe
**Form:** valhe  
**Literal meaning:** lie, falsehood  
**Typical English translations:** lie, falsehood, untruth  
**Usage:** Refers to deliberately false statements or deceptions ("puhua valhetta" = tell a lie). Used in moral, legal, and everyday contexts to denote intentional misrepresentation of facts.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### 5. väärä
**Form:** väärä  
**Literal meaning:** wrong, false, incorrect  
**Typical English translations:** wrong, false, incorrect, mistaken  
**Usage:** Describes statements, beliefs, or actions that are incorrect or not in accordance with facts or norms ("väärä vastaus" = wrong answer). Used broadly across factual, moral, and practical domains.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### 6. oikea
**Form:** oikea  
**Literal meaning:** right, correct, real  
**Typical English translations:** right, correct, true, real, genuine  
**Usage:** Polysemous term meaning both "correct" in factual/moral sense and "right" as opposed to left, as well as "real/genuine" ("oikea nimi" = real name). Context determines which sense applies.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### 7. aito
**Form:** aito  
**Literal meaning:** genuine, authentic  
**Typical English translations:** genuine, authentic, real  
**Usage:** Emphasizes authenticity and genuineness of objects, materials, or persons ("aito nahka" = genuine leather, "aito ystävä" = true friend). Used when distinguishing originals from imitations or fakes.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### 8. todellinen
**Form:** todellinen  
**Literal meaning:** real, actual (derived from *tosi* + adjectival suffix *-llinen*)  
**Typical English translations:** real, actual, true  
**Usage:** Describes what actually exists or occurs as opposed to imaginary or apparent ("todellinen tilanne" = actual situation). Used to emphasize reality versus appearance or fiction.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### 9. todistaa
**Form:** todistaa  
**Literal meaning:** to prove, to testify (verb derived from *tosi*)  
**Typical English translations:** to prove, to demonstrate, to testify, to witness  
**Usage:** Used in legal contexts for giving testimony and in general discourse for proving or demonstrating something to be true ("todistaa syyttömyytensä" = prove one's innocence). Central to evidential and legal discourse.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### 10. todiste
**Form:** todiste  
**Literal meaning:** proof, evidence (noun derived from *todistaa*)  
**Typical English translations:** proof, evidence  
**Usage:** Refers to evidence or proof that establishes the truth of something ("esittää todisteita" = present evidence). Used in legal, scientific, and everyday argumentative contexts.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### 11. luotettava
**Form:** luotettava  
**Literal meaning:** reliable, trustworthy (passive participle of *luottaa* = to trust)  
**Typical English translations:** reliable, trustworthy, dependable  
**Usage:** Describes persons, sources, or things that can be trusted or relied upon ("luotettava lähde" = reliable source). Important in evaluating credibility of information and character.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### 12. paikkansapitävä
**Form:** paikkansapitävä  
**Literal meaning:** holding its place (compound: *paikkansa* = its place + *pitävä* = holding)  
**Typical English translations:** accurate, correct, valid  
**Usage:** Formal term describing statements or information that are accurate and correspond to facts ("paikkansapitävä tieto" = accurate information). Common in formal, academic, and journalistic contexts.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### 13. virhe
**Form:** virhe  
**Literal meaning:** error, mistake  
**Typical English translations:** error, mistake, fault  
**Usage:** Refers to unintentional errors or mistakes in statements, calculations, or actions ("tehdä virhe" = make a mistake). Distinguished from deliberate falsehood; implies inadvertent incorrectness.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### 14. virheellinen
**Form:** virheellinen  
**Literal meaning:** erroneous, faulty (derived from *virhe* + adjectival suffix *-llinen*)  
**Typical English translations:** erroneous, incorrect, faulty  
**Usage:** Describes something containing errors or mistakes ("virheellinen tieto" = erroneous information). Used in formal contexts to characterize incorrect information or flawed reasoning.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### 15. rehellinen
**Form:** rehellinen  
**Literal meaning:** honest (derived from *rehelä* + suffix *-inen*)  
**Typical English translations:** honest, truthful  
**Usage:** Describes persons or statements characterized by honesty and truthfulness ("rehellinen ihminen" = honest person). Central moral term for evaluating character and communication.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### 16. epätosi
**Form:** epätosi  
**Literal meaning:** untrue (prefix *epä-* = un-/non- + *tosi* = true)  
**Typical English translations:** untrue, false  
**Usage:** Describes statements that are not true or do not correspond to facts ("epätosi väite" = false claim). More formal alternative to *väärä* when specifically denoting lack of truth.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### 17. vääristää
**Form:** vääristää  
**Literal meaning:** to distort, to falsify (verb derived from *väärä*)  
**Typical English translations:** to distort, to falsify, to misrepresent  
**Usage:** Describes the act of deliberately or accidentally misrepresenting facts or reality ("vääristää totuutta" = distort the truth). Used when discussing manipulation or inaccurate representation of information.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### 18. petollinen
**Form:** petollinen  
**Literal meaning:** deceptive, deceitful (derived from *pettää* = to deceive + suffix *-llinen*)  
**Typical English translations:** deceptive, deceitful, fraudulent  
**Usage:** Describes persons, actions, or appearances that are intended to deceive ("petollinen ulkonäkö" = deceptive appearance). Carries moral condemnation of intentional deception.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### 19. todenmukaisesti
**Form:** todenmukaisesti  
**Literal meaning:** truthfully, according to truth (adverb: *toden* = truth's + *mukainen* = according to + adverbial *-sti*)  
**Typical English translations:** truthfully, accurately, realistically  
**Usage:** Adverb describing manner of representation or description that corresponds to reality ("kuvata todenmukaisesti" = describe accurately). Used in contexts requiring faithful representation.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### 20. oikeellisuus
**Form:** oikeellisuus  
**Literal meaning:** correctness (derived from *oikea* + abstract noun suffix *-llisuus*)  
**Typical English translations:** correctness, accuracy  
**Usage:** Abstract noun referring to the quality of being correct or accurate ("tiedon oikeellisuus" = accuracy of information). Used in formal contexts evaluating conformity to standards or facts.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### 21. uskottava
**Form:** uskottava  
**Literal meaning:** believable, credible (passive participle of *uskoa* = to believe)  
**Typical English translations:** believable, credible, plausible  
**Usage:** Describes statements, persons, or accounts that can reasonably be believed ("uskottava selitys" = credible explanation). Important in evaluating testimony and claims.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### 22. vale-
**Form:** vale-  
**Literal meaning:** false, fake (prefix)  
**Typical English translations:** false, fake, pseudo-  
**Usage:** Productive prefix forming compounds indicating falseness or inauthenticity ("valepassi" = fake passport, "valenimi" = pseudonym). Used to mark something as counterfeit or not genuine.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### 23. väittää
**Form:** väittää  
**Literal meaning:** to claim, to assert  
**Typical English translations:** to claim, to assert, to maintain  
**Usage:** Verb for making claims or assertions, often with implication that the truth is uncertain or disputed ("hän väittää olevansa syytön" = he claims to be innocent). Neutral to slightly skeptical in tone.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### 24. todentaa
**Form:** todentaa  
**Literal meaning:** to verify, to authenticate (causative verb from *tosi*)  
**Typical English translations:** to verify, to authenticate, to confirm  
**Usage:** Describes the act of establishing or confirming the truth or authenticity of something ("todentaa henkilöllisyys" = verify identity). Used in formal, legal, and administrative contexts.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### 25. harhaanjohtava
**Form:** harhaanjohtava  
**Literal meaning:** misleading (compound: *harhaan* = astray + *johtava* = leading)  
**Typical English translations:** misleading, deceptive  
**Usage:** Describes information, statements, or representations that lead to false conclusions ("harhaanjohtava mainos" = misleading advertisement). Used in legal and critical discourse about communication.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### 26. pitää paikkansa
**Form:** pitää paikkansa  
**Literal meaning:** to hold its place (idiomatic expression)  
**Typical English translations:** to be true, to be correct, to hold true  
**Usage:** Fixed expression meaning that something is true or accurate ("tarina pitää paikkansa" = the story is true). Common idiom for affirming the truth or accuracy of statements.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### 27. vilpitön
**Form:** vilpitön  
**Literal meaning:** sincere, genuine (negative: *vilpi-* = deceit + *-tön* = without)  
**Typical English translations:** sincere, genuine, honest  
**Usage:** Describes persons or expressions characterized by sincerity and lack of deceit ("vilpitön hymy" = genuine smile). Used in evaluating authenticity of emotions and intentions.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### 28. todenperäinen
**Form:** todenperäinen  
**Literal meaning:** truthful, veracious (compound: *toden* = truth's + *perä* = basis + adjectival *-inen*)  
**Typical English translations:** truthful, veracious, true  
**Usage:** Formal term describing accounts or statements based on truth ("todenperäinen kertomus" = truthful account). Used in formal and literary contexts to characterize veracity.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]
