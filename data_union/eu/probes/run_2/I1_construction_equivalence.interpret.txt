## 2. Substitution Patterns (Narrative)

### 2.1 REVELATION substituted by BECOME

No dedicated REVELATION construction (a construction encoding the coming-to-light or discovery of a truth) is attested in the P4a or P4b data. The inchoative *bihurtu da* construction covers change-of-state readings including outcomes that in other languages would be expressed by a revelation verb (e.g., "it turned out to be true" is rendered as *Egia bihurtu da*, literally "it became true"). The BECOME construction therefore functions as the closest available equivalent for REVELATION readings. This is a substitution, not a structural equivalence: *bihurtu da* encodes change-of-state from the subject's perspective, not the coming-to-light of a fact for an observer. The data do not document whether a more specific revelation reading is available through other lexical means; the gap is recorded as a lexical gap rather than a conceptual gap.

### 2.2 CAUSATION (factual) partially covered; CAUSATION (character) absent

For factual causation (causing someone to verify or prove something), two strategies are attested: the periphrastic *eragin* + infinitive construction [CORE] and the morphological *-arazi* suffix [MARGINAL]. The periphrastic form is the primary strategy in this domain. The morphological *-arazi* form is grammatically productive in Basque generally but is documented as low-frequency for truth-domain verbs, and no *-arazi* causative from nominal bases (*egia*, *gezurra*) is attested. This constitutes a lexical gap within the morphological causative strategy, not a structural gap in causation overall.

For character causation (causing someone to become honest, truthful, or deceptive as a character trait), no construction is attested in the data. Character adjectives (*zintzoa*, *jatorra*) are used attributively in ATTR constructions but are not documented in any causative frame. The *behartu* + infinitive periphrastic is noted as a further causative strategy but is not exemplified for character-state causation. This is recorded as **NO COMMON EXPRESSION — circumlocution required** rather than a structural gap, because the absence may reflect the limits of the attested data rather than a categorical structural impossibility.

### 2.3 NEGATION: analytic and morphological strategies are distinct and non-interchangeable

Basque employs both analytic and morphological negation in the truth domain, but these are not interchangeable:

- **Analytic negation** (*ez* + *da*) operates at the sentential level and negates the copular attribution as a whole (*Ez da egia hori* — "That is not true"). This is the standard strategy for NEGATION of ATTR constructions.
- **Morphological negation** operates at the word-formation level and produces derived lexical items: *-gabe* (absence of property: *baliogabea*), *ezin* (impossibility: *frogaezina*), and *-gaitz*/*-kaitz* (resistant potential: *fidagaitza*). These are not sentential negations but derived property terms.

The three morphological strategies are themselves non-interchangeable: *-gabe* applies to nominal and verbal roots to express absence; *ezin* applies to verbal roots to express impossibility; *-gaitz*/*-kaitz* is the consistent negative counterpart to *-garri* potential adjectivals and expresses resistance or difficulty rather than absence or impossibility. The data show no overlap or free alternation among these three.

### 2.4 VERIFICATION and DISCONFIRMATION do not share the same construction

VERIFICATION is expressed through the *-tze* process nominal, the *-pen* result nominal, and the *-garri* potential adjectival — all of which are positive-polarity forms. DISCONFIRMATION is expressed through the *-gabe* privative, the *ezin* impossibility construction, and the *-gaitz*/*-kaitz* negative potential — all of which are negative-polarity forms. The two sets are formally and semantically distinct and do not share a common construction. There is a partial formal relationship between *-garri* (VERIFICATION potential) and *-gaitz*/*-kaitz* (DISCONFIRMATION resistant potential) in that they form a consistent antonym pair, but they are derived by different suffixes and are not the same construction with different polarity marking.

### 2.5 Agentive nominalization asymmetry: falsehood vs. truth

The *-ti* agentive nominal is attested only for the falsehood side of the domain: *gezurtia* ("the liar") is a fully lexicalized common word. No parallel *-ti* form for the truth-telling agent is attested (*egiazkoti*, *egiati* are not documented). The truth-telling agent is expressed periphrastically via relative clauses (*egia esaten duena*, "the one who tells the truth") or characterized via property adjectives (*zintzoa*, *jatorra*), which are ATTR constructions rather than agentive nominals. This is a consistent asymmetry within the agentive nominalization construction type: the construction is available on one side of the domain (falsehood) and absent on the other (truth-telling), with periphrastic substitution filling the gap on the truth-telling side.

### 2.6 BECOME: periphrastic form productive; morphological form lexically restricted

The *bihurtu da* periphrastic is the productive general inchoative strategy and applies broadly across truth-domain nominals and adjectivals. The suffixed *-tu* inchoative is lexically restricted: *okertu* (to go wrong) is well-attested, *zilegitu* (to become legitimate) is marginal, and no *-tu* inchoative from *egia* or *gezurra* is attested. The two strategies are not parallel productive alternates; *bihurtu da* is the default and the *-tu* form is a lexically idiosyncratic option for specific roots.

## 3. Missingness Summary

### 3.1 Structural Gaps
No structural gaps are identified. All ten CLCA operator categories have at least one attested construction or a documented substitution strategy. The absence of REVELATION and CAUSATION (character) reflects the limits of attested data rather than a demonstrated structural impossibility.

### 3.2 Lexical Gaps
- **REVELATION**: No dedicated revelation verb or construction is attested. The BECOME construction (*bihurtu da*) is the closest available form. The gap is lexical: no single verb or construction specifically encoding "coming to light" or "being discovered to be true" is documented.
- **CAUSATION (character)**: No construction encoding the causation of a character state (making someone honest, truthful, or deceptive) is attested. Periphrastic strategies (*behartu* + infinitive) are noted but not exemplified for this function.
- **Morphological causative from nominal bases**: No *-arazi* causative from *egia* or *gezurra* is documented. This is a lexical gap within the morphological causative strategy.
- **Agentive nominal for truth-telling agent**: No *-ti* agentive form for the truth-telling agent is attested. *Gezurtia* (liar) has no structural parallel on the truth-telling side.

### 3.3 Conceptual Gaps
None identified from the data. The data do not provide evidence that any CLCA operator category is conceptually unavailable or blocked in Basque. Absences are recorded as lexical or asymmetric gaps rather than conceptual gaps.

### 3.4 Asymmetric Gaps (only negative or only positive forms missing)
- **Agentive nominalization**: *-ti* agentive is attested for falsehood (*gezurtia*) but not for truth-telling. The truth-telling side lacks a morphological agentive nominal and relies on periphrasis. This is a one-sided lexical gap within an otherwise available construction type.
- **Inchoative *-tu* suffixation**: Attested for the negative/error side (*okertu*, "to go wrong") but not for *egia* or *gezurra* bases. The periphrastic *bihurtu da* covers both sides.
- **Morphological causative (*-arazi*)**: Attested for verbal bases in the verification sub-domain (*egiaztarazi*) but not for nominal truth/falsehood bases. No corresponding gap on the negative side is noted, but the asymmetry between verbal and nominal bases is documented.
