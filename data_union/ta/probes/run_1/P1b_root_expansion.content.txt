# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P1b ROOT EXPANSION
# Language: Tamil (ta)

---

## Step 1 — Identify Central Roots

---

**Root 1**

**Form:** உண்மை / *uṇmai*
**P1 participation:** 6 items (*uṇmai*, *uṇmaiyāna*, *uṇmaiyil*, *uṇmait-taṉmai*, *uṇmaikkum māṟāna*, and as the base of *meyppiкka* via semantic overlap)
**Core meaning contribution:** "That which is / exists" — the primary nominal and derivational base for truth, reality, and genuineness across all registers of Tamil.

---

**Root 2**

**Form:** மெய் / *mey*
**P1 participation:** 3 items (*mey*, *meyppiкka*, and as the classical/literary counterpart underlying *uṇmai* compounds in formal registers)
**Core meaning contribution:** "Body, substance, reality" — the classical root encoding truth as tangible reality or substance; highly productive in literary, philosophical, and formal compound formation.

---

**Root 3**

**Form:** நம்பு / *nampu*
**P1 participation:** 3 items (*nampakamāna*, *nampikkai*, and the base of *nampakam*)
**Core meaning contribution:** "To trust, to believe" — the primary verbal root encoding epistemic trust, credibility, and belief; productive in both nominal and adjectival derivation.

---

## Step 2 — Expand Each Root

### Root 1: உண்மை / *uṇmai*

---

**1.**
**Form:** உண்மை
**Transliteration:** uṇmai
**Morpheme breakdown:** *uḷ* (to be/exist) + *-mai* (abstract nominalizer)
**Natural English translation:** truth, reality, fact
**Context of use:** The base nominal form, used across all registers — everyday assertion, philosophical argument, legal testimony, and moral evaluation — to denote correspondence with reality.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**2.**
**Form:** உண்மையான
**Transliteration:** uṇmaiyāna
**Morpheme breakdown:** *uṇmai* + *-āna* (adjectival copular suffix)
**Natural English translation:** true, genuine, real, authentic
**Context of use:** Attributive adjective applied to persons, objects, and propositions to assert genuineness or factual accuracy; extremely common in spoken and written Tamil.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**3.**
**Form:** உண்மையில்
**Transliteration:** uṇmaiyil
**Morpheme breakdown:** *uṇmai* + *-il* (locative case suffix)
**Natural English translation:** in truth, actually, in reality
**Context of use:** Adverbial expression used to correct a misconception or contrast appearance with reality; common in both spoken and written registers.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**4.**
**Form:** உண்மைத்தன்மை
**Transliteration:** uṇmait-taṉmai
**Morpheme breakdown:** *uṇmai* + *taṉmai* (nature, inherent quality)
**Natural English translation:** truthfulness, authenticity, veracity
**Context of use:** Abstract nominal used in philosophical, literary, and formal evaluative discourse to refer to the property of being true or authentic.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**5.**
**Form:** உண்மைக்கு மாறான
**Transliteration:** uṇmaikkum māṟāna
**Morpheme breakdown:** *uṇmai* + dative *-kku* + *māṟāna* (contrary to, deviating from)
**Natural English translation:** contrary to fact, false, counterfactual
**Context of use:** Fixed evaluative expression used in legal and formal written contexts to characterize a statement or representation as factually false.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**6.**
**Form:** உண்மையிலேயே
**Transliteration:** uṇmaiyilēyē
**Morpheme breakdown:** *uṇmai* + *-il* (locative) + *-ē* (emphatic) + *-yē* (further emphatic)
**Natural English translation:** truly indeed, in actual fact, really and truly
**Context of use:** Emphatic adverbial used in spoken Tamil to strongly assert that something is genuinely the case, often in contexts of surprise or insistence.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**7.**
**Form:** உண்மைப் பிரியர்

**Morpheme breakdown:** *uṇmai* + *piriyar* (lover, devotee)
**Natural English translation:** lover of truth, truth-seeker
**Context of use:** Used in literary and philosophical contexts to describe a person devoted to truth; appears in classical and modern Tamil writing about intellectual or moral character.
**Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**8.**

---

**9.**

### Root 2: மெய் / *mey*

---

**1.**
**Form:** மெய்
**Transliteration:** mey
**Morpheme breakdown:** Monomorphemic classical root
**Natural English translation:** truth, reality, body, substance
**Context of use:** Classical and literary term for truth or reality; also the standard term for the body in classical Tamil grammar and poetics; used in philosophical and poetic contexts.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**2.**
**Form:** மெய்ப்பிக்க / மெய்ப்பித்தல்
**Transliteration:** meyppiкka / meyppittal
**Morpheme breakdown:** *mey* + causative *-ppi-* + infinitive *-kka* / verbal noun *-ttal*
**Natural English translation:** to prove, to verify, to demonstrate; proving, verification
**Context of use:** Used in legal and argumentative contexts for the act of proving or establishing the truth of a claim; the verbal noun *meyppiтtal* appears in formal written Tamil.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**3.**
**Form:** மெய்ஞ்ஞானம்
**Transliteration:** meyññāṉam

**Natural English translation:** true knowledge, genuine wisdom, knowledge of reality

### Root 3: நம்பு / *nampu*

---

**1.**
**Form:** நம்பிக்கை
**Transliteration:** nampikkai
**Morpheme breakdown:** *nampu* (to trust) + *-ikkai* (action/state nominalizer)
**Natural English translation:** trust, belief, faith, confidence
**Context of use:** The primary nominal form; used across religious, interpersonal, and institutional contexts to refer to the state of trusting or believing; extremely common in all registers.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**2.**
**Form:** நம்பகமான
**Transliteration:** nampakamāna
**Morpheme breakdown:** *nampu* + *-akam* (nominalizing infix) + *-āna* (adjectival suffix)
**Natural English translation:** reliable, trustworthy, credible, dependable
**Context of use:** Applied to persons, sources, institutions, and information judged worthy of trust; common in journalistic, legal, and everyday evaluative contexts.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**3.**
**Form:** நம்பகம்
**Transliteration:** nampakam
**Morpheme breakdown:** *nampu* + *-akam* (nominalizing infix)
**Natural English translation:** trustworthiness, reliability, credibility
**Context of use:** The base nominal form from which *nampakamāna* is derived; used in formal and written Tamil to refer to the quality of being trustworthy or reliable.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**4.**
**Form:** நம்பிக்கையான

## Step 3 — Peripheral Extensions

The following expressions involve the identified roots in contexts where the core truth-domain contribution appears to shift:

---

**மெய் / *mey* — body/physical sense:**

- *meypāṭu* (bodily expression of emotion) — *mey* functions as "body" rather than "truth"; this is a technical term in classical Tamil poetics for the physical manifestations of inner emotional states.
- *mey cilirttal* (the body thrilling, goosebumps) — *mey* as "body"; describes a physical sensation associated with aesthetic or emotional experience.
- *meykāppu* (bodyguard, physical protection) — *mey* as "body"; an administrative and everyday term with no truth-domain relevance.
- *meyvarutam* (bodily pain, physical suffering) — *mey* as "body"; describes physical rather than epistemic states.

---

**மெய் / *mey* — intensity/degree marking:**

- In some classical Tamil usages, *mey* appears to function as an intensifier meaning "truly, really" in a degree sense rather than a propositional truth sense, as in *meyyē* used to emphasize the extent or depth of a quality.

---

**உண்மை / *uṇmai* — aesthetic and artistic evaluation:**

- *uṇmai nāṭakam* (true-to-life drama) — *uṇmai* is applied to evaluate the realism or authenticity of a dramatic work, shifting from propositional truth to aesthetic fidelity.
- *uṇmaip poruḷ* (true meaning) — *uṇmai* is applied to the interpretation of texts, shifting from factual truth to hermeneutic depth or authentic meaning.
- *uṇmai uṇarvu* (sense of truth, truthful sensibility) — *uṇmai* is applied to describe an aesthetic or moral orientation rather than a specific true proposition.

---

**உண்மை / *uṇmai* — essential nature:**

- *uṇmaiyāna maṉitaṉ* (a genuine person) — *uṇmai* is applied to evaluate whether a person is authentically human or of genuine character, shifting from propositional truth to essential nature or moral authenticity.

---

**நம்பிக்கை / *nampikkai* — religious faith:**

- *nampikkai* in religious contexts (faith in God, creedal belief) — the root shifts from epistemic trust based on evidence or track record to religious faith, which may involve commitment beyond or independent of evidential grounds.
- *nampikkaiāḷar* (believer, the faithful) — the root is used to categorize persons by their religious affiliation rather than their epistemic reliability.

---

**நம்பிக்கை / *nampikkai* — motivational/psychological confidence:**

- *nampikkai ūṭṭutal* (instilling confidence) — the root is used in motivational and pedagogical contexts where "confidence" refers to self-belief or morale rather than trust in another person or the truth of a claim.
- *nampikkai iḻattal* (losing confidence) — similarly, in some contexts this refers to loss of self-confidence rather than loss of trust in another.
