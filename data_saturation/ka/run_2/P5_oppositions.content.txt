# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P5 Oppositions
# Language: Georgian (ka)

---

**Methodological note:** The following oppositions are extracted exclusively from patterns, contrasts, and explicit statements documented across P1–P4. No opposition is introduced from external sources or theoretical frameworks. Where the documentation notes an asymmetry between terms, that asymmetry is preserved rather than normalized. Where the documentation does not support a clear opposition, this is stated explicitly.

---

## Opposition 1

1. **Term A:** სიმართლე / simartle
2. **Term B:** სიყალბე / siq̇albe
3. **Transliteration:** simartle / siq̇albe
4. **English translation:** truth, correctness / falseness, falsehood, inauthenticity
5. **Description of contrast:** P1 explicitly identifies siq̇albe as the antonym of simartle. Both are abstract nouns formed by the si-...-e circumfix on their respective roots (mart- and q̇alb-), making the opposition morphologically parallel as well as lexically established.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical; morphological (parallel si-...-e formations on contrasting roots)
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## Opposition 2

1. **Term A:** ჭეშმარიტება / č̣ešmariteba
2. **Term B:** სიყალბე / siq̇albe
3. **Transliteration:** č̣ešmariteba / siq̇albe
4. **English translation:** truth (in its philosophically and theologically marked sense) / falseness, falsehood, inauthenticity
5. **Description of contrast:** P1 identifies siq̇albe as the antonym of č̣ešmariteba as well as of simartle. The contrast is asymmetric: č̣ešmariteba is the more philosophically and theologically marked term for truth, while siq̇albe is primarily legal and material in its register distribution.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 3

1. **Term A:** ნამდვილი / namdvili
2. **Term B:** ყალბი / q̇albi
3. **Transliteration:** namdvili / q̇albi
4. **English translation:** real, genuine, authentic / false, fake, counterfeit, forged
5. **Description of contrast:** These are the primary adjectival poles of the genuine/counterfeit contrast across commercial, legal, and everyday registers. P1, P1b, P2b, and P3 consistently treat namdvili and q̇albi as the central opposing adjectives in this domain. The contrast is documented across parallel collocations: namdvili oqro ("real gold") vs. q̇albi oqro ("fake gold"); namdvili megobari ("true friend") vs. q̇albi ɣimili ("false smile").
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 4

1. **Term A:** ნამდვილობა / namdviloba
2. **Term B:** გაყალბება / gaq̇albeba
3. **Transliteration:** namdviloba / gaq̇albeba
4. **English translation:** authenticity, genuineness / falsification, forgery (the act)
5. **Description of contrast:** In legal and administrative contexts, these two nominals represent the opposing outcomes of document verification: namdviloba is the property being confirmed (ნამდვილობის დადასტურება, "confirmation of authenticity"), while gaq̇albeba is the criminal act that produces a counterfeit. The contrast is between a positive property and a negating act directed against that property.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 5

1. **Term A:** ჭეშმარიტი მოწმე / č̣ešmariti mowme
2. **Term B:** ყალბი მოწმე / q̇albi mowme
3. **Transliteration:** č̣ešmariti mowme / q̇albi mowme
4. **English translation:** a truthful witness / a false witness, perjurer
5. **Description of contrast:** Both constructions are documented in P1b and P2b as legal and moral collocations with the same head noun (mowme, "witness"). They represent the opposing evaluations a court or moral discourse applies to a witness: one who testifies truthfully versus one who gives false testimony. The contrast is constructional (parallel Adjective + Noun) as well as lexical.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical; constructional (parallel Adjective + Noun with same head)
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 6

They represent the opposing evaluations applied to evidence in legal proceedings: evidence that merits belief versus evidence that has been deliberately altered or fabricated. The contrast is constructional (parallel Adjective + Noun with same head) as well as lexical.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical; constructional (parallel Adjective + Noun with same head)
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 7

1. **Term A:** სარწმუნო წყარო / sarwmuno wq̇aro
2. **Term B:** სანდო წყარო / sando wq̇aro
3. **Transliteration:** sarwmuno wq̇aro / sando wq̇aro
4. **English translation:** a reliable source, a credible source / a trustworthy source, a reliable source
5. **Description of contrast:** These are near-synonymous rather than opposing constructions, but the documentation in P1, P3, and P2b consistently distinguishes them: sarwmuno emphasizes that a source merits belief as an intrinsic property, while sando may carry a stronger interpersonal connotation of personal trust. This is a contrast of nuance within a shared semantic space rather than a polarity.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical (near-synonymous distinction, not a polarity)
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 8

1. **Term A:** სარწმუნო / sarwmuno
2. **Term B:** დამაჯერებელი / damajerebeliˈ
3. **Transliteration:** sarwmuno / damajerebeliˈ
4. **English translation:** credible, reliable, trustworthy / convincing, persuasive, plausible
5. **Description of contrast:** P1 and P3 explicitly distinguish these two terms: sarwmuno marks an intrinsic property of a source or evidence (it merits belief), while damajerebeliˈ marks the effect on a hearer (it is capable of producing conviction). The contrast is between a property-based and an effect-based evaluation of credibility. They are not antonyms but occupy distinct positions within the same evaluative domain.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical (distinction of evaluative orientation, not a polarity)
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 9

1. **Term A:** ყალბი / q̇albi
2. **Term B:** მცდარი / mcdari
3. **Transliteration:** q̇albi / mcdari
4. **English translation:** false, fake, counterfeit, forged (implying deliberate deception) / erroneous, mistaken, wrong, incorrect (honest error)
5. **Description of contrast:** P2a explicitly distinguishes these two terms: q̇albi implies deliberate falsification or intent to deceive, while mcdari describes honest mistakes or errors without implying intent. Both terms can be applied to statements or conclusions that are not true, but they carry different implications about the agent's responsibility. The documentation notes this distinction explicitly in the entry for mcdari.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 10

1. **Term A:** ლეგიტიმური / legitimuri
2. **Term B:** კანონიერი / kanonieri
3. **Transliteration:** legitimuri / kanonieri
4. **English translation:** legitimate, lawful, valid (with possible normative/moral dimension) / lawful, legal, rightful (tied to positive law)
5. **Description of contrast:** P1 and P3 distinguish these two terms: kanonieri is tied to positive law (does this conform to the rules as written?), while legitimuri can carry a broader normative dimension (is this authority recognized as valid beyond mere legality?). They are not antonyms but occupy distinct positions within the domain of legal and political validity. The documentation notes this distinction explicitly.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical (distinction of scope and normative dimension, not a polarity)
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 11

1. **Term A:** კანონიერი / kanonieri
2. **Term B:** უკანონო / ukanono
3. **Transliteration:** kanonieri / ukanono
4. **English translation:** lawful, legal / unlawful, illegal
5. **Description of contrast:** These are morphologically related antonyms: ukanono is formed by the privative prefix u- on the kanon- root, producing the direct negation of kanonieri. Both are documented in P1 and P2a as standard adjectives in Georgian legal discourse. The opposition is fully institutionalized in legal usage.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical; morphological (privative u- formation)
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 18

1. **Term A:** ზუსტი / zusti
2. **Term B:** უზუსტობა / uzustoba
3. **Transliteration:** zusti / uzustoba
4. **English translation:** exact, precise, accurate / inaccuracy, imprecision
5. **Description of contrast:** These are morphologically related but not parallel in word class: zusti is an adjective, while uzustoba is an abstract noun formed by u- (privative) + zust- + -oba. The opposition is between the positive adjectival property and the nominalized absence of that property. Both are documented in P2a. The intermediate form uzusto (privative adjective) is implied by the derivational pattern but uzustoba is the form explicitly attested in P2a.
6. **Contrast type:** morphological (privative u- + -oba nominalization on the adjectival root)
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 19

The opposition is asymmetric in word class (adjective vs. abstract noun) and in morphological structure (compound adjective vs. compound abstract noun), but the semantic contrast is explicitly documented.

## Opposition 20

1. **Term A:** გარეგნული / garegnuli
2. **Term B:** (no lexicalized single-word antonym documented)
3. **Transliteration:** garegnuli / —

The opposition is documented as implied by the usage of garegnuli (it appears in contexts where the reliability of surface appearances is evaluated, presupposing an inner reality that may differ) but the contrasting term is not attested within the P1–P4 data as a truth-domain lexical item.

## Opposition 21

1. **Term A:** ტყუილი / ẗq̇uili
2. **Term B:** სიმართლე / simartle
3. **Transliteration:** ẗq̇uili / simartle
4. **English translation:** lie, falsehood / truth, correctness
5. **Description of contrast:** P1 documents simartle as the contrasting term for ẗq̇uili in everyday moral discourse ("contrasts with simartle"). The compound binomial ტყუილი-მართალი (ẗq̇uili-martali, "half-true, a mixture of truth and lies") documented in P2b further confirms the lexical pairing of these two terms as the everyday poles of the truth/lie contrast. The opposition is asymmetric in register: ẗq̇uili is more colloquial and direct, simartle is broader in scope.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 24

1. **Term A:** სიმართლეს ვამბობ / simartles vambob
2. **Term B:** ტყუილს ამბობს / ẗq̇uils ambobs
3. **Transliteration:** simartles vambob / ẗq̇uils ambobs
4. **English translation:** I am telling the truth / (he/she) is lying, is telling a lie
5. **Description of contrast:** Both constructions use the same verb (amboba, "to say, to tell") with contrasting direct objects (simartle vs. ẗq̇uili) and contrasting person marking (first person vs. third person). P2b documents both as standard constructions for asserting truthfulness and accusing of lying respectively. The constructional parallelism (Noun-DAT + ambob-/ambobs) makes the contrast structurally transparent.
6. **Contrast type:** constructional (parallel Verb + Object constructions with contrasting objects and person marking)
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 25

1. **Term A:** ჭეშმარიტება გამარჯვებს / č̣ešmariteba gamarjvebs
2. **Term B:** ტყუილი ტყუილს ბადებს / ẗq̇uili ẗq̇uils badebs
3. **Transliteration:** č̣ešmariteba gamarjvebs / ẗq̇uili ẗq̇uils badebs
4. **English translation:** truth prevails, truth wins out / one lie breeds another, lies beget lies
5. **Description of contrast:** Both are proverbial constructions documented in P2b (Group 4 in P3) that treat truth and lies as agents with characteristic behaviors. They are not direct antonyms but represent contrasting moral propositions about the nature of truth and falsehood: truth is characterized by ultimate triumph, while lies are characterized by self-multiplication. Both appear in the same register (proverbial/moral discourse) and are documented as belonging to the same semantic group in P3.
6. **Contrast type:** constructional (contrasting proverbial predications about truth and lies as agents)
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 27

1. **Term A:** სარწმუნოება (secular sense) / sarwmunoeba
2. **Term B:** სარწმუნოება (theological sense) / sarwmunoeba
3. **Transliteration:** sarwmunoeba / sarwmunoeba
4. **English translation:** credibility, reliability, trustworthiness / faith, religion
5. **Description of contrast:** This is an internal semantic opposition within a single form. P1, P2a, P3, and P2b all document the bifurcation of sarwmunoeba across two distinct registers: in secular contexts it denotes credibility/reliability; in theological and ecclesiastical contexts the dominant meaning shifts to faith or religion. P3 notes: "These are not merely polysemous uses of the same word but distinct registers with distinct collocational patterns." The contrast is between two register-conditioned meanings of the same form rather than between two distinct forms.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical (register-conditioned semantic bifurcation within a single form)
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 28

## Opposition 29
