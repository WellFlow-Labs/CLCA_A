# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P1 CORE VOCABULARY COLLECTION
# Language: Indonesian (id)

## Lexical Items

### 1. benar
**Form:** benar  
**Transliteration:** N/A (Latin script)  
**Literal meaning:** true, correct  
**Typical English translations:** true, correct, right, accurate

Used to evaluate statements as factually accurate or actions as morally/procedurally correct. Appears in both epistemic contexts ("itu benar" = "that's true") and normative contexts ("cara yang benar" = "the right way"). Highly productive in derivations (kebenaran = truth, membenarkan = to confirm/justify).

**[CORE]**

---

### 2. salah
**Form:** salah  
**Transliteration:** N/A (Latin script)  
**Literal meaning:** wrong, mistaken  
**Typical English translations:** wrong, incorrect, mistaken, false

Primary term for negating correctness or accuracy of statements, actions, or judgments. Used in epistemic contexts ("jawaban salah" = "wrong answer") and moral contexts ("perbuatan salah" = "wrongdoing"). Forms productive derivatives (kesalahan = error/mistake, menyalahkan = to blame).

**[CORE]**

---

### 3. asli
**Form:** asli  
**Transliteration:** N/A (Latin script)  
**Literal meaning:** original, genuine  
**Typical English translations:** genuine, authentic, original, real

Evaluates objects or representations as authentic rather than counterfeit or copied. Commonly used for documents ("dokumen asli" = "original document"), products ("barang asli" = "genuine goods"), and identity ("orang asli" = "indigenous person/native"). Distinguished from imitations or fakes.

**[CORE]**

### 4. palsu
**Form:** palsu  
**Transliteration:** N/A (Latin script)  
**Literal meaning:** false, fake  
**Typical English translations:** fake, counterfeit, false, forged

Evaluates objects, documents, or representations as inauthentic or deliberately deceptive. Used for counterfeit goods ("uang palsu" = "counterfeit money"), forged documents, and false identities. Carries connotation of intentional deception.

**[CORE]**

### 5. bohong
**Form:** bohong  
**Transliteration:** N/A (Latin script)  
**Literal meaning:** lie, false  
**Typical English translations:** lie, false, untrue, dishonest

Specifically evaluates statements or speech acts as deliberately false or deceptive. Functions as both noun ("itu bohong" = "that's a lie") and verb ("dia bohong" = "he/she is lying"). Strongly implies intentional deception rather than mere error.
