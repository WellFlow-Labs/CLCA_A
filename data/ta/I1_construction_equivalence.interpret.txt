## 2. Substitution Patterns (Narrative)

### 2.1 REVELATION vs. BECOME
Tamil maintains a **clear distinction** between BECOME and REVELATION:
- **BECOME** (ஆகு āku): Expresses change of state or status, used when something previously uncertain is revealed or when status changes. Example: உண்மை ஆகிவிட்டது "it became true / turned out to be true"
- **REVELATION** (வெளிப்படு veḷippaṭu): Expresses emergence from concealment, implies prior hiddenness. Example: உண்மை வெளிப்படு "truth comes out, is revealed"

These are **not substitutes** but complementary operators with different semantic profiles. REVELATION does not replace BECOME; both coexist productively.

### 2.2 VERIFICATION and CAUSATION (factual)
Tamil shows **morphological identity** between factual causation and verification:
- The causative suffix -படுத்து (-paṭuttu) creates both:
  - **Factual causation**: உண்மைப்படுத்து "make true"
  - **Verification**: உண்மைப்படுத்து "verify, authenticate"

This is not substitution but **polysemy**: the same form covers both "making true" and "verifying as true." Context disambiguates.

Additionally, Tamil has **dedicated verification constructions**:
- Light verb: சரி பார் (cari pār) "check, verify, inspect"
- Demonstration verbs: உண்மையை காட்டு (uṇmaiyai kāṭṭu) "show the truth"; உண்மையை நிரூபி (uṇmaiyai nirūpi) "prove the truth"

### 2.3 DISCONFIRMATION
Tamil has **no dedicated disconfirmation operator**. Instead, disconfirmation is expressed through:
- **NEGATION + VERIFICATION**: "not verify" or "cannot verify"
- **Showing falsehood**: பொய் என்று காட்டு (poy eṉṟu kāṭṭu) "show that it is false"
- **Exposure of lies**: பொய் வெளிப்படு (poy veḷippaṭu) "lie is exposed"

This represents a **conceptual gap**: no single morphosyntactic operator for disconfirmation parallel to verification.

### 2.4 CAUSATION (character)
Tamil lacks a **productive pattern** for character causation ("make someone truthful/honest"). While factual causation is robust (உண்மைப்படுத்து "make true/verify"), there is no parallel *உண்மையாளராக்கு "make into a truthful person" or similar construction in common use.

This represents a **structural gap**: the language does not grammaticalize character transformation in the truth domain.

### 2.5 NEGATION: Analytic Strategy
Tamil uses **analytic negation** via negative copulas:
- இல்லை (illai) — colloquial
- அல்ல (alla) — formal
- அன்று (aṉṟu) — literary

Negation is **not morphological** (no negative prefix on truth-terms). The negative copula directly follows the truth-term: உண்மையில்லை (uṇmai-illai) "not true."

### 2.6 Asymmetries: Positive vs. Negative Forms

#### 2.6.1 Abstract Nominalization
- **உண்மை/மெய்**: Productive abstract nominalization (உண்மைத்தன்மை, மெய்ம்மை)
- **பொய்**: பொய்ம்மை exists but is **rare and archaic**; பொய் itself functions as abstract noun

#### 2.6.2 Agentive Nominalization
- **Negative terms** (liar) are **more elaborated**: பொய்யன் (masculine), பொய்யாளி (neutral), multiple variants
- **Positive terms** (truthful person) are **less elaborated**: உண்மையாளர் exists but less frequent in everyday speech

#### 2.6.3 Legal/Commercial Compounds
- **பொய்-compounds** are **highly elaborated** in fraud/forgery domains:
  - பொய் சாட்சி "false testimony"
  - பொய் சான்று "false certificate"
  - பொய் சாமான் "fake goods"
  - பொய் நாணயம் "counterfeit currency"
- **உண்மை-compounds** in parallel domains are **less elaborated**; positive authenticity terms less developed

This reflects a **cultural/pragmatic asymmetry**: negative deviations from truth are more lexically and constructionally elaborated than positive truth-telling.

### 2.7 Causative Morphology Asymmetry
- **உண்மை/மெய்**: Use -படுத்து (-paṭuttu) causative
- **பொய்**: Uses -ஆக்கு (-ākku) causative (பொய்யாக்கு "make false")

Different causative morphology for positive vs. negative truth-terms. No standard *பொய்ப்படுத்து form attested.

## 3. Missingness Summary

### 3.1 Structural Gaps
1. **CAUSATION (character)**: No productive construction for "make someone truthful/honest." The language grammaticalizes factual causation (make statements true) but not character transformation.

### 3.2 Lexical Gaps
None identified. All major truth-domain concepts have lexical expressions.

### 3.3 Conceptual Gaps
1. **DISCONFIRMATION**: No single dedicated operator for disconfirmation parallel to verification. Expressed through circumlocution (negation + verification, showing falsehood, exposure).

### 3.4 Asymmetric Gaps
1. **Abstract nominalization**: பொய்ம்மை rare/archaic; பொய் itself serves as abstract noun without further derivation
2. **Agentive nominalization**: Positive terms (truthful person) less elaborated than negative terms (liar)
3. **Legal/commercial compounds**: Negative fraud terminology highly elaborated; positive authenticity terminology less developed
4. **Causative morphology**: Different suffixes for positive (-படுத்து) vs. negative (-ஆக்கு) causation

## 4. Canonicalized Operator Inventory

### 4.1 Fully Available
- **ATTR**: Zero copula + truth-term predicate
- **ADNOM**: Truth-term + -ஆன + noun
- **BECOME**: Truth-term + ஆகு "become"
- **REVELATION**: Truth-term + வெளிப்படு "emerge/be revealed" (distinct from BECOME)
- **CAUSATION (factual)**: Truth-term + படுத்து causative
- **VERIFICATION**: Multiple strategies (causative, demonstration verbs, light verb constructions)
- **NEGATION**: Analytic via negative copula
- **NOMINALIZATION (abstract)**: Truth-root + -மை suffix; compound with தன்மை
- **NOMINALIZATION (agentive)**: Truth-root + person suffix

### 4.2 Available with Substitution
- **DISCONFIRMATION**: Expressed through NEGATION + VERIFICATION or REVELATION of falsehood (no dedicated operator)

### 4.3 Partially Available
- **NOMINALIZATION (abstract)**: Asymmetric — productive with உண்மை/மெய், rare with பொய்
- **NOMINALIZATION (agentive)**: Asymmetric — negative terms more elaborated than positive

### 4.4 Missing Entirely
- **CAUSATION (character)**: No productive construction for character transformation in truth domain

## 5. Additional Observations

### 5.1 Multi-Word Expressions as Operators
Tamil extensively uses **light verb constructions** that function as unified operators:
- **சரி பார்** (cari pār) "check, verify": Light verb பார் "see" carries tense/aspect; சரி "correct" provides semantic content. Functions syntactically as single verb.
- **சரி செய்** (cari cey) "fix, correct": Light verb செய் "do" carries inflection; சரி provides meaning. Extremely productive.
- **உண்மை சொல்லு** (uṇmai collu) "tell truth": Object + verb collocation, but highly lexicalized and functions as unit.

These are **not circumlocutions** but grammaticalized multi-word operators with unified syntactic behavior.

### 5.2 Register-Based Variation
Operator choice varies by register:
- **Colloquial**: இல்லை negation, சொல்லு speech verb, -ஆ question particle
- **Formal/Literary**: அல்ல negation, மெய்-based compounds, ஞானம் wisdom terms, நிரூபி "prove"
- **Legal/Administrative**: பொய்-compounds for fraud, நிரூபி for proof

This is **not missingness** but sociolinguistic distribution of equivalent operators.

### 5.3 Completive Auxiliaries
Many operators require **completive auxiliaries** (விடு viṭu, போ pō) for natural expression:
- உண்மை ஆகிவிட்டது "it became true (completely)"
- உண்மை வெளிப்பட்டுவிட்டது "truth came out (completely)"

These are **not separate operators** but aspectual markers obligatory in narrative past contexts.
