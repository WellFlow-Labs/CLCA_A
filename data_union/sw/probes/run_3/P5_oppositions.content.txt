# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P5 Oppositions
# Language: Swahili (sw)

---

## Opposition 1

1. **Term A:** kweli
2. **Term B:** uongo
3. **Transliteration:** kweli / uongo
4. **English translation:** truth / falsehood, lie
5. **Description of contrast:** These are the primary lexical antonyms in the Swahili truth-evaluation domain. They are explicitly contrasted in fixed constructions (*kweli au uongo*, "true or false"), in paired imperatives (*niambie ukweli, usiniambie uongo*), and in proverbial assertions (*kweli hushinda uongo*). P3 identifies them as the core contrasting pair across propositional truth assertion and deception characterization.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## Opposition 2

1. **Term A:** ukweli
2. **Term B:** uongo
3. **Transliteration:** ukweli / uongo
4. **English translation:** truth (abstract noun) / falsehood (abstract noun)
5. **Description of contrast:** The nominalized counterpart of Opposition 1, operating at the level of abstract nouns. Both carry the *u-* class 14 prefix and function as the primary abstract nominals for truth and falsehood respectively. They are contrasted in constructions such as *kuna tofauti kubwa kati ya kweli na uongo* and *uhalisi dhidi ya uongo*, and P1 explicitly identifies *uongo* as the direct antonym of *ukweli*.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## Opposition 3

1. **Term A:** ni kweli
2. **Term B:** si kweli
3. **Transliteration:** ni kweli / si kweli
4. **English translation:** it is true / it is not true
5. **Description of contrast:** A constructional opposition formed by the invariant copula *ni* versus its direct negative counterpart *si*, with the same predicate *kweli*. P4a identifies this pair as "the canonical binary truth-evaluation frame" and P3 notes that they function as direct opposites within the propositional truth assertion group. The opposition is purely morphological at the copula position.
6. **Contrast type:** morphological
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## Opposition 4

1. **Term A:** ni sahihi
2. **Term B:** si sahihi
3. **Transliteration:** ni sahihi / si sahihi
4. **English translation:** it is correct / it is not correct
5. **Description of contrast:** The same *ni* / *si* copular opposition applied to *sahihi* (correctness/accuracy). P4a identifies this pair alongside *ni kweli / si kweli* as the primary binary truth-evaluation frames. The opposition is attested in educational contexts (*jibu lako si sahihi — jaribu tena*) and in formal verification contexts (*taarifa hii imethibitishwa kuwa sahihi*).
6. **Contrast type:** morphological
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## Opposition 5

1. **Term A:** halisi
2. **Term B:** bandia
3. **Transliteration:** halisi / bandia
4. **English translation:** genuine, authentic / fake, counterfeit
5. **Description of contrast:** The core contrasting pair in the domain of authenticity and material evaluation. P3 identifies them as "the core contrasting pair" in Group 3, and they are explicitly opposed in the construction *hii ni halisi, si bandia* ("this is genuine, not fake") and in the binary frame *halisi au bandia — jinsi ya kutofautisha* ("genuine or fake — how to tell the difference"). P1 notes that *bandia* "contrasts directly with *halisi*."
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## Opposition 6

1. **Term A:** ukweli
2. **Term B:** uongo mtupu
3. **Transliteration:** ukweli / uongo mtupu
4. **English translation:** truth / pure falsehood, outright lie
5. **Description of contrast:** An asymmetric opposition in which the intensified form *uongo mtupu* is contrasted with plain *ukweli*. The construction *hii si nusu kweli — ni uongo mtupu* (P2b) places *uongo mtupu* at the extreme end of a scale running from partial truth to complete falsehood, with *ukweli* as the opposing pole. The intensifier *mtupu* ("nothing but, sheer") marks the maximal degree of the falsehood side.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## Opposition 7

1. **Term A:** nusu kweli
2. **Term B:** uongo mtupu
3. **Transliteration:** nusu kweli / uongo mtupu
4. **English translation:** half-truth / outright lie, pure falsehood
5. **Description of contrast:** A scalar opposition within the falsehood domain, explicitly attested in the construction *hii si nusu kweli — ni uongo mtupu* (P2b). *Nusu kweli* ("half-truth") treats truth as gradable and positions a statement as partially true but misleading; *uongo mtupu* positions a statement as entirely false. P3 notes that this construction "escalates a charge from partial deception to complete falsehood."
6. **Contrast type:** constructional
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## Opposition 8

1. **Term A:** kusema ukweli
2. **Term B:** kusema uongo
3. **Transliteration:** kusema ukweli / kusema uongo
4. **English translation:** to tell the truth / to tell a lie
5. **Description of contrast:** A constructional opposition using the same verb *kusema* ("to speak/say") with contrasting objects *ukweli* and *uongo*. Both are attested as fixed verbal phrases in P1 and P2b. The opposition is made explicit in the paired imperative *niambie ukweli, usiniambie uongo* ("tell me the truth, don't tell me lies"), where the positive and negative imperatives of *kuambia* parallel the same contrast.
6. **Contrast type:** constructional
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## Opposition 9

1. **Term A:** ukweli uliofichwa
2. **Term B:** ukweli uliofunuliwa
3. **Transliteration:** ukweli uliofichwa / ukweli uliofunuliwa
4. **English translation:** hidden truth / revealed truth
5. **Description of contrast:** An opposition formed by contrasting relative-clause modifiers on the same head noun *ukweli*. *-fichwa* (passive of *kuficha*, "to hide") and *-funuliwa* (passive of *kufunua*, "to reveal") are antonymous verbal stems. Both forms are documented in P1b as core items and appear in Group 9 (P3) as part of the domain of truth concealment and revelation.
6. **Contrast type:** morphological
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## Opposition 10

1. **Term A:** ukweli uliofichwa (kwa muda)
2. **Term B:** ukweli unajulikana / haufichiki milele
3. **Transliteration:** ukweli uliofichwa (kwa muda) / ukweli unajulikana; haufichiki milele
4. **English translation:** truth (temporarily) hidden / truth becomes known; cannot be hidden forever
5. **Description of contrast:** A temporal opposition between the concealed state of truth and its inevitable disclosure. Attested in the proverbial constructions *ukweli uliofichwa haufichiki milele* ("a hidden truth cannot be hidden forever") and *ukweli unaweza kufichwa kwa muda, lakini si milele* ("truth can be hidden for a time, but not forever"). The contrast is between a temporary state (concealment) and an inevitable outcome (disclosure).
6. **Contrast type:** constructional
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## Opposition 11

1. **Term A:** kudhihirisha (ukweli)
2. **Term B:** kuficha (ukweli)
3. **Transliteration:** kudhihirisha / kuficha
4. **English translation:** to make evident, to reveal (the truth) / to hide (the truth)
5. **Description of contrast:** A verbal opposition between the causative of *dhahiri* ("to make manifest") and *kuficha* ("to conceal"). Both verbs take *ukweli* as a natural object and appear in constructions documented in P1b and P2b (*kudhihirisha ukweli mbele ya wote*; *ukweli uliofichwa*). P2a documents *kudhihirisha* as a causative derivation; *kuficha* is its contextual antonym in the revelation-concealment domain.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## Opposition 12

1. **Term A:** kudhihirika
2. **Term B:** kufichika
3. **Transliteration:** kudhihirika / kufichika
4. **English translation:** to become evident, to come to light / to be hideable, to remain hidden
5. **Description of contrast:** The stative/intransitive counterparts of the causative pair in Opposition 11. *Kudhihirika* (stative of *dhahiri*, documented in P2a) means to become manifest without an explicit agent; *kufichika* (stative of *kuficha*) means to be capable of being hidden or to remain concealed. The opposition is attested in the proverbial construction *ukweli uliofichwa haufichiki milele* (P2b), where *haufichiki* is the negative stative of *kuficha*.
6. **Contrast type:** morphological
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## Opposition 13

1. **Term A:** uhalisi
2. **Term B:** uongo
3. **Transliteration:** uhalisi / uongo
4. **English translation:** reality, authenticity / falsehood
5. **Description of contrast:** Explicitly attested in the construction *uhalisi dhidi ya uongo* ("reality versus falsehood," P1b), which frames the contrast as an adversarial opposition. While *uhalisi* and *uongo* belong to different semantic domains (authenticity/reality vs. propositional falsehood), their explicit pairing in this construction constitutes an attested opposition in the data.
6. **Contrast type:** constructional
7. **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

## Opposition 14

1. **Term A:** uaminifu
2. **Term B:** udanganyifu
3. **Transliteration:** uaminifu / udanganyifu
4. **English translation:** faithfulness, trustworthiness / deception, deceitfulness
5. **Description of contrast:** Both are *u-* class 14 abstract nominals evaluating character, and they occupy opposing positions in the character-evaluation domain documented in P3 Groups 6 and 7. *Uaminifu* characterizes a person or conduct as trustworthy and honest; *udanganyifu* characterizes a person or conduct as deceptive and dishonest. The construction *mtu asiye na uaminifu hawezi kuaminiwa* (P2b) implicitly invokes this opposition by defining the untrustworthy person through the absence of *uaminifu*.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## Opposition 15

1. **Term A:** mwaminifu
2. **Term B:** mdanganyifu
3. **Transliteration:** mwaminifu / mdanganyifu
4. **English translation:** a faithful/trustworthy person / a deceiver
5. **Description of contrast:** The agentive nominal counterpart of Opposition 14, both formed with the *m-* class 1 human prefix. They occupy opposing positions in character evaluation and are both documented in P2a as core agentive nominalizations. The opposition is structurally parallel: same prefix, contrasting stems.
6. **Contrast type:** morphological
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## Opposition 16

1. **Term A:** mwaminifu
2. **Term B:** mwongo
3. **Transliteration:** mwaminifu / mwongo
4. **English translation:** a faithful/trustworthy person / a liar
5. **Description of contrast:** A second agentive opposition in the character-evaluation domain, contrasting the trustworthy person with the liar specifically. Both are *m-* class 1 human nominals. *Mwongo* is derived from the *-ongo* stem (falsehood/deception) and is documented in P2a; *mwaminifu* is derived from *amini* (to trust/believe). The construction *mtu wa kweli hasemi uongo* (P2b) implicitly links the truthful person to the absence of lying, paralleling this opposition.
6. **Contrast type:** morphological
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## Opposition 17

1. **Term A:** kuamini
2. **Term B:** kudanganya
3. **Transliteration:** kuamini / kudanganya
4. **English translation:** to believe, to trust / to deceive, to mislead
5. **Description of contrast:** A verbal opposition between the act of trusting/believing and the act of deceiving. *Kuamini* is documented in P2a as a core verbal form; *kudanganya* is documented in P1 and P2a. The construction *siwezi kumwamini tena* (P2b) describes the breakdown of trust, which is the interpersonal consequence of *kudanganya*. The two verbs occupy opposing positions in the trust-deception domain.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## Opposition 18

1. **Term A:** kuaminika
2. **Term B:** kudanganyika
3. **Transliteration:** kuaminika / kudanganyika
4. **English translation:** to be trustworthy, to be credible / to be deceived, to fall for deception
5. **Description of contrast:** Both are stative forms with the *-ka*/*-ika* suffix (documented in P2a), but they occupy opposing positions: *kuaminika* describes the state of being worthy of trust, while *kudanganyika* describes the state of having been deceived or being susceptible to deception. The structural parallelism (same suffix, contrasting stems) makes this a morphological opposition.
6. **Contrast type:** morphological
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 19

1. **Term A:** sahihi (correctness/accuracy)
2. **Term B:** kosa / makosa
3. **Transliteration:** sahihi / kosa, makosa
4. **English translation:** correct, accurate / error, mistake
5. **Description of contrast:** *Sahihi* evaluates something as conforming to a standard of correctness; *kosa* identifies a departure from that standard. The opposition is attested in the construction *kasoro hii inaweza kusahihishwa* (P2b), where *kasoro* (flaw) is corrected by *kusahihisha* (derived from *sahihi*), and in the educational construction *jibu lako si sahihi — jaribu tena*. P3 places *sahihi* in Group 4 (accuracy evaluation) and *kosa/makosa* in Group 14 (quality, defect, and error evaluation) as complementary evaluative domains.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## Opposition 20

1. **Term A:** sahihi (correctness)
2. **Term B:** kasoro
3. **Transliteration:** sahihi / kasoro
4. **English translation:** correct, without flaw / defect, flaw
5. **Description of contrast:** *Sahihi* evaluates an object, document, or statement as correct and without deficiency; *kasoro* identifies a specific deficiency or flaw. The construction *kasoro hii inaweza kusahihishwa* (P2b) explicitly links the two by using the causative of *sahihi* (*kusahihishwa*) as the remedy for *kasoro*, making the opposition structurally embedded in the construction itself.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## Opposition 21

1. **Term A:** ubora
2. **Term B:** kasoro
3. **Transliteration:** ubora / kasoro
4. **English translation:** quality, excellence / defect, flaw
5. **Description of contrast:** *Ubora* (abstract nominalization of *bora*, "excellent") refers to the positive quality of an object or product; *kasoro* identifies a deficiency in that quality. Both appear in commercial and evaluative discourse (P3 Group 14), and the construction *udhibiti wa ubora* ("quality control") implicitly invokes the detection and elimination of *kasoro*. P1 documents *kasoro* as appearing in "quality assessment" contexts alongside *bora*.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## Opposition 22

1. **Term A:** fanana
2. **Term B:** tofauti
3. **Transliteration:** fanana / tofauti
4. **English translation:** to resemble, to be alike / difference, different
5. **Description of contrast:** *Fanana* evaluates two things as similar or alike; *tofauti* evaluates them as different or identifies a discrepancy between them. Both appear in comparative evaluation contexts (P3 Group 13) and are explicitly contrasted in the construction *kuna tofauti kubwa kati ya kweli na uongo* (P2b), where *tofauti* marks the separation between the two poles. The construction *haifanani hata kidogo* (P2b) is the negative of *fanana* and approaches the meaning of *tofauti*.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## Opposition 23

1. **Term A:** kufanana na
2. **Term B:** kutofautiana
3. **Transliteration:** kufanana na / kutofautiana
4. **English translation:** to resemble, to be similar to / to differ from each other
5. **Description of contrast:** The verbal counterpart of Opposition 22, with the reciprocal suffix *-ana* on *kutofautiana* marking mutual difference. Both are documented in P2a as core derivations. The structural contrast is between a verb of similarity (*kufanana na*, requiring a comitative *na*-phrase) and a verb of mutual difference (*kutofautiana*, with the reciprocal *-ana* suffix encoding the mutual relation internally).
6. **Contrast type:** morphological
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 24

1. **Term A:** dhahiri / ni dhahiri kwamba
2. **Term B:** uliofichwa / kufichwa
3. **Transliteration:** dhahiri / uliofichwa
4. **English translation:** evident, manifest / hidden, concealed
5. **Description of contrast:** *Dhahiri* characterizes something as plainly apparent and visible; *-fichwa* (passive of *kuficha*) characterizes something as concealed. The opposition is attested across multiple constructions: *ni dhahiri kwamba* (evidential frame, P2b) asserts that something is plainly the case, while *ukweli uliofichwa* (P1b) describes truth as suppressed. P3 places *dhahiri* in Group 9 (revelation of truth) alongside the concealment vocabulary.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 25

1. **Term A:** ukweli wa kisayansi
2. **Term B:** ukweli wa kisheria
3. **Transliteration:** ukweli wa kisayansi / ukweli wa kisheria
4. **English translation:** scientific truth / legal truth
5. **Description of contrast:** Both are domain-qualified uses of *ukweli* with the *ki-* adjectival prefix, but P1b explicitly notes that *ukweli wa kisheria* ("legal truth") "may differ from everyday or scientific truth," implying that the two domain-qualified forms can be in tension with each other. This is an opposition between two standards of truth rather than between truth and falsehood. P3 Group 16 documents the *ki-* domain-qualifier pattern as productive across multiple bases.
6. **Contrast type:** constructional
7. **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

## Opposition 26

1. **Term A:** uhalisi pepe
2. **Term B:** uhalisi (unmodified)
3. **Transliteration:** uhalisi pepe / uhalisi
4. **English translation:** virtual reality / reality (unmediated)
5. **Description of contrast:** *Uhalisi pepe* ("virtual reality") uses *uhalisi* to name a technologically constructed perceptual environment, while unmodified *uhalisi* refers to actual, unmediated conditions (*uhalisi wa maisha*, "the reality of life"; *uhalisi mgumu*, "harsh reality"). P3 Group 10 notes that these two uses "appear in entirely separate discourse contexts" and that the technology-domain uses contrast with items that "use *uhalisi* to refer to unmediated actual conditions."
6. **Contrast type:** constructional
7. **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

## Opposition 27

1. **Term A:** kukabili uhalisi
2. **Term B:** mbali na uhalisi
3. **Transliteration:** kukabili uhalisi / mbali na uhalisi
4. **English translation:** to face reality / far from reality, out of touch with reality
5. **Description of contrast:** Both constructions take *uhalisi* as their object but express opposing orientations toward it: *kukabili uhalisi* describes engagement with and acceptance of actual conditions; *mbali na uhalisi* describes disconnection from them. Both are documented in P1b and P3 Group 9/10.
6. **Contrast type:** constructional
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 28

1. **Term A:** uhalisia wa kichawi
2. **Term B:** uhalisia (unmodified / uhalisia wa kijadi)
3. **Transliteration:** uhalisia wa kichawi / uhalisia; uhalisia wa kijadi
4. **English translation:** magical realism / realism; traditional realism
5. **Description of contrast:** P1b notes that *uhalisia wa kichawi* ("magical realism") "deliberately incorporates non-realistic elements, creating a tension with the root's core meaning of faithful representation." This places it in opposition to unmodified *uhalisia* or *uhalisia wa kijadi* ("traditional realism"), which denote faithful representation of reality. The opposition is between a genre that subverts the realist premise and one that upholds it.
6. **Contrast type:** constructional
7. **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

## Opposition 29

1. **Term A:** thibitisha / uthibitisho
2. **Term B:** (absence of verification)
3. **Transliteration:** thibitisha / uthibitisho
4. **English translation:** to verify, to confirm / verification, confirmation
5. **Description of contrast:** This is not a lexical opposition between two attested terms but a constructional opposition between the verified state and the unverified state. The construction *hakikisha kuwa taarifa ni sahihi kabla ya kuisambaza* (P2b) — "make sure the information is accurate before spreading it" — implies that spreading unverified information is the opposing, negatively evaluated action. The opposition is between *kuthibitishwa* (to be verified, attested) and the implicit absence of verification. This is noted as a constructional asymmetry rather than a balanced lexical pair.
6. **Contrast type:** constructional
7. **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

## Opposition 30

1. **Term A:** kuamini bila ushahidi
2. **Term B:** kuthibitisha (before accepting)
3. **Transliteration:** kuamini bila ushahidi / kuthibitisha
4. **English translation:** to believe without evidence / to verify, to confirm
5. **Description of contrast:** The construction *kuamini bila ushahidi ni hatari* (P2b) — "believing without evidence is dangerous" — positions uncritical belief as epistemically irresponsible. This is implicitly contrasted with the verification vocabulary (*kuthibitisha*, *kuhakikisha usahihi*), which represents the epistemically responsible alternative. The opposition is between an epistemic stance (belief without evidence) and an epistemic practice (verification).
6. **Contrast type:** constructional
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## Opposition 31

1. **Term A:** kweli (as propositional truth)
2. **Term B:** kweli (as authenticity/genuineness in *X wa kweli*)
3. **Transliteration:** kweli / kweli
4. **English translation:** true (of a statement) / genuine, real (of a person, relationship, or quality)
5. **Description of contrast:** P1b (Step 3, Peripheral Extensions) documents that *kweli* in the pattern *X wa kweli* (*upendo wa kweli*, "true love"; *rafiki wa kweli*, "true friend"; *mtu wa kweli*, "genuine person") evaluates "the authenticity of an emotional state rather than the truth-value of a proposition." This is an internal semantic opposition within the same lexical item between its propositional-truth use and its authenticity-evaluation use.
6. **Contrast type:** constructional
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## Opposition 32

1. **Term A:** sahihi (correctness/accuracy)
2. **Term B:** sahihi (signature)
3. **Transliteration:** sahihi / sahihi
4. **English translation:** correct, accurate / signature
5. **Description of contrast:** P1b (Step 3) and P2a document that *sahihi* has developed a distinct lexical meaning — "signature" — in administrative and legal contexts, where it refers to an authenticating mark rather than to the property of correctness. P3 places the two uses in different groups (Group 4 for correctness; Group 5 for document authentication). This is an internal semantic opposition within the same form between an evaluative meaning and a nominal/procedural meaning.
6. **Contrast type:** constructional
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]
