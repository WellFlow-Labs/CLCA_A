# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2a Derivational System
# Language: Basque (eu)

---

## Preliminary Note on Method

The following documents attested derivational forms for each P1 base item. Forms already fully documented in P1b are cross-referenced rather than re-described. Derivational coverage is uneven across items, reflecting actual patterns in the language rather than an assumed uniform productivity. Where no derivation is identified, this is stated explicitly. Forms are listed per base item without imposed categorical grouping.

## 1. egia

**Base Form:** egia

---

**Derived Form 1.1**
- **Derived Form:** egiazkoa
- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Literal Breakdown:** *egia* (truth) + *-zko* (relational/adjectival suffix) + *-a* (definite article)
- **Natural English Translation:** true, truthful, genuine
- **Usage:** Adjectival form of *egia*, used attributively and predicatively to characterize statements, persons, or things as truthful or genuine. Already documented as P1 item 2 and P1b 1.4.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 1.2**
- **Derived Form:** egiazkotasuna
- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Literal Breakdown:** *egia* + *-zko* + *-tasun* (abstract noun suffix) + *-a*
- **Natural English Translation:** truthfulness, genuineness
- **Usage:** Abstract nominal denoting the property of being truthful or genuine. Used in philosophical and ethical discourse. Already documented as P1b 1.5.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 1.3**
- **Derived Form:** egiatasuna
- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Literal Breakdown:** *egia* + *-tasun* + *-a*
- **Natural English Translation:** truthfulness, the quality of being true
- **Usage:** Abstract nominal formed directly from *egia* without the *-zko* intermediate. Slightly more abstract and less frequent than *egiazkotasuna*. Already documented as P1b 1.19.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 1.4**
- **Derived Form:** egiazki
- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Literal Breakdown:** *egiazk-* (from *egiazkoa*) + *-i* (adverbial suffix)
- **Natural English Translation:** truly, genuinely, in truth
- **Usage:** Adverbial form used as an epistemic intensifier in formal and literary registers. Already documented as P1b 1.20.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 1.5**
- **Derived Form:** egiaztatu
- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Literal Breakdown:** *egiazta-* (stem derived from *egia* via *egiazkoa*) + *-tu* (perfective/infinitive verbal suffix)
- **Natural English Translation:** to verify, to certify, to confirm
- **Usage:** Primary verb for verification and certification across legal, administrative, and scientific contexts. Already documented as P1b 1.6.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 1.6**
- **Derived Form:** egiaztatua
- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Literal Breakdown:** *egiazta-* + *-tu* + *-a*
- **Natural English Translation:** verified, certified, confirmed
- **Usage:** Participial/adjectival form of *egiaztatu*, used in legal and administrative contexts to describe documents or facts that have been officially verified. Already documented as P1 item 25.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 1.7**
- **Derived Form:** egiaztatze
- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Literal Breakdown:** *egiazta-* + *-tze* (process nominalization suffix)
- **Natural English Translation:** verification, the process of verifying
- **Usage:** Verbal noun denoting the process of verification, used in administrative and technical contexts. Already documented as P1b 1.7.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 1.8**
- **Derived Form:** egiaztapen
- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Literal Breakdown:** *egiazta-* + *-pen* (result nominalization suffix)
- **Natural English Translation:** verification, certification, attestation (as result or document)
- **Usage:** Nominal form denoting the result of verification rather than the process. Used in legal and administrative documents. Already documented as P1b 1.8.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 1.9**
- **Derived Form:** egiaztagarria
- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Literal Breakdown:** *egiazta-* + *-garri* (capable/worthy suffix) + *-a*
- **Natural English Translation:** verifiable, certifiable
- **Usage:** Characterizes claims or facts as capable of being verified. Used in scientific, legal, and journalistic discourse. Already documented as P1b 1.9.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 1.10**
- **Derived Form:** egiaztagaitza
- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Literal Breakdown:** *egiazta-* + *-gaitz* (difficult/impossible suffix) + *-a*
- **Natural English Translation:** unverifiable, difficult to verify
- **Usage:** Negative counterpart of *egiaztagarria*, characterizing claims that cannot be or are difficult to verify. Already documented as P1b 1.10.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 1.11**
- **Derived Form:** egiaztagiri
- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Literal Breakdown:** *egiazta-* + *giri* (document, certificate; from *agiri*, "document")
- **Natural English Translation:** certificate of verification, attestation document
- **Usage:** A nominal compound denoting an official document certifying that something has been verified. Used in administrative and legal contexts. Mentioned in P1b note.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 1.12**
- **Derived Form:** egiazale / egiazalea
- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Literal Breakdown:** *egia* + *-zale* (devotee/lover suffix); *egiazalea* adds *-a* (definite article)
- **Natural English Translation:** truth-lover, truth-seeker
- **Usage:** Characterizes a person devoted to truth-seeking. Appears in philosophical and literary contexts. Already documented as P1b 1.17–1.18.
- **Epistemic Status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**Derived Form 1.13**
- **Derived Form:** egiazkeria
- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Literal Breakdown:** *egiazk-* (from *egiazkoa*) + *-eria* (pejorative abstract suffix)
- **Natural English Translation:** false pretense of truth, spurious truthfulness
- **Usage:** A pejorative form denoting the performance or pretense of truthfulness without genuine commitment to truth. Appears in literary and critical registers. Mentioned in P1b note.
- **Epistemic Status:** [MARGINAL]

- **Literal Breakdown:** *benetako* (genuine, real) + *-tasun* (abstract noun suffix) + *-a*
- **Natural English Translation:** genuineness, authenticity, realness
- **Usage:** Abstract nominal denoting the property of being genuine or authentic. Used in philosophical, aesthetic, and everyday evaluative discourse when discussing the quality of authenticity as an abstract property.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 3.2**
- **Derived Form:** benetako
- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Literal Breakdown:** *bene-* + *-tako* (adjectival suffix, indefinite form without article)
- **Natural English Translation:** genuine, real, authentic (attributive, indefinite)
- **Usage:** The indefinite attributive form of *benetakoa*, used before nouns (*benetako arazoa*, "the real problem"). This is the form that appears in attributive position; *benetakoa* appears predicatively or as a standalone nominal.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

*Note: No verbal derivation from benetakoa is identified. The root does not appear to generate agent nouns or process nominals with the same productivity as egia- or zuzen-.*
