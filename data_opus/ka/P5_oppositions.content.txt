# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P5 Oppositions
## Language: Georgian (ka)
## Transliteration: ISO 9984 (1996)

Below are oppositions clearly supported by the CONTEXT (P1–P4b). Each is grounded in explicitly documented usage, contrast, or morphological relation. Asymmetries are preserved and not forced.

---

## OPPOSITION 1

1. **Term A:** ნამდვილი
2. **Term B:** ყალბი
3. **Transliteration:** namdvili / q̇albi
4. **English:** real, genuine / fake, counterfeit
5. **Description of contrast:** P1 #6 and #8 explicitly frame these as direct opposites along the authenticity axis (genuine vs. imitation/forgery); P3 Group 4 organizes them as the "genuine pole" vs. "fake pole."
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## OPPOSITION 2

1. **Term A:** ნამდვილი ოქრო
2. **Term B:** ყალბი ფული
3. **Transliteration:** namdvili okro / q̇albi puli
4. **English:** genuine gold / counterfeit money
5. **Description of contrast:** Constructional realization of the genuine/fake opposition: the Adnominal Adj+N frame (P4a #3) applied to the authenticity poles, contrasting authentic vs. counterfeit material objects (P2b #6, #8).
6. **Contrast type:** constructional
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## OPPOSITION 3

1. **Term A:** სიმართლე
2. **Term B:** სიცრუე
3. **Transliteration:** simartle / sicrue
4. **English:** truth / falsehood
5. **Description of contrast:** P1 #10 explicitly defines სიცრუე as "falsehood... as opposed to truth"; the abstract-noun truth-pole vs. falsehood-pole opposition in moral/rhetorical contexts.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## OPPOSITION 4

1. **Term A:** სიმართლის თქმა
2. **Term B:** ტყუილის თქმა
3. **Transliteration:** simartlis tkma / ṭq̇uilis tkma
4. **English:** telling the truth / telling a lie
5. **Description of contrast:** P3 Group 2 and P4a/P4b #8 explicitly note these share the same genitive + verbal-noun (tkma) frame and are "mutually contrastive" within it — a constructional opposition of truth-telling vs. lying.
6. **Contrast type:** constructional
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## OPPOSITION 5

4. **English:** "you are speaking the truth" / "you are lying"
5. **Description of contrast:** Parallel accusative + speech-verb (ambob) frames documented in P2b #4 and #11 / P3 Group 2, contrasting an affirmation of truth-telling against an accusation of lying.

7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## OPPOSITION 6

## OPPOSITION 11

1. **Term A:** მართალი ხარ
2. **Term B:** სწორი ხარ
3. **Transliteration:** martali xar / sc̣ori xar
4. **English:** "you are right" / "you are correct"
5. **Description of contrast:** P2b #4–5, P3 Group 1, and P4a #1 explicitly distinguish these same-slot attribution frames: მართალი is "more moral/propositional," სწორი "more factual/accuracy-based." This is a contrast in evaluative dimension rather than polarity.
6. **Contrast type:** constructional
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## OPPOSITION 12

1. **Term A:** მართალი
2. **Term B:** სწორი

5. **Description of contrast:** P1 #5 explicitly contrasts სწორი as "less moral than მართალი, more about accuracy and conformity to a standard" — a lexical contrast in the type of "rightness" (moral vs. accuracy), not a true/false polarity.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## OPPOSITION 14

3. **Transliteration:** č̣ešmariti / č̣ešmariteba vs. simartle

A register/depth contrast, not a polarity.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## OPPOSITION 15

1. **Term A:** ცრუ-
2. **Term B:** ტყუ-
3. **Transliteration:** cru- / ṭq̇u-
4. **English:** moral/abstract falsehood / concrete act of lying
5. **Description of contrast:** P1b and P3 Group 8 explicitly distinguish the two falsehood-pole roots: ცრუ- = moral/abstract falsity (often religious/legal), ტყუ- = the everyday concrete act of lying. A lexical (intra-pole) contrast, not an opposition of polarity.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## OPPOSITION 16

1. **Term A:** მსგავსი / მსგავსება
2. **Term B:** გასხვავებული (gansxvavebuli)
3. **Transliteration:** msgavsi / msgavseba vs. gansxvavebuli
4. **English:** similar/resemblance / dissimilar/different
5. **Description of contrast:** P2a (#16 note) explicitly states that the antonym of მსგავსი ("dissimilar") is "normally expressed with a distinct root (gansxvavebuli), not by derivation from msgavsi." The opposition is real but lexically suppletive (no productive privative from msgavs-).
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

## OPPOSITION 17

1. **Term A:** Causative/agentive at the falsehood pole (e.g., გაყალბება, მოტყუება, მატყუარა)
2. **Term B:** (absence of positive counterpart)
3. **Transliteration:** gaq̇albeba, moṭq̇ueba, maṭq̇uara / —
4. **English:** "to falsify / to deceive / liar" / (no lexicalized "make-true" or "truth-teller")
5. **Description of contrast:** P4a/P4b #5 and #7 explicitly record an asymmetric opposition: causative ("make false/forge") and agentive ("liar/forger/false witness") formations are robust at the negative pole but have no attested positive counterpart; truth-telling is expressed only phrasally (simartlis tkma).
6. **Contrast type:** morphological
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## OPPOSITION 18

1. **Term A:** უტყუარი
2. **Term B:** ტყუილი / ცრუ
3. **Transliteration:** uṭq̇uari / ṭq̇uili, cru
4. **English:** lacking falsehood / unmistakable, infallible vs. lie, false
5. **Description of contrast:** P1 #15 and P1b derive უტყუარი from u- (privative) + ṭq̇u- ("lie") + -ari, i.e., "lacking falsehood" — a morphological negation of the lie-root, standing opposed to the falsehood-pole terms built on ṭq̇u-/cru-.
6. **Contrast type:** morphological
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## OPPOSITION 19

1. **Term A:** დამაჯერებელი
2. **Term B:** სანდო
3. **Transliteration:** damaǰerebeli / sando
4. **English:** convincing/credible (causes belief) / trustworthy (reliable source)
5. **Description of contrast:** P3 Group 10 explicitly distinguishes დამაჯერებელი (whether something *causes belief*, irrespective of actual truth) from სანდო (reliability of a *source*). A lexical contrast in evaluative target, not polarity.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

## SUMMARY NOTES

- **Polarity oppositions (true/false, genuine/fake):** Oppositions 1–6 form the core polarity contrasts of the field (authenticity, truth/falsehood, statement-level right/false), realized both lexically and constructionally.
- **Morphological (ara- privative) negations:** Oppositions 7–9 are uniform in mechanism but explicitly **not uniformly available** across the field (only reliability/honesty items); two are [MARGINAL].
- **Morphological privatives on the lie-root:** Opposition 18 (უტყუარი) is a distinct privative formation; Opposition 17 records a documented **morphological asymmetry** (negative-pole causatives/agentives without positive counterparts).
- **Dimension contrasts (not polarity):** Oppositions 11–15 and 19 are explicitly documented usage distinctions *within* the positive field (moral vs. accuracy, credibility vs. reliability, elevated vs. everyday truth, abstract vs. concrete falsehood), not true/false oppositions.
- **Suppletive antonymy:** Opposition 16 is explicitly noted as expressed by a distinct root rather than by derivation; preserved as [MARGINAL].
- No oppositions were invented; all are grounded in explicit CONTEXT statements. Asymmetries (especially the falsehood-pole-only causative/agentive formations) are preserved, not balanced.
