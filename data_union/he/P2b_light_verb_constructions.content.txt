# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2b Compositional Constructions
# Language: Hebrew (he)
# Layer: P2b_light_verb_constructions

---

**Methodological note:** The following documents attested multi-word constructions involving P1 lexical items. Constructions are listed per P1 base form. Derivational forms already documented in P2a are excluded. Where a construction was already documented in P1b as a fixed phrase, it is cross-referenced rather than re-described in full. Absence of common constructions is recorded explicitly. Register variation is noted where relevant. No constructions have been generated to fill expected semantic roles; only patterns supported by attested usage are included. The distinction between a fixed compound (P2a territory) and a multi-word construction (P2b territory) is applied as follows: forms that have been lexicalized into a single orthographic unit or that function as a single lexical item are treated as P2a; forms that remain syntactically separable multi-word units, even if conventionalized, are treated as P2b. Borderline cases are noted.

---

## P1 Item 1 — אֱמֶת (*ʾemet*)

---

**1.1**
**Base Form:** אֱמֶת (*ʾemet*)
**Example:** אֱמֶת וֶאֱמוּנָה
**Transliteration:** *ʾemet ve-ʾemuna*
**English translation:** truth and faithfulness; in truth and in faith
**Usage notes:** A fixed liturgical and legal phrase affirming both factual truth and sincere intent. Already documented in P1b (Root א-מ-נ, item 14); cross-referenced here.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**1.2**
**Base Form:** אֱמֶת (*ʾemet*)
**Example:** אֱמֶת וָשָׁלוֹם
**Transliteration:** *ʾemet va-shalom*
**English translation:** truth and peace
**Usage notes:** A biblical and liturgical phrase (Zechariah 8:19) describing the tension and complementarity between strict truth-telling and social harmony. Already documented in P1b (Root א-מ-ת, item 17); cross-referenced here.
**Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL] — primarily religious/traditional register

**1.23**
**Base Form:** אֱמֶת (*ʾemet*)
**Example:** לְדַבֵּר אֱמֶת
**Transliteration:** *ledabber ʾemet*
**English translation:** to speak the truth, to tell the truth
**Usage notes:** The standard periphrastic construction for truth-telling, combining the verb *ledabber* ("to speak") with *ʾemet* as a direct object. The most neutral and common way to express truth-telling in Hebrew. Used across all registers in moral evaluation, legal testimony contexts, and everyday speech, e.g., *tsarikh ledaber ʾemet* ("one must tell the truth").
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

**1.24**
**Base Form:** אֱמֶת (*ʾemet*)
**Example:** לוֹמַר אֶת הָאֱמֶת
**Transliteration:** *lomar et ha-ʾemet*
**English translation:** to say the truth, to tell the truth
**Usage notes:** A near-synonym of *ledabber ʾemet* using the verb *lomar* ("to say") with the definite direct object *ha-ʾemet*. The definite article marks the truth as a specific, known truth rather than truth in the abstract. Slightly more colloquial than *ledabber ʾemet* and more common in everyday spoken Hebrew.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]
