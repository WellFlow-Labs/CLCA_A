## Summary Notes

- **[CORE]** ratings: Constructions 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 9, 10, and 11 are rated [CORE], as are the Hifʿil causative forms לְהַטְעוֹת, לְהַאֲמִין, and לְאַשֵּׁר (Construction 5), and the agentive דַּיְקָן and מְזַיֵּף (Construction 7). These are well-attested, idiomatic, and unrestricted or only register-restricted in predictable ways.
- **[MARGINAL]** ratings: לְהַצְדִּיק (Construction 5, Causative) is rated [MARGINAL] due to uneven root coverage across the domain; the Hif'il causative/declarative is productive only for roots with an attested Hif'il in this domain, and coverage is therefore uneven. שַׁקְרָן (Construction 7, Agentive Nominalization via *-an*) is rated [MARGINAL] due to its restriction to a single lexical item within the domain for the *-an* pattern proper (no parallel *-an* form for truth-telling is in common use), and מַאֲמִין is rated [MARGINAL] as an alternate agentive form.
- **No [UNCERTAIN]** ratings are assigned: all constructions are supported by attested examples and consistent descriptive notes.
- The most pragmatically extended items are **בֶּאֱמֶת** (discourse particle functions) and **נָכוֹן שֶׁ...** (concessive frame), both of which have moved beyond fully compositional interpretation in attested usage.
- The primary asymmetry in the dataset is in **Agentive Nominalization** (Construction 7): the *-an* pattern is productive for negative characterological agents (שַׁקְרָן) but lacks a lexicalized positive counterpart; positive agentive meaning is expressed periphrastically.
- The grammaticalization of *bediyyuq* relative to other members of the adverbial truth-framing set is the only case of differential entrenchment noted within a single construction type.
- No constructions have been added or modified beyond those attested in the probe inputs.
