Below is a construction-focused inventory using the P1 base forms. I exclude simple derivational forms already handled in P2a, and I include only common or clearly attested multi-word constructions. Where I do not identify a common construction worth listing, I state that explicitly.

## 1. Base Form: **진실**

### a. Example
**진실을 밝히다**  
**Transliteration:** jinsireul balkhida  
**English translation:** reveal the truth  
**Usage notes:** Very common in journalism, law, and public discourse. It is used when hidden or disputed facts are brought to light.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### b. Example
**진실을 말하다**  
**Transliteration:** jinsireul malhada  
**English translation:** tell the truth  
**Usage notes:** Common in everyday and formal contexts alike. It directly contrasts with lying, concealment, or evasion.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### c. Example
**진실을 숨기다**  
**Transliteration:** jinsireul sumgida  
**English translation:** hide the truth  
**Usage notes:** Common in reporting, interpersonal conflict, and institutional criticism. It implies deliberate concealment of what is really the case.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### d. Example
**진실이 드러나다**  
**Transliteration:** jinsiri deureonada  
**English translation:** the truth comes out  
**Usage notes:** Frequently used when concealed facts become publicly known. Common in news, investigations, and dramatic narration.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### e. Example
**진실 공방**  
**Transliteration:** jinsil gongbang  
**English translation:** dispute over the truth  
**Usage notes:** Common in journalism and public controversy. It refers to a clash between parties over what really happened.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]
