# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P5 Oppositions
# Language: Georgian (ka)
# Layer: P5_oppositions

---

**Methodological note:** The following oppositions are extracted exclusively from patterns documented in P1, P1b, P2a, P2b, P3, and P4. No opposition has been inferred from general linguistic knowledge or projected from English or other languages. Where the data supports multiple contrasts for a single term, each is listed separately. Asymmetries in the data are preserved without normalization. Where the documentation does not support a clear opposition, this is stated explicitly.

## Opposition 1

1. **Term A:** სიმართლე / simartle
2. **Term B:** სიცრუე / sicrue
3. **Transliteration:** simartle / sicrue
4. **English translation:** truth / lie, falsehood
5. **Description of contrast:** P3 explicitly states that sicrue "contrasts directly with simartle" and both are described as the primary everyday words for their respective concepts in moral and legal discourse. The parallel constructions simarṭle tqva ("told the truth") and sicrue tqva ("told a lie") are documented in P2b as directly parallel verb-object constructions, and simarṭlis tqma / sicruis tqma are documented as parallel genitive nominals in P2a and P1b. The paired formula sicrue da mot'q̇ueba (P2b) and the construction sicrues ambobs ("is telling a lie") stand in direct opposition to simartles vambob ("I am telling the truth") documented in P2b.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 2

1. **Term A:** სიმართლე / simartle
2. **Term B:** სიყალბე / siq̇albe
3. **Transliteration:** simartle / siq̇albe
4. **English translation:** truth, correctness / falseness, falsehood, inauthenticity, fraud
5. **Description of contrast:** P1 explicitly identifies siq̇albe as the antonym of simartle. Both are abstract nouns formed by the si-...-e circumfix on their respective roots (mart- and q̇alb-), making the opposition morphologically parallel as well as lexically established. P1 item #11 explicitly states that siq̇albe "contrasts directly with simartle." The two nominals occupy opposite poles of the truth/falsity dimension and appear in parallel constructional slots (e.g., simartle gamarjvebs vs. siq̇albe gamoašk'aravda). Both are placed in overlapping but distinct groups in P3 (Group 3 for simartle, Group 1 for siq̇albe), reflecting that their primary contexts of use differ even as they form a structural pair.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical; morphological (parallel si-...-e formations on contrasting roots)
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

Both terms are used in moral discourse about honest speech, though č̣ešmariteba carries a more elevated register than simartle in the same opposition.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [UNCERTAIN] (the construction itself is rated [UNCERTAIN] in P2b)

## Opposition 6

1. **Term A:** ნამდვილი / namdvili
2. **Term B:** ყალბი / q̇albi
3. **Transliteration:** namdvili / q̇albi
4. **English translation:** genuine, real, authentic / false, fake, counterfeit, forged
5. **Description of contrast:** P2b documents the directly parallel constructions ნამდვილი აღმოჩნდა (namdvili aġmočnda, "turned out to be genuine") and ყალბი აღმოჩნდა (q̇albi aġmočnda, "turned out to be fake/forged") using the same inchoative verb, establishing them as the primary adjectival opposition in authentication contexts. P3 notes that namdvili appears across Groups 1, 5, and 6 while q̇albi appears across Groups 1 and 5, confirming their parallel distribution. P1, P1b, P2b, and P3 consistently treat namdvili and q̇albi as the central opposing adjectives in this domain. The contrast is documented across parallel collocations: namdvili oqro ("real gold") vs. q̇albi oqro ("fake gold"); namdvili megobari ("true friend") vs. q̇albi ɣimili ("false smile"). P3 Group 5 explicitly identifies q̇albi and p'ik't'iuri as the opposites of namdvili and avt'enturi. P1 item #5 describes namdvili as asserting genuine character "as opposed to a copy, imitation, or pretense," which is precisely the domain of q̇albi.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 9

2. **Term B:** მცდარი / mcdari

4. **English translation:** false, fake, counterfeit, forged (implying deliberate deception) / erroneous, mistaken, wrong, incorrect (honest error)

Both terms can be applied to statements or conclusions that are not true, but they carry different implications about the agent's responsibility. The documentation notes this distinction explicitly in the entry for mcdari.

## Opposition 10

1. **Term A:** ნამდვილობა / namdviloba
2. **Term B:** გაყალბება / gaq̇albeba
3. **Transliteration:** namdviloba / gaq̇albeba
4. **English translation:** authenticity, genuineness / falsification, forgery (the act)
5. **Description of contrast:** In legal and administrative contexts, these two nominals represent the opposing outcomes of document verification: namdviloba is the property being confirmed (ნამდვილობის დადასტურება, "confirmation of authenticity"), while gaq̇albeba is the criminal act that produces a counterfeit. The contrast is between a positive property and a negating act directed against that property.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 11

1. **Term A:** სარწმუნო / sarc̣muno
2. **Term B:** დამაჯერებელი / damajerebeliˈ
3. **Transliteration:** sarc̣muno / damajerebeliˈ
4. **English translation:** credible, reliable, trustworthy (inherently) / convincing, persuasive (in effect)
5. **Description of contrast:** P1 explicitly distinguishes these two terms within the same semantic domain: damajerebeliˈ is "slightly more focused on the effect of persuasion than sarc̣muno, which emphasizes inherent trustworthiness." P3 places both in Group 3 but notes this distinction. This is not a polar opposition but a contrast of orientation within the credibility domain — one term characterizes a source's intrinsic property, the other characterizes the effect produced on an audience.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical (distinction of evaluative orientation, not a polarity)
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 12

1. **Term A:** სარწმუნო / sarwmuno
2. **Term B:** სანდო / sando
3. **Transliteration:** sarwmuno / sando
4. **English translation:** credible, reliable, trustworthy (formal/legal) / trustworthy, reliable, dependable (colloquial)
5. **Description of contrast:** P1 and P3 consistently distinguish these two terms: sarwmuno emphasizes that a source merits belief as an intrinsic property, while sando may carry a stronger interpersonal connotation of personal trust. P1 item #15 describes sando as "somewhat more colloquial than sarcmuno" and as tending "to emphasize practical reliability over epistemic credibility." P3 Group 6 notes that "sarcmuno and sando in source-evaluation contexts are more colloquial and journalistic than their appearances in legal proceedings." The two are near-synonyms differentiated by register and by the dimension of reliability they foreground (epistemic credibility for sarcmuno, practical dependability for sando). This is a contrast of nuance within a shared semantic space rather than a polarity.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical (near-synonymous distinction, not a polarity)
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 13

2. **Term B:** კანონიერი / kanonieri
3. **Transliteration:** legitimuri / kanonieri
4. **English translation:** legitimate, lawful, valid (with possible normative/moral dimension) / lawful, legal, rightful (tied to positive law)

6. **Contrast type:** lexical (distinction of scope and normative dimension, not a polarity)

## Opposition 14

2. **Term B:** მოჩვენებითი / močvenebiti

P3 places both in Group 4 (abstract and essential truth vocabulary) and Group 6 (aesthetic genuineness), where the contrast between genuine and merely apparent properties is the central analytical distinction.

## Opposition 15

1. **Term A:** ნამდვილი / namdvili
2. **Term B:** მოჩვენებითი / močvenebiti
3. **Transliteration:** namdvili / močvenebiti
4. **English translation:** genuine, real, actual / apparent, ostensible, illusory
5. **Description of contrast:** P2b documents the construction močvenebiti xasiati aqvs ("it has an ostensible/illusory character") as expressing that something has only the appearance of a property, while namdvili is the standard term for genuine possession of a property. P3 notes that both terms are used in legal discourse to distinguish genuine from merely apparent qualities, and P4a identifies the ostensible/apparent predication pattern as a distinct construction type. P3 Group 9 distinguishes močvenebiti from namdvili by noting that močvenebiti characterizes "something that merely appears to be what it is claimed to be, without actually being so," while namdvili asserts genuine character. P3 also distinguishes močvenebiti from q̇albi by noting that močvenebiti "does not necessarily imply deliberate human deception or legal violation."
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 18

1. **Term A:** ყალბი / q̇albi
2. **Term B:** ფიქტიური / p'ik't'iuri
3. **Transliteration:** q̇albi / p'ik't'iuri
4. **English translation:** false, fake, counterfeit, forged / fictitious, fictional, fabricated, sham
5. **Description of contrast:** P3 Group 4 explicitly distinguishes these two: q̇albi is the native root applying broadly to physical forgery (documents, currency, signatures) and to persons and statements, while p'ik't'iuri is a borrowing with more restricted use in commercial and administrative law (transactions, companies, marriages, employment, addresses). P3 states they "are not interchangeable: p'ik't'iuri garigeba is the standard phrase for a sham transaction, while q̇albi dok'ument'i is the standard phrase for a forged document."

7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 19

The opposition is asymmetric in word class (adjective vs. abstract noun) and in morphological structure (compound adjective vs. compound abstract noun), but the semantic contrast is explicitly documented.

## Opposition 25

1. **Term A:** სარწმუნოება (secular sense) / sarwmunoeba
2. **Term B:** სარწმუნოება (theological sense) / sarwmunoeba
3. **Transliteration:** sarwmunoeba / sarwmunoeba
4. **English translation:** credibility, reliability, trustworthiness / faith, religion
5. **Description of contrast:** This is an internal semantic opposition within a single form. P1, P2a, P3, and P2b all document the bifurcation of sarwmunoeba across two distinct registers: in secular contexts it denotes credibility/reliability; in theological and ecclesiastical contexts the dominant meaning shifts to faith or religion. P3 notes: "These are not merely polysemous uses of the same word but distinct registers with distinct collocational patterns." The contrast is between two register-conditioned meanings of the same form rather than between two distinct forms.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical (register-conditioned semantic bifurcation within a single form)
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]
