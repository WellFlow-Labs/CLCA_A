# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P4a CONSTRUCTIONS
# Language: Chinese (zh)

---

## 1. ATTRIBUTION

### Pattern
Subject + (是) + truth-domain adjective/stative verb + (的)

The copula 是 is optional when the predicate adjective directly follows the subject; 的 nominalizes or confirms the predication. The canonical frame is: NP + 是 + ADJ + 的.

### Examples
- 这是真的。/ Zhè shì zhēn de. / "This is true / genuine."
- 这份报告是真实的。/ Zhè fèn bàogào shì zhēnshí de. / "This report is truthful / accurate."

### Notes
The copula 是 + 的 frame is the default predicate construction for attributing truth-domain properties to a subject. Negation is formed by inserting 不 before 是: 这不是真的 "this is not true." The adjective alone (without 是…的) can also serve as predicate in more formal registers: 报道失实 "the report is inaccurate."

---

## 2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION

### Pattern
Subject + 不是 + truth-domain adjective/stative verb + 的  
OR  
Subject + 并非 + truth-domain adjective/stative verb (formal)

### Examples
- 这不是真的。/ Zhè bù shì zhēn de. / "This is not true / not genuine."
- 该陈述并非属实。/ Gāi chénshù bìngfēi shǔshí. / "The statement is not confirmed as factual."

### Notes
不是…的 is the standard spoken negation of the attribution frame. 并非 is a formal/written variant that directly negates the predicate without the 是…的 frame. For stative verbs that already carry negative polarity (失实, 错误), the negative attribution is typically expressed by their positive antonyms (真实, 正确) rather than by negating the negative form.

---

## 3. ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION

### Pattern
Truth-domain adjective/stative verb + 的 + Noun

The particle 的 links a modifying element to the head noun. Monosyllabic modifiers (真, 假) may appear without 的 in fixed compounds; disyllabic and longer modifiers require 的.

### Examples
- 真实的情况 / zhēnshí de qíngkuàng / "the true / actual situation"
- 虚假的广告 / xūjiǎ de guǎnggào / "false / fraudulent advertising"

### Notes
This is the standard Chinese attributive construction: modifier + 的 + head noun. Four-character expressions (成语) used adnominally also take 的: 名副其实的专家 "a genuine expert (one who lives up to the title)"; 货真价实的好东西 "something that is genuinely what it claims to be." The modifier precedes the noun in all cases; there is no postpositive modification in Chinese.

---

## 4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE

### Pattern
Verb/process + 成 + truth-domain noun  
OR  
Truth-domain adjective + 化 (suffix, "to become X / to make X")

### Examples
- 弄假成真 / nòng jiǎ chéng zhēn / "what started as a pretense becomes reality" (lit. make-false become-true)
- 合法化 / héfǎhuà / "to legalize / to become lawful" (lit. lawful + -ize)

### Notes
Chinese lacks a dedicated inchoative morpheme equivalent to English *become*. Two productive patterns cover this domain: (a) the resultative complement 成 ("become, turn into") in verb-complement constructions such as 弄假成真; (b) the suffix 化 attached to stative bases to form verbs meaning "to become X" or "to make X," as in 合法化 (legalize), 道德化 (moralize), 真实化 (make realistic). The 化 pattern is productive but not uniformly available across all truth-domain bases (e.g., *真化, *假化 are not attested).

---

## 5. CAUSATIVE

### Pattern
(no common expression identified)

Chinese does not have a dedicated causative morpheme for truth-domain predicates. Causative meanings are expressed periphrastically: 使/让 + NP + V ("cause NP to V"), or through verb-object constructions such as 证实 (to cause something to be confirmed), 核实 (to cause something to be verified). These are not structurally distinct from ordinary transitive constructions and do not constitute a separate causative pattern in this domain.

---

## 6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION

### Pattern
Stative verb / adjective + 性 (suffix, nominalizes to abstract property noun)

### Examples
- 准确性 / zhǔnquèxìng / "accuracy, the property of being precise"
- 可靠性 / kěkàoxìng / "reliability, the property of being dependable"

### Notes
The suffix 性 is the primary productive nominalizer for abstract properties in this domain, applying most freely to disyllabic bases: 真实性 (truthfulness/authenticity), 正确性 (correctness), 合法性 (legality/legitimacy), 违法性 (illegality), 欺骗性 (deceptiveness), 实用性 (utility). Application to monosyllabic bases is restricted and often yields technical rather than general abstract nouns (真性, 假性 function as medical classifiers rather than free abstract nouns). The suffix 主义 forms ideological nominalizations in a narrower set: 实用主义 (pragmatism), 写实主义 (realism).

## 7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION

### Pattern
Verb + 者 (suffix, "one who does X")

### Examples
- 伪造者 / wěizàozhě / "forger, counterfeiter" (one who fabricates)
- 欺骗者 / qīpiànzhě / "deceiver, cheat" (one who deceives)

### Notes
The suffix 者 is the standard formal agentive nominalizer in Chinese, attaching to verbal bases. In the truth domain it is productive where the base describes a deliberate human action (伪造, 欺骗). For the positive pole (truth-teller), no single agentive nominalization is in common use: the concept is expressed periphrastically (说真话的人 "a person who tells the truth") or by character-evaluation adjectives (诚实的人 "an honest person"). The colloquial agentive for the deception pole is 骗子 (piànzi), a suffixed form with 子 rather than 者, preferred in informal registers over 欺骗者.

## 8. VERIFICATION / CONFIRMATION CONSTRUCTION

### Pattern
经 + verification verb + (result): "upon/through [process], [outcome confirmed]"

### Examples
- 经查属实 / jīng chá shǔshí / "verified upon investigation; confirmed as factual after inquiry"
- 经调查证实 / jīng diàochá zhèngshí / "confirmed by investigation"

### Notes
This construction is specific to Chinese administrative, legal, and journalistic registers. 经 ("through, upon") introduces the verification process; the result (属实, 证实, 核实) follows directly. The pattern is highly conventionalized: 经核实, 经查实, 经调查证实 are fixed formulas for asserting that a claim has been formally checked. The negative counterpart uses 无法: 无法核实 "cannot be verified."

---

## 9. ADVERBIAL TRUTH-FRAMING CONSTRUCTION

### Pattern
Truth-domain adverb / fixed adverbial phrase + Verb phrase: manner of speaking or acting "as the facts are"

### Examples
- 如实陈述 / rúshí chénshù / "to state truthfully; to give a truthful account" (lit. as-reality state)
- 实事求是地说 / shíshì qiúshì de shuō / "to say it as it actually is; to speak empirically and honestly"

### Notes
Chinese has a productive class of truth-domain adverbials that modify speech and reporting verbs: 如实 (as reality is), 实话实说 (say it straight), 坦白说 (frankly speaking). These are not derivationally related to the attribution or nominalization patterns above; they form a distinct constructional type in which the truth-domain element specifies the manner of a communicative act. The particle 地 links longer adverbial phrases to the verb (实事求是地说); shorter adverbs (如实) attach directly without 地.

---

## 10. PROHIBITION CONSTRUCTION

### Pattern
严禁 / 不得 + truth-domain violation verb: formal prohibition of deceptive or falsifying conduct

### Examples
- 严禁弄虚作假 / yánjìn nòng xū zuò jiǎ / "it is strictly forbidden to engage in fraud and falsification"
- 不得欺骗消费者 / bùdé qīpiàn xiāofèizhě / "it is prohibited to deceive consumers"

### Notes
This construction is characteristic of Chinese legal, regulatory, and institutional discourse. 严禁 (strictly prohibit) and 不得 (must not / it is not permitted to) are the standard formal prohibition markers; they combine productively with truth-domain violation verbs (弄虚作假, 欺骗, 伪造, 作假). The construction has no direct positive counterpart in the same register; positive obligations to be truthful are expressed differently (如实陈述, 诚实守信).
