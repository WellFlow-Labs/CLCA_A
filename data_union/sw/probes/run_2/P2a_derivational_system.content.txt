# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2a Derivational System
# Language: Swahili (sw)

---

## Scope and Method

This step documents attested derivational morphology for the 30 lexical items identified in P1. Each P1 item is treated as a potential base. Derivations are recorded only where the derived form is genuinely used or strongly supported. Paradigm gaps are preserved. Where no derivation is identified, this is stated explicitly. Forms already present in P1 or P1b are cross-referenced rather than re-described.

### P1 Item 1: **kweli**

---

**Derived Form 1**

1. **Base Form:** kweli
2. **Derived Form:** ukweli
3. **Transliteration:** ukweli
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *u-* (class 11 abstract prefix) + *kweli*
5. **Natural English Translation:** truth; truthfulness; veracity
6. **Usage:** The standard abstract nominalization of *kweli*, used in philosophical, legal, and moral discourse to refer to the property or state of being true. Already documented in P1 (Item 2) and P1b (Root 1, Item 2); cross-referenced here.
7. **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2**

1. **Base Form:** kweli
2. **Derived Form:** kweli kweli
3. **Transliteration:** kweli kweli
4. **Literal Breakdown:** full reduplication of *kweli*
5. **Natural English Translation:** truly; really; genuinely
6. **Usage:** Reduplicated intensifier used to emphasize the sincerity or accuracy of an assertion. Already documented in P1 (Item 12) and P1b (Root 1, Item 3); cross-referenced here.
7. **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Note on further derivation:** No agentive nominal (*mweli*, *mkweli*) meaning "truth-teller" is in common use as a lexicalized form derived directly from *kweli*. The function of "one who tells the truth" is expressed periphrastically (*mtu anayesema kweli*). No verbal derivation from *kweli* itself (e.g., *kukweli*) is attested as a productive form.

*ukweli* is itself a derivation of *kweli* (see above). No further productive derivation from *ukweli* as a base is attested. The form participates in numerous collocations (documented in P1b) but these are not derivational.

### P1 Item 3: **uongo**

---

**Derived Form 1**

1. **Base Form:** uongo
2. **Derived Form:** mdanganyifu / muongo
3. **Transliteration:** muongo
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *m-* (class 1 person prefix) + *uongo* (with phonological adjustment: *u-* of the noun class prefix absorbed into *m-* agentive prefix)
5. **Natural English Translation:** a liar; a person who tells lies
6. **Usage:** The agentive nominal for a person who habitually tells lies. Used in moral condemnation and character evaluation across spoken and written registers (*Yeye ni muongo* "He/she is a liar").
7. **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2**

1. **Base Form:** uongo
2. **Derived Form:** waongo
3. **Transliteration:** waongo
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *wa-* (class 2 plural prefix) + *ongo* (the root, with *u-* class prefix dropped in plural)
5. **Natural English Translation:** liars; people who tell lies
6. **Usage:** The plural of *muongo*. Used in moral and social discourse to refer to a group of persons evaluated as dishonest.
7. **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 3**

1. **Base Form:** uongo
2. **Derived Form:** kuongea uongo / kusema uongo
3. **Transliteration:** kusema uongo
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *ku-* (infinitive) + *sema* (to say) + *uongo* (falsehood)
5. **Natural English Translation:** to tell lies; to speak falsely
6. **Usage:** The standard verbal phrase for the act of lying. Not a morphological derivation from *uongo* itself but the primary verbal expression of the concept; included here as the closest functional verbal equivalent.
7. **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Note:** A direct verbal derivation *kuuonga* or *kuonga* meaning "to lie" is not attested as a standard productive form. The verbal function is carried by *kudanganya* (P1 Item 22) and the periphrastic *kusema uongo*.

2. **Derived Form:** kukosekana
