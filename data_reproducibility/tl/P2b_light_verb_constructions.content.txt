# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2b Compositional Constructions
# Language: Tagalog (tl)

## MULTI-WORD CONSTRUCTIONS

### BASE: **totoo** (true, real)

#### Construction 1
- **Base Form:** totoo
- **Example:** totoo ba yan?
- **Transliteration:** totoo ba yan
- **English Translation:** Is that true? / Is that for real?
- **Usage Notes:** Standard question form seeking confirmation of truth claims. Extremely common in everyday conversation when expressing doubt or seeking verification.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

#### Construction 2
- **Base Form:** totoo
- **Example:** totoo nga
- **Transliteration:** totoo nga
- **English Translation:** it's really true / indeed it is
- **Usage Notes:** Emphatic confirmation using particle "nga." Very common for strong affirmation of truth.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

#### Construction 3
- **Base Form:** totoo
- **Example:** hindi totoo
- **Transliteration:** hindi totoo
- **English Translation:** not true / false / unreal
- **Usage Notes:** Standard full negation form. More emphatic than contracted "di-totoo."
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

#### Construction 4
- **Base Form:** totoo
- **Example:** sa totoo lang
- **Transliteration:** sa totoo lang
- **English Translation:** to be honest / truthfully / actually
- **Usage Notes:** Fixed conversational phrase introducing frank or honest statements. Extremely common discourse marker for emphasizing sincerity.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

#### Construction 5
- **Base Form:** totoo
- **Example:** totoo naman
- **Transliteration:** totoo naman
- **English Translation:** it's true though / actually it is true
- **Usage Notes:** Concessive acknowledgment using particle "naman." Common in argumentative discourse when acknowledging a point before contrasting.
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

#### Construction 6

- **Transliteration:** kung totoo

## SUMMARY OBSERVATIONS

### Construction Patterns:

1. **Question Formation:**
   - Extremely productive with particle "ba": totoo ba, tama ba, mali ba, sigurado ka ba, etc.
   - Very common pattern for seeking confirmation or expressing doubt

2. **Emphatic Particles:**
   - "nga" (emphatic): totoo nga, tunay nga, talaga nga
   - "naman" (concessive): totoo naman, tama naman
   - "pa" (still/even): totoo pa
   - "rin/din" (also): totoo rin, tama rin, katulad din
   - "lang" (only/just): totoo lang, tama lang, peke lang, talaga lang

3. **Negation Patterns:**
   - "hindi" + base: hindi totoo, hindi tama, hindi mali, hindi tapat
   - "walang" + nominalized form: walang katotohanan, walang katapatan, walang katulad

4. **Conditional Constructions:**
   - "kung" + base: kung totoo, kung tama, kung mali ako
   - Very common for expressing hypotheticals and uncertainty

5. **Fixed Expressions:**
   - "sa totoo lang" (to be honest)
   - "tama na" (that's enough)
   - "kathang-isip" (figment of imagination)
   - "tamang-tama" (exactly right)
   - "tapat sa salita" (true to one's word)
   - "tunay na kalikasan" (true nature)

6. **Comparison Constructions:**
   - "katulad mo/ng" (like you/of)
   - "kamukha mo/ng" (looks like you/of)
   - "kawangis niya" (resembles him/her)

7. **Imperative Constructions:**
   - "magtapat ka" (be honest)
   - "itama mo" (correct it)
   - "iwasto mo" (rectify it)
   - "magsigurado ka" (make sure)
   - "huwag kang magsinungaling" (don't lie)

8. **Possession/Attribution:**
   - "ang" + base + possessive: ang katotohanan ay, tama ang sagot, mali ang akala ko
   - Very productive pattern for attributing properties

9. **Asymmetries:**
   - Positive truth terms show more diverse constructions than negative terms
   - "totoo" and "tama" show particularly rich construction patterns
   - "talaga" functions primarily in emphatic/confirmatory constructions
   - Borrowed terms (peke, sigurado) show fewer idiomatic constructions

10. **Register Variation:**
    - Formal: wasto, diwa, sa katotohanan
    - Colloquial: totoo ba yan, tama na yan, peke lang
    - Some constructions span registers: hindi totoo, kung tama
