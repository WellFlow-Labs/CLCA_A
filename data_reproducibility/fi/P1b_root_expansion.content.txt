# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P1b ROOT EXPANSION
# Language: Finnish (fi)

## Step 1 — Identify Central Roots

### Root 1: **tosi / tode-**
**Form:** tosi (independent form), tode- (bound stem form)
**Participates in:** 9 P1 items directly (totuus, todellinen, todenmukaisuus, epätosi, todistaa, todiste, todenperäinen, todentaa, plus the root tosi itself)
**Core meaning contribution:** Correspondence with reality; the quality of being factually accurate or existing in actuality. This root forms the primary semantic core of truth-evaluation in Finnish.

### Root 2: **oikea / oikee-**
**Form:** oikea (adjective), oikee- (bound stem)
**Participates in:** 3 P1 items (oikea, oikeellinen, paikkansapitävä contains related concept)
**Core meaning contribution:** Correctness, rightness, genuineness. Polysemous between "correct/accurate" and "right" (directional/moral), with extensions to "genuine/real."

### Root 3: **väärä / väär-**
**Form:** väärä (adjective), väär- (bound stem)
**Participates in:** 2 P1 items (väärä, vääristää)
**Core meaning contribution:** Incorrectness, falsity, wrongness. Primary antonym to oikea, marking deviation from correctness or truth.

## Step 2 — Expand Each Root

### ROOT 1: tosi / tode-

#### 1. tosiasiallinen
**Form:** tosiasiallinen
**Literal breakdown:** tosi-asia-llinen (true-matter-ADJ)
**Translation:** actual, factual, de facto
**Context:** Used to describe what actually exists or occurs in practice, as opposed to theoretical or nominal status. Common in legal, administrative, and analytical contexts (*tosiasiallinen tilanne* "actual situation," *tosiasiallinen valta* "de facto power").
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 2. tosiasiassa
**Form:** tosiasiassa
**Literal breakdown:** tosi-asia-ssa (true-matter-INESS)
**Translation:** in fact, actually, as a matter of fact
**Context:** Adverbial expression used to introduce or emphasize factual information, often contrasting with appearances or assumptions. Very common in both spoken and written Finnish (*tosiasiassa hän oli oikeassa* "in fact, he was right").
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 3. tosiasia
**Form:** tosiasia
**Literal breakdown:** tosi-asia (true-matter)
**Translation:** fact
**Context:** Noun referring to an established or verifiable piece of information. Fundamental term in factual discourse (*kiistaton tosiasia* "indisputable fact," *tosiasiat puhuvat puolestaan* "the facts speak for themselves").

#### 4. tosielämä
**Form:** tosielämä
**Literal breakdown:** tosi-elämä (true-life)

#### 5. tositarina

**Context:** Describes someone stubbornly adhering to wrong ideas. Used in character evaluation (*vääräpäinen ihminen* "stubborn person").

## Step 3 — Peripheral Extensions

### Extensions of tosi / tode-

**Intensity/degree marking:**
- *oikein* (from oikea root, but note *tosi* also functions as intensifier: *tosi hyvä* "really good")
- *tosissaan* extends from truth-domain to seriousness/earnestness domain
- *tosikko* shifts from truth-evaluation to personality/character type

**Realization/actualization domain:**
- *todellistaa*, *todellistua*, *toteutua*, *toteuttaa* all extend from "being true/real" to "making real/actualizing"
- This represents a shift from epistemic evaluation to ontological transformation

**Media/genre classification:**
- *tosi-tv*, *tositarina*, *tosipohjainen* use truth-marking to classify narrative genres
- Extension from truth-evaluation to genre/representation taxonomy

**Character/authenticity evaluation:**
- *tosimies*, *tosiystävä*, *tosirakkauden* extend from factual truth to authentic character/genuine relationships
- Shift from correspondence-truth to authenticity/genuineness

### Extensions of oikea / oikee-

**Spatial/directional domain:**
- *oikeanpuoleinen*, *oikeakätinen* represent complete semantic divergence to spatial orientation
- No apparent truth-evaluation component in these uses

**Legal/moral domain:**
- *oikeus*, *oikeudenmukaisuus*, *oikeuttaa* extend from correctness to justice/rights
- Shift from epistemic correctness to moral/legal rightness

**Temporal appropriateness:**
- *oikea-aikainen* extends from correctness to temporal appropriateness
- Not clearly truth-related

**Intensification:**
- *oikein* functions as degree intensifier (*oikein hyvä* "really good")
- Shift from correctness to emphasis/degree

### Extensions of väärä / väär-

**Physical/anatomical domain:**
- *vääräleuka*, *väärähammas* use "wrong/false" for physical abnormality or artificiality
- Extension from epistemic falsity to physical deviation from norm

**Religious orthodoxy:**
- *vääräuskoinen*, *vääräoppisuus* extend from falsity to religious heterodoxy
- Shift from factual falsity to doctrinal incorrectness

**Character traits:**
- *vääräpäinen* extends from wrongness to stubbornness/obstinacy
- Not clearly truth-related; more about behavioral disposition

**Criminal/legal domain:**
- *väärennös*, *väärentää*, *väärennösrikos* extend from falsity to deliberate deception/fraud
- Maintains connection to falsity but adds intentionality and legal framework
