# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P4b Construction Usage & Constraints
# Language: Hebrew (he)

---

## Construction Usage Table

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Usage Notes | Attested Alternates |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Attribution** | זֶה אֱמֶת | *ze ʾemet* | "That is true / That is the truth" | [CORE] | The zero-copula nominal predicate sentence is the unmarked, default pattern for attribution in Modern Hebrew present tense; used across all registers from colloquial to legal. No restriction on subject type or predicate class within this domain. | הָעֵד נֶאֱמָן (*ha-ʿed neʾeman*, "The witness is trustworthy") |
| **2. Negative Attribution** | זֶה לֹא נָכוֹן | *ze lo nakhon* | "That is not correct" | [CORE] | Fully parallel to affirmative attribution; *lo* is the invariant negator and is unrestricted across registers. No morphological change to the predicate is required. | הָעֵד לֹא מְהֵימָן (*ha-ʿed lo mehiman*, "The witness is not credible") |
| **3. Adnominal Modification** | מָקוֹר מְהֵימָן | *maqor mehiman* | "a reliable source" | [CORE] | Post-nominal adjective agreement is obligatory and the construction is unrestricted across registers; passive participles as modifiers (e.g., *mezuyaf*) are particularly frequent in legal and administrative contexts. | מִסְמָךְ מְזוּיָּף (*mismakh mezuyaf*, "a forged document") |
| **4. Become / Inchoative** | הַמָּקוֹר הָפַךְ לְלֹא אָמִין | *ha-maqor hafakh le-lo ʾamin* | "The source became unreliable" | [CORE] | *hafakh le-* is the general inchoative strategy and is common across registers; *nimtsa* + predicate adjective is more specifically associated with legal and evaluative discourse, recording a change in assessed status. | הַמִּסְמָךְ נִמְצָא מְזוּיָּף (*ha-mismakh nimtsa mezuyaf*, "The document was found to be / turned out to be forged") |
| **5. Causative** | לְהַצְדִּיק | *lehatsddiq* | "to justify / to declare righteous / to vindicate" | [MARGINAL] | The Hif'il causative/declarative is productive only for roots with an attested Hif'il in this domain (e.g., צ-ד-ק, כ-ש-ר); roots such as ד-י-ק and ז-י-פ do not have a productive Hif'il here, and no Hif'il causative is in common use for *ʾemet* or *sheqer* directly. Coverage across the domain is therefore uneven. | לְהַכְשִׁיר (*lehakhshir*, "to certify as fit / to qualify / to render valid") |
| **6. Abstract Nominalization** | אֲמִינוּת | *ʾaminut* | "reliability, credibility" | [CORE] | The *-ut* suffix is the most consistently productive nominalization process across all adjectival bases in this domain and is unrestricted in register; the *ʾi-* prefix on *-ut* nominals to form negated abstracts is attested specifically in formal and legal registers. | אִמּוּת (*immut*, "verification, authentication") |
| **7. Agentive Nominalization** | שַׁקְרָן | *shaqran* | "liar, habitual liar" | [MARGINAL] | The *-an* agentive suffix is attested in this domain only for *shaqran* / *shaqranit*; no parallel *-an* form for truth-telling is in common use. The active participle construction (*dover ʾemet*, *mezayyef*, *medayyeq*) is the general agentive strategy for other items in the domain and is more broadly productive. | מַאֲמִין (*maʾamin*, "believer, one who trusts/believes"); *dover ʾemet* (truth-teller) |
| **8. Adverbial Construction** | בֶּאֱמֶת | *beʾemet* | "truly, really, honestly" | [CORE] | The *be-* + abstract noun pattern is highly productive in this domain and is the primary strategy for adverbializing truth-domain nominals; *beʾemet* in particular has grammaticalized as a sincerity and intensifier marker across colloquial and formal registers alike. | בְּדִיּוּק (*bediyyuq*, "exactly, precisely"); *bekhannot* (sincerely); *leʾamitto shel davar* (in actual fact) |
| **9. Concessive / Epistemic Hedge** | נָכוֹן שֶׁהוּא צוֹדֵק, אֲבָל... | *nakhon she-hu tsodeq, aval...* | "It is true that he is right, but..." | [CORE] | This construction is a discourse-level concessive marker used specifically to manage epistemic stance in argumentation; it is structurally distinct from simple attribution in that it embeds a full clause and is characteristically followed by a counterpoint. Restricted to argumentative and evaluative discourse contexts. | נָכוֹן לְהַגִּיד שֶׁהָיוּ טְעוּיוֹת (*nakhon lehagid she-hayu teʿuyot*); אֱמֶת שֶׁ- (*ʾemet she-*, "it is true that") |

## Summary Notes

- **[CORE]** ratings: Constructions 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 9 are all rated [CORE]. These are well-attested, idiomatic, and unrestricted or only register-restricted in predictable ways.
- **[MARGINAL]** ratings: Constructions 5 and 7 are rated [MARGINAL]. Construction 5 (Causative) is marginal due to uneven root coverage across the domain; Construction 7 (Agentive Nominalization via *-an*) is marginal due to its restriction to a single lexical item (*shaqran*) within the domain.
- **No [UNCERTAIN]** ratings are assigned: all constructions in P4a are supported by attested examples and consistent descriptive notes.
- No constructions have been added or modified beyond those identified in P4a.
