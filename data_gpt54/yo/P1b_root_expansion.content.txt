## Step 1 — Central roots

### 1. **tọ́ / títọ́**
- **Root form:** tọ́ / títọ́
- **Transliteration:** tọ́ / títọ́
- **Participates in:** about **8** P1 items  
  (*òtítọ́, òtọ́, òtítọ́ ni, jẹ́ òtítọ́, kì í ṣe òtítọ́, títọ́, tọ́, kò tọ́*)
- **Core meaning contribution:** rightness, correctness, straightness, truth, propriety

### 2. **rí / ẹ̀rí / ẹlẹ́rìí**
- **Root form:** rí
- **Transliteration:** rí
- **Participates in:** about **2** direct P1 items, with one clear derived-person form  
  (*ẹ̀rí, ẹlẹ́rìí*)
- **Core meaning contribution:** seeing, witnessing, evidential attestation, proof

### 3. **dodo / òdodo**
- **Root form:** dodo
- **Transliteration:** dodo
- **Participates in:** about **2** P1 items  
  (*òdodo, olódodo*)
- **Core meaning contribution:** justice, righteousness, fairness, upright rightness

## Step 2 — Root expansion

### Root: **tọ́ / títọ́**

Note: this root is highly productive. Below are the most common and well-attested expressions; additional forms exist.

1. **òtítọ́**  
   **Transliteration:** òtítọ́  
   **Literal breakdown:** ò- + títọ́  
   **Translation:** truth; correctness  
   **Context:** Common noun for truth in everyday speech, religion, argument, and moral discussion. Used for the truth of statements and the correctness of accounts.  
   **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

2. **òtọ́**  
   **Transliteration:** òtọ́  
   **Literal breakdown:** nominal form from tọ́  
   **Translation:** truth; correctness; genuineness  
   **Context:** Used for what is true or genuine, often overlapping with *òtítọ́*. Also appears in contrasts with falsity or imitation.  
   **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

3. **títọ́**  
   **Transliteration:** títọ́  
   **Literal breakdown:** nominal/adjectival form from tọ́  
   **Translation:** straightness; correctness; truth; properness  
   **Context:** Used literally for straightness and abstractly for correctness or truth. Also appears in evaluative contexts about conduct or procedure.  
   **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

4. **tọ́**  
   **Transliteration:** tọ́  
   **Literal breakdown:** simple verbal/adjectival root  
   **Translation:** be right; be correct; be proper  
   **Context:** Very common predicate in ordinary conversation for factual, practical, or moral rightness.  
   **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

5. **kò tọ́**  
   **Transliteration:** kò tọ́  
   **Literal breakdown:** NEG + tọ́  
   **Translation:** it is not right; it is wrong; it is improper  
   **Context:** Used for moral impropriety, unfairness, or incorrectness. Common in rebuke and evaluation.  
   **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

6. **ó tọ́**  
   **Transliteration:** ó tọ́  
   **Literal breakdown:** it + tọ́  
   **Translation:** it is right; that is correct  
   **Context:** Conversational affirmation that a statement, judgment, or action is right.  
   **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

7. **jẹ́ òtítọ́**  
   **Transliteration:** jẹ́ òtítọ́  
   **Literal breakdown:** be + truth  
   **Translation:** be true  
   **Context:** Used predicatively of claims, reports, rumors, and sometimes identities or appearances.  
   **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

8. **kì í ṣe òtítọ́**  
   **Transliteration:** kì í ṣe òtítọ́  
   **Literal breakdown:** NEG HAB be + truth  
   **Translation:** is not true  
   **Context:** Standard way to deny truth or accuracy without necessarily accusing someone of deliberate lying.  
   **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

9. **òtítọ́ ni**  
   **Transliteration:** òtítọ́ ni  
   **Literal breakdown:** truth + FOC  
   **Translation:** it is true  
   **Context:** Fixed affirming expression in conversation and discourse.  
   **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

10. **ní òtítọ́**  
    **Transliteration:** ní òtítọ́  
    **Literal breakdown:** in/with + truth  
    **Translation:** in truth; truly; indeed  
    **Context:** Used adverbially in speech and writing to mark sincerity or factual truth. Common in formal and literary registers as well as sermons.  
    **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

11. **l’òtítọ́ / ní òtítọ́**  
    **Transliteration:** l’òtítọ́ / ní òtítọ́  
    **Literal breakdown:** in + truth  
    **Translation:** truly; in fact  
    **Context:** A reduced spoken/written form often used to introduce a candid or factual statement.  
    **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

12. **òtítọ́ ọrọ̀**  
    **Transliteration:** òtítọ́ ọrọ̀  
    **Literal breakdown:** truth + matter/word  
    **Translation:** the truth of the matter; the real facts  
    **Context:** Used when distinguishing the actual state of affairs from rumor, appearance, or distortion.  
    **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

13. **sọ òtítọ́**  
    **Transliteration:** sọ òtítọ́  
    **Literal breakdown:** say + truth  
    **Translation:** tell the truth  
    **Context:** Very common in everyday speech, child-rearing, legal testimony, and moral instruction.  
    **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

14. **máa sọ òtítọ́**  
    **Transliteration:** máa sọ òtítọ́  
    **Literal breakdown:** HAB/FUT + say + truth  
    **Translation:** speak truth habitually; tell the truth  
    **Context:** Used in moral exhortation and character evaluation.  
    **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

15. **fẹ́ òtítọ́**  
    **Transliteration:** fẹ́ òtítọ́  
    **Literal breakdown:** love + truth  
    **Translation:** love truth  
    **Context:** Common in religious, philosophical, and moral discourse about valuing honesty and reality.  
    **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

16. **mọ̀ òtítọ́**  
    **Transliteration:** mọ̀ òtítọ́  
    **Literal breakdown:** know + truth  
    **Translation:** know the truth  
    **Context:** Used for coming to know the real facts, often in legal, religious, or interpersonal contexts.  
    **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

17. **fi òtítọ́ hàn**  
    **Transliteration:** fi òtítọ́ hàn  
    **Literal breakdown:** show + truth + visible  
    **Translation:** reveal the truth; show the truth  
    **Context:** Used when evidence, confession, or investigation makes the truth clear.  
    **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

18. **wà ní òtítọ́**  
    **Transliteration:** wà ní òtítọ́  
    **Literal breakdown:** be/exist + in + truth  
    **Translation:** be in the right; be truthful / be in truth  
    **Context:** Used in moral, religious, and argumentative contexts. Depending on context it can refer to correctness or truthful standing.  
    **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

19. **ọ̀nà títọ́**  
    **Transliteration:** ọ̀nà títọ́  
    **Literal breakdown:** path/way + straight/correct  
    **Translation:** straight path; right way  
    **Context:** Used literally for a straight route and figuratively for proper conduct or righteous living.  
    **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

20. **ìtọ́sọ́nà**  
    **Transliteration:** ìtọ́sọ́nà  
    **Literal breakdown:** nominalization + direct/straighten + way  
    **Translation:** guidance; direction; instruction  
    **Context:** Common in administrative, educational, and practical contexts for directions or guidance. Also used figuratively for moral or spiritual guidance.  
    **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

21. **tọ́ka sí**  
    **Transliteration:** tọ́ka sí  
    **Literal breakdown:** point/direct + to  
    **Translation:** point to; refer to; indicate  
    **Context:** Very common in speech, writing, law, and scholarship for reference and indication.  
    **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

22. **àtọ́ka**  
    **Transliteration:** àtọ́ka  
    **Literal breakdown:** nominalization from tọ́ka  
    **Translation:** index; reference; indication  
    **Context:** Used in books, documents, and formal writing for indexing or reference systems.  
    **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

23. **àtọ́kasí**  
    **Transliteration:** àtọ́kasí  
    **Literal breakdown:** nominalization from tọ́ka sí  
    **Translation:** reference; citation; indication  
    **Context:** Common in academic, administrative, and literary contexts for references or citations.  
    **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

24. **tọ́jú**  
    **Transliteration:** tọ́jú  
    **Literal breakdown:** direct/set right + eye/face  
    **Translation:** care for; attend to; treat carefully  
    **Context:** Very common verb for caring for a person, object, patient, or responsibility.  
    **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

25. **ìtọ́jú**  
    **Transliteration:** ìtọ́jú  
    **Literal breakdown:** nominalization from tọ́jú  
    **Translation:** care; treatment; maintenance  
    **Context:** Used in medicine, domestic life, administration, and technical maintenance.  
    **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

26. **tọ́nisọ́nà**  
    **Transliteration:** tọ́nisọ́nà  
    **Literal breakdown:** direct/show + way  
    **Translation:** guide; direct  
    **Context:** Used for physically guiding someone, giving instructions, or directing procedure.  
    **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

27. **olùtọ́nisọ́nà**  
    **Transliteration:** olùtọ́nisọ́nà  
    **Literal breakdown:** agent + guide/direct-way  
    **Translation:** guide; instructor; director  
    **Context:** Used for a person who guides, instructs, or directs, in education, travel, religion, or administration.  
    **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

28. **tọ́nà**  
    **Transliteration:** tọ́nà  
    **Literal breakdown:** direct + way  
    **Translation:** show the way; guide  
    **Context:** Common in practical movement and figurative instruction.  
    **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

29. **títọ́nà**  
    **Transliteration:** títọ́nà  
    **Literal breakdown:** nominalization from tọ́nà  
    **Translation:** guidance; direction  
    **Context:** Used for the act or process of directing someone or something.  
    **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

30. **tọ́kantọ́kan**  
    **Transliteration:** tọ́kantọ́kan  
    **Literal breakdown:** one-by-one / singly  
    **Translation:** one by one; individually  
    **Context:** Common adverb in procedural and descriptive contexts. The form is widely used, though its connection to the truth/correctness sense is peripheral.  
    **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

### Root: **rí / ẹ̀rí**

Note: this root has fewer clearly truth-domain compounds than *tọ́*, but several very common evidential and witness forms are well-attested.

1. **ẹ̀rí**  
   **Transliteration:** ẹ̀rí  
   **Literal breakdown:** nominal form from rí “see”  
   **Translation:** evidence; proof; testimony  
   **Context:** Used in legal, religious, and everyday argument for what supports a claim or establishes an event.  
   **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

2. **ẹlẹ́rìí**  
   **Transliteration:** ẹlẹ́rìí  
   **Literal breakdown:** bearer/owner of testimony  
   **Translation:** witness  
   **Context:** Standard term for a witness in court, public procedure, religious testimony, and narrative reporting.  
   **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

3. **jẹ́ ẹlẹ́rìí**  
   **Transliteration:** jẹ́ ẹlẹ́rìí  
   **Literal breakdown:** be + witness  
   **Translation:** be a witness  
   **Context:** Used when someone personally witnessed an event or serves as a formal witness.  
   **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

4. **pe ẹlẹ́rìí**  
   **Transliteration:** pe ẹlẹ́rìí  
   **Literal breakdown:** call + witness  
   **Translation:** call a witness  
   **Context:** Common in legal and procedural settings. Also used more loosely in disputes or public inquiry.  
   **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

5. **ẹ̀rí ọ̀rọ̀**  
   **Transliteration:** ẹ̀rí ọ̀rọ̀  
   **Literal breakdown:** evidence + matter/statement  
   **Translation:** evidence for a claim; verbal testimony  
   **Context:** Used when discussing what supports a statement or case.  
   **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

6. **ẹ̀rí ìdánimọ̀**  
   **Transliteration:** ẹ̀rí ìdánimọ̀  
   **Literal breakdown:** evidence + identification  
   **Translation:** proof of identity; identification evidence  
   **Context:** Common in administrative and legal contexts for documents or materials used to verify identity.  
   **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

7. **ẹ̀rí ìbí**  
   **Transliteration:** ẹ̀rí ìbí  
   **Literal breakdown:** evidence + birth  
   **Translation:** birth certificate / proof of birth  
   **Context:** Administrative and legal usage for official birth documentation.  
   **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

8. **ẹ̀rí ọkọ́**  
   **Transliteration:** ẹ̀rí ọkọ́  
   **Literal breakdown:** evidence + vehicle  
   **Translation:** vehicle papers / vehicle documentation  
   **Context:** Used in administrative and policing contexts for official vehicle documents.  
   **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

9. **ẹ̀rí ilé**  
   **Transliteration:** ẹ̀rí ilé  
   **Literal breakdown:** evidence + house/landed property  
   **Translation:** title document; property papers  
   **Context:** Used in land and property matters for documentary proof of ownership.  
   **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

10. **fi ẹ̀rí hàn**  
    **Transliteration:** fi ẹ̀rí hàn  
    **Literal breakdown:** show + evidence + visible  
    **Translation:** present evidence; show proof  
    **Context:** Used in argument, investigation, court, and scholarship.  
    **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

11. **ní ẹ̀rí**  
    **Transliteration:** ní ẹ̀rí  
    **Literal breakdown:** have + evidence  
    **Translation:** have evidence; have proof  
    **Context:** Common in disputes, legal reasoning, and ordinary argument.  
    **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

12. **kò sí ẹ̀rí**  
    **Transliteration:** kò sí ẹ̀rí  
    **Literal breakdown:** NEG exist + evidence  
    **Translation:** there is no evidence  
    **Context:** Used to deny evidential support in legal, scientific, journalistic, and everyday contexts.  
    **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

13. **ẹlẹ́rìí ojú**  
    **Transliteration:** ẹlẹ́rìí ojú  
    **Literal breakdown:** witness + eye  
    **Translation:** eyewitness  
    **Context:** Common in legal reporting and narrative description of events personally seen.  
    **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

14. **ẹlẹ́rìí àròsọ**  
    **Transliteration:** ẹlẹ́rìí àròsọ  
    **Literal breakdown:** witness + hearsay/report  
    **Translation:** hearsay witness  
    **Context:** Used where testimony is indirect rather than based on direct observation.  
    **Epistemic status:** [UNCERTAIN]

15. **rí i dájú**  
    **Transliteration:** rí i dájú  
    **Literal breakdown:** see it + certain  
    **Translation:** make sure; ascertain  
    **Context:** Very common in practical, administrative, and procedural speech for checking that something is correct or secure.  
    **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

16. **rí i pé**  
    **Transliteration:** rí i pé  
    **Literal breakdown:** see it + that  
    **Translation:** see to it that; ensure that  
    **Context:** Common in instructions, administration, and responsibility assignment.  
    **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

17. **ríran**  
    **Transliteration:** ríran  
    **Literal breakdown:** seeing  
    **Translation:** sight; seeing; vision  
    **Context:** Used literally for visual perception and in some extended evaluative or spiritual contexts.  
    **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

Because this root has relatively few stable truth/evidence compounds compared with *tọ́*, that should be noted explicitly.

## Step 3 — Peripheral extensions

The following expressions show clear extension away from narrow truth/falsity evaluation and should simply be flagged:

- **títọ́** used for literal straightness
- **ọ̀nà títọ́** used for route/path and moral path
- **ìtọ́sọ́nà** used for guidance/instruction generally
- **tọ́ka sí / àtọ́ka / àtọ́kasí** used for reference, citation, indexing
- **tọ́jú / ìtọ́jú** used for care, treatment, maintenance
- **tọ́nà / tọ́nisọ́nà / olùtọ́nisọ́nà** used for guidance and direction
- **tọ́kantọ́kan** used for distributive “one by one”
- **ríran** used for sight/vision
- **rí i dájú / rí i pé** used for ensuring or making certain procedurally
- **òdodo** and related forms used for justice/righteousness rather than factual truth
- **ọba òdodo, ọ̀nà òdodo, ní òdodo** in religious, literary, and moralized usage
