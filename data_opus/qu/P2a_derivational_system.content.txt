# CLCA P2a — DERIVATIONAL MORPHOLOGY
## Language: Quechua (qu)

Orthography: Standardized trivocalic Southern Quechua (a, i, u), consistent with P1/P1b.

Scope note: This step documents **derivational** morphology only — affixes that create new lexemes (causative *-cha-*, agentive *-q*, reflexive/middle *-ku-*, augmentative *-sapa*, causative *-chi-*, possessive *-yuq/-niyuq*, participial *-sqa*). Purely inflectional material (person markers, evidential enclitics *-mi/-si*, interrogative *-chu*, accusative/adverbial *-ta*, case *-paq*) is excluded even where it appears in conventionalized phrases, unless it has produced a distinct lexeme. Fixed multi-word phrases (X sunqu, X simi, X kawsay) are compounds, not derivations, and are excluded here.

## ROOT: *chiqa-* "true / straight / right"

### Derivation 1
1. **Base Form:** chiqaq (P1 #1)
2. **Derived Form:** chiqachay
3. **Transliteration:** chiqachay
4. **Literal Breakdown:** chiqa- "true/straight" + -cha- causative + -y infinitive
5. **English:** to correct, to straighten, to verify, to set right
6. **Usage:** Used for setting something straight or right, both physically and factually/morally; appears in contexts of justice and accuracy.
7. **Status:** [CORE]

### Derivation 2
1. **Base Form:** chiqan (P1 #13)
2. **Derived Form:** chiqanchay
3. **Transliteration:** chiqanchay
4. **Literal Breakdown:** chiqan "straight/right" + -cha- causative + -y
5. **English:** to straighten, to align, to rectify, to set right
6. **Usage:** Aligning or straightening objects and rectifying conduct/affairs; practical and administrative use.
7. **Status:** [CORE]

### Derivation 3
1. **Base Form:** chiqan (P1 #13)
2. **Derived Form:** chiqan (chiqa- + -n)
3. **Transliteration:** chiqan
4. **Literal Breakdown:** chiqa- "true/straight" + -n (adjectival/attributive)
5. **English:** straight, right, correct, just
6. **Usage:** Denotes straightness/rightness extended to justice and correct conduct or direction.
7. **Status:** [CORE]

### Derivation 4
1. **Base Form:** chiqaq (P1 #1)
2. **Derived Form:** chiqaqchakuy
3. **Transliteration:** chiqaqchakuy
4. **Literal Breakdown:** chiqaq + -cha- causative + -ku- reflexive/middle + -y
5. **English:** to verify for oneself, to make sure, to ascertain
6. **Usage:** Procedural/everyday confirming for oneself that something is true.
7. **Status:** [MARGINAL]

Note: *chiqaq* (chiqa- + -q agentive/attributive) is treated in P1b as the base of the family; the derivational chain *chiqa- → chiqaq → chiqachay/chiqanchay/chiqaqchakuy* is the productive line. Forms such as chiqaqta, chiqaqchu, chiqaqmi, chiqaqpuni, chiqaqpaq are inflectional/enclitic combinations and are excluded here.

## ROOT: *allin* "good / well / right"

### Derivation 1
1. **Base Form:** allin (P1 #11)
2. **Derived Form:** allinchay
3. **Transliteration:** allinchay
4. **Literal Breakdown:** allin "good" + -cha- causative + -y
5. **English:** to improve, to repair, to fix, to make right
6. **Usage:** Setting things right or improving them, in both practical and moral senses.
7. **Status:** [CORE]

### Derivation 2
1. **Base Form:** allin (P1 #11)
2. **Derived Form:** allinkay / allin kay
3. **Transliteration:** allin kay
4. **Literal Breakdown:** allin "good" + kay "to be / being" (nominalized)
5. **English:** goodness, well-being, quality
6. **Usage:** Nominalized abstraction of goodness or quality in moral and qualitative discourse; note that as a free combination of *kay* this borders on compounding rather than affixal derivation.
7. **Status:** [MARGINAL]

### Derivation 3
1. **Base Form:** allin (P1 #11)
2. **Derived Form:** allinniyuq
3. **Transliteration:** allinniyuq
4. **Literal Breakdown:** allin + -ni- (epenthetic) + -yuq possessive
5. **English:** well-off, having good (fortune/goods)
6. **Usage:** Describes prosperity or being well-provided; attestation is weaker and the sense shifts toward material possession.
7. **Status:** [UNCERTAIN]

Note: allinmi, allillanmi, allinta, allinpaq are inflectional/enclitic combinations (evidential, limitative, adverbial, case) and are excluded as derivations. allin kawsay, allin runa, allin sunqu, allin p'unchay, allin ruwasqa are multi-word compounds/phrases, not affixal derivations, and are excluded here.

## ROOT: *llulla* "lie / false"

### Derivation 1
1. **Base Form:** llulla (P1 #8)
2. **Derived Form:** llullakuy
3. **Transliteration:** llullakuy
4. **Literal Breakdown:** llulla + -ku- reflexive/middle + -y infinitive
5. **English:** to lie, to deceive
6. **Usage:** The verb for telling untruths or deceiving; used in moral evaluation of conduct.
7. **Status:** [CORE]

### Derivation 2
1. **Base Form:** llulla (P1 #8)
2. **Derived Form:** llullakuq
3. **Transliteration:** llullakuq
4. **Literal Breakdown:** llullaku- (verb stem) + -q agentive
5. **English:** liar, deceiver
6. **Usage:** Names a person who lies; moral character evaluation.
7. **Status:** [CORE]

### Derivation 3
1. **Base Form:** llulla (P1 #8)
2. **Derived Form:** llullasapa
3. **Transliteration:** llullasapa
4. **Literal Breakdown:** llulla + -sapa augmentative ("full of / characterized by")
5. **English:** habitual liar, big liar
6. **Usage:** Pejorative evaluation of someone who lies habitually.
7. **Status:** [CORE]

### Derivation 4
1. **Base Form:** llulla (P1 #8)
2. **Derived Form:** llullachiy
3. **Transliteration:** llullachiy
4. **Literal Breakdown:** llulla + -chi- causative + -y
5. **English:** to deceive (someone), to trick, to cause to be fooled
6. **Usage:** Causing another to believe a falsehood; deception directed at a third party.
7. **Status:** [MARGINAL]

Note: mana llullaq is a negated phrase (mana + llulla + -q), not a single-word derivation, and is excluded here. llullakuni (llullaku- + -ni 1sg) is inflectional and excluded. llulla simi and llulla kay are compounds/phrases and excluded.

## Summary observations (supported by the data)

- The **causative *-cha-*** is the single most recurrent derivational affix across this domain, attested on multiple distinct roots: chiqa- → chiqachay, chiqan → chiqanchay, allin → allinchay, sut'i → sut'inchay, kikin → kikinchay. This is the clearest productive pattern.
- The **agentive *-q*** recurs to form person/attributive nouns: llulla(ku)- → llullakuq, rikch'aku- → rikch'akuq, iñi- → iñiq (and underlies chiqaq itself).
- The **reflexive/middle *-ku-*** recurs in verb formation (llullakuy, rikch'akuy, chiqaqchakuy), often feeding further -q agentives.
- **Asymmetries:** *llulla* shows the richest single-root derivational cluster (-ku-, -q, -sapa, -chi-); *allin* derives robustly only via -cha-, with its other expansions being compounds or inflections; *chani* and *iñiy* each yield only a single attested derivation; several P1 items (variants, particles, enclitics) yield none.
- Affixes appearing only once or with weaker support (augmentative *-sapa*, causative *-chi-*, possessive *-yuq*, participial *-sqa*) are documented as attested but not claimed to be broadly productive across this specific item set.
