| GROUP 3: PRECISION AND ACCURACY | Expressions evaluating the degree of exactness, precision, or accuracy in measurements, statements, or representations. | GROUP 4: AUTHENTICITY AND GENUINENESS | Expressions distinguishing genuine items, materials, or identities from counterfeits, imitations, or false versions. | GROUP 5: SINCERITY AND HONESTY IN EXPRESSION | Expressions evaluating whether people's words, emotions, or intentions genuinely reflect their inner states, or whether they are pretending, deceiving, or being insincere. | GROUP 7: RELIABILITY AND TRUSTWORTHINESS | Expressions evaluating whether sources, people, information, or systems can be depended upon for accuracy, consistency, or proper functioning. | GROUP 8: OBJECTIVITY AND SUBJECTIVITY | Expressions distinguishing perspectives, judgments, or claims based on whether they are independent of personal bias (objective) or influenced by personal viewpoint (subjective). | GROUP 9: SUBSTANCE VERSUS APPEARANCE | Expressions contrasting what something actually is (its substance, essence, or reality) with how it appears, is named, or is represented. | GROUP 10: PRACTICAL IMPLEMENTATION AND ACTUALIZATION | Expressions describing the process of making plans, ideas, or potentials into actual reality, or evaluating whether something exists in practice versus theory. | GROUP 11: PHYSICAL SOLIDITY AND MATERIAL PROPERTIES | Expressions describing physical density, structural integrity, or material composition of objects. | GROUP 12: CHARACTER TRAITS AND PERSONAL QUALITIES | Expressions describing personality characteristics related to honesty, simplicity, reliability, or seriousness as enduring traits rather than specific acts. These terms appear in character evaluation and personality description. Distinguished from Group 5 (sincerity in expression) by focus on stable character traits rather than momentary alignment of inner/outer states. | 老实 (lǎoshi), 朴实 (pǔshí), 踏实 (tāshi), 认真 (rènzhēn), 真率 (zhēnshuài), 老老实实 (lǎolǎo shíshí), 认认真真 (rènrèn zhēnzhēn), 踏踏实实 (tàtà shíshí), 实实在在 (shíshí zàizài), 实在 (shízài), 诚实者 (chéngshízhě) | 实在 (shízài) — also appears in Groups 1 and 15; 认真 (rènzhēn) — also functions as evaluative term for work quality beyond truth-domain | Distinguished from Group 5 by focus on stable character traits rather than momentary alignment of inner/outer states. |
| GROUP 13: HYPOTHETICAL AND SUPPOSITIONAL REASONING | Expressions introducing hypothetical scenarios, assumptions, or conditional reasoning. | GROUP 14: ARTISTIC AND REPRESENTATIONAL REALISM | Expressions evaluating how realistically or vividly artistic representations, performances, or simulations capture reality. | GROUP 15: DISCOURSE MARKERS AND EMPHATIC EXPRESSIONS | Expressions functioning as discourse markers to introduce statements, emphasize truth-value, express surprise, or manage conversational flow. | GROUP 16: EVIDENTIAL AND VERIFICATION PROCESSES | Expressions describing processes of investigating, verifying, confirming, or establishing facts through evidence. | GROUP 17: INTENSIFICATION AND EMPHASIS OF TRUTH-VALUE | Expressions that intensify or emphasize the degree of truth, certainty, or genuineness. | GROUP 18: AMBIGUITY AND MIXED TRUTH-VALUE | Expressions describing situations where truth and falsehood are mixed, unclear, or difficult to distinguish. | GROUP 19: CONCRETE OBJECTS AND MATERIAL REALITY | Expressions referring to physical objects, material entities, or tangible reality as opposed to abstract concepts, representations, or monetary equivalents. | GROUP 20: FULFILLMENT AND ENRICHMENT | Expressions describing states of being fulfilled, enriched, or having substantial content, or the process of enriching something.
