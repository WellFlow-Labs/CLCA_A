## Item 1: உண்மை / *uṇmai*

**Morphological note:** *uṇmai* is itself a derived nominal (from *uḷ* + *-mai*). It functions as the base for a productive derivational series in modern Tamil. The root is highly productive; the forms below represent the attested derivational layer above *uṇmai* as a base.

---

**Derived Form 1**

1. **Base Form:** உண்மை / *uṇmai*
2. **Derived Form:** உண்மையான
3. **Transliteration:** uṇmaiyāna
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *uṇmai* + *-āna* (adjectival copular suffix, relativized form of *āku* "to become/be")
5. **Natural English translation:** true, genuine, real, authentic
6. **Usage:** Attributive adjective modifying nouns to assert genuineness or factual accuracy; applied to persons, objects, feelings, and statements. Extremely frequent across all registers of spoken and written Tamil.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2**

1. **Base Form:** உண்மை / *uṇmai*
2. **Derived Form:** உண்மையாக
3. **Transliteration:** uṇmaiyāka
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *uṇmai* + *-āka* (adverbial/predicative suffix, converbal form of *āku*)
5. **Natural English translation:** truly, genuinely, honestly, in truth; predicatively: is true/genuine
6. **Usage:** Adverbial form used to assert that an action or state is genuine or sincere; also used predicatively to assert that something is genuinely the case. Appears in constructions such as *uṇmaiyāka colla* (to speak truthfully). Common in everyday speech and formal writing.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 3**

1. **Base Form:** உண்மை / *uṇmai*
2. **Derived Form:** உண்மையில்
3. **Transliteration:** uṇmaiyil
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *uṇmai* + *-il* (locative case suffix)
5. **Natural English translation:** in truth, actually, in reality
6. **Usage:** Fixed adverbial expression used to introduce a correction or clarification of what is actually the case; functions as an epistemic discourse marker, often contrasting appearance with reality. Extremely common in spoken and written Tamil.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 4**

1. **Base Form:** உண்மை / *uṇmai*
2. **Derived Form:** உண்மையிலேயே
3. **Transliteration:** uṇmaiyilēyē
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *uṇmai* + *-il* (locative) + *-ē* (emphatic particle) + *-yē* (further emphatic reinforcement)
5. **Natural English translation:** truly indeed, in actual fact, really and truly
6. **Usage:** Emphatic adverbial used in spoken Tamil to strongly assert that something is genuinely the case, often in contexts of surprise or insistence. More emphatic than *uṇmaiyil*. Common in spoken Tamil and informal written registers.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 5**

1. **Base Form:** உண்மை / *uṇmai*
2. **Derived Form:** உண்மைத்தன்மை
3. **Transliteration:** uṇmait-taṉmai
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *uṇmai* + linking *-t-* + *taṉmai* (nature, inherent quality; itself from *taṉ* "self/own" + *-mai*)
5. **Natural English translation:** truthfulness, authenticity, veracity, the quality of being true
6. **Usage:** Abstract nominal used in philosophical, literary-critical, and formal written Tamil to refer to the property of being true or authentic. Less common in casual speech than *uṇmai* alone.
7. **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**Derived Form 6**

1. **Base Form:** உண்மை / *uṇmai*
2. **Derived Form:** உண்மைக்கு மாறான
3. **Transliteration:** uṇmaikkum māṟāna
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *uṇmai* + dative *-kku* + *māṟāna* (contrary to, deviating from; participial adjective from *māṟu*, to deviate/change)
5. **Natural English translation:** contrary to fact, false, counterfactual
6. **Usage:** Fixed evaluative expression used in legal and formal written contexts to characterize a statement or representation as factually false. More formal and explicit than *poy* alone.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 7**

1. **Base Form:** உண்மை / *uṇmai*
2. **Derived Form:** உண்மையற்ற
3. **Transliteration:** uṇmaiyaṟṟa
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *uṇmai* + *aṟṟa* (privative participial: without, lacking)
5. **Natural English translation:** untrue, lacking truth, false, inauthentic
6. **Usage:** Used to evaluate persons, statements, or representations as lacking truth or genuineness; appears in moral and formal evaluative contexts. Less common than *poy* or *poyyāna* for simple falsehood but attested in formal registers.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 8**

1. **Base Form:** உண்மை / *uṇmai*
2. **Derived Form:** உண்மையாளர்
3. **Transliteration:** uṇmaiyāḷar
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *uṇmai* + *āḷar* (agentive/person suffix, plural-honorific form of *āḷ*)
5. **Natural English translation:** truthful persons, people of truth, truth-tellers
6. **Usage:** Used to refer to persons characterized by truthfulness or commitment to truth; appears in formal, literary, and moral discourse. The singular *uṇmaiyāḷan* (masculine) and *uṇmaiyāḷ* (general/honorific) are also attested, though less frequent than the base noun.
7. **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**Derived Form 9**

1. **Base Form:** உண்மை / *uṇmai*
2. **Derived Form:** உண்மைப்படுத்து
3. **Transliteration:** uṇmaippaṭuttu
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *uṇmai* + *-p-* (linking consonant) + *paṭuttu* (causative verb stem from *paṭu* "to undergo/become"); finite/imperative form
5. **Natural English translation:** authenticate, verify, substantiate, confirm
6. **Usage:** The finite verb stem used in administrative and legal instructions and directives.
7. **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**Derived Form 10**

1. **Base Form:** உண்மை / *uṇmai*
2. **Derived Form:** உண்மைப்படுத்துதல்
3. **Transliteration:** uṇmaippaṭuttutal
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *uṇmai* + *-p-* (linking consonant) + *paṭuttu* (causative verb stem) + verbal noun suffix *-tal*
5. **Natural English translation:** authentication, verification, substantiation (as a process)
6. **Usage:** Formal and administrative verbal noun denoting the act of establishing the truth or genuineness of a document, claim, or identity.
7. **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**Note on *uṇmai* productivity:** The *-āna* adjectival suffix and the locative *-il* are the two most productive derivational operations on *uṇmai*. The dative + *māṟāna* construction is a fixed legal phrase rather than a freely productive derivation. The *taṉmai* compound is a lexicalized nominal. No verbal derivation directly from *uṇmai* is attested in P1; verbal expressions involving *uṇmai* are periphrastic (e.g., *uṇmaiyai maṟaittal*, *uṇmaiyai aṟital*) rather than morphologically derived, with the exception of the *-paṭuttu* causative series. The suffixes *-āna*, *-āka*, *-il*, *-taṉmai*, and *-aṟṟa* all attach productively. The agentive *-āḷar* form is attested but less frequent. The locative forms represent grammatical case-marking rather than lexical derivation strictly speaking, but are included here because they yield conventionalized adverbial expressions with discourse-level functions not fully predictable from the morphology alone.

## Item 2: பொய் / *poy*

**Morphological note:** *poy* is a root nominal of debated etymology. It participates in adjectival and adverbial derivation via the same suffixes as *uṇmai*, and in nominal compounding.

---

**Derived Form 1**

1. **Base Form:** பொய் / *poy*
2. **Derived Form:** பொய்யான
3. **Transliteration:** poyyāna
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *poy* + *-āna* (adjectival copular suffix; note gemination of *y* before vowel-initial suffix)
5. **Natural English translation:** false, lying, untrue, deceptive, fake
6. **Usage:** Attributive adjective applied to statements, persons, and representations judged to be false or deceptive. Parallel in formation to *uṇmaiyāna* and used contrastively with it. Common across spoken and written registers.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2**

1. **Base Form:** பொய் / *poy*
2. **Derived Form:** பொய்யாக
3. **Transliteration:** poyyāka
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *poy* + *-āka* (adverbial copular suffix)
5. **Natural English translation:** falsely, deceptively, untruthfully; predicatively: is false
6. **Usage:** Adverbial and predicative form used to characterize the manner of speech or action as false or deceptive. Common in moral and legal discourse: *poyyāka colluvāṉ* (he will speak falsely).
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 3**

1. **Base Form:** பொய் / *poy*
2. **Derived Form:** பொய்மை
3. **Transliteration:** poymai
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *poy* + *-mai* (abstract nominal suffix)
5. **Natural English translation:** falsehood, falsity, the quality of being false
6. **Usage:** Abstract nominal form denoting the quality or state of being false; used in philosophical and moral discourse as the direct antonym of *uṇmai*. Appears in classical and formal Tamil writing. Less frequent in everyday speech than *poy* itself.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 4**

1. **Base Form:** பொய் / *poy*
2. **Derived Form:** பொய்யன்
3. **Transliteration:** poyyaṉ
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *poy* + *-y-* + *-aṉ* (masculine singular agentive suffix)
5. **Natural English translation:** liar (masculine singular)
6. **Usage:** Masculine singular agentive nominal used in colloquial and literary Tamil to characterize a male person as a liar. Used in direct accusation and narrative characterization.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 5**

1. **Base Form:** பொய் / *poy*
2. **Derived Form:** பொய்யள்
3. **Transliteration:** poyyaḷ
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *poy* + *-aḷ* (feminine agentive suffix)
5. **Natural English translation:** liar (feminine), a woman who lies
6. **Usage:** Feminine counterpart of *poyyaṉ*; used in everyday moral evaluation to characterize a female person as a liar. Carries the same strong negative evaluative force.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 6**

1. **Base Form:** பொய் / *poy*
2. **Derived Form:** பொய்யர்
3. **Transliteration:** poyyar
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *poy* + *-y-* + *-ar* (agentive/person suffix, honorific or plural)
5. **Natural English translation:** liars, deceivers (plural/honorific agentive)
6. **Usage:** Agentive nominal denoting persons who lie or deceive. Used in literary and formal Tamil in moral condemnation.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 7**

1. **Base Form:** பொய் / *poy*
2. **Derived Form:** பொய்யாளன்
3. **Transliteration:** poyyāḷaṉ
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *poy* + *-y-* + *-āḷaṉ* (agentive suffix with *āḷ* "person" + masculine *-aṉ*)
5. **Natural English translation:** liar, deceiver (masculine, somewhat more formal than *poyyaṉ*)
6. **Usage:** An agentive nominal with slightly more formal or emphatic character than *poyyaṉ*, used in moral condemnation and narrative. The form *poyyāḷar* (honorific/plural) also occurs in formal written Tamil.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 8**

1. **Base Form:** பொய் / *poy*
2. **Derived Form:** பொய்த்தன்மை
3. **Transliteration:** poyttaṉmai
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *poy* + *taṉmai* (quality, nature)
5. **Natural English translation:** falseness, the quality of being untrue, spuriousness
6. **Usage:** A more formal and abstract nominal than *poymai*; used in philosophical and literary critical contexts to refer to the property of being false or inauthentic. Rare in everyday speech.
7. **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**Derived Form 9**

1. **Base Form:** பொய் / *poy*
2. **Derived Form:** பொய்யுரை
3. **Transliteration:** poyyurai
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *poy* (false) + *urai* (speech, statement, utterance)
5. **Natural English translation:** false statement, lie, false testimony
6. **Usage:** A compound nominal used in formal and legal contexts to refer to a false statement or false testimony; appears in legal proceedings and formal written Tamil. More formal than *poy* alone.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 10**

1. **Base Form:** பொய் / *poy*
2. **Derived Form:** பொய்சாட்சி
3. **Transliteration:** poycāṭci
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *poy* (false) + *cāṭci* (witness, testimony; from Sanskrit *sākṣin*)
5. **Natural English translation:** false witness, perjury, false testimony
6. **Usage:** Used in legal and moral contexts to refer to false testimony given by a witness; appears in legal proceedings and in moral discourse about the wrongness of lying under oath.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 11**

1. **Base Form:** பொய் / *poy*
2. **Derived Form:** பொய்யில்லாத
3. **Transliteration:** poyyillāta
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *poy* + *illāta* (without, not having; privative participial)
5. **Natural English translation:** without falsehood, truthful, free from lies
6. **Usage:** Used to evaluate persons or statements as entirely free from falsehood; appears in moral instruction, religious contexts, and formal evaluative discourse. Carries a strong positive evaluative force.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Note on *poy* productivity:** *poy* participates in the same *-āna* adjectival and *-mai* nominalizing derivations as other evaluative roots in this domain. The suffixes *-āna*, *-āka*, *-mai*, and agentive *-aṉ*/*-aḷ*/*-ar* attach productively. Compounding with *urai* (speech) and *cāṭci* (testimony) produces attested legal vocabulary. No verbal derivation directly from *poy* as a base (e.g., a causative or inchoative) has been identified as a common independent lexical item; *poy colluvāṉ* (he will speak falsely) uses *poy* as a nominal object rather than a verbal base. The root functions as both noun and attributive modifier without further morphological change in many contexts.

## Item 3: நேர்மை / *nērmai*

**Morphological note:** *nērmai* is itself a derivation from the root *nēr* (straight, direct) via the *-mai* nominalizer. The forms below treat *nērmai* as the base, consistent with its status as the primary lexical item in P1. Derivations from the root *nēr* directly are noted where relevant.

---

**Derived Form 1**

1. **Base Form:** நேர்மை / *nērmai*
2. **Derived Form:** நேர்மையான
3. **Transliteration:** nērmaiyāna
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *nērmai* + *-āna* (adjectival copular suffix)
5. **Natural English translation:** honest, upright, straightforward, sincere, of integrity
6. **Usage:** Attributive adjective applied to persons and conduct evaluated as honest and non-deceptive. Common in moral evaluation in personal, professional, and civic contexts across all registers.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2**

1. **Base Form:** நேர்மை / *nērmai*
2. **Derived Form:** நேர்மையாக
3. **Transliteration:** nērmaiyāka
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *nērmai* + *-āka* (adverbial copular suffix)
5. **Natural English translation:** honestly, sincerely, straightforwardly, uprightly
6. **Usage:** Adverbial form used to characterize the manner of speech or action as honest. Common in everyday and formal speech and moral instruction.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 3**

1. **Base Form:** நேர்மை / *nērmai*
2. **Derived Form:** நேர்மையில்லாத
3. **Transliteration:** nērmaiyillāta
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *nērmai* + *illāta* (lacking, without; negative existential participial adjective from *il-*)
5. **Natural English translation:** dishonest, lacking integrity, not straightforward
6. **Usage:** Negative attributive adjective used to evaluate persons or conduct as lacking honesty or integrity. Formed by the productive negative existential construction rather than a dedicated privative suffix. Common in moral and political discourse.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 4**

1. **Base Form:** நேர்மை / *nērmai*
2. **Derived Form:** நேர்மையற்ற
3. **Transliteration:** nērmaiyaṟṟa
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *nērmai* + *aṟṟa* (privative participial suffix, from *aṟu*, to cut off/lack)
5. **Natural English translation:** dishonest, lacking integrity, unprincipled
6. **Usage:** Used to evaluate persons or conduct as lacking honesty or integrity; common in moral and political discourse.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 5**

1. **Base Form:** நேர்மை / *nērmai*
2. **Derived Form:** நேர்மையின்மை
3. **Transliteration:** nērmaiyiṉmai
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *nērmai* + *iṉmai* (abstract nominal of absence/non-existence, from *il* "not exist" + *-mai*)
5. **Natural English translation:** dishonesty, lack of integrity, absence of honesty
6. **Usage:** Abstract nominal used in formal and written Tamil to denote the property of lacking honesty. Appears in moral, legal, and political discourse.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 6**

1. **Base Form:** நேர்மை / *nērmai*
2. **Derived Form:** நேர்மையுடன்
3. **Transliteration:** nērmaiyuṭaṉ
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *nērmai* + *uṭaṉ* (sociative suffix: with, together with)
5. **Natural English translation:** with honesty, honestly, with integrity
6. **Usage:** Adverbial phrase characterizing the manner of conduct as honest; common in formal and everyday Tamil, particularly in moral instruction and professional contexts.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 7**

1. **Base Form:** நேர்மை / *nērmai*
2. **Derived Form:** நேர்மையாளர்
3. **Transliteration:** nērmaiyāḷar
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *nērmai* + *āḷar* (agentive/person suffix, plural-honorific)
5. **Natural English translation:** honest persons, people of integrity
6. **Usage:** Used to refer to persons characterized by honesty and integrity; appears in formal, literary, and moral discourse, often in political or moral discourse praising or calling for honest conduct. Less frequent than the adjectival form *nērmaiyāna* but attested in formal registers.
7. **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**Derived Form 8**

1. **Base Form:** நேர்மை / *nērmai* (via root *நேர்* / *nēr*)
2. **Derived Form:** நேர்தன்மை
3. **Transliteration:** nēr-taṉmai
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *nēr* (straight, direct) + *taṉmai* (nature, quality)
5. **Natural English translation:** straightforwardness, directness, integrity
6. **Usage:** Formed directly from the root *nēr* rather than from *nērmai*; overlaps in meaning with *nērmai* but tends to appear in more explicitly philosophical or literary contexts.
7. **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**Note on *nērmai* and its base *nēr*:** *nērmai* is itself a derivation from the root *nēr* (straight, direct) via the *-mai* nominalizer. The root *nēr* also participates independently in compounds such as *nēr-mukaṃ* (face-to-face), but these are not further derivations of *nērmai* itself. No verbal derivation from *nērmai* as a base has been identified as a common independent lexical item. The *-āna*, *-āka*, *aṟṟa*, and *iṉmai* forms follow regular productive patterns. The agentive *-āḷar* form is attested but less frequent than the adjectival forms.

## Item 4: சரி / *cari*

**Morphological note:** *cari* functions as both a nominal (correctness) and a response particle. Its derivational productivity is moderate. The base also functions as a discourse particle (agreement/confirmation), but this is not a derivational form and is not listed here.

---

**Derived Form 1**

1. **Base Form:** சரி / *cari*
2. **Derived Form:** சரியான
3. **Transliteration:** cariyāna
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *cari* + *-y-* (glide) + *-āna* (adjectival copular suffix)
5. **Natural English translation:** correct, proper, right, appropriate, accurate
6. **Usage:** Adjectival form used to evaluate statements, actions, or conduct as correct or appropriate; extremely common across all registers of spoken and written Tamil.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2**

1. **Base Form:** சரி / *cari*
2. **Derived Form:** சரியாக
3. **Transliteration:** cariyāka
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *cari* + *-y-* + *-āka* (adverbial copular suffix)
5. **Natural English translation:** correctly, properly, rightly, accurately
6. **Usage:** Adverbial form used to characterize the manner of an action or the accuracy of a result as correct; very common in everyday speech, educational, and technical contexts: *cariyāka pēcu* (speak correctly/properly), *cariyāka puriyavillai* (I did not understand correctly).
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 3**

1. **Base Form:** சரி / *cari*
2. **Derived Form:** சரிபார்
3. **Transliteration:** caripār
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *cari* (correct) + *pār* (to see, look; here: to check)
5. **Natural English translation:** to verify, to check, to confirm, to validate
6. **Usage:** Verb used to describe the act of checking or verifying whether something is correct or accurate; common in everyday, administrative, and technical contexts.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 4**

1. **Base Form:** சரி / *cari*
2. **Derived Form:** சரிபார்ப்பு
3. **Transliteration:** caripārppu
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *caripār* (to verify) + *-ppu* (verbal noun suffix)
5. **Natural English translation:** verification, checking, validation, review
6. **Usage:** Verbal noun derived from *caripār*; used to refer to the act or process of verification. Common in administrative, technical, and journalistic contexts.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 5**

1. **Base Form:** சரி / *cari*
2. **Derived Form:** சரிபார்த்தல்
3. **Transliteration:** caripārtttal
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *cari* (correct) + *pār* (to see, look; here: to check) + verbal noun suffix *-ttal*
5. **Natural English translation:** verification, checking, confirmation
6. **Usage:** Verbal noun denoting the act of checking or verifying correctness. The finite form *caripārkkiṟēṉ* (I am checking/verifying) is widely used in spoken Tamil.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 6**

1. **Base Form:** சரி / *cari*
2. **Derived Form:** சரிசெய்
3. **Transliteration:** caricey
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *cari* (correct) + *cey* (to do, to make)
5. **Natural English translation:** to correct, to fix, to set right, to repair
6. **Usage:** Verb used to describe the act of correcting an error or repairing something that is not functioning properly; extremely common in everyday speech across all registers.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 7**

1. **Base Form:** சரி / *cari*
2. **Derived Form:** சரிசெய்தல்
3. **Transliteration:** cariceyttal
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *caricey* (to correct) + *-tal* (verbal noun suffix)
5. **Natural English translation:** correction, fixing, repair, rectification
6. **Usage:** Verbal noun of *caricey*; used in formal and administrative contexts to refer to the act of correction or repair. Appears in official documents and technical writing: *tavaṟai cari ceyttal* (correcting the mistake), *iyantiratai cari ceyttal* (fixing the machine).
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 8**

1. **Base Form:** சரி / *cari*
2. **Derived Form:** சரியில்லாத
3. **Transliteration:** cariyillāta
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *cari* + *y* + *illāta* (without, not having; privative participial)
5. **Natural English translation:** incorrect, improper, not right, faulty, not working properly
6. **Usage:** Used to evaluate something as not correct, not functioning properly, or not appropriate; common in both epistemic (incorrect information) and practical (malfunctioning object) contexts: *avaṉ cariyillāta āḷ* (he is not a proper/right person). Slightly less formal than *tavaṟāna* for the same meaning.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Note on *cari* productivity:** *cari* is productive in compounding with verbs (*pār*, *cey*) and in taking the standard adjectival and adverbial suffixes. The privative *illāta* construction is also attested. No *-mai* nominalization (*\*carimai*) has been identified as a common form.

## Item 5: தவறு / *tavaṟu*

**Morphological note:** *tavaṟu* functions as both a nominal (mistake, error) and a verbal root (*tavar-*, to err, deviate). Both nominal and verbal derivations are attested.

**Derived Form 1**

1. **Base Form:** தவறு / *tavaṟu*
2. **Derived Form:** தவறான
3. **Transliteration:** tavaṟāna

5. **Natural English translation:** wrong, mistaken, erroneous, incorrect
