# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P4a Constructions
## Language: Korean (ko)
### Transliteration Standard: Revised Romanization of Korean (RR)

---

## 1. ATTRIBUTION

### Pattern
**[Noun] + 이다/이 아니다** — copular predication asserting or denying that something belongs to the truth domain

### Examples
- 사실이다 → *sasil-ida* → "It is a fact / That is true"
- 거짓말이 아니다 → *geojitmal-i anida* → "It is not a lie / That is not false"

### Notes
Korean uses the copula 이다 (positive) and 이 아니다 (negative) to predicate truth-domain nouns directly of a subject. No separate "to be true" verb exists; the nominal 사실, 진실, 거짓말, etc. is predicated via copula. The negative form 이 아니다 is the standard denial construction across all registers.

---

## 2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION

### Pattern
**[Noun] + 이/가 + 없다** — existential negation asserting the absence of a truth-domain property

### Examples
- 근거가 없다 → *geungeo-ga eopda* → "There is no basis / It is groundless"
- 신빙성이 없다 → *sinbyeongseong-i eopda* → "It lacks credibility / It is implausible"

### Notes
For abstract quality nouns (근거, 신빙성, 진정성, 타당성), the primary negative construction is existential 없다 ("to not exist/have") rather than copular negation. The positive counterpart uses 있다 ("to exist/have"): 근거가 있다, 신빙성이 있다. This 있다/없다 pattern is the standard evaluative frame for this class of nouns; -하다 attachment is not available for most of them.

---

## 3. ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION

### Pattern
**[Truth-domain adjective stem] + -(으)ㄴ + [Noun]** — attributive modification of a noun by a truth-domain predicate

### Examples
- 근거 없는 주장 → *geungeo eomneun jujang* → "An unsubstantiated claim / A groundless assertion"
- 진정성 있는 사과 → *jinjeonseong inneun saga* → "A sincere apology / A genuine apology"

### Notes
Attributive modification in Korean is formed by the adnominal ending -(으)ㄴ on adjective stems or the participial form of 있다/없다 (있는/없는). Truth-domain nouns used attributively (진짜, 거짓, 허위, 올바른, 정확한) precede the head noun directly. The construction 근거 없는 is particularly fixed and functions as a near-compound modifier meaning "unsubstantiated."

---

## 4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE

### Pattern
**[Noun] + (으)로 + 드러나다 / 밝혀지다 / 판명되다** — inchoative constructions marking the transition of a truth-domain state into public knowledge

### Examples
- 진실이 드러나다 → *jinsil-i deureonada* → "The truth emerges / The truth is exposed"
- 가짜로 판명되다 → *gajja-ro panmyeong-doeda* → "To be determined to be fake / To be found counterfeit"

### Notes
Korean expresses the coming-to-be-known of truth-domain states through intransitive verbs of emergence and determination: 드러나다 (to surface, be exposed), 밝혀지다 (to be illuminated, come to light), 판명되다 (to be officially determined). The -로 particle marks the resultant state in 판명되다 constructions. These are not morphological inchoatives but lexically distinct verbs; no single inchoative suffix covers this domain.

---

## 5. CAUSATIVE (OR ABSENCE)

### Pattern
**[Agent] + 이/가 + [Truth-domain noun] + 을/를 + 밝히다 / 숨기다 / 왜곡하다** — agentive causative expressed through transitive verb selection

### Examples
- 진실을 밝히다 → *jinsil-eul balkhida* → "To reveal the truth / To bring the truth to light"
- 진실을 숨기다 → *jinsil-eul sumgida* → "To conceal the truth / To hide the truth"

### Notes
Korean has no productive morphological causative suffix in this domain. Causative meaning — causing a truth-domain state to become known or to remain hidden — is expressed entirely through transitive verb selection: 밝히다 (reveal), 숨기다 (conceal), 왜곡하다 (distort), 외면하다 (avert from). These verbs take the truth-domain noun as direct object. No form like *진실-이키다 or *사실-히다 is attested.

---

## 6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION

### Pattern
**[Sino-Korean nominal base] + 성(性)** — derivation of an abstract quality noun

### Examples
- 진실성 → *jinsilseong* → "Truthfulness / The quality of being truthful"
- 타당성 → *tadangseong* → "Validity / Soundness as a quality"

### Notes
The suffix -성 (性) attaches productively to Sino-Korean nominal bases to derive abstract quality nouns: 진실성, 정확성, 신뢰성, 공정성, 진정성, 유사성, 객관성, 주관성. Native Korean bases (진짜, 거짓말, 맞다) do not take -성. A second nominalization pattern uses -음 on native verbal stems: 올바름 (rightness), 틀림 (error). These two patterns are in complementary distribution by base type.

---

## 7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION

### Pattern
**[거짓말] + 쟁이 / 꾼** — pejorative agentive suffix deriving a person-noun from a lying-related base

### Examples
- 거짓말쟁이 → *geojitmal-jaengi* → "Liar (habitual, pejorative)"
- 위조범 → *wijobom* → "Forger / Counterfeiter (legal register)"

### Notes
Agentive nominalization in this domain is sparse and register-stratified. The native suffix -쟁이 (pejorative, habitual agent) attaches to 거짓말 to yield 거짓말쟁이; -꾼 (habitual agent, slightly less pejorative) yields 거짓말꾼. In formal/legal registers, the Sino-Korean -범 (犯, criminal) attaches to 위조 to yield 위조범. No agentive form meaning "truth-teller" is lexicalized; the concept is expressed periphrastically (진실을 말하는 사람, 정직한 사람).

---

## 8. LIGHT VERB CONSTRUCTION WITH 하다

### Pattern
**[Sino-Korean nominal base] + 하다** — conversion of a nominal into a predicate

### Examples
- 위조하다 → *wijo-hada* → "To forge / To counterfeit"
- 정직하다 → *jeongjik-hada* → "To be honest / To have integrity"

### Notes
The light verb 하다 attaches to Sino-Korean nominal bases to produce predicates across the domain: 진실하다, 정직하다, 위조하다, 신뢰하다, 타당하다, 유사하다, 근거하다. This is the primary verbalization strategy for Sino-Korean bases. Native Korean bases (진짜, 가짜, 거짓) do not take 하다 in this pattern; 거짓말하다 is an exception where the full compound 거짓말 (not 거짓 alone) takes 하다.

---

## 9. STATIVE PREDICATE WITH 되다

### Pattern
**[Nominal base] + 되다** — stative or passive predicate expressing an inherent or resultant truth-domain state

### Examples
- 모순되다 → *mosun-doeda* → "To be contradictory / To contradict"
- 거짓되다 → *geojit-doeda* → "To be false / To be deceitful"

### Notes
Where both -하다 and -되다 forms exist (진실하다 / 진실되다), the -하다 form tends toward active or agentive meaning and the -되다 form toward stative or inherent quality. 모순되다 is the standard predicate for contradiction (모순하다 is not attested). 참되다 (to be genuinely true/authentic) appears in literary and religious registers. The -되다 pattern is less productive than -하다 in this domain.

---

## 10. ADVERBIAL FORMATION

### Pattern
Three distinct adverbial patterns in complementary distribution by base type:
- **[Sino-Korean base] + 히** → 정확히, 정직히
- **[-하다 stem] + 게** → 정직하게, 공정하게, 올바르게
- **[Nominal] + 로/으로** → 진실로, 진정으로, 실제로

### Examples
- 정확히 말하면 → *jeonghwaki malhamyeon* → "To be precise / Strictly speaking"
- 실제로 일어난 일 → *siljero ileonan il* → "What actually happened"

### Notes
Korean truth-domain adverbs are formed by three non-interchangeable strategies. The -히 suffix attaches to a subset of Sino-Korean bases (정확히, 정직히) and is not freely productive. The -게 ending on -하다 stems is more broadly available and common in spoken registers. The -로/으로 particle on nominals produces adverbials of manner or perspective (진실로, 진정으로) or of actuality (실제로, 사실상). Register and base type determine which form is used; they are not synonymous.
