## 2. Substitution Patterns (Narrative)

**Revelation / Becoming.** No productive inchoative or revelation construction exists. The only attested frame is the lexical perfective *gamovida* ("turned out"), as in ტყუილი გამოვიდა (ṭq̇uili gamovida, "it turned out to be a lie"). This single frame simultaneously serves the BECOME, REVELATION, and DISCONFIRMATION functions, but only as one lexical item, not a regular pattern. Per P4a #4, it is "lexical rather than a productive inchoative derivation." There is no Adj-based "become true/genuine/honest" construction.

**Verification vs. Disconfirmation.** No dedicated VERIFICATION construction is attested in the source data. The only confirmation-adjacent material is the propositional Attribution frame (მართალი ხარ / სწორი ხარ), which affirms correctness of a person/statement but is not described as a verification operation. For disconfirmation, only the *gamovida* "turned out (a lie)" frame appears. Thus VERIFICATION and DISCONFIRMATION do **not** share a single construction; verification has no attested form, and disconfirmation surfaces only through the borderline *gamovida* frame.

**Causation asymmetry (truth vs. falsity).** Causative derivation is robust only at the negative pole: გაყალბება (gaq̇albeba, "to falsify/forge") and მოტყუება (moṭq̇ueba, "to deceive") via prefix + root + -eba. P4a #5 explicitly notes there is no symmetric "make true/genuine" causative. Factual causation is therefore available only for falsehood-creation; character-causation toward a positive trait is absent.

**Agentive asymmetry.** Agentive nominalization is likewise pole-restricted to falsehood: მატყუარა (maṭq̇uara, "liar"), ცრუმოწმე (crumoc̣me, "false witness"), q̇albismkneli ("forger"). There is no lexicalized "truth-teller"; that meaning is expressed phrasally as სიმართლის თქმა (simartlis tkma, "telling the truth"), i.e., the Speech-Act nominalization substitutes for a missing positive agentive.

**Negation.** Negation is split: morphological **ara-** (არასანდო arasando, არაპატიოსანი arap̣aṭiosani) is available only for the reliability/honesty subset, while the truth/lie and genuineness items fall back on analytic sentential negation (არ ხარ მართალი, ar xar martali). The language thus uses both a morphological and an analytic strategy, distributed by item.

**Attribution restriction.** The copular Attribution frame (მართალი ხარ, სწორი ხარ) clusters on propositional-correctness items only and does not generalize to genuineness or reliability adjectives, which rely instead on the Adnominal frame.

## 3. Missingness Summary

**Structural (morphosyntactic) Gaps**
- BECOME: no productive Adj→inchoative construction (only the single lexical *gamovida* frame).
- VERIFICATION: no attested construction.
- CAUSATION (character): no "make-honest/genuine" causative construction.

**Lexical Gaps**
- No single-word agentive for "truth-teller" (positive counterpart to მატყუარა).
- No "cause-to-be-true" verb counterpart to გაყალბება / მოტყუება.

**Conceptual Gaps**
- None explicitly stated in P4a/P4b as a blocked operation. The source notes absences as unattested patterns and asymmetries, not as conceptually impossible. (No conceptual gap can be asserted beyond the data.)

**Asymmetric Gaps (only one pole present)**
- Causation: present at falsehood pole (gaq̇albeba, moṭq̇ueba); absent at truth/genuineness pole.
- Agentive Nominalization: present at falsehood pole (maṭq̇uara, crumoc̣me, q̇albismkneli); absent for "truth-teller" (phrasal substitute only).
- Negative Attribution (ara-): present for reliability/honesty subset (arasando, arasaimedo, arap̣aṭiosani); absent for truth/lie and genuineness items (sentential negation substitutes).
- Attribution (copular): present for propositional-correctness items only; absent for genuineness/reliability.

## 4. Canonicalized Operator Inventory

**Fully available**
- ADNOM — most productive frame, spans genuineness, reliability, accuracy, honesty.
- NOMINALIZATION (abstract) — si-…-e and -eba/-oba recur broadly.

**Available with substitution**
- NEGATION — morphological **ara-** for a subset; analytic sentential negation substitutes elsewhere.
- DISCONFIRMATION — substituted by the lexical *gamovida* ("turned out a lie") frame.
- REVELATION — substituted by the same *gamovida* ("turned out") frame.
- Positive agentive notion — substituted by Speech-Act nominalization (simartlis tkma) in place of a "truth-teller" word.

**Partially available**
- ATTR — restricted to propositional-correctness items (მართალი, სწორი).
- CAUSATION (factual) — present only at falsehood pole.
- NOMINALIZATION (agentive) — present only at falsehood pole.

**Missing entirely**
- BECOME — no productive inchoative construction (structural gap; only single lexical frame).
- VERIFICATION — no attested construction.
- CAUSATION (character) — no positive character-causative construction.
