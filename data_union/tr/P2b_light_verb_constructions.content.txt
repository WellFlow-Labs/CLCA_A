# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2b Compositional Constructions
# Language: Turkish (tr)

---

## Preliminary Note on Scope and Method

This step documents attested multi-word constructions involving the P1 lexical items. The focus is on constructions that are not purely derivational (handled in P2a) but involve syntactic combination, periphrasis, or idiomatization. Many such constructions were already identified in P1b and P2a as collocations; where they were documented there, they are noted as cross-references rather than re-documented in full, unless the constructional pattern itself warrants separate treatment.

Constructions are organized by base form. Where a base form generates no attested multi-word constructions beyond those already documented, this is stated explicitly.

The following categories of construction are distinguished where the data supports the distinction:

- **Verb-object collocations** (e.g., *doğruyu söylemek*)
- **Predicate constructions** (e.g., *haklı çıkmak*)
- **Postpositional phrases** (e.g., *gerçek anlamda*)
- **Binomials and fixed nominal phrases** (e.g., *doğru dürüst*)
- **Idiomatic expressions** (e.g., *hakkını yemek*)
- **Proverbial expressions** (e.g., *doğruya doğru, eğriye eğri demek*)

Absence of constructions is recorded explicitly for each P1 item where no common construction is identified.

## CONSTRUCTIONS BY BASE FORM

---

### Base: **doğru**

---

**Construction 1**

1. **Base Form:** doğru
2. **Example:** Doğruyu söylemek gerekirse...
3. **Transliteration:** Doğruyu söylemek gerekirse...
4. **English translation:** To tell the truth... / If I'm being honest...
5. **Usage notes:** A fixed discourse-initial formula used to introduce a frank or potentially unwelcome statement. Functions as a hedging or sincerity marker rather than a propositional claim. Extremely common across spoken and written registers. The canonical collocation for the act of making a true statement; *doğru* takes the accusative as a definite noun object ("the truth"). Used in moral exhortation, legal contexts, and everyday speech. The variant *doğru söylemek* (without accusative) is also attested and common.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 2**

1. **Base Form:** doğru
2. **Example:** doğru söylemek
3. **Transliteration:** doğru söylemek
4. **English translation:** to say something correct, to tell the truth
5. **Usage notes:** Differs from *doğruyu söylemek* in that *doğru* here functions as a predicative adverb rather than a definite noun object; the construction is slightly less emphatic. Both forms are in common use.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 3**

1. **Base Form:** doğru
2. **Example:** Doğru mu yanlış mı?
3. **Transliteration:** Doğru mu yanlış mı?
4. **English translation:** True or false? / Right or wrong?
5. **Usage notes:** A fixed interrogative construction used in educational, evaluative, and rhetorical contexts to pose a binary epistemic or moral question. The paired structure is formulaic and appears in test formats, debates, and everyday speech.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 4**

1. **Base Form:** doğru
2. **Example:** doğru yanlış sorusu
3. **Transliteration:** doğru yanlış sorusu
4. **English translation:** a true/false question
5. **Usage notes:** A fixed nominal phrase used in educational contexts to describe a question format requiring a binary true-or-false judgment. The coordinate pair *doğru yanlış* functions as a compound modifier.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 5**

1. **Base Form:** doğru
2. **Example:** Ne kadar doğru?
3. **Transliteration:** Ne kadar doğru?
4. **English translation:** How accurate is it? / How true is that?
5. **Usage notes:** A common interrogative construction used to question the degree of accuracy or truth of a claim. Used in journalistic, analytical, and everyday contexts to introduce scrutiny of a statement.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 6**

1. **Base Form:** doğru
2. **Example:** Bu doğru değil.
3. **Transliteration:** Bu doğru değil.
4. **English translation:** This is not correct. / This is not true.
5. **Usage notes:** The standard negation construction for *doğru*, using the copular negation *değil*. One of the most basic and frequent constructions in the domain. Used across all registers.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 7**

1. **Base Form:** doğru
2. **Example:** Doğru söylüyorsun ama...
3. **Transliteration:** Doğru söylüyorsun ama...
4. **English translation:** You're right, but... / What you're saying is true, but...
5. **Usage notes:** A concessive construction used to acknowledge the truth of an interlocutor's statement while introducing a qualification or counterpoint. The *ama* (but) marks the concessive structure. Extremely common in conversational and argumentative discourse.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 8**

1. **Base Form:** doğru
2. **Example:** Doğrusu şu ki...
3. **Transliteration:** Doğrusu şu ki...
4. **English translation:** The truth of the matter is that... / To be honest...
5. **Usage notes:** A discourse marker construction using the possessive form *doğrusu* (its truth / the truth of it) followed by the demonstrative-complementizer sequence *şu ki*. Used to introduce a frank or corrective statement. Common in formal and informal speech.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 9**

1. **Base Form:** doğru
2. **Example:** Doğrusu, bilmiyorum.
3. **Transliteration:** Doğrusu, bilmiyorum.
4. **English translation:** Honestly, I don't know. / To tell the truth, I don't know.
5. **Usage notes:** *Doğrusu* used as a sentence-initial adverbial meaning "honestly" or "to tell the truth," followed by a statement of uncertainty or admission. Functions as a sincerity hedge. Very common in spoken Turkish.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 10**

1. **Base Form:** doğru
2. **Example:** Tam olarak doğru değil.
3. **Transliteration:** Tam olarak doğru değil.
4. **English translation:** Not exactly correct. / Not entirely true.
5. **Usage notes:** A hedged negation construction using *tam olarak* (exactly, precisely) to soften the denial. Used when a claim is partially but not fully accurate. Common in analytical and conversational registers.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 11**

1. **Base Form:** doğru
2. **Example:** Doğru yolda olmak
3. **Transliteration:** Doğru yolda olmak
4. **English translation:** To be on the right path / To be on track
5. **Usage notes:** A fixed idiomatic construction using *doğru* in its moral/directional sense. *Doğru yol* (the right/straight path) carries both literal and figurative meanings, with strong moral and religious resonances in Turkish cultural context. Used in moral, religious, and everyday discourse. The locative predicate construction combines *doğru yol* with the locative suffix *-da* and *olmak* (to be). *Doğru yoldasınız* ("You are on the right track") is a common form.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 12**

1. **Base Form:** doğru
2. **Example:** Doğruyu yanlıştan ayırt etmek
3. **Transliteration:** Doğruyu yanlıştan ayırt etmek
4. **English translation:** To distinguish truth from falsehood / To tell right from wrong
5. **Usage notes:** A standard periphrastic construction for the epistemic or moral capacity to discriminate between correct and incorrect. *Ayırt etmek* (to distinguish, to discern) is the operative verb. Used in educational, philosophical, and moral discourse. The construction uses the ablative of separation (*yanlıştan*) with *ayırt etmek*.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 13**

1. **Base Form:** doğru
2. **Example:** Bunu doğru bulmuyorum.
3. **Transliteration:** doğru bulmak
4. **English translation:** to find correct, to consider right, to approve of
5. **Usage notes:** Predicate construction in which *doğru* functions as a secondary predicate with *bulmak* (to find, to consider). Used to express evaluative judgment that something is correct or appropriate. *Bunu doğru bulmuyorum* ("I don't find this right / I don't approve of this").
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 14**

1. **Base Form:** doğru
2. **Example:** Tahmini doğru çıktı.
3. **Transliteration:** doğru çıkmak
4. **English translation:** to turn out to be correct, to prove true
5. **Usage notes:** Predicate construction used to describe a prediction, claim, or suspicion that is subsequently confirmed by events. *Tahmini doğru çıktı* ("His/her prediction turned out to be correct"). Common in everyday and journalistic language.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 15**

1. **Base Form:** doğru
2. **Example:** Doğrudan doğruya ona söyledim.
3. **Transliteration:** doğrudan doğruya
4. **English translation:** directly, straight, without any intermediary, point-blank
5. **Usage notes:** Emphatic reduplicated adverbial phrase formed from the ablative *doğrudan* and the dative *doğruya*. More emphatic than *doğrudan* alone; stresses the complete absence of mediation or indirection. Used across registers.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 16**

1. **Base Form:** doğru
2. **Example:** Doğru dürüst bir iş yapamıyor.
3. **Transliteration:** doğru dürüst
4. **English translation:** properly, decently, adequately; (of a person) decent and honest
5. **Usage notes:** Fixed binomial pairing *doğru* (correct, proper) with *dürüst* (upright, honest — from Persian *durust*). Used as an adverbial or adjectival phrase to indicate that something is done in a fully proper manner or that a person is genuinely decent. Very common in colloquial speech. The negated form *doğru dürüst çalışmamak* (to not work properly) is more common than the positive.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 17**

1. **Base Form:** doğru
2. **Example:** Doğru düzgün konuşamıyor.
3. **Transliteration:** doğru düzgün
4. **English translation:** properly, in an orderly and correct manner
5. **Usage notes:** Colloquial binomial pairing *doğru* (correct) with *düzgün* (orderly, neat). Used to indicate that something is done in a fully adequate and proper manner. Interchangeable with *doğru dürüst* in many contexts but slightly more focused on orderliness.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 18**

1. **Base Form:** doğru
2. **Example:** Doğru yoldan çıkmak tehlikelidir.
3. **Transliteration:** doğru yol
4. **English translation:** the right path, the correct way, the straight and narrow
5. **Usage notes:** Fixed nominal phrase used in moral, religious, and practical contexts to refer to the correct course of action or life. Participates in further constructions: *doğru yoldan çıkmak* ("to stray from the right path"), *doğru yolda olmak* ("to be on the right track"). The construction *doğru yoldan sapmak* (to deviate from the right path) is also attested in moral and religious discourse; *sapmak* (to deviate) is the standard verbal complement.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 19**

1. **Base Form:** doğru
2. **Example:** doğru yolu bulmak
3. **Transliteration:** doğru yolu bulmak
4. **English translation:** to find the right path, to find the correct way
5. **Usage notes:** The positive counterpart to *doğru yoldan sapmak*; used in moral, religious, and practical contexts to describe finding the correct course of action or belief. Common in everyday and literary registers.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 20**

1. **Base Form:** doğru
2. **Example:** O doğruya doğru, eğriye eğri der.
3. **Transliteration:** doğruya doğru, eğriye eğri demek
4. **English translation:** to call a spade a spade, to call things as they are
5. **Usage notes:** Proverbial expression prescribing or praising honest, unvarnished evaluation. The structure pairs *doğru* (straight/right) with its antonym *eğri* (crooked/wrong), asserting that each should be named as it is. Used to commend or demand honest speech.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 21**

1. **Base Form:** doğru
2. **Example:** Bu iddiada bir doğruluk payı var.
3. **Transliteration:** doğruluk payı olmak / doğruluk payı var
4. **English translation:** there is a grain of truth (in something)
5. **Usage notes:** Existential construction using *doğruluk payı* (grain/share of truth) with *var* (there is) or *olmak* (to be). Used in evaluative discourse to acknowledge partial truth in a claim that is not entirely accurate. Common in journalistic and critical language. The full form is typically *Bu iddiada bir doğruluk payı var* (there is a grain of truth in this claim).
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 22**

1. **Base Form:** doğru
2. **Example:** Doğru orantılı iki değişken.
3. **Transliteration:** doğru orantılı
4. **English translation:** directly proportional
5. **Usage notes:** Fixed adjectival phrase used in mathematical and scientific registers to describe two quantities that increase or decrease together. *Hız ve mesafe doğru orantılıdır* ("Speed and distance are directly proportional"). Domain-specific; not used outside technical contexts.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 23**

1. **Base Form:** doğru
2. **Example:** Doğru açı doksan derecedir.
3. **Transliteration:** doğru açı
4. **English translation:** right angle
5. **Usage notes:** Fixed nominal phrase used in geometry and mathematics education for a 90-degree angle. Domain-specific; the word *doğru* here contributes the geometric sense "right" rather than the epistemic sense "correct/true."
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 24**

1. **Base Form:** doğru
2. **Example:** doğru bilgiye ulaşmak
3. **Transliteration:** doğru bilgiye ulaşmak
4. **English translation:** to access correct information, to reach accurate knowledge
5. **Usage notes:** Used in journalistic, educational, and philosophical contexts to describe the goal of obtaining accurate information. *Doğru bilgi* (correct information/true knowledge) is a stable nominal compound; *ulaşmak* (to reach) is the standard verbal complement in this construction.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 25**

1. **Base Form:** doğru
2. **Example:** doğruluk beyanı
3. **Transliteration:** doğruluk beyanı
4. **English translation:** declaration of accuracy, declaration of truthfulness
5. **Usage notes:** A fixed nominal phrase used in administrative and legal contexts for a formal statement attesting to the accuracy of information provided. *Beyan* (declaration, from Arabic *bayān*) is the standard noun in this administrative collocation.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

**Construction 26**

2. **Example:** doğrulama kodu
3. **Transliteration:** doğrulama kodu
4. **English translation:** verification code, authentication code
5. **Usage notes:** A fixed nominal phrase in digital security and administrative contexts for a code used to verify identity or confirm a transaction. *Doğrulama* (verification) + *kod* (code, from French/English) is a standard technical compound.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

**Construction 27**

1. **Base Form:** doğru
2. **Example:** kimlik doğrulama
3. **Transliteration:** kimlik doğrulama
4. **English translation:** identity verification, authentication
5. **Usage notes:** A fixed nominal compound used in digital security, administrative, and legal contexts. *Kimlik* (identity) + *doğrulama* (verification) is the standard Turkish term for authentication procedures.

**Construction 28**

1. **Base Form:** doğru
2. **Example:** doğruluk tablosu oluşturmak
3. **Transliteration:** doğruluk tablosu oluşturmak
4. **English translation:** to construct a truth table
5. **Usage notes:** A standard collocation in formal logic and digital circuit design. *Doğruluk tablosu* (truth table) is a fixed technical compound; *oluşturmak* (to construct, to form) is the standard verbal complement.

**Construction 29**

1. **Base Form:** doğru
2. **Example:** doğruluk değeri atamak
3. **Transliteration:** doğruluk değeri atamak
4. **English translation:** to assign a truth value
5. **Usage notes:** A technical collocation in formal logic and philosophy of language. *Doğruluk değeri* (truth value) is a fixed compound; *atamak* (to assign) is the standard verbal complement in logical and computational contexts.

**Construction 30**

1. **Base Form:** doğru
2. **Example:** bu karar doğrultusunda hareket etmek
3. **Transliteration:** bu karar doğrultusunda hareket etmek
4. **English translation:** to act in accordance with this decision, to act in line with this decision
5. **Usage notes:** A standard formal and administrative construction using *doğrultusunda* (in the direction/line of) as a postpositional phrase. The construction is productive: any noun can replace *karar* (decision).

**Construction 31**

1. **Base Form:** doğru

3. **Transliteration:** doğruya en yakın açıklama
4. **English translation:** the explanation closest to the truth
5. **Usage notes:** Used in evaluative and epistemic contexts to describe the best available approximation when certainty is not achievable. The superlative construction *en yakın* (closest) with the dative *doğruya* is productive and not fixed to a single noun.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

3. **Transliteration:** doğruluk testi uygulamak
4. **English translation:** to apply a truth test, to fact-check
5. **Usage notes:** Used in journalistic fact-checking and scientific contexts. *Doğruluk testi* (truth/accuracy test) is a fixed compound; *uygulamak* (to apply) is the standard verbal complement.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

**Construction 33**

1. **Base Form:** doğru
2. **Example:** doğru parçası
3. **Transliteration:** doğru parçası
4. **English translation:** line segment
5. **Usage notes:** A fixed mathematical term in which *doğru* functions in its geometric sense (straight line) rather than its epistemic sense. *Parça* (piece, segment) + possessive suffix. Used in geometry education and technical contexts.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 34**

1. **Base Form:** doğru
2. **Example:** Doğru orantılı iki değişken.
3. **Transliteration:** doğruluk adına
4. **English translation:** in the name of truth, for the sake of truthfulness
5. **Usage notes:** Postpositional construction using *adına* (in the name of, for the sake of) with *doğruluk*. Used in formal and rhetorical registers to invoke truthfulness as a justification for a statement. Less common than *gerçek adına* but attested.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

> **Absence note:** No common construction of the type "to be true" using *doğru* as a predicate with a propositional subject has been identified as a fixed multi-word unit; this function is typically expressed through *doğru olmak* (to be correct/true), which is a regular predicative construction rather than a fixed collocation. No fixed idiom using *doğru* to mean "sincere" (as opposed to "correct") has been identified; this function is covered by *dürüst* and *özü sözü bir*.

### Base: **yanlış**

---

**Construction 1**

1. **Base Form:** yanlış
2. **Example:** Yanlış anlaşılmasın.
3. **Transliteration:** Yanlış anlaşılmasın.
4. **English translation:** Don't get me wrong. / Let there be no misunderstanding.
5. **Usage notes:** A fixed formulaic construction using the negative optative of *yanlış anlaşılmak* (to be misunderstood). Used as a preemptive disclaimer before a potentially sensitive statement. Extremely common in spoken and written Turkish. *Yanlış anlaşılmak istemiyorum* (I don't want to be misunderstood) is a parallel form.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 2**

1. **Base Form:** yanlış
2. **Example:** yanlış anlamak
3. **Transliteration:** yanlış anlamak
4. **English translation:** to misunderstand, to understand incorrectly
5. **Usage notes:** A highly frequent collocation in everyday speech for the act of misunderstanding. *Yanlış anladım* (I misunderstood). *Yanlış* functions as an adverbial modifier of *anlamak* (to understand).
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 3**

1. **Base Form:** yanlış
2. **Example:** Yanlış bir izlenim vermek
3. **Transliteration:** Yanlış bir izlenim vermek
4. **English translation:** To give a false impression / To create a misleading impression
5. **Usage notes:** A periphrastic construction describing the act of creating an inaccurate impression in another person's mind. *İzlenim vermek* (to give an impression) is the operative phrase. Used in legal, journalistic, and everyday evaluative contexts.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 4**

1. **Base Form:** yanlış
2. **Example:** yanlış bilgi
3. **Transliteration:** yanlış bilgi
4. **English translation:** incorrect information, misinformation
5. **Usage notes:** A standard nominal phrase used in journalistic, educational, and everyday contexts to describe information that is not accurate. *Yanlış bilgi yaymak* (to spread misinformation). Common across registers.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 5**

1. **Base Form:** yanlış
2. **Example:** Yanlış bilgi vermek.
3. **Transliteration:** yanlış bilgi vermek
4. **English translation:** to give incorrect information, to misinform
5. **Usage notes:** Adverbial-nominal construction in which *yanlış* modifies *bilgi* (information) and the whole phrase functions as the object of *vermek* (to give). Used in journalistic, legal, and administrative contexts to describe the provision of incorrect information.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

**Construction 6**

**Construction 7**

**Construction 8**

3. **Transliteration:** yanlış yapmak
4. **English translation:** to make a mistake, to do something wrong
5. **Usage notes:** A standard collocation in everyday speech for committing an error or doing something incorrectly. *Yanlış yaptım* (I made a mistake / I did something wrong). Extremely common.

**Construction 9**

**Construction 12**

5. **Usage notes:** A well-known Turkish proverb warning that errors in planning or judgment will eventually catch up with one. Used in everyday and proverbial discourse to caution against overconfidence or poor planning. The reference to Baghdad reflects historical Ottoman trade routes.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

**Construction 16**
