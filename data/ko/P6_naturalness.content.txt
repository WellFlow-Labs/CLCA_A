# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P6 CHANGE, CAUSATION, AND STABILITY
# Language: Korean (ko)

---

## 1. BECOMING TRUE / ACQUIRING TRUTH-STATUS

### 1.1 진실이 되다 (jinsiri doeda) — "become truth"

**Construction:** NP + 이/가 진실이 되다
**Transliteration:** NP-i/ga jinsiri doeda
**English translation:** "X becomes truth" / "X becomes true"
**Usage context:** Used when something previously false, uncertain, or hypothetical becomes established as truth. Common in proverbial expressions about lies becoming accepted as truth through repetition, or predictions becoming reality.

**Example:** 거짓말도 백번 하면 진실이 된다
**Transliteration:** geojinmaldo baekbeon hamyeon jinsiri doenda
**Translation:** "If you tell a lie a hundred times, it becomes truth"
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Proverbial expression, highly conventionalized

**Notes:** This construction is grammatically productive but semantically restricted. It typically appears in:
- Proverbial/philosophical contexts about transformation of falsehood to accepted truth
- Predictions or hypotheticals becoming reality (예언이 진실이 되다 "the prophecy becomes true")
- Less common for simple factual verification

---

### 1.2 사실이 되다 (sasiri doeda) — "become fact"

**Construction:** NP + 이/가 사실이 되다
**Transliteration:** NP-i/ga sasiri doeda
**English translation:** "X becomes fact" / "X becomes reality"
**Usage context:** Used when plans, hopes, or possibilities become actual facts or reality. More common than 진실이 되다 for everyday transformations from potential to actual.

**Example:** 꿈이 사실이 되다
**Transliteration:** kkumi sasiri doeda
**Translation:** "A dream becomes reality"
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Common in everyday speech

**Example:** 소문이 사실이 되다
**Transliteration:** somuni sasiri doeda
**Translation:** "A rumor becomes fact"
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Common in narrative contexts

**Notes:** More productive than 진실이 되다. Used for:
- Dreams, plans, hopes becoming reality
- Rumors, suspicions being confirmed as facts
- Possibilities becoming actualities

---

### 1.3 확실해지다 (hwaksilhaejida) — "become certain"

**Construction:** NP + 이/가 확실해지다
**Transliteration:** NP-i/ga hwaksilhaejida
**English translation:** "X becomes certain" / "X becomes definite"
**Usage context:** Used when something uncertain becomes certain or definite. Describes increase in certainty or definiteness of facts, situations, or knowledge.

**Example:** 사실이 확실해지다
**Transliteration:** sasiri hwaksilhaejida
**Translation:** "The facts become certain"
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Common in investigative and analytical contexts

**Notes:** Productive inchoative using -아/어지다 suffix. Can apply to:
- Facts becoming established with certainty
- Situations becoming definite
- Knowledge becoming confirmed

---

### 1.4 명백해지다 (myeongbaekaejida) — "become obvious/clear"

**Construction:** NP + 이/가 명백해지다
**Transliteration:** NP-i/ga myeongbaekaejida
**English translation:** "X becomes obvious" / "X becomes clear"
**Usage context:** Used when truth or facts that were unclear become obvious or evident. Emphasizes clarity or obviousness emerging.

**Example:** 진실이 명백해지다
**Transliteration:** jinsiri myeongbaekaejida
**Translation:** "The truth becomes obvious"
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Common in formal and analytical discourse

**Notes:** Emphasizes epistemic accessibility rather than ontological change. The truth existed but becomes clear or obvious.

---

### 1.5 분명해지다 (bunmyeonghaejida) — "become clear/evident"

**Construction:** NP + 이/가 분명해지다
**Transliteration:** NP-i/ga bunmyeonghaejida
**English translation:** "X becomes clear" / "X becomes evident"
**Usage context:** Similar to 명백해지다, used when facts or truth become clear or evident. Slightly less formal.

**Example:** 사실이 분명해지다
**Transliteration:** sasiri bunmyeonghaejida
**Translation:** "The facts become clear"
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Common across registers

**Notes:** Can apply to both facts becoming established and existing facts becoming clear to observers.

---

### 1.6 진짜가 되다 (jinjjaga doeda) — "become real"

**Construction:** NP + 이/가 진짜가 되다
**Transliteration:** NP-i/ga jinjjaga doeda
**English translation:** "X becomes real" / "X becomes genuine"
**Usage context:** Colloquial expression for something becoming real or genuine. Less common than 사실이 되다 but used in everyday speech.

**Example:** 가짜가 진짜가 되다
**Transliteration:** gajjaga jinjjaga doeda
**Translation:** "The fake becomes real"
**Naturalness:** [MARGINAL] — Grammatically possible but uncommon; more often expressed as 진짜로 밝혀지다 "revealed to be real"

**Notes:** Less productive than 사실이 되다. The transformation from fake to genuine is more commonly expressed through revelation (밝혀지다) than transformation (되다).

## 2. BECOMING FALSE / LOSING TRUTH-STATUS

### 2.1 거짓이 되다 (geojisi doeda) — "become false"

**Construction:** NP + 이/가 거짓이 되다
**Transliteration:** NP-i/ga geojisi doeda
**English translation:** "X becomes false" / "X becomes a lie"
**Usage context:** Used when something previously true or believed becomes false or revealed as false. Less common than the reverse transformation (거짓→진실).

**Example:** 진실이 거짓이 되다
**Transliteration:** jinsiri geojisi doeda
**Translation:** "Truth becomes falsehood"
**Naturalness:** [MARGINAL] — Grammatically possible but uncommon; typically used in philosophical or paradoxical contexts

**Notes:** Rare in everyday usage. The concept of truth becoming false is philosophically problematic, so this construction appears mainly in:
- Philosophical paradoxes
- Contexts where "truth" means "accepted belief" that is later disproven
- Rhetorical or poetic contexts

More common to say: 거짓으로 밝혀지다 "revealed to be false" or 거짓임이 드러나다 "exposed as false"

---

### 2.2 틀리게 되다 (teullige doeda) — "become wrong"

**Construction:** NP + 이/가 틀리게 되다
**Transliteration:** NP-i/ga teullige doeda
**English translation:** "X becomes wrong" / "X turns out to be wrong"
**Usage context:** Used when something believed to be correct turns out to be wrong, or when correct information becomes outdated/incorrect.

**Example:** 답이 틀리게 되다
**Transliteration:** dabi teullige doeda
**Translation:** "The answer becomes wrong" / "The answer turns out to be wrong"
**Naturalness:** [MARGINAL] — More commonly expressed as 틀린 것으로 드러나다 "revealed to be wrong"

**Notes:** The construction 틀리게 되다 is less natural than resultative expressions. Koreans prefer:
- 틀렸다는 것이 밝혀지다 "it was revealed to be wrong"
- 틀린 것으로 판명되다 "determined to be wrong"

---

### 2.3 가짜가 되다 (gajjaga doeda) — "become fake"

**Construction:** NP + 이/가 가짜가 되다
**Transliteration:** NP-i/ga gajjaga doeda
**English translation:** "X becomes fake" / "X becomes counterfeit"
**Usage context:** Rare construction. Authenticity is typically treated as inherent rather than acquired, so transformation to fake status is uncommon.

**Example:** (no common natural expression)
**Naturalness:** [MARGINAL] — Grammatically possible but semantically odd

**Notes:** The concept of something genuine becoming fake is problematic. More natural expressions:
- 가짜로 판명되다 "determined to be fake"
- 가짜임이 드러나다 "revealed to be fake"
- 위조되다 "be forged/counterfeited" (for the act of creating fakes)

---

## 3. REVELATION AND DISCOVERY (TRUTH COMING TO LIGHT)

### 3.1 진실이 밝혀지다 (jinsiri balkhyeojida) — "truth is revealed"

**Construction:** NP + 이/가 밝혀지다
**Transliteration:** NP-i/ga balkhyeojida
**English translation:** "X is revealed" / "X comes to light"
**Usage context:** Very common construction for truth being revealed or discovered through investigation or time. Passive form of 밝히다 "reveal."

**Example:** 진실이 밝혀지다
**Transliteration:** jinsiri balkhyeojida
**Translation:** "The truth is revealed"
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Extremely common in investigative, journalistic, and narrative contexts

**Notes:** This is the primary construction for truth revelation in Korean. Highly productive with:
- 진실 (truth)
- 사실 (facts)
- 진상 (truth of the matter)
- 실체 (actual substance/reality)

---

### 3.2 진실이 드러나다 (jinsiri deureonada) — "truth emerges"

**Construction:** NP + 이/가 드러나다
**Transliteration:** NP-i/ga deureonada
**English translation:** "X emerges" / "X is revealed" / "X comes to light"
**Usage context:** Common intransitive construction for truth emerging or becoming visible. Emphasizes natural emergence rather than active revelation.

**Example:** 진실이 드러나다
**Transliteration:** jinsiri deureonada
**Translation:** "The truth emerges" / "The truth comes to light"
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Very common, slightly less formal than 밝혀지다

**Notes:** Productive with same nouns as 밝혀지다. Subtle difference:
- 밝혀지다 implies investigative effort
- 드러나다 implies natural emergence or exposure

---

### 3.3 사실로 밝혀지다 (sasilro balkhyeojida) — "revealed to be fact"

**Construction:** NP + 이/가 사실로 밝혀지다
**Transliteration:** NP-i/ga sasilro balkhyeojida
**English translation:** "X is revealed to be fact" / "X turns out to be true"
**Usage context:** Common construction for something suspected or claimed being confirmed as factual.

**Example:** 소문이 사실로 밝혀지다
**Transliteration:** somuni sasilro balkhyeojida
**Translation:** "The rumor is revealed to be fact" / "The rumor turns out to be true"
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Very common in news and everyday narrative

**Notes:** The pattern "NP + (으)로 밝혀지다" is highly productive:
- 거짓으로 밝혀지다 "revealed to be false"
- 진짜로 밝혀지다 "revealed to be real"
- 가짜로 밝혀지다 "revealed to be fake"

---

### 3.4 진상이 드러나다 (jinsangi deureonada) — "truth of the matter emerges"

**Construction:** 진상 + 이/가 드러나다
**Transliteration:** jinsangi deureonada
**English translation:** "The truth of the matter emerges" / "The real situation is revealed"
**Usage context:** Common in investigative and journalistic contexts for the actual circumstances being revealed.

**Example:** 사건의 진상이 드러나다
**Transliteration:** sageone jinsangi deureonada
**Translation:** "The truth of the incident is revealed"
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Very common in news and investigative discourse

**Notes:** 진상 specifically refers to the true state of affairs in investigative contexts, often implying that false appearances or cover-ups are being penetrated.

---

### 3.5 실체가 드러나다 (silchega deureonada) — "actual substance is revealed"

**Construction:** 실체 + 가 드러나다
**Transliteration:** silchega deureonada
**English translation:** "The actual substance/reality is revealed" / "The true nature is exposed"
**Usage context:** Used when the actual nature or substance of something is revealed, often after being hidden or misrepresented.

**Example:** 그의 실체가 드러나다
**Transliteration:** geuui silchega deureonada
**Translation:** "His true nature is revealed"
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Common in contexts of exposure or unmasking

**Notes:** Often used for revealing true character or nature that was previously hidden or disguised.

---

## 4. CONCEALMENT AND OBSCURATION

### 4.1 진실이 숨겨지다 (jinsiri sumgyeojida) — "truth is hidden"

**Construction:** NP + 이/가 숨겨지다
**Transliteration:** NP-i/ga sumgyeojida
**English translation:** "X is hidden" / "X is concealed"
**Usage context:** Passive construction for truth being hidden or concealed. Less common than active 진실을 숨기다 "hide the truth."

**Example:** 진실이 숨겨지다
**Transliteration:** jinsiri sumgyeojida
**Translation:** "The truth is hidden"
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Common in contexts of cover-ups and concealment

**Notes:** Passive form emphasizes the state of being hidden rather than the act of hiding. Often used in:
- Investigative contexts
- Discussions of cover-ups
- Historical revelations

---

### 4.2 사실이 가려지다 (sasiri garyeojida) — "facts are obscured"

**Construction:** NP + 이/가 가려지다
**Transliteration:** NP-i/ga garyeojida
**English translation:** "X is obscured" / "X is covered up"
**Usage context:** Used when facts are obscured or covered, making them difficult to see or know.

**Example:** 사실이 가려지다
**Transliteration:** sasiri garyeojida
**Translation:** "The facts are obscured"
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Common in formal and investigative contexts

**Notes:** 가려지다 (from 가리다 "cover, obscure") emphasizes obstruction of view or knowledge rather than active hiding.

---

### 4.3 진실이 묻히다 (jinsiri muthida) — "truth is buried"

**Construction:** NP + 이/가 묻히다
**Transliteration:** NP-i/ga muthida
**English translation:** "X is buried" / "X is suppressed"
**Usage context:** Metaphorical construction for truth being buried or suppressed, often by powerful interests or passage of time.

**Example:** 진실이 묻히다
**Transliteration:** jinsiri muthida
**Translation:** "The truth is buried" / "The truth is suppressed"
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Common in contexts of historical injustice or suppression

**Notes:** Carries connotations of:
- Deliberate suppression by authorities
- Truth lost to time or neglect
- Injustice or cover-up

---

## 5. CAUSATIVE CONSTRUCTIONS (MAKING SOMETHING TRUE/FALSE)

### 5.1 진실을 밝히다 (jinsireul balkhida) — "reveal the truth"

**Construction:** Agent + 진실을 밝히다
**Transliteration:** Agent + jinsireul balkhida
**English translation:** "X reveals the truth" / "X brings the truth to light"
**Usage context:** Active causative construction where an agent reveals or clarifies truth through investigation or disclosure.

**Example:** 검사가 진실을 밝히다
**Transliteration:** geomsaga jinsireul balkhida
**Translation:** "The prosecutor reveals the truth"
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Extremely common in investigative and legal contexts

**Notes:** This is the primary active construction for truth revelation. Highly productive with:
- 진실 (truth)
- 사실 (facts)
- 진상 (truth of the matter)

---

### 5.2 사실을 확인하다 (sasireul hwaginhada) — "verify the facts"

**Construction:** Agent + 사실을 확인하다
**Transliteration:** Agent + sasireul hwaginhada
**English translation:** "X verifies the facts" / "X confirms the facts"
**Usage context:** Active construction for verifying or confirming factual accuracy.

**Example:** 기자가 사실을 확인하다
**Transliteration:** gijaga sasireul hwaginhada
**Translation:** "The reporter verifies the facts"
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Very common in journalistic and investigative contexts

**Notes:** Emphasizes active verification process rather than revelation of hidden truth.

---

### 5.3 진실을 숨기다 (jinsireul sumgida) — "hide the truth"

**Construction:** Agent + 진실을 숨기다
**Transliteration:** Agent + jinsireul sumgida
**English translation:** "X hides the truth" / "X conceals the truth"
**Usage context:** Active construction for deliberately hiding or concealing truth.

**Example:** 정부가 진실을 숨기다
**Transliteration:** jeongbuga jinsireul sumgida
**Translation:** "The government hides the truth"
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Common in contexts of cover-ups and deception

**Notes:** Implies deliberate concealment. Productive with:
- 진실 (truth)
- 사실 (facts)
- 진상 (truth of the matter)

---

### 5.4 사실을 왜곡하다 (sasireul waegokada) — "distort the facts"

**Construction:** Agent + 사실을 왜곡하다
**Transliteration:** Agent + sasireul waegokada
**English translation:** "X distorts the facts" / "X twists the truth"
**Usage context:** Active construction for deliberately misrepresenting or distorting factual information.

**Example:** 언론이 사실을 왜곡하다
**Transliteration:** eoloni sasireul waegokada
**Translation:** "The media distorts the facts"
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Common in media criticism and political discourse

**Notes:** Implies active manipulation rather than simple concealment. Used in contexts of:
- Media bias
- Propaganda
- Dishonest reporting

---

### 5.5 믿게 하다 (mitge hada) — "make believe"

**Construction:** Agent + Object + 을/를 믿게 하다
**Transliteration:** Agent + Object-eul/reul mitge hada
**English translation:** "X makes Y believe Z" / "X convinces Y"
**Usage context:** Analytic causative construction for causing someone to believe something.

**Example:** 거짓말로 사람들을 믿게 하다
**Transliteration:** geojinmallo saramdeureul mitge hada
**Translation:** "Make people believe through lies" / "Deceive people into believing"
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Common in contexts of persuasion and deception

**Notes:** This is the standard causative construction for belief. Can be used for:
- Honest persuasion (진실을 믿게 하다 "make believe the truth")
- Deception (거짓을 믿게 하다 "make believe falsehood")

---

### 5.6 속이다 (sogida) — "deceive"

**Construction:** Agent + Object + 을/를 속이다
**Transliteration:** Agent + Object-eul/reul sogida
**English translation:** "X deceives Y" / "X tricks Y"
**Usage context:** Lexical causative verb for causing someone to believe falsehood through deception.

**Example:** 거짓말로 사람을 속이다
**Transliteration:** geojinmallo sarameul sogida
**Translation:** "Deceive a person with lies"
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Very common verb for deception

**Notes:** This is a lexical causative, not a productive pattern. It specifically means causing false belief through deception. No corresponding verb for "causing true belief" exists; that concept requires construction (진실을 알리다 "inform of truth," 진실을 믿게 하다 "make believe truth").

---

### 5.7 진정시키다 (jinjeongsikida) — "calm down, pacify"

**Construction:** Agent + Object + 을/를 진정시키다
**Transliteration:** Agent + Object-eul/reul jinjeongsikida
**English translation:** "X calms Y down" / "X pacifies Y"
**Usage context:** Lexical causative using -시키다 suffix, but with specialized meaning beyond truth-domain.

**Example:** 흥분한 사람을 진정시키다
**Transliteration:** heungbunhan sarameul jinjeongsikida
**Translation:** "Calm down an excited person"
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Common in contexts of emotional regulation

**Notes:** While this uses the root 진정 (calm/settle), it has moved beyond truth-domain semantics into emotional/physical calming. Not a productive pattern for truth-domain causatives.

---

### 5.8 확실하게 하다 (hwaksilhage hada) — "make certain"

**Construction:** Agent + NP + 을/를 확실하게 하다
**Transliteration:** Agent + NP-eul/reul hwaksilhage hada
**English translation:** "X makes Y certain" / "X ensures Y"
**Usage context:** Analytic causative construction for making something certain or definite.

**Example:** 사실을 확실하게 하다
**Transliteration:** sasireul hwaksilhage hada
**Translation:** "Make the facts certain" / "Establish the facts definitively"
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Common in formal and planning contexts

**Notes:** Can mean:
- Making facts certain through verification
- Ensuring something happens definitively
- Establishing certainty about situations

---

### 5.9 명백하게 하다 (myeongbaekhage hada) — "make clear/obvious"

**Construction:** Agent + NP + 을/를 명백하게 하다
**Transliteration:** Agent + NP-eul/reul myeongbaekhage hada
**English translation:** "X makes Y clear" / "X clarifies Y"
**Usage context:** Analytic causative construction for making something clear or obvious.

**Example:** 진실을 명백하게 하다
**Transliteration:** jinsireul myeongbaekhage hada
**Translation:** "Make the truth clear" / "Clarify the truth"
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Common in formal discourse

**Notes:** Emphasizes making truth epistemically accessible rather than ontologically establishing it.

## 6. MAINTAINING TRUTH-STATUS (STABILITY)

### 6.1 진실을 지키다 (jinsireul jikida) — "protect/maintain the truth"

**Construction:** Agent + 진실을 지키다
**Transliteration:** Agent + jinsireul jikida
**English translation:** "X protects the truth" / "X maintains the truth"
**Usage context:** Construction for actively protecting or maintaining truth against threats or distortion.

**Example:** 진실을 지키다
**Transliteration:** jinsireul jikida
**Translation:** "Protect the truth" / "Uphold the truth"
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Common in moral and institutional contexts

**Notes:** Implies active effort to maintain truth against:
- Attempts at concealment
- Distortion or manipulation
- Forgetting or neglect

---

### 6.2 진실을 고수하다 (jinsireul gosuhada) — "stick to the truth"

**Construction:** Agent + 진실을 고수하다
**Transliteration:** Agent + jinsireul gosuhada
**English translation:** "X sticks to the truth" / "X adheres to the truth"
**Usage context:** Construction for maintaining commitment to truth despite pressure or temptation.

**Example:** 진실을 고수하다
**Transliteration:** jinsireul gosuhada
**Translation:** "Stick to the truth" / "Adhere to the truth"
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Common in contexts of moral courage

**Notes:** Emphasizes steadfast commitment in face of:
- Pressure to lie or distort
- Threats or intimidation
- Temptation to compromise

---

### 6.3 정직을 유지하다 (jeongjireul yujihada) — "maintain honesty"

**Construction:** Agent + 정직을 유지하다
**Transliteration:** Agent + jeongjireul yujihada
**English translation:** "X maintains honesty" / "X keeps being honest"
**Usage context:** Construction for maintaining honest character or conduct over time.

**Example:** 정직을 유지하다
**Transliteration:** jeongjireul yujihada
**Translation:** "Maintain honesty" / "Keep being honest"
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Common in moral and character contexts

**Notes:** Emphasizes ongoing maintenance of honest character rather than single acts of truth-telling.

---

### 6.4 진실이 변하지 않다 (jinsiri byeonhaji anta) — "truth does not change"

**Construction:** 진실 + 이/가 변하지 않다
**Transliteration:** jinsiri byeonhaji anta
**English translation:** "The truth does not change" / "The truth remains unchanged"
**Usage context:** Construction asserting stability or immutability of truth.

**Example:** 진실은 변하지 않는다
**Transliteration:** jinsireun byeonhaji anneunda
**Translation:** "Truth does not change"
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Common in philosophical and moral discourse

**Notes:** Used to assert:
- Objective nature of truth
- Permanence of facts
- Contrast with changing opinions or beliefs

---

### 6.5 사실은 사실이다 (sasireun sasirida) — "a fact is a fact"

**Construction:** 사실은 사실이다
**Transliteration:** sasireun sasirida
**English translation:** "A fact is a fact" / "Facts are facts"
**Usage context:** Tautological construction asserting immutability or objectivity of facts.

**Example:** 사실은 사실이다
**Transliteration:** sasireun sasirida
**Translation:** "A fact is a fact"
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Common emphatic assertion

**Notes:** Used to:
- Assert objectivity of facts
- Reject attempts to deny or distort facts
- Emphasize that facts don't change based on opinion

## 7. LOSS OF CERTAINTY OR CREDIBILITY

### 7.1 확실성을 잃다 (hwaksilseongeul ilta) — "lose certainty"

**Construction:** NP + 이/가 확실성을 잃다
**Transliteration:** NP-i/ga hwaksilseongeul ilta
**English translation:** "X loses certainty" / "X becomes uncertain"
**Usage context:** Construction for loss of certainty or definiteness about facts or situations.

**Example:** 사실이 확실성을 잃다
**Transliteration:** sasiri hwaksilseongeul ilta
**Translation:** "The facts lose certainty" / "The facts become uncertain"
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Common in analytical contexts

**Notes:** Used when:
- New evidence creates doubt
- Previously certain facts become questionable
- Confidence in knowledge decreases

---

### 7.2 신뢰를 잃다 (sinroereul ilta) — "lose trust/credibility"

**Construction:** NP + 이/가 신뢰를 잃다
**Transliteration:** NP-i/ga sinroereul ilta
**English translation:** "X loses trust" / "X loses credibility"
**Usage context:** Construction for loss of trustworthiness or credibility.

**Example:** 언론이 신뢰를 잃다
**Transliteration:** eoloni sinroereul ilta
**Translation:** "The media loses credibility"
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Very common in institutional and interpersonal contexts

**Notes:** Used for:
- Institutions losing public trust
- Persons losing credibility
- Sources losing reliability

---

### 7.3 믿음을 잃다 (mideumeul ilta) — "lose faith/trust"

**Construction:** Agent + 믿음을 잃다
**Transliteration:** Agent + mideumeul ilta
**English translation:** "X loses faith" / "X loses trust"

## 14. SUMMARY OF PATTERNS

### Highly Productive Patterns:
1. **Revelation constructions**: 밝혀지다, 드러나다 — extremely common and productive
2. **Concealment constructions**: 숨겨지다, 가려지다, 묻히다 — productive
3. **Active causatives**: 밝히다, 숨기다, 왜곡하다 — highly productive
4. **Analytic causatives**: -게 하다 pattern — productive with truth-domain terms
5. **Aspectual patterns**: -고 있다, -아/어 가다, -아/어 내다 — fully productive
6. **Modal patterns**: -(으)ㄹ 수 있다, -아/어야 하다 — fully productive

### Restricted or Uncommon Patterns:
1. **Transformation to false**: 거짓이 되다, 틀리게 되다 — grammatically possible but semantically restricted
2. **Transformation to fake**: 가짜가 되다 — rare, semantically problematic
3. **Lexical causatives**: Limited to specific verbs (속이다); no general pattern

### Asymmetries:
1. **Truth revelation vs. concealment**: Both productive, but revelation constructions more common in discourse
2. **Becoming true vs. becoming false**: Becoming true (사실이 되다) much more common than becoming false
3. **Gaining vs. losing credibility**: Both productive, but losing credibility more frequently discussed
4. **Active vs. passive**: Both productive, but passive revelation (밝혀지다) extremely common in news discourse

### Cultural Patterns:
1. **Truth as emerging**: Strong preference for constructions showing truth emerging or being revealed (밝혀지다, 드러나다)
2. **Time and truth**: Common expressions linking passage of time with truth revelation
3. **Stability of truth**: Frequent assertions that truth doesn't change (진실은 변하지 않는다)
4. **Moral dimension**: Truth revelation often framed as moral obligation (진실을 밝혀야 한다)
