# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P1b ROOT EXPANSION
# Language: Basque (eu)

## Step 1 — Identify Central Roots

### Root 1: **egia** (truth)
- **Participates in:** 5 P1 items directly (egia, egiazkoa, egiazkotasuna, egiazkotua, egiazkotu)
- **Core meaning contribution:** The fundamental concept of truth, reality, or what corresponds to facts. Serves as the primary lexical anchor for truth-related vocabulary in Basque.

### Root 2: **zuzen** (straight/correct)
- **Participates in:** 2 P1 items directly (zuzena, zuzentasuna)
- **Core meaning contribution:** Combines physical straightness with abstract correctness and moral rightness. Provides the primary term for correctness and proper alignment with standards.

### Root 3: **gezur** (lie/falsehood)
- **Participates in:** 2 P1 items directly (gezurra, gezurrezko)
- **Core meaning contribution:** The core concept of deliberate falsehood or untruth. Primary native term for lies and deception.

## Step 2 — Expand Each Root

### ROOT 1: **egia** (truth)

1. **egia esan**
   - Literal: truth say
   - Translation: to tell the truth
   - Context: Common collocation used when speaking honestly or making a confession. Appears in everyday conversation and formal testimony.

2. **egiatan**
   - Literal: egia + -tan (locative/manner)
   - Translation: in truth, truly, really
   - Context: Adverbial expression used to emphasize veracity or reality of a statement. Common in both spoken and written discourse.
   - [CORE]

3. **egiazko**
   - Literal: egia + -zko (relational suffix, without article)
   - Translation: true, real, genuine
   - Context: Attributive adjectival form used before nouns. Appears in all registers to indicate authenticity or reality.
   - [CORE]

4. **egiazki**
   - Literal: egia + -zki (adverbial suffix)
   - Translation: truly, really, indeed
   - Context: Adverbial form emphasizing truth or reality. Used in formal and literary contexts more than everyday speech.
   - [CORE]

5. **egiazko maitasuna**

   - [CORE]

### ROOT 2: **zuzen** (straight/correct)

1. **zuzendu**
   - Literal: zuzen + -du (verbalizer)
   - Translation: to correct, to straighten, to rectify
   - Context: Common verb used for correcting errors, straightening physical objects, or rectifying situations. Used across all registers.
   - [CORE]

2. **zuzenketa**
   - Literal: zuzen + -keta (action nominalization)
   - Translation: correction, rectification, amendment
   - Context: Nominal form for the act of correcting. Used in educational, editorial, and administrative contexts.
   - [CORE]

3. **zuzenean**
   - Literal: zuzen + -ean (locative)
   - Translation: directly, straight, live (broadcast)
   - Context: Adverbial expression meaning directly or without intermediary. Also used for live broadcasts in media contexts.
   - [CORE]

4. **zuzen-zuzenean**
   - Literal: straight-straight-LOC (reduplication)
   - Translation: directly, straight away, immediately
   - Context: Emphatic form indicating directness or immediacy. Common in spoken language for emphasis.
   - [CORE]

5. **zuzenbide**
   - Literal: zuzen + bide (way/path)
   - Translation: law, jurisprudence, right
   - Context: Technical term for law or legal system. Central term in legal discourse, emphasizing law as "the right way."
   - [CORE]

6. **zuzenbidea**
   - Literal: zuzen + bide + -a (article)

### ROOT 3: **gezur** (lie/falsehood)

2. **gezurti**
   - Literal: gezur + -ti (adverbial suffix)
   - Translation: falsely, lyingly, deceitfully
   - Context: Adverbial form indicating manner of speaking or acting falsely. Used in formal and literary contexts.

   - Literal: gezur + -ta- (verbalizer) + -tu (perfective)

## Step 3 — Note Peripheral Extensions

### Extensions of **egia** (truth):

1. **egiazko balioa** (true value) — The root extends from epistemic truth to aesthetic and economic evaluation, where "true" indicates essential or intrinsic worth rather than factual accuracy.

2. **egiazko maitasuna** (true love) — Extension into emotional and relational authenticity, where "true" marks genuine feeling rather than correspondence to facts.

3. **egiazko izaera** (true nature) — Extension into essential or metaphysical characterization, where "true" indicates essence or fundamental character rather than factual truth.

4. **egiaztagiri** (certificate) — Extension into administrative/documentary function, where the truth-root serves to mark official validation rather than truth-content per se.

### Extensions of **zuzen** (straight/correct):

1. **zuzenbide** (law/jurisprudence) — The root extends from correctness to legal/normative domain, where "straight" becomes institutionalized as "law" or "legal right."

2. **zuzendaritza/zuzendari** (direction/director) — Extension into organizational and artistic domains (film direction, orchestra conducting), where "straightening" becomes "directing" or "guiding."

3. **zuzenean** (live broadcast) — Extension into media/temporal domain, where "direct" acquires technical meaning of simultaneous transmission.

4. **zuzen ibili** (behave properly) — Extension from physical straightness to moral conduct, where spatial metaphor becomes ethical evaluation.

### Extensions of **gezur** (lie):

1. **gezur txikia** (white lie) — The root participates in scalar/degree marking where size modifiers create moral gradations of falsehood.

2. **gezurrezko itxura** (false appearance) — Extension from deliberate falsehood to mere appearance or semblance, where intentional deception is not necessarily implied.

3. **gezurra dirudi** (it seems like a lie) — Extension into epistemic evaluation of plausibility rather than actual falsehood, marking subjective incredulity.
