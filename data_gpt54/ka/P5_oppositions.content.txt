1. **ნამდვილი** — **ყალბი**  
   **Transliteration:** *namdvili* — *q’albi*  
   **English translation:** “real; genuine; authentic” — “false; fake; counterfeit”  
   **Description of contrast:** This is the clearest lexical opposition in the material: **ნამდვილი** marks genuineness/authenticity, while **ყალბი** marks falseness, forgery, or insincerity. The contrast is supported both at the single-word level and in parallel noun phrases such as documents and signatures.  
   **Contrast type:** lexical  
   **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

2. **ნამდვილობა** — **სიყალბე**  
   **Transliteration:** *namdviloba* — *siq’albe*  
   **English translation:** “genuineness; authenticity” — “falseness; counterfeit nature; insincerity”  
   **Description of contrast:** These abstract nouns oppose the quality of being genuine to the quality of being false or fake. The contrast is directly supported by the descriptions of each item and by their use in authentication vs falseness contexts.  
   **Contrast type:** lexical  
   **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

3. **ნამდვილი დოკუმენტი** — **ყალბი დოკუმენტი**  
   **Transliteration:** *namdvili dok’ument’i* — *q’albi dok’ument’i*  
   **English translation:** “authentic document” — “fake/forged document”  
   **Description of contrast:** This is an attested adnominal opposition built on the same noun. It shows a direct contrast between authenticated genuineness and forgery in legal/administrative use.  
   **Contrast type:** constructional  
   **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

4. **ნამდვილი ხელმოწერა** — **ყალბი ხელმოწერა**

5. **სიმართლეს შეესაბამება** — **სიმართლეს არ შეესაბამება**  
   **Transliteration:** *simartles sheesabameba* — *simartles ar sheesabameba*  
   **English translation:** “corresponds to the truth; is true” — “does not correspond to the truth; is untrue”  
   **Description of contrast:** This is an explicit positive vs negative constructional opposition. The contrast is formed analytically with **არ** and is directly presented in the context as a factual truth-assessment pattern.  
   **Contrast type:** constructional  
   **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

6. **ეს მართალია** — **ეს სწორი არ არის**  
   **Transliteration:** *es martalia* — *es sts’ori ar aris*  
   **English translation:** “this is true/right” — “this is not correct/right”  
   **Description of contrast:** The context explicitly presents positive attribution and negative attribution as contrasting predicate patterns. Although the adjectives differ, both constructions are used to oppose affirmative correctness/truth to negative evaluation.  
   **Contrast type:** constructional  
   **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

7. **სწორი** — **სწორი არ არის**

   **Description of contrast:** This is a supported negation contrast built from the same evaluative adjective. The material explicitly notes **სწორი არ არის** as a common negative evaluative phrase.  
   **Contrast type:** morphological  
   **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

9. **გამართლება აქვს** — **გამართლება არ აქვს**

10. **უტყუარი მტკიცებულება** — **სარწმუნო მტკიცებულება**

    **Contrast type:** lexical  
    **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

    **Contrast type:** lexical
