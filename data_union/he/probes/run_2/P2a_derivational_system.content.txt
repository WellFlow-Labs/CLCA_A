# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2a Derivational System
# Language: Hebrew (he)

---

## Preliminary Note on Scope

This step documents attested derivational morphology for the 30 P1 base items. Derivations already fully documented in P1b root expansion are cross-referenced rather than repeated in full. The focus here is on forms not already covered in P1b, plus systematic documentation of the derivational profile of each P1 item. Where P1b has already provided the primary derivational family for a root, this is noted and the relevant P1b entries are cited.

Derivations are listed per P1 base item. No paradigm completion is performed. Gaps and asymmetries are preserved.

---

## Item 1 — אמת (*ʾemet*)

**Base Form:** אמת (*ʾemet*, "truth")

---

**Derived Form 1:**
- **Derived Form:** אמיתי
- **Transliteration:** ʾamiti
- **Literal Breakdown:** Root א-מ-ת + adjectival suffix *-i*; "of truth, truth-like"
- **Natural English translation:** true, genuine, real, authentic
- **Usage:** The primary adjectival derivation from *ʾemet*, used attributively and predicatively to describe persons, objects, or states as genuinely what they appear to be. Already listed as P1 item 2; included here to document the derivational link.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2:**
- **Derived Form:** לאמיתו
- **Transliteration:** le-ʾamito
- **Literal Breakdown:** *le-* ("to, in") + *ʾamit-* (construct form of root א-מ-ת) + *-o* (3ms possessive suffix); "to its truth"
- **Natural English translation:** in truth, in actual fact, truly
- **Usage:** Appears in the fixed phrase *le-ʾamito shel daʾvar* ("in truth, to tell the truth about the matter"), documented in P1b. The construct form *ʾamito* is not used independently outside this fixed phrase.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 3:**
- **Derived Form:** אמיתות
- **Transliteration:** ʾamitut
- **Literal Breakdown:** Root א-מ-ת + adjectival stem *ʾamit-* + abstract noun suffix *-ut*; "the quality of being true/genuine"
- **Natural English translation:** genuineness, authenticity, truthfulness (as a quality)
- **Usage:** An abstract noun derived from the adjective *ʾamiti*, used in philosophical and evaluative discourse to refer to the property of being genuine or true. Less common than *ʾemet* itself; appears in academic and literary registers.
- **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**Note:** The core derivational family of root א-מ-ת is documented in P1b (Root א-מ-ת expansion, items 1–20). The productive derivations from *ʾemet* as a base are limited; the root generates more collocational compounds than morphological derivations.

## Item 2 — אמיתי (*ʾamiti*)

**Base Form:** אמיתי (*ʾamiti*, "true, genuine")

---

**Derived Form 1:**
- **Derived Form:** אמיתית
- **Transliteration:** ʾamitit
- **Literal Breakdown:** Root א-מ-ת + adjectival suffix *-it* (feminine form of *-i*)
- **Natural English translation:** true, genuine, real (feminine agreement)
- **Usage:** The feminine form of *ʾamiti*, used in grammatical agreement with feminine nouns, e.g., *ahavaʾh ʾamitit* ("genuine love"), *beʿayaʾh ʾamitit* ("a real problem"). Not a separate lexical item but a required inflectional form.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2:**
- **Derived Form:** אמיתיים / אמיתיות
- **Transliteration:** ʾamitiyim (m.pl.) / ʾamitiyot (f.pl.)
- **Literal Breakdown:** Root א-מ-ת + adjectival stem + plural suffixes *-im* (m.) / *-ot* (f.)
- **Natural English translation:** true, genuine, real (plural agreement)
- **Usage:** Plural forms of *ʾamiti* used in grammatical agreement with plural nouns, e.g., *reʿim ʾamitiyim* ("true friends"). Standard inflectional forms.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 3:**
- **Derived Form:** אמיתיות
- **Transliteration:** ʾamitiyut
- **Literal Breakdown:** Adjectival stem *ʾamiti-* + abstract noun suffix *-ut*
- **Natural English translation:** genuineness, authenticity, realness
- **Usage:** An abstract noun formed from the adjective *ʾamiti*, referring to the quality of being genuine or authentic. Used in philosophical, psychological, and everyday discourse, e.g., *ʾamitiyut ha-regeʃ* ("the genuineness of the feeling"). Overlaps with *ʾamitut* (see Item 1) and *ʾotentiyut*.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Note:** *ʾamiti* is itself a derivation from *ʾemet* (Item 1). Its own derivational productivity is limited to the abstract noun *ʾamitiyut* and standard inflectional forms.

## Item 3 — נכון (*nakhon*)

**Base Form:** נכון (*nakhon*, "correct, right, accurate")

---

**Derived Form 1:**
- **Derived Form:** נכונה
- **Transliteration:** nekhonah
- **Literal Breakdown:** Root כ-ו-נ + feminine adjectival suffix *-ah*
- **Natural English translation:** correct, right, accurate (feminine agreement)
- **Usage:** The feminine form of *nakhon*, used in grammatical agreement with feminine nouns, e.g., *teshuvah nekhonah* ("a correct answer"). Standard inflectional form; also used predicatively, e.g., *ze lo nekhonah* ("that is not right").
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2:**
- **Derived Form:** נכונים / נכונות
- **Transliteration:** nekhonim (m.pl.) / nekhanot (f.pl.)
- **Literal Breakdown:** Root כ-ו-נ + plural suffixes
- **Natural English translation:** correct, right (plural agreement)
- **Usage:** Plural forms of *nakhon* used in grammatical agreement, e.g., *teshuvot nekhanot* ("correct answers"). Standard inflectional forms.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 3:**
- **Derived Form:** נכונות
- **Transliteration:** nekhonot
- **Literal Breakdown:** Root כ-ו-נ + abstract noun suffix *-ut* (distinct from the plural form by context and stress pattern)
- **Natural English translation:** correctness, accuracy, rightness; also: readiness, willingness
- **Usage:** An abstract noun with two distinct senses: (a) the quality of being correct or accurate, e.g., *nekhonot ha-netunim* ("the accuracy of the data"); and (b) readiness or willingness, e.g., *nekhonot le-shituʾf peʿulaʾh* ("willingness to cooperate"). Context disambiguates. Both senses are common.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 4:**
- **Derived Form:** לנכן
- **Transliteration:** lenakhen
- **Literal Breakdown:** Root כ-ו-נ; piʿel infinitive, "to correct, to adjust"
- **Natural English translation:** to correct, to adjust, to calibrate
- **Usage:** Used in technical and everyday contexts to mean correcting or adjusting something to the proper standard, e.g., *lenakhen sheʾvaʾ* ("to correct an error"), *lenakhen maʿgal* ("to calibrate a device"). Less common than the noun forms.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 5:**
- **Derived Form:** כיוון
- **Transliteration:** kivun
- **Literal Breakdown:** Root כ-ו-נ; piʿel verbal noun, "direction, aiming, orientation"
- **Natural English translation:** direction, orientation, aiming
- **Usage:** A highly productive derivation from the same root כ-ו-נ, used in everyday, technical, and military contexts for direction or orientation, e.g., *be-eyzeh kivun?* ("in which direction?"). The connection to *nakhon* is through the shared root rather than direct derivation from the adjective.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 6:**
- **Derived Form:** הכנה
- **Transliteration:** hakhanah
- **Literal Breakdown:** Root כ-ו-נ; hifʿil verbal noun, "preparation, making ready"
- **Natural English translation:** preparation, readiness
- **Usage:** The standard word for preparation or getting ready, e.g., *hakhanah le-baḥinah* ("preparation for an exam"). Shares the root כ-ו-נ with *nakhon* through the sense of "being set, established, ready." A productive and common form.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Note:** Root כ-ו-נ is exceptionally productive in Hebrew and generates a large family of forms beyond those directly relevant to the truth-evaluation domain. The forms listed here are those most directly connected to *nakhon* in its evaluative sense. Forms such as *mekhaven* ("intentional"), *kavvanah* ("intention"), and *tikun* ("correction, repair") share the root but their connection to *nakhon* as a truth-evaluation term is indirect.

## Item 4 — שקר (*sheker*)

**Base Form:** שקר (*sheker*, "lie, falsehood")

---

**Derived Form 1:**
- **Derived Form:** שקרן
- **Transliteration:** shaqran
- **Literal Breakdown:** Root ש-ק-ר + agentive suffix *-an*; "one who lies habitually"
- **Natural English translation:** liar, habitual liar
- **Usage:** The standard word for a liar, used in everyday moral condemnation of persons who habitually tell falsehoods, e.g., *hu shaqran gadol* ("he is a big liar"). Common across all registers.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2:**
- **Derived Form:** שקרנית
- **Transliteration:** shaqranit
- **Literal Breakdown:** Root ש-ק-ר + agentive suffix *-an* + feminine suffix *-it*
- **Natural English translation:** liar (feminine)
- **Usage:** The feminine form of *shaqran*, used when referring to a female liar. Standard morphological form.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 3:**
- **Derived Form:** שקרנות
- **Transliteration:** shaqranut
- **Literal Breakdown:** Root ש-ק-ר + agentive stem *shaqran-* + abstract suffix *-ut*
- **Natural English translation:** habitual lying, mendacity
- **Usage:** An abstract noun referring to the practice or habit of lying, used in moral evaluation of persons or institutions, e.g., *shaqranut politit* ("political mendacity"). Less common than *sheker* itself but well attested.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 4:**
- **Derived Form:** שקרי
- **Transliteration:** shaqri
- **Literal Breakdown:** Root ש-ק-ר + adjectival suffix *-i*; "of falsehood, false"
- **Natural English translation:** false, spurious, mendacious
- **Usage:** An adjective used to describe things (rather than persons) as false or spurious, e.g., *ʿedut shaqrit* ("false testimony"), *divoaḥ shaqri* ("a false report"). Somewhat more formal than using *sheker* attributively.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 5:**
- **Derived Form:** לשקר
- **Transliteration:** leshaqer
- **Literal Breakdown:** Root ש-ק-ר; piʿel infinitive, "to lie, to tell falsehoods"
- **Natural English translation:** to lie, to tell a lie
- **Usage:** The standard verb for lying or telling falsehoods, used across all registers, e.g., *hu shiqer li* ("he lied to me"), *asur leshaqer* ("it is forbidden to lie").
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 6:**
- **Derived Form:** משקר
- **Transliteration:** meshaqer
- **Literal Breakdown:** Root ש-ק-ר; piʿel active participle, "one who is lying"
- **Natural English translation:** liar (in the act), one who lies
- **Usage:** The active participle used to describe someone in the act of lying or as a characterization, e.g., *hu meshaqer* ("he is lying"). Distinct from *shaqran* in that it describes the act rather than a habitual character trait.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 7:**
- **Derived Form:** עדות שקר
- **Transliteration:** ʿedut sheker
- **Literal Breakdown:** *ʿedut* ("testimony") + *sheker* (root ש-ק-ר, "falsehood")
- **Natural English translation:** false testimony, perjury
- **Usage:** A fixed legal and biblical collocation for testimony that is deliberately false, e.g., the biblical prohibition against *ʿed sheker* ("a false witness"). Used in both religious law and modern Israeli criminal law.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## Item 5 — מזויף (*mezuyaf*)

**Base Form:** מזויף (*mezuyaf*, "forged, counterfeit, fake")

---

**Derived Form 1:**
- **Derived Form:** זיוף
- **Transliteration:** ziyyuf
- **Literal Breakdown:** Root ז-י-פ; piʿel verbal noun, "the act of forging/falsifying"
- **Natural English translation:** forgery, falsification, counterfeiting
- **Usage:** Already listed as P1 item 18; included here to document the derivational link to *mezuyaf*. The verbal noun and the passive participle are the two primary derivations of this root in common use.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2:**
- **Derived Form:** מזייף
- **Transliteration:** mezayef
- **Literal Breakdown:** Root ז-י-פ; piʿel active participle, "one who forges"
- **Natural English translation:** forger, counterfeiter, falsifier
- **Usage:** The agentive form designating the person who commits forgery, used in legal and everyday contexts, e.g., *mezayef mismaḵhim* ("a document forger"). Appears in criminal law and journalism.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 3:**
- **Derived Form:** לזייף
- **Transliteration:** lezayef
- **Literal Breakdown:** Root ז-י-פ; piʿel infinitive, "to forge, to falsify"
- **Natural English translation:** to forge, to falsify, to counterfeit
- **Usage:** The standard verb for the act of forgery or falsification, e.g., *lezayef ḥatimaʾh* ("to forge a signature"), *lezayef matbeʿa* ("to counterfeit currency"). Common in legal and administrative contexts.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 4:**
- **Derived Form:** זייפן
- **Transliteration:** zayfan
- **Literal Breakdown:** Root ז-י-פ + agentive suffix *-an*; "habitual forger"
- **Natural English translation:** forger (habitual), counterfeiter
- **Usage:** An agentive noun for a person who habitually or professionally engages in forgery, e.g., *zayfan memuḥaʾ* ("a professional forger"). Less common than *mezayef* but attested in legal and journalistic contexts.
- **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**Note:** Root ז-י-פ is moderately productive but largely confined to the semantic domain of forgery and falsification. It does not generate derivations outside this domain.

## Item 6 — מקורי (*meqori*)

**Base Form:** מקורי (*meqori*, "original, authentic, genuine")

---

**Derived Form 1:**
- **Derived Form:** מקוריות
- **Transliteration:** meqoriyut
- **Literal Breakdown:** *meqori-* (adjectival stem) + abstract suffix *-ut*; "the quality of being original"
- **Natural English translation:** originality, authenticity, genuineness
- **Usage:** An abstract noun referring to the quality of being original or genuinely creative, used in aesthetic, legal, and intellectual property contexts, e.g., *meqoriyut ha-yetsirah* ("the originality of the work"), *beḥinat ha-meqoriyut* ("assessment of originality").
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2:**
- **Derived Form:** מקור
- **Transliteration:** meqor
- **Literal Breakdown:** Root ק-ו-ר; noun, "source, origin"
- **Natural English translation:** source, origin
- **Usage:** The base noun from which *meqori* is derived, meaning "source" or "origin." Used in journalistic contexts (*meqor ha-yadaʿot*, "the source of the information"), textual scholarship (*ha-meqor ha-ʿivri*, "the Hebrew original"), and everyday speech. Included here to document the derivational direction: *meqor* → *meqori* → *meqoriyut*.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 3:**
- **Derived Form:** במקור
- **Transliteration:** ba-meqor
- **Literal Breakdown:** *be-* ("in") + *ha-meqor* ("the source/original"); "in the original"
- **Natural English translation:** in the original, in the source text
- **Usage:** A fixed prepositional phrase used in translation, textual scholarship, and legal contexts to refer to the original-language version of a text, e.g., *ba-meqor ha-ʿivri* ("in the Hebrew original"), *ba-meqor ha-yevanit* ("in the Greek original").
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Note:** *meqori* is itself a derivation from *meqor* ("source"). Its own derivational productivity is primarily the abstract noun *meqoriyut*. The root ק-ו-ר generates a broader family, but most members are not directly relevant to the truth-evaluation domain.

- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Note:** As a loanword, *ʾotenti* participates in Hebrew derivational morphology only through the productive *-ut* abstract noun suffix and standard adjectival inflection. It does not generate verbal or other nominal derivations.

## Item 8 — מהימן (*meheymen*)

---

**Derived Form 1:**
- **Derived Form:** מהימנה
- **Transliteration:** meheymanah
- **Literal Breakdown:** Root א-מ-נ; hifʿil passive participle, feminine form
- **Natural English translation:** reliable, trustworthy, credible (feminine agreement)
- **Usage:** The feminine form of *meheymen*, used in grammatical agreement, e.g., *ʿedut meheymanah* ("reliable testimony"), *meqor meheymanah* ("a reliable source"). Standard inflectional form.

---

**Derived Form 2:**

- **Transliteration:** meheymanut
- **Literal Breakdown:** Root א-מ-נ; adjectival stem *meheymen-* + abstract suffix *-ut*

**Note:** *meheymen* is itself a derivation from root א-מ-נ (hifʿil passive participle pattern). Its primary derivational product in common use is the abstract noun *meheymanut*. The broader root family is documented in P1b (Root א-מ-נ expansion).

## Item 9 — אמונה (*ʾemunaʾh*)

**Base Form:** אמונה (*ʾemunaʾh*, "faith, trust, faithfulness, reliability")

---

**Derived Form 1:**
- **Derived Form:** אמוני
- **Transliteration:** ʾemuni
- **Literal Breakdown:** *ʾemunaʾh* (root א-מ-נ) + adjectival suffix *-i*; "of faith, faith-related"
- **Natural English translation:** faith-based, of faith, religious (in the sense of faith-oriented)
- **Usage:** An adjective derived from *ʾemunaʾh*, used to describe things as pertaining to religious faith or belief, e.g., *ḥinukh ʾemuni* ("faith-based education"), *ʿolam ʾemuni* ("a world of faith"). Common in religious and sociological discourse in Israel.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2:**
- **Derived Form:** אמוניות
- **Transliteration:** ʾemuniyut
- **Literal Breakdown:** *ʾemuni-* + abstract suffix *-ut*
- **Natural English translation:** faith-based character, religiosity (in the sense of faith-orientation)
- **Usage:** An abstract noun referring to the quality of being faith-oriented or religiously committed, used in sociological and educational discourse in Israel, e.g., *ramat ha-ʾemuniyut* ("the level of religious faith-orientation"). Less common than *ʾemunaʾh* itself.
- **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**Derived Form 3:**
- **Derived Form:** חוסר אמונה
- **Transliteration:** ḥoser ʾemunaʾh
- **Literal Breakdown:** *ḥoser* ("lack of") + *ʾemunaʾh* (root א-מ-נ, "faith/trust")
- **Natural English translation:** lack of faith, distrust, disbelief
- **Usage:** A fixed collocation used in religious, interpersonal, and political contexts to describe the absence of faith or trust, e.g., *ḥoser ʾemunaʾh ba-memshalaʾh* ("lack of trust in the government"). Common across registers.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Note:** The broader root family of א-מ-נ, including *neʾeman*, *ʾaminut*, *meheymen*, and *maʾamin*, is documented in P1b. *ʾemunaʾh* as a base generates limited additional derivations beyond those already in the root family.

## Item 12 — כנות (*kanut*)

**Base Form:** כנות (*kanut*, "sincerity, honesty, candor")

---

**Derived Form 1:**
- **Derived Form:** כן
- **Transliteration:** kʾen
- **Literal Breakdown:** Root כ-נ-נ/כ-ו-נ; adjective, "sincere, honest, upright"
- **Natural English translation:** sincere, honest, candid, genuine
