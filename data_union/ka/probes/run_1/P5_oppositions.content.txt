# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P5 Oppositions
# Language: Georgian (ka)

---

**Methodological note:** The following oppositions are extracted exclusively from patterns documented in P1, P1b, P2a, P2b, P3, and P4. No opposition has been inferred from general linguistic knowledge or projected from English or other languages. Where the data supports multiple contrasts for a single term, each is listed separately. Asymmetries in the data are preserved without normalization.

## Opposition 1

1. **Term A:** სიმართლე / simartle
2. **Term B:** სიცრუე / sicrue
3. **Transliteration:** simartle / sicrue
4. **English translation:** truth / lie, falsehood
5. **Description of contrast:** P3 explicitly states that sicrue "contrasts directly with simartle" and both are described as the primary everyday words for their respective concepts in moral and legal discourse. The parallel constructions simarṭle tqva ("told the truth") and sicrue tqva ("told a lie") are documented in P2b as directly parallel verb-object constructions, and simarṭlis tqma / sicruis tqma are documented as parallel genitive nominals in P2a and P1b.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 2

1. **Term A:** სიმართლე / simartle
2. **Term B:** სიყალბე / siq̇albe
3. **Transliteration:** simartle / siq̇albe
4. **English translation:** truth, correctness / falsehood, falsity, forgery
5. **Description of contrast:** Both are si-...-e circumfix nominalizations from contrasting roots (marṭ- vs. q̇alb-), and both appear in legal and administrative discourse as the primary abstract nouns for the opposing properties of truth and falsity. P3 places them in overlapping but distinct groups (Group 3 for simartle, Group 1 for siq̇albe), reflecting that their primary contexts of use differ even as they form a structural pair.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

Both terms are used in moral discourse about honest speech, though č̣ešmariteba carries a more elevated register than simartle in the same opposition.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [UNCERTAIN] (the construction itself is rated [UNCERTAIN] in P2b)

## Opposition 4

1. **Term A:** ნამდვილი / namdvili
2. **Term B:** ყალბი / q̇albi
3. **Transliteration:** namdvili / q̇albi
4. **English translation:** genuine, real, authentic / false, fake, counterfeit, forged
5. **Description of contrast:** P2b documents the directly parallel constructions ნამდვილი აღმოჩნდა (namdvili aġmočnda, "turned out to be genuine") and ყალბი აღმოჩნდა (q̇albi aġmočnda, "turned out to be fake/forged") using the same inchoative verb, establishing them as the primary adjectival opposition in authentication contexts. P3 notes that namdvili appears across Groups 1, 5, and 6 while q̇albi appears across Groups 1 and 5, confirming their parallel distribution.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 13

## Opposition 18

## Opposition 25

1. **Term A:** სარწმუნო / sarc̣muno
2. **Term B:** დამაჯერებელი / damajerebeliˈ
3. **Transliteration:** sarc̣muno / damajerebeliˈ
4. **English translation:** credible, reliable, trustworthy (inherently) / convincing, persuasive (in effect)

This is not a polar opposition but a contrast of orientation within the credibility domain — one term characterizes a source's intrinsic property, the other characterizes the effect produced on an audience.
