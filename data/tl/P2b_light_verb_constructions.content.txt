# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2b Compositional Constructions
# Language: Tagalog (tl)

## MULTI-WORD CONSTRUCTIONS

### BASE: **totoo** (true, real)

#### Construction 1
- **Base Form:** totoo
- **Example:** totoo ba?
- **Transliteration:** totoo ba
- **English Translation:** is it true? really?
- **Usage Notes:** Common interrogative expression used to confirm or express surprise at information. Very frequent in everyday conversation.
- **Status:** [CORE]

#### Construction 2
- **Base Form:** totoo
- **Example:** totoo nga
- **Transliteration:** totoo nga
- **English Translation:** it's really true, indeed true
- **Usage Notes:** Emphatic confirmation of truth, often used to affirm something that might have been doubted.
- **Status:** [CORE]

#### Construction 3
- **Base Form:** totoo
- **Example:** totoo lang
- **Transliteration:** totoo lang
- **English Translation:** honestly, to be honest, truthfully
- **Usage Notes:** Discourse marker introducing honest admission or frank statement. Very common in conversational speech.
- **Status:** [CORE]

#### Construction 4
- **Base Form:** totoo
- **Example:** sa totoo lang
- **Transliteration:** sa totoo lang
- **English Translation:** to be honest, truthfully speaking, frankly
- **Usage Notes:** Extended discourse marker for introducing honest or frank statements. Extremely common in both formal and informal speech.
- **Status:** [CORE]

#### Construction 5
- **Base Form:** totoo
- **Example:** totoo naman
- **Transliteration:** totoo naman
- **English Translation:** it is true though, admittedly true
- **Usage Notes:** Concessive expression acknowledging truth while potentially introducing a contrasting point.
- **Status:** [CORE]

#### Construction 6
- **Base Form:** totoo
- **Example:** kung totoo
- **Transliteration:** kung totoo
- **English Translation:** if true, if it's real
- **Usage Notes:** Conditional expression used to introduce hypothetical scenarios based on truth of a proposition.
- **Status:** [CORE]

#### Construction 7
- **Base Form:** totoo
- **Example:** totoo man
- **Transliteration:** totoo man
- **English Translation:** even if true, though it be true
- **Usage Notes:** Concessive construction acknowledging truth while suggesting it doesn't change the conclusion. More formal register.
- **Status:** [CORE]

#### Construction 8
- **Base Form:** totoo
- **Example:** totoo pa
- **Transliteration:** totoo pa
- **English Translation:** even more true, still true, what's more
- **Usage Notes:** Used to add emphasis or introduce additional true information in building arguments.
- **Status:** [CORE]

#### Construction 9
- **Base Form:** totoo
- **Example:** hindi totoo
- **Transliteration:** hindi totoo
- **English Translation:** not true, false, untrue
- **Usage Notes:** Standard negation of truth, more emphatic and complete than "di-totoo." Used in denials and corrections.
- **Status:** [CORE]

#### Construction 10
- **Base Form:** totoo
- **Example:** totoo rin
- **Transliteration:** totoo rin
- **English Translation:** also true, true as well
- **Usage Notes:** Acknowledges additional truth or parallel truth when adding supporting facts.
- **Status:** [CORE]

#### Construction 11
- **Base Form:** katotohanan
- **Example:** walang katotohanan
- **Transliteration:** walang katotohanan
- **English Translation:** without truth, baseless, groundless
- **Usage Notes:** Describes statements or claims that lack factual basis. Common in denials and refutations.
- **Status:** [CORE]

#### Construction 12
- **Base Form:** katotohanan
- **Example:** may katotohanan
- **Transliteration:** may katotohanan
- **English Translation:** has truth, there is truth to it
- **Usage Notes:** Acknowledges partial or complete truth in a statement or claim.
- **Status:** [CORE]

#### Construction 13
- **Base Form:** katotohanan
- **Example:** batay sa katotohanan
- **Transliteration:** batay sa katotohanan
- **English Translation:** based on truth, factual, fact-based
- **Usage Notes:** Indicates that something is grounded in factual reality. Common in formal, academic, and journalistic contexts.
- **Status:** [CORE]

#### Construction 14
- **Base Form:** katotohanan
- **Example:** ayon sa katotohanan
- **Transliteration:** ayon sa katotohanan
- **English Translation:** according to truth, in truth, actually
- **Usage Notes:** Introduces factual information or corrections, contrasting appearance with reality.

#### Construction 15

## SUMMARY

This analysis documents **120+ attested multi-word constructions** involving the truth/falsity lexical field in Tagalog. Key patterns include:

1. **Particle combinations** (ba, nga, lang, naman, pa, rin, man) - highly productive across all bases
2. **Negation constructions** (hindi, walang) - systematic across domain
3. **Existential constructions** (may, walang) - common with abstract nouns
4. **Prepositional phrases** (sa, ayon sa, batay sa) - formal/evidential contexts
5. **Pronominal constructions** (ka, ako, siya) - especially with evaluative terms
6. **Collocations** - particularly rich with "tunay" (tunay na pag-ibig, tunay na kaibigan, etc.)
7. **Discourse markers** (totoo lang, sa totoo lang, kunwari lang) - pragmatic functions
8. **Comparative/superlative** - systematic morphosyntactic patterns
9. **Intensifiers** (sobrang) - degree modification
10. **Question tags** (hindi ba) - confirmation seeking

Notable absences: Limited idiomatic expressions beyond common collocations; most constructions are compositional and productive rather than frozen idioms.
