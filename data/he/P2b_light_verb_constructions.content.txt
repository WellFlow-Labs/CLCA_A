# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2b Compositional Constructions
# Language: Hebrew (he)

## MULTI-WORD CONSTRUCTIONS IDENTIFIED

### Base Form: אמת (emet) "truth"

#### Construction 1
- **Base Form:** אמת (emet)
- **Example:** לומר את האמת
- **Transliteration:** lomar et ha'emet
- **Translation:** to tell the truth
- **Usage:** Standard expression for truthful speech. The most common way to express truth-telling in Hebrew, used across all registers.
- **Status:** [CORE]

#### Construction 2
- **Base Form:** אמת (emet)
- **Example:** האמת היא ש...
- **Transliteration:** ha'emet hi she...
- **Translation:** the truth is that...
- **Usage:** Discourse marker introducing a frank or corrective statement. Common in both spoken and written Hebrew to signal a shift to direct truth-telling.
- **Status:** [CORE]

#### Construction 3
- **Base Form:** אמת (emet)
- **Example:** לדבר אמת
- **Transliteration:** ledaber emet
- **Translation:** to speak truth
- **Usage:** More formal or elevated expression for truthful speech, parallel to lomar et ha'emet but with slightly more literary register.
- **Status:** [CORE]

#### Construction 4
- **Base Form:** אמת (emet)
- **Example:** אמת או שקר
- **Transliteration:** emet o sheker
- **Translation:** truth or falsehood, true or false
- **Usage:** Binary opposition used in questions, games, or evaluative contexts. Common in educational and interrogative contexts.
- **Status:** [CORE]

#### Construction 5
- **Base Form:** אמת (emet)
- **Example:** זו האמת
- **Transliteration:** zo ha'emet
- **Translation:** this is the truth, that's the truth
- **Usage:** Emphatic assertion of truthfulness. Used to affirm or conclude statements with emphasis on veracity.
- **Status:** [CORE]

#### Construction 6
- **Base Form:** אמת (emet)
- **Example:** האמת הקשה
- **Transliteration:** ha'emet hakasha
- **Translation:** the hard truth, the harsh truth
- **Usage:** Expression for unpleasant but necessary truths. Used when delivering difficult or unwelcome information.
- **Status:** [CORE]

#### Construction 7
- **Base Form:** אמת (emet)
- **Example:** האמת הבוערת
- **Transliteration:** ha'emet habo'eret
- **Translation:** the burning truth, the urgent truth
- **Usage:** Metaphorical expression for truth that demands attention or action. Used in journalistic and rhetorical contexts.
- **Status:** [CORE]

#### Construction 8
- **Base Form:** אמת (emet)
- **Example:** לחפש את האמת
- **Transliteration:** lehapes et ha'emet
- **Translation:** to seek the truth
- **Usage:** Expression for truth-seeking activity, common in philosophical, investigative, and spiritual contexts.
- **Status:** [CORE]

#### Construction 9
- **Base Form:** אמת (emet)
- **Example:** לגלות את האמת
- **Transliteration:** legalot et ha'emet
- **Translation:** to reveal/discover the truth
- **Usage:** Expression for uncovering or revealing truth, particularly in investigative or dramatic contexts.
- **Status:** [CORE]

#### Construction 10
- **Base Form:** אמת (emet)
- **Example:** להסתיר את האמת
- **Transliteration:** lehasתir et ha'emet
- **Translation:** to hide the truth
- **Usage:** Expression for concealing truth, used in contexts of deception or cover-up.
- **Status:** [CORE]

#### Construction 11
- **Base Form:** אמת (emet)
- **Example:** האמת תצא לאור
- **Transliteration:** ha'emet tetse la'or
- **Translation:** the truth will come to light
- **Usage:** Proverbial expression asserting that truth will eventually be revealed. Common in moral and investigative discourse.
- **Status:** [CORE]

#### Construction 12
- **Base Form:** אמת (emet)
- **Example:** רגע של אמת
- **Transliteration:** rega shel emet
- **Translation:** moment of truth
- **Usage:** Expression for decisive or revealing moments. Calque from English/European languages, now naturalized in Hebrew.

#### Construction 13

- **Transliteration:** eyn baze emet
- **Translation:** there's no truth in this

#### Construction 14

- **Transliteration:** yesh baze emet
- **Translation:** there's truth in this
- **Usage:** Expression acknowledging partial or complete truth in a statement. Used in concessions and agreements.

- Fixed comparisons (e.g., אמת או שקר "truth or falsehood")
- Proverbial expressions (e.g., האמת תצא לאור "the truth will come to light")
- Attributive compounds (e.g., מקור אמין "reliable source")

**Key observations:**
1. High-frequency items (אמת, נכון, שקר) show extensive constructional productivity
2. Formal/literary items (כזב, שווא) show minimal modern constructional use
3. Many constructions involve prepositional phrases (ב-, ל-) creating adverbial meanings
4. Question forms (נכון?, באמת?, ברור?) are highly productive
5. Negation patterns (לא + adjective) are systematic and productive across all bases
