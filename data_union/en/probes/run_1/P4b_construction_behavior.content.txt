## Cross-Construction Observations

1. **Asymmetry between positive and negative poles**: Agentive nominalization (Construction 7) shows a structural asymmetry: negative-pole agents are formed by verb suffixation (*faker*, *falsifier*), while positive-pole agents require compounding (*truth-teller*). This asymmetry is not replicated in other construction types.

2. **Register stratification**: Latinate derived forms (abstract nominalizations, dedicated causative verbs, prefixed negative adjectives) consistently pattern toward formal registers; periphrastic and *not* + adjective constructions are register-neutral.

3. **Scalar constructions**: Degree modification (Construction 8) and partial-truth constructions (Construction 10) both address the scalar nature of truth-domain adjectives but through structurally distinct means; no single construction covers the full scalar range.

4. **Discourse-operator status**: Concessive constructions (Construction 9) represent a functional shift in which truth-domain items operate as discourse markers rather than predicates; this is not a feature of any other construction type in the inventory.
