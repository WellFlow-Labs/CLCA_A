## Brief summary

The strongest attested constructional clusters in the supplied material are around:

- **òtítọ́**: *sọ òtítọ́, mọ̀ òtítọ́, fẹ́ òtítọ́, fi òtítọ́ hàn, ní/l’òtítọ́, òtítọ́ ọrọ̀*
- **ẹ̀rí / ẹlẹ́rìí**: *fi ẹ̀rí hàn, ní ẹ̀rí, kò sí ẹ̀rí, ẹ̀rí ìdánimọ̀, ẹ̀rí ìbí, jẹ́ ẹlẹ́rìí, pe ẹlẹ́rìí, ẹlẹ́rìí ojú*
- **òdodo**: *ṣe òdodo, idájọ́ òdodo, fẹ́ òdodo, dúró fún òdodo*, plus several religious/literary collocations

Many other P1 items are clearly important lexically, but the supplied evidence does **not** support additional common multi-word constructions beyond the fixed forms already listed.
