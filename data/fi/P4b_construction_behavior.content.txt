# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P4b Construction Usage & Constraints
# Language: Finnish (fi)

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Usage Notes |
|------------------|---------|-----------------|-------------|-------------|-------------|
| **1. ATTRIBUTION** | se on totta | se on totta | that is true | [CORE] | Highly frequent in all registers. Partitive case (*totta*) obligatory for abstract truth evaluation; nominative (*tosiasia*, *valhe*) for countable entities. Standard predicative construction. |
| **2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION** | se ei ole totta | se ei ole totta | that is not true | [CORE] | Standard negation pattern. Partitive maintained under negation for abstract/mass nouns. Alternative *ei pidä paikkaansa* equally common for propositional content. |
| **3. ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION** | tosi väite | tosi väite | true claim | [CORE] | Productive in all registers. Adjectives agree in case/number. Compound forms (*tosi-*, *vale-*) highly productive and lexicalized. Direct modification preferred over genitive constructions. |
| **4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE** | väite osoittautui vääräksi | väite osoittautui vääräksi | the claim proved false | [CORE] | Common in formal/written registers, especially academic and journalistic. Translative case marks result state. Multiple inchoative verbs available (*todellistua*, *toteutua*, *paljastua*). |
| **5. CAUSATIVE** | todistaa väite vääräksi | todistaa väite vääräksi | to prove the claim false | [CORE] | Frequent in formal contexts (legal, academic, scientific). Translative case marks result. Multiple causative verbs with distinct semantic nuances (*todistaa*, *todentaa*, *vääristää*). |
| **6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION** | totuus | totuus | truth | [CORE] | Highly productive derivational pattern. Used across all registers. Suffix selection (*-uus/-yys/-suus*) phonologically conditioned. Creates philosophical and abstract terminology. |
| **7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION** | todistaja | todistaja | witness | [CORE] | Productive for action verbs. Common in legal and formal contexts. Note: no standard agent noun for "liar" (*valehtelija* marginal; descriptive phrases preferred). |
| **8. RESULT NOMINALIZATION** | todiste | todiste | proof, evidence | [CORE] | Highly productive. Multiple suffixes (*-e*, *-us*, *-os*) with partly lexicalized distribution. Common in legal, academic, and formal registers. |
| **9. ACTION NOMINALIZATION** | todistaminen | todistaminen | proving, testifying | [CORE] | Fully productive for all verbs. Functions as verbal noun in all syntactic positions. Common in formal/written registers for abstract reference to processes. |
| **10. MANNER ADVERBIAL** | rehellisesti | rehellisesti | honestly | [CORE] | Fully productive from adjectives. Used across all registers. Some irregular forms (*oikein*, *väärin*). High frequency in both spoken and written language. |
| **11. LIGHT VERB (TRUTH-TELLING)** | puhua totuutta | puhua totuutta | to speak the truth | [CORE] | Common across registers. Partitive case standard. Dedicated verb *valehdella* "to lie" competes with *puhua valhetta*. Fixed expressions highly frequent. |
| **12. LIGHT VERB (ERROR)** | tehdä virhe | tehdä virhe | to make a mistake | [CORE] | Standard construction across all registers. *Tehdä virhe* universal for error commission. Correction verbs (*korjata*, *oikaista*) equally standard. |
| **13. LIGHT VERB (EVIDENCE)** | esittää todisteita | esittää todisteita | to present evidence | [CORE] | Concentrated in legal and academic registers. Partitive plural (*todisteita*) standard. Multiple verbs available for different evidence-related actions. |
| **14. PREDICATIVE STATE** | olla oikeassa | olla oikeassa | to be right | [CORE] | Fixed idiomatic expressions. High frequency in spoken and informal registers. Inessive case (*-ssa*) obligatory. Contrasts with partitive/nominative predicatives. |
| **15. FIXED IDIOM (pitää paikkansa)** | väite pitää paikkansa | väite pitää paikkansa | the claim is true | [CORE] | Highly frequent idiom for affirming accuracy. Used across registers. Literal meaning opaque. Negation *ei pidä paikkaansa* equally common. |
| **16. INTENSIFIER (COLLOQUIAL)** | tosi hyvä | tosi hyvä | really good | [MARGINAL] | Restricted to informal/colloquial registers. Grammaticalized from truth-domain to degree modification. Formal equivalent *todella* preferred in written/formal contexts. |
| **17. DISCOURSE MARKERS** | rehellisesti sanottuna | rehellisesti sanottuna | honestly speaking | [CORE] | High frequency in spoken language and informal writing. Grammaticalized from truth-evaluation to discourse-pragmatic function. Multiple fixed forms available. |
| **18. COMPARATIVE** | todenmukaisempi | todenmukaisempi | more truthful | [CORE] | Productive for gradable adjectives. Absolute terms (*tosi*, *väärä*) resist comparison; periphrastic forms required (*enemmän totta*). Common in evaluative contexts. |
| **19. NEGATIVE DERIVATION (epä-)** | epätosi | epätosi | untrue, false | [CORE] | Productive with adjectives. Competes with dedicated antonyms (*väärä* vs. *epätosi*). Semantic distinction: *epätosi* marks absence, *väärä* marks incorrectness. |
| **20. PRIVATIVE DERIVATION (-tön)** | virheetön | virheetön | error-free | [CORE] | Productive pattern marking absence of (typically negative) quality. Common in formal/technical registers. *Vilpitön* "sincere" lexicalized with positive meaning. |
| **21. COMPOUND (tosi-)** | tositarina | tositarina | true story | [CORE] | Highly productive. Marks factual basis or authenticity. Common in media, entertainment contexts distinguishing factual from fictional content. |
| **22. COMPOUND (vale-)** | valepassi | valepassi | fake passport | [CORE] | Highly productive. Marks deliberate falsification. Concentrated in legal/criminal terminology and security contexts. Productive for any counterfeit entity. |
| **23. TRANSLATIVE RESULT** | todistaa oikeaksi | todistaa oikeaksi | to prove right | [CORE] | Common in formal registers. Translative marks result state. Fixed expressions with *todistaa* highly frequent. Used in academic, legal, and argumentative contexts. |
| **24. PARTITIVE OBJECT** | etsiä totuutta | etsiä totuutta | to seek the truth | [CORE] | Partitive required for mass/abstract objects. Standard case pattern for truth-domain nouns as objects. Used across all registers with appropriate verbs. |
| **25. GENITIVE ATTRIBUTE** | totuuden hetki | totuuden hetki | moment of truth | [MARGINAL] | Less productive than compound formations. Some expressions lexicalized (*totuuden hetki*). Genitive constructions often replaced by compounds (*todennäköinen* < *toden näköinen*). |

## ALTERNATE CONSTRUCTIONS ATTESTED

### Attribution Alternates
- **olla totta** vs. **pitää paikkansa**: Both express truth of propositions; *pitää paikkansa* emphasizes accuracy/correspondence
- **olla oikeassa** vs. **olla oikealla**: Locative variation (inessive vs. adessive) both acceptable for "be right"

### Negation Alternates  
- **ei ole totta** vs. **ei pidä paikkaansa**: Functionally equivalent for propositional negation
- **ei ole tosi** (nominative) vs. **ei ole totta** (partitive): Case variation based on countable vs. mass interpretation

### Inchoative Alternates
- **osoittautua + translative** vs. **tulla + translative**: Both mark becoming; *osoittautua* emphasizes evidential basis
- **paljastua** vs. **tulla ilmi**: Both "be revealed"; *paljastua* more dramatic, *tulla ilmi* neutral

### Causative Alternates
- **todistaa** vs. **todentaa**: *Todistaa* "prove" (general), *todentaa* "verify/authenticate" (more specific)
- **vääristää** vs. **väärentää**: *Vääristää* "distort" (abstract), *väärentää* "forge/counterfeit" (concrete)

### Truth-telling Alternates
- **puhua totuutta** vs. **puhua totta**: Partitive vs. partitive of adjective; both acceptable
- **puhua valhetta** vs. **valehdella**: Light verb construction vs. dedicated verb; *valehdella* more common

### Intensifier Alternates
- **tosi** vs. **todella** vs. **oikein**: Register variation; *tosi* colloquial, *todella* neutral, *oikein* slightly formal
- **tosi paljon** vs. **todella paljon**: Colloquial vs. standard intensification

### Compound Alternates
- **tosi-** compounds vs. genitive constructions: *tositarina* preferred over *totuuden tarina*
- **vale-** compounds vs. adjective + noun: *valepassi* preferred over *väärä passi* for counterfeit items
