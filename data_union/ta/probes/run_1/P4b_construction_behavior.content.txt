# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P4b Construction Usage & Constraints
# Language: Tamil (ta)

---

## 1. ATTRIBUTION

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Predicative bare nominal + emphatic *-tāṉ* | அது உண்மைதான் | *atu uṇmaitāṉ* | "That is indeed true" | [CORE] | Extremely common in spoken and written Tamil across registers; the emphatic particle *-tāṉ* is optional but frequent in affirmative assertion contexts. |
| Attributive *-āna* adjectival + head noun | அவர் நேர்மையான ஆள் | *avar nērmaiyāna āḷ* | "He/she is an honest person" | [CORE] | The *-āna* attributive form is the standard adjectival strategy across all registers; no observable restriction on head noun type within this domain. |

**Attested alternates:** Bare predicative nominal without *-tāṉ* (e.g., *atu uṇmai*, "that is true") is attested as a simpler predicative form; past/future predication uses *iru-* as copula.

---

## 2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| *NOUN + illāta* (negative existential participial) | நேர்மையில்லாத மனிதன் | *nērmaiyillāta maṉitaṉ* | "A person lacking honesty" | [CORE] | Broadly productive across the domain; common in both spoken and written registers with no strong contextual restriction. |
| *NOUN + aṟṟa* (privative participial) | ஆதாரமற்ற குற்றச்சாட்டு | *ātāramaṟṟa kuṟṟaccāṭṭu* | "A baseless accusation" | [CORE] | Common in formal, legal, and written registers; tends to attach to quality/attribute nouns (*ātāram*, *takuti*, *poruttam*); less common in casual speech than *illāta*. |

**Attested alternates:** No single privative suffix covers the entire domain; the two strategies are not interchangeable. Both are attested as productive. No further alternates are attested in the P4a data.

---

## 3. ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| *-āna* adjectival modifier before head noun | உண்மையான தகவல் | *uṇmaiyāna taka'l* | "True/accurate information" | [CORE] | Standard and productive with abstract quality nouns (*uṇmai*, *nērmai*, *niyāyam*); common across registers. |
| Bare nominal modifier before head noun | போலி ஆவணம் | *pōli āvaṇam* | "Forged/fake document" | [CORE] | Common with *pōli*, *acal*, *nakal*, *kaṟpaṉai*, *poy*; the bare form without *-āna* is the normal pattern for these items and is not a reduced or informal variant. |

**Attested alternates:** Both strategies coexist; choice is partly lexically conditioned and partly register-sensitive. Attested bare prenominal items include *poy cāṭci* (false testimony) and *acal āvaṇam* (original document). No further alternates are attested in the P4a data.

---

## 4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| *teriya varu* (come to be known) | உண்மை தெரிய வந்தது | *uṇmai teriya vantatu* | "The truth became known / came to light" | [CORE] | Specific to the revelation-of-hidden-truth pattern; treats truth as an entity moving from concealment to visibility; common in narrative, journalistic, and conversational registers. |
| *āku* + completive *viṭu* (become, change of state) | அது உண்மையாகிவிட்டது | *atu uṇmaiyākiviṭṭatu* | "That has turned out to be true / has become true" | [CORE] | General change-of-state construction; the completive auxiliary *viṭu* adds finality or irreversibility; common across registers. |

**Attested alternates:** No morphological inchoative suffix is attested in this domain. The two periphrastic constructions are semantically distinct and not interchangeable: *teriya varu* is restricted to revelation of hidden truth; *āku* is a general change-of-state construction.

## 5. CAUSATIVE

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Morphological causative *mey* + *-ppi-* → *meyppiкka* | அதை மெய்ப்பிக்க முடியவில்லை | *atai meyppiкka muṭiyavillai* | "It was not possible to prove that" | [CORE] | The only morphologically derived causative in the truth domain; attested in formal, legal, and written registers; derived from the classical root *mey*, not from *uṇmai*. |
| Periphrastic *nilaināṭṭu* (establish, make stand firm) | உண்மையை நிலைநாட்டினார் | *uṇmaiyai nilaināṭṭiṉār* | "He/she established the truth definitively" | [CORE] | Common in formal and written registers for the proving/establishing function; periphrastic rather than morphologically derived. |

**Attested alternates:** *nirūpi* is attested as a further periphrastic alternative carrying the proving function. No morphological causative from *uṇmai* is attested; this asymmetry is noted in P4a as a structural gap.

## 6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| *poy* + *-mai* → *poymai* | பொய்மை | *poymai* | "Falsehood, the quality of being false" | [CORE] | Productive *-mai* nominalization; attested in written and literary registers; *poymai* and *meymmai* are the clearest secondary *-mai* nominalizations in the domain. |
| *uṇmai* + *taṉmai* compound | உண்மைத்தன்மை | *uṇmait-taṉmai* | "Truthfulness, the property of being true" | [MARGINAL] | Lexicalized compound used in formal and philosophical registers; not common in everyday speech; restricted to contexts requiring explicit reference to the abstract property of truthfulness. |

**Attested alternates:** *nampikkai* (*nampu* + *-ikkai*) and *nampakam* (*nampu* + *-akam*) are attested as alternative nominalizing suffixes on the same root *nampu*, indicating that multiple nominalizing strategies coexist on a single base. *uṇmai* and *nērmai* are themselves *-mai* nominals and do not reduplicate the suffix.

---

## 7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Participial relative clause (*col-* + *-pavaṉ*) | பொய் சொல்பவன் | *poy colpavaṉ* | "One who tells lies, a liar" | [CORE] | The productive and common strategy for forming agent nominals from verbal phrases in this domain; no register restriction observed. |
| *nampikkai* + *āḷar* agent suffix | நம்பிக்கையாளர் | *nampikkaiāḷar* | "Believers, persons of faith" | [MARGINAL] | Specific to religious contexts; the *āḷar* suffix here applies to a nominal base rather than a verbal phrase; not a general agentive strategy across the domain. |

**Attested alternates:** *-vāti* suffix (*uṇmaivāti*, truth-advocate) and *piriyar* (*uṇmaip piriyar*, lover of truth) are attested but rated [MARGINAL], confined to philosophical and literary registers. No dedicated agentive suffix for truth-domain roles is attested.

## 8. EVIDENTIAL / SOURCE-MARKING CONSTRUCTION

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ablative *-iliruntu* + *nampakamāna* + *teriya vantatu* | நம்பகமான வட்டாரங்களிலிருந்து தெரிய வந்தது | *nampakamāna vaṭṭāraṅkaḷiliruntu teriya vantatu* | "It became known from reliable circles" | [CORE] | Characteristic of journalistic and legal Tamil; source credibility (*nampakamāna*) and source marking (ablative *-iliruntu*) co-occur as a fixed pattern in this register. |
| Negative conditional *illāmal* + *nampakamāna ātāram* | நம்பகமான ஆதாரம் இல்லாமல் எதுவும் சொல்ல வேண்டாம் | *nampakamāna ātāram illāmal etuvum colla vēṇṭām* | "Nothing should be said without reliable evidence" | [CORE] | Common in formal, legal, and journalistic registers; the negative conditional *illāmal* (without) marks the absence of required evidentiary basis; evaluative and prescriptive in function. |

**Attested alternates:** *nampattakka* (worth trusting) is attested as an alternate to *nampakamāna* for evaluating source reliability within this construction type. No further alternates are attested in the P4a data.

## 9. CONCEALMENT / REVELATION VERBAL CONSTRUCTION

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Transitive concealment: *uṇmaiyai maṟai* (accusative object) | உண்மையை மறைக்கிறார்கள் | *uṇmaiyai maṟaikkiṟārkaḷ* | "They are hiding the truth" | [CORE] | Common across registers; requires accusative marking on *uṇmai* (*uṇmaiyai*); truth is construed as a direct object of concealment. |
| Intransitive revelation: *uṇmai veḷiyē vantatu* (truth as subject) | உண்மை வெளியே வந்தது | *uṇmai veḷiyē vantatu* | "The truth came out / emerged" | [CORE] | Common in narrative, journalistic, and conversational registers; truth is construed as subject with no case marking; structurally complementary to the concealment construction. |

**Attested alternates:** *uṇmai teriya vantatu* (the truth became known) is attested as a further intransitive revelation construction, overlapping with Construction 4 (*teriya varu*). The proverbial *poy oru nāḷ veḷiyē varum* (a lie will come out one day) uses the same intransitive revelation structure with *poy* as subject, confirming the construction extends beyond *uṇmai* to other domain items.
