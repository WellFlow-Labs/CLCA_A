# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P3 NATURAL SEMANTIC GROUPING
# Language: Georgian (ka)

---

## Preliminary note on method

Groupings below are derived from patterns observed in the P1 and P2 data: which items appear in the same contexts, which are contrasted with each other, which share typical collocates, and which participate in the same constructional frames. No external framework has been imposed. Where usage patterns do not support a clear grouping, items are left ungrouped or flagged as ambiguous.

---

## Group 1: Expressions used to assert or confirm that a statement is correct or true

**Label:** Everyday assertion of correctness or truth

**Definition:** These expressions are used in spoken and written Georgian to assert that a claim, statement, or position is accurate or right. They appear in contexts of confirmation, concession in argument, and validation of another speaker's claim. They are frequently predicative (appearing with copular constructions) and are among the highest-frequency items in the truth-evaluation domain.

**Representative expressions:**
- მართალია (martalia) — "it is true / you are right"
- მართლა (martla) — "truly, really, indeed"
- მართლაც (martlac) — "indeed, in fact" (emphatic)
- მართლაც კი (martlac ki) — "really and truly" (further emphatic)
- სიმართლეს ვამბობ (simartle s vambob) — "I am telling the truth"
- მართალს ვამბობ (martals vambob) — "I am saying what is right / I am telling the truth"
- სიმართლე გითხრა (simartle gitxra) — "to tell you the truth" (discourse marker)
- სიმართლე რომ ვთქვა (simartle rom vtqva) — "to tell the truth / truth be told" (discourse frame)
- სიმართლეში ვიყო (simartleši viq̇o) — "to be honest / frankly"
- ზუსტად ასეა (zustad asea) — "that is exactly right"
- მართალი ხარ (martali xar) — "you are right"
- მართალი ვარ (martali var) — "I am right"
- მართალია, მაგრამ... (martalia, magram...) — "it is true, but..." (concession frame)
- გულწრფელად გეუბნები (gulc̣rpelaad geubnebi) — "I am telling you sincerely"
- გულწრფელად რომ ვთქვა (gulc̣rpelaad rom vtqva) — "frankly speaking / to be sincere"

**Notes:** martalia and martla are the highest-frequency items in this group. The group includes both simple predicative forms and discourse-framing constructions that introduce candid statements. simartle rom vtqva and gulc̣rpelaad rom vtqva are functionally parallel frames, though the latter adds a sincerity dimension (see also Group 4). martals vambob and simartle s vambob are near-parallel but martals carries a slightly stronger sense of rightness or correctness rather than mere factual accuracy.

## Group 2: Expressions used in legal and administrative contexts to evaluate documents, evidence, and official acts

**Label:** Legal and administrative evaluation of genuineness, validity, and conformity

**Definition:** These expressions appear characteristically in legal proceedings, administrative processes, and official documentation to evaluate whether documents, evidence, actions, or claims meet formal standards of genuineness, validity, or conformity. They are used by courts, investigators, administrators, and legal commentators. Many of these items appear in fixed collocations with words like dokument̕i (document), mt̕kicebuleba (evidence), and moc̣me (witness).

**Representative expressions:**
- ყალბი დოკუმენტი (q̇albi dokument̕i) — "forged document"
- ყალბი ხელმოწერა (q̇albi xelmoq̇era) — "forged signature"
- ყალბი მოწმობა (q̇albi moc̣moba) — "forged certificate / false testimony"
- ყალბი მტკიცებულება (q̇albi mt̕kicebuleba) — "fabricated evidence"
- ყალბი ბრალდება (q̇albi braldeba) — "false accusation"
- გაყალბება (gaq̇albeba) — "forgery, falsification"
- გაყალბება (მტკიცებულებების) (gaq̇albeba mt̕kicebulebebis) — "fabrication of evidence"
- გამყალბებელი (gamq̇albebeli) — "forger, falsifier"
- გაყალბებული (gaq̇albebuli) — "forged, falsified"
- გაყალბებაში დაადანაშაულეს (gaq̇albebаši dadanašaules) — "was accused of forgery"
- ნამდვილობა (namdviloba) — "genuineness, authenticity"
- ნამდვილობის დადასტურება (namdvilobis dadastureba) — "confirmation of authenticity"
- ნამდვილობა დადასტურდა (namdviloba dadasturda) — "the authenticity was confirmed"
- ავთენტური დოკუმენტი (avtent̕uri dokument̕i) — "an authentic document"
- ავთენტურად ითვლება (avtent̕urad itvleba) — "is considered authentic"
- სარწმუნო მოწმე (sarc̣muno moc̣me) — "a credible witness"
- სარწმუნოდ ითვლება (sarc̣munod itvleba) — "is considered reliable"
- კანონთან შეუსაბამოა (kanontан šeusabamoа) — "is inconsistent with the law"
- შეუსაბამობა (šeusabamoba) — "inconsistency, non-conformity"
- შესაბამისობა (šesabamisoba) — "conformity, compliance"
- დამაჯერებელი მტკიცებულება (damajerebeli mt̕kicebuleba) — "convincing evidence"
- საეჭვო პირი (saečvo piri) — "a suspicious person / a suspect"
- საეჭვო (saečvo) — "doubtful, suspicious, questionable"
- ლეგიტიმური ხელისუფლება (legitimuri xelisupleba) — "legitimate authority"
- ლეგიტიმურობა (legitimuroba) — "legitimacy"
- არალეგიტიმური (aralegitimuri) — "illegitimate"
- მართლმსაჯულება (martlmsajuleba) — "justice, administration of justice"
- სამართალი (samartali) — "law, right, justice (as a system)"
- სამართლებრივი (samartalebr ivi) — "legal, juridical"
- გამართლება (gamartleba) — "acquittal, vindication"
- ყალბად წარმოდგენა (q̇albad c̣armogdgena) — "false representation, misrepresentation"
- მიკერძოებული მოსამართლე (mikerdzoebuli mosamartле) — "a biased judge"
- მიუკერძოებელი (miukerdzoebeli) — "unbiased, impartial"

**Notes:** This is the largest group by item count, reflecting the high density of legal usage across the P1 and P2 data. Several items in this group also appear in journalistic contexts (see Group 5). The distinction between namdviloba and avtent̕uroba is noted in P1: namdviloba is the native form, avtent̕uri is a more recent borrowing used in formal registers; both appear in this group. legitimuri and its derivatives occupy a sub-area of this group concerned specifically with the rightful standing of authority rather than the genuineness of documents.

## Group 3: Expressions used to characterize deliberate deception or the creation of false impressions

**Label:** Deliberate deception and false appearance

**Definition:** These expressions are used to characterize conduct in which a person or entity intentionally creates a false impression, conceals the truth, or presents something as other than it is. They appear in moral, legal, and literary contexts and typically imply agency and intent on the part of the deceiver. They are distinguished from expressions of mere error (Group 6) by the implication of deliberateness.

**Representative expressions:**
- თვალთმაქცობა (tvaltmaqcoba) — "deception, trickery, sleight of hand"
- თვალთმაქცი (tvaltmaqci) — "deceiver, trickster"
- თვალთმაქცობას მიმართა (tvaltmaqcobas mimarta) — "resorted to deception"
- თვალთმაქცობით მოიქცა (tvaltmaqcobit moiqca) — "acted through deception"
- მოჩვენებითი (močvenebiti) — "apparent, seeming, illusory, ostensible"
- მოჩვენებითი თანხმობა (močvenebiti tanxmoba) — "apparent consent"
- მოჩვენებითი სიმშვიდე (močvenebiti simšvide) — "apparent calm / surface tranquility"
- მოჩვენებითად ჩანს (močvenebitad čans) — "appears only on the surface"
- სიცრუე (sicrue) — "lie, falsehood"
- სიცრუეს ამბობს (sicrues ambobs) — "is telling a lie"
- სიცრუეში გაიჭირა (sicruеši gaič̣ira) — "was caught in a lie"
- სიცრუე გამოაშკარავდა (sicrue gamoaškaravda) — "the lie was exposed"
- სიცრუეს ვერ დამალავ (sicrues ver damalav) — "you cannot hide a lie"
- სიცრუეს ფეხი მოკლე აქვს (sicrues pexi mokle aqvs) — "a lie has short legs" (proverb)
- სიყალბე გამოაშკარავდა (siq̇albe gamoaškaravda) — "the falseness was exposed"
- სიყალბეში დაიჭირეს (siq̇albеši daič̣ires) — "was caught in the act of falsification"
- ჭეშმარიტების დამალვა (č̣ešmaritebis damalva) — "concealment of the truth"
- სიმართლის დამალვა (simartlis damalva) — "concealment of the truth"
- ყალბი ვინაობა (q̇albi vinaoba) — "false identity"
- ყალბი სახელი (q̇albi saxeli) — "false name, alias"
- ყალბი ღიმილი (q̇albi ɣimili) — "fake smile"
- ყალბი გრძნობები (q̇albi grdznobebi) — "fake feelings"
- ყალბი მეგობარი (q̇albi megobari) — "false friend" (in the interpersonal sense)
- ფსევდო- (psevdo-) — prefix marking spurious or false claims to a status
- ფსევდომეცნიერი (psevdomecnieri) — "pseudo-scientist"
- ფსევდოდემოკრატია (psevdodemoqracia) — "pseudo-democracy"

**Notes:** This group is distinguished from Group 2 (legal evaluation) by its emphasis on the act and character of deception rather than the formal status of documents or evidence. tvaltmaqcoba specifically encodes deception through appearances (the eye-deceiving compound), while močvenebiti marks the gap between appearance and reality without necessarily implying a deceiving agent. sicrue is the primary term for a lie as a speech act; siq̇albe is broader, covering the abstract quality of falseness. The proverbial constructions (sicrues pexi mokle aqvs, sicrues ver damalav) are specific to this group and do not appear in the legal evaluation group.

## Group 4: Expressions used to evaluate the sincerity or genuineness of persons, feelings, and relationships

**Label:** Sincerity and genuine inner quality of persons and expressions

**Definition:** These expressions are used to evaluate whether a person's feelings, motivations, expressions, or relationships are genuine rather than performed or feigned. They appear primarily in interpersonal, moral, and literary contexts and focus on the inner quality of a person or expression rather than on the truth-value of a proposition or the formal validity of a document.

**Representative expressions:**
- გულწრფელი (gulc̣rpeli) — "sincere, genuine, heartfelt"
- გულწრფელობა (gulc̣rpeloba) — "sincerity, candor"
- გულწრფელად (gulc̣rpelaad) — "sincerely, genuinely"
- გულწრფელი სიყვარული (gulc̣rpeli siq̇varuli) — "sincere love"
- გულწრფელი მადლობა (gulc̣rpeli madloba) — "sincere thanks"
- გულწრფელი პასუხი (gulc̣rpeli pasuxi) — "a sincere answer"
- ჭეშმარიტი სიყვარული (č̣ešmariti siq̇varuli) — "true love, genuine love"
- ჭეშმარიტი მეგობარი (č̣ešmariti megobari) — "true friend, genuine friend"
- ჭეშმარიტი პატრიოტი (č̣ešmariti pat̕riot̕i) — "true patriot, genuine patriot"
- ყალბი ღიმილი (q̇albi ɣimili) — "fake smile" (negative pole)
- ყალბი გრძნობები (q̇albi grdznobebi) — "fake feelings" (negative pole)
- ყალბი მეგობარი (q̇albi megobari) — "false friend" (negative pole, interpersonal sense)
- ნამდვილი (namdvili) — "genuine, real, authentic" (when applied to persons or relationships)
- მართალი კაცი (martali kaci) — "an honest man / a righteous man"
- მართლი (martli) — "righteous, just, upright" (in moral/religious discourse)

**Notes:** This group overlaps with Group 3 (deception) at the negative pole: ყალბი ღიმილი, ყალბი გრძნობები, and ყალბი მეგობარი (in the interpersonal sense) appear in both groups. The distinction is that Group 3 emphasizes the act of deceiving, while this group emphasizes the quality of genuineness or its absence in persons and expressions. გულწრფელი is the primary item here and does not appear prominently in the legal or propositional truth groups. ჭეშმარიტი in this group is applied to persons and relationships rather than to propositions (contrast Group 7).

## Group 5: Expressions used in journalistic and political discourse about information, claims, and sources

**Label:** Evaluation of information reliability and political claims

**Definition:** These expressions appear characteristically in journalistic, media, and political contexts to evaluate whether information is accurate, whether sources are reliable, and whether political claims or institutions are genuine. They include both native Georgian terms and borrowed terms that have become standard in these registers.

**Representative expressions:**
- ყალბი ინფორმაცია (q̇albi inpormacia) — "false information, misinformation"
- ყალბი სიახლე (q̇albi siaxle) — "fake news"
- ყალბი პროფილი (q̇albi proʼili) — "fake profile"
- გაყალბება (არჩევნების) (gaq̇albeba arčevnebis) — "election fraud"
- გაყალბება (ისტორიის) (gaq̇albeba ist̕oriis) — "falsification of history"
- სარწმუნო წყაროდან (sarc̣muno c̣q̇arodan) — "from a reliable source"
- სარწმუნო (sarc̣muno) — "credible, reliable"
- დამაჯერებელი (damajerebeli) — "convincing, credible"
- დამაჯერებლად ჟღერს (damajereblad žɣers) — "sounds convincing"
- დამაჯერებელი არ არის (damajerebeli ar aris) — "it is not convincing"
- საეჭვოდ გამოიყურება (saečvod gamoiq̇ureba) — "looks suspicious"
- საეჭვო (saečvo) — "doubtful, suspicious, questionable"
- ობიექტური შეფასება (obieqt̕uri šepaseba) — "an objective assessment"
- ობიექტურობა (obieqt̕uroba) — "objectivity"
- მიკერძოებული (mikerdzoebuli) — "biased, partial"
- მიკერძოებულობა (mikerdzoebuloba) — "bias, partiality"
- მიუკერძოებელი (miukerdzoebeli) — "unbiased, impartial"
- ფსევდო-სამეცნიერო კვლევა (psevdo-samecniero kvleva) — "pseudo-scientific research"
- ფსევდო-პატრიოტული განცხადება (psevdo-pat̕riot̕uli gancxadeba) — "pseudo-patriotic statement"
- ლეგიტიმურობის კრიზისი (legitimurobis krizisi) — "a legitimacy crisis"
- სინამდვილესთან შეუსაბამოა (sinamdvilestан šeusabamoа) — "is inconsistent with reality"
- ფაქტებთან შეუსაბამო განცხადება (paqt̕ebtан šeusabamo gancxadeba) — "a statement inconsistent with the facts"
- ჭეშმარიტების ძიება (č̣ešmaritebis dzieba) — "the search for truth" (in journalistic contexts)
- ჭეშმარიტების დადგენა (č̣ešmaritebis dadgena) — "establishment of the truth"

**Notes:** This group overlaps substantially with Group 2 (legal and administrative) because many items serve both legal and journalistic functions. The items that are more distinctively journalistic/political are: ყალბი სიახლე, ყალბი ინფორმაცია, ყალბი პროფილი, გაყალბება (არჩევნების), ობიექტური/ობიექტურობა, and მიკერძოებული/მიუკერძოებელი. ობიექტური and მიკერძოებული are noted in P1 as appearing primarily in academic, journalistic, and legal contexts; they are placed here because their most salient collocates (შეფასება "assessment," მოსამართლე "judge," მიდგომა "approach") span both domains.

## Group 6: Expressions used to characterize error or incorrectness without implying deliberate deception

**Label:** Error and incorrectness (without implication of intent)

**Definition:** These expressions are used to evaluate statements, beliefs, calculations, or actions as factually or logically incorrect, without necessarily implying that the person making the error intended to deceive. They appear in academic, scientific, educational, and everyday evaluative contexts and are typically contrasted with expressions of correctness rather than with expressions of honesty.

**Representative expressions:**
- მცდარი (mcdari) — "erroneous, mistaken, wrong"
- მცდარი შეხედულება (mcdari šexeduleba) — "a mistaken view"
- მცდარი წარმოდგენა (mcdari c̣armogdgena) — "a mistaken notion / misconception"
- მცდარი გზით მიდის (mcdari gzit midis) — "is going down the wrong path"
- მცდარად (mcdarad) — "erroneously, mistakenly"
- არასწორი (arasc̣ori) — "incorrect, wrong, inaccurate"
- არასწორი გადაწყვეტილება (arasc̣ori gadac̣q̇vet̕ileba) — "the wrong decision"
- არასწორი გაგება (arasc̣ori gageba) — "a misunderstanding"
- ეს არასწორია (es arasc̣oria) — "this is wrong / this is incorrect"
- სწორი (sc̣ori) — "correct, right, accurate" (as the positive pole of this contrast)
- სწორია თუ არა? (sc̣oria tu ara?) — "is it right or not?"
- სისწორე (sisc̣ore) — "correctness, accuracy"
- გასწორება (gasc̣oreba) — "correction, rectification"
- გასწორებული (gasc̣orebuli) — "corrected, rectified"
- მართებული (martebuli) — "correct, proper, well-founded"
- მართებულია (martebulia) — "it is correct / it is appropriate"
- უზუსტო (uzusto) — "inaccurate, imprecise"
- უზუსტობა (uzustoba) — "inaccuracy, imprecision"

**Notes:** The key distinction between this group and Group 3 (deliberate deception) is the absence of implied intent. მცდარი specifically derives from a root meaning "to err" and is used for mistakes and errors; it does not appear in contexts of forgery or deliberate falsification. არასწორი is the broadest term for incorrectness and appears across factual, normative, and procedural evaluations. სწორი and its derivatives are included here as the positive pole against which incorrectness is defined. გასწორება (correction) is the standard term for the remediation of error, further anchoring this group in the domain of non-intentional incorrectness.

## Group 7: Expressions used in philosophical, theological, and elevated literary registers to assert deep or essential truth

**Label:** Deep or essential truth in formal and elevated registers

**Definition:** These expressions appear characteristically in philosophical, theological, and elevated literary discourse to assert truth as a deep, essential, or absolute quality rather than as mere factual accuracy. They tend to appear in written rather than spoken registers and carry a higher stylistic weight than everyday truth-assertion expressions. The root č̣ešmarit- is the primary vehicle for this group.

**Representative expressions:**
- ჭეშმარიტი (č̣ešmariti) — "true, genuine, real" (in philosophical/theological use)
- ჭეშმარიტება (č̣ešmariteba) — "truth, truthfulness, veracity"
- ჭეშმარიტად (č̣ešmaritad) — "truly, in truth, verily"
- ჭეშმარიტი ღმერთი (č̣ešmariti ɣmerti) — "the true God"
- ჭეშმარიტი სარწმუნოება (č̣ešmariti sarc̣muneba) — "the true faith"
- ჭეშმარიტი შემოქმედი (č̣ešmariti šemoqmedi) — "the true Creator"
- ჭეშმარიტი ცოდნა (č̣ešmariti codna) — "true knowledge"
- ჭეშმარიტი ბუნება (č̣ešmariti buneba) — "true nature, genuine nature"
- ჭეშმარიტი მიზეზი (č̣ešmariti mizezi) — "true reason, real cause"
- ჭეშმარიტი ღირებულება (č̣ešmariti ɣirebuleba) — "true value, real worth"
- ჭეშმარიტება გამარჯვებს (č̣ešmariteba gamarjvebs) — "truth prevails"
- ჭეშმარიტების ძიება (č̣ešmaritebis dzieba) — "the search for truth"
- ჭეშმარიტების მომენტი (č̣ešmaritebis moment̕i) — "moment of truth"
- მთელი ჭეშმარიტება (mteli č̣ešmariteba) — "the whole truth"
- მთელი ჭეშმარიტება და მხოლოდ ჭეშმარიტება (mteli č̣ešmariteba da mxolod č̣ešmariteba) — "the whole truth and nothing but the truth"
- ჭეშმარიტება ითქვა (č̣ešmariteba itqva) — "the truth was spoken"
- ჭეშმარიტება თვალში გიყურებს (č̣ešmariteba tvalši giq̇urebs) — "the truth is staring you in the face"
- სიტყვის ჭეშმარიტი გაგებით (sit̕q̇vis č̣ešmariti gagebit) — "in the true sense of the word"
- ნახევრად ჭეშმარიტი (naxevrad č̣ešmariti) — "half-true"
- ჭეშმარიტი ისტორია (č̣ešmariti ist̕oria) — "true story"

**Notes:** The P1 data explicitly notes that č̣ešmariteba carries "higher register weight in philosophical and religious discourse" compared to simartle. The oath formula (მთელი ჭეშმარიტება და მხოლოდ ჭეშმარიტება) is a formal legal construction that uses č̣ešmariteba rather than simartle, suggesting that the elevated register of this root extends into formal legal ritual as well. ჭეშმარიტება გამარჯვებს (truth prevails) and ჭეშმარიტება თვალში გიყურებს (truth stares you in the face) are rhetorical/proverbial constructions that personify truth as an agent — a pattern not observed with simartle or martali.

## Group 8: Expressions used to evaluate precision and exactness of measurements, translations, and technical claims

**Label:** Precision and exactness in technical and scientific contexts

**Definition:** These expressions are used to evaluate whether a measurement, translation, calculation, or technical claim meets a standard of exactness or precision. They appear in scientific, technical, editorial, and administrative contexts and are distinguished from general correctness expressions by their focus on degree of correspondence to a standard rather than on binary right/wrong evaluation.

**Representative expressions:**
- ზუსტი (zusti) — "exact, precise, accurate"
- სიზუსტე (sizuste) — "accuracy, precision, exactness"
- ზუსტად (zustad) — "exactly, precisely, accurately"
- გაზუსტება (gazusteba) — "clarification, specification, making precise"
- გაზუსტებული (gazustebuli) — "specified, clarified, made precise"
- უზუსტო (uzusto) — "inaccurate, imprecise"
- უზუსტობა (uzustoba) — "inaccuracy, imprecision"
- სიზუსტით შეიძლება ითქვას (sizustit šeidzleba itqvas) — "it can be said with precision"
- სიზუსტის გარეშე (sizustis gareše) — "without precision"
- ზუსტი ინფორმაცია (zusti inpormacia) — "accurate information"
- ზუსტი პასუხი (zusti pasuxi) — "the exact answer"
- ზუსტად ამ მიზეზით (zustad am mizezit) — "for exactly this reason"

**Notes:** The P1 data notes that the root zust- is borrowed from Persian via a borrowing pathway, distinguishing it from the native roots mart- and č̣ešmarit-. The usage pattern of zusti/sizuste is more narrowly focused on quantitative or technical precision than sc̣ori (correct), which is used more broadly across factual and normative domains. gazusteba (making precise, specifying) is a distinctive item in this group — it refers to the process of refining a claim to greater precision, which has no direct parallel in the other groups.

## Group 9: Expressions used in the domain of law, justice, and rightness as a system

**Label:** Law, justice, and rightness as institutional or systemic concepts

**Definition:** These expressions are used to refer to law, justice, and rightness as institutional systems or domains rather than as properties of individual statements or persons. They appear in legal education, constitutional discourse, political commentary, and formal administrative language. They are all derived from the mart-/martal- root but occupy a distinct functional register from the everyday truth-assertion expressions in Group 1.

**Representative expressions:**
- სამართალი (samartali) — "law, right, justice (as a system)"
- სამართლის ფაკულტეტი (samartalis p̕akult̕et̕i) — "faculty of law"
- სამართლებრივი (samartalebr ivi) — "legal, juridical"
- სამართლებრივი სახელმწიფო (samartalebr ivi saxelmwipo) — "rule-of-law state"
- სამართლიანი (samartaliani) — "fair, just, equitable"
- სამართლიანობა (samartalianoba) — "fairness, justice, equity"
- სამართლიანად (samartalianad) — "fairly, justly"
- უსამართლო (usamartlo) — "unjust, unfair"
- უსამართლობა (usamartloba) — "injustice, unfairness"
- მართლმსაჯულება (martlmsajuleba) — "justice, administration of justice, judiciary"
- მართლმსაჯულების სისტემა (martlmsajulebis sistema) — "justice system"
- გამართლება (gamartleba) — "justification, acquittal, vindication"
- გამართლდა (gamartlda) — "was vindicated / proved to be right"
- მართლება (martleba) — "justification, vindication" (theological and legal)

**Notes:** This group is distinguished from Group 2 (legal evaluation of documents and evidence) by its focus on the system and concept of justice rather than on the evaluation of specific documents or acts. სამართალი is the standard word for law as a domain of study and practice; it does not appear in the document-forgery collocations of Group 2. სამართლიანი/სამართლიანობა are primarily moral and political terms for fairness and equity, extending beyond strictly legal contexts into everyday moral argument. The P1b data notes that this sub-paradigm (samartali → samartaliani → samartalianoba → usamartlo) represents a layered derivational productivity from the mart- root.

## Group 10: Expressions used to mark a gap between appearance and genuine reality

**Label:** Appearance versus genuine reality

**Definition:** These expressions are used to mark a contrast between how something presents itself and what it genuinely is. They appear in philosophical, literary, legal, and analytical contexts and do not necessarily imply deliberate deception — the gap between appearance and reality may be a matter of illusion, surface impression, or incomplete understanding rather than intentional fraud.

**Representative expressions:**
- მოჩვენებითი (močvenebiti) — "apparent, seeming, illusory, ostensible"
- მოჩვენებითად (močvenebitad) — "apparently, seemingly, ostensibly"
- მოჩვენებითი თანხმობა (močvenebiti tanxmoba) — "apparent consent"
- მოჩვენებითი სიმშვიდე (močvenebiti simšvide) — "apparent calm"
- მოჩვენებითად ჩანს (močvenebitad čans) — "appears only on the surface"
- ნამდვილი (namdvili) — "genuine, real, actual" (as the positive pole)
- ნამდვილი ვინ არის? (namdvili vin aris?) — "who is the real one?"
- ნამდვილი ნამდვილია (namdvili namdvilia) — "the genuine is genuine" (rhetorical tautology)
- ჭეშმარიტი ბუნება (č̣ešmariti buneba) — "true nature" (as opposed to apparent nature)
- ჭეშმარიტი მიზეზი (č̣ešmariti mizezi) — "true reason" (as opposed to stated reason)
- მიბაძვა (mibadzva) — "imitation, mimicry, copying"
- მიმბაძველი (mimbadzvelilit) — "imitator, mimic"
- მიბაძული (mibadzuli) — "imitated, copied, derivative"
- ფსევდო- (psevdo-) — prefix marking spurious claims to a status
- ნახევარი სიმართლე (naxevari simartle) — "a half-truth"
- ნახევრად ჭეშმარიტი (naxevrad č̣ešmariti) — "half-true"

**Notes:** This group overlaps with Group 3 (deliberate deception) at items like ფსევდო- and with Group 7 (deep truth) at items like ჭეშმარიტი ბუნება and ჭეშმარიტი მიზეზი. The distinguishing feature of this group is the appearance/reality contrast as such, regardless of whether deception is implied. მოჩვენებითი is the most distinctively placed item here — it marks something as merely apparent without necessarily attributing intent to any agent. მიბაძვა (imitation) is placed here because its primary evaluative function is to mark something as a copy rather than the genuine article, which is the appearance/reality contrast in the aesthetic domain.

## Ambiguous and overlapping items

The following items present genuine difficulty in placement and are noted explicitly:

**ნამდვილი (namdvili) / ნამდვილობა (namdviloba):** These items appear in Groups 2, 4, and 10. namdvili is used in legal contexts (authenticity of documents), in interpersonal contexts (genuine feelings), and in the appearance/reality contrast. The P1 data notes it as "the primary adjective for authenticity and genuineness of objects, persons, and states of affairs," which reflects this multi-group distribution.

**საეჭვო (saečvo):** Appears in Groups 2 and 5. In legal contexts it characterizes suspects and suspicious documents; in journalistic contexts it characterizes dubious claims and sources. The item does not fit cleanly into either group alone.

**სარწმუნო (sarc̣muno) / სარწმუნოება (sarc̣muneba):** sarc̣muno (credible, trustworthy) belongs to Groups 2 and 5. However, its nominalization sarc̣muneba means "faith, religion" — a significant semantic extension documented in P2a as moving from the epistemic domain into the religious domain. sarc̣muneba as "faith" does not fit any of the groups above and is noted here as an ungrouped extension.

**ყალბი მეგობარი (q̇albi megobari):** Appears in both Group 3 (deliberate deception, interpersonal sense) and Group 4 (sincerity and genuine relationships). The P1 data also notes its use in linguistics for "false friends" (faux amis), which belongs to neither group.

**გაუმართლა (gaumartla):** The P1b data flags this as a peripheral extension of the mart- root meaning "was lucky / it worked out for someone." This expression does not fit any of the groups above. Its connection to the truth-evaluation domain is etymological rather than functional in contemporary usage.

**მართლმადიდებელი (martlmadidebeli) / მართლმადიდებლობა (martlmadideblobа):** These items derive from the mart- root but refer to Eastern Orthodox Christianity. They do not fit the groups above. The P1b data flags them as a peripheral extension into the domain of religious denomination naming.

**მართლწერა (martlc̣era):** Derives from the mart- root but refers to orthography and correct spelling. It does not fit the groups above. The P1b data flags it as an extension into the domain of formal written correctness.

**სწორხაზოვანი (sc̣orxazovani):** Derives from sc̣ori but refers to rectilinear geometry. It does not fit the groups above and is noted as a technical extension of the correctness root into spatial description.

**არსებითი სახელი (arsebiti saxeli):** The grammatical term for "noun" (literally "essential name"). This is a technical extension of arsebiti into linguistic terminology and does not fit the groups above.

**ყალბი დადებითი / ყალბი უარყოფითი (q̇albi dadebiti / q̇albi uarq̇opiti):** "False positive / false negative" in scientific and medical contexts. These use the q̇alb- root but refer to systematic measurement error rather than deception or falsification. They do not fit cleanly into Group 3 (deliberate deception) or Group 6 (error without intent) and are noted as a specialized technical usage.

**ლეგიტიმაცია (legitimacia):** The process of legitimation. This item appears in political and sociological discourse and overlaps between Groups 2 and 5 without fitting either cleanly. It is noted as a borderline item.
