## 2. Substitution Patterns (Narrative)

### 2.1 REVELATION substitutes for BECOME

The CLCA category BECOME — expressing that a subject comes to instantiate a truth-domain property — has no direct morphosyntactic realization in Korean. There is no single inchoative morpheme or "become + adjective" construction equivalent to English *become true* or *turn out to be false*. Instead, the inchoative meaning is expressed entirely through a set of passive/spontaneous revelation verbs: 밝혀지다 ("to be revealed / come to light"), 드러나다 ("to be exposed"), and 판명되다 ("to be determined"). These verbs combine with the nominal + (으)로 ("as") construction to yield the inchoative-revelation reading. The result is that REVELATION and BECOME are not distinguished constructionally in Korean: a single set of forms covers both the epistemic event (something coming to be known) and the ontological change (something coming to be the case). This substitution is systematic and covers the full range of truth-domain inchoative contexts attested in P4a and P4b.

### 2.2 DISCONFIRMATION is distributed across NEGATION and REVELATION

No dedicated disconfirmation construction is attested. The function of disconfirmation — asserting that a claim is not true, or that something has been shown to be false — is expressed through two distinct constructions depending on whether the context is static or dynamic. In static contexts (direct denial), the copular negation construction 이/가 아니다 (Construction 2) is used: 그 주장은 사실이 아니다 ("That claim is not a fact"). In dynamic contexts (exposure or determination), the revelation verbs 밝혀지다 and 판명되다 (Construction 4) are used: 그 주장이 허위로 밝혀졌다 ("That claim was revealed to be false"). The two constructions are not interchangeable: 이/가 아니다 expresses a speaker's direct assertion of non-truth, while 밝혀지다/판명되다 expresses an event of coming to be known as false. DISCONFIRMATION as a unified operator is therefore distributed across NEGATION and REVELATION in Korean.

### 2.3 NEGATION is analytic, not morphological

Korean truth-domain negation is formed analytically through the separate copular negation construction 이/가 아니다, which replaces the affirmative copula 이다. There is no morphological negative affix that attaches to the predicate to negate it. The negative construction is a distinct syntactic unit (subject + nominal + 이/가 아니다) rather than a morphologically modified form of the affirmative. This is consistent with the general analytic character of Korean copular negation. No morphological negation strategy is attested for truth-domain attribution in the P4a/P4b data.

### 2.4 VERIFICATION is absent

No construction expressing the positive confirmation or verification of a truth-domain claim — a dedicated "to verify / to confirm as true" operator — is identified in the P4a or P4b data. The closest available constructions are the revelation verbs (밝혀지다, 판명되다), but these are attested only with negative truth-domain outcomes (허위로 밝혀지다, 가짜로 판명되다) in the provided data. Whether these verbs can also express positive verification (e.g., 사실로 밝혀지다, "revealed to be true") is not attested in the P4a/P4b data and cannot be confirmed under the protocol rules. VERIFICATION is therefore flagged as a structural gap based on the available data.

### 2.5 Asymmetry between falsity and truth in CAUSATION (character) and AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION

A consistent asymmetry is observed across two construction types. In agentive nominalization (Construction 7), the suffix 쟁이 attaches productively to 거짓말 to yield 거짓말쟁이 ("habitual liar"), but no parallel agentive form for a habitual truth-teller (e.g., *진실쟁이) is attested. In causation of character (Construction 5 and 7 combined), 거짓말하다 expresses the deliberate act of lying and 거짓말쟁이 nominalizes the habitual liar character, but no equivalent constructions for the deliberate act of truth-telling as a character-defining behavior are attested. This asymmetry — productive forms on the falsity side, gaps on the truth side — is preserved as found in the data and is not interpreted beyond its descriptive statement.

### 2.6 Light verb 하다 as carrier of CAUSATION semantics

In Construction 5, the verb 하다 functions as a light verb that carries the causative/agentive semantic operator when attached to act-denoting nominal bases (거짓말하다, 위조하다). The nominal base supplies the truth-domain content; 하다 supplies the agentive, transitive, and event-denoting function. This pattern is restricted to nominal bases that denote deliberate acts. For stative truth-domain adjectives (정확하다, 진실하다), 하다 functions as a predicative suffix rather than a causative light verb, and no causative reading is available from this construction alone. The periphrastic 정확하게 만들다 is noted in P4a as the available strategy for stative causation but is not attested as a productive pattern in the P1–P3 data.

### 2.7 Existential and scalar predication as functional extensions of ATTR

Constructions 8 and 9 — existential predication (이/가 있다/없다) and scalar predication (이/가 높다/낮다) — function as predication strategies for abstract quality nouns (성-nominalizations, 도-nominalizations) that do not take 이다 directly as a predicative copula in their nominalized form. These constructions extend the ATTR function to a nominal class that is blocked from the standard copular construction. They are not separate CLCA operators but are functional extensions of ATTR operating on a specific nominal subclass. They are noted here because they represent a constructional split within the ATTR domain that has no direct parallel in the canonical CLCA category list.

## 3. Missingness Summary

### 3.1 Structural Gaps

| Gap | Description |
|---|---|
| **VERIFICATION** | No dedicated construction for verifying or positively confirming a truth-domain claim is attested in P4a or P4b. The revelation verbs are attested only with negative outcomes in the available data. |
| **BECOME (bare inchoative)** | No single inchoative morpheme or "become + adjective" construction is attested. The inchoative function is structurally unavailable as an independent operator; it is subsumed by REVELATION. |

### 3.2 Lexical Gaps

| Gap | Description |
|---|---|
| **Agentive for truth-teller** | No common agentive nominalization for a habitual truth-teller is attested. The suffix 쟁이 does not attach to truth-domain positive nominals in common usage. |
| **Stative causative for truth-domain adjectives** | No common lexical verb expressing "to make [something] accurate/true/correct" is attested as a productive pattern. The periphrastic 정확하게 만들다 is noted but not confirmed as productive in the domain data. |

### 3.3 Conceptual Gaps

No conceptual gaps — cases where the meaning appears to be blocked as a category — are identifiable from the P4a/P4b data alone. The gaps identified above are structural or lexical in character. No claim is made about conceptual unavailability.

### 3.4 Asymmetric Gaps (only negative or only positive forms missing)

| Asymmetry | Positive Side | Negative Side |
|---|---|---|
| **Agentive nominalization** | No agentive for truth-teller attested (*진실쟁이 not attested) | 거짓말쟁이 [CORE] |
| **CAUSATION (character)** | No construction for habitual truth-telling character attested | 거짓말하다 [CORE]; 거짓말쟁이 [CORE] |
| **REVELATION outcomes** | Positive outcome (e.g., 사실로 밝혀지다) not attested in P4a/P4b data | 허위로 밝혀졌다; 가짜로 판명되었다 [CORE] |
| **Abstract nominalization register** | 성-forms [CORE] across registers | -(으)ㅁ-forms [MARGINAL], restricted to philosophical/formal written register |
