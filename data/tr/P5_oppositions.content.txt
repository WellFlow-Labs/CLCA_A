# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P5 OPPOSITIONS
# Language: Turkish (tr)

---

## OPPOSITION 1: doğru vs. yanlış

**Term A:** doğru  
**Term B:** yanlış  
**Transliteration:** doğru vs. yanlış  
**English translation:** true/correct vs. wrong/false/incorrect  

**Description of contrast:** Primary lexical opposition for evaluating factual correctness and accuracy of statements, information, or judgments. *Doğru* indicates correspondence to fact or standard; *yanlış* indicates deviation from fact or error.

**Contrast type:** lexical  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## OPPOSITION 51: Conditional vs. Counterfactual

**Term B:** -saydı (counterfactual)  
**Transliteration:** -sa/-se vs. -saydı  
**English translation:** if (open condition) vs. if (contrary-to-fact)

**All oppositions are marked [CORE]** — they are well-attested in the provided data and represent productive patterns in Turkish truth-evaluation vocabulary.

**Key patterns observed:**

1. **Privative morphology (*-sız/-siz/-suz/-süz*) is highly productive** for creating oppositions: haklı/haksız, tutarlı/tutarsız, geçerli/geçersiz, kusursuz/kusurlu, isabetli/isabetsiz, uygun/uygunsuz, mantıklı/mantıksız, etc.

2. **Light verb constructions create many constructional oppositions** through verb alternation (söylemek/gizlemek, kabul etmek/inkâr etmek, almak/vermek) or affirmative/negative alternation.

3. **Copular and existential constructions** show systematic affirmative/negative oppositions (olmak/olmamak, var/yok, değil negation).

4. **Question particle constructions** create binary alternative questions (doğru mu yanlış mı, gerçek mi hayal mi).

5. **Some oppositions involve asymmetric morphological marking**: doğruyu söylemek (with ACC) vs. yalan söylemek (no ACC).

6. **Spatial and directional metaphors** create oppositions: gerçek dışı/gerçek üstü (outside/above reality), akla yakın/akla uzak (near/far from reason), yerinde/yersiz (in place/without place).

7. **Register-based oppositions** exist but are limited: bile (colloquial) vs. dahi (formal) for concessive "even."

8. **Emphatic collocations** (yalan yanlış, doğru düzgün) represent cooperative rather than strictly oppositional pairings, but are included as they show systematic pairing patterns in the truth domain.
