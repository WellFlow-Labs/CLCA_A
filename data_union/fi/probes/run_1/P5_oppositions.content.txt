# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P5 OPPOSITIONS
# Language: Finnish (fi)

---

## Preliminary Note on Method

Oppositions below are extracted from patterns explicitly documented in P1, P1b, P2a, P2b, P3, P4a, and P4b. Each opposition is supported by direct textual evidence from the CONTEXT. Where the CONTEXT explicitly names a contrast, that is noted. Where the opposition is implicit but clearly supported by co-occurrence, antonym labelling, or structural pairing in the data, that is also noted. No opposition is inferred beyond what the data supports.

## Opposition 1

1. **Term A:** *tosi*
2. **Term B:** *epätosi*
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** true / untrue, false
5. **Description of contrast:** *Tosi* asserts that a proposition corresponds to reality; *epätosi* is its direct morphological negation, asserting that it does not. The CONTEXT explicitly identifies *epätosi* as "the direct negation of *tosi*" and notes it is more formal and explicitly propositional than *väärä*.
6. **Contrast type:** morphological
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 2

1. **Term A:** *tosi*
2. **Term B:** *väärä*
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** true / wrong, false
5. **Description of contrast:** The CONTEXT identifies *väärä* as "the primary antonym of both *oikea* and *tosi* in evaluative contexts." While *epätosi* is the formal propositional negation, *väärä* is the general-purpose negative evaluative term used across propositional, normative, and identity-evaluation contexts.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 3

1. **Term A:** *totuus*
2. **Term B:** *epätotuus*
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** truth / untruth, falsehood
5. **Description of contrast:** *Totuus* is the standard nominal for truth as an abstract property; *epätotuus* is its morphological negation, referring to a statement or proposition that is not true.
