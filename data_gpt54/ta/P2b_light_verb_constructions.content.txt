Below is a filtered P2b-style list of **attested multi-word constructions** built around or extending P1 items. I exclude simple derivational forms already handled in P2a, and I include only constructions that are natural and established.

## 1. Base Form: **உண்மை**
**Example:** **உண்மையைச் சொல்**  
**Transliteration:** uṇmaiyaic col  
**English translation:** tell the truth  
**Usage notes:** A very common everyday expression used in conversation, parenting, confrontation, and narrative dialogue. It directly urges someone to state what is factually true.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## 2. Base Form: **உண்மை**
**Example:** **உண்மை பேசு**  
**Transliteration:** uṇmai pēcu  
**English translation:** speak the truth  
**Usage notes:** Common in moral instruction and interpersonal situations where honesty is demanded. It overlaps with உண்மையைச் சொல் but can sound slightly more general or habitual.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## 3. Base Form: **உண்மை**
**Example:** **உண்மை தெரிந்து கொள்**  
**Transliteration:** uṇmai terintu koḷ  
**English translation:** learn the truth; come to know the truth  
**Usage notes:** Used when someone is urged to find out what really happened or what is actually the case. Common in conversation, journalism, and reflective prose.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## 4. Base Form: **உண்மை**
**Example:** **உண்மை வெளிவரு**  
**Transliteration:** uṇmai veḷivaru  
**English translation:** the truth comes out  
**Usage notes:** A common expression for hidden facts becoming known. It is frequent in media language, crime reporting, and dramatic narration.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## 5. Base Form: **உண்மை**

**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## 6. Base Form: **உண்மை**
