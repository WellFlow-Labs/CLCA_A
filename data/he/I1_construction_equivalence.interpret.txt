## 2. Substitution Patterns (Narrative)

### REVELATION substitutes for dedicated epistemic verbs
Hebrew expresses revelation through **metaphorical light verb constructions** (Construction 20) rather than dedicated revelation verbs. Truth is conceptualized as a hidden object that can emerge (לצאת לאור "come to light"), be revealed (לגלות), or be hidden (להסתיר). This pattern is highly productive and shows no restrictions, functioning as the primary means of expressing revelation semantics.

### BECOME + agent noun substitutes for CAUSATION (character)
Hebrew lacks a dedicated construction for "make X into a liar/believer" (causation of character). Instead, it uses:
- **BECOME constructions** (להפוך ל- + agent noun): הפך לשקרן "became a liar"
- The causative meaning must be expressed periphrastically through separate causative verbs + BECOME

This represents a **structural gap** where character causation requires multi-clausal expression rather than a single construction.

### VERIFICATION and DISCONFIRMATION require circumlocution
Neither verification nor disconfirmation has a dedicated constructional pattern in the truth domain. Both require:
- **Light verb constructions** with specific verbs (לאמת "verify," להוכיח "prove," להפריך "refute")
- **Negation + attribution** for disconfirmation
- No morphological or syntactic pattern specific to these operations

This represents a **lexical gap** rather than a structural one—the operations exist but are not grammaticalized into dedicated constructions.

### Asymmetry in causative lexicalization
The causative להאמין (leha'amin) "to believe/make believe" has **lexicalized** away from transparent causative meaning, while להצדיק (lehatsדik) "to justify" retains transparent causative semantics. This shows variable lexicalization within the same morphological pattern (Hif'il binyan).

### No asymmetry between positive and negative forms
Hebrew shows **symmetric availability** of positive and negative constructions:
- Attribution: זה אמת / זה לא אמת
- Adnominal: סיפור אמיתי / סיפור לא אמיתי
- Existential: יש אמת / אין אמת

Both analytic negation (לא) and existential negation (אין) are fully productive with no gaps.

### Metaphorical extensions show consistent patterns
Truth-domain nouns consistently participate in metaphorical constructions treating truth as:
- **Object that can be hidden/revealed**: להסתיר את האמת, לגלות את האמת
- **Object that can emerge**: האמת תצא לאור
- **Object that can be sought**: לחפש את האמת

This metaphorical system is productive and unrestricted, serving as the primary means of expressing discovery/revelation semantics.

## 3. Missingness Summary

### Structural Gaps
1. **CAUSATION (character)**: No dedicated construction for "make X into a liar/believer"
   - Must use BECOME + agent noun or multi-clausal periphrasis
   - Pattern: הפך לשקרן "became a liar" (not *"made into a liar" as single construction)

### Lexical Gaps
1. **VERIFICATION**: No dedicated constructional pattern
   - Requires light verb constructions: לאמת "to verify," להוכיח "to prove"
   - Not grammaticalized into truth-domain construction system

2. **DISCONFIRMATION**: No dedicated constructional pattern
   - Requires light verb constructions: להפריך "to refute," להכחיש "to deny"
   - Or negation + verification constructions

### Conceptual Gaps
**None identified.** All semantic operations appear expressible, though some require circumlocution or substitution by related operators.

### Asymmetric Gaps
**None identified.** Hebrew shows symmetric availability of:
- Positive and negative attribution
- Positive and negative adnominal modification
- Positive and negative existential constructions
- Truth and falsity terms across all construction types

## 4. Canonicalized Operator Inventory

### Fully Available
1. **ATTR**: Subject + copula + truth-predicate (Construction 1)
   - Status: Core, unrestricted, high frequency

2. **ADNOM**: Noun + attributive adjective (Construction 3)
   - Status: Core, unrestricted, high frequency

3. **BECOME**: להיות/להפוך + adjective/noun (Construction 4)
   - Status: Core, unrestricted, high frequency

4. **NEGATION**: לא/אין + predicate (Construction 2)
   - Status: Core, unrestricted, high frequency
   - Type: Analytic (not morphological)

5. **NOMINALIZATION**: Multiple productive patterns (Constructions 6, 7)
   - Abstract: -ות suffix (אמיתות "truthfulness")
   - Agentive: -ן suffix (שקרן "liar") and participles (מאמין "believer")
   - Status: Core, unrestricted, high frequency

### Available with Substitution
6. **REVELATION**: Expressed through metaphorical light verb constructions (Construction 20)
   - Substitution: Metaphorical extensions (לצאת לאור "come to light," לגלות "reveal")
   - Status: Core pattern, fully productive

7. **CAUSATION (factual)**: Hif'il binyan morphological causatives (Construction 5)
   - Direct availability: להצדיק "to justify"
   - Status: Core, productive morphological pattern

8. **CAUSATION (character)**: Substituted by BECOME + agent noun
   - Substitution: הפך לשקרן "became a liar" (not dedicated causative)
   - Status: Structural gap requiring periphrasis

### Partially Available
9. **VERIFICATION**: No dedicated construction; requires light verbs
   - Circumlocution: לאמת, להוכיח + truth-noun
   - Status: Lexical gap, not grammaticalized

10. **DISCONFIRMATION**: No dedicated construction; requires light verbs or negation
    - Circumlocution: להפריך, להכחיש or negation + verification
    - Status: Lexical gap, not grammaticalized

### Missing Entirely
**None.** All semantic operators are expressible, though some require circumlocution or substitution.

---

## Summary Statement

Hebrew provides **direct constructional support** for 7 of 10 canonical CLCA operators (ATTR, ADNOM, BECOME, NEGATION, NOMINALIZATION, CAUSATION-factual, REVELATION). 

**CAUSATION (character)** is structurally substituted by BECOME + agent noun constructions.

**VERIFICATION and DISCONFIRMATION** represent lexical gaps—they are expressible through light verb constructions but lack dedicated grammaticalized patterns within the truth-domain construction system.

The language shows **no conceptual gaps** and **no asymmetric gaps** between positive/negative or truth/falsity forms. All constructions rated [CORE] in P4b show high naturalness and productivity with no register restrictions except for specialized legal/biblical terms.
