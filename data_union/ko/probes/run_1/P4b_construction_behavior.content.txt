## Summary Observations

1. **All ten constructions rated [CORE] except one**: 거짓되다 (Construction 9, second example) is rated [MARGINAL] due to its restriction to literary and formal written registers and its lower frequency relative to competing forms (거짓이다, 거짓말이다).

2. **No construction is rated [UNCERTAIN]**: All constructions in P4a are sufficiently attested and described to support a definite naturalness rating.

3. **Register stratification is the primary axis of variation**: The constructions themselves are generally stable; what varies is register appropriateness (e.g., 거짓말쟁이 vs. 위조범; 정직히 vs. 정직하게; 진실로 vs. 실제로).

4. **Complementary distribution is confirmed**: The -성 / -음 nominalization split, the -히 / -게 adverbial split, and the 하다 / 되다 predicate split all show base-type or semantic conditioning rather than free variation.

5. **No symmetric truth-teller agentive is attested**: The absence of a lexicalized agentive noun for "truth-teller" (noted in P4a Construction 7) is confirmed as a genuine gap, not an oversight.
