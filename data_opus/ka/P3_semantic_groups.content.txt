# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P3 NATURAL SEMANTIC GROUPING
## Language: Georgian (ka)
## Transliteration: ISO 9984 (1996), consistent with prior steps

The following groupings are derived **solely from usage patterns** documented in P1, P1b, P2a, and P2b — specifically: what each term predicates of, what collocations and constructions it enters, what it is contrasted against, and its register. Groupings overlap where usage overlaps. Items that resist grouping are noted explicitly at the end.

---

## GROUP 1 — Affirming that a statement/speaker is right (everyday speech frames)

**Definition:** Terms used in direct conversational frames to confirm or contest the correctness of what someone says or of a stated proposition. In practice these appear as short predicate frames ("you are right," "you are speaking the truth") and turn on factual/propositional correctness rather than authenticity or moral character.

**Representative expressions:**
- მართალი ხარ (martali xar) "you are right"
- მართალს ამბობ (martals ambob) "you are speaking the truth"
- სწორი ხარ (sc̣ori xar) "you are correct"
- სიმართლეს ვამბობ (simartles vambob) "I am telling the truth"
- მართალი / სწორი პასუხი (sc̣ori p̣asuxi) "the correct answer"
- მართლა (martla) "really?", მართლაც (martlac) "indeed, sure enough"

**Internal contrast noted in usage:** P2b explicitly distinguishes *martali xar* (more moral) from *sc̣ori xar* (more factual/accuracy-based). Both occupy the same conversational slot.

---

## GROUP 2 — The act of telling truth or lies (speech-act collocations)

**Definition:** Noun + verbal-noun (or noun + speech verb) constructions referring to the act of truth-telling or lying. In practice these cluster around a shared genitive + *tkma* ("telling") frame and around accusatory/assertive predicates.

**Representative expressions:**
- სიმართლის თქმა (simartlis tkma) "telling the truth"
- ტყუილის თქმა (ṭq̇uilis tkma) "telling a lie"
- ტყუილს ამბობ (ṭq̇uils ambob) "you are lying"
- მართალი გითხრა / სიმართლე გითხრა (martali gitxra / simartle gitxra) "to tell you the truth" (discourse parenthetical)
- ცრუობა (cruoba) "to lie", მოტყუება (moṭq̇ueba) "to deceive"
- ტყუილი გამოვიდა (ṭq̇uili gamovida) "it turned out to be a lie"

**Note:** This group is where the truth-pole (სიმართლე) and lie-pole (ტყუილი, ცრუ-) terms share the *same* constructional frame and are mutually contrastive.

---

## GROUP 3 — Elevated / religious "deep truth" vocabulary

**Definition:** Terms reserved for absolute, ultimate, or theological truth, as opposed to everyday factual correctness. Marked by elevated register and recurrence in religious/philosophical collocations.

**Representative expressions:**
- ჭეშმარიტი (č̣ešmariti) "true, veritable"
- ჭეშმარიტება (č̣ešmariteba) "truth, verity"
- ჭეშმარიტად (č̣ešmaritad) "verily"
- ჭეშმარიტი გზა (č̣ešmariti gza) "the true path"
- ჭეშმარიტი ღმერთი (č̣ešmariti ɣmerti) "the true God"
- ჭეშმარიტების ძიება (č̣ešmaritebis dzieba) "the search for truth"

**Usage distinction:** P1 explicitly contrasts this set with "mere factual correctness." It does not enter the everyday conversational frames of Group 1.

---

## GROUP 4 — Genuine vs. fake (authenticity of objects and persons)

**Definition:** Terms predicating that an object or person is the real thing versus an imitation or forgery. In practice they pair with concrete nouns (gold, money, documents, friend) and contrast genuineness against counterfeit/fakeness.

**Representative expressions — genuine pole:**
- ნამდვილი (namdvili) "real, genuine" — ნამდვილი ოქრო, ნამდვილი მეგობარი, ნამდვილი ამბავი
- ნამდვილობა (namdviloba) "authenticity" — ნამდვილობის დადგენა
- ნაღდი (naɣdi) "genuine, solid" (colloquial) — ნაღდი კაცი
- ავთენტური (avtenṭuri) "authentic" (technical/scholarly) — ავთენტური დოკუმენტი

**Representative expressions — fake pole:**
- ყალბი (q̇albi) "fake, counterfeit" — ყალბი ფული, ყალბი დოკუმენტი
- გაყალბება (gaq̇albeba) "to falsify/forge", გაყალბებული (gaq̇albebuli) "forged"

**Internal register split noted:** ნამდვილი (everyday) / ნაღდი (colloquial) / ავთენტური (technical) and ნამდვილობა (formal) occupy the same authenticity slot at different register levels — explicitly flagged in P2a/P2b as competing (e.g., avtenṭuroba vs. namdviloba).

---

## GROUP 5 — Reliability / trustworthiness of sources and persons

**Definition:** Terms predicating that a source, person, or thing can be relied upon or counted on. In practice they attach to sources, information, persons, and equipment, and take the negating prefix *ara-*.

**Representative expressions:**
- სანდო (sando) "trustworthy" — სანდო წყარო, სანდო ინფორმაცია; სანდოობა; არასანდო
- საიმედო (saimedo) "dependable" — საიმედო ადამიანი; საიმედოობა; არასაიმედო

**Internal distinction noted in usage:** P1 #13 explicitly states სანდო emphasizes honesty/credibility while საიმედო emphasizes dependability (e.g., a car). They overlap but are not interchangeable.

---

## GROUP 6 — Accuracy / precision against a standard

**Definition:** Terms for conformity to a measurable or fixed standard — correctness of answers, calculations, measurements, time. In practice these are less moral than the "right" terms and cluster in technical and educational collocations.

**Representative expressions:**
- სწორი (sc̣ori) "correct, accurate" — სწორი პასუხი; სისწორე; სწორად; გასწორება, გამოსწორება
- ზუსტი (zusṭi) "exact, precise" — ზუსტი დრო, ზუსტი მეცნიერებები; სიზუსტე; ზუსტად; დაზუსტება
- უტყუარი (uṭq̇uari) "unmistakable, infallible" — უტყუარი მტკიცებულება (elevated)

**Overlap noted:** სწორი also appears in Group 1 (sc̣ori xar). უტყუარი is borderline — it concerns incontrovertibility of evidence (elevated register) and could attach to Group 5 (reliability) or Group 2 (lie-pole, via its privative ṭq̇u- root).

---

## GROUP 7 — Sincerity / honesty of a person or expression

**Definition:** Terms evaluating the moral honesty, openness, or heartfelt genuineness of a person or their speech. In practice they predicate of persons, conversations, and expressions of feeling, and cluster in moral assessment.

**Representative expressions:**
- გულწრფელი (gulc̣rpeli) "sincere" — გულწრფელი მადლობა; გულწრფელობა; გულწრფელად
- გულახდილი (gulaxdili) "frank, open" — გულახდილი საუბარი; გულახდილობა; გულახდილად
- პატიოსანი (p̣aṭiosani) "honest, honorable" — პატიოსანი ადამიანი, პატიოსანი სიტყვა; პატიოსნება; არაპატიოსანი

**Overlap noted:** მართალი in its "righteous person" sense (P1 #4) borders this group; but its dominant documented use is in the propositional frames of Group 1.

---

## GROUP 8 — Falsehood and deception (the negative pole)

**Definition:** Terms naming falseness, lying, or deception as a quality or act, frequently in moral, legal, and religious framing. In practice they split between abstract/moral falsehood (ცრუ-) and concrete acts of lying/deceiving (ტყუ-).

**Representative expressions — moral/abstract falsehood:**
- ცრუ (cru) "false, lying" — ცრუ მოწმე, ცრუ ფიცი, ცრუ წინასწარმეტყველი
- სიცრუე (sicrue) "falsehood, mendacity"

**Representative expressions — concrete act of lying/deceiving:**
- ტყუილი (ṭq̇uili) "a lie"
- მოტყუება (moṭq̇ueba) "deception", მატყუარა (maṭq̇uara) "liar", გატყუება (gaṭq̇ueba) "to dupe"

**Internal distinction noted in usage (from P1b):** ცრუ- = moral/abstract falsity, often religious/legal; ტყუ- = the everyday concrete act. This split is supported by the collocations (cru c̣inasc̣armeṭq̇veli "false prophet" vs. ṭq̇uilis tkma "telling a lie").

**Overlap with Group 2:** the act-of-lying members (ტყუილის თქმა, ცრუობა) also sit in the speech-act group.

---

## GROUP 9 — Proper / appropriate / due (formal-register fittingness)

**Definition:** Terms evaluating whether something conforms to what is proper, fitting, or required in a situation, used predominantly in formal, legal, and administrative texts. In practice they attach to decisions, attention, and procedures rather than to factual truth or genuineness.

**Representative expressions:**
- მართებული (martebuli) "proper, fitting" — მართებული გადაწყვეტილება; მართებულობა; მართებულად
- ჯეროვანი (ǰerovani) "due, adequate" — ჯეროვანი ყურადღება; ჯეროვნად

**Register note:** All members are flagged [MARGINAL] and register-restricted (formal/legal) in P1/P2a/P2b.

---

## GROUP 10 — Credibility / persuasiveness of an account

**Definition:** Terms evaluating whether an argument, testimony, or performance succeeds in being believed. In practice they attach to arguments and accounts and concern persuasive effect rather than the truth-value of the content.

**Representative expressions:**
- დამაჯერებელი (damaǰerebeli) "convincing, credible" — დამაჯერებელი არგუმენტი; დამაჯერებლად; დამაჯერებლობა

**Note:** This is a small group (effectively one base item with its derivatives). Usage distinguishes it from Group 5 (reliability of a *source*) and Group 1 (correctness of a *statement*): დამაჯერებელი concerns whether something *causes belief*, irrespective of its actual truth.

---

## GROUP 11 — Resemblance and sameness

**Definition:** Terms for likeness between things or identity of a thing with itself/another. In practice they enter comparison and description collocations and do not interact with the truth/lie or honesty frames.

**Representative expressions:**
- მსგავსი (msgavsi) "similar" — მსგავსი შემთხვევა, ერთმანეთის მსგავსი
- მსგავსება (msgavseba) "resemblance" — მსგავსების პოვნა
- იგივეობა (igiveoba) "identity, sameness" (technical/philosophical)

**Note:** This group stands apart from the rest of the field; its members share no constructional frames with the truth/genuineness/honesty terms. იგივეობა is [MARGINAL] and shows no documented constructions of its own (P2b).

---

## GROUP 12 — Fundamental nature / essence

**Definition:** Terms denoting the inherent nature, essence, or core of a thing or person, used in philosophical and evaluative discourse. In practice they enter genitive collocations ("the essence of the matter," "human nature") and idioms of "getting to the core."

**Representative expressions:**
- არსი (arsi) "essence, gist" — საქმის არსი, არსის ჩაწვდომა
- არსება (arseba) "essence, being" (also "creature" — sense-shifted)
- ბუნება (buneba) "nature, character" — ადამიანის ბუნება, ბუნებით

**Note:** These connect loosely to the genuineness field (what something fundamentally *is*) but in usage form their own genitive-collocation cluster. არსება and ბუნება each have a documented non-essence sense (creature; natural-science) that falls outside the field.

---

## Cross-cutting observations on overlap

- **სწორი (sc̣ori)** appears in both Group 1 (conversational confirmation) and Group 6 (accuracy against a standard); usage supports both.
- **მართალი (martali)** appears centrally in Group 1 but has a documented "righteous person" sense bordering Group 7.
- **The ტყუ-/ცრუ- members** straddle Group 2 (speech acts) and Group 8 (the falsehood pole).
- **უტყუარი (uṭq̇uari)** is genuinely ambiguous: built on the lie-root (ṭq̇u-) but used for incontrovertible *evidence*, placing it near Group 6 and Group 5 simultaneously.
- **The -ad adverbs and abstract -eba/-oba nouns** generally inherit their base's group; they were not split out separately.

---

## Items that resist clean grouping

- **მართლა / მართლაც (martla / martlac)** "really / indeed" — function primarily as discourse-confirmation particles; listed under Group 1 but their use is closer to general intensification than to predicating truth.
- **ნამდვილად / ნაღდად / ბუნებრივად** "really / for sure / naturally" — explicitly flagged in P1b/P2a as having *shifted* from their base meaning toward adverbial confirmation/intensification; they do not predicate authenticity, reliability, or nature in this use and are best treated as ungrouped intensifiers.
- **სიცრუე (#10), იგივეობა (#18), მართებულობა (#27)** — P2b records no common multi-word constructions; they participate in the field only through their base adjective/root and have thin independent usage.
- **The semantically-shifted მართ-/მართლ- forms** (e.g., მართვა "driving/governing," მმართველი "ruler," გამართვა "to fix/arrange," მართმადიდებელი "Orthodox," სამართალი "law") — flagged in P1b as having moved out of the truth-evaluation field. They are **not grouped here**, as their documented usage no longer bears on truth, genuineness, or honesty.
