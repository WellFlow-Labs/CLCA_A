## 5. CAUSATIVE

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| *i-* + [correctness adjective] | *Itama ang pagkakamali.* | Itama ang pagkakamali | "Correct the mistake." | [CORE] | Productive for correctness adjectives (*tama*, *wasto*, *akma*); used across registers in instructional and corrective contexts. |
| *i-* + [correctness adjective] | *Iwasto ang mali.* | Iwasto ang mali | "Set the wrong right. / Correct the error." | [CORE] | Same pattern; no register restrictions attested for this sub-type. |

**Attested alternates:** P4a attests a distinct causative pattern for the verification/proof domain: *pa-* + *tunay* + *-an* → *patunayan* ("to prove to someone; to cause to be proven"). This is reported as a separate morphological pattern, not an alternate of the *i-* construction. The *i-* causative is explicitly noted as **not attested** for truth-predicate adjectives (*totoo*, *tunay*).

## 6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Circumfix *ka-* … *-an* / *-han* + [root] | *katotohanan* | katotohanan | "truth / the truth / reality" | [CORE] | The primary mechanism for abstract noun formation in this field; highly consistent across both indigenous roots and integrated borrowings. |
| Circumfix *ka-* … *-an* / *-han* + [root] | *kasinungalingan* | kasinungalingan | "lie / falsehood / untruth" | [CORE] | Same pattern; no restrictions on use attested. |

**Attested alternates:** P4a attests a second nominalization pattern, **pag- + [verbal stem]**, producing action nominals: *pagpapatunay* ("the act of verifying/proving"), *pagsisinungaling* ("the act of lying"), *pagwawasto* ("the act of correcting"), *pagkakamali* ("a specific instance of error"). This is reported as a distinct pattern with a different semantic output (action / event nominal vs. abstract state/quality nominal), not a direct alternate of the *ka-…-an* circumfix.

## 7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Root form used directly as noun ("liar") | *Sinungaling ka.* | Sinungaling ka | "You are a liar." | [CORE] | *Sinungaling* is lexically supplied rather than productively derived; used freely across registers as both noun and adjective. |
| Root form used directly as noun ("liar") | *Huwag kang maging sinungaling.* | Huwag kang maging sinungaling | "Don't be a liar." | [CORE] | Same item in nominal predicate position; no restrictions attested. |

**Attested alternates:** P4a notes that no parallel single-word agentive for "truth-teller" is attested. The concept is expressed periphrastically: *ang nagsasabi ng katotohanan* ("one who tells the truth"). The *taga-* prefix is noted as capable of forming agentives (*tagapagtanggol ng katotohanan*) but these are not reported as lexicalized stable single-word items in this field. These periphrastic and prefixed forms are not rated here as they are not identified as constructions in P4a; they are reported as the absence of a parallel lexicalized item.

Its pragmatic force is reported as similarly non-compositional.
