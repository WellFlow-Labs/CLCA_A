# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2a Derivational System
# Language: Tamil (ta)

## DERIVATIONAL PATTERNS IDENTIFIED

### Pattern Analysis Note
Tamil derivational morphology for truth-related terms shows several productive patterns:
1. **Adjectival suffix -āṉa** (ஆன): Highly productive across multiple bases
2. **Adverbial suffix -āka** (ஆக): Productive for manner/state expressions
3. **Locative -il** (இல்): Used for abstract location/state
4. **Abstract noun suffix -mai** (மை): Classical pattern for abstract concepts
5. **Quality noun suffix -taṉmai** (தன்மை): Formal register for properties
6. **Agentive suffixes -aṉ/-āḷ/-ar** (அன்/ஆள்/அர்): Person markers with gender/number distinctions

## DERIVATIONS BY BASE FORM

### Base: உண்மை (uṇmai) - "truth"

#### D1.1
**Base Form:** உண்மை (uṇmai)
**Derived Form:** உண்மையான (uṇmaiyāṉa)
**Transliteration:** uṇmaiyāṉa
**Literal Breakdown:** uṇmai (truth) + āṉa (adjectival suffix)
**Translation:** true, real, genuine, authentic
**Usage:** Primary adjectival form modifying nouns. Extremely productive and common in all registers of speech and writing.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### D1.2
**Base Form:** உண்மை (uṇmai)
**Derived Form:** உண்மையாக (uṇmaiyāka)
**Transliteration:** uṇmaiyāka
**Literal Breakdown:** uṇmai (truth) + āka (adverbial suffix)
**Translation:** truly, truthfully, really
**Usage:** Adverbial form indicating manner or degree. Used both for truth-evaluation and as general intensifier.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### D1.3
**Base Form:** உண்மை (uṇmai)
**Derived Form:** உண்மையில் (uṇmaiyil)
**Transliteration:** uṇmaiyil
**Literal Breakdown:** uṇmai (truth) + il (locative case marker)
**Translation:** in truth, actually, in reality
**Usage:** Functions as discourse marker and adverbial emphasizing actual truth or reality of a statement.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### D1.4
**Base Form:** உண்மை (uṇmai)
**Derived Form:** உண்மைத்தன்மை (uṇmaittaṉmai)
**Transliteration:** uṇmaittaṉmai
**Literal Breakdown:** uṇmai (truth) + taṉmai (quality/nature)
**Translation:** truthfulness, veracity, the quality of being true
**Usage:** Formal abstract noun for the property or quality of truth. Common in academic and legal registers.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### D1.5
**Base Form:** உண்மை (uṇmai)
**Derived Form:** உண்மையற்ற (uṇmaiyaṟṟa)
**Transliteration:** uṇmaiyaṟṟa
**Literal Breakdown:** uṇmai (truth) + aṟṟa (without - negative adjectival)
**Translation:** without truth, untrue, false
**Usage:** Negative adjectival form indicating absence of truth. Used in formal and written contexts.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### D1.6
**Base Form:** உண்மை (uṇmai)
**Derived Form:** உண்மையின்மை (uṇmaiyiṉmai)
**Transliteration:** uṇmaiyiṉmai
**Literal Breakdown:** uṇmai (truth) + iṉmai (absence/non-existence)
**Translation:** absence of truth, untruth, falsity
**Usage:** Abstract noun for the state of lacking truth. Formal and literary register.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## PRODUCTIVITY NOTES

### High Productivity Patterns

1. **-āṉa adjectival derivation**: Extremely productive across nearly all nominal bases in the truth domain. This is the default adjectival formation strategy in Tamil.

2. **-āka adverbial derivation**: Highly productive for creating manner adverbs from nominal bases. Nearly parallel to -āṉa in distribution.

3. **-aṟṟa negative adjectival**: Productive pattern for creating negative adjectives meaning "without X" or "lacking X". Particularly common with abstract nouns.

### Moderate Productivity Patterns

4. **-mai abstract nominalization**: Classical Tamil pattern still productive in formal registers. Creates abstract nouns from roots and other nominals.

5. **-taṉmai quality nominalization**: Formal pattern for creating nouns denoting qualities or properties. More restricted than -mai.

### Limited Productivity Patterns

6. **Agentive suffixes (-aṉ, -āḷ, -ar)**: Only productively attested with பொய் (poy) in this domain. Other bases do not readily form agentive nouns.

7. **Locative -il as derivational**: Functions derivationally with some bases (உண்மை, பொய்) but not a general productive pattern.

### Gaps and Asymmetries

- Not all bases take all derivational suffixes
- Sanskrit loans (நிஜம், நியாயம், சாத்தியம், etc.) fully participate in Tamil derivational morphology
- Some bases (like ஏமாற்று) show verbal rather than nominal derivational patterns
- Agentive derivation is highly restricted, appearing productively only with பொய்
