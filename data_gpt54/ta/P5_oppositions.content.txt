1. **Term A:** உண்மை  
   **Term B:** பொய்  
   **Transliteration:** uṇmai / poy  
   **English translation:** truth, reality / falsehood, lie  
   **Description of contrast:** This is the clearest basic lexical opposition in the data: what is true or real versus what is false or deceptive. It appears repeatedly across P1–P4 in both standalone and constructional use.  
   **Contrast type:** lexical  
   **Epistemic status:** [CORE]
