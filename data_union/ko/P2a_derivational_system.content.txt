# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2a Derivational System
## Language: Korean (ko)
### Transliteration Standard: Revised Romanization of Korean (RR)

---

## Preliminary Note on Scope

This step documents attested derivational morphology for the P1 lexical items. "Derivational morphology" is understood here as the attachment of morphological material to a base to produce a new lexical item of a different grammatical category or with a substantially different meaning — distinct from inflection (which Korean largely handles through verbal endings) and from compounding (which was the primary focus of P1b). The principal derivational processes attested in this domain are:

- **-하다 attachment**: converts a nominal base into a verb/predicate
- **-되다 attachment**: converts a nominal base into a passive or stative predicate
- **-성 (性) suffixation**: derives an abstract quality noun from a nominal base
- **-적 (的) suffixation**: derives an adjectival/adverbial base from a nominal
- **-히 / -이 / -로 suffixation**: derives adverbs from adjectival bases
- **-음 / -기 nominalization**: derives nouns from verbal bases
- **-쟁이 / -자 (者) agentive suffixation**: derives person-denoting nouns
- **-화 (化) suffixation**: derives a transformation/process noun or verb
- **-도 (度) suffixation**: derives a degree/measure noun
- **-감 (感) suffixation**: derives a feeling/sense noun
- **Negative prefixation**: 비- (非-), 불- (不-), 무- (無-)

Not all processes apply to all bases. Gaps and asymmetries are preserved and noted.

Korean derivational morphology relevant to this domain operates primarily through:
- Nominal suffixes: -성 (性, -seong), -화 (化, -hwa), -적 (的, -jeok), -자 (者, -ja), -품 (品, -pum), -죄 (罪, -joe), -제 (制, -je)
- Verbal formation: -하다 (-hada), -되다 (-doeda), -시키다 (-sikida)
- Adverbial formation: -히 (-hi), -로/-으로 (-ro/-euro), -게 (-ge)
- Negative prefixation: 비- (非-, bi-), 불- (不-, bul-), 무- (無-, mu-)
- Intensifying or specifying prefixes: 순- (純-, sun-), 반- (反-, ban-)

Not all of these apply to all bases. Asymmetries are preserved throughout.

### 1. Base Form: 진실 (jinsil)

---

**Derived Form 1**

1. **Base Form:** 진실 (jinsil)
2. **Derived Form:** 진실하다
3. **Transliteration:** jinsil-hada
4. **Literal Breakdown:** 眞實(truth) + 하다(to do/be)
5. **Natural English Translation:** to be truthful, to be sincere, to be genuine
6. **Usage:** Predicative form used to characterize persons or their conduct as honest and without pretense. Common in moral evaluation and interpersonal contexts across spoken and written registers.
7. **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2**

1. **Base Form:** 진실 (jinsil)
2. **Derived Form:** 진실성
3. **Transliteration:** jinsilseong
4. **Literal Breakdown:** 眞實(truth) + 性(-ness, abstract quality)
5. **Natural English Translation:** truthfulness, authenticity, sincerity as a quality; veracity, truth-value
6. **Usage:** Used in evaluative discourse — journalistic, legal, and academic — to assess whether a person, account, or work possesses the quality of being truthful or genuine. Appears in constructions like 진실성을 검증하다 (to verify truthfulness).
7. **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 3**

1. **Base Form:** 진실 (jinsil)
2. **Derived Form:** 진실되다
3. **Transliteration:** jinsil-doeda
4. **Literal Breakdown:** 眞實(truth) + 되다(to become/be)
5. **Natural English Translation:** to be truthful, to be genuine
6. **Usage:** Stative predicate used to describe persons or expressions as genuinely truthful; appears in literary and formal registers with a nuance of inherent rather than performed quality, distinguishing it from 진실하다. Slightly more resultant or passive in nuance than 진실하다.
7. **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 4**

1. **Base Form:** 진실 (jinsil)
2. **Derived Form:** 진실된
3. **Transliteration:** jinsil-doen
4. **Literal Breakdown:** 眞實(truth) + 된(adnominal form of 되다)
5. **Natural English Translation:** truthful, genuine, sincere (as a modifier)
6. **Usage:** Adnominal modifier form derived from 진실되다, used to modify nouns directly: 진실된 사람 (a truthful person), 진실된 마음 (a sincere heart). Distinct from 진실한 (from 진실하다) in that 진실된 carries a slightly more evaluative or achieved quality.
7. **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 5**

1. **Base Form:** 진실 (jinsil)
2. **Derived Form:** 진실한
3. **Transliteration:** jinsil-han
4. **Literal Breakdown:** 眞實(truth) + 한(adnominal form of 하다)
5. **Natural English Translation:** truthful, sincere (as a modifier)
6. **Usage:** Adnominal modifier form derived from 진실하다, used to modify nouns directly: 진실한 사람 (a truthful person), 진실한 대답 (a truthful answer). Extremely common across all registers.
7. **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 6**

1. **Base Form:** 진실 (jinsil)
2. **Derived Form:** 진실되게
3. **Transliteration:** jinsil-doege
4. **Literal Breakdown:** 진실되다(to be truthful) + 게(adverbializing suffix)
5. **Natural English Translation:** truthfully, genuinely
6. **Usage:** Adverbial form derived from 진실되다. Used to describe how something is said or done — e.g., 진실되게 말하다 (to speak truthfully). Less frequent than 진실하게 but attested in written and spoken moral discourse.
7. **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 7**

1. **Base Form:** 진실 (jinsil)
2. **Derived Form:** 진실하게
3. **Transliteration:** jinsil-hage
4. **Literal Breakdown:** 진실하다(to be truthful) + 게(adverbializing suffix)
5. **Natural English Translation:** truthfully, honestly, sincerely
6. **Usage:** Adverbial form derived from 진실하다. Used to describe the manner of speech or conduct as honest and sincere. Appears in 진실하게 대하다 (to treat someone sincerely).
7. **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 8**

1. **Base Form:** 진실 (jinsil)
2. **Derived Form:** 진실로
3. **Transliteration:** jinsilro
4. **Literal Breakdown:** 眞實(truth) + 로(adverbial particle)
5. **Natural English Translation:** truly, in truth, sincerely
6. **Usage:** Adverbial expression used to emphasize the genuine or sincere character of a statement or action. Appears in formal speech, religious texts, and literary prose.
7. **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 9**

1. **Base Form:** 진실 (jinsil)
2. **Derived Form:** 불진실
3. **Transliteration:** buljinsil
4. **Literal Breakdown:** 不(not) + 眞實(truth)
5. **Natural English Translation:** untruthfulness, insincerity
6. **Usage:** Negated nominal form. Less common than 거짓 or 허위 for expressing falsehood; appears in formal or philosophical contexts where the negation of truthfulness (rather than the assertion of falsehood) is the relevant concept. Marginal in everyday use.
7. **Epistemic Status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**Note on 진실:** The -적 (的) suffixation to produce 진실적 is not attested as a standard form; 진실한 (the attributive adjectival form of 진실하다) serves the attributive function instead. No agentive derivation from 진실 alone is attested. No passive or causative verbal derivations from 진실 are identified.

7. **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2**

1. **Base Form:** 사실 (sasil)
2. **Derived Form:** 사실적으로

5. **Natural English Translation:** realistically, factually, in a true-to-life manner
6. **Usage:** Adverbial form of 사실적, used to characterize how something is depicted or described. Appears in literary criticism and everyday evaluative speech. Appears in 사실적으로 묘사하다 (to depict realistically).
7. **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 3**

1. **Base Form:** 사실 (sasil)
2. **Derived Form:** 사실성
3. **Transliteration:** sasilseong
4. **Literal Breakdown:** 事實(fact) + 性(-ness, abstract quality)
5. **Natural English Translation:** factuality, realism (as a quality of a representation)

7. **Epistemic Status:** [UNCERTAIN]

---

**Derived Form 4**

1. **Base Form:** 사실 (sasil)
2. **Derived Form:** 사실상
3. **Transliteration:** sasilsang

7. **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 5**

1. **Base Form:** 사실 (sasil)
2. **Derived Form:** 사실주의
3. **Transliteration:** sasil-juui
4. **Literal Breakdown:** 사실(fact/reality) + 主義(doctrine/ism)
5. **Natural English Translation:** realism (in art, literature, or philosophy)
6. **Usage:** Used in literary, artistic, and philosophical contexts to refer to the movement or approach that aims to depict reality as it actually is.
7. **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 6**

1. **Base Form:** 사실 (sasil)
2. **Derived Form:** 사실주의적
3. **Transliteration:** sasil-juuijeok
4. **Literal Breakdown:** 사실주의(realism) + 的(adjectival suffix)
5. **Natural English Translation:** realist (adjective), characteristic of realism
6. **Usage:** Used in literary and art criticism to characterize works, styles, or approaches as belonging to or consistent with the realist tradition. Appears in 사실주의적 기법 (realist technique).
7. **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 7**

1. **Base Form:** 사실 (sasil)
2. **Derived Form:** 사실관계
3. **Transliteration:** sasil-gwangye
4. **Literal Breakdown:** 사실(fact) + 關係(relationship/circumstances)
5. **Natural English Translation:** the facts of the matter, factual circumstances
6. **Usage:** Used in legal and journalistic contexts to refer to the factual circumstances of a case, as distinct from legal interpretation.
7. **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 8**

1. **Base Form:** 사실 (sasil)
2. **Derived Form:** 사실확인
3. **Transliteration:** sasil-hwagin
4. **Literal Breakdown:** 사실(fact) + 確認(confirmation/verification)
5. **Natural English Translation:** fact-checking, verification of facts
6. **Usage:** Used in journalistic, legal, and administrative contexts to describe the process of verifying whether a claim corresponds to the facts.
7. **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 9**

1. **Base Form:** 사실 (sasil)
2. **Derived Form:** 사실화하다
3. **Transliteration:** sasilhwa-hada
4. **Literal Breakdown:** 事實(fact) + 化(to become/transform into) + 하다(to do)
5. **Natural English Translation:** to make into fact, to actualize, to realize
6. **Usage:** Used to describe the process of turning something planned or theoretical into actual fact. Less common than 실현하다 for this meaning; appears in administrative and planning discourse.
7. **Epistemic Status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**Derived Form 10**

1. **Base Form:** 사실 (sasil)
2. **Derived Form:** 비사실적
3. **Transliteration:** bisasiljeok
4. **Literal Breakdown:** 非(non-/not) + 事實的(factual)
5. **Natural English Translation:** non-factual, unrealistic, contrary to fact
6. **Usage:** Used in literary criticism and analytical discourse to characterize representations or claims that do not correspond to reality. Less common than 비현실적 (unrealistic) in everyday use but attested in academic writing.
7. **Epistemic Status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**Note on 사실:** 사실 functions both as a noun and as a sentence-level adverb meaning "in fact / actually." The adverbial use is not a morphological derivation but a pragmatic extension of the nominal form. The suffix 적 (的) attaches productively to yield 사실적 and its further adverbial form 사실적으로. The suffix 주의 (主義) yields 사실주의, from which 사실주의적 is further derived. No verbal derivation of 사실 via 하다 is identified as a common independent form.

### 3. Base Form: 진짜 (jinjja)

---

**Derived Form 1**

1. **Base Form:** 진짜 (jinjja)
2. **Derived Form:** 진짜배기
3. **Transliteration:** jinjjabaegi
4. **Literal Breakdown:** 진짜(genuine) + 배기(colloquial intensifying suffix denoting the genuine article)
5. **Natural English Translation:** the real thing, the genuine article, the real deal
6. **Usage:** Colloquial and emphatic form of 진짜, used to stress that something is unambiguously authentic. Common in everyday speech and advertising.
7. **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2**

1. **Base Form:** 진짜 (jinjja)
2. **Derived Form:** 진짜로
3. **Transliteration:** jinjjaro
4. **Literal Breakdown:** 진짜(genuine/real) + 로(adverbial particle)
5. **Natural English Translation:** really, truly, for real
6. **Usage:** Adverbial form of 진짜, used in informal speech to assert that something is genuinely the case or to express strong emphasis. Extremely common in spoken Korean: 진짜로 그랬어? (Did that really happen?).
7. **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Note on 진짜:** 진짜 functions as both an adjective and an adverb in its base form and does not productively take standard derivational suffixes such as -하다, -성, or -적 in attested usage. The form 진짜이다 (to be genuine/real) is a copular construction rather than a derivation. The base form already functions as both adjective and adverb, limiting the pressure for further derivation. The primary intensification strategy is 진짜배기. The adverbial use of 진짜 is a pragmatic extension, not a morphological derivation.

### 4. Base Form: 가짜 (gajja)

---

**Derived Form 1**

1. **Base Form:** 가짜 (gajja)
2. **Derived Form:** 가짜뉴스
3. **Transliteration:** gajja-nyuseu
4. **Literal Breakdown:** 가짜(fake) + 뉴스(news — loanword from English)
5. **Natural English Translation:** fake news
6. **Usage:** Used in journalistic, political, and everyday contexts to refer to deliberately false or misleading news content. Entered widespread use in Korean public discourse from approximately 2016–2017 onward, parallel to international usage.
7. **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2**

1. **Base Form:** 가짜 (gajja)
2. **Derived Form:** 가품
3. **Transliteration:** gapum
4. **Literal Breakdown:** 가(假, false/fake) + 품(品, goods/article)
5. **Natural English Translation:** fake goods, counterfeit item
6. **Usage:** Used in consumer, legal, and art contexts to designate an item as not genuine. Contrasts with 진품 (jinpum, genuine article). More formal than 가짜 alone; common in legal and commercial contexts. Note: formed directly from 가 rather than from 가짜 as a base.
7. **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 3**

1. **Base Form:** 가짜 (gajja)
2. **Derived Form:** 가짜로
3. **Transliteration:** gajjaro
4. **Literal Breakdown:** 가짜(fake) + 로(adverbial particle)
5. **Natural English Translation:** falsely, as a fake, in a counterfeit manner
6. **Usage:** Adverbial form used to describe acting or presenting something as fake, or doing something under false pretenses: 가짜로 꾸미다 (to dress up as a fake/to disguise). Less common than the base form.
7. **Epistemic Status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**Note on 가짜:** Like 진짜, 가짜 functions as adjective and noun in its base form and does not productively take standard derivational suffixes in attested usage. 가짜이다 is a copular construction. 가짜 does not productively take 하다 to form a verb (one does not say *가짜하다 to mean "to fake"). The primary derivational extension is through compounding with nouns (가짜뉴스, 가짜 서류 "fake documents," etc.). 가품 is listed as a morphologically related form sharing the 가 (假) morpheme, though it is formed directly from 가 rather than from 가짜 as a base.

---

**Note on 올바르다:** This is a native Korean adjectival verb (형용사). It does not take -하다 (which attaches to nominal bases) or -성 in standard usage. Its derivational paradigm follows native Korean adjectival patterns: adnominal (-ㄴ), adverbial (-게), and nominal (-ㅁ) forms. No 성 (性) nominalization (*올바름성, *올바르성) is identified as a common form.

6. **Usage:** Adverbial form used to describe how something is done or judged. Appears in 옳게 판단하다 (to judge correctly). Less frequent than 올바르게 in everyday speech but attested in written and formal registers.
7. **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]
