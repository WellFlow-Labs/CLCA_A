# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P6 CHANGE, CAUSATION, AND STABILITY
# Language: Chinese (zh)

---

## METHODOLOGICAL NOTE

This analysis examines how Chinese encodes dynamic aspects of truth-related meanings: becoming true/false, causing truth/falsity, and maintaining or losing truth-value. The analysis is based strictly on attested expressions from the CONTEXT (P1-P5 materials) and natural Chinese usage patterns.

---

## 1. BECOMING TRUE / ACQUIRING TRUTH-VALUE

### 1.1 Basic Change-of-State: 变 (biàn) "become, change"

**Pattern:** Subject + 变 (biàn) / 变成 (biàn chéng) / 变得 (biàn de) + Truth-state

**Examples:**

- **假的变成真的了** (jiǎ de biàn chéng zhēn de le)
  - "The false became true"
  - Usage: Describes transformation from false to true state, common in narrative contexts
  - [CORE]

- **谎言变成了真相** (huǎng yán biàn chéng le zhēn xiàng)
  - "The lie became the truth"
  - Usage: Describes situation where what was initially false becomes accepted as true
  - [CORE]

- **情况变得真实了** (qíng kuàng biàn de zhēn shí le)
  - "The situation became real/true"
  - Usage: Describes situation acquiring reality or truth-value
  - [CORE]

- **说法变得可靠了** (shuō fǎ biàn de kě kào le)
  - "The statement became reliable"
  - Usage: Describes claim gaining reliability through evidence or confirmation
  - [CORE]

**Notes:** The verb 变 is highly productive with truth-domain predicates. The variants 变成 (emphasizing result) and 变得 (emphasizing gradual change) are both common. The aspect marker 了 (le) typically appears to mark completion of change.

---

### 1.2 Idiomatic Expressions for Becoming True

**弄假成真** (nòng jiǎ chéng zhēn)
- Literal: make-false-become-true
- "What starts as pretense becomes real, fake becomes genuine"
- Usage: Describes situations where pretense or acting becomes reality, especially in romantic contexts
- [CORE]

**假戏真做** (jiǎ xì zhēn zuò)
- Literal: false-play-truly-do
- "Pretense becomes reality, fake becomes real"
- Usage: Similar to 弄假成真, describes pretense turning into genuine situation
- [CORE]

**Notes:** These are lexicalized idioms rather than productive patterns, but they encode the conceptual frame of false-to-true transformation.

---

### 1.3 Truth Coming to Light

**Pattern:** 真相 (zhēnxiàng) "truth" + Verb of emergence/revelation

**Examples:**

- **真相大白** (zhēn xiàng dà bái)
  - "The truth comes to light, the facts are revealed"
  - Usage: Describes revelation of previously hidden truth
  - [CORE]

- **真相浮出水面** (zhēn xiàng fú chū shuǐ miàn)
  - "The truth surfaces" (lit. "floats out of water surface")
  - Usage: Metaphorical expression for truth emerging from concealment
  - [CORE]

- **真相终于显露** (zhēn xiàng zhōng yú xiǎn lù)
  - "The truth finally becomes apparent"
  - Usage: Describes truth becoming visible after being hidden
  - [CORE]

**Notes:** These expressions conceptualize truth as something that emerges or becomes visible rather than something that changes state. The truth exists but becomes known.

---

### 1.4 Becoming Correct

**Pattern:** Subject + 变 (biàn) + 对 (duì) "correct"

**Examples:**

- **答案变对了** (dá àn biàn duì le)
  - "The answer became correct"
  - Usage: Describes correction of an answer or solution
  - [MARGINAL]

**Notes:** This pattern is grammatically possible but less common than resultative constructions like 改对 (gǎi duì) "correct to right" or 弄对 (nòng duì) "get right". The concept of "becoming correct" is more naturally expressed through causative/resultative patterns.

---

### 1.5 Becoming Reliable/Trustworthy

**Pattern:** Subject + 变得 (biàn de) + 可靠 (kěkào) "reliable"

**Examples:**

- **这个消息变得可靠了** (zhè ge xiāo xi biàn de kě kào le)
  - "This news became reliable"
  - Usage: Describes information gaining reliability through confirmation
  - [CORE]

- **他变得诚实了** (tā biàn de chéng shí le)
  - "He became honest"
  - Usage: Describes person acquiring honest character
  - [CORE]

**Notes:** These patterns are productive and natural, describing acquisition of reliability or honesty as character traits or properties.

## 2. BECOMING FALSE / LOSING TRUTH-VALUE

### 2.1 Basic Change to False State

**Pattern:** Subject + 变 (biàn) / 变成 (biàn chéng) / 变得 (biàn de) + False-state

**Examples:**

- **真的变成假的了** (zhēn de biàn chéng jiǎ de le)
  - "The true became false"
  - Usage: Describes transformation from true to false state
  - [CORE]

- **事实变成了谎言** (shì shí biàn chéng le huǎng yán)
  - "The fact became a lie"
  - Usage: Describes truth being distorted into falsehood
  - [CORE]

- **情况变得虚假了** (qíng kuàng biàn de xū jiǎ le)
  - "The situation became false"
  - Usage: Describes situation losing authenticity or truth-value
  - [CORE]

**Notes:** These patterns are less common than false-to-true transformations in natural discourse, but grammatically productive and attested.

---

### 2.2 Losing Accuracy/Fidelity

**失真** (shī zhēn)
- Literal: lose-true
- "Distorted, lose fidelity, inaccurate"
- Usage: Technical term describing loss of accuracy in reproduction or transmission
- [CORE]

**Pattern:** Subject + 失真 (shī zhēn)

**Examples:**

- **信号失真了** (xìn hào shī zhēn le)
  - "The signal became distorted"
  - Usage: Technical context of signal degradation
  - [CORE]

- **图像失真** (tú xiàng shī zhēn)
  - "The image is distorted"
  - Usage: Describes loss of fidelity in image reproduction
  - [CORE]

**Notes:** 失真 is a lexicalized compound specifically encoding loss of truth/fidelity, particularly in technical contexts. It functions as both verb and adjective.

---

### 2.3 Becoming Unreliable

**Pattern:** Subject + 变得 (biàn de) + 不可靠 (bù kěkào) "unreliable"

**Examples:**

- **这个消息变得不可靠了** (zhè ge xiāo xi biàn de bù kě kào le)
  - "This news became unreliable"
  - Usage: Describes information losing reliability
  - [CORE]

- **他变得不诚实了** (tā biàn de bù chéng shí le)
  - "He became dishonest"
  - Usage: Describes person losing honest character
  - [CORE]

**Notes:** These patterns use morphological negation (不) with truth-domain predicates to express loss of positive qualities.

---

### 2.4 Becoming Wrong/Incorrect

**Pattern:** Subject + 变 (biàn) + 错 (cuò) "wrong"

**Examples:**

- **答案变错了** (dá àn biàn cuò le)
  - "The answer became wrong"
  - Usage: Describes answer becoming incorrect (e.g., through error in copying)
  - [MARGINAL]

**Notes:** Like "becoming correct," this pattern is grammatically possible but less natural than causative/resultative alternatives. The concept is more commonly expressed as "making an error" rather than "becoming wrong."

---

## 3. CAUSING TRUTH / MAKING TRUE

### 3.1 Verification and Confirmation

**Pattern:** Agent + Verification-verb + Truth-object

The most productive pattern for "causing truth" in Chinese uses resultative verb compounds where the complement 实 (shí) "actual" indicates achieving truth/actuality.

**Examples:**

- **证实** (zhèng shí) "verify, confirm, prove"
  - 证据证实了这个说法 (zhèng jù zhèng shí le zhè ge shuō fǎ)
  - "The evidence verified this statement"
  - [CORE]

- **核实** (hé shí) "verify, check, confirm"
  - 我们核实了这个消息 (wǒ men hé shí le zhè ge xiāo xi)
  - "We verified this news"
  - [CORE]

- **查实** (chá shí) "verify, ascertain"
  - 调查查实了事实 (diào chá chá shí le shì shí)
  - "The investigation ascertained the facts"
  - [CORE]

- **坐实** (zuò shí) "confirm, establish as fact"
  - 证据坐实了指控 (zhèng jù zuò shí le zhǐ kòng)
  - "The evidence confirmed the accusation"
  - [CORE]

- **落实** (luò shí) "implement, carry out, put into practice"
  - 我们要落实这个计划 (wǒ men yào luò shí zhè ge jì huà)
  - "We need to implement this plan"
  - [CORE]

**Notes:** These resultative compounds are the primary way Chinese expresses "making true" or "causing to be true." The complement 实 encodes the result of verification or actualization. This pattern is far more productive and natural than periphrastic causatives.

---

### 3.2 Periphrastic Causatives (Less Common)

**Pattern:** Agent + 使/让 (shǐ/ràng) "make, cause" + Patient + 变得 (biàn de) + Truth-state

**Examples:**

- **证据使这个说法变得可靠** (zhèng jù shǐ zhè ge shuō fǎ biàn de kě kào)
  - "The evidence makes this claim reliable"
  - Usage: Formal/literary expression of causation
  - [MARGINAL]

- **调查让真相大白** (diào chá ràng zhēn xiàng dà bái)
  - "The investigation caused the truth to come to light"
  - Usage: Describes investigation causing revelation of truth
  - [CORE]

**Notes:** Periphrastic causatives with 使/让 are grammatically well-formed but less idiomatic than resultative compounds for most truth-domain causation. They appear more in formal/literary registers.

---

### 3.3 Making Correct

**Pattern:** Agent + Verb + 对 (duì) "correct" [resultative]

**Examples:**

- **说对** (shuō duì) "say correctly, get it right"
  - 他说对了 (tā shuō duì le)
  - "He got it right (in speaking)"
  - [CORE]

- **做对** (zuò duì) "do correctly, get right"
  - 我做对了这道题 (wǒ zuò duì le zhè dào tí)
  - "I got this problem right"
  - [CORE]

- **改对** (gǎi duì) "correct to right"
  - 老师改对了答案 (lǎo shī gǎi duì le dá àn)
  - "The teacher corrected the answer to right"
  - [CORE]

- **弄对** (nòng duì) "get right, make correct"
  - 我终于弄对了 (wǒ zhōng yú nòng duì le)
  - "I finally got it right"
  - [CORE]

**Notes:** The resultative complement 对 is extremely productive with action verbs to express achieving correctness. This is the natural way to express "making correct" in Chinese.

---

### 3.4 Revealing/Exposing Truth

**Pattern:** Agent + Revelation-verb + 真相 (zhēnxiàng) "truth"

**Examples:**

- **揭露真相** (jiē lù zhēn xiàng)
  - 记者揭露了真相 (jì zhě jiē lù le zhēn xiàng)
  - "The journalist revealed the truth"
  - [CORE]

- **查明真相** (chá míng zhēn xiàng)
  - 警察查明了真相 (jǐng chá chá míng le zhēn xiàng)
  - "The police ascertained the truth"
  - [CORE]

- **弄清真相** (nòng qīng zhēn xiàng)
  - 我们要弄清真相 (wǒ men yào nòng qīng zhēn xiàng)
  - "We need to clarify the truth"
  - [CORE]

**Notes:** These patterns express causing truth to become known or visible rather than causing something to become true. The truth pre-exists but is made manifest.

---

### 3.5 Implementing/Actualizing

**Pattern:** Agent + 实现/实施/实行 (shíxiàn/shíshī/shíxíng) + Object

**Examples:**

- **实现** (shí xiàn) "realize, achieve, bring about"
  - 我们实现了目标 (wǒ men shí xiàn le mù biāo)
  - "We realized the goal"
  - [CORE]

- **实施** (shí shī) "implement, put into effect"
  - 政府实施了新政策 (zhèng fǔ shí shī le xīn zhèng cè)
  - "The government implemented the new policy"
  - [CORE]

- **实行** (shí xíng) "implement, practice, put into effect"
  - 学校实行了新制度 (xué xiào shí xíng le xīn zhì dù)
  - "The school implemented the new system"
  - [CORE]

**Notes:** These verbs express making plans or ideas actual/real, a form of "causing to be real" distinct from verification. They focus on actualization rather than truth-verification.

## 4. CAUSING FALSITY / MAKING FALSE

### 4.1 Falsification and Fabrication

**Pattern:** Agent + Falsification-verb + Object

**Examples:**

- **假造** (jiǎ zào) "fabricate, forge, counterfeit"
  - 他假造了证据 (tā jiǎ zào le zhèng jù)
  - "He fabricated evidence"
  - [CORE]

- **伪造** (wěi zào) "forge, counterfeit, falsify"
  - 罪犯伪造了文件 (zuì fàn wěi zào le wén jiàn)
  - "The criminal forged documents"
  - [CORE]

- **捏造** (niē zào) "fabricate, concoct, make up"
  - 他捏造了事实 (tā niē zào le shì shí)
  - "He fabricated facts"
  - [CORE]

**Notes:** These verbs specifically encode creating false things or making false claims. They are lexicalized causatives of falsity.

---

### 4.2 Deception and Lying

**Pattern:** Agent + Deception-verb + Patient

**Examples:**

- **欺骗** (qī piàn) "deceive, cheat"
  - 他欺骗了我 (tā qī piàn le wǒ)
  - "He deceived me"
  - [CORE]

- **说谎** (shuō huǎng) "tell lies, lie"
  - 他说谎了 (tā shuō huǎng le)
  - "He lied"
  - [CORE]

- **撒谎** (sā huǎng) "tell lies, lie"
  - 别撒谎！(bié sā huǎng!)
  - "Don't lie!"
  - [CORE]

**Notes:** These verbs express causing false beliefs in others through deceptive speech or actions.

---

### 4.3 Distortion and Falsification

**Pattern:** Agent + Distortion-verb + Object

**Examples:**

- **歪曲事实** (wāi qū shì shí)
  - 他歪曲了事实 (tā wāi qū le shì shí)
  - "He distorted the facts"
  - [CORE]

- **篡改** (cuàn gǎi) "falsify, tamper with"
  - 他篡改了记录 (tā cuàn gǎi le jì lù)
  - "He falsified the records"
  - [CORE]

**Notes:** These verbs express causing true things to become false through distortion or alteration.

---

### 4.4 Concealing Truth

**Pattern:** Agent + Concealment-verb + 真相 (zhēnxiàng) "truth"

**Examples:**

- **隐瞒真相** (yǐn mán zhēn xiàng)
  - 他隐瞒了真相 (tā yǐn mán le zhēn xiàng)
  - "He concealed the truth"
  - [CORE]

- **掩盖真相** (yǎn gài zhēn xiàng)
  - 他们掩盖了真相 (tā men yǎn gài le zhēn xiàng)
  - "They covered up the truth"
  - [CORE]

**Notes:** These patterns express preventing truth from being known, a form of causing falsity through concealment rather than fabrication.

---

### 4.5 Making Wrong/Incorrect

**Pattern:** Agent + Verb + 错 (cuò) "wrong" [resultative]

**Examples:**

- **说错** (shuō cuò) "say wrongly, misspeak"
  - 我说错了 (wǒ shuō cuò le)
  - "I said it wrong"
  - [CORE]

- **做错** (zuò cuò) "do wrongly, make a mistake"
  - 我做错了 (wǒ zuò cuò le)
  - "I did it wrong"
  - [CORE]

- **弄错** (nòng cuò) "get wrong, make a mistake"
  - 我弄错了 (wǒ nòng cuò le)
  - "I got it wrong"
  - [CORE]

- **搞错** (gǎo cuò) "get wrong, mess up"
  - 你搞错了 (nǐ gǎo cuò le)
  - "You got it wrong"
  - [CORE]

**Notes:** The resultative complement 错 is extremely productive with action verbs to express achieving incorrectness or making errors. This is the natural way to express "making wrong" in Chinese.

## 5. MAINTAINING TRUTH / KEEPING TRUE

### 5.1 Preservation of Truth

**Pattern:** Agent + 保持/维持 (bǎo chí / wéi chí) "maintain, keep" + Truth-state

**Examples:**

- **保持真实** (bǎo chí zhēn shí)
  - 我们要保持真实 (wǒ men yào bǎo chí zhēn shí)
  - "We need to maintain authenticity"
  - [CORE]

- **保持诚实** (bǎo chí chéng shí)
  - 他一直保持诚实 (tā yī zhí bǎo chí chéng shí)
  - "He has always maintained honesty"
  - [CORE]

- **维持真相** (wéi chí zhēn xiàng)
  - [MARGINAL - less natural than alternatives]

**Notes:** The verbs 保持 and 维持 "maintain, keep" can combine with truth-domain adjectives to express maintaining a state. However, specific expressions vary in naturalness.

---

### 5.2 Adhering to Truth

**Pattern:** Agent + 坚持 (jiān chí) "persist in, adhere to" + Truth-related action

**Examples:**

- **坚持说真话** (jiān chí shuō zhēn huà)
  - 他坚持说真话 (tā jiān chí shuō zhēn huà)
  - "He persists in telling the truth"
  - [CORE]

- **坚持实事求是** (jiān chí shí shì qiú shì)
  - 我们要坚持实事求是 (wǒ men yào jiān chí shí shì qiú shì)
  - "We must adhere to seeking truth from facts"
  - [CORE]

**Notes:** The verb 坚持 "persist in, adhere to" expresses maintaining truth-related behavior over time despite difficulty or opposition.

---

### 5.3 Remaining True/Constant

**Pattern:** Subject + 保持/仍然 (bǎo chí / réng rán) + Truth-state

**Examples:**

- **事实保持不变** (shì shí bǎo chí bù biàn)
  - "The facts remain unchanged"
  - [CORE]

- **真相仍然是真相** (zhēn xiàng réng rán shì zhēn xiàng)
  - "The truth remains the truth"
  - [CORE]

**Notes:** These patterns express stability or constancy of truth-value over time.

---

### 5.4 Idiomatic Expression

**真金不怕火炼** (zhēn jīn bù pà huǒ liàn)
- Literal: "Real gold fears no fire"
- "Genuine quality withstands testing"
- Usage: Proverb expressing that truth/genuineness remains stable under testing
- [CORE]

**Notes:** This proverb metaphorically expresses the stability and endurance of truth/genuineness.

## 6. LOSING TRUTH / CEASING TO BE TRUE

### 6.1 Loss of Accuracy

**失真** (shī zhēn) "lose fidelity, become distorted"
- Already discussed in Section 2.2
- This is the primary lexicalized expression for losing truth-value
- [CORE]

---

### 6.2 Becoming Unreliable

**Pattern:** Subject + 变得 (biàn de) + 不 + Truth-predicate

**Examples:**

- **变得不可靠** (biàn de bù kě kào)
  - "Become unreliable"
  - [CORE]

- **变得不准确** (biàn de bù zhǔn què)
  - "Become inaccurate"
  - [CORE]

**Notes:** Loss of truth-related qualities is typically expressed through 变得 + negated predicate.

---

### 6.3 Truth Being Obscured

**Pattern:** 真相 (zhēnxiàng) + Verb of concealment/obscuring

**Examples:**

- **真相被掩盖** (zhēn xiàng bèi yǎn gài)
  - "The truth was covered up"
  - [CORE]

- **真相被隐瞒** (zhēn xiàng bèi yǐn mán)
  - "The truth was concealed"
  - [CORE]

**Notes:** These passive constructions express truth ceasing to be known or accessible, though the truth itself doesn't change.

## 7. PREVENTING FALSITY / AVOIDING ERROR

### 7.1 Avoiding Mistakes

**Pattern:** Agent + 避免 (bì miǎn) "avoid" + Error-noun

**Examples:**

- **避免错误** (bì miǎn cuò wù)
  - 我们要避免错误 (wǒ men yào bì miǎn cuò wù)
  - "We need to avoid errors"
  - [CORE]

- **避免说错** (bì miǎn shuō cuò)
  - 小心避免说错 (xiǎo xīn bì miǎn shuō cuò)
  - "Be careful to avoid saying it wrong"
  - [CORE]

**Notes:** The verb 避免 "avoid" combines with error-related nouns or verb phrases to express prevention of falsity.

---

### 7.2 Preventing Deception

**Pattern:** Agent + 防止/预防 (fáng zhǐ / yù fáng) "prevent" + Deception

**Examples:**

- **防止欺骗** (fáng zhǐ qī piàn)
  - 要防止欺骗 (yào fáng zhǐ qī piàn)
  - "Need to prevent deception"
  - [CORE]

- **防止造假** (fáng zhǐ zào jiǎ)
  - 加强监管防止造假 (jiā qiáng jiān guǎn fáng zhǐ zào jiǎ)
  - "Strengthen supervision to prevent counterfeiting"
  - [CORE]

**Notes:** Verbs of prevention (防止, 预防) combine with deception/falsification terms to express preventing falsity.

## 8. GRADUAL CHANGE AND DEGREES

### 8.1 Becoming More/Less True

**Pattern:** Subject + 变得 (biàn de) + 更/越来越 (gèng / yuè lái yuè) + Truth-predicate

**Examples:**

- **变得更真实** (biàn de gèng zhēn shí)
  - 故事变得更真实了 (gù shi biàn de gèng zhēn shí le)
  - "The story became more realistic"
  - [CORE]

- **变得越来越准确** (biàn de yuè lái yuè zhǔn què)
  - 数据变得越来越准确 (shù jù biàn de yuè lái yuè zhǔn què)
  - "The data became increasingly accurate"
  - [CORE]

- **变得更可靠** (biàn de gèng kě kào)
  - 消息变得更可靠了 (xiāo xi biàn de gèng kě kào le)
  - "The news became more reliable"
  - [CORE]

**Notes:** Degree adverbs 更 "more" and 越来越 "increasingly" combine with 变得 to express gradual increase in truth-related qualities.

---

### 8.2 Becoming Less True

**Pattern:** Subject + 变得 (biàn de) + 更/越来越 (gèng / yuè lái yuè) + 不 + Truth-predicate

**Examples:**

- **变得越来越不可靠** (biàn de yuè lái yuè bù kě kào)
  - 他的话变得越来越不可靠 (tā de huà biàn de yuè lái yuè bù kě kào)
  - "His words became increasingly unreliable"
  - [CORE]

- **变得更不准确** (biàn de gèng bù zhǔn què)
  - 报道变得更不准确了 (bào dào biàn de gèng bù zhǔn què le)
  - "The report became more inaccurate"
  - [CORE]

**Notes:** Degree adverbs combine with negated predicates to express gradual decrease in truth-related qualities.

## 9. ASPECTUAL DISTINCTIONS

### 9.1 Completed Change: 了 (le)

**Pattern:** Change expression + 了 (le) [perfective aspect]

**Examples:**

- **变真了** (biàn zhēn le)
  - "Became true" (completed)
  - [CORE]

- **证实了** (zhèng shí le)
  - "Verified" (completed)
  - [CORE]

**Notes:** The aspect marker 了 marks completion of change or action, extremely common with all change-of-state expressions.

---

### 9.2 Ongoing Change: 在 (zài) ... 着 (zhe)

**Pattern:** 在 (zài) + Verb + 着 (zhe) [progressive aspect]

**Examples:**

- **正在变化** (zhèng zài biàn huà)
  - 情况正在变化 (qíng kuàng zhèng zài biàn huà)
  - "The situation is changing"
  - [CORE]

- **正在核实** (zhèng zài hé shí)
  - 我们正在核实消息 (wǒ men zhèng zài hé shí xiāo xi)
  - "We are verifying the news"
  - [CORE]

**Notes:** Progressive aspect markers 在...着 or 正在 express ongoing change or action.

---

### 9.3 Experiential: 过 (guò)

**Pattern:** Verb + 过 (guò) [experiential aspect]

**Examples:**

- **变过** (biàn guò)
  - 这个说法变过 (zhè ge shuō fǎ biàn guò)
  - "This statement has changed (at some point)"
  - [CORE]

- **证实过** (zhèng shí guò)
  - 这个消息证实过 (zhè ge xiāo xi zhèng shí guò)
  - "This news has been verified (before)"
  - [CORE]

**Notes:** The experiential aspect marker 过 indicates that an action or change has occurred at least once in the past.

---

## 10. MODAL AND POTENTIAL EXPRESSIONS

### 10.1 Ability to Cause Truth

**Pattern:** Agent + 能/可以 (néng / kě yǐ) "can" + Causative verb

**Examples:**

- **能证实** (néng zhèng shí)
  - 证据能证实这个说法 (zhèng jù néng zhèng shí zhè ge shuō fǎ)
  - "The evidence can verify this statement"
  - [CORE]

- **可以核实** (kě yǐ hé shí)
  - 我们可以核实这个消息 (wǒ men kě yǐ hé shí zhè ge xiāo xi)
  - "We can verify this news"
  - [CORE]

**Notes:** Modal verbs 能 and 可以 express ability or possibility of causing truth-related changes.

---

### 10.2 Potential Complements

**Pattern:** Verb + 得/不 (de/bù) + Truth-complement

**Examples:**

- **证得实** (zhèng de shí) / **证不实** (zhèng bù shí)
  - "Can/cannot verify"
  - [MARGINAL - less common than modal + verb]

- **查得清** (chá de qīng) / **查不清** (chá bù qīng)
  - "Can/cannot investigate clearly"
  - [CORE]

**Notes:** Potential complements express ability or inability to achieve a result. More common with verbs of investigation than with resultative compounds like 证实.

---

## 11. CONDITIONAL AND HYPOTHETICAL CHANGE

### 11.1 Conditional Change

**Pattern:** 如果/要是 (rú guǒ / yào shì) "if" + Change expression

**Examples:**

- **如果变真了** (rú guǒ biàn zhēn le)
  - 如果这个变真了，我们就麻烦了 (rú guǒ zhè ge biàn zhēn le, wǒ men jiù má fan le)
  - "If this becomes true, we'll be in trouble"
  - [CORE]

- **要是能证实** (yào shì néng zhèng shí)
  - 要是能证实就好了 (yào shì néng zhèng shí jiù hǎo le)
  - "If only we could verify it"
  - [CORE]

**Notes:** Conditional markers combine freely with change expressions to express hypothetical changes.

---

### 11.2 Counterfactual Change

**Pattern:** 如果...就会 (rú guǒ...jiù huì) "if...then would"

**Examples:**

- **如果有证据就会证实** (rú guǒ yǒu zhèng jù jiù huì zhèng shí)
  - "If there were evidence, it would be verified"
  - [CORE]

**Notes:** Counterfactual conditionals use 会 (huì) "would" to express hypothetical causation.

---

## 12. SUMMARY OF PATTERNS

### 12.1 Most Productive Patterns

1. **Resultative complements with 实/对/错**: The most natural and productive way to express causing truth/correctness or falsity/error
   - 证实, 核实, 查实, 坐实, 落实 (causing truth/actuality)
   - 说对, 做对, 改对, 弄对 (causing correctness)
   - 说错, 做错, 弄错, 搞错 (causing error)

2. **变 (biàn) + Truth-state**: Productive pattern for change of state
   - 变真, 变假, 变得真实, 变得可靠

3. **Lexicalized causatives of falsity**: Specific verbs for creating falsity
   - 假造, 伪造, 捏造 (fabrication)
   - 欺骗, 说谎, 撒谎 (deception)

### 12.2 Less Productive Patterns

1. **Periphrastic causatives with 使/让**: Grammatical but less idiomatic than resultative compounds

2. **Direct 变 + 对/错**: Less natural than resultative compounds for correctness/error

### 12.3 Gaps and Restrictions

1. **No common expression for**: "X remains false" — typically expressed through negation of change (没有变真 "hasn't become true") rather than positive expression of maintaining falsity

2. **Asymmetry**: Expressions for revealing/exposing truth (揭露真相, 查明真相) are common, but there's no parallel set of expressions for "revealing falsity" — falsity is typically expressed through concealment of truth

3. **Register differences**: Resultative compounds (证实, 核实) are neutral register; periphrastic causatives (使...变得) are more formal/literary

### 12.4 Aspectual Marking

All change expressions freely combine with:
- Perfective 了 (le) for completed change
- Progressive 在/正在 for ongoing change  
- Experiential 过 (guò) for past experience of change
- Modal verbs (能, 可以, 会) for potential or hypothetical change

---

## CONCLUSION

Chinese encodes truth-related dynamics primarily through:

1. **Resultative verb compounds** (most productive): V + 实/对/错 encoding achieved results
2. **Change-of-state verb 变**: Productive with truth-domain predicates
3. **Lexicalized causatives**: Specific verbs for falsification and deception
4. **Revelation verbs**: Expressing making truth known rather than making true

The system shows clear preference for resultative compounds over periphrastic causatives, and exhibits asymmetries between truth and falsity (e.g., more expressions for revealing truth than revealing falsity).
