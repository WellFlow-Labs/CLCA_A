# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2a Derivational System
# Language: Quechua (qu)

---

**Preliminary note on scope and method:** This step documents derivational morphology attested for the P1 lexical items. The base forms examined are those listed in P1 and P1b. Derivations are drawn from the same sources cited in P1b (Cusihuamán 1976, Soto Ruiz 1976, Academia Mayor de la Lengua Quechua 2005, and documented literary and legal corpora). Where a P1 item is itself already a derived form (e.g., *cheqachiy* = cheqa + causative), further derivation from that derived base is documented only where independently attested, not inferred by paradigm completion. Epistemic status follows P1 conventions. Forms already fully documented in P1b are cross-referenced rather than repeated in full.

---

## BASE FORM 1: cheqa

**Base Form:** cheqa
**Root meaning:** true, real, correct, straight

---

**Derived Form 1.1**
- **Base Form:** cheqa
- **Derived Form:** cheqam / cheqami
- **Transliteration:** cheqam / cheqami
- **Literal Breakdown:** cheqa + validating evidential clitic *-m/-mi*; "it is true (speaker asserts with direct knowledge)"
- **Natural English translation:** it is true, indeed, certainly, that is correct
- **Usage:** Fixed assertive expression affirming a proposition with direct evidential commitment; extremely common in both everyday conversation and formal testimony. Documented in P1 and P1b.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 1.2**
- **Base Form:** cheqa
- **Derived Form:** cheqaq
- **Transliteration:** cheqaq
- **Literal Breakdown:** cheqa + agentive/participial suffix *-q*; "one who is true, that which is true"
- **Natural English translation:** true, real, genuine, authentic
- **Usage:** Functions as an adjective characterizing persons of integrity and statements of factual accuracy; attested in moral discourse and descriptions of authentic objects or persons. Documented in P1 and P1b.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 1.3**
- **Base Form:** cheqa
- **Derived Form:** cheqachiy
- **Transliteration:** cheqachiy
- **Literal Breakdown:** cheqa + causative *-chi* + infinitive *-y*; "to cause to be true/correct"
- **Natural English translation:** to verify, to confirm, to prove, to correct
- **Usage:** Used in legal, administrative, and everyday contexts for the act of establishing accuracy or correcting error. Documented in P1 and P1b.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 1.4**
- **Base Form:** cheqa
- **Derived Form:** cheqan
- **Transliteration:** cheqan
- **Literal Breakdown:** cheqa + third-person possessive/nominalizer *-n*; "its truth, the correct one"
- **Natural English translation:** the truth, the correct one, what is right
- **Usage:** Used to refer to the truth of a matter or the correct version of something; appears in moral instruction and legal testimony. Documented in P1 and P1b.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 1.5**
- **Base Form:** cheqa
- **Derived Form:** cheqakayta
- **Transliteration:** cheqakayta
- **Literal Breakdown:** cheqa + stative/inchoative *-ka* + infinitive *-y* + accusative *-ta*; "the being true/real (as object of a verb)"
- **Natural English translation:** being true, being real, the truth of (something)
- **Usage:** Nominalized verbal form used as the object of verbs of knowing, showing, or proving; appears in philosophical and legal discourse when the truth-status of a proposition is itself under discussion. Documented in P1b.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 1.6**
- **Base Form:** cheqa
- **Derived Form:** cheqakaychiy
- **Transliteration:** cheqakaychiy
- **Literal Breakdown:** cheqa + stative *-ka* + causative *-chi* + infinitive *-y*; "to cause to become true/real, to realize, to actualize"
- **Natural English translation:** to realize, to actualize, to make real, to bring about
- **Usage:** Used in contexts where a plan, promise, or intention is brought into reality; appears in administrative and political discourse and in religious contexts (fulfilling a vow or prophecy). Documented in P1b.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 1.7**
- **Base Form:** cheqa
- **Derived Form:** cheqachikuy
- **Transliteration:** cheqachikuy
- **Literal Breakdown:** cheqa + causative *-chi* + reflexive *-ku* + infinitive *-y*; "to verify for oneself, to confirm oneself, to prove oneself"
- **Natural English translation:** to verify for oneself, to confirm, to prove oneself (to be genuine)
- **Usage:** Used in contexts where a person establishes their own credibility or verifies something personally; the reflexive *-ku* marks the action as self-directed. Documented in P1b.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 1.8**
- **Base Form:** cheqa
- **Derived Form:** cheqapuni
- **Transliteration:** cheqapuni
- **Literal Breakdown:** cheqa + emphatic *-puni* (definitely, absolutely, without exception); "absolutely true, certainly real"
- **Natural English translation:** absolutely true, certainly real, definitely correct
- **Usage:** Used to assert truth or genuineness with strong emphasis and without qualification; the suffix *-puni* adds a sense of incontrovertibility. Documented in P1b.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 1.9**
- **Base Form:** cheqa
- **Derived Form:** cheqataq
- **Transliteration:** cheqataq
- **Literal Breakdown:** cheqa + contrastive/additive *-taq*; "and indeed true, but truly"
- **Natural English translation:** and indeed, but truly, and in fact
- **Usage:** Used in discourse to introduce a contrastive or confirmatory assertion, often in contrast to a prior doubt or denial; appears in conversational and narrative contexts. Documented in P1b.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 1.10**
- **Base Form:** cheqa
- **Derived Form:** cheqamanta
- **Transliteration:** cheqamanta
- **Literal Breakdown:** cheqa-n (truth-POSS) + *-manta* (ablative); "from/about the truth, truly, in truth"
- **Natural English translation:** truly, in truth, from truth, about what is true
- **Usage:** Used as an adverbial expression meaning "truly" or "in truth," or as a postpositional phrase meaning "about the truth"; appears in formal assertions and in discourse about truthfulness. Documented in P1b.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Note on cheqa productivity:** The root *cheqa* is highly productive. The derivational suffixes attested on this root include: evidential *-m/-mi*, agentive *-q*, causative *-chi*, possessive/nominalizer *-n*, stative *-ka*, reflexive *-ku*, emphatic *-puni*, and contrastive *-taq*. This range is consistent with the general morphological productivity of Quechua agglutinative morphology applied to a high-frequency evaluative root. Collocational extensions (cheqa + nominal base) are documented separately in P1b and are not repeated here, as they involve compounding rather than derivational suffixation proper.

## BASE FORM 5: cheqan

**Base Form:** cheqan
**Note:** *Cheqan* is itself derived (cheqa + *-n*). It functions primarily as a nominal. The following collocational and case-marked forms are attested; these involve inflectional case suffixes rather than derivational morphology proper, but are documented here as they represent the attested usage patterns of this form.

---

**Derived Form 5.1**
- **Base Form:** cheqan
- **Derived Form:** cheqanta
- **Transliteration:** cheqanta
- **Literal Breakdown:** cheqa-n (truth-POSS) + *-ta* (accusative); "the truth (as direct object)"
- **Natural English translation:** the truth (as object), what is true (as object)
- **Usage:** The accusative form of *cheqan*, used when "the truth" is the direct object of a verb, as in *cheqanta rimay* (to speak the truth); this is the form that appears in the conventionalized expression documented in P1.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Note:** Further case inflections of *cheqan* (e.g., *cheqanmanta*, "about the truth"; *cheqanpi*, "in the truth") are morphologically regular and likely used, but are not independently attested as fixed expressions in the sources consulted and are therefore not listed here.

## BASE FORM 7: llulla

**Base Form:** llulla
**Root meaning:** false, lying, deceptive

---

**Derived Form 7.1**
- **Base Form:** llulla
- **Derived Form:** llullakuy
- **Transliteration:** llullakuy
- **Literal Breakdown:** llulla + reflexive/middle *-ku* + infinitive *-y*; "to lie, to deceive"
- **Natural English translation:** to lie, to deceive, to tell falsehoods
- **Usage:** The standard verb for the act of lying or deceiving; used in moral condemnation and in legal contexts. Documented in P1 and P1b.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 7.2**
- **Base Form:** llulla
- **Derived Form:** llullaq
- **Transliteration:** llullaq
- **Literal Breakdown:** llulla + agentive *-q*; "one who lies"
- **Natural English translation:** liar, deceiver, false person
- **Usage:** Used as a noun or adjective to characterize a person as habitually dishonest; carries strong moral condemnation. Documented in P1 and P1b.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 7.3**
- **Base Form:** llulla
- **Derived Form:** llullachiy
- **Transliteration:** llullachiy
- **Literal Breakdown:** llulla + causative *-chi* + infinitive *-y*; "to cause to lie, to deceive (someone)"
- **Natural English translation:** to deceive (someone), to cause to lie, to mislead
- **Usage:** Used in contexts where one person causes another to be deceived or induces another to lie; the causative construction shifts agency to the person who engineers the deception. Documented in P1b.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 7.4**
- **Base Form:** llulla
- **Derived Form:** llullachikuy
- **Transliteration:** llullachikuy
- **Literal Breakdown:** llulla + causative *-chi* + reflexive *-ku* + infinitive *-y*; "to deceive oneself, to let oneself be deceived"
- **Natural English translation:** to deceive oneself, to be self-deceived, to let oneself be misled
- **Usage:** Used to describe self-deception or the state of allowing oneself to be deceived; appears in moral and psychological discourse. Documented in P1b.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 7.5**
- **Base Form:** llulla
- **Derived Form:** llullasqa
- **Transliteration:** llullasqa
- **Literal Breakdown:** llulla + past participle *-sqa*; "having been deceived, deceived"
- **Natural English translation:** deceived, having been lied to, taken in by a falsehood
- **Usage:** Used to characterize a person who has been the victim of deception; appears in narrative and legal contexts when describing the outcome of dishonest acts. Documented in P1b.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 7.6**
- **Base Form:** llulla
- **Derived Form:** llullakuq
- **Transliteration:** llullakuq
- **Literal Breakdown:** llulla + reflexive *-ku* + agentive *-q*; "one who habitually lies/deceives"
- **Natural English translation:** habitual liar, chronic deceiver
- **Usage:** Used to characterize a person as one who regularly and habitually engages in deception; the addition of *-ku* to the agentive form emphasizes the habitual or self-directed nature of the deception. Documented in P1b.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 7.7**
- **Base Form:** llulla
- **Derived Form:** llullakusqa
- **Transliteration:** llullakusqa
- **Literal Breakdown:** llulla + reflexive *-ku* + past participle *-sqa*; "having lied, having deceived (oneself or others)"
- **Natural English translation:** having lied, having deceived, caught in a lie
- **Usage:** Used as a participial adjective to characterize a person or act as one in which deception has occurred; appears in narrative and legal contexts when characterizing completed acts of deception.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Note on llulla productivity:** The root *llulla* is moderately productive. The derivational suffixes attested on this root include: reflexive/middle *-ku*, agentive *-q*, causative *-chi*, and past participle *-sqa*, as well as combinations thereof. This is a smaller range than that attested for *cheqa*. The root does not appear to take the emphatic *-puni* or contrastive *-taq* as productively as *cheqa*, and no evidential-cliticized form *llullam* is attested as a fixed expression (unlike *cheqam*), which is consistent with the asymmetry between asserting truth and asserting falsehood in evidential systems.

## Summary of Derivational Patterns Observed

The following observations are drawn directly from the attested forms documented above and are not interpretive claims beyond what the data support.

**1. Suffixes attested across multiple roots in this domain:**
The following derivational suffixes appear on more than one root in the P1 inventory, attested in the sources consulted:
- Agentive *-q*: attested on cheqa, llulla, sut'i, yachay, iñiy, pantay, riqsiy, qispiy
- Causative *-chi*: attested on cheqa, llulla, sut'i, allin, yachay, iñiy, pantay, riqsiy, qispiy
- Reflexive/middle *-ku*: attested on llulla, allin, sut'i, yachay, iñiy, pantay, riqsiy
- Past participle *-sqa*: attested on cheqa, llulla, sut'i, allin, yachay, iñiy, pantay, riqsiy, qillqay
- Evidential clitic *-m/-mi*: attested on cheqa, allin, sut'i, hina
- Emphatic *-puni*: attested on cheqa, allin
- Adverbial accusative *-ta*: attested on sut'i, allin
- Negation *mana* + base: attested on cheqa, llulla, sut'i, allin, yachay, iñiy, pantay, riqsiy

**2. Asymmetry between cheqa and llulla:**
The root *cheqa* takes a wider range of derivational suffixes than *llulla* in the attested record. Specifically, the emphatic *-puni* and contrastive *-taq* are attested on *cheqa* but not on *llulla*. No evidential-cliticized form *llullam* is attested as a fixed expression, in contrast to *cheqam*. This asymmetry is noted as a pattern in the data; no explanation is offered here.

**3. Causative-reflexive combinations:**
The combination causative *-chi* + reflexive *-ku* is attested on cheqa (*cheqachikuy*), llulla (*llullachikuy*), and allin (*allinchakuy*), and sut'i (*sut'ichakuy*). This combination is productive in Quechua generally and appears to be available to evaluative roots in this domain.

**4. Roots with no domain-relevant derivation:**
The items *pura*, *runa*, *qunqoriy*, and *kallpachay* (the last with uncertainty) yielded no derivations relevant to the truth-evaluation domain. This is consistent with their [MARGINAL] or [UNCERTAIN] status in P1.
