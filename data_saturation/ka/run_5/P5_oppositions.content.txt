# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P5 Oppositions
# Language: Georgian (ka)

---

## Preliminary note on method

Oppositions below are extracted only from patterns explicitly attested in the P1–P4 data. Where the data describes a contrast, notes a positive/negative pole, or documents a morphological negation, that opposition is recorded. Where the data notes an asymmetry or absence, that is reported without correction. No external semantic framework has been imposed. Overlapping oppositions are retained as separate entries where the data supports distinct contrasts.

## Opposition 1

1. **Term A:** მართალია (martalia)
2. **Term B:** ეს სწორი არ არის (es sc̣ori ar aris) / ეს არასწორია (es arasc̣oria)
3. **Transliteration:** martalia / es sc̣ori ar aris / es arasc̣oria
4. **English translation:** "it is true / you are right" vs. "this is not correct / this is incorrect"
5. **Description of contrast:** martalia asserts correctness or truth predicatively; arasc̣ori and sc̣ori ar aris are its negations in the domain of factual and normative correctness. The P4b data documents both as [CORE] predicative constructions and notes them as alternates of each other.
6. **Contrast type:** constructional
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 2

1. **Term A:** სწორი (sc̣ori)
2. **Term B:** არასწორი (arasc̣ori)
3. **Transliteration:** sc̣ori / arasc̣ori
4. **English translation:** "correct, right, accurate" vs. "incorrect, wrong, inaccurate"
5. **Description of contrast:** arasc̣ori is formed by the productive negation prefix ara- directly on sc̣ori. The P1 data documents arasc̣ori as "the direct negation of sc̣ori" and P2a documents arasc̣ori as a derivation from sc̣ori via ara-. Both items are P1 core vocabulary.
6. **Contrast type:** morphological
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 3

2. **Term B:** უზუსტო (uzusto)
3. **Transliteration:** zusti / uzusto
4. **English translation:** "exact, precise, accurate" vs. "inaccurate, imprecise, inexact"

6. **Contrast type:** morphological
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 5

1. **Term A:** ნამდვილი (namdvili)
2. **Term B:** ყალბი (q̇albi)
3. **Transliteration:** namdvili / q̇albi
4. **English translation:** "genuine, real, authentic" vs. "false, fake, counterfeit, forged"
5. **Description of contrast:** The P1 data explicitly states that namdvili "contrasts with q̇albi (counterfeit) and is used to assert that something is what it purports to be." This is the primary lexical opposition between authenticity and inauthenticity in the domain of objects, documents, and identities.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 6

1. **Term A:** ნამდვილი აღმოჩნდა (namdvili aɣmočnda)
2. **Term B:** ყალბი აღმოჩნდა (q̇albi aɣmočnda)
3. **Transliteration:** namdvili aɣmočnda / q̇albi aɣmočnda
4. **English translation:** "turned out to be genuine / proved to be authentic" vs. "turned out to be fake / proved to be false"
5. **Description of contrast:** Both constructions use the same inchoative/resultative verb aɣmočnda and are documented in P2b as parallel constructions (Item 7 Construction 1 and Item 6 Construction 1). They form a constructional pair expressing opposite outcomes of an authenticity verification process.
6. **Contrast type:** constructional
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 8

1. **Term A:** ავთენტური (avtent̕uri)
2. **Term B:** ყალბი (q̇albi)
3. **Transliteration:** avtent̕uri / q̇albi
4. **English translation:** "authentic" vs. "false, fake, counterfeit, forged"
5. **Description of contrast:** The P1 data notes that avtent̕uri is used "in legal, archival, and academic contexts, particularly for documents and cultural artifacts" and tends to appear "alongside or instead of namdvili." In these contexts it contrasts with q̇albi as the negative pole. This is a distinct opposition from Opposition 5 because avtent̕uri is a borrowing with a more restricted formal register, while namdvili is native and broader in application.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 9

2. **Term B:** ნამდვილობა (namdviloba)
3. **Transliteration:** avtent̕uroba / namdviloba

## Opposition 10

1. **Term A:** ჭეშმარიტება (č̣ešmariteba)
2. **Term B:** სიმართლე (simartle)
3. **Transliteration:** č̣ešmariteba / simartle
4. **English translation:** "truth, truthfulness, veracity" (philosophical/elevated register) vs. "truth, correctness, rightness, justice" (everyday and moral register)
5. **Description of contrast:** The P1 data describes both as "near-synonyms" for truth but notes that č̣ešmariteba carries "higher register weight in philosophical and religious discourse" while simartle has "a stronger association with moral and legal rightness, as well as factual correctness" and is "commonly used in everyday speech." P3 places them in different groups (Group 7 vs. Group 1) on the basis of these register and contextual differences.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]
