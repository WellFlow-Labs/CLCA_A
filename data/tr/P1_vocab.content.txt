# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P1 CORE VOCABULARY COLLECTION
# Language: Turkish (tr)

---

## 1. doğru
**Transliteration:** doğru  
**Literal meaning:** straight, direct  
**Typical English translations:** true, correct, right, accurate

Used to evaluate statements as factually accurate or correct. Also used for moral rightness and proper direction/alignment. Common in everyday speech for confirming accuracy ("Bu doğru mu?" = "Is this true/correct?").

## 2. yanlış
**Transliteration:** yanlış  
**Literal meaning:** from root *yan* (side) + *-lış* (nominalizer), literally "sideways"  
**Typical English translations:** wrong, false, incorrect, mistaken

Used to evaluate statements, actions, or judgments as incorrect or erroneous. Functions both as adjective and noun. Widely used in moral, factual, and practical contexts.

**[CORE]**

## 3. gerçek
**Transliteration:** gerçek  
**Literal meaning:** from Persian *gercek*  
**Typical English translations:** real, true, genuine, actual

Evaluates authenticity and reality of objects, statements, or situations. Distinguishes genuine from fake or imaginary. Common in philosophical and everyday contexts ("gerçek hayat" = "real life").

**[CORE]**

## 4. sahte

## 5. hakikat
**Transliteration:** hakikat  
**Literal meaning:** from Arabic *ḥaqīqa* (truth, reality)  
**Typical English translations:** truth, reality, the real nature of something

Used in formal, philosophical, or religious contexts to refer to fundamental truth or ultimate reality. More elevated register than *gerçek* or *doğru*.

## 6. hak
**Transliteration:** hak  
**Literal meaning:** from Arabic *ḥaqq* (truth, right, justice)  
**Typical English translations:** right, truth, justice, what is due

Evaluates moral rightness, legal entitlement, or fundamental justice. Used in expressions like "hak etmek" (to deserve) and "haklı" (right, justified). Central to moral and legal discourse.
