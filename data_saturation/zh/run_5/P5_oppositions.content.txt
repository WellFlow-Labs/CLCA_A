# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P5 Oppositions
# Language: Chinese (zh)

---

## Opposition 1

1. **Term A:** 真 (zhēn)
2. **Term B:** 假 (jiǎ)
3. **Transliteration:** zhēn / jiǎ
4. **English translation:** genuine, real / fake, false, counterfeit
5. **Description of contrast:** These two morphemes constitute the primary positive and negative poles of the truth-evaluation domain. The source data explicitly identifies 假 as the "antonym of 真 in most contexts" and documents them as the anchoring elements of the binary compound 真假.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 2

1. **Term A:** 真 (zhēn)
2. **Term B:** 伪 (wěi)
3. **Transliteration:** zhēn / wěi
4. **English translation:** genuine, true / spurious, false, forged
5. **Description of contrast:** These two morphemes form the binary compound 真伪 (genuine vs. spurious), used specifically in authentication, legal, and documentary contexts. The source data distinguishes 伪 from 假 in that 伪 more strongly implies deliberate fabrication or forgery rather than mere inauthenticity.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 42

1. **Term A:** 欺骗者 (qīpiànzhě)
2. **Term B:** 说真话的人 (shuō zhēnhuà de rén)
3. **Transliteration:** qīpiànzhě / shuō zhēnhuà de rén
