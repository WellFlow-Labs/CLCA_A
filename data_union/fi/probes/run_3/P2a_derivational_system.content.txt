# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2a Derivational System
# Language: Finnish (fi)

---

**Preliminary note:** The following documents attested derivational forms for each P1 base item. Forms already appearing in P1 or P1b as primary entries are cross-referenced rather than re-documented in full. Derivational coverage is uneven across items; this asymmetry is preserved and not corrected.

---

## 1. tosi

**Base Form:** tosi

---

**Derived Form 1:**
- **Derived Form:** totuus
- **Transliteration:** totuus
- **Literal Breakdown:** *tosi* (true) + nominalizing *-uus* (with stem alternation *tosi* → *totu-*)
- **Natural English translation:** truth
- **Usage:** Abstract noun denoting the property of being true or a true state of affairs. Already documented as P1 item 2; cross-referenced here as the primary nominal derivation of *tosi*.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2:**
- **Derived Form:** todella
- **Transliteration:** todella
- **Literal Breakdown:** *tode-* (oblique stem of *tosi*) + adessive case *-lla*
- **Natural English translation:** really, truly, indeed
- **Usage:** Common adverb used to intensify or affirm. Already documented in P1b (Root 1, item 11); cross-referenced here.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 3:**
- **Derived Form:** todellinen
- **Transliteration:** todellinen
- **Literal Breakdown:** *tode-* + adjectival *-llinen*
- **Natural English translation:** real, actual, genuine
- **Usage:** Adjective describing something that exists in reality as opposed to appearance or fiction. Already documented in P1b (Root 1, item 12); cross-referenced here.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 4:**
- **Derived Form:** todellisuus
- **Transliteration:** todellisuus
- **Literal Breakdown:** *todellinen* + nominalizing *-uus*
- **Natural English translation:** reality, actuality
- **Usage:** Abstract noun referring to the state of being real or the totality of what is real. Already documented in P1b (Root 1, item 13); cross-referenced here.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 5:**
- **Derived Form:** todistaa
- **Transliteration:** todistaa
- **Literal Breakdown:** *tode-* / *tosi* + *-staa* (derivational verbal suffix)
- **Natural English translation:** to prove, to testify, to certify
- **Usage:** Verb covering proving a claim and giving testimony. Already documented as P1 item 18; cross-referenced here as a verbal derivation of *tosi*.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 6:**
- **Derived Form:** todentaa
- **Transliteration:** todentaa
- **Literal Breakdown:** *tode-* + causative *-ntaa*
- **Natural English translation:** to verify, to authenticate, to validate
- **Usage:** Used in technical, administrative, and legal contexts for verifying or confirming truth or authenticity. Already documented in P1b (Root 1, item 17); cross-referenced here.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 7:**
- **Derived Form:** todentua
- **Transliteration:** todentua
- **Literal Breakdown:** *tode-* + inchoative/reflexive *-ntua*
- **Natural English translation:** to come true, to be verified, to materialize
- **Usage:** Describes a prediction or expectation that proves to be true. Already documented in P1b (Root 1, item 19); cross-referenced here.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 8:**
- **Derived Form:** tosiasia
- **Transliteration:** tosiasia
- **Literal Breakdown:** *tosi* + *asia* (matter, thing)
- **Natural English translation:** fact
- **Usage:** Compound noun referring to an established or undeniable fact. Already documented in P1b (Root 1, item 5); cross-referenced here as a productive compound base.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Note on productivity:** The stem alternation *tosi* → *tode-* is irregular and must be learned; it does not follow a fully predictable pattern. The root is nonetheless highly productive, generating adverbs, adjectives, abstract nouns, and verbs. The forms *todella*, *todellinen*, *todellisuus*, *todistaa*, *todentaa* are all in common use across registers.

## 5. virheellinen

**Base Form:** virheellinen

---

**Derived Form 1:**
- **Derived Form:** virheellisyys
- **Transliteration:** virheellisyys
- **Literal Breakdown:** *virheellinen* + nominalizing *-yys*
- **Natural English translation:** erroneousness, faultiness, incorrectness
- **Usage:** Abstract noun referring to the property of being erroneous or faulty. Used in legal, technical, and administrative contexts to name the quality of containing errors ("tiedon virheellisyys" — "the incorrectness of the information").
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2:**
- **Derived Form:** virheellisesti
- **Transliteration:** virheellisesti
- **Literal Breakdown:** *virheellinen* + adverbial *-sti*
- **Natural English translation:** erroneously, incorrectly, faultily
- **Usage:** Adverbial form used in legal and administrative texts to describe actions or statements made in an erroneous manner ("virheellisesti ilmoitettu" — "incorrectly reported/declared").
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 3:**
- **Derived Form:** virhe
- **Transliteration:** virhe
- **Literal Breakdown:** *virhe* (base noun from which *virheellinen* is derived)
- **Natural English translation:** error, mistake, fault, defect
- **Usage:** The base noun underlying *virheellinen*. Used across everyday, technical, legal, and administrative contexts to refer to an error or defect ("tehdä virhe" — "to make a mistake"; "korjata virhe" — "to correct an error"). Documented here as the base of the P1 item rather than as a derivation from it.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 4:**
- **Derived Form:** virheettömyys
- **Transliteration:** virheettömyys
- **Literal Breakdown:** *virhe* + privative *-ttömyys* (absence of)
- **Natural English translation:** flawlessness, freedom from error, correctness
- **Usage:** Abstract noun referring to the absence of errors or defects. Used in quality control, legal, and administrative contexts ("asiakirjan virheettömyys" — "the correctness/flawlessness of the document").
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 5:**
- **Derived Form:** virheetön
- **Transliteration:** virheetön
- **Literal Breakdown:** *virhe* + privative *-tön*
- **Natural English translation:** flawless, error-free, correct
- **Usage:** Adjective describing something free from errors or defects. Used in quality, legal, and technical contexts ("virheetön suoritus" — "a flawless performance"; "virheetön asiakirja" — "an error-free document").
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 6:**
- **Derived Form:** virhearvio
- **Transliteration:** virhearvio
- **Literal Breakdown:** *virhe* + *arvio* (estimate, assessment)
- **Natural English translation:** error estimate, margin of error
- **Usage:** Used in statistical, scientific, and technical contexts to refer to an estimate of the magnitude of error in a measurement or calculation.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Note:** The derivations *virheetön* and *virheettömyys* are formed from the base noun *virhe* rather than directly from *virheellinen*, but are documented here given their semantic relevance and their relationship to the P1 item.

## 11. epätosi

Used in everyday, literary, and psychological contexts ("tilanne tuntui epätodelliselta" — "the situation felt unreal"). Distinct from *epätosi* in that it concerns the quality of reality rather than propositional truth.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 3:**
- **Derived Form:** epätodellisuus
- **Transliteration:** epätodellisuus
- **Literal Breakdown:** *epä-* + *todellisuus* (reality)
- **Natural English translation:** unreality, lack of reality
- **Usage:** Abstract noun referring to the quality of being unreal. Used in philosophical, literary, and psychological contexts. Less common than *todellisuus* but attested.
- **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**Note:** *Epätosi* itself does not appear to generate further derivations beyond those formed by applying *epä-* to the related *tosi/tode-* family. No verbal or agentive derivation from *epätosi* as a base has been identified.
