# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P4b Construction Usage & Constraints
# Language: Chinese (zh)

---

## Construction Usage Table

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. ATTRIBUTION** | 这是真的。 | Zhè shì zhēn de. | "This is true / genuine." | [CORE] | Ubiquitous in spoken and written Chinese across all registers; the 是…的 frame is the default predicate construction for truth-domain attribution. The bare adjective predicate (e.g., 报道失实) is restricted to formal/written registers and fixed expressions. |
| **1. ATTRIBUTION** | 这份报告是真实的。 | Zhè fèn bàogào shì zhēnshí de. | "This report is truthful / accurate." | [CORE] | Standard in both spoken and written registers; disyllabic adjectives (真实, 准确) are more typical of formal contexts than monosyllabic ones (真). |
| **2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION** | 这不是真的。 | Zhè bù shì zhēn de. | "This is not true / not genuine." | [CORE] | The standard spoken negation of the attribution frame; unrestricted across registers and contexts. No observable constraints on the truth-domain adjectives it combines with. |
| **2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION** | 该陈述并非属实。 | Gāi chénshù bìngfēi shǔshí. | "The statement is not confirmed as factual." | [MARGINAL] | Restricted to formal written registers (legal, journalistic, administrative); 并非 does not occur freely in casual speech. The demonstrative 该 further marks this as a formal register item. |
| **3. ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION** | 真实的情况 | zhēnshí de qíngkuàng | "the true / actual situation" | [CORE] | The modifier + 的 + noun pattern is the standard attributive construction in Chinese; no register restriction. Disyllabic modifiers require 的; monosyllabic modifiers may omit it in fixed compounds. |
| **3. ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION** | 虚假的广告 | xūjiǎ de guǎnggào | "false / fraudulent advertising" | [CORE] | Common in consumer, legal, and media contexts; 虚假 is a standard collocate with 广告, 信息, 宣传 in regulatory and journalistic discourse. |
| **4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE** | 弄假成真 | nòng jiǎ chéng zhēn | "what started as a pretense becomes reality" | [MARGINAL] | A fixed four-character expression (成语); not a productive open pattern. Used idiomatically to describe unintended or ironic transitions from pretense to reality; not freely generalized to other truth-domain bases. |
| **4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE** | 合法化 | héfǎhuà | "to legalize / to become lawful" | [CORE] | The 化 suffix is productive with disyllabic stative bases in formal and written registers; common in political, legal, and policy discourse. Application is not uniform: monosyllabic truth-domain bases (真, 假) do not take 化 productively. |
| **5. CAUSATIVE** | (no common expression identified) | — | — | [UNCERTAIN] | No dedicated causative construction is attested for this domain. Periphrastic expressions (使/让 + NP + V) and transitive verbs (证实, 核实) cover causative meanings but are not structurally distinct from ordinary transitives; no separate pattern can be reported. |
| **6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION** | 准确性 | zhǔnquèxìng | "accuracy, the property of being precise" | [CORE] | The 性 suffix is productive with disyllabic bases across formal and technical registers; extremely common in academic, legal, and administrative writing. Monosyllabic bases (真, 假) are restricted and yield specialized rather than general abstract nouns. |
| **6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION** | 可靠性 | kěkàoxìng | "reliability, the property of being dependable" | [CORE] | Same distribution as 准确性; freely combines with disyllabic truth-domain adjectives. Frequently appears as a nominal head in formal evaluative contexts (e.g., 信息的可靠性 "the reliability of the information"). |
| **7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION** | 伪造者 | wěizàozhě | "forger, counterfeiter" | [MARGINAL] | The 者 suffix is formal and appears primarily in written, legal, and official registers; not typical of casual speech. Productive only where the base describes a deliberate human action; no agentive nominalization with 者 is attested for the positive (truth-telling) pole. |
| **7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION** | 欺骗者 | qīpiànzhě | "deceiver, cheat" | [MARGINAL] | Same formal register restriction as 伪造者. In informal speech, 骗子 (with suffix 子) is the preferred form and is [CORE] in colloquial registers; 欺骗者 is the formal written variant. |
| **8. VERIFICATION / CONFIRMATION** | 经查属实 | jīng chá shǔshí | "verified upon investigation; confirmed as factual after inquiry" | [CORE] | Highly conventionalized formula in administrative, legal, and journalistic registers; functions as a fixed phrase rather than a productive open pattern. Restricted to formal written contexts; not used in casual speech. |
| **8. VERIFICATION / CONFIRMATION** | 经调查证实 | jīng diàochá zhèngshí | "confirmed by investigation" | [CORE] | Same register restriction as 经查属实; the 经 + verification verb + result frame is a stable formal convention. The negative counterpart 无法核实 is similarly conventionalized in the same registers. |
| **9. ADVERBIAL TRUTH-FRAMING** | 如实陈述 | rúshí chénshù | "to state truthfully; to give a truthful account" | [CORE] | 如实 is a productive adverb that attaches directly to speech and reporting verbs without 地; common in legal, administrative, and journalistic contexts. Not restricted to formal registers but most frequent there. |
| **9. ADVERBIAL TRUTH-FRAMING** | 实事求是地说 | shíshì qiúshì de shuō | "to say it as it actually is; to speak empirically and honestly" | [MARGINAL] | The full four-character phrase 实事求是 is a fixed expression associated with political and ideological discourse; its use as a manner adverbial with 地 is more formal and marked than shorter adverbials like 如实. Less common in everyday speech. |
| **10. PROHIBITION** | 严禁弄虚作假 | yánjìn nòng xū zuò jiǎ | "it is strictly forbidden to engage in fraud and falsification" | [CORE] | Characteristic of legal, regulatory, and institutional written registers; 严禁 + violation verb is a stable and productive formula in official documents and signage. Not used in casual speech. |
| **10. PROHIBITION** | 不得欺骗消费者 | bùdé qīpiàn xiāofèizhě | "it is prohibited to deceive consumers" | [CORE] | 不得 is the standard formal prohibition marker in legal and regulatory texts; productive with truth-domain violation verbs. Slightly less emphatic than 严禁 and somewhat broader in distribution across formal registers. |

## Attested Alternate Constructions

| Construction Type | Alternate Form | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **1. ATTRIBUTION** | 报道失实 (bare adjective predicate without 是…的) | Restricted to formal/written registers and fixed expressions; not a free productive alternate in spoken Chinese. |
| **2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION** | 不是…的 vs. 并非 | 不是…的 is the spoken/general form; 并非 is the formal written variant. These are register alternates of the same semantic function, not structurally equivalent. |
| **7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION** | 骗子 (colloquial, suffix 子) vs. 欺骗者 (formal, suffix 者) | Register alternates for the deception-agent concept; 骗子 is [CORE] in spoken registers, 欺骗者 is [MARGINAL] and formal. No colloquial alternate with 子 is attested for 伪造者. |
| **8. VERIFICATION / CONFIRMATION** | 无法核实 (negative counterpart) | Attested as the conventionalized negative of the verification frame; same register distribution as the positive forms. |
| **9. ADVERBIAL TRUTH-FRAMING** | 实话实说; 坦白说 | Attested shorter adverbial alternates in the same constructional type; 实话实说 and 坦白说 are less formal and more broadly distributed across registers than 实事求是地说. |
