### 1. Base Form: **உண்மை**
**Derived Form:** **உண்மையான**  
**Transliteration:** uṇmaiyāṉa  
**Literal Breakdown:** உண்மை “truth, reality” + adjectival **-ஆன**  
**Natural English translation:** true; real; genuine  
**Usage description:** A very common adjective used for true statements, real situations, and genuine persons or things. It is widely used in both speech and writing.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### 2. Base Form: **உண்மை**
**Derived Form:** **உண்மைத்தன்மை**  
**Transliteration:** uṇmaittaṉmai  
**Literal Breakdown:** உண்மை “truth” + linking **-த்-** + **தன்மை** “quality, nature”  
**Natural English translation:** truthfulness; veracity; authenticity  
**Usage description:** This noun is used in formal evaluation of whether a statement, report, portrayal, or testimony is true or authentic. It is common in journalism, criticism, and legal or administrative discussion.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]
