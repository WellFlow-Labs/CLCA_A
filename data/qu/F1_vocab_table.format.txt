# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — F1 Vocabulary Table
# Language: Quechua (qu)

| Form | Transliteration | Literal Meaning | Common Translations | Usage Description | Epistemic Status |
|------|----------------|-----------------|---------------------|-------------------|------------------|
| chiqan | chiqan | true, real, genuine | true, real, genuine, authentic, correct | Used to describe statements that correspond to reality or things that are authentic and not false. Commonly appears in both everyday speech and formal contexts to affirm accuracy or genuineness of information, objects, or situations. | [CORE] |
| chiqap | chiqap | truly, really (adverbial form) | truly, really, indeed, in truth | Adverbial form used to emphasize the truth or reality of a statement. Functions as an intensifier to confirm the veracity of what is being said or to express certainty about a claim. | [CORE] |
| llulla | llulla | false, lie, deceptive | false, lie, untrue, deceptive | Describes statements that are not true or people who deceive. Used both as a noun (a lie) and as an adjective (false, lying), commonly employed in everyday contexts to identify falsehood or dishonesty. | [CORE] |
| llullakuy | llullakuy | to lie, to deceive (verb with reflexive suffix -ku-) | to lie, to deceive, to tell falsehoods | Verbal form indicating the action of lying or deceiving. | allin | allin | good, well, correct | good, well, correct, right, proper | Widely used term for positive evaluation across moral, practical, and aesthetic domains. Applied to conduct, objects, situations, and statements to indicate they meet standards of correctness, quality, or appropriateness. | [CORE] |
| mana allin | mana allin | not good | bad, not good, wrong, improper | Negated form of allin used to express negative evaluation. Commonly employed to describe improper conduct, incorrect actions, or things that fail to meet expected standards. | [CORE] |
| chanin | chanin | valuable, worthy, legitimate | valuable, worthy, legitimate, valid, authentic | Indicates something has proper value, legitimacy, or authenticity. | mana kaq | mana kaq | not being, non-existent | non-existent, unreal, false, not actual | Negated form indicating non-existence or unreality. Used to characterize things that do not actually exist or statements that have no basis in reality. | [CORE] |
| takyay | takyay | to verify, to test, to prove | to verify, to test, to prove, to examine | Indicates the action of testing or verifying something to establish its truth or quality. Used in contexts where evidence or proof is being sought or established. | [CORE] |
