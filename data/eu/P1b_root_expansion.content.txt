# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P1b ROOT EXPANSION
# Language: Basque (eu)

## STEP 1: IDENTIFY CENTRAL ROOTS

### Root 1: **egia** (truth)
**Participates in:** 9 P1 items (egia, egiazkoa, egiazkotasuna, egiazkotu, egiazkotatu, egiatan, egiazkoa den, egiaztatu, frogagarria [contains egia-derived element])

**Core meaning contribution:** The fundamental concept of truth, factual accuracy, correspondence with reality. Serves as the primary lexical anchor for truth-related concepts in Basque.

---

### Root 2: **gezur** (lie, falsehood)
**Participates in:** 4 P1 items (gezur, gezurra, gezurrezko, gezurtia)

**Core meaning contribution:** The concept of falsehood, lying, and deliberate untruth. Primary lexical anchor for falsity and deception, functioning as the direct semantic opposite of egia.

---

### Root 3: **zuzen** (straight, correct)
**Participates in:** 4 P1 items (zuzena, zuzentasuna, zuzendu, zuzen-zuzenean)

**Core meaning contribution:** Polysemous root covering physical straightness, correctness/accuracy, and moral rightness. Bridges spatial, epistemic, and ethical domains through a single morpheme.

---

#### 1. **egia esan**
**Form:** egia esan  
**Literal breakdown:** egia (truth) + esan (to say)  
**Translation:** to tell the truth  
**Context:** Common verbal phrase used when someone is speaking truthfully or when requesting honesty. Appears in everyday conversation and formal testimony contexts.  
**[CORE]**

## STEP 3: PERIPHERAL EXTENSIONS

### Extensions of **egia** (truth)

**Noted peripheral uses:**

1. **egiazko** in aesthetic contexts: The root appears in evaluations of artistic authenticity (e.g., "egiazko artea" = true/genuine art), where the truth-domain meaning extends to judgments about artistic merit and authenticity rather than factual correspondence.

2. **egiazko** in essential nature contexts: Expressions like "egiazko izaera" (true nature) and "egiazko aurpegia" (true face) extend from truth-as-correspondence to truth-as-essence, marking what something fundamentally is rather than what is factually accurate about it.

3. **egiazko** in value judgments: Collocations like "egiazko balioa" (true value) and "egiazko maitasuna" (true love) extend the root into evaluative domains where "true" functions more as an intensifier of quality or genuineness than as a marker of factual accuracy.

4. **egiazale** in character evaluation: The compound "egiazale" (truth-lover) shifts from describing statements or facts to characterizing persons by their relationship to truth as a value, functioning in moral rather than epistemic evaluation.

---

### Extensions of **gezur** (lie, falsehood)

**Noted peripheral uses:**

1. **gezurrezko** in commercial contexts: The root extends to commercial fraud and counterfeiting (e.g., "gezurrezko dirua" = counterfeit money, "gezurrezko produktua" = fake product), where falsity relates to authenticity of objects rather than truth of statements.

2. **gezurrezko** in identity contexts: Expressions like "gezurrezko identitatea" (false identity) extend from lying-as-communication to falsity-as-impersonation, shifting from speech acts to social roles.

3. **gezurrezko** in technical contexts: Uses like "gezurrezko alarma" (false alarm) extend the root to technical malfunctions or errors that are not deliberate deceptions, shifting from moral to technical domains.

4. **gezur-gezurra** as intensifier: The reduplicated form functions as an emphatic intensifier that may extend beyond strict falsity to express strong dismissal or rejection.

---

### Extensions of **zuzen** (straight, correct)

**Noted peripheral uses:**

1. **zuzeneko** in media/broadcasting: The root's extension to "live broadcast" (zuzeneko emisioa) represents a shift from spatial straightness and epistemic correctness to temporal immediacy and lack of mediation, a distinct semantic domain.

2. **zuzeneko** in political contexts: Uses like "zuzeneko demokrazia" (direct democracy) extend from physical/epistemic directness to political organization principles, marking unmediated participation rather than correctness.

3. **zuzeneko** in grammatical terminology: The grammatical term "zuzeneko objektua" (direct object) represents a technical extension into linguistic metalanguage, where "direct" marks syntactic relationships rather than spatial or epistemic properties.

4. **zuzeneko** in economic contexts: Terms like "zuzeneko inbertsioa" (direct investment) and "zuzeneko salmentak" (direct sales) extend the root into commercial domains where directness relates to business structures rather than truth or physical straightness.

5. **zuzen** in moral contexts: While "zuzena" can mean "just" or "right" morally, this represents an extension from correctness-as-accuracy to correctness-as-righteousness, bridging epistemic and ethical domains.
