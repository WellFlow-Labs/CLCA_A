# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2a Derivational System
# Language: Indonesian (id)

---

## Preliminary Note on Scope

This step documents attested derivational morphology for the 30 base forms identified in P1. The P1b Root Expansion has already documented derivations of the three central roots (*benar*, *salah*, *asli/palsu*) in detail. Where those derivations are directly relevant to P1 base forms, they are cross-referenced rather than duplicated, but each P1 base form receives its own entry. Forms already fully documented in P1b are noted as such; new derivations not covered in P1b are documented in full.

Derivations are listed per base form. No paradigm completion is performed. Gaps and asymmetries are preserved.

---

## 1. Base Form: **benar**

*Core derivations of this root were documented extensively in P1b. The following represents the derivational inventory directly associated with the base form as listed in P1.*

---

**Derived Form 1:**
- **Base Form:** benar
- **Derived Form:** kebenaran
- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Literal Breakdown:** *ke-* + *benar* + *-an* → nominalization
- **Natural English Translation:** truth, correctness, rightness
- **Usage:** Refers to the property or state of being true or correct; used across philosophical, legal, journalistic, and everyday registers. Documented in full in P1b (Root 1, item 1).
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2:**
- **Base Form:** benar
- **Derived Form:** membenarkan
- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Literal Breakdown:** *mem-* + *benar* + *-kan* → causative/confirmatory verb
- **Natural English Translation:** to confirm, to justify, to validate
- **Usage:** Used to confirm that something is true or correct, or to justify an action; also used in the sense of physically straightening something. Documented in full in P1b (Root 1, item 2).
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 3:**
- **Base Form:** benar
- **Derived Form:** sebenarnya
- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Literal Breakdown:** *se-* + *benar* + *-nya* → adverbial form
- **Natural English Translation:** actually, in truth, in fact
- **Usage:** High-frequency discourse marker introducing a correction or clarification of what is actually the case. Documented in full in P1b (Root 1, item 3).
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 4:**
- **Base Form:** benar
- **Derived Form:** pembenaran
- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Literal Breakdown:** *pem-* + *benar* + *-an* → nominalization of *membenarkan*
- **Natural English Translation:** justification, confirmation, rationalization
- **Usage:** Refers to the act or process of justifying or confirming something; *pembenaran diri* (self-justification) is a common collocation in moral and psychological discourse. Documented in full in P1b (Root 1, item 5).
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 5:**
- **Base Form:** benar
- **Derived Form:** dibenarkan
- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Literal Breakdown:** *di-* + *benar* + *-kan* → passive of *membenarkan*
- **Natural English Translation:** confirmed, justified, permitted, sanctioned
- **Usage:** Used in legal and administrative contexts to indicate that an action or claim has been officially confirmed or is permitted. Documented in full in P1b (Root 1, item 6).
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 6:**
- **Base Form:** benar
- **Derived Form:** benar-benar
- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Literal Breakdown:** *benar* + reduplication → intensified form
- **Natural English Translation:** truly, really, genuinely, absolutely
- **Usage:** Used as an intensifier to emphasize that something is genuinely or completely the case; also extends to general degree intensification in contexts unrelated to truth. Documented in full in P1b (Root 1, item 4).
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 7:**
- **Base Form:** benar
- **Derived Form:** kebenaran
- **Transliteration:** N/A
- *(See Derived Form 1 above; no additional derivations of kebenaran itself are documented here beyond those in P1b.)*

---

> **Note:** The form *pembenar* (one who confirms/justifies; agent noun) is occasionally encountered in formal writing but is substantially less frequent than *pembenaran* and the verbal forms. It is not documented as a separate entry here due to limited attestation relative to the other forms.

---

**Derived Form 3:**
- **Base Form:** salah

- **Natural English Translation:** to be blamed, to be held at fault
- **Usage:** Describes the state of being assigned blame or fault; used in interpersonal and legal contexts. Documented in full in P1b (Root 2, item 20).

---

**Derived Form 4:**
- **Base Form:** salah
- **Derived Form:** penyalahgunaan
- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Literal Breakdown:** *pe-* + *salah* + *guna* + *-an* → nominalization of compound verb
- **Natural English Translation:** misuse, abuse, misappropriation
- **Usage:** Describes the improper or unauthorized use of something — power, drugs, funds, authority; *penyalahgunaan wewenang* (abuse of authority) is a standard legal collocation. Documented in full in P1b (Root 2, item 21).

---

**Derived Form 5:**
- **Base Form:** salah
- **Derived Form:** menyalahgunakan
- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Literal Breakdown:** *me-* + *salah* + *guna* + *-kan* → verb
- **Natural English Translation:** to misuse, to abuse, to misappropriate
- **Usage:** Describes the act of using something in an improper or unauthorized way; used in legal and moral contexts. Documented in full in P1b (Root 2, item 22).
- **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

> **Note on *salah* compounds:** P1b documents an extensive series of *salah* + [verb/noun] compounds (*salah paham*, *salah tafsir*, *salah sangka*, *salah langkah*, etc.). These are productive compounding patterns rather than affixal derivations in the strict sense. They are not repeated here but are fully documented in P1b (Root 2, items 3–19). The partitive use of *salah* in *salah satu* and *salah seorang* represents a grammaticalized function documented in P1b's peripheral extensions section.

- **Natural English Translation:** statement, declaration, announcement

---

- **Base Form:** nyata

- **Natural English Translation:** stated, declared, announced, pronounced
- **Usage:** Used in formal and legal contexts to indicate that something has been officially stated or declared; *dinyatakan bersalah* (declared guilty), *dinyatakan sah* (declared valid).

---

- **Natural English Translation:** clearly, evidently, manifestly, plainly
- **Usage:** Used as an intensifier to emphasize that something is unmistakably or undeniably the case; *nyata-nyata melanggar hukum* (clearly/manifestly violating the law). Common in journalistic and legal discourse.

---

> **Note:** The form *penyataan* (parallel to *pernyataan*) is occasionally encountered but *pernyataan* is the dominant nominalization in standard usage. *Penyataan* is not documented as a separate entry here.

**Derived Form 3:**
