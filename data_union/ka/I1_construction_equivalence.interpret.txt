## 2. Substitution Patterns (Narrative)

### 2.1 REVELATION substitutes for BECOME

The data show that Georgian does not have a single grammaticalized inchoative construction covering the full truth-domain adjective class. The -deba inchoative suffix is attested only on the mart- root (martldeba, gamartldeba). For the remaining truth-domain adjectives (namdvili, q̇albi, č̣ešmariti) and for the abstract noun simartle, the language uses revelation-type constructions instead: simartle gamovida ("the truth came out") and siq̇albe gamoašk'aravda ("the fraud was exposed"). These constructions encode the transition from a hidden or unknown state to a known or visible one, which is the functional equivalent of "X becomes known to be true/false." They are not morphologically inchoative but function as the primary expressions for this semantic role. The substitution is systematic: where -deba is unavailable, a revelation verb fills the slot.

The adessive + motion verb construction (siq̇albeze c̣asvla, "resorting to falsehood") covers a distinct sub-case: volitional entry into a state of falsity. This is rated [MARGINAL] and is restricted to contexts of deliberate deceptive behavior. It does not substitute for BECOME in the same way as the revelation constructions.

### 2.2 DISCOVERY does not substitute for CAUSATION

No evidence in the P4a/P4b data indicates that a discovery-type construction substitutes for CAUSATION in Georgian. The causative-resultative formations (gaq̇albeba, gasc̣oreba, dazusteba, gamarṭleba) are lexicalized preverb + root + -eba forms that encode caused change of state directly. The agent is marked in the ergative case in the aorist series. These are not discovery constructions. The absence of a productive causative morpheme across the full adjective class is noted, but the individual lexicalized forms cover the most semantically central causative meanings in this domain.

### 2.3 NEGATION: analytic vs. morphological

Georgian employs both analytic and morphological negation strategies in this domain, and they are not freely interchangeable. The morphological strategies divide into two non-interchangeable sub-types:

- **ara- prefix**: productive, compositional negation of adjectives (arasc̣ori, aragulc̣rpeli). Yields negations whose meanings are predictable from the components.
- **u-...-o / u- privative prefix**: produces lexicalized adjectives with meanings that are not fully predictable from the components (uẗq̇uari "infallible" rather than simply "not lying"; usamart̕lo "unjust" rather than simply "without justice").

The analytic strategy (ar + aris) is obligatory in predicative negation. The enclitic -a copula, which is the default in affirmative predicative constructions, is categorically excluded under negation. This is a morphosyntactic restriction documented in P4b as applying across all truth-domain predicates without exception.

### 2.4 VERIFICATION and DISCONFIRMATION do not share the same construction

VERIFICATION is expressed primarily through the resultative participial pattern (-ebul-: dadasturebuli, damt'k'icebuli) and through genitive nominal constructions (mt'k'icebis t'virti). DISCONFIRMATION is expressed through analytic negation (ar aris), morphological negation (ara-), and the resultative participial of falsification (gaq̇albebuli). The two operators use structurally distinct constructions. The resultative participial pattern (-ebul-) is shared in the sense that both dadasturebuli (verification) and gaq̇albebuli (disconfirmation through falsification) use the same morphological template, but they apply to different verbal bases and encode opposite semantic outcomes.

### 2.5 Asymmetry between truth-domain and falsity-domain constructions

Several asymmetries are observable in the data:

**Discourse-framing constructions** are attested only for simartle (simartle rom vt̕q̇vat̕; simartle isaa, rom...). No structural parallel in the falseness domain is documented. This is an asymmetry in the discourse-framing operator: the truth side has dedicated parenthetical and framing constructions; the falsity side does not.

**The concessive formula** (martalia, magram...) is specific to martali. Neither č̣ešmariteba nor simartle participates in this formula, and no parallel falsity-domain concessive formula is attested.

**Agentive nominals** are more developed on the falsity/deception side: mt'q̇uari, mt̕q̇uani, gamaq̇albebeli, and tvaltmaqci are all attested as common lexicalized agentive nominals. On the truth side, no common single-word agentive nominal for "truth-teller" is documented; the concept requires either the adjective martali used nominally or a verbal periphrasis (simartle ambobs, "tells the truth"; simartlis mtqveli, "one who tells the truth").

**Inchoative morphology** is attested only for the mart- root (-deba forms). No inchoative morphological forms are attested for namdvili, q̇albi, or č̣ešmariti. The revelation constructions (gamovida, gamoašk'aravda) cover both truth and falsity sides, partially compensating for this asymmetry.

### 2.6 Light verbs as semantic carriers

In the predicative truth-assertion constructions, the speech verb (ambobs, tkva) functions as a light verb carrying the assertion operator, while the truth-domain nominal (simarṭle in the dative) carries the semantic content. The construction simarṭles vambob ("I am telling the truth") is syntactically a verb + dative object construction, but functionally it operates as a first-person truth-assertion predicate. The dative case on the truth noun is structurally obligatory and is not interchangeable with other cases in this construction.

Similarly, in the genitive modification constructions (simarṭlis dadgena, mt'k'icebis t'virti), the head nominal (dadgena "determination," t'virti "burden") functions as the syntactic head while the truth-domain genitive nominal specifies the semantic content. These multi-word expressions behave syntactically as noun phrases.

## 3. Missingness Summary

### 3.1 Structural Gaps

- **BECOME (full class)**: The inchoative -deba construction is structurally available but restricted to the mart- root. No productive inchoative morphological construction applies across the full truth-domain adjective class (namdvili, q̇albi, č̣ešmariti lack attested -deba forms). This is a partial structural gap: the construction exists but is not generalized.

- **CAUSATION (productive morphological pattern across adjective class)**: No synchronically productive causative morpheme or construction applies to truth-domain adjectives as a class. The attested causative-resultative forms (gaq̇albeba, gasc̣oreba, dazusteba, gamarṭleba) are lexicalized items, not instances of a productive rule. Rated [UNCERTAIN] in P4b. This is a partial structural gap at the class level; individual lexical items cover the most central meanings.

### 3.2 Lexical Gaps

- **"Truth-teller" agentive nominal**: No common single-word lexicalized agentive nominal for the role of "truth-teller" is attested. The concept is expressed through martali used nominally or through verbal periphrasis (simartle ambobs; simartlis mtqveli). This is a lexical gap on the truth side of the agentive nominal paradigm.

- **Dedicated single-word verification verb**: No dedicated single-word verification verb is documented in the P4a/P4b data. Verification meaning is expressed through the resultative participial construction (dadasturebuli) and genitive nominal constructions (mt'k'icebis t'virti). This is a lexical gap at the verb level; construction-level expression is available.

### 3.3 Conceptual Gaps

No conceptual gaps are identifiable from the P4a/P4b data. All ten CLCA operators are expressible in Georgian, either through direct constructions or through documented substitutions. No operator is blocked at the conceptual level.

### 3.4 Asymmetric Gaps

- **Discourse-framing constructions**: Attested only for simartle (truth side). No parallel construction documented for the falsity domain. This is an asymmetric gap: the truth side has dedicated discourse-framing operators; the falsity side does not.

- **Concessive formula**: Attested only for martali. No parallel falsity-domain concessive formula is documented. Asymmetric gap on the truth side (only one truth-domain adjective participates; no falsity-domain parallel).

- **Agentive nominals**: Multiple common lexicalized agentive nominals attested for falsity/deception roles (mt'q̇uari, mt̕q̇uani, gamaq̇albebeli, tvaltmaqci). No common lexicalized agentive nominal for the truth-telling role. Asymmetric lexical gap on the truth side.

- **Inchoative morphology**: -deba inchoative attested only for mart- root. No inchoative morphological forms attested for namdvili, q̇albi, or č̣ešmariti. Asymmetric partial structural gap: mart- root has inchoative morphology; other truth-domain adjectives do not.
