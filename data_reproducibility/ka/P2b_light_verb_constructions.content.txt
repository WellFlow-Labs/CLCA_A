## MULTI-WORD CONSTRUCTIONS

### BASE: ჭეშმარიტი (ch'eshmarit'i) — "true, genuine"

#### Construction 1: ჭეშმარიტი სიტყვა (ch'eshmarit'i sit'q'va)
**Transliteration:** ch'eshmarit'i sit'q'va
**English translation:** true word, truth
**Usage notes:** Fixed phrase meaning "the truth" or "true words." Common in everyday speech when affirming truthfulness or asking someone to speak truthfully.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### Construction 2: ჭეშმარიტი ღმერთი (ch'eshmarit'i ghmerti)
**Transliteration:** ch'eshmarit'i ghmerti
**English translation:** true God
**Usage notes:** Religious phrase distinguishing the true God from false gods or idols. Common in religious discourse and liturgical contexts.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### Construction 3: ჭეშმარიტი გზა (ch'eshmarit'i gza)
**Transliteration:** ch'eshmarit'i gza
**English translation:** true path, right way
**Usage notes:** Metaphorical phrase for the correct or righteous path in life. Used in moral and religious discourse.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## SUMMARY

This analysis documents attested multi-word constructions in Georgian involving truth-domain vocabulary. Key patterns include:

1. **Noun + Adjective combinations**: Very productive across all bases (e.g., ნამდვილი მეგობარი "true friend," სანდო წყარო "reliable source")

2. **Proverbial expressions**: Several fixed idioms about truth and lies (e.g., ტყუილს მოკლე ფეხები აქვს "lies have short legs")

3. **Negation patterns**: Standard negation with არ (ar) is productive across all bases

4. **Comparative and intensification**: Regular patterns using უფრო (upro) "more" and intensifiers like ძალიან (dzalian) "very"

5. **Domain-specific collocations**: Legal (სამართლიანი სასამართლო "fair trial"), scientific (ბუნებრივი მოვლენა "natural phenomenon"), commercial (ნაღდი ფული "cash money")

Notable absences: No common periphrastic constructions for expressing degrees of truth or falsity beyond standard comparative/intensification patterns. Truth-evaluation is primarily accomplished through lexical choice rather than complex periphrasis.
