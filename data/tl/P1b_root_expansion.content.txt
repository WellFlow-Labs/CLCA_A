# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P1b ROOT EXPANSION
# Language: Tagalog (tl)

## Step 1 — Identify Central Roots

### Root 1: **totoo**
- **Form:** totoo
- **Participates in:** 6 P1 items (#1 totoo, #9 katotohanan, #12 totóhanán, #20 makatotohanan, #21 tunay na [related], #22 di-totoo)
- **Core meaning contribution:** Denotes factual truth, reality, actual existence; the quality of corresponding to what is real or what actually exists

### Root 2: **tunay**
- **Form:** tunay
- **Participates in:** 2 P1 items (#2 tunay, #21 tunay na)
- **Core meaning contribution:** Denotes genuineness, authenticity, essential nature; emphasizes the quality of being what something truly/essentially is

### Root 3: **tama**
- **Form:** tama
- **Participates in:** 1 P1 item (#4 tama)
- **Core meaning contribution:** Denotes correctness, rightness, accuracy; the quality of being without error or being appropriate/proper
