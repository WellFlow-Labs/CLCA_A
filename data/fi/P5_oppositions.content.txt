# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P5 OPPOSITIONS
# Language: Finnish (fi)

## OPPOSITION 1: tosi vs. valhe

**Term A:** tosi  
**Term B:** valhe  
**Transliteration:** tosi vs. valhe  
**Translation:** true vs. lie  
**Description:** Core lexical opposition between truth and deliberate falsehood. *Tosi* evaluates correspondence to reality; *valhe* denotes intentional misrepresentation. This is the primary truth-falsity opposition in Finnish.  
**Contrast type:** lexical  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Term B:** valhe  
**Transliteration:** totuus vs. valhe  
**Translation:** truth vs. lie  
**Description:** Abstract noun opposition between truth as concept/quality and lie as deliberate falsehood. Frequently paired in moral and philosophical discourse (*totuus ja valhe* "truth and lies").

## NOTES ON OPPOSITION PATTERNS

### Systematic morphological oppositions:
- **epä- prefix**: Highly productive for creating antonyms (*tosi/epätosi*, *rehellinen/epärehellinen*, *luotettava/epäluotettava*)
- **-tön privative**: Marks absence of quality (*virheellinen/virheetön*, *vilpillinen/vilpitön*)
- **-maton negative participle**: Marks non-occurrence of action (*todistettavissa/todistamaton*)

### Asymmetries noted:
- *Oikea/väärä* opposition is broader than *tosi/valhe*, covering correctness beyond truth
- *Virhe* (unintentional) vs. *valhe* (intentional) marks crucial intentionality distinction
- Directional *oikea/vasen* is distinct from correctness *oikea/väärä* despite shared form
- Some oppositions exist only constructionally (*pitää paikkansa/ei pidä paikkaansa*)

### Gaps identified:
- No single lexical antonym for *totuus* (uses *valhe* "lie" or *epätotuus* marginally)
- No standard agent noun opposition for truth-teller vs. liar (*totuuden puhuja* vs. *valehtelija*)
- Limited opposition for *aito* beyond *väärennös* (no *\*epäaito* in standard use)
