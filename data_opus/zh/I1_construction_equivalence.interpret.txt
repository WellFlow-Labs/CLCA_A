## 2. Substitution Patterns (Narrative)

**Revelation replaces becoming.** The data show no productive [Adj + become] template (no *变真实, *变正确). Change-of-state in the truth domain is carried either by fixed idioms (弄假成真) or, crucially, by the revelation idiom 真相大白 ("the truth comes fully to light"). Where a language might say "the truth became known," Chinese uses a revelation-type lexicalization. Thus REVELATION systematically occupies part of the BECOME space.

**Verification replaces (factual) causation.** P4a #5 explicitly notes no productive causative ("make X true/correct"). The functional space is filled by independent transitive verbs of verification and revelation: 揭露, 查明, 核实, 证实, 纠正. The language does not causativize the adjective; it predicates a separate verb on a truth-noun object. So CAUSATION(factual) is realized as VERIFICATION/correction predicates rather than as a derived causative.

**Verification and Disconfirmation partly share constructions.** Discrimination verbs 辨别/鉴定 take 真伪/真假 objects (P4a #9), covering BOTH the confirmation of genuineness and the detection of falsity within one collocational set. The disconfirmation of specific claims, however, also draws on the negation strategy (P4a #2: 假, 错误, 不真实). So the two operators overlap in the discrimination-verb construction but diverge where DISCONFIRMATION uses lexical antonyms.

**Positive vs. negative asymmetry in attribution.** ATTR uses a 是…的 frame (是真的) whose negative pole is partly lexicalized: falsity is expressed BOTH by negating the positive (不真实, 不正确) and by dedicated antonyms (假, 错, 错误, 虚伪). The form 没错 is a fixed negated affirmation (affirming correctness), not a denial — an asymmetry where a negative-marked form carries positive meaning.

**Nominalization asymmetry by syllable count and semantic class.** -性/-度 attach productively to bisyllabic evaluatives (真实性, 可信度) but are blocked on monosyllabic roots (真/假/对/错), which fall back on binomial compounds (真假, 真伪). Moral-character terms (真诚, 诚实) show a genuine derivational gap.

## 3. Missingness Summary

**Structural Gaps (missing constructions/morphosyntax):**
- Productive inchoative [Adj + become] frame — absent (no *变真实, *变正确).
- Productive causative on truth adjectives — absent (no *使真化, no productive 使+Adj).
- CAUSATION (character) — no attested causative construction.
- Agentive -者 nominalization on this vocabulary — empty slot (no *诚实者, *可靠者).

**Lexical Gaps (missing words):**
- -性 nominalizers for monosyllabic 真/假/对/错 (no *真性, *假性 in this sense).
- Standard nominalizers for moral terms 真诚/诚实 (genuine gap).
- No derived agent noun for "liar/truth-teller" from these adjectives (expressed by separate lexemes).

**Conceptual Gaps (blocked operations):**
- None explicitly identified in the data as conceptually blocked; the absent operations (causative, inchoative) are filled by alternative operators (verification verbs, revelation idioms) rather than reported as unexpressable.

**Asymmetric Gaps (only one polarity / restricted distribution):**
- 没错: negative-marked form encodes affirmation, not denial (polarity asymmetry).
- Nominalization available for bisyllabic but not monosyllabic roots.
- Adnominal collocations sharply restricted by noun class (真挚→emotion, 正宗/地道→food/culture, 可靠→source/info), so the slot is productive but distributionally asymmetric.

## 4. Canonicalized Operator Inventory

**Fully available:**
- ATTR (是…的 / bare adjectival predication)
- ADNOM (Adj + 的 + N)
- NEGATION (analytic 不/没 + lexical antonyms)
- REVELATION (transitive verbs 揭露/查明 + idiom 真相大白)
- VERIFICATION (transitive verbs 核实/证实/查明/鉴定)
- DISCONFIRMATION (discrimination verbs 辨别真伪 + lexical antonyms)

**Available with substitution:**
- BECOME → **SUBSTITUTED BY** lexicalized idioms and REVELATION (真相大白)
- CAUSATION (factual) → **SUBSTITUTED BY** VERIFICATION/correction verbs (核实, 证实, 纠正)

**Partially available:**
- NOMINALIZATION (abstract -性/-度 productive on bisyllabic terms only; agentive expressed phrasally via [Adj(+的)+人])

**Missing entirely:**
- CAUSATION (character) — **MISSING (structural gap)**
- Productive morphological causative on truth adjectives — **MISSING (structural gap)**
- Productive inchoative [Adj + become] frame — **MISSING (structural gap)**
- Agentive -者 derivation on this vocabulary — **MISSING (structural gap)**, function covered by [Adj+人]

*Note on normalization of multi-word expressions:* The verification/revelation operators (揭露真相, 核实情况, 辨别真伪) behave as **verb + object** units where the verb is the semantic operator carrier (transitive lexical verbs, not light verbs). The stance frames 事实上 / 准确地说 (P4a #8) behave **adverbially** (connective/hedge), not predicatively. 真相大白 behaves as a **clausal idiom** (subject + stative-resultative). 像…一样 / 与…相似 (P4a #10) behave as **comparative adverbial/predicative** frames. No genuine light-verb carrier (e.g., 使/做) is attested in this domain; the operators are carried by full lexical verbs.
