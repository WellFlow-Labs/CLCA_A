# Language: Georgian (ka)

## CONSTRUCTION NATURALNESS & USAGE ANALYSIS

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness Rating | Usage Notes |
|------------------|---------|-----------------|-------------|-------------------|-------------|
| **1. ATTRIBUTION** | ეს მართალია | es mart'alia | this is true/correct | [CORE] | Standard predicative construction used in all registers. The incorporated copula form (adjective + -ა) is the unmarked present tense pattern for statements of fact, evaluation, and description. |
| | ინფორმაცია სანდოა | inpormatsia sandoa | the information is reliable | [CORE] | |
| **Alternate:** | ეს არის მართალი | es aris mart'ali | this is true | [CORE] | Analytic form with separate copula არის (aris) is equally natural, may carry slight emphasis on the copula or be preferred in formal/careful speech. |
| **2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION** | ეს არ არის მართალი | es ar aris mart'ali | this is not true/correct | [CORE] | Standard negation pattern obligatory for predicative negation. The particle არ (ar) must precede the copula; no alternative word order possible. Used across all registers and contexts. |
| | ინფორმაცია არ არის სანდო | inpormatsia ar aris sando | the information is not reliable | [CORE] | |
| **3. ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION** | ნამდვილი მეგობარი | namdvili megobari | true friend | [CORE] | Unmarked attributive construction with adjective preceding noun. Used freely in all contexts with no restrictions. The invariant form applies regardless of case or number of the head noun. |
| | სანდო წყარო | sando ts'q'aro | reliable source | [CORE] | |
| **4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE** | ეს მართალი გახდა | es mart'ali gakhda | this became true/turned out to be true | [CORE] | Common construction for change of state or realization. The verb გახდომა (gakhdoma) is the standard inchoative verb, used when something comes to be recognized as true or when a state changes. |
| | ინფორმაცია საეჭვო გახდა | inpormatsia saech'vo gakhda | the information became doubtful | [CORE] | |
| **Alternate:** | ეს მართალი ხდება | es mart'ali khdeba | this becomes true | [CORE] | The verb ხდება (khdeba) is equally natural, may be preferred for ongoing or habitual change. Both verbs are fully productive with truth-domain adjectives. |
| **5. CAUSATIVE** | გაამართლა | gamart'la | justified, vindicated | [MARGINAL] | Causative morphology with truth-adjectives is restricted. Direct causatives are rare; speakers prefer specific lexical verbs for causation in the truth domain (proving, showing, revealing). |
| | დაამტკიცა | daamt'k'itsa | proved, confirmed | [CORE] | This is actually a lexical verb "to prove," not a morphological causative of a truth-adjective. Such specific verbs are the natural way to express causation of truth-states. |
| **6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION** | სიმართლე | simart'le | truth | [CORE] | Highly productive pattern სი-...-ე for quality nouns from native roots. Used freely across all registers. This is the standard way to create abstract nouns denoting qualities or states. |
| | სანდოობა | sandooba | reliability | [CORE] | The -ობა suffix is equally productive, especially with adjectives of foreign origin or technical terms. Both patterns are fully natural. |
| **Alternate:** | სიზუსტე | sizuste | precision | [CORE] | Additional examples show the სი-...-ე pattern applies broadly to truth-domain concepts (accuracy, correctness, etc.). |
| **7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION** | მატყუარა | mat'q'uara | liar | [CORE] | The მა-...-არა pattern is productive and natural for habitual or characteristic agents, especially those with negative connotations. Common in everyday speech and literature. |
| | დამაჯერებელი | damajerebeli | convincing one | [MARGINAL] | The -ელი suffix creates agent nouns but this example functions more as a participle/adjective "convincing" than as a true agent noun "one who convinces." Usage as pure agent noun is less common. |
| **Alternate:** | მაყალბებელი | maq'albebeli | forger | [CORE] | The მა-...-ელი pattern (combining prefix and suffix) is productive for agents of deceptive actions. |
| **8. EVIDENTIAL/EPISTEMIC MARKING** | მართალი თურმე იყო | mart'ali turme iq'o | apparently it was true | [CORE] | The particle თურმე (turme) is common in narrative and conversational contexts to mark reportative or mirative information (surprise at learning something). Used freely across registers. |
| | მართალი ხომ არის | mart'ali khom aris | it is true, you know | [CORE] | The particle ხომ (khom) is extremely common in conversation for seeking confirmation or presenting shared knowledge. Natural in interactive contexts. |
| **Alternate:** | მართალი მეთქი იყო | mart'ali metki iq'o | allegedly it was true | [CORE] | The particle მეთქი (metki) marks hearsay or distanced information, common in both speech and writing. |
| **9. DEGREE MODIFICATION** | ძალიან მართალი | dzalian mart'ali | very true/correct | [CORE] | Standard intensification pattern with adverbial modifier preceding adjective. Used naturally across all contexts. The intensifier ძალიან (dzalian) is the most common general intensifier. |
| | უფრო ზუსტი | upro zusti | more precise | [CORE] | The comparative particle უფრო (upro) is the standard and only way to form comparatives. Fully productive and natural with all gradable adjectives. |
| **Alternate:** | ყველაზე ზუსტი | q'velaze zusti | most precise | [CORE] | Superlative with ყველაზე (q'velaze) is equally standard and productive. |
| **10. CONTRASTIVE/CORRECTIVE** | არა მცდარია, არამედ მართალია | ara mts'daria, aramed mart'alia | it's not false, but true | [CORE] | Standard contrastive construction used for explicit correction or contrast. Common in argumentative discourse, explanations, and clarifications. The არა...არამედ frame is fixed. |
| | არა ყალბი, არამედ ნამდვილი | ara q'albi, aramed namdvili | not fake, but genuine | [CORE] | |
| **Alternate:** | კი არა მცდარია, არამედ მართალია | ki ara mts'daria, aramed mart'alia | it's not false, but true | [CORE] | The emphatic particle კი (ki) adds emphasis to the negation, equally natural in contrastive contexts. |
| **11. METALINGUISTIC TRUTH PREDICATES** | სიმართლეს ამბობს | simart'les ambobs | tells the truth | [CORE] | Fixed expression with dative case on truth noun. This is the standard idiomatic way to express truth-telling. Used across all registers and contexts. |
| | ტყუილს ამბობს | t'q'uils ambobs | tells lies | [CORE] | Parallel construction for lying, equally fixed and natural. |
| **Alternate:** | სიმართლე რომ ვთქვათ | simart'le rom vt'q'vat | to tell the truth | [CORE] | Fixed discourse marker using subjunctive mood, extremely common in conversation and narrative for introducing honest statements or corrections. |
| **12. APPEARANCE/SEEMING** | მართალი ჩანს | mart'ali ch'ans | it seems true/appears correct | [CORE] | Standard construction for appearance or seeming. The verb ჩანს (ch'ans) is the unmarked way to express how things appear. Common in all registers. |
| | მოჩვენებითი სიმართლე | moch'venebiti simart'le | apparent truth | [CORE] | Attributive construction with adjective meaning "apparent, seeming." Natural in formal and analytical contexts for distinguishing appearance from reality. |
| **Alternate:** | მე მართალი მეჩვენება | me mart'ali mech'veneba | it seems true to me | [CORE] | Construction with dative experiencer makes the subjective perspective explicit. Equally natural, perhaps preferred when emphasizing personal perception. |
| **13. VERIFICATION/CONFIRMATION** | დაამტკიცა სიმართლე | daamt'k'itsa simart'le | proved the truth | [CORE] | Standard construction with verification verb taking truth noun as direct object. This is the natural way to express proving or confirming in Georgian. |
| | გადაამოწმა ინფორმაცია | gadaamots'ma inpormatsia | verified the information | [CORE] | Parallel construction with different verification verb, equally natural for checking or verifying information. |
| **Alternate:** | დაადასტურა | dadast'ura | confirmed | [CORE] | Alternative verification verb, part of a set of lexical verbs for different aspects of verification (proving, checking, confirming, revealing). |
| **14. DOUBT/QUESTIONING** | ეჭვი მაქვს | ech'vi makvs | I have doubt / I doubt | [CORE] | Standard possessive construction for expressing doubt with dative experiencer. This is the unmarked way to express doubt in Georgian, used across all contexts. |
| | საეჭვო ინფორმაციაა | saech'vo inpormatsiaa | it is doubtful information | [CORE] | Predicative construction with adjective "doubtful." Natural for characterizing information or claims as questionable. |
| **Alternate:** | ეჭვი შემეპარა | ech'vi shemep'ara | doubt seized me | [MARGINAL] | More literary or emphatic expression for sudden onset of doubt. Less common in everyday speech but natural in narrative contexts. |
| **15. CONCESSIVE** | მართალია, მაგრამ არასრული | mart'alia, magram arasruli | it's true, but incomplete | [CORE] | Standard concessive construction acknowledging truth while introducing contrast. Common in argumentative and explanatory discourse. The comma and conjunction მაგრამ (magram) are fixed elements. |
| | სწორია, თუმცა არაზუსტი | sts'oria, tumtsa arazusti | it's correct, however imprecise | [CORE] | Alternative with conjunction თუმცა (tumtsa) "however," equally natural and perhaps slightly more formal. |
| **Alternate:** | მართალია, მაინც... | mart'alia, maints... | it's true, still... | [CORE] | The particle მაინც (maints) "still, nevertheless" provides another natural concessive option, common in conversation. |

## SUMMARY OBSERVATIONS

### High-Frequency Core Patterns:
- Attribution with incorporated copula (predicative adjectives)
- Negation with არ (ar) + copula
- Invariant attributive adjectives
- Abstract nominalization with სი-...-ე and -ობა
- Epistemic particles (თურმე, ხომ)
- Metalinguistic expressions with dative case
- Verification through lexical verbs

### Marginal or Restricted Patterns:
- Direct morphological causatives of truth-adjectives (speakers prefer lexical verbs)
- Some agentive nominalizations function more as participles than pure agent nouns
- Literary expressions for doubt (ეჭვი შემეპარა)

### No Uncertain Patterns:
All constructions in P4a are attested and have clear usage patterns. The data shows robust evidence for each construction type.
