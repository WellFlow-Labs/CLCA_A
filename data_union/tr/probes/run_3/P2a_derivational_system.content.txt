# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2a Derivational System
# Language: Turkish (tr)

---

## Preliminary Note on Scope

This step documents attested derivational morphology for the 30 lexical items identified in P1. The P1b root expansion has already established derivational families for the three central roots (*doğr-*, *gerçek-*, *hak-/hakk-*). This step therefore focuses on:

1. Derivations from P1 items not already covered in P1b
2. Any derivations from P1b items that extend the system further and are independently attested
3. Explicit notation of gaps where derivation does not occur or is not attested

Forms already fully documented in P1b are cross-referenced rather than repeated in full.

## Item 1 — Base Form: *doğru*

*Core derivational family documented in P1b (ROOT 1, items 1–30). The following summarizes and supplements.*

**Derived Form 1**
- **Base Form:** doğru
- **Derived Form:** doğruluk
- **Transliteration:** doğruluk
- **Literal Breakdown:** *doğru* + *-luk* (abstract noun suffix)
- **Natural English translation:** truthfulness, accuracy, correctness, integrity
- **Usage:** Names the property of being correct or truthful in both epistemic and moral contexts. *Doğruluk her zaman ödüllendirilmez.* ("Truthfulness is not always rewarded.")
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]
- **Cross-reference:** P1b ROOT 1, item 2

---

**Derived Form 2**
- **Base Form:** doğru
- **Derived Form:** doğrulamak
- **Transliteration:** doğrulamak
- **Literal Breakdown:** *doğru* + *-la-* (denominative verb suffix) + *-mak* (infinitive)
- **Natural English translation:** to verify, to confirm, to validate
- **Usage:** Used in journalistic, legal, and technical registers for the act of establishing the truth or validity of a claim or document.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]
- **Cross-reference:** P1b ROOT 1, item 3

---

**Derived Form 3**
- **Base Form:** doğru
- **Derived Form:** doğrulanmak
- **Transliteration:** doğrulanmak
- **Literal Breakdown:** *doğru* + *-la-* + *-n-* (passive) + *-mak*
- **Natural English translation:** to be verified, to be confirmed
- **Usage:** Passive counterpart of *doğrulamak*; used when the claim or document is the grammatical subject. *İddia henüz doğrulanmadı.* ("The claim has not yet been confirmed.")
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]
- **Cross-reference:** P1b ROOT 1, item 4

---

**Derived Form 4**
- **Base Form:** doğru
- **Derived Form:** doğrulama
- **Transliteration:** doğrulama
- **Literal Breakdown:** *doğrula-* + *-ma* (verbal noun suffix)
- **Natural English translation:** verification, confirmation, validation
- **Usage:** Names the act or process of verifying; standard in legal, technical, and journalistic contexts. *Kimlik doğrulama* ("identity verification") is a fixed technical term.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]
- **Cross-reference:** P1b ROOT 1, item 14

- **Usage:** Describes a person, mechanism, or process that performs verification. *Doğrulayıcı kod* ("verification code") is standard in digital contexts.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]
- **Cross-reference:** P1b ROOT 1, item 13

---

**Derived Form 6**

- **Natural English translation:** to straighten, to correct, to aim, to direct
- **Usage:** Used in physical contexts (straightening an object) and figurative contexts (correcting a course of action or aiming an instrument).
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 7**

---

**Derived Form 2**
- **Base Form:** gerçek

- **Natural English translation:** really, truly, genuinely, indeed

- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 3**
- **Base Form:** gerçek
- **Derived Form:** gerçekleşmek

## Item 3 — Base Form: *hakikat*

**Derived Form 1**
- **Base Form:** hakikat
- **Derived Form:** hakikaten
- **Transliteration:** hakikaten
- **Literal Breakdown:** *hakikat* + *-en* (adverbial suffix, from Arabic *-an*)
- **Natural English translation:** truly, in truth, indeed, really
- **Usage:** Formal or literary adverb asserting that something is genuinely the case. More solemn than *gerçekten*. *Hakikaten öyle mi?* ("Is that truly so?")
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]
- **Cross-reference:** P1b ROOT 3, item 8

---

**Derived Form 2**
- **Base Form:** hakikat
- **Derived Form:** hakikatperest
- **Transliteration:** hakikatperest
- **Literal Breakdown:** *hakikat* (truth) + Persian *-perest* (worshipper, devotee)
- **Natural English translation:** truth-seeker, one devoted to truth
- **Usage:** A literary and elevated term for a person devoted to seeking truth. Appears in philosophical and literary prose; not common in everyday speech.
- **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**Note on further derivation from *hakikat*:** No productive Turkish-internal derivational paradigm beyond *hakikaten* and the compound *hakikatperest* is attested for this item. The form *hakiki* is a separate Arabic-origin adjective (see Item 19), not a Turkish derivation from *hakikat*.

## Item 6 — Base Form: *yanlış*

**Derived Form 1**
- **Base Form:** yanlış
- **Derived Form:** yanlışlık
- **Transliteration:** yanlışlık
- **Literal Breakdown:** *yanlış* + *-lık* (abstract noun suffix)
- **Natural English translation:** error, mistake, wrongness, incorrectness
- **Usage:** Names the property or instance of being wrong or incorrect. Used in both epistemic contexts (an error in reasoning or fact) and moral contexts (a wrong action). *Bu bir yanlışlık.* ("This is a mistake.")
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2**
- **Base Form:** yanlış
- **Derived Form:** yanlışlamak
- **Transliteration:** yanlışlamak
- **Literal Breakdown:** *yanlış* + *-la-* (denominative verb suffix) + *-mak*
- **Natural English translation:** to falsify, to show as wrong, to refute
- **Usage:** Used in logical, scientific, and critical contexts to describe the act of demonstrating that a claim is incorrect. *Hipotezi yanlışlamak* ("to falsify the hypothesis") reflects influence from Popperian philosophy of science.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 3**
- **Base Form:** yanlış
- **Derived Form:** yanlışlanmak
- **Transliteration:** yanlışlanmak
- **Literal Breakdown:** *yanlış* + *-la-* + *-n-* (passive) + *-mak*
- **Natural English translation:** to be falsified, to be shown as wrong
- **Usage:** Passive counterpart of *yanlışlamak*; used in scientific and philosophical discourse when the claim being falsified is the grammatical subject. *Bu teori yanlışlanamaz.* ("This theory cannot be falsified.")
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 4**
- **Base Form:** yanlış
- **Derived Form:** yanlışlama
- **Transliteration:** yanlışlama
- **Literal Breakdown:** *yanlışla-* + *-ma* (verbal noun suffix)
- **Natural English translation:** falsification, refutation
- **Usage:** Names the act or process of falsifying a claim; used in philosophy of science and critical discourse. *Yanlışlama ilkesi* ("the principle of falsification") is a standard term in Turkish philosophy of science.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 5**
- **Base Form:** yanlış
- **Derived Form:** yanlışlıkla
- **Transliteration:** yanlışlıkla
- **Literal Breakdown:** *yanlışlık* + *-la* (instrumental suffix, here lexicalized as adverb)
- **Natural English translation:** by mistake, accidentally, erroneously
- **Usage:** High-frequency adverb used to indicate that an action was performed in error rather than intentionally. *Yanlışlıkla sildim.* ("I deleted it by mistake.")
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Note on further derivation from *yanlış*:** The form *yanlışça* (erroneously, in a mistaken manner) is occasionally attested but less frequent than *yanlışlıkla*. No agentive form (*yanlışlayıcı* or similar) is in common use.

## Item 12 — Base Form: *doğruluk*

**Note:** *doğruluk* is itself a derivation from *doğru* (see Item 1 above and P1b ROOT 1). The following compound expressions are attested with *doğruluk* as a base element, though these are phrasal rather than strictly morphological.

**Derived Form 1**
- **Base Form:** doğruluk
- **Derived Form:** doğruluk payı
- **Transliteration:** doğruluk payı
