# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P4b Construction Usage & Constraints
# Language: Greek (el)

---

## CONSTRUCTION USAGE ANALYSIS

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Epistemic Status | Usage Notes |
|------------------|---------|-----------------|-------------|------------------|-------------|
| **1. ATTRIBUTION** | Αυτό είναι αλήθεια | Aftó íne alítheia | This is truth/true | [CORE] | Extremely common across all registers. Nouns (αλήθεια, ψέμα) appear predicatively without article in standard usage. Adjectives require full agreement. |
| | Είναι ψέμα | Íne pséma | It is a lie/false | [CORE] | High-frequency construction for denying truth claims. Subject often omitted (pro-drop). |
| | Η ιστορία είναι αληθινή | I istoría íne alithiní | The story is true | [CORE] | Standard pattern with full NP subjects. Adjective agreement obligatory. |
| | Το έγγραφο είναι πλαστό | To éngrafo íne plastó | The document is forged | [CORE] | Common in legal/administrative contexts. πλαστό specifically for forgery/counterfeiting. |
| **2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION** | Δεν είναι αλήθεια | Den íne alítheia | It is not true/truth | [CORE] | Standard negation pattern. δεν obligatory before copula. No morphological negative forms exist. |
| | Δεν είναι σωστό | Den íne sostó | It is not correct | [CORE] | Common in evaluative contexts (answers, procedures, behavior). |
| | Αυτό δεν είναι ψέμα | Aftó den íne pséma | This is not a lie | [CORE] | Defensive construction, often used to insist on truthfulness. |
| **3. ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION** | αληθινή ιστορία | alithiní istoría | true story | [CORE] | Prenominal position standard for truth-domain adjectives. Postnominal possible for emphasis but less common. |
| | ψευδής μαρτυρία | psevdís martyría | false testimony | [CORE] | Legal register. ψευδής (learned form) preferred over ψεύτικος in formal contexts. |
| | σωστή απάντηση | sostí apántisi | correct answer | [CORE] | Extremely common in educational contexts. σωστός most frequent correctness adjective. |
| | πλαστά έγγραφα | plastá éngrafa | forged documents | [CORE] | Legal/administrative. πλαστός specifically for fraudulent documents, not general falsehood. |
| **4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE** | Η αλήθεια γίνεται γνωστή | I alítheia gínete gností | The truth becomes known | [MARGINAL] | Less common than revelation constructions (βγαίνει στη φόρα). Used when emphasizing gradual process. |
| | Το ψέμα γίνεται φανερό | To pséma gínete faneró | The lie becomes apparent | [MARGINAL] | Formal register. More common alternatives exist (αποκαλύπτεται "is revealed"). |
| **5. CAUSATIVE** | Αυτό με κάνει βέβαιο | Aftó me káni vévaio | This makes me certain | [CORE] | Common with psychological adjectives (βέβαιος, σίγουρος). Object clitic (με) obligatory. |
| | Διορθώνω το λάθος | Diorthóno to láthos | I correct the mistake | [CORE] | Standard correction verb. Common in educational and editorial contexts. |
| | Ακυρώνω το έγγραφο | Akyróno to éngrafo | I invalidate the document | [CORE] | Administrative/legal register. Marks institutional authority to cancel validity. |
| | Βεβαιώνω την πληροφορία | Vevaióno tin pliροforía | I confirm the information | [CORE] | Formal/administrative. Used for official confirmation or certification. |
| **6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION** | αληθινότητα | alithinótita | truthfulness, genuineness | [CORE] | Productive pattern. -ότητα suffix standard with -ός adjectives. Common in philosophical/abstract discourse. |
| | ειλικρίνεια | eilikríneia | sincerity | [CORE] | High-frequency in interpersonal contexts. -εια suffix with -ής adjectives. |
| | ακρίβεια | akríveia | accuracy, precision | [CORE] | Technical and everyday use. Emphasizes exactness rather than truth per se. |
| | βεβαιότητα | vevaiótita | certainty | [CORE] | Epistemic vocabulary. Common in philosophical and everyday contexts. |
| | δικαιοσύνη | dikaiosýni | justice | [CORE] | -οσύνη suffix for moral/character qualities. Fundamental moral concept. |
| **7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION** | ψεύτης | pséftis | liar (m.) | [CORE] | Highly productive for negative behaviors. Strong pejorative force. Gender-marked forms obligatory. |
| | ψεύτρα | pséftra | liar (f.) | [CORE] | Feminine form. Equally pejorative. -τρια suffix less common than -τρα for this root. |
| | υποκριτής | ipokritís | hypocrite | [CORE] | Strong negative evaluation. Common in moral/political discourse. |
| | απατεώνας | apateónas | swindler, fraud | [CORE] | Legal and colloquial. Marks criminal deception. -ώνας suffix for professional/habitual agents. |
| **8. LIGHT VERB (TRUTH/LIE)** | λέω την αλήθεια | léo tin alítheia | tell the truth | [CORE] | Unmarked construction for truth-telling. Extremely high-frequency across all registers. |
| | λέω ψέματα | léo psémata | tell lies | [CORE] | Note obligatory plural ψέματα in this construction. Singular possible but less common. |
| | λέω το ψέμα | léo to pséma | tell the lie | [CORE] | Singular with definite article for specific lie. Less frequent than plural form. |
| **9. LIGHT VERB (ERROR)** | κάνω λάθος | káno láthos | make a mistake, be wrong | [CORE] | Extremely high-frequency. Unmarked expression for error-making. No article on λάθος. |
| | κάνω μεγάλο λάθος | káno megálo láthos | make a big mistake | [CORE] | Adjective modification common for emphasis. Article still absent. |
| | έκανα λάθος | ékana láthos | I made a mistake | [CORE] | Past tense extremely common in admissions/apologies. |
| **10. PREPOSITIONAL PHRASES** | στην αλήθεια | stin alítheia | in truth, actually | [CORE] | Grammaticalized as discourse marker. Very high-frequency in spoken Greek. |
| | κατά την αλήθεια | katá tin alítheia | according to truth, truthfully | [CORE] | More formal than στην αλήθεια. Emphasizes veracity. |
| | κατά λάθος | katá láthos | by mistake, accidentally | [CORE] | Extremely common manner adverbial. Fully grammaticalized. |
| | εκ λάθους | ek láthous | by mistake | [MARGINAL] | Archaic/formal register. Genitive case. Legal and administrative writing only. |
| **11. REVELATION/DISCOVERY** | βγαίνει η αλήθεια στη φόρα | vgaíni i alítheia sti fóra | the truth comes out/to light | [CORE] | Highly idiomatic. στη φόρα (literally "to surface") obligatory in this construction. |
| | φέρνω την αλήθεια στο φως | férno tin alítheia sto fos | bring the truth to light | [CORE] | Metaphorical construction. φως "light" common target domain for truth revelation. |
| | αποκαλύπτω την αλήθεια | apokalýpto tin alítheia | reveal the truth | [CORE] | Standard verb for revelation. Common in investigative and narrative contexts. |
| **12. DETECTION/EXPOSURE** | πιάνω κάποιον στο ψέμα | piáno kápion sto pséma | catch someone in a lie | [CORE] | Highly idiomatic. στο ψέμα obligatory. Common in narrative and confrontational contexts. |
| | ξεσκεπάζω το ψέμα | xeskepázo to pséma | expose/uncover the lie | [CORE] | Metaphorical "uncovering." Common in investigative journalism and political discourse. |
| **13. INTENSIFICATION** | η γυμνή αλήθεια | i gymní alítheia | the naked truth | [CORE] | Fixed collocation. γυμνή emphasizes bareness/directness. Common in emphatic statements. |
| | η πικρή αλήθεια | i pikrí alítheia | the bitter truth | [CORE] | Fixed collocation. Emphasizes unpleasantness of truth. High-frequency. |
| | η σκληρή αλήθεια | i sklirí alítheia | the harsh truth | [CORE] | Similar to πικρή but emphasizes harshness. Common collocation. |
| | άσπρο ψέμα | áspro pséma | white lie | [CORE] | Calque from English/French. Marks harmless/socially acceptable lies. |
| | κοτσάνα ψέματα | kotsána psémata | whopping lies | [CORE] | Colloquial intensifier. κοτσάνα literally "big/thick." Informal register only. |
| **14. PROVERBIAL/GNOMIC** | το ψέμα έχει κοντά ποδάρια | to pséma échi kontá podária | lies have short legs | [CORE] | Fixed proverb. Means lies are eventually discovered. High cultural salience. |
| | η αλήθεια πονάει | i alítheia ponáei | the truth hurts | [CORE] | Common saying. Acknowledges painful nature of truth. Used in consolation/warning. |
| | η αλήθεια θα σε ελευθερώσει | i alítheia tha se eleftherósi | the truth will set you free | [CORE] | Biblical origin (John 8:32). Used in moral/philosophical discourse. |
| **15. COMPOUND (ψευδ-)** | ψευδοεπιστήμη | psevdoepistími | pseudoscience | [CORE] | Highly productive pattern. ψευδ- marks false claim to category. Common in academic/critical discourse. |
| | ψευδοδημοκρατία | psevdodimokratía | pseudo-democracy | [CORE] | Political discourse. Marks illegitimate claim to democratic status. |
| | ψευδοδιανοούμενος | psevdodianoúmenos | pseudo-intellectual | [CORE] | Pejorative. Common in cultural/political criticism. |
| | ψευδοπληροφορία | psevdopliροforía | misinformation | [CORE] | Contemporary term. Increasingly common in media/digital contexts. |
| **16. COMPOUND (ορθ-)** | ορθογραφία | orthografía | orthography, correct spelling | [CORE] | Standard linguistic term. Everyday and technical use. |
| | ορθοδοξία | orthodoxía | orthodoxy | [CORE] | Religious and general use. Marks correct belief/practice. |
| | ορθολογισμός | orthologismós | rationalism | [CORE] | Philosophical term. ορθ- marks correct reasoning. |
| | ορθοπεδική | orthopedikí | orthopedics | [CORE] | Medical term. ορθ- marks correction/straightening. |
| **17. DISCOURSE MARKERS** | αλήθεια; | alítheia? | really? is that so? | [CORE] | Fully grammaticalized question particle. Expresses surprise or requests confirmation. Very high-frequency in spoken Greek. |
| | πραγματικά | pragmatiká | really, actually | [CORE] | Epistemic marker. Confirms or emphasizes truth. Extremely common in all spoken registers. |
| | ουσιαστικά | ousiastiká | basically, essentially | [CORE] | Reformulation marker. Introduces essential point. High-frequency in explanatory discourse. |
| | ακριβώς | akrivós | exactly | [CORE] | Agreement/confirmation marker. Extremely common in conversation. |
| **18. CORRECTION** | διορθώνω το λάθος | diorthóno to láthos | I correct the mistake | [CORE] | Standard correction verb. Common in educational and editorial contexts. |
| | σωστεύω την απάντηση | sostévo tin apántisi | I correct the answer | [CORE] | Educational contexts. Less formal than διορθώνω. |
| | διόρθωση λαθών | diórthosi lathón | correction of errors | [CORE] | Nominal form. Common in editorial notes and educational materials. Genitive plural λαθών standard. |
| **19. VALIDATION/INVALIDATION** | βεβαιώνω την πληροφορία | vevaióno tin pliροforía | I confirm the information | [CORE] | Administrative/formal register. Marks official confirmation. |
| | ακυρώνω το έγγραφο | akyróno to éngrafo | I invalidate/cancel the document | [CORE] | Legal/administrative. Marks institutional authority to cancel. |
| | βεβαίωση γνησιότητας | vevaíosi gnisiótitas | certificate of authenticity | [CORE] | Official documents. Genitive γνησιότητας standard in this collocation. |
| **20. ADMISSION/ACKNOWLEDGMENT** | παραδέχομαι το λάθος μου | paradéchome to láthos mou | I admit my mistake | [CORE] | Common in apologies and conflict resolution. Possessive μου typical. |
| | ομολογώ την αλήθεια | omologó tin alítheia | I confess the truth | [CORE] | More formal/solemn than παραδέχομαι. Religious and legal connotations. |
| | παραδέχομαι ότι είχα άδικο | paradéchome óti ícha ádiko | I admit that I was wrong | [CORE] | Complement clause with ότι common. είχα άδικο "I was wrong" (literally "had injustice"). |

## ALTERNATE CONSTRUCTIONS ATTESTED

### For Truth-Telling (Construction 8):
- **αληθεύω** (alithévo) — "tell the truth" (less common than λέω την αλήθεια, more formal)
- **λέω τ' αληθινά** (léo t' alithiná) — "tell the true things" (colloquial variant)

### For Lying (Construction 8):
- **ψεύδομαι** (pséfdome) — "lie" (more formal/literary than λέω ψέματα)
- **λέω ψέμματα** (léo psémata) — dialectal/colloquial variant of ψέματα

### For Error (Construction 9):
- **έχω λάθος** (écho láthos) — "be wrong" (stative, contrasts with κάνω λάθος "make mistake")
- **κάνω σφάλμα** (káno sfálma) — "make an error" (formal, technical register)

### For "By Mistake" (Construction 10):
- **από λάθος** (apó láthos) — colloquial variant of κατά λάθος
- **λαθεμένα** (latheména) — adverbial "mistakenly" (less common)

### For Revelation (Construction 11):
- **έρχεται στο φως** (érchete sto fos) — "comes to light" (alternative to βγαίνει)
- **αποκαλύπτεται** (apokalýptete) — passive "is revealed" (more formal)

## USAGE RESTRICTIONS OBSERVED

1. **Register distinctions:**
   - εκ λάθους (formal/archaic) vs. κατά λάθος (standard) vs. από λάθος (colloquial)
   - ψεύδομαι (formal) vs. λέω ψέματα (standard)
   - διορθώνω (formal) vs. σωστεύω (colloquial)

2. **Number restrictions:**
   - ψέματα obligatorily plural in λέω ψέματα construction
   - λάθος typically singular in κάνω λάθος (plural possible but less common)

3. **Article patterns:**
   - No article in κάνω λάθος, κατά λάθος
   - Article required in λέω την αλήθεια
   - Variable in predicative constructions (είναι αλήθεια vs. είναι η αλήθεια)

4. **Domain restrictions:**
   - πλαστός specifically for forgery/counterfeiting, not general falsehood
   - ψευδής preferred in legal contexts over ψεύτικος
   - βεβαιώνω/ακυρώνω restricted to administrative/legal domains

5. **Metaphorical constraints:**
   - Truth revelation uses φως "light" and φόρα "surface"
   - Lie detection uses covering metaphors (ξεσκεπάζω) and catching (πιάνω)
   - No mixing of metaphorical domains observed
