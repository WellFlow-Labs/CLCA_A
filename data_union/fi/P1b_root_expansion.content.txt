# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P1b ROOT EXPANSION
# Language: Finnish (fi)

---

## Step 1 — Identify Central Roots

---

**Root 1: tosi / tode- / tod- / tot-**

- **Root form:** tosi (base form); tod- (before vowel-initial suffixes, e.g., todistaa, todistus); tot- (before -uus, giving totuus); tode- (oblique/derived stem)
- **P1 items participating:** tosi (1), totuus (2), todellinen (6), todellisuus (7), todistaa (16/21), todiste (19/22), todistus (17), totuudenmukaisuus (27), epätosi, todenmukainen — **multiple items**
- **Core meaning contribution:** Propositional truth; correspondence between a claim or representation and reality; the factual or actual state of affairs. This root supplies the primary epistemic vocabulary for truth as a property of statements and states of the world. Also anchors the domain of factual reality, evidentiary certification, and verification.

---

**Root 2: oikea / oikeu- / oikeel-**

- **Root form:** oikea (base adjective); oikeu- (before -s, giving oikeus; before -den, giving oikeuden-); oikeel- (before -lisuus, giving oikeellisuus)
- **P1 items participating:** oikea (4/5), oikeellisuus (5/6), oikeudenmukaisuus (26/28) — **3 items**
- **Core meaning contribution:** Normative and identificatory rightness; correctness relative to a standard, norm, or proper identity; justice and entitlement. This root covers both evaluative correctness and the domain of rights and legal order. Connects epistemic correctness to normative and legal evaluation.

---

**Root 3: väärä / vääre- / väären-**

- **Root form:** väärä (base adjective); väärent- (causative stem, väärentää); väären- (result nominal stem, väärennös)
- **P1 items participating:** väärä (13), väärentää (14/15), väärentäminen (15), väärennös (16/22), vääristely — **multiple items**
- **Core meaning contribution:** Falsity, wrongness, and deliberate falsification; the antonym cluster covering incorrect, false, crooked, and fraudulent. This root anchors the negative pole of the truth-evaluation domain. Covers epistemic falsity, moral wrongness, physical crookedness, and fraudulent production.

---

*Note: The roots tosi/tode- and oikea/oikeu- are both highly productive. The root väärä/vääre- is moderately productive. All three are expanded below. The tosi/tode- root is the most productive of the three and yields more than 30 attested forms; the oikea/oikeus- root is extremely productive particularly in the domain of law and legal institutions.*

## Step 2 — Expand Each Root

---

### ROOT 1: tosi / tode- / tod- / tot-

---

**1.**
- **Form:** tosi
- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Morpheme breakdown:** tosi (base adjective/adverb: true, real)
- **English translation:** true; real; really (intensifier)
- **Context of use:** Used predicatively (*se on tosi* — "it is true") and as a colloquial intensifier in colloquial speech (*tosi hyvä* — "really good"; *tosi paljon* — "really a lot"). The base form from which the entire cluster derives.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**2.**
- **Form:** totuus
- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Morpheme breakdown:** tot- + -uus (abstract noun suffix)
- **English translation:** truth
- **Context of use:** The standard nominal for truth as an abstract property or a specific true state of affairs. Used in philosophical, legal, and everyday discourse.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**3.**
- **Form:** totuudenmukaisuus
- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Morpheme breakdown:** totuuden (genitive of totuus) + mukainen (in accordance with) + -uus
- **English translation:** truthfulness; accuracy; fidelity to truth; conformity to truth; veracity
- **Context of use:** Used in journalistic, legal, and academic contexts to evaluate whether a representation, account, or statement faithfully corresponds to the facts (*uutisoinnin totuudenmukaisuus* — "the truthfulness of the reporting").
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**4.**
- **Form:** totuudenmukainen
- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Morpheme breakdown:** totuuden (genitive of totuus) + mukainen (in accordance with)
- **English translation:** truthful; accurate; faithful to the truth; conforming to truth
- **Context of use:** Applied to accounts, descriptions, and representations to assert that they correspond to the facts (*totuudenmukainen kuvaus* — "a truthful/accurate description"; *totuudenmukainen kertomus* — "a truthful account"). Used in legal testimony evaluation and journalistic standards.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**5.**
- **Form:** totuudenvastaisuus
- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Morpheme breakdown:** totuuden (genitive of totuus) + vastainen (contrary to) + -uus
- **English translation:** untruthfulness; falsity; contrariness to truth
- **Context of use:** Used in legal and formal contexts to characterize statements or representations that contradict the truth (*todistajan totuudenvastaisuus* — "the witness's untruthfulness"; *lausuman totuudenvastaisuus* — "the untruthfulness of the statement"). More formal than *valheellisuus*.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**6.**

- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Morpheme breakdown:** totuuden (genitive of totuus) + vastainen (contrary to)
- **English translation:** contrary to truth; untruthful; false
- **Context of use:** Applied to statements, claims, and representations in legal and formal discourse to assert that they contradict the truth (*totuudenvastainen väite* — "a claim contrary to truth"; "a statement contrary to truth; a false claim").
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**7.**

- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Morpheme breakdown:** totuus + järjestelmä (system)
- **English translation:** truth system; regime of truth
- **Context of use:** Used in philosophical and social-theoretical discourse (particularly in discussions of Foucault and epistemology) to refer to the set of norms and institutions that determine what counts as true in a given context.
- **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

- **Transliteration:** N/A

- **English translation:** truth value
- **Context of use:** Technical term in logic and formal semantics referring to the truth or falsity of a proposition (*lauseen totuusarvo* — "the truth value of a sentence"). Used in logic, mathematics, and linguistics. Standard in Finnish logic textbooks.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

- **Context of use:** Used in philosophical and political discourse, including in discussions of parrhesia and frank speech, to refer to the practice of speaking truth (*totuuspuhe vaatii rohkeutta* — "truth-telling requires courage"). Also used more broadly for honest public discourse.
