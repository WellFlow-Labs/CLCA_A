## 1. Base Form: **totta**

### Construction 1

2. Example: **Se on totta.**
3. Transliteration: —
4. English translation: “That is true.”
5. Usage notes: This is the basic predicative construction for affirming the truth of a statement or proposition. It is extremely common in both speech and writing.

### Construction 4

### Construction 5
