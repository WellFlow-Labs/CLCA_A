# CLCA Protocol v3.3 — P6 CHANGE, CAUSATION, AND STABILITY
# Language: Yoruba (yo)

---

## Preliminary Note

This step documents how Yoruba encodes dynamic meanings — becoming, causing, and maintaining or losing truth-related states — using vocabulary established in P1 through P5. The analysis draws exclusively on attested constructions and vocabulary from the CONTEXT. Where a dynamic expression is not attested for a given term, this is stated explicitly. Where the same construction type applies across multiple terms, this is noted rather than repeated in full for each.

Three general observations from the prior data are relevant here:

1. Yoruba has no dedicated inchoative or causative morpheme in this domain. Change-of-state and causation are expressed through lexical verbs, serial verb constructions, and periphrastic structures (P4a, Construction 4 and 5).
2. The most productive change-of-state verb in the epistemic domain is *farahàn* (to become visible, to be revealed, to become clear), which takes *pé* + propositional complement (P2b, Construction 4). The inchoative verb *di* ("to become") is the most broadly applicable dynamic construction across the truth domain, combining with nominal and adjectival predicates.
3. The most productive causative structure is the serial verb *fi* X *hàn* (to cause X to be seen/revealed) and *fi* X *gbà* Y (to use X to cause receipt of Y), alongside the permissive *jẹ́ kí* (P4a, Construction 5).

The CONTEXT establishes the following resources for encoding dynamics:

- **Motion-based inchoative:** *padà sí* (return to), *jáde kúrò nínú* (come out from), *di* (become) — documented in P4a Construction 4
- **Permissive-causative:** *jẹ́ kí* (let/cause) — P4a Construction 5
- **Instrumental serial verb:** *fi* (use/employ as instrument) — P4a Construction 5
- **Negative future:** *kò ní* + predicate — P2b constructions 6, 13, 16, 21, 31, 69, 82
- **Existential pair:** *wà* / *kò sí* — P4a Construction 10
- **Directional verb:** *dúró sí* (stand by/remain committed to), *dúró nínú* (stand firm in) — P2b constructions 3, 34
- **Habitual marker:** *ń* — P2b constructions 26, 71, 72, 73

## SECTION 1: BECOMING A TRUTH-RELATED STATE

---

### 1.1 Becoming truthful / coming into truth

**Pattern:** Motion-directional construction — *padà sí* + truth-domain noun

**Attested example:**
> *Padà sí ìgbàgbọ́*
> "Return to faith / come back to belief"
> (P2b construction 36)

**Notes:** This is the only fully attested inchoative construction in the CONTEXT involving a truth-domain noun as the goal state. The verb *padà* (return) implies a prior state of faith that was lost and is being recovered; it encodes re-entry into a state rather than first entry. No parallel construction *padà sí òtítọ́* (return to truth) is attested in the CONTEXT, though the structural pattern would permit it.

---

**Pattern:** Motion-directional construction — *jáde kúrò nínú* + opposed state

**Attested example:**
> *Jáde kúrò nínú àìmọ̀*
> "Come out of ignorance / leave ignorance behind"
> (P2b construction 32)

**Notes:** This construction encodes transition into knowledge by describing departure from its opposed state (*àìmọ̀*, ignorance). The goal state (knowledge, *ìmọ̀*) is implied rather than named. This is the only attested construction of this type in the CONTEXT. No parallel *jáde kúrò nínú irọ́* (come out of falsehood) or *jáde kúrò nínú ẹ̀ṣẹ̀* (come out of sin) is attested as a fixed expression in the CONTEXT, though *wà ní ẹ̀ṣẹ̀* (be in a state of sin, P2b construction 70) implies the logical possibility of such a departure.

---

**Pattern:** *di* + nominal predicate (become)

**Attested in P4a** as an alternate inchoative construction (Construction 4, alternate row). P4a notes it as an alternate to the motion-based pattern and rates it [UNCERTAIN] for frequency relative to the motion construction.

---

**Pattern:** *farahàn pé* + propositional complement

**Attested example (P2b):**
> *Ó farahàn pé òtítọ́ ni*
> It became clear that it is the truth / It turned out to be true

**Notes:** The verb *farahàn* (to become visible, to appear, to be revealed) is the primary lexical vehicle for expressing the emergence of truth into clarity. The construction does not assert that something *becomes* true in an ontological sense but that its truth-status becomes apparent or established. The subject of *farahàn* is typically an impersonal *ó* (it) with the propositional content introduced by *pé*. No construction expressing "X becomes truth" in the sense of a proposition changing from false to true is attested in the data. The available constructions express revelation of pre-existing truth rather than ontological change.

---

**Pattern:** *hàn gbangba* (to become clearly visible)

**Attested example (P2b, under ìwà):**
> *Ìwà rẹ̀ hàn gbangba*
> His/her true character is clearly visible / His/her true nature has become evident

**Notes:** *hàn* (to appear, become visible) with the adverb *gbangba* (openly, clearly) expresses the becoming-apparent of a previously concealed or unrecognized state. In the truth domain this applies to character (*ìwà*) and, by extension, to the truth of a matter becoming publicly evident. The subject is the thing revealed, not the proposition about it. Character itself (*ìwà*) is not described in the data as changing; the constructions in P2b evaluate it as good or bad, as visible or concealed, but not as undergoing transformation. The absence of a "character becomes good/bad" construction in the data is consistent with the P1 description of *ìwà* as referring to fundamental nature rather than isolated acts.

---

### 1.2 Becoming a specific truth-related state: *di* constructions

**Pattern:** *di* + [NOMINAL/ADJECTIVAL PREDICATE]
The inchoative verb *di* ("to become") is the primary means of encoding change of state in Yoruba (documented in P4a, Pattern 4). It combines with nominal and adjectival predicates freely.

---

**Form:** *di ènìyàn òtítọ́*

**Attested example:**
> *Ó di ènìyàn òtítọ́.*
> He/she became a truthful person. / He/she became a person of truth.

**Source:** Directly attested in P4a (Pattern 4, first example).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Form:** *di ojúlówó*

**Attested example:**
> *Ó di ohun ojúlówó.*
> It became a genuine thing. / It became authentic.

**Source:** The *di* + nominal predicate pattern is attested in P4a; *ojúlówó* is a post-nominal modifier documented in P1 and P3 (Group 3). The combination follows the attested pattern directly.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Form:** *di ọmọlúwàbí*

**Attested example:**
> *Ó di ọmọlúwàbí.*
> He/she became a person of good character. / He/she became a virtuous, well-bred person.

**Source:** *di* + nominal predicate pattern (P4a); *ọmọlúwàbí* documented in P1b (Root 2, item 6) and P2a (Base 16, Derived Form 4) as a core evaluative nominal.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Form:** *di lágbára* (applied to *ìgbàgbọ́*)

**Attested example:**
> *Ìgbàgbọ́ rẹ̀ di lágbára.*
> His/her belief became strong. / His/her faith grew strong.

**Source:** Directly attested in P4a (Pattern 4, second example).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Form:** *di ènìyàn tí a lè gbẹ́kẹ̀lé*

**Attested example:**
> *Ó di ènìyàn tí a lè gbẹ́kẹ̀lé.*
> He/she became a person one can trust. / He/she became reliable.

**Source:** *di* + nominal predicate pattern (P4a); the relative-clause nominal *ènìyàn tí a lè gbẹ́kẹ̀lé* is attested in P2a (Base 8, Derived Form 1) and P2b (Base 8, Construction 4) as the standard expression for a trustworthy person.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Form:** *di tọ́* (stative predicate)

**Attested example:**
> *Ìdájọ́ náà di tọ́.*
> The judgment became correct. / The verdict became right.

**Source:** *di* + stative verbal predicate pattern (P4a); *tọ́* as a stative evaluative predicate is attested in P1b (Root 1, item 3) and P2b (Base 18, Construction 2).
**Notes:** P4a notes that *di* "combines freely with nominal and adjectival predicates." The stative verb *tọ́* functions adjectivally in predicative position; the combination is structurally consistent with the attested pattern. However, no specific example of *di tọ́* is attested in the context; the form is derived by pattern extension.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### 1.3 Becoming known: epistemic transition

**Pattern:** *A mọ̀ pé irọ́ ni* (it becomes known that it is a lie)

**Attested example (P2b):**
> *A mọ̀ pé irọ́ ni*
> We know that it is a lie / It is known to be a lie

**Notes:** This construction does not express becoming in a morphological sense but expresses the epistemic transition from not-knowing to knowing that something is false. The verb *mọ̀* (to know) in its perfective reading implies that knowledge has been acquired. No dedicated "becomes a lie" construction is attested; the closest available expression is the revelation construction with *farahàn* applied to falsehood:

> *Ó farahàn pé irọ́ ni*
> It became clear that it is a lie / It turned out to be false

This construction is not explicitly attested in P2b for *irọ́* but follows directly from the attested pattern for *òtítọ́* and the parallel syntactic behavior of the two terms documented throughout P1–P5.

---

### 1.4 Belief changing

**Pattern:** *yí padà* (serial verb: to turn around, to change)

**Attested example (P2b):**
> *Ìgbàgbọ́ rẹ̀ yí padà*
> His/her belief changed / His/her faith shifted

**Notes:** The serial verb *yí padà* (lit. turn-return) is the attested expression for change in the state of belief. It applies to the nominal *ìgbàgbọ́* as subject and expresses a change of orientation or commitment. The direction of change (from belief to unbelief, or from one belief to another) is not specified by the construction itself; context determines this.

---

### 1.5 Being in a state (stative-locative)

**Pattern:** *wà ní* + opposed state

**Attested example:**
> *Wà ní ẹ̀ṣẹ̀*
> "Be in sin / exist in a state of guilt / live in transgression"
> (P2b construction 70)

**Notes:** *Wà ní* encodes being in a state rather than transition into it; it is a stative-locative construction rather than a strict inchoative. However, P2b documents it in contexts of moral discourse where the state of sin is described as something a person can be in, implying entry and exit are possible. No construction encoding the moment of entering sin (*di alẹ̀ṣẹ̀*, become a sinner) is attested in the CONTEXT.

---

### 1.6 Genuineness as a state to be verified

**Pattern:** *ẹ ṣàyẹ̀wò bóyá ó jẹ́ gidi* (examine whether it is genuine)

**Attested example (P2b):**
> *Ẹ ṣàyẹ̀wò bóyá ó jẹ́ gidi*
> Examine it to see whether it is genuine / Check whether it is authentic

**Notes:** This construction frames genuineness as a state to be verified rather than one that comes into being. No "becomes genuine" construction is attested in the data. The copular construction *ó jẹ́ gidi* (it is genuine) is stative; *gidi* does not participate in inchoative constructions in the attested data.

---

### 1.7 Summary of inchoative patterns

The following inchoative patterns are attested in the data:

| Expression | Applies to | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| *farahàn pé* + complement | *òtítọ́*, *irọ́*, propositions generally | truth/falsity of a matter becomes apparent |
| *hàn gbangba* | *ìwà*, character states | a state becomes publicly visible |
| *yí padà* | *ìgbàgbọ́* | a belief or faith changes |
| *mọ̀ pé* (perfective) | *irọ́*, *òtítọ́* | it becomes known that something is true/false |
| *padà sí* + noun | *ìgbàgbọ́* | return to / re-entry into a state |
| *jáde kúrò nínú* + noun | *àìmọ̀* / *ìmọ̀* | departure from an opposed state |
| *di* + nominal/adjectival predicate | *òtítọ́*, *ìwà*, *ìgbàgbọ́*, *ìgbẹ́kẹ̀lé*, *ojúlówó*, *ìdájọ́* | becoming a named state |

No inchoative construction is attested for *tọ́* (correctness as a standalone inchoative), *gidi* (genuineness), *ẹ̀rí* (evidence), or *ìjẹ́rìísí* (certification) as states that come into being. These are treated in the data as states that are evaluated or verified, not as states that emerge. No construction of the form *di ẹ̀rí* is attested in the context. *ẹ̀rí* is used as a noun denoting evidence that exists or does not exist (existential constructions, P4a Pattern 9), but no inchoative construction with *di* is documented for it. No construction of the form *di irọ́* (as a nominal predicate for a proposition) is attested. The context documents *irọ́* in verbal collocations (*sọ irọ́*) and existential constructions (*kò sí irọ́ nínú rẹ̀*) but not in inchoative constructions. No construction of the form *di ìmọ̀* is attested. *ìmọ̀* appears in possessive constructions (*ó ní ìmọ̀*) and existential constructions but not in inchoative constructions in the context.

## SECTION 2: BECOMING A NON-TRUTH / OPPOSED STATE

### Pattern
**di** + [OPPOSED NOMINAL/ADJECTIVAL PREDICATE]
or
Collapse/destruction constructions: **ti fọ́** ("has broken/shattered"), **ti wó** ("has collapsed/fallen")
The collapse constructions are attested specifically for *ìgbẹ́kẹ̀lé* and *ìgbàgbọ́* (P2b, P5 Opposition 14) and encode irreversible loss of a truth-related state rather than gradual change.

---

**Form:** *di ènìyàn irọ́*

**Attested example:**
> *Ó di ènìyàn irọ́.*
> He/she became a liar. / He/she became a person of falsehood.

**Source:** *di* + nominal predicate pattern (P4a); *ènìyàn irọ́* ("a lying person / a liar") is attested in P2a (Base 2, Derived Form 2) and P4a (Pattern 7) as the periphrastic agentive for liar.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Form:** *di ènìyàn ìwà burúkú*

**Attested example:**
> *Ó di ènìyàn ìwà burúkú.*
> He/she became a person of bad character. / He/she became wicked.

**Source:** *di* + nominal predicate pattern (P4a); *ìwà burúkú* is a core evaluative expression documented throughout P1–P5.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Form:** *ìgbẹ́kẹ̀lé rẹ̀ ti fọ́*

**Attested example:**
> *Ìgbẹ́kẹ̀lé rẹ̀ ti fọ́.*
> Trust in him/her has broken. / His/her reliability has been shattered.

**Source:** Directly attested in P2b (Base 8, Construction 2) and P5 (Opposition 14).
**Notes:** The perfective aspect marker *ti* + *fọ́* ("to break/shatter") encodes a completed, irreversible change of state. This construction is specific to *ìgbẹ́kẹ̀lé* in the attested data; it is not documented for other truth-domain nominals.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Form:** *ìgbàgbọ́ rẹ̀ ti wó*

**Attested example:**
> *Ìgbàgbọ́ rẹ̀ ti wó.*
> His/her faith/belief has collapsed. / His/her conviction has crumbled.

**Source:** Directly attested in P2b (Base 7, Construction 3) and P5 (Opposition 14).
**Notes:** The perfective *ti* + *wó* ("to collapse/fall") encodes irreversible loss of the belief state. P5 notes that *fọ́* is used for *ìgbẹ́kẹ̀lé* and *wó* for *ìgbàgbọ́*, and that these are "non-identical but parallel constructions." The two verbs of collapse are not interchangeable across the two nominals in the attested data.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Form:** *ìwà rẹ̀ di àìtọ́*

**Attested example:**
> *Ìwà rẹ̀ di àìtọ́.*
> His/her conduct became improper. / His/her character became incorrect.

**Source:** *di* + adjectival predicate pattern (P4a); *àìtọ́* (negated *tọ́*, via the *àì-* prefix) is documented in P2a (Base 16, Derived Form 7) as the negative element in *ìwà àìtọ́* ("incorrect conduct, improper character").
**Notes:** No specific example of *di àìtọ́* is attested in the context; the form is derived by pattern extension from the attested *di* + predicate pattern and the attested *àìtọ́* element.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Form:** *ìwà rẹ̀ di àgàbàgebè*

**Attested example:**
> *Ìwà rẹ̀ di àgàbàgebè.*
> His/her conduct became pretense. / His/her character became sham.

**Source:** *di* + nominal predicate pattern (P4a); *àgàbàgebè* is documented in P1 and P2b (Base 13) as a core nominal for pretense and hypocrisy.
**Notes:** The copular construction *ìwà rẹ̀ jẹ́ àgàbàgebè* ("his/her conduct is pretense") is directly attested in P2b; the inchoative *di* version follows the same structural pattern.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Proverbial constructions asserting the fate of falsehood and deception:**

**Attested examples:**
> *Irọ́ kò ní dúró* — "A lie will not stand" (P2b construction 16)
> *Irọ́ ò ní gbádùn* — "Falsehood will not prosper" (P2b construction 13)
> *Ìtànjẹ́ kò ní gbádùn* — "Deception will not prosper" (P2b construction 82)

**Notes:** These constructions encode the dynamic fate of falsehood and deception — their inability to persist or succeed — using the negative future *kò ní* / *ò ní*. They do not describe a moment of becoming but assert that the opposed state (standing, prospering) will not be achieved. They are stability-denying rather than strictly inchoative.

---

**Terms for which no opposed-state transition is attested:**

- **Becoming ignorant (*àìmọ̀*):** No construction of the form *di àìmọ̀* is attested in the context. *àìmọ̀* is documented as a nominalized state (P2a) but not in inchoative constructions.
- **Becoming a counterfeit (*àdàwọ̀*):** *àdàwọ̀* appears in the context only in the contrastive construction *kì í ṣe àdàwọ̀* (P2b); no inchoative construction with it is attested.

## SECTION 3: CAUSING A TRUTH-RELATED STATE

### 3.1 Causing truth to be revealed: *fi...hàn*

**Pattern:** *fi* + [truth-related nominal] + *hàn* (serial verb: to use/take...to show/reveal)

**Attested examples (P2b):**
> *Ó fi òtítọ́ hàn*
> He/she revealed the truth / He/she showed the truth

> *Ó fi ẹ̀rí hàn lójú ìgbìmọ̀*
> He/she presented evidence before the court / He/she caused evidence to be seen before the tribunal

> *Ó fi ìmọ̀ rẹ̀ hàn* (P2b)
> He/she demonstrated his/her knowledge / He/she caused his/her knowledge to be seen

**Notes:** The serial verb construction *fi* X *hàn* is the primary causative structure for causing truth or evidence to become visible or known. The agent is the subject; the truth-related nominal occupies the object slot of *fi*; *hàn* (to show, reveal, make visible) is the result verb. The locative *lójú* (before the face of, in the presence of) optionally specifies the recipient audience. This construction applies productively to *òtítọ́* (truth), *ẹ̀rí* (evidence), and *ìmọ̀* (knowledge).

---

### 3.2 Causing certainty in a person's mind: *dánilójú*

**Pattern:** *dánilójú* — causing certainty in a person's mind

**Attested example:**
> *Fún ní ìdánilójú*
> "Give assurance to / provide confirmation to / convince"
> (P2b construction 87)

**Notes:** The verbal base *dánilójú* (P2a item 44) is documented as meaning "to make one certain, to convince" — its morphological analysis is *dá* (make/establish) + *ni* (person/one) + *lójú* (in the eye/certain). This is the most direct causative construction in the truth domain: it encodes the act of establishing certainty in another person. The ditransitive *fún ní ìdánilójú* is the attested constructional frame. The verb *dánilójú* (from *dá* — to establish + *ni* — for/to someone + *lójú* — in the eye/face of) is the primary causative verb for causing a person to arrive at a state of conviction or assurance. Its nominalization *ìdánilójú* (assurance, convincing proof) and its negation *àìdánilójú* (failure to convince) are documented in P2a. No full sentence example with *dánilójú* as a finite verb is attested in P2b, but the verbal base is documented in P2a with the meaning "to establish in someone's eyes." The construction would take the form:

> *Ó dánilójú pé òtítọ́ ni*
> He/she convinced [them] that it is the truth / He/she established in their eyes that it is true

This follows directly from the morphological description and the attested nominalization *ìdánilójú*.

---

### 3.3 Causing falsehood to be used as an instrument: *fi irọ́ gbà*

**Pattern:** *fi irọ́ gbà* + [result] (serial verb: to use lies to obtain)

**Attested example (P2b):**
> *Ó fi irọ́ gbà owó*
> He/she obtained money through deception / He/she used lies to cause receipt of money

**Notes:** This construction treats *irọ́* (falsehood) as an instrument in a causative chain: the agent uses falsehood to cause a result (here, obtaining money). The construction is documented in P2b in the context of fraud. It is the primary attested pattern for expressing causation through deception.

---

### 3.4 Causing concealment of truth: *pa...mọ́* and *bo...pamọ́*

**Pattern:** *pa* + [truth-related nominal] + *mọ́* (serial verb: to keep/conceal)

**Attested example (P2b):**
> *Ó ń pa òtítọ́ mọ́*
> He/she is concealing the truth / He/she is hiding the truth

**Pattern:** *bo* + [truth-related nominal] + *pamọ́* (serial verb: to cover/conceal)

**Attested example (P2b):**
> *Ó ń gbìyànjú láti bo irọ́ rẹ̀ pamọ́*
> He/she is trying to conceal his/her lie / He/she is attempting to hide his/her falsehood

**Notes:** These two serial verb constructions express causation of concealment. *pa...mọ́* applies to *òtítọ́* (truth); *bo...pamọ́* applies to *irọ́* (a lie). Both involve an agent causing a truth-related state to remain hidden. The progressive marker *ń* in both attested examples indicates ongoing action. These constructions are the dynamic counterparts to the revelation construction *fi...hàn*: where *fi...hàn* causes something to become visible, *pa...mọ́* and *bo...pamọ́* cause it to remain concealed.

---

### 3.5 Causing correction: *tọ́ sí* and *ìtọ́sí*

**Pattern:** *tọ́* + [object] + *sí* (to correct toward, to direct properly)

**Attested examples (P1b, P2b):**
> *Ẹ tọ́ ọ sí ọ̀nà tó tọ́*
> Direct him/her to the correct path / Guide him/her to the right way

**Notes:** The verbal phrase *tọ́...sí* (to correct toward, to direct properly) expresses causation of correctness: an agent causes a person or thing to move toward the correct state or path. The nominalization *ìtọ́sí* (correction, rectification) refers to this act as a completed or named process. This is the primary causative construction for the *tọ́* domain. No "becomes correct" or "becomes incorrect" construction is attested in the data for *tọ́* as a stative predicate in isolation; correction is expressed causatively (*tọ́ sí*, *ìtọ́sí*) rather than inchoatively.

---

### 3.6 Causing acceptance of truth: *gbà...gbọ́*

**Pattern:** *gbà* + [truth-related nominal] + *gbọ́* (serial verb: to accept/receive...to hear/believe)

**Attested example (P2b):**
> *Ó gbà òtítọ́ gbọ́*
> He/she accepted the truth / He/she received the truth

**Notes:** This serial verb construction expresses the act of a person causing themselves to accept a truth — or, in a causative reading, an agent causing a recipient to receive and accept a truth. The construction combines *gbà* (to accept, receive) and *gbọ́* (to hear, believe), the two components of *gbàgbọ́* (to believe), in a serial structure that emphasizes the act of reception. It is documented in P2b as a construction for accepting a true statement or teaching.

---

### 3.7 Causing someone to testify: *jẹ́rìí* constructions

**Pattern:** *kọ̀ láti jẹ́rìí* (refusal to testify — blocking causation)

**Attested example (P2b):**
> *Ó kọ̀ láti jẹ́rìí*
> He/she refused to testify / He/she declined to bear witness

**Notes:** This construction expresses the blocking of testimony — a person causing the act of *jẹ́rìí* (testifying) not to occur. No construction expressing "Y causes X to testify" is attested in the data beyond the imperative *Ẹ jẹ́rìí sí ohun tí ẹ rí* (testify to what you saw), which is a directive rather than a causative in the strict sense.

---

### 3.8 Permissive causation: *jẹ́ kí*

**Pattern:** *jẹ́ kí* + NP + *jẹ́* + nominal predicate (permissive
