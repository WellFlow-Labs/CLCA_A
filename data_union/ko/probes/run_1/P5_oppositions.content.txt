# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P5 Oppositions
## Language: Korean (ko)
### Transliteration Standard: Revised Romanization of Korean (RR)

---

## Preliminary Note

Oppositions below are extracted exclusively from patterns documented in P1, P1b, P2a, P2b, P3, P4a, and P4b. Each opposition is supported by explicit data from those steps — collocational pairing, morphological contrast, constructional alternation, or explicit contrastive framing in the source material. No opposition has been inferred or constructed beyond what the context supports.

---

## Opposition 1

**Term A:** 진짜 (jinjja)
**Term B:** 가짜 (gajja)
**Transliteration:** jinjja / gajja
**English translation:** genuine, real / fake, counterfeit
**Description of contrast:** These two items are explicitly documented as direct contrasts throughout the context, appearing together in fixed paired constructions (진짜인지 가짜인지 모르다; 가짜를 진짜로 속이다) and assigned to the same semantic group (P3 Group 3). The morphological parallel — 진 (眞, true) versus 가 (假, false/temporary) with the shared suffix -짜 — reinforces the opposition at both the lexical and morphological levels.
**Contrast type:** lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 2

**Term A:** 진실 (jinsil)
**Term B:** 거짓 (geojit)
**Transliteration:** jinsil / geojit
**English translation:** truth, truthfulness / falsehood, lie, untruth
**Description of contrast:** Explicitly documented as contrasting items in P1 (거짓 contrasts with 진실 and 사실) and in the fixed collocation 진실과 거짓을 가리다 (to distinguish truth from falsehood). The two items occupy opposite poles of the truth-falsehood axis in everyday and formal registers.
**Contrast type:** lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 3

**Term A:** 맞다 (matda)
**Term B:** 틀리다 (teullida)
**Transliteration:** matda / teullida
**English translation:** to be right, correct / to be wrong, incorrect, mistaken
**Description of contrast:** Explicitly documented as contrasting items in P1 (틀리다 contrasts with 맞다) and in fixed paired constructions (맞는지 틀린지 모르다; 맞고 틀리고를 떠나서). Both items are assigned to P3 Group 10 as natural contrasts within the precision-and-correctness domain.
**Contrast type:** lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 4

**Term A:** 사실이다 (sasil-ida)
**Term B:** 사실이 아니다 (sasil-i anida)
**Transliteration:** sasil-ida / sasil-i anida
**English translation:** it is a fact, that is true / it is not a fact, that is not the case
**Description of contrast:** Documented in P2b and P4a as the standard positive and negative copular predication pair for factual assertion and denial. P4b explicitly notes that the negative form is as productive as the positive and that both are ubiquitous across all registers.
**Contrast type:** constructional
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 5

**Term A:** 근거가 있다 (geungeo itda)
**Term B:** 근거가 없다 (geungeo eopda)
**Transliteration:** geungeo itda / geungeo eopda
**English translation:** to be grounded, to have a basis / to be groundless, to lack basis
**Description of contrast:** Documented in P2a, P2b, P3, and P4a as the standard positive and negative evaluative construction for evidential groundedness. P4b notes that the 있다/없다 pattern is the primary evaluative frame for this class of abstract quality nouns and that -하다 attachment is not available as an alternative.
**Contrast type:** constructional
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

**Contrast type:** constructional
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 8

## Opposition 9

**Term A:** 진실이 드러나다 (jinsil-i deureonada)
**Term B:** 거짓이 드러나다 (geojit-i deureonada)
**Transliteration:** jinsil-i deureonada / geojit-i deureonada
**English translation:** the truth emerges, the truth is exposed / the falsehood is exposed, the lie comes to light
**Description of contrast:** Both constructions are documented in P2b using the same verb 드러나다 (to surface, be exposed) but with opposed subjects. The parallel structure with opposed subjects is noted in P2b, where 거짓이 드러나다 is explicitly described as having "the opposite valence" to 진실이 드러나다.
**Contrast type:** constructional
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

**Description of contrast:** Documented in P2b as contrasting collocations sharing the same object (신뢰) but with semantically opposed verbs expressing the accumulation versus loss of trust. Both are listed as [CORE] in P2b and appear together in P3 Group 9 as defining constructions of the trustworthiness domain.
**Contrast type:** constructional

## Opposition 11

**Term A:** 진실하다 (jinsil-hada)
**Term B:** 진실되다 (jinsil-doeda)
**Transliteration:** jinsil-hada / jinsil-doeda
**English translation:** to be truthful, to be sincere (active/agentive nuance) / to be truthful, to be genuine (stative/inherent nuance)
**Description of contrast:** Documented in P2a as a morphological contrast between the -하다 and -되다 forms of the same base. P2a notes that 진실하다 tends toward active or performed quality while 진실되다 carries a nuance of inherent rather than performed quality, and that 진실되다 appears more in literary and formal registers. P4b confirms the -하다 / -되다 split as showing semantic conditioning rather than free variation.
**Contrast type:** morphological
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 12

**Term A:** 위조하다 (wijo-hada)
**Term B:** 위조되다 (wijo-doeda)
**Transliteration:** wijo-hada / wijo-doeda
**English translation:** to forge, to counterfeit (agentive) / to be forged, to be counterfeited (passive/resultant)
**Description of contrast:** Documented in P2a as a morphological contrast between the agentive -하다 form and the passive/resultant -되다 form of the same base. P4b lists 위조되다 as the attested alternate of 위조하다 and notes the -하다 / -되다 split as a productive pattern in this domain.
**Contrast type:** morphological
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 13

**Term A:** 모순되다 (mosun-doeda)
**Term B:** (모순하다 — not attested)
**Transliteration:** mosun-doeda / —
**English translation:** to be contradictory, to contradict / (no -하다 form exists)
**Description of contrast:** Documented in P2a and P4b as an asymmetric case: 모순되다 is the only available predicative form for contradiction; 모순하다 is explicitly noted as not attested. This is an asymmetric opposition in which the -되다 form exists without a -하다 counterpart, unlike the symmetric 진실하다 / 진실되다 pair.
**Contrast type:** morphological
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 14

**Term A:** 정확히 (jeonghwaki)
**Term B:** 정확하게 (jeonghwak-age)
**Transliteration:** jeonghwaki / jeonghwak-age
**English translation:** accurately, exactly, precisely (formal/written) / accurately, in a precise manner (spoken/colloquial)
**Description of contrast:** Documented in P2a and P4b as two adverbial forms of the same base with overlapping meaning but different register distribution. P4b notes that -히 forms are more restricted to formal and written registers while -게 forms are more common in spoken registers, and that the two are not interchangeable in all contexts.
**Contrast type:** morphological
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 15

**Term A:** 정직히 (jeongjiki)
**Term B:** 정직하게 (jeongjik-age)

**English translation:** honestly, straightforwardly (formal/written) / honestly, in an honest manner (spoken/colloquial)

**Contrast type:** morphological
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 16

**Term A:** 진실성 (jinsilseong)
**Term B:** 올바름 (olbareum)
**Transliteration:** jinsilseong / olbareum
**English translation:** truthfulness as a quality (-성 nominalization) / rightness, correctness (-음 nominalization)
**Description of contrast:** Documented in P2a and P4b as representatives of two nominalization strategies in complementary distribution by base type: -성 (性) attaches to Sino-Korean bases (진실성, 정확성, 신뢰성) while -음 attaches to native Korean verbal stems (올바름, 틀림). P4b explicitly states these are in complementary distribution and not interchangeable.
**Contrast type:** morphological
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 17

**Term A:** 거짓말쟁이 (geojitmal-jaengi)
**Term B:** 위조범 (wijobom)
**Transliteration:** geojitmal-jaengi / wijobom
**English translation:** liar, habitual liar (pejorative, informal) / forger, counterfeiter (legal register)
**Description of contrast:** Documented in P2a and P4a as two agentive nominalizations in the falsity domain that are register-stratified: -쟁이 is a pejorative native suffix restricted to informal speech, while -범 (犯) is a Sino-Korean legal suffix restricted to formal and legal registers. P4b notes this as an instance of register stratification as the primary axis of variation.
**Contrast type:** morphological
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 18

**Term A:** 진 (眞) — morpheme
**Term B:** 위 (僞) — morpheme
**Transliteration:** jin / wi
**English translation:** true, genuine (positive pole) / false, fake (negative pole)
**Description of contrast:** Documented in P1b as the two primary Sino-Korean morphemes structuring the truth-authenticity axis in formal registers. 진 (眞) appears in 진실, 진짜, 진위, 진본, 진정성; 위 (僞) appears in 위조, 위증, 허위, 위선, 위장. P1b identifies these as the positive and negative poles of the truth-authenticity domain in Sino-Korean vocabulary.
**Contrast type:** lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 19

**Term A:** 허위 (heowi)
**Term B:** 거짓 (geojit)
**Transliteration:** heowi / geojit
**English translation:** falsehood, falsity, misrepresentation (formal/legal register) / falsehood, lie, untruth (everyday register)
**Description of contrast:** Documented in P1 and P3 as two items covering overlapping semantic ground — both designate falsity — but with distinct register distributions. P1 notes that 허위 is more formal than 거짓 and appears in legal compounds (허위진술, 허위신고, 허위광고); P3 places them in different groups (Group 4 and Group 6 respectively) on the basis of this register and collocational difference.
**Contrast type:** lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 20

**Term A:** 신뢰 (sinloe)
**Term B:** 신빙성 (sinbyeongseong)
**Transliteration:** sinloe / sinbyeongseong
**English translation:** trust, reliability (of persons and institutions) / credibility, plausibility (of claims and testimony)
**Description of contrast:** Documented in P3 as items that are semantically proximate but distinguished by their collocational partners and evaluative targets. P3 notes that 신뢰 appears with verbs of building, losing, betraying, and restoring (쌓다, 잃다, 저버리다, 회복하다) while 신빙성 appears with verbs of assessment and degree (높다, 낮다, 의심하다), and assigns them to different semantic groups (Group 9 and Group 8) on this basis.

**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 22

**Term A:** 틀리다 (teullida)
**Term B:** 다르다 (dareuda)
**Transliteration:** teullida / dareuda
**English translation:** to be wrong, incorrect / to be different
**Description of contrast:** Documented in P1 (틀리다 is distinct from 다르다, though the two are sometimes conflated in colloquial speech) and in P2b in the fixed construction 다르다고 틀린 게 아니다 (being different does not mean being wrong), which explicitly frames the two as non-equivalent. The opposition is asymmetric: the data documents the conflation as a usage problem, not as a legitimate semantic overlap.
**Contrast type:** lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 23

**Term A:** 진본 (jinbon)
**Term B:** 위조품 (wijopum)
**Transliteration:** jinbon / wijopum
**English translation:** authentic original / forged item, counterfeit goods

P1 also notes 진본 contrasts with 위본 (forged copy) and 복사본 (copy), but 위조품 is the most fully documented negative counterpart in the data.

## Opposition 24

**Term A:** 진정성 (jinjeonseong)
**Term B:** 위선 (wiseon)
**Transliteration:** jinjeonseong / wiseon
**English translation:** authenticity, genuineness, sincerity / hypocrisy, false virtue
**Description of contrast:** Documented in P3 Group 5, where 위선 is placed in the inner-state authenticity group alongside 진정성 because "its defining contrast is between professed virtue and actual inner character — the same inner-outer gap that 진정성 addresses, but from the negative side." The two items address the same evaluative dimension (alignment between inner state and outward expression) from opposite poles.
**Contrast type:** lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 25

**Term A:** 사실 (sasil)
**Term B:** 사실상 (sasilsang)
**Transliteration:** sasil / sasilsang
**English translation:** fact, actuality / in effect, de facto, for all intents and purposes
**Description of contrast:** Documented in P1b and P3 as items sharing the same base but with distinct functions. P3 notes that 사실상 belongs in Group 2 (actuality-versus-appearance) rather than Group 1 (factual correspondence) because its function is to assert de facto status despite formal or official appearances, not to verify a proposition. The contrast is between propositional fact-assertion (사실) and pragmatic de-facto-status assertion (사실상).
**Contrast type:** morphological
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 26

**Term A:** 진실을 말하다 (jinsil-eul malhada)
**Term B:** 거짓말을 하다 (geojitmal-eul hada)
**Transliteration:** jinsil-eul malhada / geojitmal-eul hada
**English translation:** to tell the truth, to speak the truth / to tell a lie, to lie
**Description of contrast:** Both constructions are documented in P2b as the standard collocations for truthful and deceptive speech respectively, sharing the same verb 하다 or 말하다 and appearing in the same semantic group (P3 Group 6). The parallel object-verb structure with opposed objects makes this a constructional contrast as well as a lexical one.
**Contrast type:** constructional
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 29

**Term A:** 신뢰를 저버리다 (sinloe-reul jeobeorida)
**Term B:** 신뢰를 회복하다 (sinloe-reul hoebokada)
**Transliteration:** sinloe-reul jeobeorida / sinloe-reul hoebokada
**English translation:** to betray trust, to abandon trust / to restore trust, to recover credibility
**Description of contrast:** Documented in P2b as contrasting collocations sharing the same object (신뢰) but with semantically opposed verbs. P2b notes that 저버리다 implies deliberate or culpable failure to honor trust while 회복하다 describes the process of rebuilding trust after damage. Both appear in P3 Group 9 as defining constructions of the trustworthiness domain.
**Contrast type:** constructional
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 30

**Term A:** 진상 (jinsang)
**Term B:** 실상 (silsang)
**Transliteration:** jinsang / silsang
**English translation:** the true state of affairs, what actually happened (investigative context) / the actual state of affairs, the reality (analytical context)
**Description of contrast:** Both items are documented in P1b and P3 as referring to what is actually the case in contrast to official accounts or appearances, and both are assigned to P3 Group 2. However, P3 notes that 진상 appears almost exclusively in investigative collocations (진상 규명, 진상 조사) implying a gap between official account and actual events, while 실상 appears in journalistic and analytical discourse more broadly. The contrast is one of collocational restriction rather than semantic opposition.
**Contrast type:** lexical
**Epistemic status:** [UNCERTAIN]

## Opposition 31

**Term A:** 참말 (chammal)
**Term B:** 거짓말 (geojitmal)
**Transliteration:** chammal / geojitmal
**English translation:** true words, truthful speech / lie, falsehood
**Description of contrast:** Documented in P2a, where 참말 is described as "native Korean parallel to 거짓말 (lie); used to refer to truthful speech or a true statement" and noted as appearing "often in contrast with 거짓말." Both items share the same second morpheme 말 (word, speech) and are structurally parallel compounds. P3 Group 6 lists 참말 alongside 거짓말 as items in the honesty-in-conduct domain.
**Contrast type:** lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 32

**Term A:** 진품 (jinpum)
**Term B:** 가품 / 위조품 (gapum / wijopum)
**Transliteration:** jinpum / gapum / wijopum
**English translation:** genuine article, authentic item / fake item / forged item, counterfeit goods
**Description of contrast:** Documented in P1b, where 진품 is explicitly noted as contrasting with 가품 (fake item) and 위조품 (forged item). The three-way structure is asymmetric: 진품 has two negative counterparts that are not synonymous — 가품 designates a non-genuine item generally while 위조품 specifically designates a fraudulently produced item. The data supports the opposition between 진품 and each negative counterpart but does not establish 가품 and 위조품 as contrasting with each other.
**Contrast type:** lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 33

**Term A:** 실체적 진실 (silchejeok jinsil)
**Term B:** (procedural or formal determination — no single lexicalized counterpart)
**Transliteration:** silchejeok jinsil / —
**English translation:** substantive truth, the truth of the matter itself / (no single lexicalized counterpart attested)
**Description of contrast:** Documented in P1b as a legal and philosophical collocation used to distinguish the actual truth of what occurred from procedural or formal determinations. The contrast is between substantive truth and formal/procedural determination, but no single lexicalized counterpart to 실체적 진실 is documented in the data. The opposition exists at the conceptual level within the collocation's definition but lacks a lexicalized Term B.
**Contrast type:** constructional
**Epistemic status:** [UNCERTAIN]
