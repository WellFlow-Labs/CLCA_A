## Summary Notes

1. All nine constructions identified in P4a are rated [CORE]. No construction in this dataset is rated [MARGINAL] or [UNCERTAIN] based on the evidence provided.
2. The most pragmatically extended items are **בֶּאֱמֶת** (discourse particle functions) and **נָכוֹן שֶׁ...** (concessive frame), both of which have moved beyond fully compositional interpretation in attested usage.
3. The primary asymmetry in the dataset is in **Agentive Nominalization** (Construction 7): the -an pattern is productive for negative characterological agents (שַׁקְרָן) but lacks a lexicalized positive counterpart; positive agentive meaning is expressed periphrastically.
4. No constructions were added or modified beyond those attested in P4a output.
