# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P4b Construction Usage & Constraints
# Language: Finnish (fi)

---

## Construction Usage Table

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. ATTRIBUTION** | *Se on tosi.* | N/A | "It is true." | [CORE] | Default predicative pattern in all registers; no contextual restriction. *Olla* + predicative adjective is the unmarked copular construction in Finnish. |
| **1. ATTRIBUTION** | *Väite on perusteeton.* | N/A | "The claim is unfounded." | [CORE] | Frequent in formal, journalistic, and legal discourse; fully productive across truth-domain adjectives. |
| **2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION (negated copula)** | *Se ei pidä paikkaansa.* | N/A | "That is not true / does not hold." | [CORE] | The fixed expression *ei pidä paikkaansa* is the most colloquial and frequent form for propositional falsity; preferred in spoken and informal written Finnish over *ei ole tosi*. |
| **2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION (*epä-* prefix)** | *Lähde on epäluotettava.* | N/A | "The source is unreliable." | [CORE] | *Epä-* prefixation is productive and common in formal and written registers; preferred when a stable negative property is being attributed rather than a momentary negation. |
| **3. ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION** | *luotettava lähde* | N/A | "a reliable source" | [CORE] | Fully productive and unrestricted; adjective precedes noun and agrees in case and number. No register restriction observed. |
| **3. ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION** | *perusteeton syytös* | N/A | "an unfounded accusation" | [CORE] | Common in legal, journalistic, and administrative discourse; the pattern itself is the default attributive construction in Finnish. |
| **4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE (*osoittautua* + translative)** | *Väite osoittautui epätodeksi.* | N/A | "The claim turned out to be false." | [CORE] | Productive general-purpose inchoative for truth-domain adjectives; common in journalistic and analytical discourse. Requires an animate or institutional agent as implicit evaluator. |
| **4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE (*toteutua*)** | *Ennuste toteutui.* | N/A | "The prediction came true." | [CORE] | Restricted to propositions with a prospective or intentional character: predictions, plans, wishes, hopes. Not interchangeable with *osoittautua* for general truth-domain inchoatives. |
| **4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE (morphological *-utua*)** | *(aitoutua)* | N/A | "to become authentic" | [MARGINAL] | Attested but marginal; not productively available across the truth-domain adjective class. Lexical coverage is sparse and individual forms should not be assumed to exist without attestation. |
| **5. CAUSATIVE (*tehdä* + translative)** | *Todisteet tekivät väitteen uskottavaksi.* | N/A | "The evidence made the claim credible." | [CORE] | Productive general-purpose causative; common in argumentative, journalistic, and academic prose. Fully productive across truth-domain adjectives in the translative. |
| **5. CAUSATIVE (morphological *väärentää*)** | *Hän väärensi asiakirjan.* | N/A | "He forged the document." | [CORE] | Lexicalized; *väärentää* is fully established and common in legal and administrative contexts. The morphological causative pattern is not productive across the domain; other forms (*todentaa*, *oikaista*) are individually lexicalized. |
| **6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION (*-uus* / *-yys*)** | *Lähteen luotettavuus on kyseenalainen.* | N/A | "The reliability of the source is questionable." | [CORE] | The *-uus* / *-yys* nominalizer is the primary and productive pattern for truth-domain quality nouns; common across all formal registers. |
| **6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION (*-uus* / *-yys*)** | *Menettelyn asianmukaisuus tarkistettiin.* | N/A | "The propriety of the procedure was checked." | [CORE] | Especially frequent in administrative, legal, and bureaucratic discourse; the genitive-compound variant (*totuudenmukaisuus*) is a secondary productive pattern in the same registers. |
| **7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION (*-ja* / *-jä*)** | *Hän on luotettava todistaja.* | N/A | "He is a reliable witness." | [CORE] | *Todistaja* is fully lexicalized and common across legal, everyday, and journalistic registers. The *-ja* / *-jä* suffix is productive on verb stems but individual truth-domain agent nouns vary in lexicalization status. |
| **7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION (*-ja* / *-jä*)** | *Asiakirjojen väärentäjä pidätettiin.* | N/A | "The forger of documents was arrested." | [CORE] | *Väärentäjä* is lexicalized and common in legal and journalistic contexts. The absence of a lexicalized agent noun for "truth-teller" is a notable gap; periphrastic expression is required for that role. |
| **8. PERIPHRASTIC STANCE (*pitää* + essive)** | *Pidän selitystä uskottavana.* | N/A | "I consider the explanation credible." | [CORE] | Productive across truth-domain adjectives; common in spoken, journalistic, and academic registers. Encodes ongoing or habitual assessment rather than a one-time ruling. |
| **8. PERIPHRASTIC STANCE (*katsoa* + translative)** | *Viranomainen katsoi todistuksen päteväksi.* | N/A | "The authority deemed the certificate valid." | [CORE] | Productive but register-restricted: most frequent in legal, administrative, and official discourse. Encodes a formal determination or ruling; less natural in casual speech than *pitää* + essive. |
| **9. VERB + *väärin* / *oikein*** | *Ymmärsit minut väärin.* | N/A | "You misunderstood me." | [CORE] | Fully productive with verbs of cognition and communication; common across all registers including spoken Finnish. Covers semantic ground handled by English *mis-* prefixation. |
| **9. VERB + *väärin* / *oikein*** | *Vastasit oikein.* | N/A | "You answered correctly." | [CORE] | Common in instructional, evaluative, and everyday contexts; *oikein* combines productively with a wide range of action and communication verbs. No register restriction observed. |
| **10. CORRESPONDENCE CONSTRUCTION** | *Suunnitelma ei vastaa todellisuutta.* | N/A | "The plan does not correspond to reality." | [CORE] | Standard construction for asserting or denying correspondence between a representation and reality; common in journalistic, analytical, and formal registers. Distinct from predicative attribution in explicitly encoding a two-place correspondence relation. |
| **10. CORRESPONDENCE CONSTRUCTION** | *Kuvaus vastaa totuutta.* | N/A | "The description corresponds to the truth." | [CORE] | Productive with *todellisuus* and *totuus* as partitive complements; also attested with *faktoja* and similar NPs. Frequent in evaluative and argumentative discourse. |

## Alternate Constructions

| Construction Type | Alternate Form | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **1. ATTRIBUTION** | *Se pitää paikkansa.* | Positive counterpart of *ei pidä paikkaansa*; attested and common for asserting propositional truth colloquially. |
| **2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION** | *ei ole tosi* / *ei ole oikein* | Compositional negated copula; grammatical and attested but less idiomatic than *ei pidä paikkaansa* for propositional falsity in spoken Finnish. |
| **4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE** | *käydä toteen* | Attested periphrastic for predictions or claims "proving true"; more formal or literary in register than *toteutua*. |
| **5. CAUSATIVE** | *todentaa*, *oikaista* | Individually lexicalized morphological causatives; not instances of a productive suffix pattern across the domain. |
| **8. PERIPHRASTIC STANCE** | *kokea* + translative (*kokea väite vääräksi*) | Attested but less frequent; encodes subjective experiential stance rather than evaluative or official determination. |
| **10. CORRESPONDENCE CONSTRUCTION** | *pitää paikkansa* (with subject NP) | Overlaps functionally with the correspondence construction when the subject is a proposition-denoting NP; encodes truth-holding rather than explicit two-place correspondence. |
