## 2. Substitution Patterns (Narrative)

### 2.1 VERIFICATION substituted by CAUSATION (factual)

The CLCA operator VERIFICATION — meaning "to establish, confirm, or prove that something is true" — does not appear as a structurally distinct construction in the P4a/P4b data. The form *cheqaqchay* ("to verify, to confirm, to prove true") is listed in P4a §5 under the causative construction, alongside *chiqanchay* ("to correct/rectify") and *llullachiy* ("to deceive"). All three are produced by the same morphological operation: adjectival/nominal root + *-chay/-chiy*. The data do not document a separate construction type for verification; the verification meaning is carried by the same morphological form that expresses causation. The two operators share a single construction in this language.

### 2.2 REVELATION: no substitution documented

REVELATION — the meaning "it turns out to be true / it is revealed to be the case" — is not attested in P4a or P4b, and no substitution by another operator is documented. The BECOME construction (*cheqaq tukun*, *chiqan tukun*) is the closest structurally available pattern, but P4a §4 glosses these as "becomes truthful / turns out to be genuine" and "becomes correct / becomes just," not as revelation of a pre-existing state. The data do not document any construction that specifically encodes the revelation reading (i.e., a state that was already true becoming known or manifest). No substitution is reported.

### 2.3 DISCONFIRMATION: no dedicated construction; negation is the closest available form

DISCONFIRMATION — the meaning "to show, prove, or demonstrate that something is false" — is not attested as a distinct construction. The *mana … -chu* negation frame (P4a §2) expresses that something is not true or not correct, but this is negative attribution, not active disconfirmation. No morphological causative parallel to *cheqaqchay* is documented for the falsity side (i.e., no form meaning "to prove false" or "to demonstrate to be a lie" is listed). The data suggest that if disconfirmation is to be expressed, it would require circumlocution, but no specific circumlocutory form is documented in P4a or P4b.

### 2.4 Asymmetry: truth-side vs. falsity-side constructions

A consistent asymmetry runs across multiple construction types:

- **Adverbial truth assertion (P4a §8):** *Cheqaqta* + verb ("speak the truth," "I truly know") is attested as a productive adverbial construction. No parallel construction is documented for *llulla* in this slot. P4b §8 explicitly notes: "No parallel construction is documented for *llulla* in P4a."

- **Prohibitive moral injunction (P4a §9):** The *ama llulla* / *ama llullakuychu* construction is attested exclusively with *llulla* and *llullakuy*. No parallel prohibitive is documented for *cheqa*, *chiqan*, or *allin*. This is the inverse asymmetry: the falsity-side item (*llulla*) has a dedicated prohibitive construction; the truth-side items do not.

- **Agentive nominalization (P4a §7):** *Llullaq* ("liar") is rated [CORE]; *cheqaqniyuq* ("one who possesses truthfulness") is rated [MARGINAL] and is described as a lexicalized form rather than a freely productive derivation. The bare agentive *-q* does not appear to apply freely to truth-side roots.

- **CAUSATION (character):** No construction is documented for causing a person to become truthful or to acquire a truth-related character trait. The causative forms attested (*cheqaqchay*, *chiqanchay*, *llullachiy*) are oriented toward factual states (verifying, correcting, deceiving) rather than character formation.

### 2.5 NEGATION: analytic + morphological hybrid

Negation in this language is neither purely analytic nor purely morphological. It uses a free negator *mana* (analytic element) in combination with the bound clitic *-chu* (morphological element attached to the predicate). The evidential clitic *-mi/-m* is variable in negative constructions: it may be absent, may attach to the predicate, or may attach to *mana*. P4b §2 notes both placements as attested with no restriction documented between them. This hybrid pattern is the standard negation strategy and applies to truth-domain predicates without any special construction.

### 2.6 BECOME: light verb *tukuy* as carrier

The BECOME operator is expressed through the light verb *tukuy* ("to become, to turn into"), which carries all tense and person inflection while the truth-domain adjective precedes it uninflected. This is structurally parallel to the general adjectival predication pattern in the language. Both attested examples are rated [UNCERTAIN], reflecting limited specific attestation for truth-domain items in this slot, not structural irregularity. The construction is described in P4a §4 as "structurally regular."

## 3. Missingness Summary

### 3.1 Structural Gaps

- **REVELATION:** No construction is attested. No substitution is documented. The BECOME construction (*[adjective] + tukuy*) is the nearest available form but is not documented as encoding the revelation reading. **MISSING (structural gap).**

- **CAUSATION (character):** No construction is attested for causing a person to acquire a truth-related character trait (e.g., "to make someone truthful" as a character change). The morphological causative forms attested are oriented toward factual states and actions, not character formation. **MISSING (structural gap).**

- **DISCONFIRMATION:** No dedicated construction is attested. The *mana … -chu* negation frame is the closest available form but expresses negative attribution, not active disconfirmation. **NO COMMON EXPRESSION — circumlocution required.**

### 3.2 Lexical Gaps

- No agentive nominal meaning "one who is truthful" derived by bare *-q* from a truth-side root is documented. The attested form *cheqaqniyuq* uses the possessive suffix *-niyuq* rather than the agentive *-q*, and is rated [MARGINAL] and described as lexicalized. This represents a lexical gap in the productive agentive derivation on the truth side.

- No adverbial form parallel to *cheqaqta* is documented for *llulla* (i.e., no *llullata* + verb construction meaning "falsely" or "in a lying manner" is attested in P4a or P4b).

### 3.3 Conceptual Gaps

- No evidence in P4a or P4b that the language encodes a conceptual distinction between REVELATION (a pre-existing truth becoming manifest) and BECOME (a state coming into existence). The data do not document any construction that specifically targets the revelation reading. This may reflect a conceptual gap or simply a gap in the available documentation; the data do not permit a stronger claim.

### 3.4 Asymmetric Gaps

| Domain | Truth side (*cheqa*, *chiqan*) | Falsity side (*llulla*) |
|---|---|---|
| Adverbial construction | *Cheqaqta* + verb [CORE] | **No parallel documented** |
| Prohibitive moral injunction | **No parallel documented** | *Ama llulla* / *Ama llullakuychu* [CORE] |
| Agentive nominalization | *Cheqaqniyuq* [MARGINAL], lexicalized | *Llullaq* / *Llullakuq* [CORE], productive |
| BECOME construction | *Cheqaq tukun* [UNCERTAIN] | *Chiqan tukun* [UNCERTAIN]; no *llulla tukun* documented |
| Prohibitive | Not attested for *cheqa* or *chiqan* | Attested and [CORE] for *llulla* |

The asymmetry is bidirectional: the truth side has the adverbial construction that the falsity side lacks; the falsity side has the prohibitive construction that the truth side lacks. The agentive nominalization is more productive on the falsity side.
