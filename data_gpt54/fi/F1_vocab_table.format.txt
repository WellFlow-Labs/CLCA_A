| totta | — | ‘true’; related to **tosi** | true, correct, right | **Totta** is a very common predicate adjective and adverbial response in evaluating whether a statement matches reality. | tosi | — | ‘true, real’ | true, real, genuine | **Tosi** is common in both colloquial and standard Finnish, though in colloquial use it also functions as an intensifier. In evaluative contexts it can refer to truth, authenticity, or realness, as in *tosi tarina* ‘a true story’ or *onko tämä tosi?* ‘is this true?’. | [CORE] |
| totuus | — | ‘truth’; from **tosi/totta** + nominal suffix **-uus** | truth | **Totuus** is the standard noun for ‘truth’ in ordinary, philosophical, moral, and legal discussion. | todellinen | — | ‘real, actual’; from **tode-** / **tosi** family + adjectival suffix **-llinen** | real, actual, genuine | **Todellinen** is used when distinguishing what is actual or genuine from what is apparent, fictional, or merely supposed. | todellisuus | — | ‘reality’; from **todellinen** + **-uus** | reality | **Todellisuus** refers to reality as opposed to illusion, appearance, or representation. It is frequent in general language and in philosophical or critical discussion, especially when evaluating whether something corresponds to the real world. | [CORE] |
| aito | — | ‘genuine, authentic’ | genuine, authentic, real | **Aito** is widely used for objects, feelings, style, and persons when evaluating genuineness rather than falseness or imitation. Typical uses include *aito nahka* ‘genuine leather’ and *aito ilo* ‘genuine joy’. | [CORE] |
| aidosti | — | ‘genuinely, authentically’; **aito** + adverbial **-sti** | genuinely, authentically, truly | **Aidosti** evaluates the manner or sincerity of an action, feeling, or representation. | väärä | — | ‘wrong, false’ | wrong, false, incorrect | **Väärä** is a basic adjective for factual incorrectness, moral wrongness, or mismatch with what is proper or intended. | virheellinen | — | ‘erroneous’; from **virhe** ‘error’ + **-ellinen** | erroneous, incorrect, faulty | **Virheellinen** is common in formal and semi-formal contexts for statements, data, reasoning, procedures, and representations that contain error. It is especially typical in administrative, technical, and academic language. | [CORE] |
| oikea | — | ‘right, correct, proper’ | right, correct, proper, genuine | **Oikea** is a very broad and frequent adjective used for correctness, appropriateness, legitimacy, and sometimes genuineness. It appears in factual, moral, practical, and legal evaluations, as in *oikea vastaus* ‘correct answer’ and *oikea menettely* ‘proper procedure’. | [CORE] |
| oikein | — | adverb from **oikea** | correctly, properly, right | **Oikein** is used to evaluate whether something is done or understood correctly. It is common in instruction, assessment, and moral or practical judgment, for example *tehdä oikein* ‘do the right thing / do correctly’. | [CORE] |
| oikeus | — | ‘rightness, justice, right’; from **oikea** + **-us** | justice, right, legality, entitlement | **Oikeus** is a central noun in legal and moral contexts, covering justice, rights, and what is rightful. | oikeudenmukainen | — | ‘justice-minded’; **oikeuden** ‘of justice’ + **mukainen** ‘in accordance with’ | just, fair | **Oikeudenmukainen** is used for persons, decisions, laws, and treatment judged to accord with justice or fairness. | laillinen | — | ‘legal, lawful’; from **laki** ‘law’ + **-llinen** | legal, lawful | **Laillinen** evaluates conduct, status, or procedure in relation to the law. | lainmukainen | — | ‘in accordance with law’; **lain** ‘of the law’ + **mukainen** ‘according to’ | lawful, in accordance with the law, legal | **Lainmukainen** is a formal adjective used especially for actions, decisions, and procedures that conform to legal rules. | epäaito | — | ‘not genuine’; **epä-** ‘un-, non-’ + **aito** | inauthentic, fake, ungenuine | **Epäaito** is used for things, expressions, or performances judged not to be genuine or sincere. | epätosi | — | ‘untrue’; **epä-** ‘un-, non-’ + **tosi** | untrue, false | **Epätosi** is the standard opposite of **tosi** in logic, argumentation, and general statement evaluation. It is common in formal and semi-formal contexts, such as *väite on epätosi* ‘the claim is false’. | [CORE] |
| paikkansapitävä | — | ‘holding its place’; from expression **pitää paikkansa** ‘to be true, to hold’ | true, accurate, valid | **Paikkansapitävä** is used especially of information, claims, reports, and descriptions that are factually accurate. | pitää paikkansa | — | ‘to hold its place’ | be true, hold true, be correct | This fixed expression is a very common way to say that a statement or report is true or accurate. | luotettava | — | ‘trustable’; from **luottaa** ‘to trust’ + **-tava** | reliable, trustworthy | **Luotettava** is used for persons, sources, methods, machines, and information judged dependable. | uskottava | — | ‘believable’; from **uskoa** ‘to believe’ + **-ttava** | believable, credible, plausible | **Uskottava** evaluates whether a claim, explanation, performance, or person appears credible. | uskottavuus | — | ‘credibility’; from **uskottava** + **-uus** | credibility | **Uskottavuus** is used for the credibility of a speaker, institution, narrative, or account. It is common in politics, media analysis, law, and rhetoric. | [CORE] |
