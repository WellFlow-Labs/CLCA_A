# CLCA Protocol v3.3 — P6 CHANGE, CAUSATION, AND STABILITY
# Language: Turkish (tr)

---

## SECTION 1: BECOMING TRUE/CORRECT (doğru)

### 1.1 X becomes true/correct

**Primary construction:** **doğru çıkmak**
- **Form:** doğru çıkmak
- **Transliteration:** doğru çıkmak
- **Literal breakdown:** doğru (true/correct) + çıkmak (to come out/emerge)
- **Translation:** to turn out to be true, to prove correct
- **Usage:** Very common for predictions, claims, or suspicions that are confirmed by events. Emphasizes revelation or discovery of truth-value that was previously uncertain.
- **Example:** *Tahminim doğru çıktı* "My prediction turned out to be correct"
- **Status:** [CORE]

**Alternative construction:** **doğru olmak** (inchoative reading)
- **Form:** doğru olmak
- **Transliteration:** doğru olmak
- **Literal breakdown:** doğru (true/correct) + olmak (to be/become)
- **Translation:** to become true, to be true
- **Usage:** Less common as pure inchoative; primarily functions as copula. Inchoative reading possible in contexts like *Artık doğru oldu* "Now it has become true," but this is marginal.
- **Status:** [MARGINAL]

**No dedicated morphological inchoative exists for doğru.** The language relies on light verb *çıkmak* for inchoative meaning.

---

### 1.2 X becomes false/wrong (from true)

**Primary construction:** **yanlış çıkmak**
- **Form:** yanlış çıkmak
- **Transliteration:** yanlış çıkmak
- **Literal breakdown:** yanlış (wrong/false) + çıkmak (to come out/emerge)
- **Translation:** to turn out to be wrong/false
- **Usage:** Common for claims or beliefs that are revealed to be incorrect. Parallel to *doğru çıkmak*.
- **Example:** *Söyledikleri yanlış çıktı* "What they said turned out to be wrong"
- **Status:** [CORE]

**Alternative construction:** **yanlış olmak** (inchoative reading)
- **Form:** yanlış olmak
- **Transliteration:** yanlış olmak
- **Literal breakdown:** yanlış (wrong) + olmak (to be/become)
- **Translation:** to become wrong, to be wrong
- **Usage:** Primarily copular. Inchoative reading marginal.
- **Status:** [MARGINAL]

**Note:** Turkish does not commonly express the transition from true to false as a single lexical event. The language focuses on the revelation of truth-value (*çıkmak* constructions) rather than change of truth-value.

## SECTION 2: BECOMING REAL/GENUINE (gerçek)

### 2.1 X becomes real (actualization)

**Primary construction:** **gerçekleşmek**
- **Form:** gerçekleşmek
- **Transliteration:** gerçekleşmek
- **Literal breakdown:** gerçek (real) + -leş- (inchoative suffix) + -mek (infinitive)
- **Translation:** to come true, to be realized, to materialize, to happen
- **Usage:** Very common for events, plans, dreams, or predictions that become actual. This is the dedicated morphological inchoative for *gerçek*.
- **Example:** *Hayalleri gerçekleşti* "His/her dreams came true"
- **Status:** [CORE]

**Alternative construction:** **gerçeğe dönüşmek**
- **Form:** gerçeğe dönüşmek
- **Transliteration:** gerçeğe dönüşmek
- **Literal breakdown:** gerçek (reality) + dative -e + dönüşmek (to transform into)
- **Translation:** to become reality, to turn into reality
- **Usage:** More formal/literary than *gerçekleşmek*. Emphasizes transformation process. Used for dreams, fantasies, or plans becoming real.
- **Example:** *Hayaller gerçeğe dönüştü* "Dreams turned into reality"
- **Status:** [MARGINAL]

**Alternative construction:** **gerçek olmak** (inchoative reading)
- **Form:** gerçek olmak
- **Transliteration:** gerçek olmak
- **Literal breakdown:** gerçek (real) + olmak (to be/become)
- **Translation:** to become real, to be real
- **Usage:** Primarily copular. Inchoative reading possible but less common than dedicated *gerçekleşmek*.
- **Status:** [MARGINAL]

---

### 2.2 X becomes fake/unreal (from real)

**Primary construction:** **sahte çıkmak**
- **Form:** sahte çıkmak
- **Transliteration:** sahte çıkmak
- **Literal breakdown:** sahte (fake/counterfeit) + çıkmak (to come out/emerge)
- **Translation:** to turn out to be fake, to be revealed as counterfeit
- **Usage:** Common for objects or claims discovered to be fake. Emphasizes revelation rather than change of state.
- **Example:** *Para sahte çıktı* "The money turned out to be counterfeit"
- **Status:** [CORE]

**No common expression for genuine object becoming fake.** Turkish focuses on revelation of pre-existing fakeness rather than transformation from genuine to fake. The concept of something genuinely real becoming fake is not lexicalized.

## SECTION 4: BECOMING CERTAIN/DEFINITE (kesin)

### 4.1 X becomes certain/definite

**Primary construction:** **kesinleşmek**
- **Form:** kesinleşmek
- **Transliteration:** kesinleşmek
- **Literal breakdown:** kesin (certain/definite) + -leş- (inchoative) + -mek (infinitive)
- **Translation:** to become definite, to be finalized, to become certain
- **Usage:** Common for decisions, plans, or information that becomes final or certain. Used in administrative and everyday contexts.
- **Example:** *Karar kesinleşti* "The decision has been finalized"
- **Status:** [CORE]

**Alternative construction:** **kesin olmak** (inchoative reading)
- **Form:** kesin olmak
- **Transliteration:** kesin olmak
- **Literal breakdown:** kesin (certain) + olmak (to be/become)
- **Translation:** to become certain, to be certain
- **Usage:** Can function as inchoative but *kesinleşmek* is preferred for clear change of state.
- **Status:** [MARGINAL]

---

### 4.2 X becomes uncertain/indefinite (from certain)

**No common dedicated expression exists.** Turkish does not lexicalize the transition from certain to uncertain as a single construction. One might use periphrastic expressions like *belirsizleşmek* "become uncertain" (from *belirsiz* "uncertain" + inchoative *-leş-*), but this is not attested in the provided corpus.

**Periphrastic alternative:** **kesin olmaktan çıkmak**
- **Form:** kesin olmaktan çıkmak
- **Transliteration:** kesin olmaktan çıkmak
- **Literal breakdown:** kesin olmak (to be certain) + ablative -tan + çıkmak (to exit)
- **Translation:** to cease being certain, to become uncertain
- **Usage:** Possible but not common. Highly periphrastic.
- **Status:** [MARGINAL]

## SECTION 7: CAUSATIVE CONSTRUCTIONS — MAKING TRUE/CORRECT

### 7.1 Y causes X to be true/correct (verification)

**Primary construction:** **doğrulamak**
- **Form:** doğrulamak
- **Transliteration:** doğrulamak
- **Literal breakdown:** doğru (true/correct) + -la- (denominative) + -mak (infinitive)
- **Translation:** to verify, to confirm, to validate
- **Usage:** Very common for confirming truth or accuracy of information. Used in technical, legal, and everyday contexts.
- **Example:** *Bilgiyi doğruladık* "We verified the information"
- **Status:** [CORE]

**Alternative construction:** **doğru göstermek**
- **Form:** doğru göstermek
- **Transliteration:** doğru göstermek
- **Literal breakdown:** doğru (true/correct) + göstermek (to show)
- **Translation:** to show to be true, to demonstrate truth
- **Usage:** Common in argumentative and evidential contexts. Emphasizes demonstration rather than simple confirmation.
- **Example:** *İddiasını doğru gösterdi* "He/she showed his/her claim to be true"
- **Status:** [CORE]

**Alternative construction:** **doğru kabul etmek**
- **Form:** doğru kabul etmek
- **Transliteration:** doğru kabul etmek
- **Literal breakdown:** doğru (true/correct) + kabul (acceptance) + etmek (to do)
- **Translation:** to accept as true, to acknowledge as correct
- **Usage:** Common in formal and argumentative contexts. Emphasizes epistemic stance rather than causation of truth.
- **Example:** *Bu iddiayı doğru kabul ediyorum* "I accept this claim as true"
- **Status:** [CORE]

---

### 7.2 Y causes X to be false/wrong (falsification)

**Primary construction:** **yanlışlamak**
- **Form:** yanlışlamak
- **Transliteration:** yanlışlamak
- **Literal breakdown:** yanlış (wrong/false) + -la- (denominative) + -mak (infinitive)
- **Translation:** to falsify, to refute, to prove wrong
- **Usage:** Less common than *doğrulamak*. Used in formal/academic contexts for refutation.
- **Example:** *Teoriyi yanlışladılar* "They falsified the theory"
- **Status:** [MARGINAL]

**Alternative construction:** **yanlış olduğunu göstermek**
- **Form:** yanlış olduğunu göstermek
- **Transliteration:** yanlış olduğunu göstermek
- **Literal breakdown:** yanlış (wrong) + ol-duğu-nu (that it is) + göstermek (to show)
- **Translation:** to show that it is wrong
- **Usage:** More common than simple *yanlışlamak*. Periphrastic but natural.
- **Example:** *İddianın yanlış olduğunu gösterdik* "We showed that the claim is wrong"
- **Status:** [CORE]

**Alternative construction:** **yalanlamak**
- **Form:** yalanlamak
- **Transliteration:** yalanlamak
- **Literal breakdown:** yalan (lie) + -la- (denominative) + -mak (infinitive)
- **Translation:** to deny, to refute, to call a lie
- **Usage:** Common for denying or refuting claims. Stronger than *yanlışlamak*, implies the claim is a deliberate lie.
- **Example:** *Haberi yalanladı* "He/she denied the news"
- **Status:** [CORE]

**More specific alternatives:**

## SECTION 13: REVEALING TRUTH (DYNAMIC PROCESSES)

### 13.1 Truth emerges/comes to light (intransitive)

**Primary construction:** **gerçek ortaya çıkmak**

- **Transliteration:** gerçek ortaya çıkmak
- **Literal breakdown:** gerçek (truth) + ortaya (into the open) + çıkmak (to come out)
- **Translation:** the truth comes out, reality emerges, truth is revealed
- **Usage:** Very common for truth being discovered or revealed. Emphasizes natural emergence rather than deliberate revelation.
- **Example:** *Sonunda gerçek ortaya çıktı* "Finally the truth came out"
- **Status:** [CORE]

**Alternative construction:** **hakikat ortaya çıkmak**

- **Translation:** the truth comes to light
- **Usage:** More formal/elevated than *gerçek ortaya çıkmak*. Same construction pattern.
- **Status:** [CORE]

## SECTION 21: ASPECTUAL DISTINCTIONS IN CHANGE

### 21.1 Perfective vs. imperfective change

Turkish aspectual system does not make strong perfective/imperfective distinctions in the Slavic sense. Instead, it uses:

**Aorist (habitual/general):**
- *Gerçek ortaya çıkar* "Truth comes out (generally/habitually)"

**Progressive (ongoing):**
- *Gerçek ortaya çıkıyor* "Truth is coming out (now/ongoing)"

**Past definite (completed):**
- *Gerçek ortaya çıktı* "Truth came out (completed event)"

**Past evidential (reported/inferred):**
- *Gerçek ortaya çıkmış* "Truth has come out (apparently/reportedly)"

These aspectual distinctions apply uniformly across all truth-domain change constructions. No special aspectual marking exists specifically for truth-related changes.

## SECTION 22: SUMMARY OF PATTERNS

### 22.1 Productive inchoative patterns

**Morphological inchoatives (with *-leş-*):**
- *gerçekleşmek* "to come true, be realized" [CORE]
- *kesinleşmek* "to become definite, be finalized" [CORE]

**Light verb inchoatives (with *çıkmak*):**
- *doğru çıkmak* "to turn out to be true" [CORE]
- *yanlış çıkmak* "to turn out to be wrong" [CORE]
- *haklı çıkmak* "to be proven right" [CORE]
- *haksız çıkmak* "to be proven wrong" [CORE]
- *sahte çıkmak* "to turn out to be fake" [CORE]

**Light verb inchoatives (with *olmak*):**
- *geçerli olmak* "to become valid" [CORE]
- *geçersiz olmak* "to become invalid" [CORE]
- Various other adjectives + *olmak* [MARGINAL]

### 22.2 Productive causative patterns

**Morphological causatives (with *-leş-tir-*):**
- *gerçekleştirmek* "to realize, accomplish" [CORE]
- *kesinleştirmek* "to finalize, confirm" [CORE]

**Denominative causatives (with *-la-*):**
- *doğrulamak* "to verify, confirm" [CORE]
- *yanlışlamak* "to falsify, refute" [MARGINAL]
- *yalanlamak* "to deny, refute" [CORE]

**Light verb causatives (with *göstermek*):**
- *doğru göstermek* "to show to be true" [CORE]
- *yanlış olduğunu göstermek* "to show to be wrong" [CORE]
- *gerçeği göstermek* "to show the truth" [CORE]

**Light verb causatives (with *etmek*):**
- *kabul etmek* "to accept" [CORE]
- *inkâr etmek* "to deny" [CORE]
- *reddetmek* "to reject" [CORE]

**Light verb causatives (with *kılmak*, formal):**
- *geçerli kılmak* "to validate" [MARGINAL]
- *geçersiz kılmak* "to invalidate" [CORE]

**Periphrastic causatives (with *hale getirmek*):**
- *X hale getirmek* "to make X, to render X" [MARGINAL]

### 22.3 Revelation and concealment patterns

**Revelation (intransitive):**
- *gerçek ortaya çıkmak* "truth comes out" [CORE]
- *hakikat ortaya çıkmak* "truth comes to light" [CORE]

**Revelation (transitive causative):**
- *gerçeği ortaya çıkarmak* "to reveal the truth" [CORE]
- *gerçeği göstermek* "to show the truth" [CORE]

**Concealment:**
- *gerçeği gizlemek* "to hide the truth" [CORE]
- *gerçeği saklamak* "to hide the truth" [CORE]
- *gerçeği örtmek* "to cover up the truth" [MARGINAL]

**Distortion:**
- *gerçeği çarpıtmak* "to distort the truth" [CORE]
- *gerçeği saptırmak* "to deflect the truth" [MARGINAL]

### 22.4 Epistemic stance patterns

**Acceptance:**
- *gerçeği kabul etmek* "to accept the truth" [CORE]
- *doğru kabul etmek* "to accept as true" [CORE]

**Rejection:**
- *gerçeği inkâr etmek* "to deny the truth" [CORE]
- *gerçeği reddetmek* "to reject the truth" [CORE]
- *yalanlamak* "to deny, refute" [CORE]

**Discovery:**
- *gerçeği öğrenmek* "to learn the truth" [CORE]
- *doğruyu öğrenmek* "to learn the truth" [CORE]

**Pursuit:**
- *gerçeğin peşinde olmak* "to be in pursuit of truth" [CORE]
- *gerçeğin peşinden koşmak* "to chase after truth" [CORE]

### 22.5 Stability and persistence patterns

**Maintaining truth-value:**
- *doğru kalmak* "to remain true" [CORE]
- *geçerliliğini korumak* "to maintain validity" [CORE]

**Losing truth-value:**
- *geçerliliğini yitirmek* "to lose validity" [CORE]
- *doğruluğunu yitirmek* "to lose truth/correctness" [MARGINAL]

### 22.6 Notable gaps and asymmetries

**Gaps:**
1. No common expression for genuine object becoming fake (only revelation of pre-existing fakeness)
2. No common expression for transition from certain to uncertain
3. No common expression for making something inconsistent
4. No lexicalized gradual change expressions for truth-values
5. Limited causative morphology for *haklı/haksız* (relies on periphrasis)

**Asymmetries:**
1. *Gerçekleşmek/gerçekleştirmek* pair is highly productive, but no parallel for other truth-terms
2. *Çıkmak* pattern emphasizes revelation rather than change of state
3. Causatives for invalidation (*geçersiz kılmak*) more common than for validation (*geçerli kılmak*)
4. More vocabulary for concealing/distorting truth than for revealing it
5. *Doğrulamak* "verify" is common, but *yanlışlamak* "falsify" is marginal

**Preferred strategies:**
1. Light verb *çıkmak* for inchoative revelation of truth-value
2. Morphological *-leş-/-leştir-* for actualization (*gerçekleşmek/gerçekleştirmek*)
3. Periphrastic constructions with *göstermek*, *etmek*, *kılmak* for causation
4. *Ortaya çıkmak/çıkarmak* for emergence/revelation of truth
5. *Gizlemek/saklamak* for concealment of truth
