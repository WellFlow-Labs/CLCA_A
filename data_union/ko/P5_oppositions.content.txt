# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P5 Oppositions
## Language: Korean (ko)
### Transliteration Standard: Revised Romanization of Korean (RR)

---

## Preliminary Note

Oppositions below are extracted exclusively from patterns documented in P1, P1b, P2a, P2b, P3, P4a, and P4b. Each opposition is supported by explicit data from those steps — collocational pairing, morphological contrast, constructional alternation, or explicit contrastive framing in the source material. No opposition has been inferred or constructed beyond what the context supports. Where asymmetries exist between the two poles of a contrast, they are reported rather than smoothed over.

## Opposition 1

**Term A:** 진짜 (jinjja)
**Term B:** 가짜 (gajja)
**Transliteration:** jinjja / gajja
**English translation:** genuine, real / fake, counterfeit
**Description of contrast:** These two items are explicitly documented as direct contrasts throughout the context, appearing together in fixed paired constructions (진짜인지 가짜인지 모르다; 가짜를 진짜로 속이다; 진짜와 가짜를 구별하다) and assigned to the same semantic group (P3 Group 3). The morphological parallel — 진 (眞, true) versus 가 (假, false/temporary) with the shared suffix -짜 — reinforces the opposition at both the lexical and morphological levels. P3 identifies these as the most colloquial pair in the authenticity-of-objects group.
**Contrast type:** lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 2

**Term A:** 진실 (jinsil)
**Term B:** 거짓 (geojit)
**Transliteration:** jinsil / geojit
**English translation:** truth, truthfulness / falsehood, lie, untruth
**Description of contrast:** Explicitly documented as contrasting items in P1 (거짓 contrasts with 진실 and 사실) and in the fixed collocations 진실과 거짓을 가리다 (to distinguish truth from falsehood), 진실과 거짓 사이 (between truth and falsehood), 거짓과 진실을 구별하다 (to distinguish falsehood from truth), and the reverse-order variant 거짓과 진실을 구분하다. P2b documents both orderings as attested. The two items occupy opposite poles of the truth-falsehood axis in everyday and formal registers. P3 identifies 진실 and 거짓 as the positive and negative poles of the truth-evaluation axis. P1 notes that 진실 "appears in moral, legal, and everyday contexts, often in opposition to 거짓."
**Contrast type:** lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 3

**Term A:** 진실 (jinsil)
**Term B:** 거짓말 (geojitmal)
**Transliteration:** jinsil / geojitmal
**English translation:** truth / lie
**Description of contrast:** P3 places 진실 as the positive pole contrasted with 거짓말 in everyday moral discourse about speech acts. The construction 진실을 말하다 ("to tell the truth") is the direct counterpart to 거짓말하다 ("to lie"), both documented in P2b and P2a respectively.
**Contrast type:** lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 4

**Term A:** 맞다 (matda)
**Term B:** 틀리다 (teullida)
**Transliteration:** matda / teullida
**English translation:** to be right, correct / to be wrong, incorrect, mistaken
**Description of contrast:** Explicitly documented as contrasting items in P1 (틀리다 contrasts with 맞다) and in fixed paired constructions (맞는지 틀린지 모르다; 맞고 틀리고를 떠나서). Both items are assigned to P3 Group 10 as natural contrasts within the precision-and-correctness domain.
**Contrast type:** lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 5

**Term A:** 옳다 (olta)
**Term B:** 그르다 (geureuda)
**Transliteration:** olta / geureuda
**English translation:** to be right, correct, just / to be wrong, incorrect, improper
**Description of contrast:** P1 explicitly identifies 그르다 as the direct antonym of 옳다 and notes the fixed expression 옳고 그름 (right and wrong). P2a and P2b document the nominalized pair 옳음 and 그름 and the collocations 옳고 그름을 가리다 and 옳고 그름을 따지다. P2b (items 11.1 and 11.2) and P4a attest 옳고 그름 as a fixed collocation. P3 (Group 11) documents these as a matched antonym pair.
**Contrast type:** lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 6

**Term A:** 옳다 (olta)
**Term B:** 틀리다 (teullida)
**Transliteration:** olta / teullida
**English translation:** to be right / to be wrong (in everyday factual contexts)
**Description of contrast:** P3 (Group 11) notes that 틀리다 has "largely displaced" 그르다 in factual-incorrectness contexts in contemporary everyday Korean, making 옳다 and 틀리다 a functional pair in everyday speech even though they are not morphologically related antonyms. P2b (item 13.1) documents 틀린 게 없다 ("there is nothing wrong") as the everyday negation of correctness.
**Contrast type:** lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 7

**Term A:** 사실이다 (sasil-ida)
**Term B:** 사실이 아니다 (sasil-i anida)
**Transliteration:** sasil-ida / sasil-i anida
**English translation:** it is a fact, that is true / it is not a fact, that is not the case
**Description of contrast:** Documented in P2b and P4a as the standard positive and negative copular predication pair for factual assertion and denial. P4b explicitly notes that the negative form is as productive as the positive and that both are ubiquitous across all registers. P4b notes that 사실이 아니다 is "the standard and neutral construction for denying factual status" and is "preferred in formal corrections and public statements over the more marked 거짓이다." P4a identifies 아니다 as the negative copula used with nominal predicates.
**Contrast type:** constructional
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 8

**Term A:** 사실이 아니다 (sasil-i anida)
**Term B:** 거짓이다 (geojit-ida)
**Transliteration:** sasil-i anida / geojit-ida
**English translation:** it is not a fact (neutral denial) / it is a falsehood (marked assertion)
**Description of contrast:** P4b (Construction 2) notes that 거짓이다 is "attested as a more marked alternative" to 사실이 아니다 in denial contexts, with 사실이 아니다 preferred for neutral correction and 거짓이다 carrying a stronger evaluative charge. This is a constructional contrast in pragmatic register rather than in truth-conditional content.
**Contrast type:** constructional
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 9

**Term A:** 사실 (sasil)
**Term B:** 허위 (heowi) / 허위사실 (heowi sasil)
**Transliteration:** sasil / heowi, heowi sasil
**English translation:** fact / falsehood; false fact
**Description of contrast:** P3 identifies 사실 and 허위 as the primary poles of the factual-status assertion group, noting that 허위사실 is explicitly formed by combining 허위 with 사실 to denote a claim presented as factual but false. The construction 사실이 아니다 ("it is not a fact") and 허위로 밝혀지다 ("to be revealed as false") are documented as contrasting constructions in P2b.
**Contrast type:** lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 10

## Opposition 11

**Term A:** 신빙성이 있다 (sinbyeongseong-i itda)
**Term B:** 신빙성이 없다 (sinbyeongseong-i eopda)
**Transliteration:** sinbyeongseong-i itda / sinbyeongseong-i eopda
**English translation:** to be credible, to have credibility / to lack credibility, to be implausible
**Description of contrast:** Documented in P1, P2a, P2b, P4a, and P4b as the standard positive and negative evaluative construction for credibility assessment. P4a notes that this is the primary predication strategy for 성-nominalized quality nouns in the truth domain because these forms do not take 하다 as a predicative suffix. P4b confirms that the 있다/없다 frame is the only available predicative construction for 신빙성; no -하다 form exists. The pair is extremely common in legal and investigative contexts.
**Contrast type:** constructional
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 12

**Description of contrast:** Documented in P1, P2a, and P2b as the standard positive and negative evaluative construction for inner-state authenticity. P2b (items 7.4 and 8.1) and P4a (Construction 8) document these as the standard affirmative and negative predication pair. P2a (18.2) documents 진정성 없는 as a prenominal modifier (진정성 없는 사과, an insincere apology), parallel to 진정성 있는 사과 (a sincere apology).

## Opposition 14

P4a notes these as the primary agentive causative constructions in the truth-revelation domain, and P4b lists them as attested alternates of each other.

## Opposition 15

**Transliteration:** jinsil-eul balkhida / jinsil-eul oemyeonhada

P2b notes that 외면하다 characterizes "deliberate avoidance of what is known to be true."

The parallel object-verb structure with opposed objects makes this a constructional contrast as well as a lexical one.

## Opposition 18

**Term A:** 진실이 드러나다 (jinsil-i deureonada)
**Term B:** 거짓이 드러나다 (geojit-i deureonada)
**Transliteration:** jinsil-i deureonada / geojit-i deureonada
**English translation:** the truth emerges, the truth is exposed / the falsehood is exposed, the lie comes to light
**Description of contrast:** Both constructions are documented in P2b using the same verb 드러나다 (to surface, be exposed) but with opposed subjects. The parallel structure with opposed subjects is noted in P2b, where 거짓이 드러나다 is explicitly described as having "the opposite valence" to 진실이 드러나다.
**Contrast type:** constructional
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 19

**Term A:** 진실이 드러나다 (jinsil-i deureonada)
**Term B:** 진실을 숨기다 (jinsil-eul sumgida)
**Transliteration:** jinsil-i deureonada / jinsil-eul sumgida
**English translation:** the truth comes to light (intransitive) / to hide the truth (transitive)
**Description of contrast:** P2b documents both constructions. These are not a simple lexical pair but a constructional contrast: 진실이 드러나다 is intransitive (truth as subject, emerging without a specified agent), while 진실을 숨기다 is transitive (truth as object, concealed by an agent). The contrast captures the difference between truth's emergence and its deliberate suppression.
**Contrast type:** constructional
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 29

**Term A:** 유효하다 (yuhyo-hada) / 여전히 유효하다 (yeojeonhi yuhyo-hada)
**Term B:** 무효가 되다 (muhyo-ga doeda) / 무효 처리되다 (muhyo cheori-doeda)
**Transliteration:** yuhyo-hada / muhyo-ga doeda
**English translation:** to be valid, to remain in force / to become void, to be processed as invalid
**Description of contrast:** P2b documents these as the operative and inoperative states in legal and administrative contexts. The contrast is not only between the base terms (유효/무효) but between the constructional frames used to assert each state: 유효하다 is a simple predicative construction; 무효가 되다 and 무효 처리되다 are inchoative and passive-administrative constructions respectively, reflecting the procedural nature of invalidation.
**Contrast type:** constructional
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 31

**Epistemic status:** [CORE]
