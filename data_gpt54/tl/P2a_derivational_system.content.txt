### 1. Base Form: **totoo**
**Derived Form:** **totoong**  
**Transliteration:** totoong  
**Literal Breakdown:** *totoo* + ligature *-ng*  
**Natural English translation:** true; real; genuine  
**Usage description:** Common attributive form before nouns, as in *totoong balita* or *totoong pangalan*. It is one of the most frequent derived surface forms of *totoo* in ordinary speech and writing.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### 2. Base Form: **totoo**
**Derived Form:** **katotohanan**  
**Transliteration:** katotohanan  
**Literal Breakdown:** *ka-* + *totoo* + *-han*  
**Natural English translation:** truth; reality; fact  
**Usage description:** Standard abstract noun referring to truth or reality. It is widely used in formal, legal, journalistic, philosophical, and everyday contexts.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### 3. Base Form: **totoo**
