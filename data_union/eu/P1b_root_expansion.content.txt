# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P1b ROOT EXPANSION
# Language: Basque (eu)

---

## Step 1 — Identify Central Roots

---

**Root 1: egia**

- **Root form:** egia (Latin script; no separate transliteration required)
- **P1 items participating:** egia (1), egiazko (2), egiaztatu (3), egiazkotasun (20) — **4 items**
- **Core meaning contribution:** The primary nominal root encoding propositional truth and factual correspondence. Serves as the base for derivation of adjectives, verbs, and abstract nouns across the truth-evaluation domain. It is the most morphologically productive root in the P1 set.

---

**Root 2: ziur**

- **Root form:** ziur
- **P1 items participating:** ziur (8), ziurtatu (9), ziurtasun (10) — **3 items**
- **Core meaning contribution:** Encodes epistemic certainty and reliability. Generates a productive paradigm of adjectives, verbs, and abstract nouns concerned with the state of being sure, confirmed, or dependably established.

---

**Root 3: zuzen**

- **Root form:** zuzen
- **P1 items participating:** zuzen (15), zuzentasun (16) — **2 items**; additionally, *zuzen* functions as a base for a large number of compounds and derivatives not yet listed in P1, making it the most morphologically productive root in the domain of correctness, justice, and rectitude.
- **Core meaning contribution:** Encodes correctness, straightness, moral rightness, and legal justice. Highly polysemous and productive across epistemic, moral, legal, and spatial domains.

---

**Root 4: balio**

- **Root form:** balio
- **P1 items participating:** balioa (12), baliozkoa (13), baliogabea (14) — **3 items**
- **Core meaning contribution:** Encodes value, worth, and validity. Anchors the sub-domain of formal and legal validity within the broader truth-evaluation field, and participates in productive compounding with privative and adjectival suffixes.

---

**Root 5: zintzo**

- **Root form:** zintzo
- **P1 items participating:** zintzoa (22), zintzotasuna (23) — **2 items**
- **Core meaning contribution:** The most productive root for personal moral integrity and honesty as character traits, a domain underrepresented by the other roots. Functions primarily as an adjective and adverb with standard suffixation patterns and verbal collocations.

---

**Root 6: itxura**

- **Root form:** itxura
- **P1 items participating:** itxurakoa, itxuragilea, itxuraz — **3 items**
- **Core meaning contribution:** The root for appearance, semblance, and outward form as potentially distinct from inner reality. Generates derivatives marking the gap between how things seem and how they are, functioning as the primary appearance-vs.-reality axis in the domain.

## Step 2 — Expand Each Root

### ROOT 1: egia

---

**1.**
- **Form:** egia esan
- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Morpheme breakdown:** egia (truth) + esan (to say) — "to say the truth"
- **Natural English translation:** to tell the truth
- **Context of use:** The most common collocation of *egia* in everyday speech; used in moral, legal, and interpersonal contexts to describe honest disclosure. Appears in direct commands (*egia esazu*, "tell the truth") and narrative description. Used across all registers, from everyday conversation to courtroom testimony.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**2.**
- **Form:** egia da
- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Morpheme breakdown:** egia (truth) + da (is, 3sg present of *izan*) — "it is truth"
- **Natural English translation:** it is true; that is true
- **Context of use:** The standard predicative construction for asserting the truth of a proposition in everyday and formal speech. Functions as a discourse marker of confirmation. Ubiquitous in everyday speech, philosophical discourse, and formal argumentation. Functions as the baseline truth-assertion formula in Basque.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**3.**
- **Form:** egia esan behar
- **Transliteration:** N/A
- **Morpheme breakdown:** egia (truth) + esan (to say) + behar (need, must) — "must say the truth"
- **Natural English translation:** to tell the truth (as an obligation); truth be told
- **Context of use:** Used as a discourse-initial phrase equivalent to "to be honest" or "truth be told," signaling that the speaker is about to make a candid or potentially unwelcome statement. Common in spoken and written Basque.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]
