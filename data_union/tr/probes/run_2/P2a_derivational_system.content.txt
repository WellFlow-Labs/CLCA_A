# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2a Derivational System
# Language: Turkish (tr)

---

> **Scope note:** This step documents attested derivational morphology for the 30 P1 base items. Forms already fully documented in P1b Root Expansion are cross-referenced rather than re-described at length. The focus here is on derivations not yet covered, or on completing the derivational picture for items whose roots were not among the three expanded in P1b. Epistemic statuses follow P1 conventions.

---

## P1 Item 1 — doğru

**Base Form:** doğru

---

**Derived Form 1**
**Derived Form:** doğruluk
**Transliteration:** doğruluk
**Literal Breakdown:** *doğru* + *-luk* (abstract noun suffix)
**Natural English Translation:** truth, correctness, accuracy, integrity
**Usage:** Names the property of being true, correct, or morally upright. Used in philosophical, moral, and everyday contexts. Fully documented in P1b Root 1, item 1.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2**
**Derived Form:** doğrulamak
**Transliteration:** doğrulamak
**Literal Breakdown:** *doğru* + *-la-* (factitive verbalizer) + *-mak* (infinitive)
**Natural English Translation:** to verify, to confirm, to validate
**Usage:** The primary verbal derivative; used in administrative, journalistic, and scientific registers. Fully documented in P1 (item 12) and P1b Root 1, item 2.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 3**
**Derived Form:** doğrulama
**Transliteration:** doğrulama
**Literal Breakdown:** *doğru* + *-la-* + *-ma* (verbal noun)
**Natural English Translation:** verification, confirmation, authentication
**Usage:** Nominalized form of *doğrulamak*; standard in technical, administrative, and journalistic registers. Documented in P1b Root 1, item 3.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 4**
**Derived Form:** doğrulanmak
**Transliteration:** doğrulanmak
**Literal Breakdown:** *doğru* + *-la-* + *-n-* (passive) + *-mak*
**Natural English Translation:** to be verified, to be confirmed
**Usage:** Passive form; used in scientific and journalistic registers. Documented in P1b Root 1, item 4.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 5**
**Derived Form:** doğrulanabilir
**Transliteration:** doğrulanabilir
**Literal Breakdown:** *doğru* + *-la-* + *-n-* + *-abil-* (potential) + *-ir*
**Natural English Translation:** verifiable
**Usage:** Technical term in philosophy of science and empirical methodology. Documented in P1b Root 1, item 5.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 6**
**Derived Form:** doğrulanabilirlik
**Transliteration:** doğrulanabilirlik
**Literal Breakdown:** *doğrulanabilir* + *-lik*
**Natural English Translation:** verifiability
**Usage:** Technical term in philosophy of science; used in Turkish discussions of logical positivism. Documented in P1b Root 1, item 6.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 7**
**Derived Form:** doğrulayıcı
**Transliteration:** doğrulayıcı
**Literal Breakdown:** *doğrula-* + *-yıcı* (agentive/adjectival suffix)
**Natural English Translation:** verifying, confirmatory, validator
**Usage:** Describes a person, process, or mechanism that verifies or confirms. Documented in P1b Root 1, item 20.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 8**
**Derived Form:** doğrultmak
**Transliteration:** doğrultmak
**Literal Breakdown:** *doğru* + *-lt-* (causative) + *-mak*
**Natural English Translation:** to straighten, to correct, to aim
**Usage:** Used in physical and figurative senses: straightening, correcting a course, or aiming an instrument. Documented in P1b Root 1, item 7.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 9**
**Derived Form:** doğrultu
**Transliteration:** doğrultu
**Literal Breakdown:** nominal derivative of *doğrultmak*
**Natural English Translation:** direction, alignment, line of direction
**Usage:** Used in technical, navigational, and everyday contexts for a direction or line of orientation. Documented in P1b Root 1, item 8.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 10**
**Derived Form:** doğrusal
**Transliteration:** doğrusal
**Literal Breakdown:** *doğru* + *-sal* (adjectival suffix for scientific/technical use)
**Natural English Translation:** linear
**Usage:** Technical term in mathematics, physics, and engineering. Documented in P1b Root 1, item 18.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

> **Note on gaps:** No commonly attested agentive noun *doğrulayıcı* functioning as a personal title (e.g., "a verifier" as a job title) has been identified beyond its adjectival and technical uses. No negative privative form *\*doğrusuz* is in common use; negation is typically expressed through *yanlış* or *hatalı* rather than a privative derivative of *doğru*.

## P1 Item 3 — hakikat

**Base Form:** hakikat

---

**Derived Form 1**
**Derived Form:** hakikaten
**Transliteration:** hakikaten
**Literal Breakdown:** *hakikat* + *-en* (adverbial suffix, from Arabic *-an*)
**Natural English Translation:** truly, really, in truth, indeed
**Usage:** Used as an adverb in formal and literary registers to assert that something is genuinely the case; also used in everyday speech as emphatic affirmation. Documented in P1b Root 2, item 15.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2**
**Derived Form:** hakikatli
**Transliteration:** hakikatli
**Literal Breakdown:** *hakikat* + *-li* (adjectival suffix)
**Natural English Translation:** truthful, veracious
**Usage:** An adjectival form meaning "characterized by truth" or "truthful"; used in formal and literary registers to describe persons or statements. Less frequent than *hakiki* or *doğru* in this function.
**Epistemic Status:** [MARGINAL]

---

> **Note on gaps:** No commonly attested abstract noun *\*hakikatsizlik* (untruthfulness) derived directly from *hakikat* has been identified; the concept is more typically expressed through *yalancılık* or *gerçek dışılık*. The form *hakikatsiz* (without truth) exists in literary usage but is rare in contemporary registers.

## P1 Item 4 — hak

**Base Form:** hak

---

**Derived Form 1**
**Derived Form:** haklı
**Transliteration:** haklı
**Literal Breakdown:** *hak* + *-lı* (adjectival suffix)
**Natural English Translation:** right, justified, in the right
**Usage:** Predicates that a person's claim or position is correct or warranted. Listed in P1 (item 5) and documented in P1b Root 2, item 2.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2**
**Derived Form:** haksız
**Transliteration:** haksız
**Literal Breakdown:** *hak* + *-sız* (privative suffix)
**Natural English Translation:** unjust, wrong, unwarranted, in the wrong
**Usage:** The direct antonym of *haklı*; used in moral, legal, and interpersonal contexts. Documented in P1b Root 2, item 3.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 3**
**Derived Form:** haklılık
**Transliteration:** haklılık
**Literal Breakdown:** *haklı* + *-lık*
**Natural English Translation:** justification, rightness, being in the right
**Usage:** Names the property of being justified or in the right; used in legal and philosophical contexts. Documented in P1b Root 2, item 5.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 4**
**Derived Form:** haksızlık
**Transliteration:** haksızlık
**Literal Breakdown:** *haksız* + *-lık*
**Natural English Translation:** injustice, wrong, unfairness
**Usage:** Names the condition or act of being unjust; used in moral, legal, and political discourse. Documented in P1b Root 2, item 4.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 5**
**Derived Form:** haklar
**Transliteration:** haklar
**Literal Breakdown:** *hak* + *-lar* (plural suffix)
**Natural English Translation:** rights, entitlements
**Usage:** The plural form used in legal and political discourse to refer to a set of rights. Documented in P1b Root 2, item 12.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 6**
**Derived Form:** haksızca
**Transliteration:** haksızca
**Literal Breakdown:** *haksız* + *-ca* (adverbial/manner suffix)
**Natural English Translation:** unjustly, wrongfully, unfairly
**Usage:** An adverbial form used in moral and legal contexts to describe actions carried out without justification or in violation of what is right. *Haksızca suçlamak* (to accuse unjustly).
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 7**
**Derived Form:** haklıca
**Transliteration:** haklıca
**Literal Breakdown:** *haklı* + *-ca* (adverbial/manner suffix)
**Natural English Translation:** justifiably, rightly, with justification
**Usage:** An adverbial form used to describe actions carried out with justification or in a manner that is warranted. Less frequent than *haksızca* but attested in formal and journalistic registers.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

> **Note on gaps:** No commonly attested verbal derivative *\*haklamak* in the sense of "to justify" or "to vindicate" has been identified in standard usage; the concept is expressed through collocations such as *haklı çıkmak* (to be proven right) or *haklı kılmak* (to justify/render justified). The form *haklamak* exists in colloquial Turkish but carries the meaning "to beat" or "to finish off" (someone), which is a separate lexical item unrelated to the truth/right domain.

## P1 Item 5 — haklı

**Base Form:** haklı

*(Derived from hak; derivations of haklı are documented under P1 Item 4 above: haklılık, haklıca. No additional derivations of haklı itself beyond those forms have been identified.)*

---

**Derived Form 1**
**Derived Form:** haklılık
**Transliteration:** haklılık
**Literal Breakdown:** *haklı* + *-lık*
**Natural English Translation:** justification, rightness
**Usage:** See P1 Item 4, Derived Form 3.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2**
**Derived Form:** haklıca
**Transliteration:** haklıca
**Literal Breakdown:** *haklı* + *-ca*
**Natural English Translation:** justifiably, rightly
**Usage:** See P1 Item 4, Derived Form 7.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 3**
**Derived Form:** haklı çıkmak
**Transliteration:** haklı çıkmak
**Literal Breakdown:** *haklı* (justified) + *çıkmak* (to come out, to emerge)
**Natural English Translation:** to be proven right, to turn out to be correct
**Usage:** A fixed collocation used in everyday and formal discourse to describe the outcome in which someone's position is vindicated by events or evidence. *Sonunda haklı çıktı* (in the end, he/she was proven right). Extremely common in spoken and written Turkish.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

> **Note:** *haklı çıkmak* is a collocation rather than a morphological derivation, but it is the primary productive construction built on *haklı* for expressing vindication, and no morphological verbal derivative of *haklı* fills this function.

**Transliteration:** yanlışlama
**Literal Breakdown:** *yanlış* + *-la-* + *-ma* (verbal noun)
**Natural English Translation:** falsification, refutation
**Usage:** The nominalized form of *yanlışlamak*; used in philosophy of science, particularly in Turkish discussions of Popperian falsificationism. *Yanlışlama ilkesi* (the principle of falsification).

---

**Derived Form 4**
**Derived Form:** yanlışlanabilir
**Transliteration:** yanlışlanabilir

**Natural English Translation:** falsifiable
**Usage:** A technical term in philosophy of science, parallel to *doğrulanabilir*; used in Turkish translations and discussions of Popper's criterion of falsifiability. *Yanlışlanabilir hipotez* (falsifiable hypothesis).

**Derived Form 5**
**Derived Form:** yanlışlanabilirlik
**Transliteration:** yanlışlanabilirlik
**Literal Breakdown:** *yanlışlanabilir* + *-lik*
**Natural English Translation:** falsifiability
**Usage:** The abstract noun form; used in philosophy of science as the Turkish rendering of Popper's concept of falsifiability. *Yanlışlanabilirlik ölçütü* (the criterion of falsifiability).

---

**Derived Form 6**
**Derived Form:** yanlışça
**Transliteration:** yanlışça
**Literal Breakdown:** *yanlış* + *-ca* (adverbial/manner suffix)
**Natural English Translation:** wrongly, incorrectly, mistakenly
**Usage:** An adverbial form used to describe actions or judgments carried out in an incorrect or mistaken manner. *Yanlışça anlamak* (to misunderstand, to understand incorrectly). Attested but less frequent than the predicative use of *yanlış*.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 7**

**Transliteration:** yanlışlamak

**Literal Breakdown:** *sahte* + *-ci* (agentive suffix)

**Usage:** Refers to a person who produces fakes or forgeries; used in legal and everyday evaluative contexts. *Sahteci biri* (a fraud, a faker).
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 3**
**Derived Form:** sahtekâr
**Transliteration:** sahtekâr
**Literal Breakdown:** *sahte* + *-kâr* (Persian agentive suffix, "doer of")
**Natural English Translation:** fraud, impostor, cheat, swindler
**Usage:** Refers to a person who deceives or acts fraudulently; carries a stronger moral condemnation than *sahteci* and is more focused on personal character than on the specific act of forgery. *Sahtekâr biri* (a fraud, an impostor).

---

**Derived Form 4**
**Derived Form:** sahtekârlık
**Transliteration:** sahtekârlık

**Natural English Translation:** fraud, deception, imposture, dishonesty
**Usage:** Names the quality or practice of being a fraud or impostor; used in moral and legal contexts. *Sahtekârlık yapmak* (to commit fraud/deception).
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

> **Note on gaps:** No commonly attested verbal derivative *\*sahtelemek* (to fake, to forge) has been identified in standard usage; the concept is expressed through *sahte yapmak*, *taklitini yapmak*, or *sahte belge düzenlemek* rather than a morphological verbal derivative of *sahte*.

## P1 Item 14 — kanıtlamak

**Base Form:** kanıtlamak

---

**Derived Form 1**
**Derived Form:** kanıt
**Transliteration:** kanıt
**Literal Breakdown:** base noun from which *kanıtlamak* is derived; *kanıt* + *-la-* + *-mak*
**Natural English Translation:** proof, evidence
**Usage:** The base noun; used in legal, scientific, and everyday contexts to refer to evidence or proof. *Kanıt sunmak* (to present evidence). Extremely common across registers.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2**
**Derived Form:** kanıtlama
**Transliteration:** kanıtlama
**Literal Breakdown:** *kanıt* + *-la-* + *-ma* (verbal noun)
**Natural English Translation:** proof, demonstration, proving
**Usage:** The nominalized form of *kanıtlamak*; used in legal, scientific, and philosophical contexts to name the act or process of proving something. *Kanıtlama yükü* (burden of proof, parallel to *ispat yükü*).
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 3**
**Derived Form:** kanıtlanmak
**Transliteration:** kanıtlanmak
**Literal Breakdown:** *kanıt* + *-la-* + *-n-* (passive) + *-mak*
**Natural English Translation:** to be proven, to be demonstrated
**Usage:** The passive form; used in scientific and legal registers to describe claims or facts that have been established by evidence. *İddia kanıtlandı* (the claim was proven).
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 4**
**Derived Form:** kanıtlanmış
**Transliteration:** kanıtlanmış
**Literal Breakdown:** *kanıt* + *-la-* + *-n-* + *-mış* (past participle)
**Natural English Translation:** proven, established, demonstrated
**Usage:** The past participial form used as an adjective to describe something that has been established by evidence. *Kanıtlanmış gerçek* (proven fact). Common in scientific and legal registers.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 5**
**Derived Form:** kanıtlayıcı
**Transliteration:** kanıtlayıcı
**Literal Breakdown:** *kanıtla-* + *-yıcı* (agentive/adjectival suffix)
