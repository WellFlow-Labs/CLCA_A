## 2. Substitution Patterns (Narrative)

### 2.1 REVELATION substitutes for epistemic BECOME

In the truth domain, Korean does not use the general inchoative verb 되다 ("to become") to express the coming-to-be-known of a truth-domain state. Instead, a set of three lexically distinct verbs — 밝혀지다 ("to be illuminated, come to light"), 드러나다 ("to surface, be exposed"), and 판명되다 ("to be officially determined") — systematically occupies the epistemic change-of-state slot. These verbs combine with the resultant-state particle -(으)로 and a truth-domain nominal to form the construction [Noun + (으)로 + Verb], as in 가짜로 판명되다 ("to be determined to be fake") and 허위로 밝혀지다 ("to be revealed as false").

The general inchoative 되다 is attested in the truth domain only for non-epistemic state changes, such as 계약이 무효가 되다 ("the contract becomes void"), where the change is administrative or logical rather than a matter of coming to be known. For epistemic contexts — where the change of state consists in a truth-domain fact becoming publicly established — the revelation verbs are the standard and expected forms. The BECOME operator is therefore partially present: it is available for non-epistemic becoming via 되다, but epistemic becoming is systematically expressed through REVELATION constructions.

The three revelation verbs are not interchangeable. P4b notes that 드러나다 carries a stronger sense of unwanted or unintended exposure; 밝혀지다 is the most frequent of the three in journalistic contexts; and 판명되다 carries an official or authoritative determination sense. This semantic differentiation within the REVELATION category is a feature of the Korean system not captured by a single operator label.

### 2.2 DISCONFIRMATION is not distinguished from NEGATION

The P4a and P4b data do not identify any construction in Korean that is specifically dedicated to DISCONFIRMATION as distinct from NEGATION. The constructions used to deny factual status — 사실이 아니다 ("it is not a fact"), 근거가 없다 ("there is no basis"), 그 정보가 정확하지 않다 ("that information is not accurate") — are the same constructions used for general truth-domain negation. There is no dedicated evidential or corrective construction that marks "I am disconfirming a prior claim" as structurally distinct from "this is not the case."

The discourse-framing construction 사실을 말하자면 ("to tell the truth / the fact of the matter is") and 정확히 말하자면 ("to be precise / strictly speaking") function pragmatically as correction or precision markers, and could be described as framing devices that introduce disconfirmatory content. However, these are pragmatic markers, not dedicated DISCONFIRMATION constructions; the disconfirmatory content itself is still expressed through standard negation constructions. DISCONFIRMATION is therefore treated here as SUBSTITUTED BY NEGATION.

### 2.3 NEGATION: analytic strategies are primary; morphological negation is register-restricted

Korean employs both analytic and morphological negation strategies in the truth domain, but they are not in free variation. The analytic strategies — copular negation (이/가 아니다), existential negation (이/가 없다), and verbal negation (-지 않다) — are the primary and broadly productive strategies across all registers. Morphological negation via the prefixes 부-, 비-, and 무- (e.g., 부정확하다, 무효하다) is attested as productive in formal and written registers but is described in P4b as secondary and register-restricted relative to the syntactic strategies.

The existential negation frame (이/가 없다) is the standard negative predication strategy specifically for abstract quality nouns formed with -성 (진정성이 없다, 신빙성이 없다, 근거가 없다). This class of nouns does not take -하다 as a predicative suffix in their nominalized form, so the existential frame fills a structural slot that copular negation cannot occupy for these items.

### 2.4 Asymmetry between truth-pole and falsity-pole constructions

A consistent asymmetry runs through the Korean truth-domain construction inventory. At the agentive nominalization level, the falsity pole is lexicalized (거짓말쟁이, "habitual liar"; 위조범, "forger"; 위선자, "hypocrite") while the truth pole has no parallel lexicalized agentive form. The concept "truth-teller" requires periphrastic expression: 진실을 말하는 사람 ("a person who tells the truth") or 정직한 사람 ("an honest person"). This asymmetry is confirmed in P4b as a genuine structural feature, not an oversight.

A related asymmetry appears in the agentive suffix distribution: the native Korean suffix -쟁이 (pejorative, habitual agent) attaches only to 거짓말 in the truth domain; no parallel positive-pole agentive with -쟁이 is attested. The Sino-Korean suffix -자 (者) in this domain also tends toward the negative pole (위선자, "hypocrite").

At the adnominal level, the construction 근거 없는 ("unsubstantiated") is described as a near-compound modifier with high fixedness, while the positive counterpart 근거 있는 is attested but described as less fixed. This suggests a degree of asymmetry in the conventionalization of negative versus positive evaluative modifiers, though both forms are rated [CORE].

### 2.5 CAUSATION (factual) is expressed through transitive verb selection, not morphological causativization

Korean has no productive morphological causative suffix that applies to truth-domain adjectives or nouns. The causative meaning — causing a truth-domain state to come about, be revealed, or be concealed — is expressed entirely through the selection of semantically appropriate transitive verbs: 밝히다 ("to reveal"), 숨기다 ("to conceal"), 왜곡하다 ("to distort"), 외면하다 ("to avert from"). These verbs take the truth-domain noun as direct object. The -화하다 suffix (무효화하다, 합법화하다) is the closest available morphological pattern but is restricted to Sino-Korean bases and expresses "rendering something into a state" rather than a general causative.

For stative truth-domain adjectives (정확하다, 진실하다), no causative form is identified in the data. Causation of a stative property is expressed periphrastically (정확하게 만들다, "to make accurate"), but this periphrastic form is noted in P4a as existing without being listed as a distinct truth-domain construction.

### 2.6 VERIFICATION: no dedicated construction attested

No construction in P4a or P4b is described as specifically encoding the act of verifying or checking the truth of a claim. The concept is not represented as a distinct construction type in the data. The closest available expressions would involve general transitive verb collocations (e.g., 사실을 확인하다, "to confirm/check a fact"), but this collocation is not listed or described in P4a or P4b as a truth-domain construction. VERIFICATION is therefore treated as a structural gap in the attested data.

## 3. Missingness Summary

### 3.1 Structural Gaps

- **VERIFICATION**: No construction in P4a or P4b is identified as encoding the act of verifying or checking the truth of a claim. The operator is absent from the attested construction inventory. **MISSING (structural gap).**

- **DISCONFIRMATION as distinct from NEGATION**: No construction is identified that specifically marks the act of disconfirming a prior claim as structurally distinct from general negation. The two functions are expressed through the same constructions. **MISSING as a distinct operator; SUBSTITUTED BY NEGATION.**

- **Epistemic BECOME as distinct from REVELATION**: The general inchoative 되다 is present for non-epistemic state changes, but epistemic becoming (a truth-domain state coming to be known) is expressed exclusively through the revelation verbs 밝혀지다, 드러나다, and 판명되다. There is no construction that expresses epistemic becoming without simultaneously encoding the revelation/discovery aspect. **SUBSTITUTED BY REVELATION for the epistemic subtype.**

### 3.2 Lexical Gaps

- **Agentive noun for "truth-teller"**: No lexicalized single-word agentive noun meaning "truth-teller" or "honest person (as habitual agent)" is attested. The concept requires periphrastic expression: 진실을 말하는 사람 or 정직한 사람. **Lexical gap at the agentive nominalization level.**

- **Causative forms of stative truth-domain adjectives**: No causative form of adjectives such as 정확하다 or 진실하다 is attested. The concept "to make [something] accurate/true" requires periphrastic expression (정확하게 만들다). **Lexical gap: no derived causative form for stative truth-domain adjectives.**

- **-기 nominalization in the truth domain**: The -기 nominalization suffix is noted in P4a as attested but is not exemplified with any truth-domain vocabulary. Its applicability to this domain is unconfirmed by the data. **Lexical gap: no truth-domain -기 nominalization is documented.**

### 3.3 Morphosyntactic Gaps

- **Morphological causative suffix**: Korean has no productive morphological causative suffix that applies to truth-domain adjectives or nouns. The -화하다 pattern is the closest available form but is restricted to Sino-Korean nominal bases and does not cover the full range of causative meanings. **Morphosyntactic gap: no general morphological causative in this domain.**

- **Single inchoative morpheme for epistemic change of state**: Korean has no single inchoative morpheme equivalent to "become + adjective" in the epistemic truth domain. The inchoative meaning is expressed periphrastically through lexically distinct revelation verbs. **Morphosyntactic gap: no morphological inchoative for epistemic state change.**

### 3.4 Asymmetric Gaps (only negative or only positive forms missing)

- **Agentive nominalization**: Negative-pole agentives are lexicalized (거짓말쟁이, 위조범, 위선자); positive-pole agentive ("truth-teller") is absent. **Asymmetric gap: positive pole only.**

- **Adnominal modifier fixedness**: 근거 없는 ("unsubstantiated") is described as a near-compound with high fixedness; the positive counterpart 근거 있는 is attested but described as less conventionalized. **Asymmetric gap: partial, at the level of conventionalization rather than grammaticality.**

- **Stative predicate with 되다**: 모순되다 ("to be contradictory") has no attested 하다 counterpart (모순하다 is not attested). 거짓되다 ("to be false/deceitful") is rated [MARGINAL] and restricted to literary registers, while no parallel positive-pole 되다 form (e.g., *진실되다 in common use) is described as [CORE]. 참되다 is noted as literary/religious. **Asymmetric gap: the 되다 stative pattern is more available at the negative/falsity pole in common registers.**

- **-(으)ㅁ nominalization register**: The native Korean -(으)ㅁ nominalizations (올바름, 옳음) are rated [MARGINAL] and restricted to philosophical and literary registers. No parallel [MARGINAL] rating is assigned to any Sino-Korean -성 nominalization, which are all [CORE]. This is not a gap in the negative/positive sense but an asymmetry between base types: Sino-Korean bases have [CORE] abstract nominalizations; native Korean bases have only [MARGINAL] abstract nominalizations in this domain.
