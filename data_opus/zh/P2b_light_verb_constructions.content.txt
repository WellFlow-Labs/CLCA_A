# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2b Compositional Constructions
# Language: Chinese (zh)

---

## SCOPE NOTE

This step documents **multi-word constructions** (phrasal collocations, verb–object patterns, fixed adverbial frames, and syntactic templates) involving P1 items. It **excludes**:
- Compounds and idioms already catalogued in P1/P1b (真假, 真伪, 实事求是, 名副其实, etc.) — these are lexicalized words/idioms, not productive multi-word constructions.
- Derivational forms (handled in P2a).

Constructions are listed only where natural and attested. Gaps are preserved explicitly.

---

## CONSTRUCTIONS BY BASE FORM

---

### 1. 真 (zhēn)

**Base Form:** 真 (zhēn)
**Example:** 这是真的吗？
**Transliteration:** zhè shì zhēn de ma?
**English:** "Is this true / real?"
**Usage notes:** The 是…的 frame around 真 forms the standard predicate-of-truth question and assertion (是真的 / 不是真的). Pervasive in all spoken registers.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

**Base Form:** 真 (zhēn)
**Example:** 真有意思
**Transliteration:** zhēn yǒu yìsi
**English:** "really interesting"
**Usage notes:** 真 as a preverbal degree adverb intensifying a following predicate. Extremely common in speech; this is the adverbial-intensifier function (distinct from the truth sense).
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### 2. 假 (jiǎ)

**Base Form:** 假 (jiǎ)
**Example:** 这是假的
**Transliteration:** zhè shì jiǎ de
**English:** "This is fake."
**Usage notes:** Mirror of 是真的; the 是假的 predicate frame is the standard assertion of falsity/fakeness. All registers.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

**Base Form:** 假 (jiǎ)
**Example:** 假装没看见
**Transliteration:** jiǎzhuāng méi kànjiàn
**English:** "pretend not to have seen"
**Usage notes:** 假装 + clause is a productive frame for feigned action. (假装 borders on a lexicalized verb; included as the head of a clausal pattern.)
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### 3. 真实 (zhēnshí)

**Base Form:** 真实 (zhēnshí)
**Example:** 真实的情况 / 真实地反映
**Transliteration:** zhēnshí de qíngkuàng / zhēnshí de fǎnyìng
**English:** "the real situation / to reflect truthfully"
**Usage notes:** 真实 enters standard attributive (真实的+N) and adverbial (真实地+V) frames. These are general modifier slots rather than fixed constructions.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### 4. 真理 (zhēnlǐ)

**Base Form:** 真理 (zhēnlǐ)
**Example:** 追求真理
**Transliteration:** zhuīqiú zhēnlǐ
**English:** "to pursue truth"
**Usage notes:** 真理 collocates with verbs of seeking/grasping (追求真理, 掌握真理, 坚持真理) in philosophical/political register. The verb–object pattern is conventionalized.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

**Base Form:** 真理 (zhēnlǐ)
**Example:** 真理面前人人平等
**Transliteration:** zhēnlǐ miànqián rénrén píngděng
**English:** "everyone is equal before the truth"
**Usage notes:** A conventionalized aphoristic frame in formal/rhetorical register. Somewhat formulaic rather than fully fixed.
**Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

### 5. 真相 (zhēnxiàng)

**Base Form:** 真相 (zhēnxiàng)
**Example:** 揭露真相 / 查明真相
**Transliteration:** jiēlù zhēnxiàng / chámíng zhēnxiàng
**English:** "to expose the truth / to ascertain the truth"
**Usage notes:** Strong verb–object collocations with verbs of revealing/discovering (揭露, 查明, 隐瞒, 掩盖真相). Pervasive in news/investigative register.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### 6. 事实 (shìshí)

**Base Form:** 事实 (shìshí)
**Example:** 事实上
**Transliteration:** shìshí shàng
**English:** "in fact, as a matter of fact"
**Usage notes:** Fixed adverbial frame (事实 + 上) functioning as a discourse connective. Very high frequency in all registers.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

**Base Form:** 事实 (shìshí)
**Example:** 摆事实，讲道理
**Transliteration:** bǎi shìshí, jiǎng dàolǐ
**English:** "present the facts and reason things out"
**Usage notes:** Conventionalized paired construction in argumentative/persuasive register. The verb–object 摆事实 is the productive part.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

**Base Form:** 事实 (shìshí)
**Example:** 事实证明
**Transliteration:** shìshí zhèngmíng
**English:** "facts prove (that)…"
**Usage notes:** Fixed sentential frame introducing an evidential clause. Common in expository and political discourse.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### 7. 正确 (zhèngquè)

**Base Form:** 正确 (zhèngquè)
**Example:** 完全正确
**Transliteration:** wánquán zhèngquè
**English:** "completely correct"
**Usage notes:** Standard degree-adverb + adjective frame (完全/绝对正确). General modification, conventionalized in evaluative/educational register.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

**Base Form:** 正确 (zhèngquè)
**Example:** 做出正确的选择
**Transliteration:** zuòchū zhèngquè de xuǎnzé
**English:** "to make the correct choice"
**Usage notes:** 正确 in attributive position with decision/judgment nouns (正确的决定/选择/方向). Conventionalized collocation.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### 8. 错误 (cuòwù)

**Base Form:** 错误 (cuòwù)
**Example:** 犯错误
**Transliteration:** fàn cuòwù
**English:** "to make a mistake / commit an error"
**Usage notes:** Highly fixed verb–object collocation (犯 + 错误). Pervasive across all registers.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

**Base Form:** 错误 (cuòwù)
**Example:** 纠正错误 / 改正错误
**Transliteration:** jiūzhèng cuòwù / gǎizhèng cuòwù
**English:** "to correct a mistake"
**Usage notes:** Verb–object collocations with verbs of correction. Common in educational and administrative register.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### 9. 对 (duì)

**Base Form:** 对 (duì)
**Example:** 你说得对
**Transliteration:** nǐ shuō de duì
**English:** "What you say is right / you're right."
**Usage notes:** The V + 得 + 对 complement frame evaluates a statement/action as correct. Pervasive in speech.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

**Base Form:** 对 (duì)
**Example:** 对不对？
**Transliteration:** duì bù duì?
**English:** "Right or not? / isn't that so?"
**Usage notes:** The A-not-A tag-question frame on 对, used to solicit confirmation. Extremely common colloquially.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### 10. 错 (cuò)

**Base Form:** 错 (cuò)
**Example:** 说错了 / 做错了
**Transliteration:** shuō cuò le / zuò cuò le
**English:** "said it wrong / did it wrong"
**Usage notes:** 错 as a resultative verb complement (V + 错 + 了) marking the action as erroneous. Highly productive in speech.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

**Base Form:** 错 (cuò)
**Example:** 没错
**Transliteration:** méi cuò
**English:** "that's right / no mistake"
**Usage notes:** Fixed negated affirmation (没 + 错) used as a confirmatory interjection. Pervasive colloquially.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### 11. 准确 (zhǔnquè)

**Transliteration:** zhǔnquè de shuō

## SUMMARY OF ATTESTED CONSTRUCTION TYPES

**1. Verb–object collocations (V + N):**
- 追求/掌握/坚持 真理
- 揭露/查明/隐瞒/掩盖 真相
- 犯 错误; 纠正/改正 错误
- 辨别/鉴定 真伪
- 摆 事实
- 如实 + V (report verbs)

**2. Fixed adverbial discourse frames (N/Adj + 上 / 地说):**
- 事实上
- 本质上
- 准确地说

**3. Predicate / 是…的 truth-assertion frames:**
- 是真的 / 是假的 / 这是真的吗

**4. Resultative & complement frames (V + 错/对/逼真/地道 + 得/了):**
- 说错了 / 做错了; 没错
- 说得对 / 对不对
- 画得逼真; 说得地道

**5. Comparative / likeness frames:**
- 像…一样; 好像
- 与…相似; 相似之处

**6. Attributive collocations (Adj + 的 + N):**
- 正确的选择/决定
- 可靠的消息
- 正宗(的)川菜
- 真挚的感情/友谊

**7. Conventionalized aphoristic / paired frames:**
- 摆事实，讲道理
- 透过现象看本质
- 诚实守信
- 真理面前人人平等 [MARGINAL]

## OBSERVED ASYMMETRIES AND GAPS (preserved, not filled)

1. **Concrete vs. abstract truth nouns pattern differently.** 真相 and 真理 take rich verb–object collocations (reveal/seek), whereas 事实 specializes into a discourse-connective frame (事实上, 事实证明). The verb classes differ: 真相 with *revealing* verbs, 真理 with *pursuit* verbs.

2. **Monosyllabic 对/错 build complement/tag constructions; their bisyllabic counterparts 正确/错误 build verb–object and attributive collocations instead.** The two registers do not share construction types (no *犯错 in the same fixed sense as 犯错误; no *说得正确 paralleling 说得对).

3. **Several evaluative adjectives yield no construction beyond ordinary modifier slots:** 可信, 虚伪 generate no conventionalized phrasal frame (their productivity is derivational, not constructional). Recorded as explicit gaps.

4. **Authentication terms split by domain:** 真伪 → discrimination verbs (legal/scholarly); 正宗/地道 → attributive food/culture frames (culinary/colloquial). No overlap in construction type.

5. **确凿 has an exceptionally narrow constructional range**, effectively fixed to 证据/事实 as subject. This is near-collocational lock-in rather than a productive frame.

6. **All four-character idioms (名副其实, 货真价实, 名不副实) and the binomial-derived items generate no further multi-word constructions** — they are constructional endpoints.

7. **真诚/真实/真挚 differ in collocational breadth despite shared 真:** 真实 modifies situations/accounts broadly, 真挚 is locked to emotion/relationship nouns, 真诚 to attitude/conduct. The shared root does not predict shared constructional range.
