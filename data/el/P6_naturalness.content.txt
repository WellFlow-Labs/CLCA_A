# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P6 CHANGE, CAUSATION, AND STABILITY
# Language: Greek (el)

---

## 1. BECOMING TRUE / TRUTH EMERGENCE

### 1.1 Truth Revelation (Intransitive)

**Pattern:** [TRUTH NOUN-NOM] + βγαίνω (vgaíno) "come out" + [LOCATIVE]

**Examples:**
- **βγαίνει η αλήθεια στη φόρα** (vgaíni i alítheia sti fóra) — "the truth comes out/to light"
- **βγήκε η αλήθεια** (vgíke i alítheia) — "the truth came out" (aorist)
- **θα βγει η αλήθεια** (tha vgi i alítheia) — "the truth will come out" (future)

**Status:** [CORE]
**Notes:** Highly idiomatic construction treating truth as hidden entity that emerges. στη φόρα (literally "to the surface") is the standard locative complement. This is the unmarked expression for truth becoming known without external agent.

---

**Pattern:** [TRUTH NOUN-NOM] + έρχομαι (érchome) "come" + στο φως (sto fos) "to light"

**Examples:**
- **έρχεται η αλήθεια στο φως** (érchete i alítheia sto fos) — "the truth comes to light"
- **ήρθε η αλήθεια στο φως** (írthe i alítheia sto fos) — "the truth came to light" (aorist)

**Status:** [CORE]
**Notes:** Alternative to βγαίνω construction, using φως "light" as metaphorical target. Slightly more formal register than βγαίνει στη φόρα.

---

**Pattern:** [TRUTH NOUN-NOM] + αποκαλύπτομαι (apokalýptome) "be revealed" (passive)

**Examples:**
- **αποκαλύπτεται η αλήθεια** (apokalýptete i alítheia) — "the truth is revealed"
- **αποκαλύφθηκε η αλήθεια** (apokalýfthike i alítheia) — "the truth was revealed" (aorist passive)

**Status:** [CORE]
**Notes:** Passive construction implying revelation without specifying agent. More formal than βγαίνω constructions. Common in journalistic and narrative contexts.

---

**Pattern:** [TRUTH NOUN-NOM] + γίνομαι (gínome) + γνωστός (gnostós) "known"

**Examples:**
- **γίνεται γνωστή η αλήθεια** (gínete gností i alítheia) — "the truth becomes known"
- **έγινε γνωστή η αλήθεια** (égine gností i alítheia) — "the truth became known" (aorist)

**Status:** [CORE]
**Notes:** Standard inchoative construction with γίνομαι + adjective. Less idiomatic than βγαίνω constructions but more productive with other adjectives (φανερός "apparent," σαφής "clear," etc.).

---

### 1.2 Statement Becoming True (Verification)

**Pattern:** [STATEMENT NP-NOM] + αποδεικνύομαι (apodikníome) + αληθινός (alithinós) "prove to be true"

**Examples:**
- **αποδεικνύεται αληθινή η κατηγορία** (apodikníete alithiní i katigoría) — "the accusation proves to be true"
- **αποδείχθηκε αληθινή** (apodíchthike alithiní) — "it proved to be true" (aorist passive)

**Status:** [CORE]
**Notes:** Passive of αποδεικνύω "prove," used when statement's truth is established through evidence or events. Adjective agrees with subject.

---

**Pattern:** [STATEMENT NP-NOM] + επιβεβαιώνομαι (epivaióme) "be confirmed"

**Examples:**
- **επιβεβαιώνεται η πληροφορία** (epivaióte i pliροforía) — "the information is confirmed"
- **επιβεβαιώθηκε** (epivaiόthike) — "it was confirmed" (aorist passive)

**Status:** [CORE]
**Notes:** Passive of επιβεβαιώνω "confirm." Common in journalistic contexts for information verification. Does not take truth-domain adjective; confirmation itself implies truth.

---

**Pattern:** [STATEMENT NP-NOM] + βγαίνω (vgaíno) + σωστός (sostós) "turn out correct"

**Examples:**
- **βγήκε σωστό** (vgíke sostó) — "it turned out correct"
- **βγήκαν σωστές οι προβλέψεις** (vgíkan sostés i provlépseis) — "the predictions turned out correct"

**Status:** [CORE]
**Notes:** Colloquial construction for predictions, guesses, or claims being verified as correct. βγαίνω here means "turn out" rather than "come out."

---

### 1.3 Object Becoming Genuine/Authentic

**NO COMMON EXPRESSION EXISTS** for objects spontaneously becoming genuine. Authenticity is typically established through verification (see causative constructions below) rather than spontaneous change.

## 2. BECOMING FALSE / FALSITY EMERGENCE

### 2.1 Lie Detection/Exposure

**Pattern:** [LIE NOUN-NOM] + αποκαλύπτομαι (apokalýptome) "be revealed/exposed"

**Examples:**
- **αποκαλύπτεται το ψέμα** (apokalýptete to pséma) — "the lie is revealed/exposed"
- **αποκαλύφθηκε το ψέμα** (apokalýfthike to pséma) — "the lie was exposed" (aorist)

**Status:** [CORE]
**Notes:** Parallel to truth revelation but less common. Lies more typically described as being actively exposed by agents (see causative section).

---

**Pattern:** [LIE NOUN-NOM] + βγαίνω (vgaíno) + στη φόρα (sti fóra) "come to light"

**Examples:**
- **βγήκε το ψέμα στη φόρα** (vgíke to pséma sti fóra) — "the lie came to light"
- **βγαίνουν τα ψέματα στη φόρα** (vgaínoun ta psémata sti fóra) — "the lies come to light"

**Status:** [CORE]
**Notes:** Same construction as truth emergence. Can be used for lies being discovered, though less frequent than for truth.

---

### 2.2 Statement Becoming False (Invalidation)

**Pattern:** [STATEMENT/CLAIM NP-NOM] + αποδεικνύομαι (apodikníome) + ψευδής (pseudís) "prove to be false"

**Examples:**
- **αποδεικνύεται ψευδής ο ισχυρισμός** (apodikníete psevdís o ischyrismós) — "the claim proves to be false"
- **αποδείχθηκε ψευδής** (apodíchthike psevdís) — "it proved to be false" (aorist)

**Status:** [CORE]
**Notes:** Parallel to becoming true construction. ψευδής (formal) or ψεύτικος (colloquial) used as predicate adjective.

---

**Pattern:** [STATEMENT NP-NOM] + διαψεύδομαι (diapsévdome) "be disproved"

**Examples:**
- **διαψεύδεται η φήμη** (diapsévdete i fími) — "the rumor is disproved"
- **διαψεύστηκε** (diapsévstike) — "it was disproved" (aorist passive)

**Status:** [CORE]
**Notes:** Passive of διαψεύδω "disprove." Specifically marks refutation of claims or rumors. More formal register.

---

### 2.3 Document/Object Becoming Invalid

**Pattern:** [DOCUMENT NP-NOM] + γίνομαι (gínome) + άκυρος (ákyros) "become invalid"

**Examples:**
- **γίνεται άκυρο το έγγραφο** (gínete ákyro to éngrafo) — "the document becomes invalid"
- **έγινε άκυρη η ψήφος** (égine ákyri i psífos) — "the vote became invalid" (aorist)

**Status:** [CORE]
**Notes:** Standard inchoative with γίνομαι. Common in administrative/legal contexts for documents losing validity.

---

**Pattern:** [DOCUMENT NP-NOM] + ακυρώνομαι (akyróme) "be invalidated/canceled" (passive)

**Examples:**
- **ακυρώνεται το συμβόλαιο** (akyróte to symvóleo) — "the contract is canceled/invalidated"
- **ακυρώθηκε** (akyrόthike) — "it was canceled" (aorist passive)

**Status:** [CORE]
**Notes:** Passive of ακυρώνω. Implies external agent causing invalidation, though agent may be unspecified.

---

## 3. BECOMING CORRECT / ERROR CORRECTION

### 3.1 Answer/Statement Becoming Correct

**Pattern:** [ANSWER NP-NOM] + γίνομαι (gínome) + σωστός (sostós) "become correct"

**Examples:**
- **γίνεται σωστή η απάντηση** (gínete sostí i apántisi) — "the answer becomes correct"
- **έγινε σωστό** (égine sostó) — "it became correct" (aorist)

**Status:** [MARGINAL]
**Notes:** Uncommon construction. Answers are typically corrected by agents (see causative) rather than spontaneously becoming correct. More natural to say "I corrected the answer" than "the answer became correct."

---

### 3.2 Error Being Corrected (Passive)

**Pattern:** [ERROR NP-NOM] + διορθώνομαι (diorthόme) "be corrected" (passive)

**Examples:**
- **διορθώνεται το λάθος** (diorthόte to láthos) — "the mistake is corrected"
- **διορθώθηκε** (diorthόthike) — "it was corrected" (aorist passive)

**Status:** [CORE]
**Notes:** Passive of διορθώνω. Common construction implying correction without specifying agent. Used in editorial and educational contexts.

---

**Pattern:** [ERROR NP-NOM] + σωστεύομαι (sostévome) "be corrected" (passive)

**Examples:**
- **σωστεύεται το λάθος** (sostévete to láthos) — "the mistake is corrected"
- **σωστεύτηκε** (sostéftike) — "it was corrected" (aorist passive)

**Status:** [CORE]
**Notes:** Colloquial alternative to διορθώνομαι. Less formal register.

---

## 4. BECOMING WRONG / ERROR EMERGENCE

### 4.1 Statement/Calculation Becoming Wrong

**Pattern:** [CALCULATION/ANSWER NP-NOM] + βγαίνω (vgaíno) + λάθος (láthos) "turn out wrong"

**Examples:**
- **βγήκε λάθος ο υπολογισμός** (vgíke láthos o ypologismós) — "the calculation turned out wrong"
- **βγήκαν λάθος** (vgíkan láthos) — "they turned out wrong" (aorist)

**Status:** [CORE]
**Notes:** Common construction for calculations, predictions, or answers being discovered to be incorrect. λάθος functions as predicate adjective (invariant).

---

**Pattern:** [PERSON NP-NOM] + αποδεικνύομαι (apodikníome) + λάθος (láthos) "prove to be wrong"

**Examples:**
- **αποδείχθηκα λάθος** (apodíchthika láthos) — "I proved to be wrong"
- **αποδεικνύεται λάθος** (apodikníete láthos) — "he/she proves to be wrong"

**Status:** [CORE]
**Notes:** Used when person's position or claim is shown to be incorrect. λάθος invariant in this construction.

---

## 5. CAUSATIVE CONSTRUCTIONS: MAKING TRUE/VERIFIED

### 5.1 Agent Reveals Truth

**Pattern:** [AGENT NP-NOM] + αποκαλύπτω (apokalýpto) + [TRUTH NOUN-ACC]

**Examples:**
- **αποκαλύπτω την αλήθεια** (apokalýpto tin alítheia) — "I reveal the truth"
- **αποκάλυψε την αλήθεια** (apokálypse tin alítheia) — "he/she revealed the truth" (aorist)

**Status:** [CORE]
**Notes:** Standard transitive verb for truth revelation by agent. Common in investigative and narrative contexts.

---

**Pattern:** [AGENT NP-NOM] + φέρνω (férno) + [TRUTH NOUN-ACC] + στο φως (sto fos) "bring to light"

**Examples:**
- **φέρνω την αλήθεια στο φως** (férno tin alítheia sto fos) — "I bring the truth to light"
- **έφερε την αλήθεια στο φως** (éfere tin alítheia sto fos) — "he/she brought the truth to light" (aorist)

**Status:** [CORE]
**Notes:** Metaphorical construction with φέρνω "bring." Emphasizes agent's active role in revelation.

---

**Pattern:** [AGENT NP-NOM] + λέω (léo) + [TRUTH NOUN-ACC]

**Examples:**
- **λέω την αλήθεια** (léo tin alítheia) — "I tell the truth"
- **είπε την αλήθεια** (ípe tin alítheia) — "he/she told the truth" (aorist)

**Status:** [CORE]
**Notes:** Basic light verb construction. Unmarked expression for truth-telling as speech act.

---

### 5.2 Agent Verifies/Confirms Statement

**Pattern:** [AGENT NP-NOM] + επιβεβαιώνω (epivaióno) + [STATEMENT NP-ACC]

**Examples:**
- **επιβεβαιώνω την πληροφορία** (epivaióno tin pliροforía) — "I confirm the information"
- **επιβεβαίωσε την είδηση** (epivαíose tin éidisi) — "he/she confirmed the news" (aorist)

**Status:** [CORE]
**Notes:** Standard verb for confirmation/verification. Common in journalistic and administrative contexts.

---

**Pattern:** [AGENT NP-NOM] + βεβαιώνω (vevaióno) + [STATEMENT/DOCUMENT NP-ACC]

**Examples:**
- **βεβαιώνω την πληροφορία** (vevaióno tin pliροforía) — "I certify the information"
- **βεβαίωσε τη γνησιότητα** (vevaíose ti gnisiótita) — "he/she certified the authenticity" (aorist)

**Status:** [CORE]
**Notes:** More formal than επιβεβαιώνω. Implies official or authoritative confirmation. Common in legal/administrative contexts.

---

**Pattern:** [AGENT NP-NOM] + αποδεικνύω (apodikníο) + ότι (óti) [CLAUSE]

**Examples:**
- **αποδεικνύω ότι είναι αλήθεια** (apodikníο óti íne alítheia) — "I prove that it is true"
- **απέδειξε ότι είχε δίκιο** (apédixe óti íche díkio) — "he/she proved that he/she was right" (aorist)

**Status:** [CORE]
**Notes:** Standard verb for proving truth of proposition. Takes ότι-clause complement.

---

### 5.3 Agent Authenticates Object

**Pattern:** [AGENT NP-NOM] + βεβαιώνω (vevaióno) + [AUTHENTICITY NOUN-ACC] + [OBJECT GEN]

**Examples:**
- **βεβαιώνω τη γνησιότητα του εγγράφου** (vevaióno ti gnisiótita tou engráfou) — "I certify the authenticity of the document"
- **βεβαίωσε την αυθεντικότητα** (vevaíose tin afthentikótita) — "he/she certified the authenticity" (aorist)

**Status:** [CORE]
**Notes:** Official certification of authenticity. Genitive object typical in this construction.

---

**Pattern:** [AGENT NP-NOM] + πιστοποιώ (pistopoió) + [AUTHENTICITY NOUN-ACC]

**Examples:**
- **πιστοποιώ τη γνησιότητα** (pistopoió ti gnisiótita) — "I certify the authenticity"
- **πιστοποίησε** (pistopoíise) — "he/she certified" (aorist)

**Status:** [CORE]
**Notes:** Alternative to βεβαιώνω, specifically for certification. Common in commercial and administrative contexts.

---

## 6. CAUSATIVE CONSTRUCTIONS: MAKING FALSE/INVALID

### 6.1 Agent Exposes Lie

**Pattern:** [AGENT NP-NOM] + ξεσκεπάζω (xeskepázo) + [LIE NOUN-ACC]

**Examples:**
- **ξεσκεπάζω το ψέμα** (xeskepázo to pséma) — "I expose/uncover the lie"
- **ξεσκέπασε το ψέμα** (xesképase to pséma) — "he/she exposed the lie" (aorist)

**Status:** [CORE]
**Notes:** Metaphorical "uncovering." Common in investigative and critical discourse.

---

**Pattern:** [AGENT NP-NOM] + πιάνω (piáno) + [PERSON ACC] + στο ψέμα (sto pséma)

**Examples:**
- **πιάνω κάποιον στο ψέμα** (piáno kápion sto pséma) — "I catch someone in a lie"
- **τον έπιασε στο ψέμα** (ton épiase sto pséma) — "he/she caught him in a lie" (aorist)

**Status:** [CORE]
**Notes:** Highly idiomatic. στο ψέμα obligatory. Common in narrative and confrontational contexts.

---

### 6.2 Agent Disproves Statement

**Pattern:** [AGENT NP-NOM] + διαψεύδω (diapsévdo) + [CLAIM NP-ACC]

**Examples:**
- **διαψεύδω τη φήμη** (diapsévdo ti fími) — "I disprove the rumor"
- **διέψευσε τους ισχυρισμούς** (diépsevse tous ischyrismούs) — "he/she disproved the claims" (aorist)

**Status:** [CORE]
**Notes:** Formal verb for refutation. Common in journalistic and academic contexts.

---

**Pattern:** [AGENT NP-NOM] + αποδεικνύω (apodikníο) + [STATEMENT ACC] + ψευδής (pseudís)

**Examples:**
- **αποδεικνύω την κατηγορία ψευδή** (apodikníο tin katigoría psevdí) — "I prove the accusation false"
- **απέδειξε τον ισχυρισμό ψευδή** (apédixe ton ischyrismó psevdí) — "he/she proved the claim false" (aorist)

**Status:** [CORE]
**Notes:** Transitive construction with double accusative (object + predicate adjective). Formal register.

---

### 6.3 Agent Invalidates Document

**Pattern:** [AGENT NP-NOM] + ακυρώνω (akyróno) + [DOCUMENT NP-ACC]

**Examples:**
- **ακυρώνω το έγγραφο** (akyróno to éngrafo) — "I invalidate/cancel the document"
- **ακύρωσε το συμβόλαιο** (akýrose to symvóleo) — "he/she canceled the contract" (aorist)

**Status:** [CORE]
**Notes:** Standard administrative/legal verb for invalidation. Implies institutional authority.

---

**Pattern:** [AGENT NP-NOM] + ακυρώνω (akyróno) + [VOTE/BALLOT NP-ACC]

**Examples:**
- **ακυρώνω την ψήφο** (akyróno tin psífo) — "I invalidate the vote"
- **ακύρωσαν τις ψήφους** (akýrosan tis psífous) — "they invalidated the votes" (aorist)

**Status:** [CORE]
**Notes:** Electoral context. Common in voting and election discourse.

---

## 7. CAUSATIVE CONSTRUCTIONS: CORRECTING ERRORS

### 7.1 Agent Corrects Mistake

**Pattern:** [AGENT NP-NOM] + διορθώνω (diorthόno) + [ERROR NP-ACC]

**Examples:**
- **διορθώνω το λάθος** (diorthόno to láthos) — "I correct the mistake"
- **διόρθωσε τα λάθη** (diόrthose ta láthi) — "he/she corrected the mistakes" (aorist)

**Status:** [CORE]
**Notes:** Standard correction verb. Common in educational and editorial contexts.

---

**Pattern:** [AGENT NP-NOM] + σωστεύω (sostévo) + [ERROR/ANSWER NP-ACC]

**Examples:**
- **σωστεύω την απάντηση** (sostévo tin apántisi) — "I correct the answer"
- **σώστεψε το λάθος** (sόstepse to láthos) — "he/she corrected the mistake" (aorist)

**Status:** [CORE]
**Notes:** Colloquial alternative to διορθώνω. Less formal register.

---

### 7.2 Agent Makes Something Correct

**Pattern:** [AGENT NP-NOM] + κάνω (káno) + [OBJECT ACC] + σωστό (sostó)

**Examples:**
- **κάνω το λάθος σωστό** (káno to láthos sostó) — "I make the mistake correct" (= correct it)
- **το έκανε σωστό** (to ékane sostó) — "he/she made it correct" (aorist)

**Status:** [MARGINAL]
**Notes:** Less common than διορθώνω/σωστεύω. Analytic causative with κάνω + adjective.

---

## 8. CAUSATIVE CONSTRUCTIONS: CREATING ERRORS

### 8.1 Agent Makes Mistake

**Pattern:** [AGENT NP-NOM] + κάνω (káno) + λάθος (láthos)

**Examples:**
- **κάνω λάθος** (káno láthos) — "I make a mistake"
- **έκανε λάθος** (ékane láthos) — "he/she made a mistake" (aorist)

**Status:** [CORE]
**Notes:** Standard light verb construction for error-making. Extremely high-frequency.

---

**Pattern:** [AGENT NP-NOM] + κάνω (káno) + [SPECIFIC ERROR NP-ACC]

**Examples:**
- **κάνω ορθογραφικό λάθος** (káno orthografikó láthos) — "I make a spelling error"
- **έκανε υπολογιστικό λάθος** (ékane ypologistikó láthos) — "he/she made a calculation error" (aorist)

**Status:** [CORE]
**Notes:** Extension with specific error types. Common in educational and technical contexts.

---

### 8.2 Agent Causes Someone to Be Wrong

**NO COMMON DIRECT CAUSATIVE EXISTS** for making someone be wrong. Greek typically uses indirect constructions:

**Pattern:** [AGENT NP-NOM] + παραπλανώ (paraplanό) + [PERSON ACC] "mislead"

**Examples:**
- **με παραπλάνησε** (me paraplánise) — "he/she misled me"
- **παραπλανούν το κοινό** (paraplanούn to kinó) — "they mislead the public"

**Status:** [CORE]
**Notes:** Indirect causation of error through misleading. Does not directly mean "cause to be wrong" but implies it.

---

## 9. PSYCHOLOGICAL CAUSATIVES: CERTAINTY/DOUBT

### 9.1 Making Someone Certain

**Pattern:** [STIMULUS NP-NOM] + κάνω (káno) + [PERSON ACC] + βέβαιος (vévaios)

**Examples:**
- **αυτό με κάνει βέβαιο** (aftó me káni vévaio) — "this makes me certain"
- **τον έκανε βέβαιο** (ton ékane vévaio) — "it made him certain" (aorist)

**Status:** [CORE]
**Notes:** Standard psychological causative. Object clitic (με, τον) obligatory. Adjective agrees with object.

---

**Pattern:** [AGENT NP-NOM] + βεβαιώνω (vevaióno) + [PERSON ACC] + ότι (óti) [CLAUSE]

**Examples:**
- **τον βεβαίωσα ότι είναι αλήθεια** (ton vevaíosa óti íne alítheia) — "I assured him that it is true"
- **με βεβαίωσε** (me vevaíose) — "he/she assured me" (aorist)

**Status:** [CORE]
**Notes:** Different sense of βεβαιώνω (assure person vs. certify document). Takes person object + ότι-clause.

---

### 9.2 Making Someone Doubt

**Pattern:** [STIMULUS NP-NOM] + κάνω (káno) + [PERSON ACC] + να αμφιβάλλω (na amfivállo)

**Examples:**
- **αυτό με κάνει να αμφιβάλλω** (aftó me káni na amfivállo) — "this makes me doubt"
- **τον έκανε να αμφιβάλλει** (ton ékane na amfivállei) — "it made him doubt" (aorist)

**Status:** [CORE]
**Notes:** Causative with να-clause (subjunctive). Verb αμφιβάλλω "doubt" in complement.

---

**NO DIRECT ADJECTIVE CAUSATIVE** like *κάνω κάποιον αμφίβολο exists. Greek uses verbal complement (να αμφιβάλλω) instead.

## 10. MAINTAINING TRUTH/CORRECTNESS (STABILITY)

### 10.1 Truth Remaining/Staying True

**Pattern:** [TRUTH/STATEMENT NP-NOM] + μένω (méno) + αληθινός (alithinós) "remain true"

**Examples:**
- **μένει αληθινή η δήλωση** (méni alithiní i dílosi) — "the statement remains true"
- **έμεινε αληθινό** (émine alithinó) — "it remained true" (aorist)

**Status:** [MARGINAL]
**Notes:** Uncommon construction. Truth typically not described as "remaining" but as "being" or "continuing to be."

---

**Pattern:** [TRUTH NP-NOM] + παραμένω (paraméno) + [ADJECTIVE] "continue to be"

**Examples:**
- **παραμένει αληθινή** (paraméni alithiní) — "it continues to be true"
- **παρέμεινε σωστή** (parémine sostí) — "it continued to be correct" (aorist)

**Status:** [MARGINAL]
**Notes:** More formal than μένω. Emphasizes continuation despite potential change. Uncommon with truth vocabulary.

---

### 10.2 Maintaining Correctness

**Pattern:** [AGENT NP-NOM] + διατηρώ (diatιró) + [CORRECTNESS NOUN-ACC]

**Examples:**
- **διατηρώ την ακρίβεια** (diatιró tin akríveia) — "I maintain the accuracy"
- **διατήρησε την ορθότητα** (diatírιse tin orthótita) — "he/she maintained the correctness" (aorist)

**Status:** [CORE]
**Notes:** Active maintenance of correctness/accuracy. Common in technical and quality-control contexts.

---

**Pattern:** [AGENT NP-NOM] + κρατώ (krató) + [OBJECT ACC] + σωστό (sostó)

**Examples:**
- **κρατώ το κείμενο σωστό** (krató to kímeno sostó) — "I keep the text correct"
- **το κράτησε σωστό** (to krátιse sostó) — "he/she kept it correct" (aorist)

**Status:** [MARGINAL]
**Notes:** Less common than διατηρώ. Emphasizes active effort to prevent errors.

---

### 10.3 Preserving Authenticity

**Pattern:** [AGENT NP-NOM] + διατηρώ (diatιró) + [AUTHENTICITY NOUN-ACC]

**Examples:**
- **διατηρώ την αυθεντικότητα** (diatιró tin afthentikótita) — "I preserve the authenticity"

## 13. ASPECTUAL DISTINCTIONS

### 13.1 Perfective vs. Imperfective in Truth Emergence

**Imperfective (ongoing/habitual):**
- **βγαίνει η αλήθεια** (vgaíni i alítheia) — "the truth comes out" (present, ongoing or habitual)
- **έβγαινε η αλήθεια** (évgene i alítheia) — "the truth was coming out" (imperfect, ongoing in past)

**Perfective (completed):**
- **βγήκε η αλήθεια** (vgíke i alítheia) — "the truth came out" (aorist, completed event)
- **έχει βγει η αλήθεια** (échi vgi i alítheia) — "the truth has come out" (perfect, completed with current relevance)

**Status:** [CORE]
**Notes:** Standard Greek aspectual system applies. Perfective (aorist) marks completed emergence; imperfective marks process or habit.

---

### 13.2 Perfective vs. Imperfective in Error-Making

**Imperfective:**
- **κάνω λάθος** (káno láthos) — "I make mistakes" (present, habitual)
- **έκανα λάθος** (ékana láthos) — "I was making mistakes" (imperfect, ongoing in past)

**Perfective:**
- **έκανα λάθος** (ékana láthos) — "I made a mistake" (aorist, single completed event)
- **έχω κάνει λάθος** (écho káni láthos) — "I have made a mistake" (perfect)

**Status:** [CORE]
**Notes:** Same form έκανα can be imperfect or aorist depending on context. Aorist typically for single mistake; imperfect for repeated or ongoing error-making.

---

### 13.3 Perfective vs. Imperfective in Correction

**Imperfective:**
- **διορθώνω το λάθος** (diorthόno to láthos) — "I correct the mistake" (present, ongoing or habitual)
- **διόρθωνα λάθη** (diόrthona láthi) — "I was correcting mistakes" (imperfect)

**Perfective:**
- **διόρθωσα το λάθος** (diόrthosa to láthos) — "I corrected the mistake" (aorist, completed)
- **έχω διορθώσει το λάθος** (écho diorthόsi to láthos) — "I have corrected the mistake" (perfect)

**Status:** [CORE]
**Notes:** Standard aspectual distinction. Perfective for completed correction; imperfective for process or habit.

## SUMMARY OBSERVATIONS

### Productive Patterns:

1. **Truth emergence:** βγαίνω στη φόρα, έρχομαι στο φως, αποκαλύπτομαι (all [CORE])
2. **Verification:** επιβεβαιώνω, βεβαιώνω, αποδεικνύω (all [CORE])
3. **Invalidation:** ακυρώνω, γίνομαι άκυρος (both [CORE])
4. **Error correction:** διορθώνω, σωστεύω (both [CORE])
5. **Error making:** κάνω λάθος ([CORE], extremely high-frequency)

### Asymmetries:

1. **Truth vs. lies:** Truth has richer emergence vocabulary (βγαίνω στη φόρα, έρχομαι στο φως) than lies
2. **Becoming correct:** Rare/marginal constructions; correction typically agent-driven
3. **Agent nouns:** Productive for error-makers (no direct causative "make someone lie")
4. **Stability:** Maintaining truth/correctness less lexicalized than causing change

### Gaps:

1. **No direct causative** for "make someone tell lies"
2. **No simple construction** for "prevent lies" (complex periphrastic only)
3. **No common expression** for objects spontaneously becoming genuine
4. **Limited stability vocabulary** for truth remaining true (μένω, παραμένω marginal)

### Metaphorical Patterns:

1. **Truth as hidden entity:** Emerges, comes to light, is brought to surface
2. **Lies as coverings:** Exposed, uncovered, caught
3. **Light metaphor:** Pervasive for truth revelation (φως, φόρα)
4. **Gradual emergence:** σιγά σιγά, σταδιακά common with truth/validity change

### Aspectual Behavior:

- Standard Greek perfective/imperfective distinction applies throughout
- Perfective (aorist) for completed events
- Imperfective for ongoing processes or habits
- Perfect for completed events with current relevance

### Modal Expressions:

- μπορώ/μπορεί (possibility/ability) + να-clause productive
- πρέπει (necessity/obligation) + να-clause productive
- Both combine freely with truth-domain verbs
