## OPPOSITION 1

**Term A:** सत्य (satya)  
**Term B:** असत्य (asatya)  
**Transliteration:** satya / asatya  
**English translation:** "truth, true, real" / "false, untrue, unreal"  
**Description of contrast:** Direct morphological negation using privative prefix a-. Asatya functions as lexicalized antonym denoting falsity, untruth, and unreality, contrasting with satya across factual, moral, and ontological domains.  
**Contrast type:** morphological  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## SUMMARY OBSERVATIONS

**Morphological negation patterns:**
- Privative prefix a-/an- is highly productive across the truth domain
- Creates lexicalized antonyms that function as independent terms (asatya, avidyā, adharma, etc.)
- Applies to base terms, compounds, and derived forms (abstract nouns, adjectives)
- Total morphological oppositions identified: 18

**Lexical oppositions:**
- Several non-morphological lexical pairs exist (satya/mithyā, pramā/bhrama, vyavahāra/paramārtha)
- These represent distinct lexical items rather than derivational relationships
- Often carry specialized philosophical or technical meanings
- Total lexical oppositions identified: 11

**Constructional oppositions:**
- Include syntactic negation (na) versus affirmation
- Parallel constructions with contrasting lexical items (pratyakṣeṇa vs. anumānena)
- Domain-marking constructions (vyavahāre vs. paramārthe)
- Dual constructions expressing internal opposition (satyāsatyayoḥ)
- Total constructional oppositions identified: 11

**Overlapping oppositions:**
- Several terms participate in multiple oppositional relationships (satya opposes both asatya and mithyā)
- Some oppositions exist at multiple levels (morphological asatya vs. lexical mithyā both oppose satya)
- Constructional and lexical oppositions often reinforce each other

**Asymmetries noted:**
- Some terms have multiple contrasts (satya has asatya, mithyā, anṛta as various opposites)
- Not all positive terms have attested negative counterparts in all constructions
- Some oppositions are more productive than others (satya/asatya more productive than ṛta/anṛta)
- Dual constructions expressing opposition (types 23-26) have no external contrasting constructions

**Domain distribution:**
- Epistemological domain shows rich oppositional structure (pramā/bhrama, pratyakṣa/anumāna)
- Moral domain similarly productive (dharma/adharma, sādhu/asādhu)
- Two-truths framework creates specialized oppositional system (vyavahāra/paramārtha)
- Soteriological domain centers on vidyā/avidyā opposition
