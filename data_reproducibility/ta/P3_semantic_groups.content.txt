# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P3 NATURAL SEMANTIC GROUPING
# Language: Tamil (ta)

## IDENTIFIED GROUPS

### GROUP 1: Truth-Telling and Lying Actions

**Definition:** This group comprises expressions centered on the communicative acts of speaking truthfully or falsely. These terms are distinguished by their focus on verbal behavior and are typically used with verbs of speaking (சொல்லு collu, பேசு pēcu, கூறு kūṟu). The primary contrast in usage is between producing truthful versus false utterances.

**Representative expressions:**
- உண்மையைச் சொல்லு (uṇmaiyaic collu) - "tell the truth"
- உண்மையைப் பேசு (uṇmaiyaip pēcu) - "speak the truth"
- பொய் சொல்லு (poy collu) - "tell lies"
- பொய் பேசு (poy pēcu) - "speak lies"
- பொய் கூறு (poy kūṟu) - "utter lies"

**Ambiguous or overlapping items:**
- நேர்மை (nērmai) - while related to truthful character, appears in different collocational patterns focused on maintaining/practicing rather than speaking
- மெய்ம்மொழி (meymoḻi) - "true word/speech" - more literary/formal, refers to speech as object rather than act

---

### GROUP 2: Concealment and Revelation of Truth

**Definition:** This group includes expressions focused on the dynamics of hiding versus revealing truth. These terms pattern with verbs of concealment (மறை maṟai) and revelation (வெளிப்படு veḷippaṭu, வெளிப்படுத்து veḷippaṭuttu), and are commonly used in investigative, journalistic, and narrative contexts where truth is treated as something that can be hidden or brought to light.

**Representative expressions:**
- உண்மையை மறை (uṇmaiyai maṟai) - "hide the truth"
- உண்மையை வெளிப்படுத்து (uṇmaiyai veḷippaṭuttu) - "reveal the truth"
- உண்மை வெளிப்படு (uṇmai veḷippaṭu) - "truth comes out"
- உண்மை வெளிச்சத்திற்கு வரு (uṇmai veḷiccattiṟku varu) - "truth comes to light"
- உண்மை கசந்து வரு (uṇmai kacantu varu) - "truth leaks out"
- பொய் அம்பலமாகு (poy ampalamāku) - "lie is exposed"
- பொய் பிடிபடு (poy piṭipaṭu) - "lie is caught"
- பொய் வெளியாகு (poy veḷiyāku) - "lie becomes exposed"

**Ambiguous or overlapping items:**
- உண்மை தெரிய வரு (uṇmai teriya varu) - "truth comes to be known" - overlaps with epistemic knowing group

---

### GROUP 3: Epistemic Pursuit and Awareness

**Definition:** This group encompasses expressions related to seeking, knowing, and becoming aware of truth. These terms pattern with verbs of searching (தேடு tēṭu), knowing (அறி aṟi), and realizing (உணர் uṇar), and are used in philosophical, spiritual, and investigative contexts where truth is treated as an object of knowledge or awareness.

**Representative expressions:**
- உண்மையைத் தேடு (uṇmaiyait tēṭu) - "search for truth"
- உண்மையை அறி (uṇmaiyai aṟi) - "know the truth"
- உண்மையை உணர் (uṇmaiyai uṇar) - "realize the truth"
- உண்மை உணர்வு (uṇmai uṇarvu) - "awareness of truth"
- உண்மை அறிவு (uṇmai aṟivu) - "true knowledge"
- மெய் உணர்வு (mey uṇarvu) - "true awareness/realization"
- மெய்ஞ்ஞானம் (meyññāṉam) - "true knowledge/enlightenment"
- மெய் அறிவு (mey aṟivu) - "true knowledge"
- மெய் கண்டு (mey kaṇṭu) - "having seen the truth"

**Ambiguous or overlapping items:**
- தெளிவு (teḷivu) - "clarity" - while related to knowing, focuses more on the quality of understanding than the act of pursuit
- நம்பிக்கை (nampikkai) - "trust/belief" - involves epistemic stance but not necessarily pursuit of knowledge

---

### GROUP 4: Acceptance and Acknowledgment

**Definition:** This group consists of expressions focused on accepting, acknowledging, or coming to terms with truth. These terms pattern with verbs of acceptance (ஏற்று ēṟṟu) and are particularly common in counseling, moral discourse, and contexts where difficult truths must be acknowledged.

**Representative expressions:**
- உண்மையை ஏற்று (uṇmaiyai ēṟṟu) - "accept the truth"

**Note:** This appears to be a smaller, more specialized group with limited representation in the current data.

---

### GROUP 5: Factual Correctness and Error

**Definition:** This group includes expressions centered on correctness versus incorrectness in factual, procedural, or computational contexts. These terms are distinguished from moral rightness and are commonly used in educational, technical, and everyday problem-solving situations. The primary contrast is between சரி (cari) "correct" and தப்பு (tappu)/தவறு (tavaṟu) "wrong/mistake."

**Representative expressions:**
- சரி (cari) - "correct, right"
- சரியான (cariyāṉa) - "correct" (adjectival)
- சரியாக (cariyāka) - "correctly" (adverbial)
- சரி இல்லை (cari illai) - "not correct"
- சரி பார் (cari pār) - "check, verify"
- தப்பு (tappu) - "wrong, mistake"
- தப்பான (tappāṉa) - "wrong" (adjectival)
- தவறு (tavaṟu) - "mistake, error"
- தவறான (tavaṟāṉa) - "wrong" (adjectival)

**Ambiguous or overlapping items:**
- சரியா? (cariyā?) - can function as both factual verification and agreement marker
- பொருத்தம் (poruttam) - "suitability" - while related to correctness, focuses on appropriateness of fit rather than factual accuracy

---

### GROUP 6: Moral Rightness and Wrongdoing

**Definition:** This group encompasses expressions related to moral virtue, righteousness, justice, and their opposites. These terms are used in ethical discourse, moral instruction, and legal contexts to evaluate the moral quality of actions and character. They contrast with the factual correctness group through their focus on ethical rather than factual evaluation.

**Representative expressions:**
- நேர்மை (nērmai) - "honesty, integrity"
- நேர்மையான (nērmaiyāṉa) - "honest, upright"
- நேர்மையைக் காத்து (nērmaiyaik kāttu) - "maintain honesty"
- நேர்மையைக் கடைப்பிடி (nērmaiyaik kaṭaipiṭi) - "practice honesty"
- நியாயம் (niyāyam) - "justice, fairness"
- நியாயமான (niyāyamāṉa) - "just, fair"
- நியாயம் இல்லை (niyāyam illai) - "not fair/just"
- அறம் (aṟam) - "virtue, righteousness"
- அறமான (aṟamāṉa) - "virtuous, righteous"
- நீதி (nīti) - "justice, righteousness"
- நீதியான (nītiyāṉa) - "just, righteous"
- குற்றம் (kuṟṟam) - "fault, crime"
- குற்றமான (kuṟṟamāṉa) - "faulty, criminal"
- குற்றமற்ற (kuṟṟamaṟṟa) - "faultless, blameless"

**Ambiguous or overlapping items:**
- தப்பு (tappu)/தவறு (tavaṟu) - can refer to both factual errors and moral wrongs depending on context

---

### GROUP 7: Authenticity and Counterfeit

**Definition:** This group comprises expressions distinguishing genuine, original, or authentic items from fake, counterfeit, or imitation ones. These terms are particularly common in commercial, legal, and material contexts where the genuineness of objects, documents, or currency is at issue.

**Representative expressions:**
- போலி (pōli) - "fake, counterfeit"
- போலியான (pōliyāṉa) - "fake, counterfeit" (adjectival)
- அசல் (acal) - "original, genuine"
- அசலான (acalāṉa) - "original, genuine" (adjectival)
- பொய் ஆவணம் (poy āvaṇam) - "false document"
- பொய் நாணயம் (poy nāṇayam) - "counterfeit money"
- பொய் கணக்கு (poy kaṇakku) - "false account"

**Ambiguous or overlapping items:**
- உண்மையான (uṇmaiyāṉa) - "true, genuine" - can refer to both authentic objects and true statements
- மெய்யான (meyyāṉa) - "true, genuine" - similar dual usage

---

### GROUP 8: Legal Truth and Falsehood

**Definition:** This group includes specialized expressions used in legal and judicial contexts, particularly relating to testimony, evidence, accusations, and legal proceedings. These terms form a distinct subset characterized by their technical legal usage and formal register.

**Representative expressions:**
- பொய் சாட்சி (poy cāṭci) - "false witness, perjury"
- பொய் குற்றச்சாட்டு (poy kuṟṟaccāṭṭu) - "false accusation"
- பொய் வழக்கு (poy vaḻakku) - "false case"
- பொய் வாக்குமூலம் (poy vākkumūlam) - "false statement/confession"
- சாட்சி (cāṭci) - "witness, testimony, evidence"
- சாட்சியான (cāṭciyāṉa) - "witnessed, evidenced"
- மெய் சான்று (mey cāṉṟu) - "true evidence"

**Ambiguous or overlapping items:**
- உறுதி (uṟuti) - "certainty, confirmation" - used in legal contexts but also more broadly

---

### GROUP 9: Deception and Fraud

**Definition:** This group encompasses expressions focused on active deception, cheating, trickery, and fraudulent behavior. These terms emphasize the intentional misleading of others and are used in contexts of dishonesty, fraud, and manipulation.

**Representative expressions:**
- ஏமாற்று (ēmāṟṟu) - "deception, cheating, fraud"
- ஏமாற்றும் (ēmāṟṟum) - "deceptive, cheating" (participial)
- மாயை (māyai) - "illusion, delusion"
- மாயையான (māyaiyāṉa) - "illusory, deceptive"
- பொய் மாயம் (poy māyam) - "false illusion, trickery"
- பொய் முகம் (poy mukam) - "false face, pretense"
- பொய் நடிப்பு (poy naṭippu) - "false acting, pretense"
- பொய் உருவம் (poy uruvam) - "false form, disguise"

**Ambiguous or overlapping items:**
- போலி (pōli) - while meaning "fake," focuses more on counterfeit objects than active deception

---

### GROUP 10: Misinformation and False Beliefs

**Definition:** This group includes expressions related to false information, rumors, unfounded beliefs, and broken promises. These terms are used in contexts of information quality, media criticism, and interpersonal trust, focusing on the content of what is communicated or believed rather than the act of lying.

**Representative expressions:**
- பொய் செய்தி (poy ceyti) - "false news, fake news"
- பொய் வதந்தி (poy vatanti) - "false rumor"
- பொய் நம்பிக்கை (poy nampikkai) - "false belief"
- பொய் வாக்குறுதி (poy vākkuṟuti) - "false promise"

**Ambiguous or overlapping items:**
- சந்தேகம் (cantēkam) - "doubt" - relates to uncertainty about beliefs but not necessarily false beliefs

---

### GROUP 11: Epistemic Certainty and Doubt

**Definition:** This group comprises expressions relating to degrees of certainty, doubt, and clarity in knowledge or belief. These terms are used in epistemic evaluations and discussions of confidence in knowledge claims.

**Representative expressions:**
- உறுதி (uṟuti) - "certainty, confirmation"
- உறுதியான (uṟutiyāṉa) - "certain, confirmed"
- உறுதியாக (uṟutiyāka) - "certainly, firmly"
- உறுதியாக சொல்ல முடியும் (uṟutiyāka colla muṭiyum) - "can say with certainty"
- சந்தேகம் (cantēkam) - "doubt, suspicion"
- சந்தேகமான (cantēkamāṉa) - "doubtful, suspicious"
- சந்தேகமற்ற (cantēkamaṟṟa) - "without doubt"
- சந்தேகம் இல்லை (cantēkam illai) - "no doubt"
- தெளிவு (teḷivu) - "clarity, clearness"
- தெளிவான (teḷivāṉa) - "clear, lucid"
- தெளிவாக (teḷivāka) - "clearly"
- தெளிவற்ற (teḷivaṟṟa) - "unclear, ambiguous"
- தெளிவாக தெரியவில்லை (teḷivāka teriyavillai) - "not clear"

**Ambiguous or overlapping items:**
- நம்பிக்கை (nampikkai) - "trust, belief" - involves epistemic stance but also faith and hope

---

### GROUP 12: Trust and Reliability

**Definition:** This group includes expressions related to trustworthiness, reliability, and credibility of persons, sources, and information. These terms are used in evaluations of whether someone or something can be trusted or relied upon.

**Representative expressions:**
- நம்பிக்கை (nampikkai) - "trust, belief, faith"
- நம்பிக்கையான (nampikkaiyāṉa) - "trustful, believing"
- நம்பிக்கையற்ற (nampikkaiyaṟṟa) - "without trust, faithless"
- நம்பகம் (nampakam) - "reliability, trustworthiness"
- நம்பகமான (nampakamāṉa) - "reliable, trustworthy"
- நம்பகமாக (nampakamāka) - "reliably"

---

### GROUP 13: Possibility and Feasibility

**Definition:** This group comprises expressions evaluating whether something is possible, feasible, or can occur. These terms are used in assessments of what can or cannot happen, distinct from evaluations of truth or correctness.

**Representative expressions:**
- சாத்தியம் (cāttiyam) - "possibility, feasibility"
- சாத்தியமான (cāttiyamāṉa) - "possible, feasible"
- சாத்தியமற்ற (cāttiyamaṟṟa) - "impossible, not feasible"
- சாத்தியம் இல்லை (cāttiyam illai) - "not possible"

---

### GROUP 14: Appropriateness and Suitability

**Definition:** This group includes expressions evaluating whether something is suitable, appropriate, or fits properly in a given context. These terms focus on contextual fit and compatibility rather than truth or correctness per se.

**Representative expressions:**
- பொருத்தம் (poruttam) - "suitability, appropriateness, compatibility"
- பொருத்தமான (poruttamāṉa) - "suitable, appropriate"
- பொருத்தமாக (poruttamāka) - "suitably, appropriately"
- பொருத்தமற்ற (poruttamaṟṟa) - "unsuitable, inappropriate"
- பொருத்தம் இல்லை (poruttam illai) - "not suitable"

---

### GROUP 15: Natural State and Essence

**Definition:** This group comprises expressions relating to the natural state, inherent qualities, or essential nature of things. These terms are used in philosophical and descriptive contexts to discuss what something fundamentally is or its true nature.

**Representative expressions:**
- இயல்பு (iyalpu) - "nature, natural state, essence"
- இயல்பான (iyalpāṉa) - "natural, inherent"
- இயல்பாக (iyalpāka) - "naturally"
- மெய் நிலை (mey nilai) - "true state, ultimate reality"
- மெய்ப்பொருள் (meypporuḷ) - "true meaning, ultimate reality"
- மெய் தன்மை (mey taṉmai) - "true nature, essential quality"

**Ambiguous or overlapping items:**
- உண்மை நிலை (uṉmai nilai) - "true state" - can refer to actual situation rather than essential nature

---

### GROUP 16: Actual Events and Real Life

**Definition:** This group includes expressions distinguishing real events, actual occurrences, and real life from fictional, imagined, or idealized versions. These terms are commonly used in narrative, historical, and journalistic contexts.

**Representative expressions:**
- உண்மை நிகழ்வு (uṇmai nikaḻvu) - "true event, actual occurrence"
- உண்மை கதை (uṇmai katai) - "true story"
- உண்மை சம்பவம் (uṇmai campavam) - "true incident"
- உண்மை வாழ்க்கை (uṇmai vāḻkkai) - "real life"
- மெய் வரலாறு (mey varalāṟu) - "true history"
- மெய் கதை (mey katai) - "true story"

---

### GROUP 17: True Character and Hidden Nature

**Definition:** This group comprises metaphorical expressions referring to someone's true character, real nature, or hidden aspects that may be revealed. These terms use body/face imagery to discuss authenticity of character.

**Representative expressions:**
- உண்மை முகம் (uṇmai mukam) - "true face, true nature"
- பொய் முகம் (poy mukam) - "false face, pretense"

---

### GROUP 18: Spiritual and Philosophical Truth

**Definition:** This group includes expressions with strong spiritual, devotional, or philosophical connotations, particularly those using the classical மெய் (mey) root. These terms are characteristic of religious and philosophical discourse and often relate to ultimate reality, enlightenment, or spiritual authenticity.

**Representative expressions:**
- மெய்ஞ்ஞானம் (meyññāṉam) - "true knowledge, enlightenment"
- மெய் உணர்வு (mey uṇarvu) - "true awareness, realization"
- மெய்யன்பு (meyyanpu) - "true love"
- மெய் தெய்வம் (mey teyvam) - "true god"
- மெய் பக்தி (mey pakti) - "true devotion"
- மெய் குரு (mey kuru) - "true teacher/guru"
- மெய் வழி (mey vaḻi) - "true path"
- மெய் நூல் (mey nūl) - "true text, authentic scripture"
- மெய்யுரை (meyyurai) - "true speech, authentic discourse"
- மெய்ம்மொழி (meymoḻi) - "true word"

**Note:** This group shows clear register distinction, with மெய் (mey) compounds clustering in literary, classical, and religious registers, while உண்மை (uṇmai) compounds span all registers.

---

### GROUP 19: Confirmation and Verification Questions

**Definition:** This group comprises interrogative constructions used to seek confirmation, verification, or clarification of truth, correctness, or reality. These are extremely common in everyday conversational Tamil.

**Representative expressions:**
- உண்மையா? (uṇmaiyā?) - "Is it true? Really?"
- நிஜமா? (nijamā?) - "Is it true? Really?"
- சரியா? (cariyā?) - "Is it correct? Right? Okay?"

---

## ITEMS WITH UNCLEAR OR MULTIPLE GROUP MEMBERSHIP

### Items appearing in multiple groups:

1. **தப்பு (tappu) / தவறு (tavaṟu)** - "wrong, mistake"
   - Appears in both GROUP 5 (Factual Correctness) and GROUP 6 (Moral Rightness)
   - Usage context determines whether factual error or moral wrong is intended

2. **உண்மையான (uṇmaiyāṉa) / மெய்யான (meyyāṉa)** - "true, genuine"
   - Can function in GROUP 7 (Authenticity) for genuine objects
   - Also functions more broadly for true statements across multiple groups

3. **உறுதி (uṟuti)** - "certainty, confirmation"
   - Appears in GROUP 11 (Epistemic Certainty)
   - Also has specialized usage in GROUP 8 (Legal contexts)

4. **நம்பிக்கை (nampikkai)** - "trust, belief, faith"
   - Appears in GROUP 12 (Trust and Reliability)
   - Overlaps with GROUP 3 (Epistemic Pursuit) in belief aspect
   - Can also appear in GROUP 10 when modified by பொய் (false belief)

### Items not clearly fitting established groups:

1. **ஒப்பு (oppu)** - "similarity, resemblance, comparison"
   - Does not fit clearly into any identified group
   - Functions more in comparative/analogical domain than truth evaluation

2. **உண்மை விளக்கம் (uṇmai viḷakkam)** - "true explanation"
   - Could relate to GROUP 3 (Epistemic) or GROUP 16 (Actual Events)
   - Primarily informational/explanatory rather than evaluative

3. **உண்மை நிலவரம் (uṇmai nilavaram)** - "actual state of affairs"
   - Overlaps between GROUP 15 (Natural State) and GROUP 16 (Actual Events)
   - More administrative/reportorial than philosophical

4. **மெய்ப்பாடு (meyppāṭu)** - "bodily experience, genuine emotion"
   - Ambiguous between physical sensation and emotional authenticity
   - Does not fit cleanly into truth-evaluation groups

## NOTES ON GROUPING PATTERNS

1. **Register stratification**: Clear distinction between colloquial (நிஜம் nijam), general (உண்மை uṇmai), and literary/classical (மெய் mey) terms, with மெய் compounds forming a distinct philosophical/spiritual cluster.

2. **Domain specificity**: Some groups (GROUP 8 Legal, GROUP 7 Authenticity) show clear domain restriction, while others (GROUP 5 Factual Correctness) span multiple domains.

3. **Overlapping semantic space**: The boundary between factual correctness (GROUP 5) and moral rightness (GROUP 6) is permeable, with some terms functioning in both domains depending on context.

4. **Compound productivity**: பொய் (poy) shows exceptional productivity in forming domain-specific compounds (legal, commercial, informational), while உண்மை (uṇmai) shows broader but less specialized compounding.

5. **Action vs. state distinction**: Clear grouping distinction between terms focused on actions (GROUP 1 Truth-telling, GROUP 2 Concealment/Revelation, GROUP 9 Deception) versus states or qualities (GROUP 11 Certainty, GROUP 12 Trust, GROUP 15 Natural State).
