# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P1b ROOT EXPANSION
# Language: Finnish (fi)

---

## Step 1 — Identify Central Roots

---

**Root 1: *tosi* / *tod-* / *tot-***

- **Root form:** *tosi* (base form); *tod-* (before vowel-initial suffixes, e.g., *todistaa*, *todistus*); *tot-* (before *-uus*, giving *totuus*)
- **P1 items participating:** *tosi* (1), *totuus* (2), *todistaa* (16), *todistus* (17), *totuudenmukaisuus* (27) — **5 items**
- **Core meaning contribution:** Correspondence between a proposition or representation and reality; the state of being factually the case. This root anchors the epistemic pole of the domain — truth as a property of statements and states of affairs.

---

**Root 2: *oikea* / *oikeu-* / *oikeel-***

- **Root form:** *oikea* (base adjective); *oikeu-* (before *-s*, giving *oikeus*; before *-den*, giving *oikeuden-*); *oikeel-* (before *-lisuus*, giving *oikeellisuus*)
- **P1 items participating:** *oikea* (5), *oikeellisuus* (6), *oikeudenmukaisuus* (28) — **3 items**
- **Core meaning contribution:** Correctness, rightness, and legitimacy — covering conformity to a standard or norm, moral rightness, and legal right. This root connects epistemic correctness to normative and legal evaluation.

---

**Root 3: *väärä* / *väärent-* / *väären-***

- **Root form:** *väärä* (base adjective); *väärent-* (causative stem, *väärentää*); *väären-* (result nominal stem, *väärennös*)
- **P1 items participating:** *väärä* (13), *väärentää* (14), *väärentäminen* (15), *väärennös* (22) — **4 items**
- **Core meaning contribution:** Falsity, incorrectness, inauthenticity, and fraudulent fabrication. This root covers the negative pole of the truth domain — from simple error to deliberate forgery.
