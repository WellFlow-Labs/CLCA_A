# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P1b ROOT EXPANSION
# Language: Finnish (fi)

## STEP 1: Identify Central Roots

### Root 1: **tosi / tode-**
**Form:** tosi (nominative), tode- (stem in derivatives)
**Frequency:** Participates in 11 P1 items (tosi, totuus, totta, todellinen, todella, todistaa, todiste, todenmukaisesti, todentaa, todenperäinen, todenmukaisuus)
**Core meaning contribution:** Correspondence to reality, factuality, truth as alignment with actual state of affairs. The most productive root in the Finnish truth domain.

### Root 2: **oikea / oikee-**
**Form:** oikea (nominative), oikee- (stem)
**Frequency:** Participates in 3 P1 items (oikea, oikeellisuus, oikein)
**Core meaning contribution:** Correctness, rightness, propriety — both factual accuracy and normative appropriateness. Polysemous between directional (right vs. left), correctness, and genuineness.

### Root 3: **väärä / väär-**
**Form:** väärä (nominative), väär- (stem)
**Frequency:** Participates in 3 P1 items (väärä, vääristää, väärin)
**Core meaning contribution:** Wrongness, falsity, incorrectness — the primary negative evaluation term covering both factual error and moral transgression.

## STEP 2: Expand Each Root

#### 1. tosiasiallinen
**Form:** tosiasiallinen
**Literal breakdown:** tosi-asia-llinen (true-matter-ADJ)
**Translation:** factual, actual, de facto
**Context:** Used to describe what is actually the case as opposed to theoretical, legal, or nominal status ("tosiasiallinen tilanne" = actual situation, "tosiasiallinen valta" = de facto power). Common in legal, administrative, and analytical discourse.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 2. tosiasia
**Form:** tosiasia
**Literal breakdown:** tosi-asia (true-matter)
**Translation:** fact
**Context:** Refers to established facts or matters of fact ("kiistaton tosiasia" = indisputable fact). Central term in argumentative, scientific, and legal discourse for distinguishing facts from opinions or interpretations.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

**Context:** Describes something as being the wrong type or kind ("vääränlainen lähestymistapa" = wrong kind of approach). Common in evaluative discourse.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 20. väärä suunta
**Form:** väärä suunta
**Literal breakdown:** väärä suunta (wrong direction)
**Translation:** wrong direction
**Context:** Expression for incorrect direction, used both literally for physical direction and figuratively for course of action. Common in navigation and planning contexts.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 21. väärä raide
**Form:** väärä raide
**Literal breakdown:** väärä raide (wrong track)
**Translation:** wrong track
**Context:** Idiomatic expression meaning to be on the wrong track or pursuing wrong course ("olla väärällä raiteella" = be on the wrong track). Used figuratively for misdirected efforts.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 22. väärä jälki
**Form:** väärä jälki
**Literal breakdown:** väärä jälki (false trail)
**Translation:** false trail, red herring
**Context:** Expression for misleading clue or false trail ("johtaa väärään jälkeen" = lead on a false trail). Used in investigative and mystery contexts.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## STEP 3: Peripheral Extensions

### Extensions of tosi/tode- root:

**Intensity/degree marking:** The use of *tosi* as a colloquial intensifier meaning "really" or "very" (e.g., "tosi hyvä" = really good) represents a shift from truth-evaluation to degree marking. This appears to be a pragmatic extension where affirming truth becomes a way of emphasizing degree.

**Seriousness vs. truth:** Forms like *totinen* (serious, solemn) and *tosissaan* (seriously, in earnest) shift from correspondence-to-reality toward seriousness of manner or intent. The connection appears to be through sincerity or non-joking attitude rather than factual accuracy.

**Realization/actualization:** The extensive family of forms around *toteutua/toteuttaa* (realize, implement, carry out) extends the root into the domain of making things actual or bringing plans into reality. This represents a shift from evaluating existing truth to creating or actualizing states of affairs.

**Probability:** Forms like *todennäköinen* (probable, literally "true-looking") extend the root into epistemic probability, where appearance of truth becomes likelihood of occurrence.

### Extensions of oikea/oikee- root:

**Directional meaning:** The use of *oikea* to mean "right" (as opposed to left) represents a completely distinct semantic domain from correctness or truth, though both senses coexist in the same form.

**Legal/moral domain:** The extensive *oikeus* family (rights, justice, courts) represents an extension from factual correctness into normative domains of entitlement and justice.

**Orthodoxy:** Forms like *oikeaoppinen* (orthodox) extend from correctness into religious/ideological conformity to doctrine.

**Timing:** *oikea-aikainen* (timely) extends from correctness to temporal appropriateness.

### Extensions of väärä/vääär- root:

**Physical distortion:** Forms like *vääristyä* (become distorted) and *vääristymä* (distortion) can apply to physical warping or deformation, not just informational distortion.

**Directional error:** Expressions like *väärä suunta* (wrong direction) apply the root to spatial/navigational contexts.

**Moral wrongdoing:** The *vääryys* (injustice) family extends from factual incorrectness into moral transgression and injustice.
