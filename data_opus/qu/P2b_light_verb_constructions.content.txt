# CLCA P2b — COMPOSITIONAL CONSTRUCTIONS
## Language: Quechua (qu)

Orthography: Standardized trivocalic Southern Quechua (a, i, u), consistent with P1/P1b/P2a.

Scope note: This step documents **multi-word constructions** (compounds, fixed phrases, periphrastic and idiomatic constructions) involving P1 items. Single-word derivations (handled in P2a) are excluded. Inflectional/enclitic single-word forms (chiqaqmi, chiqaqchu, chiqaqpuni, etc.) are also excluded as they are not multi-word. Where the construction's word order is variable or attestation weak, status reflects this.

---

## ROOT: *chiqa-* "true / straight / right"

### Construction 1
1. **Base Form:** chiqap (P1 #2) / chiqaq (P1 #1)
2. **Example:** chiqap kay
3. **Transliteration:** chiqap kay
4. **English:** truth, the truth, reality
5. **Usage notes:** Nominalized construction (true + being) invoking veracity in moral/discursive contexts. Word order and form (chiqap kay / chiqaq kay) are variable.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### Construction 2
1. **Base Form:** chiqaq (P1 #1)
2. **Example:** chiqaq simi
3. **Transliteration:** chiqaq simi
4. **English:** true word, truthful speech, the truth
5. **Usage notes:** A truthful statement or honest speech; used in moral evaluation of what someone says. Stands in opposition to *llulla simi*.
6. **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

### Construction 3
1. **Base Form:** chiqaq (P1 #1) / chiqaq sunqu (P1 #25)
2. **Example:** chiqaq sunqu
3. **Transliteration:** chiqaq sunqu
4. **English:** sincere, true-hearted, honest
5. **Usage notes:** Fixed expression (true + heart) for sincerity/uprightness of character; word order varies (sunqu chiqaq / chiqaq sunqu).
6. **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

### Construction 4
1. **Base Form:** chiqan (P1 #13)
2. **Example:** chiqan ñan
3. **Transliteration:** chiqan ñan
4. **English:** the straight path, the right way
5. **Usage notes:** Literal "direct road" and moral "right path"; common in moral-religious discourse.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### Construction 5
1. **Base Form:** chiqaq (P1 #1)
2. **Example:** chiqaq kawsay
3. **Transliteration:** chiqaq kawsay
4. **English:** true life, an upright life
5. **Usage notes:** Moral-religious discourse on living rightly/authentically; parallels *allin kawsay* but stresses truth/uprightness.
6. **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

### Construction 6 (interrogative discourse formula)
1. **Base Form:** chiqap (P1 #2)
2. **Example:** chiqaptachu?
3. **Transliteration:** chiqaptachu?
4. **English:** is it true?
5. **Usage notes:** Conventionalized conversational request for confirmation noted in P1 (#2); borderline between fixed phrase and single inflected word (chiqap + -ta + -chu).
6. **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

## ROOT: *allin* "good / well / right"

### Construction 1
1. **Base Form:** allin (P1 #11)
2. **Example:** allin kawsay
3. **Transliteration:** allin kawsay
4. **English:** good living, well-being (the "good life")
5. **Usage notes:** Andean ethical-political concept of harmonious living; used in discourse, policy, and philosophy (cf. *sumak kawsay* in other varieties).
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### Construction 2
1. **Base Form:** allin (P1 #11)
2. **Example:** allin runa
3. **Transliteration:** allin runa
4. **English:** a good person
5. **Usage notes:** Moral character evaluation of an individual.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### Construction 3
1. **Base Form:** allin (P1 #11)
2. **Example:** allin sunqu
3. **Transliteration:** allin sunqu
4. **English:** good-hearted, kind
5. **Usage notes:** Moral praise of disposition/character (good + heart).
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### Construction 4
1. **Base Form:** allin (P1 #11)
2. **Example:** allin p'unchay
3. **Transliteration:** allin p'unchay
4. **English:** good day (greeting)
5. **Usage notes:** Conventional greeting; phatic/conventionalized use.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### Construction 5
1. **Base Form:** allin (P1 #11)
2. **Example:** allin ruwasqa
3. **Transliteration:** allin ruwasqa
4. **English:** well made, well done
5. **Usage notes:** Aesthetic/qualitative evaluation of craftsmanship or work (well + made).
6. **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

### Construction 6
1. **Base Form:** mana allin (P1 #28)
2. **Example:** mana allin
3. **Transliteration:** mana allin
4. **English:** bad, wrong, not good, improper
5. **Usage notes:** Fixed negated construction (not + good) for everyday negative evaluation, moral and qualitative; very common.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### Construction 7
1. **Base Form:** allin (P1 #11) / allin kay (P2a)
2. **Example:** allin kay
3. **Transliteration:** allin kay
4. **English:** goodness, well-being, quality
5. **Usage notes:** Nominalized construction (good + being); borders on compounding. Noted in P2a as bordering compound rather than affixal derivation.
6. **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

## ROOT: *llulla* "lie / false"

### Construction 1
1. **Base Form:** mana llullaq (P1 #23)
2. **Example:** mana llullaq
3. **Transliteration:** mana llullaq
4. **English:** truthful, honest, one who does not lie
5. **Usage notes:** Fixed negated construction (not + lying-one) denoting a reliable, honest person; moral evaluation of character.
6. **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

### Construction 2
1. **Base Form:** llulla (P1 #8)
2. **Example:** llulla simi
3. **Transliteration:** llulla simi
4. **English:** lie, false word, falsehood (in speech)
5. **Usage notes:** A false statement or lying speech (false + word); moral/discursive. Stands in opposition to *chiqaq simi*.
6. **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

### Construction 3
1. **Base Form:** llulla (P1 #8)
2. **Example:** llulla kay
3. **Transliteration:** llulla kay
4. **English:** falseness, mendacity, the state of being a lie
5. **Usage notes:** Nominalized construction (lie + being); abstraction of falsehood in moral/discursive reflection. Borders on compounding.
6. **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

## ROOT: *hina* "like / as / similar to"

### Construction 1
1. **Base Form:** hina (P1 #26)
2. **Example:** X hina
3. **Transliteration:** X hina
4. **English:** like X, as X
5. **Usage notes:** Productive comparative construction; *hina* follows the noun it compares to (e.g., *kikin hina* "just the same"). Highly productive but borderline grammatical vs. lexical.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### Construction 2
1. **Base Form:** hina (P1 #26) / kikin (P1 #15)
2. **Example:** kikin hina
3. **Transliteration:** kikin hina
4. **English:** just the same, exactly alike
5. **Usage notes:** Combines identity (*kikin*) with comparative (*hina*) to affirm equivalence; noted in P1 (#26) as a productive comparison.
6. **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

## ROOT: *rikch'a-* "appearance / likeness"

### Construction 1
1. **Base Form:** rikch'akuy (P1 #17)
2. **Example:** X-man rikch'akuy / X-man rikch'akun
3. **Transliteration:** X-man rikch'akuy
4. **English:** to resemble X, to look like X
5. **Usage notes:** Periphrastic resemblance construction; the standard of comparison takes dative/allative *-man* with the verb *rikch'akuy*. Productive and natural in comparison contexts.
6. **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

## Summary observations (supported by the data)

- **Two productive construction templates dominate** this domain:
  - **[QUALITY-NOUN + sunqu]** "X-hearted" → *chiqaq sunqu* (sincere), *allin sunqu* (kind). A consistent character-evaluation frame.
  - **[QUALITY-NOUN + kawsay]** "X life/living" → *allin kawsay* (the good life), *chiqaq kawsay* (upright life). An ethical-life frame.
- **A [QUALITY + simi] "X word/speech" frame** also recurs as a minimal opposition: *chiqaq simi* (true word) vs. *llulla simi* (false word) — the clearest attested truth/falsity contrast pair at the construction level.
- **A [X + kay] "the state/abstraction of X" nominalizing frame** recurs across poles: *chiqap kay* (truth), *allin kay* (goodness), *llulla kay* (falseness). This is the most cross-cutting template, spanning truth, goodness, and falsity.
- **Negation with *mana*** forms two attested evaluative constructions: *mana allin* (bad) and *mana llullaq* (honest) — note these negate different word classes (adjective vs. agentive) and yield opposite evaluative polarity.
- **Comparison/resemblance** is handled by the *hina* particle construction (X hina) and the case-marked *rikch'akuy* construction (X-man rikch'akuy), not by fixed idioms.
- **Asymmetries / absences:** *sut'i*, *pantay*, *iñiy*, *chani*, and the *rikch'ay* noun yield **no** fixed multi-word constructions — their relevant meanings are carried by derivations or single verbs. The construction inventory clusters strongly on the *chiqa-*/*allin*/*llulla* evaluative roots, with the *kawsay*, *sunqu*, *simi*, and *kay* frames being the recurrent productive templates.
