| Representational accuracy and realism terms | These expressions are used to evaluate how accurately or convincingly a work, narrative, or representation captures reality or its source. | Apparent truth and assessed probability / Surface-plausibility terms | These expressions are used when a speaker is evaluating how convincing, probable, or believable something appears, without committing to a claim that it is actually true, or when something appears to be true, valid, or well-founded but is not, or when its actual status is uncertain.
