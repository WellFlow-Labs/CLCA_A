# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2a Derivational System
# Language: Chinese (zh)

---

## Preliminary Note on Scope

Chinese morphology differs substantially from Indo-European derivational systems. The forms documented here represent attested morphological operations that produce new lexical items from P1 bases: primarily suffixation with nominalizing or adjectivializing suffixes (化, 性, 者, 感), and a small number of prefixation and reduplication patterns. Compounding is the dominant word-formation process in Chinese but is treated in P1b as root expansion rather than derivation proper. Only forms where a P1 item functions as a direct base for a morphological operation — not merely as a component in a new compound — are included here. Where the boundary is unclear, this is noted.

Gaps are preserved. Many P1 items yield no common derivation by these criteria.

## Derived Forms

---

**1.**
**Base Form:** 真实 (zhēnshí)
**Derived Form:** 真实化
**Transliteration:** zhēnshíhuà
**Literal Breakdown:** true + solid/real + -ize (verbalizing/nominalizing suffix)
**Natural English Translation:** to make realistic, to render authentic, realization (in the sense of making real)
**Usage:** Used in literary, cinematic, and design contexts to describe the process of making a representation more faithful to reality: 人物形象的真实化 (the rendering of character images as more realistic). Less common than the base form; appears primarily in written critical discourse.
**Epistemic Status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**2.**
**Base Form:** 真实 (zhēnshí)
**Derived Form:** 真实性
**Transliteration:** zhēnshíxìng
**Literal Breakdown:** true + solid/real + -ness/-ity (nominalizing suffix)
**Natural English Translation:** truthfulness, authenticity, veracity, fidelity
**Usage:** The nominalized form of 真实, used to name the property of being truthful or authentic as an abstract quality subject to evaluation or measurement. Extremely common in legal, journalistic, and literary-critical discourse: 新闻报道的真实性 (the truthfulness of news reporting), 历史真实性 (historical authenticity). This is one of the most productive and well-established 性-nominalizations in the truth domain.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

**Usage:** Nominalizes 虚假 to name the abstract property of being false or deceptive, used in legal and philosophical discourse: 陈述的虚假性 (the falseness of a statement). Less frequent than 真实性 but attested in regulatory and academic writing.

**20.**
**Base Form:** 鉴定 (jiàndìng)
**Derived Form:** 鉴定书
**Transliteration:** jiàndìngshū
**Literal Breakdown:** to examine/appraise + to determine/fix + document/book
**Natural English Translation:** certificate of authentication, appraisal report, expert opinion (document)

## Items for Which No Common Derivation Is Identified

The following P1 items were examined for derivational productivity. No common derived form — by suffixation, prefixation, or other morphological operation — was identified beyond what is already documented in P1 and P1b. In each case, the base form is itself the primary lexical unit in use, and any extensions are compounding rather than derivation proper.

| P1 Item | Reason no derivation is documented |
|---|---|
| 真 (zhēn) | Functions as a root; its derivatives are compounds documented in P1b, not suffixed forms |
| 假 (jiǎ) | Functions as a root; derivatives are compounds; 假性 exists (see note below) |
| 真相 (zhēnxiàng) | No common suffixed or prefixed form identified beyond the base noun |
| 事实 (shìshí) | 事实上 is a fixed adverbial phrase (locative pattern); no 性-nominalization in common use |
| 真伪 (zhēnwěi) | Functions as a noun/compound; no common derivation identified |
| 伪造 (wěizào) | 伪造者 is marginally attested but 伪造人 and 造假者 are more common; not documented |
| 冒充 (màochōng) | No common derivation identified |
| 名副其实 / 名不副实 | Fixed four-character expressions; not bases for derivation |
| 如实 (rúshí) | Fixed adverbial expression; not a base for derivation |
| 辨别 (biànbié) | No common suffixed form identified |
| 是非 (shìfēi) | No common derivation identified |

## Supplementary Note: 假性

**Base Form:** 假 (jiǎ)
**Derived Form:** 假性
**Transliteration:** jiǎxìng
**Literal Breakdown:** false + -ness/-ity / false + nature
**Natural English Translation:** pseudo-, false (as a medical or technical prefix-like modifier); spurious quality
**Usage:** In medical and technical Chinese, 假性 functions as a bound modifier meaning "pseudo-" or "false," appearing in terms such as 假性近视 (pseudomyopia), 假性肥大 (pseudohypertrophy). It is debatable whether this represents suffixation of 假 or a compound with 性 functioning as a noun meaning "nature/type." The pattern is productive in medical terminology but the analysis as derivation is uncertain.
**Epistemic Status:** [UNCERTAIN]

## Summary Observation

The most productive derivational operation attested across P1 items in this domain is **suffixation with 性** to form abstract property nouns (真实性, 正确性, 准确性, 可靠性, 客观性, 主观性, 实质性). This suffix applies selectively: it attaches readily to evaluative adjectives that name properties susceptible to degree or measurement, but does not attach to all P1 items (e.g., 真相, 事实, 真伪 do not yield common 性-forms). The suffix **上** applied to 本质 and 实质 produces adverbial phrases that are highly frequent but represent a locative pattern rather than lexical derivation proper. The suffix **度** appears in the technical term 失真度. Agentive **者** is attested with 伪善 and marginally with 鉴定. The suffix **化** is attested but marginal in this domain. No prefixation pattern specific to this domain is identified beyond the general negator 不.
