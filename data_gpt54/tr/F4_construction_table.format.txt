| ATTRIBUTION | Bu bilgi **doğru**. | *Bu bilgi doğru.* | “This information is correct/true.” | Very common in everyday evaluation of information, claims, documents, and persons/sources. | ATTRIBUTION | Bu belge **geçerli** değil. | *Bu belge geçerli değil.* | “This document is not valid.” | Common especially in administrative, legal, and institutional contexts for validity judgments. This belongs to the same broad predicative pattern, with alternants attested in P4a such as **X doğru / gerçek / haklı / güvenilir (olmak)**. | [CORE] |
| NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION | Bu iddia **doğru değil**. | *Bu iddia doğru değil.* | “This claim is not true.” | A very natural way to negate truth-related predicates in general discourse. P4a also attests lexical alternants for negative evaluation, including **X sahte / asılsız / geçersiz**. | [CORE] |
| NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION | Bu haber **asılsız**. | *Bu haber asılsız.* | “This report is baseless.” | Common in media, public statements, and formal denial contexts; lexical negatives can sound more categorical than **A değil**. | ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION | **sahte belge** | *sahte belge* | “fake/forged document” | Very common for authenticity/falsity judgments, especially with documents, goods, and representations. | BECOME / INCHOATIVE | Hikâye daha **inandırıcı oldu**. | *Hikâye daha inandırıcı oldu.* | “The story became more convincing.” | **X olmak** is the regular and productive way to express change of state in this semantic area. P4a notes limited alternate inchoatives in **-leş-**. | [CORE] |

P4a notes only limited causatives of this kind, with **-leştir-** not broadly available across the whole domain. | [MARGINAL] |
| ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION | Raporun **doğruluğu** tartışılıyor. | *Raporun doğruluğu tartışılıyor.* | “The accuracy/truth of the report is being discussed.” | Very common in formal, academic, bureaucratic, and evaluative discourse. P4a attests productive alternants in **-lık/-lik/-luk/-lük**, including **gerçeklik, güvenilirlik, geçerlilik, dürüstlük, sahicilik**, plus lexical nouns such as **hakikat, meşruiyet**. | [CORE] |
| ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION | Ölçümün **güvenilirliği** düşük. | *Ölçümün güvenilirliği düşük.* | “The reliability of the measurement is low.” | Especially common in technical, scientific, and assessment contexts. Alternate attested nominalizations in P4a include **doğruluk, gerçeklik, geçerlilik, meşruiyet, dürüstlük, sahicilik**. | [CORE] |
| AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION (LIAR, TRUTH-TELLER) | **yalancı** gerçeği sakladı. | *yalancı gerçeği sakladı.* | “The liar hid the truth.” | **yalancı** is a common lexical agent noun in ordinary speech and narrative contexts. | AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION (LIAR, TRUTH-TELLER) | Herkes ona **dürüst biri** der. | *Herkes ona dürüst biri der.* | “Everyone calls him an honest person.” | This descriptive phrase is natural for positive character evaluation, especially where no compact lexical counterpart to “liar” is preferred. | VERIDICAL SPEECH / REPORTING | Bana **gerçeği söyle**. | *Bana gerçeği söyle.* | “Tell me the truth.” | A central and idiomatic pattern for honesty, disclosure, and confrontation in everyday and literary use. P4a attests alternate objects and verbs in the same construction, including **doğruyu / hakikati** with **söylemek / yansıtmak / çarpıtmak / gizlemek**. | [CORE] |
| VERIDICAL SPEECH / REPORTING | Bu açıklama **gerçeği çarpıtıyor**. | *Bu açıklama gerçeği çarpıtıyor.* | “This statement distorts the truth.” | Common in media criticism, political discourse, and evaluative commentary. Attested alternants in P4a include **doğruyu / hakikati** as objects and verbs such as **söylemek, yansıtmak, gizlemek**. | [CORE] |
| PARTIAL-TRUTH CONSTRUCTION | Bu söylentinin **gerçek payı var**. | *Bu söylentinin gerçek payı var.* | “There is some truth in this rumor.” | A compact and idiomatic way to indicate partial truth, especially in discussion of rumors, criticism, or mixed claims.
