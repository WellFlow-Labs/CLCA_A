| Subject + 是 + truth/authenticity adjective + 的 | 这是真的。 | Zhè shì zhēn de. | "This is true / genuine." | Ubiquitous in spoken and written Chinese across all registers; the 是…的 frame is the default predicate construction for truth-domain attribution. Applies to the full truth-domain vocabulary (真, 假, 正确, 错误, 可靠, 客观, etc.). Degree adverbs (很, 完全, 根本) insert before the adjective. | [CORE] |
| Subject + 是 + truth/authenticity adjective + 的 | 这份报告是真实的。 | Zhè fèn bàogào shì zhēnshí de. | "This report is truthful / accurate." | Standard in both spoken and written registers; disyllabic adjectives (真实, 准确) are more typical of formal contexts than monosyllabic ones (真). | [CORE] |
| Subject + 是 + truth/authenticity adjective + 的 | 这份报告是虚假的。 | Zhè fèn bàogào shì xūjiǎ de. | "This report is false / fraudulent." | More formal register than 这是真的; 虚假 is characteristic of written, administrative, and journalistic contexts. No restriction on noun type as subject. | [CORE] |
| Subject + 是 + truth/authenticity adjective + 的 | 这条消息是真实的。 | Zhè tiáo xiāoxi shì zhēnshí de. | "This piece of news is truthful/accurate." | Widely used across registers for predicating truth-domain adjectives; the 是…的 frame is the default spoken and written form. No observable restriction on noun type. | [CORE] |
| Bare predicate (formal/written) | 他的陈述属实。 | Tā de chénshù shǔshí. | "His statement is confirmed as accurate." | Restricted to formal, legal, and administrative registers; 属实 is a fixed predicate and does not appear in the 是…的 frame. The bare adjectival predicate (without 是) is more assertive and formal than the copular form. | [CORE] |
| Bare predicate (formal/written) | 报道失实 | bàodào shīshí | (reporting is inaccurate) | Restricted to formal/written registers and fixed expressions; not a free productive alternate in spoken Chinese. | [CORE] |
| Subject + 不是 + truth/authenticity adjective + 的 | 这不是真的。 | Zhè bú shì zhēn de. | "This is not true / not genuine." | Emphatic or contrastive negation; commonly used in spoken and written registers when contradicting a prior claim. More pragmatically marked than direct negation. The standard spoken negation of the attribution frame; unrestricted across registers and contexts. No observable constraints on the truth-domain adjectives it combines with. | [CORE] |
| Subject + 不是 + truth/authenticity adjective + 的 (paired) | 这份文件不是真的，是假的。 | Zhè fèn wénjiàn bù shì zhēn de, shì jiǎ de. | "This document is not genuine; it is fake." | Common in everyday and journalistic registers for genuineness judgments; the paired structure (不是真的，是假的) is a natural reinforcement pattern. | Subject + 不 + adjective | 他的判断不准确。 | Tā de pànduàn bù zhǔnquè. | "His judgment is not accurate." | Neutral negation; more common in analytical and evaluative prose. Applies productively across gradable truth-domain adjectives. 没有 is not used for property negation in this domain. | [CORE] |
| Lexicalized negative compound | 这篇报道严重失实。 | Zhè piān bàodào yánzhòng shīshí. | "This report is seriously inaccurate." | Characteristic of journalistic and legal registers; 失实 is a lexicalized negative compound and does not take 不. Degree modification (严重) is common and attested. | [CORE] |
| Formal written negation (并非) | 该陈述并非属实。 | Gāi chénshù bìngfēi shǔshí. | "The statement is not confirmed as factual." | Restricted to formal written registers (legal, journalistic, administrative); 并非 does not occur freely in casual speech. The demonstrative 该 further marks this as a formal register item. | [MARGINAL] |
| Truth/authenticity adjective + 的 + Noun | 可靠的证据 | kěkào de zhèngjù | "reliable evidence" | Highly productive across the full vocabulary; attested with 真实的报道, 虚假的信息, 正确的方法, 准确的描述, 道地的北京话. Standard pattern in all registers. | [CORE] |
| Truth/authenticity adjective + 的 + Noun | 客观的评价 | kèguān de píngjià | "an objective evaluation" | Same pattern; 客观 is characteristic of formal, analytical, and academic registers. No register restriction on the construction itself. | [CORE] |
| Truth/authenticity adjective + 的 + Noun | 真实的情况 | zhēnshí de qíngkuàng | "the true / actual situation" | The modifier + 的 + noun pattern is the standard attributive construction in Chinese; no register restriction. Disyllabic modifiers require 的; monosyllabic modifiers may omit it in fixed compounds. | [CORE] |
| Truth/authenticity adjective + 的 + Noun | 虚假的广告 | xūjiǎ de guǎnggào | "false / fraudulent advertising" | Common in consumer, legal, and media contexts; 虚假 is a standard collocate with 广告, 信息, 宣传 in regulatory and journalistic discourse. | [CORE] |
| 成语 + 的 + Noun | 名副其实的专家 | míng fù qí shí de zhuānjiā | "a genuine expert; an expert who truly deserves the name" | Four-character expressions used attributively require 的; this pattern is common in written and formal registers. The 成语 itself carries evaluative force beyond simple modification. | [CORE] |
| 成语 + 的 + Noun | 货真价实的好东西 | huò zhēn jià shí de hǎo dōngxi | "something that is genuinely what it claims to be" | Four-character expressions used attributively require 的; this pattern is common in written and formal registers. The 成语 itself carries evaluative force beyond simple modification. | [CORE] |
| 变得 + adjective | 情况变得更加复杂，真相变得难以查明。 | Qíngkuàng biàn de gèngjiā fùzá, zhēnxiàng biàn de nányǐ chámíng. | "The situation became more complex; the truth became harder to establish." | Applies to gradable truth-domain adjectives (准确, 可靠, 客观); semantically odd with categorical 真/假, which do not readily admit of becoming. | 变得 + adjective + 了 | 报道变得失实了。 | Bàodào biàn de shīshí le. | "The reporting has become inaccurate." | Syntactically well-formed but less common than direct predication; inchoative framing of truth-domain adjectives is not a high-frequency pattern. More natural with negative-outcome predicates (失实, 失真) than with positive ones. | [MARGINAL] |
| Lexicalized inchoative: 弄假成真 | 弄假成真 | nòng jiǎ chéng zhēn | "What began as pretense became real." | Fixed four-character expression; encodes the specific inchoative meaning of a false state transitioning to a genuine one. | Lexicalized inchoative: 假戏真做 | 假戏真做 | jiǎ xì zhēn zuò | (fiction/pretense enacted as real) | Fixed four-character expression; parallel to 弄假成真 in encoding pretense-to-reality transition. Not a productive open pattern. | [CORE] |
| 化 suffix (inchoative/resultative) | 合法化 | héfǎhuà | "to legalize / to become lawful" | The 化 suffix is productive with disyllabic stative bases in formal and written registers; common in political, legal, and policy discourse. Application is not uniform: monosyllabic truth-domain bases (真, 假) do not take 化 productively. | [CORE] |
| 使/让 + NP + adjective/verb | 这种做法使报道失真。 | Zhè zhǒng zuòfǎ shǐ bàodào shīzhēn. | "This practice causes the reporting to become distorted." | Productive periphrastic causative; 使 is more formal than 让 in this domain. Characteristic of critical, analytical, and regulatory registers (使数据失真, 让判断更客观). In the truth domain, this frame is most commonly attested with distortion/negative-outcome predicates (失真, 失实) rather than truth-positive ones. | [CORE] |
| 使/让 + NP + adjective/verb | 这使报道失真。 | Zhè shǐ bàodào shīzhēn. | "This caused the report to become distorted." | 使 is the formal causative coverb; 让 is more colloquial. In the truth domain, this frame is most commonly attested with distortion/negative-outcome predicates (失真, 失实) rather than truth-positive ones. | [CORE] |
| Verb + NP object (implicit causative) | 掩盖真相 | yǎngài zhēnxiàng | "to cover up the truth" | Highly productive verb-object pattern; causative force is implicit in the semantics of the verb rather than marked syntactically. Common across journalistic, legal, and everyday registers. | [CORE] |
| Lexicalized causative: 以假乱真 | 以假乱真 | yǐ jiǎ luàn zhēn | "using fakes to confuse [what is] genuine" | Fixed four-character expression encoding causative meaning lexically; not a productive open construction. Attested in contexts of deliberate deception and fraud. | [CORE] |
| Resultative complement: verb + 真/实 | 逼真 | bīzhēn | "lifelike" (approach + genuine) | Attested in 逼真 and 属实 but not a productive open construction in this domain. These are better treated as lexicalized compounds than as instances of a generative pattern. | [MARGINAL] |
| No dedicated causative construction | — | — | — | No dedicated causative construction is attested for this domain beyond the periphrastic 使/让 frame and transitive verbs (证实, 核实); no separate structurally distinct causative pattern can be reported. | [UNCERTAIN] |
| Verb/adjective base + 者 | 伪善者 | wěishànzhě | "a hypocrite; one who practices false virtue" | Productive agentive suffix in Chinese; in this domain attaches predominantly to negative-pole bases (伪善者, 造假者, 作假者). Characteristic of formal, written, and critical registers. | [CORE] |
| Verb/adjective base + 者 | 造假者 | zàojiǎzhě | "a fabricator; one who manufactures fakes or falsifies data" | Same pattern; 造假者 is frequent in journalistic, legal, and regulatory contexts. The asymmetry — agentives for falsifiers but not for truth-tellers via 者-suffixation — is supported by the data. | [CORE] |
| Verb/adjective base + 者 | 欺骗者 | qīpiànzhě | "deceiver; one who deceives" | Formal and written register; -者 forms are grammatically productive but less frequent in everyday speech, where 骗子 is preferred. Restricted to verbal compound bases; does not attach to adjectival items. | [MARGINAL] |
| Verb/adjective base + 者 | 伪装者 | wěizhuāngzhě | "one who disguises; impostor" | Same register restriction as 欺骗者; formal and written. The -者 suffix is productive across verbal compounds in this domain but the resulting forms are stylistically marked as formal. | [MARGINAL] |
| Verb/adjective base + 者 | 伪造者 | wěizàozhě | "forger, counterfeiter" | The 者 suffix is formal and appears primarily in written, legal, and official registers; not typical of casual speech. Productive only where the base describes a deliberate human action; no agentive nominalization with 者 is attested for the positive (truth-telling) pole. | [MARGINAL] |
| Colloquial agentive: 骗子 | 骗子 | piànzi | "cheat, swindler" | Colloquial form with suffix 子; preferred in spoken registers over 欺骗者. Register alternate for the deception-agent concept. No colloquial alternate with 子 is attested for 伪造者. | [CORE] |
| 严禁 + truth-domain verb phrase | 严禁弄虚作假。 | Yánjìn nòng xū zuò jiǎ. | "It is strictly forbidden to engage in fraud and falsification." | Restricted to the deliberate-falsification cluster (弄虚作假, 造假, 作假, 伪造); does not appear with truth-evaluation adjectives or authenticity nouns. Characteristic of regulatory, administrative, and anti-corruption discourse. Characteristic of legal, regulatory, and institutional written registers; 严禁 + violation verb is a stable and productive formula in official documents and signage. Not used in casual speech. | [CORE] |
| 不得 + truth-domain verb phrase | 不得造假。 | Bùdé zào jiǎ. | "Fabrication is not permitted." | Same domain restriction as 严禁; 不得 is slightly less emphatic and more broadly used in formal written regulations. Both 严禁 and 不得 are restricted to the falsification sub-domain and do not extend to truth-evaluation adjectives (正确, 真实) or authenticity nouns (真相, 事实). | [CORE] |
| 不得 + truth-domain verb phrase | 不得欺骗消费者 | bùdé qīpiàn xiāofèizhě | "it is prohibited to deceive consumers" | 不得 is the standard formal prohibition marker in legal and regulatory texts; productive with truth-domain violation verbs. Slightly less emphatic than 严禁 and somewhat broader in distribution across formal registers. | [CORE] |
| Epistemic/investigative verb + 真相/事实/真伪 (revealing set) | 揭露真相 | jiēlù zhēnxiàng | "to expose the truth / reveal the true state of affairs" | Most productive construction type in the domain. Revealing/establishing verbs (揭露, 查明, 还原, 尊重) collocate with 真相, 事实, or 是非 as object. Stable across legal, journalistic, and political registers. Highly productive and common across journalistic and public discourse registers; 揭露/揭示 are the most frequent verbs in this slot. Verb selection is constrained by the noun: 真相 takes investigative verbs, not commitment verbs. | [CORE] |
| Epistemic/investigative verb + 真相/事实/真伪 (concealing/distorting set) | 歪曲事实 | wāiqū shìshí | "to distort the facts / misrepresent the truth" | Concealing/distorting verbs (掩盖, 歪曲, 颠倒, 混淆) collocate with the same set of truth-nouns. Contrastive pairing with the revealing set (揭露真相 / 掩盖真相; 尊重事实 / 歪曲事实) is a stable discourse pattern. | [CORE] |
| Commitment verbs + 真理 | 追求真理 | zhuīqiú zhēnlǐ | "to pursue truth" | Common in philosophical, educational, and formal registers; 真理 collocates with commitment and pursuit verbs rather than investigative ones. The verb-noun pairing is conventionalized. | [CORE] |
| 如实 + verb | 如实陈述 | rúshí chénshù | "to state truthfully; to give a truthful account" | Fixed adverbial expression; common in legal and administrative registers. No attested negative parallel form (*如假陈述 is unattested). 如实 is a productive adverb that attaches directly to speech and reporting verbs without 地; not restricted to formal registers but most frequent there. Both 如实 and 据实 are fixed forms and not syntactically decomposable in current usage. | [CORE] |
| 据实 + verb | 据实汇报 | jùshí huìbào | "to report based on the facts" | Same register and collocational restrictions as 如实; especially common in administrative and official reporting contexts. | [CORE] |
| 实事求是地 + verb | 实事求是地说 | shíshì qiúshì de shuō | "to say it as it actually is; to speak empirically and honestly" | The full four-character phrase 实事求是 is a fixed expression associated with political and ideological discourse; its use as a manner adverbial with 地 is more formal and marked than shorter adverbials like 如实. Less common in everyday speech. | [MARGINAL] |

| Truth/reality noun + 上 → sentence-initial adverbial | 本质上，这两个问题是一样的。 | Běnzhìshàng, zhè liǎng gè wèntí shì yīyàng de. | "Essentially, these two questions are the same." | 本质上 and 实质上 function as discourse markers introducing a claim about underlying reality. Distinct from the locative-adverbial nominalization use in Construction 6 in that these forms function as sentence-level adverbials rather than as modifiers within a noun phrase. | [CORE] |

| Adjective/verb base + 性 | 真实性 | zhēnshíxìng | "truthfulness; factual accuracy (as a property)" | Productive suffix on adjectival compounds in this domain; standard in academic, journalistic, and legal registers. | [CORE] |

| Adjective/verb base + 性 | 准确性 | zhǔnquèxìng | "accuracy, the property of being precise" | The 性 suffix is productive with disyllabic bases across formal and technical registers; extremely common in academic, legal, and administrative writing. Monosyllabic bases (真, 假) are restricted and yield specialized rather than general abstract nouns. | [CORE] |
| Adjective/verb base + 上 | 本质上是一样的 | běnzhìshàng shì yīyàng de | "essentially the same" | Applied to 本质 and 实质, produces highly frequent adverbial phrases functioning as discourse markers.
