# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2b Compositional Constructions
# Language: Finnish (fi)

---

## Preliminary Note on Scope and Method

This step documents attested multi-word constructions involving the P1 base items. The focus is on periphrastic, idiomatic, and syntactically-driven constructions that are not reducible to the derivational morphology documented in P2a. Finnish is a morphologically rich language in which much semantic work done by multi-word constructions in other languages is accomplished through derivation and case inflection; this means that the inventory of genuinely multi-word constructions is smaller than it might be in a more analytic language. Absence is recorded explicitly where no common construction is identified.

Constructions are organized by P1 base item. Where a construction involves multiple P1 items, it is listed under the most semantically central one with a cross-reference. Constructions already documented in P1b as fixed collocations are cross-referenced rather than re-documented in full. The boundary between a lexicalized compound (handled in P2a) and a compositional multi-word construction is not always sharp in Finnish; where a form has been treated as a compound in P1b or P2a, it is not re-listed here. Asymmetry across items is preserved throughout.

## Item 1: tosi

---

**Construction 1**

1. **Base Form:** tosi
2. **Example:** Se on tosi.
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** It is true. / That is true. / That's real.
5. **Usage notes:** Predicative use of *tosi* as a complement to *olla* (to be). The most basic propositional truth-attribution construction in Finnish. Used in both spoken and written registers across all contexts.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 2**

1. **Base Form:** tosi
2. **Example:** Onko se tosi?
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** Is it true? / Is that true? / Is that real?
5. **Usage notes:** Interrogative form of the predicative truth-assertion construction. Extremely common in everyday spoken Finnish when seeking confirmation of a surprising or unexpected claim. High frequency in spoken Finnish.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 3**

1. **Base Form:** tosi
2. **Example:** tosi vai tarua
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** true or legend; fact or fiction; real or mythical
5. **Usage notes:** A fixed contrastive phrase pairing *tosi* with *taru* (legend, myth, tale) in the partitive. Used as a heading or framing device in journalism, popular media, and everyday speech to introduce a question about whether something is real or legendary (*tosi vai tarua: toimiiko se?* — "fact or fiction: does it work?"). Functions as a formulaic phrase.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 4**

1. **Base Form:** tosi
2. **Example:** totta kai
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** of course; naturally; certainly
5. **Usage notes:** A fixed affirmative expression using the partitive form *totta* (of truth/true) combined with *kai* (surely, I suppose). Functions as a discourse particle asserting that something is obviously or naturally the case. Very high frequency in spoken Finnish. The particle *kai* adds a sense of self-evidence. Used across registers, slightly more colloquial than *luonnollisesti*. Note: *totta* is the partitive of *tosi/totuus*.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 5**

1. **Base Form:** tosi
2. **Example:** totta puhuen
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** to tell the truth; truthfully speaking; frankly
5. **Usage notes:** Fixed adverbial phrase using the adverbial participle of *puhua*. A participial construction using *totta* (partitive of truth) + *puhuen* (speaking, present participle of *puhua*). Functions as a sentence-initial or parenthetical adverbial signalling that the speaker is about to make a frank or honest statement, often with a slight concessive nuance. *Totta puhuen, en tiedä* (to tell the truth, I don't know). Common in both spoken and written Finnish.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 6**

1. **Base Form:** tosi
2. **Example:** ei totta sanaakaan
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** not a word of truth; not a true word
5. **Usage notes:** A negated construction asserting the complete absence of truth in a statement (*siinä ei ole totta sanaakaan* — "there is not a word of truth in that"). Uses the partitive *totta* + *sanaakaan* (not even a word, partitive of *sana* with the *-kaan* additive particle). Idiomatic in force.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 7**

1. **Base Form:** tosi
2. **Example:** perillä totuudesta
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** aware of the truth; in possession of the truth; knowing the truth
5. **Usage notes:** A postpositional construction using *perillä* (informed about, aware of, literally "at the destination") + the adessive/elative of *totuus*. Used to assert that someone knows or has access to the truth of a matter (*hän on perillä totuudesta* — "he is aware of the truth"). Common in journalistic and everyday discourse.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 8**

1. **Base Form:** tosi
2. **Example:** totuus on toisenlainen
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** the truth is different; the reality is otherwise
5. **Usage notes:** A predicative construction asserting that the actual truth diverges from what has been claimed or assumed (*mutta totuus on toisenlainen* — "but the truth is different"). Functions as a corrective or contrastive framing device in journalistic and argumentative discourse.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 9**

1. **Base Form:** tosi
2. **Example:** totuus tulee ilmi
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** the truth comes out; the truth comes to light
5. **Usage notes:** A construction using *tulla ilmi* (to come to light, to be revealed) with *totuus* as subject. *Tulla ilmi* is a fixed verb-particle construction meaning "to be revealed" or "to come to light." *Ennemmin tai myöhemmin totuus tulee ilmi* (sooner or later the truth will come out). Very common in journalistic, legal, and narrative discourse across all registers.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 10**

1. **Base Form:** tosi
2. **Example:** kertoa totuus
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** to tell the truth
5. **Usage notes:** The standard verb-object construction for the act of truth-telling (*kerro totuus* — "tell the truth"; *hän kertoi totuuden* — "he told the truth"). Uses *kertoa* (to tell, to narrate) with *totuus* in the accusative/genitive. High frequency across all registers.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 11**

1. **Base Form:** tosi
2. **Example:** totuus on karvas
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** the truth is bitter; truth is hard to swallow; truth hurts
5. **Usage notes:** A predicative construction using *karvas* (bitter) as a predicate of *totuus*. Functions as a semi-proverbial expression acknowledging that true information is often unwelcome or painful. *Totuus on karvas, mutta se on parempi kuin valhe* (the truth is bitter, but it is better than a lie). Attested in journalistic and everyday discourse.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 12**

1. **Base Form:** tosi
2. **Example:** totuus on jossain siellä
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** the truth is out there
5. **Usage notes:** A construction that has entered Finnish usage partly through the influence of the television series *The X-Files* (*Salaiset kansiot*). Used both seriously and ironically in journalistic and popular discourse to suggest that the truth exists but is hidden or difficult to access. Attested in Finnish media.
6. **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**Construction 13**

1. **Base Form:** tosi
2. **Example:** puhua totta
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** to speak the truth; to tell the truth; to be truthful
5. **Usage notes:** A verb-object construction using *puhua* (to speak) + *totta* (partitive of *tosi/totuus*). The partitive *totta* is required. *Hän puhuu totta* (he/she is telling the truth). Distinct from *kertoa totuus* in that it emphasizes the manner of speaking (truthfully) rather than the disclosure of a specific truth. Contrasts with *puhua valhetta* (to tell a lie). High frequency in everyday and formal discourse.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 14**

1. **Base Form:** tosi
2. **Example:** sanoa suoraan
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** to say directly; to speak plainly; to be frank
5. **Usage notes:** A verb-adverb construction using *sanoa* (to say) + *suoraan* (directly, straight). While not derived from *tosi*, this construction functions as a near-synonym of *puhua totta* in contexts of frank truth-telling and frequently co-occurs with truth-evaluation vocabulary. Included here as an attested periphrastic alternative. High frequency in spoken Finnish.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 15**

1. **Base Form:** tosi
2. **Example:** olla totta
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** to be true; to be the case
5. **Usage notes:** The standard predicate construction for asserting truth in Finnish. The partitive *totta* (rather than nominative *tosi*) is required in this construction. *Se ei voi olla totta* (it cannot be true); *onko se totta?* (is it true?). Fundamental to everyday epistemic discourse across all registers.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 16**

1. **Base Form:** tosi
2. **Example:** sanoa totta
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** to say something true; to tell the truth
5. **Usage notes:** Collocation similar to *puhua totta* but focused on a specific utterance rather than a general disposition or ongoing act. *Hän sanoi totta* (what he/she said was true; he/she told the truth). Used across registers.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 17**

1. **Base Form:** tosi
2. **Example:** toden totta
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** truly; really; in all truth; indeed
5. **Usage notes:** Fixed adverbial phrase using genitive *toden* and partitive *totta* of the same base. Used for emphatic affirmation. *Se on toden totta niin* (it is truly so; it really is the case). Registers as slightly formal or literary compared to *tosiaan* or *oikeasti*.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 18**

1. **Base Form:** tosi
2. **Example:** tosi asia
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** a true matter; a fact; the real thing
5. **Usage notes:** Collocation used to assert that something is a genuine fact or a real concern. *Se on tosi asia* (that is a real fact; that is genuinely the case). Functions as a mild emphatic assertion. Used in everyday speech.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 19**

1. **Base Form:** tosi
2. **Example:** tosiasia on, että
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** the fact is that; the truth is that; it is a fact that
5. **Usage notes:** Very common discourse marker used to introduce a factual claim presented as incontrovertible. *Tosiasia on, että tilanne on vakava* (the fact is that the situation is serious). Used across registers from everyday speech to formal writing as a rhetorical framing device.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 20**

1. **Base Form:** tosi
2. **Example:** perustuu tositapahtumiin
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** based on true events; based on actual events
5. **Usage notes:** Fixed phrase used in film, literary, and journalistic contexts to label a narrative as factually grounded. Functions as a genre marker. *Elokuva perustuu tositapahtumiin* (the film is based on true events). Calque of the English formula.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 21**

1. **Base Form:** tosi
2. **Example:** tosi hyvä / tosi paljon / tosi vaikea
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** really good / really much / really difficult
5. **Usage notes:** *Tosi* functions as a colloquial intensifying adverb before adjectives and adverbs, equivalent to *todella* (really) or *hyvin* (very) in more formal registers. Extremely common in spoken Finnish, particularly among younger speakers. Not used in formal written registers.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 22**

1. **Base Form:** tosi
2. **Example:** pitää paikkansa
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** to be true; to hold; to be correct
5. **Usage notes:** Very common idiomatic expression for asserting that a claim is true or accurate. *Väite pitää paikkansa* (the claim is true/holds). Literally "holds its place." Used across all registers as a near-synonym of *olla totta*, often preferred in evaluative contexts where a claim is being assessed. *Tämä pitää paikkansa* (this is true/correct).
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 23**

1. **Base Form:** tosi
2. **Example:** ei pidä paikkaansa
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** it is not true; it does not hold; it is incorrect
5. **Usage notes:** Negative form of *pitää paikkansa*. *Väite ei pidä paikkaansa* (the claim is not true/does not hold). The standard colloquial and journalistic construction for asserting that a claim is false or incorrect (*tämä väite ei pidä paikkaansa* — "this claim is not true"). Used across registers to deny the truth of a claim without using the word *väärä* directly. Very high frequency in spoken and written Finnish.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 24**

1. **Base Form:** tosi
2. **Example:** Nyt on tosi kyseessä.
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** Now it's for real. / This time it's serious.
5. **Usage notes:** A colloquial construction indicating that a situation has become genuinely serious or real, as opposed to hypothetical or playful. *Kyseessä* is the inessive of *kysymys* (question, matter) in a fixed postpositional use.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 25**

1. **Base Form:** tosi
2. **Example:** Tosi on tosi.
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** True is true. / The truth is the truth. / A fact is a fact.
5. **Usage notes:** A tautological affirmation used rhetorically to assert that something cannot be denied or argued away. Carries a resigned or emphatic tone in everyday speech.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Item 2: totuus

*Note: Several constructions involving totuus are documented under Item 1 above (tosi), as the two items share a root and many constructions use the nominal form totuus. Cross-reference: Item 1, Constructions 7–11, 13. Additional constructions specific to totuus as a nominal follow.*

---

**Construction 1**

1. **Base Form:** totuus
2. **Example:** totuuden hetki
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** the moment of truth
5. **Usage notes:** A genitive-noun construction (*totuuden* — genitive of *totuus* + *hetki* — moment) functioning as a fixed phrase for a decisive or revealing moment. *Tämä on totuuden hetki* (this is the moment of truth). Calqued from Spanish/English "moment of truth" (*el momento de la verdad*), now fully established in Finnish. Attested in journalistic and everyday Finnish.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 2**

1. **Base Form:** totuus
2. **Example:** totuuden etsiminen
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** the search for truth; seeking the truth
5. **Usage notes:** A genitive-noun construction (*totuuden* + *etsiminen* — the act of searching, action nominal of *etsiä*). Used in philosophical, journalistic, and everyday discourse to describe the epistemic activity of seeking truth. Common in academic and popular contexts.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 3**

1. **Base Form:** totuus
2. **Example:** totuuden nimissä
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** in the name of truth; for the sake of truth
5. **Usage notes:** A postpositional construction using *nimissä* (in the name of, in the names of) with the genitive *totuuden*. Used in rhetorical and argumentative contexts to invoke truth as a justification for a statement or action (*totuuden nimissä on sanottava* — "in the name of truth it must be said"). Attested in formal and journalistic discourse.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 4**

1. **Base Form:** totuus
2. **Example:** totuuden mukainen
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** in accordance with the truth; truthful; accurate
5. **Usage notes:** A postpositional construction using *mukainen* (in accordance with) with the genitive *totuuden*. Functions as an adjectival phrase evaluating whether a statement or representation corresponds to the truth. Note: the lexicalized compound *totuudenmukainen* (documented in P2a) is the more common form; the syntactic construction is also attested.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 5**

1. **Base Form:** totuus
2. **Example:** totuus on se, että
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** the truth is that; the fact is that
5. **Usage notes:** A cleft-like construction using *totuus on se, että* as a framing device to introduce a frank or corrective statement. Functions as a discourse marker signalling that what follows is the speaker's honest assessment of a situation. Common in spoken and journalistic Finnish. Slightly more rhetorical than *tosiasia on, että*.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 6**

1. **Base Form:** totuus
2. **Example:** koko totuus
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** the whole truth; the full truth
5. **Usage notes:** A quantifier-noun construction using *koko* (whole, entire) + *totuus*. Used in legal, journalistic, and everyday contexts to assert that a complete and unedited account is being given. High frequency in legal and journalistic discourse.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 7**

1. **Base Form:** totuus
2. **Example:** koko totuus ja vain totuus
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** the whole truth and nothing but the truth
5. **Usage notes:** The Finnish rendering of the courtroom oath formula. *Hän vannoi kertovansa koko totuuden ja vain totuuden* (he/she swore to tell the whole truth and nothing but the truth). Used in legal contexts and also cited in journalistic and popular discourse. The construction is a calque of the English/Latin legal formula and is attested in Finnish legal and media contexts.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 8**

1. **Base Form:** totuus
2. **Example:** totuus on usein epämukava
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** the truth is often uncomfortable; truth is often inconvenient
5. **Usage notes:** A predicative construction functioning as a semi-proverbial observation about the nature of truth. Attested in journalistic and discursive contexts as a framing device for difficult disclosures. Represents a productive pattern rather than a single fixed phrase.
6. **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**Construction 9**

1. **Base Form:** totuus
2. **Example:** etsiä totuutta
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** to seek the truth; to search for truth
5. **Usage notes:** *Totuutta* is the partitive of *totuus*, used as the object of *etsiä* (to seek, to search for). *Toimittajan tehtävä on etsiä totuutta* (the journalist's task is to seek the truth). Common in philosophical, journalistic, investigative, and everyday contexts. The partitive indicates an ongoing or incomplete process.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 10**

1. **Base Form:** totuus
2. **Example:** totuus on, että
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** the truth is that
5. **Usage notes:** Discourse marker used to introduce a frank or candid statement, often one that contradicts a previous assumption. *Totuus on, että emme tiedä* (the truth is that we don't know). Used across registers.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 11**

1. **Base Form:** totuus
2. **Example:** puhua totuuden nimissä
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** to speak in the name of truth; to invoke truth
5. **Usage notes:** Collocation used in rhetorical and philosophical contexts when a speaker claims to act on behalf of truth. *Hän puhui totuuden nimissä* (he/she spoke in the name of truth). Somewhat elevated in register.
6. **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**Construction 12**

1. **Base Form:** totuus
2. **Example:** totuus ja sovinto
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** truth and reconciliation
5. **Usage notes:** Fixed phrase used in political and historical contexts, particularly in reference to truth and reconciliation processes. *Totuus ja sovinto -prosessi* (truth and reconciliation process). Used in political science, journalism, and policy discourse.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 13**

1. **Base Form:** totuus
2. **Example:** vannoa totuuden nimeen
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** to swear by the truth; to swear in the name of truth
5. **Usage notes:** Collocation used in legal and solemn contexts when a person swears to tell the truth. Less fixed than *koko totuus ja vain totuus* but attested in legal and religious registers.
6. **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**Construction 14**

1. **Base Form:** totuus
2. **Example:** totuus paljastuu
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** the truth is revealed; the truth comes out
5. **Usage notes:** Uses the reflexive/passive verb *paljastua* (to be revealed, to be exposed). Semantically overlaps with *totuus tulee ilmi* but *paljastua* carries a stronger sense of exposure or unmasking.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 15**

1. **Base Form:** totuus
2. **Example:** totuus on jotain muuta
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** the truth is something else; the reality is different
5. **Usage notes:** A corrective construction used to introduce a contradicting account. Common in journalistic and argumentative contexts.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 16**

1. **Base Form:** totuus
2. **Example:** totuuden mukainen kertomus
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** a truthful account; an account in accordance with the truth
5. **Usage notes:** A noun phrase using the genitive *totuuden* + *mukainen* (conforming to) as a modifier of *kertomus* (account, narrative). Used in legal and journalistic contexts. Note: this is the compositional phrase underlying the lexicalized compound *totuudenmukainen* (P2a); both forms are in use.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 17**

1. **Base Form:** totuus
2. **Example:** totuus ylittää kaiken kuvittelun
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** reality surpasses all imagination; truth is stranger than fiction
5. **Usage notes:** A construction functioning as a Finnish equivalent of the proverbial "truth is stranger than fiction." Uses *ylittää* (to surpass, to exceed) + *kaiken kuvittelun* (all imagination). Attested in journalistic and popular discourse as a framing device.
6. **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**Construction 18**

1. **Base Form:** totuus
2. **Example:** totuus iskee vasten kasvoja
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** reality hits you in the face; reality strikes hard
5. **Usage notes:** An idiomatic construction using *iskeä vasten kasvoja* (to strike in the face) with *todellisuus* as subject. Used in journalistic and everyday discourse to describe a sudden and unwelcome confrontation with actual conditions. Attested in Finnish media.
6. **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

## Item 3: aito

---

**Construction 1**

1. **Base Form:** aito
2. **Example:** aito ja alkuperäinen
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** genuine and original; the real thing; authentic and original
5. **Usage notes:** A coordinated adjectival construction pairing *aito* (genuine) with *alkuperäinen* (original). Functions as a fixed or semi-fixed phrase asserting both authenticity and originality, often used in commercial, legal, and everyday contexts (*aito ja alkuperäinen tuote* — "a genuine and original product"). The pairing is formulaic and functions as a reinforced assertion of authenticity.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 2**

1. **Base Form:** aito
2. **Example:** aito suomalainen
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** a true/authentic Finn; a genuine Finnish person
5. **Usage notes:** A noun phrase using *aito* as a modifier of an ethnic or national identity noun. Used in identity discourse, advertising, and cultural commentary to assert authentic national or cultural identity. Can be used straightforwardly or ironically. *Hän on aito suomalainen* (he/she is a genuine/real Finn). Common in everyday and journalistic discourse.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 3**

1. **Base Form:** aito
2. **Example:** aito nahka
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** genuine leather; real leather
5. **Usage notes:** A noun phrase used in commercial and consumer contexts to assert that a material is not synthetic or imitation. Represents a productive pattern (*aito* + material noun) used across commercial labelling and advertising.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 4**

1. **Base Form:** aito
2. **Example:** Se on aito.
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** It is genuine. / It is authentic. / It is the real thing.
5. **Usage notes:** The basic predicative construction asserting the authenticity of an object, person, or emotion. Used in art authentication, commercial, and everyday contexts. High frequency across registers.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 5**

1. **Base Form:** aito
2. **Example:** aito tunne / aito ilo / aito suru
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** a genuine feeling / genuine joy / genuine grief; an authentic emotion
5. **Usage notes:** A noun phrase used in psychological, literary, and everyday discourse to assert that an emotion or feeling is not performed or simulated. *Hänen ilonsa oli aitoa* (his/her joy was genuine). Represents a productive pattern (*aito* + emotion noun) common in existentialist and humanistic discourse.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 6**

1. **Base Form:** aito
2. **Example:** todistaa aidoksi
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** to certify as genuine; to authenticate
5. **Usage notes:** Periphrastic construction using *todistaa* + translative of *aito*. Used in legal, commercial, and art authentication contexts. *Asiantuntija todisti maalauksen aidoksi* (the expert certified the painting as genuine).
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 7**

1. **Base Form:** aito
2. **Example:** osoittautua aidoksi
3. **Transliteration:** N/A
4. **English translation:** to turn out to be genuine; to prove authentic
5. **Usage notes:** Periphrastic construction using *osoittautua* (to turn out to be) + translative *aidoksi*. Used when the authenticity of something is established after investigation or testing. *Asiakirja osoittautui aidoksi* (the document turned out to be genuine). Used in authentication and investigative contexts.
6. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 8**

1. **Base Form:** aito
2. **Example:** ei ole aito
