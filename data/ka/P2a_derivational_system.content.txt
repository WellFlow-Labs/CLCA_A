# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2a Derivational System
# Language: Georgian (ka)

## Derivational Patterns Identified

### Pattern Group 1: Abstract Nominalization with -ობა (-oba)

This suffix creates abstract nouns denoting qualities, states, or conditions. It attaches to adjectival bases.

#### 1.1 Base: ნამდვილი (namdvili) "real, genuine"
**Derived form:** ნამდვილობა (namdviloba)
**Transliteration:** namdviloba
**Literal breakdown:** namdvil- (real/genuine) + -oba (abstract noun suffix)
**Natural English translation:** reality, genuineness, authenticity
**Usage:** Denotes the abstract quality of being real or genuine. Used in philosophical discourse about reality and in practical contexts about authenticity of objects or claims.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 1.2 Base: უტყუარი (uṭq'uari) "truthful, honest"
**Derived form:** უტყუარობა (uṭq'uaroba)
**Transliteration:** uṭq'uaroba
**Literal breakdown:** u- (negative) + ṭq'u- (lie) + -ar- + -oba (abstract noun)
**Natural English translation:** truthfulness, honesty, infallibility
**Usage:** Abstract noun denoting the quality of being truthful or infallible. Used in character evaluation and epistemological contexts.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 1.3 Base: სიყალბე (siq'albe) "falseness"
**Note:** The base itself already contains nominalization. The root q'albi "false" can take -ობა:
**Derived form:** სიყალბობა (siq'alboba) — attested but less common than siq'albe
**Transliteration:** siq'alboba
**Literal breakdown:** si- (abstract prefix) + q'alb- (false) + -oba (abstract noun)
**Natural English translation:** falseness, fakeness (emphatic/intensified)
**Usage:** Alternative abstract nominalization emphasizing the quality of being false. Less common than siq'albe.
**Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

#### 1.4 Base: სამართლიანი (samart'liani) "just, fair"
**Derived form:** სამართლიანობა (samart'lianoba)
**Transliteration:** samart'lianoba
**Literal breakdown:** samart'lian- (just) + -oba (abstract noun)
**Natural English translation:** justice, fairness
**Usage:** Abstract noun for the concept of justice or fairness. Central term in ethical, legal, and political discourse.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 1.5 Base: უსამართლო (usamart'lo) "unjust"
**Derived form:** უსამართლობა (usamart'loba)
**Transliteration:** usamart'loba
**Literal breakdown:** u- (negative) + samart'l- (justice) + -oba (abstract noun)
**Natural English translation:** injustice, unfairness
**Usage:** Abstract noun denoting injustice or unfair treatment. Used in moral, legal, and political discourse.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 1.6 Base: მართლზომიერი (mart'lzomieri) "moderate, temperate"
**Derived form:** მართლზომიერება (mart'lzomiereba)
**Transliteration:** mart'lzomiereba
**Literal breakdown:** mart'lzomier- (moderate) + -eba (abstract noun variant)
**Natural English translation:** moderation, temperance
**Usage:** Abstract noun denoting the virtue of moderation. Used in ethical discourse about balanced conduct.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

**Note on -ობა/-ება variation:** Georgian shows two abstract noun suffixes: -ობა and -ება. The choice is lexically determined, with -ება often appearing after certain stem types. Both create abstract quality nouns.

### Pattern Group 2: Adverbial Derivation with -ად (-ad)

This suffix creates adverbs from adjectives, indicating manner or degree.

#### 2.1 Base: ნამდვილი (namdvili) "real, genuine"
**Derived form:** ნამდვილად (namdvilad)
**Transliteration:** namdvilad
**Literal breakdown:** namdvil- (real) + -ad (adverbial)
**Natural English translation:** really, truly, actually
**Usage:** Emphatic adverb affirming reality or truth of statements. Common in everyday speech as intensifier.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 2.2 Base: ჭეშმარიტი (č'ešmarit'i) "true, genuine"
**Derived form:** ჭეშმარიტად (č'ešmarit'ad)
**Transliteration:** č'ešmarit'ad
**Literal breakdown:** č'ešmarit'- (true) + -ad (adverbial)
**Natural English translation:** truly, genuinely
**Usage:** Adverbial form emphasizing truth or genuineness. Used in formal and elevated speech.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 2.3 Base: უტყუარი (uṭq'uari) "truthful"
**Derived form:** უტყუარად (uṭq'uarad)
**Transliteration:** uṭq'uarad
**Literal breakdown:** uṭq'uar- (truthful) + -ad (adverbial)
**Natural English translation:** truthfully, honestly, infallibly
**Usage:** Indicates manner of speaking or acting without deception. Used to characterize honest communication.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 2.4 Base: ტყუილი (ṭq'uili) "lie, false"
**Derived form:** ტყუილად (ṭq'uilad)
**Transliteration:** ṭq'uilad
**Literal breakdown:** ṭq'uil- (lie/vain) + -ad (adverbial)
**Natural English translation:** falsely, in vain, for nothing
**Usage:** Functions both for "falsely" (deception) and "in vain" (futility). Common in everyday speech.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 2.5 Base: მართებული (mart'ebuli) "correct, proper"
**Derived form:** მართებულად (mart'ebulad)
**Transliteration:** mart'ebulad

**Natural English translation:** correctly, properly
**Usage:** Indicates manner of action as correct or proper. Used to describe how something is done appropriately.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 2.6 Base: სამართლიანი (samart'liani) "just, fair"
**Derived form:** სამართლიანად (samart'lianad)
**Transliteration:** samart'lianad
**Literal breakdown:** samart'lian- (just) + -ad (adverbial)

**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 2.7 Base: ზუსტი (zusti) "precise, accurate"
**Derived form:** ზუსტად (zustad)
**Transliteration:** zustad
**Literal breakdown:** zust- (precise) + -ad (adverbial)
**Natural English translation:** precisely, accurately, exactly
**Usage:** Indicates precise or exact manner. Common in technical and everyday contexts requiring accuracy.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 2.8 Base: სწორი (sc'ori) "correct, right"
**Derived form:** სწორად (sc'orad)
**Transliteration:** sc'orad
**Literal breakdown:** sc'or- (correct) + -ad (adverbial)

### Pattern Group 3: Verbal Noun Formation with Preverbs

Georgian uses preverbs (spatial/aspectual prefixes) combined with verbal roots to create action nouns with -ება suffix.

#### 3.1 Base: ტყუილი (ṭq'uili) "lie" (root: ტყუ-)
**Derived form:** მოტყუება (moṭq'ueba)
**Transliteration:** moṭq'ueba
**Literal breakdown:** mo- (preverb) + ṭq'u- (deceive) + -eba (verbal noun)
**Natural English translation:** deception, deceiving
**Usage:** Denotes the act of deceiving or state of being deceived. Used across contexts from interpersonal to legal.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 3.2 Base: ტყუილი (ṭq'uili) (root: ტყუ-)
**Derived form:** ატყუება (aṭq'ueba)
**Transliteration:** aṭq'ueba
**Literal breakdown:** a- (preverb) + ṭq'u- (deceive) + -eba (verbal noun)
**Natural English translation:** deceiving, misleading
**Usage:** Denotes act of deceiving with different aspectual nuance from moṭq'ueba. Used in similar contexts.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 3.3 Base: ტყუილი (ṭq'uili) (root: ტყუ-)
**Derived form:** გატყუება (gaṭq'ueba)
**Transliteration:** gaṭq'ueba
**Literal breakdown:** ga- (preverb, completive) + ṭq'u- (deceive) + -eba (verbal noun)
**Natural English translation:** deceiving, cheating (completed action)
**Usage:** Denotes completed or successful deception. Used when someone has been successfully deceived.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 3.4 Base: ტყუილი (ṭq'uili) (root: ტყუ-)
**Derived form:** შეტყუება (šeṭq'ueba)
**Transliteration:** šeṭq'ueba
**Literal breakdown:** še- (preverb) + ṭq'u- (deceive) + -eba (verbal noun)
**Natural English translation:** mutual deception, collusion
**Usage:** Denotes mutual or reciprocal deception. Used in contexts of conspiracy or mutual fraud.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 3.5 Base: მართალი (mart'ali) "right, correct" (root: მართ-)
**Derived form:** გამართლება (gamart'leba)
**Transliteration:** gamart'leba
**Literal breakdown:** ga- (preverb) + mart'l- (right) + -eba (verbal noun)
**Natural English translation:** justification, vindication
**Usage:** Used in legal, moral, and argumentative contexts for justifying actions or vindicating persons.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 3.6 Base: მართალი (mart'ali) (root: მართ-)
**Derived form:** დამართლება (damart'leba)
**Transliteration:** damart'leba
**Literal breakdown:** da- (preverb) + mart'l- (correct) + -eba (verbal noun)
**Natural English translation:** confirmation, corroboration
**Usage:** Used for confirming or corroborating claims or facts. Common in formal contexts.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 3.7 Base: მართალი (mart'ali) (root: მართ-)
**Derived form:** გასამართლება (gasamart'leba)
**Transliteration:** gasamart'leba
**Literal breakdown:** ga- (preverb) + sa- (future participle) + mart'l- (judge) + -eba (verbal noun)
**Natural English translation:** trial, judgment, court proceeding
**Usage:** Legal term for trial or judicial proceeding. Used in judicial contexts.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 3.8 Base: ნამდვილი (namdvili) "real" (root: ნამდვილ-)
**Derived form:** განამდვილება (ganamdvileba)
**Transliteration:** ganamdvileba
**Literal breakdown:** ga- (preverb) + namdvil- (real) + -eba (verbal noun)
**Natural English translation:** realization, actualization
**Usage:** Denotes process of making something real or actualizing potential. Used in philosophical and practical contexts.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 3.9 Base: სანდო (sando) "reliable" (root: ნდ-)
**Derived form:** დანდობა (dandoba) — if attested
**Note:** Need to verify attestation. The root ნდ- "trust" does form verbal nouns, but specific derivation from sando needs confirmation.
**Epistemic status:** [UNCERTAIN]

### Pattern Group 5: Agentive Derivation

Georgian forms agent nouns through various means, including prefixes and suffixes.

#### 5.1 Base: ტყუილი (ṭq'uili) (root: ტყუ-)
**Derived form:** მატყუარა (maṭq'uara)
**Transliteration:** maṭq'uara
**Literal breakdown:** ma- (agentive prefix) + ṭq'u- (deceive) + -ar- (agentive) + -a
**Natural English translation:** deceiver, liar, fraud
**Usage:** Denotes person who habitually deceives. Used as character evaluation indicating untrustworthiness.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 5.2 Base: ტყუილი (ṭq'uili) (root: ტყუ-)
**Derived form:** მატყუარებელი (maṭq'uarebeli)
**Transliteration:** maṭq'uarebeli
**Literal breakdown:** ma- (agentive) + ṭq'u- (deceive) + -ar- + -ebel- (agentive participial) + -i
**Natural English translation:** deceiver, one who deceives
**Usage:** Similar to maṭq'uara but with more active participial force. Used for someone actively deceiving.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 5.3 Base: ტყუილი (ṭq'uili) (root: ტყუ-)
**Derived form:** ტყუილმოთქმელი (ṭq'uilmotxmeli)
**Transliteration:** ṭq'uilmotxmeli
**Literal breakdown:** ṭq'uil- (lie) + mo- (preverb) + txmel- (speaker) + -i
**Natural English translation:** liar, one who speaks lies
**Usage:** Formal or literary term for a liar. Used to characterize habitual false speakers.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Summary of Productive Patterns

**Highly productive patterns identified:**

1. **-ად adverbialization:** Applies broadly to adjectives in the truth domain (namdvilad, č'ešmarit'ad, uṭq'uarad, ṭq'uilad, mart'ebulad, samart'lianad, zustad, sc'orad)

2. **-ობა/-ება abstract nominalization:** Creates quality nouns from adjectives (namdviloba, uṭq'uaroba, samart'lianoba, usamart'loba, mart'lzomiereba)

3. **Preverb + root + -ება verbal nouns:** Highly productive with aspectual/spatial preverbs (moṭq'ueba, aṭq'ueba, gaṭq'ueba, šeṭq'ueba, gamart'leba, damart'leba, ganamdvileba)

4. **-ული participial adjectives:** Creates resultative/passive adjectives (mart'ebuli, gamart'lebuli, mart'uli, gamart'uli, ganamdvilebuli, moṭq'uebuli)

5. **უ- negation:** Productive with adjectives (usamart'lo, umart'ebulo, and უტყუარი as lexicalized form)

**Moderately productive patterns:**

6. **Compound formation:** Productive with მართ- root in particular (mart'lmsaǯuli, mart'lmadidebeli, mart'lzomieri, mart'lc'era, mart'lmeṭq'veleba)

7. **Agentive derivation:** Limited attestation with ტყუ- root (maṭq'uara, maṭq'uarebeli, ṭq'uilmotxmeli)

**Limited/lexicalized patterns:**

8. **-იანი adjectivalization:** Attested with samart'ali → samart'liani, but not broadly productive in this domain

9. **-ებრივი "pertaining to":** Attested with samart'ali → samart'lebrivi, domain-specific

10. **თვით- reflexive:** Attested with tviṭmoṭq'ueba, but limited examples

11. **Emphatic particles:** Lexicalized forms (mart'lac, mart'la, ṭq'uilubralod, ṭq'uilubralo)

**Gaps noted:**

- No common diminutive derivations identified
- No augmentative derivations identified  
- Limited causative derivations (mostly lexicalized in compounds)
- No regular comparative/superlative derivations specific to truth domain (Georgian uses analytic comparison)
