## Summary Notes

**No constructions in this inventory received an [UNCERTAIN] rating.** All ten construction types are represented by examples with consistent structural descriptions and cross-referenced internal contrasts (e.g., *-ncha-* vs. *-chi-* causatives; *-ka-* inchoative vs. *-ncha-* causative; prenominal vs. predicative negation). The single [MARGINAL] rating assigned to the possessive nominalization *cheqa kaynin* reflects the explicit register restriction stated in P4a (philosophical and religious registers only), not structural doubt.

**The gap in the inchoative paradigm** (*llulla* lacking a *-ka-* form) is preserved as reported and not filled by inference.

**Alternate constructions listed** are drawn exclusively from forms already attested within the P4a inventory; no new forms have been introduced.
