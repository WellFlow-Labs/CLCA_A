## SUMMARY OF CONSTRAINTS

### High Productivity Patterns
- Attribution (constructions 1-2): Fully productive with both copula and adjectival verb patterns
- Adnominal modification (3): Systematic with Sino-Korean stems
- Abstract nominalization (6): Extremely productive -성 suffix with Sino-Korean roots
- Verbal collocations (8): Highly productive across multiple verbs
- Adverbial expressions (9): Productive with both Sino-Korean and native roots
- Discourse markers (11): Grammaticalized and extremely common

### Restricted Patterns
- Causative (5): Limited productivity; truth-terms resist direct causativization
- Agentive nominalization (7): Only 거짓말쟁이 attested; no positive counterpart
- Comparative constructions (15): Truth-domain terms resist gradability
- Compound verbs (20): Lexicalized items only, not productive pattern

### Asymmetries Observed
- Agentive: 거짓말쟁이 exists, but no *진실쟁이
- Privative: 거짓없이 common, but 진실없이 rare
- Abstract nominalization: Sino-Korean only; no native Korean *거짓성
- Focus on marking deviance (lying) rather than expected behavior (truth-telling)
