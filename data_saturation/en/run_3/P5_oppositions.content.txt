# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P5 OPPOSITIONS
**Language: English (en)**

---

## Preliminary Notes on Method

Oppositions below are extracted directly from attested patterns in P1–P4b. Each opposition is supported by explicit evidence in the context: co-occurrence in contrast statements, antonym relationships named in P1 entries, morphological negation pairs documented in P2a, or constructional contrasts documented in P4a–P4b. No opposition has been inferred or constructed beyond what the data support. Where the context names a contrast explicitly, this is noted. Where the contrast is implicit but clearly supported by co-occurrence patterns, this is also noted.

The protocol instruction not to normalize or group oppositions is followed: oppositions are listed individually even where they share structural features.
