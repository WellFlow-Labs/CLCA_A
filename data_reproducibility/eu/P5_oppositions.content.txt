2. Morphological negation with "ez" provides systematic opposition for all predicative constructions
3. Many constructional oppositions show parallel structure with contrasting lexical items (e.g., egia esan / gezurra esan)
4. Some oppositions are asymmetric in frequency or productivity (e.g., gezurtatu less common than gezurra esan)
5. Several implied oppositions exist in the morphological system (e.g., -garri vs. -ezin, -gaitz) but are not all equally attested in the provided data
6. The reality-appearance distinction (benetako/itxurazko, benetan/itxuraz) forms a significant semantic axis
7. Moral evaluation shows asymmetry in derivational patterns (neutral -tasun for positive terms, pejorative -keria for negative terms)
