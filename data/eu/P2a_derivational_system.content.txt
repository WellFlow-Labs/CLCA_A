## SUMMARY OF DERIVATIONAL PATTERNS

### Productive Suffixes Observed:

1. **-zko / -ezko** (relational): Creates adjectives from nouns (egiazkoa, gezurrezko, itxurazko)

2. **-tasun** (abstract noun): Creates quality nouns (egiazkotasuna, zuzentasuna, zintzotasuna, ziurtasuna)

3. **-tu** (verbalizer): Creates verbs from adjectives/nouns (egiazkotu, zuzendu, okertu, ziurtatu)

4. **-garri** (worthy of/able to): Creates adjectives of capability or worthiness (fidagarria, sinesgarria, frogagarria, zalantzagarria)

5. **-ki** (adverbial): Creates manner adverbs (egiazki, zuzenki, zintzoki, ziurki)

6. **-tzaile** (agent): Creates agent nouns (egiaztatzailea, zuzentzailea)

7. **-keta** (action noun): Creates action nominals (zuzenketa)

8. **-tze / -pen** (nominalization): Creates action/result nominals (egiaztatze, egiaztapen)

9. **-tan / -ean** (locative/manner): Creates adverbials (egiatan, benetan, zuzenean)

10. **-zale** (lover/enthusiast): Creates characterizing compounds (egiazale)

### Asymmetries Noted:

- **egia** shows high derivational productivity (10+ distinct derivations)
- **gezur** shows limited productivity (4 derivations, no verbal or abstract nominal forms)
- **zuzen** shows high productivity with semantic extension (11 derivations across multiple domains)
- Borrowed roots (faltsu, erreala) show limited integration into native derivational patterns
- The suffix **-garri** is highly productive in the truth-evaluation domain
- Abstract nominals in **-tasun** are common for quality concepts
- Not all roots support verbal derivation (gezur, bene-, fida-, sines-)

### Gaps Identified:

- No attested verbal form *gezurtu (to lie - uses "gezurra esan" instead)
- No attested abstract nominal *gezurtasuna (uses "gezurra" itself)
- No attested agentive *egiazalea (uses "egiazale" without article in this function)
- Limited adverbial derivations from some roots (no *gezurki, *okerki)
