# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P1b ROOT EXPANSION
## Language: English (en)

Since English uses Latin script natively, no transliteration is required; native form and transliteration are identical throughout.

---

## STEP 1 — Identify Central Roots

Examining the P1 items, the morphemes that recur most frequently as building blocks of the truth-evaluation domain are:

### Root 1: **true / truth** (Old English *trēowe*, *trīewþ*)
- **Participates in P1 items:** *true* (#1), *truth(ful)* (#27), *truthful* explicitly, *faithful* (glossed "true"), and underlies *trustworthy*/*trust* (#24) historically (same Germanic root *trēow-*).
- **Direct P1 participation:** ~3–4 items (*true*, *truthful*, and the *trust-* derivatives via shared etymology).
- **Core meaning contribution:** correspondence to fact; fidelity/faithfulness; reliability of a person or statement. The central native Germanic root of the domain.

### Root 2: **ver-** (Latin *vērus* "true")
- **Participates in P1 items:** *verisimilitude* (#19), *veracious* (#20); related to *valid*-adjacent Latinate truth vocabulary.
- **Direct P1 participation:** 2 items.
- **Core meaning contribution:** truth, truthfulness, correspondence to reality (the Latinate/learned counterpart to native *true*).

### Root 3: **correct / rect-** (Latin *rectus* "straight, right") and the closely allied native **right** (OE *riht*)
- **Participates in P1 items:** *correct* (#6), *right* (#17); semantically allied with *proper*, *just*.
- **Direct P1 participation:** 2 items directly (*correct*, *right*), more via shared "straightness/rightness" sense.
- **Core meaning contribution:** conformity to a standard — factual correctness, moral rightness, normative propriety.

The three most productive truth-domain roots are therefore **true/truth**, **ver-**, and **correct/right (rect-)**. These are expanded below.

## STEP 2 — Root Expansion

### ROOT 1: **true / truth** (and *trew-/trust-*)

This root is **highly productive (well over 30 forms)**. The 20–30 most common are listed; additional rarer forms exist.

**true**
- Breakdown: *true* (adj.)
- Translation: corresponding to fact; genuine
- Context: Universal everyday predicate of statements and things ("that's true"). [CORE]

**truth**
- Breakdown: *true* + *-th* (nominal)
- Translation: the quality of being true; that which is true
- Context: Central abstract noun across philosophy, law, ethics, everyday speech. [CORE]

**truly**
- Breakdown: *true* + *-ly* (adverb)
- Translation: genuinely; in truth
- Context: Everyday intensifier and affirmation ("truly sorry," "yours truly"). [CORE]

**truthful**
- Breakdown: *truth* + *-ful*
- Translation: habitually telling the truth
- Context: Moral evaluation of persons and statements. [CORE]

**truthfulness**
- Breakdown: *truth* + *-ful* + *-ness*
- Translation: the quality of being truthful
- Context: Ethical and characterological discourse. [CORE]

**truthfully**
- Breakdown: *truth* + *-ful* + *-ly*
- Translation: in a truthful manner
- Context: Everyday and legal ("answer truthfully"). [CORE]

**untrue**
- Breakdown: *un-* + *true*
- Translation: false; not faithful
- Context: Statements ("that's untrue") and fidelity ("untrue to her vows"). [CORE]

**untruth**
- Breakdown: *un-* + *truth*
- Translation: a falsehood; a lie
- Context: Formal/euphemistic term for a lie. [CORE]

**truth-value**
- Breakdown: *truth* + *value*
- Translation: the truth or falsity of a proposition
- Context: Logic, semantics, philosophy. [CORE]

**truth-teller**
- Breakdown: *truth* + *tell* + *-er*
- Translation: one who tells the truth
- Context: Moral/journalistic characterization. [CORE]

**truth serum**
- Breakdown: *truth* + *serum*
- Translation: a drug purported to compel truthful speech
- Context: Popular, interrogation/medical contexts. [CORE]

**truth be told**
- Breakdown: *truth* + *be* + *told* (fixed phrase)
- Translation: to be honest; in fact
- Context: Conversational discourse marker. [CORE]

**the truth, the whole truth, and nothing but the truth**
- Breakdown: fixed legal formula
- Translation: complete and exclusive truthfulness
- Context: Courtroom oath. [CORE]

**home truth(s)**
- Breakdown: *home* + *truth*
- Translation: an unwelcome but accurate fact about oneself
- Context: Interpersonal, everyday. [CORE]

**gospel truth**
- Breakdown: *gospel* + *truth*
- Translation: absolute truth
- Context: Emphatic everyday/religious idiom. [CORE]

**half-truth**
- Breakdown: *half* + *truth*
- Translation: a partially true (and misleading) statement
- Context: Ethical, rhetorical evaluation. [CORE]

**God's honest truth**
- Breakdown: fixed emphatic phrase
- Translation: the absolute truth
- Context: Colloquial emphatic assertion. [CORE]

**moment of truth**
- Breakdown: *moment* + *of* + *truth*
- Translation: a decisive testing point
- Context: Everyday idiom (orig. bullfighting). [CORE]

**truth-functional**
- Breakdown: *truth* + *function* + *-al*
- Translation: determined by truth-values of components
- Context: Formal logic. [MARGINAL]

**post-truth**
- Breakdown: *post-* + *truth*
- Translation: relating to denial of objective facts in public discourse
- Context: Political/media commentary (recent). [CORE]

**true-blue**
- Breakdown: *true* + *blue*
- Translation: genuinely loyal
- Context: Colloquial loyalty/character description. [CORE]

**true-born**
- Breakdown: *true* + *born*
- Translation: genuine by birth; legitimate
- Context: Literary/archaic. [MARGINAL]

**true-life / true story**
- Breakdown: *true* + *life* / *story*
- Translation: based on real events
- Context: Film, publishing marketing. [CORE]

**true north**
- Breakdown: *true* + *north*
- Translation: geographic (vs magnetic) north; one's guiding principle
- Context: Navigation; metaphorical moral usage. [CORE]

**true colors**
- Breakdown: *true* + *colors*
- Translation: one's real character
- Context: Idiom ("show one's true colors"). [CORE]

**come true**
- Breakdown: *come* + *true*
- Translation: be realized (of wishes/predictions)
- Context: Everyday ("a dream come true"). [CORE]

**ring true**
- Breakdown: *ring* + *true*
- Translation: seem genuine/credible
- Context: Everyday evaluation of statements. [CORE]

**true to form / true to life / true to type / true to one's word**
- Breakdown: *true* + *to* + N (fixed collocations)
- Translation: consistent with the expected nature / realistic / keeping a promise
- Context: Everyday and descriptive evaluation. [CORE]

**tried and true**
- Breakdown: *tried* + *and* + *true* (fixed phrase)
- Translation: proven reliable
- Context: Everyday endorsement. [CORE]

**trueness**
- Breakdown: *true* + *-ness*
- Translation: degree of conformity (esp. metrology); straightness
- Context: Technical (measurement, mechanical alignment). [MARGINAL]

**true up / out of true**
- Breakdown: *true* (verb) + particle
- Translation: to make/being straight or aligned
- Context: Carpentry, engineering ("the wheel is out of true"). [CORE]

*(Trust-family, via the same Germanic root:)*

**trust / trustworthy / trustworthiness / distrust / mistrust / trusty / trustee**
- Breakdown: *trust* + various affixes
- Translation: reliance / deserving reliance / its lack / a reliable thing / a fiduciary
- Context: Moral, evidential, legal/financial. [CORE]

*(Additional rarer forms exist: truism, betroth/troth, truant is unrelated.)*

**truism**
- Breakdown: *true* + *-ism*
- Translation: a self-evident or hackneyed truth
- Context: Rhetorical/everyday. [CORE]

**troth / betroth**
- Breakdown: archaic *trēowþ* "faith, pledge"
- Translation: a pledge (of fidelity, esp. marriage)
- Context: Archaic/ceremonial ("plight one's troth"). [MARGINAL]

### ROOT 2: **ver-** (Latin *vērus* "true")

This root is **moderately productive**.

**verify**
- Breakdown: *ver-* + *-ify*
- Translation: to confirm the truth of
- Context: Scientific, technical, administrative, everyday. [CORE]

**verification**
- Breakdown: *ver-* + *-ification*
- Translation: the act of confirming truth
- Context: Science, logic, security, administration. [CORE]

**verifiable**
- Breakdown: *ver-* + *-ifiable*
- Translation: able to be confirmed as true
- Context: Evidential, scientific, philosophical. [CORE]

**verity**
- Breakdown: *ver-* + *-ity*
- Translation: a truth; truth itself
- Context: Formal/literary ("eternal verities"). [MARGINAL]

**veracity**
- Breakdown: *ver-* + *-acity*
- Translation: truthfulness; accuracy
- Context: Formal, legal, journalistic. [CORE]

**veracious**
- Breakdown: *ver-* + *-acious*
- Translation: habitually truthful
- Context: Formal/literary (P1 #20). [MARGINAL]

**veritable**
- Breakdown: *ver-* + *-itable*
- Translation: genuine; (often) virtual, "real" intensifier
- Context: Everyday emphatic ("a veritable feast"). [CORE]

**veritably**
- Breakdown: *veritable* + *-ly*
- Translation: genuinely; truly
- Context: Formal/literary intensifier. [MARGINAL]

**verisimilitude**
- Breakdown: *ver-* + *simil-* + *-itude*
- Translation: the appearance of truth
- Context: Literary/critical theory (P1 #19). [MARGINAL]

**verisimilar**
- Breakdown: *ver-* + *simil-* + *-ar*
- Translation: having the appearance of truth
- Context: Rare, scholarly. [MARGINAL]

**veridical**
- Breakdown: *ver-* + *dic-* "say" + *-al*
- Translation: truthful; coinciding with reality
- Context: Philosophy, psychology (esp. of perception/hallucination). [MARGINAL]

**verdict**
- Breakdown: *ver-* + *dict-* "say" (*vērē dictum* "truly said")
- Translation: a jury's finding; a judgment
- Context: Legal; extended everyday ("the verdict is in"). [CORE]

**aver**
- Breakdown: *a-* + *ver-*
- Translation: to assert as true
- Context: Formal/legal declarative. [MARGINAL]

**very**
- Breakdown: from *vērus* via Old French *verai*
- Translation: (intensifier) extremely; (archaic) true, actual
- Context: Universal everyday intensifier; archaic adjectival "the very truth," "very God." [CORE]

**verily**
- Breakdown: *very* + *-ly*
- Translation: truly, in truth
- Context: Archaic/biblical ("verily I say unto you"). [MARGINAL]

**in vino veritas**
- Breakdown: Latin *in* + *vino* + *veritas* (fixed borrowing)
- Translation: "in wine, truth"
- Context: Erudite/proverbial. [MARGINAL]

*(Note: this root has roughly 15 attested common forms — a moderate set, not exceeding 30.)*

## STEP 3 — Peripheral Extensions (flagged, not interpreted)

The following expressions involve a shift in the root's contribution away from its core truth/correspondence-to-fact sense. Flagged only:

**From true / truth:**
- **truly, true-blue, true to one's word, true colors, faithful** — loyalty/fidelity rather than factual truth.
- **true north, true up, out of true, trueness** — straightness/alignment (mechanical, navigational).
- **come true, dream come true** — realization/actualization.
- **veritable / a veritable [feast]** — intensification rather than truth-claim.
- **troth / betroth / plight one's troth** — pledging/marriage.
- **moment of truth** — decisive test/crisis.
- **truth serum, post-truth** — domain-specific (pharmacological, political).
- **out of true** — physical deviation.

**From ver-:**
- **very, verily** — degree/intensity marking, not truth.
- **veritable** — intensifier.
- **verdict** — judgment/decision (legal outcome).
- **aver** — assertion as speech act.

**From correct / rect- / right:**
- **rectangle, rectilinear, erect, direct** — physical straightness/orientation, geometry.
- **rectify (chemistry/electronics)** — purification/current conversion.
- **right (noun): human rights, right of way, a right** — entitlement.
- **right (directional): turn right, right hand, right wing** — spatial/political orientation.
- **upright, erect** — physical verticality.
- **outright, downright, all right** — intensity/degree or general approval.
- **rectitude, righteous, righteousness, upright** — moral character rather than factual correctness.
- **serve someone right** — desert/consequence.
