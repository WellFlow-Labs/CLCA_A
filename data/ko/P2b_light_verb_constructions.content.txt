# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2b Compositional Constructions
# Language: Korean (ko)

## CONSTRUCTION TYPE 1: Verbal Collocations with Truth-Domain Nouns

### 1.1 진실 (jinsil) — truth

#### 1.1.1
**Base form:** 진실
**Example:** 진실을 말하다
**Transliteration:** jinsireul malhada
**English translation:** to tell the truth, to speak the truth
**Usage notes:** Most common and basic collocation for expressing truthful speech. Used across all registers from everyday conversation to formal testimony.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 1.1.2
**Base form:** 진실
**Example:** 진실을 밝히다
**Transliteration:** jinsireul balkhida
**English translation:** to reveal the truth, to uncover the truth
**Usage notes:** Common in investigative, journalistic, and legal contexts when discussing the process of discovering or revealing what is true. Implies active investigation or disclosure.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 1.1.3
**Base form:** 진실
**Example:** 진실을 숨기다
**Transliteration:** jinsireul sumgida
**English translation:** to hide the truth, to conceal the truth
**Usage notes:** Used when someone deliberately conceals true facts or circumstances. Common in contexts of deception or cover-ups.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 1.1.4
**Base form:** 진실
**Example:** 진실을 알다
**Transliteration:** jinsireul alda
**English translation:** to know the truth
**Usage notes:** Indicates possession of knowledge about what is true. Used when someone has discovered or been informed of true facts.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 1.1.5
**Base form:** 진실
**Example:** 진실을 찾다
**Transliteration:** jinsireul chatda
**English translation:** to seek the truth, to search for the truth
**Usage notes:** Emphasizes active search or investigation for truth. Common in philosophical, investigative, and personal discovery contexts.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 1.1.6
**Base form:** 진실
**Example:** 진실을 직시하다
**Transliteration:** jinsireul jiksihada
**English translation:** to face the truth, to confront the truth
**Usage notes:** Implies confronting difficult or uncomfortable truths. Used when someone must acknowledge reality despite reluctance.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 1.1.7
**Base form:** 진실
**Example:** 진실을 왜곡하다
**Transliteration:** jinsireul waegokada
**English translation:** to distort the truth, to twist the truth
**Usage notes:** Describes deliberate misrepresentation or distortion of facts. Common in contexts of propaganda or dishonest reporting.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 1.1.8
**Base form:** 진실
**Example:** 진실을 외면하다
**Transliteration:** jinsireul oemyeonhada
**English translation:** to turn away from the truth, to ignore the truth
**Usage notes:** Describes deliberate avoidance or denial of truth. Used when someone refuses to acknowledge reality.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## CONSTRUCTION TYPE 4: Comparative and Contrastive Constructions

### 4.1 Truth vs. Falsehood Contrasts

#### 4.1.1
**Base forms:** 진실, 거짓
**Example:** 진실과 거짓
**Transliteration:** jinsilgwa geojit
**English translation:** truth and falsehood, truth and lies
**Usage notes:** Common pairing used in philosophical, moral, and investigative discourse to discuss the distinction between true and false.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 4.1.2
**Base forms:** 진실, 거짓
**Example:** 진실인지 거짓인지
**Transliteration:** jinsiljinji geojitjinji
**English translation:** whether true or false, whether truth or lie
**Usage notes:** Used when questioning or determining the truth status of something. Common in investigative contexts.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 4.1.3
**Base forms:** 참, 거짓
**Example:** 참과 거짓
**Transliteration:** chamgwa geojit
**English translation:** truth and falsehood, true and false
**Usage notes:** Alternative to 진실과 거짓, using native Korean term for truth. Common in philosophical and everyday contexts.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### 4.2 Real vs. Fake Contrasts

#### 4.2.1
**Base forms:** 진짜, 가짜
**Example:** 진짜인지 가짜인지
**Transliteration:** jinjjainji gajjainji

**Usage notes:** Common colloquial expression used when questioning authenticity. Very frequent in everyday speech.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 4.2.2
**Base forms:** 진짜, 가짜
**Example:** 진짜와 가짜를 구별하다
**Transliteration:** jinjjawa gajjareul gubyeolhada
**English translation:** to distinguish real from fake, to tell genuine from counterfeit
**Usage notes:** Used when discussing the process of determining authenticity. Common in commercial and evaluative contexts.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### 4.3 Appearance vs. Reality Contrasts

#### 4.3.1
**Base forms:** 실제, (implied contrast)
**Example:** 겉으로는 그렇지만 실제로는

## NOTES ON CONSTRUCTION PATTERNS

### High-frequency construction types:
1. **Verbal collocations with truth-domain nouns** are extremely productive, with most truth-domain nouns combining with verbs like 말하다 (speak), 밝히다 (reveal), 숨기다 (hide), 알다 (know), 찾다 (seek).

2. **Adverbial expressions** using -로/-으로 are very common and productive with truth-domain terms.

3. **Contrastive constructions** pairing truth and falsehood terms are frequent and formulaic.

4. **Discourse markers** derived from truth-domain terms (사실은, 솔직히 말하면, 정확히 말하면) are extremely common in everyday speech.

### Limited or absent construction types:
1. **Benefactive constructions** (doing something for someone's benefit) are not commonly attested with truth-domain terms.

2. **Reciprocal constructions** are rare in this domain.

3. **Aspectual constructions** (progressive, perfective) do not show special patterns with truth-domain terms beyond general Korean aspectual marking.

### Register variation:
- Formal/legal contexts favor constructions with 진실, 사실, 진위
- Colloquial contexts favor constructions with 진짜, 가짜, 거짓말
- Philosophical/abstract contexts favor constructions with 진리, 본질

### Asymmetries noted:
- 진실 shows much higher productivity in verbal collocations than 거짓
- 사실 shows unique productivity as a discourse marker (사실은, 사실상)
- 진짜/가짜 show high productivity in adjectival modification but limited verbal collocations
- Some expected constructions are not attested (e.g., no common benefactive constructions like *"진실을 위해" in the sense of "for the sake of truth" as a general principle)
