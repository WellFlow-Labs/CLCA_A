# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P6 CHANGE, CAUSATION, AND STABILITY
# Language: Chinese (zh)

---

## PRELIMINARY NOTE ON CHINESE ASPECT AND CHANGE-OF-STATE

Chinese encodes change and causation through:
1. **Change-of-state verbs** (变, 成为, 变成)
2. **Resultative verb compounds** (V-R structure where R indicates result state)
3. **Causative constructions** (使, 让, 叫 + causee + result)
4. **Disposal construction** (把 + object + V-R)
5. **Aspect markers** (了 perfective, 着 durative, 过 experiential)
6. **Inchoative patterns** (起来 "begin to")

Truth-domain terms participate in these patterns with varying productivity and naturalness.

## 1. BECOMING TRUE/AUTHENTIC (X becomes [truth-related state])

### 1.1 Becoming True/Real (真, 真实)

#### Pattern: 变真 (biàn zhēn)
**Status**: [MARGINAL]
**Example**: *变真 (biàn zhēn) - "become true/real"
**Notes**: This bare pattern is grammatically possible but rarely attested in natural usage. The more common expression is the lexicalized idiom:

#### Pattern: 弄假成真 (nòng jiǎ chéng zhēn)
**Status**: [CORE]
**Transliteration**: nòng jiǎ chéng zhēn
**Translation**: "what starts as pretense becomes real; fake it till you make it"
**Structure**: 弄 (make/do) + 假 (false) + 成 (become) + 真 (true)
**Usage**: Fixed expression describing situations where something that begins as false or pretended eventually becomes genuine. Common in discussions of relationships or situations that evolve unexpectedly.
**Example**: 他们开始是假装情侣，后来弄假成真了。
- Transliteration: tāmen kāishǐ shì jiǎzhuāng qínglǚ, hòulái nòng jiǎ chéng zhēn le.
- Translation: "They started by pretending to be a couple, but later it became real."

#### Pattern: 变得真实 (biàn de zhēnshí)
**Status**: [CORE]
**Transliteration**: biàn de zhēnshí
**Translation**: "become authentic/realistic"
**Structure**: 变 (change) + 得 (resultative particle) + 真实 (authentic/real)
**Usage**: More natural than bare 变真, used to describe something becoming more authentic or realistic.
**Example**: 这个故事变得越来越真实。
- Transliteration: zhège gùshi biàn de yuè lái yuè zhēnshí.
- Translation: "This story is becoming more and more realistic."

#### Pattern: 成为事实 (chéngwéi shìshí)
**Status**: [CORE]
**Transliteration**: chéngwéi shìshí
**Translation**: "become fact; become reality"
**Structure**: 成为 (become) + 事实 (fact)
**Usage**: Common expression for something becoming factual or actual, especially predictions or plans.
**Example**: 他的预言成为了事实。
- Transliteration: tā de yùyán chéngwéi le shìshí.
- Translation: "His prediction became reality."

#### Pattern: 变成现实 (biànchéng xiànshí)
**Status**: [CORE]
**Transliteration**: biànchéng xiànshí
**Translation**: "become reality; be realized"
**Structure**: 变成 (change into) + 现实 (reality)
**Usage**: Very common expression for dreams, plans, or ideas becoming reality.
**Example**: 梦想终于变成了现实。
- Transliteration: mèngxiǎng zhōngyú biànchéng le xiànshí.
- Translation: "The dream finally became reality."

### 1.2 Becoming Correct (对, 正确)

#### Pattern: 变对 (biàn duì)
**Status**: [MARGINAL]
**Transliteration**: biàn duì
**Translation**: "become correct"
**Notes**: Grammatically possible but rarely used. Correction is typically expressed through resultative compounds:

#### Pattern: 改对 (gǎi duì)
**Status**: [MARGINAL]
**Transliteration**: gǎi duì
**Translation**: "change to correct; correct (it)"
**Structure**: 改 (change) + 对 (correct)
**Usage**: Colloquial expression for correcting something, but less common than 改正.
**Example**: 把错误改对。
- Transliteration: bǎ cuòwù gǎi duì.
- Translation: "Correct the mistakes."

#### Pattern: 改正 (gǎizhèng)
**Status**: [CORE]
**Transliteration**: gǎizhèng
**Translation**: "correct, amend, put right"
**Structure**: 改 (change) + 正 (correct) [resultative compound]
**Usage**: Standard expression for correcting errors or mistakes. The compound itself encodes the change from wrong to correct.
**Example**: 他改正了错误。
- Transliteration: tā gǎizhèng le cuòwù.
- Translation: "He corrected the mistakes."

#### Pattern: 纠正 (jiūzhèng)
**Status**: [CORE]
**Transliteration**: jiūzhèng
**Translation**: "correct, rectify, redress"
**Structure**: 纠 (rectify) + 正 (correct) [resultative compound]
**Usage**: Common expression for correcting errors or improper situations, emphasizing rectification.
**Example**: 老师纠正了学生的发音。
- Transliteration: lǎoshī jiūzhèng le xuésheng de fāyīn.
- Translation: "The teacher corrected the student's pronunciation."

### 1.3 Becoming Substantial/Concrete (实)

#### Pattern: 变实 (biàn shí)
**Status**: [MARGINAL]
**Transliteration**: biàn shí
**Translation**: "become solid/substantial"
**Notes**: Rare as bare pattern. Substantialization is typically expressed through resultative compounds:

#### Pattern: 落实 (luòshí)
**Status**: [CORE]
**Transliteration**: luòshí
**Translation**: "implement, carry out, put into effect; be implemented"
**Structure**: 落 (fall/settle) + 实 (solid/actual) [resultative compound]
**Usage**: Very common expression for implementing plans or making something concrete. Can be used both transitively (to implement) and intransitively (to be implemented).
**Example**: 政策已经落实了。
- Transliteration: zhèngcè yǐjīng luòshí le.
- Translation: "The policy has been implemented."

#### Pattern: 坐实 (zuòshí)
**Status**: [CORE]
**Transliteration**: zuòshí
**Translation**: "confirm, establish as fact; be confirmed"
**Structure**: 坐 (sit/establish) + 实 (solid/actual) [resultative compound]
**Usage**: Legal and investigative term for confirming or establishing something as factual.
**Example**: 证据坐实了他的罪行。
- Transliteration: zhèngjù zuòshí le tā de zuìxíng.
- Translation: "The evidence confirmed his crimes."

#### Pattern: 证实 (zhèngshí)
**Status**: [CORE]
**Transliteration**: zhèngshí
**Translation**: "verify, confirm, prove true; be verified"
**Structure**: 证 (prove/verify) + 实 (solid/actual) [resultative compound]
**Usage**: Common expression for verifying or confirming claims or facts.
**Example**: 消息得到了证实。
- Transliteration: xiāoxi dédào le zhèngshí.
- Translation: "The news was confirmed."

#### Pattern: 充实 (chōngshí)
**Status**: [CORE]
**Transliteration**: chōngshí
**Translation**: "become substantial/fulfilling; enrich, fill out"
**Structure**: 充 (fill) + 实 (solid/full) [resultative compound]
**Usage**: Can function as both verb (to enrich/fill out) and adjective (substantial/fulfilling). Describes becoming more substantial or full.
**Example**: 生活变得充实了。
- Transliteration: shēnghuó biàn de chōngshí le.
- Translation: "Life became more fulfilling."

### 1.4 Becoming Normal (正常)

### 1.5 Becoming Honest/Sincere (诚实, 真诚)

#### Pattern: 变诚实 (biàn chéngshí)
**Status**: [MARGINAL]
**Transliteration**: biàn chéngshí
**Translation**: "become honest"
**Notes**: Grammatically possible but rare. Character change is typically expressed differently:

#### Pattern: 变得诚实 (biàn de chéngshí)
**Status**: [CORE]
**Transliteration**: biàn de chéngshí
**Translation**: "become honest"
**Structure**: 变 (change) + 得 (resultative particle) + 诚实 (honest)
**Usage**: More natural than bare 变诚实, used to describe character development.
**Example**: 他经历了很多事后变得诚实了。
- Transliteration: tā jīnglì le hěn duō shì hòu biàn de chéngshí le.
- Translation: "After experiencing many things, he became honest."

## 2. BECOMING FALSE/INCORRECT (X becomes [non-truth / opposed state])

**Status**: [MARGINAL]
**Transliteration**: biàn jiǎ
**Translation**: "become false/fake"
**Notes**: Rare as bare pattern. Falsification is typically expressed through causative constructions:

#### Pattern: 弄假 (nòng jiǎ)
**Status**: [CORE]
**Transliteration**: nòng jiǎ
**Translation**: "make false; falsify; fake"
**Structure**: 弄 (make/do) + 假 (false)
**Usage**: Common expression for creating something false or fake, or for pretending.
**Example**: 他弄假账。
- Transliteration: tā nòng jiǎzhàng.
- Translation: "He falsified the accounts."

#### Pattern: 变得虚假 (biàn de xūjiǎ)
**Status**: [CORE]
**Transliteration**: biàn de xūjiǎ
**Translation**: "become false/phony"
**Structure**: 变 (change) + 得 (resultative particle) + 虚假 (false/phony)
**Usage**: Used to describe something becoming false or phony, especially relationships or attitudes.
**Example**: 他们的关系变得虚假了。
- Transliteration: tāmen de guānxi biàn de xūjiǎ le.
- Translation: "Their relationship became phony."

### 2.2 Becoming Wrong/Incorrect (错)

#### Pattern: 变错 (biàn cuò)
**Status**: [MARGINAL]
**Transliteration**: biàn cuò
**Translation**: "become wrong"
**Notes**: Rare. Making errors is typically expressed through verb phrases:

#### Pattern: 弄错 (nòng cuò)
**Status**: [CORE]
**Transliteration**: nòng cuò
**Translation**: "get wrong; make a mistake; mix up"
**Structure**: 弄 (make/do) + 错 (wrong) [resultative compound]
**Usage**: Very common expression for making mistakes or getting something wrong.
**Example**: 我弄错了时间。
- Transliteration: wǒ nòng cuò le shíjiān.
- Translation: "I got the time wrong."

#### Pattern: 搞错 (gǎo cuò)
**Status**: [CORE]
**Transliteration**: gǎo cuò
**Translation**: "get wrong; make a mistake; confuse"
**Structure**: 搞 (do/make) + 错 (wrong) [resultative compound]
**Usage**: Colloquial variant of 弄错, equally common.
**Example**: 你搞错了。
- Transliteration: nǐ gǎo cuò le.
- Translation: "You got it wrong."

#### Pattern: 出错 (chū cuò)
**Status**: [CORE]
**Transliteration**: chū cuò
**Translation**: "make an error; go wrong"
**Structure**: 出 (emerge/appear) + 错 (wrong) [resultative compound]
**Usage**: Common expression for errors occurring or appearing.
**Example**: 计算出错了。
- Transliteration: jìsuàn chū cuò le.
- Translation: "The calculation went wrong."

### 2.3 Becoming Abnormal (不正常)

#### Pattern: 变得不正常 (biàn de bù zhèngcháng)
**Status**: [CORE]
**Transliteration**: biàn de bù zhèngcháng

### 2.4 Becoming Empty/Insubstantial (虚)

#### Pattern: 变虚 (biàn xū)
**Status**: [MARGINAL]
**Transliteration**: biàn xū
**Translation**: "become empty/weak"
**Notes**: Rare as bare pattern. More commonly expressed with 得:

#### Pattern: 变得虚弱 (biàn de xūruò)

## 3. CAUSATION: Y CAUSES X TO BE [TRUTH-RELATED STATE]

### 3.1 Causing to be True/Real

#### Pattern: 使...成为事实 (shǐ... chéngwéi shìshí)
**Status**: [CORE]
**Transliteration**: shǐ... chéngwéi shìshí
**Translation**: "make... become fact; make... come true"
**Structure**: 使 (cause) + [object] + 成为 (become) + 事实 (fact)
**Usage**: Formal causative construction for making something become factual or actual.
**Example**: 他的努力使梦想成为了事实。
- Transliteration: tā de nǔlì shǐ mèngxiǎng chéngwéi le shìshí.
- Translation: "His efforts made the dream become reality."

#### Pattern: 让...变成现实 (ràng... biànchéng xiànshí)
**Status**: [CORE]
**Transliteration**: ràng... biànchéng xiànshí
**Translation**: "make... become reality; make... come true"
**Structure**: 让 (let/make) + [object] + 变成 (change into) + 现实 (reality)
**Usage**: Common causative construction, less formal than 使.
**Example**: 我们要让计划变成现实。
- Transliteration: wǒmen yào ràng jìhuà biànchéng xiànshí.
- Translation: "We need to make the plan become reality."

#### Pattern: 实现 (shíxiàn)
**Status**: [CORE]
**Transliteration**: shíxiàn
**Translation**: "realize, achieve, bring about; be realized"
**Structure**: 实 (solid/actual) + 现 (appear/manifest) [resultative compound]
**Usage**: Very common verb meaning to realize or achieve something, making it actual. Can be used both transitively (to realize) and intransitively (to be realized).
**Example**: 我们实现了目标。
- Transliteration: wǒmen shíxiàn le mùbiāo.
- Translation: "We achieved the goal."

## 6. ASPECTUAL PATTERNS WITH TRUTH-DOMAIN TERMS

### 6.1 Perfective Aspect (了 le)

#### Pattern: [truth-term] + 了
**Status**: [CORE]
**Usage**: The perfective marker 了 (le) combines freely with all change-of-state expressions involving truth-domain terms to indicate completed change.

**Examples**:
- 变真实了 (biàn zhēnshí le) - "became authentic"
- 改正了 (gǎizhèng le) - "corrected (completed)"
- 落实了 (luòshí le) - "was implemented (completed)"
- 证实了 (zhèngshí le) - "was confirmed (completed)"

### 6.2 Durative Aspect (着 zhe)

#### Pattern: [truth-term] + 着
**Status**: [MARGINAL]
**Notes**: The durative marker 着 (zhe) rarely combines with truth-domain state changes, as these typically denote completed transitions rather than ongoing states. However, it can appear with maintenance verbs:

**Example**:
- 保持着真实 (bǎochí zhe zhēnshí) - "maintaining authenticity (ongoing)"

### 6.3 Experiential Aspect (过 guo)

#### Pattern: [truth-term] + 过
**Status**: [CORE]
**Usage**: The experiential marker 过 (guo) combines with truth-domain change expressions to indicate past experience of the change.

**Examples**:
- 变过真实 (biàn guo zhēnshí) - "has been authentic (at some point)"
- 改正过 (gǎizhèng guo) - "has corrected (before)"
- 弄错过 (nòng cuò guo) - "has made mistakes (before)"

## 7. GRADUAL CHANGE PATTERNS

### 7.1 Gradual Becoming

#### Pattern: 越来越 + [truth-term]
**Status**: [CORE]
**Transliteration**: yuè lái yuè + [truth-term]
**Translation**: "more and more [truth-term]"
**Structure**: 越来越 (more and more) + adjective
**Usage**: Very common pattern for expressing gradual increase in a quality.

**Examples**:
- 越来越真实 (yuè lái yuè zhēnshí) - "more and more authentic/realistic"
- 越来越正确 (yuè lái yuè zhèngquè) - "more and more correct"
- 越来越诚实 (yuè lái yuè chéngshí) - "more and more honest"

#### Pattern: 渐渐 + 变 + [truth-term]
**Status**: [CORE]
**Transliteration**: jiànjiàn + biàn + [truth-term]
**Translation**: "gradually become [truth-term]"
**Structure**: 渐渐 (gradually) + 变 (change) + adjective
**Usage**: Common pattern for expressing gradual change.

**Examples**:
- 渐渐变真实 (jiànjiàn biàn zhēnshí) - "gradually become authentic"
- 渐渐变正常 (jiànjiàn biàn zhèngcháng) - "gradually become normal"

### 7.2 Sudden Change

#### Pattern: 突然 + 变 + [truth-term]
**Status**: [CORE]
**Transliteration**: tūrán + biàn + [truth-term]
**Translation**: "suddenly become [truth-term]"
**Structure**: 突然 (suddenly) + 变 (change) + adjective
**Usage**: Common pattern for expressing sudden change.

**Examples**:
- 突然变真实 (tūrán biàn zhēnshí) - "suddenly become real"
- 突然变得不正常 (tūrán biàn de bù zhèngcháng) - "suddenly become abnormal"

## 8. PATTERNS NOT FOUND OR RARE

### 8.1 Direct Causative with 使/让 + Bare Adjective
**Status**: [MARGINAL/RARE]
**Notes**: Direct causative constructions like *使...真实, *让...正确 are grammatically possible but rarely attested. Chinese strongly prefers:
- Resultative verb compounds (改正, 证实, 落实)
- Causative + 成为/变成 + noun (使...成为事实)
- Disposal construction + resultative compound (把...改正)

### 8.2 Inchoative 起来 with Most Truth-Domain Terms
**Status**: [MARGINAL]
**Notes**: The inchoative marker 起来 (qǐlái) "begin to" rarely combines productively with truth-domain adjectives. Most attestations involve perception verbs (看起来真实 "looks authentic") rather than true inchoatives.

### 8.3 Causative Morphology
**Status**: [NOT FOUND]
**Notes**: Chinese lacks productive causative morphology (unlike languages with causative affixes). All causation is expressed periphrastically or through resultative compounding.

### 8.4 Anticausative/Decausative Patterns
**Status**: [LIMITED]
**Notes**: Chinese does not have productive anticausative morphology. Some resultative compounds can be used both transitively and intransitively (证实 "verify" / "be verified", 落实 "implement" / "be implemented"), but this is lexically determined rather than a productive pattern.

## SUMMARY OF FINDINGS

### Most Productive Patterns:

1. **Resultative verb compounds**: Highly productive for encoding change to truth-related states (改正, 纠正, 落实, 证实, 坐实, 充实)

2. **成为/变成 + Noun**: Common for becoming factual or real (成为事实, 变成现实)

3. **变得 + Adjective**: Productive pattern for general state change (变得真实, 变得诚实, 变得不正常)

4. **把 + Object + Resultative Compound**: Preferred causative pattern (把错误改正, 把计划落实, 把账目弄假)

5. **Verb + 真相 collocations**: Strong collocational patterns for revealing/concealing truth (揭露真相, 隐瞒真相, 查明真相)

### Key Asymmetries:

1. **Resultative compounds strongly preferred over periphrastic causatives**: Chinese uses V-R compounds (改正, 证实) rather than 使/让 + bare adjective for most truth-domain causation.

2. **Different roots show different productivity**:
   - 正 (zhèng) is highly productive in resultative compounds (改正, 纠正, 修正)
   - 实 (shí) is highly productive in resultative compounds (落实, 证实, 坐实, 充实)
   - 真 (zhēn) appears more in fixed expressions (弄假成真) than productive patterns
   - 对/错 (duì/cuò) appear in colloquial resultative compounds (弄错, 搞错, 改对)

3. **Lexicalized idioms vs. productive patterns**: Some changes are expressed through fixed idioms (弄假成真) rather than productive syntactic patterns.

4. **Nominal vs. adjectival patterns**: Becoming factual/real often uses nominal patterns (成为事实, 变成现实) while other truth-domain changes use adjectival patterns (变得诚实).

### Register Variation:

- **Formal**: 使...落实, 证实, 坚持真理
- **Colloquial**: 弄错, 搞错, 弄假, 变得...
- **Literary/Fixed**: 弄假成真, 去伪存真
