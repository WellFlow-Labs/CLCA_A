## Step 1 — Central roots

### 1. **진 / 眞** — *jin*
- **Participates in:** 8 P1 items
- **P1 items involved:** 진실, 진정하다, 진짜, 진실하다, 진실성, and as the first element in related truth/authenticity vocabulary across the set
- **Core meaning contribution:** “true, genuine, real, authentic”

### 2. **실 / 實** — *sil*
- **Participates in:** 6 P1 items
- **P1 items involved:** 진실, 사실, 사실이다, 진실하다, 진실성, 실체
- **Core meaning contribution:** “real, actual, factual, substantial”

### 3. **정 / 正** — *jeong*
- **Participates in:** 6 P1 items
- **P1 items involved:** 정확하다, 정확성, 정당하다, 정품, and also in the Sino-Korean layer of correctness/propriety/validity vocabulary represented in the set
- **Core meaning contribution:** “correct, proper, right, legitimate”

## Step 2 — Root expansion

## Root 1: **진 / 眞** — *jin*

This root is highly productive. Below are the most common and well-attested expressions; additional forms exist.

1. **진실**
- **Transliteration:** jinsil
- **Breakdown:** 眞 “true” + 實 “real”
- **Translation:** truth; reality; genuineness
- **Context:** Common across everyday, journalistic, legal, and moral discourse. Often used when truth is concealed, disputed, or revealed.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

2. **진실하다**
- **Transliteration:** jinsilhada
- **Breakdown:** 眞實 + 하다
- **Translation:** be truthful; be sincere; be true
- **Context:** Used for people, speech, attitudes, and sometimes emotional expression. More literary or evaluative than casual spoken wording.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

3. **진실성**
- **Transliteration:** jinsilseong
- **Breakdown:** 眞實 “truthfulness” + 性 “quality”
- **Translation:** sincerity; truthfulness; authenticity
- **Context:** Common in criticism, politics, ethics, and public discourse. Often evaluates whether a person or expression feels honest and uncontrived.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

4. **진실되다**
- **Transliteration:** jinsildoeda
- **Breakdown:** 眞實 “truth” + 되다 “become/be”
- **Translation:** be truthful; be genuine
- **Context:** Less common than 진실하다 but attested in literary, moral, and descriptive prose. Used for character, speech, and attitude.
- **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

5. **진정하다**
- **Transliteration:** jinjeonghada
- **Breakdown:** 眞正하다
- **Translation:** be genuine; be authentic; be true
- **Context:** Formal written adjective, especially in phrases like 진정한 의미, 진정한 사랑. Common in essays, speeches, and elevated prose.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

6. **진정성**
- **Transliteration:** jinjeongseong
- **Breakdown:** 眞正 “genuine” + 性 “quality”
- **Translation:** authenticity; sincerity; genuineness
- **Context:** Very common in contemporary public discourse, criticism, branding, politics, and interpersonal evaluation. Often used to assess whether motives or expression are genuine.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

7. **진정**
- **Transliteration:** jinjeong
- **Breakdown:** 眞正
- **Translation:** true genuineness; authenticity
- **Context:** Mostly appears as a bound or formal lexical element in compounds and set phrases rather than as a free everyday noun. Seen in elevated writing.
- **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

8. **진가**
- **Transliteration:** jinga
- **Breakdown:** 眞 “true” + 價 “value”
- **Translation:** true worth; real value
- **Context:** Used when something’s real merit becomes visible, often after testing or over time. Common in journalism, criticism, and promotional writing.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

9. **진면목**
- **Transliteration:** jinmyeonmok
- **Breakdown:** 眞 “true” + 面目 “face/aspect”
- **Translation:** true character; true colors; real worth
- **Context:** Used for a person, institution, work, or phenomenon whose real nature is finally shown. Common in essays, reviews, and commentary.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

10. **진상**
- **Transliteration:** jinsang
- **Breakdown:** 眞 “true” + 相 “form/aspect”
- **Translation:** true state of affairs; actual truth
- **Context:** Common in journalism, investigation, and public discussion, especially in phrases like 사건의 진상. Refers to what really happened.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

11. **진상규명**
- **Transliteration:** jinsang gyumyeong
- **Breakdown:** 眞相 “true state” + 糾明 “investigate/clarify”
- **Translation:** clarification of the truth; fact-finding
- **Context:** Frequent in legal, political, and journalistic contexts involving accidents, scandals, or historical wrongs. Refers to uncovering what actually occurred.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

12. **진의**
- **Transliteration:** jinui
- **Breakdown:** 眞 “true” + 意 “meaning/intention”
- **Translation:** true intention; real meaning
- **Context:** Used when distinguishing literal wording from underlying intent. Common in interpretation, diplomacy, criticism, and formal discussion.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

13. **진의가 아니다**
- **Transliteration:** jinuiga anida
- **Breakdown:** 眞意 “true intention” + negative copula
- **Translation:** that is not my true intention
- **Context:** Fixed-style expression used in apologies, clarifications, and public statements. Often appears when speakers deny that an utterance reflects their real intent.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

14. **진심**
- **Transliteration:** jinsim
- **Breakdown:** 眞 “true” + 心 “heart”
- **Translation:** sincerity; true feelings; heartfelt intention
- **Context:** Very common in everyday speech and writing. Used for genuine emotion, apology, gratitude, affection, or intention.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

15. **진심으로**
- **Transliteration:** jinsimeuro
- **Breakdown:** 眞心 “true heart” + 으로
- **Translation:** sincerely; from the bottom of one’s heart
- **Context:** Common adverbial phrase in speech, letters, apologies, congratulations, and formal remarks. Marks emotional sincerity rather than factual truth.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

16. **진심이다**
- **Transliteration:** jinsimida
- **Breakdown:** 眞心 “true heart” + copula
- **Translation:** be sincere; mean it genuinely
- **Context:** Common in spoken Korean when assuring someone that one is serious or heartfelt. Often contrasts with joking or empty words.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

17. **진실로**
- **Transliteration:** jinsillo
- **Breakdown:** 眞實 “truth” + 로
- **Translation:** truly; indeed
- **Context:** Literary or formal adverb. Found in sermons, essays, older prose, and elevated speech.
- **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

18. **진정한**
- **Transliteration:** jinjeonghan
- **Breakdown:** 眞正하다 attributive form
- **Translation:** true; genuine; authentic
- **Context:** Extremely common as an attributive modifier in formal writing: 진정한 사랑, 진정한 의미, 진정한 민주주의. The base lexeme is formal and productive in this use.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

19. **진짜**
- **Transliteration:** jinjja
- **Breakdown:** lexicalized form
- **Translation:** real; genuine; really
- **Context:** Very common in spoken Korean for authenticity and intensity. Used for objects, situations, and as an adverb meaning “really.”
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

20. **진짜배기**
- **Transliteration:** jinjjabaegi
- **Breakdown:** 진짜 “real” + 배기 emphatic nominal element
- **Translation:** the real thing; genuine article
- **Context:** Colloquial expression for something unmistakably authentic or first-rate. Used for people, goods, and performances.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

21. **진품**
- **Transliteration:** jinpum
- **Breakdown:** 眞 “genuine” + 品 “item/product”
- **Translation:** genuine article; authentic piece
- **Context:** Common in art, antiques, luxury goods, and appraisal contexts. Contrasts with 가품, 위작, 모조품.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

22. **진본**
- **Transliteration:** jinbon
- **Breakdown:** 眞 “true” + 本 “original”
- **Translation:** original authentic copy; genuine original
- **Context:** Used for manuscripts, documents, artworks, and archival materials. Common in textual scholarship, museums, and authentication.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

23. **진본성**
- **Transliteration:** jinbonseong
- **Breakdown:** 眞本 “authentic original” + 性 “quality”
- **Translation:** authenticity as original; original authenticity
- **Context:** Used in art history, textual criticism, and heritage discourse. Refers to whether something is genuinely original rather than reproduced or forged.
- **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

24. **진위**
- **Transliteration:** jinwi
- **Breakdown:** 眞 “true” + 僞 “false”
- **Translation:** authenticity; truth or falsity
- **Context:** Common in journalism, law, administration, and online discourse when determining whether a claim, rumor, image, or document is genuine.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

25. **진위 여부**
- **Transliteration:** jinwi yeobu
- **Breakdown:** 眞僞 “truth/falsity” + 與否 “whether or not”
- **Translation:** whether something is genuine/true
- **Context:** Frequent in formal reporting and investigation. Used for claims, evidence, statements, and media content.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

26. **진위 확인**
- **Transliteration:** jinwi hwagin
- **Breakdown:** 眞僞 “authenticity” + 確認 “confirmation”
- **Translation:** verification of authenticity; fact-checking
- **Context:** Used in media, administration, and digital-information contexts. Refers to checking whether something is true or genuine.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

27. **진리**
- **Transliteration:** jinri
- **Breakdown:** 眞 “true” + 理 “principle”
- **Translation:** truth; ultimate truth; principle
- **Context:** Common in philosophy, religion, ethics, and education. Often refers to truth in a more universal or doctrinal sense than 진실.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

28. **진리성**
- **Transliteration:** jinriseong
- **Breakdown:** 眞理 “truth” + 性 “quality”
- **Translation:** truth-value; truthfulness; validity as truth
- **Context:** Used in philosophy, logic, and academic writing. More technical and less common in everyday speech.
- **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

29. **진리값**
- **Transliteration:** jinrigap
- **Breakdown:** 眞理 “truth” + 값 “value”
- **Translation:** truth value
- **Context:** Technical term in logic, linguistics, mathematics, and computer science. Refers to whether a proposition evaluates as true or false.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

30. **진언**
- **Transliteration:** jineon
- **Breakdown:** 眞 “true” + 言 “speech”
- **Translation:** mantra; true word
- **Context:** Religious term, especially in Buddhist contexts. The root is present, but the expression functions in a specialized doctrinal register.
- **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

## Root 2: **실 / 實** — *sil*

This root is highly productive. Below are the most common and well-attested expressions; additional forms exist.

1. **사실**
- **Transliteration:** sasil
- **Breakdown:** 事 “matter” + 實 “reality”
- **Translation:** fact; actuality; truth
- **Context:** One of the most common words for factual truth. Used in everyday speech, reporting, argumentation, and formal writing.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

2. **사실상**
- **Transliteration:** sasilsang
- **Breakdown:** 事實 “fact” + 上 “on/in effect”
- **Translation:** in fact; למעשה; virtually; effectively
- **Context:** Very common in journalism, academic prose, and formal speech. Used when something is not formally stated but is true in practical effect.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

3. **사실적**
- **Transliteration:** sasiljeok
- **Breakdown:** 事實 “fact” + 的 “-al”
- **Translation:** factual; realistic
- **Context:** Used in reporting, criticism, and legal or academic writing. Can refer either to factual basis or to realism in depiction.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

4. **사실성**
- **Transliteration:** sasilseong
- **Breakdown:** 事實 “fact” + 性 “quality”
- **Translation:** factuality; realism; plausibility
- **Context:** Used in literary criticism, film, journalism, and argumentation. It can refer to factual grounding or lifelike realism.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

5. **사실주의**
- **Transliteration:** sasiljuui
- **Breakdown:** 事實 “fact/reality” + 主義 “-ism”
- **Translation:** realism
- **Context:** Common in art, literature, and intellectual history. Refers to realism as an aesthetic or philosophical orientation.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

6. **사실주의적**
- **Transliteration:** sasiljuuijeok
- **Breakdown:** 事實主義 “realism” + 的
- **Translation:** realist; realistic
- **Context:** Used in criticism of literature, film, painting, and sometimes political analysis. Describes styles or approaches associated with realism.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

7. **사실혼**
- **Transliteration:** sasilhon
- **Breakdown:** 事實 “de facto” + 婚 “marriage”
- **Translation:** de facto marriage; common-law marriage
- **Context:** Legal and social term for a marriage-like union without formal registration. The root contributes factual/de facto status rather than truth evaluation.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

8. **사실무근**
- **Transliteration:** sasilmugeun
- **Breakdown:** 事實 “fact” + 無根 “without basis”
- **Translation:** groundless; unfounded; baseless
- **Context:** Very common in denials of rumors, allegations, and reports. Frequently used in media statements and official responses.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

9. **사실 여부**
- **Transliteration:** sasil yeobu
- **Breakdown:** 事實 “fact” + 與否 “whether or not”
- **Translation:** whether something is true/factual
- **Context:** Used in investigations, journalism, and legal contexts. Refers to determining whether a claim corresponds to fact.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

10. **사실 확인**
- **Transliteration:** sasil hwagin
- **Breakdown:** 事實 “fact” + 確認 “confirmation”
- **Translation:** fact-checking; confirmation of facts
- **Context:** Common in media, administration, and workplace communication. Refers to verifying whether reported information is correct.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

11. **사실 관계**
- **Transliteration:** sasil gwangye
- **Breakdown:** 事實 “fact” + 關係 “relations”
- **Translation:** factual relations; facts of the case
- **Context:** Very common in legal, journalistic, and administrative writing. Refers to the concrete factual background of a matter.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

12. **사실 규명**
- **Transliteration:** sasil gyumyeong
- **Breakdown:** 事實 “fact” + 糾明 “clarification”
- **Translation:** clarification of facts
- **Context:** Used in investigations, hearings, and public controversies. Focuses on establishing what actually happened.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

13. **사실 보도**
- **Transliteration:** sasil bodo
- **Breakdown:** 事實 “fact” + 報道 “reporting”
- **Translation:** factual reporting
- **Context:** Used in journalism and media ethics. Contrasts with distortion, rumor, or speculative reporting.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

14. **사실 증명**
- **Transliteration:** sasil jeungmyeong
- **Breakdown:** 事實 “fact” + 證明 “proof”
- **Translation:** proof of facts
- **Context:** Used in legal and administrative settings where factual matters must be documented or demonstrated.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

15. **실체**
- **Transliteration:** silche
- **Breakdown:** 實 “real” + 體 “body/entity”
- **Translation:** substance; actual entity; true nature
- **Context:** Used in philosophy, law, journalism, and abstract discussion. Often contrasts with appearance, rumor, or formal surface.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

16. **실체적**
- **Transliteration:** silchejeok
- **Breakdown:** 實體 “substance/entity” + 的
- **Translation:** substantive; substantial
- **Context:** Common in law and philosophy. In legal usage it contrasts with procedural/formal matters, as in 실체적 진실.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

17. **실체법**
- **Transliteration:** silchebeop
- **Breakdown:** 實體 “substantive” + 法 “law”
- **Translation:** substantive law
- **Context:** Technical legal term contrasting with procedural law. Refers to law governing rights and duties themselves.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

18. **실질**
- **Transliteration:** siljil
- **Breakdown:** 實 “real” + 質 “substance/quality”
- **Translation:** substance; actual substance; practical reality
- **Context:** Common in policy, economics, law, and public discourse. Used when distinguishing real substance from nominal or formal appearance.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

19. **실질적**
- **Transliteration:** siljiljeok
- **Breakdown:** 實質 “substance” + 的
- **Translation:** substantial; substantive; practical
- **Context:** Very common in formal writing and policy language. Refers to something real in effect, not merely formal or symbolic.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

20. **실질적 진실**
- **Transliteration:** siljiljeok jinsil
- **Breakdown:** 實質的 “substantive” + 眞實 “truth”
- **Translation:** substantive truth
- **Context:** Legal term referring to the actual truth of a case rather than merely formal proof. Common in criminal procedure and legal theory.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

21. **실상**
- **Transliteration:** silsang
- **Breakdown:** 實 “real” + 狀 “state”
- **Translation:** actual state; reality of the matter
- **Context:** Used in journalism, essays, and public commentary to refer to how things really are. Often contrasts with image or assumption.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

22. **실정**
- **Transliteration:** siljeong
- **Breakdown:** 實 “actual” + 情 “condition/circumstances”
- **Translation:** actual condition; real circumstances
- **Context:** Common in policy, administration, and education. Refers to the real local or present situation on the ground.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

23. **실증**
- **Transliteration:** siljeung
- **Breakdown:** 實 “actual” + 證 “evidence/proof”
- **Translation:** empirical proof; positive evidence
- **Context:** Used in science, philosophy, and academic writing. Refers to evidence grounded in observation or demonstrable proof.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

24. **실증적**
- **Transliteration:** siljeungjeok
- **Breakdown:** 實證 “empirical proof” + 的
- **Translation:** empirical; positivistic
- **Context:** Common in academic and scientific discourse. Describes methods or arguments based on observable evidence.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

25. **실증주의**
- **Transliteration:** siljeungjuui
- **Breakdown:** 實證 “empirical proof” + 主義 “-ism”
- **Translation:** positivism
- **Context:** Philosophical and methodological term in social science, philosophy, and intellectual history.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

26. **실험**
- **Transliteration:** silheom
- **Breakdown:** 實 “actual” + 驗 “test/examine”
- **Translation:** experiment
- **Context:** Very common scientific and educational term. The root contributes the sense of practical/actual testing rather than truth directly.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

27. **실험적**
- **Transliteration:** silheomjeok
- **Breakdown:** 實驗 “experiment” + 的
- **Translation:** experimental
- **Context:** Used in science, art, music, film, and design. Can refer to both laboratory experimentation and avant-garde artistic practice.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

28. **실용**
- **Transliteration:** silyong
- **Breakdown:** 實 “practical/real” + 用 “use”
- **Translation:** practical use; utility
- **Context:** Common in education, design, commerce, and policy. The root contributes practical actuality rather than factual truth.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

29. **실용적**
- **Transliteration:** silyongjeok
- **Breakdown:** 實用 “practical use” + 的
- **Translation:** practical; utilitarian
- **Context:** Used widely for tools, plans, language learning, design, and everyday decision-making.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

30. **실명**
- **Transliteration:** silmyeong
- **Breakdown:** 實 “real” + 名 “name”
- **Translation:** real name
- **Context:** Common in online identity, law, media, and administration. Contrasts with aliases, pen names, or anonymous use.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Root 3: **정 / 正** — *jeong*

This root is highly productive. Below are the most common and well-attested expressions; additional forms exist.

1. **정확하다**
- **Transliteration:** jeonghwakhada
- **Breakdown:** 正確하다 “correct + exact”
- **Translation:** be accurate; be exact; be precise
- **Context:** Common for statements, measurements, memory, timing, and representation. Central term for accuracy.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

2. **정확성**
- **Transliteration:** jeonghwakseong
- **Breakdown:** 正確 “accuracy” + 性 “quality”
- **Translation:** accuracy; precision
- **Context:** Used in technical, academic, media, and professional evaluation. Refers to degree of exact correctness.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

3. **정확도**
- **Transliteration:** jeonghwakdo
- **Breakdown:** 正確 “accurate” + 度 “degree”
- **Translation:** accuracy rate; degree of accuracy
- **Context:** Common in statistics, machine learning, testing, medicine, and engineering. Quantifies how accurate a system or result is.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

4. **정답**
- **Transliteration:** jeongdap
- **Breakdown:** 正 “correct” + 答 “answer”
- **Translation:** correct answer
- **Context:** Very common in education, quizzes, and testing. Also used metaphorically for the best or right solution.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

5. **정오**
- **Transliteration:** jeongo
- **Breakdown:** 正 “correct” + 誤 “error”
- **Translation:** correctness and error; right and wrong
- **Context:** Used in editing, grading, logic, and formal evaluation. Refers to whether something is correct or mistaken.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

6. **정정**
- **Transliteration:** jeongjeong
- **Breakdown:** 正 “correct” + 正 “correct”
- **Translation:** correction; rectification
- **Context:** Common in publishing, administration, and public statements. Used when errors in text, records, or claims are corrected.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

7. **정정하다**
- **Transliteration:** jeongjeonghada
- **Breakdown:** 正正하다
- **Translation:** correct; amend; rectify
- **Context:** Used for correcting documents, statements, records, and misunderstandings. Common in formal and administrative language.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

8. **정정 보도**
- **Transliteration:** jeongjeong bodo
- **Breakdown:** 訂正/正正 “correction” + 報道 “report”
- **Translation:** correction report; corrected news report
- **Context:** Common in journalism and media law. Refers to a published correction of inaccurate reporting.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

9. **정론**
- **Transliteration:** jeongnon
- **Breakdown:** 正 “correct/right” + 論 “argument/discourse”
- **Translation:** sound argument; orthodox view
- **Context:** Used in editorials, political commentary, and intellectual discourse. Often refers to a view presented as proper or sound.
- **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

10. **정론직필**
- **Transliteration:** jeongnonjikpil
- **Breakdown:** 正論 “sound discourse” + 直筆 “straight writing”
- **Translation:** upright writing of just opinions
- **Context:** Journalistic and literary expression praising principled, direct writing. Formal and somewhat rhetorical.
- **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

11. **정당하다**
- **Transliteration:** jeongdanghada
- **Breakdown:** 正當하다
- **Translation:** be just; be legitimate; be justified
- **Context:** Common in law, politics, ethics, and public argument. Evaluates legitimacy rather than factual truth.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

12. **정당성**
- **Transliteration:** jeongdangseong
- **Breakdown:** 正當 “legitimate” + 性 “quality”
- **Translation:** legitimacy; justifiability
- **Context:** Very common in political theory, law, ethics, and institutional critique. Refers to whether authority or action is justified.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

13. **정당방위**
- **Transliteration:** jeongdangbangwi
- **Breakdown:** 正當 “justified” + 防衛 “defense”
- **Translation:** self-defense; justified defense
- **Context:** Legal term for justified defensive action. Common in criminal law and news reporting.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

14. **정당행위**
- **Transliteration:** jeongdanghaengwi
- **Breakdown:** 正當 “justified” + 行爲 “act”
- **Translation:** justifiable act
- **Context:** Legal term for acts exempted from wrongfulness under certain conditions. Technical and formal.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

15. **정품**
- **Transliteration:** jeongpum
- **Breakdown:** 正 “genuine/proper” + 品 “product”
- **Translation:** genuine product; authentic item
- **Context:** Very common in commerce and consumer protection. Contrasts with counterfeit or imitation goods.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

16. **정품 인증**
- **Transliteration:** jeongpum injeung
- **Breakdown:** 正品 “genuine product” + 認證 “certification”
- **Translation:** authenticity certification
- **Context:** Used in retail, luxury goods, electronics, and online marketplaces. Refers to proving that an item is genuine.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

17. **정품 여부**
- **Transliteration:** jeongpum yeobu
- **Breakdown:** 正品 “genuine product” + 與否 “whether or not”
- **Translation:** whether an item is genuine
