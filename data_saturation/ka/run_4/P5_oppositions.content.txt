# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P5 Oppositions
# Language: Georgian (ka)

---

**Methodological note:** The following oppositions are extracted exclusively from patterns, contrasts, and explicit statements documented in P1–P4b. No opposition is inferred beyond what the data supports. Where the data describes a contrast as partial, asymmetric, or register-conditioned, this is preserved. Where no clear opposition exists, this is stated explicitly.

---

## Opposition 1

1. **Term A:** სიმართლე / simartle
2. **Term B:** სიცრუე / sicrue
3. **Transliteration:** simartle / sicrue
4. **English translation:** truth / lie, falsehood
5. **Description of contrast:** P1 explicitly states that sicrue "contrasts with simartle" and is used in moral condemnation of dishonest speech. The contrast is between a true statement and a deliberate untruth, and is the primary opposition in everyday moral discourse about honesty. The proverbial construction "simartle naxevari sicruea" (a half-truth is a lie) directly pairs the two terms.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 4

1. **Term A:** ნამდვილი / namdvili
2. **Term B:** ყალბი / q̇albi
3. **Transliteration:** namdvili / q̇albi
4. **English translation:** genuine, real, authentic / false, fake, counterfeit, forged
5. **Description of contrast:** The contrast is documented repeatedly and explicitly across P1, P1b, and P2b. P1b lists namdvili oq̇ro (real gold) as directly contrasting with q̇albi oq̇ro (fake gold), and namdvili dokumenti as contrasting with q̇albi dokumenti. The detection constructions namdvili gamodga / q̇albi gamodga and namdvili q̇opila / q̇albi q̇opila are parallel and contrastive. P3 Group 5 notes that the contrast with fake or counterfeit alternatives is "often implicit or explicit" in namdvili usage.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

The contrast is between a sincere communicative act and a deceptive one, expressed through structurally distinct constructions.
6. **Contrast type:** constructional

## Opposition 22

1. **Term A:** მართალია, მაგრამ... / martalia, magram...
2. **Term B:** მოჩვენებითი / močvenebiti (in analytical contexts)
3. **Transliteration:** martalia, magram... / močvenebiti
4. **English translation:** "true, but... / admittedly, but..." / "apparent, seeming, ostensible"
5. **Description of contrast:** P2b and P4b document martalia, magram... as a concessive construction acknowledging a point as correct before introducing a counterargument. P1 and P3 document močvenebiti as the adjective used to characterize something as only apparently the case. Both constructions operate in argumentative and analytical discourse to qualify the status of a claim, but through different structural means: martalia, magram... concedes truth while redirecting, while močvenebiti denies full truth by attributing only apparent status.
6. **Contrast type:** constructional
7. **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

## Opposition 28

3. **Transliteration:** č̣ešmariti / cru

## Opposition 30
