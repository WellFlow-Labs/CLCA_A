## 2. Substitution Patterns (Narrative)

### 2.1 REVELATION substitutes for BECOME (positive-truth direction)

In Chinese, the inchoative meaning "truth comes to be known / becomes apparent" is not primarily expressed through a general BECOME construction (变得 + ADJ). Instead, it is expressed through lexicalized inchoative expressions (真相大白, 坐实) and through verb-internal causative-resultative verbs (揭露, 揭示). These forms encode the transition from hidden to revealed as a single lexical or fixed-expression unit. The periphrastic BECOME frame (变得 + ADJ) is reported as grammatically available but marginal in this domain. The practical result is that REVELATION — the event of truth becoming known — systematically substitutes for a compositional BECOME construction in the positive-truth direction. This substitution is not available in the negative direction (there is no parallel lexicalized expression for "falsehood coming to light as falsehood").

### 2.2 Verb-internal causatives substitute for periphrastic CAUSATION

The periphrastic causative frames (让/使 + NP + predicate) are available and attested, but the primary expression of causation in this domain is through verb-internal causative verbs: 揭露, 揭示, 戳穿, 坐实. These verbs encode both the causer-agent and the caused-result within a single lexical item, taking a truth-domain noun as direct object. The periphrastic frame is used when the caused result is itself a fixed expression (使真相大白), effectively combining the periphrastic causative with a lexicalized inchoative complement. There is no case in P4a where a bare truth-domain adjective appears as the complement of a periphrastic causative.

### 2.3 DISCONFIRMATION is substituted by NEGATION and verb-object collocations

No dedicated DISCONFIRMATION construction is attested. The function of asserting that a claim is false or that a prior assertion is wrong is distributed across two patterns: (a) the NEGATION construction (不 + ADJ, or 是 + 假的/错误的), which predicates falsity or incorrectness of a proposition; and (b) verb-object collocations such as 戳穿谎言 ("to puncture/expose a lie") and 歪曲事实 ("to distort the facts"), which encode the act of disconfirming or challenging a claim. These two patterns together cover the functional space of DISCONFIRMATION without a single dedicated construction.

### 2.4 VERIFICATION lacks a dedicated construction

The function of verifying or confirming a claim is expressed through the verb-object collocation network (核实, 查实, 查明 + truth-domain noun) and through the adverbial frame NOUN + 上 + 是 (实质上是, 本质上是), which asserts what something fundamentally amounts to. Neither of these constitutes a dedicated VERIFICATION construction; both are general-purpose patterns applied to verification contexts. This is a lexical gap rather than a structural one: the constructions exist, but none is specialized for VERIFICATION.

### 2.5 Asymmetry between positive and negative domains

Two asymmetries are reported in P4a and P4b:

**Agentive nominalization**: The suffix 者 productively derives agentive nouns for negative-domain agents (欺骗者, 伪造者, 伪装者, 伪善者). No parallel single-word agentive nominalization exists for positive-domain agents (truth-tellers). The concept is expressed periphrastically as 说真话的人 ("a person who tells the truth"). This is described in P4a as a structural feature of the domain.

**Abstract nominalization frequency**: The 性 suffix attaches to both positive-domain (真实性, 准确性, 可靠性) and negative-domain (欺骗性, 虚假性) bases, but positive-domain nominalizations are higher-frequency and less register-restricted. Negative-domain 性 nominalizations (虚假性) and some positive-domain ones (诚实性, 真诚性) are lower-frequency and restricted to formal or analytical registers.

### 2.6 Negation is uniformly analytic

Negation in this domain is fully analytic. The morpheme 不 prefixes to truth-domain adjectives without exception (不真实, 不正确, 不准确, 不可靠, 不诚实). No suppletive negative forms exist. The copular negative frame (是 + 假的/虚假的/错误的) is available for nominal or emphatic predications and is slightly more emphatic than bare 不 negation, but it is not a distinct morphological pattern — it uses the same copular frame as positive attribution with a negative-polarity complement.

## 3. Missingness Summary

### 3.1 Structural Gaps

**None identified.** All CLCA operator categories have at least one attested construction or a documented substitution pattern. No operator is entirely without a grammatical means of expression.

### 3.2 Lexical Gaps

**VERIFICATION**: No single dedicated lexical item or construction specialized for the verification function. The function is covered by the general verb-object collocation network (核实, 查实, 查明) and the adverbial frame NOUN + 上 + 是. These are general-purpose patterns, not VERIFICATION-specific constructions.

**DISCONFIRMATION**: No single dedicated construction. The function is distributed across NEGATION constructions and verb-object collocations. **SUBSTITUTED BY NEGATION + verb-object collocations.**

**Agentive nominalization for truth-tellers**: No single-word form exists. **NO COMMON EXPRESSION — circumlocution required** (说真话的人).

### 3.3 Conceptual Gaps

**None identified from P4a and P4b data.** All operators appear to be conceptually accessible in Chinese; the gaps identified are lexical or distributional, not conceptual.

### 3.4 Asymmetric Gaps

**Agentive nominalization (CAUSATION (character) / NOMINALIZATION)**: The 者 suffix is available for negative-domain agents (欺骗者, 伪造者) but not for positive-domain agents (truth-tellers). The positive-domain form requires periphrasis (说真话的人). This is an asymmetric lexical gap: the negative side is lexically filled; the positive side is not.

**Abstract nominalization frequency (NOMINALIZATION)**: Lower-frequency 性 nominalizations (诚实性, 真诚性, 虚假性) are marginal and register-restricted. This is an asymmetric frequency gap rather than a structural one: the forms are grammatically well-formed but not in common use.

**BECOME / REVELATION directionality**: Lexicalized inchoative expressions (真相大白, 弄假成真, 坐实) are available for the positive-truth direction (truth coming to light, fake becoming real). No parallel lexicalized inchoative expression is attested for the negative direction (truth being obscured or lost). This is an asymmetric lexical gap in the BECOME domain.
