# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P5 OPPOSITIONS
# Language: Chinese (zh)

---

## Preliminary Note on Method

Oppositions below are extracted exclusively from patterns documented in P1, P1b, P2a, P2b, P3, P4a, and P4b. Each opposition is supported by explicit textual evidence from the CONTEXT. Where the CONTEXT notes an opposition explicitly, this is indicated. Where an opposition is reconstructed from co-occurrence patterns, collocational data, or constructional contrasts documented across steps, this is also noted. No opposition is inferred from general linguistic knowledge not grounded in the CONTEXT data.

Asymmetries between terms are preserved and noted where the data support them. No attempt is made to force symmetry.

## Opposition 1

**Term A:** 真 (zhēn)
**Term B:** 假 (jiǎ)
**Transliteration:** zhēn / jiǎ
**English Translation:** true, genuine / false, fake
**Description of Contrast:** The most basic lexical opposition in the domain, explicitly documented in P1 as a direct contrast (P1 #1 and #2: "Directly contrasts with 真"). The pair names the positive and negative poles of the authenticity/truth axis and generates the binary question 真假 (P1b Root 3a #4) and the construction 是真是假 (P2b Construction 2).
**Contrast Type:** lexical
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 2

**Term A:** 真实 (zhēnshí)
**Term B:** 虚假 (xūjiǎ)
**Transliteration:** zhēnshí / xūjiǎ
**English Translation:** truthful, authentic, real / false, fictitious, fraudulent
**Description of Contrast:** Documented in P3 Group 1 as near-antonyms in journalistic and legal usage. 真实 evaluates a representation as faithfully reflecting reality; 虚假 evaluates a claim or representation as without factual basis, typically with a stronger negative connotation implying deliberate deception.
**Contrast Type:** lexical
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 16

**Term A:** 真 (zhēn) — predicative: 这是真的
**Term B:** 假 (jiǎ) — predicative: 这是假的
**Transliteration:** zhè shì zhēn de / zhè shì jiǎ de
**English Translation:** "This is true / genuine." / "This is fake / false."
**Description of Contrast:** Documented in P2b (Constructions 1 and 8) and in P4a Construction 1 as the two poles of the basic copular attribution pattern. The constructions are formally parallel (Subject + 是 + adjective + 的) and semantically opposed.

**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 24

## Opposition 25

**Term A:** 弄假成真 (nòng jiǎ chéng zhēn)
**Term B:** 以假乱真 (yǐ jiǎ luàn zhēn)
**Transliteration:** nòng jiǎ chéng zhēn / yǐ jiǎ luàn zhēn
**English Translation:** what started as pretense becomes real / using fakes to confuse the genuine
**Description of Contrast:** Both are documented in P1b and P2b. They share the 假/真 axis but describe different dynamics: 弄假成真 describes an unintended or emergent transition from false to genuine; 以假乱真 describes a deliberate act of using the false to undermine the genuine. The contrast is between an inchoative process and a causative act of deception.

**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]
