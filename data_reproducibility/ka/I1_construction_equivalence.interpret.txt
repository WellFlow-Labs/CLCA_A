## 2. Substitution Patterns (Narrative)

### REVELATION → BECOME Substitution
Georgian lacks a dedicated grammatical construction for revelation (truth coming to light, being discovered). The concept is expressed through:
- **BECOME constructions**: ეს მართალი გახდა (es mart'ali gakhda) "this became true/turned out to be true" - the inchoative verb გახდომა serves double duty for both change of state and realization/discovery
- **Lexical verbs**: გამოვლენა (gamovlena) "revealing," გამოჩენა (gamoch'ena) "appearing," გამოვლინდა (gamovlinda) "was revealed" - these are specific lexical items, not productive constructions with truth-adjectives

The P4a notes explicitly state: "Some contexts prefer specific verbs (e.g., გამოვლინდა gamovlinda 'was revealed' for truth emergence)." This indicates that revelation is not a grammaticalized operator but rather a lexical domain.

### CAUSATION → VERIFICATION Substitution
Georgian shows a systematic avoidance of morphological causatives for truth-domain adjectives. The P4a notes state: "Direct causatives of truth-adjectives are rare" and "Georgian uses specific verbs like დამტკიცება (damt'k'itseba) 'proving,' გამოვლენა (gamovlena) 'revealing,' or ჩვენება (ch'veneba) 'showing' to express causation in the truth domain."

The P4b ratings confirm this: გაამართლა (gamart'la) "justified" is marked [MARGINAL] with the note "Causative morphology with truth-adjectives is restricted."

Instead, Georgian uses:
- **VERIFICATION verbs** as the functional equivalent of factual causation: დაამტკიცა (daamt'k'itsa) "proved," დადასტურა (dadast'ura) "confirmed"
- These verbs take truth nouns (not adjectives) as direct objects
- The construction is: VERB + სიმართლე (simart'le) "truth" or VERB + ინფორმაცია (inpormatsia) "information"

### DISCONFIRMATION Gap
Georgian lacks a productive construction for disconfirmation parallel to verification. While verification has multiple lexical verbs (დამტკიცება, გადამოწმება, დადასტურება), disconfirmation requires:
- Specific lexical verbs: უარყოფა (uarq'opa) "refutation," გაბათილება (gabat'ileba) "invalidation"
- Circumlocution: negation + verification (არ დაადასტურა ar dadast'ura "did not confirm")
- No parallel construction exists where disconfirmation verbs take truth-adjectives or truth nouns in a productive pattern

### Analytic vs. Morphological Negation
Georgian negation is **strictly analytic**:
- The particle არ (ar) is obligatory and precedes the copula
- No morphological negation exists on adjectives themselves
- P4a states: "The negative particle არ (ar) is obligatory and precedes the copula"
- Alternative negation with არა (ara) is for constituent negation, not predicate negation

### Verification and Disconfirmation Asymmetry
These do **not** share the same construction:
- **Verification** has a productive class of lexical verbs that pattern together
- **Disconfirmation** lacks such a class and requires different strategies
- This represents a structural asymmetry in the language

### Agent Nouns vs. Causation
The agentive nominalization pattern (მა-...-არა) creates habitual agent nouns but does not function as causation:
- მატყუარა (mat'q'uara) "liar" denotes a person characterized by lying
- This is not a causative construction (making someone/something true)
- It serves the function of CAUSATION (character) in the sense of creating a noun for someone who characteristically performs truth-related actions

## 3. Missingness Summary

### Structural Gaps (Missing Constructions)

1. **REVELATION**: No grammatical construction for truth coming to light or being discovered
   - The language uses BECOME constructions or lexical verbs instead
   - No productive pattern exists with truth-adjectives for revelation

2. **DISCONFIRMATION**: No productive construction parallel to verification
   - Only scattered lexical verbs available
   - No construction taking truth-adjectives or truth nouns in a systematic pattern

3. **CAUSATION (factual)**: No morphological causative construction with truth-adjectives
   - Direct causatives are marginal/restricted
   - The language systematically substitutes verification verbs

### Lexical Gaps
None identified. The language has rich lexical resources for truth-domain concepts (სიმართლე "truth," ტყუილი "lie," ნამდვილი "genuine," ყალბი "fake," etc.).

### Conceptual Gaps
None identified. All truth-domain concepts can be expressed, though some require circumlocution or lexical verbs rather than grammatical constructions.

### Asymmetric Gaps

1. **Verification vs. Disconfirmation**: 
   - Verification has multiple productive lexical verbs forming a coherent class
   - Disconfirmation lacks such a class
   - This is a structural asymmetry, not merely lexical

2. **Positive vs. Negative Causation**:
   - No morphological causatives exist for either making true or making false
   - However, verification (positive) has more resources than disconfirmation (negative)

3. **Revelation vs. Concealment**:
   - Neither has a dedicated grammatical construction
   - Both rely on lexical verbs
   - Symmetrical gap rather than asymmetric

## 4. Canonicalized Operator Inventory

### Fully Available
- **ATTR**: Copular construction with predicative adjectives (incorporated or analytic copula)
- **ADNOM**: Invariant attributive adjectives preceding nouns
- **BECOME**: Inchoative verb გახდომა (gakhdoma) with adjectives
- **NEGATION**: Analytic negation with particle არ (ar) + copula
- **NOMINALIZATION**: Multiple productive patterns (სი-...-ე, -ობა, -ება)
- **VERIFICATION**: Lexical verb class with systematic patterns

### Available with Substitution
- **REVELATION**: Substituted by BECOME constructions or lexical verbs (გამოვლენა, გამოჩენა)
  - Functional equivalent exists but not as distinct grammatical operator
  
- **CAUSATION (factual)**: Substituted by VERIFICATION verbs
  - No morphological causative, but verification verbs serve the functional role of "making known to be true"

### Partially Available
- **CAUSATION (character)**: Agentive nominalization (მა-...-არა) creates agent nouns
  - Not causative construction per se
  - Serves related function of denoting habitual agents of truth/falsity actions

### Missing Entirely
- **DISCONFIRMATION**: No productive construction
  - Only scattered lexical verbs
  - Requires circumlocution (negation + verification) or specific lexical items
  - Represents genuine structural gap in the constructional inventory

## Additional Observations

### Multi-word Expression Analysis
Several constructions involve multi-word expressions that function as units:

1. **Metalinguistic predicates**: სიმართლეს ამბობს (simart'les ambobs) "tells the truth"
   - Functions as a verbal predicate
   - Fixed expression with obligatory dative case
   - Not compositional (cannot freely substitute other verbs)

2. **Epistemic particles**: თურმე (turme), ხომ (khom), მეთქი (metki)
   - Function as adverbial modifiers of entire propositions
   - Syntactically independent
   - Can combine with any predicate type

3. **Contrastive frame**: არა X, არამედ Y (ara X, aramed Y)
   - Functions as a discourse-level construction
   - Fixed frame with two slots
   - არამედ (aramed) is obligatory for the corrective element

### Light Verb Patterns
Georgian uses light verbs in some truth-domain expressions:

1. **ეჭვი მაქვს** (ech'vi makvs) "I have doubt"
   - Light verb ქონა (kona) "have" carries grammatical information
   - Semantic content in noun ეჭვი (ech'vi) "doubt"
   - Dative experiencer construction

2. **ეჭვი შემეპარა** (ech'vi shemep'ara) "doubt seized me"
   - More lexically specific verb
   - Still uses noun + verb pattern
   - More literary/emphatic than light verb construction

### Degree Modification Integration
Degree modification is fully productive with truth-domain adjectives:
- Intensifiers: ძალიან (dzalian) "very," სრულიად (sruliad) "completely"
- Comparative: უფრო (upro) "more"
- Superlative: ყველაზე (q'velaze) "most"
- No restrictions on which truth-adjectives can be modified
