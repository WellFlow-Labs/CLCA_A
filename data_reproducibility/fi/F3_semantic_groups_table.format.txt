| GROUP 1: FACTUAL CORRESPONDENCE TERMS | Terms used to evaluate whether statements, information, or representations correspond to actual states of affairs. These expressions appear in contexts where the primary concern is matching claims to reality, particularly in reporting, verification, and correction contexts. They are commonly used with *tieto* "information," *väite* "claim," and *kertomus* "account." | *tosi* "true", *totuus* "truth", *tosiasia* "fact", *tosiasiallinen* "actual, factual", *todellinen* "real, actual", *paikkansapitävä* "accurate, correct", *todenmukainen* "truthful, veracious", *todenperäinen* "truthful, veracious", *pitää paikkansa* "to be true, to hold", *vastata totuutta* "to correspond to truth", *ei pidä paikkaansa* "is not true", *ei ole totta* "is not true", *paikkansapitävä tieto* "accurate information", *tosiasioihin perustuva* "based on facts" | *todellinen* appears here when used with *tilanne* "situation," *ongelma* "problem"; *väärä* appears here when used with *väite* "claim," *tieto* "information"; *tosiasia* appears here as basic term and in Group 16; *totuus* appears here as abstract noun, in Group 16, and in Group 12 | Typical usage contexts: Verification and fact-checking, News reporting and journalism, Administrative documentation, Distinguishing actual from hypothetical situations |
| GROUP 2: CORRECTNESS EVALUATION TERMS | Terms used to evaluate whether answers, procedures, methods, or choices are correct or proper according to standards, norms, or expectations. These expressions commonly appear in educational, instructional, and evaluative contexts, often with *vastaus* "answer," *tapa* "way/method," and *ratkaisu* "solution." | *oikea* "right, correct", *oikeellinen* "correct, accurate", *oikein* "correctly, right", *väärä* "wrong, incorrect", *väärin* "wrongly, incorrectly", *virheellinen* "erroneous, faulty", *olla oikeassa* "to be right", *olla väärässä* "to be wrong", *oikea vastaus* "correct answer", *oikealla tavalla* "the right way", *väärä käsitys* "wrong impression", *virheellinen tieto* "erroneous information" | *oikea* appears here when used with *vastaus* "answer," *tapa* "way" and also in Group 3; *väärä* appears here when used with *vastaus* "answer", also in Group 1 and Group 13 | Typical usage contexts: Educational assessment, Instructions and procedures, Problem-solving, Evaluating understanding or interpretation. Note: *Oikea* and *väärä* also appear in Group 1 contexts, but with distinct usage patterns |
| GROUP 3: AUTHENTICITY AND GENUINENESS TERMS | Terms used to distinguish genuine, authentic, or real things from imitations, fakes, or pretense. These expressions commonly appear with nouns denoting objects (*nahka* "leather," *taide* "art"), persons (*ystävä* "friend"), and emotions (*rakkaus* "love"), emphasizing the contrast between authentic and counterfeit. | *aito* "genuine, authentic", *aitous* "genuineness, authenticity", *aidosti* "genuinely", *todellinen* "real, actual" (in some contexts), *oikea* "real, genuine" (in some contexts), *oikea nimi* "real name", *tosimies* "real man", *tosiystävä* "true friend", *tosirakkauden* "true love", *aito nahka* "genuine leather", *aito ystävä* "true friend" | *todellinen* appears here when used with *ystävä* "friend," *rakkaus* "love" and also in Group 1; *oikea* appears here when used with *nimi* "name," *nahka* "leather" and also in Group 2 | Typical usage contexts: Product authentication, Character evaluation, Distinguishing sincere from insincere, Evaluating relationships |
| GROUP 4: DECEPTION AND FALSIFICATION TERMS | Terms used for deliberate acts of creating false representations, lying, or deceiving. These expressions emphasize intentional misrepresentation and commonly appear in moral, legal, and interpersonal contexts. They contrast with unintentional error terms. | *valhe* "lie, falsehood", *valehdella* "to lie", *valehtelija* "liar", *valheellinen* "false, mendacious", *väärentää* "to forge, to counterfeit", *väärennös* "forgery, counterfeit", *väärennösrikos* "forgery (crime)", *petollinen* "deceptive, deceitful", *petollisuus* "deceitfulness", *vääristää* "to distort, to falsify", *vääristely* "distortion, falsification", *harhaanjohtava* "misleading, deceptive", *puhua valhetta* "tell a lie", *elää valheessa* "live a lie", *valkoinen valhe* "white lie", *vääristää totuutta* "distort the truth", *harhaanjohtava mainonta* "misleading advertising", *väärä todistus* "false testimony" | *vääristää* appears here when emphasizing intentional falsification and also in Group 12 | Typical usage contexts: Moral evaluation, Legal proceedings, Interpersonal trust, Media criticism |
| GROUP 5: HONESTY AND SINCERITY TERMS | Terms used to characterize persons, statements, or intentions as honest, truthful, or sincere. These expressions emphasize moral character and genuine intent, commonly appearing in character evaluations and personal communication. They form the positive counterpart to Group 4. | *rehellinen* "honest, truthful", *rehellisyys* "honesty", *rehellisesti* "honestly", *vilpitön* "sincere, genuine", *vilpittömyys* "sincerity", *vilpittömästi* "sincerely", *rehellisesti sanottuna* "honestly speaking", *olla rehellinen* "to be honest", *vilpitön halu* "sincere desire", *vilpittömästi pahoillaan* "sincerely sorry", *aito ja rehellinen* "genuine and honest" | None noted | Typical usage contexts: Character evaluation, Personal communication, Moral discourse, Apologies and admissions |
| GROUP 6: EVIDENTIAL AND PROOF TERMS | Terms used in contexts of establishing truth through evidence, demonstration, or testimony. These expressions commonly appear in legal, scientific, and argumentative contexts where claims must be supported or verified through evidence. | *todistaa* "to prove, to testify", *todiste* "proof, evidence", *todistaja* "witness", *todistus* "certificate, testimony", *todistusaineisto* "body of evidence", *todistustaakka* "burden of proof", *todistuskappale* "piece of evidence", *todistettava* "provable", *todistettavasti* "provably, demonstrably", *todentaa* "to verify, to authenticate", *todentaminen* "verification", *esittää todisteita* "present evidence", *kiistaton todiste* "indisputable evidence", *todistaa syyttömyytensä* "prove one's innocence", *todistaa vääräksi* "prove false", *todistaa oikeaksi* "prove correct" | None noted | Typical usage contexts: Legal proceedings, Scientific demonstration, Argumentation, Administrative verification |
| GROUP 7: RELIABILITY AND CREDIBILITY TERMS | Terms used to evaluate whether sources, persons, or information can be trusted or believed. These expressions focus on the trustworthiness of information sources and the believability of claims, commonly appearing in contexts of information evaluation and source assessment. | *luotettava* "reliable, trustworthy", *luotettavuus* "reliability", *uskottava* "believable, credible", *uskottavuus* "credibility", *luotettava lähde* "reliable source", *luotettava tieto* "reliable information", *uskottava selitys* "credible explanation", *ei kuulosta uskottavalta* "doesn't sound credible" | None noted | Typical usage contexts: Source evaluation, Information assessment, Testimony evaluation, Media literacy |
| GROUP 8: PROBABILITY AND LIKELIHOOD TERMS | Terms used to express degrees of likelihood or probability that something is true or will occur. These expressions appear in predictive and epistemic contexts where certainty is qualified, commonly with complement clauses. | *todennäköinen* "probable, likely", *todennäköisyys* "probability, likelihood", *todennäköisesti* "probably, likely", *on todennäköistä että* "it is probable that", *hyvin todennäköinen* "very probable" | None noted | Typical usage contexts: Predictions, Risk assessment, Epistemic qualification, Scientific probability |
| GROUP 9: CLAIM AND ASSERTION TERMS | Terms used for making claims or assertions without necessarily committing to their truth. These expressions are neutral regarding truth value and commonly appear in contexts of reported speech, debate, and argumentation. | *väittää* "to claim, to assert", *väite* "claim, assertion", *väittely* "debate, argumentation", *väittelijä* "debater", *väitös* "dissertation defense", *väittää olevansa* "claim to be", *väittää että* "claim that", *väittää vastaan* "argue against" | None noted | Typical usage contexts: Reported speech, Debate and argumentation, Academic discourse, Neutral claim reporting |
| GROUP 10: REALIZATION AND ACTUALIZATION TERMS | Terms used for processes of making something real or actual, or for things becoming real. These expressions focus on the transition from potential, planned, or hoped-for states to actual existence or occurrence. | *todellistaa* "to realize, to actualize", *todellistua* "to come true, to be realized", *toteutua* "to come true, to be realized", *toteuttaa* "to realize, to implement", *toteutus* "realization, implementation", *toteuttaminen* "realization, implementation", *todentua* "to be verified, to be confirmed", *todellisuus* "reality", *suunnitelman toteuttaminen* "implementation of plan", *unelman todellistua* "dream coming true" | None noted | Typical usage contexts: Project management, Goal achievement, Plans and predictions, Hopes and aspirations |
| GROUP 11: OBSERVATION AND STATEMENT TERMS | Terms used for stating, noting, or observing facts in a relatively neutral manner. These expressions commonly appear in formal discourse where facts are being established or recorded without strong epistemic commitment. | *todeta* "to state, to note, to observe", *toteamus* "statement, observation", *on todettava että* "it must be noted that", *voidaan todeta* "it can be stated" | None noted | Typical usage contexts: Formal reporting, Academic writing, Official documentation, Neutral fact-stating |
| GROUP 12: DISTORTION AND MISREPRESENTATION TERMS | Terms used for processes of making something false, inaccurate, or misleading, whether through distortion of existing truth or creation of false representations. These expressions emphasize the transformation or corruption of truth. | *vääristää* "to distort, to falsify", *vääristyä* "to become distorted", *vääristymä* "distortion, deformation", *vääristely* "distortion, falsification", *väärentää* "to forge, to counterfeit", *väärennös* "forgery, counterfeit", *harhaanjohtava* "misleading", *vääristää totuutta* "distort truth", *vääristää tosiasioita* "distort facts", *harhaanjohtava tieto* "misleading information" | *vääristää* appears here when emphasizing transformation/corruption and also in Group 4; *totuus* appears here as object of *vääristää* and also in Groups 1 and 16 | Typical usage contexts: Media criticism, Accusation of misrepresentation, Forensic analysis, Critical discourse. Note: Overlaps with Group 4 but emphasizes transformation/corruption rather than creation of falsehood |
| GROUP 13: ERROR AND MISTAKE TERMS | Terms used for unintentional incorrectness or mistakes, as opposed to deliberate deception. These expressions commonly appear in contexts of correction and clarification where the focus is on error rather than moral failing. | *virheellinen* "erroneous, faulty", *virheellisuus* "erroneousness", *väärinkäsitys* "misunderstanding, misconception", *väärä käsitys* "wrong impression", *virheellinen tieto* "erroneous information", *virheellinen käsitys* "erroneous understanding" | *väärä* appears here when used with *käsitys* "impression" and also in Groups 1 and 2 | Typical usage contexts: Corrections, Clarifications, Educational feedback, Technical documentation. Note: Contrasts with Group 4 (deliberate deception) and Group 2 (correctness evaluation) |
| GROUP 14: MISUSE AND ABUSE TERMS | Terms used for improper or harmful use of something, particularly in contexts of power, trust, or resources. These expressions emphasize wrongful application rather than falsity per se. | *väärinkäyttö* "misuse, abuse", *väärinkäyttää* "to misuse, to abuse", *vallan väärinkäyttö* "abuse of power", *väärinkäyttää luottamusta* "abuse trust" | None noted | Typical usage contexts: Legal discourse, Ethical evaluation, Power relations, Trust violations. Note: Peripheral to core truth-domain; included due to *väärä* root but semantically distinct |
| GROUP 15: REAL-LIFE AND ACTUALITY TERMS | Terms used to distinguish actual lived experience or real-world situations from fiction, simulation, theory, or hypothetical scenarios. These expressions emphasize the contrast between reality and various forms of non-reality. | *tosielämä* "real life", *tositarina* "true story", *tosi-tv* "reality TV", *tosipohjainen* "based on facts, fact-based", *tosielämän sankari* "real-life hero", *tosielämässä* "in real life", *todellisuudessa* "in reality" | None noted | Typical usage contexts: Media and entertainment, Contrasting reality with fiction, Everyday discourse, Narrative framing |
| GROUP 16: EMPHATIC TRUTH EXPRESSIONS | Fixed or semi-fixed expressions used to emphasize or intensify truth claims, often in informal or conversational contexts. These expressions go beyond simple truth assertion to stress certainty or genuineness. | *tosi juttu* "true story, for real", *ihan tosi* "completely true", *totta kai* "of course, certainly", *totta tosiaan* "indeed, as a matter of fact", *tosiaankin* "indeed, really", *puhdas totuus* "pure truth", *koko totuus* "whole truth", *kiistaton tosiasia* "indisputable fact" | *tosiasia* appears here in *kiistaton tosiasia* "indisputable fact" and also in Group 1; *totuus* appears here in *puhdas totuus*, *koko totuus* and also in Groups 1 and 12 | Typical usage contexts: Conversational emphasis, Strong assertions, Confirmations, Informal speech |
| (Ungrouped Items) | Items that do not fit cleanly into groups | Spatial/directional uses: *oikeanpuoleinen* "right-hand, on the right side", *oikeakätinen* "right-handed"; Legal/moral uses: *oikeus* "right, justice, law, court", *oikeudenmukaisuus* "justice, fairness", *oikeuttaa* "to justify"; Seriousness/earnestness: *tosissaan* "seriously, in earnest", *tosikko* "overly serious person"; Religious orthodoxy: *vääräuskoinen* "heretic", *vääräoppisuus* "heresy", *oikeaoppinen* "orthodox"; Physical/anatomical: *vääräleuka* "underbite", *väärähammas* "false tooth"; Character traits: *vääräpäinen* "stubborn, wrongheaded", *oikeamielinen* "righteous, right-minded"; Temporal: *oikea-aikainen* "timely"; Uncertain grouping: *epätosi* "untrue, false", *epärehellinen* "dishonest", *oikeakielisyys* "linguistic correctness", *tosiasiallisuus* "factuality, actuality" | Spatial/directional uses represent homonymy rather than polysemy; Legal/moral uses have developed into distinct semantic domain; Seriousness/earnestness uses extend from truth-domain to behavioral/attitudinal domain; Religious orthodoxy terms are specialized with distinct usage patterns; Physical/anatomical terms use *väärä* for physical abnormality/artificiality; Character trait terms extend from correctness to character evaluation; Temporal appropriateness not clearly truth-related; Items with uncertain grouping lack distinctive usage patterns or are highly domain-specific | These items either represent complete semantic divergence from truth-domain (spatial, anatomical), specialized domains (legal, religious), peripheral extensions (seriousness, character), or items lacking sufficient distinctive usage patterns to establish clear group membership |
