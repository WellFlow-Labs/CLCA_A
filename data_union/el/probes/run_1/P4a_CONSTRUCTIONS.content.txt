# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P4a CONSTRUCTIONS
# Language: Greek (el)

---

## 1. ATTRIBUTION

### Pattern
Subject + copula (είναι) + truth-domain adjective (αληθής / αληθινός / σωστός / ορθός / ψευδής / ψεύτικος / έγκυρος / αξιόπιστος / αμφίβολος)

### Examples
- Αυτό είναι αληθινό. / *Aftó íne alithinó.* / "This is true / real."
- Η κατάθεσή του είναι ψευδής. / *I katáthesí tu íne psevdhís.* / "His testimony is false."

### Notes
Copular attribution is the canonical frame. Register determines adjective choice: αληθής / ψευδής are formal/legal; αληθινός / ψεύτικος are colloquial. The copula είναι is invariant; the adjective agrees in gender, number, and case with the subject.

---

## 2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION

### Pattern
Subject + δεν + είναι + truth-domain adjective; or subject + είναι + privative-prefixed adjective (αν-/ανα-)

### Examples
- Αυτό δεν είναι αληθινό. / *Aftó dhen íne alithinó.* / "This is not true."
- Η πηγή είναι αναξιόπιστη. / *I pigí íne anaksiópistos.* / "The source is unreliable."

### Notes
Two strategies coexist: (a) sentential negation with δεν + είναι + positive adjective, and (b) a lexicalized privative adjective (αναληθής, αναξιόπιστος, ανακριβής, αναμφίβολος, άκυρος). Strategy (b) is preferred in formal and legal registers; strategy (a) is the default in everyday speech. Not all positive adjectives have a lexicalized privative counterpart.

---

## 3. ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION

### Pattern
Truth-domain adjective (prenominal or postnominal) + noun; adjective agrees in gender, number, case

### Examples
- ψευδής κατάθεση / *psevdhís katáthesi* / "false testimony"
- αξιόπιστη πηγή / *aksiópistos pigí* / "reliable source"

### Notes
Greek adjectives may appear prenominally or postnominally; in the truth domain, prenominal position is slightly more marked (emphatic or literary), while postnominal is neutral. Several collocations have achieved semi-fixed status in legal and journalistic registers (ψευδής κατάθεση, ψευδής δήλωση, έγκυρο έγγραφο, αξιόπιστος μάρτυρας).

---

## 4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE

### Pattern
γίνομαι + truth-domain adjective; or αποδεικνύεται / αποδεικνύομαι + adjective/ότι-clause

### Examples
- Αποδείχτηκε ψευδής. / *Apodhíchtike psevdhís.* / "It turned out to be false / was proven false."
- Η πληροφορία αποδείχτηκε αναληθής. / *I pliroforía apodhíchtike analithís.* / "The information turned out to be untrue."

### Notes
Greek lacks a dedicated inchoative copula equivalent to English "become + adjective" that is idiomatic in this domain. The passive of αποδεικνύω (αποδείχτηκε, "it was proven / it turned out") is the natural expression for the transition from unverified to established-as-true or established-as-false. γίνομαι + adjective is grammatical but less natural for truth-value transitions specifically.

---

## 5. CAUSATIVE

### Pattern
Causative of truth-domain states is expressed periphrastically: αποδεικνύω ("to prove"), επαληθεύω ("to verify"), διαψεύδω ("to refute / show to be false"), νοθεύω ("to adulterate / falsify"), πλαστογραφώ ("to forge")

### Examples
- Απέδειξε ότι η κατάθεση ήταν ψευδής. / *Apédhikse óti i katáthesi ítan psevdhís.* / "He proved that the testimony was false."
- Διέψευσε τους ισχυρισμούς. / *Dhiépsefse tus ischirismús.* / "He refuted / disproved the claims."

### Notes
Greek has no morphological causative. Causation of truth-states is expressed through dedicated verbs: αποδεικνύω (cause to be proven true), επαληθεύω (cause to be verified), διαψεύδω (cause to be shown false), νοθεύω (cause to be adulterated), πλαστογραφώ (cause a document to be forged). These are lexically distinct rather than derived from the adjectives via a productive causative morpheme.

## 6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION

### Pattern
Truth-domain adjective → abstract noun via suffixes -εια / -ία / -ότητα / -ση

### Examples
- αλήθεια / *alíthia* / "truth" (← αληθής + -εια)
- αξιοπιστία / *aksiopistía* / "credibility, reliability" (← αξιόπιστος + -ία)

### Notes
Three productive suffixes operate in this domain: -εια (αλήθεια, ακρίβεια), -ία (αξιοπιστία, αυθεντία, αμφιβολία, εγκυρότητα via -ότητα), and -ότητα (αυθεντικότητα, γνησιότητα, ορθότητα, εγκυρότητα). The -ότητα suffix is the most productive for newer or borrowed adjectives. Verbal nominalizations in -ση (επαλήθευση, πιστοποίηση, αμφισβήτηση, τεκμηρίωση) form a parallel set denoting processes rather than properties.

---

## 7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION

### Pattern
Verb/noun stem + agentive suffix -τής (masc.) / -τρια (fem.) / -ας (-έας)

### Examples
- ψεύτης / ψεύτρα / *psévtis / psévtra* / "liar (masc. / fem.)"
- αξιολογητής / *aksioloyitís* / "evaluator, assessor"

### Notes
The primary agentive for the liar is ψεύτης (masc.) / ψεύτρα (fem.), derived from the ψευτ- root. A truth-teller agentive (*αληθευτής) is not in common use; the concept is expressed periphrastically (αυτός που λέει την αλήθεια). Other agentives in the domain include πλαστογράφος (forger), αμφισβητίας (habitual disputant, marginal), and πιστωτής (creditor, financial register only).

## 8. DISCOURSE-FRAMING CONSTRUCTIONS

### Pattern
Fixed clausal or phrasal frame + proposition; functions as sincerity marker, candor introducer, or truth-asserting device

### Examples
- Η αλήθεια είναι ότι δεν το ήξερα. / *I alíthia íne óti dhen to íksera.* / "The truth is that I didn't know."
- Για να λέμε την αλήθεια, δεν συμφωνώ. / *Ya na léme tin alíthia, dhen simfonó.* / "To tell the truth, I don't agree."

### Notes
A productive set of fixed frames uses αλήθεια as a discourse particle to introduce frank or corrective statements. These constructions are pragmatically distinct from propositional attribution (§1): they signal speaker stance (candor, frankness) rather than evaluating a proposition's truth-value. The frame η αλήθεια είναι ότι… is the most frequent written form; για να λέμε την αλήθεια and αν θέλεις την αλήθεια are characteristic of spoken registers.

---

## 9. VERIFICATION / ESTABLISHMENT CONSTRUCTIONS

### Pattern
Verb of verification or establishment (επαληθεύω, διαπιστώνω, αποδεικνύω, τεκμηριώνω) + noun phrase or ότι-clause

### Examples
- Διαπίστωσε ότι τα έγγραφα ήταν πλαστά. / *Dhiapístoce óti ta éngrafa ítan plastá.* / "He established / found that the documents were forged."
- Τεκμηριώνει τη θέση του με στοιχεία. / *Tekmirióni ti thési tu me stoichía.* / "He substantiates his position with evidence."

### Notes
This construction type is structurally distinct from simple attribution: it encodes an epistemic process (checking, establishing, proving) rather than a static property. The verb selects either an ότι-complement (propositional content) or a direct object (the thing verified). Passive forms (επαληθεύτηκε, τεκμηριώθηκε) are common in formal and journalistic registers to background the agent of verification.

---

## 10. PSEUDO- COMPOUND CONSTRUCTIONS

### Pattern
ψευδο- (prefix) + domain noun → compound noun or adjective denoting a category that has the form but not the substance of the base category

### Examples
- ψευδοεπιστήμη / *psevdhoepistími* / "pseudoscience"
- ψευδοδίλημμα / *psevdhodhílima* / "false dilemma"

### Notes
ψευδο- is a productive prefix that combines freely with domain nouns across registers (scientific, political, rhetorical, philological). The resulting compound denotes an entity that presents the appearance of the base category without its defining substance. This is structurally distinct from simple negation (δεν είναι επιστήμη) in that it asserts a positive category of simulacrum rather than mere absence. The prefix is invariant; the compound follows the inflectional pattern of the base noun.
