# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2a Derivational System
# Language: Finnish (fi)

## Methodology Note

This analysis documents **attested derivational morphology** based on the P1 vocabulary and P1b root expansion. Finnish exhibits rich agglutinative morphology with productive derivational suffixes. The analysis focuses on forms that are genuinely used in the language, avoiding paradigm completion or theoretical possibilities.

## DERIVATIONAL PATTERNS: Root 1 (tosi / tode-)

### Base: tosi

#### Derived Form 1: totuus
**Base:** tosi  
**Derived Form:** totuus  
**Literal Breakdown:** tosi + -uus (abstract noun suffix)  
**Translation:** truth  
**Usage:** Standard nominalization creating abstract noun from adjective. Highly productive pattern in Finnish (*korkea* → *korkeus* "height", *hyvä* → *hyvyys* "goodness").  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### Derived Form 2: tosiasiallinen
**Base:** tosiasia (compound: tosi + asia "matter")  
**Derived Form:** tosiasiallinen  
**Literal Breakdown:** tosi-asia + -llinen (adjectival suffix)  
**Translation:** actual, factual, de facto  
**Usage:** Productive adjectival suffix creating quality adjectives from nouns. Common pattern (*laki* → *lakillinen* "legal").  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### Derived Form 3: tosiasiassa
**Base:** tosiasia  
**Derived Form:** tosiasiassa  
**Literal Breakdown:** tosi-asia + -ssa (inessive case)  
**Translation:** in fact, actually  
**Context:** Grammaticalized case form functioning as adverb. Common pattern for creating adverbials from nouns.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### Derived Form 4: tosissaan
**Base:** tosi  
**Derived Form:** tosissaan  
**Literal Breakdown:** tosi + -ssa (inessive) + -an (3rd person possessive)  
**Translation:** seriously, in earnest  
**Usage:** Grammaticalized case + possessive construction functioning as adverb. Idiomatic expression.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### Derived Form 5: tosikko
**Base:** tosi  
**Derived Form:** tosikko  
**Literal Breakdown:** tosi + -kko (agent/characteristic noun suffix)  
**Translation:** overly serious person  
**Usage:** Derivational suffix creating person nouns with characteristic quality, often with pejorative or diminutive connotation (*juoma* → *juomari* "drinker", *laula-* → *laulaja* "singer"). The -kko variant often carries negative or excessive meaning.  
**Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

### Base: todellinen

#### Derived Form 6: todellisuus
**Base:** todellinen  
**Derived Form:** todellisuus  
**Literal Breakdown:** tode-lli-nen → tode-lli-suus (adjective + -suus abstract noun suffix)

#### Derived Form 7: todellistaa
**Base:** todellinen  
**Derived Form:** todellistaa

**Translation:** to realize, to actualize  
**Usage:** Productive causative derivation from adjectives (*selkeä* → *selkeyttää* "to clarify"). The -sta- variant appears with -llinen adjectives.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### Derived Form 8: todellistua
**Base:** todellistaa  
**Derived Form:** todellistua  
**Literal Breakdown:** tode-llistu- + -a (reflexive/inchoative verb suffix)  
**Translation:** to come true, to be realized  
**Usage:** Productive reflexive/inchoative derivation from causative verbs, indicating spontaneous or intransitive action (*avata* → *avautua* "to open (intrans.)").  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## DERIVATIONAL PATTERNS: Root 2 (oikea / oikee-)

### Base: oikea

#### Derived Form 22: oikeasti
**Base:** oikea  
**Derived Form:** oikeasti  
**Literal Breakdown:** oikea + -sti (adverbial suffix)  
**Translation:** really, actually  
**Usage:** Standard adverb formation from adjectives. Note: Finnish has two common adverbial patterns from oikea: *oikeasti* and *oikein*, with slightly different semantic ranges.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### Derived Form 23: oikein
**Base:** oikea  
**Derived Form:** oikein  
**Literal Breakdown:** oikea + -in (adverbial suffix, archaic variant)  
**Translation:** correctly, right; really, very  
**Usage:** Older adverbial formation pattern, now lexicalized with dual function (manner adverb and intensifier). The -in suffix is no longer productive but appears in frozen forms (*kaunis* → *kauniin* archaic "beautifully").  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### Derived Form 24: oikeellinen
**Base:** oikea  
**Derived Form:** oikeellinen  
**Literal Breakdown:** oikee- + -llinen (adjectival suffix)  
**Translation:** correct, accurate  
**Usage:** Productive adjectival derivation, often creating more formal or technical variants (*laki* → *lakillinen* "legal").  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### Derived Form 25: oikeellisuus
**Base:** oikeellinen  
**Derived Form:** oikeellisuus  
**Literal Breakdown:** oikee-lli- + -suus (abstract noun suffix)  
**Translation:** correctness, accuracy  
**Usage:** Standard nominalization from -llinen adjectives.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### Derived Form 26: oikeuttaa
**Base:** oikea (via oikeus)  
**Derived Form:** oikeuttaa  
**Literal Breakdown:** oikeut- + -ta- (causative) + -a  
**Translation:** to justify, to warrant  
**Usage:** Causative verb derivation. Note: This derives from the noun *oikeus* "right, justice" rather than directly from the adjective *oikea*.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### Derived Form 27: oikeutettu
**Base:** oikeuttaa  
**Derived Form:** oikeutettu  
**Literal Breakdown:** oikeute- + -ttu (past passive participle)  
**Translation:** justified, warranted  
**Usage:** Standard passive participle formation, often functioning as adjective (*rakennettu* "built", *kirjoitettu* "written").  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### Derived Form 28: oikeutus
**Base:** oikeuttaa  
**Derived Form:** oikeutus  
**Literal Breakdown:** oikeut- + -us (action/result noun)  
**Translation:** justification, authorization  
**Usage:** Standard nominalization from verbs.  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### Derived Form 29: oikeus
**Base:** oikea  
**Derived Form:** oikeus  
**Literal Breakdown:** oikea + -us (abstract noun suffix)  
**Translation:** right, justice, law, court  
**Usage:** Standard nominalization, though semantically this has developed into a highly polysemous legal/moral term distinct from simple "correctness."

## CROSS-CUTTING DERIVATIONAL OBSERVATIONS

### Productive Suffixes in Truth Domain

**Abstract noun formation (-uus/-yys/-suus):**
- Highly productive across all roots
- Creates abstract quality nouns from adjectives
- Examples: *totuus*, *oikeellisuus*, *vääryys*, *aitous*, *rehellisyys*

**Adverbial formation (-sti):**
- Highly productive from adjectives and participles
- Standard manner adverb formation
- Examples: *oikeasti*, *rehellisesti*, *todennäköisesti*, *vilpittömästi*

**Archaic adverbial formation (-in):**
- No longer productive, but preserved in high-frequency forms
- Examples: *oikein*, *väärin*
- These forms often develop additional functions (intensification)

**Adjectival formation (-llinen):**
- Highly productive from nouns
- Creates quality adjectives
- Examples: *todellinen*, *oikeellinen*, *valheellinen*, *petollinen*, *rehellinen*

**Agent noun formation (-ja/-jä):**
- Productive from verbs
- Creates person nouns
- Examples: *todistaja*, *valehtelija*, *väittelijä*

**Causative verb formation (-tta-/-ttä-, -sta-/-stä-):**
- Productive from adjectives and nouns
- Creates transitive verbs of causing/making
- Examples: *todellistaa*, *toteuttaa*, *oikeuttaa*, *vääristää*, *väärentää*

**Reflexive/inchoative formation (-u-/-y-):**
- Productive from causative verbs
- Creates intransitive counterparts
- Examples: *todellistua*, *toteutua*, *vääristyä*, *todentua*

**Result noun formation (-us/-ys, -e, -ma/-mä, -os/-ös, -nös/-nys):**
- Multiple productive patterns
- Create nouns denoting results or products of actions
- Examples: *todistus*, *todiste*, *toteamus*, *vääristymä*, *väärennös*, *väitös*

**Gerund formation (-minen):**
- Highly productive from all verbs
- Creates action/process nouns
- Examples: *toteuttaminen*, *todentaminen*

**Participle formation (-va/-vä, -ttu/-tty):**
- Productive verbal adjective formation
- Often lexicalized as adjectives
- Examples: *luotettava*, *uskottava*, *todistettava*, *oikeutettu*

### Asymmetries and Gaps

**Limited derivation from some bases:**
- *paikkansapitävä*: no further derivation (already complex)
- *harhaanjohtava*: no further derivation (already complex)
- *epätosi*: relies on *tosi* derivational family
- *aito*: no causative verb (*aitouttaa not attested)

**Semantic drift in derivational chains:**
- *oikea* → *oikeus*: shift from correctness to legal/moral rights
- *väärä* → *vääryys*: shift from incorrectness to moral injustice
- *tosi* → *tosissaan*: shift from truth to seriousness

**Multiple derivational paths from single root:**
- *väärä* → *vääristää* (distort) vs. *väärentää* (forge): distinct semantic developments
- *tosi* → *todellinen* (real) vs. *todistaa* (prove): different aspectual focus

**Compound formation productivity:**
- *tosi-* highly productive in compounds: *tosielämä*, *tositarina*, *tosimies*, *tosi-tv*
- *oikea-* moderately productive: *oikeaoppinen*, *oikeamielinen*
- *väärä-* less productive in truth-domain compounds
