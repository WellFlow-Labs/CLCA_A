# CLCA P1b — ROOT EXPANSION
## Language: Quechua (qu)

Orthography: Standardized trivocalic Southern Quechua (a, i, u), consistent with P1.

---

## STEP 1 — Central Roots

### Root 1: *chiqa-* (chiqa-)
**Native/transliteration:** chiqa- (variant cheqa-, chiqap-/chiqaq-)
**P1 items it participates in:** Items 1, 2, 3, 4, 7, 13, 14, 20, 25 → **9 items**
**Core meaning contribution:** "true / straight / right / direct." This is the central truth-and-rectitude root of the domain, spanning veracity (true, real), physical straightness, and moral-legal correctness (just, right). It is the primary building block for affirming factual truth and for derivations of correcting/verifying.

### Root 2: *allin* (allin)
**Native/transliteration:** allin
**P1 items it participates in:** Items 11, 12, 28 → **3 items**
**Core meaning contribution:** "good / well / right / proper / correct." The broad evaluative root for goodness and correctness across moral, qualitative, and practical domains. Highly productive in everyday judgment.

### Root 3: *llulla* (llulla)
**Native/transliteration:** llulla
**P1 items it participates in:** Items 8, 9, 23 → **3 items**
**Core meaning contribution:** "lie / false / deceit." The central root for falsehood and deception, strongly evaluative and morally charged; the negative pole of the truth domain.

## STEP 2 — Root Expansion

### Expansion of Root 1: *chiqa-*

**Form:** chiqaq
**Transliteration:** chiqaq
**Literal:** chiqa- "true/straight" + -q agentive/attributive
**English:** true, real, genuine, correct
**Context:** Everyday affirmation that a statement matches fact; contrast to falsehood. (In P1, listed here as the base of the family.)
**Status:** [CORE]

**Form:** chiqaqta
**Transliteration:** chiqaqta
**Literal:** chiqaq + -ta adverbial
**English:** truly, really, rightly
**Context:** Emphatic adverbial assertion that something is genuinely the case. (P1 item 14.)
**Status:** [CORE]

**Form:** chiqaqchu
**Transliteration:** chiqaqchu
**Literal:** chiqaq + -chu interrogative
**English:** is it true? really?
**Context:** Conversational request for confirmation of a claim. (P1 item 20.)
**Status:** [CORE]

**Form:** chiqaqmi
**Transliteration:** chiqaqmi
**Literal:** chiqaq + -mi direct-evidential affirmative
**English:** it's true (I affirm), truly so
**Context:** Affirmation of truth with direct evidential commitment; very common in assertion.
**Status:** [CORE]

**Form:** chiqaqpuni
**Transliteration:** chiqaqpuni
**Literal:** chiqaq + -puni certainly
**English:** definitely true, surely true
**Context:** Strong affirmation of a fact's certainty in discussion or dispute.
**Status:** [CORE]

**Form:** chiqap
**Transliteration:** chiqap
**Literal:** variant of chiqaq
**English:** true, truly, real
**Context:** Dialectal/adverbial variant marking truth/reality. (P1 item 2.)
**Status:** [CORE]

**Form:** chiqap kay
**Transliteration:** chiqap kay
**Literal:** chiqap "true" + kay "to be/being"
**English:** truth, the truth, reality
**Context:** Nominalized expression invoking veracity in moral/discursive contexts. (P1 item 3.)
**Status:** [CORE]

**Form:** chiqachay
**Transliteration:** chiqachay
**Literal:** chiqa- + -chay causative
**English:** to correct, straighten, verify, set right
**Context:** Correcting errors or setting things straight, physically and factually/morally. (P1 item 7.)
**Status:** [CORE]

**Form:** chiqan
**Transliteration:** chiqan
**Literal:** chiqa- + -n

**Status:** [CORE]

**Form:** chiqan ñan
**Transliteration:** chiqan ñan
**Literal:** chiqan "straight/right" + ñan "road/path"

**Context:** Literal "direct road" and moral "right path"; common in moral-religious discourse.

**Form:** chiqanchay

**Form:** chiqaq simi

**Status:** [MARGINAL]

**Form:** chiqaq sunqu
**Transliteration:** chiqaq sunqu

**Context:** Fixed expression for sincerity/uprightness of character. (P1 item 25.)
**Status:** [MARGINAL]

**Form:** chiqaq kawsay
**Transliteration:** chiqaq kawsay
**Literal:** chiqaq "true" + kawsay "life"
**English:** true life, an upright life
**Context:** Moral-religious discourse on living rightly/authentically.
**Status:** [MARGINAL]

**Form:** chiqaqchakuy
**Transliteration:** chiqaqchakuy
**Literal:** chiqaq + -cha- + -ku- reflexive + -y

**Status:** [MARGINAL]

**Transliteration:** cheqaq

**Transliteration:** chiqaqpaq

### Expansion of Root 3: *llulla*

**Form:** llulla
**Transliteration:** llulla
**Literal:** root "lie/false"
**English:** lie, liar, false
**Context:** Names the lie, the liar, or the quality of falseness; strongly evaluative. (P1 item 8.)
**Status:** [CORE]

**Form:** llullakuy
**Transliteration:** llullakuy
**Literal:** llulla + -ku- reflexive + -y
**English:** to lie, to deceive
**Context:** The verb for telling untruths; moral evaluation of conduct. (P1 item 9.)
**Status:** [CORE]

**Form:** mana llullaq
**Transliteration:** mana llullaq
**Literal:** mana "not" + llulla + -q agentive
**English:** truthful, honest, one who does not lie
**Context:** Fixed negated phrase for a reliable, honest person. (P1 item 23.)
**Status:** [MARGINAL]

**Form:** llullasapa
**Transliteration:** llullasapa
**Literal:** llulla + -sapa augmentative ("full of")
**English:** habitual liar, big liar
**Context:** Pejorative character evaluation of someone who lies habitually.
**Status:** [CORE]

**Form:** llullakuq
**Transliteration:** llullakuq
**Literal:** llullaku- + -q agentive
**English:** liar, deceiver
**Context:** Names a person who lies; moral evaluation.
**Status:** [CORE]

**Form:** llulla simi
**Transliteration:** llulla simi
**Literal:** llulla "false" + simi "word/speech"
**English:** lie, false word, falsehood (in speech)
**Context:** A false statement or lying speech; moral/discursive.
**Status:** [MARGINAL]

**Form:** llullachiy
**Transliteration:** llullachiy
**Literal:** llulla + -chi- causative + -y
**English:** to deceive (someone), to cause to be fooled, to trick
**Context:** Causing another to believe a falsehood; deception of a third party.
**Status:** [MARGINAL]

**Form:** llullakuni
**Transliteration:** llullakuni
**Literal:** llullaku- + -ni 1sg
**English:** I lie / I'm lying
**Context:** *Excluded as a P1 inflected form example — listed only to mark the boundary; not counted.*
**Status:** [UNCERTAIN]

**Form:** llulla kay
**Transliteration:** llulla kay
**Literal:** llulla + kay "being"
**English:** falseness, mendacity, the state of being a lie
**Context:** Abstraction of falsehood in moral/discursive reflection.
**Status:** [MARGINAL]

Note: Compared to *chiqa-* and *allin*, the *llulla* root has a somewhat smaller compound set; core productive forms cluster around the liar/lying senses (llullakuy, llullasapa, llullakuq, llullachiy). Several of the above approach the lower end (close to ~5 robustly attested derivations).

## STEP 3 — Peripheral Extensions (flagged only)

These are forms where the root's contribution appears to shift away from, or extend beyond, its core truth/falsity sense. Flagged without interpretation:

**From *chiqa-*:**
- **chiqan / chiqanchay** — shift toward physical "straight/straighten" and alignment of objects (spatial domain rather than veracity).
- **chiqan ñan** — shift toward path/direction and moral "right path."
- **chiqaq sunqu**, **chiqaq kawsay** — shift toward character/sincerity and ethical living rather than factual truth.
- **chiqaqpaq** — shift toward colloquial intensity/seriousness ("for real").

**From *allin*:**
- **allin kawsay** — shift into ethical-political concept of well-being/"the good life."
- **allinta** — shift toward adverbial degree/manner ("well, thoroughly").
- **allillanmi**, **allin p'unchay** — shift into greeting/phatic conventional use.
- **allin sunqu**, **allin runa** — shift toward moral character evaluation.
- **allin ruwasqa** — shift toward aesthetic/craftsmanship evaluation.
- **allinniyuq** — shift toward material prosperity/possession.
- **allinpaq** — shift toward benefactive reasoning ("for the best").

**From *llulla*:**
- **llullasapa** — shift toward character-trait/habitual evaluation (augmentative intensity).
- **llullachiy** — shift toward third-party causation/trickery rather than self-asserted falsehood.

---

### Summary notes on limitations
- Evidential enclitics (-mi/-si/-chá) and -puni recur in combination with these roots (e.g., chiqaqmi, chiqaqpuni, allinmi) and are highly relevant to truth/reliability marking; combinations are included only where lexicalized/conventional, per the inclusion criteria.
- Dialectal/orthographic variants (cheqa-/chiqa-) collapse onto the same root family.
- Fixed phrases (X sunqu, X kawsay, X simi, X runa) show variable word order; status reflects this variability.
