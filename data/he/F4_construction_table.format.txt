# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — F4 Construction Table
# Language: Hebrew (he)

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Usage Notes | Epistemic Status |
|------------------|---------|-----------------|-------------|-------------|------------------|
| ATTRIBUTION | זה אמת | ze emet | this is truth / this is true | Extremely common in everyday speech and writing. Zero copula in present tense is the default pattern. Used across all registers. No restrictions observed. | [CORE] |
| ATTRIBUTION | הדבר נכון | hadavar nakhon | the thing is correct | Standard predicative construction. Frequent in formal and informal contexts. Adjectives must agree in gender/number with subject. | [CORE] |
| NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION | זה לא נכון | ze lo nakhon | this is not correct | Default negation pattern, used constantly in all contexts. לא precedes the predicate in standard word order. No restrictions. | [CORE] |
| NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION | אין בזה אמת | eyn baze emet | there is no truth in this | Common in both spoken and written Hebrew. Often used for emphatic denial or philosophical statements. Slightly more formal than simple לא negation. | [CORE] |
| ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION | סיפור אמיתי | sipur amiti | true story | Highly productive pattern used constantly. Adjective follows noun in standard Hebrew word order. Definiteness agreement obligatory (הסיפור האמיתי). | [CORE] |
| ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION | עדות שקר | edut sheker | false testimony | Common in legal and formal registers. שקר functions as noun in construct state here. Fixed collocation in legal contexts. | [CORE] |
| BECOME / INCHOATIVE | להיות כנה | lihyot kene | to become honest / to be honest | Ambiguous between stative and inchoative readings; context disambiguates. Used across all registers. Very common construction. | [CORE] |
| BECOME / INCHOATIVE | הפך לשקרן | hafakh leshakran | became a liar | Explicitly inchoative, no ambiguity. Common with agent nouns and role terms. Typical in narrative contexts describing character change. | [CORE] |
| CAUSATIVE | להאמין | leha'amin | to cause to be firm → to believe, to make believe | Extremely frequent verb in all contexts. Hif'il pattern productive for causatives. This particular verb has lexicalized meaning "believe" rather than transparent causative. | [CORE] |
| CAUSATIVE | להצדיק | lehatsдik | to cause to be right → to justify | Common in argumentative and formal discourse. Retains transparent causative meaning. Used in legal, ethical, and everyday contexts. | [CORE] |
| ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION | אמיתי → אמיתות | amiti → amitiyut | true → authenticity/truthfulness | Highly productive morphological pattern. Used freely to create abstract nouns from adjectives. Common in formal and philosophical registers. | [CORE] |
| ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION | נאמן → נאמנות | ne'eman → ne'emanut | faithful → faithfulness/loyalty | Same productive pattern. This particular form very common in relationship and institutional contexts. No restrictions on formation. | [CORE] |
| AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION | שקר → שקרן | sheker → shakran | lie → liar (habitual) | Productive pattern for habitual/characteristic agents. Often carries pejorative connotation. Common in colloquial speech. | [CORE] |
| AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION | מאמין | ma'amin | believer | Active participle functioning as agent noun. Very common pattern in Hebrew. Used in religious and general contexts without restriction. | [CORE] |
| VERBAL CONSTRUCTIONS WITH LIGHT VERBS | לומר את האמת | lomar et ha'emet | to say the truth → to tell the truth | Extremely common collocation, used constantly. את marks definite direct object. Standard across all registers. | [CORE] |
| VERBAL CONSTRUCTIONS WITH LIGHT VERBS | לעשות צדק | la'asot tsedek | to do justice | Common in legal, ethical, and religious discourse. Fixed collocation. Slightly more formal than everyday speech. | [CORE] |
| INTENSIFICATION / DEGREE MODIFICATION | ברור לחלוטין | barur leḥalutin | clear completely → completely clear | Common intensification pattern. לחלוטין follows adjective. Used in formal and informal contexts for emphasis. | [CORE] |
| INTENSIFICATION / DEGREE MODIFICATION | שקר גמור | sheker gamur | lie complete → complete lie | Very common emphatic pattern. גמור follows noun/adjective. Used across registers for strong emphasis. | [CORE] |
| COMPARATIVE CONSTRUCTIONS | יותר נכון | yoter nakhon | more correct | Standard analytic comparative. Used constantly in all contexts. Only comparative strategy available in Hebrew (no synthetic forms). | [CORE] |
| COMPARATIVE CONSTRUCTIONS | הכי מדויק | hakhi meduyak | most precise | Standard analytic superlative. Extremely common. הכי precedes adjective in fixed order. No restrictions. | [CORE] |
| PREPOSITIONAL PHRASE ADVERBIALS | באמת | be'emet | in truth → truly, really | Extremely high frequency in spoken Hebrew. Functions as adverb and discourse marker. Used for emphasis, confirmation, or surprise. | [CORE] |
| PREPOSITIONAL PHRASE ADVERBIALS | בצדק | betsedek | in justice → justly, rightly | Common in formal and evaluative contexts. Used to express approval or correctness of action. Slightly more formal than באמת. | [CORE] |
| DOMAIN-SPECIFIC COMPOUNDS | אמת מדעית | emet mada'it | truth scientific → scientific truth | Productive pattern for creating technical/specialized terms. Common in academic and formal discourse. Adjective must agree in gender/number. | [CORE] |
| DOMAIN-SPECIFIC COMPOUNDS | צדק חברתי | tsedek ḥevrati | justice social → social justice | Very common political/social term. This specific collocation is fixed and frequent. Pattern productive for other domain specifications. | [CORE] |
| CONSTRUCT STATE COMPOUNDS | אמת מידה | amat mida | truth-of measure → criterion, standard | Fixed lexicalized compound. Very common in evaluative contexts. First noun takes construct form (אמת → אמת, no change here). | [CORE] |
| CONSTRUCT STATE COMPOUNDS | עד שקר | ed sheker | witness-of falsehood → false witness | Fixed legal term, very common in legal and biblical contexts. Construct state creates tight compound. Formal register. | [CORE] |
| DISCOURSE MARKERS AND TAG QUESTIONS | נכון? | nakhon? | correct? → right?, isn't it? | Extremely frequent tag question in spoken Hebrew. Used to seek confirmation or agreement. Colloquial register, very high frequency. | [CORE] |
| DISCOURSE MARKERS AND TAG QUESTIONS | האמת היא ש... | ha'emet hi she... | the truth is that... | Common discourse frame for introducing honest/frank statements. Used across registers. Functions to mark speaker stance. | [CORE] |
| NEGATION OF POSSIBILITY/ABILITY | אי אפשר להאמין | i efshar leha'amin | impossible to believe → unbelievable | Very common modal construction. Used to express incredulity or impossibility. Frequent in both spoken and written Hebrew. | [CORE] |
| NEGATION OF POSSIBILITY/ABILITY | קשה להאמין | kashe leha'amin | difficult to believe → hard to believe | Common epistemic construction. Slightly weaker than אי אפשר. Used frequently in narrative and evaluative contexts. | [CORE] |
| EXISTENTIAL CONSTRUCTIONS | יש בזה אמת | yesh baze emet | there-is in-this truth → there's truth in this | Common concessive construction. Used to acknowledge partial truth in claims. Frequent in argumentative discourse. | [CORE] |
| EXISTENTIAL CONSTRUCTIONS | אין זה שקר | eyn ze sheker | there-is-not this lie → this is not a lie | Common denial pattern. אין functions as existential negator. Used across registers for emphatic negation. | [CORE] |
| BINYAN-BASED VOICE ALTERNATIONS | לזייף → מזויף | lezayef → mezuyaf | to forge → forged/fake | Productive morphological pattern (pi'el → pu'al). Passive participle functions as adjective. Common in commercial and legal contexts. | [CORE] |
| BINYAN-BASED VOICE ALTERNATIONS | להצדיק → מוצדק | lehatsдik → mutsдak | to justify → justified | Productive pattern (hif'il → huf'al). Very common in argumentative and evaluative discourse. Participle fully adjectival. | [CORE] |
| COORDINATE COMPOUNDS | אמת או שקר | emet o sheker | truth or falsehood | Common binary opposition. Used in philosophical, legal, and everyday contexts. או marks exclusive or inclusive disjunction. | [CORE] |
| COORDINATE COMPOUNDS | צדק וחסד | tsedek vaḥesed | justice and mercy | Fixed collocation in religious/ethical discourse. Common in biblical and liturgical contexts. ו- "and" creates value pairing. | [CORE] |
| EMPHATIC REDUPLICATION | אמת לאמיתה | emet la'amita | truth to-its-truth → absolute truth | Limited productivity; mainly with אמת. Colloquial and emphatic. Less common than other intensification strategies. Somewhat idiomatic. | [MARGINAL] |
| METAPHORICAL EXTENSIONS | האמת תצא לאור | ha'emet tetse la'or | the truth will-exit to-light → the truth will come to light | Common metaphorical construction. Truth conceptualized as hidden object that emerges. Frequent in narrative and investigative contexts. | [CORE] |
| METAPHORICAL EXTENSIONS | להסתיר את האמת | lehasתir et ha'emet | to hide the truth | Very common metaphorical construction. Truth as concealable object. Used across all registers. Productive with various verbs of concealment/revelation. | [CORE] |
