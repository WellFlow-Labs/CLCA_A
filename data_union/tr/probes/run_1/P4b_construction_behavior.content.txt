# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P4b Construction Usage & Constraints
# Language: Turkish (tr)

---

## Construction Usage Table

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. ATTRIBUTION** | Bu iddia doğru. | *Bu iddia doğru.* | "This claim is correct." | [CORE] | Ubiquitous across all registers; the zero-copula 3rd-person present is the unmarked default. Tense and evidential variants (*doğruydu*, *doğruymuş*) are fully productive and common in journalistic and spoken registers. |
| **1. ATTRIBUTION** | Belge sahte. | *Belge sahte.* | "The document is fake." | [CORE] | Same pattern; no restrictions on which truth-domain adjective fills the predicate slot. |
| **2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION** | Bu bilgi doğru değil. | *Bu bilgi doğru değil.* | "This information is not correct." | [CORE] | The *değil* negation strategy for adjectival predicates is the standard and obligatory form; no alternate negation strategy exists for this construction type. |
| **2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION** | İddia asılsız değil. | *İddia asılsız değil.* | "The allegation is not unfounded." | [CORE] | Double negation (*asılsız değil* = "not unfounded") is grammatical and used for rhetorical attenuation; pragmatically marked but structurally regular. |
| **3. ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION** | Güvenilir kaynak | *güvenilir kaynak* | "reliable source" | [CORE] | The dominant and unmarked adnominal pattern; no agreement morphology required. Freely productive across all P1 adjectives and all noun types. |
| **3. ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION** | Asılsız iddia | *asılsız iddia* | "unfounded allegation" | [CORE] | Same pattern; collocational preferences exist (e.g., *asılsız iddia* is a near-fixed pairing in legal and journalistic registers) but the construction itself is fully general. |
| **4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE** | Meşrulaşmak | *meşrulaşmak* | "to become legitimate" | [CORE] | The morphological *-laş-* strategy is core for lexically licensed items (*meşrulaşmak*, *yasalaşmak*); not all P1 bases accept it productively. |
| **4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE** | Geçersiz hale gelmek | *geçersiz hale gelmek* | "to become invalid / to come to be void" | [CORE] | The periphrastic *hale gelmek* strategy is core and universally available for all adjectives; it is the default where *-laş-* is unavailable or marginal. |
| **5. CAUSATIVE** | Meşrulaştırmak | *meşrulaştırmak* | "to legitimize, to render legitimate" | [CORE] | The morphological *-laştır-* strategy is core for lexically licensed bases; common in political, legal, and academic discourse. |
| **5. CAUSATIVE** | Geçersiz kılmak | *geçersiz kılmak* | "to invalidate, to render void" | [CORE] | The periphrastic *kılmak* strategy is core and available for all adjectives; *kılmak* carries a formal or written register preference over colloquial alternatives. |
| **6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION** | Doğruluk | *doğruluk* | "truthfulness, correctness, accuracy" | [CORE] | The *-lık* suffix (with vowel harmony variants) is the primary and productive nominalizer across all P1 items; attested for the full set listed in P4a notes. |
| **6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION** | Güvenilirlik | *güvenilirlik* | "reliability, trustworthiness" | [CORE] | Layered forms (*güvenilir* + *-lik*) are fully productive; the Arabic-derived *-iyet* alternative (*meşruiyet*, *hakkaniyet*) is restricted to items of Arabic origin and carries a formal or legalistic register preference. |
| **7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION** | Yalancı | *yalancı* | "liar" | [CORE] | The *-cı* agentive on noun bases is core for habitual role agents; *yalancı* is a fully lexicalized common word with no register restriction. |
| **7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION** | Yanıltıcı | *yanıltıcı* | "misleader, misleading agent" | [CORE] | The *-ıcı* participial agentive is core but functions more commonly as an adjective than as a pure agent noun; adjectival use (*yanıltıcı bilgi*, "misleading information") is more frequent than nominal use. |
| **8. PROCESS NOMINALIZATION** | Doğrulama | *doğrulama* | "verification, confirmation" | [CORE] | Fully productive across the domain; frequent in legal, journalistic, and administrative registers as an action nominal. Distinct in meaning from the *-lık* abstract property nominal. |
| **8. PROCESS NOMINALIZATION** | Yalanlama | *yalanlama* | "denial, refutation" | [CORE] | Same pattern; *yalanlama* is a standard term in press and legal contexts. The *-ma/-me* verbal noun series is among the most productive nominalizing strategies in Turkish. |
| **9. ABILITY / POTENTIAL NOMINALIZATION** | Doğrulanabilirlik | *doğrulanabilirlik* | "verifiability" | [MARGINAL] | Restricted to philosophical, scientific, and academic registers; not used in everyday speech. The layered morphological complexity (*passive + potential + aorist participial + nominalizer*) marks these as technical terms rather than general vocabulary. |
| **9. ABILITY / POTENTIAL NOMINALIZATION** | Yanlışlanabilirlik | *yanlışlanabilirlik* | "falsifiability" | [MARGINAL] | Same register restriction as *doğrulanabilirlik*; the pair directly encodes a philosophical distinction (logical positivism / Popperian falsificationism) and is confined to academic discourse on epistemology and philosophy of science. |
| **10. SINCERITY / CORRECTION DISCOURSE MARKER** | Doğrusu, bilmiyorum. | *Doğrusu, bilmiyorum.* | "Honestly, I don't know." | [CORE] | Fully grammaticalized as a sentence-initial sincerity hedge; common across spoken and written registers. Functions as a discourse marker rather than a propositional predicate; the possessive form *doğrusu* is no longer compositionally interpreted in this use. |
| **10. SINCERITY / CORRECTION DISCOURSE MARKER** | Aslında öyle değil. | *Aslında öyle değil.* | "Actually, it's not like that." | [CORE] | *Aslında* is among the most frequent discourse markers in Turkish; used as a corrective or contrastive frame across all registers. Fully grammaticalized; the locative etymology (*asl-ında*, "in its origin") is not synchronically active. |
| **11. APPROXIMATION CONSTRUCTION** | Doğruya en yakın cevap | *doğruya en yakın cevap* | "the answer closest to correct" | [CORE] | Productive and compositional; the spatial metaphor (*yakın/uzak* + dative truth noun) is natural and unrestricted. Used in evaluative and epistemic contexts across registers. |
| **11. APPROXIMATION CONSTRUCTION** | Gerçekten uzak bir senaryo | *gerçekten uzak bir senaryo* | "a scenario far from reality" | [CORE] | Same spatial metaphor pattern; *gerçekten uzak* is a common collocation in journalistic and critical discourse. Note: *gerçekten* also functions as an adverb meaning "truly/really," creating a potential surface ambiguity that context resolves. |
| **12. CONTRASTIVE DISTINCTION CONSTRUCTION** | Yasal ama meşru değil. | *Yasal ama meşru değil.* | "Legal but not legitimate." | [CORE] | The *ama* concessive is the most frequent connector in this pattern; natural and common in legal, political, and philosophical discourse wherever paired concepts require explicit differentiation. |
| **12. CONTRASTIVE DISTINCTION CONSTRUCTION** | Yanıltıcı olmakla birlikte yanlış olmamak. | *Yanıltıcı olmakla birlikte yanlış olmamak.* | "To be misleading without being false." | [MARGINAL] | The *olmakla birlikte* concessive is more formal and written than *ama*; this specific infinitival framing (*olmamak* as the second predicate) is characteristic of analytical or philosophical prose rather than conversational or journalistic usage. |

## Alternate Constructions Attested in P4a

| Construction Type | Alternate Form | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE** | *-laş-* morphological vs. *hale gelmek* periphrastic | Both attested; *hale gelmek* is the universally available default; *-laş-* is lexically restricted. |
| **5. CAUSATIVE** | *-laştır-* morphological vs. *kılmak* periphrastic | Both attested; *kılmak* is universally available; *-laştır-* is lexically restricted. *Etmek* is listed in P4a as a further periphrastic option but without a distinct example. |
| **6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION** | *-lık* suffix vs. Arabic *-iyet* suffix | Both attested; *-iyet* is restricted to Arabic-origin bases (*meşruiyet*, *hakkaniyet*). |
| **7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION** | *-cı* (noun-based habitual agent) vs. *-ıcı* (verb-based participial agent) | Both attested; distinct bases and distinct primary functions (nominal vs. adjectival). |
| **10. SINCERITY / CORRECTION DISCOURSE MARKER** | *doğrusu*, *aslında*, *gerçek şu ki*, *hakikaten* | All attested in P4a as members of the same functional class; vary in degree of grammaticalization and register. |
