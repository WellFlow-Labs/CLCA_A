## 2. Substitution Patterns (Narrative)

### 2.1 CAUSATION (factual) → Resultative Compounds

The most significant substitution pattern in Chinese truth-domain constructions involves **CAUSATION (factual)**. While the language possesses a grammatically well-formed periphrastic causative construction (Construction 5: 使/让 + Patient + 变得 + Predicate), this pattern is rated [MARGINAL] and described as "less idiomatic than resultative verb compounds." 

The P4b notes explicitly state: "Colloquial speech strongly prefers compound nouns (骗子) or descriptive phrases (说谎的人)" and "resultative compounds dominate in actual usage." The resultative complement construction (Construction 8) systematically substitutes for periphrastic causation, with compounds like:
- 证实 (zhèng shí) "prove true" (证 "prove" + 实 "actual")
- 查实 (chá shí) "verify as true" 
- 核实 (hé shí) "confirm as true"

These resultative compounds encode both the action and its achieved result in a single lexical unit, making them functionally causative but morphologically distinct from the periphrastic pattern.

### 2.2 REVELATION and BECOME: Distinct but Related

Unlike some languages where revelation might substitute for becoming, Chinese maintains both operators as distinct:
- **BECOME** (Construction 4) uses change-of-state verbs 变/成为 and is [CORE]
- **REVELATION** (Construction 15) uses evidential verbs like 查明 "investigate and clarify" and 揭露 "reveal, expose"

These do not substitute for each other. BECOME describes ontological change in truth-value; REVELATION describes epistemic discovery or disclosure. The language keeps these conceptually separate.

### 2.3 VERIFICATION and DISCONFIRMATION: Shared Construction Base

Constructions 15, 16, and 18 show that VERIFICATION and DISCONFIRMATION share the same constructional patterns but with opposite polarity:
- **Positive verification**: 符合事实 (fú hé shì shí) "conform to facts"
- **Disconfirmation**: 不符合事实 (bù fú hé shì shí) "not conform to facts"

The negation is analytic (using 不), not a separate construction. Similarly:
- **Positive**: 有根据 (yǒu gēn jù) "have basis"
- **Negative**: 没有根据 (méi yǒu gēn jù) "without basis"

### 2.4 Register-Based Substitution: Agentive Nominalization

Construction 7 (agentive nominalization with -者) shows register-based substitution. The formal suffix -者 is [MARGINAL] in actual usage, with colloquial speech substituting:
- Compound nouns: 骗子 (piàn zi) "liar" instead of 欺骗者 (qī piàn zhě)
- Descriptive phrases: 说谎的人 (shuō huǎng de rén) "person who lies"

This represents a functional gap in colloquial registers where the morphological nominalization is avoided in favor of periphrastic alternatives.

### 2.5 Asymmetries: No Truth/Falsity Asymmetry Detected

The constructions show no systematic asymmetry between truth and falsity. Both positive and negative truth-domain predicates participate equally in all constructions:
- Attribution: 是真的 "is true" / 是假的 "is false"
- Become: 变成真的 "become true" / 变成假的 "become false"
- Adnominal: 真实的情况 "true situation" / 虚假的信息 "false information"

## 3. Missingness Summary

### 3.1 Structural Gaps

**CAUSATION (character)**: No dedicated construction exists for causing someone to become truthful/honest as a character trait. While Construction 5 (periphrastic causative) is grammatically available, there is no evidence in P4a or P4b of its natural use with character predicates in the truth domain. This would require circumlocution such as:
- *使某人变得诚实 (shǐ mǒu rén biàn de chéng shí) "make someone become honest"

However, this pattern is not attested in the data and would likely be expressed through other means (e.g., education/influence verbs + character development).

### 3.2 Lexical Gaps

No pure lexical gaps are identified. The language has rich vocabulary across all truth-domain concepts (真/假, 实/虚, 诚实/欺骗, etc.).

### 3.3 Conceptual Gaps

**CAUSATION (character)** may represent a conceptual gap rather than merely a structural one. The absence of natural expressions for "making someone truthful" suggests this may not be a readily encoded concept in Chinese truth-domain grammar. Character change is more typically expressed through:
- Self-cultivation: 变得诚实 "become honest" (without external causation)
- Educational influence: 教育 "educate", 培养 "cultivate" (not specific to truth-domain)

### 3.4 Asymmetric Gaps

**Register asymmetry in agentive nominalization**: The -者 suffix (Construction 7) is [MARGINAL] in colloquial speech but [CORE] in formal/literary registers. This creates a functional gap where colloquial speakers must use alternative strategies (compound nouns, descriptive phrases) to express the same meaning.

**No positive/negative asymmetry**: Unlike some languages that might have truth-operators but lack falsity-operators (or vice versa), Chinese shows complete symmetry. All constructions work equally with positive truth-predicates (真, 实, 对) and negative ones (假, 虚, 错).

## 4. Canonicalized Operator Inventory

### 4.1 Fully Available

The following operators are fully supported with [CORE] constructions:

1. **ATTR** - Attribution via 是 copula (Construction 1)
2. **ADNOM** - Adnominal modification via 的 particle (Construction 3)
3. **BECOME** - Inchoative via 变/成为 (Construction 4)
4. **REVELATION** - Evidential constructions with verification verbs (Construction 15)
5. **VERIFICATION** - Multiple constructions (15, 16) with verification and conformity verbs
6. **DISCONFIRMATION** - Negation of verification/conformity constructions (16, 18)
7. **NEGATION** - Analytic negation via 不是/不 (Construction 2)
8. **NOMINALIZATION (abstract)** - Suffixal derivation with -性/-度 (Construction 6)

### 4.2 Available with Substitution

9. **CAUSATION (factual)** - Periphrastic causative [MARGINAL]; **strongly substituted by resultative verb compounds** (Construction 8), which are [CORE]. The language prefers lexicalized resultative compounds (证实, 核实, 查实) over productive periphrastic causatives.

### 4.3 Partially Available (Register-Restricted)

10. **NOMINALIZATION (agentive)** - Morphological derivation with -者 available in formal/literary registers [MARGINAL]; colloquial speech uses alternative strategies (compound nouns, descriptive phrases). Functionally available across all registers but through different means.

### 4.4 Missing Entirely

11. **CAUSATION (character)** - **MISSING (structural gap)**. No common expression exists for causing character change in the truth domain. Would require circumlocution, but no natural usage patterns are attested. This may represent a conceptual gap where character change is not readily encoded as externally caused in truth-domain grammar.
