| Correspondence between name/label and actual nature | These terms are used when the question is whether a person, institution, or thing actually lives up to the description, title, or reputation applied to it. | Correctness and precision relative to a standard | These terms evaluate whether something — an answer, a method, a measurement, a translation — conforms to a relevant standard or is free from error.
