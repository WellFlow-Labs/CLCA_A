# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2b Compositional Constructions
# Language: Korean (ko)

---

**Transliteration standard:** Revised Romanization of Korean (국립국어원 표준 로마자 표기법), applied consistently throughout.

---

**Scope note:** This document records attested multi-word constructions — periphrastic, idiomatic, and syntactically-driven — involving the 30 P1 base items. Derivational forms already documented in P2a are excluded. Fixed collocations already documented in P2a (e.g., 진위 여부, 실체를 밝히다) are cross-referenced rather than re-described in full. Absence of constructions is recorded explicitly. The organization follows the P1 item order.

---

## 1. 진실 (jinsil)

---

**1.1**
**Base Form:** 진실 (jinsil)
**Example:** 진실을 말하다
**Transliteration:** jinshireul malhada
**English Translation:** to tell the truth, to speak the truth
**Usage Notes:** The most basic and frequent verbal construction with 진실. Used across all registers in moral, legal, and everyday contexts. Appears in both affirmative and negative forms (진실을 말하지 않다, to not tell the truth).
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**1.2**
**Base Form:** 진실 (jinsil)
**Example:** 진실을 밝히다
**Transliteration:** jinshireul balkida
**English Translation:** to reveal the truth, to bring the truth to light
**Usage Notes:** A highly conventionalized collocation used in investigative, journalistic, and legal contexts. 밝히다 (to illuminate/reveal) is the standard verb pairing with 진실 in contexts of uncovering what was hidden.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**1.3**
**Base Form:** 진실 (jinsil)
**Example:** 진실을 숨기다
**Transliteration:** jinshireul sumgida
**English Translation:** to hide the truth, to conceal the truth
**Usage Notes:** Used in moral, legal, and journalistic contexts to describe the act of withholding or concealing what is actually the case. Frequently appears in opposition to 진실을 밝히다.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**1.4**
**Base Form:** 진실 (jinsil)
**Example:** 진실을 알다
**Transliteration:** jinshireul alda
**English Translation:** to know the truth
**Usage Notes:** Used across all registers to describe possession of knowledge of what is actually the case. Appears in constructions such as 진실을 알고 싶다 (to want to know the truth) and 진실을 알게 되다 (to come to know the truth).
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**1.5**
**Base Form:** 진실 (jinsil)
**Example:** 진실을 외면하다
**Transliteration:** jinshireul oemyeonhada
**English Translation:** to turn away from the truth, to ignore the truth
**Usage Notes:** Used in moral and journalistic contexts to characterize deliberate avoidance of what is known to be true. 외면하다 (to avert one's gaze/to ignore) is a conventionalized verb pairing in this construction.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**1.6**
**Base Form:** 진실 (jinsil)
**Example:** 진실과 거짓 사이
**Transliteration:** jinsil-gwa geojit sai
**English Translation:** between truth and falsehood
**Usage Notes:** A fixed framing construction used in titles, discourse, and analytical contexts to position something in the space between truth and lie. The pairing 진실과 거짓 is highly conventionalized as a polar opposition.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**1.7**
**Base Form:** 진실 (jinsil)
**Example:** 진실이 밝혀지다
**Transliteration:** jinsili balkyeojida
**English Translation:** the truth comes to light, the truth is revealed
**Usage Notes:** The passive/spontaneous counterpart to 진실을 밝히다. Used to describe the emergence of truth without necessarily specifying an agent. Common in journalistic and narrative contexts.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**1.8**
**Base Form:** 진실 (jinsil)
**Example:** 진실의 순간
**Transliteration:** jinsile sungan
**English Translation:** the moment of truth
**Usage Notes:** Used in both literal and figurative senses to describe a decisive moment when what is actually the case becomes apparent or must be confronted. Appears in everyday speech and media discourse.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**1.9**
**Base Form:** 진실 (jinsil)
**Example:** 진실은 하나다
**Transliteration:** jinshireun hanada
**English Translation:** there is only one truth, the truth is singular
**Usage Notes:** A formulaic assertion used in investigative, legal, and popular culture contexts (notably associated with the detective fiction genre in Korean popular culture) to assert that facts are determinate and singular. Functions as a rhetorical claim rather than a philosophical proposition in most uses.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**1.10**
**Base Form:** 진실 (jinsil)
**Example:** 진실을 왜곡하다
**Transliteration:** jinshireul waegok-hada
**English Translation:** to distort the truth
**Usage Notes:** Used in journalistic, political, and legal contexts to characterize deliberate misrepresentation of what is actually the case. The pairing of 진실 with 왜곡하다 is highly conventionalized.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

## 2. 사실 (sasil)

---

**2.1**
**Base Form:** 사실 (sasil)
**Example:** 사실을 확인하다
**Transliteration:** sasireul hwagin-hada
**English Translation:** to verify the facts, to confirm the facts
**Usage Notes:** Used in journalistic, legal, and administrative contexts to describe the process of checking whether a claim corresponds to what actually occurred. Closely related to the compound 사실확인 (documented in P2a) but here as a verbal construction.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**2.2**
**Base Form:** 사실 (sasil)
**Example:** 사실에 근거하다
**Transliteration:** sasie geungeo-hada
**English Translation:** to be based on facts, to be grounded in fact
**Usage Notes:** Used in academic, journalistic, and legal contexts to assert that a claim or argument has factual grounding. 근거하다 (to be based on/grounded in) is a standard verb pairing with 사실 in this construction.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**2.3**
**Base Form:** 사실 (sasil)
**Example:** 사실과 다르다
**Transliteration:** sasil-gwa dareuda
**English Translation:** to differ from the facts, to be contrary to the facts
**Usage Notes:** A standard construction used in legal, journalistic, and everyday contexts to characterize a claim as factually incorrect without necessarily implying deliberate deception. Appears in formal corrections and rebuttals.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**2.4**
**Base Form:** 사실 (sasil)
**Example:** 사실이 아니다
**Transliteration:** sasiri anida
**English Translation:** it is not a fact, that is not true
**Usage Notes:** The standard negation construction used to deny the factual status of a claim. Extremely common in everyday speech, legal proceedings, and public statements. More neutral in register than 거짓말이다 (that is a lie).
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**2.5**
**Base Form:** 사실 (sasil)
**Example:** 사실을 왜곡하다
**Transliteration:** sasireul waegok-hada
**English Translation:** to distort the facts
**Usage Notes:** Used in journalistic and political contexts to characterize deliberate misrepresentation of factual circumstances. Parallel to 진실을 왜곡하다 but with emphasis on specific factual claims rather than truth in general.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**2.6**
**Base Form:** 사실 (sasil)
**Example:** 사실로 밝혀지다
**Transliteration:** sasillo balkyeojida
**English Translation:** to turn out to be true, to be revealed as fact
**Usage Notes:** Used in journalistic and everyday contexts to describe the confirmation of a claim as factually accurate. The construction 사실로 + 밝혀지다/드러나다 is highly conventionalized.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**2.7**
**Base Form:** 사실 (sasil)
**Example:** 사실을 말하자면
**Transliteration:** sasireul malhajamyeon
**English Translation:** to tell the truth, if I'm being honest, the fact of the matter is
**Usage Notes:** A discourse-framing construction used to introduce a frank or corrective statement. Functions as a pragmatic marker signaling that what follows is the speaker's genuine assessment. Common in spoken and informal written registers.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**2.8**
**Base Form:** 사실 (sasil)
**Example:** 엄연한 사실
**Transliteration:** eomyeonhan sasil
**English Translation:** an undeniable fact, a plain fact, an incontrovertible fact
**Usage Notes:** A fixed nominal collocation using 엄연한 (eomyeonhan, undeniable/plain/manifest) as an intensifying modifier. Used to assert that something is unambiguously factual and cannot be disputed. Common in argumentative and rhetorical contexts.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**2.9**
**Base Form:** 사실 (sasil)
**Example:** 사실 여부를 따지다
**Transliteration:** sasil yeobureul ttajida
**English Translation:** to examine whether something is factual, to question the factual basis
**Usage Notes:** Used in legal, journalistic, and deliberative contexts to describe the act of scrutinizing whether a claim is actually true. 따지다 (to examine/question closely) is a standard verb pairing in this construction.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

## 3. 거짓 (geojit)

---

**3.1**
**Base Form:** 거짓 (geojit)
**Example:** 거짓으로 꾸미다
**Transliteration:** geojiseuro kkumida
**English Translation:** to fabricate, to dress up as false, to make up
**Usage Notes:** Used to describe the act of constructing a false account or appearance. 꾸미다 (to decorate/fabricate) in this construction carries the sense of deliberate construction of a false narrative.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**3.2**
**Base Form:** 거짓 (geojit)
**Example:** 거짓 없이
**Transliteration:** geojit eopsi
**English Translation:** without falsehood, honestly, truthfully
**Usage Notes:** A fixed adverbial construction meaning "without any deception." Used in moral and interpersonal contexts to assert complete honesty. Appears in 거짓 없이 말하다 (to speak without falsehood).
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**3.3**
**Base Form:** 거짓 (geojit)
**Example:** 거짓이 드러나다
**Transliteration:** geojisi deureonada
**English Translation:** the falsehood is exposed, the lie comes to light
**Usage Notes:** Used in journalistic and everyday contexts to describe the revelation of deception. 드러나다 (to be revealed/exposed) is a standard verb pairing with 거짓 in this construction.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**3.4**
**Base Form:** 거짓 (geojit)
**Example:** 거짓과 진실을 구별하다
**Transliteration:** geojit-gwa jinshireul gubyeol-hada
**English Translation:** to distinguish falsehood from truth
**Usage Notes:** Used in epistemological, educational, and journalistic contexts to describe the cognitive or investigative act of separating what is false from what is true. The pairing 거짓과 진실 is a highly conventionalized polar opposition.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

## 4. 거짓말 (geojitmal)

---

**4.1**
**Base Form:** 거짓말 (geojitmal)
**Example:** 거짓말을 하다
**Transliteration:** geojitmareul hada
**English Translation:** to tell a lie, to lie
**Usage Notes:** The standard periphrastic verbal construction for the act of lying, parallel to the compound verb 거짓말하다 (documented in P2a). The two forms are functionally equivalent; the periphrastic form with 을 is slightly more emphatic in some contexts.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**4.2**
**Base Form:** 거짓말 (geojitmal)
**Example:** 거짓말이 들키다
**Transliteration:** geojitmari deulkida
**English Translation:** to be caught in a lie, to have one's lie found out
**Usage Notes:** Used in everyday moral and interpersonal contexts to describe the discovery of a deception. 들키다 (to be caught/found out) is the standard verb pairing for this construction.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**4.3**
**Base Form:** 거짓말 (geojitmal)
**Example:** 거짓말도 방편이다
**Transliteration:** geojitmalto bangpyeonida
**English Translation:** even a lie can be a means (to an end); a white lie is sometimes justified
**Usage Notes:** A proverbial expression used to acknowledge that deception may sometimes be pragmatically or morally justified. Functions as a folk-ethical claim rather than a universal endorsement of lying. Common in everyday moral discourse.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**4.4**
**Base Form:** 거짓말 (geojitmal)
**Example:** 거짓말에 거짓말을 보태다
**Transliteration:** geojitmare geojitmareul botaeda
**English Translation:** to add lie upon lie, to compound a lie with another lie
**Usage Notes:** Used to characterize a pattern of escalating deception in which one lie necessitates further lies. 보태다 (to add/supplement) in this construction carries a pejorative sense of accumulation.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**4.5**
**Base Form:** 거짓말 (geojitmal)
**Example:** 거짓말로 일관하다
**Transliteration:** geojitmallo ilgwan-hada
**English Translation:** to maintain a consistent lie throughout, to stick to one's false story
**Usage Notes:** Used in legal and journalistic contexts to describe a person who persists in a false account despite evidence or questioning. 일관하다 (to be consistent/to maintain throughout) is a standard verb pairing in this construction.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**4.6**
**Base Form:** 거짓말 (geojitmal)
**Example:** 거짓말인 줄 알면서도
**Transliteration:** geojitmalin jul almyeonseodo
**English Translation:** even knowing it is a lie, even while knowing it to be false
**Usage Notes:** A fixed concessive construction used to describe situations where someone accepts or repeats a known falsehood. The construction 인 줄 알면서도 (even knowing that it is) is a productive syntactic pattern; its application to 거짓말 is highly conventionalized.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

## 5. 진짜 (jinjja)

---

**5.1**
**Base Form:** 진짜 (jinjja)
**Example:** 진짜인지 가짜인지 모르다
**Transliteration:** jinjjainji gajjainji moreuda
**English Translation:** not to know whether it is real or fake
**Usage Notes:** A fixed alternative-question construction used to express uncertainty about the authenticity of something. The pairing 진짜인지 가짜인지 is highly conventionalized as a framing for authenticity doubt.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**5.2**
**Base Form:** 진짜 (jinjja)
**Example:** 진짜 같다
**Transliteration:** jinjja gatda
**English Translation:** it seems real, it looks genuine, it appears authentic
**Usage Notes:** Used to express that something has the appearance of being genuine without asserting that it actually is. 같다 (to seem/appear like) is a standard verb pairing for this epistemic hedging construction.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**5.3**
**Base Form:** 진짜 (jinjja)
**Example:** 진짜로 받아들이다
**Transliteration:** jinjjaro badadeulida
**English Translation:** to accept as real, to take as genuine
**Usage Notes:** Used to describe the cognitive act of treating something as authentic. Appears in contexts where the speaker notes that something false was mistakenly accepted as genuine.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**5.4**
**Base Form:** 진짜 (jinjja)
**Example:** 이게 진짜야?
**Transliteration:** ige jinjjaya?
**English Translation:** Is this real? Is this for real?
**Usage Notes:** A highly frequent colloquial interrogative construction used to express surprise or disbelief and to question the authenticity or truth of something. Functions both as a genuine question and as an exclamation of surprise. Extremely common in spoken Korean.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

## 6. 가짜 (gajja)

---

**6.1**
**Base Form:** 가짜 (gajja)
**Example:** 가짜로 판명되다
**Transliteration:** gajjaro panmyeongdoeda
**English Translation:** to be determined to be fake, to be found to be counterfeit
**Usage Notes:** Used in legal, journalistic, and consumer contexts to describe the official or investigative determination that something is not genuine. 판명되다 (to be determined/established) is a standard verb pairing in this construction.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**6.2**
**Base Form:** 가짜 (gajja)
**Example:** 가짜를 진짜로 속이다
**Transliteration:** gajjareul jinjjaro sogida
**English Translation:** to deceive someone into thinking a fake is real, to pass off a fake as genuine
**Usage Notes:** Used in legal and consumer contexts to describe the act of fraudulent misrepresentation of an inauthentic item as genuine. The construction 가짜를 진짜로 속이다 is highly conventionalized in fraud and counterfeiting discourse.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**6.3**
**Base Form:** 가짜 (gajja)
**Example:** 가짜인 줄 몰랐다
**Transliteration:** gajjain jul mollassda
**English Translation:** I didn't know it was fake, I was unaware it was counterfeit
**Usage Notes:** Used in legal and everyday contexts to assert ignorance of inauthenticity, often as a defense or explanation. The construction 인 줄 몰랐다 (didn't know that it was) is a productive syntactic pattern applied here to 가짜.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**6.4**
**Base Form:** 가짜 (gajja)
**Example:** 가짜 뉴스를 퍼뜨리다
**Transliteration:** gajja nyuseureul peotteurida
**English Translation:** to spread fake news
**Usage Notes:** Used in journalistic and political contexts to describe the dissemination of deliberately false or misleading news content. 퍼뜨리다 (to spread/disseminate) is the standard verb pairing in this construction.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

## 7. 진정 (jinjeong)

---

**7.1**
**Base Form:** 진정 (jinjeong)
**Example:** 진정으로 사과하다
**Transliteration:** jinjeong-euro sagwa-hada
**English Translation:** to apologize sincerely, to offer a genuine apology
**Usage Notes:** Used in interpersonal and public contexts to assert that an apology reflects genuine remorse rather than performative compliance. The adverbial 진정으로 is the standard intensifier for sincerity in apology contexts.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**7.2**
**Base Form:** 진정 (jinjeong)
**Example:** 진정한 의미에서
**Transliteration:** jinjeonghan uimieseo
**English Translation:** in the true sense of the word, in the genuine meaning
**Usage Notes:** A fixed framing construction used to assert that something qualifies as a genuine instance of a category, as opposed to a nominal or superficial one. Extremely common in analytical and rhetorical discourse.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**7.3**
**Base Form:** 진정 (jinjeong)
**Example:** 진정성이 느껴지다
**Transliteration:** jinjeongseong-i neukkkyeojida
**English Translation:** sincerity is felt, one can sense the genuineness
**Usage Notes:** Used in evaluative contexts to describe the perception of authentic feeling or intention in another person's expression or conduct. 느껴지다 (to be felt/sensed) is the standard verb pairing for this experiential construction.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**7.4**
**Base Form:** 진정 (jinjeong)
**Example:** 진정성이 없다
**Transliteration:** jinjeongseong-i eopda
**English Translation:** to lack sincerity, to be inauthentic
**Usage Notes:** The standard negation construction for 진정성, using the existential predicate 없다 (to not exist/to lack). Extremely common in public and interpersonal evaluation of conduct and expression. Cross-referenced from P2a note on 진정성.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**7.5**
**Base Form:** 진정 (jinjeong)
**Example:** 진정성에 의문을 제기하다
**Transliteration:** jinjeongseong-e uimun-eul jegi-hada
**English Translation:** to raise doubts about sincerity, to question the authenticity of
**Usage Notes:** Used in public discourse, journalism, and criticism to formally challenge whether a person's expressed feelings or commitments are genuine. 의문을 제기하다 (to raise a question/doubt) is a standard verb construction in evaluative discourse.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

## 9. 정확 (jeonghwak)

---

**9.1**
**Base Form:** 정확 (jeonghwak)
**Example:** 정확히 말하자면
**Transliteration:** jeonghwaki malhajamyeon
**English Translation:** to be precise, to put it exactly, strictly speaking
**Usage Notes:** A fixed discourse-framing construction used to introduce a more precise or corrective formulation. Functions as a pragmatic marker signaling increased precision. Common across registers.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**9.2**
**Base Form:** 정확 (jeonghwak)
**Example:** 정확한 정보를 제공하다
**Transliteration:** jeonghwakan jeongboreul jegong-hada
**English Translation:** to provide accurate information
**Usage Notes:** A standard construction in journalistic, public health, and administrative contexts emphasizing the obligation to supply factually correct information. The collocation 정확한 정보 is itself highly conventionalized (cross-referenced from P1b).
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**9.3**
**Base Form:** 정확 (jeonghwak)
**Example:** 정확하게 짚어내다
**Transliteration:** jeonghwakage jjipeoneda
**English Translation:** to pinpoint accurately, to identify precisely
**Usage Notes:** Used in analytical and evaluative contexts to describe the act of identifying something with precision. 짚어내다 (to pinpoint/identify) is a standard verb pairing with 정확하게 in this construction.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**9.4**
**Base Form:** 정확 (jeonghwak)
**Example:** 정확도가 높다 / 정확도가 낮다
**Transliteration:** jeonghwakdoga nopda / jeonghwakdoga natda
**English Translation:** accuracy is high / accuracy is low
**Usage Notes:** Standard evaluative constructions used in scientific, technical, and journalistic contexts to assess the degree of accuracy of a measurement, prediction, or report. The pairing of 정확도 with 높다/낮다 is highly conventionalized.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

## 10. 올바르다 (olbareuda)

---

**10.2**

**Transliteration:** olbareun pandaneul naerida

**Usage Notes:** Used in deliberative, legal, and everyday contexts to describe the act of arriving at a normatively or factually correct conclusion. 판단을 내리다 (to render a judgment) is a standard verbal construction.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**10.3**
**Base Form:** 올바르다 (olbareuda)
**Example:** 올바른 방향으로 나아가다
**Transliteration:** olbareun banghyang-euro naagada
**English Translation:** to move in the right direction
**Usage Notes:** Used in policy, organizational, and moral discourse to characterize progress as heading toward a correct or desirable outcome. A common construction in public and institutional rhetoric.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

## 11. 옳다 (olta)

---

**11.1**
**Base Form:** 옳다 (olta)
**Example:** 옳고 그름을 따지다
**Transliteration:** olko geureumreul ttajida
**English Translation:** to examine right and wrong, to distinguish right from wrong
**Usage Notes:** A fixed construction using the coordinated pair 옳고 그름 (right and wrong) as a compound object. Used in moral, philosophical, and educational contexts to describe the act of moral evaluation. The pairing 옳고 그름 is highly conventionalized.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**11.2**
**Base Form:** 옳다 (olta)
**Example:** 옳고 그름을 가리다
**Transliteration:** olko geureumreul garida
**English Translation:** to determine right from wrong, to adjudicate what is right
**Usage Notes:** Parallel to 옳고 그름을 따지다 but with 가리다 (to distinguish/determine), which carries a slightly more decisive or adjudicative sense. Used in moral and legal discourse.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**11.3**
**Base Form:** 옳다 (olta)
**Example:** 네 말이 옳다
**Transliteration:** ne mari olta

**Example:** 주관적으로 판단하다
**Transliteration:** jugwanjeogeuro pandanhada
**English Translation:** to judge subjectively, to evaluate from a personal perspective
**Usage Notes:** Used in analytical and critical contexts to characterize a judgment as based on personal perspective rather than independent fact. Appears both neutrally (in aesthetic contexts) and critically (in epistemic contexts).
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**27.2**
**Base Form:** 주관적 (jugwanjeok)
**Example:** 지극히 주관적인 의견
**Transliteration:** jigeuki jugwanjeok-in uigyeon
**English Translation:** a highly subjective opinion, a purely personal view
**Usage Notes:** A fixed nominal construction using 지극히 (extremely/purely) as an intensifier. Used to characterize a view as entirely personal and not claiming objective validity. Common in online discourse and informal writing as a disclaimer.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**27.3**
**Base Form:** 주관적 (jugwanjeok)
**Example:** 주관적 해석을 배제하다
**Transliteration:** jugwanjeok haeseogeul baeje-hada
**English Translation:** to exclude subjective interpretation, to set aside personal readings
**Usage Notes:** Used in academic and legal contexts to describe the methodological exclusion of personal perspective in favor of objective analysis. 배제하다 (to exclude/set aside) is a standard verb pairing in this construction.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

## 28. 모조 (mojo)

---

**28.1**
**Base Form:** 모조 (mojo)
**Example:** 모조품으로 판매하다
**Transliteration:** mojopumeuro panmaehada
**English Translation:** to sell as an imitation, to market as a replica
**Usage Notes:** Used in consumer and legal contexts to describe the sale of imitation goods. The construction distinguishes cases where the imitation status is disclosed (모조품으로 판매하다) from cases of fraudulent misrepresentation.
**Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**28.2**
**Base Form:** 모조 (mojo)
**Example:** 진품과 모조품을 구별하다
**Transliteration:** jinpum-gwa mojopumeul gubyeol-hada
**English Translation:** to distinguish genuine articles from imitations
**Usage Notes:** Used in consumer, legal, and art contexts to describe the act of differentiating authentic items from replicas. The pairing 진품과 모조품 is a conventionalized polar opposition in authentication discourse.
