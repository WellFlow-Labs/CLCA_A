## 2. Substitution Patterns (Narrative)

### 2.1 REVELATION vs. BECOME

No REVELATION construction is identified in P4a or P4b. The data contain no construction expressing the pattern "X turned out to be true" or "it was revealed/discovered that X" as a distinct grammatical operator. The BECOME operator is fully attested through two strategies (*-leş-* morphological and *olmak* periphrastic), but these express a change-of-state reading, not a discovery or revelation reading. No substitution of REVELATION by BECOME is documented in the data; the REVELATION slot is simply absent from the attested inventory.

### 2.2 VERIFICATION and DISCONFIRMATION sharing a construction

Both VERIFICATION and DISCONFIRMATION are expressed through the same factitive verbalization construction: [truth-domain noun or adjective] + *-la-/-le-* + *-mak*. The examples *doğrulamak* (to verify) and *yanlışlamak* (to falsify/disconfirm) are morphologically parallel and syntactically identical. The construction is therefore shared. The asymmetry between them is not constructional but lexical and register-based: *doğrulamak*, *kanıtlamak*, and *ispatlamak* are in common use across registers, while *yanlışlamak* in the Popperian sense is restricted to academic and philosophical registers and is rated [MARGINAL] in P4b. The construction itself is the same; the restriction is on the specific lexical item *yanlışlamak*, not on the construction type.

### 2.3 NEGATION: analytic vs. morphological

NEGATION in the predicate slot is exclusively analytic: the invariant particle *değil* follows the predicate adjective. No morphological negation construction operates in the predicate slot. Morphological privative forms (*asılsız*, *geçersiz*, *kanıtsız*) exist but are documented in P4a and P4b as lexicalized items that occupy the ATTR and ADNOM slots directly, not as a productive negation construction alternating with *değil*. The negation operator is therefore fully analytic in its constructional behavior.

### 2.4 Asymmetry in agentive nominalization

A consistent asymmetry is documented in P4a and P4b: agentive nouns are lexicalized on the deceptive or negative side of the truth domain (*sahteci*, *taklitçi*, *sahtekâr*), while the truth-telling or verifying side has no lexicalized agentive noun in common use. The concept "one who tells the truth" is expressed through participial relative clauses (*doğruyu söyleyen biri*) or through character-evaluation adjectives (*dürüst biri*). This asymmetry is explicitly noted in P4a as a documented feature of the domain. The agentive nominalization construction itself is available; the gap is in the lexicalization of a specific semantic role on the positive side.

### 2.5 CAUSATION (factual): three overlapping strategies

Three strategies cover the CAUSATION (factual) slot, and they are not fully interchangeable. The morphological *-leş-tir-* form is available only where *-leş-* is already conventionalized on the base. The periphrastic *kılmak* form covers formal registers where morphological causative is unavailable or stylistically inappropriate. The factitive *-la-* verbalization covers cases where the base is a noun rather than an adjective. P4a notes these as related but distinct patterns; P4b rates the *kılmak* construction as [MARGINAL] due to its restriction to formal and literary registers. No single construction covers the full CAUSATION (factual) range.

### 2.6 CAUSATION (character): absent

No construction expressing "X makes someone honest/truthful" or "X causes a person to acquire a truth-related character property" is identified in P4a or P4b. The CAUSATION (factual) constructions documented apply to propositions, documents, actions, and states, not to the induction of character traits. This is a structural gap, not a lexical one: no construction type for this operator is described.

### 2.7 Positive vs. negative form asymmetries

Beyond the agentive asymmetry noted above, the data show that privative adjectives (*asılsız*, *geçersiz*, *kanıtsız*) are lexicalized items occupying ATTR and ADNOM slots, while their positive counterparts (*asıllı*, *geçerli*, *kanıtlı*) may or may not be equally frequent or lexicalized. P4a does not document this as a systematic asymmetry, so no further claim is made here. The correspondence postpositional construction (Construction 9) shows a symmetric pair: *gerçeğe aykırı* (contrary to fact) and *gerçeğe uygun* (conforming to fact) are both attested as fixed legal phrases.

## 3. Missingness Summary

### 3.1 Structural Gaps

- **REVELATION**: No construction expressing "X turned out to be true / it was revealed that X" is identified in P4a or P4b. The slot is absent from the attested inventory. **MISSING (structural gap).**
- **CAUSATION (character)**: No construction expressing "X causes a person to become honest/truthful" or "X instills a truth-related character property" is identified in P4a or P4b. **MISSING (structural gap).**

### 3.2 Lexical Gaps

- **DISCONFIRMATION in everyday register**: The construction for DISCONFIRMATION (*yanlışlamak*) is grammatical and morphologically regular, but the specific lexical item is restricted to academic and philosophical registers. In everyday speech, disconfirmation of a claim would require circumlocution or a different expression not documented in P4a. This is a lexical gap in the everyday register, not a structural gap in the construction.
- **Agentive noun for truth-teller/verifier**: No lexicalized agentive noun meaning "one who tells the truth" or "verifier" as a personal title is in common use. The construction type (agentive *-ci*) is available; the specific lexical item is absent. This is a lexical gap, not a morphosyntactic one.

### 3.3 Conceptual Gaps

- No conceptual gaps are identifiable from the P4a and P4b data alone. All documented absences can be described as structural or lexical gaps without invoking conceptual unavailability.

### 3.4 Asymmetric Gaps

- **Agentive nominalization**: Deceptive-side agentive nouns are lexicalized (*sahteci*, *taklitçi*, *sahtekâr*); truth-telling-side agentive nouns are not lexicalized in common use. The positive agentive slot is expressed periphrastically. **Asymmetric gap: positive (truth-telling) agentive form absent as a lexicalized item.**
- **CAUSATION (factual) register restriction**: The *kılmak* periphrastic causative is available only in formal registers; the morphological *-leş-tir-* causative is available only where *-leş-* is conventionalized. Neither strategy covers the full range of bases across all registers. This is a partial asymmetry in coverage, not a total gap.
