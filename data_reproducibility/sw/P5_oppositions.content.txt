## SUMMARY

**Total oppositions identified:** 28

**Distribution by contrast type:**
- Lexical: 10 oppositions
- Morphological: 4 oppositions  
- Constructional: 14 oppositions

**Distribution by epistemic status:**
- [CORE]: 27 oppositions
- [UNCERTAIN]: 1 opposition (implied contrast in #22)

**Key patterns observed:**
1. Strong lexical opposition between kweli/ukweli (truth) and uongo (falsehood) forms the semantic core
2. Morphological negation using si (negative copula) is highly productive
3. Constructional oppositions dominate, particularly with contrasting verbs taking same objects
4. Moral character domain shows rich lexical oppositions (-adilifu/-bovu, -nyofu/-bovu)
5. Rights domain shows both action oppositions (linda/vunja haki) and type distinctions (kisheria/kimila)
6. Modal and evidential oppositions expressed constructionally rather than lexically
7. Scalar oppositions (mkubwa/mdogo) and visibility oppositions (wazi/siri) operate on truth nouns
8. No dedicated antonymic pairs for many concepts; opposition expressed through negation or contrasting constructions
