## SUMMARY OBSERVATIONS

### Distribution of Naturalness Ratings:
- **[CORE]**: 38 constructions (95%)
- **[MARGINAL]**: 2 constructions (5%) - both proverbs/fixed expressions
- **[UNCERTAIN]**: 0 constructions

### Key Patterns:
1. **Overwhelming core status**: Nearly all constructions are highly productive and natural in everyday usage
2. **Register variation minimal**: Most patterns function across formal and informal contexts
3. **Restricted patterns**: Only traditional proverbs show usage restrictions (cultural/didactic contexts)
4. **No alternate constructions attested**: Data shows single dominant pattern for each construction type
5. **Arabic loans**: Non-inflecting adjectives like *sahihi* create lexically restricted modification patterns
6. **Periphrastic alternatives**: Only agentive nominalization shows attested alternatives (Construction 7)

### Domain-Specific Observations:
- Administrative/legal constructions (15, 13) are core within their specialized domains
- Causative derivations (5) show exceptional productivity
- Abstract nominalization (6) is the most productive derivational pattern
- Complementizer constructions (8) are standard for propositional truth claims
