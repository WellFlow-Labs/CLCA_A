# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P5 OPPOSITIONS
# Language: Tamil (ta)

## IDENTIFIED OPPOSITIONS

### Opposition 1
**Term A:** உண்மை (uṇmai)  
**Term B:** பொய் (poy)  
**Transliteration:** uṇmai vs. poy  
**Translation:** truth vs. lie/falsehood  
**Description:** Primary lexical opposition in the truth domain. உண்மை refers to truth, reality, and what genuinely exists, while பொய் refers to lies, falsehood, and deception. This is the most fundamental and productive opposition in Tamil truth-related vocabulary.  
**Contrast type:** lexical  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### Opposition 2
**Term A:** மெய் (mey)  
**Term B:** பொய் (poy)  
**Transliteration:** mey vs. poy  
**Translation:** truth/reality vs. lie/falsehood  
**Description:** Classical Tamil lexical opposition between truth/reality and falsehood. மெய் carries literary and philosophical connotations and is particularly productive in spiritual/devotional compounds, while பொய் remains the primary term for falsehood across registers.  
**Contrast type:** lexical  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### Opposition 3
**Term A:** சரி (cari)  
**Term B:** தப்பு (tappu)  
**Transliteration:** cari vs. tappu  
**Translation:** correct/right vs. wrong/mistake  
**Description:** Primary opposition for correctness versus error in factual, procedural, and computational contexts. Both terms are extremely common in everyday speech and span both factual correctness and moral rightness domains.  
**Contrast type:** lexical  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### Opposition 4
**Term A:** சரி (cari)  
**Term B:** தவறு (tavaṟu)  
**Transliteration:** cari vs. tavaṟu  
**Translation:** correct/right vs. mistake/error/wrong  
**Description:** Alternative opposition for correctness versus error. தவறு is slightly more formal than தப்பு and is used in both factual contexts (errors in work) and moral contexts (wrongful conduct).  
**Contrast type:** lexical  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### Opposition 5

### Opposition 25
