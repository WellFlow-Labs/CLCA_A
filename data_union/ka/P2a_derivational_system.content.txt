# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2a Derivational System
# Language: Georgian (ka)
# Layer: P2a_derivational_system

---

**Methodological note:** The following documents attested derivational morphology for P1 base forms. Forms already fully documented in P1 or P1b are cross-referenced rather than re-described in full. Derivations are listed per base form; where a derived form has itself been listed as a P1 base, this is noted. No paradigm completion has been attempted. Gaps are preserved.

---

## 1. Base Form: ჭეშმარიტი / č̣ešmariti

---

**Derived Form 1**

1. **Base Form:** ჭეშმარიტი / č̣ešmariti
2. **Derived Form:** ჭეშმარიტება / č̣ešmariteba
3. **Transliteration:** č̣ešmariteba
4. **Literal Breakdown:** č̣ešmariti + nominalizing suffix -eba; "the state/quality of being true"
5. **Natural English Translation:** truth, truthfulness, veracity
6. **Usage:** The standard abstract noun for truth in Georgian, used in philosophical, theological, and everyday discourse. Already documented as P1 item #2; listed here to anchor the derivational relationship.
7. **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2**

1. **Base Form:** ჭეშმარიტი / č̣ešmariti
2. **Derived Form:** ჭეშმარიტად / č̣ešmaritad
3. **Transliteration:** č̣ešmaritad
4. **Literal Breakdown:** č̣ešmariti + adverbial suffix -ad; "in a true/genuine manner"
5. **Natural English Translation:** truly, genuinely, in truth, verily
6. **Usage:** Adverbial form used in formal, philosophical, theological, and literary registers to assert that something is genuinely or truly the case. Appears in classical Georgian texts and contemporary formal prose; less common in everyday speech than martla. Documented in P1b (Root 3, item 2); listed here to anchor the adverbial derivation.
7. **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 3**

1. **Base Form:** ჭეშმარიტი / č̣ešmariti
2. **Derived Form:** უჭეშმარიტო / uč̣ešmarito
3. **Transliteration:** uč̣ešmarito
4. **Literal Breakdown:** privative prefix u- + č̣ešmarit- + suffix -o; "lacking truth, not genuine"
5. **Natural English Translation:** untrue, untruthful, not genuine
6. **Usage:** Negated adjectival form used in formal and literary registers to characterize something as lacking genuine truth or authenticity. Less frequent than the base form; appears in theological and philosophical writing.
7. **Epistemic Status:** [UNCERTAIN]

---

**Note on further derivation:** No productive derivation from č̣ešmariteba itself (e.g., a further suffixed form beyond the adverb and noun) is attested with sufficient frequency to document here. The root č̣ešmar- does not appear to generate verbal forms in contemporary Georgian. No further well-attested morphological derivatives of č̣ešmariti beyond č̣ešmariteba and č̣ešmaritad have been identified in current standard usage.

## 2. Base Form: ჭეშმარიტება / č̣ešmariteba

---

**Derived Form 1**

1. **Base Form:** ჭეშმარიტება / č̣ešmariteba
2. **Derived Form:** ჭეშმარიტებრივი / č̣ešmariтebrivi
3. **Transliteration:** č̣ešmariтebrivi
4. **Literal Breakdown:** č̣ešmariteba + adjectival suffix -brivi; "of or pertaining to truth"
5. **Natural English Translation:** truth-related, pertaining to truth, of the nature of truth
6. **Usage:** Adjectival form derived from the nominal, used in philosophical and theological contexts to characterize something as belonging to or pertaining to the domain of truth. Appears in academic and theological writing.
7. **Epistemic Status:** [UNCERTAIN]

---

**Note:** č̣ešmariteba is itself a derivation from č̣ešmariti (see above). The nominal itself is the primary derived form from č̣ešmariti and functions as the base for genitive constructions documented in P1b rather than for further morphological derivation. The form does not take additional nominalizing or verbalizing suffixes in attested usage.

## 3. Base Form: სიმართლე / simartle

---

**Derived Form 1**

1. **Base Form:** სიმართლე / simartle
2. **Derived Form:** სიმართლის თქმა / simarṭlis tqma
3. **Transliteration:** simarṭlis tqma
4. **Literal Breakdown:** simarṭle (truth, genitive -is) + tqma (verbal noun of tkva, "to say"); "the saying of truth"
5. **Natural English Translation:** truth-telling, telling the truth
6. **Usage:** Used in moral, legal, and everyday contexts to describe the act of speaking truthfully. Highly frequent in legal oath contexts and moral discourse. Documented in P1b (Root 1, item 6). This is a genitive nominal compound rather than a suffixal derivation; included here because it functions as a lexicalized unit.
7. **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2**

1. **Base Form:** სიმართლე / simartle
2. **Derived Form:** სიმართლიანი / simartliani
3. **Transliteration:** simartliani
4. **Literal Breakdown:** simartle + adjectival suffix -iani; "characterized by truth, truthful"
5. **Natural English Translation:** truthful, honest, truth-bearing
6. **Usage:** Adjectival form used to characterize persons or statements as truthful. Less common than martali in this function; appears in literary and formal registers.
7. **Epistemic Status:** [UNCERTAIN]

---

**Note:** simartle is itself a circumfix derivation (si-...-e) from the root martal-/marṭ-. It does not appear to generate further suffixal derivations as a base in its own right beyond the genitive collocations and the adjectival form above. The productive derivational work in this family is done from the root marṭ- directly (see item #4, martali, below). The genitive construction simartlis is productive in collocations but this is inflectional rather than derivational.

**Note on the marṭ- family:** The root marṭ- is the most derivationally productive root in this domain. The asymmetry between this root and others in the P1 set is preserved here without normalization.

5. **Natural English Translation:** signs of forgery, indicators of falsity
6. **Usage:** Used in forensic, legal, and investigative contexts to describe observable markers indicating that a document or item is forged. Documented in P1b (Root 2, item 13).
7. **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

## 6. Base Form: ყალბი / q̇albi

---

**Derived Form 1**

1. **Base Form:** ყალბი / q̇albi
2. **Derived Form:** სიყალბე / siq̇albe
3. **Transliteration:** siq̇albe
4. **Literal Breakdown:** si- (circumfix front) + q̇alb- + -e (circumfix back); "the quality of being false/counterfeit"
5. **Natural English Translation:** falsehood, falsity, forgery, counterfeit, inauthenticity
6. **Usage:** The standard abstract noun for the quality of being false or counterfeit, used in legal and moral contexts. Already documented as P1 item #5; listed here to anchor the derivational relationship from q̇albi.
7. **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2**

1. **Base Form:** ყალბი / q̇albi
2. **Derived Form:** ყალბობა / q̇alboba
3. **Transliteration:** q̇alboba
4. **Literal Breakdown:** q̇alb- + -oba (nominalizing suffix)
5. **Natural English Translation:** falseness, the quality or practice of being fake or counterfeit
6. **Usage:** Less common than siq̇albe but used in moral and evaluative discourse as an abstract noun for the property or practice of falseness. Documented in P1b.
7. **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 3**

1. **Base Form:** ყალბი / q̇albi
2. **Derived Form:** გაყალბება / gaq̇albeba
3. **Transliteration:** gaq̇albeba
4. **Literal Breakdown:** ga- (preverb) + q̇alb- + -eba (nominalizing suffix); "making false/counterfeit"
5. **Natural English Translation:** forgery, falsification, counterfeiting, fabrication
6. **Usage:** The standard noun for the act of forging or falsifying, used in legal contexts for the criminal act of forgery. Already documented as P1 item #23 and P1b (Root 2, item 9).
7. **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 4**

1. **Base Form:** ყალბი / q̇albi
2. **Derived Form:** გაყალბებული / gaq̇albebuli
3. **Transliteration:** gaq̇albebuli
4. **Literal Breakdown:** gaq̇albeba + resultative participial suffix -uli; "that which has been forged/falsified"
5. **Natural English Translation:** forged, falsified, counterfeited, fabricated
6. **Usage:** Participial adjective describing documents, evidence, or data that have been deliberately falsified. Widely used in legal and investigative contexts. Documented in P1b (Root 2, item 10).
7. **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 5**

1. **Base Form:** ყალბი / q̇albi
2. **Derived Form:** ყალბად / q̇albad
3. **Transliteration:** q̇albad
4. **Literal Breakdown:** q̇albi + adverbial suffix -ad; "falsely, in a false manner"
5. **Natural English Translation:** falsely, fraudulently
6. **Usage:** Adverbial form used in legal and administrative contexts, as in q̇albad c̣armogdena ("false representation"). Documented in P1b (Root 2, item 14) as part of the compound ყალბად წარმოდგენა.
7. **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 6**

1. **Base Form:** ყალბი / q̇albi
2. **Derived Form:** გამაყალბებელი / gamaq̇albebeli
3. **Transliteration:** gamaq̇albebeli
4. **Literal Breakdown:** ga- + ma- (agentive prefix) + q̇alb- + -ebeli (agentive participial suffix); "one who falsifies"
5. **Natural English Translation:** forger, falsifier, counterfeiter
6. **Usage:** Agentive nominal used in legal contexts to refer to a person who commits forgery or falsification. Appears in criminal proceedings and legal journalism.
7. **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Note on q̇alb- productivity:** The root generates both a si-...-e abstract noun (siq̇albe) and an -oba abstract noun (q̇alboba), as well as a ga- prefixed verbal form (gaq̇albeba) and its participial derivative (gaq̇albebuli), an adverbial form (q̇albad), and an agentive form (gamaq̇albebeli). The verbal form gaayalbebs ("will forge/falsify") is noted in P1 as productive and common; its full paradigm is not documented here.

## 9. Base Form: სიცრუე / sicrue

4. **Literal Breakdown:** m- (adjectival prefix) + cru- (root: false, lying)
5. **Natural English Translation:** false, lying, deceptive
6. **Usage:** Adjectival form from the same cru- root as sicrue, used to describe persons or statements as false or deceptive. Appears in formal and literary registers; less common in everyday speech than q̇albi.
