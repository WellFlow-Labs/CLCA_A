## Summary Observations

- All nine construction types identified in P4a receive a **[CORE]** naturalness rating based on the attested data. No construction in the P4a output is rated [MARGINAL] or [UNCERTAIN] at the construction-type level, though the **postnominal attributive** variant within Construction 3 is rated [MARGINAL] as a sub-pattern, consistent with the P4a note that it is marked and restricted.
- The absence of a lexicalized agentive noun for "truth-teller" (Construction 7) is a notable asymmetry in the domain, attested explicitly in P4a and preserved here without inference.
- The pragmatic distinction between δήθεν/τάχα (speaker believes claim is false) and φερόμενος ως (claim is unconfirmed) within Construction 8 is the only case where two sub-patterns within a single construction type carry observably different epistemic force.
