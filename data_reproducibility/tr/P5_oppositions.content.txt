### OPPOSITION 1
**Term A:** doğru  
**Term B:** yanlış  
**Transliteration:** doğru / yanlış  
**Translation:** true, correct / wrong, false, incorrect  
**Description:** Primary lexical opposition for factual correctness evaluation. *Doğru* indicates alignment with facts or proper standards, while *yanlış* indicates deviation or incorrectness. This is the most basic and frequent opposition in the truth domain.  
**Contrast type:** lexical  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## SUMMARY OBSERVATIONS

**Total oppositions identified:** 37

**Distribution by contrast type:**
- Lexical: 12 oppositions
- Morphological: 14 oppositions  
- Constructional: 11 oppositions

**Epistemic status distribution:**
- [CORE]: 35 oppositions (or components thereof)
- [MARGINAL]: 4 items or components
- [UNCERTAIN]: 2 items or components

**Key patterns observed:**

1. **Morphological productivity**: Turkish shows extensive use of possessive (*-lı/-li*) vs. privative (*-sız/-siz*) suffixes to create systematic oppositions (haklı/haksız, adaletli/adaletsiz, tutarlı/tutarsız, etc.).

2. **Asymmetric oppositions**: Several oppositions show asymmetry where one term is more lexicalized or frequent than its counterpart (e.g., yanlışsız is common but yanlışlı is rare; hak vermek is common but its negative is not lexicalized).

3. **Constructional parallelism**: Many oppositions maintain parallel constructional frames with contrasting lexical items (*doğru çıkmak/yanlış çıkmak*, *doğru söylemek/yalan söylemek*).

4. **Multiple contrasts per term**: Several terms participate in multiple oppositions (*gerçek* contrasts with *sahte*, *yapay*, *gerçekdışı*, and *gerçeküstü*; *doğru* contrasts with *yanlış* across multiple constructions).

5. **Register variation**: Some oppositions show register differences, with certain forms preferred in formal contexts (adaletli/adaletsiz) vs. colloquial speech (doğru dürüst).

6. **No clear oppositions identified for**: Some terms in the vocabulary lack clear lexical opposites (e.g., *hakikat* has no single direct opposite; *hakkında* as grammaticalized postposition has no opposition).
