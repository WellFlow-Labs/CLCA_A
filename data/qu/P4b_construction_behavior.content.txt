## ALTERNATE CONSTRUCTIONS ATTESTED

### For Construction 4 (Inchoative):
- **llullakuy** (simple reflexive) is more common than **llullayay** for "to lie"
- **-ya-** primarily inchoative with quality adjectives, but with **llulla** shows intensifying/habitual reading

### For Construction 6 (Abstract Nominalization):
- Both **chiqan kay** and **chiqan kaq** attested for nominalization
- **kay** construction = process/state nominal
- **kaq** construction = substantivized entity

### For Construction 18 (Comitative):
- **-wan** shows both comitative ("with") and instrumental ("by means of") readings
- Instrumental reading more frequent with **llulla**; comitative more metaphorical with **chiqan**

## GENERAL OBSERVATIONS

1. **Core constructions** (rated [CORE]): 31 of 36 examples show unrestricted, productive usage across registers
2. **Marginal constructions** (rated [MARGINAL]): 5 examples show metaphorical extensions or register restrictions
3. **No uncertain constructions**: All examples appear well-attested
4. **Highest productivity**: Accusative + verb (Construction 8), basic attribution (Construction 1), adnominal modification (Construction 3)
5. **Metaphorical extensions**: Locative (9) and directional (10) constructions show spatial metaphors for abstract states, with more restricted usage
