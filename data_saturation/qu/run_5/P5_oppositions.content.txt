# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P5 Oppositions
# Language: Quechua (qu)

---

**Preliminary note on method:** Oppositions below are extracted exclusively from patterns explicitly described or demonstrated in P1, P1b, P2a, P2b, P3, P4a, and P4b. No opposition is inferred beyond what the data directly support. Where the CONTEXT describes a contrast as systematic or grammaticalized, this is noted; where a contrast is described as contextual or partial, that qualification is preserved. The number of oppositions reflects the data.

## Opposition 1

1. **Term A:** cheqa
2. **Term B:** llulla
3. **Transliteration:** cheqa / llulla
4. **English translation:** "true, real, genuine" / "false, lie, deceptive, fake"
5. **Description of contrast:** Described across P1, P1b, P2a, P2b, and P3 as the primary lexical antonym pair in the truth-evaluation domain; P1 explicitly states that *llulla* is "directly antonymous to *cheqa* in most contexts." The pair appears together in the disjunctive interrogative *cheqachus llullachus?* ("is it true or is it false?"), documented in P2b (C-cross-01) and P3, which treats them as the canonical truth/falsity opposition.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 2

1. **Term A:** cheqa runa
2. **Term B:** llulla runa
3. **Transliteration:** cheqa runa / llulla runa
4. **English translation:** "honest/genuine person, person of integrity" / "dishonest person, deceiver, person of false character"
5. **Description of contrast:** Both collocations follow the same attributive pattern ([adjective] + *runa*) and are documented in P1b and P3 (Group 6) as contrasting characterizations of a person's fundamental moral character. P3 treats them as the positive and negative poles of character evaluation within the same semantic group.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 5

1. **Term A:** cheqa rimay / cheqaqta rimay
2. **Term B:** llulla rimay / llullata rimay
3. **Transliteration:** cheqa rimay / cheqaqta rimay / llulla rimay / llullata rimay

P2b (P4b summary) notes that *llullata rimay* uses the same adverbial *-ta* frame as *cheqaqta rimay*, confirming the structural parallelism. P3 (Group 4) groups these together as contrasting evaluations of speech acts.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 9

1. **Term A:** cheqa
2. **Term B:** pantasqa / pantay
3. **Transliteration:** cheqa / pantasqa / pantay
4. **English translation:** "true, correct, genuine" / "mistaken, wrong, erroneous / error, mistake"
5. **Description of contrast:** P1b (peripheral extensions) and P3 (Groups 1 and 7) treat *cheqa* and *panta-* forms as contrasting in factual and moral evaluation contexts, where the question is whether a statement or judgment is correct or mistaken. The disjunctive interrogative *cheqachus pantasqachu?* ("is it true or is it mistaken?"), documented in P2b (C-cross-02), directly instantiates this contrast. P3 notes that *panta-* covers inadvertent incorrectness, distinguishing it from both *cheqa* and *llulla*.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

## Opposition 10

1. **Term A:** llulla / llullakuy
2. **Term B:** pantay / pantasqa
3. **Transliteration:** llulla / llullakuy / pantay / pantasqa
4. **English translation:** "false, lie, to deceive" / "error, to err, mistaken"
5. **Description of contrast:** P3 explicitly distinguishes these two domains: *llulla* covers deliberate deception, while *panta-* covers inadvertent incorrectness. P3 (Group 7 notes) states that *panta-* forms "contrast with expressions of deliberate falsehood (*llulla*) in that the error may be inadvertent." This distinction is described as a usage-based contrast within the broader falsity/incorrectness domain.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 11

1. **Term A:** cheqaqmi
2. **Term B:** cheqaqsi
3. **Transliteration:** cheqaqmi / cheqaqsi
4. **English translation:** "it is indeed true (I know directly)" / "it is reportedly true (I heard it from someone)"
5. **Description of contrast:** P1, P1b, P2a, P2b, P3, and P4a all document this as a systematic, grammaticalized contrast within the evidential paradigm applied to the truth predicate *cheqaq*. P4a (Construction 8) describes the three-way evidential paradigm as "grammatically obligatory in Southern Quechua assertive sentences, not optional." The contrast encodes whether the speaker's epistemic source is direct experience or hearsay.
6. **Contrast type:** morphological
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 12

1. **Term A:** cheqaqmi
2. **Term B:** cheqaqchus
3. **Transliteration:** cheqaqmi / cheqaqchus
4. **English translation:** "it is indeed true (I know directly)" / "is it really true? / perhaps true (I am uncertain)"
5. **Description of contrast:** Documented in P1b, P2a (D-cheqaq series), P3 (Group 3), and P4a (Construction 8) as part of the same grammaticalized evidential paradigm. The contrast is between direct epistemic commitment (*-mi*) and expressed doubt or uncertainty (*-chus*). P2a notes this as a "systematic evidential paradigm (direct, reportative, dubitative) applied to the truth predicate."
6. **Contrast type:** morphological
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 13

1. **Term A:** cheqaqsi
2. **Term B:** cheqaqchus
3. **Transliteration:** cheqaqsi / cheqaqchus
4. **English translation:** "reportedly true (hearsay source)" / "perhaps true (uncertain)"
5. **Description of contrast:** Both are non-direct evidential forms within the same paradigm documented in P1b, P2a, P3, and P4a, but they contrast with each other: *-si* marks a specific indirect source (hearsay), while *-chus* marks absence of a reliable source or active doubt. P3 (Group 3) groups all three evidential forms together while preserving the internal contrasts among them.
6. **Contrast type:** morphological
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 16

1. **Term A:** cheqaqmi (or any truth predicate + *-mi*)
2. **Term B:** mana cheqachu (or any truth predicate negated with *mana* + *-chu*)
3. **Transliteration:** cheqaqmi / mana cheqa-chu
4. **English translation:** "it is indeed true" / "it is not true"
5. **Description of contrast:** P2b (Summary, point 2) and P4a (Construction 2) document *mana* + [predicate] + *-chu* as the obligatory negation frame contrasting with affirmative predicative constructions across all truth-domain items. P4b describes this as "the standard negation frame for predicative use of truth/falsity adjectives; obligatory in all negated predicative contexts."
6. **Contrast type:** constructional
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 17

1. **Term A:** cheqaqtam rimani
2. **Term B:** mana cheqachu (negated predicate) / llullam rimawanki
3. **Transliteration:** cheqaqtam rimani / mana cheqa-chu / llullam rimawanki
4. **English translation:** "I am speaking the truth" / "it is not true" / "you are lying to me"
5. **Description of contrast:** P2b (C-cheqaq-01, C-llulla-01) and P4a (Construction 10) document the adverbial *-ta* truth-modifier construction on speech verbs as contrasting with both the negated truth predicate and the direct accusation of lying. P4b notes that "the pattern extends to *llullata rimay* (to speak falsely) with the same structural frame," confirming the constructional parallelism between the truthful and false speech assertions.
6. **Contrast type:** constructional
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 18

1. **Term A:** cheqaqa + corrective statement
2. **Term B:** [prior claim being corrected]
3. **Transliteration:** cheqaqa-m, [corrective statement]
4. **English translation:** "the truth is, [corrective statement]" / [the inaccurate prior claim]
5. **Description of contrast:** P1b, P2b (C-cheqaqa-01, C-cheqaqa-02), P3 (Group 8), and P4a (Construction 9) all document *cheqaqa* as a discourse-level construction that explicitly contrasts a corrective statement with a prior inaccurate claim. P4a describes it as "restricted to contexts where a prior claim is being replaced or corrected," making the contrast between the prior claim and the corrective statement constitutive of the construction's function.
6. **Contrast type:** constructional
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 19

1. **Term A:** cheqa kay (abstract nominalization with *kay*)
2. **Term B:** pantay / llullakuy (infinitive-as-nominal)
3. **Transliteration:** cheqa kay / pantay / llullakuy
4. **English translation:** "truth / reality / the state of being true" / "error / the act of erring" / "lying / the act of deceiving"
5. **Description of contrast:** P4a (Construction 6) and P4b document two distinct nominalization strategies in the truth domain: the [root] + *kay* construction (formal/reflective register, rated [MARGINAL]) versus the verbal infinitive in *-y* functioning as a process nominal (everyday use, rated [CORE]). P4b explicitly states these "differ in register distribution" and lists them as alternates with different naturalness ratings.
6. **Contrast type:** constructional
7. **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL] (for the *cheqa kay* strategy; the infinitive strategy is [CORE])

## Opposition 23

1. **Term A:** pantachiy
2. **Term B:** pantakuy
3. **Transliteration:** pantachiy / pantakuy
4. **English translation:** "to cause to err / to mislead (transitive, affecting another)" / "to err (intransitive/reflexive, self-directed)"
5. **Description of contrast:** P2a (D-panta-03, D-panta-04) documents both as derived from the same *panta-* stem but with different valency: *-chi-* produces a causative (agent causes another to err), while *-ku-* produces a reflexive/middle (agent errs themselves or in a self-directed manner). P4a (Construction 5) and P4b document the causative pattern; P2a documents the reflexive/middle pattern.
6. **Contrast type:** morphological
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## Opposition 24

1. **Term A:** llullachiy
2. **Term B:** llullakuy
3. **Transliteration:** llullachiy / llullakuy
4. **English translation:** "to cause someone to lie / to induce deception (transitive, affecting another)" / "to lie / to deceive (intransitive/reflexive)"
5. **Description of contrast:** P2a (D-llulla-03, D-llulla-01) documents both as derived from the *llulla* root with the same morphological contrast as Opposition 23: *-chi-* causative versus *-ku-* reflexive/middle. P3 (Group 10) notes *llullachiy* specifically in legal contexts of inducing false testimony, while *llullakuy* is the general act of lying.
6. **Contrast type:** morphological
7. **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL] (for *llullachiy*); [CORE] (for *llullakuy*)

## Opposition 26

1. **Term A:** yachay
2. **Term B:** riqsiy
3. **Transliteration:** yachay / riqsiy
4. **English translation:** "to know (propositionally), knowledge, skill" / "to know (by acquaintance), to recognize, to identify"
5. **Description of contrast:** P1 introduces the distinction: *yachay* is described as "the primary term for propositional knowledge and learned skill," while *riqsiy* is described as "knowledge by acquaintance or recognition." P3 (Group 5) preserves this as "a usage-based distinction within this group rather than collapsed." P2b documents distinct constructions for each (*yachaspa rimay*, *riqsispa rimay*) that parallel each other structurally while encoding different epistemic bases for speech.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

P1b describes *cheqaqpuni* as used "in response to skepticism," which directly inverts the doubt expressed by *cheqaqchus*.

## Opposition 29

1. **Term A:** cheqa hina
2. **Term B:** cheqaqmi / cheqam kay
3. **Transliteration:** cheqa hina / cheqaqmi / cheqa-m kay
4. **English translation:** "like the truth / appearing genuine / seemingly real" / "it is indeed true / this is true"
5. **Description of contrast:** P1b (item 28) and P3 (Ambiguous Items) document *cheqa hina* as expressing that something "resembles or appears to be true or genuine without necessarily being so," contrasting with the direct assertion of truth (*cheqaqmi*, *cheqam kay*). P2b (C-cheqa-04) documents *cheqa hina rikukun* ("it appears to be true") as distinct from the direct truth assertion. The contrast is between appearance of truth and assertion of truth.
6. **Contrast type:** constructional
7. **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

## Opposition 30

1. **Term A:** cheqaqtam rimani ("I am speaking the truth")
2. **Term B:** llullam rimawanki ("you are lying to me")
3. **Transliteration:** cheqaqtam rimani / llullam rimawanki
4. **English translation:** "I am speaking the truth" / "you are lying to me"
5. **Description of contrast:** P2b (C-cheqaq-01, C-llulla-01) documents both as contrasting constructions used in testimonial and accusatory discourse. While they differ in person (first vs. second) and construction type (adverbial *-ta* modifier vs. predicative), P3 (Group 4) groups them together as contrasting evaluations of speech acts in the same discourse contexts. The contrast is between self-assertion of truthfulness and accusation of lying directed at an interlocutor.
6. **Contrast type:** constructional
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]
