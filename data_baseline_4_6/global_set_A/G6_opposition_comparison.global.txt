- frogagarria (provable) vs. frogaezina (unprovable) — -garri vs. -ezin suffix opposition

**Constructional contrasts:**

- sọ òtítọ́! (tell truth! imperative) vs. má purọ́! (don't lie! prohibitive) — positive vs. negative imperative

- tọ́ púpọ̀ (very correct) vs. tọ́ díẹ̀ (somewhat correct) — degree scalar opposition
- bẹ́ẹ̀ ni gan-an (exactly right) vs. kì í ṣe bẹ́ẹ̀ (it is not so) — emphatic affirmation vs. emphatic negation
