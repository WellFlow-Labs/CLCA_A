# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P6 CHANGE, CAUSATION, AND STABILITY
# Language: Finnish (fi)

## Methodology Note

This analysis documents **attested dynamic expressions** involving truth-domain vocabulary from P1-P5. Finnish exhibits rich morphological resources for encoding change and causation through derivational suffixes. I examine only patterns actually used in the language, noting gaps where expected patterns do not occur.

---

## 1. BECOMING TRUE / REAL

### 1.1 todellistua — "become real, be realized"

**Pattern:** [SUBJECT] + todellistua
**Morphology:** tode-llistu-a (true-ADJ-BECOME-INF)
**Derivation:** Inchoative from causative *todellistaa*

**Examples:**
- **Unelma todellistui.** / unelma todellistui / "The dream came true."
- **Pelko todellistui.** / pelko todellistui / "The fear was realized."
- **Suunnitelma ei todellistunut.** / suunnitelma ei todellistunut / "The plan did not materialize."

**Usage notes:** Common with abstract subjects (dreams, fears, plans, predictions). Emphasizes transition from potential/imagined to actual. Neutral register. Implies complete realization.

**Aspectual properties:** Telic (implies completion). Perfective aspect typical (*todellistui* "came true" more common than progressive *on todellistumassa* "is coming true").

**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### 1.2 toteutua — "come true, be realized, be implemented"

**Pattern:** [SUBJECT] + toteutua
**Morphology:** tote-utu-a (true-BECOME-INF)
**Derivation:** Inchoative from causative *toteuttaa*

**Examples:**
- **Suunnitelma toteutui.** / suunnitelma toteutui / "The plan was realized."
- **Ennustus toteutui.** / ennustus toteutui / "The prediction came true."
- **Hanke toteutuu ensi vuonna.** / hanke toteutuu ensi vuonna / "The project will be implemented next year."

**Usage notes:** Very common with plans, projects, predictions. More concrete/practical than *todellistua*. Used in project management, planning contexts. Can take temporal modifiers.

**Aspectual properties:** Telic. Both perfective (*toteutui*) and progressive (*toteutuu*, *on toteutumassa*) common. Progressive emphasizes ongoing implementation.

**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### 1.3 todentua — "be verified, be confirmed"

**Pattern:** [SUBJECT] + todentua
**Morphology:** tode-ntu-a (true-BECOME-INF)
**Derivation:** Inchoative from causative *todentaa*

**Examples:**
- **Epäilyt todentuivat.** / epäilyt todentuivat / "The suspicions were confirmed."
- **Väite todentui.** / väite todentui / "The claim was verified."
- **Tiedot eivät todentuneet.** / tiedot eivät todentuneet / "The information was not confirmed."

**Usage notes:** More formal than *toteutua*. Emphasizes verification/confirmation rather than simple realization. Common in investigative, journalistic contexts. Subjects typically claims, suspicions, information.

**Aspectual properties:** Telic. Perfective aspect typical. Progressive rare.

**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### 1.4 paljastua — "be revealed, come to light" (with totuus)

**Pattern:** totuus + paljastuu
**Morphology:** paljast-u-a (reveal-REFL-INF)
**Note:** *Paljastua* not derived from P1 vocabulary but commonly used with truth-terms

**Examples:**
- **Totuus paljastuu lopulta.** / totuus paljastuu lopulta / "The truth will be revealed eventually."
- **Totuus paljastui.** / totuus paljastui / "The truth came to light."
- **Tosiasiat paljastuivat.** / tosiasiat paljastuivat / "The facts were revealed."

**Usage notes:** Very common collocation with *totuus* and *tosiasiat*. Implies hidden truth becoming known. Narrative and investigative contexts. Emphasizes discovery/uncovering.

**Aspectual properties:** Telic. Both perfective and progressive common. Progressive (*paljastuu*) emphasizes gradual revelation.

**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### 1.5 käydä todeksi — "come true" (idiomatic)

**Pattern:** [SUBJECT] + käy todeksi
**Morphology:** käy-dä tode-ksi (go-INF true-TRANSLATIVE)
**Note:** Fixed expression using translative case

**Examples:**
- **Unelma kävi todeksi.** / unelma kävi todeksi / "The dream came true."
- **Ennustus kävi todeksi.** / ennustus kävi todeksi / "The prediction came true."

**Usage notes:** Somewhat literary/emphatic. Less common than *todellistua* or *toteutua* but established idiom. Emphasizes transformation to truth/reality. Often with dreams, wishes, predictions.

**Aspectual properties:** Telic. Perfective typical.

**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### 1.6 pitää paikkansa (stative, not change)

**Pattern:** [SUBJECT] + pitää paikkansa
**Note:** This is stative ("is true"), not change expression

**Examples:**
- **Väite pitää paikkansa.** / väite pitää paikkansa / "The claim is true."

**Usage notes:** Does not encode becoming true, only being true. No inchoative form (*\*alkaa pitää paikkansa* "start to be true" not idiomatic). Included for contrast with change expressions.

**Epistemic status:** [CORE] (but not change expression)

## 2. BECOMING FALSE / WRONG

### 2.1 vääristyä — "become distorted"

**Pattern:** [SUBJECT] + vääristyä
**Morphology:** vääriste-ä (distort-REFL-INF)
**Derivation:** Inchoative from causative *vääristää*

**Examples:**
- **Totuus vääristyy.** / totuus vääristyy / "The truth becomes distorted."
- **Kuva vääristyi.** / kuva vääristyi / "The image became distorted."
- **Merkitys vääristyy helposti.** / merkitys vääristyy helposti / "The meaning easily becomes distorted."

**Usage notes:** Common with *totuus*, *kuva* "image," *merkitys* "meaning." Emphasizes gradual corruption or transformation. Implies deviation from original true/correct state.

**Aspectual properties:** Atelic (process-oriented). Progressive common (*vääristyy*, *on vääristymässä*). Can be gradual or sudden.

**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### 2.2 mennä pieleen — "go wrong" (idiomatic)

**Pattern:** [SUBJECT] + menee pieleen
**Morphology:** men-nä pieleen (go-INF astray)
**Note:** *Pieleen* not in P1 but common idiom for "going wrong"

**Examples:**
- **Suunnitelma meni pieleen.** / suunnitelma meni pieleen / "The plan went wrong."
- **Kaikki meni pieleen.** / kaikki meni pieleen / "Everything went wrong."

**Usage notes:** Very common idiom. Not specifically truth-domain but used for plans, procedures failing. Informal register. Implies failure or error.

**Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL] (not core truth-domain)

---

### 2.3 osoittautua vääräksi — "turn out to be false"

**Pattern:** [SUBJECT] + osoittautuu vääräksi
**Morphology:** osoittautu-a väärä-ksi (show.REFL-INF wrong-TRANSLATIVE)
**Note:** *Osoittautua* not in P1 but standard construction

**Examples:**
- **Väite osoittautui vääräksi.** / väite osoittautui vääräksi / "The claim turned out to be false."
- **Teoria osoittautui vääräksi.** / teoria osoittautui vääräksi / "The theory turned out to be wrong."

**Usage notes:** Common construction for claims/theories proven false. Formal/neutral register. Emphasizes discovery of falsity through evidence or events.

**Aspectual properties:** Telic. Perfective typical (*osoittautui*). Implies completed discovery.

**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### 2.4 paljastua valheeksi — "be revealed as a lie"

**Pattern:** [SUBJECT] + paljastuu valheeksi
**Morphology:** paljast-u-a valhe-eksi (reveal-REFL-INF lie-TRANSLATIVE)

**Examples:**
- **Väite paljastui valheeksi.** / väite paljastui valheeksi / "The claim was revealed to be a lie."
- **Tarina paljastui valheeksi.** / tarina paljastui valheeksi / "The story was revealed to be a lie."

**Usage notes:** Common construction emphasizing discovery of deliberate falsehood. Stronger than *osoittautua vääräksi* — implies intentional deception. Narrative and investigative contexts.

**Aspectual properties:** Telic. Perfective typical.

**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### 2.5 (no common expression for "become untrue")

**Pattern:** (no common expression identified)
**Note:** Finnish lacks simple inchoative from *epätosi* or *väärä* meaning "become false/untrue"

**Explanation:** While *vääristyä* exists for "become distorted," there is no simple *\*väärtyä* "become false" or *\*epätoistua* "become untrue." The concept is expressed through constructions like *osoittautua vääräksi* "turn out to be false" or *paljastua valheeksi* "be revealed as a lie."

**Epistemic status:** [UNCERTAIN] (gap in paradigm)

## 3. CAUSING TO BE TRUE / REAL

### 3.1 todellistaa — "realize, actualize, make real"

**Pattern:** [AGENT] + todellistaa + [OBJECT]
**Morphology:** tode-llista-a (true-ADJ-CAUS-INF)
**Derivation:** Causative from adjective *todellinen*

**Examples:**
- **Hän todellistaa unelmansa.** / hän todellistaa unelmansa / "He/she realizes his/her dreams."
- **Yritys todellistaa suunnitelman.** / yritys todellistaa suunnitelman / "The company realizes the plan."
- **Todellistaa tavoitteet** / todellistaa tavoitteet / "realize the goals"

**Usage notes:** Common with abstract objects (dreams, goals, plans). Emphasizes making potential/imagined things actual. Neutral register. Implies active agency.

**Aspectual properties:** Telic. Can be progressive (*todellistaa*, *on todellistamassa*) or perfective (*totellisti*).

**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### 3.2 toteuttaa — "realize, implement, carry out"

**Pattern:** [AGENT] + toteuttaa + [OBJECT]
**Morphology:** tote-utta-a (true-CAUS-INF)
**Derivation:** Causative from inchoative *toteutua*

**Examples:**
- **Toteuttaa suunnitelma** / toteuttaa suunnitelma / "implement the plan"
- **Toteuttaa päätös** / toteuttaa päätös / "carry out the decision"
- **Hallitus toteuttaa uudistuksen.** / hallitus toteuttaa uudistuksen / "The government implements the reform."

**Usage notes:** Very common in planning, project management, administrative contexts. More concrete/practical than *todellistaa*. Emphasizes execution and implementation. Neutral/formal register.

**Aspectual properties:** Telic. Both progressive and perfective common. Progressive emphasizes ongoing implementation.

**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### 3.3 todistaa — "prove, demonstrate"

**Pattern:** [AGENT] + todistaa + [OBJECT/CLAUSE]
**Morphology:** todista-a (prove-INF)
**Derivation:** Causative from *tosi* root (irregular formation)

**Examples:**
- **Todistaa väite oikeaksi** / todistaa väite oikeaksi / "prove the claim correct"
- **Todistaa syyttömyytensä** / todistaa syyttömyytensä / "prove one's innocence"
- **Hän todisti, että oli oikeassa.** / hän todisti että oli oikeassa / "He/she proved that he/she was right."

**Usage notes:** Very common in argumentative, legal, scientific contexts. Emphasizes establishing truth through evidence or demonstration. Can take object + translative or complement clause.

**Aspectual properties:** Telic. Perfective typical (*todisti*) but progressive possible (*todistaa*).

**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### 3.4 todentaa — "verify, authenticate, confirm"

**Pattern:** [AGENT] + todentaa + [OBJECT]
**Morphology:** tode-nta-a (true-CAUS-INF)
**Derivation:** Causative from *tosi* root

**Examples:**
- **Todentaa henkilöllisyys** / todentaa henkilöllisyys / "verify identity"
- **Todentaa tiedot** / todentaa tiedot / "verify the information"
- **Viranomainen todentaa asiakirjat.** / viranomainen todentaa asiakirjat / "The authority authenticates the documents."

**Usage notes:** Formal/technical term. Common in administrative, legal, technical contexts. Emphasizes verification and authentication. More formal than *todistaa*.

**Aspectual properties:** Telic. Perfective typical.

**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### 3.5 oikeuttaa — "justify, warrant"

**Pattern:** [SUBJECT] + oikeuttaa + [OBJECT]
**Morphology:** oikeut-ta-a (right-CAUS-INF)
**Derivation:** Causative from *oikeus* "right, justice"

**Examples:**
- **Oikeuttaa toimensa** / oikeuttaa toimensa / "justify one's actions"
- **Tilanne oikeuttaa toimenpiteet.** / tilanne oikeuttaa toimenpiteet / "The situation warrants the measures."
- **Mikään ei oikeuta väkivaltaa.** / mikään ei oikeuta väkivaltaa / "Nothing justifies violence."

**Usage notes:** Common in moral, legal, political discourse. Emphasizes providing justification or legitimate grounds. Can have abstract or concrete subjects. Neutral/formal register.

**Aspectual properties:** Stative-like. Present tense common (*oikeuttaa*). Less clearly telic than other causatives.

**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### 3.6 paljastaa — "reveal, expose" (with totuus, valhe)

**Pattern:** [AGENT] + paljastaa + [OBJECT]
**Morphology:** paljasta-a (reveal-INF)
**Note:** *Paljastaa* not derived from P1 vocabulary but common with truth-terms

**Examples:**
- **Paljastaa totuus** / paljastaa totuus / "reveal the truth"
- **Paljastaa valhe** / paljastaa valhe / "expose the lie"
- **Toimittaja paljasti valheen.** / toimittaja paljasti valheen / "The journalist exposed the lie."

**Usage notes:** Very common collocation with *totuus* and *valhe*. Emphasizes making hidden truth/falsehood known. Investigative and journalistic contexts. Implies uncovering.

**Aspectual properties:** Telic. Perfective typical (*paljasti*).

**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### 3.7 (no common causative from aito)

**Pattern:** (no common expression identified)
**Note:** *Aito* "genuine" lacks causative *\*aitouttaa* "make genuine"

**Explanation:** While many truth-domain adjectives generate causatives (*todellinen* → *todellistaa*, *väärä* → *vääristää*), *aito* does not. The concept "make genuine/authentic" is not expressed through simple causative derivation. Alternative constructions would use *tehdä aidoksi* "make into genuine" (translative) or descriptive phrases.

**Epistemic status:** [UNCERTAIN] (gap in paradigm)

## 4. CAUSING TO BE FALSE / WRONG

### 4.1 vääristää — "distort, falsify"

**Pattern:** [AGENT] + vääristää + [OBJECT]
**Morphology:** vääriste-ä (distort-CAUS-INF)
**Derivation:** Causative from *väärä*

**Examples:**
- **Vääristää totuutta** / vääristää totuutta / "distort the truth"
- **Vääristää tosiasioita** / vääristää tosiasioita / "distort the facts"
- **Media vääristää tietoja.** / media vääristää tietoja / "The media distorts information."

**Usage notes:** Very common in critical discourse (media criticism, political debate). Emphasizes transformation/corruption of truth. Can be intentional or unintentional. Negative connotation.

**Aspectual properties:** Atelic (can be ongoing process) or telic (completed distortion). Both progressive and perfective common.

**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### 4.2 väärentää — "forge, counterfeit, falsify"

**Pattern:** [AGENT] + väärentää + [OBJECT]
**Morphology:** väären-tä-a (false-CAUS-INF)
**Derivation:** Causative from *väärä* (different stem vowel from *vääristää*)

**Examples:**
- **Väärentää allekirjoitus** / väärentää allekirjoitus / "forge a signature"
- **Väärentää asiakirja** / väärentää asiakirja / "falsify a document"
- **Hän väärenti todistuksen.** / hän väärenti todistuksen / "He/she forged the certificate."

**Usage notes:** Legal/criminal term. Emphasizes deliberate creation of false copies or falsification. Always intentional. Formal/legal register. Distinct from *vääristää* (distort).

**Aspectual properties:** Telic. Perfective typical (*väärenti*).

**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### 4.3 tehdä vääräksi — "make false/wrong" (translative construction)

**Pattern:** [AGENT] + tekee + [OBJECT] + vääräksi
**Morphology:** teh-dä väärä-ksi (make-INF wrong-TRANSLATIVE)
**Note:** Productive construction but less common than specific verbs

**Examples:**
- **Se tekee väitteen vääräksi.** / se tekee väitteen vääräksi / "That makes the claim false."
- (less common than *todistaa vääräksi* "prove false")

**Usage notes:** Grammatically possible but less idiomatic than specific verbs (*vääristää*, *väärentää*) or *todistaa vääräksi* "prove false." Productive pattern but not preferred for truth-domain.

**Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

### 4.4 kumota — "refute, overturn" (not in P1 but common)

**Pattern:** [AGENT] + kumoaa + [OBJECT]
**Morphology:** kumoa-a (overturn-INF)
**Note:** *Kumota* not in P1 but standard for refutation

**Examples:**
- **Kumota väite** / kumota väite / "refute the claim"
- **Kumota teoria** / kumota teoria / "overturn the theory"

**Usage notes:** Common in argumentative and scientific contexts. Emphasizes proving false or overturning established claims. Formal/neutral register.

**Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL] (not P1 vocabulary)

---

### 4.5 (no simple causative "make untrue")

**Pattern:** (no common expression identified)
**Note:** No simple causative from *epätosi* or general "make false" verb

**Explanation:** Finnish uses specific verbs (*vääristää* "distort," *väärentää* "forge") rather than general causative "make false/untrue." The concept is expressed through these specialized verbs or constructions like *todistaa vääräksi* "prove false."

**Epistemic status:** [UNCERTAIN] (gap in paradigm)

## 5. MAINTAINING TRUTH / STABILITY

### 5.1 pitää paikkansa — "be true, hold" (stative)

**Pattern:** [SUBJECT] + pitää paikkansa
**Morphology:** pitä-ä paikka-nsa (hold-INF place-3POSS)
**Note:** Stative expression, not change

**Examples:**
- **Väite pitää paikkansa.** / väite pitää paikkansa / "The claim is true."
- **Tiedot pitävät paikkansa.** / tiedot pitävät paikkansa / "The information is accurate."

**Usage notes:** Expresses stable truth state. No inchoative (*\*alkaa pitää paikkansa* not idiomatic). Negation *ei pidä paikkaansa* common. Formal/neutral register.

**Aspectual properties:** Stative. Present tense typical. Not change expression.

**Epistemic status:** [CORE] (but stative, not dynamic)

---

### 5.2 pysyä totena — "remain true" (rare)

**Pattern:** [SUBJECT] + pysyy totena
**Morphology:** pysy-ä tote-na (remain-INF true-ESSIVE)
**Note:** Grammatically possible but rare

**Examples:**
- **Väite pysyy totena.** / väite pysyy totena / "The claim remains true."
- (uncommon; more natural: *pitää edelleen paikkansa* "still holds")

**Usage notes:** Grammatically correct but not idiomatic. Finnish prefers *pitää paikkansa* for stable truth or *pitää edelleen paikkansa* "still holds" for continued truth.

**Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

### 5.3 säilyttää totuus — "preserve the truth" (rare)

**Pattern:** [AGENT] + säilyttää + totuuden
**Morphology:** säilyttä-ä totuude-n (preserve-INF truth-GEN)
**Note:** Grammatically possible but uncommon

**Examples:**
- **Säilyttää totuus** / säilyttää totuus / "preserve the truth"
- (rare; more common: *puhua totuutta* "speak the truth")

**Usage notes:** Grammatically correct but not common collocation. *Säilyttää* "preserve" more common with concrete objects. Truth-preservation more often expressed through *puhua totuutta* "speak truth" or *pysyä totuudessa* "stay in truth."

**Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

### 5.4 (no common expression for "keep true")

**Pattern:** (no common expression identified)
**Note:** Finnish lacks idiomatic "keep X true" construction

**Explanation:** While *pitää* "keep, hold" is common verb, *pitää totena* "keep true" is not idiomatic. Truth-maintenance expressed through stative *pitää paikkansa* "be true" or *pysyä voimassa* "remain valid" (legal contexts).

**Epistemic status:** [UNCERTAIN] (gap in paradigm)

## 9. ASPECTUAL MODIFIERS WITH TRUTH-TERMS

### 9.1 alkaa + infinitive — "begin to..."

**Pattern:** [SUBJECT] + alkaa + [INFINITIVE]
**Morphology:** alka-a + INF (begin-INF + INF)

**Examples:**

## 12. SUMMARY OF PATTERNS

### 12.1 Productive inchoative patterns

**Highly productive:**
- *todellistua* "become real" — common with dreams, plans, fears
- *toteutua* "be realized" — common with plans, projects, predictions
- *paljastua* "be revealed" — common with *totuus*, *valhe*, *tosiasiat*
- *vääristyä* "become distorted" — common with *totuus*, *tieto*, *merkitys*

**Less productive:**
- *todentua* "be verified" — formal, restricted contexts
- *käydä todeksi* "come true" — literary/emphatic

**Gaps:**
- No simple inchoative from *väärä* "false" (*\*väärtyä* does not exist)
- No simple inchoative from *epätosi* "untrue"
- No simple inchoative from *aito* "genuine"

### 12.2 Productive causative patterns

**Highly productive:**
- *todellistaa* "realize" — common with abstract objects
- *toteuttaa* "implement" — common with plans, decisions
- *todistaa* "prove" — common with claims, theories
- *vääristää* "distort" — common with *totuus*, *tosiasiat*
- *väärentää* "forge" — common with documents, signatures

**Moderately productive:**
- *todentaa* "verify" — formal/technical
- *oikeuttaa* "justify" — moral/legal discourse
- *paljastaa* "reveal" — common with *totuus*, *valhe*

**Gaps:**
- No causative from *aito* "genuine" (*\*aitouttaa* does not exist)
- No general causative "make false" (uses specific verbs)

### 12.3 Aspectual compatibility

**Compatible with progressive aspect:**
- *paljastua* "be revealed" — *on paljastumassa*
- *toteutua* "be realized" — *on toteutumassa*
- *vääristyä* "become distorted" — *on vääristymässä*

**Compatible with gradual change:**
- *vääristyä* "become distorted" — *vääristyy vähitellen*
- *paljastua* "be revealed" — *paljastuu vähitellen*

**Typically telic (sudden/complete):**
- *todellistua* "come true"
- *todentua* "be verified"
- *käydä todeksi* "come true"

### 12.4 Stative vs. dynamic

**Stative (no change):**
- *pitää paikkansa* "be true" — no inchoative form
- *olla totta* "be true" — stative only

**Dynamic (change):**
- All inchoative verbs (*-stua*, *-tua*, *-ua* patterns)
- All causative verbs (*-staa*, *-taa*, *-aa* patterns)

### 12.5 Constructional patterns

**Translative constructions (result state):**
- *todistaa vääräksi* "prove false"
- *todistaa oikeaksi* "prove correct"
- *paljastua valheeksi* "be revealed as lie"
- *osoittautua vääräksi* "turn out to be false"
- *käydä todeksi* "come true"

**Aspectual constructions:**
- *alkaa* + infinitive — inception
- *olla* + *-massa* — progressive
- *lopettaa* + gerund — cessation

**Causative chains:**
- *saada* + object + infinitive — enabling
- *antaa* + object + infinitive — permissive
- *estää* + gerund — preventive

**Negation:**
- *ei* + verb — simple negation
- *ei anna* + infinitive — negative permissive
- *estää* + gerund — active prevention

## 13. CROSS-LINGUISTIC OBSERVATIONS

### 13.1 Asymmetries in Finnish truth-domain dynamics

**1. Becoming true vs. becoming false:**
- Rich vocabulary for "becoming true/real": *todellistua*, *toteutua*, *todentua*, *käydä todeksi*
- Limited vocabulary for "becoming false": *vääristyä* (distort), constructions with *paljastua valheeksi* (be revealed as lie)
- No simple inchoative *\*väärtyä* "become false"

**2. Making true vs. making false:**
- Multiple causatives for "making true/real": *todellistaa*, *toteuttaa*, *todistaa*, *todentaa*
- Specific causatives for "making false": *vääristää* (distort), *väärentää* (forge)
- No general causative "make false" — uses specialized verbs

**3. Revelation vs. concealment:**
- Common verbs for revelation: *paljastua* "be revealed," *paljastaa* "reveal"
- Less elaborated vocabulary for concealment (outside P1 scope)

**4. Gradual vs. sudden change:**
- *Vääristyä* "become distorted" naturally gradual
- *Todellistua*, *toteutua* typically telic/sudden
- Aspectual modifiers can adjust but natural tendencies exist

### 13.2 Morphological productivity

**Highly productive patterns:**
- Inchoative *-stua/-tua/-ua* from causatives
- Causative *-staa/-taa/-aa* from adjectives/nouns
- Progressive *olla* + *-massa*
- Gerund formation for all verbs

**Selective productivity:**
- Not all adjectives generate causatives (*aito* lacks *\*aitouttaa*)
- Not all causatives generate inchoatives (though most do)
- Some gaps filled by constructions (translative, *tehdä* + translative)

### 13.3 Lexical vs. constructional expression

**Lexicalized:**
- *Valehdella* "to lie" (frequentative from *valhe*)
- *Pitää paikkansa* "be true" (fixed expression)
- *Olla oikeassa/väärässä* "be right/wrong" (fixed inessive)

**Constructional:**
- *Todistaa vääräksi* "prove false" (verb + translative)
- *Paljastua valheeksi* "be revealed as lie" (verb + translative)
- *Estää vääristyminen* "prevent distortion" (verb + gerund)

**Hybrid:**
- Some concepts expressed both ways: *vääristää* (lexical) vs. *tehdä vääräksi* (constructional)
- Lexical forms typically preferred when available

## FINAL NOTES

This analysis documents **attested dynamic expressions** in Finnish truth-domain vocabulary. Key findings:

1. **Rich inchoative morphology**: Productive *-stua/-tua/-ua* patterns create "become X" verbs
2. **Rich causative morphology**: Productive *-staa/-taa/-aa* patterns create "make X" verbs
3. **Systematic gaps**: Not all roots generate full paradigms (*aito*, *väärä* lack some expected forms)
4. **Aspectual flexibility**: Progressive, inception, cessation constructions productive
5. **Translative constructions**: Common for result states (*todistaa vääräksi*, *paljastua valheeksi*)
6. **Asymmetries**: More elaborated vocabulary for "becoming/making true" than "becoming/making false"
7. **Stative vs. dynamic**: Some expressions inherently stative (*pitää paikkansa*), others inherently dynamic (*todellistua*)

The system shows both productive morphological patterns and lexicalized expressions, with some concepts requiring constructional rather than lexical expression.
