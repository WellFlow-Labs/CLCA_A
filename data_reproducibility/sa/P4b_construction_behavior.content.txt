# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P4b Construction Usage & Constraints
# Language: Sanskrit (sa)

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Epistemic Status | Usage Notes |
|------------------|---------|-----------------|-------------|------------------|-------------|
| **1. ATTRIBUTION** | एतत् सत्यम् (अस्ति) | etat satyam (asti) | "this is true" | [CORE] | Extremely common in all registers. Copula omission in present tense is standard, not marked. Explicit copula appears in past/future or for emphasis. |
| | तद् वचनं मृषा | tad vacanaṃ mṛṣā | "that statement is false" | [CORE] | Adverbial predicates (mṛṣā, mithyā) function without copula across all tenses. No register restrictions observed. |
| **2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION** | एतत् न सत्यम् | etat na satyam | "this is not true" | [CORE] | Particle negation is unmarked, neutral strategy. Used across all contexts without restriction. |
| | तद् असत्यम् अस्ति | tad asatyam asti | "that is untrue" | [CORE] | Morphological negation (a-satya) is categorical/definitional rather than simple denial. Copula more frequently explicit with negative-prefixed adjectives. |
| **3. ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION** | सत्यं वचनम् | satyaṃ vacanam | "true statement" | [CORE] | Completely productive with all truth-domain terms. No restrictions on noun classes. Adjective-initial order slightly preferred but both orders fully acceptable. |
| | मिथ्या ज्ञानम् | mithyā jñānam | "false knowledge" | [CORE] | Adverbial forms (mithyā, mṛṣā) modify nouns without inflection, particularly common in philosophical texts. No register restrictions. |
| **4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE** | तत् सत्यं भवति | tat satyaṃ bhavati | "that becomes true / proves true" | [CORE] | Common in narrative and argumentative contexts expressing verification or revelation. No restrictions observed. |
| | मिथ्या भूतम् | mithyā bhūtam | "became false / proved illusory" | [CORE] | Past participle bhūta frequently used for completed state changes. Particularly common in Vedāntic contexts for describing illusory nature. |
| **5. CAUSATIVE** | प्रमाणं करोति | pramāṇaṃ karoti | "makes (it) authoritative, validates" | [MARGINAL] | Less grammaticalized than inchoative. Restricted primarily to technical/philosophical contexts. Light verb √kṛ construction not fully idiomatic for all truth-domain terms. |
| | मृषा करोति | mṛṣā karoti | "makes false, falsifies" | [MARGINAL] | Attested but less common than other strategies. Verification/falsification more commonly expressed through passive constructions or dedicated verbs. |
| **6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION** | सत्यता | satyatā | "truthfulness, truth (quality)" | [CORE] | Highly productive with -tā/-tva suffixation. No restrictions on base adjectives. Both suffixes fully acceptable. |
| | प्रामाण्यम् | prāmāṇyam | "validity, authoritativeness" | [CORE] | Vṛddhi-grade derivatives (prāmāṇya from pramāṇa) common in technical philosophical vocabulary. Standard in epistemological discourse. |
| **7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION** | सत्यवादिन् | satyavādin | "truth-speaker, truthful person" | [CORE] | Extremely productive compound pattern with -vādin. Used across ethical, legal, and narrative contexts without restriction. |
| | मृषावादिन् | mṛṣāvādin | "liar, one who speaks falsely" | [CORE] | Parallel productivity with negative/false terms. No register restrictions. Standard term for "liar" in ethical/legal contexts. |
| **8. VERBAL COMPLEMENTATION (SPEECH VERBS)** | सत्यं वदति | satyaṃ vadati | "speaks truth, tells the truth" | [CORE] | Extremely common across all registers. Accusative internal object construction fully productive with all speech verbs. |
| | मृषा वदति | mṛṣā vadati | "speaks falsely, lies" | [CORE] | Adverbial manner modification equally productive. Standard expression for lying. No contextual restrictions. |
| **9. EPISTEMIC VERB COMPLEMENTATION** | यथार्थं जानाति | yathārthaṃ jānāti | "knows accurately, knows truly" | [CORE] | Productive with all cognitive verbs. Common in both philosophical and general contexts. No restrictions observed. |
| | मिथ्या जानाति | mithyā jānāti | "knows falsely, has false knowledge" | [CORE] | Manner adverbs modify cognitive verbs freely. Particularly common in epistemological discussions of error/illusion. |
| **10. INSTRUMENTAL OF MEANS/MANNER** | सत्येन विजयते | satyena vijayate | "conquers by truth, triumphs through truth" | [CORE] | Extremely productive construction. Used across ethical, philosophical, and narrative contexts. No restrictions. |
| | प्रमाणेन सिद्धम् | pramāṇena siddham | "established by valid means, proven" | [CORE] | Standard construction in philosophical/logical discourse for expressing evidential basis. Completely productive. |
| **11. ABLATIVE OF SOURCE/STANDARD** | भ्रमात् उत्पन्नम् | bhramāt utpannam | "arisen from error, caused by confusion" | [CORE] | Common in causal explanations across all registers. Productive with all truth/error terms. |
| | धर्मात् न चलितव्यम् | dharmāt na chalitavyam | "one should not deviate from dharma" | [CORE] | Standard construction for expressing deviation from standards. Common in prescriptive/ethical contexts. |
| **12. GERUNDIVE OBLIGATION** | सत्यं वक्तव्यम् | satyaṃ vaktavyam | "truth must be spoken" | [CORE] | Extremely common in dharmaśāstra and ethical instruction. Fully productive with all verbal roots. |
| | मृषा न वक्तव्यम् | mṛṣā na vaktavyam | "falsehood must not be spoken" | [CORE] | Negative gerundive construction standard for prohibitions. No register restrictions. |
| **13. CONTRASTIVE COORDINATION** | सत्यं वा असत्यं वा | satyaṃ vā asatyaṃ vā | "whether true or false" | [CORE] | Common in presenting alternatives or exhaustive options. Used across all contexts. |
| | धर्माधर्मौ | dharmādharmau | "dharma and adharma, right and wrong" | [CORE] | Dvandva dual compounds for paired opposites extremely common in philosophical texts. Standard pattern for conceptual pairs. |
| **14. LOCATIVE OF DOMAIN/SPHERE** | धर्मे स्थितः | dharme sthitaḥ | "standing in dharma, established in righteousness" | [CORE] | Common with verbs of standing/remaining. Used across ethical and philosophical contexts. |
| | तत्त्वे | tattve | "in reality, with respect to truth" | [CORE] | Locative expressing "with respect to" fully productive. Common in philosophical analysis. |
| **15. INTERROGATIVE CHALLENGE** | किं प्रमाणम् | kiṃ pramāṇam | "what is the proof/authority?" | [CORE] | Extremely common in philosophical debate and dialectical contexts. Standard challenge formula. |
| | कथं तत् सत्यम् | kathaṃ tat satyam | "how is that true?" | [CORE] | Productive with all interrogatives. Used across argumentative and narrative contexts. |
| **16. EMPHATIC ASSERTION** | सत्यमेव जयते | satyam eva jayate | "truth alone conquers" | [CORE] | Particle eva extremely common for emphasis. This specific phrase is famous Upaniṣadic motto, but pattern fully productive. |
| | मिथ्यैव | mithyaiva | "falsely indeed, merely illusory" | [CORE] | Eva with negative/false terms equally productive. Common in Vedāntic contexts for emphasizing illusory nature. |
| **17. COMPARATIVE CONSTRUCTION** | अस्मात् सत्यतरम् | asmāt satyataram | "truer than this" | [MARGINAL] | Comparative -tara suffix productive but less commonly applied to truth-domain adjectives than to scalar adjectives. Context-dependent acceptability. |
| | प्रत्यक्षात् अनुमानम् अल्पप्रामाण्यम् | pratyakṣāt anumānam alpaprāmāṇyam | "inference has less validity than perception" | [CORE] | Ablative comparison with explicit degree terms (alpa, adhika) common in philosophical texts comparing epistemic sources. |
| **18. RESULTATIVE/EVIDENTIAL CONSTRUCTION** | प्रत्यक्षेण दृष्टम् | pratyakṣeṇa dṛṣṭam | "seen by perception, directly perceived" | [CORE] | Standard construction in epistemological contexts. Instrumental + passive participle fully productive for expressing evidential basis. |
| | प्रमाणेन सिद्धम् | pramāṇena siddham | "established by valid means, proven" | [CORE] | Extremely common in philosophical argumentation. No restrictions. Standard formula for validated knowledge. |
| **19. CONDITIONAL TRUTH** | यदि सत्यम् तदा धर्मः | yadi satyam tadā dharmaḥ | "if true, then (it is) dharma" | [CORE] | Standard conditional construction. Used across all registers for hypothetical reasoning. |
| | सत्ये सति धर्मः भवति | satye sati dharmaḥ bhavati | "truth existing, dharma arises" | [CORE] | Locative absolute construction common in philosophical texts for expressing conditions. Slightly more formal than yadi...tadā. |
| **20. NEGATIVE EXISTENTIAL (ABSENCE OF TRUTH)** | प्रमाणाभावात् | pramāṇābhāvāt | "due to absence of proof" | [CORE] | Abhāva-compounds extremely productive. Common in philosophical refutation and causal reasoning. |
| | न सत्यम् अस्ति | na satyam asti | "there is no truth" | [CORE] | Simple negative existential fully productive. Used across all contexts without restriction. |

## ALTERNATE CONSTRUCTIONS ATTESTED

### Construction 5 (Causative) - Alternates:
- **Denominative verbs**: pramāṇīkaroti "authenticates" (noted as less common)
- **Passive with instrumental**: pramāṇena sidh "is established by valid means" (noted as more common alternative)

### Construction 12 (Gerundive) - Alternates:
- **Potential participle in -ya**: vācya "to be spoken" (noted as alternative to -tavya)

### Construction 17 (Comparative) - Alternates:
- **Explicit degree terms**: adhika "more," nyūna "less," uttara "superior" (noted as alternatives to -tara suffix)

### Construction 19 (Conditional) - Alternates:
- **Gerund in -tvā**: for temporal sequence (noted as alternative to conditional constructions)

## GENERAL OBSERVATIONS

1. **High productivity**: Constructions 1-4, 6-14, 16, 18-20 show [CORE] status with no observable restrictions
2. **Marginal patterns**: Only constructions 5 (Causative) and 17 (Comparative with -tara) show [MARGINAL] status
3. **Register variation**: Minimal. Most constructions function across philosophical, ethical, legal, and narrative contexts
4. **Copula behavior**: Systematic omission in present tense nominal sentences (Construction 1) is standard, not marked
5. **Philosophical specialization**: Several constructions (10, 17, 18) particularly common in epistemological discourse but not restricted to it
