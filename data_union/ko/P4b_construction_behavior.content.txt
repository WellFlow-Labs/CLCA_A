## Summary Observations

1. **All constructions rated [CORE] except three**: 거짓되다 (Construction 9, stative predicate with 되다) is rated [MARGINAL] due to its restriction to literary and formal written registers and its lower frequency relative to competing forms (거짓이다, 거짓말이다). 올바름 and 옳음 (Construction 6, -(으)ㅁ nominalization) are rated [MARGINAL] due to restriction to philosophical and literary registers.

2. **No construction is rated [UNCERTAIN]**: All constructions are sufficiently attested and described to support a definite naturalness rating.

3. **Register stratification is the primary axis of variation**: The constructions themselves are generally stable; what varies is register appropriateness (e.g., 거짓말쟁이 vs. 위조범; 정직히 vs. 정직하게; 진실로 vs. 실제로; 판명되다 vs. 밝혀지다 vs. 드러나다).

4. **Complementary distribution is confirmed**: The -성 / -음 nominalization split, the -히 / -게 adverbial split, and the 하다 / 되다 predicate split all show base-type or semantic conditioning rather than free variation.

5. **No symmetric truth-teller agentive is attested**: The absence of a lexicalized agentive noun for "truth-teller" parallel to 거짓말쟁이 is confirmed as a genuine structural gap, not an oversight. The concept requires periphrastic expression (진실을 말하는 사람).

6. **The -화하다 causative pattern is restricted to Sino-Korean bases**: Native Korean bases (진짜, 가짜, 거짓, 옳-) do not participate in this pattern; no forms such as *진짜화하다 or *옳화하다 are attested.

7. **The existential predication frame (있다/없다) and the scalar degree frame (높다/낮다) are related but distinct**: Both apply to 성-nominalized and 도-nominalized quality nouns, but they are not interchangeable. The existential frame treats the property as a binary possession; the scalar frame treats it as a quantifiable degree. This construction is restricted to nominal bases denoting assessable properties and is not available for all truth-domain nominals.

8. **Morphological negation (부정확하다, 무효하다) is attested as a formal/written strategy**: Reported as productive in that register but noted as distinct from the more broadly productive syntactic negation (-지 않다) and copular negation (아니다) patterns.

9. **The -기 nominalization pattern is noted in P4a as attested but is not exemplified with truth-domain vocabulary**: It is not expanded beyond what P4a reports.

10. **The periphrastic causative -게 하다 exists but is not specific to the truth domain**: It is not exemplified here as a distinct truth-domain construction.
