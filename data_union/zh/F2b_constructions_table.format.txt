| 真 (zhēn) | 这是真的 | zhè shì zhēn de | This is true / This is real / This is genuine. | The copular construction X + 是 + 真的 is the most basic predication of truth or genuineness in Chinese. The particle 的 nominalizes or substantivizes 真, producing a predicative structure. Ubiquitous across all registers and contexts. Functions as the default construction for asserting that something is not fake or not false. | [CORE] |
| 真 (zhēn) | 是真是假 | shì zhēn shì jiǎ | Whether it is true or false; is it real or fake? | The parallel structure 是X是Y presents two alternatives for evaluation. This construction frames the authenticity question as an open binary and is common in investigative, commercial, and everyday discourse. Used in everyday speech and in contexts of evaluating claims, objects, or situations ("说不清是真是假" — can't tell whether it's true or false). Closely related to 真假难辨. | [CORE] |
| 真 (zhēn) | 说真的 | shuō zhēn de | To be honest; speaking truthfully; honestly speaking. | A discourse-initial or parenthetical expression used to signal that the speaker is about to make a frank or sincere statement, or to assert that one is speaking seriously rather than jokingly. Parallel in function to 说实话 and 老实说. Common in conversational registers. Functions as a discourse marker across all registers of spoken Chinese. | [CORE] |
| 真 (zhēn) | 来真的 | lái zhēn de | To do it for real; to get serious; to mean business. | A colloquial construction using the verb 来 (to come/do) with 真的 to assert that something is being done genuinely rather than as a pretense or game. Contrasts with 来假的 (to go through the motions). Used in everyday speech ("他是来真的" — he's serious about this / he means it for real). Common in informal spoken registers. | [CORE] |
| 真 (zhēn) | 不是真的 | bù shì zhēn de | It is not true / It is not real / It is not genuine. | The negated copular construction, parallel to Construction 1. | 真 (zhēn) | 当真吗？ | dàng zhēn ma? | Are you serious? Do you really mean it? Distinct from the lexicalized 当真 (P1b) in that it functions as a full interrogative construction. | [CORE] |

Used in evaluative discourse about accounts, claims, or representations. | [CORE] |
| 真 (zhēn) | 说真话 | shuō zhēnhuà | To tell the truth; to speak truthfully. | A common verb-object collocation meaning to speak truthfully. Contrasts directly with 说假话 (to tell lies). Used across moral, legal, and everyday contexts. | [CORE] |
| 真 (zhēn) | 真有其事 | zhēn yǒu qí shì | It really happened; the matter is real; it's a true story. | A fixed predicate construction asserting that a reported event or matter actually occurred. | 真 (zhēn) | 假戏真做 | jiǎ xì zhēn zuò | To play a fake role as if it were real; to act out a fiction as though it were genuine. | A fixed expression describing the situation in which someone performs a pretense with genuine commitment, so that the boundary between fiction and reality blurs. | 假 (jiǎ) | 这是假的 | zhè shì jiǎ de | This is fake / This is false / This is not genuine. | The copular construction X + 是 + 假的, parallel to Construction 1 for 真. Overlaps with 造假 but 作假 more often implies procedural or documentary falsification. | [CORE] |
| 假 (jiǎ) | 说假话 | shuō jiǎhuà | To tell a lie; to speak falsely. | The standard verb-object collocation for lying or making false statements. Directly contrasts with 说真话. Extremely common across all registers. | [CORE] |
