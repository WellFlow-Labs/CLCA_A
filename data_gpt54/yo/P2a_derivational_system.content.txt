## 1. Base Form: **tọ́**
### Derived Form: **òtítọ́**
- **Transliteration:** òtítọ́
- **Literal Breakdown:** ò- + títọ́
- **Natural English translation:** truth; correctness
- **Usage description:** Common noun for truth or correctness in everyday speech, argument, religion, and moral discussion. It refers to the truth of statements and the correctness of accounts or matters.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## 2. Base Form: **tọ́**
### Derived Form: **òtọ́**
- **Transliteration:** òtọ́
- **Literal Breakdown:** nominal form from tọ́
- **Natural English translation:** truth; correctness; genuineness
- **Usage description:** Used for what is true, correct, or genuine, often overlapping with *òtítọ́*. It also appears in contrasts with falsity or imitation.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## 3. Base Form: **tọ́**
### Derived Form: **títọ́**
- **Transliteration:** títọ́
- **Literal Breakdown:** nominal/adjectival form from tọ́
- **Natural English translation:** straightness; correctness; truth; properness
- **Usage description:** Used both literally for straightness and abstractly for correctness or truth. It also appears in evaluations of conduct, procedure, or statements.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## 4. Base Form: **tọ́**
### Derived Form: **ìtọ́sọ́nà**
- **Transliteration:** ìtọ́sọ́nà
- **Literal Breakdown:** nominalization + direct/straighten + way
- **Natural English translation:** guidance; direction; instruction
- **Usage description:** Common in educational, administrative, and practical contexts for directions or guidance. It is also used figuratively for moral or spiritual guidance.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## 5. Base Form: **tọ́ka sí**

- **Usage description:** Used in books, documents, and formal writing for indexing or reference. It is a stable nominal derivative in literate and administrative usage.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## 6. Base Form: **tọ́ka sí**

## 10. Base Form: **rí**
### Derived Form: **ẹ̀rí**
- **Transliteration:** ẹ̀rí
- **Literal Breakdown:** nominal form from rí “see”
- **Natural English translation:** evidence; proof; testimony
- **Usage description:** Used for evidence or proof supporting a claim or establishing an event. It is common in legal, religious, and everyday argumentative contexts.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## 11. Base Form: **ẹ̀rí**
### Derived Form: **ẹlẹ́rìí**
- **Transliteration:** ẹlẹ́rìí
- **Literal Breakdown:** bearer/owner of testimony
- **Natural English translation:** witness
- **Usage description:** Standard term for a witness in court, public procedure, religious testimony, and narrative reporting. It is a clear person-denoting derivative from the testimony/evidence form.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## 12. Base Form: **rí**
### Derived Form: **ríran**
- **Transliteration:** ríran
- **Literal Breakdown:** seeing
- **Natural English translation:** sight; seeing; vision
- **Usage description:** Used literally for visual perception and sometimes in extended evaluative or spiritual contexts. Its connection to the truth domain is peripheral, but the form itself is established.
- **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]
