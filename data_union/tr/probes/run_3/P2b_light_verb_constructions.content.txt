# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2b Compositional Constructions
# Language: Turkish (tr)

---

## Preliminary Note on Scope

This step documents attested multi-word constructions involving the 30 P1 lexical items. Constructions documented here are phrasal, periphrastic, idiomatic, or syntactically-driven combinations — not morphological derivations (handled in P2a). Where P1b or P2a already cited a multi-word expression as a collocation or fixed phrase, it is included here with full documentation if it meets the construction criteria; cross-references are provided.

The following categories of construction are distinguished where the data supports the distinction:

- **Verb-object collocations** (e.g., *doğruyu söylemek*)
- **Predicate constructions** (e.g., *haklı çıkmak*)
- **Postpositional phrases** (e.g., *gerçek anlamda*)
- **Binomials and fixed nominal phrases** (e.g., *doğru dürüst*)
- **Idiomatic expressions** (e.g., *hakkını yemek*)
- **Proverbial expressions** (e.g., *doğruya doğru, eğriye eğri demek*)

Absence of constructions is recorded explicitly for each P1 item where no common construction is identified.

## Item 1 — Base Form: *doğru*

---

**Construction 1**
- **Base Form:** doğru
- **Example:** Doğruyu söylemek gerekiyor.
- **Transliteration:** doğruyu söylemek
- **English translation:** to tell the truth
- **Usage notes:** Standard verb-object collocation in which *doğru* takes accusative marking as the direct object of *söylemek* (to say/tell). Used across all registers in moral and epistemic contexts. The variant *doğru söylemek* (without accusative) is also attested and common.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 2**
- **Base Form:** doğru
- **Example:** Bunu doğru bulmuyorum.
- **Transliteration:** doğru bulmak
- **English translation:** to find correct, to consider right, to approve of
- **Usage notes:** Predicate construction in which *doğru* functions as a secondary predicate with *bulmak* (to find, to consider). Used to express evaluative judgment that something is correct or appropriate. *Bunu doğru bulmuyorum* ("I don't find this right / I don't approve of this").
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 3**
- **Base Form:** doğru
- **Example:** Tahmini doğru çıktı.
- **Transliteration:** doğru çıkmak
- **English translation:** to turn out to be correct, to prove true
- **Usage notes:** Predicate construction used to describe a prediction, claim, or suspicion that is subsequently confirmed by events. *Tahmini doğru çıktı* ("His/her prediction turned out to be correct"). Common in everyday and journalistic language.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 4**
- **Base Form:** doğru
- **Example:** Doğrudan doğruya ona söyledim.
- **Transliteration:** doğrudan doğruya
- **English translation:** directly, straight, without any intermediary, point-blank
- **Usage notes:** Emphatic reduplicated adverbial phrase formed from the ablative *doğrudan* and the dative *doğruya*. More emphatic than *doğrudan* alone; stresses the complete absence of mediation or indirection. Used across registers.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 5**
- **Base Form:** doğru
- **Example:** Doğru dürüst bir iş yapamıyor.
- **Transliteration:** doğru dürüst
- **English translation:** properly, decently, adequately; (of a person) decent and honest
- **Usage notes:** Fixed binomial pairing *doğru* (correct, proper) with *dürüst* (upright, honest — from Persian *durust*). Used as an adverbial or adjectival phrase to indicate that something is done in a fully proper manner or that a person is genuinely decent. Very common in colloquial speech.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 6**
- **Base Form:** doğru
- **Example:** Doğru düzgün konuşamıyor.
- **Transliteration:** doğru düzgün
- **English translation:** properly, in an orderly and correct manner
- **Usage notes:** Colloquial binomial pairing *doğru* (correct) with *düzgün* (orderly, neat). Used to indicate that something is done in a fully adequate and proper manner. Interchangeable with *doğru dürüst* in many contexts but slightly more focused on orderliness.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 7**
- **Base Form:** doğru
- **Example:** Doğru yoldan çıkmak tehlikelidir.
- **Transliteration:** doğru yol
- **English translation:** the right path, the correct way, the straight and narrow
- **Usage notes:** Fixed nominal phrase used in moral, religious, and practical contexts to refer to the correct course of action or life. Participates in further constructions: *doğru yoldan çıkmak* ("to stray from the right path"), *doğru yolda olmak* ("to be on the right track").
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 8**
- **Base Form:** doğru
- **Example:** Doğru yoldasınız.
- **Transliteration:** doğru yolda olmak
- **English translation:** to be on the right track, to be on the right path
- **Usage notes:** Locative predicate construction combining *doğru yol* with the locative suffix *-da* and *olmak* (to be). Used in moral, practical, and evaluative contexts to indicate that a person or process is proceeding correctly.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 9**
- **Base Form:** doğru
- **Example:** O doğruya doğru, eğriye eğri der.
- **Transliteration:** doğruya doğru, eğriye eğri demek
- **English translation:** to call a spade a spade, to call things as they are
- **Usage notes:** Proverbial expression prescribing or praising honest, unvarnished evaluation. The structure pairs *doğru* (straight/right) with its antonym *eğri* (crooked/wrong), asserting that each should be named as it is. Used to commend or demand honest speech.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 10**
- **Base Form:** doğru
- **Example:** Bu iddiada bir doğruluk payı var.
- **Transliteration:** doğruluk payı olmak / doğruluk payı var
- **English translation:** there is a grain of truth (in something)
- **Usage notes:** Existential construction using *doğruluk payı* (grain/share of truth) with *var* (there is) or *olmak* (to be). Used in evaluative discourse to acknowledge partial truth in a claim that is not entirely accurate. Common in journalistic and critical language.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 11**
- **Base Form:** doğru
- **Example:** Doğru orantılı iki değişken.
- **Transliteration:** doğru orantılı
- **English translation:** directly proportional
- **Usage notes:** Fixed adjectival phrase used in mathematical and scientific registers to describe two quantities that increase or decrease together. *Hız ve mesafe doğru orantılıdır* ("Speed and distance are directly proportional"). Domain-specific; not used outside technical contexts.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 12**
- **Base Form:** doğru
- **Example:** Doğru açı doksan derecedir.
- **Transliteration:** doğru açı
- **English translation:** right angle
- **Usage notes:** Fixed nominal phrase used in geometry and mathematics education for a 90-degree angle. Domain-specific; the word *doğru* here contributes the geometric sense "right" rather than the epistemic sense "correct/true."
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Item 2 — Base Form: *gerçek*

---

**Construction 1**
- **Base Form:** gerçek
- **Example:** Gerçek anlamda bir lider.
- **Transliteration:** gerçek anlamda
- **English translation:** in the true sense, in the real sense of the word
- **Usage notes:** Postpositional phrase using *anlam* (meaning) in the locative. Used to specify that a term is being applied in its full, genuine sense rather than loosely or metaphorically. Common in evaluative and critical prose.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 2**
- **Base Form:** gerçek
- **Example:** Gerçek anlamıyla bir trajedi.
- **Transliteration:** gerçek anlamıyla
- **English translation:** in the true sense of the word, literally, in the full meaning
- **Usage notes:** Instrumental variant of *gerçek anlamda*; uses the possessed instrumental form *anlamıyla*. Slightly more emphatic than *gerçek anlamda*; both are common in formal and journalistic prose.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 3**
- **Base Form:** gerçek
- **Example:** Gerçek hayatta böyle olmaz.
- **Transliteration:** gerçek hayatta
- **English translation:** in real life
- **Usage notes:** Locative phrase contrasting actual lived experience with fictional, hypothetical, or digital contexts. Very common in everyday speech and writing. *Gerçek hayat* (real life) also functions as a standalone nominal phrase.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 4**
- **Base Form:** gerçek
- **Example:** Film gerçeği yansıtıyor mu?
- **Transliteration:** gerçeği yansıtmak
- **English translation:** to reflect reality, to reflect the truth
- **Usage notes:** Verb-object collocation in which *gerçek* takes accusative marking as the direct object of *yansıtmak* (to reflect, to mirror). Used in journalistic, artistic, and critical contexts to evaluate whether a representation accurately mirrors actual conditions.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 5**
- **Base Form:** gerçek
- **Example:** Gerçeği çarpıtıyorsunuz.
- **Transliteration:** gerçeği çarpıtmak
- **English translation:** to distort the truth, to distort reality
- **Usage notes:** Verb-object collocation used in critical and journalistic discourse to accuse someone of misrepresenting facts or reality. *Çarpıtmak* (to distort, to twist) is the standard verb in this collocation.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 6**
- **Base Form:** gerçek
- **Example:** Gerçeği kabul etmek zor.
- **Transliteration:** gerçeği kabul etmek
- **English translation:** to accept the truth, to face reality
- **Usage notes:** Verb-object collocation describing the act of acknowledging an unwelcome truth. *Kabul etmek* (to accept) is the standard verb; the construction is common in psychological, moral, and everyday contexts.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 7**
- **Base Form:** gerçek
- **Example:** Gerçekle yüzleşmek gerekiyor.
- **Transliteration:** gerçekle yüzleşmek / gerçek ile yüzleşmek
- **English translation:** to confront the truth, to face reality
- **Usage notes:** Comitative construction using *ile/le* (with) and *yüzleşmek* (to confront, to face). Used in psychological and moral discourse to describe the act of directly confronting an uncomfortable truth. The contracted form *gerçekle* is more common in speech.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 8**
- **Base Form:** gerçek
- **Example:** Gerçek dışı bir durum.
- **Transliteration:** gerçek dışı
- **English translation:** unreal, surreal, beyond reality, fictitious
- **Usage notes:** Postpositional phrase using *dışı* (outside of) to negate or exceed the domain of reality. Used to describe surreal experiences, fictional scenarios, or implausible claims. Functions as an attributive adjective.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 9**
- **Base Form:** gerçek
- **Example:** Gerçek zamanlı veri işleme.
- **Transliteration:** gerçek zamanlı
- **English translation:** real-time
- **Usage notes:** Fixed adjectival phrase used in computing, broadcasting, and communications to describe processes that occur without delay. *Gerçek zamanlı veri* ("real-time data") is a standard technical compound. Domain-specific.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 10**
- **Base Form:** gerçek
- **Example:** Sanal gerçeklik gözlüğü taktı.
- **Transliteration:** sanal gerçeklik
- **English translation:** virtual reality
- **Usage notes:** Fixed nominal compound used as the standard Turkish term for virtual reality technology. *Sanal* (virtual) modifies *gerçeklik* (reality). Domain-specific to computing and digital media.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 11**
- **Base Form:** gerçek
- **Example:** Artırılmış gerçeklik uygulaması indirdi.
- **Transliteration:** artırılmış gerçeklik
- **English translation:** augmented reality
- **Usage notes:** Fixed nominal compound used as the standard Turkish term for augmented reality technology. *Artırılmış* (augmented, passive participle of *artırmak*) modifies *gerçeklik*. Domain-specific.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Item 3 — Base Form: *hakikat*

---

**Construction 1**
- **Base Form:** hakikat
- **Example:** Hakikati söylemek her zaman kolay değildir.
- **Transliteration:** hakikati söylemek
- **English translation:** to tell the truth, to speak the truth (in a formal or weighty sense)
- **Usage notes:** Verb-object collocation in which *hakikat* takes accusative marking as the direct object of *söylemek*. More solemn and formal than *doğruyu söylemek*; used in philosophical, literary, and religious registers.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 2**
- **Base Form:** hakikat
- **Example:** Hakikat arayışı felsefenin özündedir.
- **Transliteration:** hakikat arayışı
- **English translation:** the search for truth, the quest for truth
- **Usage notes:** Fixed nominal phrase combining *hakikat* (truth) with *arayış* (search, quest). Used in philosophical, religious, and intellectual discourse to describe the pursuit of fundamental truth. Common in academic and literary prose.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 3**
- **Base Form:** hakikat
- **Example:** Hakikat karşısında susmak.
- **Transliteration:** hakikat karşısında
- **English translation:** in the face of truth, confronted with the truth
- **Usage notes:** Postpositional phrase using *karşısında* (in the face of, opposite). Used in philosophical and rhetorical contexts to describe a situation where one must respond to or acknowledge fundamental truth.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 4**
- **Base Form:** hakikat
- **Example:** Hakikati aramak insanın doğasında vardır.
- **Transliteration:** hakikati aramak
- **English translation:** to seek the truth, to search for truth
- **Usage notes:** Verb-object collocation in which *hakikat* takes accusative marking as the direct object of *aramak* (to seek, to search for). Used in philosophical and religious registers. Closely related to *hakikat arayışı* but verbal rather than nominal.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 5**
- **Base Form:** hakikat
- **Example:** Hakikat acı olabilir.
- **Transliteration:** hakikat acı olabilir / acı hakikat
- **English translation:** the truth can be bitter / bitter truth
- **Usage notes:** Predicative and attributive constructions pairing *hakikat* with *acı* (bitter, painful). Used in proverbial and reflective discourse to acknowledge that truth is sometimes unwelcome. *Acı hakikat* functions as a fixed nominal phrase.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 6**
- **Base Form:** hakikat
- **Example:** Hakikat er ya da geç ortaya çıkar.
- **Transliteration:** hakikat ortaya çıkmak
- **English translation:** the truth comes out, truth will out
- **Usage notes:** Predicate construction using *ortaya çıkmak* (to come out, to emerge) with *hakikat* as subject. Used in proverbial and moral discourse to assert that truth eventually becomes known. *Er ya da geç* ("sooner or later") is a common accompanying phrase.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Item 4 — Base Form: *hak*

---

**Construction 1**
- **Base Form:** hak
- **Example:** Sana hak veriyorum.
- **Transliteration:** hak vermek
- **English translation:** to concede that someone is right, to grant that someone has a point
- **Usage notes:** Verb-object collocation in which *hak* is the direct object of *vermek* (to give). Used in everyday discourse to acknowledge that another person's position or claim is correct. *Sana hak veriyorum* ("I concede you are right"). Very common in conversation.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 2**
- **Base Form:** hak
- **Example:** İşinin hakkını vermek.
- **Transliteration:** hakkını vermek
- **English translation:** to give something its due, to do justice to something, to give credit where it is due
- **Usage notes:** Idiomatic construction using the possessed accusative *hakkını* with *vermek*. Indicates that something or someone receives the recognition or treatment they deserve. Distinct from *hak vermek* (to concede someone is right) by the possessive marking.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 3**
- **Base Form:** hak
- **Example:** Mahkemede hakkını aradı.
- **Transliteration:** hakkını aramak
- **English translation:** to seek one's rights, to pursue justice for oneself
- **Usage notes:** Verb-object collocation using the possessed accusative *hakkını* with *aramak* (to seek). Used in legal and moral contexts to describe the act of pursuing one's entitlements or seeking redress.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 4**
- **Base Form:** hak
- **Example:** Kimsenin hakkını yemem.
- **Transliteration:** hakkını yemek
- **English translation:** to deprive someone of their due, to take what is not one's right, to wrong someone
- **Usage notes:** Idiomatic construction using *yemek* (to eat) in the sense of consuming or taking wrongfully. Used to describe the act of taking or withholding what rightfully belongs to another. *Kimsenin hakkını yemem* ("I won't deprive anyone of their due") is a common assertion of personal integrity.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 5**
- **Base Form:** hak
- **Example:** Oy kullanma hakkını kazandı.
- **Transliteration:** hak kazanmak
- **English translation:** to earn the right, to acquire entitlement
- **Usage notes:** Verb-object collocation using *kazanmak* (to earn, to win) with *hak* as direct object. Used in legal and everyday contexts to describe the acquisition of a right or entitlement through action or qualification.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 6**
- **Base Form:** hak
- **Example:** Bunu hak etti.
- **Transliteration:** hak etmek
- **English translation:** to deserve, to merit, to earn
- **Usage notes:** Light-verb construction using *etmek* with *hak* as the nominal element. Used to assert that a person or thing merits a particular outcome or treatment. Very common across all registers.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 7**
- **Base Form:** hak
- **Example:** Azınlıkların haklarını tanımamak.
- **Transliteration:** hak tanımamak
- **English translation:** to not recognize someone's rights, to deny rights
- **Usage notes:** Negative verb-object construction using *tanımamak* (to not recognize, to refuse to acknowledge) with *hak* as direct object. Used in legal and political discourse to describe the refusal to acknowledge a person's or group's entitlements.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 8**
- **Base Form:** hak
- **Example:** Herkese hakkını gözetmek.
- **Transliteration:** hak gözetmek
- **English translation:** to uphold rights, to protect what is due, to observe equity
- **Usage notes:** Verb-object collocation using *gözetmek* (to watch over, to protect) with *hak* as direct object. Used in legal and moral contexts to describe the act of protecting or respecting rights and entitlements.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 9**
- **Base Form:** hak
- **Example:** Hak ve adalet için mücadele etmek.
- **Transliteration:** hak ve adalet
- **English translation:** right and justice, truth and justice
- **Usage notes:** Fixed binomial pairing *hak* with *adalet* (justice — from Arabic *ʿadāla*). Used in moral, legal, and political discourse to invoke the combined principles of rightness and justice. Common in formal and rhetorical registers.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 10**
- **Base Form:** hak
- **Example:** Hak-hukukunu bilmek lazım.
- **Transliteration:** hak-hukuk
- **English translation:** one's rights and legal entitlements
- **Usage notes:** Colloquial binomial pairing *hak* with *hukuk* (law — from Arabic *ḥuqūq*, plural of *ḥaqq*). Used to refer collectively to a person's legal rights and entitlements. *Hak-hukukunu bilmek* ("to know one's rights") is a common expression.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 11**
- **Base Form:** hak
- **Example:** İnsan hakları ihlal edildi.
- **Transliteration:** insan hakları
- **English translation:** human rights
- **Usage notes:** Fixed nominal compound used as the standard legal and political term for the domain of fundamental human rights. Central to international law, political discourse, and civil society. *İnsan hakları ihlali* ("human rights violation") is a standard further compound.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 12**
- **Base Form:** hak
- **Example:** Telif hakkı sahibi itiraz etti.
- **Transliteration:** telif hakkı
- **English translation:** copyright
- **Usage notes:** Fixed nominal compound used as the standard legal term for copyright. *Telif* (composition, authorship — from Arabic *taʾlīf*) + *hakkı* (its right, possessive). Used in publishing, media, and intellectual property law.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 13**
- **Base Form:** hak
- **Example:** Hak ihlalleri raporlandı.
- **Transliteration:** hak ihlali
- **English translation:** rights violation, infringement of rights
- **Usage notes:** Fixed nominal compound used as the standard legal and human rights term for the violation of a person's rights. *İhlal* (violation — from Arabic *iḫlāl*) takes possessive marking in the compound.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Item 5 — Base Form: *haklı*

---

**Construction 1**
- **Base Form:** haklı
- **Example:** Sonunda haklı çıktı.
- **Transliteration:** haklı çıkmak
- **English translation:** to be proven right, to turn out to be correct
- **Usage notes:** Predicate construction using *çıkmak* (to come out, to turn out) with *haklı* as secondary predicate. Used to describe the outcome in which a person's claim or position is subsequently confirmed. Common in everyday and journalistic language.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 2**
- **Base Form:** haklı
- **Example:** Haklı bir şikâyet bu.
- **Transliteration:** haklı şikâyet / haklı eleştiri / haklı itiraz
- **English translation:** a justified complaint / a justified criticism / a justified objection
- **Usage notes:** Attributive construction in which *haklı* modifies nouns denoting complaints, criticisms, or objections to indicate that they are warranted. Productive pattern; *haklı* combines freely with nouns in this evaluative function.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 3**
- **Base Form:** haklı
- **Example:** Haklı olduğunu kanıtladı.
- **Transliteration:** haklı olmak
- **English translation:** to be right, to be in the right, to be justified
- **Usage notes:** Copular construction using *olmak* (to be) with *haklı* as predicate. The standard way of asserting that a person is in the right. *Haklısın* ("You are right") is the most common conversational form.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 4**
- **Base Form:** haklı
- **Example:** Haklı olarak itiraz etti.
- **Transliteration:** haklı olarak
- **English translation:** rightly, justifiably, with justification
- **Usage notes:** Adverbial construction using the converb *olarak* (as, being) with *haklı*. Used to characterize an action as justified or warranted. Common in formal and journalistic registers.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## Item 6 — Base Form: *yanlış*

---

**Construction 1**
- **Base Form:** yanlış
- **Example:** Yanlış anlamak istemiyorum.
- **Transliteration:** yanlış anlamak
- **English translation:** to misunderstand, to understand incorrectly
- **Usage notes:** Adverbial construction in which *yanlış* modifies *anlamak* (to understand). Used to describe the act of understanding something incorrectly. *Yanlış anlaşılmak* (to be misunderstood) is the passive counterpart. Very common in everyday speech.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 2**
- **Base Form:** yanlış
- **Example:** Yanlış bilgi vermek.
- **Transliteration:** yanlış bilgi vermek
- **English translation:** to give incorrect information, to misinform
- **Usage notes:** Adverbial-nominal construction in which *yanlış* modifies *bilgi* (information) and the whole phrase functions as the object of *vermek* (to give). Used in journalistic, legal, and administrative contexts to describe the provision of incorrect information.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 3**
- **Base Form:** yanlış
- **Example:** Yanlış yapmak insani bir şeydir.
- **Transliteration:** yanlış yapmak
- **English translation:** to make a mistake, to do wrong
- **Usage notes:** Verb-object construction in which *yanlış* functions as the direct object of *yapmak* (to do, to make). Used across all registers to describe committing an error or doing something wrong. *Yanlış yaptım* ("I made a mistake / I did wrong").
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 4**
- **Base Form:** yanlış
- **Example:** Yanlış gitmek istemiyorum.
- **Transliteration:** yanlış gitmek
- **English translation:** to go wrong, to take a wrong turn
- **Usage notes:** Adverbial construction in which *yanlış* modifies *gitmek* (to go). Used in both literal (taking a wrong road) and figurative (a plan going wrong) contexts. Common in everyday speech.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 5**
- **Base Form:** yanlış
- **Example:** Yanlış hesap Bağdat'tan döner.
- **Transliteration:** yanlış hesap Bağdat'tan döner
- **English translation:** A wrong calculation comes back from Baghdad (proverb: miscalculations have consequences; you can't escape the results of your errors)
- **Usage notes:** Proverbial expression warning that errors in planning or calculation will eventually return to cause problems. *Hesap* (calculation, account) + *Bağdat* (Baghdad, used as a distant place). Common in everyday moral discourse.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 6**
- **Base Form:** yanlış
- **Example:** Yanlış anlaşılmasın.
- **Transliteration:** yanlış anlaşılmak
- **English translation:** to be misunderstood
- **Usage notes:** Passive construction using *anlaşılmak* (to be understood) with *yanlış* as adverbial modifier. *Yanlış anlaşılmasın* ("Let it not be misunderstood") is a very common hedging phrase used to preempt misinterpretation.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Item 7 — Base Form: *sahte*

---

**Construction 1**
- **Base Form:** sahte
- **Example:** Sahte para basmak suçtur.
- **Transliteration:** sahte para
- **English translation:** counterfeit money, fake currency
- **Usage notes:** Fixed attributive compound used in legal and commercial contexts. *Sahte para basmak* ("to print counterfeit money") is a standard legal phrase. One of the most common collocations of *sahte*.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 2**
- **Base Form:** sahte
- **Example:** Sahte kimlikle yakalandı.

- **Base Form:** geçersiz
- **Example:** Oy geçersiz sayıldı.
- **Transliteration:** geçersiz saymak
- **English translation:** to count as invalid, to declare void
- **Usage notes:** Predicate construction using *saymak* (to count, to consider) with *geçersiz
