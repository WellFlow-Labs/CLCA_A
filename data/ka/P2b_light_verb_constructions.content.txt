# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2b Compositional Constructions
# Language: Georgian (ka)

## Multi-Word Constructions

### Construction Group 1: Truth-Telling Expressions

#### 1.1 Base: სიმართლე (simart'le) "truth"
**Construction:** სიმართლე თქვა (simart'le t'qva)
**Transliteration:** simart'le t'qva
**English translation:** to tell the truth, to speak the truth
**Usage notes:** Standard expression for truthful speech. The verb თქვა (t'qva) "to say" combines with სიმართლე in accusative case. Very common in everyday and formal discourse.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 1.2 Base: სიმართლე (simart'le) "truth"
**Construction:** სიმართლე გამოთქვა (simart'le gamot'qva)
**Transliteration:** simart'le gamot'qva
**English translation:** to express the truth, to state the truth
**Usage notes:** More formal variant using the preverb გა- (ga-) with მო- (mo-) prefix on "say." Used in formal contexts and written discourse for expressing truth.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 1.3 Base: სიმართლე (simart'le) "truth"
**Construction:** სიმართლე ამბობს (simart'le ambobs)
**Transliteration:** simart'le ambobs
**English translation:** tells the truth, speaks the truth (habitual)
**Usage notes:** Uses the verb ამბობს (ambobs) "tells/says" for habitual truth-telling. Common in character descriptions.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 1.4 Base: ჭეშმარიტი (č'ešmarit'i) "true"
**Construction:** ჭეშმარიტი სიტყვა თქვა (č'ešmarit'i sit'q'va t'qva)
**Transliteration:** č'ešmarit'i sit'q'va t'qva
**English translation:** to speak a true word, to tell the truth
**Usage notes:** Literally "to say a true word." More elevated or emphatic than simple სიმართლე თქვა. Used in formal or solemn contexts.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

#### 1.5 Base: მართალი (mart'ali) "right, correct"
**Construction:** მართალს ამბობს (mart'als ambobs)
**Transliteration:** mart'als ambobs
**English translation:** tells what is right, speaks correctly
**Usage notes:** Uses mart'ali in dative case with "tells." Common expression for speaking correctly or truthfully.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

**Asymmetries noted:**
- More constructions for truth-telling/revealing than for concealment
- Rich set of emphatic affirmation constructions

- Verification and authentication have specialized constructions
