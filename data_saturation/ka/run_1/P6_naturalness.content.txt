# CLCA Protocol v3.3 — P6 CHANGE, CAUSATION, AND STABILITY
# Language: Georgian (ka)

---

**Methodological note:** This step documents how Georgian encodes dynamic truth-related meanings — becoming, causing, and maintaining truth-related states — using exclusively vocabulary established in P1–P5. Auxiliary verbs, aspect markers, tense markers, and function words are added as needed to form natural Georgian expressions; these additions are flagged. Where a given term does not participate in a dynamic construction, this is stated explicitly. Constructions are organized by semantic type and then by base term. Naturalness ratings reflect patterns observable in the P1–P5 data.

---

## Section 1: "X becomes [truth-related state]" — Inchoative constructions

### 1.1 Inchoative constructions with gamodga / aġmočnda

**Background from P4a and P5:** The verbs gamodga ("turned out to be, proved to be") and aġmočnda ("turned out to be, was found to be") are documented in P4a as the standard inchoative verbs for truth-domain predicates, expressing discovery or confirmation of a property. P5 documents four attested minimal pairs using these verbs (Oppositions 4, 22, 23). These are the primary inchoative constructions in the documented data.

---

**Construction 1.1.1**

1. **Base term:** მართალი / martali
2. **Example:** მართალი გამოდგა
3. **Transliteration:** martali gamodga
4. **English translation:** turned out to be right; proved to be correct
5. **Structural analysis:** [Adjective] + [gamodga (aorist of gamogdeba, "to turn out to be")] — the adjective functions as a predicative complement; gamodga is the inchoative/resultative verb. *gamodga* is an added function verb.
6. **Usage notes:** Documented in P2b and P4a as [CORE]; used in everyday, journalistic, and academic discourse when a claim or judgment is retrospectively confirmed as correct. The subject is typically a person (whose claim proved right) or a statement (which proved correct).
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 1.1.2**

1. **Base term:** მცდარი / mcdari
2. **Example:** მცდარი გამოდგა
3. **Transliteration:** mcdari gamodga
4. **English translation:** turned out to be wrong; proved to be erroneous
5. **Structural analysis:** [Adjective] + [gamodga] — parallel to 1.1.1 with the opposing adjective.
6. **Usage notes:** Documented in P2b as [CORE]; the direct inchoative counterpart to martali gamodga. Used when a belief, claim, or judgment is subsequently shown to be incorrect.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 1.1.3**

1. **Base term:** ყალბი / q̇albi
2. **Example:** ყალბი აღმოჩნდა
3. **Transliteration:** q̇albi aġmočnda
4. **English translation:** turned out to be fake; proved to be forged
5. **Structural analysis:** [Adjective] + [aġmočnda (aorist of aġmočndeba, "to turn out to be, to be found to be")] — aġmočnda is the inchoative/resultative verb. *aġmočnda* is an added function verb.
6. **Usage notes:** Documented in P2b as [CORE]; used in legal, forensic, and everyday contexts when examination reveals that an item is counterfeit or forged.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 1.1.4**

1. **Base term:** ნამდვილი / namdvili
2. **Example:** ნამდვილი აღმოჩნდა
3. **Transliteration:** namdvili aġmočnda
4. **English translation:** turned out to be genuine; proved to be real
5. **Structural analysis:** [Adjective] + [aġmočnda] — parallel to 1.1.3 with the opposing adjective.
6. **Usage notes:** Documented in P2b as [CORE]; the direct inchoative counterpart to q̇albi aġmočnda. Used when examination confirms the genuine nature of an item.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 1.1.5**

1. **Base term:** სიმართლე / simartle
2. **Example:** სიმართლე გამოვიდა
3. **Transliteration:** simarṭle gamovida
4. **English translation:** the truth came out; the truth emerged
5. **Structural analysis:** [Noun (subject)] + [gamovida (aorist of gamosvla, "to come out, to emerge")] — gamovida is an inchoative/directional verb expressing emergence from concealment. *gamovida* is an added function verb.
6. **Usage notes:** Documented in P2b as [CORE]; used in narrative and conversational registers when previously concealed truth becomes known. Semantically distinct from the gamodga/aġmočnda pattern: gamovida implies emergence from concealment rather than discovery through examination.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### 1.2 Inchoative constructions with ხდება / xdeba ("becomes, happens")

**Note:** The verb xdeba / moxda ("happens, occurs, becomes") is a general inchoative/eventive verb in Georgian. It is documented in P2b in the constructions dadastureba ver moxda ("confirmation could not occur") and gamarṭleba ver moxda ("justification did not occur"). This verb can combine with nominalized truth-domain expressions to express the coming-about of a truth-related state.

---

**Construction 1.2.1**

1. **Base term:** დადასტურება / dadastureba
2. **Example:** დადასტურება მოხდა
3. **Transliteration:** dadastureba moxda
4. **English translation:** confirmation occurred; verification took place
5. **Structural analysis:** [Verbal noun (subject)] + [moxda (aorist of moxdeba, "to occur, to happen")] — the verbal noun functions as the subject of the eventive verb. *moxda* is an added function verb.
6. **Usage notes:** Directly derived from the documented construction dadastureba ver moxda (P2b, [CORE]) by removing the negation. Used in administrative and journalistic discourse to assert that a verification process was completed.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 1.2.2**

1. **Base term:** გამართლება / gamarṭleba
2. **Example:** გამართლება მოხდა
3. **Transliteration:** gamarṭleba moxda
4. **English translation:** justification occurred; vindication took place; acquittal was granted
5. **Structural analysis:** [Verbal noun (subject)] + [moxda] — parallel to 1.2.1.
6. **Usage notes:** Directly derived from the documented construction gamarṭleba ver moxda (P1b, [CORE]) by removing the negation. Used in legal discourse to assert that an acquittal or vindication was granted.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### 1.3 Inchoative constructions: terms for which no common inchoative expression is documented

The following P1 terms do not participate in attested inchoative constructions in the P1–P5 data. For each, the reason is noted.

- **ჭეშმარიტება / č̣ešmariteba:** Used as the subject of passive and predicative constructions (č̣ešmariteba itqva, č̣ešmariteba gamarjvdeba, č̣ešmariteba edzebeba) but not documented in an inchoative construction expressing that something "becomes true" in the č̣ešmariteba sense. The abstract noun does not readily combine with gamodga or aġmočnda.
- **სიზუსტე / sizuste, სიზუსტე / sisc̣ore:** Abstract quality nouns; no inchoative construction documented.
- **სარწმუნო / sarc̣muno, დამაჯერებელი / damajerebeliˈ:** No inchoative construction documented; these adjectives describe stable properties of sources and arguments rather than states that come about through a process.
- **გულწრფელი / gulc̣rpeli, თვალთმაქცობა / tvaltmaqcoba:** No inchoative construction documented in the data.
- **მოჩვენებითი / močvenebiti, ფიქტიური / piqtiuri:** These adjectives describe a static characterization (ostensible, fictitious) rather than a state that comes about; no inchoative construction documented.

## Section 2: "X becomes [non-truth / opposed state]" — Inchoative constructions for the opposed pole

**Note:** The inchoative constructions documented in Section 1 already cover both poles of the primary oppositions (martali gamodga / mcdari gamodga; q̇albi aġmočnda / namdvili aġmočnda). This section documents additional inchoative constructions for the non-truth or falsity pole where these are distinct from those already listed.

---

**Construction 2.1**

1. **Base term:** სიცრუე / sicrue
2. **Example:** სიცრუეში გაიჭირა
3. **Transliteration:** sicrueši gaič̣ira
4. **English translation:** got caught in a lie; was caught lying
5. **Structural analysis:** [sicrue-ši (postpositional phrase: "in a lie")] + [gaič̣ira (aorist of gaič̣ireba, "to get caught, to get into difficulty")] — the postpositional phrase locates the subject in the state of lying; gaič̣ira expresses the inchoative entry into that state (getting caught). *gaič̣ira* is an added function verb.
6. **Usage notes:** Documented in P2b as [CORE]. Expresses the transition from concealed lying to exposed lying — a change of state from the perspective of social recognition.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 2.2**

1. **Base term:** სიცრუე / sicrue
2. **Example:** სიცრუეზე დაიჭირეს
3. **Transliteration:** sicruezeˈ daič̣ires
4. **English translation:** was caught in a lie; was caught lying (more formal)
5. **Structural analysis:** [sicrue-ze (postpositional phrase: "on/at a lie")] + [daič̣ires (aorist passive of daič̣ireba, "to be caught")] — parallel to 2.1 with a different postposition and a passive verb form. *daič̣ires* is an added function verb.
6. **Usage notes:** Documented in P2b as [CORE]; slightly more formal than sicrueši gaič̣ira. Used in legal and journalistic contexts.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 2.3**

1. **Base term:** ყალბი / q̇albi
2. **Example:** ყალბი გამოდგა
3. **Transliteration:** q̇albi gamodga
4. **English translation:** turned out to be fake; proved to be false
5. **Structural analysis:** [Adjective] + [gamodga] — parallel to 1.1.1 and 1.1.2 with q̇albi as the adjectival predicate.
6. **Usage notes:** The construction q̇albi aġmočnda is documented in P2b as [CORE] (see 1.1.3); q̇albi gamodga follows the same pattern documented for martali gamodga and mcdari gamodga and is equally natural. Both inchoative verbs (gamodga, aġmočnda) are attested with truth-domain adjectives.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Section 3: "Y causes X to be [truth-related state]" — Causative constructions

**Background from P2a and P4a:** P4a documents that Georgian does not have a single productive causative suffix for this domain. Caused-state meanings are expressed through preverb + root + -eba verbal nouns (gamarṭleba "making right/justifying," gasc̣oreba "correcting," gaq̇albeba "forging," dazusteba "making precise," dadastureba "confirming"). These are lexicalized rather than generated by a transparent causative rule. This section documents how these forms participate in causative constructions.

---

### 3.1 Causative constructions: Y causes X to be confirmed / verified

**Construction 3.1.1**

1. **Base term:** დადასტურება / dadastureba
2. **Example:** ექსპერტმა ნამდვილობა დაადასტურა
3. **Transliteration:** eqspertma namdviloba daadasturaˈ
4. **English translation:** the expert confirmed the authenticity; the expert verified the genuineness
5. **Structural analysis:** [eqspertma (agent, ergative case)] + [namdviloba (object, nominative)] + [daadasturaˈ (aorist of daadasturebs, "confirms, verifies")] — the agent in the ergative case causes the state of confirmation to obtain with respect to the object. *daadasturaˈ* is the finite verbal form of the documented verbal noun dadastureba; the ergative case marking on the agent is an added grammatical element.
6. **Usage notes:** The verbal noun dadastureba and its finite form daadasturebs are documented in P1 as [CORE]. The construction with an explicit agent in the ergative case is the standard transitive causative frame in Georgian for this verb.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 3.1.2**

1. **Base term:** დადასტურება / dadastureba
2. **Example:** მტკიცებულებამ სიყალბე დაადასტურა
3. **Transliteration:** mtkicebulebam siq̇albe daadasturaˈ
4. **English translation:** the evidence confirmed the forgery; the evidence established the falsity
5. **Structural analysis:** [mtkicebulebam (agent/instrument, ergative)] + [siq̇albe (object, nominative)] + [daadasturaˈ (aorist)] — parallel to 3.1.1 with siq̇albe as the object.
6. **Usage notes:** The collocation siq̇albis dadastureba is documented in P1 as a fixed legal phrase [MARGINAL]; the transitive verbal construction with an explicit agent is the dynamic realization of that nominal phrase.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### 3.2 Causative constructions: Y causes X to be corrected / made accurate

**Construction 3.2.1**

1. **Base term:** გასწორება / gasc̣oreba
2. **Example:** რედაქტორმა შეცდომა გაასწორა
3. **Transliteration:** redaqtorma šecdoma gaasṭoraˈ
4. **English translation:** the editor corrected the error; the editor rectified the mistake
5. **Structural analysis:** [redaqtorma (agent, ergative)] + [šecdoma (object, nominative)] + [gaasṭoraˈ (aorist of gaasṭorebs, "corrects, rectifies")] — the agent causes the object to transition from an incorrect to a correct state. *redaqtorma*, *šecdoma*, and *gaasṭoraˈ* are added elements; gasc̣oreba is the documented base.
6. **Usage notes:** gasc̣oreba is documented in P2a as [CORE] as the verbal noun for correction and rectification. The transitive construction with an explicit agent is the standard dynamic realization.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 3.2.2**

1. **Base term:** დაზუსტება / dazusteba
2. **Example:** ოფიციალურმა პირმა ინფორმაცია დააზუსტა
3. **Transliteration:** opiciˈalurma pirma inpormaciˈa daazustaˈ
4. **English translation:** the official clarified the information; the official specified the information precisely
5. **Structural analysis:** [opiciˈalurma pirma (agent, ergative)] + [inpormaciˈa (object, nominative)] + [daazustaˈ (aorist of daazustebs, "clarifies, specifies precisely")] — the agent causes the object to transition from an imprecise to a precise state. *opiciˈalurma pirma* and *daazustaˈ* are added elements; dazusteba is the documented base.
6. **Usage notes:** dazusteba is documented in P2a as [CORE] as the verbal noun for making precise or clarifying. The construction dazusteba saჭiroa ("clarification is needed") is documented in P2a; the transitive construction with an explicit agent is its dynamic realization.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

### 3.3 Causative constructions: Y causes X to be justified / vindicated

**Construction 3.3.1**

1. **Base term:** გამართლება / gamarṭleba
2. **Example:** სასამართლომ ბრალდებული გაამართლა
3. **Transliteration:** sasamarṭlom braldebuli gaamarṭlaˈ
4. **English translation:** the court acquitted the accused; the court vindicated the defendant
5. **Structural analysis:** [sasamarṭlom (agent, ergative)] + [braldebuli (object, nominative)] + [gaamarṭlaˈ (aorist of gaamarṭlebs, "acquits, vindicates, justifies")] — the agent (court) causes the object (accused) to attain the state of being justified/acquitted. *sasamarṭlom*, *braldebuli*, and *gaamarṭlaˈ* are added elements; gamarṭleba is the documented base.
6. **Usage notes:** gamarṭleba is documented in P1 as [CORE] and in P1b as the standard legal term for acquittal. The verbal form gaamarṭlebs is implied by the documented nominal gamarṭleba and the pattern of preverb + root + -eba verbal nouns in this domain.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Construction 3.3.2**

1. **Base term:** გამართლება / gamarṭleba
2. **Example:** ქმედება ვერ გაამართლა
3. **Transliteration:** qmedeba ver gaamarṭlaˈ
4. **English translation:** could not justify the action; failed to vindicate the conduct
5. **Structural analysis:** [qmedeba (object, nominative)] + [ver (negation of ability)] + [gaamarṭlaˈ (aorist)] — the negation ver expresses inability to cause the justification state to obtain. *ver* is an added function word; the construction is derived from the documented gaumarṭlebeliˈ ("unjustifiable") and gamarṭleba ver moxda patterns.
6. **Usage notes:** The construction gamarṭleba ver moxda is documented in P1b as [CORE]; the transitive version with ver and an explicit object is the agentive realization of the same inability.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### 3.4 Causative constructions: Y causes X to be forged / falsified

**Construction 3.4.1**

1. **Base term:** გაყალბება / gaq̇albeba
2. **Example:** თაღლითმა დოკუმენტი გაყალბა
3. **Transliteration:** taġliṭma dokumenti gaq̇albaˈ

## Section 5: Summary of dynamic patterns observed

The following observations are drawn directly from the attested constructions above and from the P1–P5 data. No projection beyond the documented evidence is made.

---

**5.1 Two inchoative verbs serve the truth domain**

The verbs gamodga ("turned out to be") and aġmočnda ("turned out to be, was found to be") are both documented as inchoative verbs for truth-domain predicates. They are not interchangeable in all contexts: gamodga is attested with martali and mcdari (epistemic correctness of claims and persons), while aġmočnda is attested with q̇albi and namdvili (authentication of objects). The verb gamovida ("came out") is attested specifically with simarṭle as subject, expressing emergence from concealment rather than discovery through examination. These three verbs cover distinct sub-types of inchoative truth-related change.

**5.2 Causative constructions are lexicalized, not rule-generated**

As documented in P4a, Georgian does not have a single productive causative suffix for this domain. Each causative construction is built on a specific lexicalized verbal noun (gamarṭleba, gasc̣oreba, gaq̇albeba, dazusteba, dadastureba), and the dynamic transitive construction is the finite verbal realization of that nominal. The agent takes the ergative case (a grammatical addition), and the object takes the nominative. This pattern is consistent across all documented causative constructions.

**5.3 Negation of causative constructions**

The particle ver ("cannot, is unable to") is documented in P1b and P2b as the standard negation for causative constructions in this domain (gamarṭleba ver moxda, dadastureba ver moxda). This negation expresses inability to bring about the truth-related state rather than simple non-occurrence.

**5.4 Stability constructions are primarily stative or progressive**

Stability and persistence are expressed through: (a) the future tense with gamarjvdeba ("will prevail") for assertions about the ultimate persistence of truth; (b) the present passive with edzebeba ("is being sought") for ongoing processes; (c) the present passive with itˈvleba ("is considered") for attributed stable states; and (d) the copular construction with mc̣area ("is bitter") for inherent properties. No dedicated "remain true" or "stay genuine" construction is documented in the P1–P5 data.

**5.5 Asymmetry between terms**

Not all truth-domain terms participate equally in dynamic constructions. The terms most productively attested in dynamic constructions are: simarṭle (inchoative, causative, stability), q̇albi / namdvili (inchoative), dadastureba (causative, stability), gamarṭleba (causative), gaq̇albeba (causative), and sicrue (inchoative/exposure). The terms č̣ešmariteba, sizuste, sarc̣muno, gulc̣rpeli, močvenebiti, and piqtiuri participate in fewer or no dynamic constructions in the documented data. This asymmetry is preserved without normalization.
