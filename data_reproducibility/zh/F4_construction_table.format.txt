| **1. ATTRIBUTION** | 这个说法是真的 | zhè ge shuō fǎ shì zhēn de | This statement is true | The copula 是 (shì) "be" links subjects to truth-domain predicates. This is the basic attributive construction in Chinese. The particle 的 (de) typically follows the predicate adjective in predicative position. Without 是, bare adjectives can also function predicatively (e.g., 这个说法真 "this statement [is] true"), but 是 + ADJ + 的 is more emphatic and common in full sentences. Extremely common in all registers. The 是...的 frame is the default predicative structure. Bare adjective predicates (without 是) occur but are less emphatic and more restricted to informal speech. | [CORE] |

| **18. NEGATION OF EVIDENCE/BASIS** | 毫无证据 | háo wú zhèng jù | completely without evidence | Negation of evidential basis uses 没有 (méi yǒu) "not have" or 毫无 (háo wú) "completely without". This construction criticizes claims lacking factual support. Common objects: 根据 (gēn jù) "basis, grounds", 证据 (zhèng jù) "evidence", 事实根据 (shì shí gēn jù) "factual basis". The emphatic 毫无 (háo wú) "not the slightest bit" strengthens the negation. Common pattern for criticizing unsupported claims across all registers. Both 没有 and the emphatic 毫无 are productive with evidential nouns (根据, 证据, 事实根据). Frequent in argumentative and evaluative contexts. | [CORE] |
