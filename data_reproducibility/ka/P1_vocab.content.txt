# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P1 CORE VOCABULARY COLLECTION
# Language: Georgian (ka)

## Lexical Items

### 1. ჭეშმარიტი (ch'eshmarit'i)
**Transliteration:** ch'eshmarit'i  
**Literal meaning:** true, genuine  
**Typical English translations:** true, real, genuine, authentic

Used to describe statements that correspond to reality or things that are authentic and not false. Commonly appears in both everyday speech and formal contexts to affirm the veracity of claims or the genuineness of objects or feelings.

**[CORE]**

---

### 2. სიმართლე (simart'le)
**Transliteration:** simart'le  
**Literal meaning:** truth (noun derived from mart'ali "right")  
**Typical English translations:** truth, veracity

The primary noun for truth in Georgian, used in philosophical, legal, and everyday contexts. Functions as the standard term when discussing truthfulness, factual accuracy, or speaking honestly.

**[CORE]**

---

### 3. მართალი (mart'ali)
**Transliteration:** mart'ali  
**Literal meaning:** right, correct  
**Typical English translations:** right, correct, just, righteous

Describes both factual correctness and moral righteousness. Used to evaluate statements as accurate or to characterize persons as morally upright or just in their conduct.

**[CORE]**

### 4. სწორი (sts'ori)
**Transliteration:** sts'ori  
**Literal meaning:** straight, correct  
**Typical English translations:** correct, right, proper, accurate

Commonly used for correctness in practical, factual, and procedural contexts. Often preferred over mart'ali when discussing technical accuracy, proper methods, or correct answers.

**[CORE]**

### 5. ტყუილი (t'q'uili)
**Transliteration:** t'q'uili
