### BASE: benar

#### 1. tidak benar
**Form:** tidak benar
**Transliteration:** N/A (Latin script)
**Translation:** not true, incorrect, untrue
**Usage:** Standard negation of benar. Very common in everyday speech and formal discourse ("itu tidak benar" = "that's not true").
**[CORE]**

#### 2. benar atau salah
**Form:** benar atau salah
**Transliteration:** N/A (Latin script)
**Translation:** true or false, right or wrong
**Usage:** Contrastive pair used in questions, tests, and evaluations ("jawab benar atau salah" = "answer true or false").
**[CORE]**

#### 3. benar-benar tidak
**Form:** benar-benar tidak
**Transliteration:** N/A (Latin script)
**Translation:** really not, absolutely not
**Usage:** Emphatic negation combining intensifier with negation ("saya benar-benar tidak tahu" = "I really don't know").
**[CORE]**

#### 4. yang benar saja
**Form:** yang benar saja
**Transliteration:** N/A (Latin script)
**Translation:** you must be kidding, really?, no way
**Usage:** Idiomatic expression of disbelief or surprise. Common in colloquial speech ("yang benar saja! dia menang?" = "no way! he won?").
**[CORE]**

#### 5. kalau tidak salah
**Form:** kalau tidak salah
**Transliteration:** N/A (Latin script)
**Translation:** if I'm not mistaken, if I remember correctly
**Usage:** Hedging expression indicating uncertainty. Very common in conversation ("kalau tidak salah, dia dari Jakarta" = "if I'm not mistaken, he's from Jakarta").
**[CORE]**

#### 6. benar sekali
**Form:** benar sekali
**Transliteration:** N/A (Latin script)
**Translation:** exactly right, absolutely correct
**Usage:** Strong affirmation or agreement. Common in confirmations ("benar sekali!" = "exactly right!").
**[CORE]**

#### 7. apa benar
**Form:** apa benar
**Transliteration:** N/A (Latin script)
**Translation:** is it true, is it correct
**Usage:** Question formation asking for confirmation ("apa benar dia sakit?" = "is it true he's sick?").
**[CORE]**

#### 8. memang benar
**Form:** memang benar
**Transliteration:** N/A (Latin script)

**[CORE]**

#### 9. tidak salah lagi
**Form:** tidak salah lagi

## SUMMARY OBSERVATIONS

### High-frequency construction patterns:

1. **Negation with "tidak"**: Nearly universal pattern across all bases
2. **Intensification with "sekali"**: Very common for gradable terms
3. **Hedging with "kurang"**: Common for polite or softened negation
4. **Contrastive pairs with "atau"**: Frequent for binary oppositions
5. **Emphatic constructions with "benar-benar"**: Common for emphasis
6. **Discourse markers**: Several terms (jujur saja, sebenarnya, wajar saja) function as discourse markers

### Semantic patterns:

1. **Truth domain (benar, salah, bohong)**: Rich in idiomatic expressions and discourse functions
2. **Authenticity domain (asli, palsu, tulen)**: Primarily collocational with specific nouns
3. **Validity domain (sah, valid, batal)**: Formal/legal constructions predominate
4. **Similarity domain (sama, mirip)**: Rich in comparative constructions
5. **Appropriateness domain (tepat, cocok, pas, wajar, pantas)**: Gradable with hedging patterns

### Register variation:

- Colloquial intensifiers: "banget," "kok"
- Formal/legal: "secara," "demi hukum"
- Literary/elevated: constructions with "sejati," "dusta," "hakikat"

### Asymmetries noted:

- Some bases (khilaf, kesejatian) show very limited multi-word constructions
- Borrowed terms (valid, faktual, akurat) show restricted construction patterns
- Literary terms show fewer colloquial constructions
- Legal terms show specialized fixed expressions
