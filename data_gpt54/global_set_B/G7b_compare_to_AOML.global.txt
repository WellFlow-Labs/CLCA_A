## Summary

### Confirmed Patterns
- **Statement truth vs falsehood** aligns well with **VER**, especially for:
  - simple predication
  - restricted becoming
  - abstract nominalization
  - separation from verification
- **Honesty / sincerity** aligns strongly with **SINC** for:
  - predication
  - speech-related evaluation
  - person-labeling
  - causative blocking
- **Genuineness / authenticity** aligns strongly with **AUTH** for:
  - predication
  - noun classification
  - abstract nominalization
  - authentication procedures
  - asymmetry between creating and destroying authenticity
- **Fake / counterfeit / forged** aligns strongly with the negative side of **AUTH**, especially with:
  - causative falsification/forgery
  - authentication contrasts
- **Verification / confirmed status** aligns strongly with **VERIF**, especially for:
  - proof/confirmation
  - support/basis
  - procedural checking
- **Lawfulness / legitimacy** and **justice / fairness** align well with **NORM** in their static evaluative uses.
- **Essence / substance** strongly supports AOML constraints:
  - attribution allowed
  - becoming blocked
  - causation blocked
- **Resemblance / fidelity** aligns strongly with **LIFE** in correspondence/fidelity constructions.

### Partial or Conditional Matches
- **Correctness / rightness** only partially aligns because AOML has no dedicated primary axis for it; it is distributed across **VER** and **NORM** subregions.
- **Precision / exactness** is only partially captured; AOML lacks a dedicated precision axis.
- **Credibility / trustworthiness** only partially aligns with **SINC**:
  - some empirical cases are dispositional
  - others are source-based and epistemic
  - some overlap with **VERIF**
- **Reality / actuality** is distributed across **VER**, **AUTH**, **ESS**, and sometimes **LIFE**, so alignment is diffuse.
- **Appearance-of-truth / plausibility** partially aligns with **LIFE**, but empirical plausibility is broader than representational fidelity.
- For several dimensions, empirical matrices mark **BLOCKED** where AOML predicts **restricted** rather than impossible, especially for:
  - inchoatives in authenticity
  - inchoatives in justice/legitimacy
  - inchoatives in resemblance/plausibility

### Conflicts
- **Credibility / trustworthiness + causative / induced result** conflicts with AOML if mapped to **SINC**, because:
  - empirical data allows some causative-like confidence/credibility effects
  - AOML universally blocks CAUSE × DISPOSITIONAL
- **Honesty / sincerity + change into state** conflicts:
  - empirical matrix marks BLOCKED
  - AOML gives SINC + BECOME as restricted, not blocked
- **Resemblance / correspondence / sameness + abstract nominal evaluation** conflicts:
  - empirical matrix allows VARIABLE
  - AOML blocks **LIFE + ABSTR**
- More generally, any empirical credibility cases that are clearly source-evaluative rather than characterological fit AOML only imperfectly if forced into **SINC**.

### Unmodeled Empirical Findings
- **Correctness** as a robust cross-linguistic dimension distinct from both truth and normativity is not modeled as a primary axis.
- **Precision / exactness** is also a robust empirical dimension without a dedicated AOML axis.
- **Discourse framing / stance marking** (“to tell the truth,” “actually,” “in reality,” “in essence”) is strongly recurrent empirically but not represented as an AOML operator or layer.
- **Interrogative checking** is an empirical constructional function not represented in AOML.
- **Basis / support** is partly modeled under VERIF, but empirical support-status constructions may deserve stronger independent treatment.
- Empirical asymmetries such as:
  - stronger lexicalization of liar/fake/forged than truth-teller/become-genuine
  - speech-action preference over state-change
  are not directly encoded in AOML except indirectly through operator restrictions.

### Unattested AOML Structures
- The **LIFE + ABSTR blocked universally** claim is not clearly supported by the empirical matrix, which gives VARIABLE abstract nominalization for resemblance/correspondence and plausibility domains.
- The **SINC + BECOME restricted** structure is not clearly supported; empirical matrix suggests stronger blocking for honesty/sincerity inchoatives.
- The **CAUSE × SINC universally blocked** rule is only partly supported if empirical credibility/trustworthiness causatives are grouped with sincerity.
- The provisional **REVEAL** operator is not directly comparable to the empirical matrix because G7a does not isolate revelation as a separate constructional column.

## Theoretical Discussion

### Where AOML appears strongly supported
AOML is strongest where the empirical data shows:
1. **clear semantic partitioning**
2. **operator asymmetry**
3. **constructional restriction**

This is especially true for:
- **AUTH**
  - empirical data strongly supports a distinct authenticity/fakery domain
  - positive creation of authenticity is weak
  - destructive/falsifying causation is strong
- **ESS**
  - empirical data strongly supports static essence/substance framing
  - dynamic becoming and causation are broadly absent
- **VERIF**
  - empirical data repeatedly distinguishes truth from checked/confirmed/proved status
  - support, proof, authentication, and confirmation cluster here well
- **NORM**
  - lawfulness, legitimacy, justice, and fairness are clearly not reducible to factual truth
- **LIFE**
  - fidelity/resemblance/true-to-life domains are empirically real and distinct from truth proper

### Where AOML may require refinement
1. **Correctness**
   - Empirically, correctness/right answer/proper procedure is one of the strongest recurring dimensions.
   - In AOML it is distributed across subregions rather than treated as a primary axis.
   - This creates repeated partial matches and loss of empirical granularity.

2. **Precision / exactness**
   - Empirically robust across many languages.
   - AOML has no dedicated primitive for this.
   - Mapping precision into VER or LIFE is often lossy.

3. **Credibility / trustworthiness**
   - AOML places reliability under **SINC** subregions, but empirical data often distinguishes:
     - source credibility
     - evidential reliability
     - personal honesty
   - These do not always behave alike constructionally.
   - The strongest tension is with the universal causation block on dispositional targets.

4. **LIFE abstraction**
   - AOML states **LIFE + ABSTR** is blocked.
   - Empirical matrix suggests at least variable support for abstract nominalization in resemblance/plausibility domains.
   - This may indicate that the empirical resemblance/plausibility field is broader than AOML LIFE, or that the block is too strong.

5. **Discourse framing**
   - Empirically very strong across languages.
   - AOML does not model it.
   - This is not necessarily a contradiction, but it is a major omission relative to the empirical architecture.

### Where empirical findings suggest new dimensions
Empirical findings suggest at least two candidate dimensions that are stronger than AOML currently reflects:

1. **Correctness**
   - distinct from factual truth
   - distinct from normativity
   - distinct from precision
   - strongly lexicalized cross-linguistically

2. **Precision / exactness**
   - distinct from correctness
   - distinct from truth
   - often structurally important in measurement, wording, and representation

A third possible candidate is:
3. **Credibility / evidential trust**
   - if not reducible to sincerity or verification
   - especially because many languages lexicalize it separately from both truth and honesty

### Where empirical findings suggest new constructional constraints
1. **Speech-action preference**
   - Many languages prefer “tell the truth / lie / answer truthfully” over state-change constructions.
   - This preference is stronger than AOML currently encodes.

2. **Verification/result over inchoative**
   - Empirical data repeatedly shows that truth-related change is often expressed as:
     - proved true
     - verified
     - came out
     - was shown false
   - rather than by ordinary becoming.
   - AOML partly captures this through VERIF and RESULT, but the empirical tendency may be stronger than currently stated.

3. **Negative-side lexicalization asymmetry**
   - Fake/forged/liar terms are often more compact and more productive than positive counterparts.
   - This asymmetry is visible across languages and could merit stronger formal recognition.

### Where empirical findings suggest revised constraint strength
- **SINC + BECOME** may need stronger restriction if empirical honesty/sincerity inchoatives are broadly blocked.
- **LIFE + ABSTR** may need weaker restriction if empirical resemblance/plausibility nominalizations are genuinely recurrent.
- **CAUSE × SINC** may need refinement if empirical credibility causatives are not actually sincerity causatives but belong to a distinct credibility domain.

## Overall Assessment
The empirical matrix supports AOML most strongly in its separation of:
- **truth**
- **authenticity**
- **verification**
- **normativity**
- **essence**
- **lifelikeness/fidelity**

It supports AOML less strongly where AOML compresses empirically robust distinctions:
- **correctness**
- **precision**
- **credibility**

And it leaves unmodeled several major empirical constructional facts:
- discourse framing
- interrogative checking
- strong speech-action packaging
- asymmetries in lexicalization and dynamicity

So the alignment is substantial, but not complete: AOML captures several major high-level partitions and operator asymmetries, while the empirical matrix suggests that some non-primitive subregions in AOML behave more like independent dimensions in actual cross-linguistic structure.
