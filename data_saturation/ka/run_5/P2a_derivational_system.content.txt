# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2a Derivational System
# Language: Georgian (ka)

---

**Preliminary note on scope:** This step documents attested derivational morphology for the 30 P1 base items. Many of these items are themselves already derived forms (e.g., č̣ešmariteba is derived from č̣ešmariti; siq̇albe is derived from q̇albi). Where a P1 item is a base that generates further derivatives, those are documented here. Where a P1 item is already a terminal derivative with no further productive derivation, that is stated explicitly. Cross-reference to P1b items is noted where relevant to avoid redundancy, but P1b items are not re-documented here unless they serve as bases for further derivation.

---

## Item 1: ჭეშმარიტი (č̣ešmariti)

---

**Derived Form 1**

1. **Base Form:** ჭეშმარიტი (č̣ešmariti)
2. **Derived Form:** ჭეშმარიტება (č̣ešmariteba)
3. **Transliteration:** č̣ešmariteba
4. **Literal Breakdown:** č̣ešmariti + nominalizing suffix -eba
5. **Natural English translation:** truth, truthfulness, veracity
6. **Usage:** The standard abstract noun for truth in Georgian, documented as P1 item #2. Noted here for completeness as the primary derivation from č̣ešmariti.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2**

1. **Base Form:** ჭეშმარიტი (č̣ešmariti)
2. **Derived Form:** ჭეშმარიტად (č̣ešmaritad)
3. **Transliteration:** č̣ešmaritad
4. **Literal Breakdown:** č̣ešmariti + adverbial suffix -ad
5. **Natural English translation:** truly, in truth, verily
6. **Usage:** Adverbial form used in formal, literary, and religious registers to assert that something is genuinely the case. Documented in P1b Root 3, item 16; noted here as a direct derivation from the P1 base.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Note on further derivation:** No derivations from č̣ešmariteba itself (beyond those documented in P1b) are identified as productive independent stems. The root č̣ešmarit- does not appear to generate agentive nouns, verbal stems, or comparative forms in common use.

## Item 3: სიმართლე (simartle)

---

**Derived Form 1**

1. **Base Form:** სიმართლე (simartle)
2. **Derived Form:** სიმართლის თქმა (simartlis tqma)
3. **Transliteration:** simartlis tqma
4. **Literal Breakdown:** simartle (genitive -is) + tqma (saying, verbal noun of tqva, to say)
5. **Natural English translation:** telling the truth, saying the truth
6. **Usage:** A fixed nominal collocation used in moral and legal discourse to refer to the act of stating what is true. Appears in oaths, moral instruction, and legal testimony contexts.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2**

1. **Base Form:** სიმართლე (simartle)
2. **Derived Form:** სიმართლის დამალვა (simartlis damalva)
3. **Transliteration:** simartlis damalva
4. **Literal Breakdown:** simartle (genitive -is) + damalva (concealment, verbal noun of damalva)
5. **Natural English translation:** concealment of the truth, hiding the truth
6. **Usage:** Used in moral and legal contexts to characterize the deliberate suppression of true information. Parallel to the č̣ešmariteba construction documented in P1b.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Note on morphological derivation proper:** simartle is itself a nominalization (si- + martal- + -e) and does not appear to generate further independent derived stems through suffixation. Its productivity is collocational rather than morphological.
