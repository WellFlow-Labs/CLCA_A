| **10. LIGHT VERB CONSTRUCTION** — Nominal + light verb | நியாயம் கேட்கிறேன் | *niyāyam kēṭkiṟēṉ* | "I am asking for justice / I am demanding what is right" | [CORE] | Fully idiomatic; the light verb carries tense, aspect, and agreement while the nominal carries semantic content.
