# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P5 OPPOSITIONS
# Language: Tamil (ta)

---

## OPPOSITION 1

**Term A:** உண்மை (uṇmai)  
**Term B:** பொய் (poy)  
**Transliteration:** uṇmai vs. poy  
**English translation:** "truth, reality, fact" vs. "lie, falsehood, fake"  
**Description of contrast:** This is the primary lexical opposition in Tamil truth-domain vocabulary. உண்மை denotes correspondence to reality and factual truth, while பொய் denotes deliberate falsehood, lies, and inauthenticity. Both terms are extremely frequent across all registers and contexts.  
**Contrast type:** lexical  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## OPPOSITION 2

**Term A:** உண்மையான (uṇmaiyāṉa)  
**Term B:** பொய்யான (poyyāṉa)  
**Transliteration:** uṇmaiyāṉa vs. poyyāṉa  
**English translation:** "true, genuine, authentic" vs. "false, fake, untrue"  
**Description of contrast:** Adjectival forms of the primary opposition, used attributively to modify nouns. This opposition is equally productive and frequent as the nominal forms, appearing in all contexts where truth/falsity of objects, statements, or situations is evaluated.  
**Contrast type:** lexical (derived)  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## OPPOSITION 3

This opposition is particularly common in philosophical, literary, and classical Tamil contexts, with மெய் carrying more metaphysical weight than உண்மை.  
**Contrast type:** lexical  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## SUMMARY OBSERVATIONS

### Symmetry and Asymmetry Patterns

**Highly Symmetric Oppositions:**
- Core lexical pairs (உண்மை/பொய், மெய்/பொய், சரி/தப்பு) show balanced productivity
- Adjectival and adverbial derivations maintain symmetry
- Basic predicative and interrogative constructions are symmetric
- Information/narrative compounds (தகவல், செய்தி, கதை) show symmetric patterning

**Asymmetric Oppositions:**
- **Causative morphology:** உண்மைப்படுத்து uses -படுத்து, but பொய் uses -ஆக்கு (different causative strategy)
- **Agentive morphology:** Different suffixes and registers for positive vs. negative agents
- **Abstract nominalization:** பொய்ம்மை rare compared to மெய்ம்மை
- **Emotional/behavioral sincerity:** Negative forms (பொய் கண்ணீர், பொய் நாடகம்) lexically marked, positive forms unmarked
- **Commercial/legal fraud:** Extensive பொய்-based fraud terminology, minimal உண்மை-based authentication terminology

### Negative Bias in Elaboration

The data shows consistent **negative elaboration bias**: terms for falsehood, deception, and fraud are more extensively elaborated than terms for truth and authenticity, particularly in:
- Legal/documentary fraud (Oppositions 21, 49-50)
- Commercial counterfeiting (Opposition 22)
- Emotional insincerity (Oppositions 22-23, 49-50)
- Character evaluation (Opposition 30)

This suggests cultural/pragmatic focus on identifying and condemning deception rather than celebrating truth.

### Register Stratification

Several oppositions show register-based variation:
- உண்மை/பொய் (colloquial-neutral)
- மெய்/பொய் (literary-philosophical)
- நேர்மை/கள்ளம் (moral-character)
