# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P1b ROOT EXPANSION
# Language: Turkish (tr)

---

## STEP 1: IDENTIFY CENTRAL ROOTS

### Root 1: **doğru**
**Form:** doğru (Latin script)
**Transliteration:** doğru
**P1 items:** 1 direct item (*doğru*)
**Core meaning contribution:** Straightness, directness, correctness, alignment with fact or standard

This root appears as a complete lexical item rather than a bound morpheme. It is highly productive in compounds and collocations, serving as the primary native Turkish term for truth, correctness, and rightness across factual, moral, and directional domains.

---

### Root 2: **hak / hakk-**
**Form:** hak (Latin script)
**Transliteration:** hak
**P1 items:** 4 items (*hak*, *hakikat*, *haklı*, *haksız*)
**Core meaning contribution:** Right, truth, justice, what is due or proper

From Arabic *ḥaqq*, this root is central to moral, legal, and epistemic evaluation in Turkish. It appears in numerous compounds relating to rights, justice, truth, and deservingness.

---

### Root 3: **gerçek**
**Form:** gerçek (Latin script)
**Transliteration:** gerçek
**P1 items:** 2 items (*gerçek*, *gerçekçi*)
**Core meaning contribution:** Reality, genuineness, authenticity, what actually exists

From Persian, this root is fundamental for distinguishing real from unreal, genuine from fake, and actual from imaginary. Highly productive in philosophical, everyday, and technical contexts.

## ROOT 1 EXPANSION: **doğru**

### 1. doğruluk
**Form:** doğruluk
**Transliteration:** doğruluk
**Literal breakdown:** *doğru* (true/correct) + *-luk* (abstract noun suffix)
**English translation:** truth, correctness, honesty, integrity
**Context:** Abstract noun used for the quality of being true or correct, and for moral honesty. Common in both epistemic contexts ("ifadenin doğruluğu" = "the truth of the statement") and moral contexts ("doğruluk ve dürüstlük" = "honesty and integrity").
**[CORE]**

### 2. doğrulamak
**Form:** doğrulamak
**Transliteration:** doğrulamak
**Literal breakdown:** *doğru* (true/correct) + *-la-* (denominative verb suffix) + *-mak* (infinitive)
**English translation:** to verify, to confirm, to validate, to authenticate
**Context:** Used for confirming the truth or accuracy of information, verifying facts, or validating claims. Common in technical, legal, and everyday contexts ("bilgiyi doğrulamak" = "to verify the information").
**[CORE]**

### 3. doğrulama

**Transliteration:** doğrulama

### 4. doğru söylemek

## STEP 3: PERIPHERAL EXTENSIONS

### Root: **doğru**

**Directional/spatial extensions:**
- *doğru* as postposition meaning "toward" (e.g., "eve doğru" = "toward home")
- *doğrultu* used in navigation and geometry for direction/bearing
- *doğru açı* (right angle) - geometric term where "straight" becomes technical mathematical concept

**Temporal extensions:**
- *doğrudan doğruya* can indicate immediacy in time, not just directness in space or communication

**Electrical/technical extensions:**
- *doğru akım* (direct current) - technical term where "direct/straight" becomes property of electrical flow

**Moral/behavioral domain:**
- *doğru dürüst* emphasizes propriety and respectability beyond truth-value
- *düzgün* (derived from same root concept of straightness) focuses on orderliness and propriety

**Intensity/completeness:**
- *doğru* in some contexts functions as intensifier or marker of completeness rather than truth-evaluation

---

### Root: **hak / hakk-**

**Legal/entitlement domain:**
- *hak sahibi*, *hak iddia etmek*, *hak aramak* - shift from truth to legal rights and entitlements
- *hakkını almak*, *hakkını vermek* - focus on what is due rather than what is true

**Religious/theological domain:**
- *Hak* as divine name/epithet for God
- *hak dini* (true religion) - truth becomes religious orthodoxy
- *hakkın rahmetine kavuşmak* (euphemism for death) - *hak* as God rather than truth/right

**Deservingness domain:**
- *hak etmek* (to deserve) - shifts from truth/right to merit and deservingness
- Can be used for both positive and negative outcomes

**Topical/aboutness domain:**
- *hakkında* (about, concerning) - grammaticalized as postposition with no clear truth-evaluation function
- Extremely common and semantically bleached in this usage

**Forgiveness/waiving domain:**
- *hakkını helal etmek* - religious/moral concept of forgiving debts or wrongs
- Shifts from asserting rights to relinquishing them

---

### Root: **gerçek**

**Artistic/aesthetic domain:**
- *gerçekçilik* (realism) - artistic movement and style
- *gerçek üstü* (surrealism) - artistic movement explicitly beyond reality
- Shifts from truth-evaluation to aesthetic categories

**Ontological/existential domain:**
- *gerçeklik* can refer to existence or being, not just truth
- *gerçek olmak* can mean "to exist" as well as "to be true"

**Character/nature domain:**
- *gerçek yüz* (true face/character) - metaphorical extension to personality
- *gerçek kimlik* (true identity) - shifts to questions of identity rather than truth per se

**Value/worth domain:**
- *gerçek değer* (true value) - extends to economic and evaluative contexts
- May refer to actual market value rather than truth-value

**Causation domain:**
- *gerçek neden* (real reason) - shifts to causal explanation
- Distinguishes actual causes from stated or apparent ones

**Emotional/experiential domain:**
- *acı gerçek* (bitter truth) - collocates with emotional/affective terms
- *gerçek gibi* (like real) - used for verisimilitude in fiction and simulation
