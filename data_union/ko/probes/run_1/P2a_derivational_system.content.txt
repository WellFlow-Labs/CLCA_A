# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2a Derivational System
## Language: Korean (ko)
### Transliteration Standard: Revised Romanization of Korean (RR)

---

## Preliminary Note on Scope

This step documents attested derivational morphology for the 30 P1 lexical items. "Derivational morphology" is understood here as the attachment of morphological material to a base to produce a new lexical item of a different grammatical category or with a substantially different meaning — distinct from inflection (which Korean largely handles through verbal endings) and from compounding (which was the primary focus of P1b). The principal derivational processes attested in this domain are:

- **-하다 attachment**: converts a nominal base into a verb/predicate
- **-되다 attachment**: converts a nominal base into a passive or stative predicate
- **-성 (性) suffixation**: derives an abstract quality noun from a nominal base
- **-적 (的) suffixation**: derives an adjectival/adverbial base from a nominal
- **-히 / -이 / -로 suffixation**: derives adverbs from adjectival bases
- **-음 / -기 nominalization**: derives nouns from verbal bases
- **-쟁이 / -자 (者) agentive suffixation**: derives person-denoting nouns

Not all processes apply to all bases. Gaps and asymmetries are preserved and noted.

### 1. Base Form: 진실 (jinsil)

---

**Derived Form 1**

1. **Base Form:** 진실 (jinsil)
2. **Derived Form:** 진실하다
3. **Transliteration:** jinsil-hada
4. **Literal Breakdown:** 眞實(truth) + 하다(to do/be)
5. **Natural English Translation:** to be truthful, to be sincere, to be genuine
6. **Usage:** Predicative form used to characterize persons or their conduct as honest and without pretense. Common in moral evaluation and interpersonal contexts across spoken and written registers.
7. **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2**

1. **Base Form:** 진실 (jinsil)
2. **Derived Form:** 진실성
3. **Transliteration:** jinsilseong
4. **Literal Breakdown:** 眞實(truth) + 性(-ness, abstract quality)
5. **Natural English Translation:** truthfulness, authenticity, sincerity as a quality
6. **Usage:** Used in evaluative discourse — journalistic, legal, and academic — to assess whether a person, account, or work possesses the quality of being truthful or genuine.
7. **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 3**

1. **Base Form:** 진실 (jinsil)
2. **Derived Form:** 진실되다
3. **Transliteration:** jinsil-doeda
4. **Literal Breakdown:** 眞實(truth) + 되다(to become/be)
5. **Natural English Translation:** to be truthful, to be genuine
6. **Usage:** Stative predicate used to describe persons or expressions as genuinely truthful; appears in literary and formal registers with a nuance of inherent rather than performed quality, distinguishing it from 진실하다.
7. **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 4**

1. **Base Form:** 진실 (jinsil)
2. **Derived Form:** 진실로
3. **Transliteration:** jinsilro
4. **Literal Breakdown:** 眞實(truth) + 로(adverbial particle)
5. **Natural English Translation:** truly, in truth, sincerely
6. **Usage:** Adverbial expression used to emphasize the genuine or sincere character of a statement or action. Appears in formal speech, religious texts, and literary prose.
7. **Epistemic Status:** [CORE]

---

**Note on 진실:** The -적 (的) suffixation to produce 진실적 is not attested as a standard form; 진실한 (the attributive adjectival form of 진실하다) serves the attributive function instead. No agentive derivation from 진실 alone is attested.

**Derived Form 1**

2. **Derived Form:** 맞음
3. **Transliteration:** maeum
4. **Literal Breakdown:** 맞- (stem: to be correct/fit) + -음 (nominalizing suffix)
5. **Natural English Translation:** correctness, being right, the fact of matching

## Summary Observations

The following patterns are directly supported by the data above:

**1. Uneven derivational productivity:** Items with Sino-Korean nominal bases (정확, 정직, 신뢰, 타당, 공정, 모순, 유사) show the fullest derivational paradigms, consistently generating -하다 predicates, -성 quality nouns, and adverbial forms. Native Korean bases (진짜, 가짜, 맞다, 참) show more restricted derivational output.

**2. The -하다 / -되다 asymmetry:** Where both -하다 and -되다 forms exist (진실하다 / 진실되다; 거짓되다; 모순되다), the -하다 form tends to be more active/agentive and the -되다 form more stative or passive. Not all bases take both; 모순되다 is standard while 모순하다 is not.

**3. Adverbial formation is multiply realized:** Adverbs are formed by -히 (정확히, 정직히), by -게 on -하다 stems (정직하게, 공정하게), and by -로/-으로 on nominals (진실로, 진정으로, 실제로). These are not interchangeable; which form is preferred varies by base and register.

**4. The 있다/없다 construction:** Several items (신빙성, 진정성, 근거) show that the primary predicative construction is not -하다 suffixation but rather the collocation with 있다 (to have/exist) and 없다 (to lack/not exist). This pattern is productive for abstract quality nouns where -하다 attachment is not standard.

**5. Gaps are real:** 진짜 and 가짜 show no productive morphological derivation. 허위 and 진위 show limited derivational output despite being common in use. These gaps are preserved without explanation.
