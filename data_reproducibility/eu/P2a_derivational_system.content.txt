## SUMMARY OF DERIVATIONAL PATTERNS

### Productive Suffixes Identified:

1. **-tasun** (abstract noun): Highly productive for creating abstract quality nouns
   - Examples: egiazkotasuna, zuzentasuna, ziurtasuna, faltsutasuna, berdintasuna, egokitasuna, zehaztasuna, zintzotasuna

2. **-tu** (verbalizer/perfective): Productive for creating verbs from adjectives and nouns
   - Examples: egiaztatu, ziurtatu, faltsutu, berdindu, egokitu, zehaztu, agertu

3. **-ki** (adverbial): Productive for creating adverbs from adjectives
   - Examples: egiazki, ziurki, zehazki, zintzoki

4. **-pen** (action/result nominalization): Productive for creating action nouns from verb stems
   - Examples: egiaztapen, ziurtapen, zehaztapen

5. **-zko/-ezko** (relational): Productive for creating attributive adjectives
   - Examples: egiazko, gezurrezko, benetako, antzeko

6. **-keta** (action nominalization): Productive for creating action nouns
   - Examples: zuzenketa, berdinketa

7. **-garri** (worthy of/able to be): Productive for creating potential/evaluative adjectives
   - Examples: egiaztagarri, fidagarri, sinesgarria, zalantzagarria, frogagarria

8. **-tzaile** (agent): Productive for creating agent nouns
   - Example: zuzentzaile

9. **-keria** (pejorative/collective): Productive for creating pejorative nouns
   - Examples: gezurkeria, faltsukeria, iruzurkeria

10. **-giri** (document): Specialized suffix for official documents
    - Examples: egiaztagiri, ziurtagiri

### Asymmetries and Gaps:

- Not all bases show equal derivational productivity
- Some forms (fidagarria, sinesgarria, zalantzagarria, frogagarria) appear to be lexicalized -garri forms without further productive derivation
- The -keria suffix appears primarily with negative/deceptive concepts (gezur, faltsu, iruzur)
- Some bases show rich derivation (egia, zuzen, ziur) while others show minimal derivation
- Borrowed terms (faltsu from Latin, fida from Spanish) show integration into native derivational patterns
