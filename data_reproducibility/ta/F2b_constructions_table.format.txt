| உண்மை (uṇmai) - "truth" | உண்மை இறுதியில் வெல்லும் | uṉmai iṟutiyil vellum | truth will triumph in the end, truth ultimately prevails | Proverbial expression about truth's ultimate victory. Common in moral and philosophical discourse. | [CORE] |
| உண்மை (uṇmai) - "truth" | முழு உண்மை | muḻu uṇmai | whole truth, complete truth, full truth | Expression emphasizing completeness of truth. Common in legal contexts and truth-telling situations. | [CORE] |
| உண்மை (uṇmai) - "truth" | பகுதி உண்மை | pakuti uṇmai | partial truth, part of the truth | Expression indicating incomplete or partial truth. Used when truth is not fully disclosed. | [CORE] |

| சரி (cari) - "correct, right" | அது சரி | atu cari | that is correct, that is right | Simple copular construction affirming correctness. Extremely common in everyday speech for agreement. | [CORE] |
