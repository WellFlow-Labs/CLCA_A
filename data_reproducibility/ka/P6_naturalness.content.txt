# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P6 CHANGE, CAUSATION, AND STABILITY
# Language: Georgian (ka)

---

## 1. BECOMING TRUE / CORRECT

### Pattern: X + TRUTH-ADJECTIVE + გახდა (gakhda) "became"

Georgian uses the verb გახდომა (gakhdoma) "to become" with truth-adjectives to express change into a true or correct state.

### Examples

#### 1.1 მართალი გახდა (mart'ali gakhda)
**Transliteration:** mart'ali gakhda
**English translation:** became true, turned out to be true, proved to be correct
**Usage notes:** Common expression for when something is revealed to be true or when a prediction proves correct. Used in narrative and explanatory contexts when facts emerge or suspicions are confirmed.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

**Example in context:**
- **მისი სიტყვები მართალი გახდა** (misi sit'q'vebi mart'ali gakhda) → "his words became true / turned out to be true"

#### 1.2 სწორი გახდა (sts'ori gakhda)
**Transliteration:** sts'ori gakhda
**English translation:** became correct, turned out to be right
**Usage notes:** Similar to მართალი გახდა but emphasizes procedural or technical correctness. Common when a method or answer proves to be right.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

**Example in context:**
- **ჩემი პასუხი სწორი გახდა** (chemi p'asukhi sts'ori gakhda) → "my answer turned out to be correct"

#### 1.3 ნამდვილი გახდა (namdvili gakhდა)
**Transliteration:** namdvili gakhდა
**English translation:** became real, became genuine, came true
**Usage notes:** Used when something imagined or hoped for becomes reality, or when authenticity is established. Common in contexts of dreams coming true or verification of authenticity.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

**Example in context:**
- **ოცნება ნამდვილი გახდა** (otsneba namdvili gakhდა) → "the dream became real / came true"

#### 1.4 ზუსტი გახდა (zusti gakhდა)
**Transliteration:** zusti gakhდა
**English translation:** became precise, became exact
**Usage notes:** Less common than other truth-adjectives with გახდა. Used when information or measurements become more precise through refinement.
**Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

**Example in context:**
- **მონაცემები უფრო ზუსტი გახდა** (monatsemebi upro zusti gakhდა) → "the data became more precise"

## 2. BECOMING FALSE / INCORRECT

### Pattern: X + FALSITY-ADJECTIVE + გახდა (gakhდა) "became"

Georgian uses the same verb გახდომა with falsity-adjectives to express change into a false or incorrect state.

### Examples

#### 2.1 მცდარი გახდა (mts'dari gakhდა)
**Transliteration:** mts'dari gakhდა
**English translation:** became false, became erroneous, turned out to be wrong
**Usage notes:** Common expression for when something previously believed true is revealed to be false or when a claim is disproven.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

**Example in context:**
- **ეს ინფორმაცია მცდარი გახდა** (es inpormatsia mts'dari gakhდა) → "this information became false / turned out to be wrong"

#### 2.2 საეჭვო გახდა (saech'vo gakhდა)
**Transliteration:** saech'vo gakhდა
**English translation:** became doubtful, became questionable
**Usage notes:** Common expression for when something previously trusted becomes questionable or when doubts arise about reliability.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

**Example in context:**
- **მისი სიტყვები საეჭვო გახდა** (misi sit'q'vebi saech'vo gakhდა) → "his words became doubtful"

#### 2.3 ყალბი გახდა (q'albi gakhდა)
**Transliteration:** q'albi gakhდა
**English translation:** became fake, turned out to be counterfeit
**Usage notes:** Used when something is discovered to be fake or counterfeit. Less common than მცდარი გახდა because ყალბი typically describes inherent properties rather than changes.
**Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

**Example in context:**
- **დოკუმენტი ყალბი გახდა** (dok'ument'i q'albi gakhდა) → "the document turned out to be fake"

## 3. ALTERNATIVE INCHOATIVE: ხდება (khdeba) "becomes"

### Pattern: X + ADJECTIVE + ხდება (khdeba) "becomes"

The verb ხდება (khdeba) is an alternative to გახდა (gakhდა), often preferred for ongoing or habitual change, or in present/future tense contexts.

### Examples

#### 3.1 მართალი ხდება (mart'ali khdeba)
**Transliteration:** mart'ali khdeba
**English translation:** becomes true, is becoming true
**Usage notes:** Used for ongoing process of becoming true or for general statements about truth emergence. More common in present/future contexts than გახდა.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

**Example in context:**
- **დროთა განმავლობაში ყველაფერი მართალი ხდება** (drota ganmavlobashi q'velaperi mart'ali khdeba) → "over time everything becomes true / comes to light"

#### 3.2 ცხადი ხდება (tskhadi khdeba)
**Transliteration:** tskhadi khdeba
**English translation:** becomes clear, becomes evident
**Usage notes:** Common expression for when truth or facts become clear or evident. Uses the adjective ცხადი (tskhadi) "clear, evident" rather than core truth-terms.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

**Example in context:**
- **სიმართლე ცხადი ხდება** (simart'le tskhadi khdeba) → "the truth becomes clear"

## 4. REVELATION / EMERGENCE OF TRUTH

### Pattern: TRUTH-NOUN + გამოვლინდა (gamovlinda) "was revealed"

Georgian commonly uses the verb გამოვლენა (gamovlena) "to reveal" in passive form to express truth coming to light.

### Examples

#### 4.1 სიმართლე გამოვლინდა (simart'le gamovlinda)
**Transliteration:** simart'le gamovlinda
**English translation:** the truth was revealed, the truth came to light
**Usage notes:** Very common expression for truth emerging or being discovered. The verb გამოვლენა emphasizes the process of revelation or uncovering.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

**Example in context:**
- **საბოლოოდ სიმართლე გამოვლინდა** (sabolood simart'le gamovlinda) → "finally the truth was revealed"

#### 4.2 სიმართლე გაირკვა (simart'le gairkva)
**Transliteration:** simart'le gairkva
**English translation:** the truth became clear, the truth was clarified
**Usage notes:** Common expression using the verb გარკვევა (garkveva) "to become clear, to be clarified." Emphasizes the process of clarification.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

**Example in context:**
- **საქმის სიმართლე გაირკვა** (sakmis simart'le gairkva) → "the truth of the matter became clear"

#### 4.3 სიმართლე ამოვიდა (simart'le amovida)
**Transliteration:** simart'le amovida
**English translation:** the truth came out, the truth emerged
**Usage notes:** Colloquial expression using the verb ამოსვლა (amosvla) "to come out, to emerge." Very common in everyday speech for truth emerging.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

**Example in context:**
- **ყველაფერი ამოვიდა ზღვის ფსკერზეც კი** (q'velaperi amovida zghvis pskerzetsk'i) → "everything came out, even from the bottom of the sea" (proverbial: truth will emerge)

## 5. CAUSATIVE: MAKING TRUE / PROVING

### Pattern: Y + დაამტკიცა (daamt'k'itsa) "proved" + TRUTH-NOUN

Georgian does not have productive morphological causatives for truth-adjectives. Instead, specific lexical verbs express causation of truth-states.

### Examples

#### 5.1 დაამტკიცა სიმართლე (daamt'k'itsa simart'le)
**Transliteration:** daamt'k'itsa simart'le
**English translation:** proved the truth, established the truth
**Usage notes:** Standard expression for proving or establishing truth. The verb დამტკიცება (damt'k'itseba) is the primary verb for "proving" in Georgian.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

**Example in context:**
- **მან დაამტკიცა თავისი სიტყვების სიმართლე** (man daamt'k'itsa tavisi sit'q'vebis simart'le) → "he proved the truth of his words"

#### 5.2 დაადასტურა (dadast'ura)
**Transliteration:** dadast'ura
**English translation:** confirmed, verified
**Usage notes:** Common verb for confirming or verifying truth. Takes truth-nouns or information-nouns as direct objects.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

**Example in context:**
- **დაადასტურა ინფორმაციის სიზუსტე** (dadast'ura inpormatsiis sizuste) → "confirmed the accuracy of the information"

#### 5.3 გამოავლინა სიმართლე (gamoavlina simart'le)
**Transliteration:** gamoavlina simart'le
**English translation:** revealed the truth, brought the truth to light
**Usage notes:** Active form of გამოვლენა (gamovlena) "to reveal." Common for deliberately revealing or uncovering truth.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

**Example in context:**
- **გამოძიებამ გამოავლინა სიმართლე** (gamodziebam gamoavlina simart'le) → "the investigation revealed the truth"

#### 5.4 გაამართლა (gamart'la)
**Transliteration:** gamart'la
**English translation:** justified, vindicated, proved right
**Usage notes:** Verb from the მართ- root meaning to justify or vindicate. Used when someone or something is proven right or justified.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

**Example in context:**
- **დრომ გაამართლა მისი გადაწყვეტილება** (drom gamart'la misi gadats'q'vet'ileba) → "time justified his decision / proved his decision right"

## 6. CAUSATIVE: MAKING FALSE / FALSIFYING

### Pattern: Y + გააყალბა (gaaq'alba) "falsified" + OBJECT

Georgian uses specific verbs for causing falsity, particularly in contexts of deliberate falsification.

### Examples

#### 6.1 გააყალბა (gaaq'alba)
**Transliteration:** gaaq'alba
**English translation:** falsified, counterfeited, forged
**Usage notes:** Standard verb for deliberately making something false or counterfeit. Common in legal and administrative contexts.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

**Example in context:**
- **გააყალბა დოკუმენტი** (gaaq'alba dok'ument'i) → "falsified the document"
- **გააყალბა ხელმოწერა** (gaaq'alba khelmots'era) → "forged the signature"

#### 6.2 მოატყუა (moat'q'ua)
**Transliteration:** moat'q'ua
**English translation:** deceived, fooled, cheated
**Usage notes:** Causative verb from the ტყუ- root meaning to deceive or fool someone. Common for deliberate deception.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

**Example in context:**
- **მოატყუა ხალხი** (moat'q'ua khalkhi) → "deceived the people"

#### 6.3 შეცდომაში შეიყვანა (shets'domashi sheiq'vana)
**Transliteration:** shets'domashi sheiq'vana
**English translation:** led into error, caused to err
**Usage notes:** Periphrastic causative meaning "to lead someone into error." Uses the noun შეცდომა (shets'doma) "error" with the verb შეყვანა (sheq'vana) "to lead into."
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

**Example in context:**
- **შეცდომაში შეიყვანა მოსამართლე** (shets'domashi sheiq'vana mosamart'le) → "led the judge into error"

## 10. STABILITY: REMAINING TRUE

### Pattern: X + რჩება (rcheba) "remains" + ADJECTIVE

Georgian uses the verb რჩება (rcheba) "to remain" to express stability or persistence of truth-states.

**Transliteration:** mart'ali rcheba
**English translation:** remains true, stays correct

**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

**Example in context:**
- **ეს მართალი რჩება დღესაც** (es mart'ali rcheba dghesats) → "this remains true even today"

#### 10.2 სანდო რჩება (sando rcheba)
**Transliteration:** sando rcheba
**English translation:** remains reliable, stays trustworthy
**Usage notes:** Common for expressing continued reliability or trustworthiness over time.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

**Example in context:**
- **ეს წყარო სანდო რჩება** (es ts'q'aro sando rcheba) → "this source remains reliable"

#### 10.3 უცვლელი რჩება (utsvleli rcheba)
**Transliteration:** utsvleli rcheba
**English translation:** remains unchanged, stays constant
**Usage notes:** Expression using the adjective უცვლელი (utsvleli) "unchanged" to emphasize stability. Common in contexts about enduring truths.
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

**Example in context:**

## 11. STABILITY: CONTINUING TO BE TRUE

### Pattern: X + აგრძელებს (agrdzelebs) + ADJECTIVE + ყოფნას (q'opnas) "continues to be"

Georgian can express continued state using the verb აგრძელებს (agrdzelebs) "continues" with infinitive constructions.

### Examples

#### 11.1 აგრძელებს მართალი ყოფნას (agrdzelebs mart'ali q'opnas)
**Transliteration:** agrdzelebs mart'ali q'opnas
**English translation:** continues to be true
**Usage notes:** Formal or emphatic expression for continued truth. Less common than simple რჩება (rcheba) "remains" but used for emphasis on ongoing validity.
**Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

**Example in context:**
- **ეს დებულება აგრძელებს მართალი ყოფნას** (es debuleba agrdzelebs mart'ali q'opnas) → "this statement continues to be true"

## SUMMARY

Georgian encodes truth-related dynamics through:

1. **Inchoative verbs:** გახდა (gakhდა) and ხდება (khdeba) "become" are highly productive with truth-adjectives
2. **Revelation verbs:** გამოვლინდა (gamovlinda) "was revealed," გაირკვა (gairkva) "became clear," ამოვიდა (amovida) "came out"
3. **Lexical causatives:** No productive morphological causatives; instead, specific verbs like დამტკიცება (damt'k'itseba) "proving," გამოვლენა (gamovlena) "revealing," გაყალბება (gaq'albeba) "falsifying"
4. **Verification verbs:** გადამოწმება (gadamots'meba) "verifying," დადასტურება (dadast'ureba) "confirming"
5. **Correction verbs:** გასწორება (gasts'oreba) "correcting," გაზუსტება (gazusteba) "clarifying"
6. **Stability expressions:** რჩება (rcheba) "remains," აგრძელებს (agrdzelebs) "continues"
7. **Change of degree:** უფრო...ხდება (upro...khdeba) "becomes more," ნაკლებად...ხდება (nak'lebad...khdeba) "becomes less"
8. **Acquisition/loss:** მოპოვება (mop'oveba) "acquiring," დაკარგვა (dak'argva) "losing"
9. **Epistemic actions:** დარწმუნება (darats'muneba) "convincing," ეჭვქვეშ დაყენება (ech'vkvesh daq'eneba) "casting doubt"
10. **Truth manipulation:** დამალვა (damalva) "hiding," დამახინჯება (damakhinjeba) "distorting," დაცვა (datsva) "defending"

Notable patterns:
- Truth-adjectives freely combine with inchoative verbs
- Causation requires specific lexical verbs rather than morphological causatives
- Georgian has rich vocabulary for epistemic actions (proving, verifying, revealing, concealing)
- Gradual change is expressed through comparative constructions with "become"
- Stability is expressed through "remain" rather than stative constructions
