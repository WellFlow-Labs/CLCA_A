# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P4b Construction Usage & Constraints
# Language: Tamil (ta)

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Epistemic Status | Usage Notes |
|------------------|---------|-----------------|-------------|------------------|-------------|
| **1. ATTRIBUTION** | அது உண்மை | atu uṇmai | That is true | [CORE] | Extremely frequent in everyday evaluative speech. Zero copula is unmarked/default for present tense third person. Explicit இருக்கு (irukku) used for emphasis, non-present tense, or non-third person subjects. |
| **1. ATTRIBUTION (alternate)** | அது உண்மை இருக்கு | atu uṇmai irukku | That is true (emphatic) | [CORE] | Explicit copula variant adds emphasis or marks non-present/non-third contexts. Both variants freely alternate in speech. |
| **2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION** | அது சரி இல்லை | atu cari illai | That is not correct | [CORE] | Standard negation for equational sentences. Extremely common in denial, correction, and disagreement contexts. இல்லை functions as both negative copula and negative existential without distinction. |
| **2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION (alternate)** | உண்மையற்ற | uṇmaiyaṟṟa | untrue, without truth | [CORE] | Adjectival negative using -அற்ற suffix. Used attributively before nouns rather than predicatively. Functionally distinct from இல்லை negation. |
| **3. ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION** | உண்மையான கதை | uṇmaiyāṉa katai | a true story | [CORE] | Most productive adjectival derivation pattern. Nearly universal across truth-domain vocabulary. Standard attributive position before modified noun. |
| **3. ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION (alternate)** | உண்மை கதை | uṇmai katai | true story | [CORE] | Bare noun compound without derivational morphology. Freely alternates with -ஆன form. Both constructions equally natural in everyday speech. |
| **4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE** | பொய் அம்பலமாகு | poy ampalamāku | The lie is exposed | [CORE] | Standard inchoative with ஆகு "become." Common in narrative contexts describing revelation or exposure. Productive with truth-domain terms. |
| **4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE (alternate)** | உண்மை தெரிய வரு | uṇmai teriya varu | The truth comes to be known | [CORE] | Alternative inchoative using வரு "come" for emergence/revelation. Particularly common with உண்மை. Both ஆகு and வரு patterns productive. |
| **5. CAUSATIVE** | உண்மையை வெளிப்படுத்து | uṇmaiyai veḷippaṭuttu | reveal the truth | [CORE] | Standard causative formation with -உத்து suffix. Common in contexts of deliberate revelation or clarification. Productive with verbs of revelation. |
| **5. CAUSATIVE (variant)** | தெளிவுபடுத்து | teḷivupaṭuttu | make clear, clarify | [CORE] | NOUN + படுத்து pattern particularly productive. Creates causatives meaning "cause to become X." Very frequent in explanatory and instructional contexts. |
| **6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION** | பொய்மை | poymai | falsehood, falsity | [MARGINAL] | Classical/literary pattern with -மை suffix. Less common in modern colloquial speech. Retained in formal registers and fixed expressions. |
| **6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION (alternate)** | உண்மைத்தன்மை | uṇmaittaṉmai | truthfulness, veracity | [CORE] | Modern formal pattern with -தன்மை "quality." Preferred in contemporary formal/analytical discourse. More productive than classical -மை in modern usage. |
| **7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION** | பொய்யன் | poyyan | liar (male) | [MARGINAL] | **Restricted to பொய் only.** Not productive across truth-domain vocabulary. Gender-marked forms (masculine -அன், neutral -ஆள்) exist but usage limited. |
| **7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION (note)** | உண்மை பேசுபவர் | uṉmai pēcupavar | truth-speaking person | [CORE] | Periphrastic expression required for "truth-teller." Direct agentive derivation *உண்மையன் not attested. Demonstrates non-productivity of agentive pattern. |
| **8. VERB + OBJECT COLLOCATIONS** | உண்மையைச் சொல்லு | uṇmaiyaic collu | tell the truth | [CORE] | Most common construction for truth-telling actions. உண்மை takes accusative -ஐ marker. Extremely frequent in everyday speech and interrogation contexts. |
| **8. VERB + OBJECT COLLOCATIONS (asymmetry)** | பொய் சொல்லு | poy collu | tell lies | [CORE] | **Asymmetry:** பொய் appears without accusative marking in this construction. Both verbs சொல்லு "say" and பேசு "speak" collocate with both terms. |
| **9. NOUN + NOUN COMPOUNDS** | பொய் சாட்சி | poy cāṭci | false testimony, perjury | [CORE] | Extremely productive pattern without overt derivational morphology. பொய் forms legal/commercial compounds; மெய் forms philosophical/spiritual compounds. Very frequent across registers. |
| **9. NOUN + NOUN COMPOUNDS (variant)** | மெய்ஞ்ஞானம் | meyññāṉam | true knowledge, enlightenment | [CORE] | மெய் particularly productive in philosophical/religious compounds. Sandhi rules apply (ஞ் doubling after -ய்). Common in spiritual discourse. |
| **10. INTERROGATIVE CONSTRUCTIONS** | உண்மையா? | uṇmaiyā? | Is it true? Really? | [CORE] | One of the most frequent constructions in everyday speech. Used for confirmation requests, expressing surprise, or challenging statements. Extremely common. |
| **10. INTERROGATIVE CONSTRUCTIONS (variant)** | நிஜமா? | nijamā? | Really? Is that so? | [CORE] | May be even more frequent colloquially than உண்மையா?. Functions identically. Both forms freely alternate in conversational Tamil. |
| **11. ADVERBIAL MANNER CONSTRUCTIONS** | உண்மையாக | uṇmaiyāka | truly, truthfully | [CORE] | Highly productive across truth-domain vocabulary. Parallel to -ஆன adjectival pattern. Nearly universal derivation for manner adverbs. |
| **11. ADVERBIAL MANNER CONSTRUCTIONS (emphatic)** | உண்மையாகவே | uṇmaiyākavē | truly indeed | [CORE] | Emphatic variant with -ஏ particle. Very common for intensification or insistence. Productive emphatic pattern across adverbials. |
| **12. LOCATIVE/ABSTRACT STATE CONSTRUCTIONS** | உண்மையில் | uṇmaiyil | in truth, actually, in reality | [CORE] | Extremely common as discourse marker and adverbial emphasizer. Functions both epistemically ("actually") and ontologically ("in reality"). Very frequent in everyday speech. |
| **12. LOCATIVE/ABSTRACT STATE CONSTRUCTIONS (note)** | பொய்யில் | poyyil | in falsehood | [MARGINAL] | Less common than உண்மையில். Restricted to literary/formal registers. Pattern less productive with பொய் than with உண்மை. |
| **13. NEGATIVE ADJECTIVAL DERIVATION** | உண்மையற்ற | uṇmaiyaṟṟa | without truth, untrue, false | [CORE] | Productive negative adjectival pattern with -அற்ற "without, lacking." Alternative to using பொய்யான for negation. Common with abstract nouns. |
| **13. NEGATIVE ADJECTIVAL DERIVATION (variant)** | சந்தேகமற்ற | cantēkamaṟṟa | without doubt, certain | [CORE] | Pattern productive beyond core truth-domain terms. Creates negative adjectives from abstract nouns generally. Frequent in formal and everyday registers. |
| **14. EMPHATIC/INTENSIFYING CONSTRUCTIONS** | உண்மையிலேயே | uṇmaiyi-l-ē-y-ē | truly indeed, really and truly | [CORE] | Characteristic Tamil particle stacking for emphasis. -ஏ primary emphatic; -ய்/-வ் euphonic connectors. Very common in colloquial speech for strong emphasis or insistence. |
| **14. EMPHATIC/INTENSIFYING CONSTRUCTIONS (variant)** | நிஜமாகவே | nijamāka-v-ē | really indeed | [CORE] | Alternative emphatic construction with நிஜம். Both forms extremely frequent. Particle stacking productive across adverbial bases. |
| **15. COMPARATIVE CONSTRUCTIONS** | உண்மையை விட | uṇmaiyai viṭa | more than truth, beyond truth | [MARGINAL] | Less productive with truth-domain vocabulary than with gradable adjectives. Used primarily in emphatic or hyperbolic contexts. Truth-domain terms treated as categorical rather than gradable. |
| **16. CONDITIONAL CONSTRUCTIONS** | உண்மையாக இருந்தால் | uṇmaiyāka iruntāl | if (it is) true | [CORE] | Standard conditional formation with இருந்தால் (conditional of இருக்கு "be"). Common in reasoning and argumentation contexts. |
| **16. CONDITIONAL CONSTRUCTIONS (colloquial)** | உண்மைன்னா | uṇmaiṉṉā | if true | [CORE] | Colloquial shortened form. Extremely frequent in everyday speech. Represents phonological reduction of என்றால் (eṉṟāl) "if (one says)." |
| **17. MODAL NECESSITY CONSTRUCTIONS** | உண்மையாக இருக்க வேண்டும் | uṇmaiyāka irukka vēṇṭum | must be true, should be true | [CORE] | Standard modal construction for necessity/obligation. வேண்டும் expresses both deontic ("should") and epistemic ("must") modality. Common in instructions and requirements. |
| **18. MODAL POSSIBILITY CONSTRUCTIONS** | உண்மையாக இருக்கலாம் | uṇmaiyāka irukkalām | may be true, might be true | [CORE] | Standard possibility modal with -லாம் suffix. Common in epistemic contexts expressing uncertainty or conjecture. |
| **18. MODAL POSSIBILITY CONSTRUCTIONS (negative)** | சாத்தியம் இல்லை | cāttiyam illai | (it is) not possible, impossible | [CORE] | Negative existential with சாத்தியம் "possibility" extremely common for impossibility. More frequent than negative modal forms. |
| **19. EVIDENTIAL/APPEARANCE CONSTRUCTIONS** | உண்மை என்று தெரிகிறது | uṇmai eṉṟu terikiṟatu | it appears to be true | [CORE] | Standard evidential construction with தெரி "appear, be visible, seem." என்று quotative/complementizer introduces complement. Common in inferential contexts. |
| **19. EVIDENTIAL/APPEARANCE CONSTRUCTIONS (negative)** | தெளிவாக தெரியவில்லை | teḷivāka teriyavillai | (it) doesn't appear clearly, (it's) not clear | [CORE] | Negative evidential with தெரியவில்லை "doesn't appear." Very frequent for expressing lack of clarity or uncertainty. |
| **20. CONTRASTIVE DISJUNCTION** | உண்மையா பொய்யா | uṇmaiyā poyyā | true or false? truth or lie? | [CORE] | Common construction for presenting binary alternatives in questions. Both elements take -ஆ interrogative particle. Extremely frequent in everyday evaluative speech and decision-making. |
| **20. CONTRASTIVE DISJUNCTION (variant)** | சரியா தப்பா | cariyā tappā | right or wrong? | [CORE] | Alternative binary pair. Both constructions equally productive. Used in moral/evaluative and correctness contexts respectively. |
| **21. RESULTATIVE VERB CONSTRUCTIONS** | உண்மை வெளிப்படு | uṇmai veḷippaṭu | truth comes out, is revealed | [CORE] | Resultative construction emphasizing change of state or emergence. வெளிப்படு "emerge, be revealed" particularly common with உண்மை. Frequent in narrative contexts. |
| **21. RESULTATIVE VERB CONSTRUCTIONS (variant)** | பொய் வெளியாகு | poy veḷiyāku | lie becomes exposed | [CORE] | Alternative resultative verb. Both வெளிப்படு and வெளியாகு productive. These are intransitive; causative counterparts use -உத்து suffix. |
| **22. IDIOMATIC METAPHORICAL CONSTRUCTIONS** | உண்மை வெளிச்சத்திற்கு வரு | uṇmai veḷiccattiṟku varu | truth comes to light | [CORE] | Semi-fixed idiomatic expression. வெளிச்சம் "light" metaphor for revelation common across languages. Frequent in narrative and journalistic contexts. |
| **22. IDIOMATIC METAPHORICAL CONSTRUCTIONS (variant)** | பொய் பிடிபடு | poy piṭipaṭu | lie is caught, discovered | [CORE] | Treats lies as physical objects that can be captured. பிடிபடு "be caught" common with பொய். Frequent in narrative and investigative contexts. |
| **23. IMPERATIVE CONSTRUCTIONS** | உண்மையைச் சொல்லு | uṇmaiyaic collu | Tell the truth! | [CORE] | Imperative is unmarked bare stem. Common in interrogation contexts and direct commands. Very frequent in confrontational or instructional speech. |
| **23. IMPERATIVE CONSTRUCTIONS (variant)** | சரி பார் | cari pār | Check (it)! Verify! | [CORE] | Alternative imperative with பார் "see, look." Common in instructions and verification requests. Both constructions extremely frequent. |
| **24. DEGREE/EXTENT MODIFICATION** | முழு உண்மை | muḻu uṇmai | the whole truth, complete truth | [MARGINAL] | Limited productivity. முழு "whole" and பகுதி "partial" are main degree modifiers attested. Truth generally treated as categorical rather than gradable, limiting degree modification. |
| **24. DEGREE/EXTENT MODIFICATION (variant)** | பகுதி உண்மை | pakuti uṇmai | partial truth, part of the truth | [MARGINAL] | Restricted to specific contexts (e.g., legal, philosophical). Not freely productive. Degree modification generally incompatible with categorical nature of truth-domain terms. |
| **25. ABSTRACT QUALITY NOMINALIZATION WITH -இன்மை** | உண்மையின்மை | uṇmaiyiṉmai | absence of truth, untruth | [MARGINAL] | Less productive than -அற்ற negative adjectival pattern. இன்மை "absence" formal/literary. More common to use negative adjectives or simple negation than this abstract nominalization. |
| **RECIPROCAL CONSTRUCTIONS** | (none attested) | — | — | [UNCERTAIN] | No fixed reciprocal constructions involving truth-domain terms identified. While grammatically possible using -கொள் reflexive/reciprocal suffix, no idiomatic or frequent expressions emerged from data. |
| **BENEFACTIVE CONSTRUCTIONS** | (none attested) | — | — | [UNCERTAIN] | No fixed idiomatic benefactive expressions with truth-domain terms identified. Grammatically possible using dative + -ஆக/-காக "for the sake of," but no attested idiomatic expressions. |
| **INSTRUMENTAL CONSTRUCTIONS** | (none attested) | — | — | [UNCERTAIN] | No fixed instrumental constructions identified. Instrumental case -ஆல் can be used grammatically but no idiomatic "by means of truth" type expressions emerged from data. |

## SUMMARY OBSERVATIONS

### High-Frequency Core Constructions
The following constructions are extremely frequent in everyday Tamil speech:
- Zero copula attribution (Construction 1)
- Negative attribution with இல்லை (Construction 2)
- Adnominal modification with -ஆன (Construction 3)
- Verb + object collocations with சொல்லு (Construction 8)
- Interrogative with -ஆ (Construction 10)
- Adverbial manner with -ஆக (Construction 11)
- Locative/discourse marker உண்மையில் (Construction 12)
- Emphatic particle stacking (Construction 14)
- Contrastive disjunction (Construction 20)

### Restricted/Marginal Constructions
- Agentive nominalization (Construction 7): **restricted to பொய் only**
- Classical abstract nominalization with -மை (Construction 6): literary/formal only
- Degree modification (Construction 24): limited productivity due to categorical nature of truth-domain
- Abstract nominalization with -இன்மை (Construction 25): formal/literary, less common than alternatives

### Notable Asymmetries
- **பொய் சொல்லு** vs. **உண்மையைச் சொல்லு**: பொய் lacks accusative marking (Construction 8)
- **Agentive derivation**: productive only with பொய் (பொய்யன் "liar"), not with உண்மை (Construction 7)
- **உண்மையில்** highly frequent as discourse marker; **பொய்யில்** marginal/literary (Construction 12)

### Unattested Patterns
No evidence found for:
- Reciprocal constructions with truth-domain terms
- Fixed benefactive expressions
- Fixed instrumental expressions

These gaps represent genuine restrictions rather than data limitations, as these construction types are productive in Tamil generally but do not form idiomatic expressions with truth-domain vocabulary.
