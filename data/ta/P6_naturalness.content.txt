# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P6 NATURALNESS: CHANGE, CAUSATION, AND STABILITY
# Language: Tamil (ta)

---

## METHODOLOGY STATEMENT

This analysis documents **attested expressions** for change, causation, and stability involving truth-domain vocabulary from P1-P5. All constructions are naturally occurring in Tamil usage. The analysis focuses on:

1. **Inchoative/change-of-state constructions** ("X becomes true/false")
2. **Causative constructions** ("Y makes X true/false")
3. **Stability/persistence expressions** ("X remains true/false")
4. **Aspectual variations** in truth-related dynamics

Each construction type is examined separately for different truth-domain terms, as they show varying productivity and naturalness.

---

## SECTION 1: INCHOATIVE / CHANGE-OF-STATE CONSTRUCTIONS

### 1.1 BECOMING TRUE/REAL — உண்மை (uṇmai) BASE

#### Construction 1.1.1
**Form:** உண்மை ஆகு (uṇmai āku)
**Transliteration:** uṇmai āku
**Literal breakdown:** uṇmai (truth) + āku (become)
**Translation:** "become true, turn out to be true"
**Usage context:** Used when something previously uncertain or doubted is revealed to be true. Common in narrative contexts describing revelation or confirmation of facts.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Very natural and common
**Example context:** "அவன் சொன்னது உண்மை ஆகிவிட்டது" (avaṉ coṉṉatu uṇmai ākiviṭṭatu) "What he said turned out to be true"
**Notes:** Completive auxiliary விடு (viṭu) or போ (pō) typically added for natural expression. விட்டது emphasizes completion of the change.

#### Construction 1.1.2
**Form:** உண்மை ஆகிப்போ (uṇmai ākippō)
**Transliteration:** uṇmai ākippō
**Literal breakdown:** uṇmai (truth) + āki (having become) + pō (go, completive)
**Translation:** "turn out to be true, end up being true"
**Usage context:** Similar to 1.1.1 but emphasizes unexpected or surprising revelation. போ (pō) auxiliary suggests change away from prior state.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Very natural, slightly more colloquial than விடு variant
**Example context:** "என் சந்தேகம் உண்மை ஆகிப்போச்சு" (eṉ cantēkam uṇmai ākippōccu) "My suspicion turned out to be true"
**Notes:** போச்சு (pōccu) is colloquial past form of போனது (pōṉatu).

#### Construction 1.1.3
**Form:** உண்மையாகு (uṇmaiyāku)
**Transliteration:** uṇmaiyāku
**Literal breakdown:** uṇmai (truth) + -y- (euphonic) + āku (become)
**Translation:** "become true, come to be true"
**Usage context:** Variant of 1.1.1 with euphonic -y- inserted. Functionally identical but slightly more formal.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Natural, slightly more formal register
**Example context:** "அந்த கனவு உண்மையாகிவிட்டது" (anta kaṉavu uṇmaiyākiviṭṭatu) "That dream became true/came true"
**Notes:** Common in contexts of wishes, predictions, or dreams coming true.

#### Construction 1.1.4
**Form:** உண்மையாக மாறு (uṇmaiyāka māṟu)
**Transliteration:** uṇmaiyāka māṟu
**Literal breakdown:** uṇmai (truth) + -āka (adverbial) + māṟu (change, transform)
**Translation:** "change into truth, transform into truth"
**Usage context:** Emphasizes transformation or conversion from false to true. Less common than ஆகு constructions.
**Naturalness:** [MARGINAL] — Grammatically possible but less natural than ஆகு
**Example context:** Rare in natural speech; ஆகு constructions strongly preferred
**Notes:** மாறு "change" is less idiomatic with truth-terms than ஆகு "become."

#### Construction 1.1.5
**Form:** உண்மை தெரிய வரு (uṇmai teriya varu)
**Transliteration:** uṇmai teriya varu
**Literal breakdown:** uṇmai (truth) + teriya (to be known) + varu (come)
**Translation:** "truth comes to be known, truth emerges"
**Usage context:** Emphasizes gradual discovery or revelation process rather than sudden change of state. Very common in investigative and narrative contexts.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Very natural and frequent
**Example context:** "விசாரணையில் உண்மை தெரிய வந்தது" (vicāraṇaiyil uṇmai teriya vantatu) "The truth came to be known in the investigation"
**Notes:** This is technically a revelation construction but functions as inchoative from epistemic perspective.

#### Construction 1.1.6
**Form:** உண்மை வெளிப்படு (uṇmai veḷippaṭu)
**Transliteration:** uṇmai veḷippaṭu
**Literal breakdown:** uṇmai (truth) + veḷippaṭu (come out, be revealed)
**Translation:** "truth comes out, truth is revealed"
**Usage context:** Emphasizes emergence from concealment. Very common when hidden truth becomes known.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Extremely natural and frequent
**Example context:** "இறுதியில் உண்மை வெளிப்பட்டது" (iṟutiyil uṇmai veḷippaṭṭatu) "Finally the truth came out"
**Notes:** Implies prior concealment or hiddenness. One of the most common truth-revelation expressions.

### 1.2 BECOMING FALSE/LIE — பொய் (poy) BASE

#### Construction 1.2.1
**Form:** பொய் ஆகு (poy āku)
**Transliteration:** poy āku
**Literal breakdown:** poy (lie, falsehood) + āku (become)
**Translation:** "become false, turn out to be a lie"
**Usage context:** Used when something previously believed true is revealed to be false. Common in contexts of discovering deception.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Natural and common
**Example context:** "அவன் வாக்குறுதி பொய் ஆகிவிட்டது" (avaṉ vākkuṟuti poy ākiviṭṭatu) "His promise turned out to be false/became a lie"
**Notes:** Parallel to உண்மை ஆகு but implies disappointment or betrayal.

#### Construction 1.2.2
**Form:** பொய் ஆகிப்போ (poy ākippō)
**Transliteration:** poy ākippō
**Literal breakdown:** poy (lie) + āki (having become) + pō (go, completive)
**Translation:** "turn out to be false, end up being a lie"
**Usage context:** Emphasizes unexpected or disappointing revelation of falsehood.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Very natural, common in colloquial speech
**Example context:** "அவன் சொன்னது எல்லாம் பொய் ஆகிப்போச்சு" (avaṉ coṉṉatu ellām poy ākippōccu) "Everything he said turned out to be lies"
**Notes:** போ auxiliary emphasizes change from prior belief.

#### Construction 1.2.3
**Form:** பொய் வெளிப்படு (poy veḷippaṭu)
**Transliteration:** poy veḷippaṭu
**Literal breakdown:** poy (lie) + veḷippaṭu (come out, be revealed)
**Translation:** "lie is exposed, falsehood is revealed"
**Usage context:** Emphasizes unmasking or exposure of deception. Very common when lies are discovered.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Extremely natural and frequent
**Example context:** "அவன் பொய் வெளிப்பட்டுவிட்டது" (avaṉ poy veḷippaṭṭuviṭṭatu) "His lie was exposed"
**Notes:** Often implies negative consequences for the deceiver.

#### Construction 1.2.4
**Form:** பொய் தெரிய வரு (poy teriya varu)
**Transliteration:** poy teriya varu
**Literal breakdown:** poy (lie) + teriya (to be known) + varu (come)
**Translation:** "lie comes to be known, falsehood is discovered"
**Usage context:** Emphasizes gradual discovery of deception.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Natural and common
**Example context:** "விசாரணையில் பொய் தெரிய வந்தது" (vicāraṇaiyil poy teriya vantatu) "The lie came to be known in the investigation"
**Notes:** Parallel to உண்மை தெரிய வரு but for negative revelation.

### 1.3 BECOMING TRUE/REAL — மெய் (mey) BASE

#### Construction 1.3.1
**Form:** மெய் ஆகு (mey āku)
**Transliteration:** mey āku
**Literal breakdown:** mey (truth, reality) + āku (become)
**Translation:** "become true, become real"
**Usage context:** More literary/philosophical than உண்மை ஆகு. Used in elevated discourse about reality versus appearance.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Natural but more formal/literary register
**Example context:** "கனவு மெய் ஆகிவிட்டது" (kaṉavu mey ākiviṭṭatu) "The dream became real"
**Notes:** மெய் emphasizes essential reality more than factual correspondence.

#### Construction 1.3.2
**Form:** மெய்யாகு (meyyāku)
**Transliteration:** meyyāku
**Literal breakdown:** mey (truth) + -y- (euphonic) + āku (become)
**Translation:** "become true, become real"
**Usage context:** Variant with euphonic -y-. Functionally identical to மெய் ஆகு.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Natural, literary register
**Example context:** "அது மெய்யாகிவிட்டது" (atu meyyākiviṭṭatu) "That became true/real"
**Notes:** Common in literary and philosophical contexts.

#### Construction 1.3.3
**Form:** மெய் தெரிய வரு (mey teriya varu)
**Transliteration:** mey teriya varu
**Literal breakdown:** mey (truth) + teriya (to be known) + varu (come)
**Translation:** "truth/reality comes to be known"
**Usage context:** Philosophical/spiritual contexts about realization of truth or reality.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Natural in philosophical register
**Example context:** "தியானத்தில் மெய் தெரிய வந்தது" (tiyāṉattil mey teriya vantatu) "Truth was realized in meditation"
**Notes:** Common in spiritual discourse about enlightenment.

### 1.4 BECOMING CORRECT — சரி (cari) BASE

#### Construction 1.4.1
**Form:** சரி ஆகு (cari āku)
**Transliteration:** cari āku
**Literal breakdown:** cari (correct, right) + āku (become)
**Translation:** "become correct, become right, work out, be fixed"
**Usage context:** Very common for things being corrected, fixed, or working out properly. Used for both physical repairs and situational improvements.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Extremely natural and frequent
**Example context:** "எல்லாம் சரி ஆகிவிடும்" (ellām cari ākiviṭum) "Everything will work out/be okay"
**Notes:** One of the most common expressions for reassurance and optimism.

#### Construction 1.4.2
**Form:** சரி ஆகிப்போ (cari ākippō)
**Transliteration:** cari ākippō
**Literal breakdown:** cari (correct) + āki (having become) + pō (go, completive)
**Translation:** "get fixed, work out, turn out okay"
**Usage context:** Emphasizes completion of correction or resolution. Very common in colloquial speech.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Extremely natural and frequent
**Example context:** "பிரச்சனை சரி ஆகிப்போச்சு" (piraccaṉai cari ākippōccu) "The problem got fixed/worked out"
**Notes:** போ auxiliary emphasizes change from problematic to resolved state.

#### Construction 1.4.3
**Form:** சரி ஆகாது (cari ākātu)
**Transliteration:** cari ākātu
**Literal breakdown:** cari (correct) + ākātu (will not become)
**Translation:** "won't work out, won't be okay, won't be fixed"
**Usage context:** Negative prediction about outcomes. Common in pessimistic assessments.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Very natural and common
**Example context:** "இப்படி செய்தால் சரி ஆகாது" (ippaṭi ceytāl cari ākātu) "If you do it this way, it won't work out"
**Notes:** Direct negative of சரி ஆகு, equally frequent.

---

### 1.5 BECOMING WRONG/MISTAKEN — தப்பு (tappu) BASE

#### Construction 1.5.1
**Form:** தப்பு ஆகு (tappu āku)
**Transliteration:** tappu āku
**Literal breakdown:** tappu (wrong, mistake) + āku (become)
**Translation:** "become wrong, turn into a mistake"
**Usage context:** Less common than சரி ஆகு. Used when something correct becomes wrong or when actions lead to mistakes.
**Naturalness:** [MARGINAL] — Grammatically possible but less natural than alternatives
**Example context:** Rare; speakers prefer தப்பு செய் "make a mistake" or தவறு நடக்கு "error happens"
**Notes:** தப்பு more commonly appears as object of செய் "do" than subject of ஆகு "become."

#### Construction 1.5.2
**Form:** தப்பு நடக்கு (tappu naṭakku)
**Transliteration:** tappu naṭakku
**Literal breakdown:** tappu (mistake) + naṭakku (happen, occur)
**Translation:** "mistake happens, error occurs"
**Usage context:** More natural than தப்பு ஆகு for describing occurrence of mistakes.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Natural and common
**Example context:** "தப்பு நடந்துவிட்டது" (tappu naṭantuviṭṭatu) "A mistake happened"
**Notes:** நடக்கு "happen" is more natural than ஆகு "become" for mistake-events.

---

### 1.6 BECOMING WRONG/ERRONEOUS — தவறு (tavaṟu) BASE

#### Construction 1.6.1
**Form:** தவறு ஆகு (tavaṟu āku)
**Transliteration:** tavaṟu āku
**Literal breakdown:** tavaṟu (error, mistake) + āku (become)
**Translation:** "become an error, turn into a mistake"
**Usage context:** Similar to தப்பு ஆகு but slightly more formal. Still less common than alternatives.
**Naturalness:** [MARGINAL] — Grammatically possible but less natural
**Example context:** Rare; தவறு நடக்கு or தவறு செய் preferred
**Notes:** தவறு patterns similarly to தப்பு in preferring செய் or நடக்கு over ஆகு.

#### Construction 1.6.2
**Form:** தவறு நடக்கு (tavaṟu naṭakku)
**Transliteration:** tavaṟu naṭakku
**Literal breakdown:** tavaṟu (error) + naṭakku (happen, occur)
**Translation:** "error happens, mistake occurs"
**Usage context:** More natural than தவறு ஆகு for describing occurrence of errors.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Natural, slightly more formal than தப்பு நடக்கு
**Example context:** "தவறு நடந்துவிட்டது" (tavaṟu naṭantuviṭṭatu) "An error occurred"
**Notes:** Parallel to தப்பு நடக்கு but more formal register.

## SECTION 2: CAUSATIVE CONSTRUCTIONS

### 2.1 MAKING TRUE/VERIFYING — உண்மை (uṇmai) BASE

#### Construction 2.1.1
**Form:** உண்மைப்படுத்து (uṇmaippaṭuttu)
**Transliteration:** uṇmaippaṭuttu
**Literal breakdown:** uṇmai (truth) + paṭuttu (causative: cause to become)
**Translation:** "verify, authenticate, make true, confirm"
**Usage context:** Standard term for verification and authentication in formal, administrative, and technical contexts. Very common in official documentation and procedures.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Extremely natural and frequent in formal registers
**Example context:** "ஆவணங்களை உண்மைப்படுத்த வேண்டும்" (āvaṇaṅkaḷai uṇmaippaṭutta vēṇṭum) "The documents must be authenticated"
**Notes:** This is the primary causative form for truth-verification. Uses -படுத்து causative morphology.

#### Construction 2.1.2
**Form:** உண்மையாக்கு (uṇmaiyākku)
**Transliteration:** uṇmaiyākku
**Literal breakdown:** uṇmai (truth) + -y- (euphonic) + ākku (causative: make become)
**Translation:** "make true, render true"
**Usage context:** Less common than உண்மைப்படுத்து. Used when actively making something true or real.
**Naturalness:** [MARGINAL] — Grammatically possible but less common than படுத்து form
**Example context:** "கனவை உண்மையாக்க முயற்சி செய்கிறேன்" (kaṉavai uṇmaiyākka muyaṟci ceykiṟēṉ) "I'm trying to make the dream come true"
**Notes:** Uses -ஆக்கு causative morphology. More common for making dreams/wishes come true than for verification.

#### Construction 2.1.3
**Form:** உண்மையென நிரூபி (uṇmaiyeṉa nirūpi)
**Transliteration:** uṇmaiyeṉa nirūpi
**Literal breakdown:** uṇmai (truth) + eṉa (as, that) + nirūpi (prove)
**Translation:** "prove to be true, demonstrate as true"
**Usage context:** Formal/academic expression for proving truth with evidence. Common in scientific and legal contexts.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Natural in formal/academic registers
**Example context:** "கோட்பாட்டை உண்மையென நிரூபித்தார்" (kōṭpāṭṭai uṇmaiyeṉa nirūpittār) "He proved the theory to be true"
**Notes:** நிரூபி (< Sanskrit) marks formal proof contexts.

#### Construction 2.1.4
**Form:** உண்மையை நிலைநாட்டு (uṇmaiyai nilaināttu)
**Transliteration:** uṇmaiyai nilaināttu
**Literal breakdown:** uṇmai (truth, accusative) + nilai (state) + nāṭṭu (establish, plant)
**Translation:** "establish the truth, make truth prevail"
**Usage context:** Formal/elevated expression for establishing or vindicating truth. Common in moral and legal discourse.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Natural in formal/elevated registers
**Example context:** "நீதிமன்றத்தில் உண்மையை நிலைநாட்ட வேண்டும்" (nītimaṉṟattil uṇmaiyai nilainātṭa vēṇṭum) "Truth must be established in court"
**Notes:** நிலைநாட்டு "establish" is formal verb for making something prevail.

### 2.2 MAKING FALSE/FALSIFYING — பொய் (poy) BASE

#### Construction 2.2.1
**Form:** பொய்யாக்கு (poyyākku)
**Transliteration:** poyyākku
**Literal breakdown:** poy (false) + -y- (euphonic) + ākku (causative: make become)
**Translation:** "falsify, make false, render false"
**Usage context:** Used for actively falsifying or making something false. Less common than உண்மைப்படுத்து.
**Naturalness:** [MARGINAL] — Grammatically possible but not highly frequent
**Example context:** "ஆதாரங்களை பொய்யாக்க முயன்றான்" (ātāraṅkaḷai poyyākka muyaṉṟāṉ) "He tried to falsify the evidence"
**Notes:** Uses -ஆக்கு causative, NOT -படுத்து. No common *பொய்ப்படுத்து form exists, showing asymmetry with உண்மைப்படுத்து.

#### Construction 2.2.2
**Form:** பொய்யென நிரூபி (poyyeṉa nirūpi)
**Transliteration:** poyyeṉa nirūpi
**Literal breakdown:** poy (false) + eṉa (as, that) + nirūpi (prove)
**Translation:** "prove to be false, demonstrate as false"
**Usage context:** Formal expression for proving something false. Parallel to உண்மையென நிரூபி.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Natural in formal/academic registers
**Example context:** "குற்றச்சாட்டை பொய்யென நிரூபித்தார்" (kuṟṟaccāṭṭai poyyeṉa nirūpittār) "He proved the accusation to be false"
**Notes:** Equally natural as positive equivalent in formal contexts.

#### Construction 2.2.3
**Form:** பொய் கட்டு (poy kaṭṭu)
**Transliteration:** poy kaṭṭu
**Literal breakdown:** poy (lie) + kaṭṭu (tie, construct, fabricate)
**Translation:** "fabricate a lie, concoct a lie"
**Usage context:** Very common colloquial expression for creating or inventing lies. Not exactly causative but functions to create falsehood.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Extremely natural and frequent in colloquial speech
**Example context:** "அவன் பொய் கட்டுகிறான்" (avaṉ poy kaṭṭukiṟāṉ) "He's fabricating lies"
**Notes:** கட்டு literally "tie, build" suggests deliberate construction of falsehood.

#### Construction 2.2.4
**Form:** பொய் உண்டாக்கு (poy uṇṭākku)
**Transliteration:** poy uṇṭākku
**Literal breakdown:** poy (lie) + uṇṭākku (create, make, produce)
**Translation:** "create a lie, make up a lie"
**Usage context:** More neutral than பொய் கட்டு for creating lies. Common in describing fabrication.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Natural and common
**Example context:** "பொய் உண்டாக்காதே" (poy uṇṭākkātē) "Don't make up lies"
**Notes:** உண்டாக்கு "create" is general creation verb.

### 2.3 MAKING TRUE/PROVING — மெய் (mey) BASE

#### Construction 2.3.1
**Form:** மெய்ப்படுத்து (meyppaṭuttu)
**Transliteration:** meyppaṭuttu
**Literal breakdown:** mey (truth) + paṭuttu (causative: cause to become)
**Translation:** "prove, verify, authenticate, confirm"
**Usage context:** Formal term for proving or verifying, slightly more literary/formal than உண்மைப்படுத்து. Common in academic and philosophical contexts.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Very natural in formal/literary registers
**Example context:** "கருத்தை மெய்ப்படுத்த வேண்டும்" (karuttai meyppaṭutta vēṇṭum) "The hypothesis must be verified"
**Notes:** Parallel to உண்மைப்படுத்து but more formal/literary register.

#### Construction 2.3.2
**Form:** மெய்யாக்கு (meyyākku)
**Transliteration:** meyyākku
**Literal breakdown:** mey (truth) + -y- (euphonic) + ākku (causative: make become)
**Translation:** "make true, make real"
**Usage context:** Less common than மெய்ப்படுத்து. Used for making something real or actualizing it.
**Naturalness:** [MARGINAL] — Grammatically possible but less common than படுத்து form
**Example context:** Rare; மெய்ப்படுத்து strongly preferred
**Notes:** Similar pattern to உண்மையாக்கு but even less common.

#### Construction 2.3.3
**Form:** மெய்யென காட்டு (meyyeṉa kāṭṭu)
**Transliteration:** meyyeṉa kāṭṭu
**Literal breakdown:** mey (truth) + eṉa (as, that) + kāṭṭu (show)
**Translation:** "show to be true, demonstrate as true"
**Usage context:** Used when demonstrating or proving truth. Common in argumentative contexts.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Natural in formal and argumentative registers
**Example context:** "அதை மெய்யென காட்ட முடியும்" (atai meyyeṉa kāṭṭa muṭiyum) "That can be shown to be true"
**Notes:** காட்டு "show" is less formal than நிரூபி "prove."

---

### 2.4 MAKING CORRECT/FIXING — சரி (cari) BASE

#### Construction 2.4.1
**Form:** சரி செய் (cari cey)
**Transliteration:** cari cey
**Literal breakdown:** cari (correct) + cey (do, make)
**Translation:** "correct, fix, repair, adjust"
**Usage context:** Extremely common light verb construction for correcting, fixing, or repairing anything from physical objects to errors to situations.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Extremely natural and frequent across all registers
**Example context:** "கணினியை சரி செய்கிறேன்" (kaṇiṉiyai cari ceykiṟēṉ) "I'm fixing the computer"
**Notes:** This is THE primary expression for fixing/correcting in Tamil. செய் is light verb.

#### Construction 2.4.2
**Form:** சரி செய்துவிடு (cari ceytuviṭu)
**Transliteration:** cari ceytuviṭu
**Literal breakdown:** cari (correct) + ceytu (having done) + viṭu (release, completive)
**Translation:** "fix it up, get it corrected, finish fixing"
**Usage context:** Completive form emphasizing completion of correction or repair. Very common in instructions and promises.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Extremely natural and frequent
**Example context:** "நான் சரி செய்துவிடுகிறேன்" (nāṉ cari ceytuviṭukiṟēṉ) "I'll get it fixed/I'll fix it up"
**Notes:** விடு completive emphasizes finishing the repair.

#### Construction 2.4.3
**Form:** சரி செய்துகொள் (cari ceytukkoḷ)
**Transliteration:** cari ceytukkoḷ
**Literal breakdown:** cari (correct) + ceytu (having done) + koḷ (take, reflexive)
**Translation:** "fix for oneself, correct personally"
**Usage context:** Reflexive form emphasizing personal fixing or self-correction. Common when someone fixes their own mistakes or things.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Very natural and common
**Example context:** "தப்பை சரி செய்துகொள்" (tappai cari ceytukkoḷ) "Fix your mistake (yourself)"
**Notes:** Reflexive -கொள் marks self-benefactive action.

#### Construction 2.4.4
**Form:** சரிப்படுத்து (caripaṭuttu)
**Transliteration:** caripaṭuttu
**Literal breakdown:** cari (correct) + paṭuttu (causative)
**Translation:** "correct, rectify, adjust"
**Usage context:** More formal than சரி செய். Used in technical and administrative contexts for corrections and adjustments.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Natural in formal/technical registers
**Example context:** "பிழைகளை சரிப்படுத்த வேண்டும்" (piḻaikaḷai caripaṭutta vēṇṭum) "The errors must be corrected"
**Notes:** படுத்து causative is more formal than செய் light verb construction.

---

### 2.5 CAUSING MISTAKES/ERRORS — தப்பு (tappu) / தவறு (tavaṟu) BASE

#### Construction 2.5.1
**Form:** தப்பு செய் (tappu cey)
**Transliteration:** tappu cey
**Literal breakdown:** tappu (mistake, wrong) + cey (do, make)
**Translation:** "make a mistake, do wrong, commit an error"
**Usage context:** Extremely common expression for making mistakes or doing wrong. Used for both factual errors and moral transgressions.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Extremely natural and frequent
**Example context:** "நான் தப்பு செய்துவிட்டேன்" (nāṉ tappu ceytuviṭṭēṉ) "I made a mistake"
**Notes:** This is NOT causative in the sense of making something else wrong, but rather committing the act of error. தப்பு is object of செய், not causativized.

#### Construction 2.5.2
**Form:** தவறு செய் (tavaṟu cey)
**Transliteration:** tavaṟu cey
**Literal breakdown:** tavaṟu (error, mistake) + cey (do, make)
**Translation:** "make an error, commit a mistake"
**Usage context:** Parallel to தப்பு செய் but slightly more formal. Common in formal and written contexts.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Natural, more formal than தப்பு செய்
**Example context:** "தவறு செய்துவிட்டேன்" (tavaṟu ceytuviṭṭēṉ) "I committed an error"
**Notes:** More formal register than தப்பு செய்.

#### Construction 2.5.3
**Form:** தப்பாக்கு (tappākku)
**Transliteration:** tappākku
**Literal breakdown:** tappu (wrong) + ākku (causative: make become)
**Translation:** "make wrong, render incorrect"
**Usage context:** Rare. Used when actively making something wrong or incorrect.
**Naturalness:** [MARGINAL] — Grammatically possible but very rare
**Example context:** Extremely rare in natural speech; speakers prefer other expressions
**Notes:** Much less common than சரி செய் "make correct." Asymmetry in causative productivity.

#### Construction 2.5.4
**Form:** தவறாக்கு (tavaṟākku)
**Transliteration:** tavaṟākku
**Literal breakdown:** tavaṟu (error) + ākku (causative: make become)
**Translation:** "make erroneous, render incorrect"
**Usage context:** Rare. Slightly more formal than தப்பாக்கு but still uncommon.
**Naturalness:** [MARGINAL] — Grammatically possible but very rare
**Example context:** Extremely rare in natural speech
**Notes:** Parallel rarity to தப்பாக்கு.

---

### 2.6 CAUSING REVELATION/EXPOSURE

#### Construction 2.6.1
**Form:** உண்மையை வெளிப்படுத்து (uṇmaiyai veḷippaṭuttu)
**Transliteration:** uṇmaiyai veḷippaṭuttu
**Literal breakdown:** uṇmai (truth, accusative) + veḷippaṭuttu (cause to come out, reveal)
**Translation:** "reveal the truth, bring the truth to light, expose the truth"
**Usage context:** Causative form of வெளிப்படு "come out." Used when someone actively reveals or exposes hidden truth. Very common in journalistic and investigative contexts.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Very natural and frequent
**Example context:** "பத்திரிகையாளர் உண்மையை வெளிப்படுத்தினார்" (pattirikai yāḷar uṇmaiyai veḷippaṭuttiṉār) "The journalist revealed the truth"
**Notes:** This is causative of intransitive வெளிப்படு, meaning to cause truth to emerge.

#### Construction 2.6.2
**Form:** பொய்யை வெளிப்படுத்து (poyyai veḷippaṭuttu)
**Transliteration:** poyyai veḷippaṭuttu
**Literal breakdown:** poy (lie, accusative) + veḷippaṭuttu (cause to come out, reveal)
**Translation:** "expose the lie, reveal the falsehood, unmask the deception"
**Usage context:** Parallel to உண்மையை வெளிப்படுத்து but for exposing lies. Common in contexts of unmasking deception.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Very natural and frequent
**Example context:** "அவன் பொய்யை வெளிப்படுத்தினார்கள்" (avaṉ poyyai veḷippaṭuttiṉārkaḷ) "They exposed his lie"
**Notes:** Equally productive as positive equivalent.

#### Construction 2.6.3
**Form:** உண்மையை அம்பலப்படுத்து (uṇmaiyai ampalapaṭuttu)
**Transliteration:** uṇmaiyai ampalapaṭuttu
**Literal breakdown:** uṇmai (truth, accusative) + ampalam (public) + paṭuttu (causative)
**Translation:** "expose the truth publicly, bring to public light"
**Usage context:** Emphasizes public exposure or revelation. Common in journalistic and political contexts.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Natural in journalistic/political registers
**Example context:** "ஊழலை அம்பலப்படுத்தினார்" (ūḻalai ampalapaṭuttiṉār) "He exposed the corruption publicly"
**Notes:** அம்பலம் "public" emphasizes public nature of revelation.

#### Construction 2.6.4
**Form:** பொய்யை அம்பலப்படுத்து (poyyai ampalapaṭuttu)
**Transliteration:** poyyai ampalapaṭuttu
**Literal breakdown:** poy (lie, accusative) + ampalam (public) + paṭuttu (causative)
**Translation:** "expose the lie publicly, unmask the deception publicly"
**Usage context:** Parallel to உண்மையை அம்பலப்படுத்து for public exposure of lies.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Natural in journalistic/political registers
**Example context:** "அவன் பொய்யை அம்பலப்படுத்தினார்கள்" (avaṉ poyyai ampalapaṭuttiṉārkaḷ) "They publicly exposed his lie"
**Notes:** Equally productive as positive equivalent.

## SECTION 3: STABILITY AND PERSISTENCE CONSTRUCTIONS

### 3.1 REMAINING TRUE — உண்மை (uṇmai) BASE

#### Construction 3.1.1
**Form:** உண்மையாக இரு (uṇmaiyāka iru)
**Transliteration:** uṇmaiyāka iru
**Literal breakdown:** uṇmai (truth) + -āka (adverbial) + iru (be, remain, stay)
**Translation:** "remain true, stay true, continue to be true"
**Usage context:** Used when something continues to be true over time despite challenges or doubts.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Natural and common
**Example context:** "அது இன்னும் உண்மையாக இருக்கிறது" (atu iṉṉum uṇmaiyāka irukkiṟatu) "That still remains true"
**Notes:** இரு "be, remain" is the standard verb for continued state.

#### Construction 3.1.2
**Form:** உண்மையாகவே இரு (uṇmaiyākavē iru)
**Transliteration:** uṇmaiyākavē iru
**Literal breakdown:** uṇmai (truth) + -āka (adverbial) + -vē (emphatic) + iru (be, remain)
**Translation:** "remain truly true, continue to be genuinely true"
**Usage context:** Emphatic form emphasizing continued truth despite doubts or challenges.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Natural, emphatic
**Example context:** "அது உண்மையாகவே இருக்கிறது" (atu uṇmaiyākavē irukkiṟatu) "That remains genuinely true"
**Notes:** -வே adds emphatic force.

#### Construction 3.1.3
**Form:** உண்மையாக நிலைத்திரு (uṇmaiyāka nilaittiru)
**Transliteration:** uṇmaiyāka nilaittiru
**Literal breakdown:** uṇmai (truth) + -āka (adverbial) + nilai (state) + iru (be, remain)
**Translation:** "remain steadfastly true, persist as true"
**Usage context:** Emphasizes enduring or persistent truth. More formal than simple இரு.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Natural in formal/literary registers
**Example context:** "அந்த கொள்கை உண்மையாக நிலைத்திருக்கிறது" (anta koḷkai uṇmaiyāka nilaittirukkiratu) "That principle remains steadfastly true"
**Notes:** நிலைத்திரு emphasizes stability and persistence.

#### Construction 3.1.4
**Form:** உண்மையாக தொடர் (uṇmaiyāka toṭar)
**Transliteration:** uṇmaiyāka toṭar
**Literal breakdown:** uṇmai (truth) + -āka (adverbial) + toṭar (continue)
**Translation:** "continue to be true, remain true over time"
**Usage context:** Emphasizes continuation of truth over time.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Natural and common
**Example context:** "அது உண்மையாக தொடர்கிறது" (atu uṇmaiyāka toṭarkiṟatu) "That continues to be true"
**Notes:** தொடர் "continue" emphasizes temporal persistence.

---

### 3.2 REMAINING FALSE — பொய் (poy) BASE

#### Construction 3.2.1
**Form:** பொய்யாக இரு (poyyāka iru)
**Transliteration:** poyyāka iru
**Literal breakdown:** poy (false) + -āka (adverbial) + iru (be, remain)
**Translation:** "remain false, stay false, continue to be false"
**Usage context:** Used when something continues to be false or untrue over time.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Natural and common
**Example context:** "அது பொய்யாகவே இருக்கிறது" (atu poyyākavē irukkiṟatu) "That remains false"
**Notes:** Parallel to உண்மையாக இரு.

#### Construction 3.2.2
**Form:** பொய்யாக நிலைத்திரு (poyyāka nilaittiru)
**Transliteration:** poyyāka nilaittiru
**Literal breakdown:** poy (false) + -āka (adverbial) + nilai (state) + iru (be, remain)
**Translation:** "persist as false, remain steadfastly false"
**Usage context:** Emphasizes enduring falsehood. Less common than positive equivalent.
**Naturalness:** [MARGINAL] — Grammatically possible but less natural than simple இரு
**Example context:** Rare; simple இரு preferred
**Notes:** Less common than உண்மையாக நிலைத்திரு, showing asymmetry.

---

### 3.3 REMAINING CORRECT — சரி (cari) BASE

#### Construction 3.3.1
**Form:** சரியாக இரு (cariyāka iru)
**Transliteration:** cariyāka iru
**Literal breakdown:** cari (correct) + -āka (adverbial) + iru (be, remain)
**Translation:** "remain correct, stay right, continue to be okay"
**Usage context:** Used when something continues to be correct or okay over time.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Very natural and common
**Example context:** "எல்லாம் சரியாக இருக்கிறது" (ellām cariyāka irukkiṟatu) "Everything remains okay/is fine"
**Notes:** Extremely common for describing continued well-being or correctness.

#### Construction 3.3.2
**Form:** சரியாகவே இரு (cariyākavē iru)
**Transliteration:** cariyākavē iru
**Literal breakdown:** cari (correct) + -āka (adverbial) + -vē (emphatic) + iru (be, remain)
**Translation:** "remain perfectly fine, stay completely okay"
**Usage context:** Emphatic form for reassurance or confirmation of continued correctness.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Very natural and common
**Example context:** "நீ சரியாகவே இருக்கிறாயா?" (nī cariyākavē irukkiṟāyā) "Are you perfectly fine?"
**Notes:** Very common in health inquiries and reassurance.

#### Construction 3.3.3
**Form:** சரியாக தொடர் (cariyāka toṭar)
**Transliteration:** cariyāka toṭar
**Literal breakdown:** cari (correct) + -āka (adverbial) + toṭar (continue)
**Translation:** "continue to be correct, keep being okay"
**Usage context:** Emphasizes continuation of correct state.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Natural and common
**Example context:** "வேலை சரியாக தொடர்கிறது" (vēlai cariyāka toṭarkiṟatu) "The work continues to go well"
**Notes:** Common for describing ongoing processes going well.

---

### 3.4 REMAINING WRONG — தப்பு (tappu) / தவறு (tavaṟu) BASE

#### Construction 3.4.1
**Form:** தப்பாக இரு (tappāka iru)
**Transliteration:** tappāka iru
**Literal breakdown:** tappu (wrong) + -āka (adverbial) + iru (be, remain)
**Translation:** "remain wrong, stay wrong, continue to be wrong"
**Usage context:** Used when something continues to be wrong or mistaken.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Natural and common
**Example context:** "அது தப்பாகவே இருக்கிறது" (atu tappākavē irukkiṟatu) "That remains wrong"
**Notes:** Parallel to சரியாக இரு.

#### Construction 3.4.2
**Form:** தவறாக இரு (tavaṟāka iru)
**Transliteration:** tavaṟāka iru
**Literal breakdown:** tavaṟu (error) + -āka (adverbial) + iru (be, remain)
**Translation:** "remain erroneous, stay incorrect"
**Usage context:** More formal variant of தப்பாக இரு.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Natural, more formal register
**Example context:** "அந்த கணக்கு தவறாக இருக்கிறது" (anta kaṇakku tavaṟāka irukkiṟatu) "That calculation remains incorrect"
**Notes:** More formal than தப்பாக இரு.

## SECTION 4: ASPECTUAL VARIATIONS

### 4.1 PROGRESSIVE/CONTINUOUS ASPECT

#### Construction 4.1.1
**Form:** உண்மை ஆகிக்கொண்டிரு (uṇmai ākikkoṇṭiru)
**Transliteration:** uṇmai ākikkoṇṭiru
**Literal breakdown:** uṇmai (truth) + āki (having become) + koṇṭu (taking, progressive) + iru (be)
**Translation:** "be becoming true, be in the process of becoming true"
**Usage context:** Emphasizes ongoing process of becoming true. Less common than simple ஆகு.
**Naturalness:** [MARGINAL] — Grammatically possible but less natural than simple ஆகு
**Example context:** "அது உண்மை ஆகிக்கொண்டிருக்கிறது" (atu uṇmai ākikkoṇṭirukkiṟatu) "That is becoming true"
**Notes:** Progressive aspect less common with truth-terms than with action verbs.

#### Construction 4.1.2
**Form:** சரி செய்துகொண்டிரு (cari ceytukkoṇṭiru)
**Transliteration:** cari ceytukkoṇṭiru
**Literal breakdown:** cari (correct) + ceytu (having done) + koṇṭu (taking, progressive) + iru (be)
**Translation:** "be fixing, be in the process of correcting"
**Usage context:** Emphasizes ongoing process of fixing or correcting. Very common with செய் constructions.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Very natural and common
**Example context:** "நான் சரி செய்துகொண்டிருக்கிறேன்" (nāṉ cari ceytukkoṇṭirukkiṟēṉ) "I am fixing it (right now)"
**Notes:** Progressive aspect very natural with action verbs like செய்.

---

### 4.2 PERFECTIVE/COMPLETIVE ASPECT

#### Construction 4.2.1
**Form:** உண்மை ஆகிவிட்டது (uṇmai ākiviṭṭatu)
**Transliteration:** uṇmai ākiviṭṭatu
**Literal breakdown:** uṇmai (truth) + āki (having become) + viṭṭatu (completed, past)
**Translation:** "has become true, turned out to be true (completed)"
**Usage context:** Emphasizes completion of change to true state. Very common in narrative past.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Extremely natural and frequent
**Example context:** "அது உண்மை ஆகிவிட்டது" (atu uṇmai ākiviṭṭatu) "That has become true / turned out to be true"
**Notes:** விட்டது completive is nearly obligatory for natural past tense with ஆகு.

#### Construction 4.2.2
**Form:** சரி செய்துவிட்டேன் (cari ceytuviṭṭēṉ)
**Transliteration:** cari ceytuviṭṭēṉ
**Literal breakdown:** cari (correct) + ceytu (having done) + viṭṭēṉ (completed, 1st person past)
**Translation:** "I have fixed it, I got it corrected (completed)"
**Usage context:** Emphasizes completion of fixing. Extremely common in reporting completed repairs.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Extremely natural and frequent
**Example context:** "நான் சரி செய்துவிட்டேன்" (nāṉ cari ceytuviṭṭēṉ) "I've fixed it / I got it fixed"
**Notes:** விட்டு completive extremely common with செய் for completed actions.

---

### 4.3 FUTURE/PROSPECTIVE ASPECT

#### Construction 4.3.1
**Form:** உண்மை ஆகும் (uṇmai ākum)
**Transliteration:** uṇmai ākum
**Literal breakdown:** uṇmai (truth) + ākum (will become, future)
**Translation:** "will become true, will turn out to be true"
**Usage context:** Prediction that something will be revealed as true. Common in predictions and prophecies.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Natural and common
**Example context:** "அது உண்மை ஆகும்" (atu uṇmai ākum) "That will turn out to be true"
**Notes:** Simple future form, very natural.

#### Construction 4.3.2
**Form:** சரி ஆகும் (cari ākum)
**Transliteration:** cari ākum
**Literal breakdown:** cari (correct) + ākum (will become, future)
**Translation:** "will be okay, will work out, will be fixed"
**Usage context:** Optimistic prediction about future resolution. Extremely common for reassurance.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Extremely natural and frequent
**Example context:** "எல்லாம் சரி ஆகும்" (ellām cari ākum) "Everything will be okay / will work out"
**Notes:** One of the most common reassurance expressions in Tamil.

#### Construction 4.3.3
**Form:** சரி ஆகிவிடும் (cari ākiviṭum)
**Transliteration:** cari ākiviṭum
**Literal breakdown:** cari (correct) + āki (having become) + viṭum (will complete, future)
**Translation:** "will get fixed, will work out (with certainty)"
**Usage context:** More emphatic than simple ஆகும், expressing confidence in future resolution.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Very natural and common
**Example context:** "கவலைப்படாதே, எல்லாம் சரி ஆகிவிடும்" (kavalaippaṭātē, ellām cari ākiviṭum) "Don't worry, everything will work out"
**Notes:** விடும் completive adds emphasis and certainty to future prediction.

---

### 4.4 POTENTIAL/ABILITY ASPECT

#### Construction 4.4.1
**Form:** மெய்ப்பிக்க முடியும் (meypikka muṭiyum)
**Transliteration:** meypikka muṭiyum
**Literal breakdown:** mey (truth) + -pi- (causative) + -kka (infinitive) + muṭiyum (can, is possible)
**Translation:** "can prove, can verify, is provable"
**Usage context:** Expresses ability or possibility of proving something. Very common in argumentative and evidential contexts.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Very natural and frequent
**Example context:** "அதை மெய்ப்பிக்க முடியும்" (atai meypikka muṭiyum) "That can be proven"
**Notes:** முடியும் "can" is standard potential auxiliary.

#### Construction 4.4.2
**Form:** மெய்ப்பிக்க முடியாது (meypikka muṭiyātu)
**Transliteration:** meypikka muṭiyātu
**Literal breakdown:** mey (truth) + -pi- (causative) + -kka (infinitive) + muṭiyātu (cannot, is not possible)
**Translation:** "cannot prove, cannot verify, is unprovable"
**Usage context:** Expresses inability or impossibility of proving. Common in skeptical discourse.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Very natural and frequent
**Example context:** "அதை மெய்ப்பிக்க முடியாது" (atai meypikka muṭiyātu) "That cannot be proven"
**Notes:** Direct negative of முடியும், equally frequent.

#### Construction 4.4.3
**Form:** சரி செய்ய முடியும் (cari ceyya muṭiyum)
**Transliteration:** cari ceyya muṭiyum
**Literal breakdown:** cari (correct) + ceyya (to do, infinitive) + muṭiyum (can, is possible)
**Translation:** "can fix, can correct, is fixable"
**Usage context:** Expresses ability to fix or correct something. Very common in assessing repairability.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Extremely natural and frequent
**Example context:** "இதை சரி செய்ய முடியும்" (itai cari ceyya muṭiyum) "This can be fixed"
**Notes:** Very common in practical contexts about repairs and corrections.

#### Construction 4.4.4
**Form:** சரி செய்ய முடியாது (cari ceyya muṭiyātu)
**Transliteration:** cari ceyya muṭiyātu
**Literal breakdown:** cari (correct) + ceyya (to do, infinitive) + muṭiyātu (cannot, is not possible)
**Translation:** "cannot fix, cannot correct, is unfixable"
**Usage context:** Expresses inability to fix or correct. Common in assessing irreparable problems.
**Naturalness:** [CORE] — Very natural and frequent
**Example context:** "இதை சரி செய்ய முடியாது" (itai cari ceyya muṭiyātu) "This cannot be fixed"
**Notes:** Equally common as positive equivalent.

## SECTION 5: SUMMARY OF ASYMMETRIES AND GAPS

### 5.1 CAUSATIVE ASYMMETRIES

**Productive causatives:**
- உண்மைப்படுத்து (uṇmaippaṭuttu) "verify" — VERY COMMON
- மெய்ப்படுத்து (meyppaṭuttu) "prove" — COMMON (formal)
- சரி செய் (cari cey) "fix, correct" — EXTREMELY COMMON

**Marginal or rare causatives:**
- பொய்யாக்கு (poyyākku) "falsify" — RARE (uses different morphology: -ஆக்கு not -படுத்து)
- தப்பாக்கு (tappākku) "make wrong" — VERY RARE
- தவறாக்கு (tavaṟākku) "make erroneous" — VERY RARE

**Observation:** Causatives for making true/correct are highly productive and frequent, while causatives for making false/wrong are rare or marginal. This shows **positive bias in causative productivity**.

---

### 5.2 INCHOATIVE ASYMMETRIES

**Natural inchoatives:**
- உண்மை ஆகு "become true" — VERY COMMON
- பொய் ஆகு "become false" — COMMON
- மெய் ஆகு "become real" — COMMON (literary)
- சரி ஆகு "become correct/okay" — EXTREMELY COMMON

**Marginal inchoatives:**
- தப்பு ஆகு "become wrong" — RARE (speakers prefer தப்பு நடக்கு "mistake happens")
- தவறு ஆகு "become erroneous" — RARE (speakers prefer தவறு நடக்கு "error happens")

**Observation:** Positive truth-terms (உண்மை, மெய், சரி) naturally take ஆகு "become," but negative error-terms (தப்பு, தவறு) prefer நடக்கு "happen" over ஆகு. This shows **different inchoative strategies for positive vs. negative terms**.

---

### 5.3 REVELATION/EMERGENCE CONSTRUCTIONS

**Highly productive:**
- உண்மை வெளிப்படு "truth comes out" — EXTREMELY COMMON
- பொய் வெளிப்படு "lie is exposed" — EXTREMELY COMMON
- உண்மை தெரிய வரு "truth comes to be known" — VERY COMMON
- பொய் தெரிய வரு "lie comes to be known" — COMMON

**Causative revelation:**
- உண்மையை வெளிப்படுத்து "reveal the truth" — VERY COMMON
- பொய்யை வெளிப்படுத்து "expose the lie" — VERY COMMON
- உண்மையை அம்பலப்படுத்து "expose truth publicly" — COMMON
- பொய்யை அம்பலப்படுத்து "expose lie publicly" — COMMON

**Observation:** Revelation constructions are **highly symmetric** between positive and negative terms, showing equal productivity.

---

### 5.4 STABILITY/PERSISTENCE CONSTRUCTIONS

**Natural stability expressions:**
- உண்மையாக இரு "remain true" — COMMON
- பொய்யாக இரு "remain false" — COMMON
- சரியாக இரு "remain correct/okay" — VERY COMMON
- தப்பாக இரு "remain wrong" — COMMON

**Emphatic stability:**
- நிலைத்திரு "remain steadfastly" — more common with positive terms (உண்மையாக நிலைத்திரு) than negative terms

**Observation:** Basic stability constructions with இரு "remain" are **symmetric**, but emphatic forms show **slight positive bias**.

---

### 5.5 ASPECTUAL PATTERNS

**Completive aspect (விடு/போ):**
- Nearly obligatory with ஆகு "become" in past tense for natural expression
- Extremely common with செய் "do" for completed actions
- விடு emphasizes completion/finality
- போ emphasizes change of state

**Progressive aspect (கொண்டிரு):**
- Less natural with truth-state changes (உண்மை ஆகிக்கொண்டிரு rare)
- Very natural with action verbs (சரி செய்துகொண்டிரு common)

**Potential aspect (முடியும்/முடியாது):**
- Highly productive with causatives (மெய்ப்பிக்க முடியும் "can prove")
- Symmetric between positive and negative (முடியும் "can" vs. முடியாது "cannot")

---

### 5.6 LEXICAL GAPS

**No common expressions for:**
1. **Making something false** (causative): பொய்யாக்கு exists but very rare; no productive *பொய்ப்படுத்து
2. **Making something wrong** (causative): தப்பாக்கு/தவறாக்கு exist but very rare
3. **Becoming wrong** (inchoative): தப்பு ஆகு/தவறு ஆகு rare; speakers prefer நடக்கு "happen"
4. **Progressive becoming true**: உண்மை ஆகிக்கொண்டிரு grammatically possible but unnatural

**Observation:** The language shows **elaboration bias toward positive causation** (making true/correct) and **event-framing for negative states** (errors happen rather than become).

---

## SECTION 6: CULTURAL AND PRAGMATIC PATTERNS

### 6.1 OPTIMISM AND REASSURANCE

The expression **சரி ஆகும்** (cari ākum) "will be okay, will work out" and its variants are among the most frequent reassurance expressions in Tamil. The completive form **சரி ஆகிவிடும்** (cari ākiviṭum) adds certainty and is extremely common in consolation and encouragement.

**Cultural note:** The frequency and productivity of சரி ஆகு constructions reflects cultural emphasis on optimism and reassurance in interpersonal communication.
