# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P4b Construction Usage & Constraints
# Language: Georgian (ka)

---

## Construction Usage Table

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. ATTRIBUTION** | ჭეშმარიტი ამბავი | č̣ešmariti ambavi | "a true account; a factual story" | [CORE] | Unmarked attributive pattern across all registers; no observable restriction on noun type or discourse context. Prenominal position and case agreement are obligatory. |
| **1. ATTRIBUTION** | ყალბი დოკუმენტი | q̇albi dokumenti | "a forged document" | [CORE] | Equally unmarked; q̇albi is particularly frequent in legal and administrative contexts but is not restricted to them. |
| **2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION** | არასწორი პასუხი | arasc̣ori pasuxi | "an incorrect answer" | [CORE] | The ara- prefix strategy is the productive and default negation pattern for this adjective class; attested across registers. |
| **2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION** | არაგულწრფელი ბოდიში | aragulc̣rpeli bodišiˈ | "an insincere apology" | [MARGINAL] | Less frequent than arasc̣ori; the base adjective gulc̣rpeli is itself somewhat formal, and the negated form is correspondingly restricted to more careful or formal usage. |
| **3. ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION (genitive)** | სიმართლის დადგენა | simarṭlis dadgena | "establishment of the truth; determination of the facts" | [CORE] | Fully productive across registers; the genitive + head noun pattern is the standard multi-word nominal construction in this domain. No observable restriction on head noun type. |
| **3. ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION (genitive)** | სიყალბის ნიშნები | siq̇albis nišnebi | "signs of forgery; indicators of falsity" | [CORE] | Equally productive; particularly frequent in legal and investigative discourse where indicators of falsity are enumerated. |
| **4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE** | სიმართლე გამოვიდა | simarṭle gamovida | "the truth came out; the truth emerged" | [CORE] | Common and idiomatic in narrative and conversational registers; gamovida with simarṭle as subject is a fixed-feeling collocation for the revelation of truth. |
| **4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE** | მართალი გამოდგა | martali gamodga | "turned out to be right; proved correct" | [CORE] | Equally natural; gamodga with a truth-domain adjective as predicate is the standard way to express retrospective confirmation of correctness. Attested with multiple adjectives in this class. |
| **5. CAUSATIVE** | გაყალბება | gaq̇albeba | "forgery; the act of making [something] false" | [CORE] | Fully lexicalized; frequent in legal and administrative registers. The verbal noun form is the primary nominalization for the act of forgery. |
| **5. CAUSATIVE** | დაზუსტება | dazusteba | "making precise; specifying exactly" | [CORE] | Lexicalized and common in formal, technical, and bureaucratic registers; used wherever precision or specification is required. These forms are not generated transparently by a productive causative rule but are individually established lexical items. |
| **6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION (si-...-e)** | სიმართლე | simartle | "truth; correctness" | [CORE] | The most frequent truth-domain abstract noun in Georgian; unrestricted across all registers and discourse types. The si-...-e circumfix pattern is the older native strategy. |
| **6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION (-eba)** | ჭეშმარიტება | č̣ešmariteba | "truth; truthfulness; veracity" | [CORE] | Common but carries a more elevated or philosophical register than simartle; frequent in religious, philosophical, and formal written discourse. The -eba suffix pattern is more productive with borrowed and compound bases. |
| **6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION (-oba)** | *(namdviloba, gulc̣rpelobaˈ, avtentiuroba — cited in notes)* | namdviloba / gulc̣rpelobaˈ / avtentiuroba | "genuineness / sincerity / authenticity" | [MARGINAL] | The -oba suffix yields state or quality nouns and is attested in this domain, but the individual forms are less frequent than simartle or č̣ešmariteba; avtentiuroba in particular is restricted to formal and technical registers. |
| **7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION (genitive + agentive noun)** | სიმართლის მაძიებელი | simarṭlis madziebeliˈ | "seeker of truth; truth-seeker" | [MARGINAL] | Attested but not high-frequency in everyday speech; more characteristic of elevated, literary, or philosophical registers. The genitive + agentive noun pattern is the documented strategy where single-word agentive forms are absent. |
| **7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION (-veli suffix)** | მიმბაძველი | mimbadzveliˈ | "imitator; one who copies" | [MARGINAL] | Attested as a lexical item; the -veli suffix is not broadly productive across the truth domain. This form is the only clearly attested single-word agentive of this type in the P1–P3 data. |
| **7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION (compound)** | თვალთმაქცი | tvaltmaqci | "deceiver; hypocrite" | [CORE] | The compound form is fully lexicalized and common; functions as the primary single-word agentive for "deceiver" in this domain. No transparent morphological decomposition required for use. |
| **8. PREDICATIVE TRUTH-ASSERTION (framing)** | სიმართლე ისაა, რომ | simarṭle isaa, rom | "the truth is that…" | [CORE] | Highly productive framing construction across spoken and written registers; functions as a standard discourse-level device for introducing a corrective or confirmatory statement. |
| **8. PREDICATIVE TRUTH-ASSERTION (first-person)** | სიმართლეს ვამბობ | simarṭles vambob | "I am telling the truth" | [CORE] | Common and idiomatic in conversational registers; the dative case on the truth noun is structurally obligatory and is the standard first-person truthfulness assertion. No single-word equivalent is documented. |
| **9. OSTENSIBLE / APPARENT PREDICATION (attributive)** | მოჩვენებითი ხასიათი აქვს | močvenebiti xasiati aqvs | "it has an ostensible/illusory character; it is merely apparent in nature" | [MARGINAL] | Restricted to legal and analytical discourse; not attested in conversational registers. The construction presupposes a contrast between appearance and reality that is characteristic of formal argumentation. |
| **9. OSTENSIBLE / APPARENT PREDICATION (adverbial + passive)** | ფიქტიურად ირიცხება | piqtiurad iricxeba | "is fictitiously registered; is nominally enrolled" | [MARGINAL] | Equally restricted to legal and administrative registers; piqtiurad as an adverbial modifier of a passive verb is a technical construction with no evident conversational equivalent in the P1–P3 data. |
