## 1. Base Form: **kweli**

### Derived Form: **ukweli**
1. **Base Form:** kweli  
2. **Derived Form:** ukweli

5. **Natural English translation:** truth  
6. **Usage description:** This is the main abstract noun corresponding to *kweli*. It is used for truth as a property of statements and for the actual facts of a matter.  
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### Derived Form: **mkweli**
1. **Base Form:** kweli  
2. **Derived Form:** mkweli  
3. **Transliteration:** mkweli

5. **Natural English translation:** truthful person; honest person  
6. **Usage description:** This form refers to a person characterized by truthfulness. It is used in moral, religious, and everyday evaluation of character.  
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

### Derived Form: **uongozi**
1. **Base Form:** uongo  
2. **Derived Form:** uongozi  
3. **Transliteration:** uongozi
