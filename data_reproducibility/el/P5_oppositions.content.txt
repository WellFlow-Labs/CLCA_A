## OPPOSITION 1: αλήθεια vs ψέμα

**Term A:** αλήθεια (alítheia) "truth"
**Term B:** ψέμα (pséma) "lie"

**Description of contrast:** Core lexical opposition between truth as correspondence to facts and lie as deliberate false statement. This is the primary truth-falsity opposition in everyday Greek discourse.

**Contrast type:** lexical

**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## NOTES ON OPPOSITION PATTERNS

### Observed asymmetries:

1. **Agentive nominalization asymmetry**: Highly productive for negative agents (ψεύτης, απατεώνας, ψευταράς) but limited for positive agents (no common equivalent for "truth-teller").

2. **Lexicalization asymmetry**: Negative cases often lexicalized (ψευδομαρτυρία, ψευδής συναγερμός) while positive cases use phrasal constructions or remain unmarked.

3. **Register asymmetry**: Some oppositions show register differences (ψεύδομαι vs λέω ψέματα; αληθής vs αληθινός).

4. **Morphological productivity asymmetry**: Privative α- prefix productive for creating negatives (άδικος, άκυρος, αναληθής) but no equivalent productive positive prefix.

### Cross-cutting patterns:

1. **Multiple contrasts for single terms**: Terms like αληθινός contrast with both ψευδής (propositional) and πλαστός (material authenticity).

2. **Constructional vs. lexical contrasts**: Same semantic opposition may be expressed lexically (σωστός/λάθος) or constructionally (κάνω σωστά/κάνω λάθος).

3. **Domain-specific oppositions**: Some oppositions restricted to specific domains (legal: έγκυρος/άκυρος; justice: δίκαιος/άδικος).

### Absent oppositions:

1. **No productive opposition** for degrees of truth (*"very true" vs "somewhat true").

2. **No clear opposition** for temporal aspects of truth (eternal vs contingent).

3. **No grammaticalized evidential opposition** (source of knowledge not marked on truth terms).
