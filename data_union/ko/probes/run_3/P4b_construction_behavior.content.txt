# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P4b Construction Usage & Constraints
## Language: Korean (ko)

---

## 1. ATTRIBUTION — N이/가 + Adj

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Epistemic Status | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Predicative attribution (nominal) | 그 말이 사실이다 | geu mali sasirida | "That statement is a fact / That is true." | [CORE] | Ubiquitous in spoken and written Korean across all registers; the default frame for asserting truth-domain nominal predicates. No observable register restriction. |
| Predicative attribution (adjectival) | 그의 주장이 타당하다 | geuui jujangi tadanghada | "His claim is valid/reasonable." | [CORE] | Common in evaluative and argumentative contexts; slightly more formal than the nominal pattern in everyday speech, but not restricted to writing. |

**Alternate constructions attested:** None beyond those listed in P4a.

---

## 2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION — N이/가 + Neg-Adj / 아니다

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Epistemic Status | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Syntactic negation (-지 않다) | 그 정보가 정확하지 않다 | geu jeongboga jeonghwakaji anta | "That information is not accurate." | [CORE] | The most broadly productive negation strategy; common across spoken and written registers. Preferred in conversational and informal contexts over morphological negation. |
| Negative copula (아니다) | 그것은 사실이 아니다 | geugeoseun sasiri anida | "That is not a fact / That is not true." | [CORE] | Standard and idiomatic for nominal predicates; widely used in both spoken and written Korean. The subject particle on the embedded nominal (사실이) is a fixed feature of this construction. |

**Alternate constructions attested:** Morphological negation (부정확하다, 무효하다) noted in P4a as formal/written; not separately exemplified but reported as productive in that register.

---

## 3. ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION — Adj-stem + -(으)ㄴ + N

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Epistemic Status | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sino-Korean adnominal (-한) | 정확한 정보 | jeonghwakhan jeongbo | "accurate information" | [CORE] | Fully productive and unrestricted across registers; the standard attributive position for Sino-Korean -하다 adjectives. No postnominal alternative exists in Korean. |
| Native Korean adnominal (-(으)ㄴ) | 올바른 판단 | olbareun pandan | "a correct/right judgment" | [CORE] | Equally productive; native Korean adjective stems follow the same pre-nominal position. No register restriction observed for this pattern. |

**Alternate constructions attested:** None. P4a explicitly notes there is no postnominal adjectival modification in Korean.

---

## 4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE — N이/가 되다 / (으)로 판명되다

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Epistemic Status | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| General inchoative (되다) | 계약이 무효가 되다 | gyeyagi muhyoga doeda | "The contract becomes void." | [CORE] | Common in legal, administrative, and everyday contexts; 되다 is the general-purpose inchoative and is not restricted to the truth domain. |
| Epistemic result inchoative (판명되다) | 가짜로 판명되다 | gajjaro panmyeong doeda | "To be determined to be fake." | [CORE] | More restricted than 되다; specific to investigative, journalistic, and legal contexts where a determination results from an epistemic process. The -로 frame is fixed with this verb. |

**Alternate constructions attested:** 밝혀지다 ("to be revealed as") noted in P4a as a parallel form to 판명되다 in investigative/verification contexts; not separately exemplified.

---

## 5. CAUSATIVE — N을/를 + -화하다

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Epistemic Status | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| -화하다 causative (Sino-Korean base) | 계약을 무효화하다 | gyeyageul muhyohwa hada | "To invalidate the contract / to render the contract void." | [CORE] | Productive and idiomatic with Sino-Korean bases in formal, legal, and policy discourse. Transitive; takes object with 을/를. |
| -화하다 causative (Sino-Korean base) | 마리화나를 합법화하다 | marihwanareul hapbeophwa hada | "To legalize marijuana." | [CORE] | Same pattern; common in policy and journalistic registers. |

**Restrictions attested:** P4a explicitly notes this pattern is not available with native Korean bases (no *진짜화하다, *옳화하다). The periphrastic causative -게 하다 exists but is reported as not specific to the truth domain and is not exemplified here.

## 6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION — Base + -성 / Verb-stem + -(으)ㅁ

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Epistemic Status | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sino-Korean -성 nominalization | 정확성을 높이다 | jeonghwakseong eul nopida | "To improve accuracy / to raise the level of precision." | [CORE] | Highly productive with Sino-Korean bases across formal, academic, and journalistic registers. Combines readily with scalar predicates (높이다, 낮추다, 평가하다). |
| Native Korean -(으)ㅁ nominalization | 올바름의 기준 | olbareum ui gijun | "The standard of rightness/correctness." | [MARGINAL] | Less common in everyday use; P4a reports these forms (올바름, 옳음, 그름) as tending toward philosophical or literary registers. Not restricted grammatically but restricted by register and frequency. |

**Alternate constructions attested:** -기 nominalization noted in P4a as a pattern but not exemplified with truth-domain vocabulary; not reported here beyond what P4a attests.

---

## 7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION — Base + -자 / -쟁이

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Epistemic Status | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Colloquial habitual agentive (-쟁이) | 거짓말쟁이 | geojitmaljaengi | "Liar" (habitual; colloquial) | [CORE] | Fully lexicalized and common in everyday spoken Korean; colloquial register. Applied habitually rather than to a single act of lying. |
| Formal Sino-Korean agentive (-자) | 위선자 | wiseonja | "Hypocrite" (person of false virtue; formal) | [CORE] | Common in written and formal spoken registers; tends toward the negative/false pole as noted in P4a. |

**Restrictions attested:** P4a explicitly notes the asymmetry: no common agentive nominalization for "truth-teller" exists parallel to 거짓말쟁이. The concept requires periphrastic expression (진실을 말하는 사람). This asymmetry is reported as structurally significant and is preserved here without inference.

---

## 8. VERB-OBJECT COLLOCATION (LIGHT VERB CONSTRUCTION) — N을/를 + V

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Epistemic Status | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Truth-domain V-O collocation (reveal) | 진실을 밝히다 | jinsileul balkhida | "To reveal the truth." | [CORE] | Highly productive; P4a identifies this as the primary means of expressing truth-domain actions. The same nominal participates in multiple collocations (밝히다, 숨기다, 왜곡하다, 외면하다). |
| Truth-domain V-O collocation (lose) | 신뢰를 잃다 | sinloereul ilta | "To lose trust/credibility." | [CORE] | Common across journalistic, interpersonal, and evaluative contexts. Collocationally fixed; the verb choice is not freely substitutable. |

**Alternate constructions attested:** P4a notes the same nominal base 진실 collocates with 숨기다, 왜곡하다, and 외면하다 in addition to 밝히다; these are reported but not separately exemplified in P4a and are not expanded here.

---

## 9. SCALAR PREDICATIVE COLLOCATION — N이/가 있다/없다 / 높다/낮다

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Epistemic Status | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Existential possession frame (있다/없다) | 신빙성이 있다 / 없다 | sinbyeongseong i itda / eopda | "To be credible / to lack credibility." | [CORE] | Common in evaluative, journalistic, and legal discourse; treats the property as a binary possession. P4a notes this is not available for all truth-domain nominals (사실이 있다 does not function equivalently). |
| Scalar degree frame (높다) | 신뢰도가 높다 | sinloedo ga nopda | "To have high credibility/trustworthiness." | [CORE] | Common in evaluative and analytical contexts; treats the property as a scalar quantity. The -도 suffix on 신뢰도 (degree/level of trust) is characteristic of nominals that participate in this frame. |

**Restrictions attested:** P4a explicitly restricts this construction to nominal bases denoting assessable properties (신빙성, 타당성, 진정성, 근거, 신뢰도); it is not available for all truth-domain nominals. This restriction is preserved as reported.
