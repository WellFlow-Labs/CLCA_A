# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2a Derivational System
# Language: Quechua (qu)

## DERIVATIONAL ANALYSIS

---

### BASE FORM 1: chiqaq (true, truthful)

#### Derived Form 1.1: chiqay
**Transliteration:** chiqay  
**Literal breakdown:** chiq-a-y (truth.root-VBLZ-INF)  
**Translation:** to be true, to be real  
**Usage:** Verbal form used to assert or verify the truth of something. Common in everyday verification contexts like "kayqa chiqanmi" (this is true).  
**Status:** [CORE]

#### Derived Form 1.2: chiqanchay
**Transliteration:** chiqanchay  
**Literal breakdown:** chiq-a-ncha-y (truth.root-VBLZ-CAUS-INF)  
**Translation:** to verify, to authenticate, to make true  
**Usage:** Causative form meaning to establish truth or authenticate something. Used in administrative, legal, and verification contexts.  
**Status:** [CORE]

#### Derived Form 1.3: chiqanchaq
**Transliteration:** chiqanchaq  
**Literal breakdown:** chiq-a-ncha-q (truth.root-VBLZ-CAUS-AG)  
**Translation:** verifier, authenticator, one who establishes truth  
**Usage:** Agentive form referring to persons who verify or authenticate. Used for officials, witnesses, or validators.  
**Status:** [CORE]

#### Derived Form 1.4: chiqanchana
**Transliteration:** chiqanchana  
**Literal breakdown:** chiq-a-ncha-na (truth.root-VBLZ-CAUS-NMLZ)  
**Translation:** means of verification, instrument of authentication  
**Usage:** Nominalizer indicating tools, methods, or procedures used for verification or authentication.  
**Status:** [CORE]

#### Derived Form 1.5: chiqanchakuy
**Transliteration:** chiqanchakuy  
**Literal breakdown:** chiq-a-ncha-ku-y (truth.root-VBLZ-CAUS-REFL-INF)  
**Translation:** to verify for oneself, to authenticate oneself  
**Usage:** Reflexive causative used when someone verifies something for their own benefit or authenticates their own claims.  
**Status:** [CORE]

#### Derived Form 1.6: chiqaqyay
**Transliteration:** chiqaqyay  
**Literal breakdown:** chiq-aq-ya-y (truth.root-AG-INCH-INF)  
**Translation:** to become true, to be verified, to turn out true  
**Usage:** Inchoative verb indicating the process of becoming true or being confirmed. Used when something is in the process of verification.  
**Status:** [CORE]

#### Derived Form 1.7: chiqaqsapa
**Transliteration:** chiqaqsapa  
**Literal breakdown:** chiq-aq-sapa (truth.root-AG-ABUND)  
**Translation:** very truthful, full of truth, characterized by truthfulness  
**Usage:** Abundance suffix indicating persons or things characterized by much truth. Used to describe truthful persons or authentic situations.  
**Status:** [CORE]

#### Derived Form 1.8: chiqaqniyuq
**Transliteration:** chiqaqniyuq  
**Literal breakdown:** chiq-aq-ni-yuq (truth.root-AG-POSS-POSSESSOR)  
**Translation:** having truth, possessing authenticity  
**Usage:** Possessor suffix indicating something that has or possesses truth. Used for claims, documents, or persons that have genuine truth.  
**Status:** [CORE]

### BASE FORM 2: cheqaq (true, real - dialectal variant)

**Note:** This dialectal variant of chiqaq shows the same derivational potential as chiqaq in varieties where it is used. ### BASE FORM 5: mana allin (not good, bad)

**Note:** This is a fixed negative expression. Derivational morphology applies to the base *allin* rather than to the negated phrase as a unit. No distinct derivations specific to *mana allin* as a lexicalized unit were identified beyond the compositional combination of negator + derived forms of allin (e.g., mana allinyaychu "it doesn't improve").

## SUMMARY OF DERIVATIONAL PATTERNS

### Highly Productive Patterns Observed:

1. **Causative -chi-/-cha-**: Extremely productive across most bases, creating forms meaning "to make X" or "to cause to be X"

2. **Inchoative -ya-**: Very productive for stative bases, creating forms meaning "to become X"

3. **Agentive -q**: Highly productive, creating agent nouns from verbal bases

4. **Nominalizer -na**: Productive for creating instrumental or locative nominals from verbs

5. **Reflexive -ku-**: Productive across verbal bases, often with specialized meanings

6. **Past Participle -sqa**: Regular formation from verbal bases

7. **Abundance -sapa**: Productive for creating intensified or characterized forms

8. **Possessor -yuq/-niyuq**: Productive for creating "having X" forms

### Asymmetries and Gaps:

- Not all bases show equal derivational potential
- Some bases (like *mana allin*, *chiqamanta*, *pantasqa*) are already derived or fixed expressions with limited further derivation
- Causative formations are more common than other derivational types
- Some expected forms may exist but were not attested in source material
- Diminutive -cha- appears less productive than causative -chi-/-cha-

### Notes on Productivity:

The causative and inchoative derivations are particularly productive in the evaluative/epistemic domain, allowing speakers to express processes of verification, clarification, improvement, and change of state. The agentive forms are crucial for creating role terms (teachers, verifiers, deceivers, etc.) relevant to epistemic and moral evaluation.
