# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2b Compositional Constructions
# Language: Finnish (fi)

## Methodology Statement

This analysis documents **attested multi-word constructions** involving the P1 vocabulary items. All constructions listed are naturally occurring in Finnish usage. The analysis preserves asymmetries—some lexical items participate in many constructions, others in few or none. Gaps are explicitly noted.

---

## CONSTRUCTIONS BY BASE FORM

### 1. BASE: tosi

#### 1.1 Predicative constructions

**se on totta**
- Form: se on totta
- Transliteration: se on totta
- Translation: that is true
- Usage: Standard predicative construction affirming truth of a proposition. Very common across all registers.
- Epistemic status: [CORE]

**onko se totta?**
- Form: onko se totta?
- Transliteration: onko se totta
- Translation: is that true?
- Usage: Interrogative construction questioning truth of a statement. Common in everyday discourse.
- Epistemic status: [CORE]

**ei ole totta**
- Form: ei ole totta
- Transliteration: ei ole totta
- Translation: is not true
- Usage: Negative predicative construction denying truth. Common across registers.
- Epistemic status: [CORE]

**se ei voi olla totta**
- Form: se ei voi olla totta
- Transliteration: se ei voi olla totta
- Translation: that can't be true
- Usage: Construction expressing disbelief or impossibility of truth. Common in everyday speech.
- Epistemic status: [CORE]

**liian hyvää ollakseen totta**
- Form: liian hyvää ollakseen totta
- Transliteration: liian hyvää ollakseen totta
- Translation: too good to be true
- Usage: Idiomatic construction expressing skepticism about something seemingly advantageous. Common idiom.
- Epistemic status: [CORE]

## SUMMARY OBSERVATIONS

### Construction Productivity Patterns

**Highly productive bases:**
- **tosi/totuus**: Extensive constructions across predicative, intensifier, seriousness, and fact-based domains
- **väärä**: Rich set of constructions for errors, directions, impressions, and legal contexts
- **oikea**: Productive in correctness, direction, timing, and rights domains
- **todistaa/todiste**: Extensive legal and evidential constructions

**Moderately productive bases:**
- **valhe**: Standard lie-telling and detection constructions
- **virhe**: Regular error-making, correction, and discovery constructions
- **rehellinen**: Standard honesty constructions and discourse markers

**Limited construction productivity:**
- **aito**: Mainly adjectival collocations, few verbal constructions
- **luotettava**: Primarily adjectival collocations
- **uskottava**: Primarily adjectival collocations
- **vilpitön**: Primarily adjectival collocations

### Asymmetries Noted

1. **Predicative asymmetry**: *tosi* shows extensive predicative constructions via its partitive form *totta*, while other truth-domain terms show fewer predicative patterns.

2. **Intensifier use**: Only *tosi* functions productively as a colloquial intensifier ("tosi hyvä" = really good).

3. **Legal domain concentration**: *todistaa*, *todiste*, *väärä*, and *oikea* show concentrated construction patterns in legal contexts, while other terms show minimal legal usage.

4. **Directional polysemy**: *oikea* and *väärä* show parallel but distinct construction sets for directional versus correctness meanings.

5. **Adjectival limitation**: Several bases (*aito*, *luotettava*, *uskottava*, *vilpitön*, *petollinen*, *harhaanjohtava*) participate primarily in adjectival collocations rather than verbal constructions.

6. **Discourse marker development**: *rehellisesti sanottuna/puhuen* and *totta puhuen* have grammaticalized as discourse markers, while other truth-domain terms have not.

### Gaps Explicitly Noted

- No common probability constructions for *todennäköinen* beyond single-word adverbial use
- No common verbal constructions for *aito*, *luotettava*, *uskottava*, *vilpitön* beyond adjectival modification
- Limited multi-word constructions for *epätosi*, *petollinen*, *harhaanjohtava*, *todenperäinen* beyond adjectival collocations
- No productive construction patterns for *paikkansapitävä* beyond the fixed expression *pitää paikkansa*
