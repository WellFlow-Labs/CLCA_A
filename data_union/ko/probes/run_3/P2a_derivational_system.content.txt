# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2a Derivational System
## Language: Korean (ko)
### Transliteration Standard: Revised Romanization of Korean (RR)

---

## Preliminary Note on Scope

This step documents attested derivational morphology for the 30 P1 base forms. "Derivational morphology" is understood here as the attachment of morphological material to a base to produce a new lexical item with a distinct meaning or grammatical category — as opposed to inflection (which Korean largely handles through verbal endings) or compounding already documented in P1b. Forms already fully documented in P1 or P1b are cross-referenced rather than re-described. The focus is on forms not yet entered into the record.

Korean derivational morphology relevant to this domain operates primarily through:
- Nominal suffixes: -성 (性, -seong), -화 (化, -hwa), -적 (的, -jeok), -자 (者, -ja), -품 (品, -pum), -죄 (罪, -joe), -제 (制, -je)
- Verbal formation: -하다 (-hada), -되다 (-doeda), -시키다 (-sikida)
- Adverbial formation: -히 (-hi), -로/-으로 (-ro/-euro), -게 (-ge)
- Negative prefixation: 비- (非-, bi-), 불- (不-, bul-), 무- (無-, mu-)
- Intensifying or specifying prefixes: 순- (純-, sun-), 반- (反-, ban-)

Not all of these apply to all bases. Asymmetries are preserved throughout.

## 1. 진실 (jinsil) — truth, truthfulness

---

**1.1**
- **Base Form:** 진실 (jinsil)
- **Derived Form:** 진실하다
- **Transliteration:** jinsil hada
- **Literal Breakdown:** 眞實 (truth) + 하다 (to be/do)
- **Natural English translation:** to be truthful, to be sincere
- **Usage:** Predicative form used to characterize a person or their conduct as genuinely honest. Common in character evaluation and interpersonal discourse. *Already documented in P1 and P1b.*
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**1.2**
- **Base Form:** 진실 (jinsil)
- **Derived Form:** 진실성
- **Transliteration:** jinsilseong
- **Literal Breakdown:** 眞實 (truth) + 性 (nature/quality)
- **Natural English translation:** truthfulness, sincerity as a quality
- **Usage:** Nominal form evaluating the quality of being truthful in a person, statement, or representation. Common in ethical, journalistic, and legal discourse. *Already documented in P1 and P1b.*
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**1.3**
- **Base Form:** 진실 (jinsil)
- **Derived Form:** 진실되다
- **Transliteration:** jinsil doeda
- **Literal Breakdown:** 眞實 (truth) + 되다 (to become/be)
- **Natural English translation:** to be truthful, to be genuine
- **Usage:** Alternative predicative form to 진실하다, with a slightly more resultant or passive nuance. Used in character and conduct evaluation. *Already documented in P1b.*
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**1.4**
- **Base Form:** 진실 (jinsil)
- **Derived Form:** 진실된
- **Transliteration:** jinsil doen
- **Literal Breakdown:** 眞實 (truth) + 된 (adnominal form of 되다)
- **Natural English translation:** truthful, genuine, sincere (as a modifier)
- **Usage:** Adnominal modifier form derived from 진실되다, used to modify nouns directly: 진실된 사람 (a truthful person), 진실된 마음 (a sincere heart). Distinct from 진실한 (from 진실하다) in that 진실된 carries a slightly more evaluative or achieved quality.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**1.5**
- **Base Form:** 진실 (jinsil)
- **Derived Form:** 진실한
- **Transliteration:** jinsil han
- **Literal Breakdown:** 眞實 (truth) + 한 (adnominal form of 하다)
- **Natural English translation:** truthful, sincere (as a modifier)
- **Usage:** Adnominal modifier form derived from 진실하다, used to modify nouns directly: 진실한 사람 (a truthful person), 진실한 대답 (a truthful answer). Extremely common across all registers.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**1.6**
- **Base Form:** 진실 (jinsil)
- **Derived Form:** 불진실
- **Transliteration:** buljinsil
- **Literal Breakdown:** 不 (not) + 眞實 (truth)
- **Natural English translation:** untruthfulness, insincerity
- **Usage:** Negated nominal form. Less common than 거짓 or 허위 for expressing falsehood; appears in formal or philosophical contexts where the negation of truthfulness (rather than the assertion of falsehood) is the relevant concept. Marginal in everyday use.
- **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

## 2. 사실 (sasil) — fact, actuality

**2.2**

---

**2.3**

- **Derived Form:** 사실주의
- **Transliteration:** sasiljuui
- **Literal Breakdown:** 事實 (fact) + 主義 (doctrine/ism)

- **Usage:** Standard term for realism in literary, artistic, and philosophical discourse. *Already documented in P1b.*

---

**2.4**
- **Base Form:** 사실 (sasil)
- **Derived Form:** 사실관계
- **Transliteration:** sasil gwangye
- **Literal Breakdown:** 事實 (fact) + 關係 (relationship/connection)
- **Natural English translation:** the facts of the case, factual circumstances
- **Usage:** Used in legal and journalistic contexts to refer to the factual circumstances of a case. *Already documented in P1b.*
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**2.5**
- **Base Form:** 사실 (sasil)
- **Derived Form:** 사실화하다
- **Transliteration:** sasilhwa hada
- **Literal Breakdown:** 事實 (fact) + 化 (to become/transform into) + 하다 (to do)
- **Natural English translation:** to make into fact, to actualize, to realize
- **Usage:** Used to describe the process of turning something planned or theoretical into actual fact. Less common than 실현하다 for this meaning; appears in administrative and planning discourse.
- **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

---

**2.6**
- **Base Form:** 사실 (sasil)
- **Derived Form:** 비사실적
- **Transliteration:** bisasiljeok
- **Literal Breakdown:** 非 (non-/not) + 事實的 (factual)
- **Natural English translation:** non-factual, unrealistic, contrary to fact
- **Usage:** Used in literary criticism and analytical discourse to characterize representations or claims that do not correspond to reality. Less common than 비현실적 (unrealistic) in everyday use but attested in academic writing.
- **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

## 3. 진짜 (jinjja) — real, genuine, authentic

---

**3.1**
- **Base Form:** 진짜 (jinjja)
- **Derived Form:** 진짜배기
- **Transliteration:** jinjjabaegi
- **Literal Breakdown:** 진짜 (genuine) + 배기 (colloquial intensifying suffix)
- **Natural English translation:** the real deal, the genuine article
- **Usage:** Emphatic colloquial form stressing authentic quality. Common in informal speech and advertising. *Already documented in P1b.*
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**3.2**
- **Base Form:** 진짜 (jinjja)
- **Derived Form:** 진짜로
- **Transliteration:** jinjjaro
- **Literal Breakdown:** 진짜 (genuine/real) + 로 (adverbial particle)
- **Natural English translation:** really, truly, for real
- **Usage:** Adverbial form of 진짜, used in informal speech to assert that something is genuinely the case or to express strong emphasis. Extremely common in spoken Korean: 진짜로 그랬어? (Did that really happen?).
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Note on 진짜:** The base form already functions as both adjective and adverb (as noted in P1), limiting the pressure for further derivation. No -하다, -성, or -적 forms are in common use. The derivational paradigm is notably sparse compared to Sino-Korean bases.

## 5. 정확 (jeonghwak) — accuracy, precision

---

**5.1**
- **Base Form:** 정확 (jeonghwak)
- **Derived Form:** 정확하다
- **Transliteration:** jeonghwak hada
- **Literal Breakdown:** 正確 (accuracy) + 하다 (to be)
- **Natural English translation:** to be accurate, to be precise, to be exact
- **Usage:** Primary predicative form. Used across all registers to evaluate whether a statement, measurement, or representation corresponds precisely to what it describes. *Already documented in P1.*
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**5.2**
- **Base Form:** 정확 (jeonghwak)
- **Derived Form:** 정확성
- **Transliteration:** jeonghwakseong
- **Literal Breakdown:** 正確 (accuracy) + 性 (nature/quality)
- **Natural English translation:** accuracy, precision as a property
- **Usage:** Nominal form referring to the quality of being accurate. Common in academic, technical, and evaluative discourse: 정확성을 높이다 (to improve accuracy). *Already documented in P1.*
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**5.3**
- **Base Form:** 정확 (jeonghwak)
- **Derived Form:** 정확히
- **Transliteration:** jeonghwaki
- **Literal Breakdown:** 正確 (accuracy) + 히 (adverbial suffix)
- **Natural English translation:** accurately, precisely, exactly
- **Usage:** Adverbial form used to modify verbs and adjectives. Extremely common across all registers: 정확히 말하면 (to be precise, literally "if one speaks accurately"). *Already documented in P1.*
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**5.4**
- **Base Form:** 정확 (jeonghwak)
- **Derived Form:** 정확한
- **Transliteration:** jeonghwak han
- **Literal Breakdown:** 正確 (accuracy) + 한 (adnominal form of 하다)
- **Natural English translation:** accurate, precise, exact (as a modifier)
- **Usage:** Adnominal modifier form used to modify nouns directly: 정확한 정보 (accurate information), 정확한 수치 (exact figure). Extremely common.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**5.5**
- **Base Form:** 정확 (jeonghwak)
- **Derived Form:** 부정확
- **Transliteration:** bujeonghwak
- **Literal Breakdown:** 不 (not) + 正確 (accuracy)
- **Natural English translation:** inaccuracy, imprecision
- **Usage:** Negated nominal form. Used to characterize statements, measurements, or representations that fail to correspond precisely to what they describe. Participates in 부정확하다 (to be inaccurate), 부정확성 (inaccuracy as a property).
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**5.6**
- **Base Form:** 정확 (jeonghwak)
- **Derived Form:** 부정확하다
- **Transliteration:** bujeonghwak hada
- **Literal Breakdown:** 不正確 (inaccuracy) + 하다 (to be)
- **Natural English translation:** to be inaccurate, to be imprecise
- **Usage:** Predicative form of 부정확. Used to evaluate statements or representations as failing to meet standards of precision. Common in academic and technical discourse.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**5.7**
- **Base Form:** 정확 (jeonghwak)
- **Derived Form:** 부정확성
- **Transliteration:** bujeonghwakseong
- **Literal Breakdown:** 不正確 (inaccuracy) + 性 (nature/quality)
- **Natural English translation:** inaccuracy as a property, imprecision
- **Usage:** Nominal form referring to the quality of being inaccurate. Less common than 정확성 but attested in academic and evaluative writing.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## 6. 올바르다 (olbareuda) — to be correct, right, proper

---

**6.1**
- **Base Form:** 올바르다 (olbareuda)
- **Derived Form:** 올바른
- **Transliteration:** olbareun
- **Literal Breakdown:** 올바르- (stem) + -ㄴ (adnominal suffix)
- **Natural English translation:** correct, right, proper (as a modifier)
- **Usage:** Adnominal modifier form used to modify nouns directly: 올바른 판단 (correct judgment), 올바른 행동 (proper conduct). Extremely common in ethical and educational discourse. *Already documented in P1.*
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**6.2**
- **Base Form:** 올바르다 (olbareuda)
- **Derived Form:** 올바르게
- **Transliteration:** olbareuge
- **Literal Breakdown:** 올바르- (stem) + -게 (adverbial suffix)
- **Natural English translation:** correctly, rightly, properly
- **Usage:** Adverbial form used to modify verbs: 올바르게 행동하다 (to act properly), 올바르게 이해하다 (to understand correctly). Common in ethical, educational, and instructional discourse.
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**6.3**
- **Base Form:** 올바르다 (olbareuda)
- **Derived Form:** 올바름
- **Transliteration:** olbareum
- **Literal Breakdown:** 올바르- (stem) + -ㅁ (nominalizing suffix)
- **Natural English translation:** correctness, rightness, propriety
- **Usage:** Nominalized form referring to the quality or state of being correct or proper. Less common than 올바른 or 올바르게 but attested in philosophical and ethical writing: 올바름의 기준 (the standard of rightness).
- **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Note on 올바르다:** This is a native Korean adjective. It does not take Sino-Korean suffixes such as -성 or -적. The derivational paradigm follows native Korean adjectival patterns: adnominal (-ㄴ), adverbial (-게), and nominal (-ㅁ) forms. No -하다 derivation exists because the base is already an adjective.
