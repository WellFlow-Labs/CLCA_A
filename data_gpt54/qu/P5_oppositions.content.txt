1. **Term A:** **chiqaq**  
   **Term B:** **llulla**  
   **Transliteration:** *chiqaq* vs *llulla*  
   **English translation:** ‘true; real; genuine’ vs ‘lie; falsehood; false; liar; deceitful’  
   **Description of contrast:** These are the clearest lexical opposites in the dataset for truth versus falsehood/deception. *chiqaq* marks truth or genuineness, while *llulla* marks falsehood and lying with strong moral force.  
   **Contrast type:** lexical  
   **Epistemic status:** [CORE]
