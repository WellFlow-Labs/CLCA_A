## Items that do not fit cleanly into one group

Some expressions are clearly usable across more than one pattern in the material:

- **tama** — spans factual correctness, practical correctness, and moral rightness.
- **totoo / totoong / tunay** — move between factual truth and genuineness/authenticity.
- **wasto** — covers correctness, propriety, and suitability depending on context.
- **tapat / matapat** — can refer to honesty, sincerity, loyalty, or fidelity.
- **saligang batas / saligang prinsipyo / saligang kaisipan** — linked by the *saligan* family, but their use is more about foundational status than everyday truth-evaluation.
- **pananalig / manalig** — present in the derivational material, but their usage leans toward faith/trust and does not fit neatly with the more evidence- or correctness-oriented clusters above.
