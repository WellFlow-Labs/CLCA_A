# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P5 Oppositions
# Language: Greek (el)

---

## Opposition 1

1. **Term A:** αληθής
2. **Term B:** ψευδής
3. **Transliteration:** alithís / psevdís
4. **English translation:** true / false
5. **Description of contrast:** These are the primary symmetrical pair for truth-value predication of statements and claims, appearing in parallel legal constructions: *Ο ισχυρισμός αποδείχθηκε αληθής* vs. *Ο ισχυρισμός κρίθηκε ψευδής*. They are explicitly documented as the most symmetrical pair in Group 1 (P3) and appear in parallel passive predicative constructions throughout P2b.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## Opposition 2

1. **Term A:** αλήθεια
2. **Term B:** ψεύδος
3. **Transliteration:** alíthia / psévdos
4. **English translation:** truth / falsehood, lie
5. **Description of contrast:** The primary abstract noun pair for truth and falsehood, documented as the root nouns of the two most productive families in P1b (Root 1 and Root 2). They appear as direct contrasts in moral, philosophical, and everyday discourse, and each generates a large derivational family oriented around the same opposition.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 3

1. **Term A:** έγκυρος
2. **Term B:** άκυρος
3. **Transliteration:** éngiros / ákiros
4. **English translation:** valid / invalid, null, void
5. **Description of contrast:** Documented in P2a as the direct antonym pair for legal and administrative validity; άκυρος is derived from the same κῦρος base by privative ἀ-. They appear in parallel constructions: *Το συμβόλαιο παραμένει έγκυρο* vs. *Άκυρο εισιτήριο*; *άκυρη ψήφος*.
6. **Contrast type:** morphological
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 4

1. **Term A:** νόμιμος
2. **Term B:** παράνομος
3. **Transliteration:** nómimos / paránomος
4. **English translation:** legal, lawful / illegal, unlawful
5. **Description of contrast:** Documented in P1 (#10) and P2a as the primary antonym pair in the legal conformity domain; παράνομος is formed by παρά- (against) + νόμος. They are explicitly contrasted in P1 (*Contrasts with παράνομος*) and appear in parallel constructions throughout P2b.
6. **Contrast type:** morphological
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 5

1. **Term A:** αξιόπιστος
2. **Term B:** αναξιόπιστος
3. **Transliteration:** aksiópistos / anaksiópistos
4. **English translation:** reliable, trustworthy / unreliable, untrustworthy
5. **Description of contrast:** Documented in P1 (#16, #17) as a direct privative pair; αναξιόπιστος is formed by ἀν- + αξιόπιστος. They appear in parallel legal constructions: *Αποδεικνύεται αξιόπιστος στην πράξη* vs. *Αποδείχθηκε αναξιόπιστος*; *αξιόπιστη πηγή* vs. *αναξιόπιστος μάρτυρας*.
6. **Contrast type:** morphological
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 6

1. **Term A:** αξιοπιστία
2. **Term B:** αναξιοπιστία
3. **Transliteration:** aksiopistía / anaksiopistía
4. **English translation:** credibility, reliability / unreliability, lack of credibility
5. **Description of contrast:** The abstract noun pair corresponding to Opposition 5, documented in P1b Root 3 (#8, #9) and P2a. They are the direct nominal counterparts of the adjectival opposition and appear in the same evaluative contexts.
6. **Contrast type:** morphological
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 7

1. **Term A:** εμπιστοσύνη
2. **Term B:** δυσπιστία
3. **Transliteration:** empistoséni / dispistía
4. **English translation:** trust, confidence / distrust, mistrust, skepticism
5. **Description of contrast:** Documented in P1b Root 3 (#17, #29) as contrasting dispositions toward persons and claims; εμπιστοσύνη is formed by ἐν- + πιστ-, δυσπιστία by δυσ- + πιστ-. They appear in contrasting parliamentary constructions: *ψήφος εμπιστοσύνης* vs. *ψήφος δυσπιστίας*.
6. **Contrast type:** morphological
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 8

1. **Term A:** ψήφος εμπιστοσύνης
2. **Term B:** ψήφος δυσπιστίας
3. **Transliteration:** psífos empistosénis / psífos dispistías
4. **English translation:** vote of confidence / vote of no confidence
5. **Description of contrast:** Documented in P1b Root 3 (#27, #28) as a fixed parliamentary pair; the two constructions share the same head noun (ψήφος) and differ only in the genitive complement, making the contrast structurally parallel and institutionally defined.
6. **Contrast type:** constructional
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 9

1. **Term A:** ουσιαστικός / ουσιαστικά / κατ' ουσίαν
2. **Term B:** τυπικός / τυπικά
3. **Transliteration:** usiástikos / usiástika / kat' usían — tipikós / tipikά
4. **English translation:** substantive, essential / formal, nominal, merely technical
5. **Description of contrast:** Documented throughout P1 (#25, #26, #27), P3 (Group 5), and P2b as the core contrast between substance and form. They appear in explicit opposition in attested constructions: *Τυπικά συμμορφώθηκε αλλά ουσιαστικά όχι* ("He formally complied but not substantively"). P3 identifies this as the defining contrast of Group 5.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 10

1. **Term A:** κατ' ουσίαν
2. **Term B:** κατά φαινόμενον
3. **Transliteration:** kat' usían / katá fenómenon
4. **English translation:** in substance, essentially / in appearance, ostensibly
5. **Description of contrast:** Documented in P1 (#27, #28) as a paired philosophical formula; P1 explicitly states that κατά φαινόμενον contrasts with κατ' ουσίαν, and P3 (Group 5) notes both as participants in the substance/appearance contrast. The pair is found in philosophical and formal registers.
6. **Contrast type:** constructional
7. **Epistemic status:** [MARGINAL]

## Opposition 11

1. **Term A:** φαινομενικός / φαινομενικά
2. **Term B:** ουσιαστικός / ουσιαστικά
3. **Transliteration:** fenomenikós / fenomenikά — usiástikos / usiástika
4. **English translation:** apparent, seeming / substantive, real
5. **Description of contrast:** Documented in P3 (Group 5) as participants in the appearance/substance contrast, and in P2b where φαινομενικά functions as a hedging adverb indicating that the speaker is reporting appearance rather than confirmed reality. This contrast is distinct from Opposition 9 in that it specifically concerns the appearance/reality dimension rather than the form/substance dimension.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 12

1. **Term A:** γνήσιος
2. **Term B:** νόθος
3. **Transliteration:** gnísios / nóthos
4. **English translation:** genuine, legitimate / spurious, adulterated, illegitimate
5. **Description of contrast:** Documented in P1 (#4, #11) as contrasting terms for genuine versus spurious origin; γνήσιος derives from γένος (legitimate birth) and νόθος means illegitimate or adulterated. They appear in parallel philological and commercial contexts: *γνήσιο προϊόν* vs. *νόθα προϊόντα*; *γνήσιο έργο* vs. *νόθο κείμενο*.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 13

1. **Term A:** γνήσιος
2. **Term B:** πλαστός
3. **Transliteration:** gnísios / plastós
4. **English translation:** genuine / forged, fabricated
5. **Description of contrast:** Documented in P3 (Group 3) as contrasting terms within the genuineness domain; γνήσιος asserts legitimate origin while πλαστός asserts deliberate falsification. They appear in parallel legal and commercial contexts: *γνήσιο αντίγραφο* vs. *πλαστό διαβατήριο*; *πιστοποιείται ως γνήσιο* vs. *αποδείχθηκε ότι η υπογραφή ήταν πλαστή*.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 15

## Opposition 16

1. **Term A:** αδιαμφισβήτητος
2. **Term B:** αμφισβητήσιμος
3. **Transliteration:** adiamfisvitiitos / amfisvitiísimos
4. **English translation:** indisputable, unquestionable / disputable, contestable
5. **Description of contrast:** Documented in P1 (#19, #22) and P3 (Group 1) as contrasting terms for the challengeability of claims; αδιαμφισβήτητος asserts that a claim is beyond challenge, while αμφισβητήσιμος asserts that it is open to active contestation. Both derive from the same αμφισβητ- base, with αδιαμφισβήτητος carrying a double privative (ἀ- + δια-).
6. **Contrast type:** morphological
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 17

1. **Term A:** αδιαμφισβήτητος
2. **Term B:** αμφίβολος
3. **Transliteration:** adiamfisvitiitos / amfívolos
4. **English translation:** indisputable / doubtful, uncertain
5. **Description of contrast:** Documented in P3 (Group 1) as occupying opposite ends of a certainty scale; αδιαμφισβήτητος asserts that something is beyond reasonable challenge, while αμφίβολος marks it as uncertain or open to question. P3 notes that αμφίβολος leans toward epistemic uncertainty while αδιαμφισβήτητος asserts unchallengeable status.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 18

1. **Term A:** τεκμηριωμένος
2. **Term B:** αναπόδεικτος
3. **Transliteration:** tekmirio̱ménos / anapódiktos
4. **English translation:** documented, substantiated / unproven, undemonstrated

They appear in parallel evaluative constructions: *βασισμένο σε τεκμηριωμένα στοιχεία* vs. *αναπόδεικτος ισχυρισμός*.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical

## Opposition 19

1. **Term A:** πιστός (in translation contexts)
2. **Term B:** νόθος (in textual contexts)
3. **Transliteration:** pistós / nóthos
4. **English translation:** faithful, accurate (of a translation or text) / spurious, falsely attributed (of a text)
5. **Description of contrast:** Documented in P3 (Group 9 and Group 3 respectively) as contrasting evaluations applied to texts; πιστός in *πιστή μετάφραση* asserts close correspondence to a source, while νόθος in *νόθο κείμενο* asserts that a text is not of genuine authorship. Both are applied to written works in philological and literary contexts, though from different evaluative angles.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 20

1. **Term A:** αληθοφανής / αληθοφάνεια
2. **Term B:** αληθής / αλήθεια
3. **Transliteration:** alithofanís / alithofánia — alithís / alíthia
4. **English translation:** verisimilar, appearing true / true, truth
5. **Description of contrast:** Documented in P1b Root 2 (#3, #4) and P3 (Ambiguous items) as a contrast between the appearance of truth and truth itself; αληθοφανής describes what appears true or lifelike without asserting that it is actually true, while αληθής predicates actual truth. P3 notes that this contrast is aesthetic and rhetorical rather than propositional.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 21

1. **Term A:** ψεύτικος
2. **Term B:** αληθινός
3. **Transliteration:** pséftikos / alithinós
4. **English translation:** fake, false / true, genuine, real
5. **Description of contrast:** Documented in P3 (Group 3) as the most colloquial contrast in the genuineness domain; ψεύτικος is described as broader and less formal than πλαστός and is applied to objects, persons, and emotional expressions, while αληθινός is its positive counterpart in everyday registers. They appear in parallel everyday constructions: *ψεύτικο χαμόγελο* vs. *αληθινός φίλος*.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 22

1. **Term A:** εικονικός (in legal/administrative uses)
2. **Term B:** ουσιαστικός
3. **Transliteration:** eikonikós / usiástikos
4. **English translation:** nominal, fictitious / substantive, real
5. **Description of contrast:** Documented in P3 (Group 6 and Group 5) as a contrast between nominal or fictitious status and genuine substance; εικονικός in *εικονικός διευθυντής* (nominal director) and *εικονική συναλλαγή* (fictitious transaction) contrasts with ουσιαστικός in asserting that something has the form but not the substance of what it purports to be. P3 notes the overlap between these groups.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 23

1. **Term A:** πιστεύω ότι… (I believe that…)
2. **Term B:** αποδεδειγμένως / αποδείχθηκε ότι… (it has been proven that…)
3. **Transliteration:** pistévo̱ óti… / apodedeignménos / apodíhthike óti…
4. **English translation:** I believe that… / it has been demonstrably proven that…
5. **Description of contrast:** Documented in P1b Root 3 (#2) and P2a (#20) as a constructional contrast between personal belief and established proof; πιστεύω ότι marks a proposition as held on the basis of personal conviction or trust, while αποδεδειγμένως and αποδείχθηκε ότι mark a proposition as established through evidence or argument. P3 (Group 8) distinguishes the evidence/proof domain from simple truth-predication.
6. **Contrast type:** constructional
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 24

1. **Term A:** εν γνώσει ψευδής
2. **Term B:** ψευδής (without εν γνώσει)
3. **Transliteration:** en gnósi psevdís / psevdís
4. **English translation:** knowingly false / false (without specification of intent)
5. **Description of contrast:** Documented in P2b (Construction 15, ψευδής Construction 3) as a legal constructional contrast; εν γνώσει ψευδής specifies that a false statement was made with awareness of its falsity, which is a requirement for criminal liability, while bare ψευδής asserts falsity without implying knowledge or intent. The addition of εν γνώσει shifts the construction from descriptive to legally operative.
6. **Contrast type:** constructional
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 25

1. **Term A:** ακριβής / ακριβώς
2. **Term B:** αμφίβολος
3. **Transliteration:** akrivís / akrivós — amfívolos
4. **English translation:** accurate, precise / doubtful, uncertain
5. **Description of contrast:** Documented in P3 (Groups 1 and 9) as contrasting epistemic evaluations; ακριβής asserts close correspondence to fact or standard, while αμφίβολος marks a claim or situation as uncertain or open to question. They appear in contrasting discourse-framing constructions: *για να είμαι ακριβής* (precision-marking) vs. *έχω αμφιβολίες* (uncertainty-marking).
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 26

1. **Term A:** ορθός / ορθώς
2. **Term B:** σωστός / σωστά
3. **Transliteration:** orthós / orthós — sostós / sostá
4. **English translation:** correct, right (formal) / correct, right (colloquial)
5. **Description of contrast:** Documented in P3 (Group 9) and P2a as register variants of the same correctness concept; ορθός is described as more formal, technical, and philosophical, while σωστός is the most common everyday term. They are not semantic opposites but occupy different register positions within the same semantic space, and P3 notes this distributional difference explicitly.
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 27

1. **Term A:** αληθής (predicative, formal)
2. **Term B:** αληθινός (predicative/attributive, colloquial)
3. **Transliteration:** alithís / alithinós
4. **English translation:** true (formal) / true, real, genuine (colloquial)
5. **Description of contrast:** Documented in P2a (#1, Derived Form 3) and P4b (Attribution table) as register variants within the αληθ- family; αληθής is described as more formal and legal, preferred in predicative constructions in official contexts, while αληθινός is the more common everyday form and is preferred in attributive position. P4b notes: "αληθής is more formal/legal, αληθινός more colloquial."
6. **Contrast type:** lexical
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 28

1. **Term A:** χωρίς αμφιβολία / πέρα από κάθε αμφιβολία
2. **Term B:** έχω αμφιβολίες / αμφίβολο παραμένει
3. **Transliteration:** horís amfivolía / péra apó káthe amfivolía — ého amfivolíes / amfívolo paraméni
4. **English translation:** without doubt / beyond any doubt — I have doubts / it remains uncertain
5. **Description of contrast:** Documented in P2b (αμφίβολος Constructions 1, 2, 3, 5) as a constructional contrast between asserting certainty and asserting uncertainty; the fixed expressions χωρίς αμφιβολία and πέρα από κάθε αμφιβολία function as certainty markers, while έχω αμφιβολίες and αμφίβολο παραμένει express epistemic uncertainty. Both sets derive from the same base noun αμφιβολία.
6. **Contrast type:** constructional
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 29

1. **Term A:** νοθεύω εκλογές / νόθευση εκλογικών αποτελεσμάτων
2. **Term B:** έγκυρα εκλογικά αποτελέσματα (implied by έγκυρος)
3. **Transliteration:** nothévo eklogés / nóthefsi eklogikón apotelesmáton — éngira eklogika apotelesmata
4. **English translation:** to rig elections / falsification of election results — valid election results
5. **Description of contrast:** Documented in P2a (νόθος Derived Form 2, 3) and P2b (νόθος Construction 3) and P2a (έγκυρος family) as a domain-specific constructional contrast; νοθεύω and νόθευση assert that electoral results have been falsified, while έγκυρος asserts that a vote or result has proper standing. The contrast is between the corrupted and the valid state of the same institutional object.
6. **Contrast type:** constructional
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 30

1. **Term A:** ψευδο- + [X] (e.g., ψευδοεπιστήμη, ψευδοδημοκρατία)
2. **Term B:** [X] without prefix (επιστήμη, δημοκρατία)
3. **Transliteration:** psevdo- + [X] / [X]
4. **English translation:** pseudo-X, sham X / genuine X
5. **Description of contrast:** Documented in P1b Root 1 (#12–26), P3 (Group 6), and P4a (Construction 10) as the productive ψευδο- compound pattern; each ψευδο- compound asserts that something has the form or appearance of the base category without possessing its genuine properties. The contrast is always between the ψευδο- compound and the unmodified base noun, and is open-ended across domains.
6. **Contrast type:** morphological
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]
