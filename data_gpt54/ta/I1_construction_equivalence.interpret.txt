## 2. Substitution Patterns (Narrative)

Tamil shows direct constructions for **ATTR**, **ADNOM**, **BECOME**, **VERIFICATION**, **NEGATION**, and **abstract NOMINALIZATION**.

For **REVELATION**, no construction is provided in P4a/P4b. On the basis of the supplied material, this category is therefore **missing as a listed construction**. There is also no evidence here that REVELATION systematically substitutes for **BECOME**. The attested change-of-state meaning is expressed by **ஆகு / ஆகி போ**, as in **எல்லாம் சரி ஆகும்.** and **அந்தக் கூற்று பொய்யாகி போனது.**

For **CAUSATION (factual)**, Tamil does not show a single productive morphological causative meaning “make true/correct” across the domain. Instead, the language uses **lexical/light-verb constructions** such as **சரிசெய்** and **உறுதிப்படுத்து**. In these, the light verb carries the operator-like verbal function:
- **சரி + செய்** behaves as a verbal complex meaning ‘correct / make right’.
- **உறுதி + படுத்து** behaves as a verbal complex meaning ‘confirm / make certain’.

So factual causation is **available**, but through lexicalized or light-verb expressions rather than one uniform derivational pattern.

For **CAUSATION (character)**, the data provide abstract quality nouns such as **உண்மைத்தன்மை** and **நம்பகத்தன்மை**, but no construction meaning “make X truthful / reliable / veracious” is listed. Based on the provided material, this is **NO COMMON EXPRESSION - circumlocution required**.

For **DISCONFIRMATION**, no dedicated construction is listed. The available negative operator is **NEGATION**, e.g. **இது உண்மையில்லை.**, **அது சரியல்ல.** Thus disconfirmation is not independently represented in the supplied data and is best described here as **SUBSTITUTED BY NEGATION**.

**NEGATION** is described in P4a/P4b as mainly **analytic**, using **இல்லை** or **அல்ல** after the predicate. Some forms appear orthographically fused, such as **உண்மையில்லை**, but the constructional description remains predicate + negative element. So the negation pattern is best treated as **analytic predicate negation**, not as a dedicated truth-domain morphological opposite.

**VERIFICATION** and **DISCONFIRMATION** do **not** share the same construction in the supplied material. Verification uses checking/confirmation verbs such as **சரிபார்**, **உறுதி செய்**, **உறுதிப்படுத்து**. Disconfirmation is not separately attested and is instead expressed through **NEGATION**.

There are also asymmetries:
- Positive and negative attribution are both well supported.
- **BECOME** is attested, but with lexical unevenness: P4a notes it is natural with **சரி** and **பொய்**, and “less so with some other items.”
- Agentive nominalization is semantically available, but not through a single salient dedicated suffix; Tamil prefers **relative participial/person-denoting circumlocutions** such as **பொய் சொல்பவன்** and **உண்மை பேசுபவர்**.

## 3. Missingness Summary

### Structural Gaps
- **REVELATION**: **MISSING (structural gap)**
- **DISCONFIRMATION** as a dedicated operator: **SUBSTITUTED BY NEGATION**
- **CAUSATION (character)**: **NO COMMON EXPRESSION - circumlocution required**

### Lexical Gaps
- No single highly salient dedicated **agentive suffix/form** for “liar” / “truth-teller” is identified in the provided data; instead:
  - **பொய் சொல்பவன்**
  - **உண்மை பேசுபவர்**
- For factual causation, there is no single general lexical item meaning “make true” across the domain; instead different lexical/light-verb expressions are used:
  - **சரிசெய்**
  - **உறுதிப்படுத்து**

### Conceptual Gaps
- No conceptual gap is directly evidenced in P4a/P4b. The missingness described is constructional or lexical, not stated as impossibility of expressing the meanings.

### Asymmetric Gaps
- **BECOME** is available, but P4a notes lexical asymmetry: natural with **சரி** and **பொய்**, less natural with some other truth-domain items.
- **VERIFICATION** is directly attested, but **DISCONFIRMATION** is not independently attested and is instead handled by **NEGATION**.
- Abstract nominalization is productive, but agentive nominalization is only periphrastic.

### Fully available
- **ATTR**
- **ADNOM**
- **BECOME**
- **VERIFICATION**
- **NEGATION**
- **NOMINALIZATION** (abstract nominalization)

### Available with substitution
- **CAUSATION (factual)** — available through lexical/light-verb constructions such as **சரிசெய்** and **உறுதிப்படுத்து**, rather than a single productive causative pattern

- **BECOME** — structurally available, but lexically uneven according to P4a notes

### Missing entirely
- **REVELATION** — **MISSING (structural gap)**

### Additional normalization notes
- **உண்மையான / பொய்யான / சரியான / தவறான / நிஜமான** function syntactically as **adjectival modifiers** in adnominal position.
- **உண்மையில்** functions as a **discourse-marking adverbial**; **சரியாக** as a **manner adverb**.
- **சரிசெய்**, **உறுதி செய்**, **உறுதிப்படுத்து**, **சரிபார்** function as **verbal predicates**, with **செய்**, **படுத்து**, and similar elements serving as **light-verb carriers** of the operator.
- **உண்மையைச் சொல்**, **உண்மை பேசு**, **பொய் சொல்**, **பொய் பேசு** are **noun + speech verb** verbal expressions rather than simplex lexical verbs.
