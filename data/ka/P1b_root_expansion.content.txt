# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P1b ROOT EXPANSION
# Language: Georgian (ka)

## Step 1 — Identify Central Roots

### Root 1: მართ- (mart'-)
**Native script + transliteration:** მართ- / mart'-
**P1 items:** 5 items (მართალი mart'ali, სიმართლე simart'le, მართლაც mart'lac, სამართლიანი samart'liani, უსამართლო usamart'lo)
**Core meaning contribution:** Correctness, rightness, justice. This root encompasses both factual correctness and moral/legal rightness, serving as a bridge between epistemic and ethical evaluation.

### Root 2: ტყუ- (ṭq'u-)
**Native script + transliteration:** ტყუ- / ṭq'u-
**P1 items:** 3 items (ტყუილი ṭq'uili, უტყუარი uṭq'uari, მოტყუება moṭq'ueba)
**Core meaning contribution:** Falsehood, lying, deception. This root specifically denotes deliberate falsification and deceptive communication, carrying moral implications beyond mere incorrectness.

### Root 3: ნამდვილ- (namdvil-)
**Native script + transliteration:** ნამდვილ- / namdvil-
**P1 items:** 2 items (ნამდვილი namdvili, ნამდვილად namdvilad)
**Core meaning contribution:** Reality, genuineness, authenticity. This root emphasizes the distinction between what is real/genuine versus what is apparent/fake, focusing on ontological authenticity.

## Step 3 — Note Peripheral Extensions

### Extensions of მართ- (mart'-)

**3.1** The root მართ- shows significant semantic extension from "correct/right" to "manage/direct/govern" in forms like მართვა (mart'va "management"), მართული (mart'uli "managed"), and related compounds. This extension appears to conceptualize management as "directing correctly" or "keeping on the right course."

**3.2** The root appears in the religious term მართლმადიდებელი (mart'lmadidebeli "Orthodox"), where it combines with "glorify" to create a denominational identifier. This represents a specialized theological usage.

**3.3** The compound მართლწერა (mart'lc'era "orthography") extends the root into linguistic-technical domain, where "correct writing" becomes a specialized term for spelling conventions.

**3.4** The form მართლზომიერი (mart'lzomieri "moderate, temperate") combines the root with "measure" to create a virtue term that extends beyond truth evaluation into moral character assessment, though it retains the sense of "right measure."

**3.5** Several forms like გამართული (gamart'uli "well-organized, successful") apply the root to evaluating the success or proper arrangement of events and activities, extending from correctness of statements to successful execution of plans.

### Extensions of ტყუ- (ṭq'u-)

**3.6** The root ტყუ- appears in forms like ტყუილუბრალოდ (ṭq'uilubralod "simply, merely") and ტყუილუბრალო (ṭq'uilubralo "simple, plain"), where the sense of "vain/empty" extends to mean "without special reason" or "ordinary," moving away from deception toward simplicity or lack of pretension.

**3.7** The adverbial ტყუილად (ṭq'uilad) functions both for "falsely" (deception domain) and "in vain, for nothing" (futility domain), showing extension from falsehood to fruitlessness.

### Extensions of ნამდვილ- (namdvil-)

**3.8** The root ნამდვილ- appears extensively in collocation with abstract nouns (ნამდვილი ბუნება "true nature," ნამდვილი ღირებულება "true value," ნამდვილი მიზეზი "true reason") where it functions to distinguish essential or actual properties from apparent or superficial ones. This represents extension from authenticity of objects to essential versus accidental properties.

**3.9** In expressions like ნამდვილი მეგობარი (namdvili megobari "true friend") and ნამდვილი სიყვარული (namdvili siq'varuli "true love"), the root extends into evaluation of the authenticity or depth of relationships and emotions, moving from ontological genuineness to qualitative assessment of human bonds.

**3.10** The verbal forms განამდვილება (ganamdvileba "realization, actualization") extend the root into philosophical territory concerning the actualization of potentials, representing a shift from static authenticity to dynamic process of becoming real.
