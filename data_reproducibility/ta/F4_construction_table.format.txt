# F4 Construction Table — Tamil (ta)

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Usage Notes | Epistemic Status |
|------------------|---------|-----------------|-------------|-------------|------------------|
| ATTRIBUTION | அது உண்மை | atu uṇmai | That is true | Extremely frequent in everyday evaluative speech. Zero copula is unmarked/default for present tense third person. Explicit இருக்கு (irukku) used for emphasis, non-present tense, or non-third person subjects. | [CORE] |
| ATTRIBUTION (alternate) | அது உண்மை இருக்கு | atu uṇmai irukku | That is true (emphatic) | Explicit copula variant adds emphasis or marks non-present/non-third contexts. Both variants freely alternate in speech. | [CORE] |
| NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION | அது சரி இல்லை | atu cari illai | That is not correct | Standard negation for equational sentences. Extremely common in denial, correction, and disagreement contexts. இல்லை functions as both negative copula and negative existential without distinction. | [CORE] |
| NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION (alternate) | உண்மையற்ற | uṇmaiyaṟṟa | untrue, without truth | Adjectival negative using -அற்ற suffix. Used attributively before nouns rather than predicatively. Functionally distinct from இல்லை negation. | [CORE] |
| ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION | உண்மையான கதை | uṇmaiyāṉa katai | a true story | Most productive adjectival derivation pattern. Nearly universal across truth-domain vocabulary. Standard attributive position before modified noun. | [CORE] |
| ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION (alternate) | உண்மை கதை | uṇmai katai | true story | Bare noun compound without derivational morphology. Freely alternates with -ஆன form. Both constructions equally natural in everyday speech. | [CORE] |
| BECOME / INCHOATIVE | பொய் அம்பலமாகு | poy ampalamāku | The lie is exposed | Standard inchoative with ஆகு "become." Common in narrative contexts describing revelation or exposure. Productive with truth-domain terms. | [CORE] |
| BECOME / INCHOATIVE (alternate) | உண்மை தெரிய வரு | uṇmai teriya varu | The truth comes to be known | Alternative inchoative using வரு "come" for emergence/revelation. Particularly common with உண்மை. Both ஆகு and வரு patterns productive. | [CORE] |
| CAUSATIVE | உண்மையை வெளிப்படுத்து | uṇmaiyai veḷippaṭuttu | reveal the truth | Standard causative formation with -உத்து suffix. Common in contexts of deliberate revelation or clarification. Productive with verbs of revelation. | [CORE] |
| CAUSATIVE (variant) | தெளிவுபடுத்து | teḷivupaṭuttu | make clear, clarify | NOUN + படுத்து pattern particularly productive. Creates causatives meaning "cause to become X." Very frequent in explanatory and instructional contexts. | [CORE] |
| ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION | பொய்மை | poymai | falsehood, falsity | Classical/literary pattern with -மை suffix. Less common in modern colloquial speech. Retained in formal registers and fixed expressions. | [MARGINAL] |
| ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION (alternate) | உண்மைத்தன்மை | uṇmaittaṉmai | truthfulness, veracity | Modern formal pattern with -தன்மை "quality." Preferred in contemporary formal/analytical discourse. More productive than classical -மை in modern usage. | [CORE] |
| AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION | பொய்யன் | poyyan | liar (male) | **Restricted to பொய் only.** Not productive across truth-domain vocabulary. Gender-marked forms (masculine -அன், neutral -ஆள்) exist but usage limited. | [MARGINAL] |
| AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION (note) | உண்மை பேசுபவர் | uṉmai pēcupavar | truth-speaking person | Periphrastic expression required for "truth-teller." Direct agentive derivation *உண்மையன் not attested. Demonstrates non-productivity of agentive pattern. | [CORE] |
| VERB + OBJECT COLLOCATIONS | உண்மையைச் சொல்லு | uṇmaiyaic collu | tell the truth | Most common construction for truth-telling actions. உண்மை takes accusative -ஐ marker. Extremely frequent in everyday speech and interrogation contexts. | [CORE] |
| VERB + OBJECT COLLOCATIONS (asymmetry) | பொய் சொல்லு | poy collu | tell lies | **Asymmetry:** பொய் appears without accusative marking in this construction. Both verbs சொல்லு "say" and பேசு "speak" collocate with both terms. | [CORE] |
| NOUN + NOUN COMPOUNDS | பொய் சாட்சி | poy cāṭci | false testimony, perjury | Extremely productive pattern without overt derivational morphology. பொய் forms legal/commercial compounds; மெய் forms philosophical/spiritual compounds. Very frequent across registers. | [CORE] |
| NOUN + NOUN COMPOUNDS (variant) | மெய்ஞ்ஞானம் | meyññāṉam | true knowledge, enlightenment | மெய் particularly productive in philosophical/religious compounds. Sandhi rules apply (ஞ் doubling after -ய்). Common in spiritual discourse. | [CORE] |
| INTERROGATIVE CONSTRUCTIONS | உண்மையா? | uṇmaiyā? | Is it true? Really? | One of the most frequent constructions in everyday speech. Used for confirmation requests, expressing surprise, or challenging statements. Extremely common. | [CORE] |
| INTERROGATIVE CONSTRUCTIONS (variant) | நிஜமா? | nijamā? | Really? Is that so? | May be even more frequent colloquially than உண்மையா?. Functions identically. Both forms freely alternate in conversational Tamil. | [CORE] |
| ADVERBIAL MANNER CONSTRUCTIONS | உண்மையாக | uṇmaiyāka | truly, truthfully | Highly productive across truth-domain vocabulary. Parallel to -ஆன adjectival pattern. Nearly universal derivation for manner adverbs. | [CORE] |
| ADVERBIAL MANNER CONSTRUCTIONS (emphatic) | உண்மையாகவே | uṇmaiyākavē | truly indeed | Emphatic variant with -ஏ particle. Very common for intensification or insistence. Productive emphatic pattern across adverbials. | [CORE] |
| LOCATIVE/ABSTRACT STATE CONSTRUCTIONS | உண்மையில் | uṇmaiyil | in truth, actually, in reality | Extremely common as discourse marker and adverbial emphasizer. Functions both epistemically ("actually") and ontologically ("in reality"). Very frequent in everyday speech. | [CORE] |
| LOCATIVE/ABSTRACT STATE CONSTRUCTIONS (note) | பொய்யில் | poyyil | in falsehood | Less common than உண்மையில். Restricted to literary/formal registers. Pattern less productive with பொய் than with உண்மை. | [MARGINAL] |
| NEGATIVE ADJECTIVAL DERIVATION | உண்மையற்ற | uṇmaiyaṟṟa | without truth, untrue, false | Productive negative adjectival pattern with -அற்ற "without, lacking." Alternative to using பொய்யான for negation. Common with abstract nouns. | [CORE] |
| NEGATIVE ADJECTIVAL DERIVATION (variant) | சந்தேகமற்ற | cantēkamaṟṟa | without doubt, certain | Pattern productive beyond core truth-domain terms. Creates negative adjectives from abstract nouns generally. Frequent in formal and everyday registers. | [CORE] |
| EMPHATIC/INTENSIFYING CONSTRUCTIONS | உண்மையிலேயே | uṇmaiyi-l-ē-y-ē | truly indeed, really and truly | Characteristic Tamil particle stacking for emphasis. -ஏ primary emphatic; -ய்/-வ் euphonic connectors. Very common in colloquial speech for strong emphasis or insistence. | [CORE] |
| EMPHATIC/INTENSIFYING CONSTRUCTIONS (variant) | நிஜமாகவே | nijamāka-v-ē | really indeed | Alternative emphatic construction with நிஜம். Both forms extremely frequent. Particle stacking productive across adverbial bases. | [CORE] |
| COMPARATIVE CONSTRUCTIONS | உண்மையை விட | uṇmaiyai viṭa | more than truth, beyond truth | Less productive with truth-domain vocabulary than with gradable adjectives. Used primarily in emphatic or hyperbolic contexts. Truth-domain terms treated as categorical rather than gradable. | [MARGINAL] |
| CONDITIONAL CONSTRUCTIONS | உண்மையாக இருந்தால் | uṇmaiyāka iruntāl | if (it is) true | Standard conditional formation with இருந்தால் (conditional of இருக்கு "be"). Common in reasoning and argumentation contexts. | [CORE] |
| CONDITIONAL CONSTRUCTIONS (colloquial) | உண்மைன்னா | uṇmaiṉṉā | if true | Colloquial shortened form. Extremely frequent in everyday speech. Represents phonological reduction of என்றால் (eṉṟāl) "if (one says)." | [CORE] |
| MODAL NECESSITY CONSTRUCTIONS | உண்மையாக இருக்க வேண்டும் | uṇmaiyāka irukka vēṇṭum | must be true, should be true | Standard modal construction for necessity/obligation. வேண்டும் expresses both deontic ("should") and epistemic ("must") modality. Common in instructions and requirements. | [CORE] |
| MODAL POSSIBILITY CONSTRUCTIONS | உண்மையாக இருக்கலாம் | uṇmaiyāka irukkalām | may be true, might be true | Standard possibility modal with -லாம் suffix. Common in epistemic contexts expressing uncertainty or conjecture. | [CORE] |
| MODAL POSSIBILITY CONSTRUCTIONS (negative) | சாத்தியம் இல்லை | cāttiyam illai | (it is) not possible, impossible | Negative existential with சாத்தியம் "possibility" extremely common for impossibility. More frequent than negative modal forms. | [CORE] |
| EVIDENTIAL/APPEARANCE CONSTRUCTIONS | உண்மை என்று தெரிகிறது | uṇmai eṉṟu terikiṟatu | it appears to be true | Standard evidential construction with தெரி "appear, be visible, seem." என்று quotative/complementizer introduces complement. Common in inferential contexts. | [CORE] |
| EVIDENTIAL/APPEARANCE CONSTRUCTIONS (negative) | தெளிவாக தெரியவில்லை | teḷivāka teriyavillai | (it) doesn't appear clearly, (it's) not clear | Negative evidential with தெரியவில்லை "doesn't appear." Very frequent for expressing lack of clarity or uncertainty. | [CORE] |
| CONTRASTIVE DISJUNCTION | உண்மையா பொய்யா | uṇmaiyā poyyā | true or false? truth or lie? | Common construction for presenting binary alternatives in questions. Both elements take -ஆ interrogative particle. Extremely frequent in everyday evaluative speech and decision-making. | [CORE] |
| CONTRASTIVE DISJUNCTION (variant) | சரியா தப்பா | cariyā tappā | right or wrong? | Alternative binary pair. Both constructions equally productive. Used in moral/evaluative and correctness contexts respectively. | [CORE] |
| RESULTATIVE VERB CONSTRUCTIONS | உண்மை வெளிப்படு | uṇmai veḷippaṭu | truth comes out, is revealed | Resultative construction emphasizing change of state or emergence. வெளிப்படு "emerge, be revealed" particularly common with உண்மை. Frequent in narrative contexts. | [CORE] |
| RESULTATIVE VERB CONSTRUCTIONS (variant) | பொய் வெளியாகு | poy veḷiyāku | lie becomes exposed | Alternative resultative verb. Both வெளிப்படு and வெளியாகு productive. These are intransitive; causative counterparts use -உத்து suffix. | [CORE] |
| IDIOMATIC METAPHORICAL CONSTRUCTIONS | உண்மை வெளிச்சத்திற்கு வரு | uṇmai veḷiccattiṟku varu | truth comes to light | Semi-fixed idiomatic expression. வெளிச்சம் "light" metaphor for revelation common across languages. Frequent in narrative and journalistic contexts. | [CORE] |
| IDIOMATIC METAPHORICAL CONSTRUCTIONS (variant) | பொய் பிடிபடு | poy piṭipaṭu | lie is caught, discovered | Treats lies as physical objects that can be captured. பிடிபடு "be caught" common with பொய். Frequent in narrative and investigative contexts. | [CORE] |
| IMPERATIVE CONSTRUCTIONS | உண்மையைச் சொல்லு | uṇmaiyaic collu | Tell the truth! | Imperative is unmarked bare stem. Common in interrogation contexts and direct commands. Very frequent in confrontational or instructional speech. | [CORE] |
| IMPERATIVE CONSTRUCTIONS (variant) | சரி பார் | cari pār | Check (it)! Verify! | Alternative imperative with பார் "see, look." Common in instructions and verification requests. Both constructions extremely frequent. | [CORE] |
| DEGREE/EXTENT MODIFICATION | முழு உண்மை | muḻu uṇmai | the whole truth, complete truth | Limited productivity. முழு "whole" and பகுதி "partial" are main degree modifiers attested. Truth generally treated as categorical rather than gradable, limiting degree modification. | [MARGINAL] |
| DEGREE/EXTENT MODIFICATION (variant) | பகுதி உண்மை | pakuti uṇmai | partial truth, part of the truth | Restricted to specific contexts (e.g., legal, philosophical). Not freely productive. Degree modification generally incompatible with categorical nature of truth-domain terms. | [MARGINAL] |
| ABSTRACT QUALITY NOMINALIZATION WITH -இன்மை | உண்மையின்மை | uṇmaiyiṉmai | absence of truth, untruth | Less productive than -அற்ற negative adjectival pattern. இன்மை "absence" formal/literary. More common to use negative adjectives or simple negation than this abstract nominalization. | [MARGINAL] |
| RECIPROCAL CONSTRUCTIONS | (none attested) | — | — | No fixed reciprocal constructions involving truth-domain terms identified. While grammatically possible using -கொள் reflexive/reciprocal suffix, no idiomatic or frequent expressions emerged from data. | [UNCERTAIN] |
| BENEFACTIVE CONSTRUCTIONS | (none attested) | — | — | No fixed idiomatic benefactive expressions with truth-domain terms identified. Grammatically possible using dative + -ஆக/-காக "for the sake of," but no attested idiomatic expressions. | [UNCERTAIN] |
| INSTRUMENTAL CONSTRUCTIONS | (none attested) | — | — | No fixed instrumental constructions identified. Instrumental case -ஆல் can be used grammatically but no idiomatic "by means of truth" type expressions emerged from data. | [UNCERTAIN] |
