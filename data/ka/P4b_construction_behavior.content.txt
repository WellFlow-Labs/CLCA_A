## ALTERNATE CONSTRUCTIONS ATTESTED

### Construction 2 (Negative Attribution)
- **Alternates**: Syntactic negation (არ + copula) vs. Lexical negation (უ- prefix)
- **Distribution**: Not freely interchangeable; უ- creates stable antonyms with distinct meanings

### Construction 4 (Become/Inchoative)
- **Alternates**: Specialized verb გამოვლინდა vs. Generic გახდა + instrumental
- **Distribution**: Specialized verb strongly preferred in truth domain; generic construction marginal

### Construction 7 (Agentive Nominalization)
- **Alternates**: მატყუარა vs. მატყუარებელი
- **Distribution**: Both active; latter slightly more formal register

### Construction 8 (Verbal Noun with Preverbs)
- **Alternates**: Multiple preverbs possible (მო-, გა-, შე-, ა-)
- **Distribution**: Different preverbs create aspectual distinctions; all productive

### Construction 11 (Truth-Seeking)
- **Alternates**: გაარკვია (completive) vs. იძიოს (processual)
- **Distribution**: Aspectual distinction; both equally productive

### Construction 15 (Concealment)
- **Alternates**: დამალა (hide) vs. დაფარა (cover)
- **Distribution**: Slight semantic difference; both equally idiomatic

### Construction 16 (Revelation)
- **Alternates**: Intransitive გამოვლინდა vs. Transitive გამოაჩინა
- **Distribution**: Voice distinction; both equally frequent

## SUMMARY OBSERVATIONS

- **[CORE]**: 28 of 30 construction types
- **[MARGINAL]**: 2 construction types (generic "become" with truth predicates; biblical formulas)
- **[UNCERTAIN]**: 1 construction type (causative - no systematic data)

Most constructions show no register or frequency restrictions. The two marginal constructions are restricted by either semantic preference (specialized verbs preferred over generic) or register (biblical formulas limited to religious contexts).
