| APPEARANCE-OF-TRUTH / PLAUSIBILITY | *His story rings true.* | N/A | “His story rings true.” | [CORE] | Common for intuitive judgments of plausibility or authenticity without full proof. | APPEARANCE-OF-TRUTH / PLAUSIBILITY | *The excuse seems credible.* | N/A | “The excuse seems credible.” | [CORE] | Natural in evaluative contexts where the speaker withholds full commitment and judges plausibility instead.
