# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P4b Construction Usage & Constraints
# Language: Korean (ko)

---

**Transliteration standard:** Revised Romanization of Korean (국립국어원 표준 로마자 표기법)

---

## 1. ATTRIBUTION

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Noun + 이다 (predicative copula) | 그것은 사실이다 | geugeoseun sasirida | "That is a fact." | [CORE] | Used across all registers for asserting factual or categorical status; the standard construction for truth-domain attribution in Korean. No register restriction observed. |
| Noun + 이다 (predicative copula) | 이것은 거짓말이다 | igeoseun geojitmarida | "This is a lie." | [CORE] | Direct and unambiguous; common in spoken correction and written formal contexts alike. |

**Attested alternate constructions:** Negation via 이/가 아니다 (see Construction 2).

---

## 2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Noun + 이/가 아니다 (copular negation) | 그 주장은 사실이 아니다 | geu jujangeun sasiri anida | "That claim is not a fact." | [CORE] | The standard and neutral construction for denying factual status; preferred in formal corrections and public statements over the more marked 거짓이다. |
| Noun + 이/가 아니다 (copular negation) | 이건 진짜가 아니다 | igeon jinjjaga anida | "This is not genuine." | [CORE] | Common in both spoken and written registers; no observable restriction on context. |

**Attested alternate constructions:** 거짓이다 ("it is a falsehood") is attested as a more marked alternative but is noted as less preferred in neutral correction contexts.

---

## 3. ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Adjective stem + -(으)ㄴ + Noun (하다-adjective) | 정확한 정보 | jeonghwakan jeongbo | "accurate information" | [CORE] | Highly productive with Sino-Korean 하다-adjectives in the truth domain; common across journalistic, academic, and everyday registers. |
| Adjective stem + -(으)ㄴ + Noun (native adjective) | 올바른 판단 | olbareun pandan | "correct judgment" | [CORE] | Native adjective stems follow standard conjugation patterns; this form is common in formal and evaluative contexts. Irregular stems (e.g., 올바르다) require attention to conjugation. |

**Attested alternate constructions:** None identified beyond the two sub-patterns (하다-adjective and native adjective) documented in P4a.

---

## 4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Noun + (으)로 밝혀지다 | 그 주장이 허위로 밝혀졌다 | geu jujangi heowiro balkyeojyeossda | "That claim was revealed to be false." | [CORE] | Common in journalistic and legal reporting contexts; 밝혀지다 is the most frequent of the three inchoative verbs in the truth domain. |
| Noun + (으)로 판명되다 | 가짜로 판명되었다 | gajjaro panmyeongdoeeossda | "It was determined to be fake." | [CORE] | Characteristic of formal, legal, and investigative contexts; slightly more formal in register than 밝혀지다. |

**Attested alternate constructions:** 드러나다 ("to be exposed") is attested as a third inchoative verb in this pattern. Noun + (이)라는 것이 밝혀지다 (embedded clause variant) is also attested in P4a.

## 5. CAUSATIVE

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Noun + 하다 (transitive, act-denoting base) | 거짓말하다 | geojitmal-hada | "to lie" | [CORE] | Fully lexicalized and common across all registers; the standard verb for the act of lying in Korean. |
| Noun + 하다 (transitive, act-denoting base) | 위조하다 | wijo-hada | "to forge / to counterfeit" | [CORE] | Common in legal and formal contexts; restricted to nominal bases denoting deliberate acts rather than states. |

**Attested alternate constructions:** For stative adjectives, periphrastic 정확하게 만들다 ("to make accurate") is noted in P4a as the available strategy, but is not attested as a productive pattern in the P1–P3 data and is not listed here as a separate construction.

## 6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Noun/Adjective stem + 성 (性) | 진실성 | jinsilseong | "truthfulness / veracity" | [CORE] | Highly productive with Sino-Korean bases in the truth domain; dominant in formal, academic, journalistic, and legal registers. |
| Noun/Adjective stem + 성 (性) | 신뢰성 | sinloeseong | "reliability / trustworthiness" | [CORE] | Same productivity and register distribution as 진실성; the 성 suffix is the default abstract nominalizer for this domain. |
| Adjective stem + -(으)ㅁ | 올바름 | olbareum | "rightness / correctness" | [MARGINAL] | Restricted to native adjective stems; appears primarily in philosophical and formal written registers. Not interchangeable with 성-forms. |
| Adjective stem + -(으)ㅁ | 옳음 | oreum | "rightness / correctness" | [MARGINAL] | More formal and philosophical in tone than 올바름; limited to written and academic contexts. |

**Attested alternate constructions:** The two sub-patterns (성 and -(으)ㅁ) are documented as non-interchangeable in P4a; no additional alternates identified.

## 7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Noun + 쟁이 (colloquial agentive) | 거짓말쟁이 | geojitmaljaengi | "liar / habitual liar" | [CORE] | Common in colloquial and informal spoken registers; denotes habitual character rather than a single act. Restricted to 거짓말 in the truth domain; no parallel truth-teller form is attested. |

**Attested alternate constructions:** 위조범 ("forger") is noted in P4a as a legal category label using the suffix 범 (犯), but is identified as a distinct legal pattern rather than a productive agentive alternant. No further alternates attested.

## 8. EXISTENTIAL PREDICATION OF ABSTRACT QUALITY

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Abstract quality noun + 이/가 있다 | 진정성이 있다 | jinjeongseong-i itda | "to have sincerity / to be authentic" | [CORE] | The primary predication strategy for 성-nominalized quality nouns in the truth domain; common across spoken and written registers. |
| Abstract quality noun + 이/가 없다 | 신빙성이 없다 | sinbyeongseong-i eopda | "to lack credibility / to be implausible" | [CORE] | Equally productive as the affirmative form; the standard negative predication for abstract quality nouns. Highly frequent in evaluative and journalistic contexts. |

**Attested alternate constructions:** Scalar predication with 높다/낮다 (see Construction 9) is attested as a related but distinct pattern for the same nominal class.

---

## 9. SCALAR PREDICATION OF ABSTRACT QUALITY

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Abstract quality noun + 이/가 높다 | 신뢰성이 높다 | sinloeseong-i nopda | "credibility is high" | [CORE] | Common in journalistic, legal, and scientific evaluation contexts; 성-forms and 도-forms both participate in this construction. |
| Abstract quality noun + 이/가 낮다 | 정확도가 낮다 | jeonghwakdoga natda | "accuracy is low" | [CORE] | The 도 (度) suffix is noted as more frequent in this scalar construction than 성-forms, reflecting its association with quantifiable degree. |

**Attested alternate constructions:** Existential predication with 있다/없다 (Construction 8) is the related non-scalar predication strategy for the same nominal class.

## 10. ADVERBIAL MANNER MODIFICATION

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Adjective stem + 히 | 정확히 말하다 | jeonghwaki malhada | "to speak exactly / to state precisely" | [CORE] | Restricted to a limited set of Sino-Korean bases; 정확히 is the primary truth-domain example. Not interchangeable with 하게 for all bases. |
| Adjective stem + 하게 | 정직하게 말하다 | jeongjik-hage malhada | "to speak honestly" | [CORE] | Productive across all 하다-adjectives in the truth domain; common in both spoken and written registers with no observable restriction. |
| Noun + (으)로 | 진정으로 사과하다 | jinjeong-euro sagwa-hada | "to apologize sincerely" | [CORE] | Applies to nominal bases that do not form 하다-adjectives; common with 진정으로, 진심으로, 허위로, 가짜로. Particularly frequent in formal and written contexts. |
| Noun + (으)로 | 진심으로 감사하다 | jinsimeuro gamsahada | "to thank sincerely / from the heart" | [CORE] | Same pattern as above; 진심으로 is highly conventionalized and common across registers. |

**Attested alternate constructions:** The three sub-patterns (히, 하게, (으)로) are documented as non-interchangeable in P4a; no additional alternates identified.

## 11. DISCOURSE-FRAMING CONSTRUCTION

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Noun/Adverb + (을/를) 말하자면 | 정확히 말하자면 | jeonghwaki malhajamyeon | "to be precise / strictly speaking" | [CORE] | Highly conventionalized pragmatic marker; common across spoken and written registers as a framing device for correction or precision. |
| Noun/Adverb + (을/를) 말하자면 | 사실을 말하자면 | sasireul malhajamyeon | "to tell the truth / the fact of the matter is" | [CORE] | Functions as a frank-disclosure marker; common in both spoken conversation and written argumentation. No register restriction observed. |

**Attested alternate constructions:** 말하다면 is noted in P4a as a variant alongside 말하자면; no further alternates identified.

## 12. POLAR OPPOSITION FRAMING

| Construction Type | Example | Transliteration | Translation | Naturalness | Usage Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Noun₁ + 과/와 + Noun₂ (coordinated nominal pair) | 진실과 거짓 | jinsil-gwa geojit | "truth and falsehood" | [CORE] | Highly conventionalized fixed collocation; common in moral, philosophical, and rhetorical contexts. Not freely productive with arbitrary truth-domain noun pairs. |
| Adjective stem coordination | 옳고 그름 | olko geureum | "right and wrong" | [CORE] | Fixed collocation combining a verbal/adjectival coordinate with a nominalized form; common in ethical and evaluative discourse. |

**Attested alternate constructions:** 진짜인지 가짜인지 ("whether real or fake") is attested in P4a as an extension of this pattern into an alternative-question construction expressing authenticity doubt.
