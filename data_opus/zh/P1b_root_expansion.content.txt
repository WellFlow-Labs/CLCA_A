# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P1b ROOT EXPANSION
# Language: Chinese (zh)

---

## STEP 1 — Central Roots

Examining the 30 P1 items, the three most frequent root morphemes in the truth-evaluation domain are:

**Root 1: 真 (zhēn)**
- Participates in: 11 P1 items (真, 真实, 真理, 真相, 真诚, 真伪, 逼真, 真切, 真挚, 货真价实, [also semantically central to others])
- Core meaning contribution: Asserts genuineness, correspondence to reality, and authenticity. The primary positive pole of the truth/genuineness axis. Functions both as a free morpheme and as the dominant bound element in compounds asserting that something is real, genuine, or sincere.

**Root 2: 实 (shí)**
- Participates in: 6 P1 items (真实, 事实, 诚实, 如实, 名副其实, 货真价实)
- Core meaning contribution: Denotes solidity, substance, the actual/factual. Anchors expressions to "what is actually the case" (fact, substance, reality-as-substance). Complements 真 by emphasizing factual/substantial reality rather than genuineness.

**Root 3: 确 (què)**
- Participates in: 3 P1 items (正确, 准确, 确凿)
- Core meaning contribution: Denotes accuracy, certainty, and exactness — correctness measured against a standard. (Note: this root participates in fewer than 5 P1 items but is included as the third-most-frequent and a distinct sub-domain — accuracy/certainty — not covered by 真 or 实.)

## STEP 2 — Root Expansion

### Root 1: 真 (zhēn)

真 is highly productive (well over 30 forms exist). The 20–30 most common are listed; additional forms exist beyond these.

**真的 (zhēn de)**
- 真 (true) + 的 (particle)
- "really; real; truly"
- Pervasive colloquial term, both adjectival ("是真的") and adverbial intensifier ("真的好吃"). Everyday speech.
- [CORE]

**真正 (zhēnzhèng)**
- 真 (true) + 正 (upright/proper)
- "genuine, real, true (in the full sense)"
- Extremely common attributive emphasizing that something is the genuine instance of its kind (真正的朋友). All registers.
- [CORE]

**真是 (zhēn shì)**
- 真 (truly) + 是 (be)
- "really is; what a..."
- Colloquial emphatic expression of evaluation or exclamation (真是太好了). Everyday speech.
- [CORE]

**当真 (dàngzhēn)**
- 当 (treat as) + 真 (real)
- "take seriously; take as true"
- Common in speech, often negated (别当真). Concerns whether to believe/act on something as real.
- [CORE]

**认真 (rènzhēn)**
- 认 (recognize) + 真 (true)
- "earnest, conscientious, serious"
- Very high-frequency term describing diligent, careful attitude (认真学习). All registers. [Meaning shifts toward diligence — see Step 3.]
- [CORE]

**天真 (tiānzhēn)**
- 天 (heaven/natural) + 真 (true)
- "naive, innocent"
- Common term for childlike innocence or naivety (天真无邪). Everyday and literary. [Shift — see Step 3.]
- [CORE]

**纯真 (chúnzhēn)**
- 纯 (pure) + 真 (true)
- "pure and innocent, unaffected"
- Describes innocence and unspoiled genuineness, esp. of children/feelings. Literary and everyday.
- [CORE]

**率真 (shuàizhēn)**
- 率 (frank/straightforward) + 真 (true)
- "frank and sincere, candid"
- Describes a personality that is straightforward and genuine. Somewhat literary/evaluative.
- [MARGINAL]

**真心 (zhēnxīn)**
- 真 (true) + 心 (heart)
- "sincere, wholehearted; truly"
- Common in interpersonal contexts (真心话, 真心对你好). Everyday speech.
- [CORE]

**真情 (zhēnqíng)**
- 真 (true) + 情 (feeling)
- "true feelings, genuine emotion"
- Describes authentic emotion (真情流露). Literary and everyday.
- [CORE]

**真意 (zhēnyì)**
- 真 (true) + 意 (meaning/intention)
- "true meaning, real intention"
- Refers to the genuine intent or true meaning behind words. Somewhat formal/literary.
- [MARGINAL]

**真迹 (zhēnjì)**
- 真 (genuine) + 迹 (trace/mark)
- "authentic work (of calligraphy/painting), original"
- Used in art/authentication contexts to denote a genuine original vs. forgery. Specialized.
- [CORE]

**真品 (zhēnpǐn)**
- 真 (genuine) + 品 (item)
- "genuine article, authentic product"
- Used in commerce and authentication contexts, contrasted with 赝品/假货.
- [CORE]

**真假 (zhēnjiǎ)**
- 真 (true) + 假 (false)
- "true or false, authenticity"
- Fixed binomial referring to the genuineness question (分辨真假). Everyday and formal.
- [CORE]

**真理 → 真知 (zhēnzhī)**
- 真 (true) + 知 (knowledge)
- "true knowledge, genuine insight"
- Often in 真知灼见. Intellectual/formal register.
- [MARGINAL]

**真实性 (zhēnshíxìng)**
- 真实 (real) + 性 (-ness)
- "authenticity, truthfulness (as a property)"
- Used in journalism, scholarship, and law to assess the reliability of accounts/evidence.
- [CORE]

**真假难辨 (zhēn jiǎ nán biàn)**
- 真假 (true-false) + 难 (hard) + 辨 (distinguish)
- "hard to tell true from false"
- Set expression in everyday and journalistic use.
- [CORE]

**真凭实据 (zhēn píng shí jù)**
- 真 (true) + 凭 (proof) + 实 (real) + 据 (evidence)
- "solid evidence and proof"
- Fixed idiom in legal/argumentative contexts. (Also contains 实.)
- [CORE]

**真相大白 (zhēnxiàng dàbái)**
- 真相 (truth) + 大白 (made fully clear)
- "the truth comes fully to light"
- Set idiom in news/narrative contexts when facts are revealed.
- [CORE]

**返璞归真 (fǎn pú guī zhēn)**
- 返 (return) + 璞 (uncarved jade) + 归 (return) + 真 (true/natural)
- "return to simplicity/naturalness, back to one's true nature"
- Idiom in philosophical/aesthetic/lifestyle discourse. [Shift toward natural state — see Step 3.]
- [CORE]

**弄假成真 (nòng jiǎ chéng zhēn)**
- 弄 (make) + 假 (fake) + 成 (become) + 真 (real)
- "what began as pretense becomes real"
- Fixed idiom, everyday and narrative.
- [CORE]

**假戏真做 (jiǎ xì zhēn zuò)**
- 假 (fake) + 戏 (play) + 真 (real) + 做 (do)
- "to play a fake game in earnest; pretense done for real"
- Set idiom, colloquial/narrative.
- [MARGINAL]

**真知灼见 (zhēn zhī zhuó jiàn)**
- 真 (true) + 知 (knowledge) + 灼 (penetrating) + 见 (insight)
- "genuine insight, penetrating wisdom"
- Formal/literary praise of someone's views.
- [CORE]

**信以为真 (xìn yǐ wéi zhēn)**
- 信 (believe) + 以为 (take as) + 真 (true)
- "to believe (something false) to be true"
- Common idiom in everyday and narrative contexts.
- [CORE]

**千真万确 (qiān zhēn wàn què)**
- 千 (thousand) + 真 (true) + 万 (ten-thousand) + 确 (certain)
- "absolutely true, beyond all doubt"
- Emphatic idiom asserting certainty. Everyday/colloquial. (Also contains 确.)
- [CORE]

(Additional 真 forms exist, e.g. 真切, 真挚, 逼真 [in P1], 真菌 [biology — see Step 3], 真空 [physics — see Step 3].)

### Root 2: 实 (shí)

实 is highly productive. Most common truth-relevant and general forms below; additional forms exist.

**实在 (shízài)**
- 实 (solid) + 在 (exist)
- "real, true; honest; really (adv.)"
- Common both as "genuine/down-to-earth" and as intensifier (实在太累). Everyday speech.
- [CORE]

**实际 (shíjì)**
- 实 (real) + 际 (boundary/juncture)
- "actual, practical, real(ity)"
- Very high-frequency (实际上, 实际情况). All registers.
- [CORE]

**现实 (xiànshí)**
- 现 (present) + 实 (real)
- "reality; realistic"
- Pervasive across registers (面对现实, 现实主义). Philosophical and everyday.
- [CORE]

**实话 (shíhuà)**
- 实 (true) + 话 (speech)
- "the truth, honest words"
- Everyday speech (说实话, 实话实说).
- [CORE]

**实情 (shíqíng)**
- 实 (real) + 情 (situation)
- "the real situation, true state of affairs"
- Common in everyday and reporting contexts.
- [CORE]

**实事求是 (shí shì qiú shì)**
- 实 (real) + 事 (matter) + 求 (seek) + 是 (truth/what-is)
- "seek truth from facts; be realistic and pragmatic"
- Major idiom in political, administrative, and everyday discourse.
- [CORE]

**属实 (shǔshí)**
- 属 (belong to) + 实 (fact)
- "be confirmed as true, accord with fact"
- Common in administrative, legal, and journalistic contexts (情况属实).
- [CORE]

**核实 (héshí)**
- 核 (verify) + 实 (fact)
- "to verify, check the facts"
- Administrative, journalistic, procedural contexts.
- [CORE]

**证实 (zhèngshí)**
- 证 (prove) + 实 (fact)
- "to confirm, verify, corroborate"
- Common in news, science, and legal contexts.
- [CORE]

**确实 (quèshí)**
- 确 (certain) + 实 (real)
- "indeed, really; true, reliable"
- Very high-frequency confirmatory term. All registers. (Also contains 确.)
- [CORE]

**真实 → 实质 (shízhì)**
- 实 (substance) + 质 (nature)
- "substance, essence, substantive"
- Used in analytical/legal contexts (实质性). 
- [CORE]

**事实 → 实事 (shíshì)**
- 实 (real) + 事 (matter)
- "real/practical matters"
- Chiefly in 实事求是 and 办实事. Administrative/everyday.
- [MARGINAL]

**老实 (lǎoshi)**
- 老 (old/very) + 实 (honest)
- "honest, well-behaved, naive"
- Very common character evaluation (老实人). Everyday.
- [CORE]

**朴实 (pǔshí)**
- 朴 (plain) + 实 (solid)
- "plain, simple, unadorned, sincere"
- Describes character/style as unpretentious. Literary and everyday. [Shift toward plainness — see Step 3.]
- [CORE]

**踏实 (tāshi)**
- 踏 (step firmly) + 实 (solid)
- "steady, dependable; (feeling) at ease"
- Describes diligent reliability or peace of mind (心里踏实). Everyday. [Shift — see Step 3.]
- [CORE]

**充实 (chōngshí)**
- 充 (fill) + 实 (solid)
- "substantial, full, enriched"
- Describes a full/rich state (充实的生活). [Shift toward fullness — see Step 3.]
- [CORE]

**实力 (shílì)**
- 实 (real/substantive) + 力 (strength)
- "actual strength, real power/capability"
- Common in sports, business, geopolitics. [Shift toward capability — see Step 3.]
- [CORE]

**落实 (luòshí)**
- 落 (fall/settle) + 实 (solid)
- "to implement, put into practice"
- Heavy use in administrative/political discourse (落实政策). [Shift toward implementation — see Step 3.]
- [CORE]

**切实 (qièshí)**
- 切 (close/earnest) + 实 (real)
- "practical, earnest, concrete(ly)"
- Administrative/formal (切实可行). 
- [CORE]

**如实 → 据实 (jùshí)**
- 据 (according to) + 实 (fact)
- "according to the facts, truthfully"
- Formal/administrative (据实上报).
- [MARGINAL]

**名实相符 (míng shí xiāng fú)**
- 名 (name) + 实 (reality) + 相符 (match)
- "name and reality correspond"
- Formal idiom; variant of 名副其实.
- [CORE]

**名存实亡 (míng cún shí wáng)**
- 名 (name) + 存 (exists) + 实 (reality) + 亡 (gone)
- "exists in name only, defunct in substance"
- Formal/journalistic idiom.
- [CORE]

**华而不实 (huá ér bù shí)**
- 华 (flashy) + 而 (yet) + 不 (not) + 实 (substantial)
- "flashy but without substance"
- Critical evaluation, formal and everyday.
- [CORE]

**名不副实 (in P1) / 名副其实 (in P1)** — already listed.

(Additional 实 forms exist, e.g. 实验 [experiment], 实践 [practice], 诚实 [P1] — see Step 3 for non-truth uses.)

### Root 3: 确 (què)

确 participates in fewer than 5 P1 items but yields a moderate set of compounds. (Note: moderately productive; key forms below.)

**确定 (quèdìng)**
- 确 (certain) + 定 (fix)
- "to determine; certain, definite"
- Very high-frequency across all registers (确定无疑, 不确定).
- [CORE]

**确认 (quèrèn)**
- 确 (certain) + 认 (recognize)
- "to confirm, acknowledge"
- Common in procedural, technical, and everyday contexts (确认信息).
- [CORE]

**确切 (quèqiè)**
- 确 (certain) + 切 (close/exact)
- "exact, precise, definite"
- Formal/written (确切的数字). Emphasizes exactness.
- [CORE]

**明确 (míngquè)**
- 明 (clear) + 确 (certain)
- "clear, explicit, unambiguous"
- Very common (明确规定, 明确表态). All registers.
- [CORE]

**确信 (quèxìn)**
- 确 (certain) + 信 (believe)
- "to be convinced, firmly believe"
- Common in formal and everyday contexts.
- [CORE]

**确实 (quèshí)** — also listed under 实.
- "indeed, really; reliable"
- [CORE]

**的确 (díquè)**
- 的 (truly) + 确 (certain)
- "indeed, really"
- High-frequency confirmatory adverb. All registers.
- [CORE]

**正确 / 准确 / 确凿 (in P1)** — already listed.

**千真万确 (qiān zhēn wàn què)** — also listed under 真.

**精确 (jīngquè)**
- 精 (fine/precise) + 确 (accurate)
- "precise, exact (technical)"
- Scientific/technical contexts (精确测量).
- [CORE]

(确 has fewer than ~15 common compounds; this is a relatively contained set compared to 真 and 实.)

## STEP 3 — Peripheral Extensions (flagged, not interpreted)

Expressions where the root's contribution appears to shift away from its core truth-domain sense:

**Root 真 (zhēn):**
- 认真 (rènzhēn) — "conscientious/diligent" (attitude/effort)
- 天真 (tiānzhēn) — "naive/innocent" (character)
- 纯真 (chúnzhēn) — "innocent" (character)
- 返璞归真 (fǎn pú guī zhēn) — "return to natural simplicity" (natural state)
- 真菌 (zhēnjūn) — "fungus" (biological taxonomy)
- 真空 (zhēnkōng) — "vacuum" (physics)
- 真皮 (zhēnpí) — "dermis; genuine leather" (anatomy/material)
- 真的/真是 (zhēn de / zhēn shì) — intensifier/degree-marking function

**Root 实 (shí):**
- 朴实 (pǔshí) — "plain/unadorned" (style/character)
- 踏实 (tāshi) — "steady; at ease" (feeling/disposition)
- 充实 (chōngshí) — "full/enriched" (fullness)
- 实力 (shílì) — "strength/capability" (capacity)
- 落实 (luòshí) — "to implement" (action/execution)
- 实在 (shízài) — intensifier "really/truly" (degree-marking)
- 实验 (shíyàn) — "experiment" (procedure)
- 实践 (shíjiàn) — "practice" (activity)
- 果实 (guǒshí) / 结实 (jiēshí/jiēshi) — "fruit; sturdy" (botanical/physical solidity)

**Root 确 (què):**
- 确切 / 精确 / 准确 — shift toward exactness/precision (measurement) rather than truth per se
- 确定 / 确信 / 确认 — shift toward certainty/psychological conviction and procedural confirmation rather than correspondence-truth
