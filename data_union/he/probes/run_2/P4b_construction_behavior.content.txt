## Summary Note

All ten construction types identified in P4a receive a [CORE] rating. No construction in the attested data is restricted to marginal or uncertain status. Observable restrictions are internal to constructions rather than indicators of marginality: the *-an* / active participle distinction in agentive nominalization, the declarative / interrogative split in truth-predicate frames, the indefinite / definite contrast in verbal periphrasis, and the non-availability of a productive negative attributive prefix are all systematic constraints within otherwise fully productive patterns. The grammaticalization of *be-diyuq* relative to other members of the adverbial truth-framing set is the only case of differential entrenchment noted within a single construction type.
