# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P2a Derivational System
# Language: Turkish (tr)
# Layer: P2a_derivational_system

---

## Preliminary Note on Scope

This step documents attested derivational morphology for the lexical items identified in P1. The P1b root expansion has already established derivational families for the three central roots (*doğr-*, *gerçek-*, *hak-/hakk-*). This step therefore focuses on:

1. Derivations from P1 items not already covered in P1b
2. Any derivations from P1b items that extend the system further and are independently attested
3. Explicit notation of gaps where derivation does not occur or is not attested

Forms already fully documented in P1b are cross-referenced rather than repeated in full.

### Base: **doğru**

*Core derivations already documented in P1b: doğruluk, doğrulamak, doğrulama, doğrulanmak, doğrulatmak, doğrultmak, doğrultu, doğrudan, doğrusal, doğrulayıcı, doğrulanabilir, doğrulanabilirlik.*

---

**Derived Form 1**

1. **Base Form:** doğru
2. **Derived Form:** doğruluk
3. **Transliteration:** doğruluk
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *doğru* + *-luk* (abstract noun suffix)
5. **Natural English translation:** truth, correctness, accuracy, integrity, truthfulness
6. **Usage:** Names the property of being true, correct, or morally upright. Used in philosophical, moral, and everyday contexts.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2**

1. **Base Form:** doğru
2. **Derived Form:** doğrulamak
3. **Transliteration:** doğrulamak
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *doğru* + *-la-* (factitive/denominative verbalizer) + *-mak* (infinitive)
5. **Natural English translation:** to verify, to confirm, to validate
6. **Usage:** The primary verbal derivative; used in administrative, journalistic, legal, and scientific registers for the act of establishing the truth or validity of a claim or document.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 3**

1. **Base Form:** doğru
2. **Derived Form:** doğrulama
3. **Transliteration:** doğrulama
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *doğru* + *-la-* + *-ma* (verbal noun)
5. **Natural English translation:** verification, confirmation, authentication, validation
6. **Usage:** Nominalized form of *doğrulamak*; standard in technical, administrative, and journalistic registers. *Kimlik doğrulama* ("identity verification") is a fixed technical term.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 4**

1. **Base Form:** doğru
2. **Derived Form:** doğrulanmak
3. **Transliteration:** doğrulanmak
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *doğru* + *-la-* + *-n-* (passive) + *-mak*
5. **Natural English translation:** to be verified, to be confirmed
6. **Usage:** Passive form; used in scientific and journalistic registers when the claim or document is the grammatical subject. *İddia henüz doğrulanmadı.* ("The claim has not yet been confirmed.")
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 5**

5. **Natural English translation:** to be unable to verify, to fail to confirm
6. **Usage:** Used in journalistic and legal contexts to describe the failure or inability to confirm a claim. *Haberi doğrulayamadık.* ("We were unable to verify the news.")
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

> **Note on gaps:** No commonly attested negative privative form *\*doğrusuz* is in common use; negation is typically expressed through *yanlış* or *hatalı* rather than a privative derivative of *doğru*. No commonly attested agentive noun *doğrulayıcı* functioning as a personal title (e.g., "a verifier" as a job title) has been identified beyond its adjectival and technical uses.

### Base: **yanlış**

---

**Derived Form 1**

1. **Base Form:** yanlış
2. **Derived Form:** yanlışlık
3. **Transliteration:** yanlışlık
4. **Literal Breakdown:** yanlış (wrong/incorrect) + -lık (nominal suffix)
5. **Natural English translation:** error, mistake, incorrectness, wrongness
6. **Usage:** Names the quality or instance of being wrong or incorrect. *Yanlışlık yapmak* (to make a mistake) is a common collocation in everyday and formal speech.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 2**

1. **Base Form:** yanlış
2. **Derived Form:** yanlışlamak
3. **Transliteration:** yanlışlamak
4. **Literal Breakdown:** yanlış (wrong) + -la- (verbalizer) + -mak (infinitive)
5. **Natural English translation:** to falsify, to show to be wrong, to refute, to disprove
6. **Usage:** Used in philosophical, scientific, and argumentative contexts to describe demonstrating that a claim is incorrect. Associated with Popperian falsificationism in Turkish academic discourse: *bir hipotezi yanlışlamak* (to falsify a hypothesis).
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 3**

1. **Base Form:** yanlış
2. **Derived Form:** yanlışlama
3. **Transliteration:** yanlışlama
4. **Literal Breakdown:** yanlış + -la- + -ma (verbal noun)
5. **Natural English translation:** falsification, refutation
6. **Usage:** The nominalized process of falsifying or refuting. Used in philosophy of science: *yanlışlama ilkesi* (the principle of falsification). Directly parallels *doğrulama* in structure and register.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 4**

1. **Base Form:** yanlış
2. **Derived Form:** yanlışlanabilir
3. **Transliteration:** yanlışlanabilir
4. **Literal Breakdown:** yanlış + -la- + -n- (passive) + -abilir (potential suffix)
5. **Natural English translation:** falsifiable
6. **Usage:** Used in philosophy of science to describe claims that are in principle capable of being shown to be false. *Yanlışlanabilir hipotez* (falsifiable hypothesis). Directly parallels *doğrulanabilir* in structure and domain.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 5**

1. **Base Form:** yanlış
2. **Derived Form:** yanlışlanabilirlik
3. **Transliteration:** yanlışlanabilirlik
4. **Literal Breakdown:** yanlış + -la- + -n- + -abilir + -lik (nominal suffix)
5. **Natural English translation:** falsifiability
6. **Usage:** The abstract property of being falsifiable. Used in philosophy of science: *yanlışlanabilirlik ölçütü* (the criterion of falsifiability, associated with Popper). Directly parallels *doğrulanabilirlik*.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 6**

1. **Base Form:** yanlış
2. **Derived Form:** yanlışlanmak
3. **Transliteration:** yanlışlanmak
4. **Literal Breakdown:** yanlış + -la- + -n- (passive) + -mak
5. **Natural English translation:** to be falsified, to be shown to be wrong
6. **Usage:** Passive form; used when a claim or hypothesis is demonstrated to be incorrect by evidence or argument. *Hipotez yanlışlandı* (the hypothesis was falsified). *Bu teori yanlışlanamaz.* ("This theory cannot be falsified.")
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 7**

1. **Base Form:** yanlış
2. **Derived Form:** yanlışlıkla
3. **Transliteration:** yanlışlıkla
4. **Literal Breakdown:** yanlış + -lık + -la (instrumental/adverbial suffix, lexicalized as adverb)
5. **Natural English translation:** by mistake, accidentally, erroneously
6. **Usage:** An extremely common adverbial expression used to indicate that an action was performed in error rather than intentionally. *Yanlışlıkla gönderdi* (he sent it by mistake). *Yanlışlıkla sildim.* ("I deleted it by mistake.") Used across all registers.
7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

**Derived Form 8**

1. **Base Form:** yanlış
2. **Derived Form:** yanlışça
3. **Transliteration:** yanlışça
4. **Literal Breakdown:** *yanlış* + *-ca* (adverbial/manner suffix)
5. **Natural English translation:** wrongly, incorrectly, mistakenly
6. **Usage:** An adverbial form used to describe actions or judgments carried out in an incorrect or mistaken manner. *Yanlışça anlamak* (to misunderstand, to understand incorrectly). Attested but less frequent than the predicative use of *yanlış* and less frequent than *yanlışlıkla*.

**Derived Form 9**

**Derived Form 10**

4. **Literal Breakdown:** yanlış + anlaşılmak (to be understood, passive)

5. **Natural English translation:** misinformation, incorrect information

**Derived Form 12**

7. **Epistemic status:** [CORE]
