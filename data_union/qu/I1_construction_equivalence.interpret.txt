## 2. Substitution Patterns (Narrative)

### 2.1 REVELATION and BECOME

No construction specifically labeled or functioning as REVELATION (i.e., a construction encoding the event of a truth-value being disclosed or discovered by a subject) is documented in P4a or P4b. The closest attested forms are:

- The past-copula construction *cheqa kasqam* ("it turned out to be true"), which is described in P4a as appearing in "third-person past or reported contexts." This form encodes a retrospective or reported truth-state but is described as a contextual variant of the ATTR construction (with overt copula *kasqa*), not as a distinct REVELATION construction.
- The propositional knowledge construction *cheqa kasqanta yachani* ("I know that it is true") encodes the subject's epistemic access to a truth-state but is classified in P4a/P4b under propositional knowledge (§10), not as a revelation event.

The BECOME constructions (*cheqakay*, *allinkay*, *allinyay*) encode a change of state (becoming true/good) but do not encode the disclosure or discovery of a pre-existing truth. On the basis of the data in P4a and P4b, REVELATION is a structural gap: no construction is documented that specifically encodes the event of a truth being revealed or discovered as distinct from the state of being true or the act of becoming true.

### 2.2 CAUSATION (factual) vs. CAUSATION (character)

The two causative pathways (*-ncha-* and *-chi-*) both map onto CAUSATION (factual): they encode the act of making something true, correcting an error, or causing someone to err or believe a falsehood. Neither pathway is described as specifically encoding character-level causation (i.e., the act of making someone into a liar or a truthful person as a stable trait).

Character attribution is instead expressed through agentive nominalization: *llullaq* ("liar"), *llullakuq* ("habitual liar"), *cheqaqniyuq* ("one who possesses truthfulness"). The habitual nuance of *llullakuq* (reflexive *-ku* + agentive *-q*) is the closest attested form to a character-level description, but it is a nominal, not a causative construction. There is no attested construction of the form "to make someone into a liar" (as a character transformation) distinct from "to cause someone to lie" (as a single act). The two meanings are not formally distinguished in the data.

### 2.3 VERIFICATION vs. DISCONFIRMATION

VERIFICATION and DISCONFIRMATION do not share the same construction in Southern Quechua as documented in P4a/P4b.

- VERIFICATION is expressed by the *-ncha-* causative (*cheqanchay*, *cheqachiy*) and its reflexive extension (*cheqanchakuy*).
- DISCONFIRMATION is expressed by the *mana…-chu* negation frame (*Manan cheqachu*, *Mana cheqachu*) and by the corrective causative *chiqanchay* ("to correct a mistake").

The corrective causative *chiqanchay* is functionally adjacent to DISCONFIRMATION in that it encodes the act of rectifying something that was wrong, but it is morphologically a CAUSATION construction, not a negation construction. The *mana…-chu* frame is the primary formal expression of DISCONFIRMATION.

### 2.4 Asymmetries: Truth vs. Falsity

Several systematic asymmetries between the *cheqa* (truth) and *llulla* (falsehood) paradigms are documented:

- **BECOME:** *cheqakay* and *allinkay* are attested; no BECOME form for *llulla* is documented. P4a explicitly notes: "No inchoative form of *llulla* is attested as a common expression in the sources consulted."
- **Abstract nominalization:** *cheqa kay*, *cheqan*, *cheqanta* are attested as abstract nominals for "truth." No parallel abstract nominal (*llulla kay* or equivalent) for "falsehood" as a standalone noun is attested. P4a states: "No parallel abstract nominal is attested for *llulla* in the sources consulted; the concept of 'falsehood' as an abstract noun is expressed through the bare adjective or through verbal nominalizations."
- **Agentive nominalization:** *llullaq* and *llullakuq* function primarily as person-characterizations ("liar"). *Cheqaq* functions primarily as an adjective ("genuine, authentic") rather than as a person-characterization. This asymmetry is explicitly noted in P4a as present in the attested data.
- **Adverbial construction:** The adverbial truth-assertion construction (*cheqaqta rimay*, *cheqaqtam yachani*) is documented for *cheqa* but P4a notes "no direct parallel in the *llulla* paradigm."
- **Prohibitive moral injunction:** The *ama…-chu* prohibitive construction is attested exclusively with *llulla* and *llullakuy* among truth-domain items. No parallel prohibitive is documented for *cheqa*, *chiqan*, or *allin*.
- **Reflexive/self-directed:** The *-ncha-ku-y* reflexive construction is attested for *cheqa*, *allin*, and *sut'i*; no reflexive self-directed form is documented for *llulla* in this pattern.

### 2.5 Negation: Analytic vs. Morphological

Negation in Southern Quechua as documented is **both analytic and morphological**, depending on syntactic environment:

- In **predicative** contexts, negation is expressed by the discontinuous frame *mana…-chu*, where *mana* is a free negation particle and *-chu* is an obligatory suffix on the predicate. This is described as a morphological requirement: omission of *-chu* would be ungrammatical.
- In **prenominal attributive** contexts, negation is expressed by *mana* alone preceding the adjective-noun sequence, without *-chu*. This is the analytic pattern.

The two environments are structurally distinct and the choice between them is conditioned by syntactic position, not by the lexical content of the truth-domain item.

### 2.6 Light Verbs and Periphrastic Constructions

The following constructions use light verbs as semantic carriers:

- *tukuy* ("to become") in the BECOME periphrasis (*Chiqan tukun*, *Cheqaq tukun*) — *tukuy* carries tense and person inflection; the truth-domain adjective is the semantic head. Rated [UNCERTAIN] due to limited specific attestation.
- *tiyan* ("it is necessary / it exists") in the deontic periphrasis (*Cheqanta rimanayki tiyan*) — *tiyan* is the deontic carrier; the nominalized verb phrase carries the propositional content.
- *atiy* ("to be able to") in the ability periphrasis (*Cheqachiyta atiy*) — *atiy* is the modal carrier; the infinitive of the truth-evaluation verb carries the propositional content.
- *yachay* ("to know") in the propositional knowledge construction (*cheqa kasqanta yachani*) — *yachay* is the epistemic carrier; the nominalized predicate is the propositional argument.

In all four cases, the truth-domain item is expressed in the nominalized or adjectival complement, and the light verb carries grammatical categories (tense, person, modality).

## 3. Missingness Summary

### 3.1 Structural Gaps

| Gap | Description |
|---|---|
| **REVELATION** | No construction specifically encoding the event of a truth being revealed or discovered (as distinct from BECOME or propositional knowledge) is documented in P4a or P4b. The past-copula form *cheqa kasqam* is the closest attested form but is described as a contextual variant of ATTR, not a distinct REVELATION construction. **MISSING (structural gap).** |

### 3.2 Lexical Gaps

| Gap | Description |
|---|---|
| **BECOME for *llulla*** | No inchoative form (*llullakay*, *llullayay*, or equivalent) is attested. P4a explicitly notes this absence. The gap is lexically specific to the *llulla* root; the BECOME construction itself is available on other roots. |
| **Abstract nominal for 'falsehood'** | No abstract nominal equivalent to *cheqa kay* / *cheqan* is attested for *llulla*. The concept of "falsehood" as an abstract noun is expressed through the bare adjective or verbal nominalizations. P4a explicitly notes this absence. |
| **Adverbial form for *llulla*** | The adverbial construction (*cheqaqta*, *cheqaqtam*) has no documented parallel in the *llulla* paradigm. P4a explicitly notes this. |

### 3.3 Conceptual Gaps

| Gap | Description |
|---|---|
| **CAUSATION (character) as unified construction** | No single construction simultaneously encodes both the causal act and the character attribution (i.e., "to make someone into a liar" as a stable trait transformation). The meaning is distributed across the causative verb (*llullachiy*) and the agentive nominal (*llullakuq*). This is not described as a blocked operation but as an absence of a unified form. |

### 3.4 Asymmetric Gaps (only one polarity attested)

| Gap | Description |
|---|---|
| **BECOME: truth-polarity only** | BECOME constructions are attested for *cheqa* and *allin* (truth/good polarity); none are attested for *llulla* (false polarity). |
| **Abstract nominalization: truth-polarity only** | Abstract nominals (*cheqa kay*, *cheqan*) are attested for the truth polarity; no parallel form is attested for the false polarity. |
| **Adverbial assertion: truth-polarity only** | The adverbial construction (*cheqaqta rimay*) is attested for the truth polarity; no parallel is documented for *llulla*. |
| **Prohibitive moral injunction: false-polarity only** | The *ama…-chu* prohibitive is attested exclusively with *llulla* and *llullakuy*; no parallel prohibitive is documented for *cheqa*, *chiqan*, or *allin*. |
| **Reflexive/self-directed (*-ncha-ku-y*): truth-polarity only** | Attested for *cheqa*, *allin*, *sut'i*; not documented for *llulla*. |
| **Agentive nominalization as person-characterization: false-polarity primary** | *Llullaq* / *llullakuq* function primarily as person-characterizations ("liar"). *Cheqaq* functions primarily as an adjective ("genuine") rather than a person-characterization. The asymmetry is explicitly noted in P4a. |
