# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P1b ROOT EXPANSION
# Language: Tamil (ta)

## Step 1 — Identify Central Roots

### Root 1: உண்மை (uṇmai)
**Form:** உண்மை / uṇmai  
**Participates in:** 4 P1 items directly (uṇmai, uṇmaiyāṉa, uṇmaiyil, plus related forms)  
**Core meaning contribution:** Derives from the verb உண் (uṇ) "to eat, to exist, to be" + abstract noun suffix -மை (-mai). Contributes the semantic core of "that which is/exists" → "truth, reality, genuineness." This root anchors the primary truth-evaluation vocabulary in Tamil, emphasizing ontological reality and factual correspondence.

### Root 2: பொய் (poy)
**Form:** பொய் / poy  
**Participates in:** 4 P1 items directly (poy, poyyāṉa, poyyāka, poymai)  
**Core meaning contribution:** Standalone root meaning "falsehood, lie." Serves as the primary antonym to uṇmai across all registers. Contributes the semantic core of deliberate falsity, inauthenticity, and deception.

### Root 3: மெய் (mey)
**Form:** மெய் / mey  
**Participates in:** 4 P1 items directly (mey, meypporuḷ, meyppu, meyyāka)  
**Core meaning contribution:** Polysemous root with primary meanings "body" and "truth/reality." In truth-domain contexts, contributes the semantic core of substantial reality, genuineness, and essential truth, often with literary or philosophical register.

## Step 3 — Peripheral Extensions

### Extensions of உண்மை (uṇmai):

1. **உண்மை வெளிச்சம் (uṇmai veḷiccam)** — "light of truth": Uses truth metaphorically as illumination, extending into metaphorical/poetic domain beyond literal truth-evaluation.

2. **உண்மை வழி (uṇmai vaḻi)** — "path of truth": Extends from truth-evaluation into moral/ethical guidance domain, where truth becomes a way of life rather than property of statements.

3. **உண்மை உணர்வு (uṇmai uṇarvu)** — "awareness of truth": Extends into psychological/phenomenological domain, treating truth as object of consciousness rather than property of propositions.

4. **உண்மை முகம் (uṇmai mukam)** — "true face": Metaphorical extension where truth applies to character revelation rather than factual correspondence.

### Extensions of பொய் (poy):

1. **பொய் கண்ணீர் (poy kaṇṇīr)** — "crocodile tears": Extends from truth-domain into evaluation of emotional authenticity and sincerity of affect.

2. **பொய் நாடகம் (poy nāṭakam)** — "false drama": Extends into theatrical/performance domain, evaluating sincerity of behavior rather than truth of statements.

3. **பொய் நேசம் (poy nēcam)** — "false love": Extends into evaluation of emotional authenticity and sincerity of affective states.

4. **பொய் நட்பு (poy naṭpu)** — "false friendship": Extends into social relationship evaluation, assessing genuineness of interpersonal bonds.

5. **பொய் முகம் (poy mukam)** — "false face": Metaphorical extension into character evaluation and assessment of pretense.

### Extensions of மெய் (mey):

1. **மெய்யுணர்வு (meyyuṇarvu)** — "true awareness/enlightenment": Extends into spiritual/mystical domain, where truth becomes object of spiritual realization rather than propositional content.

2. **மெய்ஞ்ஞானம் (meyññāṉam)** — "true wisdom": Extends into religious/spiritual knowledge domain, treating truth as salvific wisdom.

3. **மெய்யன்பு (meyyaṉpu)** — "true love": Extends into evaluation of emotional authenticity and depth of affective states.

4. **மெய்யியல் (meyyiyal)** — "metaphysics": Technical philosophical extension into academic discipline studying nature of reality.

5. **மெய்யுலகம் (meyyulakam)** — "real world": Extends into ontological categorization, distinguishing actual from possible or fictional worlds.

6. **Note on polysemy**: The root மெய் (mey) also means "body" in Tamil, and this meaning appears in numerous compounds unrelated to truth (e.g., மெய்க்காப்பாளர் "bodyguard," மெய் பயிற்சி "physical exercise"). These body-related uses are not included in this expansion as they represent a distinct semantic domain, though the polysemy itself may be historically significant.
