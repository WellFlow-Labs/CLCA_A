# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P4a CONSTRUCTIONS
# Language: Georgian (ka)

---

## 1. ATTRIBUTION

### Pattern
[Adjective] + [Noun] — prenominal attributive; adjective precedes noun and agrees in case.

Subject + Adjective + Copula (-a / aris) — predicative attribution; the copula is enclitic (-a) when the predicate adjective is final, or the full form aris in more formal registers.

Adjective (truth/falsity predicate) + copula (aris / -a clitic) functioning as a predicate, assigning a truth-domain property to a subject NP.

### Examples
- ჭეშმარიტი ამბავი / č̣ešmariti ambavi / "a true account; a factual story"
- ყალბი დოკუმენტი / q̇albi dokumenti / "a forged document"
- სიმართლე მწარეა / simartle mwarea / "The truth is bitter"
- ეს დოკუმენტი ნამდვილია / es dokumenti namdvilia / "This document is genuine"
- მართალია → martalia → "it is true / that is right"
- სიცრუეა → sicruea → "it is a lie / that is false"

### Notes
Georgian adjectives precede the noun and take the same case suffix as the noun they modify. No copula is involved in the attributive pattern. This is the unmarked attributive pattern across all truth-domain adjectives (č̣ešmariti, martali, sc̣ori, zusti, q̇albi, namdvili, etc.).

Standard predicative attribution in Georgian places the adjective before the copula clitic. The copula -a attaches to the final element of the predicate. No agreement morphology on the adjective distinguishes this from adnominal use.

The adjective inflects for case in parallel with the noun (e.g., genitive: ყალბი დოკუმენტის / q̇albi dokumentis). No gender or number agreement morphology is involved. The pattern is fully productive across all truth-domain adjectives documented in P1.

The copula frequently cliticizes to the predicate nominal or adjective (-a suffix), producing a single phonological word. The construction is used both as a full predicate and as a discourse-level response particle confirming or denying a prior claim.

## 2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION

### Pattern
[ara- + Adjective] + [Noun] — privative prefix ara- attached to adjective; or [Adjective] + [Noun] + [ar aris] for predicative negation.

Subject + Adjective + არ არის / ar aris (negated copula).

Privative prefix **u-** + adjectival stem + suffix **-o**, producing a negated adjective that is then used predicatively or attributively. Alternatively, the negative particle **ar** + copula with a truth-domain nominal.

### Examples
- არასწორი პასუხი / arasc̣ori pasuxiˈ / "an incorrect answer"
- არაგულწრფელი ბოდიში / aragulc̣rpeli bodišiˈ / "an insincere apology"
- დამაჯერებელი არ არის / damajerebeliˈ ar aris / "It is not convincing"
- ეს ინფორმაცია სარწმუნო არ არის / es inp̕ormacia sarwmuno ar aris / "This information is not reliable"
- უზუსტო → uzusto → "inaccurate, imprecise" (privative adjective)
- სიცრუე არ არის → sicrue ar aris → "it is not a lie / it is not false"

### Notes
The prefix ara- is the productive negation strategy for native adjectives in this domain (arasc̣ori, arasc̣muno, aragulc̣rpeli). The privative circumfix u-...-o is attested for one item (uzusto) but is less productive across the set. Predicative negation uses the separate negative particle ar with the copula.

The full copula aris is obligatory under negation; the enclitic -a form found in affirmative attribution is unavailable here. Word order is flexible but adjective-before-copula is the neutral order.

The **u-...-o** privative pattern is morphologically productive across the adjectival bases in this domain (uzusto, usarcmuno, usando, uk'anono, unamdvilo). The analytic negation with **ar aris** is available for all nominal predicates and is not restricted to this domain.

The u- and ara- prefixes are both available for negation but are not freely interchangeable: u- tends to produce lexicalized adjectives with specific meanings, while ara- produces more compositional negations.

## 3. ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION (genitive)

### Pattern
[Noun-GEN] + [Head Noun] — genitive modifier (suffix -is / -s) precedes head noun.

Attributive adjective (truth/falsity/authenticity predicate) precedes the head noun directly, with no agreement morphology required (Georgian adjectives do not inflect for gender or number in attributive position).

### Examples
- სიმართლის დადგენა / simarṭlis dadgena / "establishment of the truth; determination of the facts"
- სიყალბის ნიშნები / siq̇albis nišnebi / "signs of forgery; indicators of falsity"
- ყალბი დოკუმენტი / q̇albi dokumenti / "forged document"
- სარწმუნო მოწმე / sarcmuno mc̣me / "credible witness"

### Notes
The genitive suffix -is attaches to the modifying noun, which precedes the head. This pattern generates the majority of multi-word truth-domain nominals documented in P1b and P2b (simarṭlis tqma, č̣ešmaritebis dzieba, namdvilobis šemoc̣meba, etc.). The construction is fully productive.

Attributive adjectives are invariant in form regardless of the case, number, or definiteness of the head noun. When the head noun is inflected (e.g., genitive, dative), the adjective takes the same case suffix: **ყალბი დოკუმენტის** (q̇albi dok'ument'is, genitive). This is the primary construction for characterizing documents, persons, and statements as true, false, or authentic.

## 4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE

### Pattern
[Noun/Adjective] + gamodga / aġmočnda — "turned out to be X; proved to be X"; or gamovida — "came out, emerged" (for truth becoming known).

Verb stem + -დება / -deba (inchoative/passive suffix, present) or preverb გა- + stem + -დება / ga-...-deba (completive inchoative).

Two sub-patterns are attested:
- (a) მართლდება / martldeba — "becomes justified / is being vindicated" (ongoing)
- (b) გამართლდება / gamartldeba — "will be vindicated / becomes justified" (completive, with preverb)

Truth-domain nominal in the adessive case (**-ze** suffix) + motion verb **წასვლა** (c̣asvla, "going/resorting to"), or resultative verb **გამოაშკარავდა** (gamoašk'aravda, "was exposed/came to light") with a truth-domain subject.

### Examples
- სიმართლე გამოვიდა / simarṭle gamovida / "the truth came out; the truth emerged"
- მართალი გამოდგა / martali gamodga / "turned out to be right; proved correct"
- ის მართლდება / is martldeba / "He is being vindicated / He is proven right"
- სიმართლე გამოვიდა / simartle gamovida / "The truth came out" (inchoative event: truth entering visibility)
- სიყალბეზე წასვლა → siq̇albeze c̣asvla → "resorting to falsehood / descending into fraud"
- სიყალბე გამოაშკარავდა → siq̇albe gamoašk'aravda → "the fraud was exposed / the falsity came to light"

### Notes
Georgian lacks a single inchoative copula equivalent to "become." The verbs gamodga ("turned out to be") and aġmočnda ("proved to be, was found to be") fill this function for truth-domain predicates, expressing the discovery or confirmation of a property. Both are attested with truth adjectives (martali, mcdari, q̇albi, namdvili) and with the noun simarṭle.

The -deba suffix on the mart- root produces a genuine inchoative ("becomes right/justified"). The simartle gamovida construction is not morphologically inchoative but functions as the standard expression for truth transitioning from concealed to revealed; it is the closest natural equivalent for "truth becomes known." No productive inchoative form is attested for namdvili, q̇albi, or č̣ešmariti.

Georgian does not have a single dedicated inchoative construction for truth-domain meanings. The adessive + motion verb construction expresses a transition into a state of falsity or deception. The resultative verb construction expresses the coming-to-light of a hidden truth or fraud. Both are attested but neither is a grammaticalized inchoative morpheme.

## 5. CAUSATIVE

### Pattern
[da-/ga- preverb] + [root] + [-eba nominalization] / finite verb — preverb + root constructions express caused change of state; no dedicated causative morpheme isolated to this domain.

Preverb გა- + root + -ება / ga-...-eba (completive verbal noun / causative-resultative).

### Examples
- გაყალბება / gaq̇albeba / "forgery; the act of making [something] false" (from q̇alb-)
- დაზუსტება / dazusteba / "making precise; specifying exactly" (from zust-)
- მან დოკუმენტი გაყალბა / man dokumenti gaq̇alba / "He falsified the document"
- სასამართლომ იგი გაამართლა / sasamart̕lom igi gaamart̕la / "The court acquitted / vindicated him"

### Notes
Georgian does not have a single productive causative suffix equivalent to English "make X true/false." Caused-state meanings in this domain are expressed through preverb + root + -eba verbal nouns (gamarṭleba "making right/justifying," gasc̣oreba "correcting," gaq̇albeba "forging"). The preverb ga- is most frequent in this set; da- also appears (dazusteba, dadastureba). These forms are lexicalized rather than generated by a transparent causative rule.

The agent is marked in the ergative case (man, sasamart̕lom) in the aorist series, following standard Georgian transitive morphosyntax. No productive causative form is attested for č̣ešmariti or sarwmuno.

No productive morphological causative construction specific to truth-domain predicates is attested in the data. The verbal derivations documented (gaq̇albeba "to falsify," gasc̣oreba "to correct") involve preverb + root + verbal noun morphology and encode the causing of a false or correct state, but these are lexical formations rather than a synchronically productive causative pattern applied to truth-domain adjectives as a class.

## 6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION

### Pattern
Two productive patterns:
(a) si-...-e circumfix on root: simartle, siq̇albe, sicrue, sizuste, sisc̣ore
(b) Adjective + -eba suffix: č̣ešmariteba, namdviloba, gulc̣rpelobaˈ, marṭebuloba, avtentiuroba

Three patterns are attested:
(a) სი-...-ე / si-...-e circumfix on adjectival stem
(b) Adjective/participle + -ობა / -oba suffix
(c) Adjective/participle + -ება / -eba suffix

All three patterns are productive in the truth domain. Pattern (a) produces the most semantically central abstract nouns (simartle, siq̇albe, sizuste, sicrue). Pattern (b) produces property nouns from adjectives (namdviloba, sandooba, legitimuroba, gulwrp̕eloba). Pattern (c) produces verbal/event nouns (č̣ešmariteba, gaq̇albeba, gamartleba, šesabamisoba).

### Examples
- სიმართლე / simartle / "truth; correctness" (si- + marṭ- + -e)
- ჭეშმარიტება / č̣ešmariteba / "truth; truthfulness; veracity" (č̣ešmariti + -eba)
- ნამდვილობა / namdviloba / "authenticity, genuineness" (from namdvili + -oba)
- სიმართლე → simartle → "truth, correctness" (from mart- root)
- ჭეშმარიტება → č̣ešmariteba → "truth, truthfulness, genuineness" (from č̣ešmariti)

### Notes
The circumfix si-...-e operates on bare roots and yields abstract quality nouns; it is the older native pattern. The suffix -eba attaches to adjectival bases and is more productive with borrowed and compound adjectives. Both patterns are well-attested in this domain. The suffix -oba (namdviloba, gulc̣rpelobaˈ, avtentiuroba) is a third nominalizing strategy, typically yielding nouns denoting a state or quality associated with the adjective base.

The si-...-e circumfix and the -oba suffix are not interchangeable: si-...-e forms tend to name the abstract quality as a domain or substance (truth, falsehood, precision), while -oba forms name the property as possessed by a specific entity (the authenticity of this object, the trustworthiness of this person). The -eba suffix produces forms that can be either abstract nouns or verbal nouns depending on context (č̣ešmariteba is purely nominal; gaq̇albeba is both "the act of falsifying" and "falsification as a category").

Within the truth domain, **si-...-e/le** nominals (simartle, siq̇albe, sizuste) tend toward colloquial and everyday use, while **-eba** nominals (č̣ešmariteba, k'anoniereba) tend toward formal, philosophical, or legal registers. Both patterns produce nominals that serve as subjects and objects of predicates and as bases for genitive constructions.

## 7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION

### Pattern
(a) [Noun-GEN] + moq̇vare / madziebeliˈ — "lover/seeker of [noun]": genitive + agentive noun
(b) [Verb root] + -veli / -eli suffix: mimbadzveliˈ, tvaltmaqci
(c) თ-/მ- prefix + verbal stem + agentive suffix (-ელი / -eli, -ი / -i)
(d) Compound: noun + agentive element
(e) Root + agentive suffix **-ari**: **mt'q̇uari** ("liar")
(f) Preverb **ga-** + agentive prefix **ma-** + root + suffix **-ebeli**: **gamaq̇albebeli** ("forger, falsifier")

Attested agentive nominals in this domain:
- თვალთმაქცი / t̕valt̕maq̇c̕i — "deceiver, hypocrite, charlatan" (compound: eye + deceiver)
- ფსევდომეცნიერი / psevdomecnieri — "pseudo-scientist" (prefix + agentive noun)
- მართლმადიდებელი / martlmadidebeli — "Orthodox Christian; right-glorifier" (compound agentive)
- მტყუანი / mt̕q̇uani — "liar" (m- prefix + ẗq̇uan- root + -i)

### Examples
- სიმართლის მაძიებელი / simarṭlis madziebeliˈ / "seeker of truth; truth-seeker"
- მიმბაძველი / mimbadzveliˈ / "imitator; one who copies"
- ის მტყუანია / is mt̕q̇uania / "He is a liar"
- თვალთმაქცი პოლიტიკოსი / t̕valt̕maq̇c̕i p̕olit̕ikosi / "a hypocritical / charlatan politician"
- მტყუარი → mt'q̇uari → "liar, untruthful person"
- გამაყალბებელი → gamaq̇albebeli → "forger, falsifier, counterfeiter"

### Notes
Georgian has no single agentive suffix dedicated to truth-domain roles. "Truth-teller" and "liar" as single-word agentive nouns are not prominently attested in the P1–P3 data; the documented expressions use genitive + agentive noun compounds (simarṭlis moq̇vare, simarṭlis madziebeliˈ) or the verbal noun construction (simarṭlis tqma, sicruis tqma) rather than a single derived agentive form. The agentive suffix -veli is attested in mimbadzveliˈ; the compound tvaltmaqci functions as an agentive for "deceiver/hypocrite."

A dedicated agentive nominal for "truth-teller" (parallel to "liar") is not a common lexicalized form in Georgian; the concept is typically expressed through the adjective martali ("honest, truthful person") used nominally, or through verbal constructions (simartle ambobs, "tells the truth"). The agentive -eli suffix is productive in the domain of deception (t̕valt̕maq̇c̕i, martlmadidebeli) but not for truth-telling roles.

**mt'q̇uari** is the primary agentive nominal for a person who lies, derived directly from the lying root with the **-ari** suffix. **gamaq̇albebeli** is derived from the verbal stem via the **ma-...-ebeli** agentive participial pattern, which is productive for forming agent nouns from prefixed verbs. No parallel agentive nominal for "truth-teller" is attested as a single lexicalized form; the concept is expressed periphrastically (e.g., **simartlis mtqveli**, "one who tells the truth").

## 8. PREDICATIVE TRUTH-ASSERTION

### Pattern
[simarṭle / č̣ešmariteba] + [copula -a / itqva / isaa rom] — truth-noun as subject of copular or passive construction; or [simarṭle] + [tkva / vambob / gitxra] — truth-noun as object of speech verb.

### Examples
- სიმართლე ისაა, რომ / simarṭle isaa, rom / "the truth is that…" (framing construction)
- სიმართლეს ვამბობ / simarṭles vambob / "I am telling the truth" (first-person assertion)

### Notes
This pattern is highly productive in Georgian and has no single-word equivalent. The truth noun (simarṭle or č̣ešmariteba) appears as subject or object in a clause whose main verb is a speech verb (tkva, ambobs, axseneba) or the copula. The dative object construction (simarṭles vambob, sicrues ver etq̇vi) is structurally distinctive: the truth/lie noun takes dative case as the object of the speech verb. These constructions function as the primary everyday mechanism for asserting or denying one's own truthfulness.

## 9. OSTENSIBLE / APPARENT PREDICATION

### Pattern
[močvenebiti / piqtiuri] + [Noun] + [aqvs / aris] — adjective in attributive or predicative position asserting that a property is apparent rather than real; or [piqtiurad / q̇albad] + [passive verb] — adverbial form with passive verb asserting nominal or fictitious status.

### Examples
- მოჩვენებითი ხასიათი აქვს / močvenebiti xasiati aqvs / "it has an ostensible/illusory character; it is merely apparent in nature"
- ფიქტიურად ირიცხება / piqtiurad iricxeba / "is fictitiously registered; is nominally enrolled"

### Notes
This pattern is specific to legal and analytical discourse and expresses the distinction between appearance and reality. The adjectival form (močvenebiti, piqtiuri) in attributive position asserts that a property is surface-level only; the adverbial form (piqtiurad, q̇albad) with a passive verb asserts that an action or status is nominal rather than genuine. Both constructions are documented primarily in legal registers.

## 10. DISCOURSE-FRAMING CONSTRUCTIONS

### Pattern
Nominal phrase in nominative + discourse particle or subjunctive verb form, functioning as a sentence-initial or parenthetical frame.

This pattern is specific to simartle and has no parallel with č̣ešmariteba or other truth-domain nouns. It marks the speaker's positioning as honest before delivering a potentially unwelcome or frank statement.

### Examples
- სიმართლე რომ ვთქვათ / simartle rom vt̕q̇vat̕ / "to tell the truth, frankly speaking" (parenthetical)
- სიმართლე ისაა, რომ... / simartle isaa, rom... / "the truth is that..., the fact is that..."

### Notes
These constructions function as epistemic or evidential frames rather than simple predications. სიმართლე რომ ვთქვათ uses the first-person plural subjunctive (vt̕q̇vat̕, "let us say") to create a deliberative, inclusive framing. სიმართლე ისაა, რომ... uses the demonstrative copula (isaa, "it is that") to introduce a corrective or clarifying assertion. Both are highly frequent in spoken and written Georgian and have no close structural parallel in the falseness domain.

## 11. CONCESSIVE CONSTRUCTION

### Pattern
მართალია, მაგრამ... / martalia, magram... — "it is true that..., but..."

Predicate adjective + copula clitic (-ia, third person singular) + adversative conjunction მაგრამ (magram, "but")

### Examples
- მართალია, მაგრამ საკმარისი არ არის / martalia, magram sakmaris ar aris / "It is true, but it is not sufficient"
- მართალია, ძნელია, მაგრამ შესაძლებელია / martalia, ʒnelia, magram šesaʒlebelia / "True, it is difficult, but it is possible"

### Notes
This is a highly lexicalized concessive formula in Georgian, functioning similarly to English "granted, but..." or "admittedly, but...". The adjective martali here predicates truth of an implicit proposition (what the interlocutor has just said), not of a named subject. The construction is specific to martali; č̣ešmariteba and simartle do not participate in this formula.

## 12. PRIVATIVE / NEGATED PROPERTY FORMATION

### Pattern
უ- (u-, privative prefix) + stem + -ი / -ო / -ობა

This is a morphological pattern rather than a syntactic construction, but it is sufficiently systematic in the truth domain to warrant documentation as a structural pattern. It produces adjectives and nouns marking the absence of a truth-domain property.

### Examples
- უტყუარი / uẗq̇uari / "infallible, beyond doubt" (lit. "without lie")
- უსამართლო / usamart̕lo / "unjust, unfair" (lit. "without justice")

### Notes
The privative u- prefix is productive across the truth domain: uẗq̇uari (without lie → infallible), usamart̕lo (without justice → unjust), ukanono (without law → unlawful), umartloba (without rightness → injustice), uzustoba (without precision → inaccuracy), arasando (not trustworthy, using the ara- negating prefix as an alternative strategy). The u- and ara- prefixes are both available for negation but are not freely interchangeable: u- tends to produce lexicalized adjectives with specific meanings, while ara- produces more compositional negations.

## 13. GENITIVE MODIFICATION

### Pattern
Truth-domain nominal in the genitive case (**-is** / **-s** suffix) + deverbal or relational nominal head, forming a noun phrase denoting an action, process, or property related to truth, falsity, or verification.

### Examples
- მტკიცების ტვირთი → mt'k'icebis t'virti → "burden of proof"
- სიმართლის დადგენა → simartlis dadgena → "establishing the truth / determination of the truth"

### Notes
This is the most productive multi-word construction pattern in the truth domain. The genitive nominal specifies the truth-related content (truth, falsity, proof, authenticity) while the head nominal specifies the action or relation (burden, determination, search, criterion, confirmation, violation). The pattern generates the majority of the fixed legal and philosophical collocations documented in P1b and P2b.

## 14. RESULTATIVE PARTICIPIAL ATTRIBUTION

### Pattern
Preverb + root + **-ebul-** (resultative participial infix) + **-i** adjectival suffix, producing an adjective meaning "that which has been [verb]ed," used attributively or predicatively.

### Examples
- დადასტურებული → dadasturebuli → "confirmed, verified, attested"
- გაყალბებული → gaq̇albebuli → "falsified, forged, counterfeited"

### Notes
The **-ebul-** resultative participial pattern is productive across the verbal system and generates attributive adjectives encoding the completed state resulting from an action. In the truth domain it is attested for verification (dadasturebuli), proof (damt'k'icebuli), falsification (gaq̇albebuli), and cross-checking (gadamomc̣mebuli). These forms appear primarily in legal and administrative attributive contexts.
