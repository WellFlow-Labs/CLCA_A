# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P5 Oppositions
# Language: Basque (eu)

---

## Preliminary Note on Method

Oppositions below are extracted exclusively from patterns documented in P1–P4. Each opposition is supported by explicit evidence: items defined in terms of each other, items appearing in fixed binary constructions, items whose derivational families mirror each other, or items whose syntactic environments are explicitly contrasted in the data. No opposition is inferred from semantic intuition alone. Where the data supports only a partial or asymmetric contrast, this is stated.

---

## Opposition 1

**Term A:** *egia*
**Term B:** *gezurra*
**Transliteration:** N/A
**English translation:** truth / lie, falsehood
**Description of contrast:** These two nominals are explicitly defined as forming a semantic opposition in P1 (items 1 and 4), appear together in the fixed binary construction *gezurra eta egia nahastu* (P2b 4.7), and have parallel derivational families (*egiazkoa*/*gezurrezkoa*; *egiatasuna*/*gezurtasuna*; *egia esan*/*gezurra esan*; *egia da*/*gezurra da*) that mirror each other systematically across P1b, P2a, and P2b. The pair constitutes the baseline binary for propositional evaluation documented in P3 Group 1.
**Contrast type:** lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 2

**Term A:** *egia da*
**Term B:** *gezurra da*
**Transliteration:** N/A
**English translation:** it is true / it is false (it is a lie)
**Description of contrast:** These are the two primary predicative formulas for asserting propositional truth and falsity respectively, documented as direct parallels in P1b (1.2 and 1.22) and P2b (1.2 and 4.2). They occupy identical syntactic positions and are explicitly treated as a contrasting pair in P3 Group 1.
**Contrast type:** constructional
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 7

**Term A:** *zuzen ala oker*
**Term B:** (fixed binary; internal contrast between the two poles)
**Transliteration:** N/A
**English translation:** right or wrong; correct or incorrect
**Description of contrast:** This is a fixed binary construction documented in P2b (6.6) that frames *zuzena* and *okerra* as the two poles of a binary evaluative choice. It is used in everyday and philosophical discourse to frame moral and epistemic evaluation as a binary. The construction itself encodes the opposition rather than either term alone.
**Contrast type:** constructional
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 8

**Term A:** *oker nago* / *oker zaude*
**Term B:** (no single fixed positive counterpart construction; the positive is expressed predicatively as *zuzena da* or *zuzen dago*)
**Transliteration:** N/A
**English translation:** I am wrong / you are wrong — vs. — it is correct / he/she is right
**Description of contrast:** The *oker* + *egon* construction (P2b 7.1, 7.2) uses the stative copula *egon* to mark a contingent, reversible state of being mistaken, while the positive correctness claim uses *zuzena da* with the equative copula *da* (*izan*). This is an asymmetry documented in P4a (§4): *egon* marks contingent states, *izan* marks inherent properties. The two constructions are not morphologically parallel despite being semantically contrasting.
**Contrast type:** constructional
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 9

**Term A:** *egiaztagarria*
**Term B:** *egiaztagaitza*

**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 10

**Term A:** *frogagarria*
**Term B:** *frogatugabea* / *frogarik gabe*
**Transliteration:** N/A
**English translation:** provable, demonstrable / unproven, without proof
**Description of contrast:** *frogagarria* (P1b 1.30; P2a 26.2) characterizes a claim as capable of being proven, while *frogatugabea* (P2a 26.6) and *frogarik gabe* (P1b 1.29; P2a 26.3) characterize claims that have not been or cannot be proven. The contrast is between potential provability and actual absence of proof; the two negative forms use different morphological strategies (*-gabe* suffix vs. partitive *-rik* + postposition *gabe*).
**Contrast type:** morphological
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 14

**Term A:** *autentikoa* / *benetakoa*
**Term B:** *faltsua*
**Transliteration:** N/A
**English translation:** authentic, genuine / false, fake, counterfeit
**Description of contrast:** In the domain of object and document authenticity (P3 Group 3), *autentikoa* and *benetakoa* function as the positive poles and *faltsua* as the negative pole. This is documented in P1 (items 3, 14, 15) and P3 Group 3, where *faltsua* is identified as the primary antonym for object-authenticity. The contrast is asymmetric: two positive terms contrast with one negative term, and the two positive terms differ in register (*autentikoa* more formal, *benetakoa* more everyday).
**Contrast type:** lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 20

**Term A:** *itxuraz*
**Term B:** *egiazki* / *benetako*
**Transliteration:** N/A
**English translation:** apparently, seemingly / truly, genuinely; real, genuine
**Description of contrast:** *itxuraz* (P1 item 27) marks a claim as based on appearance rather than established fact, while *egiazki* (P1b 1.20) and *benetako* (P2a 3.2) assert that something is genuinely or truly the case. This contrast is the basis of the appearance-versus-reality axis documented in P3 Groups 8 and 9. The contrast is not a fixed binary pair but an opposition between the appearance-marking and reality-asserting poles of the same evaluative dimension.
**Contrast type:** lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 21

**Term A:** *itxuraz* (adverbial hedge)
**Term B:** *ustez* (adverbial hedge)
**Transliteration:** N/A
**English translation:** apparently, seemingly (based on appearance) / supposedly, allegedly (based on belief or supposition)
**Description of contrast:** Both are instrumental *-z* adverbials functioning as epistemic hedges (P4a §10; P3 Group 8), but they differ in the basis of the hedge: *itxuraz* hedges based on observable appearance, *ustez* hedges based on belief or supposition. This distinction is explicitly documented in P1 (items 27 and 28) and P3 Group 8. They are not interchangeable despite occupying the same syntactic position.
**Contrast type:** lexical
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 22

**Term A:** *itxuraz* (adverbial)
**Term B:** *itxurazko* (adnominal)
**Transliteration:** N/A
**English translation:** apparently (sentence modifier) / apparent, seeming (noun modifier)
**Description of contrast:** These are two forms derived from the same base *itxura* by the same instrumental *-z* suffix, but they differ in syntactic function: *itxuraz* is a sentence-level adverbial hedge, while *itxurazko* is an adnominal modifier preceding a head noun (e.g., *itxurazko adostasuna*, "apparent consensus"). This morphological alternation is documented in P4a (§3 and §10) and P4b.
**Contrast type:** constructional
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 23

**Term A:** *ustez* (adverbial)
**Term B:** *ustezko* (adnominal)

The same morphological alternation applies: the adverbial form modifies the sentence, the adnominal form precedes a head noun (*ustezko erruduna*, "the alleged culprit").

## Opposition 29

**Term A:** *-tasun* abstract nominal (e.g., *egiazkotasuna*, "truthfulness")
**Term B:** *-keria* pejorative nominal (e.g., *ustelkeria*, "corruption"; *itxurakeria*, "pretense")
**Transliteration:** N/A
**English translation:** neutral abstract property nominal / pejorative conduct nominal
**Description of contrast:** Both *-tasun* and *-keria* are nominalizing suffixes documented in P4a (§6 and §9) and P4b, but they differ systematically in evaluative force: *-tasun* yields neutral abstract property nominals, while *-keria* yields pejorative nominals implying a pattern of blameworthy conduct. This contrast is explicitly stated in P4a §9 and is consistent across all attested instances in the domain.
**Contrast type:** morphological
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 30

**Term A:** *-tze* process nominal (e.g., *egiaztatze*, "the process of verifying")
**Term B:** *-pen* result nominal (e.g., *egiaztapen*, "verification as result/document")
**Transliteration:** N/A
**English translation:** the ongoing process of verifying / the result or document of verification
**Description of contrast:** Both suffixes nominalize the same verbal stem *egiazta-* but yield nominals with different aspectual and functional profiles: *-tze* foregrounds the ongoing process, *-pen* foregrounds the result or product. This contrast is explicitly documented in P4a (§6 and §8) and P4b, where the three nominalization types (*-tze*, *-pen*, *-keta*) are stated to be non-interchangeable.
**Contrast type:** morphological
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 31

**Term A:** *-garri* potential/worthy suffix (e.g., *fidagarria*, "worthy of trust"; *sinesgarria*, "worthy of belief")
**Term B:** *-gaitz* difficult/impossible suffix (e.g., *egiaztagaitza*, "unverifiable")
**Transliteration:** N/A
**English translation:** worthy of / capable of X — vs. — difficult / impossible to X
**Description of contrast:** These two suffixes attach to the same verbal stems and yield contrasting evaluations of possibility or worthiness. The *-garri*/*-gaitz* contrast is explicitly documented in P1b (1.9 and 1.10) for the *egiazta-* stem. The *-garri* suffix is more widely attested in this domain (*fidagarria*, *sinesgarria*, *frogagarria*, *egiaztagarria*); *-gaitz* is attested only in *egiaztagaitza* in the documented data.
**Contrast type:** morphological
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 32

**Term A:** *egon* (stative copula) in *oker nago* ("I am wrong/mistaken")
**Term B:** *izan* (equative copula) in *zuzena da* ("it is correct")
**Transliteration:** N/A
**English translation:** to be (contingently, statively) wrong / to be (inherently, equatively) correct
**Description of contrast:** The use of *egon* vs. *izan* in truth-domain predications is documented in P4a (§4) and P4b as a systematic constructional contrast: *egon* marks contingent, reversible states (being mistaken is a state one can exit), while *izan* marks inherent or definitional properties. This asymmetry is noted in P1 (item 7) and P3 Group 5. The contrast is not lexical but grammatical, encoded in the choice of copula.
**Contrast type:** constructional
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## Opposition 40

## Opposition 41
