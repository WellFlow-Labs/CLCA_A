# CLCA Protocol v3.4 — P4a CONSTRUCTIONS
# Language: Chinese (zh)

---

## 1. ATTRIBUTION

### Pattern
**Subject + 是 (shì) + Truth-domain predicate**

The copula 是 (shì) "be" links subjects to truth-domain predicates. This is the basic attributive construction in Chinese.

### Examples
- 这个说法是真的 (zhè ge shuō fǎ shì zhēn de)
  - zhè ge shuō fǎ shì zhēn de
  - "This statement is true"

- 他的话是假的 (tā de huà shì jiǎ de)
  - tā de huà shì jiǎ de
  - "His words are false"

### Notes
The particle 的 (de) typically follows the predicate adjective in predicative position. Without 是, bare adjectives can also function predicatively (e.g., 这个说法真 "this statement [is] true"), but 是 + ADJ + 的 is more emphatic and common in full sentences.

---

## 2. NEGATIVE ATTRIBUTION

### Pattern
**Subject + 不是 (bù shì) + Truth-domain predicate**

Negation of attribution uses 不是 (bù shì) "not be" before the predicate.

### Examples
- 这不是真的 (zhè bù shì zhēn de)
  - zhè bù shì zhēn de
  - "This is not true"

- 这个消息不是事实 (zhè ge xiāo xi bù shì shì shí)
  - zhè ge xiāo xi bù shì shì shí
  - "This news is not factual"

### Notes
Alternative negation pattern uses 不 (bù) directly before adjectives: 不真 (bù zhēn) "not true", 不对 (bù duì) "not correct". The 不是 pattern is more emphatic and categorical.

---

## 3. ADNOMINAL MODIFICATION

### Pattern
**Truth-domain modifier + 的 (de) + Noun**

The particle 的 (de) links modifying adjectives to head nouns.

### Examples
- 真实的情况 (zhēn shí de qíng kuàng)
  - zhēn shí de qíng kuàng
  - "the true/actual situation"

- 虚假的信息 (xū jiǎ de xìn xī)
  - xū jiǎ de xìn xī
  - "false information"

### Notes
Some high-frequency combinations allow 的 deletion: 真相 (zhēn xiàng) "truth" (< 真的相 "true appearance"), 事实 (shì shí) "fact" (< 事的实 "matter's actuality"). Most productive modification requires 的.

---

## 4. BECOME / INCHOATIVE

### Pattern
**Subject + 变 (biàn) / 变得 (biàn de) / 成为 (chéng wéi) + Truth-domain predicate**

Change-of-state uses verbs 变 (biàn) "become, change" or 成为 (chéng wéi) "become".

### Examples
- 谎言变成了真相 (huǎng yán biàn chéng le zhēn xiàng)
  - huǎng yán biàn chéng le zhēn xiàng
  - "The lie became the truth"

- 假的变成真的了 (jiǎ de biàn chéng zhēn de le)
  - jiǎ de biàn chéng zhēn de le
  - "The false became true"

### Notes
The construction 弄假成真 (nòng jiǎ chéng zhēn) "make false become true" is a lexicalized idiom. The verb 成 (chéng) "become" can also appear alone: 假戏真做 (jiǎ xì zhēn zuò) "pretense becomes reality" (lit. "false play truly done").

---

## 5. CAUSATIVE

### Pattern
**Agent + 使/让 (shǐ/ràng) + Patient + 变得 (biàn de) + Truth-domain predicate**

Causative constructions use 使 (shǐ) or 让 (ràng) "make, cause" to express causing a change in truth-value.

### Examples
- 证据使这个说法变得可靠 (zhèng jù shǐ zhè ge shuō fǎ biàn de kě kào)
  - zhèng jù shǐ zhè ge shuō fǎ biàn de kě kào
  - "The evidence makes this claim reliable"

- 调查让真相大白 (diào chá ràng zhēn xiàng dà bái)
  - diào chá ràng zhēn xiàng dà bái
  - "The investigation caused the truth to come to light"

### Notes
More common are resultative verb compounds that encode causation: 证实 (zhèng shí) "prove true", 查实 (chá shí) "verify as true", 核实 (hé shí) "confirm as true". These are more idiomatic than periphrastic causatives.

---

## 6. ABSTRACT NOMINALIZATION

### Pattern
**Truth-domain base + 性 (-xìng) / 度 (-dù)**

Suffixes -性 (-xìng) "quality, -ness" and -度 (-dù) "degree" derive abstract nouns.

### Examples
- 真实性 (zhēn shí xìng)
  - zhēn shí xìng
  - "authenticity, truthfulness" (< 真实 "true/real")

- 准确度 (zhǔn què dù)
  - zhǔn què dù
  - "degree of accuracy" (< 准确 "accurate")

### Notes
The -性 suffix is highly productive with truth-domain adjectives: 可靠性 (kě kào xìng) "reliability", 客观性 (kè guān xìng) "objectivity", 主观性 (zhǔ guān xìng) "subjectivity". The -度 suffix emphasizes measurable degree: 可靠度 (kě kào dù) "reliability level", 真实度 (zhēn shí dù) "degree of authenticity".

---

## 7. AGENTIVE NOMINALIZATION

### Pattern
**Verb + 者 (-zhě) "person who"**

The suffix -者 (-zhě) derives agent nouns from verbs.

### Examples
- 欺骗者 (qī piàn zhě)
  - qī piàn zhě
  - "deceiver, liar" (< 欺骗 "deceive")

- 诚实者 (chéng shí zhě)
  - chéng shí zhě
  - "honest person" (< 诚实 "honest")

### Notes
This pattern is productive but formal/literary. More common are compound nouns: 骗子 (piàn zi) "liar, cheater" (骗 "deceive" + 子 diminutive), or descriptive phrases: 说谎的人 (shuō huǎng de rén) "person who lies". The -者 suffix appears more in written/formal registers.

---

## 8. RESULTATIVE COMPLEMENTS

### Pattern
**Verb + Truth-domain complement (indicating result achieved)**

Resultative verb compounds encode the outcome of an action using truth-domain morphemes as complements.

### Examples
- 说对了 (shuō duì le)
  - shuō duì le
  - "said correctly, got it right" (说 "say" + 对 "correct")

- 证实了 (zhèng shí le)
  - zhèng shí le
  - "verified, confirmed" (证 "prove" + 实 "actual")

### Notes
This is an extremely productive pattern in Chinese. Common resultative complements include: 对 (duì) "correct" (说对 "say correctly", 做对 "do correctly"), 错 (cuò) "wrong" (说错 "say wrongly", 看错 "see wrongly"), 实 (shí) "actual" (证实 "verify", 核实 "confirm", 查实 "ascertain", 落实 "implement"). The aspect marker 了 (le) often follows to indicate completion.

---

## 9. POTENTIAL COMPLEMENTS

### Pattern
**Verb + 得/不得 (de/bù de) + Truth-domain complement**

Potential forms indicate whether a result can or cannot be achieved, using 得 (de) for positive potential and 不 (bù) for negative.

### Examples
- 说得对 (shuō de duì)
  - shuō de duì
  - "can say correctly, be right in saying"

- 看不清真相 (kàn bù qīng zhēn xiàng)
  - kàn bù qīng zhēn xiàng
  - "cannot see the truth clearly"

### Notes
The potential infix 得/不 splits the verb from its complement. Common patterns: 说得对/说不对 (shuō de duì / shuō bù duì) "can/cannot say correctly", 做得对/做不对 (zuò de duì / zuò bù duì) "can/cannot do correctly". This construction evaluates possibility of achieving the result.

---

## 10. COMPARATIVE CONSTRUCTIONS

### Pattern
**X + 比 (bǐ) + Y + 更/还 (gèng/hái) + Truth-domain predicate**

Comparison uses 比 (bǐ) "compared to" with degree adverbs 更 (gèng) or 还 (hái) "more, even more".

### Examples
- 这个说法比那个更真实 (zhè ge shuō fǎ bǐ nà ge gèng zhēn shí)
  - zhè ge shuō fǎ bǐ nà ge gèng zhēn shí
  - "This statement is more true/authentic than that one"

- 他比我更诚实 (tā bǐ wǒ gèng chéng shí)
  - tā bǐ wǒ gèng chéng shí
  - "He is more honest than me"

### Notes
Alternative comparative pattern uses 没有 (méi yǒu) "not have" for negative comparison: X 没有 Y (这么/那么) ADJ "X is not as ADJ as Y". Example: 这个没有那个真实 (zhè ge méi yǒu nà ge zhēn shí) "This is not as true as that".

---

## 11. QUESTION FORMATIONS

### Pattern
**Multiple interrogative patterns for truth-value**

Chinese uses several question strategies: A-not-A pattern, question particles, and question words.

### Examples
- 这是真的还是假的？(zhè shì zhēn de hái shì jiǎ de?)
  - zhè shì zhēn de hái shì jiǎ de
  - "Is this true or false?" (alternative question)

- 对不对？(duì bù duì?)
  - duì bù duì
  - "Right or not? Correct?" (A-not-A pattern)

### Notes
Common question patterns include: (1) A-not-A: 对不对 (duì bù duì) "correct or not", 是不是真的 (shì bù shì zhēn de) "is or isn't true"; (2) Question particle 吗 (ma): 真的吗 (zhēn de ma) "really?"; (3) Alternative questions with 还是 (hái shì) "or": 真的还是假的 (zhēn de hái shì jiǎ de) "true or false".

---

## 12. ADVERBIAL MODIFICATION

### Pattern
**Truth-domain adverb + 地 (de) + Verb**

Adverbs derived from truth-domain adjectives use the particle 地 (de) before verbs.

### Examples
- 真诚地道歉 (zhēn chéng de dào qiàn)
  - zhēn chéng de dào qiàn
  - "sincerely apologize"

- 客观地评价 (kè guān de píng jià)
  - kè guān de píng jià
  - "objectively evaluate"

### Notes
The particle 地 (de) marks adverbial function. Common adverbial forms: 真实地 (zhēn shí de) "truly", 诚实地 (chéng shí de) "honestly", 实际地 (shí jì de) "actually", 客观地 (kè guān de) "objectively", 主观地 (zhǔ guān de) "subjectively". Some high-frequency adverbs can omit 地: 确实 (què shí) "indeed", 的确 (dí què) "certainly".

---

## 13. REDUPLICATION PATTERNS

### Pattern
**AABB reduplication for intensification**

Full reduplication of two-syllable adjectives (AABB pattern) intensifies meaning and adds vividness.

### Examples
- 真真实实 (zhēn zhēn shí shí)
  - zhēn zhēn shí shí
  - "very real, absolutely true" (< 真实 "real/true")

- 老老实实 (lǎo lǎo shí shí)
  - lǎo lǎo shí shí
  - "very honest, obediently" (< 老实 "honest")

### Notes
AABB reduplication is productive with certain truth-domain adjectives: 实实在在 (shí shí zài zài) "very real", 认认真真 (rèn rèn zhēn zhēn) "very seriously", 踏踏实实 (tà tà shí shí) "very steady". This pattern adds emphasis and often colloquial flavor. Not all adjectives allow this reduplication.

---

## 14. DISCOURSE MARKERS

### Pattern
**Truth-domain expression + (的话) functioning as discourse frame**

Certain truth-domain expressions have grammaticalized into discourse markers that frame utterances.

### Examples
- 说实话 (shuō shí huà)
  - shuō shí huà
  - "to tell the truth, honestly speaking" (frames honest statement)

- 实际上 (shí jì shàng)
  - shí jì shàng
  - "actually, in fact" (introduces actual situation)

### Notes
Common discourse markers: 说真的 (shuō zhēn de) "to tell the truth", 老实说 (lǎo shí shuō) "honestly speaking", 事实上 (shì shí shàng) "in fact", 实际上 (shí jì shàng) "actually". These function pragmatically to signal speaker stance or frame the following utterance, often with reduced truth-evaluative force.

---

## 15. EVIDENTIAL CONSTRUCTIONS

### Pattern
**Verification verb + Truth-domain object**

Verbs of verification, investigation, or confirmation take truth-domain objects.

### Examples
- 证实事实 (zhèng shí shì shí)
  - zhèng shí shì shí
  - "verify the facts, confirm the truth"

- 查明真相 (chá míng zhēn xiàng)
  - chá míng zhēn xiàng
  - "ascertain the truth, investigate the facts"

### Notes
Common verification verbs include: 证实 (zhèng shí) "verify", 核实 (hé shí) "confirm", 查实 (chá shí) "ascertain", 查明 (chá míng) "investigate and clarify", 揭露 (jiē lù) "reveal, expose". These combine with truth-domain nouns like 真相 (zhēn xiàng) "truth", 事实 (shì shí) "facts", 实情 (shí qíng) "actual situation".

---

## 16. CONFORMITY CONSTRUCTIONS

### Pattern
**Subject + 符合/不符合 (fú hé / bù fú hé) + Truth-standard**

The verb 符合 (fú hé) "conform to, accord with" expresses correspondence between claims and reality.

### Examples
- 符合事实 (fú hé shì shí)
  - fú hé shì shí
  - "conform to facts, accord with reality"

- 与事实不符 (yǔ shì shí bù fú)
  - yǔ shì shí bù fú
  - "not correspond with facts, contradict reality"

### Notes
This construction is formal and common in official, legal, and journalistic contexts. The pattern 与 X 相符/不符 (yǔ X xiāng fú / bù fú) "correspond/not correspond with X" is an alternative structure. Common objects: 事实 (shì shí) "facts", 实际 (shí jì) "reality", 真相 (zhēn xiàng) "truth".

---

## 17. INTENSIFICATION WITH FIXED EXPRESSIONS

### Pattern
**Hyperbolic or metaphorical fixed expressions emphasizing truth-value**

Idiomatic expressions use hyperbole or metaphor to intensify truth claims.

### Examples
- 千真万确 (qiān zhēn wàn què)
  - qiān zhēn wàn què
  - "absolutely true, completely certain" (lit. "thousand-true ten-thousand-certain")

- 铁一般的事实 (tiě yī bān de shì shí)
  - tiě yī bān de shì shí
  - "iron-clad facts" (lit. "iron-like facts")

### Notes
These expressions are conventionalized intensifiers. Other examples: 不容置疑 (bù róng zhì yí) "beyond doubt", 毫无疑问 (háo wú yí wèn) "without any doubt". They function rhetorically to strengthen assertions and remove uncertainty.

---

## 18. NEGATION OF EVIDENCE/BASIS

### Pattern
**没有/毫无 (méi yǒu / háo wú) + 根据/证据 (gēn jù / zhèng jù)**

Negation of evidential basis uses 没有 (méi yǒu) "not have" or 毫无 (háo wú) "completely without".

### Examples
- 没有根据 (méi yǒu gēn jù)
  - méi yǒu gēn jù
  - "groundless, without basis, unfounded"

- 毫无证据 (háo wú zhèng jù)
  - háo wú zhèng jù
  - "completely without evidence"

### Notes
This construction criticizes claims lacking factual support. Common objects: 根据 (gēn jù) "basis, grounds", 证据 (zhèng jù) "evidence", 事实根据 (shì shí gēn jù) "factual basis". The emphatic 毫无 (háo wú) "not the slightest bit" strengthens the negation.
