## SUMMARY OBSERVATIONS

### High-Productivity Patterns
- **Attribution** (Construction 1): Unmarked, core predication strategy
- **Negative Attribution** (Construction 2): Standard negation with "tidak"
- **Adnominal Modification** (Construction 3): Highly productive prenominal pattern
- **Abstract Nominalization** (Construction 6): Nearly universal ke-...-an pattern
- **Passive Voice** (Construction 12): Preferred over active in many contexts

### Restricted Patterns
- **Agentive Nominalization** (Construction 7): Productive primarily for negative/deceptive agents; positive agents use alternative strategies
- **Evidential/Discovery** (Construction 10): "Ternyata" fully grammaticalized; other ter- forms more restricted
- **Reciprocal/Collective** (Construction 15): Limited to specific lexicalized items, not productive

### Grammaticalized Functions
- **Discourse Markers** (Construction 14): Truth-terms like "jujur saja," "sebenarnya" have shifted to epistemic/pragmatic functions
- **Intensification** (Construction 8): Reduplication (benar-benar) creates general intensifiers beyond truth-evaluation

### Register Variation
- Formal written Indonesian shows higher frequency of:
  - Copula "adalah" in attribution
  - Agent phrases with "oleh" in passives
  - Ke-...-an abstract nominalizations
- Colloquial speech prefers:
  - Zero copula
  - Agentless passives
  - "Banget" over "sekali" for intensification
