## Summary Observations

1. **All eleven constructions rated [CORE]** with the single exception of the *-sal/-sel* adnominal modifier, which is rated [MARGINAL] due to its restriction to technical registers. No construction in P4a is rated [UNCERTAIN].

2. **The *-sal/-sel* construction** (§3, *doğrusal*) is the only construction showing clear register restriction; it does not participate in general truth-domain discourse.

3. **Lexicalization varies within constructions**: within a single productive pattern (e.g., *-laş-*, *-lık/-lik*, *hakkını* + verb), individual derivatives range from fully lexicalized fixed items (*gerçekleşmek*, *yalancı*, *hakkını yemek*) to more compositionally transparent forms.

4. **The Predicate Outcome Construction** (§9) and the **Concessive Partial-Truth Construction** (§10) show the strongest semantic restrictions: the set of predicates entering §9 appears limited to truth-evaluation adjectives, and the nominal phrase *doğruluk payı* in §10 is not substitutable with *hakikat payı* or *gerçek payı* based on P4a data.

5. **The Rights construction** (§11) shows a closed verb set (*vermek*, *aramak*, *yemek*, *gözetmek*) with obligatory possessive morphology; this distinguishes it structurally from the other constructions, which are more open.
