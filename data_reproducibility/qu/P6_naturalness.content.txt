# CLCA Protocol v3.3 — P6 NATURALNESS (Change, Causation, and Stability)
# Language: Quechua (qu)

---

## 1. BECOMING TRUE / VERIFIED

### Pattern
**[SUBJECT] + chiqaqyay** or **[SUBJECT] + chiqaq-man kutiy**
Inchoative construction using -ya- suffix or directional "return to truth"

### Examples
- **kayqa chiqaqyapun** → kayqa chiqaq-ya-pu-n → "this becomes true, this turns out to be true"
- **willakuyniyki chiqaqyamun** → willakuy-ni-yki chiqaq-ya-mu-n → "your report becomes verified, your story turns out true"

### Notes
The inchoative *chiqaqyay* (chiqaq-ya-y) is the standard expression for something becoming true or being verified. The directional suffix -pu- "away" or -mu- "toward speaker" can be added for aspectual nuance. An alternate construction uses *chiqaqman kutiy* "return to truth" for restoration of truth. This is a [CORE] natural expression.

---

## 2. BECOMING FALSE / TURNING OUT FALSE

### Pattern
**[SUBJECT] + llulla kay / llulla tukuy**
Stative "be false" or "become/turn into false" using *tukuy* "to become, to turn into"

### Examples
- **willakuyqa llullan** → willakuy-qa llulla-n → "the report is false" (stative)
- **chiqaq nisqanqa llulla tukupun** → chiqaq ni-sqa-n-qa llulla tuku-pu-n → "what was said to be true turned out false"

### Notes
There is NO dedicated inchoative *llullayay for "become false" attested in the data. The concept is expressed either statively (*llulla kay* "be false") or using the general change-of-state verb *tukuy* "become, turn into" with *llulla* as complement. The construction *llulla tukuy* "turn into a lie, turn out false" is used when something previously thought true is discovered to be false. This asymmetry (chiqaqyay exists, *llullayay does not) is notable. Status: [CORE] for stative, [MARGINAL] for *llulla tukuy*.

---

## 3. BECOMING GOOD / IMPROVING

### Pattern
**[SUBJECT] + allinyay**
Inchoative construction with -ya- suffix

### Examples
- **unqusqaqa allinyapun** → unqu-sqa-qa allin-ya-pu-n → "the sick one gets better, the patient improves"
- **ruwaynikiqa allinyamuchkan** → ruway-ni-ki-qa allin-ya-mu-chka-n → "your work is improving, your deed is getting better"

### Notes
The inchoative *allinyay* (allin-ya-y) is extremely common and natural for expressing improvement, getting better, or healing. It applies to health (*unqusqa allinyay* "sick person gets better"), conduct (*ruway allinyay* "deed improves"), and general states. The progressive -chka- and directional suffixes (-pu-, -mu-) combine freely. This is a [CORE] highly frequent expression.

---

## 4. BECOMING BAD / DETERIORATING

### Pattern
**[SUBJECT] + mana allinyay / [SUBJECT] + mana allin tukuy**
Negated inchoative or "become not-good" with *tukuy*

### Examples
- **kayqa mana allinyapunchu** → kayqa mana allin-ya-pu-n-chu → "this doesn't improve, this doesn't get better"
- **ruwaynin mana allin tukupun** → ruway-ni-n mana allin tuku-pu-n → "his/her deed became bad, his/her action turned bad"

### Notes
There is NO dedicated inchoative for "become bad, worsen, deteriorate" in the data. The concept is expressed either through negated *allinyay* (mana allinyay "not improve") or through *mana allin tukuy* "become not-good, turn bad." The asymmetry with *allinyay* is notable—positive change has dedicated morphology, negative change uses compositional expressions. Status: [CORE] for negated form, [MARGINAL] for *tukuy* construction.

## 5. BECOMING CLEAR / BEING CLARIFIED

### Pattern
**[SUBJECT] + sut'iyay**
Inchoative construction with -ya- suffix

### Examples
- **willakuyqa sut'iyapun** → willakuy-qa sut'i-ya-pu-n → "the report becomes clear, the story is clarified"

### Notes
The inchoative *sut'iyay* (sut'i-ya-y) is the standard natural expression for something becoming clear or evident. It is commonly used when information, situations, or meanings become clarified or revealed. Combines freely with aspect and directional markers. This is a [CORE] natural expression.

## 6. BECOMING OBSCURE / UNCLEAR

## 7. BECOMING MISTAKEN / FALLING INTO ERROR

### Pattern
**[SUBJECT] + pantay / [SUBJECT] + pantaypi urmaykuy**
Simple error verb or "fall into error" construction

### Examples
- **ñuqaqa pantarqani** → ñuqa-qa panta-rqa-ni → "I made a mistake (suddenly), I erred"
- **payqa pantaypi urmaykupun** → pay-qa panta-y-pi urmay-ku-pu-n → "he/she fell into error"

### Notes
The verb *pantay* "to err, to make a mistake" itself expresses the change from correct to mistaken state. There is no separate inchoative *pantayayay. The construction *pantaypi urmaykuy* "fall into error" (lit. "fall-REFL in error-LOC") is a more elaborate expression emphasizing the transition into error. The sudden action suffix -rqo- often appears with *pantay* to mark unexpected error. Status: [CORE] for simple *pantay*, [MARGINAL] for *urmaykuy* construction.

## 8. BECOMING CORRECT / BEING CORRECTED

### Pattern
**[SUBJECT] + allinman kutiy / [SUBJECT] + chiqanman chayay**
"Return to good" or "arrive at correctness" constructions

### Examples
- **pantasqaqa allinman kutirimun** → panta-sqa-qa allin-man kuti-ri-mu-n → "the mistaken one returns to good, the error is corrected"
- **kayqa chiqanman chayapun** → kayqa chiqan-man chaya-pu-n → "this arrives at correctness, this becomes exact"

### Notes
There is NO dedicated inchoative *chiqanyay for "become correct." The concept is expressed through directional constructions: *allinman kutiy* "return to good" (using allative case + motion verb) or *chiqanman chayay* "arrive at correctness." These are compositional rather than lexicalized. Status: [CORE] for directional constructions, [NO COMMON EXPRESSION] for dedicated inchoative.

## 19. CAUSING TO BE TRUE / VERIFYING

### Pattern
**[AGENT] + [OBJECT] + chiqanchay**
Causative construction with -ncha- (causative variant)

### Examples
- **payqa willakuyta chiqanchan** → pay-qa willakuy-ta chiqan-cha-n → "he/she verifies the report, he/she authenticates the story"
- **ñuqaykuqa kayta chiqanchanchik** → ñuqayku-qa kay-ta chiqan-cha-nchik → "we verify this, we authenticate this"

### Notes
The causative *chiqanchay* (chiqaq-ncha-y, with epenthetic -n-) is the standard expression for causing something to be true, i.e., verifying or authenticating. This is a highly productive and natural causative used in administrative, legal, and everyday contexts for verification. This is a [CORE] natural expression.

## 20. CAUSING TO BE FALSE / FALSIFYING

### Pattern
**[AGENT] + [OBJECT] + llulla ruway / llullaman tukuchiy**
"Make false" using general verb or "cause to become false"

### Examples
- **payqa willakuyta llulla ruwan** → pay-qa willakuy-ta llulla ruwa-n → "he/she makes the report false, he/she falsifies the story"
- **llullaman tukuchipun** → llulla-man tuku-chi-pu-n → "he/she caused it to become false, he/she falsified it"

### Notes
There is NO dedicated causative *llullachay for "cause to be false, falsify." The concept is expressed compositionally through *llulla ruway* "make false" (using general verb *ruway* "do, make") or *llullaman tukuchiy* "cause to become false" (using causative of *tukuy* "become"). This asymmetry with *chiqanchay* is notable. Status: [MARGINAL] for both constructions.

## 21. CAUSING TO IMPROVE / MAKING GOOD

### Pattern
**[AGENT] + [OBJECT] + allinyachiy**
Causative construction with -ya-chi- (inchoative + causative)

### Examples
- **hampiqqa unqusqata allinyachin** → hampi-q-qa unqu-sqa-ta allin-ya-chi-n → "the healer makes the sick one better, the doctor heals the patient"
- **ñuqaqa ruwayta allinyachini** → ñuqa-qa ruwa-y-ta allin-ya-chi-ni → "I improve the work, I fix the deed"

### Notes
The causative *allinyachiy* (allin-ya-chi-y) is extremely common and natural for causing improvement, fixing, healing, or making good. It stacks inchoative -ya- with causative -chi- to create "cause to become good" meaning. This is a [CORE] highly frequent expression used across medical, technical, and moral domains.

## 37. REMAINING TRUE / STAYING TRUE

### Pattern
**[SUBJECT] + chiqaq-raq kay / chiqaq-puni kay**
"Still true" or "certainly true" with continuative/emphatic suffixes

### Examples
- **kayqa chiqaqraqmi** → kayqa chiqaq-raq-mi → "this is still true, this remains true"
- **willakuyniyki chiqaqpunim** → willakuy-ni-yki chiqaq-puni-mi → "your report is certainly true, your story remains definitely true"

### Notes
Stability or continuation of truth is expressed through the continuative suffix -raq "still, yet" or emphatic -puni "certainly, definitely" rather than a dedicated "remain true" verb. The construction *chiqaq-raq kay* "be still true" is the standard way to express continued truth. Status: [CORE] for suffix constructions, [NO COMMON EXPRESSION] for dedicated stability verb.

## 38. REMAINING FALSE / STAYING FALSE

### Pattern
**[SUBJECT] + llulla-raq kay / llulla-puni kay**
"Still false" or "certainly false" with continuative/emphatic suffixes

### Examples
- **kayqa llullaraqmi** → kayqa llulla-raq-mi → "this is still false, this remains false"
- **willakuyqa llullapunim** → willakuy-qa llulla-puni-mi → "the report is certainly false, the story remains definitely false"

### Notes
Like truth, stability of falsity is expressed through continuative -raq or emphatic -puni rather than a dedicated verb. The construction *llulla-raq kay* "be still false" is parallel to the truth construction. Status: [CORE] for suffix constructions, [NO COMMON EXPRESSION] for dedicated stability verb.

## 41. PREVENTING FROM BECOMING FALSE / MAINTAINING TRUTH

### Pattern
**[AGENT] + [OBJECT] + chiqaqpi waqaychay / mana llullaman tukukunanpaq**
"Guard in truth" or "so that it not become false"

### Examples
- **chiqaqpi waqaychay munani** → chiqaq-pi waqaycha-y muna-ni → "I want to guard (it) in truth, I want to maintain (it) true"
- **mana llullaman tukukunanpaq** → mana llulla-man tuku-ku-na-n-paq → "so that it not become false, to prevent it from becoming false"

### Notes
There is NO dedicated verb for "prevent from becoming false" or "maintain truth." The concept is expressed compositionally through *waqaychay* "guard, protect" with locative *chiqaqpi* "in truth," or through purpose clauses with negation (*mana...tukukunanpaq* "so that it not become"). Status: [MARGINAL] for both constructions.

## 42. PREVENTING FROM ERRING / KEEPING FROM MISTAKE

### Pattern
**[AGENT] + [OBJECT] + mana pantananpaq / pantaymanta waqaychay**
"So that (one) not err" or "guard from error"

### Examples
- **mana pantananpaq yachachini** → mana panta-na-n-paq yachachi-ni → "I teach so that (one) not err, I instruct to prevent mistakes"
- **pantaymanta waqaychay** → panta-y-manta waqaycha-y → "to guard from error, to protect from mistakes"

### Notes
There is NO dedicated verb for "prevent from erring." The concept is expressed through purpose clauses with negation (*mana pantananpaq* "so that not err") or through *waqaychay* "guard" with ablative *pantaymanta* "from error." Status: [MARGINAL] for both constructions.

## SUMMARY OF PATTERNS

### INCHOATIVE FORMATIONS (Becoming X)

**Highly productive with -ya-:**
- Positive states: chiqaqyay, allinyay, sut'iyay, hunt'ayay, hatunyay, sumaqyay, qhapaqyay
- Negative states: ch'usaqyay, huch'uyyay, millayyay, wakchayay, k'uchiyay
- **Asymmetries:** NO inchoatives for: llullayay (become false), mana allinyay (worsen), sut'i opposite (become obscure), k'uchi opposite (become straight)

**Status:** [CORE] for attested forms, [NO COMMON EXPRESSION] for gaps

---

### CAUSATIVE FORMATIONS (Causing X to be Y)

**Highly productive with -chi-/-cha- (often stacking with -ya-):**
- Verification/improvement: chiqanchay, allinyachiy, sut'iyachiy
- Filling/emptying: hunt'achiy, ch'usaqchay
- Size change: hatunchay, huch'uyachiy
- Aesthetic change: sumaqyachiy, millaychay
- Social change: qhapaqchay, wakchachay
- Physical/moral: k'uchichiy
- Misleading: pantachiy

**Asymmetries:** NO causatives for: llullachay (falsify), mana allinyachiy (make worse), sut'i opposite (obscure), k'uchi opposite (straighten)

**Status:** [CORE] for attested forms, [NO COMMON EXPRESSION] for gaps

---

### STABILITY EXPRESSIONS (Remaining X)

**Pattern:** Continuative -raq "still" or emphatic -puni "certainly" + stative predicate
- No dedicated "remain X" verbs
- Stability expressed through aspectual suffixes on stative predicates
- Highly productive across all evaluative terms

**Status:** [CORE] for suffix strategy, [NO COMMON EXPRESSION] for dedicated verbs

---

### PREVENTION EXPRESSIONS (Preventing from becoming X)

**Pattern:** Purpose clauses with negation or "guard from X" constructions
- No dedicated prevention verbs
- Expressed compositionally through *waqaychay* "guard" + locative/ablative or purpose clauses
- Less grammaticalized than change expressions

**Status:** [MARGINAL] for compositional constructions

---

### KEY ASYMMETRIES IDENTIFIED

1. **Truth domain:** chiqaqyay exists, *llullayay does not; chiqanchay exists, *llullachay does not
2. **Goodness domain:** allinyay exists, *mana allinyay (worsen) does not; allinyachiy exists, *mana allinyachiy does not
3. **Clarity domain:** sut'iyay and sut'iyachiy exist, opposites do not
4. **Straightness domain:** k'uchiyay and k'uchichiy exist, opposites do not
5. **Symmetries:** Full/empty, big/small, beautiful/ugly, rich/poor all show symmetric inchoative and causative formations

**General pattern:** Positive epistemic and moral changes have dedicated morphology; negative changes often lack dedicated forms and use compositional expressions or negation.
