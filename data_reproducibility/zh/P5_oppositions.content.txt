# CLCA Protocol v3.2 — P5 OPPOSITIONS
# Language: Chinese (zh)

## OPPOSITION 1: 真 (zhēn) vs. 假 (jiǎ)

**Term A:** 真 (zhēn)  
**Term B:** 假 (jiǎ)  
**Transliteration:** zhēn vs. jiǎ  
**Translation:** "true, real, genuine" vs. "false, fake, counterfeit"

**Description of contrast:** This is the fundamental lexical opposition in the Chinese truth domain, contrasting genuine/authentic items and true statements with counterfeit/fake items and false statements. The opposition appears across multiple semantic domains: propositional truth (真的说法 "true statement" vs. 假话 "false statement"), material authenticity (真金 "real gold" vs. 假货 "fake goods"), and identity (真人 "real person" vs. 假装 "pretend").

**Contrast type:** Lexical  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## OPPOSITION 2: 对 (duì) vs. 错 (cuò)

**Term A:** 对 (duì)  
**Term B:** 错 (cuò)  
**Transliteration:** duì vs. cuò  
**Translation:** "correct, right" vs. "wrong, mistaken, error"

**Description of contrast:** This opposition evaluates correctness of judgments, answers, decisions, or actions rather than factual correspondence. It appears prominently in educational contexts (right/wrong answers), practical decision-making, and evaluations of whether actions were performed properly, with extensive productivity in resultative compounds (说对/说错 "say correctly/wrongly", 做对/做错 "do correctly/wrongly").

**Contrast type:** Lexical  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## OPPOSITION 3: 实 (shí) vs. 虚 (xū)

**Term A:** 实 (shí)  
**Term B:** 虚 (xū)  
**Transliteration:** shí vs. xū  
**Translation:** "solid, real, actual" vs. "empty, void, false, unreal"

**Description of contrast:** This opposition contrasts substance and actuality with emptiness and lack of substance. It appears in evaluations of whether things have real content versus mere appearance (华而不实 "showy but lacking substance", 徒有虚名 "have an undeserved reputation"), and in the fixed expression 弄虚作假 "practice fraud" which combines both terms to emphasize deceptive falsification.

**Contrast type:** Lexical  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## OPPOSITION 33: Affirmative vs. Negative Attribution Construction

**Construction A:** Subject + 是 (shì) + Truth-domain predicate  
**Construction B:** Subject + 不是 (bù shì) + Truth-domain predicate  
**Example A:** 这个说法是真的 (zhè ge shuō fǎ shì zhēn de) "This statement is true"  
**Example B:** 这不是真的 (zhè bù shì zhēn de) "This is not true"

**Description of contrast:** This is a constructional opposition between affirmative and negative attribution, using the copula 是 and its negation 不是. The opposition is systematic and applies across all truth-domain predicates, representing the basic grammatical contrast between assertion and denial.

**Contrast type:** Constructional  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

## OPPOSITION 34: Resultative vs. Potential Complement Construction

**Construction A:** Verb + Truth-domain complement (resultative)  
**Construction B:** Verb + 得/不得 + Truth-domain complement (potential)  
**Example A:** 说对了 (shuō duì le) "said correctly, got it right"  
**Example B:** 说得对 (shuō de duì) "can say correctly, be right in saying"

**Description of contrast:** This constructional opposition contrasts achieved results (resultative) with potential or ability to achieve results (potential). The potential form inserts 得 (positive potential) or 不 (negative potential) between verb and complement, systematically deriving potential meanings from resultative constructions.

**Contrast type:** Constructional  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]

---

## OPPOSITION 35: Positive vs. Negative Potential Construction

**Description of contrast:** This constructional opposition within the potential complement pattern contrasts positive potential (得) with negative potential (不), expressing ability versus inability to achieve a result. This is a systematic grammatical opposition applicable across all verb-complement combinations.

**Contrast type:** Constructional  
**Epistemic status:** [CORE]
